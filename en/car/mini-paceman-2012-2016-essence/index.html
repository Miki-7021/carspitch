MINI Paceman

NEGOTIATE Reliability 58/100 — moyen

Summary

The **MINI Paceman (R61)** is a 3-door coupe crossover, directly derived from the MINI Countryman (R60). Launched in late 2012, it positions itself as a more dynamic and stylized alternative, sacrificing some practicality (strict 4-seater, rear seat access) in favor of a sloping roofline and 'go-kart' like road handling, true to the brand's DNA. It benefited from a very light facelift (LCI) in 2014 (new gauges, minor aesthetic touches, Euro 6 compliance for some engines). Due to lack of commercial success, it was not renewed. Mechanically, it inherits the 'Prince' petrol engines (co-developed by PSA and BMW) in their updated versions (N16/N18), and BMW diesel blocks (N47). Although more reliable than the previous generation (R56 phase 1), these engines require meticulous maintenance.

Weaknesses 5

  • Rear passenger space and difficult access (3 doors, strict 4-seater)
  • Very firm ride comfort, especially with 18 or 19-inch wheels
  • Almost non-existent rear visibility
  • Limited boot volume (330 liters) and high loading sill
  • High maintenance costs and temperamental mechanical reliability

Check before buying

  • **Oil level (Petrol):** Prince engines naturally consume oil. A lack of oil destroys the chain tensioner.
  • **Timing chain noise:** Cold knocking on petrol engines (tensioner) or scraping/cricket noise on N47 diesels (slack chain).
  • **FRM (Footwell Module):** Manages lighting and windows. If defective (often after a battery voltage drop), windows or headlights act erratically.
  • **ALL4 Transmission:** Check tire wear. If front and rear tires are of different brands or have very uneven wear, the transfer case may have suffered (replacement cost > 2500€).
  • **High-Pressure Pump (Cooper S/JCW):** Unstable idle when cold, loss of power, or engine light (fuel pressure fault code).

Handling

  • Go-Kart Feeling Driving Pleasure — Enjoy a sporty and engaging driving experience. Despite its crossover stature, the Paceman maintains remarkable agility, precise steering, and firm road holding, offering unparalleled driving pleasure.

Safety

  • Optimal Safety for All Passengers — Travel with peace of mind thanks to a robust structure and state-of-the-art safety equipment. The Paceman achieved excellent results in crash tests, effectively protecting adults and children.

Braking

  • Total Control with Efficient Braking — Benefit from a high-performance braking system, ensuring short stopping distances and excellent resistance. You maintain control in all circumstances, for safer and more serene driving.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Enjoyable Petrol Engines — The N16 and N18 petrol engines offer a perfect combination of liveliness and smoothness. Whether it's the 1.6 Cooper or the powerful 1.6T Cooper S and JCW, they guarantee strong acceleration and exceptional driving pleasure.

Comfort

  • Distinctive Cabin and Refined Finish — The Paceman's interior stands out for its originality and quality finish. Enjoy an excellent driving position and a unique atmosphere, true to MINI's premium spirit.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Range Rover Evoque Coupé (more luxurious, more expensive), Volkswagen Scirocco (lower, FWD), Nissan Juke (less premium, 5 doors but quirky look). - **North America:** Range Rover Evoque Coupe, Hyundai Veloster (for its atypical side). - **Asia:** Audi Q2 (arrived later, 5 doors but premium urban crossover spirit).

Verdict

The MINI Paceman is a 'love at first sight' purchase. It's a niche vehicle whose design and road handling are its main selling points. As a used car, **caution is advised**. Avoid diesel engines (N47) as the risk of timing chain breakage is too high and costly. Focus on petrol versions (Cooper S or JCW for driving pleasure), but demand a clear service history, with oil changes every 10,000 to 15,000 km maximum. A well-maintained Paceman will offer a lot of enjoyment, but keep a budget aside for temperamental peripherals (thermostat, HP pump, coils).
