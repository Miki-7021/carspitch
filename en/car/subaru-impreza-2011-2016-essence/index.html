Subaru Impreza

IV (GJ) · 2011 – 2016

NEGOTIATE Reliability 74/100 — bon

Summary

The fourth-generation Subaru Impreza (GJ chassis for the sedan, GP for the 5-door hatchback) marks a major technical turning point for the brand. It abandons the historic EJ engine family (belt-driven) in favor of the new FB generation (timing chain), designed to improve fuel efficiency and reduce emissions. It also introduces the Lineartronic continuously variable transmission (CVT), replacing the antiquated 4-speed automatic gearbox. Still equipped with symmetrical all-wheel drive (Symmetrical AWD) as standard in almost all markets, it positions itself as a safe, spacious compact car that is formidable in precarious grip conditions. Although its design is considered conservative and its soundproofing could be improved, it shines with its overall robustness, provided attention is paid to the early teething problems of the first FB engines (2012-2014).

*Note: From this generation onwards, the sporty WRX and STI versions began to detach from the Impreza nameplate to become a standalone model (VA chassis from 2014).*

**Overall reliability rating: Good (post-2014), Average (2012-2013)**

Safety

  • Benchmark Passive and Active Safety — The Subaru Impreza IV achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult and child protection, confirming its commitment to the safety of all occupants.
  • Exceptional Visibility — With its slim pillars and large windows, the Impreza offers remarkable all-around visibility, facilitating maneuvers and contributing to a safer and more pleasant driving experience.

Handling

  • Exceptional Road Handling — Thanks to its low-center-of-gravity Boxer engine and standard Symmetrical AWD, the Impreza offers remarkable stability and predictability, ensuring safe and reassuring road holding in all circumstances.

Braking

  • Effective and Controlled Braking — The Impreza IV's braking system is designed for solid and predictable performance. It ensures safe stops and competitive braking distances, even in varied conditions, boosting driver confidence.

Performance

  • Unrivaled All-Season Grip — Subaru's signature Symmetrical AWD provides formidable traction and stability in rain, snow, or on slippery roads, allowing you to drive with complete confidence, whatever the weather.

Practicality

  • Optimal Cabin Space and Comfort — Enjoy generous interior space, especially in the rear seats, offering significant comfort for all passengers and great versatility for your daily commutes or getaways.

Reliability

  • Reliable and Robust Petrol Engines — The Impreza IV's petrol engines, particularly from the 2015 model year onwards, benefit from an excellent reputation for robustness and reliability, ensuring long-term peace of mind.
