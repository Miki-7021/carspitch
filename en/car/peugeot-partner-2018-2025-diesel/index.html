Peugeot Partner

III (K9) (2018–2025) · 2018 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The Peugeot Partner III, known by the internal code K9, is the third generation of the lion brand's leisure activity vehicle/van, launched in 2018. It shares its EMP2 platform with its Stellantis group cousins (Citroën Berlingo III, Opel Combo Life/Cargo, Toyota Proace City/Verso, and later the Fiat Doblò). For passenger versions, Peugeot chose to rename it Rifter, while the Partner name is retained for utility versions. This generation stands out with a more assertive design, a modernized interior integrating the i-Cockpit (for passenger versions), and a significant improvement in comfort and onboard technologies compared to the previous generation. It is available in two lengths (Standard and Long) and offers gasoline, diesel, and, since 2021, a 100% electric version (e-Partner). Overall, it is a practical, versatile, and comfortable vehicle, but its reliability can vary depending on the engine and maintenance.

  • Reliability of the AdBlue system on 1.5 BlueHDi
  • Timing belt issues on 1.2 PureTech (models before 2020)
  • Quality of some interior plastics (especially on utility versions)
  • Limited range for the e-Partner on long journeys
  • Some electronic bugs (infotainment, driving aids)

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for timing belt replacements and AdBlue interventions)
  • AdBlue system operation (warning lights, error messages) on diesels
  • Condition of the timing belt on 1.2 PureTech (request a visual inspection if possible)
  • Absence of engine warning lights or error messages on the dashboard
  • Air conditioning and heating operation
  • Tire and brake wear (especially on utility or heavily loaded versions)
  • Battery and charging system condition on e-Partner

Verdict

The Peugeot Partner III (K9) is an excellent vehicle for those seeking versatility, space, and comfort. However, the choice of engine is crucial for long-term reliability.

* **For urban and suburban use** with short trips, the **e-Partner** is the most reliable and economical choice to use, provided that the range meets your needs. The **1.2 PureTech** can be considered, but requires increased vigilance regarding the timing belt maintenance history, especially on models before 2020. * **For road, highway, or intensive professional use**, the **1.5 BlueHDi** remains a very relevant option for its fuel efficiency and torque. However, it is imperative to ensure that the AdBlue system has been maintained and, ideally, that it has benefited from the latest updates or component replacements. A history of AdBlue failures should prompt caution or significant negotiation.

Overall, the Partner III is a vehicle to **NEGOTIATE** if you opt for a diesel with AdBlue history or a PureTech without proof of timing belt replacement. It can be a **BUY** if the history is clear, if recalls have been performed, and if you choose the electric version or a well-maintained diesel.
