Summary

The Mercedes-Benz A-Class (W177 generation for the hatchback, V177 for the sedan) marks a major technological breakthrough with the introduction of the MBUX infotainment system. Positioned in the premium compact segment, the **220** version represents the upper core range, offering an excellent compromise between performance and daily comfort. It is available in petrol (A 220) and diesel (A 220 d), with 4MATIC all-wheel drive as an option. The 2022 facelift brought slight aesthetic tweaks, an update to the MBUX system, and the integration of 48V mild-hybridization on the petrol engine.

Strengths 5

  • Onboard technology and benchmark MBUX system
  • Very rewarding and modern interior presentation
  • Driving pleasure and performance of the 2.0L block (both petrol and diesel)
  • Controlled consumption on the A 220 d version
  • Availability of 4MATIC all-wheel drive

Weaknesses 5

  • Parasitic noises and rattles in the cabin (B-pillars, dashboard) unworthy of the brand
  • Cylinder head reliability on early M260 petrol models (2018-2020)
  • Frequent electronic bugs on 2018 and 2019 model years
  • Firm comfort on AMG Line trims without adaptive suspension
  • Dual-clutch gearbox jerks at low speeds

Check before buying

  • Complete maintenance history in the Mercedes-Benz network (crucial for warranty coverage)
  • Absence of check engine light or cold shaking on the A 220 petrol (compression test recommended if in doubt about the cylinder head)
  • Fluidity of DCT gearbox shifts (no shocks when downshifting)
  • Full operation of both MBUX system screens
  • Absence of oil leaks around the turbocharger (M260)

Verdict

The Mercedes A-Class 220 (W177) is a very attractive premium compact, driven by a spectacular interior design and high-performance 2.0L engines. However, the 2018 to 2020 model years require caution: MBUX electronic bugs and, above all, cases of cylinder head failure on the M260 petrol engine require an impeccable maintenance history. The A 220 d diesel (OM654q) is an excellent surprise, reliable and very economical. For a worry-free petrol purchase, favor a model produced after late 2020, or ideally the 2022 facelifted version which corrects early defects and improves assembly quality.
