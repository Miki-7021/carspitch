Summary

The second-generation Audi Q3, known by the internal code F3, was launched in 2018, succeeding the first generation (8U). This premium compact SUV has established itself as a pillar of the Audi range, offering a blend of modern design, advanced technologies, and versatility. Based on the Volkswagen Group's MQB platform, it shares many components with models like the Volkswagen Tiguan or Skoda Kodiaq, but stands out with a typically Audi interior finish and driving experience.

The Q3 F3 benefited from a complete redesign compared to its predecessor, with sharper lines, a more imposing octagonal Singleframe grille, and a fully digital interior (Audi Virtual Cockpit) as standard or optional, depending on the trim levels. It is also available in a Sportback version, with a more dynamic, coupé-like profile. Although no major facelift has occurred to date (2024), software updates and powertrain evolutions have been introduced over the years.

**Expert's Overall Rating:** The Q3 F3 is a well-designed vehicle, offering an excellent compromise between comfort, dynamism, and practicality. Its general reliability is good, placing it in the upper average of the premium segment, provided rigorous maintenance. Petrol powertrains, especially the 2.0 TFSI, are safe bets.

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for S tronic gearboxes and diesels with AdBlue)
  • Functionality of the MMI system (touchscreen, Virtual Cockpit)
  • Condition of tires and brakes (especially on quattro and RS Q3 versions)
  • Absence of illuminated engine or AdBlue warning lights
  • Condition of GPF/DPF (for 1.5 TFSI and all TDI)
  • For PHEVs: state of charge and electric range, charging history

Verdict

The Audi Q3 F3 is an excellent choice for anyone looking for a premium compact SUV, combining style, technology, and impeccable build quality. Its overall reliability is very good, especially with the 2.0 TFSI petrol engines, which are safe bets. The 2.0 TDI diesel versions are also very reliable for high-mileage drivers, provided the AdBlue system and DPF are monitored.

**Buying advice:**

* **For mixed/dynamic use:** Opt for the 40 or 45 TFSI (2.0 L petrol) with quattro transmission and S tronic gearbox. These are the most balanced and reliable versions in the long term. * **For urban/suburban use:** The 35 TFSI (1.5 L petrol) is sufficient and more economical. If you can recharge regularly, the 45 TFSI e (PHEV) is an interesting option to reduce fuel costs, but its mechanical complexity should be considered. * **For high-mileage drivers:** The 35 or 40 TDI (2.0 L diesel) are perfect for long journeys, offering torque and range. Ensure that the AdBlue system has been meticulously maintained.

Regardless of the choice, a complete service history and a thorough inspection are essential. Manufacturer recalls must have been carried out. The Q3 F3 is a safe investment that will retain good resale value.
