CUPRA Ateca

I · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The CUPRA Ateca is the very first model launched by the CUPRA brand following its emancipation from SEAT in 2018. It is a sporty compact SUV based on the SEAT Ateca. Initially offered with a single, radical 300 hp 2.0 TSI engine coupled with the 4Drive AWD system, it received a facelift in 2020 (technological and aesthetic update). In 2023, to broaden its customer base, CUPRA introduced more sensible engines (150 hp 1.5 TSI and 190 hp 2.0 TSI).

**⚠️ IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:** As an expert, it is crucial to emphasize that the **CUPRA Ateca has NEVER been marketed with a diesel engine**. Since the brand's vocation is sporty, only gasoline blocks were selected. If you are looking for a diesel Ateca (TDI), you must absolutely turn to the **SEAT Ateca**. To meet your request, the reliability data for the Volkswagen group's 2.0 TDI (available on the SEAT Ateca) will still be included for informational purposes in this sheet.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional performances and dynamic handling (300 hp version)
  • Everyday versatility (roominess and trunk space preserved compared to the SEAT Ateca)
  • Very satisfactory overall mechanical reliability (mature EA888 Gen3 block)
  • Very rich standard equipment (especially after the 2020 facelift)
  • Flawless traction thanks to the 4Drive system

Weaknesses 5

  • No diesel offering at CUPRA (penalizing for very heavy drivers)
  • High fuel consumption on the 300 hp 2.0 TSI in sporty driving (easily > 11 L/100km)
  • Very firm damping, even in Comfort mode (19-inch wheels)
  • High maintenance cost (optional Brembo brakes are exorbitantly priced to replace)
  • Disappointing quality of some interior plastics before the 2020 facelift

Check before buying

  • Clear maintenance history: oil changes for the DSG gearbox (60,000 km) and the Haldex differential (30,000 km / 3 years) are non-negotiable.
  • Coolant level: a drop indicates a leak from the water housing/thermostat (repair cost: approx. €600-800).
  • Brake condition: if the vehicle is equipped with Brembo front brakes (4-piston calipers, drilled rotors), check the wear. Replacing rotors + pads often exceeds €1,500.
  • Tire wear: camber and sporty driving can lead to asymmetrical or premature tire wear (often Pirelli P Zero or Hankook S1 evo3).
  • Presence of the engine cover: check if it has been removed or replaced following the official recall.

Verdict

The CUPRA Ateca is an excellent choice for anyone looking for a compact SUV that is both family-friendly and truly sporty. The 300 hp 2.0 TSI block (EA888 Gen3) has reached an excellent level of maturity and proves to be very reliable, provided that peripheral maintenance (DSG, Haldex) is scrupulously respected. The absence of a diesel engine under the CUPRA badge is a deliberate choice; heavy drivers will have to turn to the SEAT Ateca 2.0 TDI, which is just as practical but lacks the explosive temperament of the CUPRA. The 150 and 190 hp versions, which arrived late, are good alternatives for a sporty look with reduced running costs.
