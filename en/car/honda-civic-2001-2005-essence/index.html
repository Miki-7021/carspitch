Honda Civic

VII (EP/EU/ES) · 2001 – 2005

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

All Honda models

Summary

The 7th generation Honda Civic (EP chassis for the 3-door, EU for the 5-door, ES for the 4-door sedan, and EM for the coupe) marks a radical break in the model's history. Honda abandoned its famous double wishbone front suspension in favor of a more compact MacPherson system, allowing for an exceptionally spacious cabin with a flat rear floor. The design of the hatchback versions (EP/EU) almost resembles a small MPV, with a gear lever originally positioned on the center console. While purists criticized the loss of some sportiness on standard models, this generation distinguished itself by its practicality, comfort, and the legendary reliability of its petrol engines (D and K series). The Type R (EP3) version with its 200 hp K20A2 engine is now considered one of the best 'hot hatches' of all time. A facelift occurred in late 2003, bringing headlights with integrated turn signals, new bumpers, and improvements to the electric power steering (EPS).

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional reliability of petrol engines (D and K series)
  • Record-breaking interior space and flat rear floor (3/5-door versions)
  • Ergonomics of the gear lever on the center console
  • Healthy and safe road handling
  • Driving pleasure and performance of the Type R (EP3) version
  • Reduced maintenance cost for standard petrol versions

Weaknesses 6

  • Chronic fragility of manual gearbox bearings (1.4 and 1.6 petrol)
  • Electric power steering (EPS) lacking feel and prone to noises/clunks
  • Imperfect sound insulation (road and wind noise on the highway)
  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.7 CTDi diesel engine (Isuzu origin)
  • Quality of some interior plastics that scratch easily
  • Firm suspension, especially on facelifted and Type R models

Check before buying

  • Manual gearbox: listen for a growling noise in neutral (clutch released) that disappears when the clutch is pressed (symptom of a faulty primary shaft bearing).
  • Steering: turn from lock to lock while stationary to detect clunking noises in the rack (EPS).
  • Petrol engines: check that the idle is stable and that there are no oil leaks around the VTEC solenoid.
  • Type R (EP3): check that 2nd gear engages without crunching at high revs (synchro).
  • IMA Hybrid: check the battery charge status on the dashboard and the absence of an illuminated IMA warning light.
  • Running gear: wear of front wishbone bushings and anti-roll bar drop links (noises over bumps).

What sets it apart 6

Reliability

  • Legendary Petrol Engine Reliability — Enjoy unparalleled peace of mind thanks to the exceptional robustness of Honda's petrol engines (D and K series), renowned for their longevity and unwavering reliability.

Practicality

  • Record-Breaking Space and Clever Modularity — Discover an astonishingly spacious interior with a flat rear floor, offering generous room for all passengers and modularity that simplifies transporting your belongings.

Safety

  • Sound and Secure Road Handling — Benefit from balanced and predictable road holding, ensuring a serene and secure drive in all conditions, whether in the city or on the open road.

Economy

  • Controlled Running Costs — The standard petrol versions of the 7th generation Civic stand out for their reasonable fuel consumption and reduced maintenance costs, ensuring an optimized automotive budget.

Performance

  • Dynamic Driving Pleasure — Appreciate responsive and enjoyable petrol engines, offering noticeable dynamism for daily commutes and exhilarating performance for thrill-seekers, especially with the legendary Type R.

Comfort

  • Intuitive Ergonomics and Daily Comfort — The interior layout, particularly the gear lever on the central console, offers driver-focused ergonomics, complemented by appreciable comfort for all occupants.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Volkswagen Golf IV / V (superior trim but less reliable petrol engines), Ford Focus Mk1 (excellent chassis), Toyota Corolla E120 (equivalent reliability, more classic), Peugeot 307 (more comfortable but electronic issues).
  • Japan / Asia: Toyota Corolla, Nissan Sunny/Sentra, Mazda 323 / Mazda 3 (BK).
  • North America: Toyota Corolla (E130), Nissan Sentra, Ford Focus, Chevrolet Cavalier/Cobalt.

Verdict

The 7th generation Honda Civic is an extremely rational and reliable used car choice, provided you exclusively target petrol engines. The 1.4i and 1.6i units are truly indestructible workhorses, perfect for economical daily use, although it is imperative to check for any gearbox bearing noise before purchase. The Type R (EP3) is a future collector's item, offering pure mechanical sensations that are now hard to find. On the other hand, avoid the 1.7 CTDi diesel, which accumulates costly breakdowns, and be very cautious with IMA hybrid versions whose batteries are now at the end of their life. Opt for a facelifted petrol model (2004-2005) for better finish and recalibrated steering.
