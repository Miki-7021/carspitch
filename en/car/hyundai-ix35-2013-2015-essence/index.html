Summary

The Hyundai ix35 (Generation I - Type LM), marketed under the name Tucson in other markets (North America, Korea), received a facelift at the end of 2013. This mid-cycle refresh brought LED daytime running lights, new taillights, improved interior materials, and, above all, suspension adjustments to compensate for the excessive firmness of phase 1. Mechanically, the petrol range evolved with the introduction of direct injection (GDI) and the gradual replacement of the aging 2.0 Theta II block by the new 'Nu' engine family. It is a compact SUV with an assertive design (Fluidic Sculpture), offering an excellent price/equipment ratio. Its overall reliability is good, although the direct injection petrol engines require rigorous maintenance.

Strengths 5

  • Very competitive price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Generous roominess and trunk volume (465 liters)
  • Overall reliability of the 2.0 CRDi diesel and 1.6 GDI petrol engines
  • Design that ages well (especially with the facelift's LED lights)
  • Original 5-year manufacturer warranty (guarantee of good initial dealer maintenance)

Weaknesses 5

  • Suspension comfort still a bit firm despite the facelift improvements
  • Hard interior plastics that scratch easily
  • Electric power steering lacking feel and precision
  • Soundproofing could be improved on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Naturally aspirated petrol engines lacking low-end torque compared to turbocharged competitors

Check before buying

  • ABS/ESP system: Check if the recall for short-circuit risk has been performed (see recalls section).
  • Steering: Turn the steering wheel from left to right while stationary. A 'clunk' indicates wear of the steering flex disc (inexpensive part but requires labor).
  • GDI Engines (Petrol): Unstable idle or loss of power = intake valve fouling.
  • Manual gearbox: Catching on 1st and 2nd gears when cold (gearbox oil change often beneficial).
  • Electronics: Operation of the reversing camera (lens that becomes opaque or loose connection) and the multimedia system.

Official recalls

  • [2023-02-09] Braking system (ABS): The anti-lock braking system (ABS) modules can develop an internal electrical short circuit, which can increase the risk of fire. Risk of injury. (Concerns passenger ix35s).
  • [2022-01-11] 12V socket: The 12V socket can overheat when the tire repair kit compressor is used. This could increase the risk of fire. (Concerns several models including the ix35).

Verdict

The facelifted Hyundai ix35 (2013-2015) is a rational and reassuring used choice. When targeting a petrol engine, the 1.6 GDI 135 hp is the most relevant choice for Europe: it is reliable, quiet, and economical to maintain (timing chain), provided that the valve fouling linked to direct injection is monitored. The 2.0 GDI offers more reach but consumes significantly more. It is imperative to check that the safety recalls (especially the ABS module) have been performed at a dealership. Avoid imported versions equipped with the 2.4 Theta II, known for its serious connecting rod weaknesses.
