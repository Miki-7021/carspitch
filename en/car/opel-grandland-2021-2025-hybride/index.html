NEGOTIATE Reliability 70/100 — bon

Summary

The Grandland, in its facelifted version launched at the end of 2021 (often referred to as 'Generation II' or 'new Grandland' after dropping the 'X' suffix), is a compact SUV that shares its EMP2 V2 platform (or V3 according to sources, an evolution of EMP2) with its Stellantis group cousins, notably the Peugeot 3008 and Citroën C5 Aircross. This facelift brought a new front end incorporating Opel's 'Vizor', a modernized interior with a dual digital screen, and an update to driving aids. The Grandland positions itself as a more understated and pragmatic alternative to French SUVs, focusing on comfort and a clean presentation. Its overall reliability is average for the category, with specific points of attention for shared powertrains, particularly the plug-in hybrids which are central to the offering.
