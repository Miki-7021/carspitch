DS DS5

I (M32) · 2012 – 2015

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

All DS models

Summary

The DS5 (marketed under the name Citroën DS5 during this phase 1) is an atypical premium sedan halfway between a shooting brake and a crossover. Based on the PF2 platform (that of the Peugeot 3008 I), it stands out with its spectacular design and aeronautics-inspired cabin. While the finish quality is a clear step up for the brand, this first generation was heavily criticized for its very firm comfort (jittery suspension) and average rear roominess. The gasoline range is built exclusively around the notorious 1.6 THP block (Prince engine).

Strengths 5

  • Spectacular and timeless exterior design
  • Premium interior presentation (watchstrap leather, cockpit)
  • Very careful soundproofing
  • Excellent reliability of the 2.0 HDi 160 block
  • Rich equipment from mid-level trims

Weaknesses 5

  • Extremely firm suspension comfort (jittering at low speeds)
  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.6 THP 155 (EP6)
  • Slow and jerky BMP6/ETG6 automated gearbox (on e-HDi and Hybrid4)
  • Almost non-existent rear visibility
  • Disappointing rear roominess and trunk volume for its size

Check before buying

  • Metallic noise on cold start on THP gasoline engines (sign of a stretched timing chain).
  • Oil level on THP engines (frequent overconsumption).
  • Smooth gear shifts on the AM6 automatic gearbox (Aisin): an oil change is recommended every 80,000 km although sold as 'lubricated for life'.
  • UREA warning light on for BlueHDi versions (emissions control system fault).
  • Premature wear of front tires (heavy front axle, especially on 2.0 HDi and Hybrid4).

Official recalls

  • [2024-02-19] Takata airbags: The inflator structure in the driver and front passenger airbags may deteriorate. If the airbag deploys, it could propel fragments of the inflator casing and injure occupants. Affects C3, C4, DS3, DS4, DS5 models.
  • [2015] Wiring harness: Risk of the starter harness rubbing, which could lead to a short circuit (models produced up to 2013).
  • [2014] Seat mounting: Inspection and retightening of the front seat mountings on a series of 2012-2013 models.

What sets it apart 7

Design

  • Spectacular Exterior Design — The DS5 stands out with its bold look and avant-garde lines, offering a unique and timeless presence on the road.

Comfort

  • Premium and Innovative Interior — Step into an aeronautics-inspired cockpit, featuring a premium presentation and high-quality materials, such as 'bracelet' leather, for an immersive driving experience.
  • Serene Journeys — The meticulously crafted soundproofing of the cabin guarantees a calm and soothing atmosphere, transforming every journey into a moment of relaxation, away from external noise.

Practicality

  • Generous Equipment — From intermediate trims upwards, the DS5 offers very comprehensive equipment, ensuring comfort and technology are at your fingertips for all occupants.

Safety

  • Certified Maximum Safety — The DS5 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult and child protection, ensuring peace of mind on the road.
  • Powerful and Stable Braking — Benefit from an efficient and reassuring braking system, offering excellent power and sufficient bite for short and stable stops, even in emergency situations.

Performance

  • Precise Road Handling — The DS5 offers rigorous road holding and dynamic behavior. Its precise steering and stable chassis provide a sense of agility and security.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 508 I, Audi A4/A5 Sportback, Volkswagen CC, BMW 3 Series Gran Turismo.
  • China: Volkswagen Magotan, Buick Regal, Ford Mondeo.

Verdict

The DS5 Phase 1 is a passion-driven car with an assertive design, but it requires compromises on comfort. If you are looking for a gasoline engine, caution is advised: the 1.6 THP 155 is to be avoided due to its congenital flaws (timing, oil consumption). The THP 200 offers better drivability but requires meticulous maintenance. The most rational choice for this heavy model paradoxically remains the 2.0 HDi 160 diesel, coupled with the 6-speed automatic gearbox, which offers remarkable reliability and torque suited to the vehicle's weight.
