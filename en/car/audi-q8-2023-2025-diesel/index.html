Summary

The first-generation Audi Q8 (type 4M8) received a facelift in late 2023. The flagship of the brand with the four rings' coupe SUVs, it is based on the MLB Evo platform shared with the Porsche Cayenne and the Volkswagen Touareg. This facelift mainly brings aesthetic tweaks (new Singleframe grille, HD Matrix LED headlights with laser technology, customizable OLED taillights) and an update to the infotainment system.

Diesel Focus (TDI): Although the market is shifting towards hybridization, the 3.0 V6 TDI (available as 45 TDI and 50 TDI) remains the most coherent engine for this behemoth weighing over 2.2 tonnes. Thanks to its truck-like torque (up to 600 Nm) and its 48V mild-hybrid system (MHEV), it offers remarkable highway range and excellent towing capabilities, while keeping its real-world fuel consumption around 8 to 9 L/100 km. It is the ultimate pragmatic choice for high-mileage drivers.

Strengths 5

  • Imperial driving pleasure of the 3.0 V6 TDI on long trips (torque and soundproofing)
  • Fit and finish quality at the top of the category
  • Comfort of the adaptive air suspension (almost mandatory option)
  • Generous interior space despite the sloping roofline
  • Cutting-edge lighting technology (Matrix LED / OLED)

Weaknesses 5

  • Hit-and-miss reliability of the 48V mild-hybrid system (BSG alternator-starter)
  • Mammoth weight (over 2.2 tonnes) penalizing agility and wear of consumables
  • All-touch ergonomics (dual screen) distracting while driving
  • Elitist purchase and maintenance prices
  • Tiptronic gearbox sometimes hesitant when restarting (lag) on the TDIs

Check before buying

  • Replacement history of the 48V alternator-starter (BSG): if it hasn't been changed, it's a sword of Damocles.
  • Tire wear (especially in 22 or 23 inches) and brakes: the vehicle's weight destroys them quickly.
  • Operation of the air suspension: check that it raises and lowers without excessive compressor noise.
  • Body panel alignment and operation of driving aids (cameras, sensors) very sensitive to impacts.
  • On TDIs: check for the absence of an AdBlue/DPF warning light, a sign of too much urban use.

Official recalls

Rappel Conso (France) & NHTSA (United States)

  • [2025-12-19] Seat belts (Rappel Conso / NHTSA 25V777000): On Q7, Q8, RS Q8, SQ8 (2025 models). A rivet may be missing on the driver and front passenger seat belt buckle, resulting in insufficient restraint in the event of an accident. Risk of injury.
  • [2025-12-19] Rearview camera (NHTSA 25V900000): Software error that may prevent the rearview camera image from displaying.
  • [2025-11-07] High-Voltage Battery (Rappel Conso): On Q7, Q8, A8 (PHEV versions). The cell modules of the high-voltage battery can overheat during charging, leading to a fire risk.
  • [2024-07-12] Braking (Rappel Conso): Specific to the Q8 e-tron. Excessive tightening of the screws on the brake pipes could cause them to break and leak brake fluid.
  • [2024-04-22] Side airbags (Rappel Conso / NHTSA 23V868000): On Q7, Q8, RS Q8 (2023-2024). The driver's side airbag may be insufficiently secured to the seat frame, preventing it from deploying properly.
  • [2024-04-03] Passenger classification sensor (NHTSA 24V251000): Defect in the front passenger seat occupancy sensor affecting airbag deployment.
  • [2024-01-12] Seat frame (Rappel Conso): On Q7, Q8, A4, A5, Q3. A defective weld on the rear frame of the front seat can break in the event of an accident.

Verdict

The facelifted Audi Q8 (2023+) remains one of the most accomplished coupe SUVs on the market. In its V6 TDI version (45 or 50 TDI), it offers exceptional versatility for high-mileage drivers, combining inexhaustible torque and reasonable fuel consumption for its size. However, the reliability of its 48V electrical network (the infamous BSG alternator-starter) is a major black mark that tarnishes the record of all the group's V6 engines (TDI and TFSI).

Buying advice: Negotiate. If you opt for a TDI, demand a crystal-clear history, check if the BSG has already been replaced by the revised version, and favor a model benefiting from a manufacturer's extended warranty. Avoid TDIs if your usage is mostly urban (guaranteed DPF/EGR clogging).
