Nissan Murano

III (Z52) · 2015 – 2024

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The third-generation Nissan Murano (Z52) is a midsize SUV with a bold design, characterized by its V-Motion grille and floating roof. Focused on ride comfort and interior space rather than sportiness, it stands out with its excellent 'Zero Gravity' seats and well-crafted interior finish. Primarily designed for the North American market, it was not officially sold in Western Europe. Although its V6 engine block is renowned for being indestructible, the overall reliability of the model is heavily dependent on the maintenance of its continuously variable transmission (CVT Xtronic). The model was discontinued at the end of 2024.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (soft suspension)
  • 'Zero Gravity' seats among the best in the category
  • Legendary reliability of the V6 engine block (VQ35DE)
  • Decent interior finish that ages well
  • Distinctive exterior design that has aged well

Weaknesses 5

  • Fragile CVT Xtronic transmission if neglected (prohibitive replacement cost)
  • Dated, slow, and bug-prone infotainment system (Nissan Connect)
  • High fuel consumption (V6)
  • Sluggish handling, no dynamic pretensions
  • Rear visibility limited by the C-pillar design

Check before buying

  • CVT gearbox maintenance history (invoices for oil changes every 60,000 km required)
  • Absence of jerking, excessive slipping, or whining during acceleration (symptoms of a dying CVT)
  • Traces of oil leaks under the vehicle at the transfer case (AWD models)
  • Verification of the execution of recalls concerning the ABS actuator (fire risk)
  • Proper operation of the reversing camera and touch screen

Verdict

The Nissan Murano (Z52) is an excellent highway cruiser, offering top-tier comfort and a V6 engine with proven reliability. However, it is imperative to approach a used purchase with caution due to the CVT Xtronic transmission. If the vehicle has more than 100,000 km and no proof of a CVT gearbox oil change is provided, it is better to walk away. Additionally, ensure that the important recalls concerning the ABS module have been performed. It is a rational purchase for comfort enthusiasts, provided you are uncompromising about the maintenance history.
