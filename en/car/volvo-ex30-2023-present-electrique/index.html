Summary

The Volvo EX30 (Generation I) is a 100% electric urban SUV (B-segment) that marks a turning point for the Swedish manufacturer. Based on the modular SEA (Sustainable Experience Architecture) platform developed by parent company Geely (shared with the Smart #1, Smart #3, and Zeekr X), it positions itself as the entry-level model in the Volvo range. Its minimalist design results in an extremely streamlined interior, where almost all controls and instrumentation are grouped on a single 12.3-inch central touchscreen powered by Google Automotive. While its performance and road handling are praised, its early life has been accompanied by numerous software bugs (infotainment, driving aids) typical of highly digitized vehicle launches. Overall score: 7.5/10 (penalized by the all-touch ergonomics and early software errors).

Strengths 5

  • Very competitive price/performance ratio (especially in Twin Motor version)
  • Successful exterior design and ideal compactness for the city (4.23 m)
  • Native Google Automotive system (Google Maps, Spotify, Assistant very responsive when not buggy)
  • Dynamic road handling and suspension comfort
  • Reduced production carbon footprint (recycled materials)

Weaknesses 5

  • Dangerous all-touch ergonomics (mirror adjustment, glove compartment, and gear selection on the central screen)
  • Recurring software bugs during the first year of commercialization (2023-2024)
  • Very restricted rear legroom and average trunk (318 L)
  • Absence of instrument cluster behind the steering wheel or head-up display (HUD)
  • Impractical power window controls (shared front/rear buttons in the center)

Check before buying

  • System software version: check that no OTA update is pending.
  • Central screen responsiveness: test reverse gear engagement (rearview camera) to ensure there is no latency.
  • Tire wear: particularly on the Twin Motor version (very high torque).
  • Key functionality: test opening/closing with the NFC card on the B-pillar and with the smartphone app.
  • Driving aids: check for absence of error messages related to cameras or front radar.

Verdict

The Volvo EX30 is a mechanically very well-designed vehicle thanks to Geely's SEA platform, offering first-class performance and decent efficiency. However, its launch was marred by immature software architecture, causing numerous electronic inconveniences. If you are considering a recent used purchase, demand that the vehicle is up-to-date with the latest software fixes (OTA). The *Single Motor Extended Range* version is the most rational choice. Be aware, however, of the radical ergonomics (everything on the central screen) which requires significant adaptation time and will not suit all drivers.
