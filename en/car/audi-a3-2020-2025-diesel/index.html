Audi A3

8Y · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

Summary

The fourth-generation Audi A3 (code name 8Y) is based on the Volkswagen Group's MQB Evo platform. Launched in 2020 and facelifted in 2024, it marks a technological shift with the massive introduction of digital features (MIB3 system) and mild hybridization (MHEV). Although the market is moving towards gasoline and hybrid, **this report places particular emphasis on the Diesel (TDI) offering**. Equipped with the 2.0 TDI engine from the EA288 evo family, the A3 8Y benefits from the *Twin-Dosing* system (double AdBlue injection), which makes it extremely clean and remarkably efficient for high-mileage drivers. While the perceived quality of some interior plastics has slightly decreased compared to the 8V generation, road handling and sound insulation remain at the top of the premium category.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional efficiency of the 2.0 TDI (range often exceeding 1,000 km)
  • Sound insulation and ride comfort (especially with adaptive suspension)
  • Perfect driving ergonomics and seating position
  • Superb and safe road handling
  • Very high-performance Matrix LED lighting technology

Weaknesses 5

  • Recurring electronic and software bugs on 2020-2022 models (MIB3 infotainment)
  • Regression in the quality of some plastics in the lower part of the cabin
  • S tronic gearbox (DQ200 dry clutch) on smaller gasoline engines sometimes hesitant
  • Prohibitive purchase prices and option costs
  • Sometimes abrupt intrusion of driving aids (Lane Assist)

Check before buying

  • History of software updates (multimedia system, SOS module, engine management)
  • On TDIs: absence of AdBlue/Engine warning light and trip history (avoid ex-city vehicles)
  • On S tronic (TDI and 2.0 TFSI): gearbox oil change invoice at 60,000 km
  • Smooth operation of the 'shift-by-wire' gear selector (small joystick)
  • Condition of the engine cover (often removed following a recall for fire risk, must have been replaced)

What sets it apart 12

economy

  • Unrivaled Diesel Efficiency — The 2.0 TDI (EA288 evo) engine offers record-breaking fuel consumption, often below 5L/100km, and exceptional range, frequently exceeding 1000 km. Its advanced design with the Twin-Dosing system ensures remarkable environmental cleanliness.

comfort

  • Premium Ride Comfort and Soundproofing — The Audi A3 8Y stands out with its state-of-the-art soundproofing and superior ride comfort, especially with adaptive suspension. The driving ergonomics are perfect, offering a serene and pleasant experience over all distances.

safety

  • Benchmark Passive Safety — The Audi A3 8Y achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, demonstrating exceptional protection for adult and child occupants, thanks to a robust structure and advanced restraint systems.
  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — Enjoy excellent, powerful, and consistent braking performance, with remarkable resistance to fade. Integrated assistance systems ensure short stopping distances and maximum stability in all circumstances.

Performance

  • Imperial Road Handling — The Audi A3 8Y's chassis offers imperial road handling that is very safe and predictable. Its road holding is stable, balanced, and agile, with precise steering that inspires confidence even in the most demanding conditions.

reliability

  • Mechanical Reliability of the 2.0 TDI — The 2.0 TDI (EA288 evo) engine is renowned for its very high level of mechanical robustness. It benefits from the latest technological advancements, ensuring durability and peace of mind for high-mileage drivers.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: BMW 1 Series (F40 - excellent alternative in 118d/120d), Mercedes-Benz A-Class (W177 - very technological, powerful OM654q diesel engines), Volkswagen Golf 8 (shares the same technical base and TDI engines), Peugeot 308 III (BlueHDi 130, less powerful but very comfortable). - **North America**: Mercedes-Benz A-Class Sedan, BMW 2 Series Gran Coupé, Acura Integra. - **Asia/China**: BMW 1 Series Sedan (F52), Mercedes-Benz A-Class Sedan (Z177).

Verdict

The Audi A3 8Y is a well-developed premium compact, whose main weakness lies in the software bugs of its first years of production (2020-2022). **If you are looking for a Diesel, the 2.0 TDI (EA288 evo) is a masterpiece of efficiency.** It offers superior driving pleasure, negligible fuel consumption on the highway, and very high mechanical reliability thanks to the correction of defects from older generations (notably via Twin-Dosing). Prioritize a 35 TDI (150 hp) model from after 2022, whose electronics will have been stabilized, and ensure that its past use corresponds to a 'high-mileage driver' profile to avoid a clogged DPF.
