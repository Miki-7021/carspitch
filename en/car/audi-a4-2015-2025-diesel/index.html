Audi A4

B9 (8W) · 2015 – 2025

BUY Reliability 83/100 — bon

Summary

The Audi A4 B9 (internal code name 8W) is based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MLB Evo platform. Launched in late 2015 and facelifted in 2019 (Phase 2), it marks a major technological leap compared to the B8, notably introducing the famous *Virtual Cockpit* and an array of driving aids. Although the market is shifting towards gasoline and hybrid, the A4 B9 remains a quintessential long-distance cruiser, where diesel engines (TDI) excel with their exceptional fuel efficiency and comfort on long journeys. The finish quality is an absolute benchmark in its category. Overall reliability is very good, with the early issues of previous generations (TFSI oil consumption, fragile flywheels) having been largely corrected, although diesel emission control systems (AdBlue/DPF) remain points of concern in urban use.

Strengths 5

  • Top-tier finish and assembly quality
  • Remarkable sound insulation, especially with acoustic glazing
  • Extremely low fuel consumption for 2.0 TDI engines (often < 5L/100km on highways)
  • Ergonomics of the MMI multimedia system and Virtual Cockpit
  • Superb and safe road handling (especially with Quattro)

Weaknesses 5

  • Maintenance cost and spare parts prices (Audi network)
  • AdBlue emission control system (TDI) prone to costly failures
  • Recurrent coolant leaks (water pump/thermostat) on 4-cylinder engines
  • Average rear passenger space and boot volume for its size (4.73 m)
  • S-tronic gearbox sometimes hesitant at low speeds (jerks)

Check before buying

  • S-tronic gearbox (DL382) service history: mandatory oil change every 60,000 km, otherwise risk of mechatronics failure.
  • Coolant level: a drop indicates a water pump leak (TDI and TFSI).
  • AdBlue system (TDI) operation: no warning light on the dashboard, history of refills.
  • Metallic noises on cold start on V6 engines (TDI and TFSI): sign of timing chain tensioner wear.
  • Onboard electronics: test all functions of the MMI, Virtual Cockpit, and driving aids (cameras, sensors).

The 2.0 TDI engine is particularly renowned for its longevity and ability to cover many miles with complete peace of mind.

Comfort

  • Exceptional Cabin Comfort and Quality — Immerse yourself in an interior where the quality of finish and assembly reaches the top of its class. The remarkable soundproofing, enhanced by available acoustic glazing, transforms every journey into an experience of absolute serenity, even over long distances.

Economy

  • Remarkable Fuel Consumption — The Audi A4 B9's diesel engines stand out for their exemplary fuel efficiency. The 2.0 TDI, in particular, boasts extremely low consumption, often less than 5L/100km on the highway, allowing you to make substantial savings on your long journeys.

Safety

  • Certified Maximum Safety — The Audi A4 B9 guarantees optimal protection for all its occupants. It achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 87% for child protection. It was also recognized as an IIHS Top Safety Pick+ for several model years.
  • Excellent Road Handling and Braking — Enjoy imperial road handling, combining stability and agility, for a feeling of safety and confidence at all times. The braking system is remarkably effective, offering short stopping distances, measured at approximately 48.8 meters from 113 km/h, and excellent fade resistance.

Practicality

  • Advanced Technologies and Intuitive Ergonomics — The Audi A4 B9 integrates cutting-edge technological innovations, such as the revolutionary Virtual Cockpit and an MMI multimedia system with intuitive ergonomics. These sophisticated features enrich the driving experience and facilitate access to all functionalities.

Alternatives

* **Europe:** BMW 3 Series (F30 then G20 - the dynamic benchmark), Mercedes-Benz C-Class (W205 then W206 - comfort-oriented), Volvo S60/V60 (premium Swedish alternative), Volkswagen Passat B8 (technical cousin, more spacious). * **North America:** Lexus IS (legendary reliability), Acura TLX, Genesis G70, BMW 3 Series. * **Asia:** BMW 3 Series Li, Mercedes C-Class L (long-wheelbase versions).

Verdict

The Audi A4 B9 is a safe bet on the used car market. It has successfully corrected the serious reliability flaws of the previous generation (B8). **For high-mileage drivers, the 2.0 TDI is an excellent choice**: it is mechanically indestructible, quiet, and exceptionally fuel-efficient. However, a budget should be kept for potential AdBlue system quirks or preventive water pump replacement. V6 TDI versions offer superior driving pleasure but require a well-stocked wallet for maintenance. If you drive a lot in the city, avoid diesel and opt for a 2.0 TFSI, which is very reliable in this generation (Gen3/Gen4). Prioritize facelifted models (post-2019) which benefit from more reliable electronics and a modernized multimedia system.
