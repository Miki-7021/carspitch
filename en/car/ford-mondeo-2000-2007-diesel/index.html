NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

Summary

The third-generation Ford Mondeo (chassis codes B4Y for the 4-door sedan, B5Y for the 5-door sedan, and BWY for the wagon) was launched in 2000 and marked a turning point for Ford in Europe. Based on a new CD132 platform, it stood out with a more modern design, a spacious interior, and significantly improved finish quality compared to its predecessor. Renowned for its excellent road handling, precise steering, and comfort, it quickly became a benchmark in the family executive car segment. Two restyling phases took place: a first in 2003 (headlights, grille, interior) and a second in 2005 (taillights, some interior details). Despite its dynamic qualities, the reliability of certain diesel engines, particularly the early TDCi versions, may have tarnished its image, although the gasoline engines are generally very robust.
