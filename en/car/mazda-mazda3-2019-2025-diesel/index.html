Mazda Mazda3

IV (BP) · 2019 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The fourth-generation Mazda3 (internal code BP) marks a decisive turning point for the Hiroshima-based manufacturer, with a clearly assumed positioning towards the premium segment. Featuring the refined 'Kodo' design, it offers interior finish quality that rivals German benchmarks. Technically, it is based on the Skyactiv-Vehicle Architecture platform. Although global demand has massively shifted towards gasoline (with the innovative e-Skyactiv G and X engines), Mazda initially offered a 1.8 Skyactiv-D diesel engine. The latter had a very short career in Europe (withdrawn in late 2020/early 2021 depending on the country) due to taxation and the decline in popularity of diesel, but it remains relevant in the used car market for high-mileage drivers. Overall, the Mazda3 BP is an extremely reliable car, penalized only by reduced rear passenger space and limited rear three-quarter visibility on the 5-door version.

Weaknesses 5

  • 1.8L diesel engine lacking punch (116 hp) compared to the very capable chassis
  • Cramped rear passenger space and feeling of confinement (reduced glazing on the 5-door)
  • Very poor rear three-quarter visibility (reversing camera essential)
  • Paintwork (especially Soul Red Crystal) very sensitive to stone chips
  • 'Piano Black' plastics on the center console that scratch at the slightest touch

Check before buying

  • On the 1.8 Diesel: Oil level on the dipstick (if above the 'X', the oil is diluted by diesel fuel, immediate oil change required)
  • On the 1.8 Diesel: Trip history (avoid models that have only been driven in the city)
  • Operation of electric folding mirrors (internal gears break on early model years)
  • Parasitic noises (squeaks) from the head-up display (HUD) or A-pillars on degraded roads
  • Condition of the 12V battery (a weak battery generates malfunction alerts for i-Activsense driving aids)

reliability

safety

  • Certified State-of-the-Art Safety — Designed for protection, the Mazda3 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult (98%) and child (87%) protection, and was designated an IIHS Top Safety Pick+.

braking

  • Absolute Braking Control — Enjoy complete confidence thanks to a remarkably efficient braking system. The Mazda3 offers powerful, progressive, and enduring brakes, ensuring very short stopping distances for optimal safety.

handling

  • Dynamic Driving and Precision — Experience engaging driving with a balanced chassis, surgically precise steering, and the G-Vectoring Control Plus (GVC Plus) system that optimizes stability and agility in every turn.

comfort

  • Premium Interior and Acoustic Comfort — Step into a cabin whose finish quality rivals the premium segment. Sound insulation has been significantly improved, offering superior serenity and acoustic comfort on all your journeys.

economy

  • Optimized Fuel Efficiency for Long Journeys — The 1.8 Skyactiv-D diesel engine is designed for high-mileage drivers, offering very low fuel consumption on highways, which translates into significant long-term savings.

Design

  • Refined and Timeless Kodo Design — Be captivated by the Mazda3's distinctive and timeless 'Kodo' design, a refined aesthetic that turns heads and asserts its premium positioning on the road.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Volkswagen Golf VIII 2.0 TDI (diesel benchmark, but electronic issues), Peugeot 308 1.5 BlueHDi (camshaft chain and AdBlue problems), Toyota Corolla Hybrid (very frugal gasoline alternative). - **North America**: Honda Civic (more spacious), Toyota Corolla, Volkswagen Jetta. - **Asia / Oceania**: Subaru Impreza, Honda Civic, Hyundai i30.

Verdict

The Mazda3 (BP) is one of the most reliable compact cars on the market. If you are specifically targeting the 1.8 Skyactiv-D diesel engine, it is a wise choice **only** if you are a high-mileage driver. Its immense advantage lies in the absence of an AdBlue system, saving you from the ruinous failures that plague contemporary French or German diesels. However, it does not tolerate exclusive urban use (risk of clogging and oil dilution). If your annual mileage is less than 20,000 km, confidently opt for the gasoline engines (e-Skyactiv G or X), which are models of reliability and fuel efficiency.
