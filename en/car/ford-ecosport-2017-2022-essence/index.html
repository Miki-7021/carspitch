Ford EcoSport

II (B515) · 2017 – 2022

AVOID Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The **Ford EcoSport II (B515) Phase 2** (2017-2022) represents a major facelift of Ford's small urban SUV. Initially designed for emerging markets (Brazil, India), this model was extensively reworked to meet European and North American standards. This phase 2 corrects the main flaws of the first iteration: the interior is transformed with a dashboard inspired by the Fiesta Mk8 integrating the excellent SYNC 3 multimedia system, soundproofing is improved, and the backpack-style spare wheel disappears as standard in Europe. Above all, the catastrophic PowerShift dual-clutch automatic gearbox is replaced by a classic torque converter gearbox (6F15). Although it remains based on the old Fiesta Mk6 platform, it offers a highly appreciated high driving position. It was gradually replaced by the Ford Puma, which is much more modern.

Strengths 5

  • Highly responsive and ergonomic SYNC 3 infotainment system (Apple CarPlay / Android Auto)
  • High driving position, genuine feeling of driving an SUV
  • Ultra-compact size (4.10 m) ideal for city parking
  • Availability of a true all-wheel drive (AWD) on certain versions
  • Reliability of the torque converter automatic gearbox (finally rid of the PowerShift)

Weaknesses 5

  • Side-opening trunk door: very impractical in the city (requires a lot of clearance)
  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.0 EcoBoost (wet belt) if maintenance is not meticulously followed
  • Very firm suspension comfort, juddering at low speeds
  • High fuel consumption of the 1.0 EcoBoost compared to the competition
  • Aging platform (2008 Fiesta Mk6 base), clumsy road handling

Check before buying

  • 1.0 EcoBoost maintenance history: demand invoices proving the use of the correct oil. Walk away if maintenance has been neglected.
  • Oil pressure warning light: if it comes on or flashes on a 1.0 EcoBoost, the strainer is probably clogged (engine in danger).
  • Trunk door hinges: check for sagging or squeaking due to the weight of the door (especially if a spare wheel is present).
  • Front suspension noises: premature wear of the wishbone silent blocks and anti-roll bar drop links.
  • SYNC 3 system operation: check that the touch screen does not freeze (a simple USB update often solves the problem).

Verdict

The Ford EcoSport Phase 2 is a vehicle of contrasts. Its 2017 facelift significantly improved its interior presentation and onboard technology, finally making it competitive. The abandonment of the PowerShift gearbox is also excellent news for reliability. However, in Europe, it is predominantly powered by the 1.0 EcoBoost, whose wet belt design is a real sword of Damocles. If you are looking for a small Ford SUV, the Puma is infinitely superior in every respect.

**Buying advice:** AVOID the 1.0 EcoBoost unless the belt has just been changed and the history is crystal clear. NEGOTIATE the diesel version (1.5 TDCi/EcoBlue) for high mileage drivers. BUY without hesitation in non-European markets if equipped with the naturally aspirated 1.5 Ti-VCT or 2.0 Ti-VCT engines, which are very robust.
