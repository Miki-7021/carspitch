Summary

The **4th generation Lexus RX (code name AL20)**, launched in late 2015 for the 2016 model year, marks a stylistic departure with a highly angular design and a gaping trapezoidal grille. Positioned as an E-segment premium SUV, it prioritizes absolute comfort, quiet operation, and unparalleled reliability over sporty driving dynamics.

In Europe, it was primarily sold in its hybrid version, the **RX 450h**, although a 4-cylinder turbo (RX 200t, renamed RX 300) was offered. In North America and other markets, the naturally aspirated V6 (RX 350) remained a pillar. In 2018, Lexus introduced an extended 7-seater version (**RX L**). The late 2019 facelift (2020 model year) corrected the model's biggest flaw by replacing the unergonomic multimedia interface (Remote Touch) with a touchscreen compatible with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, while also stiffening the chassis. It is one of the most reliable vehicles in its class worldwide.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability (often ranked #1 among premium SUVs)
  • Very high level of ride comfort and soundproofing
  • Flawless build and assembly quality
  • Very reasonable fuel consumption for the RX 450h for a 2.1-ton SUV
  • Very high resale value
  • Proven hybrid system with no clutch/starter/alternator wear

Weaknesses 5

  • Pre-2020 infotainment system (touchpad) is frustrating and distracting
  • Clumsy road handling, body roll in corners (not sporty)
  • Engine revving effect (rubber-band effect) on the 450h during hard acceleration (e-CVT)
  • Very cramped 3rd-row space on the extended version (RX L)
  • Trunk volume penalized by the sloping roofline and the battery (on 450h)

Check before buying

  • Lexus network maintenance history (essential to benefit from the extended hybrid system warranty up to 10 years).
  • Condition of the 12V (accessory) battery: a weak battery generates multiple phantom error messages on the dashboard.
  • Tire and brake wear: the high weight of the vehicle (especially the hybrid) puts stress on the running gear, although regenerative braking saves the brake pads.
  • Operation of the central screen and touchpad on 2016-2019 models.
  • Verification of recall campaigns (especially Denso fuel pumps).

What sets it apart 14

Reliability

Comfort

  • Absolute Comfort and Silent Operation — Enjoy a very high level of ride comfort and remarkable sound insulation. The impeccable manufacturing and assembly quality creates a serene and luxurious atmosphere on board.

Economy

  • Optimized Fuel Consumption (RX 450h) — Despite its size, the RX 450h offers surprisingly reasonable fuel consumption for a 2.1-ton SUV, thanks to its efficient hybrid system, especially in urban and suburban environments.
  • Exceptional Resale Value — Thanks to its reputation for reliability and manufacturing quality, the Lexus RX maintains a very high resale value, making it an excellent long-term investment.

Safety

  • High-Level Passive and Active Safety — The Lexus RX achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult (91%) and child (87%) protection. It was also named an IIHS Top Safety Pick+ in 2016.
  • Powerful and Progressive Braking — The RX's braking system is powerful and offers excellent control. The pedal feel is reassuring and consistent, ensuring competitive stopping distances for an SUV of this size.
  • Stable and Reassuring Road Holding — Focused on comfort and stability, the Lexus RX offers safe and predictable road holding. It effectively absorbs road imperfections and inspires confidence, even if the steering prioritizes smoothness over sportiness.

Alternatives

**Europe:** * **BMW X5 (F15 / G05):** More dynamic, excellent PHEV engines (xDrive40e / xDrive45e), but more temperamental electronic and pneumatic reliability. * **Volvo XC90 (Gen 2):** Sleek design, more spacious 7-seater version, plug-in hybrid (T8), but aging multimedia system and uneven engine reliability (2.0 twin-charged). * **Mercedes-Benz GLE (W166 / V167):** Great comfort, excellent diesels, but significantly higher maintenance costs.

**North America / Asia:** * **Acura MDX:** Excellent V6, sportier handling (SH-AWD), more usable 3rd row, but interior finish is a step down. * **Genesis GV80:** Recent Korean premium alternative, very luxurious, but less hindsight on long-term reliability.

Verdict

The 4th generation Lexus RX (AL20) is a **highly recommended** purchase, particularly in its RX 450h hybrid version. It is aimed at drivers who prioritize absolute peace of mind, plush comfort, and low long-term running costs over sporty performance. For a used purchase, favor a facelifted model if possible (from late 2019 / 2020 model year) to benefit from the touchscreen and Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, which transform the onboard experience. Ensure that recalls (especially on fuel pumps) have been performed and demand a complete Lexus maintenance history to take advantage of the extended hybrid warranty.
