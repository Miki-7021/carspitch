Honda e

Summary

The Honda e (Generation I) is a 100% electric city car with a highly endearing neo-retro design, conceived as a technological showcase for the brand. **Crucial expert note: the Honda e was developed exclusively as a battery electric vehicle (BEV). It has never existed with a diesel, petrol, or hybrid engine.** It is a rear-wheel drive vehicle equipped with a small 35.5 kWh battery, favoring urban dynamism and contained weight at the expense of range (approximately 210-222 km WLTP). Its interior is spectacular with its digital dashboard composed of five screens and its standard camera side mirrors. Its career was halted in early 2024 due to marginal sales, weighed down by an elitist price and very limited versatility.

Strengths 5

  • Exemplary mechanical and electrical reliability (highly advanced Honda technology)
  • Unique neo-retro design and immense charm
  • Exceptional maneuverability (4.3-meter turning radius)
  • Premium finish quality and interior presentation
  • Quiet operation and driving pleasure in the city

Weaknesses 5

  • Very low real-world range (often under 150 km in winter)
  • Disappointing fast charging speed (often peaks around 40-50 kW real-world)
  • Tiny trunk (171 liters)
  • Very high purchase and used price for the road performance
  • Slightly high electrical consumption for its size

Check before buying

  • Condition of the 12V battery (accessories): number 1 cause of breakdowns on urban EVs
  • Proper functioning of the 5 dashboard screens and camera side mirrors (prohibitive replacement cost)
  • Rear tire wear (RWD + electric torque)
  • Condition of the alloy wheels (highly exposed to curbs in urban use)
  • State of Health (SOH) report of the high-voltage battery (although it is thermally regulated and ages very well)

Verdict

The Honda e is a future 'collector's item'. It is a luxurious urban mobility object, extremely endearing and endowed with exemplary reliability, typical of Honda. **It is imperative to remember that it does not exist in diesel.** Its purchase must be made with full knowledge of its limits: it is absolutely not a long-distance cruiser. Its restricted range and small trunk confine it to the role of a second car for daily commutes. If this usage matches your needs, it is a highly recommended purchase (BUY verdict) for its mechanical peace of mind and originality, provided you negotiate the price, which is often overvalued on the used market.
