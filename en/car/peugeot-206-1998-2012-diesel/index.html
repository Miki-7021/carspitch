Peugeot 206

· 1998 – 2012 (production principale en Europe)

NÉGOCIER Reliability 65/100 — moyen

Summary

The Peugeot 206 is one of the most successful European cars in history, with over 8.3 million units produced worldwide. Launched as the successor to the legendary 205, it revolutionized the B-segment with its sharp, feline styling and excellent chassis dynamics. Available in multiple body styles including a 3-door and 5-door hatchback, a practical SW (estate), and the popular CC (Coupé-Cabriolet) with a folding hardtop. In 2001, the 206 transitioned to a multiplexed electrical architecture, which modernized its features but introduced early electrical gremlins. In 2009, it was heavily facelifted and sold as the '206+' in Europe to serve as a budget alternative alongside the newer 207.

Strengths 6

  • Timeless and attractive exterior design
  • Excellent chassis dynamics and handling
  • Very low running costs and cheap spare parts
  • Huge availability on the used market

Weaknesses 6

  • Notorious rear axle trailing arm bearing failures (expensive repair)
  • COM2000 indicator stalk unit failures on multiplexed models
  • Poor driving position (steering wheel not adjustable for reach)
  • Cheap interior plastics prone to rattling and scratching
  • Cramped rear seats in the hatchback
  • 1.6 HDi 110 engine is a reliability nightmare

Check before buying

  • **Rear Axle (Train Arrière):** Look at the car from the rear. If the rear wheels tilt inwards at the top (/ \) or if you hear creaking/clunking over bumps, the trailing arm bearings are destroyed. Requires a full axle rebuild or replacement.
  • **COM2000 Unit:** Test all stalks. If the indicators drop down on their own, or if the airbag light is on (due to a broken clock spring), the entire unit behind the steering wheel needs replacing.
  • **Head Gasket (TU Petrols):** Check the front right corner of the engine block (alternator side) for oil weeping. Very common on 1.1i and 1.4i engines.
  • **Clutch Cable:** The automatic adjuster on the clutch cable frequently fails, leading to a heavy pedal or difficulty engaging gears.
  • **CC Roof Mechanism:** On Coupé-Cabriolet models, check the roof operation multiple times. Listen for struggling hydraulic pumps and check the boot for water ingress.

Alternatives

The primary rival is the **Renault Clio II**, which offers a more comfortable ride and better interior quality, though slightly less engaging handling. The **Volkswagen Polo IV** provides superior build quality and cabin refinement but is more expensive to buy and maintain. The **Ford Fiesta Mk5** is the closest match in terms of driving dynamics and chassis fun. The **Opel Corsa C** is a sensible, cheap-to-run alternative but lacks the character of the 206.

Verdict

The Peugeot 206 is a modern classic that democratized sharp handling and stylish design in the late 90s and 2000s. Today, it serves as an excellent first car or cheap daily commuter, provided you buy the right version. The 1.4i petrol and 2.0 HDi diesel are the picks of the bunch for longevity. However, buyers must be extremely vigilant regarding the rear axle condition and electrical gremlins (COM2000). Avoid the 1.6 HDi entirely. If you find a well-maintained example with a healthy rear axle, it represents fantastic value for money.
