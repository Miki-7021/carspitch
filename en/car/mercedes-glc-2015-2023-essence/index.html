Mercedes GLC

X253 · 2015 – 2023

NEGOTIATE Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The **Mercedes-Benz GLC (X253 Generation / C253 for the Coupé)** succeeded the GLK in 2015. Based on the C-Class (W205) MRA platform, it marked a complete stylistic break from its predecessor, adopting fluid lines and a luxurious interior. Becoming the brand's global best-seller, it benefited from a **major restyling (facelift) in 2019**, introducing the MBUX infotainment system, new driving aids, and a complete overhaul of its engine range (transition from M274/OM651 blocks to M264/OM654 with 48V mild hybridization). Although generally well-designed, the first model years (2015-2018) require particular vigilance, especially regarding the M274 petrol engines and the air suspension.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with air suspension)
  • Interior finish quality and sound insulation
  • Highly performing MBUX multimedia system (post-2019)
  • Excellent 9G-Tronic automatic gearbox (once updated)
  • Wide choice of engines (Petrol, Diesel, PHEV, AMG)

Weaknesses 5

  • Variable reliability of M274 petrol engines (2015-2018)
  • Very high maintenance and spare parts cost
  • Expensive to repair air suspension (Air Body Control)
  • Cracking of center console plastics (especially pre-2019)
  • High weight penalizing agility and consumption (especially PHEV)

Check before buying

  • Clear service history (Mercedes network or independent specialist)
  • Metallic noises on cold start on GLC 250/300 (M274)
  • Smooth operation of the 9G-Tronic gearbox (no jerks at low speed)
  • Vehicle sagging when stopped (sign of air suspension leak)
  • Tire condition (frequent asymmetrical wear due to camber adjustment)

Official recalls

  • **Starter (2017-2018):** Risk of overheating and fire of the starter current limiter if the engine is blocked. Addition of a fuse.
  • **Steering (2017):** Electrical harness of the steering column may break, leading to unintended deployment of the driver's airbag.
  • **Panoramic roof (2021):** The polycarbonate panel between the windshield and the sunroof may detach while driving (bonding defect).
  • **eCall (2021):** Software bug that may transmit an erroneous position to emergency services in case of an accident. OTA or dealership update.
  • **Takata Airbags (Various years):** Replacement of defective gas generators (depending on markets).

  • Ultra-Performing Braking System — Benefit from remarkable braking capability, ensuring short stopping distances and exemplary stability even in emergency situations, for total vehicle control.

Verdict

The Mercedes GLC X253 is an extremely well-rounded premium SUV, offering undeniable comfort and status. If you are targeting a **petrol** engine, absolutely prioritize a **facelifted (post-2019)** model equipped with the M264 engine (GLC 200 or 300). Pre-2019 petrol models (M274) present a significant mechanical risk (piston pin) which can be very expensive. For sportiness enthusiasts, the GLC 43 AMG (V6) is an excellent compromise, offering superior reliability to the 63 AMG's V8 while maintaining (relatively) reasonable running costs. Avoid air suspension if you are buying a high-mileage model without a warranty.
