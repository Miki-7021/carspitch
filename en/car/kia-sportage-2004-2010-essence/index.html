Kia Sportage

Summary

The **second-generation Kia Sportage (Type KM)** marks a radical turning point for the model. Abandoning the ladder frame chassis of the first generation for a unibody structure shared with the Hyundai Tucson (JM), it transitioned from a rustic off-roader to a versatile and comfortable compact SUV.

Launched in 2004, it benefited from two minor facelifts (late 2007 and late 2008) improving interior finish, sound insulation, and lighting signature. Positioned as an economical alternative to market leaders (Toyota RAV4, Honda CR-V), it stands out for its spaciousness, generous standard equipment, and overall robustness, particularly with gasoline engines. Although its road handling is sluggish and its interior presentation very plastic, it remains an excellent choice on the used market today for a small budget, provided you monitor the fuel consumption of its older-design atmospheric engines.

**Expert's overall rating:** A pragmatic, no-frills SUV, whose reliability of the gasoline engines (Beta II and Delta) largely compensates for its thirst at the pump.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of gasoline engines (2.0 and V6)
  • Generous interior space and modularity (flat-folding rear seats)
  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Appreciable suspension comfort on degraded roads
  • Effective all-wheel drive (BorgWarner) on snow or trails

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high fuel consumption for gasoline engines
  • Slow, dated 4-speed automatic gearbox (BVA4) that penalizes fuel consumption
  • Quality of interior plastics (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds (wind and road noise)
  • Sluggish road handling, significant body roll

Check before buying

  • **Engine subframe corrosion:** Critical point on this model, especially if the vehicle has been driven in mountainous or snowy regions (salt).
  • **ABS/ESP module (HECU):** Check if the recall for short-circuit risk has been performed.
  • **Timing belt (Gasoline):** Demand invoices. Breakage leads to engine destruction (interference engines).
  • **Driveshaft (4WD versions):** Check the condition of the center bearing (growling noise or vibrations under the floor during acceleration).
  • **Flywheel and clutch (Diesel manual gearbox):** Test for absence of judder on startup and knocking at idle.

Verdict

The Kia Sportage II (KM) is an extremely rational used car choice for anyone looking for a robust, spacious, and inexpensive SUV to buy. **With the gasoline engine (2.0i 141hp), it shines with almost irreproachable mechanical reliability**, far superior to the average diesels of its era. Its main drawback remains its outdated fuel consumption, especially if paired with the sluggish 4-speed automatic gearbox.

**Buying advice:** Opt for a 2.0 gasoline model with a manual gearbox, preferably post-facelift (2008+), with a clear service history (timing belt up to date). Imperatively inspect the underbody for rust on the front subframe. Avoid high-mileage diesels without a service history, as repair costs (turbo, flywheel, injectors) will exceed the vehicle's value.
