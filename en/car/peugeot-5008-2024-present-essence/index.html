Summary

The third-generation Peugeot 5008 (based on Stellantis' new STLA Medium platform) marks a major turning point. Extended to 4.79 m, it retains its family vocation with 7 seats as standard while adopting a more massive and aerodynamic silhouette. The big revolution is under the hood: total abandonment of diesel in favor of massive electrification (MHEV, PHEV, and BEV). The gasoline engine (now 48V micro-hybrid) inaugurates the 3rd generation of the 1.2 PureTech engine, which finally abandons the problematic wet belt for a timing chain. Although the vehicle is very recent, this technical evolution raises great hope for the improvement of overall reliability.

Strengths 5

  • Abandonment of the wet belt on the gasoline engine (finally a timing chain)
  • Exceptional spaciousness and retention of 7 seats even in the 100% electric version
  • Spectacular interior presentation (21-inch Panoramic i-Cockpit)
  • Very competitive announced range for electric versions (up to 660 km)
  • Significantly improved sound insulation and ride comfort

Weaknesses 5

  • Significantly increased weight (electric versions easily exceed 2.2 tons)
  • Total disappearance of diesel engines (penalizing for very high-mileage drivers towing loads)
  • Infotainment system ergonomics sometimes complex (haptic i-Toggles)
  • Absolute lack of hindsight on the reliability of the new e-DCS gearboxes (Punch Powertrain)
  • Prices significantly increased compared to the previous generation

Check before buying

  • Smooth operation of the e-DCS6 gearbox on the 1.2 Hybrid (absence of jerks at low speed)
  • Stability of the infotainment system (black screens, slow startup)
  • Body panel alignment and interior finishes (on the very first models off the production line)
  • Operation of driving aids (ADAS) which can be intrusive or generate phantom alerts

What sets it apart 6

Reliability

  • Optimized engine reliability with timing chain — The new generation 1.2 Hybrid 136 petrol engine now features a timing chain, a major advancement that ensures increased robustness and lasting peace of mind for your journeys.

Practicality

  • Generous habitability and 7 standard seats — Designed for families, the new 5008 offers exceptional interior space and retains its 7 standard seats, ensuring comfort and modularity for all your passengers and luggage.

Comfort

  • 21-inch Panoramic i-Cockpit for total immersion — Dive into a futuristic driving experience thanks to the spectacular 21-inch Panoramic i-Cockpit, combining aesthetics and technology for an intuitive and captivating interface.
  • High-level ride comfort and soundproofing — Every journey becomes a moment of relaxation thanks to significantly improved soundproofing and optimized ride comfort, offering remarkable serenity on board.

Economy

  • 48V mild-hybrid engine for efficient driving — Enjoy optimized fuel consumption and great driving smoothness, especially in the city, thanks to the 1.2 Hybrid 136 engine featuring 48V mild-hybrid technology.

Design

  • Modern and aerodynamic silhouette on STLA Medium platform — The 5008 III stands out with its bold design and massive yet aerodynamic silhouette, based on the new STLA Medium platform, a symbol of innovation and modernity.

Alternatives

Europe

  • Renault Espace VI : Its direct rival. Offers a very efficient 200 hp E-Tech Full Hybrid engine, but less space in the 3rd row and no 100% electric version.
  • Skoda Kodiaq II : A benchmark for spaciousness, still offers diesel (TDI) and PHEV powertrains with over 100 km of range.
  • Volkswagen Tayron : Tiguan Allspace replacement, very technologically advanced.
  • Nissan X-Trail : Original e-Power powertrain (electric powered by a gasoline generator), very pleasant for daily use.

Asia / North America

  • *The Peugeot 5008 is not sold in North America.* In Asia, it competes with models like the Toyota Highlander or the Hyundai Santa Fe.

Verdict

The Peugeot 5008 III breaks with the past. For buyers targeting a gasoline engine, the new 1.2 Hybrid 136 is the only option, but it's excellent news: the adoption of a timing chain erases the biggest fear associated with older PureTech engines. However, this vehicle inaugurates an entirely new platform (STLA Medium), new engines, new gearboxes, and a new electronic architecture.

Buying Advice: If you buy new, an extended warranty is highly recommended to protect against the inevitable electronic and mechanical "teething problems" of the first series. The potential is excellent, but caution remains advisable during the first year of production.
