Citroën C4

III · 2020 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The **third-generation Citroën C4 (C4 III)** marks a deliberate stylistic break, blending compact sedan, coupé, and SUV codes. Based on the Stellantis group's multi-energy CMP/e-CMP platform (shared with the Peugeot 208/2008), it stands out with exceptional ride comfort thanks to its progressive hydraulic cushion suspensions (Advanced Comfort) and plush seats.

However, its career is marred by the presence under its hood of the infamous 1.2 PureTech wet-belt petrol engines, whose reliability reputation has severely declined. The arrival of the micro-hybrid (MHEV) version with a timing chain in late 2023 finally corrects this issue. The C4 III is a very comfortable and atypical car, but it requires a rigorous selection of its engine to avoid financial pitfalls.

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of 1.2 PureTech engines (before the chain hybrid)
  • Slow and sometimes buggy multimedia system (especially before 2023)
  • Quality of some interior plastics (lower dashboard, door panels)
  • Limited rear headroom due to the sloping roofline
  • Highway range of the 136 hp electric version too limited

Check before buying

  • Service book for 1.2 PureTech: the slightest delayed oil change cancels Stellantis' coverage in case of failure.
  • Condition of the timing belt (cracks, fraying) via the oil cap on non-hybrid PureTechs.
  • UREA light or Engine Fault on BlueHDi (sign of a faulty AdBlue system).
  • Operation of the air conditioning and heat pump on ë-C4 versions.
  • Interior trim noises or rear axle creaks on 2020-2021 models.

Official recalls

  • **November 2022 (Software / Emissions)**: Engine control unit update on 1.2 PureTech to prevent abnormal emissions related to oil degradation.
  • **May 2021 (Safety)**: Recall on certain models manufactured in 2020 for non-compliant tightening of rear seat belt fixing screws.
  • **2023 (Electric)**: Reprogramming of the traction control unit on ë-C4 to prevent an unexpected propulsion cut-off while driving.
  • **Internal Note (Non-official safety recall)**: Extended coverage (10 years / 175,000 km) for the replacement of the 1.2 PureTech wet belt if the maintenance plan has been scrupulously followed.

What sets it apart 14

Comfort

  • Exceptional Ride and Seating Comfort — The Citroën C4 III redefines comfort with its Progressive Hydraulic Cushions suspension and plush Advanced Comfort seats, ensuring a most pleasant driving and travel experience. Careful soundproofing also contributes to a serene interior ambiance.

Safety

  • Proven Safety for All Passengers — With a 4-star Euro NCAP rating, the C4 III ensures solid protection. It scores 76% for adult occupant protection and an excellent 83% for child occupant protection, offering peace of mind on the road.
  • Efficient and Reassuring Braking — The Citroën C4 III offers reliable and safe braking performance, with stopping distances from 100 km/h typically measured between 38 and 40 meters, ensuring optimal vehicle control in all circumstances.

Performance

  • Stable and Serene Road Holding — Focused on well-being, the C4 III offers stable and predictable road behavior. Its Progressive Hydraulic Cushions suspension guarantees excellent absorption of road irregularities and controlled body roll, for a smooth ride.

Reliability

  • Hybrid 136 Engine with Timing Chain — Benefit from the new 1.2 Hybrid 136 engine, equipped with a robust timing chain, offering increased reliability and a redesigned concept for lasting peace of mind.

Economy

  • Efficiency and Controlled Consumption with Hybrid 136 — The 1.2 Hybrid 136 engine combines driving pleasure with reduced consumption, especially in urban environments, thanks to its advanced mild-hybrid technology, optimizing your fuel budget.

Practicality

  • Intuitive Ergonomics and Physical Controls — The C4 III's interior has been designed for ease of use, with a welcome return of physical controls for air conditioning, making access to essential functions simple and direct.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Peugeot 308 III (same engines, more dynamic styling), Renault Megane IV (very reliable 1.3 TCe), Volkswagen Golf VIII, Toyota Corolla (unbeatable hybrid reliability). - **Electric (Europe)**: Renault Megane E-Tech, MG4, Volkswagen ID.3. - **Emerging Markets**: Renault Arkana, Hyundai Elantra, Kia Cerato.

Verdict

The Citroën C4 III is a compact car full of qualities for those seeking absolute comfort. Unfortunately, its record is hampered by the classic 1.2 PureTech engines (100, 130, 155 hp) whose wet-belt design is a financial sword of Damocles.

**Our advice:** Avoid used 100% thermal petrol versions, unless they are heavily discounted and the belt has just been changed with a clear service history. Absolutely prioritize the new **1.2 Hybrid 136** version (with chain) that appeared in late 2023, or turn to the electric **ë-C4** version which, despite some early bugs, offers much superior mechanical reliability.
