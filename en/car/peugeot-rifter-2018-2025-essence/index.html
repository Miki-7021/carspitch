Peugeot Rifter

I (K9) (2018–2025) · 2018 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 67/100 — moyen

Summary

The first-generation Peugeot Rifter (internal code K9) was launched in 2018, succeeding the Partner Tepee. It falls within the leisure activity vehicle segment, offering a more modern and stylish alternative than its predecessors, with a SUV-inspired design and the integration of Peugeot's i-Cockpit. Sharing its EMP2 platform with the Citroën Berlingo, Opel Combo Life, and Toyota Proace City Verso, it stands out for its generous spaciousness, modularity, and comfort. It is available in Standard and Long versions. A 100% electric version, the e-Rifter, joined the range in 2021. Overall, the Rifter is a practical and versatile vehicle, but its reliability is mixed, particularly due to the PureTech petrol engines which have experienced recurring problems.

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for the 1.2 PureTech)
  • Condition and replacement date of the timing belt (1.2 PureTech)
  • AdBlue system operation (warning lights, error messages) on BlueHDi engines
  • Absence of leaks or abnormal noises from the high-pressure pump (1.2 PureTech)
  • General condition of the DPF and EGR valve (BlueHDi)
  • Operation of infotainment and electrical equipment
  • Condition of the traction battery and charging history (e-Rifter)

Verdict

The Peugeot Rifter is an excellent choice for families or professionals needing space, modularity, and practicality. However, the choice of engine is crucial for long-term reliability.

* **For predominantly urban or suburban use:** The **e-Rifter** is a very relevant option if the range meets your daily needs and you have access to a charging station. Its driving pleasure is undeniable. * **For mixed or road/highway use:** The **1.5 BlueHDi** engines are to be preferred. They are economical, powerful, and generally reliable, provided the AdBlue system is carefully monitored and regular DPF maintenance is performed. * **The 1.2 PureTech petrol engines:** Should be considered with the utmost caution. If you opt for this engine, it is imperative to ensure that the timing belt has been preventively replaced with an improved version, that the service history is impeccable with the recommended oil, and plan for a more frequent belt replacement budget. An extended warranty can be a plus. Post-2021 models are theoretically less at risk, but vigilance remains essential.

In all cases, a clear service history is essential. The Rifter offers excellent value for money for those who know how to choose their engine and take care of it.
