Mercedes A 180

· 2012 – 2018

NEGOTIATE Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The Mercedes-Benz A-Class (W176 generation) marks a radical break in the model's history. Gone is the small urban MPV (W168/W169); the W176 becomes a premium compact hatchback with a dynamic design, conceived to compete directly with the Audi A3 and BMW 1 Series. The **A 180** designation constitutes the entry/core range of the model, available in petrol (A 180) and diesel (A 180 CDI, renamed A 180 d in 2015) engines. The model benefited from a facelift in late 2015, bringing higher quality interior materials, a recalibrated suspension (the damping of the early phases being considered too firm), and the integration of Apple CarPlay/Android Auto. Overall, it is a highly sought-after vehicle on the used market, offering an excellent compromise between brand image, safety, and running costs, although the finish quality of pre-2015 models is sometimes below historical Mercedes standards.

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic and status-enhancing exterior design that ages very well
  • Very low fuel consumption on diesel versions (OM607)
  • Safe and precise road handling
  • Standard active safety equipment (emergency braking, drowsiness detection)
  • Excellent value retention on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Very firm comfort, especially with the sport chassis (AMG Line) and Run-Flat tires (pre-2015)
  • Quality of some interior plastics disappointing for a Mercedes (frequent squeaks and rattles on phase 1)
  • Limited rear passenger space and boot volume (341 liters)
  • Poor rear visibility (reversing camera almost essential)
  • 7G-DCT gearbox sometimes hesitant or jerky at low speeds

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history of the 7G-DCT automatic gearbox (invoices are essential)
  • Parasitic noises in the cabin (dashboard, door panels, central screen)
  • Condition of the DPF and EGR valve on diesels (check for the absence of engine warning light and jerks during acceleration)
  • Operation of the panoramic sunroof (drying seals, wind noise, seizing mechanism)
  • Front tire wear (the front axle is heavy and tends to wear out tires quickly)

Verdict

The Mercedes A 180 (W176) is an attractive premium compact that successfully democratized the brand with the star. If you are looking for a status-enhancing vehicle with a strong design, it is an excellent choice. **Favor the facelifted models (after late 2015)** to benefit from better suspension comfort and an improved interior finish.

The best mechanical choice is undoubtedly the **A 180 petrol version (122 hp)**, which is very reliable and versatile. The **A 180 d version (Renault 1.5 engine)** is a pragmatic and economical choice for high-mileage drivers, provided the timing belt maintenance is respected. Avoid diesels used exclusively in the city (risk of severe clogging) and be extremely vigilant about the maintenance history of the 7G-DCT automatic gearbox, as neglecting it can be very expensive.
