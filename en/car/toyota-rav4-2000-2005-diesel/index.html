Toyota RAV4

II (XA20) · 2000 – 2005

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

All Toyota models

Summary

The second-generation Toyota RAV4 (XA20) marked a decisive turning point in the history of compact SUVs. Larger, more refined, and more road-oriented than its predecessor (XA10), it shed its 'toy' image to become a versatile family vehicle, available in 3 and 5 doors. It was with this generation that Toyota introduced its common rail diesel engine (D-4D) in Europe, essential to counter emerging competition. Although the RAV4 XA20 benefits from Toyota's reputation for reliability, this reputation needs to be strongly qualified for the diesel engine (1CD-FTV), which bore the brunt of the first generations of high-pressure injection and modern emissions control. A facelift occurred in late 2003, bringing redesigned bumpers, clear headlights, and minor technical improvements.

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic and agile road handling (monocoque chassis)
  • Efficient permanent all-wheel drive (50/50 distribution with limited-slip center differential on 4WD)
  • Overall reliability of petrol engines (excluding automatic transmission and 1.8 segmentation)
  • Rear modularity (sliding, folding, and removable seats)
  • Compact size ideal for city driving (especially 3-door)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of 2.0 D-4D engine peripherals
  • Tiny boot on the 3-door version (150 liters)
  • Poor sound insulation (wind and road noise on the highway)
  • Side-opening tailgate (requires a lot of space at the rear)
  • Hard interior plastics prone to scratches

Check before buying

  • On the D-4D: Cold start, unstable idle, or stalling (sign of faulty SCV valves).
  • On the D-4D: Vibrations in the clutch pedal or metallic noise at idle (faulty dual-mass flywheel).
  • On manual transmissions: 5th gear popping out (known synchro/gearing issue on early models).
  • On automatic transmissions (Petrol): Smoothness of gear changes. Any jerking is unacceptable (faulty ECU/transmission).
  • On the 1.8 Petrol: Traces of blue smoke from the exhaust or very low oil level (segmentation issue).
  • Underbody: Traces of off-road impacts or corrosion on suspension arms.

What sets it apart 7

handling

  • Road mastery in all conditions — Enjoy a permanent 4x4 all-wheel drive system with 50/50 distribution and a limited-slip central differential, ensuring exceptional traction and enhanced safety on all types of surfaces.

Performance

  • Responsive and torquey D-4D Diesel Engine — The 2.0 D-4D engine delivers generous torque from low RPMs, guaranteeing dynamic acceleration and a pleasant driving experience, whether in the city or on the highway.

economy

  • Optimized Fuel Consumption — Thanks to its D-4D diesel engine, the RAV4 II benefits from controlled fuel consumption, allowing you to travel longer distances with a reduced fuel budget.

practicality

  • Modular and Versatile Interior — The cabin offers remarkable modularity with rear seats that slide, fold, and are even removable, adapting to all your transport needs, from passengers to bulky cargo.

comfort

  • A Refined and Road-Oriented SUV — This generation of the RAV4 was designed to offer increased comfort and a more refined driving experience, transforming it into a versatile and enjoyable family vehicle for long journeys.

maneuverability

  • Urban Agility and Easy Parking — Its compact size, especially in the 3-door version, makes it particularly agile in the city and greatly facilitates parking maneuvers, without compromising its road presence.

dynamism

  • Dynamic Driving Behavior — The RAV4 II's monocoque chassis provides dynamic and agile road handling, offering an engaging and secure driving experience.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Nissan X-Trail I (more spacious, good off-roader but fragile dCi), Honda CR-V II (petrol benchmark, very roomy, excellent i-CTDi engine appeared late), Land Rover Freelander I (comfortable but catastrophic reliability).
  • North America: Honda CR-V II, Subaru Forester SG (excellent AWD, beware of head gaskets), Ford Escape / Mazda Tribute.
  • Asia / Oceania: Mitsubishi Outlander I, Subaru Forester, Honda CR-V.

Verdict

The Toyota RAV4 XA20 is an excellent compact SUV... provided you choose the right engine. If you're considering the diesel (2.0 D-4D), extreme caution is advised. This engine has concentrated most of the costly failures (flywheel, SCV solenoid valves, turbo). Only buy a D-4D if the service history is clear and these parts have already been replaced. The best choice for peace of mind remains the 2.0 VVT-i petrol with a manual gearbox, which offers reliability worthy of Toyota's reputation, although it is more fuel-intensive.
