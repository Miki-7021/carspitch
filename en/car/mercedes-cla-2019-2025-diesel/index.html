Summary

The second-generation Mercedes-Benz CLA, chassis code C118, was launched in 2019, succeeding the first CLA (C117). Based on the MFA2 platform shared with the A-Class, it positions itself as a compact and elegant four-door coupé, offering a more stylish alternative to traditional sedans. A Shooting Brake version (X118) is also available. The model received a minor facelift in 2023, bringing subtle aesthetic tweaks and technological updates. The CLA C118 stands out with its assertive design, modern interior featuring the MBUX system, and a varied range of powertrains, from efficient petrol and diesel engines to plug-in hybrid and sporty AMG versions. Overall, it's a vehicle that emphasizes style and technology, with solid mechanical reliability on certain powertrains, particularly Mercedes diesels.

  • Limited rear passenger space (headroom and legroom)

  • M282 (1.3L) petrol engines less reliable than others
  • Complexity and cost of maintenance for hybrid and AMG versions
  • High purchase price and expensive options
  • Some reported electronic and software issues

Check before buying

  • Complete and regular service history, especially for M282 and AMG
  • Abnormal noises on cold start (M282)
  • Operation of the MBUX system and driving aids (software updates)
  • Condition of the DPF and AdBlue system on diesels (suitable use)
  • Tire and brake wear (particularly on powerful versions)
  • High-voltage battery condition and charging system on PHEVs
  • Check if manufacturer recalls have been performed

Verdict

The Mercedes CLA C118 is an attractive choice for those seeking a compact vehicle with an assertive style and advanced onboard technology. However, the choice of engine is crucial for long-term reliability and suitability for your use.

* **For high-mileage drivers and maximum reliability**, the OM654q diesel engines (CLA 200 d, 220 d, and 180 d after 2020) are clearly the most recommended. They offer an excellent compromise between performance, fuel efficiency, and proven mechanical robustness. * **For versatile petrol use**, the CLA 220 and 250 (M260 engine) are very good choices, reliable and pleasant. * **The CLA 180 and 200 (M282 engine)** are suitable for less intensive use, but require increased vigilance regarding maintenance and history due to potential timing chain and oil consumption issues. * **The CLA 250 e (PHEV)** is interesting for daily electric commutes, but its technical complexity and potential additional cost in case of hybrid issues make it less universally recommendable. * **AMG versions** are performance beasts, but their maintenance is very specific and costly, reserved for informed enthusiasts and substantial budgets.

Overall, the CLA C118 is a good vehicle, but a thorough inspection and an impeccable service history are essential, especially for the less reliable engines. Prioritize the OM654q diesels or M260 petrol engines for peace of mind.
