Skoda Kamiq

I (NW4) · 2019 – 2025

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The **first-generation Skoda Kamiq (NW4)** is the urban SUV from the Czech brand, based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MQB-A0 platform (shared with the VW T-Cross, Polo, and Seat Arona). Launched in 2019, it stands out with record-breaking interior space for its category, a rational design, and the famous « Simply Clever » features. A facelift occurred at the end of 2023, bringing aesthetic touches (optional Matrix LED headlights) and a technological update. Although it does not offer all-wheel drive, its pragmatic positioning makes it one of the most rational choices in the B-SUV segment. Overall reliability is very good, particularly for the petrol (TSI) engines, which account for the vast majority of sales, although the model suffered from some electronic teething problems (MIB3 multimedia system).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional rear passenger space for a 4.24 m vehicle
  • Ergonomics of controls (retention of physical buttons for air conditioning)
  • Very efficient and powerful 1.0 TSI and 1.5 TSI petrol engines
  • Suspension comfort (softly sprung, ideal for daily use)
  • « Simply Clever » features (umbrella in the door, ice scraper in the fuel filler flap)

Weaknesses 5

  • Recurring bugs in the infotainment system (MIB3) on pre-facelift models
  • DSG7 gearbox (DQ200) sometimes harsh at low speeds and fragile in intensive urban use
  • Hard plastics omnipresent on door panels and the lower part of the dashboard
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind noise)
  • No sliding rear bench seat (unlike the VW T-Cross)

Check before buying

  • History of software updates (touchscreen and engine control unit).
  • DSG7 gearbox behavior: no jerking or excessive slipping should be felt during 1-2 and 2-3 shifts.
  • Absence of driving assistance warning lights (Travel Assist, SOS eCall) on the dashboard.
  • On the 1.5 TSI: check for the presence of the engine cover (often removed after a recall and sometimes not replaced).
  • On the 1.6 TDI: check AdBlue refill history and absence of engine warning light (DPF/EGR clogging).

Verdict

The Skoda Kamiq is a pragmatic and highly recommendable choice on the used car market. By avoiding diesel engines (unsuited to the vehicle's urban vocation) and favoring TSI petrol units, you get a reliable, spacious, and economical SUV. The **1.0 TSI 110 hp with manual gearbox** represents the perfect balance, avoiding potential costs associated with the DSG7 gearbox at high mileage. When purchasing a 2019-2021 model, ensure that all electronic updates (multimedia and steering wheel sensors) have been carried out by the Skoda network.
