Summary

The DS7, initially launched as the DS7 Crossback in 2017, is the first premium SUV from the DS Automobiles brand, positioned in the C-SUV segment. It embodies French expertise in luxury and technology, with a distinctive design, a refined interior, and remarkable suspension comfort (Active Scan Suspension). The first generation (internal code X74) underwent a significant facelift in 2022, dropping the 'Crossback' suffix to become simply 'DS7', and introducing new, more powerful plug-in hybrid powertrains. Overall, the DS7 offers a pleasant driving experience and a high level of finish, but its reliability can vary depending on the engine, particularly the early petrol versions and the AdBlue systems on diesel models.

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for 1.2 PureTech and AdBlue)
  • Condition of the timing belt on 1.2 PureTech (visual inspection by a professional)
  • Functioning of the AdBlue system (warning lights, error messages)
  • Absence of engine warning lights or electronic faults (PHEV)
  • Condition of brakes (wear, seizing) on PHEV versions
  • Proper functioning of air conditioning and interior electronic equipment

Verdict

The DS7 is an SUV that appeals with its style, comfort, and interior presentation. However, reliability is a point to consider carefully. **1.2 PureTech versions are to be avoided** in the absence of a perfect service history and proof of preventive timing belt replacement. **BlueHDi diesels are good choices for high-mileage drivers**, provided you are vigilant about the AdBlue system. **1.6 PureTech versions are a balanced option** for petrol, but require rigorous maintenance to prevent carbon buildup. **E-Tense (PHEV) versions are promising** in terms of performance and consumption (if recharged), but their technological complexity and market youth require careful monitoring. For a confident purchase, prioritize well-maintained models, ideally with a warranty, and opt for the 1.6 PureTech or 2.0 BlueHDi (pre-2020) engines or the E-Tense if your usage is suitable.
