Mazda CX-5

II (KF) (2017–2025) · 2017 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The second-generation Mazda CX-5 (KF code), launched in 2017, is a major evolution of the compact SUV that has captivated with its Kodo design and Skyactiv engines. Building on the solid foundations of the first generation, it refines its style, improves its sound insulation and interior quality, while retaining its excellent dynamic qualities. Positioned in the highly competitive family SUV segment, it stands out with a more premium approach and a more engaging driving experience than most of its rivals. The model benefited from a slight facelift in late 2021 (2022 model year), bringing minor aesthetic touches, technological improvements (infotainment, driving aids), and the introduction of M Hybrid technology (mild hybrid) on some gasoline engines in Europe. Overall, the CX-5 KF is a very well-built and reliable vehicle, especially in its gasoline versions.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent overall reliability of naturally aspirated gasoline engines
  • Timeless and elegant design (Kodo)
  • Quality of finish and interior materials
  • Dynamic and balanced road handling
  • Improved suspension comfort compared to Gen 1
  • Efficient all-wheel drive (AWD)

Weaknesses 5

  • Outdated infotainment system on pre-facelift models (not touch-sensitive while driving)
  • Rear seat space tight for tall adults
  • Trunk volume in the lower average of the segment
  • Fuel consumption of 2.5L and 2.5L Turbo gasoline versions can be high if pushed
  • Complexity and demanding maintenance of diesel engines

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for diesels)
  • DPF condition and regeneration frequency (for diesels)
  • Engine oil level (for diesels, risk of dilution)
  • Verification that all manufacturer recalls have been performed (especially fuel pump for 2.5T)
  • Operation of the infotainment system and driving aids
  • Tire and brake wear (the CX-5 is a heavy vehicle)

Verdict

The second-generation Mazda CX-5 is an excellent choice on the used car market, offering a rare blend of elegant design, driving pleasure, and reliability. **The Skyactiv-G gasoline engines (2.0L and 2.5L naturally aspirated, including M Hybrid versions) are the most recommended for their almost exemplary robustness and mechanical simplicity.** They are suitable for all uses and guarantee peace of mind. The 2.5 Turbo version is also reliable but requires checking for fuel pump recalls and a more substantial fuel budget.

**The Skyactiv-D 2.2 diesel engine is more sensitive.** If you primarily do long highway trips, it can be an interesting option for its fuel efficiency and torque. However, for urban or mixed use with many short trips, the risks of DPF clogging and oil dilution are real, making its maintenance more demanding and potentially costly. In this case, it is best to avoid it.

Regardless of the engine, prioritize a model with a clear and up-to-date service history. The CX-5 KF is a vehicle that ages very well and offers a credible and more distinctive alternative to mainstream SUVs.
