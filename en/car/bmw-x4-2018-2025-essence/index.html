BMW X4

G02 (2018–2025) · 2018–2025

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation BMW X4, known by the internal code G02, was launched in 2018, succeeding the F26. This SUV coupé, based on the CLAR platform shared with the X3 G01, positions itself as a sportier and more stylish alternative to the traditional SUV. It offers a blend of driving dynamics, luxury, and practicality, although the coupé design slightly compromises rear space and trunk volume.

The model received a **facelift (LCI)** in mid-2021 (for the 2022 model year), bringing aesthetic updates (bumpers, headlights, taillights), a modernized interior with iDrive 7 (then iDrive 8 on later models), and the integration of 48V mild-hybrid (MHEV) technology on certain engines, particularly diesel and gasoline.

Overall, the X4 G02 is a well-built and reliable vehicle, relying on proven BMW engines. Major problems are rare, but like any premium vehicle, maintenance can be costly. General reliability is good, especially for the B48 and B58 gasoline engines, known for their robustness.

Strengths 6

  • Build quality and interior materials
  • Driving dynamics and agility for an SUV
  • Very reliable and powerful gasoline engines (B48, B58)
  • Excellent and robust ZF 8HP automatic gearbox
  • Comfort on long journeys (especially with adaptive suspension)
  • Advanced onboard technology (iDrive, driving aids)

Weaknesses 6

  • High purchase price and expensive options
  • High maintenance and spare parts costs
  • Limited rear passenger space and trunk volume due to coupé design
  • Reduced rear visibility
  • Fuel consumption of M and M40i versions can be high in sporty driving
  • Firm suspension on sporty versions (M Sport, M) without adaptive suspension

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for M and M40i versions)
  • Condition of tires and brakes (rapid wear on sporty versions)
  • Functionality of the iDrive system and electronic equipment
  • Absence of engine oil leaks (valve cover, oil filter housing)
  • Check if manufacturer recalls have been performed (especially EGR for diesels, crankshaft sensor for some B58)
  • Condition of the suspension (noises, behavior) and wheels (warping, impacts)

Verdict

The BMW X4 G02 is an excellent choice for those seeking a premium, dynamic, and well-equipped SUV coupé. Its general reliability is very good, especially with the B48 (4-cylinder) and B58 (6-cylinder) gasoline engines, which are benchmarks in their class.

**Verdict: BUY** if you prioritize driving pleasure, build quality, and prestige.

**Conditions:** * **For gasoline engines (B48, B58):** This is a safe purchase. Prioritize a clear service history. The B58 (M40i) offers an ideal compromise between performance and reliability. The B48 is perfect for more reasonable daily use. * **For M engines (S58):** **NEGOTIATE** if maintenance is not impeccable or if consumable wear is advanced. This purchase is reserved for enthusiasts with a substantial budget for maintenance and insurance. * **For diesel engines (B47, B57):** **NEGOTIATE** if the EGR recall has not been performed or if the vehicle has made many short trips (DPF/AdBlue risk). Check the AdBlue system history. These engines are reliable for high-mileage drivers.

Regardless of the choice, a thorough inspection by a BMW specialist is highly recommended before purchase, especially for the most powerful or highest-mileage models. Be prepared for higher-than-average maintenance costs.
