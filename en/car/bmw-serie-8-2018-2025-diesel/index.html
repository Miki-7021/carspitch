Summary

The BMW 8 Series (G15 Coupé, G14 Cabriolet, G16 Gran Coupé) marks the return of luxury grand touring for the Bavarian manufacturer, effectively replacing the 6 Series. Designed to compete with the Porsche Panamera, Mercedes S-Class Coupé, or Audi A7, it combines spectacular design with cutting-edge technologies. Although the market is massively shifting towards gasoline and hybrid, BMW has maintained a particularly relevant diesel offering (840d) for high-mileage European drivers. This version, powered by the robust B57 inline 6-cylinder engine, offers an exceptional compromise between GT performance (680 Nm torque) and fuel efficiency (often under 7 L/100 km on the highway). The model received a facelift (LCI) in 2022, primarily bringing technological updates (enlarged central screen) and aesthetic changes ('Iconic Glow' illuminated grille).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional B57 diesel engine (840d): a rare combination of performance (0-100 in 4.9s) and efficiency (range > 1,000 km).
  • Perfectly calibrated and indestructible ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (Steptronic) if serviced.
  • Very high-quality interior finish and sound insulation.
  • Imperial road handling thanks to rear-wheel steering (Integral Active Steering) and xDrive transmission.
  • Very ergonomic and reliable iDrive 7.0 (then 8.0) infotainment system.

Weaknesses 5

  • Steep depreciation, particularly on V8 and Diesel engines.
  • Symbolic rear passenger space on the Coupé (G15) and Cabriolet (G14).
  • Very high weight (often over 2 tons), which quickly wears out tires and brakes.
  • Prohibitive maintenance and insurance costs (very high-end vehicle).
  • Recurring recalls on the EGR system of diesel engines.

Check before buying

  • On the 840d: Imperatively check if the EGR cooler recall has been performed (fire risk). Inspect the coolant level.
  • On the 840d: Listen to the engine when cold. A metallic knocking sound may indicate early timing chain wear (rare on B57, but possible if oil changes are spaced out).
  • On all versions: Asymmetrical tire wear (geometry sensitive to weight and potholes).
  • On V8s (M850i/M8): Traces of oil seepage around the turbos (in the V) and blue smoke from the exhaust after prolonged idling.
  • Electronics: Test all driving aids (360° cameras, adaptive cruise control), sensors are exposed to gravel.

Official recalls

  • EGR Cooler (2019-2023): Major recall (various codes including 0011970500) concerning diesel engines (including the 840d). Risk of glycol leakage into the intake, potentially causing a fire. Free replacement by BMW.
  • Brake Assist (2024): Recall on the integrated braking system (IBS) which may lead to a loss of brake assist and ABS. Concerns several recent models including the 8 Series.
  • Rearview Camera (2019): Software bug preventing the rearview camera display (non-compliance with US/EU standards). Software update.
  • Starter (2024): On some models, repeated starting attempts can cause the starter to overheat (fire risk). Software update to limit activation time.

What sets it apart 6

performance|economy

  • B57 Diesel Engine: Exceptional Power and Efficiency — The 840d (B57) diesel engine offers a rare combination of dynamic performance, with 0 to 100 km/h in just 4.9 seconds, and remarkable efficiency, allowing for a range of over 1000 km. Its generous torque of 680 Nm ensures exhilarating acceleration.

comfort

  • A Refined and Quiet Grand Touring Interior — Enjoy a very high level of interior finish quality and exceptional soundproofing, transforming every journey into a luxurious and serene grand touring experience.

handling

  • Imperial Road Handling and Surprising Agility — Thanks to xDrive all-wheel drive and Integral Active Steering, the 8 Series offers imperial road handling, surprising agility for its size, and exemplary steering precision, ensuring first-class stability and active safety.

safety

  • Maximum Safety with 5-Star Euro NCAP Rating — The BMW 8 Series achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 82% for child protection, attesting to an exceptional level of safety.

braking

  • Powerful and Enduring High-Performance Braking — Benefit from excellent braking performance, powerful and enduring, with a short stopping distance for its category, ensuring optimal control and safety in all circumstances.

praticity

  • Ergonomic and Reliable iDrive Infotainment System — The iDrive 7.0 (then 8.0) infotainment system is renowned for its intuitive ergonomics and reliability, offering effortless connectivity and control of vehicle functions.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Porsche Panamera (hybrid or diesel on older generations), Mercedes-Benz S-Class Coupé / AMG GT 4-Door, Audi A7 / S7 Sportback (TDI in Europe).
  • North America: Lexus LC 500 (very reliable naturally aspirated V8), Porsche Panamera, Mercedes-Benz SL or AMG GT.
  • Asia: Lexus LC, Porsche Panamera.

Verdict

The BMW 8 Series is a formidable touring machine. If your choice is the 840d, you are opting for one of the best diesel engines in the world (B57): torquey, quiet, and incredibly fuel-efficient. It is the rational choice par excellence for high-mileage drivers, provided that the EGR valve recall has been performed and that exclusively urban use, which would clog the DPF, is avoided. For more versatile use or if diesel puts you off, the 840i (B58) is the absolute best buy in terms of gasoline reliability. The V8 versions (M850i, M8) are reserved for enthusiasts willing to bear an exponential maintenance and fuel budget.
