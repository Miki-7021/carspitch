Summary

The BMW 4 Series G22 (Coupé) and G23 (Cabriolet), launched in 2020, represents the second generation of the 4 Series, derived from the 3 Series G20. It stands out with a bold design, notably its controversial vertical grille, marking a strong aesthetic departure from previous generations. Positioned as a sporty and elegant coupé and cabriolet, it offers a dynamic driving experience, a refined interior, and cutting-edge technologies. This generation also introduced mild-hybrid (MHEV) powertrains to improve efficiency. Overall, the 4 Series G22/G23 is praised for its road qualities and comfort, although its styling is divisive. General reliability is good, relying on proven BMW platforms and powertrains.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional driving dynamics (precision, balance)
  • High-quality interior finish and premium materials
  • Suspension comfort (adaptive optional) and sound insulation
  • Very powerful and reliable B58 and S58 gasoline engines
  • Advanced onboard technology (iDrive 7, driving aids)
  • Excellent ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox

Weaknesses 5

  • Very polarizing front grille design
  • Limited rear visibility on the coupé
  • Symbolic rear seats on the coupé, difficult access
  • High purchase price and expensive options
  • High maintenance and insurance costs, especially for M versions

Hybrid (PHEV) / Electric

*The BMW 4 Series Coupé/Cabriolet (G22/G23) is not available in plug-in hybrid (PHEV) or electric (BEV) versions. The BMW i4 (G26) is the electric version of the 4 Series Gran Coupé.*

Check before buying

  • Complete and up-to-date service history (BMW digital service record)
  • Condition of tires and brakes (wear, warped discs)
  • Functionality of all electronic equipment (iDrive, driving aids, sunroof/retractable roof for cabriolet)
  • Absence of oil or coolant leaks under the vehicle or on the engine
  • Abnormal engine noises (knocking, turbo whistling) or transmission
  • Body panel alignment (sign of an accident)
  • Thorough road test to evaluate road handling and engine/gearbox responsiveness

Official recalls

Official recalls for the BMW 4 Series G22/G23 are relatively few and target specific issues, often related to components shared with other BMW models on the same platform (G20/G21).

  • 2020-2021: Integrated Brake System (IBS) Control Module Software
  • Issue: A software defect could lead to a loss of braking assistance under certain conditions, increasing the risk of an accident.
  • Affected Population: Certain vehicles produced between 2020 and 2021, including the 4 Series G22/G23.
  • Action: Software update for the IBS module.
  • 2021: Low-Pressure Fuel Pump (LPFP) Control Unit
  • Issue: A manufacturing defect in the low-pressure fuel pump control unit could lead to an unexpected engine shutdown.
  • Affected Population: A limited number of vehicles produced in 2021, including the 4 Series G22/G23.
  • Action: Replacement of the fuel pump control unit.
  • 2022: Advanced Driver-Assistance System (ADAS) Software
  • Issue: A software defect could lead to temporary malfunction of certain driver-assistance functions (e.g., adaptive cruise control, lane keeping assist).
  • Affected Population: Certain vehicles produced in 2021-2022.
  • Action: ADAS software update.
  • 2023: Passenger Seat Position Sensor
  • Issue: A sensor defect could prevent the correct deployment of the passenger airbag in the event of a collision.
  • Affected Population: Certain vehicles produced in 2022-2023.
  • Action: Inspection and replacement of the sensor if necessary.

*It is imperative to check with a BMW dealership whether the targeted vehicle was affected by these recalls and if the corrective actions have been performed.*

Verdict

The BMW 4 Series G22/G23 is an excellent choice for anyone looking for a premium, dynamic, and well-finished coupé or cabriolet. Its reliability is generally very good, especially for the B48 and B58 gasoline engines, which are benchmarks in their categories. The choice of engine will depend on your usage and budget.

  • For daily and versatile use: The 420i and 430i are sensible choices. They offer an excellent compromise between performance, fuel consumption, and maintenance costs. The 430i provides a very noticeable boost in dynamism.
  • For performance and thrill seekers: The M440i xDrive is a favorite. Its B58 6-cylinder engine is a masterpiece of smoothness and power, with an enchanting sound. It's the rational choice for pleasure.
  • For purists and track enthusiasts: The M4 Competition is a racing beast. It requires a substantial budget for purchase and maintenance but offers unparalleled performance and sensations. Reserved for connoisseurs and those with unlimited budgets.
  • For high-mileage drivers: The 420d, 430d, and M440d diesel versions are very efficient and powerful but require regular trips for the proper functioning of the DPF and AdBlue system.

Whatever your choice, prioritize a model with a clear service history performed at a dealership or by a BMW specialist. The G22/G23 is a modern and complex car; rigorous follow-up is key to its longevity. Despite its divisive grille, it is a BMW true to the brand's spirit in terms of driving pleasure.
