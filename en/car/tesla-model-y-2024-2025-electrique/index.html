II (Juniper) · 2024 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Tesla models

Summary

The second-generation Tesla Model Y, known by the internal codename "Juniper", represents the mid-cycle refresh (deep facelift) of the world's best-selling electric SUV. Modeled after the Model 3 "Highland" evolutions, this Model Y corrects the main flaws of the first generation. It benefits from optimized aerodynamics, significantly superior soundproofing (full acoustic glazing), and, above all, a completely redesigned suspension to offer better filtering. Inside, the cabin moves upmarket with the integration of more luxurious materials, ambient lighting, a screen for rear passengers, and ventilated seats. However, the removal of stalks (turn signals on the steering wheel) and the absence of ultrasonic sensors (replaced by Tesla Vision) divide users.

Overall update note: A major evolution that transforms a technological and efficient SUV into a truly comfortable and accomplished vehicle.

Strengths 6

  • Energy efficiency and real-world range among the best on the market
  • Tesla Supercharger network (perfect software integration for trips)
  • Suspension comfort transformed compared to the first generation
  • Soundproofing worthy of the German premium segment
  • Gigantic interior space (rear trunk + front frunk)
  • Ultra-responsive infotainment system and regular OTA updates

Weaknesses 5

  • Divisive ergonomics: absence of stalks (turn signals, wipers, gear selector)
  • Tesla Vision (High Fidelity Park Assist) still less precise than real ultrasonic sensors
  • Automatic wipers sometimes erratic (camera-based)
  • Large turning circle for urban use
  • Insurance cost often higher than the category average

Check before buying

  • Body panel alignment (though greatly improved at Berlin and Shanghai factories)
  • Running gear geometry upon delivery (asymmetrical tire wear if poorly adjusted)
  • Operation of door actuators and frameless windows in freezing weather
  • Condition of rims (very exposed, especially on 20 and 21-inch versions)
  • Absence of parasitic noises (squeaks/rattles) in the new cabin on degraded roads

What sets it apart 5

safety

  • Exceptional Safety and Road Handling — The Model Y Juniper excels in safety, attested by its 5 Euro NCAP stars and "Best in Class" distinction. Its low center of gravity and balanced weight distribution ensure agile and stable road holding, complemented by very powerful and durable braking.

comfort

  • Unrivaled Comfort and Travel Silence — Thanks to its completely redesigned suspension with FSD dampers and full acoustic glazing, the Model Y Juniper offers ride comfort and soundproofing worthy of the premium segment, transforming every journey into a serene experience. Ventilated seats add to the well-being.

economy

  • Superior Energy Efficiency and Charging Network — The Model Y stands out for its cutting-edge energy efficiency and one of the best real-world ranges. Access to the Tesla Supercharger network, perfectly integrated into the navigation system, ensures long journeys without constraints and optimized planning.

practicality

  • Optimal Family Space and Modularity — With its gigantic cabin, generous rear trunk, and front "frunk," the Model Y offers exceptional loading capacity. The rear screen for passengers and regular software updates enrich the onboard experience for the whole family.

technology

  • Advanced Technology and Proven Reliability — The Model Y Juniper integrates Hardware 4 for Autopilot, an ultra-responsive infotainment system with OTA updates. The reliability of electric powertrains and batteries, especially LFP, is widely recognized, ensuring lasting peace of mind.

Verdict

The Tesla Model Y "Juniper" delivers. By building on the excellent foundations of the first generation (efficiency, space, charging network) and correcting its two biggest flaws (the harsh suspension and average soundproofing), it becomes the quintessential family electric SUV. The reliability of the powertrains and batteries (especially LFP) is now well-proven. The only real deterrent to purchase lies in Tesla's radical ergonomic choices (removal of stalks and parking sensors), which require an adaptation period. If you accept this minimalist philosophy, it is a highly recommended purchase.
