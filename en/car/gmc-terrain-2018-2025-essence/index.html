GMC Terrain

II · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 76/100 — bon

Summary

The **second-generation GMC Terrain** marks a major evolution compared to its predecessor, adopting a lighter platform and slightly reduced dimensions to better fit into the compact SUV segment. A technical cousin of the Chevrolet Equinox, it stands out with a more assertive design, higher-quality interior materials, and an "accessible premium" positioning typical of the GMC brand. It received a facelift for the 2022 model year, bringing aesthetic tweaks (redesigned LED headlights) and a technological update, as well as the introduction of the rugged AT4 trim. Overall, its reliability is average for the category, with a clear preference for the base gasoline engines, as the diesel and 2.0T versions were prematurely removed from the catalog.

Strengths 5

  • Solid reliability of the base 1.5T engine (above average according to Consumer Reports)
  • Extensive and competent GMC/Buick after-sales network in North America
  • Good residual value on the used market
  • Cabin soundproofing (Active Noise Cancellation technology)
  • Intuitive and responsive infotainment system (Apple CarPlay / Android Auto as standard)

Weaknesses 5

  • Problematic 1.6L Diesel engine (EGR, DPF)
  • Disappearance of the 2.0T engine after 2020, limiting towing performance
  • Un-ergonomic push-button gear selector on the dashboard
  • Quality of some plastics in the lower part of the cabin
  • Numerous manufacturer recalls on the early production years (2018-2019)

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history of the all-wheel drive (AWD) transmission: the transfer case is fragile if fluid changes are neglected.
  • Verification of the application of all recalls (especially airbags, brakes, and transmission on 2018-2020 models).
  • On 1.6 Diesel models: history of DPF regenerations and condition of the EGR valve.
  • Smooth operation of the 9-speed automatic gearbox (jerking possible if the software is not up to date).

Alternatives

- **North America**: Chevrolet Equinox (technical twin, cheaper), Honda CR-V (reliability benchmark), Toyota RAV4, Mazda CX-5 (better interior finish), Ford Escape. - **Europe**: The GMC Terrain is not officially sold in Europe. Alternatives of similar size are the Peugeot 3008, Volkswagen Tiguan, or Ford Kuga. - **Middle East**: Hyundai Tucson, Kia Sportage, Nissan X-Trail.

Verdict

The second-generation GMC Terrain is a generally recommendable compact SUV, provided you choose the engine and year carefully. **The 1.5T gasoline engine is the safest and most rational choice**, offering solid reliability and controlled maintenance costs. The 2.0T, although high-performing, requires more rigorous monitoring. On the other hand, **the 1.6L Diesel is to be avoided** due to its chronic emissions control problems (EGR/DPF). The 2018 and 2019 models suffered teething problems with numerous early recalls; it is therefore preferable to look for a model from 2020 onwards, or ideally the 2022 facelifted version. Pay special attention to the maintenance of the all-wheel drive (AWD) transmission when buying used.
