I phase 2 (2A/C) · 2003 – 2009

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The **Peugeot 206 Phase 2** (appeared in spring 2003) is the facelift of Peugeot's best-selling city car in history. It is distinguished by smooth-lens headlights, redesigned taillights, a honeycomb grille, and the widespread adoption of the multiplexed electrical network (CAN/VAN). Mechanically, this period marks the peak of **HDi** (High Pressure Direct Injection) diesel engines developed with Ford, definitively replacing the old atmospheric diesels. Although its road handling remains a benchmark, this generation suffered from chronic issues (rear axle, electronics) that require particular vigilance when buying used today.

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic and precise road handling (excellent chassis)
  • Exceptional fuel efficiency of 1.4 HDi and 2.0 HDi engines
  • Legendary reliability of the 2.0 HDi 90hp block
  • Availability and negligible cost of spare parts
  • Timeless design that ages very well

Weaknesses 5

  • Very fragile rear axle with bearings (PSA issue: wheels develop negative camber /-\)
  • Capricious multiplexed electronics (BSI, phantom warning lights)
  • Faulty headlight/indicator stalk (COM2000)
  • Driving position unsuitable for tall drivers (steering wheel not depth-adjustable)
  • Light soundproofing, especially on diesels

Check before buying

  • **Rear axle:** Play, squeaks over speed bumps, or rear wheels tilted inwards (repair cost: 400 to 600€).
  • **Injector seals (1.4 HDi / 1.6 HDi):** Remove the engine cover, listen for a "hissing" sound and look for traces of black tar around the injectors.
  • **COM2000:** Test all stalk functions (indicators skipping, headlights turning on by themselves).
  • **Damper Pulley (2.0 HDi):** Metallic noise at idle on the passenger side.
  • **1.6 HDi service history:** Demand invoices proving strict oil changes every 15,000 km or 1 year.

Official recalls

  • - **2004 (Electronics):** Risk of water infiltration into the Intelligent Service Unit (BSI) potentially causing major electrical malfunctions. - **2005 (Starter):** Incorrect positioning of the starter harness risking rubbing against the EGR valve (short-circuit risk) on HDi engines. - **2007 (1.4 HDi Engine):** Engine management ECU software update to correct jerks or untimely stalling. - **2008 (Braking):** Replacement of the vacuum pump valve on some 1.4 HDi models (loss of braking assistance).

Verdict

The Peugeot 206 Phase 2 diesel is a car with two faces. If you opt for the **2.0 HDi 90**, you are buying a true tank capable of exceeding 400,000 km with basic maintenance. The **1.4 HDi 68** is an excellent economical choice for new drivers, provided you absolutely monitor the injector seals. However, the **1.6 HDi 110 is to be avoided** on this generation due to its chronic turbo failures. Regardless of the model, the purchase is conditional on the condition of the rear axle and the COM2000. It is a rational purchase if these points have already been addressed by the seller.
