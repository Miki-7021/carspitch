Summary

The **Mercedes-Benz GLB (X247)** is a premium compact SUV based on the MFA2 platform (shared with the A-Class, B-Class, and GLA). Its positioning is unique in its category: it adopts an assertive cubic design (reminiscent of the old GLK or G-Class) allowing it to offer up to **7 seats** in a footprint of only 4.63 m.

Launched in late 2019, it received a **facelift in 2023**, bringing a revised light signature, the latest generation of the MBUX system, and, most importantly, **48V mild-hybridization (MHEV)** across all gasoline engines. Although this report focuses on gasoline engines (highly popular on this model), the GLB also offers excellent diesels. Note: there is no plug-in hybrid (PHEV) version due to the lack of space related to the 3rd row of seats; the 100% electric alternative is the distinct **EQB (X243)** model.

**Overall Reliability Score:** The GLB benefits from the maturity of the MFA2 platform. The entry-level gasoline engines (1.33L) are reliable, but the 2.0L (GLB 250) requires more vigilance. Electronics and the dual-clutch transmission remain the main points of concern.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional spaciousness for its size (4.63 m) and a very practical 7-seat option for occasional use.
  • Sliding rear bench seat as standard, offering great modularity.
  • Spectacular, fluid, and technologically advanced MBUX interface.
  • Suspension comfort (especially with the optional adaptive damping).
  • Very well-balanced and economical 1.33L gasoline engine (GLB 200).
