Isuzu D-Max

III (TFR) · 2020 – 2024

BUY Reliability 90/100 — excellent

Summary

The **3rd generation Isuzu D-Max (TFR/TFS)**, launched in late 2019 and arriving on global markets in 2020, marks a major turning point for the Japanese manufacturer. Known for its pure, hard-working utility DNA, this new D-Max (which also serves as the basis for the Mazda BT-50) makes a spectacular move upmarket: a modernized interior, improved soundproofing, and the massive integration of driving aids (ADAS) allowing it to achieve 5 stars in the Euro NCAP crash test.

**Note on the petrol engine:** Although the request specifically targets petrol, it is my duty as an expert to point out that **the 3rd generation D-Max does not offer any petrol engine** on its global markets (Europe, Oceania, Asia, South America). Isuzu is an industrial engine manufacturer historically specialized in diesel. The analysis will therefore focus on the absence of this offering and detail the diesel blocks (1.9L and 3.0L) which make up 100% of the sales for this model.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of Isuzu diesel blocks (utility/truck design)
  • Off-road capabilities (standard rear differential lock on many trims)
  • Towing capacity of 3.5 tonnes and payload exceeding one tonne
  • Very comprehensive active safety equipment (ADAS) as standard
  • Often generous manufacturer warranty (5 years / 100,000 km depending on markets)

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of petrol or hybrid alternatives (until the arrival of MHEV/BEV planned for 2025)
  • Rough and noisy diesel engines under load (agricultural sound)
  • Very firm rear leaf suspension when unladen (spartan comfort)
  • Slow infotainment system sometimes prone to display bugs
  • Less dense dealer network than the competition (Toyota, Ford)

Check before buying

  • Condition of the DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter) and EGR valve: check the history if the vehicle has done a lot of city driving.
  • Underbody and chassis: look for signs of impacts or premature rust related to intensive off-road use.
  • Rear leaf springs: check if they have been replaced as part of the official recall (W9080).
  • Steering column: ensure the tightening torque has been checked (2023 recall).

What sets it apart 14

Safety

  • Top-tier 5-Star Safety — The D-Max III is engineered to protect its occupants, achieving a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult (83%) and child (86%) protection. It also integrates a very comprehensive suite of active safety features (ADAS) as standard.

Braking

  • Powerful and Reliable Braking — Benefit from braking performance rated excellent for its category, ensuring effective stopping power and a reassuring pedal feel. Recent models feature four-wheel disc brakes for enhanced consistency and performance.

Handling

  • Refined Road Handling — The D-Max III offers significantly improved road handling and dynamic behavior, providing a driving experience closer to that of a passenger car. Enjoy excellent high-speed stability, well-controlled body roll, and more precise, responsive steering.

Reliability

  • Legendary Mechanical Robustness — Inheriting Isuzu's utility engineering DNA, the D-Max III is built to last. Its robust design and proven components ensure exceptional reliability, making it a tireless work partner.

Practicality

  • Unrivaled Load and Towing Capacities — Designed for professionals, the D-Max III excels in the most demanding tasks. It offers an impressive towing capacity of 3.5 tonnes and a payload exceeding one tonne, allowing you to transport all your equipment without compromise.

Performance

  • Off-Road Master — With its exceptional off-road capabilities, including a standard rear differential lock on many trims, the D-Max III is ready to tackle the toughest terrains, taking you wherever your work or adventures demand.

Comfort

  • Modernized and Comfortable Interior — The D-Max III marks a spectacular upgrade with a modernized interior and improved soundproofing. Enjoy a more pleasant and better-equipped cabin, making long journeys and workdays more comfortable.

Alternatives

- **Europe** : Toyota Hilux (absolute benchmark for reliability), Ford Ranger (more comfortable and technological), Volkswagen Amarok (twin of the Ranger). - **Asia / Oceania** : Mazda BT-50 (technical clone of the D-Max with a different design), Mitsubishi Triton/L200, Nissan Navara. - **Americas** : The D-Max is not sold in North America (market dominated by the Ford F-150, Toyota Tacoma, Chevrolet Colorado).

Verdict

The 3rd generation Isuzu D-Max is a formidable work tool. If you are looking for a petrol pickup, you will have to turn to other markets or other brands (such as the Ford Ranger 2.3 EcoBoost or the Toyota Tacoma), as Isuzu fully embraces its status as a heavy diesel specialist. By accepting this engine (the 1.9L in Europe or the indestructible 3.0L elsewhere), you are buying one of the most robust vehicles in its class. It is less refined than a Ford Ranger, but its design derived from the heavy truck world guarantees exceptional longevity. A highly recommended purchase for professionals and adventurers.
