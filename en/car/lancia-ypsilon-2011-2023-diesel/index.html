Summary

The Lancia Ypsilon type 846 (often considered the 4th generation if the Autobianchi Y10 is included, or 3rd under the Y/Ypsilon name) is a premium versatile city car based on the Fiat Mini platform (shared with the Fiat 500 and Panda). Unlike its predecessors, it is exclusively offered as a 5-door, with rear door handles hidden in the pillars to maintain a coupe-like appearance.

Positioned as a chic and urban car, it has enjoyed an exceptionally long career, supported by multiple facelifts (2015, 2021) and unwavering commercial success in Italy, its almost exclusive market towards the end of its career.

Focus on the Diesel (1.3 MultiJet): Although the Ypsilon is a city car, it was offered with Fiat's excellent small 1.3 MultiJet (SDE) diesel engine. This engine is inherently robust, but its integration into a car intended for urban use often led to unsuitable driving profiles (short cold trips), resulting in severe clogging of the DPF and EGR valve, as well as premature wear of the timing chain due to oil dilution by diesel fuel during aborted regenerations.

Strengths 5

  • Chic, timeless, and customizable design
  • Ideal size for the city (3.84 m) with 5 practical doors
  • Exceptional reliability of the 1.2 Fire engine
  • Ridiculously low fuel consumption of the 1.3 MultiJet on the road
  • Overall very low maintenance cost (Fiat parts)

Weaknesses 5

  • 1.3 MultiJet engine unsuited to the car's urban vocation (DPF/EGR)
  • Clumsy road handling and uncommunicative steering
  • Light soundproofing on the highway (especially in TwinAir and Diesel)
  • Limited rear roominess and trunk volume
  • Slow Dualogic automated gearbox (optional) prone to actuator failures

Check before buying

  • On the 1.3 MultiJet: Timing chain noise on cold start (sign of slack, risk of breakage).
  • On the 1.3 MultiJet: Oil change history (must be annual) and DPF warning light on the dashboard.
  • On the 0.9 TwinAir: Smooth operation without jerking (otherwise, MultiAir module has failed, expensive repair).
  • On all versions: Premature wear of the front axle silent blocks (clunking on speed bumps).
  • On all versions: Operation of the electric power steering (City mode), the column can sometimes fail.

Official recalls

  • [2026-06-05] NOx emissions (1.3 SDE Euro 6b engine): Vehicles equipped with the 1.3-liter diesel engine (Euro 6b) may produce excessive nitrogen oxide (NOx) emissions due to degradation of a part of the emission control system. Environmental risk.
  • [2026-03-27] Fire risk (Petrol GPF): On certain models (VIN: VYCU...83420 to VYCU...11057), there is insufficient clearance (< 10 mm) between the gasoline particulate filter (GPF) tube and the alternator terminal protection cap. Risk of thermal contact and fire.
  • [2025-12-15] Fuel leak: The vehicle may have a fuel leak in the high-pressure pipe, located between the high-pressure pump and the injection rail, due to the absence of a fastening component. Fire risk.

Verdict

The Lancia Ypsilon (846) is an endearing city car, whose longevity is explained by its charm and its highly amortized Fiat technical base.

Regarding the 1.3 MultiJet Diesel: It is a paradoxical engine for this car. It is efficient and very frugal, but it needs to be driven over long distances for its emission control system (DPF/EGR) to function properly. If you buy a used diesel Ypsilon that has only been driven in the city, steer clear: the timing chain is probably worn out by oil dilution, and the DPF is clogged. If you find a model that has been driven on the highway with a strict oil change history (every 15,000 km), it is a good choice for a high mileage driver.

For urban use (90% of cases): Without hesitation, favor the 1.2 Fire 69 hp. It is slow, but its reliability is legendary and its maintenance costs next to nothing. The recent 1.0 FireFly hybrid is also an excellent modern alternative.
