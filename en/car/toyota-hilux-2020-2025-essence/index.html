Summary

Toyota Hilux VIII Facelift (AN120)

Initially launched in 2015, the eighth generation of the Toyota Hilux received a major facelift in 2020. This *facelift* brought significant improvements: a redesigned front end, a modernized infotainment system (compatible with Apple CarPlay/Android Auto), a recalibrated suspension for better unladen comfort, and above all, an update to its engines.

Although the European market is almost exclusively dominated by diesel engines (2.4 and 2.8 D-4D), this sheet focuses on the petrol engines, which are extremely popular in international markets (Middle East, Australia, South America, Asia) for their legendary reliability and indestructibility. The Hilux remains the absolute benchmark for utility pickups, offering unfailing robustness thanks to its ladder frame chassis, at the expense of ride comfort which remains rustic compared to modern SUVs.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of the petrol engines (2TR-FE and 1GR-FE)
  • Off-road capabilities (4x4, rear differential lock)
  • Indestructible ladder frame chassis and payload/towing capacity (up to 3.5t)
  • Notable improvement in suspension comfort since the 2020 facelift
  • Stratospheric resale value on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Gargantuan fuel consumption of the petrol engines (especially the 4.0L V6)
  • Petrol engines unavailable on the official European market (importation mandatory)
  • Rear seat comfort (bouncy leaf suspension when unladen)
  • Hard interior plastics sensitive to scratches
  • Imposing size (5.32 m) making urban parking difficult

Check before buying

  • Underbody and chassis: look for signs of impacts, rust, or dried mud (sign of intensive off-road use).
  • Maintenance history: petrol engines are robust but require regular oil changes, especially in hot/dusty climates.
  • 4x4 transmission: check the smooth engagement of 4H and 4L modes, and the absence of metallic noises in the axles.
  • DPF and EGR (on Diesel versions): check for the absence of a check engine light and favor a model that has been driven on highways.
  • Rear suspension: check the condition of the leaf springs (sagging if the vehicle has been constantly overloaded).

Official recalls

Although the Hilux is renowned for being very reliable, it is affected by certain global recall campaigns from the Toyota group (including shared platforms or common components):

  • [2024-08-22] Water infiltration (Cameras): The camera housings on several vehicles (Lexus, Toyota) can separate under stress due to improper welding, allowing water to enter. Risk of visual assistance failure.
  • [2024-06-26] Immobilizer: The design of the immobilizer device is not robust enough on certain hybrid/PHEV models (C-HR, RAV4, Lexus NX/RX). *Note: Primarily concerns TNGA platforms, marginal impact on Hilux.*
  • [2024-06-10] Transmission oil leak: A defective seal can cause an oil leak at the front transmission, which can seize the bearing. *Note: Recall targeting Stellantis/Toyota commercial vehicles (Proace City, Berlingo, Partner).*
  • [2023-07-03] Auxiliary battery terminal: On several models including the Hilux, Yaris, and Land Cruiser, the auxiliary battery terminal may be defective. This can disable steering assist and present a fire risk.

What sets it apart 6

Reliability

  • Legendary Reliability of Petrol Engines — The Hilux petrol engines (2.7L VVT-i and 4.0L V6) are renowned for their exceptional robustness and simple design, ensuring outstanding longevity and absolute peace of mind, even in the most demanding conditions.

Performance

  • Unrivalled Off-Road Capabilities — Thanks to its proven 4x4 system and rear differential lock, the petrol Hilux offers exceptional off-road capabilities, allowing you to overcome the toughest obstacles with ease.
  • Smooth and Powerful Engine Performance (V6 4.0L) — The 4.0L V6 petrol engine delivers smooth power and generous torque, ensuring strong acceleration and dynamic driving, perfect for heavy loads or challenging terrains.

Practicality

  • Indestructible Robustness and High Load Capacity — Its ultra-robust ladder frame gives the Hilux unwavering resistance and an impressive load and towing capacity, reaching up to 3.5 tonnes, ideal for professionals and adventurers.

Comfort

  • Optimized Suspension Comfort — The 2020 facelift brought a significant recalibration of the suspension, offering noticeably improved driving comfort, even when unladen, for more pleasant daily journeys.

Economy

  • Exceptional Resale Value — The Toyota Hilux is known for its stratospheric resale value on the used car market, guaranteeing an excellent investment and minimal depreciation over time.

Alternatives

Europe

  • Ford Ranger (Gen 3 / Gen 4): More comfortable, superior onboard technologies, but more temperamental diesel engines (2.0 EcoBlue).
  • Isuzu D-Max: The direct competitor in terms of ruggedness and utility reliability (very robust 1.9 diesel engine).
  • Volkswagen Amarok: More premium, high-performance V6 TDI, but more expensive maintenance.

Middle East / Asia / Americas (Petrol Markets)

  • Nissan Navara / Frontier (D23): Good 2.5L petrol engine (QR25DE), more comfortable multi-link rear suspension.
  • Mitsubishi L200 / Triton: Excellent 4x4 system (Super Select), reliable but older design 2.4L petrol engine (4G64).
  • Ford Ranger (2.3 EcoBoost): Modern turbo petrol engine, very high performance but more complex than Toyota's naturally aspirated blocks.

Verdict

The Toyota Hilux VIII (facelifted in 2020) fully deserves its reputation as an indestructible vehicle. If you have the opportunity to acquire a petrol version (2.7L 2TR-FE or 4.0L 1GR-FE) via importation or on an international market, you are buying absolute peace of mind. These engines, devoid of DPF, AdBlue, and complex turbos, are designed to exceed 500,000 km with basic maintenance. The only real compromise will be the fuel budget. For the diesel versions (the most common in Europe), the 2020 facelift corrected the early flaws of the 2.8L, making it an excellent choice provided it is driven enough to avoid clogging.
