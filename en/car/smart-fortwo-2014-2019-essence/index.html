NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The Smart Fortwo III (generation 453), developed in partnership with Renault (platform shared with the Twingo III), marks a major technological breakthrough for the two-seater city car. Gone is the dreaded slow and fragile automated manual gearbox (Softouch) of the 450/451 generations: the 453 adopts a manual gearbox or an excellent dual-clutch transmission (Twinamic). Still featuring a rear-engine, rear-wheel-drive architecture, it offers a record turning circle (6.95 m) that makes it the undisputed queen of the city. However, despite generally robust Renault-sourced engine blocks, the model suffers from a mixed reliability record. Statistics from the TÜV (21.5% defect rate at 3 years) and What Car? (score of 2/5) place it below the average for its segment, penalized by cooling, electronics, and finish issues.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional maneuverability (6.95 m turning circle)
  • Twinamic automatic gearbox (dual-clutch) infinitely superior to the old Softouch
  • Renault-sourced petrol engines (H4D/H4Bt) with timing chain, generally durable
  • Surprising interior space for the two passengers
  • Unmatched ease of parking

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high TÜV defect rate (21.5% at 3 years) and poor What Car? score (2/5, 35% breakdown rate)
  • Vulnerable cooling system on petrol versions (especially 0.9 Turbo)
  • Very firm suspension, degraded comfort on bumpy roads
  • Soundproofing could be improved (wind and road noise on highways)
  • High maintenance costs in the Mercedes-Benz network for a city car

Check before buying

  • Coolant level and color (risk of leak at the water pump on 0.9 Turbo)
  • Behavior of the Twinamic gearbox (gear shifts must be smooth, without shuddering)
  • Condition of the roof and windshield seals (subject to recalls for lack of adhesion)
  • Front tire wear and condition of the steering ball joints (front axle heavily stressed in the city)
  • Operation of the onboard electronics and multimedia system (frequent bugs)

Verdict

The Smart Fortwo III (453) is undoubtedly the best generation produced. The abandonment of the jerky automated manual gearbox in favor of the Twinamic and the adoption of Renault engines have transformed the driving experience. However, reliability data (TÜV, What Car?, ADAC) urges caution: with 21.5% of defects noted at the technical inspection at 3 years and 35% of owners reporting breakdowns, it remains a temperamental car. Problems are often concentrated on cooling (especially on the 0.9 Turbo), electronics, and assembly defects (seals, windshield).

Buying advice: Favor a 1.0 MPI 71 hp version with the Twinamic gearbox for maximum reliability in 100% urban use. If you opt for the 0.9 Turbo (more versatile), demand an impeccable service record, with particular attention paid to the cooling system. Avoid models that have neglected the maintenance of the dual-clutch gearbox.
