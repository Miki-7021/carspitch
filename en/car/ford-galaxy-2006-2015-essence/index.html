Ford Galaxy

II (WA6) (2006–2015) · 2006 – 2015

Summary

The second-generation Ford Galaxy (chassis code WA6) is a large family MPV, launched in 2006 and produced until 2015. It shares its platform (Ford EUCD) with the S-Max and Mondeo of the time, which gives it surprisingly dynamic road handling for its category. Positioned as a spacious and modular vehicle, it offers up to seven true seats and a substantial loading volume. A significant facelift occurred in 2010, bringing aesthetic changes (front fascia, rear lights) and the introduction of new powertrains, notably EcoBoost petrol engines and more powerful and efficient TDCi diesels. Overall, the Galaxy II is a vehicle appreciated for its space and driving pleasure, but its reliability can vary considerably depending on the chosen engine and the rigor of maintenance.

Strengths 6

  • Exemplary spaciousness and modularity (true 7-seater)
  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation
  • Dynamic road handling for an MPV
  • Choice of powerful and relatively reliable diesel engines (2.0/2.2 TDCi)
  • Solid build quality (excluding some interior plastics)
  • Good road holding and precise steering

Weaknesses 6

  • High fuel consumption of naturally aspirated petrol engines
  • Problematic reliability of the 1.6 EcoBoost
  • Classic problems of modern diesels (DPF, EGR, dual-mass flywheel)
  • Quality of some interior plastics on early versions
  • Sometimes capricious electronics (multimedia system, sensors)
  • Potentially high maintenance cost for turbo petrol and diesel versions

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for EcoBoost and TDCi)
  • Condition of DPF and EGR valve on diesels (warning lights, smoke)
  • Abnormal engine noises (ticking, knocking) on EcoBoost (timing chain)
  • Coolant leaks or overheating on the 1.6 EcoBoost
  • Functioning of electronics (air conditioning, GPS, parking sensors, power windows)
  • Condition of suspension (shock absorbers, silent blocks) and tires (uneven wear)
  • Presence of noises or vibrations in the clutch/flywheel on diesels

Verdict

The Ford Galaxy II is an excellent MPV for large families, offering generous space, well-designed modularity, and pleasant road handling. However, the choice of engine is crucial for long-term reliability. The **2.0 and 2.3 Duratec naturally aspirated petrol engines** are the most reliable and recommended for low-mileage drivers or those who prioritize peace of mind, despite higher fuel consumption. The **2.0 and 2.2 TDCi** are good choices for high-mileage drivers, provided rigorous maintenance and vigilance regarding known weak points (DPF, EGR, flywheel). In contrast, the **1.6 EcoBoost is to be avoided** or only purchased with a clear history and a thorough mechanical inspection, due to its recurring reliability issues. The **2.0 EcoBoost** offers better performance but also requires preventive maintenance.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize a model with a complete and transparent service history, ideally from Ford. A pre-purchase inspection by a professional is highly recommended, especially for EcoBoost engines and high-mileage diesels. Negotiate the price based on potential costs for wear parts specific to diesels (flywheel, DPF) or EcoBoost (decarbonization, turbo). This is a vehicle that can offer many years of service if well-chosen and maintained.
