Summary

The **fourth-generation Kia Sorento (MQ4)** marks a spectacular move upmarket for the Korean manufacturer. Based on the new N3 platform, it offers an assertive design, a premium interior (up to 7 seats), and cutting-edge technology.

Although electrification (HEV and PHEV) has taken center stage in Europe, the **2.2 CRDi (Smartstream) diesel** remains a cornerstone for high-mileage drivers and those who tow. This engine has been thoroughly revised compared to the previous generation (UM): it adopts an aluminum block (a weight saving of 19.5 kg) and is paired with a new 8-speed wet dual-clutch gearbox (8DCT), replacing the old torque converter gearbox. A mid-cycle refresh (facelift) was presented at the end of 2023 (marketed in 2024), bringing a front end inspired by the EV9 and a modernized infotainment system (ccNC).

**Expert's overall rating:** A formidable family SUV, whose diesel version excels on the highway, offering a price/performance/spaciousness ratio that is hard to beat in the current market.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional interior space and true modularity (usable 7 seats)
  • Very torquey 2.2 CRDi engine, fuel-efficient on the road and perfectly suited to its size
  • Fast and smooth 8DCT gearbox (on the diesel) compared to the previous generation
  • Premium interior finish and careful soundproofing
  • Reassuring 7-year / 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty
  • Excellent towing capacity in diesel version (up to 2500 kg)

Weaknesses 5

  • Suspension a bit firm at low speeds, especially with 20-inch wheels
  • Imposing size (4.81 m) making urban maneuvers tricky
  • Diesel engine unsuitable for short urban trips (rapid DPF clogging)
  • Pre-facelift infotainment system sometimes slow to start
  • Trunk volume slightly reduced on the PHEV version

Check before buying

  • Recall history: imperatively check if the 8DCT gearbox oil pump recall has been carried out (concerns the 2.2 CRDi and 2.5 T-GDi).
  • 8DCT gearbox behavior: no jerking should be felt during low-speed gear changes.
  • Diesel particulate filter (DPF) on the diesel: check for absence of engine warning light and inquire about the previous owner's driving habits.
  • Tire wear: the significant weight of the vehicle (especially in PHEV) tends to wear out the running gear quickly.
  • Onboard electronics: test all driving aids (ADAS) and the proper functioning of the electric tailgate.

Verdict

The Kia Sorento IV (MQ4) is an undeniable success that encroaches on premium SUV territory. **In its 2.2 CRDi engine version, it represents the sensible choice for large families, high-mileage drivers, and those who need to tow.** The aluminum block and 8DCT gearbox form a powerful and relatively fuel-efficient duo on the highway.

However, this modern diesel dislikes city driving: if your use is predominantly urban or suburban, opt for the HEV or PHEV versions to avoid costly clogging problems (DPF/EGR). Before purchasing a used model (2020-2022), demand proof that the gearbox recall has been carried out. If the history is clear, it is a highly recommendable purchase.
