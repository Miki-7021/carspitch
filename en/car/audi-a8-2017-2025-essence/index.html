Summary

The fourth-generation Audi A8 (D5, type 4N) represents the technological and status pinnacle of the Ingolstadt manufacturer. Launched at the end of 2017, it introduces a new styling language, a fully touch-sensitive interior interface (MMI Touch Response), and the generalization of 48V mild-hybridization (MHEV) across all its engines. A true technological showcase, it offers predictive active suspension, rear-wheel steering, and Matrix LED/OLED headlights. A facelift occurred at the end of 2021, modernizing the light signatures and the grille. Although its build quality is exceptional, its reliability is marred by recurring problems related to its 48V onboard network (alternator-starter), a major defect on the V6 engines of this generation within the VAG group.

Strengths 5

  • Imperial ride comfort (standard air suspension)
  • Exceptional soundproofing (acoustic double glazing)
  • Finish and assembly quality at the top of the category
  • Onboard technology (Matrix LED headlights, rear-wheel steering)
  • Performance and sound of the V8 on the S8 version

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 48V alternator-starter (immobilizing breakdown)
  • Ergonomics of the all-touch (MMI) distracting while driving
  • Steep depreciation on the used market
  • Very high weight (often over 2.1 tons) wearing out brakes and tires quickly
  • Prohibitive cost of parts and labor

Check before buying

  • Replacement history of the 48V alternator-starter (BSG): essential.
  • Operation of the air suspension (sagging after a night of parking = leak or tired compressor).
  • Rear tire wear (known alignment problem, see recalls).
  • Responsiveness and absence of dead pixels on the two central touch screens and the Virtual Cockpit.
  • Traces of coolant leaks in the V of the engines (TFSI and TDI).

Official recalls

Rappel Conso (France) & NHTSA (United States)

Electrical & Hybrid System:

  • [2025-11-07] (FR) / [2023-12-13] (US): Fire risk. The cellular modules of the high-voltage battery (PHEV) can overheat during charging. Onboard charging cable also affected in the US.
  • [2021-02-24] (US): Moisture infiltration into the engine control unit (ECU) due to a missing sealing pin (A8/S8 2019-2021).

Chassis & Suspension:

  • [2025-01-10] (FR): Possible crack on a rocker arm of the front axle (2024-2025 Models).
  • [2022-09-12] (FR) / [2022-01-26] (US): Alteration of the camber/alignment values of the rear axle leading to premature and uneven tire wear.
  • [2021-11-15] (FR) / [2021-04-26] (US): Possible breakage of the eccentric bolt nuts of the rear axle control arm due to corrosion.
  • [2020-05-08] (FR): Defective rear axle suspension forks, risk of contact between the tire and the bodywork.

Airbags & Safety:

  • [2024-04-03] (US): Defect in the front passenger seat occupant classification sensor.
  • [2023-02-15] (US): Incorrect folding of the front passenger airbag protection flap (A8/S8 2022-2023).
  • [2022-06-09] & [2020-06-12] (FR): Massive recall related to defective Takata (NADI) airbag gas generators.
  • [2021-11-15] (FR): Defective crash sensors in the front doors.
  • [2021-08-05] (US): Seat belt retractor defect.

Engine & Mechanics:

  • [2022-07-29] (FR): Deformation of the radiator tank seal due to temperature variations, fire risk.
  • [2022-03-23] (US): Blockage of the turbocharger oil supply strainer (mainly concerns 2013-2017 models, end of D4 generation/start of D5).
  • [2020-07-10] (FR): Manufacturing defect of an automatic gearbox oil line.
  • [2019-01-16] (US): Possible leak from the fuel injection rails (2015-2018 models).

Software & Electronics:

  • [2025-12-19] (US) / [2022-10-05] (US) / [2021-10-20] (US): Software errors preventing the display of the rearview camera.
  • [2025-03-31] (US): Dashboard software error (Virtual Cockpit).
  • [2022-10-26] (US): Infotainment touch screen defect.
  • [2021-08-20] (FR): Unauthorized engine control software (diesel emissions scandal).

Alternatives

  • Mercedes-Benz S-Class (W222 / W223): The absolute benchmark of the segment. Slightly superior comfort, cutting-edge technologies, but even more elitist prices.
  • BMW 7 Series (G11 / G70): More dynamic to drive, excellent engines (B58/B57), iDrive interface often considered more ergonomic than Audi's all-touch system.
  • Lexus LS (XF50): The Japanese alternative. Exceptional reliability, handcrafted finish, proven hybrid powertrain, but dated infotainment and reduced trunk volume.
  • Porsche Panamera (971): For those looking for a limousine with true sports car handling, sharing some engines (V8) with the A8.

Verdict

The Audi A8 (D5) is an engineering prowess offering masterful ride comfort and silence. The gasoline engines (55 TFSI and 60 TFSI) are smooth and high-performing, perfectly suited to the car's standing. However, buying a used A8 requires extreme caution: the 48V mild-hybrid system (alternator-starter) is a true sword of Damocles whose failure is almost systematic and very expensive. Only buy an example with a crystal-clear history at an Audi dealership, with proof of the 48V BSG replacement, and ideally covered by an extended warranty. The significant depreciation makes it a tempting deal on paper, but maintenance costs remain those of a very high-luxury limousine.
