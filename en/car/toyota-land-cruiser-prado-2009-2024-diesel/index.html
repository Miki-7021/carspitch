Toyota Land Cruiser Prado

150 (J150) · 2009 – 2024

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

Summary

The Toyota Land Cruiser Prado (J150 generation) is a true automotive icon, renowned worldwide for its foolproof robustness and exceptional off-road capabilities. Based on a traditional ladder frame chassis, it received two major facelifts (2013 and 2017) to modernize its equipment and engines. While it concedes clumsy road handling compared to modern unibody SUVs, it compensates with extraordinary reliability and longevity that make it an extremely safe investment. The overall reliability rating is one of the highest in the global automotive market.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability and extraordinary longevity (TÜV Report 3-year defect rate: 9.5% - very good)
  • Off-road capabilities (permanent 4x4, low-range gearbox, differential locks)
  • Exceptional resale value (confirmed by NRMA/RACQ in Australia)
  • Robustness of the automatic gearboxes (A340F, A750F, AC60F)
  • Build quality and resistance of interior materials

Weaknesses 5

  • Clumsy road handling (body roll, braking lacking bite)
  • High fuel consumption, particularly on petrol engines (V6)
  • Dated infotainment system on pre-2020 models
  • DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter) problems on early 2.8 D-4D (2015-2018) in urban use
  • Imposing size and maneuverability in the city

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: oil changes must be strict (ideally every 10,000 km / 1 year for diesels).
  • Chassis condition: check for the absence of perforating corrosion on the side members and underbody (especially in mountainous/salty areas).
  • KDSS (Kinetic Dynamic Suspension System): check for the absence of leaks on the hydraulic cylinders and corrosion of the valve block.
  • Oil strainer (3.0 D-4D): ask if the injector seals have been replaced and the strainer checked.
  • DPF (2.8 D-4D): check the regeneration history and the absence of white/grey smoke from the exhaust.

Verdict

The Toyota Land Cruiser Prado J150 is a highly recommended purchase (**BUY**). It is one of the most reliable vehicles in the world, as confirmed by data from ADAC (1.5 breakdowns/1000 veh/year) and What Car? (4.5/5). If you drive a lot and tow, the **2.8 D-4D (post-2018 or 204 hp version)** is the optimal choice, combining torque, reliability, and reasonable fuel consumption. The 3.0 D-4D remains an excellent used choice provided its injectors are monitored. For absolute reliability without considering the fuel budget, the **4.0 V6 petrol** is indestructible. Simply avoid diesels if your use is exclusively urban, in order to preserve the DPF and the EGR valve.
