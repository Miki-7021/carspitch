Summary

The **Renault Talisman (Generation I - KFE)**, available as a sedan and wagon (Estate), had the challenging task of replacing both the Laguna III and the Latitude. Positioned in the D-segment, it stands out with a stately design, generous spaciousness, and road behavior transformed by the **4Control** four-wheel steering system (depending on trim). While its road qualities are undeniable, its career was marred by chaotic beginnings in electronic reliability (R-Link 2 multimedia system) and early issues with certain engines (notably the 1.6 dCi 160). The 2020 facelift (Phase 2) corrected almost all of these flaws, introducing the Easy Link system, Matrix LED headlights, and updated engines (Blue dCi and TCe), making end-of-life models excellent used car deals.

Strengths 6

  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation (especially with acoustic glazing)
  • Exceptional agility with the 4Control chassis (4-wheel steering)
  • Generous boot volume (608 liters in sedan, 572 liters in Estate)
  • Very successful 2.0 Blue dCi and 1.3 TCe engines
  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Elegant design that ages very well

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.6 dCi 160 before 2018
  • Frequent electronic bugs on the R-Link 2 (Phase 1)
  • Quality of some interior plastics and assemblies (rattles)
  • Rapid wear of front tires (accentuated by 4Control)
  • EDC gearbox sometimes hesitant at low speeds
  • Very significant depreciation (although an advantage on the used market)

Check before buying

  • Complete service history, particularly on diesels (EGR, DPF).
  • Coolant level and color on the 1.6 dCi 160 (risk of head gasket failure).
  • Smooth operation of the EDC gearbox (no juddering on startup or jerks when shifting gears).
  • Tire wear (sensitive geometry with 4Control).
  • Operation of the R-Link 2 touchscreen (responsiveness, absence of intermittent restarts).
  • Absence of 'Check Anti-pollution System' message on Blue dCi versions (AdBlue problem).

Official recalls

  • - **January 2016**: Child safety. Risk of unexpected deactivation of child safety lock on rear doors. - **May 2018**: Emissions control (1.6 dCi). Injection computer reprogramming due to a NOx sensor fault. - **September 2019**: Front axle. Risk of loosening of the front knuckle mounting screws, potentially leading to loss of control. - **October 2020**: Fire risk. Incorrect battery cable routing potentially rubbing and creating a short circuit (models manufactured between Sep 2015 and Nov 2019). - **2021**: EGR cooler (1.6 dCi). Unofficial recall (OTS) for the replacement of the leaking exhaust gas cooler.

Verdict

The Renault Talisman is an excellent road car that suffered from a rushed launch. **If you are looking for a diesel**, by all means AVOID the 1.6 dCi 160 from 2015-2017. Opt for the very economical **1.5 dCi 110** for fuel efficiency, or the excellent **2.0 Blue dCi (160 or 200 hp)** for long journeys and mechanical reliability (despite the inherent AdBlue risk). Phase 2 (from 2020 onwards) is by far the best choice: it corrects finish defects, replaces the capricious R-Link 2 with Easy Link, and offers mature engines. On the used market, its significant depreciation makes it a formidable alternative to German cars, provided you choose the right version.
