Summary

The BMW X4 G02, launched in 2018, represents the second generation of the Bavarian brand's mid-size Sports Activity Coupé (SAC). Based on the CLAR platform, it shares its foundations with the X3 G01, offering a more dynamic and sporty silhouette at the expense of slightly reduced rear passenger space. Positioned as a more stylish and less conventional alternative to the traditional SUV, the X4 G02 stands out with its bold design and road performance.

In 2021, the model received a facelift (LCI - Life Cycle Impulse) bringing subtle aesthetic modifications to the front and rear, an interior update with a larger central screen, and the integration of 48V mild-hybrid technology on most six-cylinder engines, improving efficiency and responsiveness.

Overall, the X4 G02 is perceived as a well-built premium vehicle, offering an engaging driving experience and a high level of comfort, although its suspension can be firm on sporty versions. Its reliability is generally good, in line with recent BMW productions, with a few specific points of attention regarding engines and onboard electronics.

Strengths 6

Weaknesses 6

  • Firm suspension on sporty versions or with large wheels
  • Rear passenger space and trunk volume slightly less than the X3
  • Limited rear visibility due to the sloping roofline
  • High maintenance and spare parts cost
  • Fuel consumption of powerful gasoline versions
  • Potential problems related to the AdBlue and DPF system on diesels if used improperly

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for EGR recalls on diesels)
  • Condition of the emission control system (DPF, AdBlue) on diesels, check warning lights
  • Abnormal noises on cold start (timing chain on B47/B48 at high mileage)
  • Functionality of infotainment (iDrive) and electronic systems
  • Tire and brake wear, especially on M or M Performance versions
  • Absence of coolant or oil leaks under the vehicle
  • Condition of shock absorbers and bushings (road test on degraded roads)

Verdict

The BMW X4 G02 is an excellent choice for those looking for a premium, dynamic, and well-equipped SUV coupé. Its reliability is generally very good, especially for the six-cylinder gasoline (B58) and diesel (B57) engines, which are benchmarks in this regard.

**For diesel engines (B47, B57)**, which were your target, they offer an excellent compromise between performance and fuel consumption. The generous torque is ideal for long journeys and highway driving. However, it is **crucial to ensure that recalls concerning the EGR cooler have been performed** and that the vehicle has been regularly used on long journeys to preserve the DPF and AdBlue system. An impeccable service history is a guarantee of peace of mind.

Gasoline versions, especially the B58 (M40i), are safe bets in terms of reliability and driving pleasure. The M versions (S58) are performance beasts but require a significant maintenance budget.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize a model with a clear service history, ideally at BMW. Check for manufacturer recalls and perform a thorough test drive to detect any suspension noises or electronic anomalies. If usage is primarily urban, gasoline versions will be more suitable to avoid DPF/AdBlue issues with diesels. For high-mileage drivers, the B57 diesels are a wise and very reliable choice.
