Summary

The sixth-generation Vauxhall Corsa (Corsa F, sold as Opel outside the UK) marks a historic turning point for the model. It is the first Corsa developed under the PSA (now Stellantis) era, based on the modular CMP platform shared with the Peugeot 208 II. This change brings significant weight reduction (up to 108 kg), transformed road handling, and the introduction of electric powertrains. A major facelift occurred in late 2023, introducing the 'Vauxhall Vizor' grille, new micro-hybrid (MHEV) powertrains with a timing chain, and a more powerful electric version. While very appealing and dynamic, its reliability is heavily conditioned by the 1.2 petrol engines (PureTech/EB family) which require rigorous monitoring.

Strengths 5

  • Very successful exterior design, especially after the facelift (Vizor)
  • Dynamic road handling and well-balanced chassis
  • Excellent EAT8 automatic gearbox (smooth and responsive)
  • Rich technological equipment (Matrix LED headlights rare in the segment)
  • Low and sporty driving position

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of 1.2 petrol engines with wet timing belt (before 2024)
  • Very limited rear passenger space (legroom and headroom)
  • Difficult rear seat access (narrow doors)
  • Pre-facelift infotainment system slow and dated
  • Quality of some interior plastics inferior to competitors

Check before buying

  • Condition of the timing belt on 1.2 petrol engines (cracks visible via the oil filler cap).
  • Service history: oil changes must have been done every year WITHOUT FAIL on 1.2 petrol engines.
  • Engine light or 'Oil pressure fault' message (sign of a clogged oil strainer).
  • Functioning of the AdBlue system on the 1.5 Diesel (UREA light).
  • On electric versions: test charging on an AC station (to check the onboard charger OBC).

Official recalls

  • November 2020 : Risk of loss of steering control (improperly tightened steering rod bolts).
  • May 2021 : Electrical ground connection problem that could lead to engine stalling or non-deployment of airbags.
  • December 2021 : Possible fuel leak at the high-pressure pump on 1.2 Turbo engines.
  • 2023 : Software recall for emissions management on certain diesel versions.
  • 2024 : Calibration software update to prevent premature belt degradation on 1.2 PureTech (global Stellantis campaign).

What sets it apart 7

Practicality

  • Modern and Appealing Design — The Vauxhall Corsa F stands out with its highly successful exterior design, especially after the facelift featuring the 'Vizor' grille, giving it a contemporary and dynamic look that is sure to turn heads.

Safety

  • Optimal Safety — With a 4-star Euro NCAP rating, the Corsa F ensures solid protection for its occupants, scoring 84% for adults and 86% for children, guaranteeing peace of mind on the road.
  • Efficient and Predictable Braking — The Corsa F's braking system is rated good and predictable, complemented by standard emergency braking assistance, ensuring safe stops and competitive braking distances for its category.

Performance

  • Dynamic Driving Behaviour — Thanks to its well-balanced chassis and modern platform, the Corsa F offers agile and precise road handling, responsive steering, and stable road holding, for everyday driving pleasure.

Comfort

  • Driving Comfort with EAT8 Gearbox — Enjoy a smooth and responsive driving experience thanks to the excellent EAT8 automatic gearbox available on certain petrol engines, offering fluid gear changes and superior driving pleasure.
  • Advanced Onboard Technology — The Corsa F offers rich technological equipment, including rare options in its category such as Matrix LED headlights, for optimal visibility and a modern, connected driving experience.

Reliability

  • Improved Engine Reliability (Hybrid Versions) — The new 1.2 Turbo Hybrid (MHEV) petrol engines, available from 2024, benefit from an improved design with the adoption of a timing chain, ensuring increased reliability and greater peace of mind.

Alternatives

  • Europe / United Kingdom : Peugeot 208 (its technical twin), Renault Clio V (more reliable in petrol), Ford Fiesta (excellent dynamics), Volkswagen Polo (more spacious), Toyota Yaris (hybrid benchmark).
  • Asia / Japan : Honda Fit/Jazz, Mazda 2.
  • North America : N/A (Model not sold).

Verdict

The Vauxhall/Opel Corsa F is a city car with a striking design and undeniable road qualities. However, buying a used petrol version (2019-2023) is a minefield due to the 1.2 (PureTech) engine with a wet timing belt. If you opt for these models, demand an impeccable service history and budget for preventive belt replacement. The best choice lies in the new 1.2 Hybrid (MHEV) versions that appeared in 2024, which finally adopt a timing chain, or in the electric version for urban use.
