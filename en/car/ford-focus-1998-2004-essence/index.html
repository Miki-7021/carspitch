Ford Focus

I (DAW) · 1998 – 2004

NEGOTIATE Reliability 75/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation Ford Focus (codename DAW for the 3-door, DBW for the 5-door, DFW for the sedan, DNW for the wagon) marked a true revolution upon its release. Replacing the aging Escort, it introduced Ford's "New Edge" design and, most notably, an unprecedented multi-link rear suspension in its category ("Control Blade"). This innovation gave it exceptional road handling, combining comfort and dynamism, making it the benchmark in its segment for years (Voted Car of the Year 1999).

A facelift (Phase 2) occurred in late 2001, bringing modified bumpers, turn signals integrated into the headlights, improved sound insulation, a redesigned center console, and the introduction of common rail diesel engines (TDCi). While the petrol engines (Zetec) proved to be extremely robust, the diesel versions and susceptibility to corrosion with age slightly tarnish an overall very positive assessment.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional road handling (Control Blade multi-link rear suspension)
  • Remarkable reliability of Zetec-SE and Zetec-E petrol engines
  • Precise and communicative hydraulic steering
  • Generous interior space and boot volume for its time
  • Exterior and interior design that has aged well (New Edge)
  • Negligible maintenance cost for petrol versions

Weaknesses 5

  • Extreme susceptibility to rust (door bottoms, wheel arches, subframes)
  • Disastrous reliability of early TDCi diesels (Delphi pump, injectors, dual-mass flywheel)
  • Light sound insulation on Phase 1 (road and wind noise)
  • Quality of interior plastics on models before 2001
  • Recurring electrical problems (instrument cluster, central locking)

Check before buying

  • **Corrosion:** Inspect the sills, inner door edges, tailgate, and especially the front/rear subframes.
  • **Speedometer:** If the needle drops to zero while driving (sometimes with a loss of power), the VSS sensor on the gearbox is faulty.
  • **Running gear:** Clunking noises from the rear (worn trailing arm bushings or anti-roll bar drop links).
  • **Central locking:** Water ingress into the doors damaging the locks (GEM module).
  • **Petrol Engines:** Unstable idle or acceleration hesitations (cracked ignition coil, spark plug leads, or punctured PCV hose).
  • **Diesel Engines:** Difficult starting, black smoke, flashing glow plug light (metal filings in the Delphi injection system on TDCi, or faulty VP30 pump on TDDi).

Official recalls

  • - **1999 (Alternator Cable):** Risk of short circuit and fire due to incorrect routing of the alternator cable (1998-1999 models).
  • - **1999 (Brake Master Cylinder):** Possible brake fluid leak requiring master cylinder replacement.
  • - **2000 (Rear Hubs):** Risk of rear hub nut loosening, potentially causing wheel play.
  • - **2003 (Wiper Motor):** Possible water ingress into the front wiper motor, causing it to stop (models built between 1998 and 2000).
  • - **2005 (Door Locks - North America):** Massive recall for rear door latches that did not lock correctly.

Handling

  • Unrivalled Driving Pleasure — Thanks to its revolutionary chassis with "Control Blade" multi-link rear suspension and precise hydraulic steering, the Focus I offers dynamic and safe road handling, acclaimed many times over.

Economy

  • Optimized Running Costs — The petrol versions of the Ford Focus I stand out for their particularly low maintenance costs, making daily use very economical.

Practicality

  • Space and Versatility for Everyday Use — The Focus I offers generous space for its passengers and an appreciable boot volume, making it perfectly suited to family needs and versatile uses.

Comfort

  • Timeless "New Edge" Aesthetic — Its bold and modern "New Edge" design for its time has aged remarkably well, and its title of "Car of the Year 1999" testifies to its impact and relevance.

Performance

  • Lively and Smooth Petrol Engines — The Zetec petrol engines, especially the 1.6, are known for their liveliness, smoothness, and driving pleasure, offering balanced performance for all journeys.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Volkswagen Golf IV (higher trim, sluggish chassis), Peugeot 307 (comfortable but electronic issues), Renault Mégane I/II, Opel Astra G, Honda Civic (very reliable in petrol). - **North America:** Honda Civic, Toyota Corolla, Chevrolet Cavalier, Dodge Neon. - **Asia / Others:** Mazda 323 / Familia, Nissan Sunny / Sentra.

Verdict

The Ford Focus Mk1 is a true icon of the 2000s. If you're looking for an economical, fun-to-drive, and reliable "daily," **the petrol versions (1.6 Zetec leading the way) are excellent choices**, provided you find an example spared from rust. The chassis remains impressively effective even by current standards. However, **steer clear of the diesel engines (TDDi and TDCi)**, whose repair costs (injection pump, flywheel) easily exceed the vehicle's current market value. In the United States, absolutely avoid the 2.0 SPI engine.
