GMC Acadia

II · 2017 – 2023

BUY Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The **second-generation GMC Acadia** marks a change in philosophy compared to its predecessor: it was shortened and lightened to become a more agile midsize SUV, while retaining 3 rows of seats. Sharing its platform (C1XX) with the Chevrolet Traverse and Cadillac XT5, it offers a comfortable and well-finished cabin. A facelift occurred in 2020, introducing a new 2.0L Turbo engine, a 9-speed automatic gearbox, and aesthetic tweaks. Overall, it is a solid family vehicle, whose reliability depends heavily on the chosen engine.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent reliability of the 3.6L V6 (LGX) engine
  • Comfortable interior space with 3 rows of seats
  • Intuitive and responsive infotainment system
  • More agile road handling than the previous generation
  • Good cabin soundproofing

Weaknesses 4

  • Underpowered 2.5L 4-cylinder engine prone to breakdowns (head gasket)
  • Jerks from the automatic gearbox (especially 2017-2018, before update)
  • Trunk volume behind the 3rd row is lower than its competitors (e.g., Chevrolet Traverse)
  • Quality of some interior plastics falls below category standards

Check before buying

  • Software update history for the automatic gearbox
  • Signs of coolant leaks or overheating on the 2.5L engine
  • Abnormal noises or growling from the transfer case (AWD) during tight turns
  • Operation of the rearview camera (coaxial harness subject to recall)
  • Verification that the numerous safety recalls (airbags, transmission) have been performed

Verdict

The second-generation GMC Acadia is a competent family SUV, but its reliability record is highly mixed depending on the engine. **The obvious choice is the 3.6L V6 (LGX)**, which proves to be very robust, powerful, and perfectly suited to the vehicle's size. On the other hand, **the 2.5L 4-cylinder engine is to be avoided** due to its recurring head gasket issues and lack of breath. When buying a used model, favor an AWD V6, ensure the transfer case has had its oil changed, and verify that the automatic gearbox updates (especially on the 2017-2018 model years) as well as the numerous manufacturer recalls have been performed.
