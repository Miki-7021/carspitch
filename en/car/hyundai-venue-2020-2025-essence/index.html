Hyundai Venue

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The **Hyundai Venue (Generation I - Code QX)** is an ultra-compact urban SUV (B-segment/A-SUV) designed to be one of the brand's most affordable vehicles. Indirectly replacing the Hyundai Accent in certain North American markets, it stands out with its cubic design, great maneuverability, and purely urban vocation.

It is exclusively offered in front-wheel drive (FWD), which simplifies its mechanics and reduces maintenance costs. Not sold in Western Europe (where Hyundai offers the Bayon), the Venue is highly successful in North America, Australia, and India. A facelift was introduced in 2022 for Asian markets, bringing a modernized front end and taillights connected by a light bar. Its overall reliability rating is excellent, driven by proven naturally aspirated engines and simple onboard electronics.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of the 1.6L MPI engine (no turbo or direct injection)
  • Very low maintenance and repair costs
  • Intuitive and responsive infotainment system (wireless Apple CarPlay / Android Auto on certain trims)
  • Excellent maneuverability in the city (compact size, good turning radius)
  • Ergonomics of physical controls (climate control, volume)

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of all-wheel drive (AWD), even as an option
  • Soundproofing could be improved on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Hard interior plastics prone to scratching
  • Firm suspension, degraded comfort on bumpy roads
  • Anemic 1.2L MPI engine (emerging markets)

Check before buying

  • Verify that the recalls on the seat belt pretensioners have been performed (2020-2022 models).
  • On 2024 models (1.6L), ensure there is no loss of power related to the EGR valve short circuit.
  • Behavior of the IVT (CVT) gearbox during the test drive: it must not show any jerking or metallic whining.
  • On 1.0 T-GDI versions with the 7DCT gearbox, test hill starts to detect any excessive slipping or clutch shudder.

Verdict

The Hyundai Venue is an extremely rational choice for anyone looking for an economical and reliable urban vehicle. According to data from J.D. Power and the ADAC, it ranks among the best in its class for initial quality and long-term reliability. Its secret lies in its simplicity: the naturally aspirated 1.6L MPI engine coupled with the IVT gearbox forms a trouble-free duo, free from the complex problems associated with turbos or all-wheel drive systems. If you can accept its hard plastics and average highway soundproofing, it is a highly recommended purchase, particularly on the used market where its depreciation makes it very attractive.
