Summary

The Dacia Duster II (codename HM) had the challenging task of succeeding an absolute best-seller. Launched in late 2017, it retains its predecessor's B0+ platform but completely modernizes its bodywork, interior, and soundproofing. Positioned as the compact SUV with an unbeatable price/performance ratio, it underwent two major evolutions: a technical and aesthetic facelift in 2021 (new Y-shaped light signature, 8-inch screen, EDC gearbox) and the integration of Dacia's new visual identity (new 'Dacia Link' logo) in late 2022. While diesel engines suffered from anti-pollution standards (AdBlue issues), the petrol offering was brilliantly renewed with the arrival of the 1.0 TCe (including the excellent Eco-G LPG version) and 1.3 TCe units, relegating the notorious 1.2 TCe from the first months of sales to oblivion. Overall generation rating: Excellent, provided you choose the right engine.

Strengths 6

  • Unbeatable price/performance/spaciousness ratio
  • Remarkable reliability of 1.0 TCe and 1.3 TCe petrol engines
  • Eco-G (LPG) version extremely economical to run
  • Genuine off-road capabilities on 4x4 versions
  • Suspension comfort and soundproofing significantly improved compared to Duster I
  • Reduced maintenance and insurance costs

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.2 TCe 125 (early production)
  • Recurring AdBlue system problems on Blue dCi
  • Interior plastic quality (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Aerodynamic soundproofing on highways (wind noise above 110 km/h)
  • Frequent manual gearbox whistling
  • Fragile paint (prone to stone chips)

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: Crucial, especially for LPG versions (specific filters) and the 1.2 TCe.
  • MediaNav multimedia system: Check that it doesn't reboot in a loop and that the touchscreen responds well.
  • Air conditioning: Ensure it produces cold air quickly (known weakness of the compressor/condenser).
  • Transmission (especially 4x4): Listen for rolling noises or whistling from the gearbox/rear axle.
  • Dashboard warning lights (Blue dCi): An anti-pollution light or orange wrench often indicates an AdBlue problem (costly repair).
  • Fuel gauge: On some models, the gauge (petrol or LPG) lacks precision or gets stuck.

Official recalls

  • November 2018 (Electrical): Risk of short circuit due to incorrect positioning of the battery wiring harness (models manufactured until June 2018).
  • August 2019 (Steering): Replacement of the steering column due to a welding defect (production from June to July 2019).
  • February 2021 (LPG): Inspection and possible replacement of the LPG tank due to an internal welding defect (very small series produced late 2020).
  • May 2022 (Airbag): Risk of non-deployment of the left curtain airbag (production February-April 2022).

Verdict

The Dacia Duster II is a highly recommendable used purchase, on the strict condition of opting for the right engine. If you are looking for a petrol model, the 1.0 TCe, 1.0 Eco-G (LPG), and 1.3 TCe units are excellent choices, combining reliability, driving pleasure, and controlled costs. The LPG version is the absolute best buy to counter fuel inflation. However, flee like the plague from 2017-2018 models equipped with the 1.2 TCe 125, whose design flaws are irreversible. On the diesel side, the older 1.5 dCi (without AdBlue) are robust, but the recent Blue dCi are plagued by chronic and expensive failures of the anti-pollution system. Prioritize a facelifted model (post-2021) for better equipment and refined ergonomics.
