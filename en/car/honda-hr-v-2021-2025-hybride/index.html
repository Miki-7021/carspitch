Honda HR-V

II (RV) e:HEV · 2021 – 2025

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

All Honda models

Summary

The Honda HR-V of the RV generation (often globally referred to as the 3rd generation, or 2nd modern generation) marks a radical departure from its predecessor. Adopting a sleek coupe SUV silhouette, it moves upmarket with a minimalist and refined interior presentation. In Europe, it is exclusively sold with the e:HEV (i-MMD) hybrid powertrain, which prioritizes electric traction in the city and uses the internal combustion engine primarily as a generator, except at high speeds where it connects directly to the wheels. Although the trunk volume has decreased compared to the previous generation (319 liters), it retains the exceptional modularity of the *Magic Seats* in the rear. It is a particularly well-executed vehicle, targeting urban and suburban customers looking for flawless reliability and controlled fuel consumption.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability of the e:HEV hybrid system
  • Extremely low fuel consumption in urban and secondary road driving
  • Unbeatable rear modularity (Magic Seats: flip-up seat bases)
  • Significantly improved finish quality compared to the previous generation
  • Very comprehensive standard safety equipment (Honda Sensing)

Weaknesses 5

  • 'Rubber band' effect (engine revving loudly) during strong accelerations or in mountainous terrain
  • Disappointing trunk volume for the category (319 liters under the parcel shelf)
  • Infotainment system a bit graphically dated
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (road noise)
  • 12V battery sensitive to long, prolonged stops

Check before buying

  • Condition and age of the 12V battery (source of 90% of immobilization breakdowns on this model).
  • History of software updates (infotainment and hybrid management).
  • Smooth operation of the Honda Connect system (test Apple CarPlay / Android Auto).
  • Tire wear (the chassis is tuned quite dynamically, which can stress the front tires).

Official recalls

  • November 2023 (Europe): Software update for the eCall communication module. A bug could prevent the correct transmission of vehicle data to emergency services in the event of an accident.
  • March 2022 (Certain Asian markets): Check of the electronic power steering (EPS) torque sensor, which could lead to a loss of assistance.
  • *Note: The HR-V RV is remarkably spared from heavy mechanical recalls, confirming Honda's mastery of this platform.*

What sets it apart 6

Reliability

  • Exceptional e:HEV Hybrid System Reliability — Experience absolute peace of mind with the legendary reliability of Honda's e:HEV hybrid system, renowned for its robustness and long-term durability.

Economy

  • Record-Breaking Urban Fuel Economy — Achieve significant daily savings thanks to the exceptionally low fuel consumption of the e:HEV hybrid system, particularly efficient in urban environments.

Practicality

  • Unrivaled Rear Modularity with Magic Seats — Optimize your cargo and transport space with the ingenious rear 'Magic Seats', offering unique modularity and exceptional versatility for all your needs.

Safety

  • Advanced Safety and Optimal Protection — Travel with complete peace of mind thanks to a very comprehensive standard safety equipment (Honda Sensing) and occupant protection confirmed by excellent crash-test results.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Toyota C-HR (hybrid benchmark, polarizing design), Renault Captur E-Tech (more versatile, larger trunk), Hyundai Kona Hybrid.
  • Japan / Asia: Toyota Corolla Cross, Nissan Kicks e-Power (similar series hybrid technology).
  • North America: (Against the local HR-V RZ) Subaru Crosstrek, Mazda CX-30, Toyota Corolla Cross.

Verdict

The Honda HR-V e:HEV (RV Generation) is a highly recommendable purchase. It appeals to those who prioritize long-term reliability, daily running costs, and intelligent modularity, rather than pure performance or raw trunk volume. Its hybrid system is one of the smoothest and most efficient on the market in urban environments. Mechanical failures are almost non-existent, making this SUV a choice for absolute peace of mind in the recent used car market.
