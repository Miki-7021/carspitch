Summary

The sixth-generation Outback (BT code) is based on the Subaru Global Platform (SGP), offering a significant leap forward in rigidity, sound insulation, and safety (EyeSight v4). **IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:** As an expert, it is crucial to specify that **Subaru definitively abandoned diesel engines (the famous 2.0D Boxer EE20) at the end of the previous generation (BS) in 2018/2019**. The BT generation Outback (2020-2025) has **NEVER** been produced with a diesel engine, in any global market, due to anti-pollution standards (Euro 6d) and development costs. The range is exclusively built around Boxer gasoline engines (naturally aspirated and turbocharged). This model remains the absolute benchmark for raised wagons, offering off-road capabilities superior to many SUVs thanks to its permanent symmetrical all-wheel drive.

Weaknesses 6

  • TOTAL ABSENCE OF DIESEL ENGINE (penalizing for high-mileage European drivers)
  • Absence of hybrid powertrain (HEV/PHEV) leading to high ecological penalties in Europe
  • 11.6-inch infotainment system slow and buggy on early model years (2020-2022)
  • Fragile windshield (frequent cracks from minor impacts)
  • Mandatory CVT gearbox (smooth but lacks dynamism)
  • High urban fuel consumption (especially on the 2.4 Turbo)

Check before buying

  • Infotainment system update history (black screen, CarPlay/Android Auto disconnection).
  • Windshield condition (look for the slightest micro-crack, particularly near the pillars or defroster).
  • 12V battery replacement history (known parasitic discharge issues).
  • On the 2.5i: check if the Thermo Control Valve (TCV) has been replaced under warranty (check engine light on).
  • Smooth operation of the CVT gearbox (no jerks at low speed, fluid change recommended every 60,000 km despite 'lifetime' recommendations from some dealerships).

What sets it apart 12

safety

  • Unrivaled Safety with EyeSight — The Subaru Outback is an undisputed leader in safety, equipped as standard with the advanced EyeSight v4 system. It achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 88% for adult protection and 89% for child protection. It is also a consistent winner of the prestigious IIHS Top Safety Pick+ award.

braking

  • Absolute Braking Control — Enjoy complete confidence thanks to the Outback's excellent braking performance. Its firm pedal and short stopping distances, combined with the EyeSight system's automatic emergency braking, ensure optimal safety in all situations.

handling

  • Exceptional Grip and Stability — Subaru's standard Symmetrical AWD provides stable and predictable road holding, ensuring superior grip and a serene driving experience, even in the most demanding conditions. Its road behavior is exemplary for a vehicle in its category.

practicality

  • Generous Space and Versatility — The Outback stands out with its very generous interior space and trunk volume, offering optimal comfort for all passengers and impressive loading capacity for all your adventures.

Performance

  • Superior All-Terrain Capabilities — With its unparalleled Symmetrical AWD and an exceptional ground clearance of 21.3 cm (or 24 cm on the Wilderness version), the Outback surpasses many SUVs in all-terrain capabilities, allowing you to explore new horizons with complete confidence.

comfort

  • Refined Driving Comfort — The Subaru Global Platform (SGP) brings a significant leap forward in comfort. Enjoy a soft suspension and significantly improved sound insulation, transforming every journey into a pleasant and relaxing experience.

Alternatives

* **Europe:** Volvo V60 Cross Country (available in mild-hybrid/PHEV), Audi A4 Allroad (available in TDI), Volkswagen Passat Alltrack (available in TDI), Skoda Superb Scout. * **North America:** Honda Passport, Toyota RAV4 TRD Off-Road, Ford Bronco Sport, Audi A6 Allroad. * **Japan / Asia:** Toyota Harrier, Mazda CX-60.

Verdict

The 6th generation Outback (BT) is an exceptional vehicle for those seeking the comfort of a wagon, safety, and true winter/off-road capabilities. **However, buyers looking for a Diesel must turn to competitors (Audi Allroad, VW Alltrack) or to Subaru models from before 2018 (with caution given the reliability of the EE20 engine).** In gasoline, the 2.5i is a pragmatic and very mechanically reliable choice. The main drawbacks of this generation are electronic (fickle infotainment on early model years) and hardware-related (fragile windshield, weak 12V battery). Prioritize a 2023 or later model (Facelift) where most software bugs and early issues have been corrected at the factory.
