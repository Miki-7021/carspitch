Summary

The second-generation Chevrolet Traverse (C1XX) is a large family SUV (3-row Midsize in North America, but with European Full-size dimensions). It replaces the first generation (Lambda platform) by offering significant weight loss, a squarer design inspired by true 4x4s, and an interior space that ranks among the vastest in its class, rivaling minivans. A facelift was introduced for the 2022 model year (delayed by a year due to COVID-19), bringing a modernized front end and technological updates. Overall, it is a pragmatic vehicle, very popular in North America, offering average reliability for the segment, conditional on the choice of engine.

Strengths 5

  • Gigantic interior space (3rd row genuinely habitable for adults)
  • Exceptional trunk volume, even with all 3 rows in place
  • High-performing and reliable 3.6L V6 (LGX) engine
  • Intuitive and responsive infotainment system (Apple CarPlay / Android Auto)
  • Highway ride comfort

Weaknesses 5

  • Calibration of the 9-speed automatic gearbox sometimes hesitant (jerks)
  • 2.0L Turbo engine (2018-2019) fragile and unsuited to the weight
  • Quality of some interior plastics on lower trims (LS/LT)
  • City fuel consumption with the V6
  • Imposing size making urban parking difficult

Check before buying

  • Gearbox behavior during the test drive (smooth shifts, no clunks at low speeds)
  • Recall history (very numerous for airbags and transmission, see list)
  • Signs of oil leaks if buying a 2.0T RS version (2018-2019)
  • Touchscreen operation (some cases of 'ghost touch' or black screens)
  • Brake and tire wear (heavy vehicle that stresses consumables)

Official recalls

The Gen II Traverse has been the subject of several NHTSA recalls (United States/Canada). It is imperative to verify via the VIN that these fixes have been applied:

  • [2023-03-16] Transmission (23V172000): Incorrect sun gear in the transmission that could lead to a loss of drive power (2023 Models).
  • [2023-02-02] Transmission (23V042000): Missing retaining ring on the left transmission half-shaft (2023 Models).
  • [2022-03-31] Airbags (22V210000): Left/right curtain airbag harness connector potentially incompatible (2020-2022).
  • [2021-10-18] PRM Conversion (21V817000): Mounting issue on versions modified for wheelchairs (Braun Corporation).
  • [2021-06-24] Airbags (21V472000): Incorrect airbag calibration software installed during dealership maintenance (2021 Models).
  • [2021-04-08] Airbags (21V246000): Potentially defective mounting of the side curtain airbags (2010-2019).
  • [2021-02-25 & 2020-11-05] Tires (21V115000 / 20V684000): Tires potentially damaged during factory mounting (2020 Models).
  • [2020-10-29] Transmission (20V668000): Transmission fluid leak due to missing bolts on the start/stop accumulator (2018-2020).
  • [2020-10-15] Fuel (20V639000): Fuel pump nozzle may have a plastic manufacturing residue (2020 Models).
  • [2020-07-30] Airbags (20V446000): Defect in the curtain airbag inflator diffuser (2020 Models).
  • [2019-09-06] Structure (19V643000): Possible crack on the right frame rail in the engine compartment (2020 Models).
  • [2018-11-01] Airbags (18V774000): Diagnostic module (SDM) left in 'manufacturing mode', preventing airbag deployment (2019 Models).

What sets it apart 11

Practicality

  • Third Row Fits Adults Comfortably — The third row is genuinely habitable for adults, so larger families can carry up to seven or eight passengers without forcing anyone into a cramped space on longer journeys.
  • Huge Cargo Volume With All Seats — The trunk remains exceptionally large even with all three rows in place, letting you carry luggage, strollers or shopping for the whole family without folding any seats.
  • Minivan Space Without Minivan Look — It offers the interior space of a minivan without its appearance, letting families who need maximum room avoid the styling they may not want.

Reliability

  • Proven And Reliable V6 Engine — The 3.6L V6 (LGX) is very reliable and has corrected the timing chain issues of older GM V6 units, giving buyers a robust powertrain suited to the vehicle's weight.

Performance

  • Smooth And Linear V6 Power — The V6 delivers linear power and smooth operation that is well matched to the Traverse's size, making long trips, light towing and full-load family driving effortless.
  • Significant Weight Reduction Over Predecessor — The second generation sheds significant weight compared with the previous Lambda-platform model, which supports efficiency and handling without sacrificing the vast interior space.
  • V6 Suited To Towing And Long Trips — The 3.6L V6 is described as polyvalent and suited to long journeys and light towing, matching the needs of families who regularly load the vehicle heavily.

Comfort

  • Intuitive Infotainment With Smartphone Mirroring — The infotainment system is intuitive and responsive and includes Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, so you can use familiar navigation and media apps directly on the screen.
  • Comfortable Highway Cruising — Ride comfort on the highway is a strong point, reducing fatigue on long journeys and making the Traverse a pleasant companion for family road trips.

Design

  • Boxy Design Inspired By Real 4x4s — The squarer styling draws inspiration from true 4x4s, giving the Traverse a more purposeful, rugged look than a conventional family MPV.
  • Updated Face And Tech After Facelift — The 2022 facelift brought a modernized front end and technology updates, so later models offer a fresher appearance and more current equipment.

Verdict

The second-generation Chevrolet Traverse is an excellent choice for families needing the space of a minivan without the look. Imperatively favor the 3.6L V6 (LGX) engine, which proves to be very reliable, powerful, and perfectly suited to the vehicle's size. Avoid the rare 2.0L Turbo version (2018-2019 RS trim), which is undersized and prone to turbo issues. The main point of attention when buying concerns the 9-speed automatic transmission: ensure it does not jerk and that software updates have been performed. Finally, given the long list of recalls (especially on the 2019-2020 years concerning airbags and structure), a strict verification of the maintenance history via the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) is mandatory before any purchase.
