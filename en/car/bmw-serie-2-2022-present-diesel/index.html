Summary

The **BMW 2 Series Active Tourer (U06)** represents the second generation of the Bavarian compact MPV. Based on the FAAR platform (an evolution of UKL2), it abandons the proportions of the previous generation (F45) for a more assertive design, marked by an hypertrophied grille and flush door handles. Inside, the revolution is total with the adoption of the *BMW Curved Display* (OS 8 then OS 9) and the controversial removal of the iDrive controller, forcing an all-touch or voice-controlled ergonomics. Although the MPV market is declining in favor of SUVs, the U06 stands out as a formidable premium offering, providing generous spaciousness, a high-quality finish, and very efficient powertrains. The diesel version, powered by the excellent B47 engine, remains a preferred choice for high-mileage drivers thanks to its exceptional fuel efficiency.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional fuel efficiency and driving pleasure from the 218d (B47) diesel engine.
  • Interior finish quality worthy of higher segments (materials, assembly).
  • Generous rear spaciousness and sliding rear bench (depending on versions).
  • Very careful soundproofing, perfectly masking the diesel clatter.
  • Dynamic road handling for an MPV (typical BMW).

Weaknesses 5

  • Confusing multimedia ergonomics: disappearance of the physical iDrive controller, everything goes through the touchscreen.
  • Frequent software bugs on early models (OS 8): black screens, CarPlay/Android Auto disconnections.
  • Firm suspension comfort, especially with M Sport trim and 18/19-inch wheels.
  • Boot volume reduced on MHEV and PHEV versions (battery under the floor).
  • Elite pricing and always expensive options policy.

Check before buying

  • Integrated Brake system: check if the vehicle is affected by the massive Continental recall (loss of braking assistance).
  • Software update history (iDrive): numerous fixes have been deployed to stabilize the infotainment system.
  • Front tire wear: the significant torque (especially on 218d, 223d, and PHEV) tends to wear out the rubber on the front axle quickly.
  • DKG7 gearbox operation: it must be smooth. Jerks at low speed may require mechatronics reprogramming.
  • On diesels: ensure the vehicle has not been used exclusively in the city (risk of DPF/EGR clogging).

Verdict

The BMW 2 Series Active Tourer (U06) is undoubtedly the most accomplished compact MPV on the current market. With the diesel engine (218d or 223d), it shines with remarkable mechanical reliability, the B47 block being today one of the most robust and efficient engines in automotive production. It is a highly recommended choice for high-mileage drivers. The main pitfalls of this model are not mechanical, but electronic (iDrive 8 bugs) and related to external suppliers (critical Continental braking system recall). Before purchasing, demand proof that safety recalls have been carried out and prioritize a model whose multimedia system has been updated. Avoid diesel if your use is strictly urban, otherwise, you risk clogging the emission control systems.
