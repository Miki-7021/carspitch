Honda ZR-V

I (RZ) e:HEV · 2022 – 2025

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

Summary

The **Honda ZR-V** (RZ generation) is a compact SUV strategically positioned between the HR-V and CR-V. Based on the excellent 11th-generation Civic platform, it inherits its undeniable dynamic qualities, careful ergonomics, and powertrains. In Europe, it is exclusively offered with the **2.0 i-MMD e:HEV** hybrid powertrain, renowned for its efficiency and driving pleasure. In other markets (particularly North America, where it is paradoxically badged 'HR-V'), it adopts conventional internal combustion engines (1.5 Turbo or 2.0 naturally aspirated). It is a very well-designed vehicle, offering one of the best comfort/handling compromises in its category, although its trunk volume is behind the competition.

**Overall design score: 8.5/10**

Strengths 5

  • First-class road handling (Civic chassis)
  • Formidable efficiency of the e:HEV powertrain in urban and road driving
  • Interior finish quality and physical ergonomics (real climate controls)
  • Overall reliability of powertrains
  • Careful sound insulation (except under strong acceleration)

Weaknesses 4

  • Disappointing trunk volume for its size (380 to 390 liters)
  • Engine revving effect (motorboating) during strong accelerations (e:HEV)
  • Infotainment system a bit graphically dated
  • High purchase price in Europe (only available as a hybrid)

Check before buying

  • Condition of the 12V battery (known weakness on Honda hybrids if prolonged immobilization)
  • Wear of front tires (the incisive front axle can encourage dynamic driving)
  • Steering operation (no hard spots or 'sticking' should be felt in a straight line)
  • History of software updates (hybrid management and multimedia)

Verdict

The **Honda ZR-V** is an extremely rational and reassuring choice for anyone looking for a reliable, dynamic, and economical compact SUV. Its **e:HEV** powertrain is a masterpiece of engineering that combines the smoothness of electric power with the range of an internal combustion engine, without the constraints of charging. Although it suffers from a somewhat small trunk for large families and an elite price in Europe, it compensates with rigorous manufacturing quality and reliability that promises to be excellent, directly in line with the Civic from which it derives. It is a 'peace of mind' purchase par excellence.
