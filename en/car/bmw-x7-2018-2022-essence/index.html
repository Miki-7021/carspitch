BMW X7

I (G07) · 2018 – 2022

NEGOTIATE Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation BMW X7 (G07) (before its late 2022 facelift) is the Bavarian brand's first true luxury 7-seater SUV. Designed primarily for the North American and Asian markets, and assembled in Spartanburg (United States), it is positioned against the Mercedes-Benz GLS and the Range Rover. It stands out for its imperial comfort, top-flight soundproofing, and the excellent standard 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox. All versions are equipped with adaptive air suspension. Although mechanically robust (especially with the B58 inline 6-cylinder petrol engine), the X7 is an extremely heavy vehicle (over 2.3t) and packed with electronics, which exposes it to software bugs and premature wear of the running gear. The significant number of official recalls (braking, steering, electronics) requires a rigorous check of the history before purchase.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (standard air suspension)
  • 6-cylinder petrol engine (B58) with remarkable reliability and drivability
  • Soundproofing worthy of a 7 Series limousine
  • Royal interior space, including in the third row
  • Very high level of finish and assembly quality

Weaknesses 5

  • Excessive weight (rapidly wears out brakes, tires, and running gear)
  • Electronic complexity leading to frequent bugs
  • Maintenance and repair costs (especially the air suspension and M Sport brakes)
  • V8 (N63) and Quad-Turbo diesel (M50d) engines very costly to maintain long-term
  • Size unsuited to European city centers

Check before buying

  • Complete recall history (very numerous on this model, see list).
  • Air suspension operation: the vehicle must rise and lower without suspicious noise from the compressor, and not sag after a night of parking.
  • Tire wear (often asymmetrical due to camber and weight) and brake discs.
  • On B58 petrol engines: traces of coolant around the water pump and oil filter housing.
  • On N63 V8 engines: oil change history (ideally every 10,000 km/1 year) and oil consumption.
  • Operation of all electrical equipment (3rd-row electric seats, panoramic sunroof, 360° cameras).

Alternatives

Verdict

The BMW X7 is a formidable flagship, offering superior driving pleasure to most of its direct competitors. The **xDrive40i (B58)** petrol engine is by far the most rational and reliable choice: it offers more than adequate performance while avoiding the financial money pits associated with the V8s (N63) or the complex 4-turbo diesel (M50d). However, the impressive list of official recalls affecting vital components (brakes, steering, starter) and the inherent fragility of air suspensions on a vehicle of this weight require the utmost caution. A used purchase is only recommendable with a crystal-clear history, proof that all recalls have been performed, and ideally an extended warranty.
