Alpine A110

II · 2017 – 2021

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The Alpine A110 (Generation II) marks the spectacular rebirth of the legendary French brand. Designed around a 100% aluminum chassis and body, it stands out for its featherweight (around 1,100 kg) and exceptional agility. Positioned as a pure, uncompromising sports car, it directly competes with the Porsche 718 Cayman.

**⚠️ EXPERT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:** It is crucial to specify that **the Alpine A110 has NEVER been offered with a diesel engine**, nor as a hybrid or electric vehicle during this period. The request targeting a diesel model is based on a factual error. The A110 is exclusively powered by a mid-rear mounted 1.8-liter turbocharged 4-cylinder gasoline engine (code M5P). This report will therefore focus on the actual (gasoline) engines that form the very essence of this model.
