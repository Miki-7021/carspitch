Ford Galaxy

II (WA6) (2006–2015) · 2006 – 2015

NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

Summary

The second-generation Ford Galaxy (chassis code WA6) was launched in 2006, sharing its EUCD platform with the S-Max and Mondeo. Positioned as a large family MPV, it stands out for its generous spaciousness, its seven modular seats, and its dynamic road handling, inherited from the Mondeo. It received a facelift in 2010, bringing aesthetic changes (front fascia, rear lights) and the introduction of new engines, notably the EcoBoost petrol units and Euro 5 TDCi engines. Overall, the Galaxy WA6 is a reliable vehicle for its category, but like any heavy and frequently used MPV, it presents specific points of vigilance, particularly concerning the very common diesel engines in Europe.

Strengths 6

  • Exemplary spaciousness and modularity (7 true seats)
  • Dynamic and comfortable road handling
  • Good sound insulation
  • Solid build quality
  • Wide choice of engines (especially diesel)
  • Good value for money on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Bulkiness in the city
  • High fuel consumption for petrol engines
  • Uneven reliability of certain diesels (1.6 TDCi, 1.8 TDCi) and EcoBoost
  • Potentially high maintenance cost for the most powerful diesel versions
  • Sometimes capricious electronics (multimedia system, sensors)

Check before buying

  • **Complete service history** : crucial for diesels and EcoBoost.
  • **Dual-mass flywheel and clutch** : noises, vibrations, slipping (especially 1.8 TDCi, 2.0 TDCi).
  • **DPF and EGR valve** : condition, cleaning/replacement history (especially if urban use).
  • **Turbo** : abnormal whistling, oil consumption (1.6 TDCi, EcoBoost).
  • **Injectors** : leaks, knocking, smoke (all diesels).
  • **Timing system** : belt on diesel (age/km), chain on EcoBoost (noises on startup).
  • **Suspension and running gear** : noises, wear of silent blocks (heavy vehicle).
  • **Air conditioning** : operation, leaks.
  • **Electronics** : operation of all equipment (windows, GPS, sensors).
  • **Bodywork** : general condition, chassis corrosion (rare but possible).

Verdict

The Ford Galaxy II (WA6) is an excellent family MPV, offering generous space and driving pleasure superior to the average in its category. Its road handling is a major asset. However, reliability depends heavily on the chosen engine and maintenance history.

**For intensive family use and long journeys, the 2.0 TDCi (140/163 hp) are the most recommendable**, provided rigorous maintenance and monitoring of classic weak points (DPF, EGR, flywheel). Avoid the 1.6 TDCi and 1.8 TDCi if you cannot guarantee perfect maintenance or if your use is predominantly urban, as their weaknesses can become costly.

**EcoBoost petrol engines** are powerful but require even more meticulous maintenance to avoid timing and clogging issues. **Naturally aspirated Duratec engines** are robust but thirsty and lack torque.

**Buying advice**: Prioritize a model with a clear maintenance history, ideally at a Ford dealership, and do not hesitate to have the vehicle inspected by a professional, especially for diesel and EcoBoost versions. The Galaxy is a heavy vehicle that stresses its components, so the general condition of the running gear and brakes should also be checked. With a good example and consistent maintenance, it remains a very relevant choice for large families.
