Summary

The Mercedes-Benz C-Class W204 represents the third generation of the German manufacturer's premium compact sedan. Launched in March 2007, it succeeded the W203 with a more dynamic approach and a more assertive design, inspired by the S-Class W221. Available as a sedan (W204), wagon (S204), and coupe (C204, from 2011), it was a major commercial success for Mercedes-Benz.

A significant **facelift** occurred in **2011**, bringing aesthetic changes (headlights, bumpers, completely revised interior) and, more importantly, technical updates, with the introduction of new, more efficient engines (notably the M271 CGI and M276 V6 gasoline engines, as well as the evolution of the OM651 diesel).

Overall, the W204 is perceived as a reliable car, marking a return to better build quality after some criticism of the W203. However, like any complex vehicle, it presents specific points of concern for certain engines and model years, particularly on the early V6 gasoline engines (M272) and the first versions of the four-cylinder diesels (OM651).

Strengths 6

  • Build quality and interior finish (especially post-facelift)
  • Ride comfort and sound insulation
  • Balanced road handling (RWD, precise steering)
  • Wide choice of powerful and efficient engines (post-facelift)
  • Brand image and decent residual value
  • Improved overall reliability compared to the W203

Weaknesses 5

  • Cost of parts and maintenance at Mercedes-Benz
  • Average rear passenger space and trunk volume for the category
  • Standard equipment sometimes sparse on entry-level versions
  • Dated COMAND multimedia system on pre-facelift versions

Check before buying

  • **Complete service history and invoices**: Essential for all engines.
  • **M272 V6 engines (pre-2011)**: Check engine serial number for the balance shaft sprocket issue (before late 2006/early 2007). Listen for abnormal engine noises.
  • **M271 engines (Kompressor and CGI)**: Timing chain noises on cold start, check camshaft sensors. On CGI, check injector and HP pump history.
  • **OM651 Diesel engines (post-2009)**: Check injector replacement history (recall or warranty claim).
  • **OM642 V6 Diesel engines**: Look for traces of oil leaks (particularly around the cooler and turbo).
  • **7G-Tronic automatic gearbox**: Check shifting smoothness, fluid change history (every 60,000 km).
  • **Electronics**: Functionality of all equipment (air conditioning, GPS, power windows, etc.).
  • **Bodywork**: Rust spots (rare but possible on wheel arches or sills), condition of the rims.

What sets it apart 14

security

  • Exemplary and Recognized Safety — The C-Class W204 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with an impressive 97% score for adult occupant protection. It was also designated 'Top Safety Pick' by the IIHS, attesting to its superior level of protection.
  • Powerful and Precise Braking — Equipped with powerful disc brakes on all four wheels and a perfectly calibrated ABS system, the W204 offers excellent braking performance, with typical stopping distances of approximately 35 to 38 meters from 100 km/h, ensuring optimal control in all circumstances.

Verdict

The Mercedes-Benz C-Class W204 is an excellent premium sedan, offering a successful compromise between comfort, dynamism, and brand image. Its overall reliability is good, especially after the 2011 facelift.

**For a confident purchase:** * **Prioritize post-facelift models (from 2011 onwards)**: They benefit from more modern engines (M271 CGI, M276 V6, improved OM651) and a significantly higher quality interior. * **Gasoline**: The M271 CGI engines (C180, C200, C250) are good choices, provided the injector and chain history is checked. The M276 V6 (C350 post-2011) is the most reliable and pleasant of the gasoline V6s. Pre-2011 M272 V6s should be considered with the utmost caution and an imperative check of the engine serial number for the balance shaft sprocket issue. The C63 AMG is a performance monster, but requires a very substantial maintenance budget and a check of the cylinder head bolts on pre-2011 models. * **Diesel**: The OM651 (C200, C220, C250 CDI) are very efficient, but injector history is crucial. The OM642 V6 (C320, C350 CDI) offer superior refinement but can suffer from costly oil leaks.

Regardless of the choice, a **complete and clear service history** is non-negotiable. An inspection by a Mercedes-Benz specialist is highly recommended, especially for at-risk engines. With these precautions, the W204 remains an excellent investment in the used car market.
