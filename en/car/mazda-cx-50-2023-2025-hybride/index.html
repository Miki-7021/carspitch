Mazda CX-50

I · 2023 – 2025

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

Summary

The **Mazda CX-50 (Generation I)** is a compact SUV with a "rugged" positioning, designed primarily for the North American market and manufactured at the joint Mazda-Toyota plant (MMNA) in Alabama, United States. Wider, longer, and featuring higher ground clearance than the CX-5, it targets an active clientele.

The major innovation of this model is the introduction of a **hybrid powertrain (HEV)** that marries the excellence of Mazda's chassis and finish with Toyota's proven hybrid system (derived from the RAV4). According to the *J.D. Power IQS 2023* study, Mazda ranked as the best brand in initial quality, with the CX-50 taking the lead in the compact SUV category. It is an extremely well-engineered vehicle from the outset, combining SKYACTIV combustion engines already proven reliable on other models with a hybrid system that is untouchable in terms of durability.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of the Hybrid version (integrated Toyota RAV4 technology)
  • Build and finish quality above average (J.D. Power IQS 2023)
  • Dynamic road handling, typical of Mazda, despite the rugged vocation
  • Highly effective i-ACTIV AWD all-wheel drive, available as standard in most markets
  • SKYACTIV-Drive automatic gearbox (6-speed) on combustion engines: very robust and responsive

Weaknesses 4

  • Rear seat space and trunk volume slightly lower than competitors like the Honda CR-V
  • Infotainment system not touch-sensitive while driving (an ergonomic choice by Mazda that may frustrate some users)
  • Rear visibility hindered by thick pillars
  • Model unavailable on the European market (reserved for North America and China)

Check before buying

  • Front suspension mounts (subject to a recall on 2025 models)
  • Installation of towing accessories (OEM tow hitch): check bolt torque and wiring harness
  • Operation of the front camera (FSC) managing advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS)
  • History of software updates (OTA) for control modules (Airbags, engine management)

Alternatives

- **North America:** Toyota RAV4 Hybrid (the technical cousin), Honda CR-V Hybrid (more spacious), Subaru Outback (more of a lifted wagon style). - **Europe:** The CX-50 is not sold there. The internal alternative is the **Mazda CX-60 PHEV** or the **Mazda CX-5**. From competitors: Toyota RAV4, Honda ZR-V. - **China:** Toyota RAV4, Honda Breeze, Volkswagen Tayron.

Verdict

The **Mazda CX-50** is an extremely wise choice, particularly in its **Hybrid powertrain**. By combining Mazda's expertise in design, interior finish, and driving dynamics with the invulnerability of Toyota's hybrid system, this SUV borders on flawless in terms of reliability. The combustion versions (2.5 and 2.5T) are also highly recommendable, benefiting from widely proven SKYACTIV-G engine blocks that have been cleared of their teething problems. The only points of vigilance concern peripheral assemblies (tow hitch, some suspension bolts) covered by official recalls. It is a highly recommended purchase for anyone looking for a robust and durable compact SUV.
