Summary

The second-generation Volvo V60 (code name Z252) is based on the modular SPA (Scalable Product Architecture) platform. This D-segment premium estate stands out for its clean Scandinavian design, its top-tier passive and active safety, and its ride comfort. Initially launched with 100% internal combustion engines (petrol T and diesel D), the range quickly evolved towards massive electrification with the introduction of mild-hybrid (B) and plug-in hybrid (Recharge T6/T8) powertrains. In 2022, a slight facelift brought the Android Automotive infotainment system and a major update to the PHEV batteries (increasing from 11.6 to 18.8 kWh). Overall, the V60 II is a very reliable vehicle, having corrected the early issues of the first SPA models (like the 2016 XC90).

Strengths 5

  • Timeless design and exceptional interior finish quality.
  • Seats among the most comfortable in the automotive market (especially in Inscription/Ultimate trim).
  • Benchmark active and passive safety (City Safety, Pilot Assist).
  • Bowers & Wilkins audio system (option) with rare acoustic quality.
  • Real-world electric range of post-2022 PHEV versions (often > 70 km).

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high weight (up to 2.1 tons in PHEV), impacting agility and tire wear.
  • Android Automotive system very unstable at launch (2022), causing connectivity losses.
  • Imposing transmission tunnel at the rear, making the central seat uncomfortable.
  • Rapid disappearance of diesel powertrains, penalizing very high mileage drivers.
  • Trunk volume (529 L) correct but lower than a VW Passat or Skoda Superb.

Check before buying

  • TCAM Module (Shark fin antenna): Verify that GPS, LTE network, and keys function perfectly (2022-2023 models).
  • ERAD (PHEV): Listen for any whistling or rolling noise coming from the rear axle in 100% electric mode.
  • Tire and brake wear: The significant weight of hybrid versions accelerates consumable wear.
  • Panoramic sunroof: Check for absence of creaking on degraded roads and clean water drainage channels.
  • Software update history: Crucial on this generation highly dependent on electronics.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Audi A4 Avant (comparable trim, efficient TDI engines), BMW 3 Series Touring (G21 - more dynamic handling), Mercedes-Benz C-Class Estate (S206 - onboard technology), Volkswagen Passat SW (more cargo space).
  • North America: Audi A4 Allroad (direct alternative to the V60 Cross Country), Subaru Outback (less premium but very capable AWD).
  • Asia: Lexus IS (sedan only, but reliable premium alternative), Audi A4 Avant.

Verdict

The Volvo V60 II is an excellent choice for those seeking a premium estate focused on comfort, safety, and design rather than pure sportiness. The petrol (T4/T5) and mild-hybrid (B3/B4) powertrains are safe bets in terms of mechanical reliability. The plug-in hybrid (Recharge) versions are fantastic to use, especially post-2022 models with the larger battery, but require rigorous verification of the maintenance history (particularly the ERAD rear electric motor). Avoid 2022 models that have not received their software updates (Android bugs).
