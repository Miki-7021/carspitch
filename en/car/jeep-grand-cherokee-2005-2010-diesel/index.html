Summary

The third-generation Jeep Grand Cherokee (code name WK) marks a major turning point in the model's history, the result of the DaimlerChrysler alliance. It abandons the solid front axle in favor of independent suspension, significantly improving road handling while retaining excellent off-road capabilities thanks to advanced AWD systems (Quadra-Trac II and Quadra-Drive II with ELSD electronic limited-slip differentials).

On the European market, this generation was overwhelmingly sold with a diesel engine, taking advantage of the Mercedes-Benz parts bin with the excellent (but demanding) 3.0 CRD V6 (code OM642) coupled with the robust NAG1 5-speed automatic gearbox. Although the engines (especially the Hemi V8s and the diesel V6) and gearboxes are mechanically very robust, the model suffers from complex and temperamental electronics, typical of this transitional era, as well as an interior finish below European standards.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional off-road capabilities (Quadra-Drive II system with 3 limited-slip differentials)
  • Extremely robust 5-speed automatic gearbox (Mercedes-sourced NAG1 / W5A580)
  • 3.0 CRD engine offering truck-like torque (510 Nm) ideal for towing
  • Mechanically very reliable V8 engines (4.7 and 5.7 Hemi) (confirmed by TÜV/ADAC)
  • Significantly improved ride comfort compared to the previous generation (WJ)

Weaknesses 5

  • Complex electronics and source of recurring breakdowns (ADAC/TÜV)
  • Demanding 3.0 CRD engine: peripheral breakdowns that are costly in labor (oil cooler, Swirl motor)
  • Very average interior plastics and assembly quality (typical 2000s Chrysler)
  • Gargantuan fuel consumption of the petrol engines
  • Recurring problems with control modules (WIN, ABS, transfer case)

Check before buying

  • On the 3.0 CRD: Remove the engine cover and inspect the base of the injectors (presence of black tar = leaking copper seals).
  • On the 3.0 CRD: Look under the turbo (in the center of the V) to detect traces of oil (oil cooler leak or intake seal).
  • Transfer case: Verify that shifting into low range (4WD Low) occurs without an error message (very common problem related to the N23 recall / transfer case actuator).
  • On-board electronics: Absolutely test all functions (air conditioning, windows, heated seats, radio) because the modules (FCM, WIN) are fragile.
  • Running gear: Premature wear of the front differential bushings (clunking during P-R-N-D gear changes).

Verdict

The Jeep Grand Cherokee WK (2005-2010) is an endearing 4x4, offering exceptional off-road capabilities and modernized road handling.

**For the Diesel engine (3.0 CRD):** It is the most logical choice in Europe. The Mercedes OM642 engine is intrinsically bulletproof, but its peripherals (oil cooler seals, intake flap actuator, injector seals) are financial time bombs if maintenance has not been scrupulous. Only buy an example with a crystal-clear history proving that these chronic ailments have already been addressed.

**For the Petrol engines:** The 5.7 Hemi is the king choice for pure mechanical reliability, provided you accept the fuel consumption.

In all cases, the temperamental electronics (modules, sensors, transfer case recalls) will require patience. It is a vehicle to firmly **NEGOTIATE**, keeping a reserve budget for electrical hazards and diesel V6 leaks.
