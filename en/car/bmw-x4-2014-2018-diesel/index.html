Summary

The first-generation BMW X4 (code F26) was launched in 2014 as the X6's smaller sibling. Based on the platform and underpinnings of the facelifted X3 (F25), it inaugurated the compact SAC (Sports Activity Coupe) segment. It stands out with a sloping roofline, a lowered center of gravity, and firmer suspension settings than the X3 for more dynamic handling. While it sacrifices some rear passenger space and trunk volume for style, it retains the brand's excellent build quality and advanced powertrains. In the used car market, diesel versions (which represent the majority of sales in Europe) are highly sought after for their performance/consumption compromise, but require rigorous monitoring, particularly concerning the xDrive system and emissions control.

Strengths 5

  • Driving pleasure superior to competitors (Porsche Macan excepted)
  • Exceptional 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox, smooth and responsive
  • 6-cylinder diesel engines (30d/35d) offering unbeatable performance/consumption ratio
  • Interior finish and assembly quality (very intuitive iDrive system)
  • Dynamic road handling despite the weight (approx. 1.9 tons)

Weaknesses 5

  • Extreme sensitivity of the xDrive transfer case to tire wear
  • Limited rear passenger space and headroom for tall occupants
  • Very poor rear visibility (reversing camera essential)
  • Firm suspension, especially with 19 or 20-inch wheels and Runflat tires
  • High maintenance cost at official BMW dealerships

Check before buying

  • Recall history: The EGR cooler on diesels MUST have been checked/replaced.
  • Transfer case (xDrive): Turn the steering wheel fully at low speed and listen/feel for any jerks or scrubbing. Sign of a worn transfer case.
  • Tires: Check for the star (*) marking on the sidewalls and uniform wear between front and rear (max tolerance of 2mm circumference difference).
  • Steering rack: Clunking noises on degraded roads (known issue on the F25/F26 platform).
  • Automatic gearbox maintenance: Check if the ZF8 gearbox has been serviced if the vehicle has more than 100,000 km.

Official recalls

  • EGR Cooler (2018, 2019, 2022): Risk of coolant leakage into the intake, potentially creating incandescent soot and a fire hazard. Affects almost all 4 and 6-cylinder diesel engines (B47, N57).
  • Takata Airbags (2020+): Replacement of the driver's airbag gas generator due to a risk of metallic fragment projection.
  • ISOFIX Anchors (2016): Potential weakness in the welds of the rear ISOFIX anchor points. Affects models produced between 2014 and 2016.
  • Fuel Pump (2015): On some petrol models, risk of engine stalling due to a defect in the in-tank fuel pump.

What sets it apart 7

Safety

  • Certified Maximum Safety — The BMW X4 F26 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, ensuring exceptional protection for all occupants.
  • Powerful and Precise Braking — Benefit from remarkable braking performance, with a very short stopping distance for its category, ensuring perfect control and peace of mind.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Agile Road Handling — The X4 F26 is renowned for its sporty road holding, precise steering, and controlled body roll, offering an engaging and agile driving experience.
  • Exceptional ZF 8-Speed Automatic Transmission — The ZF 8-speed automatic transmission is praised for its smoothness, responsiveness, and significant contribution to the vehicle's superior driving pleasure.
  • 6-Cylinder Diesel Engines: Power and Efficiency — The 6-cylinder diesel engines (30d/35d) offer an unbeatable performance-to-consumption ratio, combining generous power with excellent efficiency for long journeys.

Comfort

  • Premium and Intuitive Interior — The X4 F26's cabin stands out with impeccable finish quality and assembly, complemented by the highly intuitive iDrive system, for superior comfort and ergonomics.

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability of the 3.0d Engine — The 3.0d (N57) engine is recognized for its robustness and mechanical reliability, offering enhanced peace of mind and durability for demanding drivers.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Porsche Macan (sportier, more expensive), Mercedes-Benz GLC Coupé (more comfortable), Audi Q5 Sportback (appeared later), Range Rover Evoque.
  • North America: Porsche Macan, Lexus NX, Acura RDX, Mercedes-Benz GLC Coupe.
  • Asia: Lexus NX, Mercedes-Benz GLC Coupé.

Verdict

The BMW X4 F26 is a highly desirable SUV Coupé that ages well aesthetically and dynamically. For diesel engines, the 30d (6-cylinder) is the top choice: it offers superior mechanical reliability to the 4-cylinder while delivering high-level driving pleasure. The 20d (B47) remains an excellent rational choice, provided the EGR valve recall has been scrupulously performed. However, buying a used X4 requires absolute vigilance regarding the xDrive system: avoid models equipped with mismatched tires or without BMW marking, as the transfer case will not forgive it. A vehicle with a clear history and a serviced automatic gearbox is a recommendable purchase.
