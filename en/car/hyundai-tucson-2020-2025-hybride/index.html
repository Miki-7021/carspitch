Hyundai Tucson

IV (NX4) · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The fourth-generation Hyundai Tucson (code name NX4) marks a radical departure from its predecessors. Introduced late 2020, it features the “Sensuous Sportiness” design with its grille integrating parametric daytime running lights invisible when stationary. It is based on the N3 platform, shared with the Kia Sportage, offering increased rigidity and optimal integration of electrified powertrains.

The model is available in two wheelbases: short (Europe, Middle East) and long (North America, Korea, Australia). The interior takes a leap forward in terms of perceived quality and technology. A major facelift was presented late 2023 for the 2024/2025 model year, correcting the main ergonomic flaw (the all-touch interface) by reintroducing physical controls and adopting a dual panoramic display (ccNC system).

**Hybrid Focus (HEV):** This is the core engine range in Europe. Combining the 1.6 T-GDI Smartstream with a 44.2 kW electric motor and a 6-speed automatic gearbox, it offers an excellent compromise between smoothness, performance (230 hp, then 215 hp post-facelift) and consumption, without the charging constraints of the PHEV.

Strengths 6

  • Bold design and distinctive light signature
  • Excellent spaciousness and generous boot volume (especially in HEV)
  • Pleasant hybrid powertrain (HEV) with smooth 6-speed automatic gearbox
  • Significantly improved finish quality and sound insulation
  • Reassuring manufacturer's warranty (5 years unlimited mileage in Europe, 10 years/100k miles in USA)
  • Very relevant 2024 facelift (corrected interior ergonomics)

Weaknesses 5

  • Ergonomics of the pre-facelift model (100% touch central console, fingerprints on gloss black finish)
  • Very intrusive driver assistance systems (ADAS) (overspeed audible alerts difficult to deactivate)
  • Slightly firm comfort at low speeds with 19-inch wheels
  • DCT-7 gearbox (on MHEV) less smooth than the 6-speed automatic of hybrid versions
  • Average rear visibility (thick C-pillar)

Check before buying

  • Exhaust warning light (GPF/DPF) on HEV/PHEV versions used exclusively in the city.
  • 12V battery condition (unexplained discharges on 2021-2022 models).
  • Smoothness of the DCT-7 gearbox on mild-hybrid versions (juddering on startup = worn clutches).
  • Infotainment system operation (black screens or intermittent reboots before update).
  • Front tire wear on PHEV versions (high torque and heavy weight).

Comfort

  • Superior Comfort and Refined Finish — Enjoy an interior with significantly improved finish quality and excellent sound insulation, offering a most pleasant driving experience. The 2024 facelift further enhanced ergonomics for optimal well-being.

Performance

  • Powerful and Smooth Hybrid Powertrain — The 1.6 T-GDI HEV powertrain offers an excellent balance of power (up to 230 hp), smooth operation thanks to its 6-speed automatic transmission, and controlled fuel consumption, for an exceptional driving experience.

Safety

  • Award-Winning Maximum Safety — The Hyundai Tucson IV achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with high scores for adult (86%) and child (87%) protection, and has been named an IIHS Top Safety Pick+ for several consecutive years.

Braking

  • Powerful Braking and Short Stopping Distances — Benefit from efficient and stable braking performance, with competitive stopping distances for its category, generally around 37 to 39 meters for a complete stop from 100 km/h, ensuring increased safety.

tenue de route

  • Stable and Serene Road Handling — The Tucson offers balanced and predictable road handling, with well-controlled body roll and light, precise steering, ensuring serene and safe driving in all circumstances.

Practicality

  • Bold Design and Generous Cabin Space — Its distinctive "Sensuous Sportiness" design and unique lighting signature are sure to turn heads. Inside, the cabin space is excellent and the boot volume generous, ideal for families.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Kia Sportage (technical cousin, different design), Peugeot 3008 (dynamic but controversial PureTech engines), Toyota RAV4 (hybrid benchmark, more rustic), Volkswagen Tiguan, Nissan Qashqai (very relevant e-Power). - **North America:** Honda CR-V Hybrid, Toyota RAV4 Hybrid, Mazda CX-50, Subaru Forester. - **Asia / Emerging Markets:** Haval H6, Chery Tiggo 7 Pro, Honda ZR-V, Toyota Corolla Cross.

Verdict

The Hyundai Tucson IV (NX4) is an extremely accomplished compact SUV that has managed to challenge European and Japanese benchmarks. **The hybrid powertrain (1.6 T-GDI HEV) is undoubtedly the best choice in the range**: it offers excellent driving pleasure, controlled consumption, and avoids the issues of the dual-clutch gearbox (DCT) thanks to its robust 6-speed automatic. Overall reliability is very good, with the rare teething problems (12V battery, software bugs) having been corrected under warranty. If your budget allows, opt for a post-facelift model (2024+) which corrects the interior ergonomic flaws (end of the all-touch interface) and improves the multimedia interface. The diesel and mild-hybrid petrol versions are decent but less relevant compared to the excellence of the simple hybrid.
