MINI One

· 2001 – 2006

NEGOTIATE Reliability 50/100 — faible

Summary

The first-generation MINI One under BMW's era (codename R50 for the 3-door hatchback, R52 for the convertible introduced in 2004) marks the rebirth of the legendary model. Positioned as the entry-level model below the Cooper, the One shares the same 1.6L petrol engine block (electronically detuned to 90 hp) from the Tritec joint venture (BMW/Chrysler). It stands out for its exceptional road handling, often compared to a go-kart, and its very successful neo-retro design. However, the first years of production suffered from serious early defects, particularly concerning the transmission and power steering, largely corrected during the July 2004 facelift.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road handling (go-kart feeling)
  • Timeless design and strong appeal
  • Extremely reliable Toyota-sourced diesel engine (One D)
  • Excellent driving position
  • Very low depreciation on the used car market

Weaknesses 6

  • Catastrophic Midland manual gearbox (2001-2004)
  • CVT automatic gearbox to avoid (frequent failures)
  • Very fragile electro-hydraulic power steering pump
  • Interior finish and parasitic noises on pre-2004 models
  • Very firm suspension and limited comfort
  • Ridiculous rear passenger space and boot volume

Reliability by engine

EngineReliabilityKnown Issues
**1.4i / 1.6i (Petrol)**AverageEngine itself is robust but peripherals are fragile. Oil leaks (rocker cover gasket, crankshaft oil seal), coolant leaks (plastic thermostat housing that cracks), fragile engine mounts.
**1.4 D (Diesel)**ExcellentVery reliable Toyota-sourced block (from the Yaris) with a timing chain. Some cases of EGR valve or turbo clogging if used exclusively in urban environments.

Check before buying

  • **Gearbox (Petrol 2001-2004)**: Listen carefully for any whistling or grinding noise in the first gears. If the gearbox snags or whistles, the bearings are dead (imminent gearbox replacement).
  • **Power Steering**: Check for excessive whistling when turning the steering wheel at a standstill and ensure that the assistance does not cut out. The electro-hydraulic pump is a known weak point.
  • **Front Shock Absorber Mounts**: Open the bonnet and check that the shock absorber towers (mushrooms) are not deformed or bulging upwards (common due to the stiff suspension and potholes).
  • **Cooling System (Petrol)**: Check the fluid level and look for whitish traces around the water housing (thermostat) and the radiator.
  • **Electronics and Interior**: Test the power windows (motors wear out), central locking, and check for any illuminated airbag warning light (often due to the connector under the passenger seat).

Alternatives

If you're looking for a city car with a strong style: **Fiat 500** (smaller but very reliable with the 1.2 69hp engine), **Volkswagen Polo IV** (more austere but better finished and more spacious), **Peugeot 206** (excellent road handling, cheaper to maintain).

Verdict

The MINI One R50 is a car you fall in love with, but it can turn into a financial nightmare if poorly chosen. **The golden rule is to absolutely avoid petrol models from before July 2004 equipped with the Midland manual gearbox**, as well as all versions with a CVT automatic gearbox. Opt for a facelifted petrol model (late 2004-2006) with the Getrag gearbox, or turn to the One D (Toyota diesel), which is by far the most reliable version of this generation, although less sporty.
