Renault Laguna

III (BT0) · 2007 – 2015

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Renault models

Summary

The Renault Laguna III (BT0 generation for the sedan, KT0 for the Estate wagon, DT0 for the Coupé) had the difficult task of making people forget the catastrophic reliability of the first Laguna II models. Designed under Carlos Ghosn's quality excellence plan, it is paradoxically one of the most reliable family cars of its generation. While its exterior design was often deemed too conservative or divisive, its road qualities are exceptional, enhanced by the '4Control' four-wheel steering system (introduced on GT trims). On the used car market, it still suffers from an image deficit linked to its predecessor, which makes it an absolute bargain, particularly with petrol engines, where it shines with robustness worthy of the best Japanese cars (thanks in particular to synergies with Nissan).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability (especially M4R petrol and M9R diesel)
  • 4Control chassis, formidablely efficient and surprisingly reliable
  • Unbeatable value for money on the used market (unjustified depreciation)
  • Significantly improved finish quality and material aging
  • Sound insulation and ride comfort (especially with 16/17-inch wheels)

Weaknesses 5

  • Divisive exterior design (especially the front end of Phase 1)
  • Center console plastics that can crack with temperature variations
  • Average rear passenger space and boot volume for the category (sedan)
  • Fuel consumption of petrol engines (especially 2.0T and V6)
  • Premature wear of the left bolster of the driver's seat (cracking synthetic leather)

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: prioritize annual oil changes, even on chain-driven engines.
  • 4Control system: check for no warning light on the dashboard and the specific alignment of the rear axle.
  • Cabin electronics: proper functioning of the GPS (Carminat/R-Link), hands-free card, and electric parking brake.
  • Automatic gearbox (6-speed auto - AJ0): demand oil change invoices (ideally every 60-80k km) to avoid jerks.
  • Running gear: wear of anti-roll bar bushes (clunking on degraded roads).

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Unrivaled Petrol Reliability — The Laguna III stands out for its remarkable robustness, particularly with its Nissan-sourced petrol engines (M4R), renowned for their durability and chain-driven timing, ensuring long-term peace of mind.
  • 2.0 16v (M4R) Petrol Engine: Robustness and Smoothness — The Nissan-sourced 2.0 16v (M4R) engine is the ideal choice: indestructible, featuring a timing chain, and offering great flexibility, it is also E85 compatible in some versions for increased versatility.

Safety

  • High-Level Passive Safety — Designed to protect its occupants, the Laguna III achieved excellent crash-test results, offering maximum protection for adults and children, ensuring a serene driving experience.
  • Efficient and Controlled Braking — Enjoy a very high-performance braking system, offering short stopping distances and great efficiency, for increased safety in all situations.

Performance

  • Safe and Dynamic Road Handling — Its balanced chassis ensures excellent, stable, and predictable road holding. Versions equipped with the 4Control system offer unparalleled agility and driving precision for a family car.

Comfort

  • Travel with Complete Serenity — Appreciate remarkable ride comfort and refined soundproofing, transforming every journey into a pleasant and relaxing experience, even over long distances.

Economy

  • The Unmissable Used Car Bargain — Despite its exceptional qualities, the Laguna III offers an unbeatable value for money in the used car market, representing a smart and advantageous investment.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 508 I (more modern but unreliable THP petrol engines), Citroën C5 II (hydraulic comfort, THP petrol to avoid), Volkswagen Passat B6/B7 (more prestigious, problematic chain-driven TSI engines), Ford Mondeo III (very spacious, good chassis).
  • Japan / Asia: Honda Accord VIII (equivalent reliability, excellent VTEC petrol engines), Toyota Avensis III (very reliable but austere).

Verdict

The Renault Laguna III is the archetype of a 'good deal' on the used market. A victim of the disastrous reputation of the Laguna II, it was shunned when new and sold off cheaply as a used car. Yet, it is one of the most reliable family cars of its decade. If you are looking for a petrol engine, the 2.0 16v 140 hp (M4R) is a masterful choice: chain-driven timing, Nissan origin, smooth operation, and E85 compatibility (on some versions). For more dynamism, the 2.0T (F4Rt) coupled with the 4Control chassis (GT trim) offers high-flying road performance for a derisory budget. Buy with your eyes closed if the maintenance history is clear.
