Summary

Presentation of the Lexus ES VII Facelift (XZ10)

The seventh-generation Lexus ES (based on the TNGA-K platform) received a mid-cycle facelift in 2021. This update brought targeted improvements: increased rear axle rigidity, brake pedal recalibration, minor aesthetic tweaks (grille, headlights), and, most importantly, the advancement of the multimedia screen, which became a touchscreen. In 2023, the infotainment system was completely overhauled to abandon the much-criticized touchpad (Remote Touch) in favor of the new Lexus interface.

⚠️ IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL: Contrary to the initial request, the Lexus ES has NEVER been offered with a diesel engine, on any recent generation, let alone the XZ10. Lexus definitively abandoned diesel long before the release of this model, favoring its self-charging hybrid technology (HEV), which offers highway fuel consumption comparable to that of a diesel engine, while guaranteeing significantly superior reliability and reduced local emissions. This sheet will therefore address the absence of diesel and detail the gasoline and hybrid engines actually available.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability (often ranked #1 globally)
  • Fuel consumption of the 300h version equivalent to a good diesel engine (5.0 to 6.0 L/100 km)
  • Top-tier soundproofing and ride comfort
  • Flawless build and assembly quality
  • Exceptional Mark Levinson audio system (optional)
  • Very high resale value

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of a diesel engine (may frustrate some traditional European buyers)
  • Non-folding rear bench seat (presence of a V-brace behind the seats for rigidity)
  • Pre-2023 infotainment system (with touchpad) dated and not very ergonomic
  • Imposing size (nearly 5 meters) poorly suited to European city centers
  • No station wagon (Estate) version available

Check before buying

  • Condition of the 12V battery (known discharge issue on Toyota/Lexus hybrids if parked for extended periods)
  • Maintenance history of the hybrid system (annual Hybrid Health Check required for warranty extension)
  • Operation of the central screen and peripheral cameras (subject to software recalls)
  • Tire wear on the front axle (ES 300h FWD)
  • Cleanliness of the high-voltage battery ventilation filter (cabin)

Official recalls

Official manufacturer recalls

  • [2025-11-07] (Rappel Conso France) / [2025-10-30] (NHTSA 25V744000) - 360° Camera (PVM): A software issue in the parking assist control unit (Panoramic View Monitor) can prevent the rearview camera image from displaying, increasing the risk of a collision. Affects many models including the Lexus ES (2022-2026).
  • [2024-08-22] (Rappel Conso France) - Camera water infiltration: The camera housings can separate under stress due to improper welding, allowing water entry and system failure. Affects model codes AXZA10/AXZH10 (Lexus ES).
  • [2024-02-21] (NHTSA 24V124000) - Rearview camera: Software programming issue causing a black screen when reversing. Affects the Lexus ES250, ES300H, ES350 (years 2023-2024).
  • [2023-12-20] (NHTSA 23V865000) - Airbag sensor (OCS): A short circuit in the front passenger seat occupant classification sensor may prevent the airbag from deploying in a crash. Affects the Lexus ES350 (2020-2021).
  • *(Note: Other minor Toyota/Lexus recalls exist during this period regarding fuel pumps or immobilizer systems, but the facelifted ES XZ10 is very rarely affected by major mechanical failures).*

Verdict

Final verdict

The facelifted Lexus ES VII (XZ10) is one of the most reliable sedans in the world. If you are looking for a diesel engine, you must look elsewhere, as Lexus made the radical (and visionary) choice of total hybridization in Europe. However, the ES 300h engine is an excellent replacement for diesel: it offers real-world highway fuel consumption around 6.0 L/100 km, and less than 5.0 L/100 km in the city, all with unmatched quietness of operation and an almost total absence of costly breakdowns (no DPF, no clogged EGR valve, no timing belt, no clutch, no conventional alternator).

The 2021 facelift (and especially the 2023 multimedia update) corrected the model's main flaw: its obsolete infotainment. It is a highly recommended purchase for high-mileage drivers, taxis/ride-hailing services, and lovers of absolute comfort, provided you accept the lack of a folding rear bench seat and a size poorly suited to cramped underground parking lots.
