Summary

The Ford Mustang Mach-E (Generation I) marks a historic turning point for the American manufacturer: it is its first 100% electric SUV designed specifically as such. By taking the name and certain styling cues of the legendary 'pony car', Ford made a bold bet. Positioned against the Tesla Model Y, it stands out with a strong design, dynamic road handling (RWD-biased even in AWD) and a spacious cabin dominated by a huge vertical touchscreen (Sync 4A). While the dynamic qualities and driving pleasure are present, the early years of production were marred by early software issues (12V battery drain) and, above all, by a critical hardware weakness in the high-voltage battery contactors (HVBJB). The 2023 models benefit from numerous hardware and software (OTA) corrections.

Strengths 5

  • Distinctive and successful exterior design (Mustang evocation)
  • Dynamic and pleasant road handling (RWD-biased)
  • Generous interior space and practical, washable front trunk (Frunk)
  • Responsive Sync 4A infotainment system (after updates) with large screen
  • Frequent and effective OTA (Over-The-Air) updates to fix bugs

Weaknesses 5

  • Major design flaw of the high voltage contactors (HVBJB) on 2020-2022 models
  • Lack of a heat pump: significant loss of range in cold weather
  • Very firm and jittery suspension at low speeds on non-GT versions
  • Disappointing DC fast charging curve: drastic drop in power after 80%
  • Early electronic bugs (PAAK, black screen, 12V battery) on the first model years

Check before buying

  • HVBJB (Contactors) recall status: Demand proof of the HARDWARE replacement of the part, not just the software update.
  • 12V battery condition: Past deep discharges may have damaged it prematurely.
  • Operation of the electric door handles (push buttons) and the keypad.
  • Sealing and mounting of the windshield and panoramic roof (subjects of official recalls).
  • Tire wear, particularly on AWD and GT versions (high instant torque).

Official recalls

  • [2022-03-25] Windshield: The windshield is not sufficiently attached to the body structure. In the event of a frontal collision, the windshield may not be held in place. Risk of injury.
  • [2022-03-18] Panoramic roof: The glass roof is not properly attached and could detach from the vehicle, creating a risk of accidents. Risk of injury.
  • [2022-06] High Voltage Contactors (HVBJB) - *Major Global Recall*: Possible overheating of the main high voltage battery contactors during DC fast charging or repeated acceleration. Can lead to a sudden loss of motive power. Fix: Software update (preventive limitation) AND hardware replacement of the junction box on affected vehicles or in case of failure.

  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Enjoy total control thanks to a high-performance braking system, designed to provide short stopping distances and a confident feel in all conditions.

Verdict

The Ford Mustang Mach-E is an endearing electric SUV, endowed with a real stylistic and dynamic personality. However, buying a used model from the 2020 to early 2022 model years requires extreme vigilance. The design flaw of the high voltage contactors (HVBJB) is a sword of Damocles that can immobilize the vehicle. Only buy a model from those years if you have formal proof that the high voltage junction box has been physically replaced with the new reinforced part by Ford. Models produced from late 2022 / 2023 are much more reliable, as the early defects (software, glass mountings, 12V battery) have been corrected at the factory. Favor an Extended Range RWD version for the best range/price compromise.
