Summary

The **third-generation Nissan Qashqai (J12)** marks a major turning point for the pioneer of compact SUVs. Based on the Renault-Nissan-Mitsubishi Alliance's CMF-C platform, it definitively abandons diesel to focus exclusively on electrified powertrains (12V mild hybrid and an innovative e-Power hybrid system).

The **e-Power** system is particularly innovative: the internal combustion engine (a 1.5 3-cylinder with variable compression ratio) serves only as a generator to power the battery and the electric motor, which is the sole driver of the wheels. This offers a driving experience close to a 100% electric vehicle without the constraint of recharging. A significant facelift was carried out in 2024, modernizing the front end and correcting the shortcomings of the infotainment system from the early years. Overall, the J12 shows a spectacular move upmarket in terms of finish and road behavior compared to its predecessor (J11).

Strengths 5

  • Driving pleasure of the e-Power system (100% electric feel)
  • Significantly improved finish and material quality (especially Tekna/Tekna+ trims)
  • Improved rear spaciousness and boot volume compared to the J11
  • Reliability of the 1.3 DIG-T engine now largely proven
  • Highly effective active safety equipment (ProPILOT)

Weaknesses 5

  • e-Power fuel consumption on the highway (the thermal engine runs at high rpm to provide energy)
  • Slow and buggy infotainment system on pre-facelift models (2021-2023)
  • Ride comfort a bit firm with 20-inch wheels
  • No plug-in hybrid (PHEV) version available
  • High purchase price for the e-Power version

Check before buying

  • Condition and voltage of the 12V battery (source of 90% of electronic bugs on this model)
  • Fluidity of the central screen and wireless Apple CarPlay / Android Auto functionality
  • Alignment of body panels (tailgate and doors) on 2021/2022 models
  • Absence of jerks on the Xtronic automatic gearbox (1.3 DIG-T 158)
  • History of software updates (crucial for the e-Power system)

Handling

  • Balanced and Stable Road Handling — The Qashqai offers composed and predictable road holding, ensuring optimal driving comfort and remarkable stability. It demonstrates excellent control in various conditions, marking a significant improvement over previous generations.

Practicality

  • Generous and Versatile Interior Space — The Qashqai III offers improved rear passenger space and boot volume, providing more room and comfort for all occupants and their luggage, making every journey more enjoyable.

Alternatives

Alternatives are the Nissan Rogue (X-Trail) or the Nissan Kicks. - **Emerging markets**: Hyundai Creta, Kia Seltos.

Verdict

The **Nissan Qashqai III (J12)** is a mature, well-built, and technologically advanced SUV. The choice of engine will strictly depend on your usage.

If you primarily drive in the city and peri-urban areas, the **1.5 e-Power** is a marvel of smoothness and efficiency, offering the pleasure of electric driving without range anxiety. However, its mechanical complexity (variable compression engine) suggests favoring a model under warranty or with a clear service history within the network.

For more versatile drivers or those seeking a more contained purchase budget, the **1.3 DIG-T MHEV** is an excellent, very reliable, and proven choice. Prioritize models from late 2022 onwards to avoid early infotainment bugs, or ideally the 2024 facelifted model.
