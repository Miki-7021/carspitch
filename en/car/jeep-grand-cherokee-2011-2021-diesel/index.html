Jeep Grand Cherokee

IV (WK2) · 2011 – 2021

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The fourth-generation Jeep Grand Cherokee (codename WK2) marks a turning point for the model. Developed jointly with Mercedes-Benz (sharing its platform with the ML W166), it abandons the solid axle for independent suspension, offering transformed road handling while retaining excellent off-road capabilities (Quadra-Trac II and Quadra-Drive II systems). It benefited from a major facelift in 2014 (introduction of the excellent 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox and an updated cabin) and a slight retouch in 2017. While its petrol engines (V6 Pentastar and V8 Hemi) shine with their robustness, the diesel version (3.0 CRD / EcoDiesel), highly prized in Europe, suffers from serious reliability flaws. Furthermore, the onboard electronics and the optional air suspension weigh on its overall reliability record.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional off-road capabilities (Quadra-Drive II, low range)
  • Extremely robust and reliable petrol engines (Pentastar and Hemi)
  • Ride comfort and soundproofing (especially with the air suspension)
  • Smooth and responsive 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox (from 2014)
  • Timeless and statutory design
  • High towing capacity (up to 3,500 kg)

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of the 3.0 diesel engine (EGR, HP pump, crankshaft position sensor)
  • Recurring electronic bugs (Uconnect multimedia system, various sensors)
  • Fragile Quadra-Lift air suspension that is very expensive to repair
  • Gargantuan fuel consumption of the V8 engines
  • Uneven interior finish and fitment on models before the 2014 facelift
  • Very high weight penalizing agility and brake/tire wear

Check before buying

  • Recall history: the vehicle has been the subject of dozens of official recalls, verify that they have been performed.
  • On the 3.0 CRD/EcoDiesel: coolant level (EGR leak) and presence of metal shavings in the fuel system.
  • Air suspension (Quadra-Lift): ensure it raises and lowers without error messages or abnormal compressor noise.
  • Onboard electronics: test all functions of the Uconnect touchscreen, the reversing camera, and the driving aids.
  • Transfer case and differentials: check for leaks and demand oil change invoices (often neglected).

Verdict

The Jeep Grand Cherokee WK2 is an undeniably charming SUV, offering a rare blend of luxury, comfort, and genuine off-road capabilities. However, the choice of engine entirely dictates the ownership experience. The 3.0 V6 diesel engine (CRD/EcoDiesel), which accounts for the overwhelming majority of sales in Europe, is a documented **problem engine** (fragile injection pump, cracked EGR cooler, faulty crankshaft position sensor). Conversely, the petrol engines (3.6 V6 Pentastar and 5.7 V8 Hemi) are praised by inspection bodies (ADAC, TÜV) for their great mechanical robustness. Regardless of the engine, the temperamental electronics and aging air suspension require a substantial maintenance budget. Buy in petrol if possible, or in diesel only if all critical recalls have been performed with supporting invoices.
