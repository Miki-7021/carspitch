Nissan Juke

I (F15) · 2010 – 2019

NEGOTIATE Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation Nissan Juke (F15) is the undisputed pioneer of the urban SUV (B-SUV) segment. Launched in 2010, it disrupted the market with its polarizing design, inspired by buggies and sports motorcycles. Based on the Renault-Nissan Alliance's B platform, it prioritizes style over practicality (cramped rear passenger space, tiny 251L trunk on Phase 1).

**Facelift (Phase 2 - 2014):** The facelift corrected its biggest flaw by increasing the trunk volume to 354L (only on 2-wheel drive versions), modernized the infotainment system (NissanConnect), and introduced new engines, including the notoriously famous 1.2 DIG-T.

**Expert's overall rating:** A vehicle with a timeless look and fun dynamic handling, but whose reliability varies greatly depending on the chosen engine. The 1.5 dCi diesel version, targeted here, is one of the most recommendable, provided its preferred use (extra-urban driving) is respected.

Strengths 6

  • Bold and unique design that ages very well
  • Excellent reliability and fuel efficiency of the 1.5 dCi (K9K) engine
  • Dynamic road handling (close to a compact sedan)
  • Very effective Torque Vectoring AWD system (on 1.6 DIG-T 4x4)
  • Appreciated raised driving position
  • Trunk volume corrected on Phase 2 (354 liters in 2WD)

Weaknesses 6

  • Very poor rear passenger space (legroom and headroom)
  • Ridiculous trunk on Phase 1 (251 liters) and on all 4x4 versions (207 liters)
  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.2 DIG-T engine (frequent engine failure)
  • Firm suspension, degraded comfort on bumpy roads (especially with 18-inch wheels)
  • Quality of interior plastics (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)

Check before buying

  • On the 1.5 dCi: DPF regeneration history (warning light on dashboard?) and EGR valve condition (jerks during acceleration).
  • On the 1.5 dCi: Proof of timing belt replacement (due: 5 years or 150,000 km).
  • On the 1.2 DIG-T: Flee if the oil level is low or if the service book is not 100% up-to-date within the network.
  • On CVT (Xtronic) gearboxes: Check for absence of jerks, excessive slipping, or whistling noises. Demand a gearbox oil change invoice.
  • Running gear: Asymmetrical wear of front tires (geometry often misaligned) and wear of front wishbone bushings.

Verdict

The Nissan Juke I is an 'impulse buy' that requires rationality under the hood.

**Focus on the 1.5 dCi:** This is an excellent choice. The Renault/Nissan K9K block has reached its full maturity in the Juke. It is extremely fuel-efficient and durable. However, it is imperative to buy it for the right use: it needs to be driven on roads/highways to clean its DPF. A diesel Juke that has only been driven in the city will be a hotbed of problems (DPF, EGR, injectors). Scrupulously check that the timing belt has been replaced on time.

**For other engines:** The 1.6 atmospheric (94/117 hp) is the top choice for city driving thanks to its foolproof reliability. On the other hand, **flee the 1.2 DIG-T 115 hp** like the plague (proven risk of engine failure). The 1.6 DIG-T versions are fun but thirsty and require rigorous maintenance (especially the CVT gearbox).
