Nissan Ariya

I (FE0) · 2022 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 76/100 — bon

Summary

The Nissan Ariya (FE0 generation) is Nissan's flagship 100% electric SUV, marking a new era for the brand after the pioneering success of the Leaf. Based on the Renault-Nissan-Mitsubishi Alliance's modular CMF-EV platform (shared with the Renault Megane E-Tech and Scenic E-Tech), the Ariya stands out with its bold design, a highly premium lounge-like cabin, and excellent soundproofing. While it shines with its comfort and finish quality, its debut was marred by production delays and early teething problems (software and motors) that led to several major recalls on the 2022 and 2023 model years.

**Overall reliability score: 76/100** (Penalized by early-career recalls, but the technical base remains solid once the fixes are applied).

Strengths 5

  • Very high-level interior finish (materials, sleek design, thick carpets)
  • Suspension comfort and soundproofing worthy of the segment above
  • Generous onboard space, flat floor, and sliding center console
  • Highly effective e-4ORCE system for traction management and pitch limitation
  • Fluid multimedia interface and well-thought-out general ergonomics (haptic buttons integrated into the wood)

Weaknesses 5

  • DC fast charging power limited to 130 kW (flat curve but disappointing peak compared to 800V competition)
  • Very high weight (up to 2.3 tonnes), penalizing agility and high-speed consumption
  • Absence of a front trunk (frunk), the space being occupied by the air conditioning
  • Average rear trunk volume on e-4ORCE versions (415 liters)
  • Numerous early teething recalls (software and mechanical) on the first model years

Check before buying

  • Complete history of manufacturer recalls (crucial on 2022 and 2023 models)
  • Absence of oil leaks under the front traction motor (O-ring assembly defect)
  • Proper functioning of the infotainment system (possible sluggishness if not updated)
  • Tire wear condition (the heavy weight and torque degrade the rubber quickly)
  • Steering wheel bolt tightening (specific recall on 2023 models)

Verdict

The Nissan Ariya is an extremely comfortable, luxurious, and accomplished electric SUV in terms of the onboard experience. However, its launch was chaotic, marked by software (inverter) and hardware (motor seals, slip rings) issues that required multiple recalls.

**Buying advice:** Favor models produced from late 2023 / 2024 onwards, which benefited from factory fixes. If you are targeting a 2022 or 2023 used model, demand proof that **all recalls** (especially those related to the inverter and traction motor) have been performed by Nissan. The 2WD 87 kWh version represents the best range-to-price compromise.
