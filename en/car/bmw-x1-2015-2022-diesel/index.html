Summary

The second-generation BMW X1 (code name F48) marks a major technological shift for the Bavarian brand. Abandoning the rear-wheel-drive architecture of its predecessor (E84) for the modular UKL2 platform (shared with MINI and the 2 Series Active Tourer), it switched to front-wheel drive (sDrive) or Haldex-type all-wheel drive (xDrive). While purists regretted the loss of typical BMW handling and inline 6-cylinder engines, this choice allowed for a spectacular leap in terms of interior space, trunk volume, and versatility. Facelifted (LCI) in 2019 with an enlarged grille, new bumpers, and a technological update, the F48 established itself as one of the best-selling premium SUVs worldwide. In terms of reliability, the modular 'B' series engines (B37/B47 for diesel, B38/B48 for gasoline) have erased the serious timing chain issues of the previous generation (N47), although diesels remain subject to massive recalls concerning the EGR valve cooler.

Weaknesses 5

  • Road noise insulation could be improved on the highway

Check before buying

  • Recall history: Has the EGR valve cooler been checked/replaced? (Crucial on diesels)
  • Automatic gearbox: Presence of jerks when cold or during R/D shifts? (Requires an oil change if > 80,000 km)
  • Running gear: Dull noises over speed bumps at the front (fragile suspension wishbone bushings)
  • Electronics: Central screen operation (cases of delamination or intermittent restarts)
  • Tires: Irregular wear at the front (sensitive geometry) and cost of Runflat tires

Official recalls

  • EGR Cooler (2018, 2019, 2020, 2022): Major global recall. Possible coolant leak into the EGR module, mixing with soot and creating a risk of intake manifold fire. Affects almost all 4-cylinder (B47) and 3-cylinder (B37) diesels produced between 2015 and 2020.
  • Airbags (2024): Replacement of the driver's airbag gas generator (Takata) on certain models produced between 2015 and 2017.
  • Seat belts / ISOFIX (2016): Poor welding of ISOFIX anchors on the rear bench seat. Models produced late 2015 / early 2016.
  • BDC Module (2017): Reprogramming of the Body Domain Controller following lighting or wiper bugs.
  • PHEV - High Voltage Battery (2020): Risk of short circuit due to impurities in battery cells on xDrive25e models produced in 2020.

Verdict

The BMW X1 F48 is an excellent choice on the used car market. By abandoning rear-wheel drive, it has become a true family vehicle, spacious and practical, while retaining above-average dynamism for the segment. For diesels, the B47 engines (18d, 20d) are inherently very robust and have corrected the timing chain flaws of the past. However, the sword of Damocles of the EGR cooler demands absolute vigilance: only buy a diesel model if the BMW recall history is clear and documented. If you drive less than 20,000 km/year or do a lot of city driving, opt without hesitation for the excellent gasoline engines (18i or 20i), which are less prone to costly emissions system failures. Prioritize versions equipped with the 8-speed Aisin automatic gearbox and 'Advanced' seats for optimal comfort.
