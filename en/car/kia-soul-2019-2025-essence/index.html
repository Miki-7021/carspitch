III (SK3) (2019–2025) · 2019 – 2025

Summary

The third-generation Kia Soul, known by the code SK3, was launched in 2019, continuing the tradition of bold and unconventional design that made the model's reputation. Positioned as a compact urban crossover, it stands out with its cubic silhouette and distinctive lines. This generation marked a major turning point in Europe, where the Soul became almost exclusively electric (e-Soul), while North American and Asian markets retained gasoline powertrains.

The Soul III offers a spacious cabin for its category, good technological equipment, and generally appreciated suspension comfort, although the electric version may be firmer due to the weight of the batteries. It benefited from a slight facelift in 2022 for some markets, mainly aesthetic and technological. Overall, the Soul III is a reliable vehicle, especially in its naturally aspirated gasoline versions, and the e-Soul has established itself as a credible electric alternative with competitive range.

Strengths 6

  • Original and distinctive design
  • Generous interior space for a compact vehicle
  • Good standard equipment (depending on trim and market)
  • Very reliable and economical to maintain naturally aspirated gasoline versions
  • Electric version (e-Soul) offering competitive range and high driving pleasure
  • Long manufacturer's warranty (7 years in Europe, 10 years/100,000 miles in the USA on powertrain)

Weaknesses 6

  • Suspension sometimes firm on certain versions (especially EV)
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds on gasoline versions
  • Very limited engine choice in Europe (almost exclusively EV)
  • Fuel consumption of turbo gasoline versions can be high in dynamic driving
  • Potential problems with the ICCU on electric versions (recalls)
  • Polarizing design that doesn't appeal to everyone

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for T-GDI and EV versions)
  • Condition of tires and brakes (rapid wear on EV due to torque and weight)
  • Air conditioning and infotainment system operation
  • On T-GDI versions: presence of abnormal engine noises (turbo, timing chain) and decarbonization history
  • On EV versions: battery health status (SOH) via diagnosis, recall history (ICCU)
  • Absence of engine warning lights or dashboard faults
  • DCT gearbox operation (no excessive jerks, smooth shifts)

Verdict

The Kia Soul III (SK3) is a vehicle that stands out for its style and pragmatic approach.

**For markets where gasoline versions are available (North America, some emerging markets):** The naturally aspirated 2.0L Nu MPI and 1.6L Gamma MPI engines are excellent choices for their unwavering reliability and reduced maintenance costs. They are perfect for hassle-free daily use. The 1.6L T-GDI offers more punch but requires more rigorous monitoring (decarbonization, DCT fluid change).

**For Europe (mainly e-Soul):** The e-Soul is a very interesting proposition, offering real-world range and high driving pleasure. However, it is crucial to ensure that all recalls, especially the one concerning the ICCU, have been carried out. The 7-year battery warranty is a major asset.

**In summary:** The Soul III is a wise purchase if you choose the engine suited to your needs and if you check the maintenance history and recalls. The naturally aspirated gasoline versions are safe bets. The e-Soul is an excellent electric option, provided that manufacturer fixes have been applied. Its unique design can be an asset or a hindrance to resale, but its general reliability is a strong point.
