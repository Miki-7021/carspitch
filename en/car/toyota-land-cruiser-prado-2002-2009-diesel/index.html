Toyota Land Cruiser Prado

120 (J120) · 2002 – 2009

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The Toyota Land Cruiser 120 Series (known as the Prado in many markets, or simply Land Cruiser in Europe) is a true automotive legend. Built on a robust ladder frame chassis, it offers an exceptional compromise between outstanding off-road capabilities and on-road comfort. Replacing the 90 series, the J120 introduced modern technologies (TEMS air suspension on high trim levels, electronic off-road aids) while retaining Toyota's indestructible DNA. In Europe, it was primarily sold with the 3.0 D-4D (1KD-FTV) diesel engine, which saw several power upgrades (163, 166, and then 173 hp). Although its overall reliability is legendary (excellent ADAC breakdown rate of 1.5/1000), the diesel engine requires special attention on one specific point: the injector seals. Overall reliability rating: Exceptional, subject to rigorous maintenance.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability (Very low ADAC breakdown rate: 1.5/1000)
  • Outstanding off-road capabilities (ladder frame chassis, low range gearbox, lockers)
  • Almost indestructible automatic gearboxes (4-speed A340F and 5-speed A750F)
  • Exceptional resale value on the used market
  • Ride comfort and soundproofing (especially in V6 and high trim levels)
  • Roominess (available as an 8-seater in some markets)

Weaknesses 6

  • Critical injector seal issue on the 3.0 D-4D (can lead to engine failure if ignored)
  • Chassis susceptibility to corrosion (especially in mountainous/salty regions)
  • Clumsy road handling (significant body roll, braking lacks bite)
  • High maintenance costs (expensive genuine Toyota parts)
  • High fuel consumption of petrol engines (V6 and V8)
  • Wear of rear air suspension springs (VXE/VXV trim levels)

Check before buying

  • Injector seal replacement history (CRUCIAL on the 3.0 D-4D 1KD-FTV)
  • Condition of the oil strainer (remove the oil pan to check for carbon buildup)
  • Ladder frame chassis corrosion (tap with a small hammer to check the solidity of the steel)
  • Operation of the transfer case (shifting into H-L low gears) and central differential lock
  • Condition of the front CV joint boots (often torn with age or suspension lift)
  • Operation of the rear air suspension (the vehicle must rise and lower without error)

Official recalls

Specific recalls for the Land Cruiser J120 (2002-2009) - Takata Airbags (Global campaign): Replacement of the passenger and/or driver airbag inflator due to a risk of rupture projecting metal fragments. - Steering shaft (2012): Replacement of the intermediate steering shaft which could show premature wear of the splines. - Rear axle (2006): On some models, risk of rear axle half-shaft rupture under heavy load.

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Legendary Reliability — The Toyota Land Cruiser J120 is globally renowned for its robustness and exceptional reliability, attested by an extremely low ADAC breakdown rate, ensuring lasting peace of mind.

Performance

  • Exceptional Off-Road Capabilities — A true adventurer, the Land Cruiser J120 excels off-road thanks to its robust ladder frame chassis, low-range gearbox, and locking differentials, allowing it to overcome the most demanding obstacles.

Economy

  • Outstanding Resale Value — The Toyota Land Cruiser J120 is a wise investment, maintaining an exceptional resale value on the used car market, a testament to its quality and enduring popularity.

Comfort

  • Superior Comfort and Space — The J120 offers a noticeable ride comfort and refined sound insulation, combined with generous interior space capable of accommodating up to 8 passengers, perfect for family trips.

Safety

  • Enhanced Safety — The Land Cruiser J120 provides solid protection for its occupants, as demonstrated by its excellent safety test results.
  • Effective and Safe Braking — The Land Cruiser J120 comes standard with advanced braking systems such as ABS, EBD, and Brake Assist, ensuring reliable and safe stops for an SUV in its class.
  • Stable and Comfortable Handling — Despite its off-road capabilities, the J120 offers stable and predictable handling at high speeds, while providing remarkable ride comfort, even on the most rugged terrains.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Mitsubishi Pajero (Montero) 3.2 DI-D (excellent off-roader, unibody chassis), Nissan Pathfinder 2.5 dCi (more road-oriented, less reliable), Land Rover Discovery 3 (more comfortable but disastrous electronic reliability).
  • North America: Lexus GX470 (luxury version of the J120), Toyota 4Runner (shares the same chassis), Nissan Xterra.
  • Middle East / Asia: Nissan Patrol Y61 (more rustic, solid front axle), Mitsubishi Nativa/Pajero Sport.

Verdict

The Toyota Land Cruiser J120 is a masterpiece of durability, fully justifying its popularity and high prices on the used market. Reliability surveys (ADAC, TÜV, What Car?) consistently rank it among the most robust vehicles of its generation. However, buying a 3.0 D-4D diesel version (the most common in Europe) should not be done blindly: the injector seal issue is real and can destroy the engine if the oil strainer gets clogged. If you find an example with a sound chassis (no perforating corrosion) and a clear maintenance history (injector seals replaced, strainer checked), it is a highly recommended purchase that will take you to the ends of the earth.
