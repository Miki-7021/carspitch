Summary

Toyota C-HR II (AX30): The Bold Hybrid Comes of Age

Launched in late 2023, the second generation of the Toyota C-HR (codename AX30) retains its predecessor's polarizing SUV coupe design, but profoundly modernizes its underpinnings and technologies. Based on the optimized TNGA-C platform, this model marks a turning point: **it completely abandons 100% internal combustion engines** to focus exclusively on 5th-generation hybrid (HEV) powertrains and, for the first time, a plug-in hybrid (PHEV) version inherited from the Prius 5.

Designed, developed, and manufactured in Europe (TMMT plant in Turkey, with battery assembly in Europe), the C-HR II primarily targets the European market. In terms of reliability, it builds on Toyota's undisputed expertise in hybridization. Initial data is exceptional, confirming the brand's reputation.

**Overall reliability rating: Excellent.**

Weaknesses 4

  • Rear passenger space still cramped (small window area, feeling of claustrophobia)
  • Limited trunk volume, especially on PHEV (310 L) and AWD-i (362 L) versions
  • Significantly higher purchase price compared to the first generation
  • Absence of a 100% electric (BEV) version or an entry-level internal combustion engine to reduce the price

Verdict

Purchase Verdict

The second-generation Toyota C-HR (AX30) is a **highly recommended** purchase. It capitalizes on Toyota's hybrid expertise by integrating the 5th generation of its HSD system, smoothing out driving experience flaws (reduced 'rubber band' effect) while maintaining outstanding reliability.

The reliability data is eloquent: with a 5/5 score from *What Car?* (only 9% of minor breakdowns reported) and excellent ADAC/TÜV statistics, it is one of the most reliable SUVs in its category.

**Which engine to choose?** * The **1.8 HEV (140 hp)** is the sensible choice: sufficient for daily use, ultra-frugal, and less expensive. * The **2.0 HEV (197 hp)** is ideal for those who do a lot of highway or mountain driving, offering a much better driving experience. * The **2.0 PHEV (223 hp)** is best reserved for those who can charge daily at home or work, and who have the budget to absorb its significant extra cost, while accepting a reduced trunk space.
