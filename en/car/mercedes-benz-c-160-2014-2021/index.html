Summary

The **Mercedes-Benz C-Class (W205)** marked a major stylistic and technological break from the previous generation, adopting lines inspired by the S-Class. The **C 160** version, introduced in April 2015, constitutes the absolute entry-level petrol model. Designed primarily to meet strict tax constraints in certain European and Asian countries (such as Singapore), it offers the premium setting of the C-Class with a modest 129 hp engine. The 2018 facelift brought a notable improvement in interior assembly quality, an infotainment update, and the introduction of the 9G-Tronic gearbox replacing the 7G-Tronic Plus or the manual gearbox. **Overall rating: 7.5/10** - An excellent choice for driving premium at a lower tax cost, provided you are not looking for dynamic performance.

Strengths 5

  • Elegant and status-enhancing exterior design (mini S-Class)
  • Suspension comfort (especially with the Agility Control option)
  • Very low insurance and tax costs (low fiscal horsepower)
  • Reasonable real-world consumption (6.5 to 7.5 L/100 km in mixed use)
  • Significantly improved finish quality on facelifted models (post-2018)

Weaknesses 5

  • Power (129 hp) a bit tight for the vehicle's weight (around 1400 kg), especially when loaded or on the highway
  • Parasitic noises (creaking from the gloss black plastic center console) on pre-2018 models
  • 6-speed manual gearbox (standard at launch) notchy and unpleasant
  • Standard equipment sometimes poor on base trims
  • Wind noise at the front windows at high speed (if acoustic glazing is absent)

Check before buying

  • Cold start: track down any metallic rattling noise (sign of wear on the camshaft phasers on the M274).
  • Center console: press on the plastics to check for excessive creaking (a common issue on W205 phase 1).
  • Front axle: check for clunking noises over speed bumps (premature wear of the suspension arm silent blocks).
  • Electronics: test all functions of the center screen (Audio 20 or Comand), Garmin GPS SD cards tend to fry.
  • Automatic gearbox history: mandatory oil change at 100,000 km or 5 years for the 7G-Tronic and 125,000 km for the 9G-Tronic.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** BMW 318i (F30/G20 - 1.5L 3-cylinder or 2.0L 4-cylinder, more dynamic), Audi A4 35 TFSI (front-wheel drive, superior interior finish), Volvo S60 T3. - **Asia:** Lexus IS 300 (more powerful but a direct competitor in terms of premium size), Audi A4 1.4 TFSI. - **North America:** Not applicable (the market starts with more powerful engines like the C 300). - **Emerging Markets:** Volkswagen Passat 1.4 TSI, Skoda Superb 1.4 TSI (more spacious but less status-enhancing).

Verdict

The Mercedes C 160 (W205) is a rational purchase for those who want the image, comfort, and safety of a Mercedes-Benz without paying a high price for purchase or insurance. The 129 hp engine is sufficient for peaceful daily use, but will run out of steam for demanding drivers. **Our advice:** Absolutely favor a facelifted model (from mid-2018) equipped with the 1.5L engine (M264) and the 9G-Tronic automatic gearbox. You will avoid the timing chain issues of the M274, the interior noises of the early years, and benefit from significantly superior driving pleasure.
