Suzuki SX4 S-Cross

I facelift (JY) · 2016 – 2021

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The facelifted first-generation Suzuki SX4 S-Cross (code JY) was introduced in late 2016. This major facelift corrected the design of phase 1, which was considered too conservative, with an imposing chrome grille, while improving the perceived quality of the cabin. Mechanically, it marked the arrival of the excellent 'Boosterjet' turbo petrol engines (1.0 and 1.4) replacing the aging naturally aspirated 1.6 in Europe.

**Diesel Focus (1.6 DDiS):** Although the market trend has moved away from diesel, the S-Cross retained the excellent 1.6 DDiS block of Fiat origin (Multijet II). This engine is renowned for its great robustness, generous torque (320 Nm), and exemplary fuel efficiency. It is a particularly relevant choice for high-mileage drivers and those who tow, often coupled with the efficient AllGrip AWD. The model shines with its price/performance/reliability ratio, positioning itself as a pragmatic, no-nonsense crossover.
