Alfa Romeo 159

I (939) · 2005 – 2012

All Alfa Romeo models

Summary

The Alfa Romeo 159 (Type 939) succeeded the legendary 156 with the heavy task of moving upmarket. Designed by Giorgetto Giugiaro, it remains one of the most elegant sedans of its generation. Based on the 'Premium' platform developed jointly by Fiat and General Motors, it shines with its exceptional torsional rigidity and imperial road handling (double wishbone front suspension). However, this platform is extremely heavy, which penalizes performance, fuel consumption, and running gear wear on Phase 1 models (2005-2007).

A major technical facelift (Series 2) occurred in 2008, bringing a weight reduction of about 45 kg (aluminum parts), an interior upgrade, and the introduction of the Q2 electronic differential. In 2009, new, much more modern and reliable engines (1.75 TBi in petrol, 2.0 JTDm in diesel) replaced the older blocks. The 159 is an enthusiast's car: sublime and rewarding to drive, but demanding in maintenance.

Strengths 6

  • Timeless design by Giugiaro, aggressive and elegant lines
  • Exceptional road handling, incisive front axle (especially on the lightened post-2008 versions)
  • Very communicative and precise hydraulic steering
  • Finish quality significantly improved compared to the 156 (materials, assembly)
  • Very successful and reliable 2.0 JTDm and 1.75 TBi engines (if well maintained)
  • Excellent driving position and comfortable seats (especially in TI trim)

Weaknesses 6

  • Excessive weight (especially Phase 1), penalizing agility, fuel consumption, and performance
  • Notoriously fragile M32 manual gearbox (GM) (bearings)
  • Premature wear of the front tires on the inner edge (factory geometry defect)
  • Very capricious anti-pollution systems (EGR, FAP, Swirl flaps) on the 1.9 16V and 2.4 diesels
  • Fragile power steering rack (frequent leaks)
  • Disappointing rear roominess and trunk volume for its size (4.66 m)

Check before buying

  • Gearbox (M32): On 1.9 JTDm, 1.9/2.2 JTS, and 1.75 TBi. Drive in 5th and 6th gear at 90 km/h. A whining noise or movement of the gear lever upon acceleration indicates worn bearings (repair: €800 to €1,500).
  • Power steering: Check the color of the fluid in the reservoir. It MUST be green (Tutela GI/R). If it is red (GI/E), the rack is at risk of leaking or groaning when cold. Check for leaks at the steering rack boots.
  • Running gear: Listen for squeaks or clunks over speed bumps (fragile front upper wishbones). Check the wear of the front tires on the inside (requires a wheel alignment set to 0°).
  • 1.9 JTDm 16V / 2.4 JTDm engine: Check if the Swirl flap linkage (on top of the intake manifold) is still in place. If it has fallen off, the flaps leak or risk being swallowed by the engine.
  • 2.0 JTDm engine: Ask if the oil pump seal has been replaced. Start cold and verify that no 'Insufficient oil pressure' message appears.
  • Electronics: Test the window regulators (fragile mechanisms) and the Blue&Me Bluetooth system (the module tends to burn out, causing the mileage to flash on the dashboard).

Official recalls

List of official manufacturer recalls * May 2007: Risk of brake fluid leak at the rear hoses (models produced up to March 2007). * December 2007: Steering rack mounting problem that could lead to a loss of control (models produced in 2007). * February 2009: Defect in the engine hood locking system, risk of opening while driving (models produced between 2005 and 2008). * October 2010: Risk of short circuit on the cooling fan wiring harness (1.9 JTDm models). * March 2011: Non-compliance of the rear seat belt anchorages (models produced in 2010).

What sets it apart 6

Performance

  • Timeless Italian Design — The Alfa Romeo 159 stands out with its iconic Giugiaro design, an aggressive yet elegant line that captures every gaze and retains its appeal over time.
  • Exceptional Road Handling — Enjoy a sporty and engaging driving experience thanks to a remarkably rigid chassis, a sharp front axle, and highly communicative hydraulic steering.

Safety

  • High-Level Passive Safety — The 159 ensures optimal protection for its occupants, as evidenced by its excellent Euro NCAP test score.
  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Equipped with high-performance brakes, often Brembo on higher versions, it offers excellent endurance and short stopping distances, for total control in all circumstances.

Comfort

  • Interior Quality and Comfort — The 159's cabin benefits from significantly improved finish, with quality materials and an excellent driving position, ensuring appreciable comfort on all journeys.

Reliability

  • Reliability of the 2.0 JTDm Diesel Engine — The 2.0 JTDm engine (136 or 170 hp), recognized as the best diesel choice, offers a modern design and appreciable robustness for serene long-term use.

Alternatives

Competing alternatives * Europe: BMW 3 Series (E90) (more dynamic, RWD, but problematic N47 diesel engines), Audi A4 (B7/B8) (superior finish, reliable TDI engines in 1.9, less so in 2.0 Pumpe-Düse), Peugeot 407 (excellent chassis, reliable HDi engines but polarizing design), Mercedes C-Class (W204) (very reliable, superior comfort). * Japan/Asia: Honda Accord (8th Generation) (exceptional reliability, very robust i-DTEC diesel engine, but less Latin charm), Lexus IS (XE20) (exemplary reliability in petrol, 220d diesel to be avoided absolutely). * North America: *The Alfa 159 was not sold there.* Local alternatives: Acura TSX, BMW 3 Series, Audi A4.

Verdict

The Alfa Romeo 159 is a car bought with the heart that requires a rational purchase. Flee from the 1.9 JTDm 16V and 2.4 JTDm diesel models with a vague history or whose intake flaps have not been neutralized. Also avoid pre-2008 models, which are too heavy and prone to teething problems. The ideal choice in diesel is the 2.0 JTDm (136 or 170 hp) from after 2009, provided the oil pump seal is monitored. In petrol, the 1.75 TBi is a gem. Expect a higher-than-average maintenance budget (tires, wishbones, M32 gearbox oil changes) to enjoy one of the best chassis of its decade.
