Summary

The Renault Kangoo II (codename KW for the civilian/leisure activity vehicle version, FW for the Express utility vehicle) marks a turning point compared to the first generation. Based on the Scénic II platform, it abandons its purely utilitarian side to offer compact MPV road performance: wider tracks, better suspension comfort, and significantly increased interior space. It underwent two major facelifts (Phase 2 in 2013, Phase 3 in 2017). While diesel engines (1.5 dCi) represented the overwhelming majority of sales in Europe, petrol versions are experiencing a strong resurgence in the used market due to traffic restrictions (ZFE). However, be aware that the petrol offering is split into two eras: the very robust but thirsty naturally aspirated (1.6) engines, and the direct-injection turbocharged (1.2 TCe) engine, sadly famous for its serious design flaws.

Strengths 5

  • Giant interior space and exceptional boot volume (up to 2600 liters with rear seats folded)
  • Ride comfort worthy of an MPV (Scénic II running gear)
  • Very practical sliding side doors for families
  • Reliability of 1.6 petrol engines (8v and 16v)
  • Excellent peripheral visibility

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic reliability of the 1.2 TCe 115 engine
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • High fuel consumption of 1.6 petrol engines
  • Very utilitarian interior finish (hard plastics, basic adjustments)
  • Significant susceptibility to side wind

Check before buying

  • Clear service history: avoid any 1.2 TCe without rigorous network follow-up (and even with it, caution is advised).
  • Condition of the sliding door rails and rollers (wear, jamming, abnormal noises).
  • Wear of the running gear: steering ball joints, anti-roll bar bushes (heavy vehicle often loaded).
  • Condition of the interior: leisure activity vehicles are often mistreated by children or used for moving.
  • Air conditioning operation (condenser exposed to gravel).

Official recalls

  • January 2022: Recall on certain models produced in 2021 concerning a risk of airbag non-deployment (ECU issue).
  • May 2019: Risk of short-circuit on the ignition switch (Neiman) potentially leading to loss of power steering or fire (2017-2018 models).
  • September 2018: Inspection and possible replacement of the injection ECU on 1.5 dCi (NOx emissions non-compliance).
  • February 2016: Premature wear of front brake hoses due to incorrect routing (models produced until 2015).
  • March 2011: Reprogramming of the electric power steering (risk of loss of assistance).

What sets it apart 7

practicality

  • Exceptional Cargo Volume and Sliding Doors — Enjoy giant habitability and a boot volume of up to 2600 liters with the seats folded down, complemented by ultra-practical sliding side doors for easy access.

comfort

  • Monospace-level Driving Comfort — Thanks to its running gear derived from the Scénic II, the Kangoo II offers remarkable suspension comfort, transforming every journey into a pleasant experience for all passengers.

reliability

  • 1.6 Petrol Engines Renowned for Robustness — The 1.6 petrol engines (8v and 16v) are praised for their exemplary reliability and durability, offering long-term peace of mind.

safety

  • Passive Safety and Optimal Visibility — Benefit from solid protection with 4 Euro NCAP stars and excellent peripheral visibility, ensuring a calm and secure driving experience.

braking

  • Effective and Predictable Braking — The Kangoo II's braking system is considered effective and predictable, providing adequate safety and reassuring control in all driving situations.

handling

  • Sound and Stable Road Holding — Enjoy stable and secure road holding, with well-controlled body roll for its category, guaranteeing a serene and predictable drive.

economy

  • Economy Potential with Ethanol (E85) — The 1.6 petrol engines are known to readily accept conversion to ethanol (E85) with a suitable kit, thus offering an economical option for use.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Citroën Berlingo II / Peugeot Partner II (excellent competitors, comfortable, beware of VTi/PureTech engines), Volkswagen Caddy III/IV (superior finish, reliable TSI engines after 2015), Fiat Doblò II (very voluminous).
  • Japan / Asia: Renault Kangoo is an icon in Japan, often preferred over local kei-vans for its European style. Competition: Toyota Sienta, Honda Freed.
  • North America: Not marketed (the market prefers larger minivans like the Dodge Grand Caravan or the Ford Transit Connect for utility).

Verdict

The Renault Kangoo II is a leisure activity vehicle extremely rich in family and practical qualities. On the used market, the choice of petrol engine is crucial. We strongly recommend the 1.6 (8v or 16v) versions: although rustic and thirsty, they offer complete peace of mind and often tolerate ethanol (E85) conversions very well. In contrast, the 1.2 TCe 115 must be avoided due to its design flaws (Motorgate) which can ruin your investment. If you drive a lot, a well-maintained 1.5 dCi remains a very relevant and economical choice.
