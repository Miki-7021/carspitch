Kia Picanto

III (JA) · 2017 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The third-generation Kia Picanto (code name JA), marketed as the Kia Morning in some Asian markets, is an A-segment city car. Based on a platform shared with the Hyundai i10, it stands out for its assertive design (especially in GT-Line trim), its rich technological equipment for the category, and its 7-year manufacturer's warranty. The model has undergone two major facelifts: a first in 2020 (introduction of 'Smartstream' engines with dual injection and an AMT automated manual transmission replacing the old torque converter automatic gearbox) and a second, very extensive one in 2023/2024 (new front end inspired by the brand's electric models). It is a vehicle renowned for its excellent overall reliability, although attention must be paid to the choice of transmission.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability of naturally aspirated engines (1.0 and 1.2 MPI/DPi)
  • 7-year or 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty (reassuring for used cars)
  • Dynamic road handling and rigorous chassis
  • Rich technological equipment for the A-segment (Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, driving aids)
  • Front passenger space and trunk volume (255 liters) among the best in the category

Weaknesses 5

  • AMT automated manual transmission (post-2020) is slow, generates jerks, and is less pleasant than the old 4-speed AT
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Firm suspension, particularly on the GT-Line trim (16-inch wheels)
  • Hard interior plastics and prone to scratches
  • 67 hp 1.0 MPI engine a bit underpowered for overtaking on expressways

Check before buying

  • Behavior of the AMT automated manual transmission (if equipped): test hill starts and look for any juddering or excessive slowness.
  • Clutch wear on manual gearboxes, often heavily used in urban environments.
  • Maintenance history: essential to retain the benefit of the 7-year warranty (services must have been performed on time).
  • Condition of bumpers and wheels (urban-oriented vehicle, often exposed to parking bumps).
  • On the 1.0 T-GDi: listen to the engine when cold (timing chain noises) and check for absence of exhaust smoke (sign of turbo fatigue).

Official recalls

  • **June 2018**: Recall on certain 1.0 MPI models produced between 2017 and 2018 for a risk of fuel hose leakage (possible rubbing against the engine block).
  • **December 2020**: Software update for the emergency call system (eCall) which might not transmit correct GPS coordinates in case of an accident.
  • **February 2021**: (Specific markets) Check of rear brake caliper tightening on a small series of vehicles.

Verdict

The Kia Picanto III (JA) is one of the most recommendable city cars on the used car market. Its mechanical reliability, particularly with the 1.0 and 1.2 naturally aspirated engines, is almost flawless. The ideal choice is the 84 hp 1.2 MPI/DPi engine with a manual gearbox, which offers rare versatility for the A-segment. The 67 hp 1.0 MPI will be ample for strictly urban use. The main pitfall of this model concerns the automatic transmission: if you are looking for an automatic, prioritize models from before 2020 equipped with the true torque converter gearbox (4-speed AT), which is certainly thirstier, but infinitely smoother and more reliable than the automated manual transmission (AMT) introduced during the facelift.
