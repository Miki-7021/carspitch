Summary

The Volkswagen Touareg II (Type 7P) marks a major evolution compared to the first generation. Based on the PL52 platform shared with the Porsche Cayenne II, it sheds up to 200 kg, abandons extreme engines (W12, V10 TDI) in favor of efficiency, and adopts the excellent Aisin 8-speed automatic gearbox as standard. The standard 4-wheel drive system (4Motion) uses a Torsen differential, while the *Terrain Tech* pack (4XMotion) offers a true transfer case with low range and differential locks for off-road purists. It was facelifted in late 2014 (MY2015) with aesthetic tweaks, standard bi-xenon headlights, and the transition to Euro 6 standards for diesels. It's a formidable premium SUV for towing (3.5 tons) and traveling, but it demands a maintenance budget commensurate with its standing.

Strengths 6

  • Finish and assembly quality worthy of the upper segment (Audi/Porsche)
  • Exceptional towing capacity (3,500 kg)
  • Supreme comfort, especially with air suspension
  • Very reliable and smooth Aisin 8-speed automatic gearbox
  • High-level cabin sound insulation
  • Interior space and modularity (sliding rear bench seat)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high maintenance and repair costs (premium pricing)
  • Significant weight leading to rapid tire and brake wear
  • Consequences of the Dieselgate update on V6 TDI (EGR/DPF)
  • Aging and slow infotainment system (RNS 850)
  • Complex mechanical accessibility (especially on the V8 TDI)

Check before buying

  • Timing chain (V6/V8 TDI): Listen carefully to the engine during a cold start. A metallic clatter lasting more than 3 seconds indicates worn tensioners (very costly repair).
  • Oil leaks (V6 TDI): Inspect the engine/gearbox junction (crankshaft seal) and the engine's V (EGR cooler / upper oil pan).
  • Air suspension: Test all ride heights. The vehicle should raise and lower smoothly, and the compressor should not run continuously.
  • Water ingress: Check for moisture under the front floor mats. Panoramic sunroof drains easily clog, risking damage to control units.
  • Gearbox history: Demand proof of fluid change for the Aisin automatic gearbox, transfer case, and differentials (ideally every 60,000 to 80,000 km).

What sets it apart 7

Safety

  • Top-tier Safety for the Whole Family — The Touareg II achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 82% for child protection, ensuring absolute peace of mind.

Braking

  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Equipped with an excellent braking system, the Touareg II offers solid and progressive performance. It stops from 60 to 0 mph in just 118 feet (approx. 35.97 meters), a feat for an SUV in its category, with a firm and reassuring pedal feel.

Handling

  • Dynamic Road Handling and Supreme Comfort — Despite its size, the Touareg II surprises with its agility, controlled body roll, and exceptional stability. Its precise steering and high ride comfort, especially with the optional adaptive air suspension, make it an ideal travel companion on all roads.

Performance

  • Exceptional Power and Towing Capacity — The 3.0 V6 TDI diesel engine delivers generous torque and robust performance, allowing the Touareg II to tow up to 3,500 kg with remarkable ease, ideal for caravans, trailers, or boats.

Comfort

  • Premium Quality and High-Level Soundproofing — The Touareg II's interior boasts a finish and assembly quality worthy of the upper segment. Its exceptional soundproofing ensures quiet operation and optimal acoustic comfort for all passengers.

Reliability

  • Smooth and Reliable Aisin 8-speed Automatic Gearbox — Equipped as standard with the excellent Aisin 8-speed automatic gearbox, the Touareg II offers fluid gear changes and optimized management, contributing to a serene and efficient driving experience with proven reliability.

Practicality

  • Generous Space and Smart Modularity — The Touareg II offers substantial interior space and well-thought-out modularity, notably thanks to its sliding rear bench seat, allowing the cabin to be easily adapted to loading needs or passenger comfort.

Verdict

The Volkswagen Touareg II (7P) is an excellent road SUV, often underestimated compared to its Audi Q7 and Porsche Cayenne cousins, although it shares the same platform and offers similar performance. The 3.0 V6 TDI (245 hp or 262 hp) is the most balanced choice, but it requires absolute vigilance regarding service history (gearbox fluid change, chain noises, oil leaks). Avoid neglected examples or those whose air suspension shows signs of weakness. Purchase is recommended provided a "Premium" level maintenance budget is anticipated and a post-facelift model (2015+) with a clear history is prioritized.
