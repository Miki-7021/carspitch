Toyota Corolla Verso

II (AR10) · 2004 – 2009

NEGOTIATE Reliability 58/100 — moyen

All Toyota models

Summary

The second-generation Toyota Corolla Verso (code AR10) is a compact minivan designed specifically for the European market and assembled in Turkey. Launched in 2004, it stands out with its *Easy Flat-7* system, allowing the rear seats to fold flat into the floor, offering excellent modularity. It benefited from a facelift in 2007 (new bumpers, taillights, and equipment updates). While the gasoline versions uphold Toyota's reputation for reliability, the diesel engines (which represent the majority of sales in Europe) have experienced major failures, tarnishing the model's overall record. Overall design score: 7/10 (penalized by the 2.2 diesels).

Strengths 5

  • Exemplary modularity (very practical Easy Flat-7 system)
  • Spacious interior and cabin for a compact size (4.36 m)
  • Exceptional reliability of gasoline engines
  • Passive safety (5 Euro NCAP stars)
  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation (especially after 2007)

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic reliability of 2.2 diesel engines (D-4D and D-CAT)
  • Unpleasant and unreliable MMT automated manual gearbox
  • Hard interior plastics, very prone to scratches
  • Large turning radius
  • Ridiculous trunk volume in 7-seat configuration (65 liters)

Check before buying

  • On 2.2 diesels: traces of coolant around the expansion tank (sign of overpressure / head gasket failure).
  • On diesels: black or white exhaust smoke (DPF, injectors, EGR).
  • Maintenance history: demand invoices proving engine block replacement on 2.2 D-4D/D-CAT.
  • Gearbox operation: avoid models equipped with the MMT automated manual gearbox if gear changes are slow or harsh.
  • Front tire wear (the front axle is heavy, especially in diesel models).
  • Headlight opacity (polycarbonate yellows and dulls over time, requiring adjustment for technical inspection).

Official recalls

  • Takata Airbags (2015-2019): Replacement of the passenger and/or driver airbag inflator (risk of metallic fragment projection).
  • Accelerator Pedal (2010): Risk of the accelerator pedal sticking in the depressed position (addition of a friction shim).
  • Steering Column (2012): Replacement of the intermediate steering shaft which could show excessive play or crack.
  • Curtain Airbags (2008): Reprogramming or replacement of curtain airbag sensors which could deploy inadvertently.

safety

  • Efficient and Reassuring Braking — Benefit from a powerful and durable braking system, offering short and reliable stopping distances for increased safety in all circumstances.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Volkswagen Touran I (more austere but large trunk), Renault Grand Scénic II (comfortable but electronic issues), Opel Zafira B (similar Flex7 system), Mazda 5 (sliding doors), Honda FR-V (3 front seats).

Verdict

The Toyota Corolla Verso II is a two-faced vehicle. If you opt for a gasoline version (1.6 or 1.8 with a manual gearbox), it's a highly recommended purchase: you'll get an indestructible, practical, and economical family minivan. However, if you're targeting a diesel (which was the core of the range in France), extreme caution is advised. The 116 hp 2.0 D-4D is acceptable but aging. The 2.2 D-4D (136 hp) and D-CAT (177 hp) are to be absolutely avoided due to their engine block design flaw, unless the seller provides a Toyota invoice proving the complete engine replacement. Also avoid the MMT automated manual gearbox.
