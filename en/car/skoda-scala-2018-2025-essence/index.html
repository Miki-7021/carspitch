Skoda Scala

I (NW2) (2018–2025) · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The Skoda Scala (internal code NW2) is a compact hatchback launched in 2018, marking a break from the Rapid it replaces. Based on the Volkswagen Group's MQB A0 platform (shared with the Polo, Ibiza, Kamiq), it positions itself as a more spacious and pragmatic alternative to traditional compact cars like the VW Golf or Ford Focus, while offering a very competitive price/performance ratio. Its design is modern and its lines are more assertive than those of its predecessors. It benefits from a generous cabin for its category and one of the largest boots. A facelift was carried out in 2023, bringing minor aesthetic changes and technological updates, particularly for the petrol engines. Overall, the Scala is a reliable, well-built car offering an excellent compromise for daily use.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent value for money/performance ratio
  • Generous interior space and large boot (467 liters)
  • Reliable and fuel-efficient TSI petrol engines
  • Balanced and comfortable road handling
  • Modern technological equipment (depending on trim level)
  • Serious build quality

Weaknesses 5

  • Functional but uninspired interior design
  • DSG 7 (DQ200) gearbox sometimes hesitant at low speeds and potentially fragile on early models
  • Some hard plastics in the cabin
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds on some versions
  • Numerous official recalls, mainly software-related, but which can be concerning

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for the DSG)
  • Operation of the DSG 7 gearbox (jerks, slipping, abnormal noises)
  • Condition of the multimedia system (bugs, slowness, updates performed)
  • Presence of parasitic noises in the cabin (plastics, trim)
  • Check if all manufacturer recalls have been carried out (service book or dealership)
  • For 1.0/1.5 TSI, check for abnormal smoke or loss of power (fouling)

Verdict

The Skoda Scala is an excellent choice for anyone looking for a spacious, practical, and reliable compact hatchback without frills. The TSI petrol engines are the most recommendable, offering a good compromise between performance and fuel efficiency. The 1.0 TSI 110/116 hp is sufficient for most uses, while the 1.5 TSI 150 hp provides superior driving pleasure for long journeys. The DSG 7 (DQ200) gearbox is the main point of vigilance, especially on pre-2020 models; opt for a version with a clear service history or a manual gearbox for maximum peace of mind. The numerous recalls, although often software-related, indicate a certain youthfulness of the model but also the manufacturer's responsiveness. By ensuring that all campaigns have been carried out, the Scala represents a very rational and reliable purchase. Avoid the 1.6 TDI if you only do short urban trips.
