Summary

The BMW 4 Series F32 (Coupe) and F36 (Gran Coupe) represents the first generation of the 4 Series, marking the separation of coupes and convertibles from the 3 Series. Launched in 2013, it is based on the F30 3 Series platform, but with a lower, wider chassis and specific suspension settings for increased driving dynamics. The Coupe (F32) and Convertible (F33) were followed by the Gran Coupe (F36) in 2014, a four-door sedan with a coupe profile and a practical tailgate. A minor facelift (LCI - Life Cycle Impulse) occurred in 2017, bringing minor aesthetic touches, technological improvements, and an engine update (transition from Nxx to Bxx engines). The 4 Series F32/F36 is renowned for its balance of sportiness, comfort, and build quality, offering a typical BMW driving experience. Overall, it is a reliable model, especially with the B-series engines (B48, B58, B47, B57) introduced or generalized after the facelift.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional driving dynamics (chassis, steering)
  • Quality of interior finish and materials
  • Wide choice of powerful and efficient engines (especially B-series)
  • Elegant and timeless design (Coupe and Gran Coupe)
  • Comfort on long journeys (except for overly firm M Sport suspensions)
  • Highly successful ZF 8HP automatic gearbox

Weaknesses 6

  • Reliability of N20/N26 (timing chain) and N47 (timing chain, EGR) engines on early models
  • Cost of options and maintenance at BMW
  • M Sport suspension sometimes too firm on degraded roads
  • Limited rear passenger space on the Coupe (F32)
  • Rear visibility on the Gran Coupe (F36) and Coupe (F32)
  • Oil leak issues on N20/N55 engines

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for recalls and chain replacements)
  • Timing chain noise on cold start (N20/N26, N47)
  • Presence of oil leaks under the engine (valve cover, oil filter housing on N20/N55)
  • Condition of the charge pipe on 435i (N55)
  • EGR system operation and verification of recall performed (diesel)
  • Condition of tires, brakes, and discs (rapid wear on sporty versions)
  • Operation of onboard electronics (iDrive, sensors)
  • Condition of suspensions and silent-blocks, especially on M Sport versions

Verdict

The BMW 4 Series F32/F36 is an excellent choice for those looking for a car that is elegant, powerful, and enjoyable to drive. Reliability is generally good, but it depends heavily on the engine and production year.

**For a confident purchase:** * **Prioritize post-LCI models (from 2017 onwards)** equipped with **B-series engines (B48, B58, B47, B57)**. These engines are considered very reliable and offer an excellent compromise between performance and consumption. The **440i (B58)** is a favorite for its reliability and performance. The **430i (B48)** is an excellent versatile choice. * **If you opt for a pre-LCI model (2013-2016):** * **Petrol (N20/N26):** Be extremely vigilant about the service history and any timing chain replacement. A model whose chain has been replaced with an improved version is preferable. Otherwise, negotiate the price heavily to anticipate this costly repair. * **Diesel (N47):** Imperatively check that the EGR cooler recall has been performed. Pay attention to chain noises, although the risk is lower than on N47s of previous generations. * **Regardless of the model:** A complete and clear service history is crucial. The ZF 8HP automatic gearbox is robust, but a preventive fluid change every 80,000-120,000 km is strongly recommended despite the absence of a BMW recommendation.

In summary, the 4 Series F32/F36 is a desirable car. With the right engines and consistent maintenance, it will provide a lot of pleasure. The B48 and B58 petrol versions are the most recommendable for their reliability and performance.
