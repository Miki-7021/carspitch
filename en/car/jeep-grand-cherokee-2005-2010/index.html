Jeep Grand Cherokee

· 2005 – 2010

NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

Summary

The 3rd generation Jeep Grand Cherokee (code name WK in North America, WH in Europe) marks a major turning point for the model. Developed under the DaimlerChrysler era, it abandons the solid front axle in favor of independent suspension, significantly improving its road handling while maintaining excellent off-road capabilities thanks to the Quadra-Trac II and Quadra-Drive II systems. In Europe, it was primarily sold with the Mercedes-Benz sourced diesel V6 (OM642). A facelift occurred in late 2007 (2008 model year), bringing a much higher quality interior finish (the hard plastics of the 2005-2007 models being highly criticized), optional xenon headlights, and an overhaul of the 4.7L V8. It is a robust 4x4, but its electronics and certain engine peripherals require rigorous monitoring.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional off-road capabilities with Quadra-Drive II (front and rear Electronic Limited Slip Differentials ELSD)
  • Driving pleasure and torque of the 3.0 CRD V6 (Mercedes sourced)
  • Road handling significantly improved compared to the WJ generation
  • Range of highly capable Hemi V8 engines
  • Towing capacity (up to 3,500 kg depending on engine)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very poor interior plastic quality and assembly before the 2008 facelift
  • Gargantuan fuel consumption of gasoline engines (especially in the city)
  • Reliability of electronic peripherals (modules, sensors)
  • High maintenance costs (especially if the oil cooler leaks on the 3.0 CRD)
  • Disappointing rear seat roominess given the exterior size

Check before buying

  • **Transfer case (Recall N23/P73)**: Verify that shifting into low range (4WD Low) occurs without a 'Service 4WD System' error message on the dashboard.
  • **Axles and transmission**: Listen for the absence of groaning noises during tight turns at low speeds. The Quadra-Drive II system requires a friction modifier additive (Mopar Friction Modifier) during axle fluid changes.
  • **Front axle bushings**: A 'clunk' when shifting from P to D or R indicates worn bushings (very common).
  • **Air conditioning / Heating**: Test all zones. The blend doors often break. The repair requires the complete removal of the dashboard (very expensive).
  • **Door wiring harness**: Check the operation of the windows and central locking (wires that break in the hinge boot).
  • **3.0 CRD Engine**: Inspect under the turbo resonator for the presence of oil (a sign that the Swirl motor is in danger) and look for traces of oil at the rear of the block (oil cooler leak).

Practicality

  • Legendary Off-Road Capabilities — Thanks to advanced systems like Quadra-Drive II with electronic limited-slip differentials, the Grand Cherokee offers exceptional off-road capabilities, confidently taking you anywhere.
  • Exceptional Towing Capacity — With a towing capacity of up to 3,500 kg depending on the engine, the Grand Cherokee is the ideal partner for safely towing caravans, trailers, or boats.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Volkswagen Touareg I (very good off-roader, robust V6 TDI but temperamental electronics), Land Rover Discovery 3 (royal comfort, but hit-or-miss pneumatic/electrical reliability), Volvo XC90 I (more family-oriented, less off-road capable), Toyota Land Cruiser Prado J120 (benchmark for reliability and off-roading). - **North America**: Ford Explorer, Chevrolet TrailBlazer, Toyota 4Runner (more reliable, holds a very high resale value). - **Asia / Emerging Markets**: Mitsubishi Pajero/Montero, Nissan Patrol, Toyota Fortuner.

Verdict

The Jeep Grand Cherokee WK (2005-2010) is a 4x4 with character, offering an excellent compromise between on-road and off-road. The most rational choice in Europe remains the 3.0 CRD, provided you buy an example whose known ailments (turbo seal, oil cooler, Swirl actuator) have already been addressed with invoices. V8 enthusiasts will turn to the 5.7 Hemi, which is very robust if maintained with the correct oil. It is imperative to favor a facelifted model (2008-2010) to benefit from a worthy interior and slightly more reliable electronics. Avoid examples without a maintenance history for the axles and transfer case.
