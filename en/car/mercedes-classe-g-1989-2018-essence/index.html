Summary

The Mercedes-Benz G-Class (W463) is one of the most iconic vehicles in automotive history. Launched in 1989 as a luxurious and civilian evolution of the W460 (more utilitarian), the W463 enjoyed an exceptionally long career (29 years) before being replaced by a new generation in 2018.

Initially designed as a hardcore off-roader with its ladder frame chassis, rigid axles, and three differential locks, it gradually morphed into an ultra-luxurious status symbol, culminating with the overpowered AMG versions (G 63, G 65).

Positioning: Luxury 4x4 / Extreme off-roader.

Major evolutions:

  • 1990: Introduction of the 3 differential locks as standard.
  • 1998: Arrival of the legendary V8 (G 500).
  • 2001: Major interior redesign (modern electronics, ESP).
  • 2007: New instrument cluster, LED taillights.
  • 2012: Major facelift (LED daytime running lights, new interior, arrival of the G 63 AMG V8 Biturbo).

Overall reliability rating: Very mixed. The chassis and drivetrain are indestructible if maintained, but the bodywork is highly susceptible to rust. Mechanical reliability depends entirely on the chosen engine (ranging from the indestructible V8 M113 to the catastrophic V8 diesel OM628).

Strengths 6

  • Outstanding off-road capabilities (3 differential locks, low-range gearbox)
  • Exceptional reliability of the M113 V8 engines (G 500 / G 55 AMG)
  • Charisma, timeless design, and iconic status
  • Extremely high resale value (almost zero depreciation on certain models)
  • Sturdiness of the ladder frame chassis and rigid axles
  • Constantly improving interior finish quality (especially post-2012)

Weaknesses 6

  • Extreme susceptibility to corrosion (windshield cowl, hinges, rocker panels, shock absorber mounts)
  • Outdated road handling (vague steering, body roll, marginal braking given the weight)
  • Astronomical fuel consumption (aerodynamics of a brick)
  • Disappointing interior space compared to the exterior footprint (especially in the rear seats)
  • Cost of maintenance and spare parts (Mercedes S-Class or even AMG pricing)
  • Disastrous reliability of the V8 diesel engine (G 400 CDI)

Check before buying

  • RUST: Carefully inspect the windshield cowl, rear door hinges, wheel arches, rear shock absorber mounts, and rocker panels.
  • DIFFERENTIAL LOCKS: Test the 3 buttons (Center, Rear, Front) on a dirt road. The red indicator lights should illuminate quickly. If the system is seized (due to lack of use), repair is expensive.
  • STEERING PLAY: The recirculating ball steering box develops play over time and with the weight. Check for vagueness on-center.
  • DRIVETRAIN: Listen for axle and transfer case noises (whining, vibrations at high speed). The driveshaft must be greased regularly.
  • PETROL ENGINE (M273): On 2007-2008 G 500s, demand proof of the balance shaft replacement.
  • DIESEL ENGINE (OM642): On G 350 CDIs, look for traces of oil at the bottom of the engine's V (oil cooler leak).

Official recalls

Although the G-Class W463 is robust, it has been subject to recalls, just like other models in the Mercedes lineup. Here is the official data (Rappel Conso) including the G-Class and other models sharing components:

  • [2022-01-28] G-Class (and AMG GT, CLS, EQC, GLC, E-Class, S-Class): The electrical connector for the side impact sensors might not be properly locked and could detach. This would lead to a delay in airbag deployment. Risk of injury.
  • [2021-06-18] Various models (including G-Class/GL/GLS): Due to a defect in the emergency call system, a temporary drop in onboard voltage levels resulting from an accident could cause a malfunction. Risk of injury.

*Expert note: The other recalls provided in the database (GLE hybrid [2024-01-26], C-Class [2023-02-22, 2022-10-14, 2021-06-30], SL [2022-10-28], AMG GT [2022-02-25, 2021-09-01], Sprinter [2020-01-10], Vito [2019-11-22]) concern distinct models or more recent generations and do not apply directly to the W463.*

Historical recalls specific to the W463 (outside recent database):

  • 2016: Recall on the steering column (universal joint that may show premature wear).
  • 2011: Reprogramming of the ESP (intervention too abrupt, which could destabilize the vehicle).

What sets it apart 6

Performance

  • Legendary Off-Road Capabilities — Equipped with three differential locks and a low-range gearbox, the G-Class offers exceptional off-road prowess, allowing you to conquer the most demanding terrains with remarkable ease.

Reliability

  • Proven V8 Petrol Engine Reliability — The M113 V8 petrol engines (G 500, G 55 AMG) are renowned for their robustness and exceptional longevity, ensuring lasting performance and peace of mind both on and off the road.

Practicality

  • Timeless Design and Iconic Status — Its inimitable allure and timeless charisma make the G-Class a true symbol of prestige and exclusivity, attracting all eyes and asserting your presence with elegance.

Economy

  • Exceptional Resale Value — The Mercedes G-Class benefits from a remarkably high resale value, with almost no depreciation on many models, making it one of the most judicious and sustainable automotive investments.

Safety

  • Structural Solidity and Durability — Built on a robust ladder frame chassis and equipped with rigid axles, the G-Class is designed to withstand the most extreme conditions, offering unparalleled durability and a sense of security.

Comfort

  • Luxurious and Refined Interior — Enjoy a cabin where luxury and functionality meet. The interior finishes, especially on recent models, offer superior comfort and impeccable material quality.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Range Rover (L322 / L405) for more comfort and luxury on the road, but less reliable. Land Rover Defender (classic) for the rustic side.
  • Japan / Asia: Toyota Land Cruiser (J100 / J200) or Lexus LX. Less of a status symbol but infinitely more reliable and just as capable off-road.
  • North America: Cadillac Escalade (for status and space), Lexus LX 570 (for absolute reliability).
  • Emerging markets: Nissan Patrol (Y61 / Y62), Toyota Land Cruiser V8.

Verdict

The Mercedes G-Class W463 is the quintessential passion purchase, defying all rational logic. Its aerodynamics are poor, its road handling is clumsy, and its maintenance costs a fortune. Yet, its charisma is unmatched.

The expert's choice: If you are looking for absolute reliability and the original spirit of the G, opt for a G 500 equipped with the M113 V8 (1998-2007). It is the perfect balance between power, sound, and mechanical indestructibility. The G 55 AMG (M113K) is an excellent alternative for collectors.

To avoid absolutely: The G 400 CDI (OM628), a true financial bottomless pit, and models showing perforating corrosion (repairing the bodywork of a G is extremely expensive). Buy the condition of the bodywork and the maintenance history before buying the mileage.
