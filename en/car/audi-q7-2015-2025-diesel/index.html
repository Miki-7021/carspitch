Summary

The second-generation Audi Q7, internal code 4M, was launched in 2015, succeeding the first generation (4L). It marks a major stylistic and technical break, adopting the Volkswagen Group's MLB Evo platform, shared with models like the Bentley Bentayga, Porsche Cayenne, and Lamborghini Urus. Several hundred kilograms lighter than its predecessor thanks to the extensive use of aluminum, it offers improved agility and efficiency.

Positioned in the large 5 or 7-seater premium SUV segment, the Q7 4M stands out for its luxurious interior, advanced onboard technology (Virtual Cockpit, MMI Touch Response after the facelift), and a high level of ride comfort, particularly with the optional adaptive air suspension. It benefited from a significant **facelift in 2019**, which modernized its exterior design (octagonal grille, new headlights) and interior (dual MMI touchscreen, removal of the MMI rotary controller). All engines were also electrified to mild-hybrid (MHEV) at this time, and new plug-in hybrid (PHEV) versions were introduced.

Overall, the Q7 4M is a very well-built vehicle, offering a premium experience. Its reliability is good for a vehicle of this complexity, but maintenance and repair costs can be high, especially for the most sophisticated versions.

  • Safe and dynamic road handling for an SUV of this size

Weaknesses 6

  • High maintenance and repair costs, typical of the premium segment
  • Complexity of electronic systems (potential MMI bugs, sensors)
  • Recurring problems related to the AdBlue system on diesels
  • High fuel consumption for V6/V8 gasoline and PHEV versions on long journeys
  • High purchase price new and used
  • Bulkiness in the city and parking difficulty

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for diesels and PHEVs)
  • AdBlue system operation (warning lights, error messages)
  • DPF and EGR valve condition (for diesels, check previous usage)
  • Absence of abnormal noises on cold start (timing chain)
  • Air suspension operation (if present, check heights and absence of leaks)
  • Condition and operation of all electronic equipment (MMI, driving aids)
  • For PHEVs: battery health status (via an Audi diagnostic), charging history
  • Tire and brake wear (heavy and powerful vehicle)

What sets it apart 12

safety

  • Advanced Safety and Maximum Protection — The Audi Q7 4M achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 94% for adult occupant protection and 88% for child occupant protection, confirming its status as an ultra-safe family vehicle. It was also designated an IIHS Top Safety Pick+.
  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — Equipped with a remarkably efficient braking system, the Audi Q7 4M ensures short and controlled stops. It brakes from 60 to 0 mph in just 34.4 meters (113 feet), offering reassuring power and consistency in all circumstances.

Verdict

The Audi Q7 (4M) is an excellent choice for those seeking a large premium SUV that is comfortable, technologically advanced, and well-finished. Its reliability is generally good for a vehicle in this category, but the maintenance and repair costs, which are commensurate with its high-end positioning, should not be underestimated.

**For diesel engines (3.0 TDI and 4.0 TDI)**, which are the core of the range in Europe, they offer excellent driving pleasure and controlled consumption on long journeys. However, the AdBlue system and EGR valve are major points of concern. An impeccable service history and verification of recalls (especially Dieselgate) are essential. Prioritize highway use over urban driving to preserve the DPF.

**Gasoline engines (2.0 TFSI and 3.0 TFSI)** are rarer in Europe but offer an interesting alternative for those who drive less or prefer the pleasure of gasoline. The 3.0 TFSI is particularly recommendable for its smoothness and power.

**Plug-in hybrid versions (TFSI e)** are relevant for mixed use with regular charging, but their complexity and the lack of long-term data on battery and hybrid system longevity warrant caution. A thorough diagnosis is crucial.

**In summary:** BUY a Q7 4M if you have a substantial budget for purchase and maintenance, and if you prioritize comfort, technology, and space. NEGOTIATE if the service history is incomplete or if recalls have not been performed. AVOID poorly maintained models or those with illuminated engine warning lights, as repairs can be very expensive.
