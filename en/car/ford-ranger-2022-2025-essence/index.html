Summary

The T6.2 generation Ford Ranger (codename P703) marks a major evolution for the best-selling pickup in Europe. Developed in Australia and sharing its platform with the second-generation Volkswagen Amarok, it moves upmarket with an ultra-modern cabin (vertical SYNC 4 screen), widened tracks for better stability, and the introduction of V6 engines (petrol and diesel). Although the European market is historically dominated by diesel, this generation places a strong emphasis on petrol with the spectacular 3.0L V6 EcoBoost of the Raptor, the 4-cylinder 2.3L EcoBoost (very popular in North America), and the arrival of a plug-in hybrid (PHEV) version based on the 2.3L petrol. Overall, the Ranger T6.2 confirms its leadership position thanks to an excellent compromise between utility capabilities and SUV comfort.

Strengths 5

  • Transformed road handling, close to a modern SUV
  • High-performance petrol engine (EcoBoost) offering, rare in this segment in Europe
  • Cutting-edge onboard technology (highly responsive 10 or 12-inch SYNC 4 screen)
  • Off-road and towing capabilities (up to 3500 kg) preserved
  • Raptor version with the 3.0L V6 petrol engine, unparalleled on the European market

Weaknesses 4

  • Imposing size (wider than the previous generation), difficult to park in the city
  • Very high fuel consumption on V6 petrol engines (often > 13L/100km)
  • Significant number of early recalls (assembly, electronics, running gear) on 2022-2024 models
  • 10-speed automatic gearbox sometimes hesitant or abrupt at low speeds

Check before buying

  • Recall history: Has the vehicle undergone the fixes for the suspension ball joints and fuel hoses?
  • SYNC 4 multimedia system: Check for the absence of bugs, black screens, or unexpected restarts (often requires OTA updates).
  • Front axle: Listen for clunking noises over speed bumps (recall on the upper suspension arm ball joint nuts).
  • Transmission: Test the engagement of reverse and the first gears when cold (recall on the incorrectly machined valve body).
  • Windshield: Check for leaks and wind noise (bonding defect reported on some 2025 models).

Official recalls

The Ranger T6.2 has been the subject of numerous early recalls (NHTSA / Rappel Conso). Here are the most critical ones for the 2022-2025 models:

  • Engine / Fuel (2.3L & 3.0L Petrol):
  • *2024*: Risk of fuel leak due to incorrect tightening of the high-pressure pump bolts (NHTSA 25V597000).
  • *2024*: Possible damage to the fuel vapor line (NHTSA 24V848000).
  • *2022*: Loose fuel hose on the 2.3L engine (NHTSA 22V685000).
  • *2025-2026*: Risk of engine failure leading to a loss of power (NHTSA 26V343000).
  • Chassis / Transmission:
  • *2024-2025*: Missing or improperly tightened front upper suspension arm ball joint nut, risk of detachment (NHTSA 24V770000, 25V310000).
  • *2024*: Improperly machined transmission valve body, causing harsh engagement of reverse gear (NHTSA 25V164000).
  • *2023*: Driveshaft may detach from the transfer case (Rappel Conso).
  • Electronics / Safety:
  • *2025*: Malfunction of the EBB electronic braking module (NHTSA 25V823000).
  • *2024-2026*: Curtain airbags may tear upon contact with the B-pillar during deployment (NHTSA 25V541000).
  • *2025*: Windshield bonding defect, may detach in the event of an accident (NHTSA 25V683000).
  • *2025*: Improperly configured dashboard failing to display warning lights (NHTSA 25V399000).

What sets it apart 6

Safety

  • Certified 5-Star Safety — The Ford Ranger T6.2 ensures maximum protection for all occupants, as demonstrated by its exceptional Euro NCAP crash test scores.
  • Powerful and Controlled Braking — Benefit from strong and consistent braking performance, with excellent pedal feel and advanced assistance systems for optimal stability in all situations.

Comfort

  • Refined Driving Comfort and Surprising Agility — Enjoy a sophisticated driving experience, akin to a modern SUV, with precise steering and excellent body control, even on varied terrains.
  • Advanced Onboard Technology — The ultra-modern cabin features a large SYNC 4 screen, providing an intuitive and responsive interface for a connected experience and easy control.

Performance

  • Exhilarating EcoBoost Petrol Performance — Discover the pleasure and power of EcoBoost petrol engines, including the Raptor's 3.0L V6, offering unparalleled responsiveness and driving sensations in its segment.

Practicality

  • Robust Towing and Off-Road Capabilities — The Ranger T6.2 maintains its exceptional utility capabilities, able to tow up to 3500 kg and conquer the most demanding off-road challenges.

Alternatives

  • Europe / Asia: Volkswagen Amarok (its technical twin, more premium-oriented), Toyota Hilux (absolute benchmark for reliability, but rustic), Isuzu D-Max (very robust, utility-oriented).
  • North America: Toyota Tacoma (highly competitive new hybrid generation), Chevrolet Colorado / GMC Canyon (excellent turbo petrol engines), Nissan Frontier (reliable naturally aspirated V6).

Verdict

The Ford Ranger T6.2 is undoubtedly one of the best current pickups in terms of road performance, technology, and capabilities. The choice of a petrol engine (EcoBoost) is particularly relevant for those who make short journeys, seek driving pleasure, or want to avoid the constraints of modern diesels (DPF, AdBlue). The 2.3L EcoBoost is an excellent rational choice, while the 3.0L V6 of the Raptor is a passion purchase. However, the reliability of the early production years (2022-2024) is marred by a long list of recalls (fuel, suspension, transmission). It is imperative to verify that all manufacturer fixes have been applied before purchasing a used model. Once these early defects are corrected, it is a highly recommendable vehicle.
