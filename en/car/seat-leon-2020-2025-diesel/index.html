Summary

The fourth-generation Seat Leon (KL) is based on the Volkswagen Group's MQB Evo platform, shared with the Golf 8, Audi A3 (8Y), and Skoda Octavia IV. Positioned as the dynamic and Latin alternative to the Golf, it offers a sharp design and incisive road handling. This generation marks a technological break with the massive introduction of all-digital features (MIB3 system) and hybridization. Diesel Focus: Unlike the previous generation, the small 1.6 TDI was abandoned in favor of the exclusive 2.0 TDI (EA288 evo) unit, available in 115 and 150 hp. This engine incorporates 'Twin Dosing' technology (double AdBlue injection) to drastically reduce NOx. Although the market is moving away from diesel, this engine remains the preferred choice for high-mileage drivers thanks to exceptional fuel efficiency and great mechanical reliability, although the overall experience of the first model years was marred by recurring electronic bugs.

Strengths 5

  • Extremely fuel-efficient and mechanically refined 2.0 TDI engine
  • Dynamic road handling, superior to the Golf 8
  • Rear spaciousness and boot volume (especially in Sportstourer wagon)
  • Very successful exterior design and powerful LED lighting
  • Attractive price/equipment ratio on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous all-touch ergonomics (non-backlit climate controls)
  • Omnipresent electronic bugs on 2020-2021 model years
  • Quality of some interior plastics regressing compared to the Leon III
  • Driving aids (Lane Assist) sometimes intrusive and abrupt
  • DSG7 (DQ200) gearbox on small petrol engines still prone to wear

Check before buying

  • Multimedia system stability (test Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, GPS, radio during test drive)
  • Absence of error lights related to Travel Assist or Front Assist
  • On the 2.0 TDI: AdBlue refill history and absence of engine light (SCR system)
  • On the 2.0 TDI: DPF condition if the vehicle has low mileage (sign of short trips)
  • History of manufacturer recalls (especially engine cover and PHEV fuses)

Official recalls

  • March 2022 (Engine Cover): Fire risk. The design engine cover can detach and touch hot parts (mainly concerns 2.0 TSI and PHEV). Cover removal by the dealership.
  • May 2022 (PHEV): Risk of short circuit or fire related to a defective high-voltage battery fuse. Installation of an insulating mat.
  • November 2020 (Seatbelts): The seatbelt retractor may be defective on a small series of 2020 models.
  • February 2024 (PHEV): Missing or improperly secured heat shield near the exhaust system, fire risk.

What sets it apart 7

fiabilite|economie

  • 2.0 TDI Diesel Engine: Exceptional Reliability and Fuel Efficiency — The 2.0 TDI (EA288 evo) engine is renowned for its mechanical robustness and record-breaking fuel consumption, often below 5L/100km, making it ideal for high-mileage drivers.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Precise Road Handling — Built on the MQB Evo platform, the Leon offers an agile chassis, precise steering, and rigorous road holding, ensuring superior driving pleasure and great confidence.

Safety

  • Maximum Safety with 5-Star Euro NCAP Rating — The Seat Leon IV achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 92% for adult protection and 88% for child protection, ensuring optimal safety for all occupants.
  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — Enjoy excellent braking performance, with short stopping distances and remarkable stability during emergency braking, enhancing the vehicle's active safety.

Practicality

  • Generous Interior Space and Elegant Design — The Leon offers comfortable rear passenger space and a generous boot volume (especially in the Sportstourer version), all wrapped in a very successful and modern exterior design.

Economy

  • Excellent Value for Money on the Used Market — Benefit from a particularly attractive price-to-equipment ratio on the used car market, offering you a modern and well-equipped vehicle at an advantageous cost.

Comfort

  • Comfort and Sporty Balance — The Leon's chassis offers an excellent balance between comfort and sportiness, ensuring pleasant and dynamic journeys, whether in the city or on long trips.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Volkswagen Golf 8 (2.0 TDI - same platform but more expensive), Peugeot 308 III (1.5 BlueHDi 130 - higher trim but less reliable diesel engine), Ford Focus IV (1.5 EcoBlue - excellent chassis), Toyota Corolla (Hybrid only, no diesel).
  • North America / Mexico: Mazda 3 (2.5L naturally aspirated - premium trim), Kia Forte / Cerato, Volkswagen Jetta.
  • Asia / Other: Skoda Octavia IV (for more space with the same 2.0 TDI).

Verdict

The Seat Leon IV is an excellent compact car mechanically and dynamically, particularly with the 2.0 TDI engine, which is a true workhorse designed to cover miles without complaint. However, its launch was marred by an unfinished electronic architecture (MQB Evo). For a diesel purchase: Go for a 2022 model or later, or ensure that a 2020/2021 model has received all software updates at the dealership. Absolutely avoid diesel if you primarily drive in the city, at the risk of ruining the DPF and the complex AdBlue Twin Dosing system.
