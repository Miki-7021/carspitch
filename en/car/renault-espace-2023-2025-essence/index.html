Summary

Renault Espace (Sixth Generation - SUV)

*Expert Note: Although the request mentions the V generation, the model produced between 2023 and 2025 with an SUV body is officially the Renault Espace VI. The Espace V (2015-2023) was more of a crossover/minivan.*

The sixth-generation Renault Espace marks a complete break with its predecessors. Directly derived from the Austral (CMF-CD platform), it is extended by 21 cm to offer up to 7 seats, transforming into a true family SUV. It abandons diesel to focus exclusively on electrified petrol engines (Full Hybrid). Its interior is dominated by the excellent OpenR Link multimedia system based on Google Automotive. While it loses some of the Espace's historical modularity (independent seats replaced by a sliding bench), it gains in dynamism, particularly thanks to the *4Control Advanced* four-wheel steering system.

Strengths 5

  • Remarkable fuel consumption for its size (E-Tech 200)
  • Impressive agility with the 4Control Advanced system (rear-wheel steering)
  • Ultra-responsive and intuitive OpenR Link infotainment system (Google)
  • Spaciousness in row 2 (sliding bench seat over 22 cm)
  • Significantly improved finish (especially on the Esprit Alpine trim)

Weaknesses 5

  • Dog-clutch gearbox sometimes hesitant or generating jerks in dynamic driving
  • Very cramped space in row 3 (reserved for children or occasional use)
  • Firm comfort at low speeds, especially with 20-inch wheels
  • Absence of diesel or plug-in hybrid (PHEV) engine for very high mileage drivers
  • Reduced modularity compared to the Espace V (no more individual seats)

Check before buying

  • Software update history (crucial for hybrid management and multimedia)
  • Transmission smoothness during the test drive (electric/thermal transitions without major jerks)
  • Rear tire wear (the 4Control system can increase wear if geometry is misaligned)
  • Operation of driving aids (ADAS) and absence of error messages on the dashboard
  • Condition of the 12V battery (cases of premature discharge have been reported on early models)

Official recalls

Official Manufacturer Recalls

  • Late 2023 / Early 2024 (Hybrid Software): Recall for reprogramming of the hybrid system management computer. A software bug could lead to a loss of motive power or the display of a critical error message on the dashboard. Concerns models produced in 2023.
  • 2024 (Multimedia System): Update campaign (often performed OTA - Over The Air) to correct black screens or intermittent restarts of the OpenR Link system.
  • 2024 (Electrical Harness): Verification of an electrical harness near the steering column that could rub and cause a short circuit (concerns a small production series shared with the Austral).

Verdict

Purchase Verdict

The Renault Espace VI is an excellent family SUV that has successfully reinvented itself. Its major asset lies in its E-Tech 200 engine, which offers an exceptional performance/consumption ratio for a vehicle of this size, coupled with onboard technology (OpenR) that is among the best on the market.

However, as this is a recent model equipped with a complex powertrain (new 3-cylinder engine + clutchless dog-clutch gearbox), caution is advised. The first 2023 models experienced teething problems with software bugs and transmission jerks.

Advice: Prioritize a late 2023 or 2024 model that has benefited from all software updates. Purchase is recommended, but an extended warranty is strongly advised to guard against potential electronic issues or those related to the hybrid transmission.
