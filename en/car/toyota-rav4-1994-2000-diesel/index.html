Toyota RAV4

I (XA10) · 1994 – 2000

Summary

**PRELIMINARY NOTE:** The first-generation Toyota RAV4 (XA10) was **never produced with a diesel engine**. The first diesel engine (2.0 D-4D) only appeared in 2001 on the second generation (XA20). This report therefore exhaustively covers the gasoline (and electric) powertrains actually offered.

Launched in 1994, the Toyota RAV4 (Recreational Active Vehicle with 4-wheel drive) is considered the pioneer of modern compact SUVs. Based on a monocoque platform (derived from the Corolla/Carina) and equipped with independent suspension, it offered road handling similar to a sedan while retaining true off-road capabilities thanks to its permanent all-wheel drive (with central differential lock on manual gearboxes). Initially offered as a 3-door, it was later available as a 5-door in 1995, then as a convertible (soft-top) in 1998. A facelift occurred in late 1997 (smooth-lens headlights, modified grille, minor interior revisions). It is a vehicle that has become cult, renowned for its exceptional mechanical reliability.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability (the 3S-FE engine easily surpasses 300,000 km)
  • True permanent all-wheel drive (very effective on snow/mud)
  • Compact size, agility in town and off-road
  • Timeless look (especially the 3-door), future collector's item
  • Dynamic road handling for a 4x4 of its era

Weaknesses 6

  • Total absence of diesel engine (high fuel consumption: 9 to 11 L/100 km)
  • Extreme susceptibility to corrosion (frequent perforating rust)
  • Recurring 5th gear issue on manual gearboxes
  • Poor sound insulation on the highway
  • Tiny trunk on the 3-door version
  • Braking lacking bite (drums at the rear)

Check before buying

  • Presence of rust (rear wheel arches under the plastics, sills, fuel filler neck).
  • Road test at 90-110 km/h: check that 5th gear does not pop out when lifting off the accelerator.
  • Oil traces on the right side of the engine (distributor seal, oil pump).
  • Timing belt replacement history.
  • Condition of rear differential bushings (clunking during gear changes).

Verdict

The Toyota RAV4 XA10 is a legend in automotive history. If you're looking for a small, reliable, endearing SUV capable of driving in snow without flinching, it's an excellent choice. **Forget looking for a diesel, it doesn't exist.** The 2.0 3S-FE gasoline engine is a monument to reliability, though it is fuel-thirsty. The main buying criterion today is not mileage, but **the absence of corrosion** and the good condition of the manual gearbox (5th gear synchro). Prioritize a healthy model, even if it means paying a bit more, as bodywork costs can quickly exceed the vehicle's value.
