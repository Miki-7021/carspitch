Summary

The fourth-generation Hyundai Santa Fe (code name TM) marks a spectacular move upmarket for the Korean manufacturer. Launched in 2018, it underwent a major facelift (almost a new generation) at the end of 2020, adopting the new N3 platform shared with the Kia Sorento to integrate hybrid and plug-in hybrid powertrains. This family SUV (available in 5 or 7 seats) stands out for its supreme comfort, advanced sound insulation, and abundant technological features. While Europe primarily saw Diesel and then Hybrid (HEV/PHEV) versions, the North American and Asian markets benefited from a wide range of gasoline engines (naturally aspirated and turbocharged). Overall reliability is good, but some early-career gasoline engines (Theta II family) and the DCT-8 gearbox require particular vigilance.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent spaciousness and trunk volume
  • Interior finish and sound insulation worthy of the premium segment (especially post-2020)
  • Very comprehensive technological features and driving aids (ADAS)
  • Manufacturer's warranty of 5 years (Europe) or 10 years/100k miles (USA)
  • Remarkable efficiency of hybrid versions (HEV/PHEV)

Weaknesses 5

  • Uncertain reliability of Theta II gasoline engines (2018-2020)
  • Early issues with the wet DCT-8 gearbox (2021+)
  • Clumsy road behavior, focused solely on comfort
  • High fuel consumption of non-hybrid gasoline versions (especially 2.0T and V6)
  • Infotainment system sometimes slow on pre-facelift models

Check before buying

  • Recall history: imperatively check if the ABS/HECU module has been inspected/replaced (fire risk).
  • DCT-8 gearbox (2.5T and 2.2 CRDi 2021+): test for absence of jerks at low speed and verify the oil pump recall.
  • Theta II engines (2.4 and 2.0T): listen carefully to the cold engine (knocking) and check oil change history.
  • Hybrids (1.6T): check oil level with the dipstick (a strong gasoline smell indicates dilution).
  • Electronics: test all cameras (360°), the electric tailgate, and the touchscreen.

Official recalls

  • ABS/HECU Module (2020-2022): Risk of short circuit in the anti-lock braking module that could lead to an engine compartment fire, even with the ignition off. Recommendation to park outdoors before repair.
  • DCT-8 Gearbox (2022-2023): Failure of the high-pressure electric oil pump in the dual-clutch transmission, leading to a limp-home mode and then a total loss of motive power (concerns 2.5 T-GDi and 2.2 CRDi).
  • Seatbelt Pretensioners (2021-2022): Risk of pyrotechnic pretensioner explosion during an accident, projecting metal fragments into the cabin.
  • Fuel Leak (2021): On the 2.5 T-GDi engine, a poorly secured fuel supply hose could leak and cause a fire.
  • Dashboard (2021-2022): The digital instrument cluster screen could turn off or invert at startup (software update).

What sets it apart 7

safety

  • Recognized Top-Tier Safety — The Santa Fe achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with high scores for adult (89%) and child (82%) protection, and was designated an IIHS "Top Safety Pick+", ensuring optimal protection for all passengers.

braking

  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Enjoy excellent and consistent braking performance, with competitive stopping distances of approximately 36 to 37 meters from 96 km/h (60 mph), ensuring perfect vehicle control in all circumstances.

handling

  • Stable and Comfortable Road Handling — The Santa Fe offers predictable and reassuring road handling, focused on comfort and stability. Its body roll is well-controlled and its steering precise, for a serene drive on all types of roads.

comfort

  • Premium Interior Comfort and Refinement — Immerse yourself in a supremely comfortable cabin, featuring advanced soundproofing and a premium interior finish, offering a most pleasant travel experience for all occupants.

practicality

  • Exceptional Space and Modularity — Ideal for families, the Santa Fe stands out with its excellent spaciousness and generous boot volume, available in 5 or 7-seat configurations, to adapt to all your needs.

technology

  • Ultra-Comprehensive Technological Features — Benefit from a complete suite of advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and cutting-edge technological features, making every journey safer, easier, and more connected.

Performance

  • Efficient and Reliable Gasoline Engines — The gasoline versions, particularly the Smartstream and hybrid (HEV/PHEV) powertrains, offer an excellent balance of performance, smooth driving, and reliability, with controlled fuel consumption for the electrified versions.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Kia Sorento (technical cousin, more angular design), Skoda Kodiaq (more pragmatic, excellent spaciousness), Peugeot 5008 (more dynamic, but less powerful engines), Seat Tarraco.
  • North America: Toyota Highlander (hybrid reliability benchmark), Honda Pilot (very reliable V6, spacious), Ford Edge (5 seats only), Subaru Ascent.
  • Asia / Emerging Markets: Toyota Fortuner (ladder frame, more robust off-road), Mazda CX-8, Nissan Terra.

Verdict

The Hyundai Santa Fe IV is an excellent family SUV that has reinvented itself during its career. If you are looking for a gasoline engine, avoid 2018-2020 models equipped with 2.4 GDi and 2.0 T-GDi (Theta II) engines due to their reliability track record. Prioritize facelifted models (from 2021 onwards). The 1.6 T-GDi Hybrid (HEV) represents the best overall compromise: reliable, smooth, and economical. The 2.5 T-GDi is powerful but requires ensuring that the DCT-8 gearbox has undergone its recalls. In summary: a highly recommendable purchase in its Hybrid version, but one that requires vigilance regarding 100% thermal powertrains.
