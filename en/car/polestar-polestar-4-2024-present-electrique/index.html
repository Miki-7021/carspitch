Summary

The Polestar 4 (Generation I) is a 100% electric D-segment coupe SUV, positioned in terms of size and price between the Polestar 2 sedan and the large Polestar 3 SUV. Based on the Geely group's modular SEA (Sustainable Experience Architecture) platform (shared with the Zeekr 001 and Volvo EX30), it stands out with a radical design choice: the total absence of a rear window, replaced by a high-definition digital interior rearview mirror connected to a roof camera. This choice allows the panoramic roof to stretch beyond the heads of the rear passengers, offering exceptional roominess. Although very recent, the model promises excellent performance and generous range thanks to its 100 kWh battery. **Estimated overall rating: 8/10** (pending long-term reliability feedback).

Strengths 6

  • Bold design and highly refined aerodynamics
  • Exceptional rear roominess (legroom and headroom)
  • Highly responsive Android Automotive infotainment system (Google built-in)
  • Proven SEA platform within the Geely group (Zeekr, Volvo)
  • Charging performance (up to 200 kW in DC)
  • Dynamic road handling despite the weight

Weaknesses 5

  • Absence of a rear window requiring an adaptation period (digital rearview mirror disruptive for presbyopia)
  • Very high weight (over 2.2 tonnes unladen)
  • Frequent early software bugs on the first delivered models
  • Poor rear 3/4 visibility
  • Imposing width (difficult in European urban environments)

Check before buying

  • Operation and latency of the digital rearview mirror (rear camera) in different lighting and rain conditions.
  • Stability of the infotainment system (unexpected restarts, 4G/5G connection losses).
  • Body panel alignment (some uneven adjustments reported on the very first Chinese batches).
  • Tire wear, particularly on the Dual Motor version.
  • Wind noise around the frameless windows at high speeds.

Verdict

The Polestar 4 is a technologically advanced and bold electric vehicle. Its choice to remove the rear window is polarizing but offers a real benefit in terms of roominess. Based on Geely's excellent SEA platform, its mechanical and electrical fundamentals are solid. However, as with many ultra-connected new-generation vehicles, early buyers often bear the brunt of software bugs (Android Automotive, driving aids management).

**Buying advice:** The **Long Range Single Motor** version is the most rational choice, offering the best range and performance that is already more than sufficient. It is advisable to ensure the vehicle has the latest software updates before delivery. If you are sensitive to visual fatigue, it is imperative to test the digital rearview mirror before purchasing.
