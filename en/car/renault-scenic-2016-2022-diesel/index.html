Renault Scenic

IV (J95) · 2016 – 2022

NEGOTIATE Reliability 74/100 — bon

Summary

The Renault Scénic IV (codename J95 for the short version, R95 for the Grand Scénic) marks a radical departure from its predecessors. To counter the hegemony of SUVs, Renault transformed its compact MPV into a crossover-designed vehicle, characterized by raised ground clearance, a sloping roofline, and, unique in the segment, 20-inch wheels as standard on all trim levels (with an atypical tire size of 195/55 R20). While the style gains significantly, the Scénic's historical modularity regresses (disappearance of individual rear seats in favor of a 2/3-1/3 folding bench seat that stows into the floor). In terms of reliability, the Scénic IV's career is split into two: an initial period marred by the disastrous 1.2 TCe petrol engine and some electronic bugs (R-Link 2), and a second, much more serene period (post-2018) with the arrival of the 1.3 TCe and Blue dCi diesels, although the latter suffer from the chronic issue of AdBlue systems.

Strengths 5

  • Very successful design, ages very well aesthetically
  • Dynamic and safe road handling
  • Very reliable and fuel-efficient 1.5 dCi diesel and 1.3 TCe petrol engines
  • Generous boot volume (especially on Grand Scénic)
  • Rich technological equipment on high trim levels (Intens, Initiale Paris)

Weaknesses 6

  • Catastrophic 1.2 TCe engine (absolutely to avoid)
  • Very fragile AdBlue system on 1.7 Blue dCi
  • Standard 20-inch wheels: firm comfort at low speeds and high tire cost
  • Reduced modularity (no more removable individual rear seats)
  • R-Link 2 multimedia system slow and prone to bugs on 2016-2018 models
  • Rapid front tire wear on high-torque engines (dCi 160)

Check before buying

  • Clear service history (especially for diesels with DPF and AdBlue)
  • Condition of 20-inch tires (frequent asymmetrical wear, replacement cost)
  • Functionality of the R-Link 2 multimedia system (test Bluetooth, GPS, touchscreen)
  • EDC automatic gearbox: check for absence of jerks on startup and during gear changes (fragile clutches in intensive urban use)
  • On 1.7 Blue dCi: ask if the AdBlue tank has already been replaced
  • Body panel alignment and wind noise (assembly sometimes imperfect on early model years)

Official recalls

  • - **November 2016**: Risk of deactivation of the rear door child safety lock (models manufactured until June 2016).
  • - **February 2017**: Possible incorrect deployment of curtain airbags (late 2016 production).
  • - **May 2018**: Malfunction of the electric folding system for the rear bench seat (One Touch).
  • - **October 2019**: On 1.7 Blue dCi engines, reprogramming of the injection ECU is necessary to correct a NOx sensor fault.
  • - **December 2019**: Risk of fuel leak from a hose (concerns some 1.3 TCe models).

Reliability

  • Proven and Reliable Diesel Engines — Diesel engines, particularly the 1.5 dCi, are renowned for their robustness and exemplary reliability. They offer significant longevity and peace of mind on the road.

Economy

  • Remarkable Fuel Efficiency of Diesel Engines — Enjoy controlled fuel consumption thanks to efficient diesel powertrains, such as the 1.5 dCi, which allow you to cover long distances with an optimized fuel budget.

Safety

  • High-Level Passive Safety — The Scénic IV achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult and child protection, ensuring optimal safety for all passengers.
  • Dynamic and Serene Road Handling — Benefit from safe and balanced road holding, offering an excellent compromise between comfort and dynamism. Precise steering and controlled body roll ensure stability and serenity, even at high speeds.
  • Powerful and Effective Braking — The Scénic IV's braking system is powerful, durable, and offers short stopping distances. Its good modulation actively contributes to vehicle safety in all situations.

Practicality

  • Generous Boot Volume and Smart Modularity — With a generous boot volume, especially in the Grand Scénic version, this vehicle offers a large loading capacity for all your family adventures and daily needs.

Alternatives

Verdict

The Renault Scénic IV is a polarizing vehicle. Its attractive design came at the expense of the model's historical modularity. In terms of reliability, it's a mixed bag. **If you're looking for a diesel**, the old but robust 1.5 dCi 110 is the safest and most economical choice. The 1.6 dCi 130 is an excellent compromise for families. The newer 1.7 Blue dCi offers superior driving pleasure but carries the risk of a costly AdBlue system failure. **For petrol**, flee the 1.2 TCe like the plague, and go for the excellent 1.3 TCe (140 or 160 hp) with your eyes closed. When buying, always negotiate if the 20-inch tires are close to the wear indicator, as their replacement represents a significant budget.
