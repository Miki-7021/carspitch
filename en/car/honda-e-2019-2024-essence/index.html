Honda e

I · 2019 – 2024

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

Summary

**Model presentation** The Honda e is a city car with a highly endearing neo-retro design, marking Honda's first serious foray into 100% electric vehicles (BEV) in Europe. Designed exclusively for the city, it stands out with its ultra-technological interior (dashboard composed of multiple screens), its standard camera-mirrors, and its exceptional turning radius thanks to its rear-wheel drive architecture (rear-mounted engine).

*Important note regarding the engine:* Although the request targets a petrol engine, **the Honda e was never produced with a combustion engine (petrol or diesel)**. It is an exclusively electric model. Its production was halted in early 2024 due to disappointing sales, penalized by an elitist price and very limited range (35.5 kWh battery offering around 220 km WLTP, or 150 to 180 km in real-world conditions).

**Overall reliability rating: Excellent.** The electric mechanics and running gear are over-engineered and extremely reliable. The only inconveniences concern the software and the 12V auxiliary battery.

Strengths 5

  • Mechanical and electrical reliability (very robust traction motor and high-voltage battery)
  • Exceptional maneuverability (4.3-meter turning radius)
  • Unique neo-retro exterior design and endearing appeal
  • Interior finish quality and technological presentation (screens, cameras)
  • Dynamic road handling (RWD, low center of gravity)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very low range (often less than 150 km in winter or on the highway)
  • Tiny trunk volume (171 liters)
  • Very high purchase price (both new and used)
  • Relatively high electrical consumption for its size
  • My Honda+ mobile app sometimes temperamental

Check before buying

  • Condition of the 12V battery (main source of immobilization breakdowns on this model)
  • Rear tire wear (instant torque and RWD wear out the rubber quickly)
  • Smooth operation of all screens and camera-mirrors (prohibitive replacement cost)
  • Condition of the alloy wheels (highly exposed to curbs in urban use)
  • History of software updates performed at the dealership

Verdict

**BUY (under usage conditions)**

The Honda e is a masterpiece of urban engineering and an extremely reliable vehicle. The absence of a combustion engine, a complex gearbox, and Honda's mastery of power electronics make it a car without major mechanical worries.

However, the purchase must be made with full knowledge of the facts: it is a niche vehicle, strictly limited to urban or peri-urban use due to its low range and small trunk. If your profile matches these constraints and you have a home charging solution, it is a highly recommendable passion purchase. Favor models whose 12V battery has been recently replaced and whose software updates are up to date.
