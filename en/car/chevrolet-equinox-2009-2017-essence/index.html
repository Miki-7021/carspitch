Summary

The second-generation Chevrolet Equinox is a compact/mid-size SUV that enjoyed immense success, particularly in North America. Based on the General Motors Theta platform (shared with the GMC Terrain), it stands out for its ride comfort, excellent soundproofing, and sliding rear bench seat (Multi-Flex system) offering legroom worthy of a higher category. Although recent statistics from J.D. Power and Consumer Reports rank it above the class average in terms of overall reliability, this generation had a difficult start with its 4-cylinder engine, before reaching excellent maturity at the end of its career (after the 2016 facelift).

Strengths 5

  • Above-average reliability on late-career models (2014-2017) and V6s
  • Sliding rear bench seat offering exceptional legroom
  • Very successful cabin soundproofing (active noise cancellation system on 4-cylinder models)
  • High-performance 3.6L V6 engine (301 hp) rare in this category
  • Extensive after-sales network and highly accessible spare parts (especially in North America)

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic oil consumption problems on the 2.4L from 2010 to 2013
  • Poor rear and rear three-quarter visibility (very wide C-pillars)
  • High fuel consumption on V6 engines
  • Quality of some interior plastics that scratch easily
  • Large turning radius for an SUV of this size

Check before buying

  • Engine oil level (crucial on the 2.4L, ask for maintenance invoices or proof of piston ring replacement under warranty).
  • Metallic rattling noises on cold start (symptom of a stretched timing chain).
  • Operation of the front wipers (subject to several recalls for linkage corrosion).
  • Automatic gearbox shifting (jerks between 3rd and 4th gear on 2010-2011 models indicate internal wear).

Official recalls

  • March 2022 & August 2016 (Wipers): Recalls (22V165000 / 16V582000) concerning 2013-2015 models. The ball joints of the wiper module can corrode, leading to the failure of one or both wipers.
  • July 2016 (Ignition key): Recall (16V502000) on certain 2016 models. The electronic lock lever may allow the key to be removed without the transmission being in the PARK position.
  • June 2016 (Labels): Recall (16V449000) on 2016 models. Incorrect information on rim/tire size and cold pressure on the certification label.
  • October 2015 (Airbags): Recall (15V666000) on 2015 models. Potential problem with the side airbags mounted in the front seats.
  • July 2014 (Power seats): Recall (14V447000) on 2010-2012 models. Defect on the height adjustment mechanism of the front power seats.
  • October 2011 (TPMS): Recall (11V511000) on 2012 models. Non-compliance of the tire pressure monitoring system.
  • December 2010 (Seat belts): Recall (10V623000) on 2011 models. The front seat belt buckle anchor can fracture.
  • December 2009 (Defrosting): Recall (09V489000) on 2010 models. Non-compliance of the windshield defrosting/defogging system.

What sets it apart 6

reliability

  • Proven and Recognized Reliability — Enjoy peace of mind thanks to reliability recognized by independent organizations like J.D. Power and Consumer Reports, especially for V6 engines and 2.4L models from 2014 onwards.

comfort

  • Exceptional Ride Comfort — Experience a serene and refined driving experience with remarkable ride comfort and excellent cabin soundproofing, enhanced by an active noise reduction system on 4-cylinder versions.

practicality

  • Versatile and Generous Interior Space — The Equinox stands out with its sliding rear bench seat (Multi-Flex system) offering exceptional legroom, worthy of a higher category, ensuring optimal comfort for all passengers and great versatility.

Performance

  • Powerful and Refined V6 Engines — Discover dynamic performance with the 301 hp 3.6L V6 engines, offering rare power in this category, ideal for long journeys and towing, while maintaining great flexibility.

economy

  • Easy Maintenance and Extensive Network — Benefit from easy maintenance thanks to an extensive after-sales service network and excellent availability of spare parts, ensuring quick and efficient support for your vehicle.
  • Optimized Efficiency (Recent 2.4L Models) — The 2.4L Ecotec versions from 2014 offer reasonable fuel consumption for the category, combining reliability and everyday economy.

Alternatives

  • North America: Honda CR-V (more spacious, very reliable), Toyota RAV4 (benchmark for reliability), Ford Escape (more dynamic but uneven EcoBoost engines), Nissan Rogue (more economical but fragile CVT gearbox).
  • Emerging markets / Middle East: Hyundai Tucson, Kia Sportage, Mazda CX-5 (excellent alternative for driving pleasure).

Verdict

The second-generation Chevrolet Equinox is a vehicle with two faces. Statistics from J.D. Power and Consumer Reports place it above average, which is entirely true for models equipped with the robust V6 engines (3.0L and 3.6L) as well as the 2.4L 4-cylinders produced from 2014 onwards. These versions offer an excellent price/quality ratio, undeniable comfort, and high reliability. On the other hand, you must absolutely avoid the 2.4L models from 2010 to 2013 unless the engine has been rebuilt (piston rings/timing chain) under warranty by GM, otherwise you risk facing engine failure due to chronic oil consumption.
