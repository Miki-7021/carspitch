Chevrolet Colorado

2nd gen (GMT31XX) · 2015 – 2022

BUY Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The 2nd generation Chevrolet Colorado (codename GMT31XX for North America) marked the highly anticipated return of General Motors to the midsize pickup segment. Positioned below the Silverado, it stands out with an excellent compromise between utility capabilities, ride comfort, and dimensions suited for daily use. Unlike the global version (sold in Asia and South America since 2012), the North American version launched in 2015 benefits from a specific chassis, dedicated gasoline engines, and a more plush interior. It received a mild facelift in 2021. Overall, it is a reliable model that ranks in the upper average of its category, offering a more comfortable alternative to the Toyota Tacoma, although the early model years (2015-2016) suffered several early recalls.

Strengths 5

  • Remarkable reliability of the 2.5L gasoline and 2.8L Duramax diesel engines
  • Ride comfort superior to the competition (notably the Toyota Tacoma)
  • Excellent towing capacity (up to 3,490 kg with the diesel)
  • Intuitive and responsive infotainment system
  • Moderate maintenance costs (according to J.D. Power)

Weaknesses 5

  • Interior plastic quality falls short relative to the price
  • Early issues on the 2015-2016 model years (steering, seats, airbags)
  • Capricious 8-speed automatic gearbox (8L45) on 2017-2019 models before corrective fluid change
  • Original halogen headlight illumination considered weak
  • Limited rear legroom (Crew Cab)

Check before buying

  • Recall history: crucial on 2015 and 2016 models (power steering, airbags).
  • Automatic gearbox behavior (V6 2017+): test the vehicle at a steady speed (60-80 km/h) to detect any shudder.
  • Emission control system (Diesel): check for the absence of warning lights related to the DPF or DEF system, especially if the vehicle has been driven mostly in the city.
  • Chassis corrosion: to be inspected if the vehicle comes from a region with harsh winters (road salt).
  • Electric power steering operation: ensure there are no stiff spots or loss of assistance.

Verdict

The 2nd generation Chevrolet Colorado is an excellent choice in the midsize pickup category, offering ride comfort that is often superior to its direct rivals. **The 2.5L gasoline and 2.8L Duramax diesel engines are the true stars of the lineup in terms of reliability**, showing excellent long-term records. The V6 is high-performing but requires more rigorous maintenance.

**Buying advice:** Avoid the 2015 and 2016 model years if possible, as they concentrated almost all the recalls (steering, airbags, brakes). Favor a model from 2017 onwards. If you opt for a V6 (2017-2019) equipped with the 8-speed automatic gearbox, demand proof that the transmission fluid change was performed with the updated GM fluid to avoid torque converter issues.
