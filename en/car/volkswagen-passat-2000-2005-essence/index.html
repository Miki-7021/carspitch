Summary

The Volkswagen Passat B5.5 (internal code name 3BG or Type 3B3 for the sedan / 3B6 for the SW wagon) is a deep facelift of the B5 generation. Launched in late 2000 (model year 2001), it embodies Ferdinand Piëch's desire to push Volkswagen towards the premium segment. It stands out for its exceptional interior finish quality for the time, a longitudinal engine architecture (shared with the Audi A4 B5/B6), and the use of a complex multi-link front suspension. While diesel engines (notably the legendary 1.9 TDI) made its success in Europe, its gasoline range is particularly rich, ranging from the modest 1.6 MPI to the very exotic (and fragile) 4.0 W8. Today, it is an affordable youngtimer, but its advanced age requires strict vigilance regarding peripherals and comfort electronics.

Strengths 6

  • Interior finish and material quality (ages very well if cared for)
  • Superb road handling on the highway
  • Excellent sound insulation
  • Formidable 4Motion (Torsen) system in snow (on V6 and W8)
  • Very robust and powerful 1.8T 20V engine
  • Gigantic trunk volume on the wagon version (Variant/SW)

Weaknesses 6

  • Fragile multi-link front suspension (rapid wear of silentblocks and ball joints)
  • Chronic water ingress (windshield cowl)
  • Capricious comfort electronics (comfort module under the driver's carpet)
  • Door lock microswitches failing (car no longer detects opening)
  • Maintenance cost of V6 and W8 engines (front end removal required for timing belt/chain)
  • Polycarbonate headlights that heavily tarnish with UV

Check before buying

  • Water ingress: Lift the front carpets (especially on the driver's side). If it's damp, the comfort module (CCM) will burn out. Check the drains under the battery and the brake booster (often clogged with leaves).
  • Front suspension: Listen for clunking noises on degraded roads or speed bumps. The complete replacement of the suspension arms (multi-link kit) costs between 500€ and 1000€.
  • Locks: Check that the dome light illuminates when EACH door is opened. If not, the lock microswitch is faulty.
  • 1.8T Engine: Check oil change history. Open the oil filler cap and look for traces of black sludge.
  • CV joint boots: Very exposed on this generation, they often tear, leading to the failure of the constant velocity joint.

Official recalls

  • 2001 (Steering ball joints): Replacement of steering ball joints on early models (premature wear potentially leading to loss of control).
  • 2004 (Heated seats): Risk of overheating and local combustion of the heating element in the driver/passenger seat.
  • 2006 (Ignition coils): Free replacement of defective ignition coils on 1.8T, V5, V6, and W8 engines.
  • 2008 (Windshield cowl drains): Removal of rubber plugs under the battery to prevent water accumulation and flooding of the cabin/brake booster.

What sets it apart 6

Quality & Comfort

  • Exceptional Build Quality and Comfort — The Passat B5.5 stands out with remarkable interior finish and materials, ensuring a durable cabin and excellent sound insulation for serene and pleasant journeys.

Road Handling

  • Imperial Stability on the Road — Designed for long journeys, the Passat B5.5 offers highly stable road handling, particularly appreciated on highways, guaranteeing a safe and relaxing drive.

Performance & Reliability

  • The 1.8T 20V Engine: Dynamism and Robustness — The 1.8T 20V engine is a preferred choice, combining dynamic performance with proven robustness, while offering an excellent balance between power and fuel efficiency.

Practicality & Space

  • Generous Interior Space and Boot Volume — Ideal for families or professional needs, the Passat B5.5, especially the Variant version, offers remarkable boot volume and interior space, suitable for all requirements.

Road Holding & Safety

  • Control and Grip with the 4Motion System — For enhanced safety and optimal grip in all circumstances, versions equipped with the 4Motion (Torsen) all-wheel-drive system offer exceptional road holding, even on challenging surfaces.

Driving Pleasure

  • The Smoothness and Silence of V6 Engines — The V6 petrol engines provide a refined driving experience, characterized by flexibility, quiet operation, and generous torque, for superior driving pleasure.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 406 / 407 (excellent chassis), Ford Mondeo Mk3 (very reliable in gasoline), BMW 3 Series E46 (rear-wheel drive, 6-cylinder engines), Audi A4 B6 (direct technical cousin).
  • Japan/Asia: Honda Accord (superior mechanical reliability), Toyota Avensis (indestructible but austere).
  • North America: Honda Accord, Toyota Camry, Nissan Altima.

Verdict

The Volkswagen Passat B5.5 is an excellent road car that marked its era with its manufacturing quality. In terms of gasoline engines, the 1.8T 20V is undeniably the best choice: powerful, reliable (if regularly serviced), and economical to use. The V6 versions offer superior driving pleasure but significantly increase the maintenance budget. Avoid the W8, a true financial black hole. The crucial point before purchase is not so much the engine (often robust) as the condition of the multi-link front suspension and the absence of water ingress in the cabin. Prioritize a model with a clear history and cleaned windshield cowl drains.
