Summary

The **first-generation Chevrolet Cruze (J300)** is a global compact sedan developed by General Motors on the Delta II platform (shared with the Opel Astra J). Designed to replace the aging Nubira/Lacetti/Optra, it marked a significant leap forward in terms of design, road handling, and safety. Initially available as a 4-door sedan, it was later offered as a 5-door hatchback (2011) and a Station Wagon (2012). Although it offers an excellent price-to-performance ratio and generous interior space, it suffers from a high weight that penalizes entry-level engines and an interior finish dominated by hard plastics. The withdrawal of the Chevrolet brand from the European market in late 2014 / early 2015 caused its value to plummet, making it an excellent used car bargain today, especially since maintenance is still provided by the Opel/GM network.

Strengths 5

  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Safe and secure road handling (Astra J chassis)
  • Generous interior space and large trunk (especially in the SW station wagon)
  • 1.7 VCDi 130 and 2.0 VCDi 163 engines perform very well on the road
  • Exterior design that has aged well

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high weight (over 1400 kg) penalizing fuel consumption and smaller engines
  • Hard interior plastics sensitive to scratches
  • Withdrawal of Chevrolet from Europe (massive depreciation, although GM/Opel parts are available)
  • Very temperamental diesel particulate filters (DPF) on diesels in urban use
  • Notchy manual gearbox on some versions

Check before buying

  • DPF regeneration history (warning light on, oil level too high on diesels)
  • Presence of oil in the coolant reservoir (common issue on 1.6 and 1.8 petrols)
  • Whistling at idle on the 1.4 Turbo (sign of a failed PCV valve requiring a new valve cover)
  • Metallic noises in neutral on manual gearbox diesels (dual-mass flywheel)
  • Smooth operation of the automatic gearbox (jerks = urgent oil change or damaged gearbox)

Braking

  • Effective and Reassuring Braking — Equipped with a high-performance braking system, the Cruze offers short stopping distances and great stability, guaranteeing perfect control in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Sound and Comfortable Road Handling — Its robust chassis, shared with the Opel Astra J, ensures stable and predictable road holding, prioritizing driving comfort on all types of journeys.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Powerful Diesel Engines — The 1.7 VCDi 130 hp and 2.0 VCDi 163 hp engines deliver excellent road performance, with generous torque for strong acceleration and efficient recovery.

Reliability

  • Proven Diesel Engine Reliability — The Isuzu-sourced 1.7 VCDi engine is renowned for its robustness and endurance, while the 2.0 VCDi 163 hp benefits from a timing chain, ensuring longevity.

Practicality

  • Generous Cabin Space and Large Boot — The Cruze stands out for its welcoming interior space for passengers and its substantial boot volume, particularly valuable for families and long trips.

Economy

  • Excellent Value for Money in Used Market — Enjoy a very well-equipped vehicle at a highly attractive price on the used car market, offering exceptional value for your investment.

Alternatives

* **Europe:** Opel Astra J (its technical cousin, better finished), Renault Mégane III (excellent dCi diesels), Peugeot 308 I/II, Ford Focus III. * **North America:** Honda Civic, Toyota Corolla, Ford Focus, Hyundai Elantra. * **Asia / Emerging:** Hyundai Verna/Elantra, Kia Cerato, Honda City.

Verdict

The Chevrolet Cruze I is the archetype of a smart used car purchase, provided you choose the right engine and buy it at the right price (the depreciation linked to Chevrolet's departure should work in your favor). **For high mileage drivers, the 1.7 VCDi 130 diesel (Isuzu origin) is the most rational and reliable choice**, followed by the powerful 2.0 VCDi 163 hp. Absolutely avoid the diesels if you drive in the city (guaranteed DPF clogging). In petrol, the naturally aspirated 1.8 is robust but thirsty (ideal for an E85 conversion), while the 1.4 Turbo is pleasant but requires monitoring of its cooling system and PCV valve. It is imperative to verify that the recalls (especially the Takata airbags) have been carried out.
