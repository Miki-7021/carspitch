Mercedes EQS

I (V297) · 2021 – 2024

NEGOTIATE Reliability 83/100 — bon

Summary

The EQS (code name V297) is the 100% electric flagship of Mercedes-Benz, the zero-emission equivalent of the S-Class. Based on the dedicated EVA2 platform, it stands out with its 'One-Bow' design dictated by aerodynamics, giving it a record drag coefficient (Cx) of 0.20. Inside, it introduces the impressive (and optional) MBUX Hyperscreen, a glass panel covering the entire dashboard. Although the mechanical reliability of the electric motors is excellent, this ultra-technological model is prone to software bugs and a dizzying depreciation on the used market.

**Overall design rating: 8.5/10** - An impressive technological showcase, but its software complexity requires rigorous monitoring.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional real-world range (especially on the 450+ version)
  • Absolute benchmark ride comfort and soundproofing
  • Record aerodynamics (Cx 0.20) favoring highway efficiency
  • Rear-wheel steering (up to 10°) making this land yacht very maneuverable in the city
  • Onboard technology and the 'Wow' effect of the Hyperscreen

Weaknesses 5

  • Colossal depreciation on the used market
  • Very artificial and confusing brake pedal feel
  • Excessive weight (over 2.5 tons) penalizing agility
  • Rear headroom limited by the sloping roofline
  • Frequent software bugs on early model years (MBUX)

Check before buying

  • Software update history (OTA and dealership)
  • Condition and wear of tires (very expensive and wear out quickly given the weight)
  • Smooth operation of the Hyperscreen (no random reboots)
  • Condition of the supplied charging cables (see official recalls)
  • Absence of interior trim noises (some cases of creaking reported, unworthy of the segment)

Verdict

The EQS is an engineering feat focused on absolute comfort and aerodynamic efficiency. Mechanically, the electric motors and batteries prove to be very reliable. However, its Achilles heel lies in its software complexity (MBUX bugs) and brutal financial depreciation. **It is strongly advised against buying it new.** Used, it is an exceptional deal for anyone looking for an ultra-luxury electric cruiser at a fraction of its original price. Favor the **450+** version for its unbeatable range and greater simplicity (RWD).
