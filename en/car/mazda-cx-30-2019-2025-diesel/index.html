Summary

The Mazda CX-30 (DM generation) is a compact SUV positioned between the aging CX-3 and the family-oriented CX-5. Based on the Mazda 3 (BP) platform, it stands out with its very fluid 'Kodo' design, an interior finish bordering on the premium segment, and dynamic road handling. Although European demand has massively shifted towards mild-hybrid gasoline engines (e-Skyactiv G and the revolutionary e-Skyactiv X), the CX-30 was launched with a 1.8 Skyactiv-D diesel engine. This block, designed to meet Euro 6d-Temp standards without resorting to SCR (AdBlue) after-treatment thanks to a very low compression ratio, had a short career in Europe (withdrawn at the end of 2021) but remains relevant in the used car market for high-mileage drivers, and continues to be sold in other markets (Japan, Oceania). Overall, the CX-30 shows an excellent reliability record, true to the reputation of the Hiroshima brand.

Strengths 6

  • Very high-quality interior finish (materials, assembly)
  • Ergonomics of controls (intuitive central dial, no all-touchscreen)
  • Excellent sound insulation for the category
  • Dynamic road handling and precise steering
  • Overall reliability of naturally aspirated gasoline engines (Skyactiv-G)
  • Smooth and very reliable torque converter automatic gearbox (BVA6)

Weaknesses 5

  • Rear passenger space and trunk volume (430 L) are average for its size (4.39 m)
  • Peripheral visibility (very thick C-pillars, narrow rear window)
  • 1.8 diesel engine unsuitable for short trips (rapid clogging)
  • High maintenance cost for the e-Skyactiv X engine (spark plugs)
  • Infotainment system sometimes slow to start on early model years

Check before buying

  • For the 1.8 Diesel: Oil level on the dipstick (check that it does not exceed the 'X' dilution mark) and DPF regeneration history.
  • Operation of the electric tailgate (struts sometimes faulty or noisy).
  • Wear of front tires (the front axle can be incisive and wear soft compounds).
  • History of software updates (engine management on Skyactiv-X, multimedia system, driving aids).
  • Condition of the 12V battery (cases of rapid discharge 'parasitic drain' have been reported on 2019-2020 models).

Official recalls

  • Fuel Pump (November 2021): Major global recall (including the CX-30). The low-pressure fuel pump impeller (supplied by Denso) can deform and block the pump, leading to a loss of power or engine stalling.
  • Autonomous Emergency Braking / SBS (December 2019): The Smart Brake Support system can falsely detect an obstacle and abruptly brake the vehicle. Correction via software update.
  • Electric Tailgate (February 2021): The electric tailgate may unexpectedly lower if parked on a slope in hot weather. Replacement of struts or control module update.
  • Tire Valves (2021): On some models, the inflation valves can crack and cause a slow pressure loss.

Alternatives

Verdict

The Mazda CX-30 is one of the most reliable and best-finished compact SUVs of its generation. If you are specifically targeting the 1.8 Skyactiv-D Diesel engine, the purchase is conditional on your driving profile: it is excellent and frugal for high-mileage drivers covering many kilometers on roads and highways. However, if your usage includes city driving or short trips (< 20 km), AVOID the diesel to prevent chronic DPF, EGR valve, and oil dilution problems. In this case, opt for the excellent 2.0 e-Skyactiv G (or the 2.5 depending on your market), which offers almost irreproachable reliability and complete peace of mind.
