Summary

The second-generation Opel Mokka (Mokka B) marks a complete break with its predecessor. Gone is the General Motors era; this model is based on the Stellantis group's modular CMP (Common Modular Platform) platform, shared with the Peugeot 2008 and DS 3 Crossback. It inaugurates the brand's new stylistic language with the "Opel Vizor" grille and the "Pure Panel" digital dashboard. Shorter, lighter, and more dynamic than the Mokka X, it sacrifices some interior space for style. Although demand has strongly shifted towards gasoline and electric, the Mokka B was offered with the 1.5 Diesel engine (derived from the 1.5 BlueHDi PSA), a frugal powertrain that requires particular attention on the used market due to known design weaknesses in this generation of Stellantis engines.

Strengths 5

  • Bold and successful design (Opel Vizor)
  • Dynamic road handling and rigorous chassis
  • Extremely low fuel consumption of the 1.5 Diesel on road/highway
  • Interior ergonomics (Pure Panel) with retention of physical controls for air conditioning
  • Comprehensive technological equipment (Matrix LED headlights depending on trim level)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very restricted rear passenger space (legroom and access)
  • Average boot volume for the category (350 L, and 310 L for the electric)
  • Disastrous reliability of early Stellantis engines (1.2 belt, 1.5D chain, AdBlue)
  • Firm suspension, especially with 18-inch wheels
  • Limited peripheral visibility (thick pillars, small rear windows)

Check before buying

  • On the 1.5 Diesel: Listen carefully to the top of the engine when cold (metallic rattling noise = loose camshaft chain).
  • On the 1.5 Diesel: Check the AdBlue refill history and the absence of an 'Antipollution Fault' warning light.
  • On the 1.2 Turbo: Inspect the condition of the timing belt via the oil filler cap (cracks, fraying).
  • On all versions: Check body panel alignment and interior trim noises.
  • On the electric: Test the air conditioning and heating (known compressor failure).

Official recalls

  • December 2021 (Software): Engine control unit update on 1.5 Diesel versions to correct a NOx emissions diagnostic fault.
  • October 2021 (Wipers): Risk of wiper motor malfunction in cold weather (water infiltration).
  • 2022 (DPF - Diesel): Recall on certain 1.5 Diesel models for a risk of particulate filter cracking.
  • 2023 (Traction Battery - EV): Inspection and software update for the high-voltage battery management system to prevent a risk of traction loss.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 2008 II (technical cousin, same engines), Renault Captur II (excellent hybrid engines and reliable dCi diesels), Ford Puma (dynamic, mild hybrid), Volkswagen T-Cross (superior spaciousness).
  • United Kingdom: Vauxhall Mokka (identical), Nissan Juke, Skoda Kamiq.

Verdict

The Opel Mokka B is a city SUV with a striking style, but its Stellantis underpinnings demand great caution on the used market. If you are specifically targeting the 1.5 Diesel, it is imperative to prioritize a model whose timing (camshaft chain) has been updated with the 8 mm kit, or a very recent model (post-2023). The AdBlue system remains a sword of Damocles. Avoid classic 1.2 Turbo gasoline versions (wet belt) unless they have an impeccable service history. The most recommendable versions are the recent mild-hybrid version (with chain) or the electric one, if suitable for your use.
