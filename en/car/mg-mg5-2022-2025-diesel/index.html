Summary

Presentation of the MG5 (Gen I Facelift)

The MG5 is a model with an atypical positioning that has a dual identity depending on the market. In **Europe**, it is the first 100% electric station wagon on the market (MG5 EV), which received a major facelift in 2022 (abandoning the fake grille for a solid nose, new interior). On **international markets** (Asia, Middle East, Oceania, Latin America), the MG5 is a compact combustion-engine sedan (often derived from the Roewe i5).

**⚠️ EXPERT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:** Although your search targets a **diesel** engine, it is imperative to specify that **no MG5 of this generation has ever been produced with a diesel engine**, on any global market. The SAIC group (owner of MG) has focused its strategy on electric (for Europe) and small gasoline engines (naturally aspirated and turbocharged) for emerging markets, completely abandoning diesel for its passenger vehicles. The analysis will therefore focus on the actually existing engines (Electric and Gasoline) to guide you as best as possible.

**Overall model rating:** A pragmatic vehicle, offering an excellent price/roominess/equipment ratio, although penalized by a slow infotainment system and intrusive advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS).

Strengths 5

  • Only affordable 100% electric station wagon on the European market
  • Very practical trunk volume (479 to 1367 liters)
  • Reassuring 7-year or 150,000 km manufacturer warranty
  • Extremely durable LFP battery (Standard Range)
  • V2L (Vehicle-to-Load) function allowing external electrical devices to be powered

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of a diesel offering (for heavy combustion-engine drivers)
  • Disappointing DC fast charging speed (87 kW max), limiting long trips
  • Slow infotainment system and room for improvement in menu ergonomics
  • Often intrusive and harsh advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS)
  • Average soundproofing on the highway

Check before buying

  • Software update history (crucial for system stability)
  • Condition of the underbody and battery protection pan (EV models)
  • Smooth operation of the front charging flap
  • Behavior of the DCT gearbox when cold and at low speeds (on imported gasoline versions)
  • Front tire wear (the immediate torque of the electric motor tends to wear out tires quickly)

Alternatives

Competing alternatives

**In Europe (Electric Station Wagons / Compacts):** - **Peugeot e-308 SW:** Better finished, superior road handling, but significantly more expensive. - **Opel Astra Sports Tourer Electric:** Technical cousin of the 308, same qualities and flaws. - **Nissan Leaf:** Compact sedan alternative, similar pricing, but aging battery technology (CHAdeMO).

**On international markets (Combustion-Engine Sedans):** - **Toyota Corolla Sedan:** Absolute benchmark in reliability, available as a hybrid. - **Hyundai Elantra / Kia Cerato:** Excellent price/quality ratio, proven gasoline engines.

Verdict

Final verdict

The MG5 (2022+ facelift) is an eminently rational choice for anyone looking for a practical and economical family vehicle.

**Regarding your search for a diesel:** You have to give up on this engine at MG. If you are a very heavy driver (more than 30,000 km/year on the highway) and diesel is imperative for you, you will have to turn to the used market at Peugeot (308 SW BlueHDi), Volkswagen (Golf SW TDI) or Skoda (Octavia Combi TDI).

**If you are ready to switch to electric:** The MG5 EV is an excellent deal, particularly in the **Standard Range (LFP battery)** version which offers the best long-term durability. It is a vehicle to **BUY** for daily, suburban and family use, provided you accept slightly temperamental onboard technology (screens, driver assistance systems) and longer-than-average highway charging times.
