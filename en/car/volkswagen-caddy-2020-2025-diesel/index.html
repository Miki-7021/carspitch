Summary

The fifth-generation Volkswagen Caddy (type SB), launched in 2020, marks a major evolution by adopting the Volkswagen Group's modular MQB Evo platform, shared with models like the Golf 8. This transition brings significant improvements in terms of onboard technologies, safety, driving comfort, and efficiency. Available in commercial (Cargo) and family (Maxi, California) versions, the Caddy V positions itself as a versatile, modern, and connected vehicle. It stands out with a more dynamic design, a completely redesigned interior with digital screens, and improved ergonomics. Overall, the Caddy V is a well-designed vehicle, but like any new platform, the first few years may have experienced some software adjustments and specific points of vigilance for modern powertrains.

Strengths 6

  • Versatility (commercial and family)
  • Driving comfort and sound insulation (MQB Evo platform)
  • Modern onboard technologies (infotainment, driving aids)
  • Build quality and interior finish
  • Economical and torquey diesel engines (2.0 TDI)
  • Large loading volume and modularity

Weaknesses 5

  • High purchase price
  • Some early software bugs (infotainment, driving aids)
  • Sensitivity of diesel engines (DPF, AdBlue) to short journeys
  • Potentially higher maintenance cost than competitors for certain parts
  • Rear suspension sometimes firm when unladen (depending on version)

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for DPF/AdBlue)
  • Functionality of infotainment and driving aids (updates performed)
  • Absence of illuminated engine or AdBlue warning lights
  • DPF status (for diesel and gasoline): check recent regenerations
  • Abnormal noises from the DSG gearbox (DQ381) and proof of oil change every 60,000 km
  • Tire and brake wear (vehicle often used under load)
  • General condition of the bodywork and interior (professional use)

Official recalls

Official Recalls (Volkswagen Caddy V - SB)

  • 2020-2021: Subject: Software issues affecting the infotainment system and/or driving assistance systems (Front Assist, Lane Assist). Affected population: Early production models. Action: Software update at dealership.
  • 2021: Subject: Potential malfunction of the eCall emergency call system. Affected population: Certain vehicles produced over a specific period. Action: Software update.
  • 2021-2022: Subject: Risk of loosening of the rear anti-roll bar bracket fixing screws. Affected population: A limited number of vehicles. Action: Inspection and tightening/replacement of screws.
  • 2022: Subject: Rear bench seatback fastening issue (family version). Affected population: Certain Caddy Life/Maxi. Action: Inspection and reinforcement of the fastening.

*Note: This list is not exhaustive and is based on public recalls. It is imperative to check with a Volkswagen dealership whether the targeted vehicle has been subject to all recalls and if corrective actions have been performed.*

Verdict

The Volkswagen Caddy V is an excellent vehicle, modern, comfortable, and very versatile. Its transition to the MQB Evo platform gives it first-class road qualities and technological features. However, its price reflects this. For diesel engines (2.0 TDI), which are the core of the range, reliability is generally good, but requires rigorous maintenance and appropriate use. Avoid diesels if your use is predominantly urban and consists of short journeys, at the risk of encountering problems with the DPF and AdBlue. In this case, the 1.5 TSI is a better option. The 1.5 TGI is interesting for professionals or individuals with easy access to CNG.

Buying advice: Prioritize a model with a clear service history, ideally from Volkswagen, and ensure that all software updates and recalls have been carried out. Post-2021 versions are generally more stable in terms of software. The Caddy V is a safe investment if these conditions are met, offering an excellent compromise between a commercial and a family vehicle.
