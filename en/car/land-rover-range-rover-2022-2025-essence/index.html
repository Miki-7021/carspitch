Summary

The fifth-generation Range Rover (codename L460) represents the pinnacle of British off-road luxury. Based on the new MLA-Flex (Modular Longitudinal Architecture) platform, it offers a 50% increase in rigidity and exceptional soundproofing thanks to an active noise cancellation system. This generation marks a major turning point under the hood: the abandonment of the aging in-house supercharged 5.0L V8 in favor of a more efficient and higher-performing 4.4L biturbo V8 sourced from BMW (N63/S68). The model also emphasizes electrification with MHEV (48V mild hybrid) powertrains and PHEV (plug-in hybrid) versions offering up to 113 km of theoretical electric range. Although the build quality and comfort are stratospheric, the technological complexity (standard rear-wheel steering, predictive air suspension, EVA 2.0 electronic architecture) makes it vulnerable to software bugs and teething problems in the early model years.

Strengths 5

  • Unrivaled ride comfort (predictive air suspension and rear-wheel steering)
  • Rolls-Royce level soundproofing (active noise cancellation in the headrests)
  • Significantly improved material quality and interior finish
  • Performance and drivability of the BMW-sourced V8
  • Real-world electric range of the PHEV versions (38.2 kWh battery)

Weaknesses 5

  • Hit-or-miss electronic reliability on early model years (2022-2023)
  • Pachydermic weight (up to 2,810 kg unladen for the PHEVs)
  • Steep depreciation in the first few years
  • Prohibitive maintenance and insurance costs
  • Very imposing size (5.05m in SWB, 5.25m in LWB) complicating parking

Check before buying

  • Complete recall history: the vehicle has been subject to numerous safety campaigns (see recalls section).
  • Operation of the flush door handles (deployment sometimes temperamental in cold weather).
  • Pivi Pro infotainment system: test responsiveness, 3D surround cameras, and the absence of black screens.
  • Air suspension: verify that it raises and lowers without suspicious compressor noise and does not sag when parked.
  • Body panel alignment (a recall addresses a structural bonding defect on certain 2024 models).

Verdict

The Range Rover L460 is arguably the most accomplished luxury SUV of its generation in terms of comfort, refinement, and off-road capabilities. The switch to the BMW V8 (P530) is excellent news for drivability and efficiency compared to the previous generation. However, the impressive list of official recalls (affecting the engine, electronics, and even the structure) on the 2022 to 2024 model years dictates the utmost caution. It is imperative to buy a model with a crystal-clear maintenance history and on which all manufacturer fixes have been applied. An extended warranty is highly recommended. Favor a model from late 2023 / 2024 onwards, where the main teething problems were corrected at the factory.
