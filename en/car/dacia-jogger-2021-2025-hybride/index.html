Summary

The Dacia Jogger, launched in 2021, is a versatile family vehicle positioned at the crossroads of a raised wagon, an MPV, and a compact SUV. It replaces both the Lodgy and Dokker in the Dacia range, offering 5 or 7 seats. Based on the Renault group's CMF-B platform (shared with the Clio V and Captur II), it benefits from a modern architecture. Its robust design and passenger capacity make it a pragmatic choice for families. This first generation has not yet undergone a major facelift. Overall, the Jogger is praised for its unbeatable price/performance ratio and its modularity, perfectly embodying Dacia's philosophy of doing the essentials well.

Strengths 7

  • Unbeatable price/performance ratio on the market
  • Exemplary modularity and spaciousness (real 5 or 7 seats)
  • Generous boot volume, even in 7-seater configuration
  • Suspension comfort adapted to degraded roads
  • Efficient and proven petrol and hybrid powertrains
  • Reasonable maintenance and insurance costs
  • General robustness and ease of use

Weaknesses 5

  • Simple interior materials, though robust and easy to maintain
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds, especially on motorways
  • Modest performance with basic petrol engines under full load
  • Divisive design for some, less consensual than a classic SUV
  • Fewer comfort and driving assistance features than more expensive competitors

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices, especially for LPG and Hybrid versions
  • Operation of the dog-clutch gearbox on the hybrid (smoothness of transitions, absence of jerks)
  • Condition of tires and braking system (specific wear on hybrid)
  • Presence of parasitic noises in the cabin (assembly, plastics)
  • Operation of all electrical and electronic equipment (multimedia screen, air conditioning)
  • Verify that all manufacturer recalls have been carried out

Verdict

The Dacia Jogger is an excellent choice for families or those looking for a spacious, practical, and economical vehicle, without sacrificing reliability. Its general robustness is in line with Dacia's reputation, and its powertrains are generally reliable.

* **For predominantly urban and suburban use**, the **1.6 E-Tech Hybrid 140** version is the most relevant choice. Its fuel consumption is exemplary in the city, and its driving pleasure in electric mode is a real plus. It is the most accomplished and most reliable powertrain in the long term thanks to its proven atmospheric engine and a hybrid system that has proven itself on other Renault models. * **For high-mileage drivers or those with access to LPG**, the **1.0 TCe 100 ECO-G** offers unbeatable running costs, provided that the specific maintenance of the LPG system is diligently followed. * **For versatile use with regular motorway journeys**, the **1.0 TCe 110** is an excellent compromise, offering sufficient performance and controlled fuel consumption.

Whatever the choice, prioritize a model with a clear service history and ensure that all manufacturer recalls have been carried out. The Jogger is a safe bet on the used car market, offering an excellent quality/price/reliability ratio.
