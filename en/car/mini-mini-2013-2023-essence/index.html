MINI Mini

III (F55/F56) · 2013 – 2023

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

Summary

The third-generation MINI (F56 for the 3-door, F55 for the 5-door) marks a major turning point in the brand's history. Based on BMW's modular UKL1 platform, it abandons the notorious 'Prince' engines (developed with PSA) in favor of BMW's modular B-series blocks (B38 3-cylinder, B48 4-cylinder). This change transformed the model's reliability, elevating the MINI from a 'problem car' to one of the most reliable premium city cars on the market. It underwent two facelifts (LCI in 2018 with the arrival of 'Union Jack' lights and DKG dual-clutch gearboxes, then LCI 2 in 2021 with a streamlined front end). Although the focus is on petrol, the range included diesel until 2019 and introduced a 100% electric version (Cooper SE) in 2020.

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability transformed compared to the R56 generation
  • Very robust and powerful BMW B-series engines (B38/B48)
  • Significantly improved interior finish quality (materials, assembly)
  • Driving behavior still 'go-kart' like, very fun
  • Responsive and ergonomic infotainment system (BMW iDrive)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very firm suspension comfort (especially with 17/18-inch wheels and sport chassis)
  • Ridiculous rear passenger space and boot volume (211L) on the 3-door (F56)
  • Chronic wear of the upper right engine mount (known issue)
  • Very high used purchase price and cost of options
  • Average peripheral visibility

Check before buying

  • Condition of the upper right engine mount (passenger side): traces of black fluid or torn rubber.
  • On 2014-2015 manual B38 models: check if the crankshaft recall has been performed (suspicious clutch noise).
  • Maintenance history: prefer models with oil changes every 15,000 km / 1 year.
  • On diesels: check that the EGR cooler recall has been performed at a BMW/MINI dealership.
  • Behavior of the DKG7 gearbox (after 2018): absence of violent jerks during maneuvers.

It consistently ranks in the Top 15 most reliable vehicles in its category according to the TÜV Report, with the 2.0L petrol engine (B48) achieving a score of 88/100.

Performance

  • Unrivalled 'Go-Kart' Driving Pleasure — True to its heritage, the MINI F55/F56 offers an exceptionally dynamic and fun driving experience. Its precise steering and agility provide unique sensations, often compared to a go-kart, for an engaging journey every time.
  • Agile and Precise Road Holding — Its sharp chassis and direct steering give the MINI exceptional road holding. It proves to be very agile, with minimal body roll, offering responsiveness and precision that boost driver confidence in all situations.

Comfort

  • Premium Interior Quality and Technology — The MINI III's cabin benefits from a significant upgrade in terms of material quality and meticulous assembly. The infotainment system, based on BMW's iDrive interface, is responsive, ergonomic, and contributes to a decidedly premium atmosphere.

Safety

  • Optimal Safety for All Occupants — The MINI III ensures robust protection for its occupants. It achieved 4 stars in Euro NCAP tests, with scores of 79% for adult protection and 73% for child protection, guaranteeing peace of mind on the road.
  • Powerful and Responsive Braking — Renowned for its excellent braking system, the MINI F56 offers powerful and responsive brakes, ensuring short stopping distances and enhanced safety. The Cooper S version, for example, comes to a halt in just 46.6 meters from 112 km/h (70 mph).

Verdict

The MINI F55/F56 is an excellent used car purchase. The adoption of BMW engines has definitively erased the reliability nightmares of the previous generation (THP/Prince engines). The B38 and B48 petrol engines are among the most reliable in their category. For a worry-free purchase, opt for a post-2015 petrol model (to avoid the crankshaft issue of early manual models). The only recurring weak point is the upper engine mount, a low-cost repair if anticipated. The Cooper (136 hp) represents the best compromise, while the Cooper S (192 hp) will delight sports enthusiasts with impeccable reliability.
