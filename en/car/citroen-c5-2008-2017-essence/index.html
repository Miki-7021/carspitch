Summary

The second-generation Citroën C5 (RD codes for the sedan, TD for the Tourer estate) was launched in 2008, marking a turning point for the chevron brand. Abandoning the bold design of its predecessor, it adopted a more stately and classic line, aiming to compete with German D-segment sedans. Its positioning is that of a comfortable and elegant grand tourer.

It stands out for its exceptional ride comfort, particularly thanks to the Hydractive III+ suspension (available on high trim levels and certain engines), which offers controlled damping and a constant ride height. A version with classic metallic suspension was also offered on entry-level trims.

A minor facelift occurred in 2010 (LED headlights, new engines) and another in 2012 (new wheels, equipment). The C5 II was praised for its significantly improved finish quality and soundproofing. However, its high weight and imposing size can make it less agile than some competitors. General reliability is respectable, but certain engines, particularly petrol (THP) and some diesels, require particular vigilance.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional suspension comfort (Hydractive III+)
  • High level of finish quality and soundproofing
  • Stately and elegant design (sedan and Tourer)
  • Generous interior space and large boot (Tourer)
  • Good road holding and sense of security
  • Generally reliable and powerful 2.0 HDi Diesel engines

Weaknesses 6

  • High weight penalizing performance and consumption
  • Limited rear visibility on the sedan
  • 1.6 THP (EP6CDT) engine prone to major reliability issues
  • Potentially high maintenance cost for V6 and Hydractive versions
  • Some minor electronic issues possible
  • Lack of agility compared to some competitors

Check before buying

  • 1.6 THP Engine: Complete service history, invoices for timing chain, HP pump, turbo replacement. Listen for abnormal noises on cold start.
  • Hydractive III+ Suspension: Check for leaks, proper functioning of modes (Sport/Comfort), responsiveness of raising/lowering. Ask for the date of the last sphere replacement.
  • Diesel Engines: Check proper functioning of the DPF (no warning light, no excessive smoke), and the EGR valve. Listen for the dual-mass flywheel (noise on start/stop, jerks).
  • Automatic Gearbox: Check for smooth gear changes, absence of jerks. Ask if the fluid change has been performed (not recommended by Citroën but advised every 60,000 km).
  • Electronics: Test all equipment (GPS, air conditioning, cruise control, power windows, mirrors).
  • General Wear: Condition of tires, brakes, discs, shock absorbers (if classic suspension).
  • Bodywork: Check for corrosion (rare), panel alignment, headlight condition (fogging).

Official recalls

Official recalls for the Citroën C5 II primarily concerned safety elements or software updates. Here are some notable examples:

  • 2008-2009 (early production models): Potential issue with steering column or brake pedal attachment. Inspection and reinforcement if necessary.
  • 2010-2011 (models equipped with 1.6 THP): Software update for engine management to correct timing and high-pressure pump issues. Inspection and replacement of the timing chain tensioner if necessary.
  • 2012-2013: Risk of non-compliance for certain front or side airbags. Inspection and replacement of affected modules.
  • 2014-2015: Potential fuel leak issue at the fuel filter on some diesel models. Inspection and replacement of the filter or connection.
  • 2015-2016 (BlueHDi models): NOx sensor or AdBlue system issue potentially leading to a malfunction of the emissions control system. Software update or component replacement.

It is always recommended to check with a Citroën dealership, using the vehicle's VIN, to ensure all recalls have been performed.

What sets it apart 6

Comfort

  • Exceptional Ride Comfort — Enjoy an unparalleled driving experience thanks to the Hydractive III+ suspension (depending on version) which absorbs all road imperfections, combined with high-level soundproofing for absolute serenity.

Safety

  • Advanced Active and Passive Safety — The Citroën C5 II achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 82% for child protection, ensuring peace of mind for all occupants.
  • Effective and Stable Braking — Benefit from a powerful and reassuring braking system, ensuring effective deceleration and remarkable stability even during hard braking, for total control in all circumstances.
  • Serene and Precise Road Holding — The C5 II offers road holding focused on stability and comfort, with predictable and secure handling. Its precise steering contributes to a relaxed drive on all types of roads.

qualite

  • Statutory Design and Refined Finish — Its elegant and timeless design gives the C5 II a stately presence on the road. The interior benefits from a high level of finish quality, with pleasant-to-touch materials and rigorous assembly.

Performance

  • Pleasant and Proven Petrol Engines — The petrol engines, particularly the naturally aspirated 1.8i, 2.0i, and the rare 3.0 V6 versions, offer smooth and reliable driving, ideal for long journeys and a definite driving pleasure.

Verdict

The Citroën C5 II is an excellent road car, offering first-class comfort and a very respectable finish quality for its segment. It's a car that invites travel and serenity.

  • BUY without hesitation: Versions equipped with 2.0 HDi engines (140, 160, 150, 180 hp) are the most recommendable. They offer an excellent compromise between performance, consumption, and reliability. The 1.6 HDi 115 hp is also a good choice for less intensive use, provided rigorous maintenance.
  • NEGOTIATE strongly and with caution: 1.6 THP 155 hp versions should be approached with the utmost suspicion. A complete service history and proof of replacement of problematic parts (chain, HP pump) are imperative. The 1.6 THP 165 hp is slightly better but remains a gamble. The V6 Diesel (2.7 and 3.0 HDi) offer exceptional driving pleasure but can lead to very high maintenance costs in case of breakdown.
  • AVOID: 1.6 THP 155 hp without a clear history or with signs of weakness. The naturally aspirated 1.8i and 2.0i are reliable but lack vigor for the car's weight, making the on-road experience less enjoyable.

The Hydractive III+ suspension is a major asset for comfort but represents a specific maintenance item not to be overlooked. A well-maintained C5 II, especially with the 2.0 HDi, is an excellent deal on the used car market, offering luxury and comfort often superior to its competitors for an affordable price.
