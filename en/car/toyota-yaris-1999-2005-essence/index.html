Summary

The Toyota Yaris I (XP10), also known as the Vitz in Japan and Echo in North America/Australia, is a landmark vehicle in the supermini (B-segment) class. Winning the European Car of the Year award in 2000, it revolutionized the segment with its 'tall boy' design, offering exceptional interior space within a compact footprint. Key innovations included a central 3D digital instrument cluster and a sliding rear bench seat. A mid-cycle facelift occurred in 2003, introducing 'teardrop' headlights, revised bumpers, and interior material upgrades. Renowned for its virtually indestructible petrol engines, the XP10 remains a highly sought-after used car for its legendary reliability and low running costs. Overall Rating: Excellent.

Strengths 6

  • Legendary mechanical reliability (especially petrol engines)
  • Timing chains on all petrol engines (no belts to replace)
  • Clever interior packaging with a sliding rear bench seat
  • Low running costs, cheap insurance, and excellent fuel economy
  • Excellent visibility and maneuverability in the city
  • Quirky but highly readable 3D digital instrument cluster

Weaknesses 6

  • Cheap, scratch-prone interior plastics
  • Poor sound insulation (noisy at highway speeds)
  • Small boot capacity when the rear seats are pushed all the way back
  • Clear coat peeling on solid red and blue paint colors
  • Digital dashboard can fade or fail entirely over time (capacitor issues)
  • Driving position is a bit high and steering wheel lacks reach adjustment

Check before buying

  • Water Pump: Look for pink/red crusty residue on the right side of the engine (petrol models).
  • Gearbox Synchros: Test 2nd and 3rd gear engagement at high RPMs (especially on 1.5 T-Sport).
  • Digital Dash: Ensure the 3D display is bright and fully functional; replacements are getting scarce.
  • Rust: Check the rear subframe, exhaust system, and lower door sills, especially in cold/salty climates.
  • Headlights: Plastic lenses are highly prone to yellowing/oxidation (requires polishing).
  • Idle Quality: A rough idle on the 1.0L usually means a dirty MAF sensor or throttle body.

Official recalls

  • Brake Master Cylinder (2000-2002): Potential brake fluid leak from the master cylinder seal, leading to reduced braking performance.
  • Airbags (Takata): Some later models (2003-2005) were included in the massive Takata passenger airbag inflator recall. Crucial to verify if this was completed.
  • Rear Wheel Bearings (1999-2000): Early models had a recall for premature wear of the rear wheel hub/bearing assembly.
  • Brake Pipes (Specific regions): Corrosion on rear brake pipes in areas with heavy road salt usage.

Alternatives

Verdict

The Toyota Yaris I (XP10) is arguably one of the most reliable used cars money can buy. If you are looking for a cheap, dependable, and practical city car, it is an absolute must-buy. The petrol engines are virtually indestructible if given basic maintenance. The 1.3 VVT-i is the sweet spot, offering enough power for the highway while maintaining excellent city economy. The 1.0L is great for pure city use, and the 1.5L T-Sport is a fantastic, reliable warm hatch. Avoid the diesel unless you do significant mileage, as the petrols are so efficient and less prone to age-related issues. Buy on condition, check for rust, and you will have a car that outlasts much newer vehicles.
