Audi e-tron GT

I facelift (F8) · 2024 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 74/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation Audi e-tron GT (code F8) received a major facelift in 2024. Based on the J1 platform developed jointly with Porsche (Taycan), this update focuses on technical optimization rather than aesthetics. The lineup has been redesigned with the introduction of the S e-tron GT, RS e-tron GT, and the extreme RS e-tron GT performance versions. Major improvements include a new, lighter battery with an increased net capacity of 97 kWh (105 kWh gross), fast charging power increased to 320 kW (800V architecture), and lighter, more powerful rear electric motors. Although spectacular and technologically advanced, its reliability is marred by critical recalls concerning the high-voltage battery modules and the air suspension.

Strengths 5

  • 800V architecture allowing ultra-fast charging (10 to 80% in 18 minutes)
  • Supercar-level dynamic performance and acceleration
  • Exceptional road handling (very low center of gravity, J1 platform)
  • Top-flight interior finish quality and soundproofing
  • Spectacular exterior design preserved during the facelift

Weaknesses 5

  • History of very heavy recalls (battery fire risks, suspension, brakes)
  • Rear passenger space and trunk volume (405 L) limited for its size (4.99 m)
  • Very high weight (around 2.3 tons) impacting consumable wear
  • Infotainment system ergonomics sometimes complex and software bugs
  • Steep depreciation on the used market

Check before buying

  • Recall history: Demand the manufacturer's certificate for the replacement/inspection of the high-voltage battery modules.
  • Air suspension: Verify that the vehicle maintains its ride height after prolonged parking (no asymmetrical sagging).
  • Front brake hoses: Inspect for tears or brake fluid leaks (known defect).
  • On-board charging cable: Check its condition (overheating risk, subject to recall).
  • Rearview camera and MMI: Test the proper display of the camera when shifting into reverse (frequent software bugs).

Verdict

The facelifted Audi e-tron GT (2024+) is an engineering marvel offering breathtaking performance and some of the best charging capabilities on the market thanks to its 800V architecture. However, the reliability record is heavily burdened by major industrial issues shared with the Porsche Taycan. The multiple recalls concerning short-circuit and fire risks of the high-voltage battery, as well as failures of the suspension and brake hoses, require absolute vigilance. Purchasing a recent used model or end-of-stock vehicle should **only be considered if all recall campaigns have been certified by an official Audi dealer**. Favor the 'S e-tron GT' version, which is more than sufficient for daily use and offers the best range.
