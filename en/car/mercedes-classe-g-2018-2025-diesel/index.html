Summary

Mercedes-Benz Classe G (W463A)

Launched in 2018, the W463A generation (sometimes unofficially called W464) represents the greatest technical evolution of the Classe G since its birth in 1979. While retaining its iconic cubic design, ladder frame, and three differential locks, it adopts an independent front suspension, rack-and-pinion steering, and an ultra-modern interior (dual screen).

**Positioning:** Absolute luxury off-road vehicle, status-enhancing and exclusive. **Facelift (2024):** Slight aerodynamic tweaks, transition to the MBUX multimedia system, mild hybridization (MHEV) on all combustion engines, and introduction of the 100% electric version (G 580 EQ). **Overall rating:** An engineering masterpiece that achieves the impossible: marrying pure off-road capabilities with ride comfort worthy of an S-Class. The diesel engine (OM656) is undoubtedly the most logical choice for this 2.5-ton behemoth.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional OM656 diesel engine: torquey, smooth, and reliable
  • Intact off-road capabilities (3 locks, low range)
  • Ride comfort and steering precision transformed compared to the previous generation
  • Interior finish quality worthy of an S-Class
  • Almost non-existent depreciation (highly sought-after vehicle on the used market)

Weaknesses 5

  • Aerodynamic wind noise very present above 110 km/h (inherent to the design)
  • Flat windshield extremely vulnerable to stone chips
  • High AdBlue consumption on diesel versions
  • Stratospheric purchase price, insurance, and spare parts costs
  • Colossal weight (2.5 tons) rapidly wearing out consumables (brakes, tires)

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: must be 100% Mercedes network, mechanical tolerances are strict.
  • Underbody: check for the absence of impacts on the ladder frame, axles, and sumps if the vehicle has been off-road.
  • Windshield: meticulously inspect for the presence of micro-impacts or incipient cracks.
  • Door hinges: check that they do not squeak and that there are no signs of incipient rust (require regular maintenance).
  • Emission control system (Diesel): ensure no check engine light is on (capricious NOx sensors or AdBlue injector if used for short trips).

What sets it apart 14

Reliability

  • OM656 Diesel Engine: Unrivaled Power and Reliability — The OM656 inline-six diesel engine is renowned for its exceptional torque, smooth operation, and legendary robustness. It offers a refined driving experience and controlled fuel consumption, perfectly suited to the G-Class's stature.

Comfort

  • S-Class Level Interior Luxury and Transformed Ride Comfort — The interior of the G-Class W463A elevates luxury to a higher level, with a finish quality worthy of an S-Class. Ride comfort has been significantly improved thanks to the new suspension, offering unparalleled serenity on all roads.

Practicality

  • Undisputed Master of Off-Road — True to its heritage, the G-Class retains its outstanding off-road capabilities, thanks to its three differential locks and low-range gearbox. It takes you anywhere with absolute ease and confidence, even on the most demanding terrains.

Safety

  • Exceptional Passive Safety — The Mercedes G-Class W463A ensures first-class passive safety, as evidenced by its excellent Euro NCAP rating. It offers robust protection for adults and children, guaranteeing peace of mind on every journey.

Braking

  • Powerful and Controlled Braking — Despite its imposing weight, the G-Class W463A benefits from a remarkably efficient braking system. Performance is excellent, ensuring short stopping distances and perfect vehicle control, even in emergency situations.

Handling

  • Refined Road Holding and Increased Stability — Thanks to its major technical redesign, including an independent front suspension and rack-and-pinion steering, the G-Class offers much more precise and stable road holding. Roll is reduced, and high-speed stability is exemplary, for a serene drive.

Economy

  • Virtually Non-Existent Depreciation and Secure Investment — The Mercedes G-Class is a highly sought-after vehicle in the used car market, resulting in virtually non-existent depreciation. Acquiring a diesel G-Class is a wise choice that preserves your capital in the long term.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Land Rover Defender (L663) D250/D300 (more modern, cheaper, excellent off-road but less status-enhancing), Range Rover L460 D300/D350 (more luxurious and comfortable, less of an off-roader). - **North America:** Lexus LX 600 (legendary reliability, ladder frame), Cadillac Escalade (more spacious, less off-road oriented). - **Japan / Asia:** Toyota Land Cruiser 300 (the king of reliability, but less luxurious than the G).

Verdict

The Mercedes Classe G W463A is a rolling paradox, but that is what gives it its charm. If you opt for this model, **the diesel engine (G 400 d as a priority, or G 350 d) is by far the smartest choice**. The OM656 engine is a paragon of reliability, offers perfect torque to move this mass, and allows for decent range. However, avoid this diesel if you only drive in the city (risk of DPF/EGR clogging). The V8 versions (G 500 / G 63) are exhilarating but impose outrageous running costs. A passion purchase, but one that, thanks to its very low depreciation, proves to be less financially risky than it seems if maintenance is rigorous.
