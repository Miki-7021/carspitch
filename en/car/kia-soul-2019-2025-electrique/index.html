Summary

The third-generation Kia Soul (SK3), launched in 2019, continued its tradition as a distinctive, boxy compact crossover, but with a significant shift towards electrification in many key markets. While retaining its unique styling, the SK3 generation brought a more refined interior, improved technology, and a focus on its electric variant, the e-Soul. In Europe, it was exclusively offered as an EV, while North America initially retained internal combustion engine (ICE) options before largely transitioning to EV-only for later model years. It received a minor facelift in 2022, updating styling and technology features. Overall, the Soul III is a practical, characterful, and increasingly eco-conscious choice in the compact segment.

Strengths 5

  • Distinctive and practical design with good interior space for its footprint.
  • Excellent EV range and performance (64 kWh version).
  • User-friendly infotainment system and modern technology features.
  • Strong warranty coverage (especially for EV components).
  • Comfortable ride quality.

Weaknesses 5

  • Rear visibility can be limited due to the design.
  • ICE models can feel underpowered with the 2.0L MPI, especially when loaded.
  • DC fast charging speeds for the e-Soul are not class-leading compared to newer EVs.
  • Some interior plastics can feel hard, though build quality is good.
  • Limited availability of ICE models in later years/certain markets.

Check before buying

  • **EV Battery Health:** Request a battery health report (SOH) for used e-Soul models.
  • **Charging System:** Inspect the charging port for damage and test both AC and DC charging.
  • **12V Battery:** Ensure the 12V battery is in good condition, as it's critical for starting the EV's systems.
  • **IVT/DCT Transmission:** For ICE models, check for smooth operation, no jerking or slipping.
  • **Engine Oil Levels/History:** For ICE models, verify regular oil changes and check for any signs of excessive oil consumption.
  • **Suspension Components:** Listen for any clunks or squeaks, especially over bumps.
  • **Infotainment System:** Test all functions, including Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, and ensure software is up to date.

Verdict

The Kia Soul III (SK3) is a compelling choice, particularly in its electric e-Soul guise. For those in markets where the EV is the primary or sole offering, it represents a highly practical, stylish, and reliable electric vehicle with excellent range (especially the 64 kWh version) and a strong warranty. It's an ideal daily driver for city and suburban life, capable of longer trips.

For the ICE versions, the 2.0L MPI is a solid, if unexciting, choice for budget-conscious buyers prioritizing simplicity and fuel economy, provided the IVT is well-maintained. The 1.6L T-GDI offers more spirited performance but comes with the typical GDI and DCT maintenance considerations.

**Verdict:** **BUY** the e-Soul, especially the Long Range variant, if you're looking for a reliable, practical, and characterful EV. **NÉGOCIER** for ICE models, particularly the 1.6L T-GDI, ensuring thorough inspection of the engine and DCT. The 2.0L MPI is a safe **BUY** for basic transport. Ensure all recalls, especially the EV battery recall, have been addressed.
