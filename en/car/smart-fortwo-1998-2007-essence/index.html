Summary

The **first-generation Smart Fortwo (W450)**, initially marketed under the name *City-Coupé* until 2004, is a revolutionary micro-city car born from a partnership between Swatch and Mercedes-Benz. Designed exclusively for the urban environment with its unbeatable length of 2.50 m, it stands out with its visible safety cell (Tridion) and interchangeable plastic body panels (Bodypanels).

It received a major facelift in 2003 (Phase 2), marking the abandonment of the fragile 0.6L engine in favor of a slightly more robust 0.7L, and the introduction of ESP as standard (replacing the basic Trust+ system).

**Overall reliability rating:** Very problematic on petrol versions. Official statistics (TÜV, ADAC, What Car?) regularly rank the W450 among the least reliable vehicles of its decade, mainly due to the extreme fragility of its petrol engines (frequent rebuilds before 80,000 km) and its temperamental electronics.
