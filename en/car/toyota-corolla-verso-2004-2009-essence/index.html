Toyota Corolla Verso

II (AR10) · 2004 – 2009

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The second-generation Toyota Corolla Verso (code AR10) is a compact MPV designed specifically for the European market and assembled in Turkey. Replacing a first generation with mixed success, it established itself thanks to its 'Toyota Easy Flat' modularity system, allowing the rear seats (5 or 7 seats) to be folded flat into the floor with one hand. Restyled in 2007 (new bumpers, lights, and equipment), it shines with the exceptional reliability of its petrol engines, which contrasts sharply with the misfortunes of its diesel versions (especially the 2.2 blocks). It is a rational, robust family vehicle, highly sought after on the used market in its unleaded variants.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of petrol engines (1.6 and 1.8 VVT-i)
  • Exemplary modularity (very practical Easy Flat 7-seat system)
  • Generous interior space and roominess for its size (4.36 m)
  • Good aging of upholstery and bodywork
  • Sound and safe road handling

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic MMT automated manual gearbox (zero driving pleasure and costly breakdowns)
  • Disastrous reliability of 2.2 diesel engines (D-4D and D-CAT)
  • Hard interior plastics, very prone to scratches
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (aerodynamic and road noise)
  • Large turning radius

Check before buying

  • On petrol versions: smooth operation of the manual gearbox and no leakage around the water pump.
  • On the 1.8 MMT: demand a test drive. Any jerkiness on startup or excessive slipping indicates a faulty clutch or actuator (bill > €1500).
  • On 2.2 diesels: whitish traces around the expansion tank (sign of overpressure and faulty cylinder head gasket).
  • Wear of front tires (the front axle is heavy, especially in diesel versions).
  • Verify that recalls concerning Takata airbags have been carried out (proof on invoice or Toyota portal).

Official recalls

  • - **Airbags (2015-2019)**: Massive recall (Takata scandal) for the replacement of the front passenger and/or driver airbag inflator, risk of metallic fragment projection.
  • - **Steering Column (2006)**: Replacement of the intermediate steering shaft on certain models produced between 2004 and 2006 (risk of loss of control).
  • - **Clock Spring (2014)**: Airbag spiral cable in the steering wheel may break, deactivating the driver's airbag (warning light on).
  • - **Accelerator Pedal (2010)**: Risk of a hard spot or slow return of the pedal (worldwide recall, although the Verso was less affected than other models).

Verdict

The Toyota Corolla Verso II is a vehicle with two faces. If you opt for a petrol engine (1.6 or 1.8 VVT-i) paired with a manual gearbox, it's a 'no-brainer' purchase: you'll benefit from an indestructible, practical, and economical-to-maintain family MPV. However, the MMT automated manual gearbox is absolutely to be avoided due to its deplorable driving experience and fragility. As for the diesel versions, particularly the 2.2 D-4D and D-CAT, they concentrate the worst flaws of the modern diesel era (cylinder head, EGR, DPF) and should be avoided, unless you have formal proof of engine replacement by Toyota.
