Summary

The Alfa Romeo Giulia (Generation I, Type 952) marks the great return of the cloverleaf brand to rear-wheel drive and characterful sedans. Based on the excellent Giorgio platform, it was designed with an ideal weight distribution (50/50) and a standard carbon fiber driveshaft. The 2016-2020 period covers the launch up to the interior redesign (MY2020) which corrected many finish defects and updated the infotainment. While its road behavior is unanimously praised as the category benchmark, the first years of production were marred by electronic bugs and teething problems. The 2.2 JTDm diesel engine (Alfa Romeo's first 100% aluminum diesel engine) accounted for the bulk of sales in Europe and proves to be mechanically very robust, although it requires appropriate use to avoid clogging.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional chassis (Giorgio platform) offering the best dynamism in its category
  • Ultra-direct, precise, and communicative steering
  • Perfectly calibrated ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox
  • Very fuel-efficient 2.2 diesel engines on roads and highways
  • Timeless and distinctive design

Weaknesses 5

  • Very capricious electronics (especially on 2016-2018 model years)
  • Assembly quality and some interior plastics below German standards (before MY2020)
  • Dated, slow infotainment system, and screen prone to internal micro-cracks (delamination)
  • Sparse dealer network and sometimes inconsistent after-sales service
  • Parasitic noises in the cabin over time

Check before buying

  • Battery and IBS sensor condition: a weak battery causes 80% of dashboard alerts (Start&Stop unavailable, DNA modes blocked).
  • Software update history: crucial for engine management and infotainment.
  • Traces of coolant leaks: check the EGR cooler on diesels and turbo hoses on petrol engines.
  • Front tire wear: the aggressive front axle geometry (Ackermann steering geometry) causes scrubbing at low speeds and asymmetrical wear.
  • Central screen operation: check for cracks or bubbles under the glass (Magneti Marelli manufacturing defect).

Official recalls

  • 2019 (Fuel Gauge): Erroneous fuel level indication potentially leading to running out of fuel (BCM software update).
  • 2018 (Braking): Risk of brake fluid leak due to a hose rubbing against a clamp in the engine compartment.
  • 2018 (Adaptive Cruise Control): In rare conditions, the adaptive cruise control (ACC) might not deactivate when the brake pedal was pressed (software update).
  • 2017 (Water Ingress): Sealing defect at the windshield cowl leading to water ingress into the body control module (BCM), causing multiple electrical failures.

What sets it apart 7

Performance

  • Unrivaled Driving Dynamics — Thanks to its Giorgio platform, ideal weight distribution (50/50), and ultra-precise steering, the Giulia offers a sporty and engaging driving experience with exceptional road holding.

Safety

  • High-Level Active and Passive Safety — The Giulia achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult and child protection, ensuring peace of mind on the road.
  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — Equipped with a very high-performance braking system, the Giulia ensures some of the best stopping distances in its category, with remarkable endurance even under intensive use, often enhanced by Brembo calipers.

economy

  • Remarkable Fuel Efficiency — The 2.2 JTDm diesel engines stand out for their exemplary fuel economy on roads and highways, offering optimized fuel consumption for long journeys.

Comfort

  • ZF 8-Speed Automatic Transmission: Smoothness and Responsiveness — The ZF 8-speed automatic transmission is perfectly calibrated, offering fluid and fast gear changes, contributing to an exceptional driving pleasure and optimal comfort.

Reliability

  • 2.2 JTDm Diesel Engine: Robustness and Durability — The 2.2 JTDm diesel engine, Alfa Romeo's first 100% aluminum block, is renowned for its robust mechanical design, including a solid timing chain, ensuring long-term reliability.

Practicality

  • Timeless and Distinctive Italian Design — With its elegant and sporty lines, the Giulia boasts a design that stands the test of time, asserting a unique and recognizable presence on the road.

Alternatives

  • Europe: BMW 3 Series (F30 / G20) for technological rigor, Audi A4 (B9) for finish and traction/Quattro, Peugeot 508 II for style and comfort/handling compromise.
  • North America: Lexus IS (for absolute reliability), Genesis G70 (excellent value for money/equipment/dynamism), Cadillac CT4.
  • Asia / Emerging Markets: Mercedes-Benz C-Class (W205), Lexus IS.

Verdict

The Alfa Romeo Giulia is an engineer's car, designed primarily for driving pleasure. The 2.2 JTDm unit is mechanically very robust (aluminum block, solid timing chain, excellent ZF gearbox), but it suffers from the typical ailments of modern diesels if used in the city (EGR/DPF clogging). 2016-2017 models experienced teething problems with numerous electronic bugs often linked to imperfect battery management. Verdict: NEGOTIATE. Prioritize a model from late 2018, or ideally a MY2020, with a clear service history. Avoid diesels that have been primarily used in the city.
