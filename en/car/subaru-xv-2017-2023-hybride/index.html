Subaru XV

II (GT) · 2017 – 2023

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation Subaru XV (code GT), also known as the Crosstrek in North America, marks a major turning point for the brand thanks to the adoption of the new Subaru Global Platform (SGP). This architecture significantly improves torsional rigidity (+70%), comfort, and passive safety. Faithful to Subaru's DNA, it retains its permanent all-wheel drive (Symmetrical AWD) and an excellent ground clearance of 22 cm, making it formidable on snow and rutted tracks.

On the European market, this generation introduced the **e-Boxer** engine, a mild/medium hybrid system coupling the 2.0L flat-4 engine to a small electric motor integrated into the Lineartronic CVT gearbox. Although this technology slightly reduces CO2 emissions and offers short 100% electric driving at very low speeds, it does not rival the efficiency of Toyota's hybrid systems. The XV GT remains a niche vehicle, extremely reliable mechanically, ideal for mountain dwellers and rural drivers, but penalized by a combined fuel consumption that remains high for a hybrid vehicle.

Strengths 6

  • Formidably efficient Symmetrical AWD permanent all-wheel drive
  • Exceptional ground clearance (22 cm) worthy of a true off-roader
  • Top-tier active and passive safety (highly effective standard EyeSight system)
  • Excellent overall mechanical reliability (new generation Boxer engines)
  • Rigid chassis offering very good suspension comfort and sound handling
  • Excellent peripheral visibility (thin pillars, large glass areas)

Weaknesses 6

  • Disappointing fuel consumption on the e-Boxer (the hybrid system does not work miracles)
  • Very limited trunk volume (340 liters on the e-Boxer due to the batteries)
  • Modest performance (especially on the 1.6i and the 2.0 e-Boxer during acceleration)
  • Lineartronic CVT gearbox that tends to make the engine "drone" under hard acceleration
  • Starlink infotainment system sometimes slow and prone to bugs
  • Sparse dealership network in Europe (particularly in France)

Check before buying

  • CVT gearbox maintenance history (oil change every 60,000 km / 4 years highly recommended)
  • Condition of the windshield (the slightest chip in the field of the EyeSight cameras requires replacement and recalibration)
  • Wheel bearings (frequent premature wear on Subarus, listen for humming at 80-100 km/h)
  • Smooth operation of the electric/thermal transition on the e-Boxer (no major jerks)
  • Tire wear: must be perfectly symmetrical (all 4 tires must be changed at the same time to preserve the AWD differential)
  • Touchscreen bugs (test Bluetooth, navigation, and general responsiveness)

Handling

  • Exceptional Control and Grip — Enjoy dynamic and secure handling, enhanced by the Symmetrical AWD system and a low center of gravity, ensuring remarkable stability and grip in all conditions.

Braking

  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — The braking system is designed for efficiency and stability, integrating ABS, EBD, and emergency brake assist for short stopping distances and controlled deceleration.

Reliability

  • Proven Mechanical Reliability — The Subaru XV e-Boxer is renowned for its excellent overall mechanical reliability, particularly thanks to its new-generation Boxer engines, ensuring lasting peace of mind.

Practicality

  • Unrivaled All-Terrain Capabilities — With its formidable Symmetrical AWD and an exceptional ground clearance of 22 cm, the XV is ready to tackle the most demanding terrains with ease.

Comfort

  • Suspension Comfort and Optimal Visibility — Its rigid chassis offers very good suspension comfort, complemented by excellent peripheral visibility for a pleasant and serene drive.

Performance

  • Smoothness and Responsiveness of the e-Boxer Powertrain — The e-Boxer powertrain provides noticeable operational smoothness and responsive low-end torque thanks to electric assistance, for fluid driving in urban and rural areas.

Alternatives

Europe * **Toyota C-HR (Hybrid)**: Much more fuel-efficient, polarizing design, but no true mechanical all-wheel drive. * **Mazda CX-30 (AWD)**: Superior interior finish, reliable naturally aspirated engines, all-wheel drive available but less capable off-road. * **Dacia Duster (4x4)**: Budget alternative, excellent off-road capabilities, but road performance and safety (EyeSight) are vastly inferior.

North America * **Honda HR-V (AWD)**: More spacious inside, very reliable, but basic AWD capabilities. * **Subaru Forester**: For those who find the Crosstrek's trunk too small, offers the same mechanics in a more family-friendly format. * **Jeep Renegade (Trailhawk)**: True off-road competitor, but overall reliability (electronics, automatic gearbox) significantly lower than Subaru.

Verdict

The second-generation Subaru XV (Crosstrek) is a sensible choice for drivers living in mountainous, snowy, or rural regions. Its mechanical reliability is excellent, surpassing many European competitors. The **e-Boxer** engine brings a slight pleasantness in the city thanks to its silence at low speeds, but do not buy it expecting drastic fuel savings: it is a mild hybrid system that struggles to compensate for the vehicle's weight and the friction of the permanent all-wheel drive.

For a used purchase, favor a model with a crystal-clear maintenance history, specifically checking that the CVT gearbox oil has been changed. It is a robust, safe (impressive EyeSight), and endearing vehicle, provided you accept a reduced trunk and a sometimes temperamental multimedia interface.
