Peugeot 107

I phase 2 (PM/PN) · 2009 – 2014

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

All Peugeot models

Summary

The Peugeot 107 is the result of the B-Zero project (TPCA), developed jointly by PSA and Toyota. Produced in the Czech Republic alongside its siblings (Citroën C1 and Toyota Aygo), it is positioned as an economical and pragmatic A-segment mini-city car. The 2009-2014 period covers Phase 2 (2009-2012), which brought a redesigned front bumper and improved sound insulation, as well as Phase 3 (2012-2014), recognizable by its LED daytime running lights. Its major asset lies in its Japanese-origin mechanical architecture, giving it formidable reliability. It is ideal for new drivers or as a second urban vehicle.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of the Toyota engine block (1KR-FE)
  • Negligible maintenance and insurance costs
  • Chain timing (no belt to replace)
  • Very low fuel consumption (often under 5.5 L/100 km in real-world driving)
  • Maneuverability and compactness ideal for urban parking

Weaknesses 5

  • Tiny boot volume (139 liters) and impractical access (glass tailgate)
  • Very light sound insulation, even after 2009 improvements
  • Basic interior finish (hard plastics, exposed sheet metal)
  • 2-Tronic automated manual gearbox is slow, unpleasant, and prone to breakdowns
  • Recurring problem of water ingress into the cabin and boot

Check before buying

  • Lift the boot carpet and check for any water or rust in the spare wheel well.
  • Test the clutch: if it judders on starting or if the pedal is very stiff, a replacement is likely needed.
  • Inspect the left side of the engine (accessory belt side) for traces of pink coolant (water pump leak).
  • Check the condition of the exhaust line and rear silencer, which are highly exposed to corrosion in urban use (short trips).
  • Ensure proper functioning of the hazard warning light button (frequent loose contacts).

Official recalls

  • February 2010 (Accelerator pedal): Worldwide recall (linked to the Toyota issue). Risk of a stiff point or slow return of the accelerator pedal. Installation of a reinforcement shim.
  • August 2010 (Rear windows): On 3-door models, risk of detachment of the rear quarter window hinges. Replacement of fasteners.
  • November 2013 (Starter): On a small series, risk of internal short circuit in the starter motor potentially leading to overheating.

What sets it apart 6

Reliability

  • Exceptional Toyota Engine Reliability — The 1.0i 12V Toyota-sourced engine is renowned for its robustness and chain-driven timing, ensuring remarkable longevity and reduced maintenance costs.

Economy

  • Ultra-Economical Fuel Consumption — With real-world consumption often below 5.5 L/100 km, the Peugeot 107 is a champion of fuel economy, ideal for managing your fuel budget.

Practicality

  • Urban Maneuverability and Agility — Its compactness and light steering make it the queen of the city, facilitating parking and maneuvers in the tightest spaces.

Safety

  • Proven Euro NCAP Safety — The Peugeot 107 achieved an excellent score in Euro NCAP safety tests, offering reassuring protection for its occupants.
  • Efficient and Adapted Braking — Its braking system is perfectly sized for its light weight and urban use, ensuring short stopping distances and optimal safety.

Comfort

  • Improved Comfort in Phases 2 and 3 — Models from 2009 to 2014 benefit from enhanced soundproofing and a redesigned front bumper, offering a more pleasant driving experience than the previous generation.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Renault Twingo II (more versatile and spacious), Fiat Panda II/III (more practical with 5 real seats), Volkswagen Up! / Skoda Citigo / Seat Mii (better finished and soundproofed), Hyundai i10.
  • Japan / Asia: Toyota Aygo (strict twin), Daihatsu Mira.
  • Americas / Emerging Markets: Model generally not marketed (replaced by local models like Peugeot 206+ or 207 Compact).

Verdict

The Peugeot 107 (Phases 2 and 3) is a highly recommendable purchase for strictly urban or peri-urban use. By opting for models produced between 2009 and 2014, you avoid the early issues of Phase 1 (notably the undersized clutch). Its chain-driven Toyota engine is a model of robustness. Absolute buying condition: prioritize the manual gearbox and avoid the 2-Tronic automated manual gearbox. Accept its spartan comfort and symbolic boot, and it will reward you with unbeatable running costs. However, be sure to check the boot's watertightness before purchase.
