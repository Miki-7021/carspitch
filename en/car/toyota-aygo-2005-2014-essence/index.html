Toyota Aygo

I (AB10) · 2005 – 2014

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The first-generation Toyota Aygo (codename AB10) is the result of a historic joint venture between Toyota and the PSA group (Peugeot 107, Citroën C1). Assembled in the TPCA factory in Kolín, Czech Republic, it positions itself as an ultra-compact, economical, and agile micro-city car (A-segment). Designed for the urban jungle, it benefited from two facelifts (2009 and 2012) which modernized its front end and corrected early defects. While the interior finish is basic and sound insulation almost non-existent, the Aygo shines with its exceptional mechanical reliability, driven by its famous 3-cylinder petrol engine of Daihatsu/Toyota origin. It is a benchmark choice on the used market for new drivers or as an economical second vehicle.

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability of the 1.0 VVT-i engine (Toyota/Daihatsu design)
  • Extremely low maintenance and insurance costs
  • Very low fuel consumption (often under 5L/100km in real-world driving)
  • Agility in the city (turning radius, compactness)
  • Timing chain on the petrol engine

Weaknesses 5

  • Frequent water ingress (door seals, rear lights, 3rd brake light)
  • Very poor sound insulation (wind and road noise on the highway)
  • Slow, jerky, and fragile MMT automated manual gearbox
  • Tiny boot volume (139 liters) and impractical access (glass tailgate)
  • Basic interior finish and plastics prone to scratches

Check before buying

  • Lift the boot mat to check for water or rust in the spare wheel well.
  • Test the clutch: a very stiff pedal or a very high biting point indicates it's nearing the end of its life (common around 60,000 - 80,000 km on early models).
  • Inspect the exhaust line, particularly the rear silencer, which is very prone to corrosion and tends to detach.
  • Listen to the engine at idle: a rattling noise or a whistling sound on the passenger side indicates a worn water pump.
  • Check the rear defroster operation (the terminals often detach from the glass tailgate).

Verdict

The Toyota Aygo I is a true urban camel. If one accepts its spartan comfort and light sound insulation, it proves to be one of the most economical and reliable cars on the used market. The 1.0 VVT-i engine is indestructible, provided the water pump and clutch are monitored. **Absolutely avoid the diesel engine (useless) and the problematic MMT automated manual gearbox.** Opt for a petrol model with a manual gearbox, ideally post-2009 (first facelift) to benefit from a reinforced clutch and better sealing. Systematically check for water in the boot before purchase.
