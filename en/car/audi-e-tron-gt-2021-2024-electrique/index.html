Summary

The Audi e-tron GT (F8 generation) is a 100% electric grand touring sedan based on the J1 platform, developed jointly with Porsche (shared with the Taycan). Launched in 2021, it stands out for its spectacular design, typical Audi finish quality, and 800-volt architecture allowing ultra-fast charging (up to 270 kW). While its road handling masterfully combines comfort and dynamism, it suffers from average rear passenger space and a limited trunk volume for its size. In terms of reliability, although the physical construction is excellent, the model has been marred by major recalls concerning the high-voltage battery (fire risk) and recurring software bugs.

Check before buying

  • Recall history: Demand proof of inspection/replacement of the high-voltage battery modules.
  • Air suspension: Check for the absence of sagging on one side (known recall on the retaining ring).
  • Front brake hoses: Visually inspect for the absence of tears or fluid leaks.
  • Home charging cable: Check that it shows no signs of overheating (specific recall).
  • On-board electronics: Test all MMI functions, the rearview camera, and the front passenger detection system.

Official recalls

Major Safety Recalls (Sources: Rappel Conso FR & NHTSA US)

High-Voltage Battery (Fire risk): * 2024-03-22 / 2024-06-14 / 2024-09-30 / 2024-10-23 / 2025-03-13 / 2025-06-03: Multiple recall campaigns (NHTSA 24V228000, 24V229000, 24V726000 & Rappel Conso). Certain battery modules present production anomalies (torn anode tab, detached cathode) that can lead to an internal short circuit and a fire risk.

Suspension and Braking: * 2021-12-01 / 2022-12-14 (NHTSA 21V935000, 22V927000) & 2026-02-02 (Rappel Conso): A retaining ring on the air suspension strut can loosen, leading to air loss, a drop in vehicle ride height, and a loss of control. * 2024-06-20 (NHTSA 24V465000): The front axle brake hoses can tear, causing a brake fluid leak and a failure of the braking circuit.

Electronics and Software: * 2021-08-27 (Rappel Conso): Software defect that can cause a sporadic loss of performance and throttle response while driving. * 2022-10-05 / 2022-10-26 / 2025-12-19 (NHTSA 22V742000, 22V806000, 25V900000): Software errors preventing the display of the rearview camera image or the infotainment screen. * 2025-04-15 (NHTSA 25V227000): Wiring defect in the passenger occupant detection system (PODS) that can deactivate the front passenger airbag.

Ancillary Equipment: * 2023-12-13 (NHTSA 23V842000): Possible overheating of the on-board charging cable. * 2022-02-16 (NHTSA 22V084000): The center seat belt buckle harness can obstruct the right rear ISOFIX anchors.

Verdict

The Audi e-tron GT is an impressive technological and stylistic showcase, offering very high-level road performance. However, its reliability record is heavily marred by critical early-life problems, notably the multiple recalls concerning the high-voltage battery modules presenting fire risks. Buying used is conceivable only if the vehicle has a crystal-clear history at an Audi dealership and all recall campaigns (battery, suspension, brake hoses) have been officially resolved. The model's steep depreciation makes it a financially interesting deal on the used market, but caution is advised.
