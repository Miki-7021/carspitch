Mercedes Classe S

W222 · 2013 – 2020

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The Mercedes-Benz S-Class (W222) represented the pinnacle of luxury and technology for the brand with the star upon its release. Replacing the W221, it introduced major innovations such as the predictive *Magic Body Control* suspension, 100% LED lighting (without a single conventional bulb), and a dual 12.3-inch screen interface.

**Evolution:** A major facelift (Phase 2) occurred in mid-2017. It brought a new lighting signature, improved driving aids, but above all a **revolution under the hood**: the abandonment of V6 engines in favor of new inline 6-cylinders (M256 petrol, OM656 diesel) with 48V mild-hybrid technology, and the replacement of the 4.7L V8 (M278) with the 4.0L V8 (M176/M177).

**Overall rating:** It is an absolute benchmark in terms of comfort. However, its technological complexity requires a substantial maintenance budget. The petrol engines of Phase 2 (post-2017) are significantly more reliable than those of Phase 1.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with Magic Body Control)
  • Benchmark soundproofing in the category
  • Sumptuous finish quality and interior materials
  • On-board technology still up to date (screens, driving aids)
  • Reliability of the inline 6-cylinder (Phase 2) and 4.0L V8 engines

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high maintenance, insurance, and repair costs
  • Disastrous reliability of the 4.7L V8 (M278) in the Phase 1 S 500
  • Frequent electronic bugs on early model years (2013-2015)
  • Temperamental 48V hybrid system on M256 engines
  • Steep depreciation (although this is an advantage on the used market)

Check before buying

  • Airmatic suspension: check that the car raises and lowers without excessive compressor noise, and that it does not sag after prolonged parking.
  • Magic Body Control (if equipped): complex hydraulic system (ABC), check for Pentosin fluid leaks and the proper operation of the tandem pump.
  • Electronics: absolutely test ALL functions (massage seats, electric blinds, 360° cameras, screens, Burmester audio system).
  • Panoramic roof: frequent creaking on rough roads, requires specific lubrication or recalibration.
  • Maintenance history: avoid any model that does not have a 100% Mercedes or recognized specialist service book.

Verdict

The Mercedes S-Class W222 is an engineering masterpiece, but it requires a well-lined wallet for its maintenance.

**Our advice:** Absolutely favor a **Phase 2 (post-2017)** version. The inline 6-cylinder engines (S 450 / S 500) are excellent despite some quirks from the 48V system, and the new 4.0L V8 (S 560) erases the serious reliability issues of its predecessor.

**To avoid:** The Phase 1 (2013-2017) S 500 and AMG S 63 equipped with the M278 and M157 V8s, unless you have a meticulous maintenance history, a perfect endoscopic inspection of the cylinders, and a solid warranty. The replacement costs of a scored V8 engine often exceed the residual value of the vehicle.
