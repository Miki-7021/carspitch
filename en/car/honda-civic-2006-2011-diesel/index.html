Honda Civic

VIII (FN/FK) · 2006 – 2011

NEGOTIATE Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The 8th generation Honda Civic (FN chassis for 3-door, FK for 5-door in Europe) marked a radical stylistic break, earning it the nickname 'UFO'. Beyond its futuristic design, it shines with exceptional spaciousness thanks to its fuel tank placed under the front seats and its foldable rear bench seat system ('Magic Seats'). The 485-liter boot is worthy of the upper segment. A facelift occurred in 2009, bringing higher quality interior plastics, a modified grille, and suspension adjustments. Although the demand here targets the 2.2 i-CTDi diesel engine (the first 100% Honda-designed diesel engine, very powerful but prone to some peripheral weaknesses), the petrol range remains the absolute benchmark for reliability.

Weaknesses 6

  • Fragility of the clutch and flywheel on the 2.2 i-CTDi
  • Extremely thin paint, very sensitive to stone chips
  • Very firm suspension, degraded comfort on bumpy roads
  • Poor rear visibility (spoiler cutting the rear window in half)
  • Frequent parasitic noises and creaking from interior plastics
  • Large turning radius

Check before buying

  • On the 2.2 i-CTDi: test acceleration in 3rd, 4th, and 5th gear at 2000 rpm. If the engine speed increases without the vehicle speed increasing, the clutch is worn out.
  • On the 2.2 i-CTDi: turn on the ventilation when stationary. An exhaust gas smell indicates a cracked manifold.
  • Central display (radio/air conditioning): pixels tend to disappear with heat (cold solder joint issue).
  • Electrically folding mirrors: check that they fold correctly (the thermal protection module/polyfuse often fails).
  • Rear shock absorbers: check for leaks and listen for knocking noises over speed bumps (fragile bump stops).
  • Exterior door handles: check that they do not remain stuck in the open position.

Official recalls

  • - **Parking Brake (2006-2007)** : Risk of unintended handbrake release. Modification of the locking pawl.
  • - **Wiring Harness (2006-2007)** : Risk of short circuit near the steering column.
  • - **Takata Airbags (2006-2011)** : Replacement of defective airbag inflators (mainly concerns FD/FA sedan versions, but should be checked on all models via VIN).
  • - **Fuel Pump Relay (2006-2007)** : Risk of engine cut-out while driving.

Verdict

The Honda Civic VIII is a brilliant, practical, and timelessly designed car. **Regarding the 2.2 i-CTDi diesel**, the assessment is mixed: the engine itself is robust, but its peripherals (clutch, flywheel, manifold) are costly weak points. It should only be bought if these components have already been recently replaced with supporting invoices. If you drive less than 20,000 km per year, **definitely opt for the 1.8 i-VTEC petrol**, which offers almost perfect reliability and will save you heavy maintenance bills.
