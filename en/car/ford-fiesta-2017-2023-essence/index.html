Summary

The seventh-generation Ford Fiesta (often called Mk8 in Europe) was launched in 2017 and bowed out in July 2023, marking the end of an era for Ford's iconic city car. Based on an evolution of Ford's global B platform, it distinguished itself with benchmark road handling, significantly improved finish quality compared to the Mk7, and the integration of the SYNC 3 multimedia system. A facelift occurred in late 2021, modernizing the front fascia (logo in the grille) and introducing Matrix LED headlights. Unlike the previous generation, this Fiesta was not marketed in North America, where Ford prioritized SUVs. In terms of reliability, the assessment is mixed: excellent for recent engines (mHEV) and diesels, but darkened by the wet belt design of the early 1.0 EcoBoost blocks.

Weaknesses 4

  • Rear passenger space and boot volume are behind the competition (Clio V, Polo)
  • Variable reliability of 1.0 EcoBoost (pre-2020) (wet belt issue)
  • Quality of some plastics in the lower part of the cabin
  • Premature disappearance from the catalog (end of production in 2023)

Check before buying

  • Service book: DEMAND invoices proving the use of Ford 5W-20 oil (WSS-M2C948-B) on 1.0 EcoBoost and 1.1 Ti-VCT.
  • Oil strainer: On pre-2020 models, a flashing oil pressure light often means a strainer clogged by belt debris. Flee.
  • Cooling system: Check coolant level, particularly on 2018-2019 1.5 EcoBoost (ST) models.
  • Electronics: Test all functions of the touchscreen, rearview camera, and driving aids (lane keeping).
  • Running gear: On the ST, check the condition of the silent blocks and asymmetrical wear of the front tires.

What sets it apart 6

Safety

  • Benchmark Safety — The Ford Fiesta Mk8 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, ensuring optimal protection for adult and child occupants thanks to its advanced safety features.

Braking

  • Effective and Reassuring Braking — Enjoy a high-performance braking system, offering a progressive pedal feel and excellent stability, ensuring short and controlled stops in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Dynamic and Agile Road Handling — Recognized as a benchmark in its category, the Fiesta Mk8 offers an exceptional chassis, precise steering, and a sharp front axle, for unparalleled driving pleasure and remarkable agility.

Reliability

  • Reliable and Powerful Gasoline Engines — The 1.0 EcoBoost mHEV versions (post-2020) feature a chain timing system, offering increased reliability, excellent fuel efficiency, and dynamic performance. The ST version is also renowned for its mechanical robustness.

Performance

  • Driving Pleasure and Dynamic Powertrains — Whether with the 1.0 EcoBoost mHEV engines offering an electric boost or the fantastic 200 hp ST version, the Fiesta Mk8 provides a sporty and engaging driving experience.

Comfort

  • Ergonomic and Connected Interior — The Fiesta Mk8's cabin stands out with its meticulous ergonomics and responsive SYNC 3 multimedia system, offering an intuitive interface and modern connectivity for optimal user comfort.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 208 II (strong design, but same belt issues on the PureTech), Renault Clio V (excellent finish, very relevant E-Tech hybrid), Volkswagen Polo VI (more spacious, austere finish), Toyota Yaris IV (queen of urban hybrids).
  • Oceania / South Africa: Mazda 2 (very reliable, naturally aspirated), Kia Rio (long warranty, space), Suzuki Swift (light, fun).

Verdict

The Ford Fiesta Mk8 is a city car packed with dynamic qualities, offering a rare driving pleasure in this segment. However, buying a used model requires great vigilance regarding the chosen engine. AVOID or negotiate firmly 1.0 EcoBoost versions from before 2020 if the service history is not 100% clear and exclusive to the Ford network (risk of engine failure related to the wet belt). On the other hand, go for 1.0 EcoBoost mHEV versions (mild hybrid, post-2020) which have swapped this problematic belt for a timing chain, thus offering excellent reliability coupled with good fuel economy. The ST version remains a 'must-have' for enthusiasts.
