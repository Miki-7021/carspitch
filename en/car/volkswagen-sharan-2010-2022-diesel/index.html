Volkswagen Sharan

II (7N) (2010–2022) · 2010–2022

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation Volkswagen Sharan (type 7N), launched in 2010, represents the German brand's commitment to the large minivan segment. Sharing its platform (PQ46) and many components with the SEAT Alhambra, it positions itself as a spacious, practical, and comfortable family vehicle, capable of accommodating up to seven passengers. Its design, more dynamic than the first generation, remains understated and functional, true to the Volkswagen spirit.

A significant facelift occurred in 2015, bringing minor aesthetic changes (lights, grille) but, more importantly, technological updates (infotainment systems, driving aids) and a redesign of the engine range, moving from EA111/EA189 blocks to the more modern and efficient EA211/EA288. The Sharan II is appreciated for its spaciousness, modularity (foldable seats, electric sliding doors), and safe and comfortable road handling, ideal for long journeys. Its reliability is generally good, but certain points of attention, particularly concerning the early engines and the DSG transmission, warrant special vigilance.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional spaciousness and modularity (true 7-seater)
  • Very practical electric sliding doors
  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation on long journeys
  • Safe and balanced road handling
  • Build quality and interior finish
  • Economical and powerful diesel engines (EA288)

Weaknesses 6

  • Bulkiness in town and parking difficulty
  • Reliability of early TSI engines (chain, oil consumption)
  • TDI sensitivity (EGR, DPF) to short trips
  • Maintenance and repair cost of DSG gearboxes
  • Some electronic issues (multimedia system, electric parking brake)
  • High new price, slower depreciation than some competitors

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for DSG and timing)
  • Condition and operation of the DSG gearbox (jerks, slipping, oil changes performed)
  • Abnormal noises on cold start (timing chain on 1.4/2.0 TSI pre-2015)
  • Engine warning lights on (EGR, DPF on TDI)
  • Excessive oil consumption (2.0 TSI pre-2015)
  • Operation of electric sliding doors and electric parking brake
  • Condition of the dual-mass flywheel (noises or vibrations at idle/start on TDI)

Verdict

The Volkswagen Sharan II is an excellent choice for large families or those who need ample volume and exemplary modularity. Its reliability is generally good, especially after the 2015 facelift and with the EA211/EA288 engines.

**For intensive road and highway use, the 2.0 TDI versions (particularly the post-2015 EA288) are to be preferred.** They offer an excellent compromise between performance and consumption, provided that only short trips are avoided, which would clog the DPF and EGR. Rigorous maintenance of the DSG gearbox is imperative if the vehicle is equipped with one.

**For more mixed or lower mileage use, the 1.4 TSI (CZDA post-2015) or 2.0 TSI (DEDA post-2015) are safer options** than their predecessors, but fuel consumption will logically be higher.

Whatever the choice, a clear service history is crucial. Negotiate the price if the history is incomplete or if known weaknesses have not been addressed. This is a vehicle that, if well-maintained, will provide excellent service for many kilometers.
