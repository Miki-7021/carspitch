Peugeot Partner

II (B9) (2008–2018) · 2008 – 2018

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation Peugeot Partner, known by its internal code B9, was launched in 2008 and shared its platform with the Citroën Berlingo II. Positioned as a versatile leisure activity vehicle and light commercial vehicle, it attracted a wide customer base thanks to its generous interior space, modularity, and relative comfort for its category. It benefited from two major facelifts, in 2012 and 2015, bringing aesthetic and technical evolutions, notably the introduction of more efficient engines (BlueHDi, PureTech) and the integration of new technologies.

Overall, the Partner II is a robust and reliable vehicle, especially in its simpler versions. However, the arrival of more complex engines (1.6 VTi, 1.2 PureTech, 1.6 BlueHDi) introduced specific points of vigilance that require rigorous maintenance and knowledge of potential weaknesses. Its versatility makes it an excellent choice for families and professionals, provided the right engine is chosen and its maintenance history is checked.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional spaciousness and cargo volume
  • Suspension comfort for its category
  • Very robust and simple TU petrol engines (1.4i, 1.6i)
  • Very practical and modular utility versions
  • Good visibility and maneuverability in the city
  • Reasonable maintenance cost for petrol versions and early diesels

Weaknesses 6

  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway
  • Limited performance with small petrol and diesel engines
  • Reliability of 1.6 VTi and 1.2 PureTech engines (belt, oil consumption)
  • Recurring problems on early 1.6 HDi (turbo, DPF)
  • Limited range and battery degradation on the electric version
  • Basic interior finish and scratch-sensitive materials

Check before buying

  • Complete maintenance history and invoices (especially for 1.6 VTi, 1.2 PureTech, 1.6 HDi)
  • Oil level and consumption (VTi and PureTech petrol engines)
  • Condition of the timing belt (1.2 PureTech) or chain (1.6 VTi)
  • Turbo operation (1.6 HDi) and absence of abnormal noises
  • Condition of the DPF and AdBlue system (BlueHDi)
  • Presence of leaks (oil, coolant)
  • Air conditioning and heating operation
  • Condition of running gear (shock absorbers, ball joints, bearings)
  • Battery condition and real-world range (Partner Electric)

Verdict

The Peugeot Partner II (B9) is an extremely versatile vehicle, ideal for large families or professionals needing space and modularity. Its used market value for money is often very attractive.

**For serene and economical use, prioritize the TU petrol engines (1.4i, 1.6i) from the early years (before 2010) or the 1.6 HDi Euro 5 diesels (92 hp) after 2010, provided there is an impeccable maintenance history.** These engines, although less powerful, are exemplarily robust and their failures are generally less costly.

**Be very vigilant with the 1.6 VTi and 1.2 PureTech engines.** If you opt for one of them, a complete maintenance history and invoices proving rigorous follow-up (frequent oil changes, belt replacement for the PureTech) are non-negotiable. A thorough inspection by a professional is highly recommended.

BlueHDi versions are more modern and efficient, but the complexity of the AdBlue system can lead to additional maintenance costs in case of failure. The electric version is best reserved for very specific urban use, taking into account range and battery condition.

In summary, the Partner II is a good purchase if you choose the right engine and are prepared for regular maintenance. It is a faithful companion for those who appreciate its practical qualities rather than its performance or refinement.
