All Peugeot models

Summary

The Peugeot 508 II (R8 generation) marks a radical break from its predecessor. Gone is the classic three-box sedan; Peugeot opts for a 5-door 'fastback' coupe silhouette (and a very sleek SW estate) with frameless windows. Based on the EMP2 platform, it targets the premium segment (Audi A5 Sportback, VW Arteon) with high-flying road handling and a spectacular interior (i-Cockpit). Restyled in early 2023 (new grille, integration of the new emblem, 3-claw light signature), it has progressively abandoned its pure thermal powertrains in favor of plug-in hybrid. Expert's overall rating: An exceptional road car dynamically, but with very heterogeneous mechanical reliability. While the 2.0 BlueHDi engines are excellent asphalt devourers, the 1.5 BlueHDi and the depollution systems (AdBlue) require absolute vigilance.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road handling (benchmark comfort/dynamism compromise)
  • Spectacular and timeless exterior design (fastback and SW)
  • Intrinsic mechanical reliability of 2.0 BlueHDi engines
  • Significantly improved finish quality (materials, assembly)
  • Excellent EAT8 automatic gearbox (smooth and responsive)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the AdBlue depollution system (all diesels)
  • Possible engine failure on the 1.5 BlueHDi (7mm chain)
  • Limited rear legroom and headroom (a consequence of the design)
  • Slow and sometimes buggy multimedia system (NAC) before the facelift
  • Frequent wind noise from the frameless windows

Check before buying

  • AdBlue system history: has the tank already been replaced? (Invoice of approximately €1200 otherwise).
  • On 1.5 BlueHDi: listen carefully to the top of the engine when cold (clicking = loose camshaft chain).
  • Alignment and sealing of frameless windows (water marks, whistling on the highway).
  • Functionality of the touchscreen and piano keys (screen bugs).
  • Tire wear, particularly on loaded SW versions and the PSE version (sensitive geometry).

Official recalls

  • DPF (November 2020): Risk of particulate filter cracking on 2.0 BlueHDi engines manufactured between 2018 and 2019. DPF replacement.
  • Software (January 2021): Engine ECU update to prevent non-compliant NOx emissions (BlueHDi).
  • PHEV Battery (October 2023): Risk of traction battery overheating on plug-in hybrid models. Software update or module replacement.
  • Rear Axle (February 2021): Insufficient tightening of rear subframe bolts on a small series from 2020.
  • Vacuum Pump (2020): On 1.2 PureTech, risk of vacuum pump clogging by belt debris. Inspection and replacement if necessary.

What sets it apart 6

Design

  • Spectacular and Timeless Design — The Peugeot 508 II stands out with its fastback coupé silhouette or its sleek SW estate, featuring frameless windows, offering a bold and resolutely modern aesthetic that leaves no one indifferent.

Handling

  • Benchmark Road Handling — Enjoy a dynamic and agile chassis, offering a remarkable balance between comfort and sportiness. Precise steering and a sharp front end ensure very safe and engaging road holding, even at high speeds.

Safety

  • Certified Optimal Safety — Travel with complete peace of mind thanks to maximum protection for all occupants, attested by excellent safety test results.

Braking

  • Powerful and Controlled Braking — Benefit from a high-performance, powerful, and enduring braking system, offering short stopping distances and excellent responsiveness for increased safety in all circumstances.

Performance

  • Robust and Efficient Diesel Engine — The 2.0 BlueHDi block is renowned for its robustness and endurance, making the 508 II an ideal road-eater for long journeys, combining power with controlled consumption.

Comfort

  • Premium Interior and Driving Comfort — Discover a significantly improved finish quality with meticulous materials and assemblies. The smooth and responsive EAT8 automatic gearbox ensures exceptional driving pleasure.

Alternatives

  • Europe : Volkswagen Arteon (more spacious, reliable TDIs but austere), Audi A5 Sportback (premium, expensive), Renault Talisman (comfortable, reliable dCi at end of life, but discontinued early), BMW Série 3 (dynamic benchmark).
  • China (vs 508L) : Volkswagen Magotan, Honda Accord, Toyota Camry.
  • Hybrid : Skoda Superb iV (better electric range and giant space).

Verdict

The Peugeot 508 II is a car to fall in love with, designed for high-mileage drivers. If you're looking for a diesel, the choice is binary: avoid the 1.5 BlueHDi 130 (unless it has been made reliable with the 8mm chain and a new AdBlue tank) and absolutely prioritize the 2.0 BlueHDi 160 or 180. This 2.0-liter engine is one of the best diesels of its generation, capable of devouring kilometers without flinching, provided you budget for the inevitable AdBlue tank quirk. In petrol, the 1.6 PureTech is a good compromise, while the 1.2 PureTech is to be avoided in this segment. PHEV versions are fiscally interesting but complex in the long term.
