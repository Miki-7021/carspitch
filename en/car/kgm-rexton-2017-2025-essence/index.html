KGM Rexton

Summary

The third-generation KGM Rexton (code name Y400, often called G4 Rexton), initially marketed under the SsangYong brand before the takeover by KG Mobility, is a large family SUV based on an authentic ladder frame (body-on-frame). Designed to compete with the Toyota Land Cruiser Prado or Ford Everest, it stands out with its unbeatable price-to-performance ratio, its gigantic interior space (available in 5 or 7 seats), and its impressive towing capacity of 3,500 kg. It received a major facelift at the end of 2020 (new front fascia, increase from 181 to 202 hp for the diesel, new 8-speed automatic gearbox) and an interior technological update in 2023. Although the European market is dominated by diesel, a 2.0 Turbo petrol engine was developed to meet the requirements of other global markets.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional towing capacity (3,500 kg braked)
  • Gigantic interior space and voluminous trunk (up to 820L in 5-seat configuration)
  • Ultra-robust ladder frame for off-roading and heavy loads
  • Unbeatable price-to-equipment ratio on the used market
  • Very satisfactory overall mechanical reliability (well-designed original SsangYong engines)
  • Plush interior presentation (especially on the high-end Ultimate/Icon trims)

Weaknesses 6

  • Gargantuan fuel consumption of the 2.0 petrol engine (curb weight > 2 tons)
  • Clumsy road handling and pronounced body roll (ladder frame)
  • Jittery damping at low speeds and on sharp bumps
  • Sparse dealer network (SsangYong to KGM transition)
  • Significant depreciation on the used market
  • Slow and dated infotainment system in the early years (2017-2020)

Check before buying

  • Scrupulous maintenance history, particularly automatic gearbox and axle oil changes (essential on a heavy 4x4).
  • Underbody and chassis: check for the absence of rust or impacts related to intensive off-road use.
  • Transfer case operation (shifting into 4H and 4L): must engage without crunching or excessive delay.
  • On the 2.0 Petrol: unstable idle or loss of power (sign of GDI valve carbon build-up).
  • On the 2.2 Diesel: engine warning lights related to the AdBlue system or the FAP (very common if the vehicle has only been driven in the city).
  • Tire and brake wear: the weight of the vehicle (up to 2.2 tons) wears out these consumables quickly.

Verdict

The KGM (SsangYong) Rexton Y400 is a pragmatic and formidable choice for those who need a true, robust 4x4 capable of heavy towing and accommodating 7 people, all for a fraction of the price of a Toyota Land Cruiser. **Regarding the 2.0 Turbo Petrol engine**, it offers the advantage of peace of mind regarding anti-pollution standards (no FAP/AdBlue) and is quiet. However, its ferocious appetite for fuel and the need to monitor carbon build-up in the direct injection (GDI) reserve it for markets where petrol is affordable or for low-mileage drivers. For 90% of European or Australian buyers, the 2.2 Diesel remains the most logical and easiest choice to resell. The brand's transition to KGM ensures the continuity of parts, but the network remains a weak point to consider.
