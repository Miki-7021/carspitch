Summary

The third-generation Hyundai Santa Fe, known by the internal code DM, was launched in 2012 and produced until 2018. It marked a major stylistic break with its predecessors, adopting Hyundai's "Fluidic Sculpture" design language, giving it a more modern and dynamic look. Positioned in the family SUV segment, it was available in a 5-seater version and a long 7-seater version (called Santa Fe Grand or Santa Fe XL in some markets). It benefited from a facelift in 2015 (sometimes called "phase 2") bringing minor aesthetic touches (bumpers, lights) and technological improvements (infotainment systems, driving aids). Overall, the Santa Fe DM is renowned for its excellent value for money, generous spaciousness, and comfortable ride. Its reliability is generally good, particularly for well-maintained diesel engines, which are the most common in Europe.

Strengths 6

  • Spaciousness and overall comfort (especially in 7-seater versions).
  • Generous equipment from mid-range trims.
  • Modern and stately design.
  • Good sound insulation.
  • Efficient and relatively economical diesel engines (2.2 CRDi).
  • Manufacturer's warranty (5 years) often an asset for recent used cars.

Weaknesses 6

  • Fuel consumption of GDI petrol engines (especially the 3.3 GDI).
  • Suspension sometimes a bit firm on certain versions/roads.
  • Quality of some interior plastics (lower parts) could be improved.
  • Pre-facelift multimedia system a bit dated.
  • Large turning radius.
  • Cost of parts and maintenance at dealerships.

Check before buying

  • **Service history:** Essential, especially for diesels (oil changes, DPF).
  • **DPF/EGR:** On diesels, check for absence of warning lights, suspicious smoke, and journey history (avoid vehicles that have done a lot of city driving).
  • **Engine noises:** Turbo whistling, knocking (injectors), chain noises (rare but possible).
  • **Automatic gearbox:** Smooth shifts, absence of jerks.
  • **Running gear:** Tire wear, suspension noises (bushings, shock absorbers).
  • **Electronics:** Functioning of all equipment (GPS, air conditioning, driving aids).
  • **Bodywork:** Check for absence of corrosion under the chassis (especially on 4x4s that have done light off-roading).

Verdict

The Hyundai Santa Fe III (DM) is an excellent choice for families looking for a spacious, comfortable, and well-equipped SUV. Its reliability is generally good, especially if maintenance has been meticulously followed.

**For diesels (2.0 CRDi, 2.2 CRDi):** This is the most relevant choice in Europe. Prioritize the 2.2 CRDi for its performance and versatility. Ensure the vehicle has made sufficiently long journeys for the DPF and that oil changes have been done on time with the correct oil. A clear service history is crucial.

**For petrol engines (2.4 GDI, 2.0T GDI, 3.3 GDI):** They are more suitable for markets where petrol is cheaper or for low-mileage drivers. Be vigilant about the maintenance of the direct injection system (fouling) and oil consumption. The 3.3 GDI is powerful but very thirsty.

In all cases, a thorough inspection by a professional is recommended, especially for high-mileage vehicles. The Santa Fe DM offers excellent value for money on the used market.
