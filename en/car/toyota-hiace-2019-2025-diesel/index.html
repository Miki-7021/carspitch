Toyota HiAce

H300 (7e gen) · 2019 – 2025

BUY Reliability 90/100 — excellent

Summary

The **Toyota HiAce H300** (introduced in early 2019, often considered the 6th global generation, though sometimes classified as 7th depending on regional nomenclatures) marks a true revolution for this legendary model. It abandons the "cab-over" architecture (cabin advanced over the engine) in favor of a **semi-bonnet** design. This radical change, based on a new platform derived from the TNGA architecture, drastically improves passive safety (5 ANCAP stars), acoustic comfort, ergonomics, and road holding.

Note: This model is not marketed in Western Europe (where Toyota sells the ProAce, a Stellantis clone) nor in Japan (which retains the older H200 generation due to size regulations). It is the undisputed king of commercial vehicles in Australia, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Latin America. The diesel engine (1GD-FTV) accounts for the vast majority of sales and offers very high reliability, provided that the constraints related to modern emission control systems are respected.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability and longevity of mechanical components
  • Significantly improved passive safety (front bonnet, 5 ANCAP stars)
  • Greatly improved ride comfort and soundproofing compared to the H200
  • Extremely robust Aisin 6-speed automatic gearbox (AC60E/F)
  • Unbeatable resale value on the used car market

Weaknesses 5

  • Significantly increased size: no longer fits in many underground car parks (height > 1.99m depending on versions)
  • Turning radius penalized by the new long wheelbase
  • High fuel consumption on petrol versions (3.5L V6)
  • DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter) sensitivity on diesel in case of repeated short trips
  • Very utilitarian interior finish, hard plastics prone to scratches

Check before buying

  • DPF regeneration history (via OBD scanner) on the 2.8 Diesel
  • Condition of the EGR valve and presence of excessive soot in the intake (1GD-FTV)
  • Wear of front tires (the new MacPherson front axle is more heavily loaded)
  • Smooth operation of the automatic gearbox (jerks = oil change needed)
  • Traces of impacts on the underbody and driveshaft (construction site use)

Verdict

The Toyota HiAce H300 is undoubtedly one of the most reliable and durable commercial vehicles currently in production worldwide. The switch to a semi-bonnet architecture corrected the main flaw of the previous generation: crash safety. The 2.8L diesel engine (1GD-FTV) is a powerhouse of robustness, provided it is not used exclusively for urban deliveries over very short distances, which could saturate the DPF. For intensive city use, the petrol V6 is an indestructible alternative, albeit very thirsty. This is a highly recommended purchase for professionals seeking absolute peace of mind.
