Summary

The **Porsche Cayenne II (Type 958)** marks a major break from the first generation (955/957). Lightened by nearly 200 kg, it abandons the low-range transfer case to favor driving dynamics and efficiency. This generation introduces hybrid powertrains for the first time: the **S Hybrid** (full hybrid, 2010-2014) and then the **S E-Hybrid** (plug-in hybrid PHEV, 2014-2018). Although overall reliability is significantly improved compared to its predecessor (TÜV ranks it average for its segment), the Cayenne 958 remains a heavy and ultra-complex SUV. Recurring black spots concern the transfer case (endemic problem), the air suspension, and the onboard electronics. A facelift (Phase 2) occurred in late 2014, notably replacing the naturally aspirated V8 of the S with a V6 Bi-Turbo.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road handling for an SUV of this size
  • Interior finish quality and ergonomics (Panamera style)
  • Remarkable efficiency of the V6 TDI and S E-Hybrid versions
  • Overall reliability significantly improved compared to the 955/957 generation
  • Smooth and reliable Tiptronic S automatic gearbox (Aisin 8-speed) (if fluid is changed)

Weaknesses 5

  • Extremely fragile transfer case (jerks, very expensive replacement)
  • Complexity and replacement cost of the battery on hybrid versions
  • Air suspension prone to leaks (air springs, compressor) after 100,000 km
  • Rapid and expensive wear of consumables (brakes, tires) due to weight
  • Chronic oil leaks on the timing cover of the V6 Bi-Turbo engines

Check before buying

  • Transmission behavior: accelerate hard at low speed to detect juddering (failed transfer case).
  • Air suspension: verify that the vehicle raises and lowers without dashboard errors, and that it does not sag after being parked overnight.
  • Hybrid: request the battery's SOH certificate. Verify the operation of the 100% electric mode (E-Power).
  • V6 Bi-Turbo: inspect the timing cover looking for oil leaks.
  • Maintenance history: a Cayenne without a Porsche or recognized independent specialist service book is to be avoided.

Official recalls

  • - **[2022-08-30] Headlights (NHTSA 22V656000):** Risk of incorrect headlight adjustment on several models including the Cayenne.
  • - **[2022-01-12] Emissions (Rappel Conso France):** On the 4.2 Diesel V8s (Euro 5 and Euro 6), non-compliance with emission limits requiring an ECU update.
  • - **[2021-12-10] Steering column (France):** Defective screw connection that could lead to a loss of control.
  • - **[2021-10-01] Axle (France):** Stress corrosion cracking on the axle tie rod and wheel carrier.
  • - **[2020-07-31] Fuel leak (France):** Weak element of the quick connector on the fuel line, risk of fire.
  • - **[2016-03-23] Brake pedal (NHTSA 16V169000):** Possible absence of a circlip on the brake pedal pivot pin (2011-2016 models).
  • - **[2014-12-29] Suspension (NHTSA 14V824000):** Suspension alignment error on 2015 Diesel and S models.
  • - **[2013-10-17] Instrument cluster (NHTSA 13V506000):** Display defect on 2013-2014 models.
  • - **[2012-07-12] Turbo (NHTSA 12V329000):** Risk of fracture of the turbocharger turbine wheel on 2012 Cayenne Turbos.
  • - **[2012-02-21] Headlights (NHTSA 12V065000):** Risk of detachment of the front headlight assemblies (2011-2012 models).

What sets it apart 12

Safety

  • 5-Star Euro NCAP Safety — The Porsche Cayenne II ensures maximum protection for all occupants, as evidenced by its 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with exceptional results of 89% for adult protection and 82% for child protection.
  • Absolute Control with Benchmark Braking — Equipped with a powerful and enduring braking system, the Cayenne II offers some of the shortest stopping distances in its category. Its resistance to fade, even under intensive use, ensures first-class active safety.

Performance

  • Sports Car-Like Road Handling — Despite its SUV size, the Cayenne II offers remarkable road handling and driving dynamics, akin to a sports sedan. Its precise steering and surprising agility guarantee an exhilarating and confident driving experience.

Economy

  • Advanced and Economical Hybrid Technology — The hybrid versions of the Cayenne II, particularly the S E-Hybrid, combine remarkable efficiency with significant tax advantages. Enjoy controlled fuel consumption and silent electric driving in urban areas.

Comfort

  • Premium Interior Quality and Comfort — The Cayenne II's cabin stands out with exceptional finish quality and Panamera-inspired ergonomics. Every detail is designed for passenger comfort and well-being, offering a premium experience on every journey.

Reliability

  • Significantly Improved Overall Reliability — The Cayenne II benefits from significantly improved overall reliability compared to the previous generation. ADAC statistics confirm a low breakdown rate, attesting to the robustness and durability of this model.

Alternatives

In Europe: BMW X5 (F15) (notably the xDrive40e hybrid), Mercedes-Benz GLE (W166) 500e, Range Rover Sport (L494) hybrid or SDV6, Audi Q7 (4M) e-tron. In North America / Asia: Lexus RX 450h (absolute benchmark in hybrid reliability, but less sporty), Acura MDX Sport Hybrid.

Verdict

The Porsche Cayenne II (958) is an exceptional SUV that managed to correct the early flaws of the first generation. The **S Hybrid** and **S E-Hybrid** versions are attractive on paper (taxation, urban silence), but they add a layer of complexity (battery, inverter, temperamental supercharged V6) that can turn into a financial bottomless pit in the event of a breakdown out of warranty. If you opt for the hybrid, favor a Phase 2 model (S E-Hybrid) with a certified battery test (SOH > 80%) and a Porsche Approved warranty. Paradoxically, the most rational and reliable choice remains the **3.0 V6 TDI** (despite the anti-diesel context), while the **naturally aspirated 3.6 V6** is the king of peace of mind. Regardless of the model, checking the transfer case and the air suspension is **mandatory** before purchase.
