Summary

The Volvo C40 Recharge (recently renamed EC40 in 2024 to harmonize the electric range) is a premium compact coupe SUV based on the CMA platform, shared with the XC40 and the Polestar 2. The technical update (facelift/MY24) introduced in 2023 is major: Volvo abandoned front-wheel drive (FWD) in favor of rear-wheel drive (RWD) for the single-motor versions, and introduced new in-house developed electric motors along with an 82 kWh battery. This change radically improved efficiency, range, and driving pleasure. Exclusively available as a 100% electric vehicle (unlike the XC40 which had ICE and PHEV versions), the C40 stands out with its sloping roofline design, integrated Google infotainment system, and excellent safety level. Overall rating: 8.5/10.

Strengths 5

  • Efficiency and range transformed by the switch to rear-wheel drive (RWD)
  • Highly intuitive Google Automotive system (Google Maps, Assistant, Play Store)
  • Top-flight performance, particularly in the Twin Motor version
  • Interior finish quality and eco-responsible materials (leather-free)
  • Active and passive safety at the highest level (Volvo standard)

Weaknesses 5

  • Almost non-existent rear visibility (rearview camera essential)
  • Rear headroom penalized by the sloping roofline
  • Firm damping, especially with 20-inch wheels
  • Very high weight (over 2.1 tons) weighing on agility and consumables
  • Lack of a physical sunshade for the panoramic roof (tinted film only)

Check before buying

  • TCAM module: Verify that the GPS, LTE/4G network, and Volvo Cars mobile app work without latency.
  • Tire wear: Inspect the inner tread, particularly on the rear axle of Twin Motor versions.
  • OTA update history: Ensure the vehicle has the latest software version (version 2.14 or later) to avoid early bugs.
  • Steering wheel vibrations: Test the vehicle between 110 and 130 km/h. Some models on the CMA platform require fine balancing (Road Force) or tires with acoustic foam to eliminate resonances.

Verdict

The Volvo C40 Recharge (EC40) in its facelifted version (2023+) is an extremely accomplished electric vehicle. The switch from front-wheel drive to rear-wheel drive corrected the main flaw of the early versions: excessively high consumption. Mechanical and electrical reliability is excellent, with the only real annoyances coming from software bugs (Android Automotive) or the connectivity module (TCAM), largely corrected by OTA updates. The Single Motor Extended Range (252 hp / 82 kWh) version is the most rational choice, offering the best range and very fast charging (200 kW). Buy with complete confidence, checking the condition of the tires and the proper connectivity of the system.
