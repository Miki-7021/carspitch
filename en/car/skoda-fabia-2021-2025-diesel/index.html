All Skoda models

Summary

The fourth-generation Skoda Fabia (code name PJ) marks a major turning point for the Czech city car. Finally based on the Volkswagen group's modular MQB-A0 platform (shared with the Polo VI and Ibiza V), it has grown considerably to exceed 4.10 meters, offering record interior space and a 380-liter boot worthy of the segment above.

⚠️ CRUCIAL POINT REGARDING DIESEL: Although your search targets a diesel engine, Skoda has definitively removed the diesel (TDI) offering from the Fabia starting with this 4th generation. The B-segment (city cars) has seen diesel disappear due to anti-pollution standards (Euro 6d / Euro 7) making depollution systems (SCR/AdBlue) too expensive for this vehicle size. This report therefore focuses on explaining this absence and exhaustively details the petrol engines (EA211 evo family) that make up 100% of the range, while also guiding you towards alternatives if diesel is an absolute criterion for you.

Strengths 5

  • Rear passenger space and boot volume (380L) worthy of the segment above (Golf 8).
  • Excellent overall reliability thanks to the use of petrol engines (EA211 evo) extensively proven by the VW group.
  • Interior ergonomics retaining physical controls for air conditioning (unlike the Polo or Golf).
  • Significantly improved sound insulation compared to the previous generation.
  • "Simply Clever" features (umbrella in the door, ice scraper in the fuel filler flap, multiple storage compartments).

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of diesel engines (penalizing for very high-mileage drivers).
  • No hybrid offering (not even mild MHEV), which limits fuel consumption reduction in pure city driving.
  • Hard plastics omnipresent on the lower parts of the cabin and door panels.
  • DSG7 gearbox (DQ200) still a bit hesitant at very low speeds or in traffic jams.
  • Frequent software bugs on the infotainment system (black screens, unexpected reboots) requiring OTA or dealership updates.

Check before buying

  • Multimedia system: Check the fluidity of the central screen and ensure that the latest software updates have been applied by the Skoda network.
  • DSG7 Gearbox: During the test drive, simulate traffic jams (slow starts, frequent stops). No juddering, vibration, or metallic noise should be felt between 1st and 2nd gear.
  • Oil consumption: On recent models (especially 1.5 TSI), check the oil level. VW group's very low-friction engines can consume up to 0.5L/1,000 km during the break-in period.
  • Driving aids (ADAS): Test lane keeping assist (Lane Assist) and Front Assist, whose sensors can sometimes deactivate without reason (often related to a dirty windshield or a software bug).

What sets it apart 6

Safety

  • Top-tier Safety — The Skoda Fabia IV achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with high scores for adult (85%) and child (81%) protection, ensuring peace of mind on the road.

Practicality

  • Record-breaking Space and Generous Boot — Thanks to its MQB-A0 platform, the Fabia IV offers rear passenger space and a boot volume of 380 litres, comparable to a higher segment, for unparalleled comfort and practicality.
  • "Simply Clever" Solutions for Everyday Life — Discover Skoda's ingenious 'Simply Clever' features, such as the integrated umbrella or ice scraper, which simplify your daily life and add a unique touch of practicality.

Reliability

  • Reliable and Efficient Petrol Engines — The EA211 evo petrol engines (1.0 TSI, 1.5 TSI) are renowned for their excellent reliability and remarkable fuel efficiency, often achieving under 5.5 L/100 km on the road, offering a powerful and economical alternative.

Comfort

  • Enhanced Acoustic Comfort and Refined Ergonomics — Enjoy significantly improved sound insulation and intuitive interior ergonomics, retaining physical controls for air conditioning, for a serene and pleasant driving experience.

Handling

  • Safe Road Handling and Effective Braking — The Fabia IV ensures predictable and balanced road handling, offering great stability and effective, reassuring braking, for confident driving in all circumstances.

Verdict

The Skoda Fabia IV is undoubtedly one of the most rational and accomplished city cars on the market. Its transition to the MQB-A0 platform gives it road-car performance. However, if you are absolutely looking for a diesel, you must look elsewhere: Skoda has made the choice (logical for the segment) to abandon it.

To compensate, the 1.0 TSI petrol engines (95 or 110 hp) are remarkably economical (often under 5.5 L/100 km on the road) and show excellent reliability, with the early issues of the EA211 family having been corrected long ago.

Our buying advice: Forget diesel and opt for a 1.0 TSI 95 hp manual gearbox version for the best price/reliability/versatility ratio, or the 1.0 TSI 110 hp if you do a lot of highway driving. If you are a very high-mileage driver (more than 30,000 km/year) and diesel is non-negotiable, turn to a Renault Clio V 1.5 Blue dCi.
