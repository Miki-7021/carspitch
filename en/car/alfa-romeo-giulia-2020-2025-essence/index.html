Alfa Romeo Giulia

I phase 2 (952) · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The **Alfa Romeo Giulia (Type 952) Phase 2** marks the maturity of the Italian sedan. Initially launched in 2016, it benefited from a major update (MY2020) correcting its main youthful flaws: the cabin receives better quality materials, infotainment becomes touch-sensitive (8.8 inches) with a redesigned interface, and driving aids (ADAS) reach level 2. In 2023, a second facelift (MY2023) brings Full LED Matrix 'Trilobo' headlights and a 100% digital 12.3-inch instrument cluster. Based on the excellent Giorgio platform, it remains the dynamic benchmark in its segment. Unlike its competitors, it completely foregoes hybridization, which penalizes its sales but delights purists of thermal mechanics.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional chassis (Giorgio platform) and ultra-direct steering
  • Perfectly calibrated ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox
  • Overall reliability far superior to the brand's reputation (especially in Phase 2)
  • Timeless design, enhanced by Matrix LED headlights (MY2023)
  • Physical ergonomics preserved (real climate control buttons)

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of mild-hybrid or plug-in hybrid technology
  • Average rear passenger space and access
  • Sparse dealer network and sometimes inconsistent after-sales service
  • Faster depreciation than German competitors
  • Infotainment system still a step below premium standards (reversing camera resolution)

Check before buying

  • Battery and IBS (Intelligent Battery Sensor) condition: a weak battery generates multiple false error messages.
  • Tire wear: the aggressive front axle geometry (Ackermann steering geometry) causes cold scrubbing and normal but monitorable asymmetrical wear.
  • Maintenance history: engines (especially the V6 and 2.0T with MultiAir) require specific oil and frequent oil changes.
  • Touchscreen operation (MY2020+): check for absence of 'ghost touch' or intermittent restarts (often resolved by software update).

Performance

  • Unrivaled Driving Pleasure — Built on the exceptional Giorgio platform, the Giulia offers a chassis of rare agility and precision. Its ultra-direct steering and perfectly calibrated 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox guarantee sporty and captivating driving sensations.

safety

  • High-Level Passive and Active Safety — The Giulia ensures maximum protection for its occupants, as evidenced by its excellent Euro NCAP test score. It also integrates Level 2 driving aids for increased serenity on the road.

braking

  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — Equipped with high-performance braking systems, often supplied by Brembo, the Giulia offers exceptional braking power, great endurance, and short stopping distances, ensuring total confidence in all circumstances.

road_holding

  • Exemplary Road Holding — Its dynamic behavior is exceptional, with an agile and balanced chassis that provides excellent feedback. The Giulia maintains impeccable road holding, even at high speeds or in tight corners, for a most rewarding driving experience.

aesthetics

  • Timeless and Refined Italian Design — The Giulia captivates with its elegant and timeless design, enhanced by modern details such as the Matrix LED headlights (MY2023). It embodies the essence of Alfa Romeo style, combining sportiness and sophistication.

comfort

  • Improved Interior and Intuitive Ergonomics — Phase 2 benefits from an interior with higher quality materials and a redesigned 8.8-inch touchscreen infotainment system. The physical ergonomics of the climate controls are retained, offering intuitive use and increased comfort.

Verdict

The **Giulia Phase 2 (2020+)** is one of the most captivating sedans on the market. By correcting the finish and electronics flaws of Phase 1, Alfa Romeo has made its sedan highly recommendable. The **2.0 Turbo petrol engine (200 or 280 hp)** is the preferred choice: it is very reliable, powerful, and perfectly matches the masterful chassis. While the absence of hybridization may deter company fleets, it guarantees private owners a simpler mechanical system less prone to complex long-term failures. A passionate purchase that has become, with this Phase 2, a reasonable purchase.
