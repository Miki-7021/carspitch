Suzuki Vitara

IV (LY) · 2015 – 2025

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The fourth-generation Suzuki Vitara (codename LY) marks a radical turning point for the model: it abandons its ladder frame chassis of a pure off-roader to adopt a unibody platform of an urban SUV (B-SUV segment). Lighter, more agile, and more economical, it nevertheless retains true off-road capabilities thanks to its excellent optional **AllGrip** all-wheel drive system.

Launched in 2015 with 1.6 naturally aspirated (petrol) and 1.6 DDiS (Fiat-sourced diesel) engines, it underwent a first facelift in 2018 (dropping the diesel in favor of 1.0 and 1.4 Boosterjet turbo petrol engines), then a 48V mild hybridisation in 2020, and finally the introduction of a 'Strong Hybrid' (1.5 Dualjet) powertrain in 2022. A final aesthetic and technological facelift occurred in 2024. Although the target demand here is the **diesel (1.6 DDiS)**, the latter was only marketed from 2015 to 2018 in Europe, a victim of anti-pollution standards, but remains a very relevant choice on the used market for high-mileage drivers.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent overall reliability, well above the segment average
  • Very effective AllGrip 4x4 system (rare in this category)
  • Torquey and very economical 1.6 DDiS engine (often under 5.5 L/100 km)
  • Contained weight (often under 1200 kg) promoting agility and fuel consumption
  • Generous standard equipment (especially on Privilège and Pack trims)

Weaknesses 5

  • Interior plastic quality (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Infotainment system (Bosch) slow and sometimes temperamental (black screen bugs)
  • Thin body paint, susceptible to stone chips
  • AGS robotic gearbox on the Strong Hybrid version unpleasant to use

Check before buying

  • On the 1.6 DDiS: Service history (timing belt changed on time?) and no DPF/EGR warning light illuminated.
  • On the 1.6 DDiS: Test the clutch. A very high biting point or vibrations at idle can indicate a worn dual-mass flywheel.
  • Rear axle: Listen for any clunking noises on degraded roads (rear axle bushings sometimes noisy, requiring greasing or replacement).
  • Electronics: Check the proper functioning of the touchscreen, GPS, and smartphone connectivity.
  • AllGrip transmission: Ensure that modes (Snow, Sport, Lock) engage without an error message on the dashboard.

Economy

  • Efficient and Torquey Diesel Engine — Benefit from a robust and powerful 1.6 DDiS engine, offering generous torque and remarkably low fuel consumption, often below 5.5 L/100 km.

Safety

  • Optimal Safety — The Suzuki Vitara IV achieved the maximum 5-star Euro NCAP rating, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 82% for child protection, ensuring maximum safety.

Braking

  • Powerful and Precise Braking — Enjoy excellent braking performance, with a firm and easily modulated pedal, allowing for quick and controlled stops with full confidence, even in emergency situations.

Handling

  • Agile and Stable Road Handling — Its dynamic behavior is agile and composed, offering excellent road holding, effective body control, and precise steering for stable and predictable driving on all roads.

Practicality

  • Advanced All-Terrain Capabilities with AllGrip — Thanks to its highly effective 4x4 AllGrip system, the Vitara retains genuine off-road capabilities, a rare feature in its category, allowing you to explore all horizons with confidence.

Comfort

  • Generous and Comprehensive Standard Equipment — The Vitara offers particularly rich and comprehensive standard equipment, especially on Privilège and Pack trims, for enhanced daily comfort and convenience.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Peugeot 2008 (more premium but PureTech/BlueHDi engines less reliable), Renault Captur (more comfortable, reliable dCi), Dacia Duster (more spacious, excellent 1.5 dCi), Toyota Yaris Cross (hybrid benchmark). - **Asia / Japan:** Honda HR-V (Vezel), Mazda CX-3 (excellent finish, 1.5 Skyactiv-D diesel to watch out for). - **Emerging markets:** Hyundai Creta, Kia Seltos.

Verdict

The Suzuki Vitara IV is one of the most reliable urban SUVs on the market. If you are specifically targeting the **1.6 DDiS**, it is an excellent choice for high-mileage drivers or those living in mountainous areas (coupled with AllGrip). The Fiat-sourced block is durable, provided its operating environment is respected: it dislikes city driving which clogs its DPF and EGR valve. For mixed or urban use, absolutely prioritize the petrol versions (1.6 VVT or 1.4 Boosterjet), whose reliability is almost irreproachable.
