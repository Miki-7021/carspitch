Volkswagen Golf

IV · 1997 – 2004 (jusqu'à 2010 sur certains marchés émergents et nord-américains sous l'appellation City Golf)

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The Volkswagen Golf IV (Type 1J) marked a major turning point in the history of the compact car category. Upon its release, it redefined the standards of finish and perceived quality, bordering on the premium segment thanks to rich materials and rigorous assembly. Mechanically, it is famous for having democratized and pushed direct injection diesel technology to its peak, first with distributor pump (VP37), then with the formidable Unit Injector (TDI IP) technology. Although its road handling is often judged too placid (soft suspensions favoring comfort at the expense of dynamism), it remains an icon of mechanical reliability if maintenance is respected. Today, it is a prized 'youngtimer', particularly in its TDI 130/150 variants and its sporty versions (GTI, V6, R32).

Strengths 6

  • Interior finish and material quality (unprecedented for its time)
  • Excellent corrosion protection (fully galvanized body)
  • Exceptional efficiency, torque, and fuel economy of the 1.9 TDI engines
  • Massive availability of spare parts and huge support community
  • Well-designed control ergonomics and driving position
  • Careful sound insulation (especially on high-end versions)

Weaknesses 6

  • Interior cosmetic wear: the 'soft-touch' coating on plastics peels and becomes sticky
  • Faulty door lock micro-switches (car no longer detects door opening, alarm/light issues)
  • Sluggish road handling, original damping too soft (pumping effect)
  • Reliability of electric windows (fragile plastic clips)
  • Fragile dual-mass flywheel on TDI 100/115/130/150
  • Front axle and anti-roll bar bushings that age poorly (squeaks)

Check before buying

  • Engine oil history on Unit Injector TDIs (invoices mentioning VW 505.01 standard mandatory)
  • Symptoms of the dual-mass flywheel: vibrations at idle, knocking when the engine is switched off, juddering at the biting point
  • Turbo operation on TDIs: perform a full-load test in 3rd/4th gear to check for no cut-off (limp mode)
  • Condition of carpets (front and rear): water ingress is common (clogged sunroof drains, door seals, ECU housing in the windshield scuttle)
  • Air conditioning operation (compressor and radiator fans that tend to burn out their first speed)
  • Gear shifting on 1.4/1.6/SDI (bearing noises or crunching = 02K gearbox at end of life)

Official recalls

  • - **2001 (ABS/ESP)**: Risk of short circuit in the ABS control module (Continental Teves) potentially causing a fire. - **2002 (Airbags)**: Possible replacement of the driver or passenger airbag module due to deployment defects. - **2004 (Vacuum pump - TDI)**: Possible leak on the tandem pump hose (vacuum) leading to a loss of braking assistance (hard pedal). - **2006 (Heated seats)**: Risk of local overheating and burning of the driver's seat upholstery. - **2007 (Ignition coils - 1.8T/V5/V6)**: Replacement of defective ignition coils causing misfires.

Comfort

  • Premium Finish Quality and Comfort — The Golf IV redefined its category's standards with interior finish quality and materials worthy of a higher segment, offering refined acoustic comfort and exemplary ergonomics.

Economy

  • Exceptional Diesel Engine Fuel Economy — The 1.9 TDI engines are famous for their remarkable energy efficiency, offering very low fuel consumption and generous torque, ideal for economical long journeys.

Safety

  • Proven Safety — The Volkswagen Golf IV achieved excellent results in Euro NCAP safety tests, providing reassuring protection for its occupants.
  • Solid and Reassuring Braking — Equipped with a high-performance braking system, the Golf IV ensures short stopping distances and great stability, contributing to serene driving in all circumstances.
  • Stable and Predictable Handling — Its balanced chassis and precise steering give the Golf IV very stable road holding and predictable behavior, inspiring driver confidence daily.

Practicality

  • Ease of Maintenance and Large Community — Thanks to its popularity, the Golf IV benefits from a massive availability of spare parts and a vast support community, simplifying its long-term upkeep and maintenance.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Peugeot 307 (2.0 HDi - better chassis, lower finish), Ford Focus Mk1 (1.8 TDCi - benchmark in road handling), Renault Mégane II (1.9 dCi - beware of the disastrous reliability of the dCi 120 before 2005), Audi A3 8L (technical cousin, even higher finish). - **Japan / Asia**: Honda Civic 7th generation (1.7 CTDi - very reliable but noisy Isuzu engine), Toyota Corolla E120 (2.0 D-4D - exemplary reliability, austere finish). - **North America**: Honda Civic, Toyota Corolla, Ford Focus (market dominated by gasoline, the Golf TDI was a highly sought-after niche offering there for its fuel economy).

Verdict

The Volkswagen Golf IV is a monument of the modern automotive era. If you are looking for an economical and indestructible 'daily driver', the **1.9 TDI 90 hp (ALH/AGR)** with a rotary pump is the safest and least expensive choice for maintenance. For road use combining first-class performance and ridiculous fuel consumption, the **1.9 TDI 130 hp (ASZ)** is the absolute best buy, provided the maintenance history is scrupulously checked (505.01 oil, timing belt, dual-mass flywheel). Avoid entry-level gasoline engines (1.4 16v) and the first-generation TDI 115. Despite its minor electrical flaws and interior plastics that age poorly, a well-maintained Golf IV TDI can easily exceed 400,000 km.
