Summary

The second-generation Mercedes-Benz B-Class (codename **W246**) marks a complete break with its predecessor (W245). Gone is the complex and costly sandwich floor: the W246 is based on the MFA (Modular Front Architecture) platform, shared with the A-Class (W176). This change lowers the center of gravity, drastically improves road handling, and allows for a more conventional but significantly more premium interior. Facelifted in late 2014 (Phase 2), it corrected its early flaws (notably the firmness of the suspension) and modernized its infotainment. On the used car market, gasoline versions (M270 engine) are particularly sought after for their superior reliability compared to diesels in urban/suburban use, and their perfect suitability for the model's family vocation.

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability of gasoline engines (M270) very reassuring
  • Finish quality and interior presentation significantly improved (especially Phase 2)
  • Generous interior space and practical trunk (optional sliding rear bench)
  • Safe and dynamic road handling
  • Standard active safety equipment (Collision Prevention Assist)

Weaknesses 5

  • 7G-DCT automatic gearbox sometimes harsh at low speeds (especially Phase 1)
  • Firm suspension comfort on Phase 1 models (2011-2014) and AMG Line trims
  • Poor rear visibility (reversing camera almost essential)
  • High maintenance cost in the Mercedes network for a compact MPV
  • Random reliability of the electric version (B 250 e)

Check before buying

  • Service history of the 7G-DCT automatic gearbox (oil change invoice mandatory if > 100,000 km or 5 years).
  • Thermostat operation on gasoline engines (temperature needle should stabilize at 90°C quickly and not drop on the highway).
  • Absence of metallic noises on cold start (chain tensioner on M270 gasoline and OM651 diesel).
  • Condition of the panoramic sunroof (drain cleaning often neglected, risk of water infiltration into the cabin).
  • Operation of the central screen and control knob (Command), sometimes prone to bugs or loose contacts.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** BMW 2 Series Active Tourer (F45) (its direct rival, more dynamic but also front-wheel drive), Volkswagen Golf Sportsvan (more rational, less premium), Ford C-Max (excellent chassis, cheaper). - **North America:** The market for premium compact MPVs is almost non-existent there. The B-Class indirectly competed with crossovers like the BMW X1 or Audi Q3. - **Asia:** BMW 2 Series Active Tourer, Volkswagen Touran.

Verdict

The Mercedes B-Class W246 is an excellent used car choice, particularly with its gasoline engines (B 180 and B 200). The M270 engine proves to be robust, quiet, and perfectly suited to the vehicle's philosophy, avoiding the DPF and EGR hassles of diesels in urban use. Opt for a facelifted model (after November 2014) to benefit from better suspension comfort, a refined finish, and a better calibrated 7G-DCT gearbox. The purchase is highly recommended under one strict condition: demand a clear service history, especially regarding the oil change for the dual-clutch automatic gearbox.
