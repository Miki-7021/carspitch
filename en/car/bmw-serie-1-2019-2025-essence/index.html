BMW Série 1

F40 · 2019 – 2025

ACHETER Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The **BMW Série 1 F40** marks a historic and controversial break for the Bavarian brand: the abandonment of rear-wheel drive and inline 6-cylinder engines in favor of the UKL2 platform (front-wheel drive or xDrive all-wheel drive) shared with MINI. While purists criticized this loss of DNA, this change significantly improved rear passenger space and trunk volume, thus correcting the main flaws of the previous generation (F20). The build quality takes a leap forward, aligning with Série 3 standards, and the technological integration (BMW OS 7.0) is excellent. In terms of reliability, the F40 proves to be generally more reassuring than its predecessors, with the modular petrol (B38/B48) and diesel (B37/B47) blocks now being mature.

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability of the modular petrol engines (B38 and B48) is very reassuring
  • Interior build quality and ergonomics of the infotainment system (OS 7.0)
  • Rear passenger space and trunk volume significantly increased compared to the F20
  • Efficiency of the 8-speed Aisin automatic transmission (smoothness and responsiveness)
  • Safe and effective road handling, particularly in xDrive

Weaknesses 5

  • TÜV 3-year defect rate (17.2%) higher than average, pointing to suspension and lighting weaknesses
  • Loss of the typical BMW character (rear-wheel drive) and more understeering behavior
  • Very firm damping, particularly with the M Sport suspension or on the 128ti / M135i versions
  • Average What Car? reliability score (3/5, with 23% of faults reported over 12 months)
  • High purchase and maintenance prices in the official network

Check before buying

  • **Suspension and running gear:** The 2024 TÜV report indicates premature wear of suspension components (wishbones, silent blocks). To be checked during the test drive (thuds over speed bumps).
  • **Lighting:** LED headlight alignment defects or presence of moisture in the lenses (frequent according to TÜV).
  • **Coolant (Diesels):** Check the level. A drop may indicate an EGR cooler failure.
  • **Gearbox history:** Ensure there are no jerks on the DKG7 gearbox (dual-clutch on 116i/118i) or BVA8 (Aisin on other models).

Verdict

The BMW Série 1 F40 is a rational premium compact that traded its purist soul for better interior space and cutting-edge technology. In terms of reliability, it is a solid choice, particularly with petrol engines. The B38 (118i) and B48 (120i, 128ti) engines are mature and spared from the serious design flaws of the past. However, the TÜV (17.2% defect rate at 3 years) and What Car? (3/5) statistics urge caution regarding peripherals: the suspension and lighting must be rigorously inspected before purchase.

**Buying advice:** Favor a **118i** for versatile daily use, or a **120i / 128ti** to rediscover the dynamism expected of a BMW. Avoid diesels if you drive in the city, and demand a clear maintenance history, particularly for the suspension components.
