DS DS5

I (M32) · 2012 – 2015

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

Initially launched under the Citroën badge in late 2011 before becoming a standalone DS brand model during its 2015 facelift, the DS5 (generation I, code M32) is an unclassifiable premium crossover. Based on the PF2 platform (that of the Peugeot 3008 I), it stands out with its spectacular design and aeronautics-inspired cabin. **Focus on the Hybrid4 version:** This powertrain was a world first, combining a diesel combustion engine (driving the front wheels) with an electric motor (driving the rear wheels), thus offering a combined 200 hp and a non-permanent AWD. While the concept was innovative, the driving experience was penalized by the very slow automated manual gearbox (BMP6) and a particularly firm suspension. The overall reliability rating is average, dragged down by the THP petrol engines and the complexity of the aging hybrid system, although the classic 2.0 HDi diesel blocks are very robust.

Strengths 5

  • Bold and timeless exterior design
  • Spectacular interior presentation (airplane cockpit, 3-part glass roof)
  • Material and assembly quality significantly improved for its time
  • Innovative Hybrid4 technology (AWD without a driveshaft)
  • Remarkable reliability of the 2.0 HDi combustion engine (excluding hybrid and AdBlue)

Weaknesses 6

  • Extremely firm suspension comfort (juddering at low speeds), especially with 19-inch wheels
  • Drivability of the BMP6/ETG6 automated gearbox on the Hybrid4 (slowness, jerking)
  • Rear passenger space and trunk volume (reduced by the batteries on the Hybrid4)
  • Almost non-existent rear visibility (reversing camera is essential)
  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.6 THP petrol engines
  • Multimedia system (eMyWay/RT6) slow, dated, and prone to bugs

Check before buying

  • On Hybrid4: Test the switch to ZEV mode (100% electric). If it is refused while the battery is charged, the hybrid system has a fault.
  • On Hybrid4: Smoothness of gear shifts. Heavy jerking indicates a worn clutch or actuator.
  • On 1.6 THP: Metallic clattering noise when cold (passenger side) indicating a stretched timing chain.
  • On BlueHDi: Presence of white marks (crystallization) under the vehicle around the AdBlue tank.
  • Electronics: Proper operation of the central screen, GPS, head-up display, and hands-free access (temperamental door handles).
  • Running gear: Irregular wear of the rear tires and condition of the shock absorbers (frequent leaks before 100,000 km).

Verdict

The DS5 is a 'heart over head' purchase for its unique design. **Regarding the Hybrid4:** It is a powertrain to approach with caution today. While the combustion engine is indestructible, the high-voltage battery is over 10 years old and the BMP6 gearbox severely lacks drivability. Only buy with a crystal-clear maintenance history and after a rigorous test drive. **The rational choice:** Flee the 1.6 THP petrol engines (chronic problems) and favor a model equipped with the 160 hp 2.0 HDi with an automatic gearbox (BVA6), infinitely more reliable and pleasant on a daily basis.
