Volvo XC60

I (DZ) · 2008 – 2017

BUY Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The **first-generation Volvo XC60 (Type DZ)** was a true best-seller for the Swedish brand, becoming the best-selling premium SUV in Europe for several years. Based on the EUCD platform (shared with Ford and Land Rover), it stood out for its timeless design, supreme comfort, and safety innovations (introduction of the *City Safety* system as standard).

The model underwent a **major facelift in 2013 (Model Year 2014)**, modernizing the front end (monobloc headlights), instrumentation (digital displays), and multimedia system.

Mechanically, its career is marked by a major transition: it began with the iconic 5-cylinder engines (diesel and petrol) and Ford/PSA blocks, to end its career with the new in-house modular 4-cylinder architecture (VEA - *Volvo Engine Architecture*). Although the demand here targets diesel, it is crucial to distinguish the robust 5-cylinders from the first VEA 4-cylinders, whose initial reliability was more temperamental.

Strengths 6

  • High-level ride comfort and sound insulation
  • Seats among the best in automotive production (ergonomics)
  • Passive and active safety (City Safety, BLIS, adaptive cruise control)
  • Pleasure and sound of 5-cylinder engines (D4, D5)
  • Finish quality and aging of interior materials
  • Exterior design that ages very well

Weaknesses 6

  • High weight (often > 1.8 tons) penalizing agility and fuel consumption
  • Very large turning radius (difficult in urban areas)
  • Powershift dual-clutch gearbox (on some 4-cylinders) unreliable
  • Infotainment system (Sensus) dated and not very intuitive before 2014
  • Official network maintenance cost comparable to German brands
  • Average boot volume (495 L) for the vehicle's size

Check before buying

  • Accessory belt replacement history (crucial on 5-cylinder diesels).
  • Invoices for automatic gearbox (Aisin or Powershift) and Haldex coupler (AWD) oil changes.
  • Operation of electric tailgate struts (known weakness, high replacement cost).
  • Absence of leaks at the steering rack.
  • Condition of the DPF and EGR valve (ask for the previous owner's driving profile).
  • On VEA diesels (post-2014): check oil consumption and EGR recall history.

What sets it apart 12

Safety

  • Benchmark Passive and Active Safety — The Volvo XC60 I is a safety benchmark, having achieved 5 Euro NCAP stars (89% for adults, 79% for children) and multiple IIHS Top Safety Pick awards. It integrates innovations like City Safety and pedestrian detection with automatic full braking, ensuring maximum protection.
  • Powerful Braking and Absolute Confidence — Enjoy a remarkably efficient braking system, offering short and consistent stopping distances, often around 36-38 meters from 100 km/h. This performance inspires great confidence and enhances the vehicle's active safety.
  • Stable and Predictable Road Handling — The XC60 I ensures a serene drive thanks to its very stable and predictable road handling. Body roll is well controlled for an SUV, contributing to a high sense of security and enjoyable driving comfort.

Comfort

  • Exceptional Travel Comfort — Benefit from high-level ride comfort and refined sound insulation. The seats, recognized among the best in the industry for their ergonomics, transform every journey into a relaxing and fatigue-free experience.

Performance

  • Enjoyment and Robustness of 5-Cylinder Diesel Engines — The 5-cylinder diesel engines (D3, D4, D5) offer a rare driving pleasure, a distinctive sound, and generous torque. Their robustness is proven, making these blocks a preferred choice for longevity and high mileage drivers.

Practicality

  • Timeless Design and Premium Interior Quality — The exterior design of the XC60 I has gracefully stood the test of time, maintaining a modern and distinctive look. Inside, the quality of finish and the exemplary aging of materials contribute to a premium and durable atmosphere.

Alternatives

* **Europe**: Audi Q5 (I), BMW X3 (F25), Mercedes-Benz GLK / GLC, Land Rover Freelander 2. * **Japan / Asia**: Lexus NX, Mazda CX-5, Subaru Forester. * **North America**: Acura RDX, Infiniti QX50, Cadillac SRX.

Verdict

The first-generation Volvo XC60 is an excellent family SUV, particularly recommendable in its **5-cylinder diesel versions (D3, D4, D5 in 2.0L or 2.4L)** associated with the Aisin automatic gearbox (Geartronic) and AWD transmission. These engines offer rare driving pleasure and proven robustness, on the sole condition of being uncompromising on accessory belt maintenance.

However, **be very cautious with the first VEA 4-cylinder diesels (2014-2016)** due to EGR and oil consumption issues, as well as with all versions equipped with the Powershift dual-clutch gearbox. Prioritize a facelifted model (post-2014) with a 5-cylinder engine for the best compromise between modernity, reliability, and driving pleasure.
