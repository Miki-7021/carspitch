Audi TT

8S (2014–2023) · 2014 – 2023

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

All Audi models

Summary

The third-generation Audi TT (Type 8S) is based on Volkswagen Group's excellent modular MQB platform. Launched in 2014, it marks a sharpened stylistic evolution compared to the 8J generation and introduces a major innovation in the cabin: the Audi Virtual Cockpit, eliminating the central screen in favor of a 100% digital instrument cluster. Available as a Coupé and Roadster, the TT 8S received a facelift in late 2018, introducing a new engine nomenclature (40 TFSI, 45 TFSI) and the discontinuation of the diesel engine. Production ceased at the end of 2023 without a direct successor, marking the end of an icon. Overall, the TT 8S is a very accomplished sports coupé, offering an excellent compromise between performance, build quality, and reliability, with the EA888 Gen 3/4 blocks having corrected the serious oil consumption issues of the previous generation.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional interior finish quality and material aging
  • Overall reliability of gasoline engines (EA888 Gen 3/4) much superior to the previous generation
  • Ergonomics and timeless design of the Audi Virtual Cockpit
  • Safe and efficient road handling (especially with Quattro)
  • TT RS 5-cylinder engine with unique character on the market

Weaknesses 5

  • Rear seats (Coupé) strictly reserved for children or luggage
  • High maintenance cost at official Audi dealerships
  • Very firm damping, especially with 19 or 20-inch wheels
  • Magnetic Ride suspension (option) very expensive to replace in case of leakage
  • Possible interior trim or tailgate noises on degraded roads

Check before buying

  • Clear service history: S tronic gearbox and Haldex coupler (Quattro) oil changes must be scrupulously respected.
  • Coolant level: a drop often indicates a leak from the thermostat housing/water pump (a common issue with EA888 engines).
  • Audi Virtual Cockpit operation: check for dead pixels, flickering, or intermittent restarts (very expensive to replace).
  • Condition of the outer bolsters of the sport seats (leather/Alcantara) which wear quickly with friction when getting in/out.
  • On Quattro models: test strong acceleration on slippery surfaces. If the front axle slips excessively, the Haldex pump is likely faulty (clogged strainer).
  • Operation of the power windows (automatic lowering/raising when opening doors) and condition of the seals.

Official recalls

  • November 2019 (Fuel Tank): On certain models produced between 2015 and 2019, a fuel tank heat shield bracket may damage the tank in the event of a frontal collision, leading to a leak.
  • March 2020 (Airbags): Recall concerning a possible defect in the deployment of passenger airbags on a small series of vehicles.
  • 2016 (Lighting): Software update to correct a possible malfunction of the side marker lights (primarily North American market).

Verdict

The Audi TT 8S is undoubtedly the most accomplished and reliable generation of the model. By correcting the major oil consumption defects of the 8J generation, Audi has made this coupé highly recommendable on the used market. The ideal choice for versatile use is the 2.0 TFSI (230 hp or 45 TFSI), which offers first-class performance with excellent reliability, provided that maintenance (especially S tronic gearbox and Haldex) has been rigorous. The TT RS remains a future collector's item thanks to its 5-cylinder engine, but requires a significant maintenance budget. Buy with confidence if the history is complete and transparent.
