Summary

The fourth-generation Seat Ibiza (Type 6J, then 6P after the major 2015 facelift) is based on the Volkswagen Group's PQ25 platform, shared with the VW Polo V and Audi A1. Designed by Luc Donckerwolke, it stands out with its dynamic design ("Arrow Design") and youthful positioning. It underwent two facelifts: a minor one in 2012, and a major one in 2015 (6P) which transformed the interior (significantly improved finish) and introduced the new generation of engines (EA211). While the chassis is praised for its comfort/dynamism compromise, the reliability of this generation is split in two: temperamental before 2015 (chain-driven EA111 TSI engines, DSG7 gearbox), it becomes excellent on post-2015 versions.

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic road handling and rigorous chassis
  • Exterior design that has aged very well
  • Generous boot volume for the category (292 liters)
  • Excellent reliability of naturally aspirated petrol engines (MPI) and post-2015 TSI engines (EA211)
  • Ergonomics and finish quality significantly improved after the 2015 facelift

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.4 TSI 180hp (pre-2015 Cupra) and erratic reliability of the 1.2 TSI (pre-2015)
  • Fragile DSG7 automatic gearbox (DQ200) (mechatronics and dry dual clutch)
  • Very basic interior plastic quality before 2015 (easy scratches, rattles)
  • Firm suspension, especially in FR or Cupra trim
  • Average sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)

Check before buying

  • Timing chain noise on cold start for 1.2 TSI (2010-2015) and 1.4 TSI.
  • DSG7 gearbox behavior: no jerks on startup, smooth gear changes without slipping.
  • Oil consumption history on TSI engines (especially 1.4 TSI Twincharger).
  • Air conditioning operation (fragile compressor on early model years).
  • Condition of the EGR valve and DPF on TDI engines (dashboard warning lights, acceleration hesitations).
  • Possible water ingress in the boot (rear light seals) or at passengers' feet (clogged drains).

Official recalls

  • Takata Airbags (2020-2023): Replacement of the driver's airbag gas generator (risk of metallic fragment projection).
  • DSG7 Gearbox (2014): Replacement of gearbox oil (synthetic to mineral) and software update to prevent mechatronics corrosion.
  • Fuel Filter (2011): Risk of fuel leakage on 1.2 TDI engines (replacement of filter cover).
  • Child Lock (2016): Manual rear door child lock may unexpectedly deactivate.
  • ABS/ESC Unit (2017): Software update for the ESC control unit due to a thermomechanical connection defect.

Verdict

The Seat Ibiza IV is a city car with two faces. Models produced between 2008 and 2014 require great caution: while the naturally aspirated (MPI) engines are indestructible, the chain-driven TSI engines (EA111) and the DSG7 gearbox can turn into financial black holes. However, the 2015 facelift (6P generation) transformed the car. Equipped with EA211 engines (1.0 TSI, 1.2 TSI with belt) and featuring a much better-finished interior, it becomes an extremely relevant, reliable, and economical used car choice. Expert tip: Prioritize a petrol model from after 2015 (1.2 TSI 90/110 or 1.0 TSI). Avoid the Cupra 1.4 TSI 180hp.
