Subaru Impreza

V (GK) · 2016 – 2023

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The fifth-generation Subaru Impreza (GK chassis for the sedan, GT for the 5-door hatchback) marks a major turning point for the brand by inaugurating the new SGP (Subaru Global Platform). This architecture significantly improves structural rigidity (+70%), road handling, and passive safety. True to its DNA, it retains symmetrical all-wheel drive (Symmetrical AWD) as standard in almost all markets, paired with flat Boxer engines. This generation definitively abandons diesel and focuses on naturally aspirated gasoline engines, later supplemented by a mild hybrid version (e-Boxer) in Europe. The EyeSight preventive safety system is standardized on models equipped with the Lineartronic CVT gearbox. Although very safe and comfortable, this generation suffers from placid-tempered engines, with the sporty WRX variant now being a separate model.

Strengths 5

  • Active and passive safety at the highest level (highly effective EyeSight system)
  • Imperial traction thanks to the standard Symmetrical AWD transmission
  • SGP chassis offering an excellent comfort/handling compromise
  • Exceptional all-around visibility (thin pillars, large glass areas)
  • Generous rear passenger space for the category

Weaknesses 5

  • Naturally aspirated engines lacking low-end torque (especially the 1.6i)
  • Rubber-band effect of the CVT gearbox during hard acceleration
  • Fuel consumption higher than the category average (the price of AWD)
  • Temperamental infotainment system on 2017-2019 models
  • Soundproofing could be improved on the highway

Check before buying

  • Recall history: Crucial on 2017-2019 models (Fuel pump, PCV valve, ECM).
  • 12V battery: Frequent parasitic drain issues linked to the DCM module (Starlink) or the infotainment system.
  • Wheel bearings: Premature wear (growling noise at the rear or front), a recurring defect at Subaru.
  • Touchscreen: Check for the absence of air bubbles under the glass (delamination) and the responsiveness of the system (2017-2019 models).
  • CVT gearbox: Ensure there is no jerking at low speeds and check if a fluid change has been performed around 60,000 - 80,000 km.

Verdict

The Subaru Impreza V (GK/GT) is an atypical compact car aimed primarily at those seeking maximum safety and infallible traction (AWD) in difficult weather conditions. Mechanically, the naturally aspirated Boxer engines are very robust, although placid and somewhat thirsty. The main point of vigilance concerns the 2017 to 2019 model years, which suffered numerous early recalls (fuel pump, PCV valve, electronics). If you opt for these years, demand a clear history proving that all recalls have been performed. Models from 2020 onwards (and the e-Boxer versions) are significantly more mature and recommendable without major reservations, provided you accept the particular character of the CVT gearbox.
