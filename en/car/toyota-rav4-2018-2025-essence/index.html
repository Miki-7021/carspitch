Toyota RAV4

V (XA50) · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 90/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The fifth-generation Toyota RAV4 (code name XA50) marks a major stylistic departure from its predecessors, adopting a more robust and angular design inspired by true off-road vehicles. Based on the excellent TNGA-K modular platform, it offers transformed road behavior, 57% increased rigidity, and generous interior space. Although the European market is heavily dominated by hybrid (HEV) and plug-in hybrid (PHEV) versions, the RAV4 continued to be offered with 100% internal combustion (petrol) engines in many markets (North America, Asia, Oceania, Eastern Europe, and briefly in some Western European markets). These *Dynamic Force* family engines stand out for their exceptional thermal efficiency (up to 40%) and unparalleled reliability. A mid-cycle refresh (facelift) occurred in late 2021/early 2022 (new lighting signature), followed by a significant technological update in late 2022 (new infotainment system and digital gauges).

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of petrol engines (Dynamic Force)
  • D-4S injection (direct + indirect) which eliminates the problem of intake valve carbon buildup typical of direct injection engines
  • Very generous interior space and boot volume (580 litres)
  • TNGA-K chassis offering an excellent comfort/handling compromise
  • Extremely high resale value on the used car market
  • Reasonable maintenance cost for the category

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation during strong accelerations (runaway effect on CVT/e-CVT)
  • Very dated and slow infotainment system on pre-2023 models
  • Quality of some interior plastics below European standards
  • High-voltage cable corrosion issue (Cablegate) on AWD-i hybrids
  • Possible water ingress through roof rail fixings (2019-2021 models)

Check before buying

  • On petrol versions: check for any coolant leaks around the bypass valve (electronic thermostat).
  • On 2019-2021 models: inspect the headliner for any traces of water (roof rail leak).
  • On 2.5L 8-speed automatic versions (import or North America): ensure the gearbox does not jerk between 1st and 2nd gear.
  • On AWD-i hybrid versions: demand an underbody inspection of the rear high-voltage cable (corrosion).
  • Test the 12V battery, known to discharge quickly if the vehicle is driven infrequently (electronic standby issue).

Official recalls

  • November 2020 (Fuel Pump): Replacement of the low-pressure fuel pump (Denso supplier) whose impeller may deform, leading to engine stalling. Affects models produced between 2018 and 2020.
  • May 2020 (Suspension Arms): Replacement of the front lower suspension arms due to a metallurgical defect that could lead to cracks. Affects a limited series produced in late 2019.
  • April 2022 (VSC Software): Update of the Vehicle Stability Control (VSC) module which might not reactivate by default when the vehicle starts.
  • July 2023 (PHEV - DC-DC Converter): Replacement or repair of the DC-DC converter on plug-in hybrid versions due to a risk of short circuit and fire.

What sets it apart 6

safety

  • Recognized cutting-edge safety — The RAV4 achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with excellent scores for adult and child protection, and has been repeatedly awarded by IIHS for its exemplary safety.

reliability

  • Legendary mechanical reliability — Equipped with Dynamic Force petrol engines featuring D-4S injection, the RAV4 offers exceptional longevity. This advanced technology prevents valve fouling, ensuring lasting peace of mind.

practicality

  • Generous space and versatility — Enjoy very generous cabin space for all passengers and an impressive 580-liter boot volume, ideal for families and adventures.

road_holding

  • Stable and comfortable road handling — Thanks to its TNGA-K chassis, the RAV4 offers predictable and secure road holding, with precise steering and an excellent balance between comfort and dynamism for pleasant daily driving.

braking

  • Efficient and reassuring braking — The RAV4 ensures short and controlled stopping distances, guaranteeing optimal safety in all driving situations.

economy

  • Durable and profitable investment — The Toyota RAV4 benefits from an extremely high resale value in the used car market and reasonable maintenance costs, making it an economical choice in the long run.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Volkswagen Tiguan (more premium but less reliable in petrol), Peugeot 3008 (problematic PureTech engines, to be avoided as used), Honda CR-V (excellent, very reliable alternative).
  • North America: Honda CR-V (the main rival, reliable 1.5T but sensitive to oil dilution in cold weather), Mazda CX-5 (excellent reliability of the 2.5L naturally aspirated, superior finish), Subaru Forester.
  • Asia / Oceania: Nissan X-Trail / Rogue, Hyundai Tucson, Kia Sportage.

Verdict

The 5th generation Toyota RAV4 is a true 'tank' in terms of reliability, particularly in its 100% petrol versions (2.0L and 2.5L). The Dynamic Force family engines are masterpieces of thermal engineering, combining chain timing, dual injection (D-4S), and the absence of a turbo, which guarantees exceptional longevity with minimal maintenance. If you find a petrol version (often shunned in Western Europe in favor of the hybrid, but very common elsewhere), it's a risk-free purchase. The only downsides concern minor finish details, an aging multimedia system (before 2023), and the famous 'Cablegate' which fortunately only affects all-wheel-drive hybrid versions. A rational, durable, and extremely safe choice.
