Fiat Tipo

I (357) · 2015 – 2023

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

Summary

The modern-generation Fiat Tipo (Type 356 for the sedan, 357 for the 5-door compact, 358 for the SW wagon) marks Fiat's return to the C-segment with a pragmatic philosophy: offering the essentials at the best price, without falling into pure 'low-cost'. Designed in Italy and manufactured in Turkey (where it is sold as the Fiat Egea), it stands out for its generous interior space and unbeatable price/equipment ratio. The late 2020 facelift brought a modernized front end (new lettered logo), an unprecedented 'Cross' raised version, updated driving aids (ADAS), and especially the introduction of the new 3-cylinder FireFly petrol engines (1.0 T3) to replace the aging Fire blocks, followed in 2022 by a micro-hybrid powertrain (1.5 T4 MHEV). Overall, it is a rational vehicle, whose mechanical reliability is excellent thanks to the use of proven components from the FCA parts bin.

Strengths 5

  • Unbeatable price/space/equipment ratio on the used car market
  • Excellent overall mechanical reliability, particularly for the 1.4 petrol engines
  • Generous boot volume (440L in 5-door, 520L in sedan, 550L in SW wagon)
  • Simple ergonomics and retained physical climate controls
  • Appreciable suspension comfort on degraded roads

Weaknesses 5

  • Interior plastic quality (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • High fuel consumption of older petrol engines (1.4 T-Jet and 1.6 E.torQ)
  • Uconnect multimedia system (pre-2020) sometimes slow and prone to black screen bugs
  • Sometimes premature wear of front suspension bushings (clunking noises over speed bumps)

Check before buying

  • **Oil consumption:** On the 1.4 Fire 95 hp, ask the seller if they top up regularly.
  • **Running gear:** Listen carefully for front suspension noises (stabilizer bar links or wishbones often need replacing around 80,000 km).
  • **Onboard electronics:** Test all functions of the Uconnect screen, Bluetooth, and the rearview camera, which can take on water.
  • **Clutch:** On the 1.4 T-Jet and diesels, check that the pedal isn't too stiff and that the biting point is clear.
  • **DPF/EGR (Diesels):** Check for the absence of an engine warning light and ask about the type of journeys made by the previous owner (avoid diesels that have only been driven in the city).

Verdict

The Fiat Tipo I (356/357) is an extremely rational and intelligent used car choice. By embracing its position as an 'essential' car, it avoids superfluous and capricious technologies, making it one of the most reliable compacts of its generation. If you're looking for a petrol engine, the **1.4 T-Jet 120 hp** is the best compromise for versatility, despite its fuel consumption. For purely urban or suburban use, the modest **1.4 Fire 95 hp** is indestructible and very economical to maintain. Post-facelift versions (2020+) with the **1.0 FireFly 100 hp** offer better driving pleasure and a more modern presentation (especially in Cross trim), justifying a slightly higher price. Buy with confidence if the service history is clear.
