Toyota Venza

II (AV25) · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 94/100 — excellent

Summary

The **second-generation Toyota Venza (AV25)** marks the return of this nameplate after several years of absence. Based on the **TNGA-K** modular platform (shared with the RAV4 and Highlander), it is positioned as a premium-oriented mid-size SUV, prioritizing comfort, soundproofing, and design over pure utility volume. In the North American market, it is offered **exclusively with a hybrid powertrain and all-wheel drive (e-AWD)**. In other markets (notably in Japan where it is sold under the name **Toyota Harrier**), it offers a wider range of powertrains. It is a vehicle praised for its exceptional reliability, very low fuel consumption for its size, and plush interior, although it sacrifices a bit of cargo space compared to its sibling, the RAV4. Its production ends in 2025 to make way for the Toyota Crown Signia.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional mechanical and electronic reliability (components shared with the RAV4)
  • Extremely low fuel consumption for an SUV of this size (~6.0L/100km in hybrid)
  • Premium interior finish, soft-touch materials, and superior soundproofing compared to the RAV4
  • Standard e-AWD all-wheel drive (in North America)
  • Highly effective and standard driving aids (Toyota Safety Sense 2.0/2.5)
  • Innovative electrochromic panoramic roof (Star Gaze) (depending on trim)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disappointing trunk volume for the category (smaller than the RAV4 due to the sloping roofline)
  • Limited headroom in the rear seats
  • Noisy combustion engine during hard acceleration (rubber-band effect of the e-CVT gearbox)
  • No towing capacity officially recommended by Toyota
  • Infotainment system on early models (2020-2022) somewhat dated before the update

Check before buying

  • Corrosion of the high-voltage cable (MGR) powering the rear electric motor (known issue called 'Cablegate' on hybrid RAV4/Venza models in regions using a lot of road salt).
  • Cleanliness of the hybrid battery cooling filter (an obstruction leads to overheating and loss of efficiency).
  • Software update history (especially for the ESC stability control module).
  • Condition of body seals and camera housings (recent recall regarding water leaks).

Verdict

The Toyota Venza (AV25) is an exceptional choice for buyers looking for a reliable, extremely fuel-efficient mid-size SUV with a finish close to that of a Lexus. Its 2.5L hybrid powertrain is one of the most robust on the current market. Although it sacrifices a bit of practicality (smaller trunk) on the altar of design compared to a RAV4, it compensates with superior acoustic comfort and refinement. It is a highly recommended purchase, with the only real precaution being to check the rear high-voltage cable if the vehicle has been driven in regions with harsh winters.
