Honda ZR-V

I (RZ) e:HEV · 2022 – Présent

BUY Reliability 91/100 — excellent

Summary

The Honda ZR-V (RZ generation) is a compact SUV designed to bridge the gap between the HR-V and CR-V. Based on the excellent 11th-generation Civic platform, it stands out with superior driving dynamics for the segment, a sedan-like driving position, and a refined interior finish. In Europe, it is exclusively marketed with the 2.0 e:HEV non-rechargeable hybrid powertrain. In other markets (Japan, China, Oceania), it offers a 1.5 VTEC Turbo engine, while in North America, where it is sold under the 'HR-V' name, it is content with a 2.0 naturally aspirated engine. It is a very accomplished vehicle, penalized mainly by a modest boot volume for its category.

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic and precise road handling (Civic chassis)
  • Formidable efficiency of the e:HEV powertrain in real-world use
  • Interior finish quality and physical ergonomics (real climate controls)
  • Overall reliability of Honda powertrains
  • Above-average sound insulation for the segment

Weaknesses 5

  • Disappointing boot volume for a 4.57m SUV (380 to 390 liters)
  • Infotainment system a bit graphically dated
  • High prices in Europe (only available as a high-end hybrid)
  • No plug-in hybrid (PHEV) version available
  • Average rear visibility

Check before buying

  • Steering (EPS) : Sensation of 'hard point' or sticky steering at steady speed (known issue on the Civic/ZR-V/HR-V US platform, covered under warranty).
  • 12V Battery : Tendency to discharge quickly if the vehicle remains stationary for several weeks (frequent on modern hybrids).
  • Wheel alignment : Premature or asymmetrical wear of rear tires reported on some early models.
  • Onboard electronics : Slowness or unexpected restarts of the central screen (often resolved by OTA update or at the dealership).

Verdict

The Honda ZR-V is a rational choice coupled with a passionate one for those who enjoy driving. Its Civic-derived platform gives it exceptional road qualities for an SUV. The 2.0 e:HEV powertrain is a masterpiece of engineering, combining smoothness, performance, and very low fuel consumption without the constraints of charging. Although its boot is small and its price is high, its almost irreproachable reliability makes it a highly recommended purchase in the long term. The 1.5 Turbo and 2.0 Atmo versions (depending on the market) are also excellent, very robust choices.
