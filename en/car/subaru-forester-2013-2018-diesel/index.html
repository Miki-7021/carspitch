Subaru Forester

IV (SJ) (2013–2018) · 2013 – 2018

NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

Summary

The fourth-generation Subaru Forester (chassis code SJ) was launched in 2013, marking a significant evolution towards a more modern and spacious SUV, while retaining the brand's fundamentals: Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive (AWD) and Boxer engines. Larger and more spacious than its predecessor, it focused on comfort, safety, and versatility. A minor facelift in 2016 brought subtle aesthetic changes (headlights, grille) and interior improvements (materials, multimedia system). Overall, the Forester SJ is a robust vehicle, very capable off-road, and appreciated for its mechanical reliability in petrol versions, but it suffers from an interior finish that is somewhat behind certain European competitors and, above all, very mixed reliability for its diesel engine.

Strengths 6

  • Highly effective and high-performance Symmetrical AWD.
  • Generous cabin space and boot volume, ideal for families.
  • Suspension comfort, even on degraded roads.
  • High level of passive safety (excellent crash test scores).
  • Boxer petrol engines (FB20/FB25) generally reliable and durable.
  • Genuine off-road capabilities, high ground clearance.

Weaknesses 5

  • Very problematic reliability of the diesel engine (EE20Z).
  • Fuel consumption of petrol engines (especially the 2.0 XT under dynamic driving).
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds on the motorway.
  • Somewhat dated interior finish and less premium materials than some European competitors.
  • Lineartronic (CVT) gearbox which can smooth out driving sensations and lacks responsiveness.

Check before buying

  • **For the diesel (EE20Z)**: Complete maintenance history (invoices), DPF status (warning lights, complete regenerations), engine noises (injectors, turbo, flywheel), exhaust smoke. An extended road test is imperative.
  • **For the petrol (FB20/FB25)**: Engine oil level (especially on early 2013-2014 model years), CVT fluid change history (imperative every 60,000 km).
  • **For the 2.0 XT (FA20F DIT)**: Very rigorous maintenance history, turbo condition, absence of abnormal noises, check for valve fouling if possible.
  • **General**: Suspension condition (shock absorbers, silent blocks), rust under the chassis (particularly in regions where roads are salted in winter), all-wheel drive operation (no noises or jerks), regular tire wear (sign of good alignment).

Verdict

The Subaru Forester IV (SJ) is an excellent choice for those seeking a safe, spacious family SUV with genuine off-road capabilities thanks to its Symmetrical AWD system. It is a vehicle that stands out for its general robustness and versatility.

* **Petrol engines (FB20, FB25, FA20 DIT)**: Are generally reliable and represent the safest choice. They require rigorous maintenance, especially for the Lineartronic (CVT) gearbox and oil level monitoring on early FB model years. The 2.0 XT is powerful but demands particular attention due to its complexity (direct injection, turbo).

* **Diesel engine (EE20Z)**: **To be approached with the utmost caution, or even avoided by most buyers.** Its reliability is very inconsistent, and the repair costs for recurring problems (DPF, injectors, turbo, flywheel) can be exorbitant. It is only suitable for very high-mileage drivers primarily undertaking long motorway journeys, with an impeccable maintenance history and an awareness of the risks. For mixed or urban use, it is strongly advised against.

In summary, prioritize petrol versions for peace of mind. The Forester SJ is a faithful companion for active families and outdoor enthusiasts, provided you choose the right engine and adhere to maintenance.
