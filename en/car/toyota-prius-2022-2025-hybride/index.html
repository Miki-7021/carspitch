Toyota Prius

V (XW60) · 2022 – 2025 (en cours)

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

Summary

The 5th generation Toyota Prius (XW60) marks a radical break in the model's history. Gone is the divisive design purely dictated by aerodynamics: this iteration adopts a sleek coupe silhouette, lowered by 50 mm, and is based on the second-generation TNGA-C platform. While it sacrifices some rear passenger space and trunk volume for style, it gains significantly in dynamism and performance. The 5th generation hybrid system offers a spectacular leap in power (up to 223 hp in PHEV) while maintaining formidable efficiency. In Europe, it is only sold as a plug-in hybrid (PHEV) version, while other markets benefit from standard hybrid (HEV) versions.

Strengths 6

  • Spectacular design and highly refined aerodynamics
  • Significantly improved performance (especially in 2.0 PHEV with 223 hp)
  • Energy efficiency still at the top of its class
  • Very convincing electric range for the PHEV version (often > 70 km in real-world conditions)
  • Legendary reliability of the Toyota hybrid system (5th generation HSD)
  • Transformed road handling, rigid chassis, and precise steering

Weaknesses 6

  • Reduced rear passenger space (limited headroom for those over 1.80m)
  • Disappointing trunk volume (284 liters on the European PHEV)
  • Poor rear visibility (very sloped rear window, no rear wiper)
  • Infotainment system ergonomics could be improved
  • Combustion engine noise (e-CVT 'rubber band' effect) still present during strong accelerations
  • Purchase price has become elitist (especially in Europe where only the PHEV is offered)

Check before buying

  • 12V battery: Tends to discharge quickly if the vehicle remains stationary for several weeks (recurrent problem on modern hybrids).
  • Rear door handles: Check the proper functioning of the electronic latches (subject to a major recall).
  • High-voltage battery cooling filter: To be inspected and cleaned regularly (located in the cabin) to prevent battery overheating.
  • Software updates (OTA): Ensure that the multimedia system and ECUs are up to date to avoid interface bugs.

economy

  • Record Energy Efficiency and Generous Electric Range — Always at the top of its category for energy efficiency, the Prius PHEV offers a very convincing electric range, often exceeding 70 km in real-world conditions, allowing it to cover most daily commutes without consuming a drop of fuel.

reliability

safety

  • Maximum Safety with Recognized Distinctions — The Toyota Prius (XW60) achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with excellent scores for adult (87%) and child (87%) protection. It was also named "Top Safety Pick+" by the IIHS in 2023, attesting to its exceptional level of safety.
  • Efficient and Secure Braking — The Prius (XW60) ensures good braking performance, with a stopping distance of approximately 52 meters from 113 km/h. The system harmoniously combines regenerative braking and friction braking for controlled deceleration and enhanced safety.
  • Transformed Road Holding for Serene Driving — Thanks to a lower center of gravity, a more rigid platform, and optimized suspension, the Prius (XW60) offers precise and stable road holding. Roll control is excellent, and the steering is responsive, providing a sense of security and control in all circumstances.

Verdict

The 5th generation Toyota Prius is a tour de force. By sacrificing some of its purely utilitarian aspects (trunk, headroom) in favor of a desirable design and top-tier performance, Toyota has reinvented its icon. In terms of reliability, the TNGA platform and powertrains (proven 1.8L or 2.0L from the Dynamic Force family) are absolute benchmarks in the market. The only real drawback concerns the recall of the rear door handles (to be checked imperatively on recent used models) and the very high entry price in Europe due to the exclusivity of the PHEV powertrain. It is a highly recommended purchase for its peace of mind and negligible running costs.
