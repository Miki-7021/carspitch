Volkswagen Caddy

V (SB) (2020–2025) · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

All Volkswagen models

Summary

The fifth-generation Volkswagen Caddy (Type SB) marks a major technological breakthrough in the model's history. Now based on the modular MQB evo platform (shared with the Golf VIII), it abandons its utility origins (leaf springs at the rear) in favor of a rigid axle with Panhard rod and helical springs, offering comfort and road handling worthy of a sedan.

Positioned at the top of the MPV segment, it stands out with rich technological features but suffers from the same early issues as the Golf VIII: questionable all-touch ergonomics and an unstable infotainment system (MIB3) at its launch. Although historical demand has been for diesel (TDI), the 1.5 TSI (EA211 evo) petrol engine is gaining popularity in the face of urban traffic restrictions, offering an excellent compromise thanks to cylinder deactivation (ACT).

Strengths 5

  • Transformed road handling (MQB evo platform, helical spring rear axle)
  • Very balanced 1.5 TSI engine, quiet and economical for a vehicle of this size
  • Gigantic interior space and modularity (removable seats)
  • Technology and driving aids (Travel Assist) at a sedan level
  • Significantly improved sound insulation compared to the Caddy IV

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous multimedia system ergonomics (unlit touch sliders, complex menus)
  • Frequent electronic bugs on 2020-2022 models (black screens, phantom alerts)
  • Hard interior plastics prone to scratches (acknowledged utility heritage but disappointing given the price)
  • DSG7 gearbox (DQ200) on the 1.5 TSI less robust than the oil-bath DSG7 (DQ381) of the 122 TDI
  • Steeply rising prices, now elitist for an MPV

Check before buying

  • Infotainment system stability (test navigation, radio, and check for absence of SOS/eCall error messages)
  • DSG7 gearbox behavior on the 1.5 TSI: no jerks or vibrations should be felt during hill starts or at low speeds
  • Condition of sliding door rails (cleaning and lubrication necessary to prevent sticking)
  • On TDIs: service history and absence of engine light related to the emission control system (AdBlue/DPF)
  • Front tire wear (the torque of the 1.5 TSI and especially the TDIs can prematurely wear the front axle)

Official recalls

  • November 2021 (Code 91CU): Malfunction of the communication control unit (eCall). Software update required.
  • February 2022 (Code 42L7): Risk of loosening of the rear axle mounting bolts. Replacement and torque tightening of the fasteners.
  • May 2022 (Code 69CR): Welding defect on the front door collision sensor, potentially delaying the deployment of side airbags.
  • March 2023 (Code 69DU): Defective seat belt retractor on certain passenger seats. Mechanism replacement.
  • August 2023 (Code 60G3): Possible sealing defect on the panoramic glass roof (bonding defect).

Alternatives

  • Europe: Ford Tourneo Connect (literally the technical twin of the Caddy V, manufactured by VW), Renault Kangoo III / Nissan Townstar (excellent 1.3 TCe engine), Peugeot Rifter / Citroën Berlingo (often limited to electric or diesel, PureTech petrol to be avoided).
  • Japan / Asia: Toyota Sienta, Honda Freed (more compact but very modular).
  • North America: Ford Transit Connect (previous generation, as the Caddy is not sold there).
  • Emerging Markets: Dacia Jogger (very economical estate/MPV alternative).

Verdict

The Volkswagen Caddy V is undoubtedly the most dynamically accomplished MPV on the market, almost completely erasing its utility origins. The 1.5 TSI 114 hp engine is the sensible choice for families: it is reliable, quiet, and avoids the complex emission control constraints of modern diesels (DPF, AdBlue) while offering reasonable fuel consumption.

However, it is imperative to favor a model produced after mid-2022 or to ensure that all software updates have been carried out at the dealership, as the first model years were veritable nests of electronic bugs. If you opt for the automatic gearbox, pay attention to the smoothness of the DSG7 (DQ200).
