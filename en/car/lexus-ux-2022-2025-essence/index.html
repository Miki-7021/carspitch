Lexus UX

I facelift (AYX10) · 2022 – 2025

BUY Reliability 94/100 — excellent

All Lexus models

Summary

The Lexus UX (generation I, code AYX10) benefited from a major restyling in 2022. This *facelift* corrects the main flaw of the first iteration: the infotainment system. The frustrating old trackpad was replaced by an intuitive touchscreen (8 or 12.3 inches) brought closer to the driver. Lexus took the opportunity to stiffen the chassis (20 additional spot welds), recalibrate the steering and shock absorbers, and update the driving aids (Lexus Safety System+ 2.5). Although European demand is massively concentrated on the hybrid (250h then 300h) and the electric (300e), the 100% petrol version (UX 200) remains a relevant offering in certain international markets. Based on the TNGA-C platform, the UX shines with its exceptional build quality and mechanical reliability that ranks among the best in the global automotive industry. Overall rating: Excellent.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional mechanical and electronic reliability (M20A engines)
  • Flawless finish and assembly quality
  • New touchscreen multimedia system finally up to par (fast and intuitive)
  • Very high-level cabin soundproofing
  • Very low real-world fuel consumption (especially in hybrid)
  • Direct-Shift CVT gearbox (on UX 200) more pleasant than a classic CVT thanks to its physical first gear

Weaknesses 5

  • Ridiculous trunk volume for the category (320 L in FWD, 283 L in AWD)
  • Very limited rear seat space (headroom and legroom)
  • Mediocre rear visibility (very thick C-pillars)
  • UX 300e: outdated CHAdeMO fast charging standard and slow charging speed (50 kW max)
  • Disappearance of the 100% petrol version (UX 200) on the European market in favor of all-hybrid

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history of the hybrid battery filter (on 250h/300h)
  • Condition of the alloy wheels (highly exposed to curbs in the city)
  • Operation of the reversing camera / 360° vision (subject to a recall for water infiltration)
  • Front tire wear on FWD versions (UX 200, UX 250h 2WD, UX 300e)
  • Application of software updates (OTA or at the dealership) for the multimedia system

Official recalls

  • November 2025 (Source date): Software recall concerning the parking assist control unit and the panoramic view monitor (PVM). Risk of display malfunction.
  • September 2025 (Source date): Concerns the UX 300e. Possible failure of the electric compressor causing the HVAC (heating/ventilation) system to enter fail-safe mode, preventing defogging.
  • January 2025: Concerns the UX 300h. Improper routing of the primary wiring harness wires supplying the motor generator electronic control unit (MG ECU). Risk of short circuit or loss of power.
  • August 2024: Concerns several Lexus models. Camera housings can separate under stress (incorrect welding), allowing water infiltration and camera failure.
  • June 2024: Concerns hybrid/PHEV models. Design of the accessory immobilizer device insufficiently robust, allowing the vehicle to be in "Ready On" mode without proper authorization.

Verdict

The restyled Lexus UX (2022+) is an absolute rational choice for anyone looking for a premium compact SUV. By correcting its main flaw (infotainment), it becomes highly recommendable. The 100% petrol version (UX 200), although rare or absent in Europe today, is a monument of reliability thanks to its naturally aspirated dual-injection engine and clever CVT gearbox. The hybrid versions (250h/300h) dominate the used market and offer unparalleled peace of mind. The only real deterrent to buying remains its rear roominess and trunk, unworthy of the category. If you travel alone or as a couple, it is a purchase you can make with your eyes closed.
