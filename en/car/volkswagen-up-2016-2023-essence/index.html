Volkswagen Up

I facelift · 2016 – 2023

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The first-generation Volkswagen Up! received a facelift (Phase 2) in 2016. This facelift brought redesigned bumpers, mirrors integrating turn signals, new LED daytime running lights, and a redesigned infotainment system based on smartphone integration (Maps + More). Crucially, this Phase 2 marked the arrival of the 90 hp 1.0 TSI turbocharged engine, transforming this purely urban city car into a versatile vehicle, followed in 2018 by the lively 115 hp GTI version. In 2019, the electric e-Up! version received a major battery update (increasing from 18.7 kWh to 32.3 kWh usable), doubling its range. The Up! remains one of the most accomplished A-segment city cars, offering road handling worthy of the segment above and excellent overall reliability thanks to the maturity of its EA211 family engines.

**Overall generation rating: 8.5/10**

Strengths 6

  • Remarkable interior space for a 3.60 m vehicle
  • Very sound and safe road handling (worthy of the B-segment)
  • Overall reliability of gasoline engines (well-proven EA211 family)
  • e-Up! 2.0 version (32.3 kWh) offering an unbeatable price/range ratio on the used market
  • Serious assembly quality despite hard plastics
  • Enjoyment and versatility of the 1.0 TSI 90 hp engine

Weaknesses 5

  • ASG5 automated manual gearbox is slow, unpleasant, and less reliable than a manual gearbox
  • Light sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • 'Maps + More' infotainment system dependent on smartphone (VW app is no longer optimally updated)
  • Hard interior plastics, prone to scratches
  • Standard equipment sometimes sparse on entry-level trims

Check before buying

  • **Air conditioning condenser:** Very exposed to gravel behind the lower grille. Check that the AC produces cold air.
  • **Water ingress:** Check for moisture under the trunk mat (often due to decompression vents behind the rear bumper or the tailgate seal).
  • **Manual gearbox:** Reverse gear can sometimes crunch (absence of synchronizer on reverse, normal but requires a complete stop).
  • **Clutch (TSI/GTI):** Test the biting point; some owners with sporty driving habits wear out the clutch prematurely.
  • **Fuel flap:** The locking mechanism can sometimes seize.

Verdict

The facelifted Volkswagen Up! (2016-2023) is an **excellent choice** on the used market. It has corrected the minor teething problems of Phase 1 and offers extremely reliable engines. The 1.0 MPI engine (60 or 65 hp) is perfect and indestructible for urban use. If you venture outside the city, the 90 hp 1.0 TSI is the best buy, transforming the Up! into a true small road car. The electric e-Up! version (especially post-2019 with the larger battery) is one of the best electric city cars on the used market. **One golden rule: avoid the ASG5 automated manual gearbox**, always prefer the manual gearbox.
