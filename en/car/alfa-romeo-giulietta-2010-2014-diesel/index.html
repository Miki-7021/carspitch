Summary

The first-generation Alfa Romeo Giulietta (Type 940) (Phase 1) has the difficult task of succeeding the 147. Based on the brand new Fiat Group 'Compact' platform, it marks a leap forward in terms of safety (5 Euro NCAP stars) and road handling thanks to sophisticated suspensions and the DNA system (driving mode selector). Although this report focuses on **diesel (JTDM)** engines, highly valued in Europe for their robustness and torque, the Giulietta also offered excellent petrol engines. Phase 1 (2010-2014) is distinguished by its basic multimedia system (Blue&Me) before the integration of the Uconnect system during the 2014 facelift. It is a compact car with a timeless design, offering an excellent comfort/dynamism compromise, but penalized by some finish shortcomings and peripheral electrical issues.

Strengths 5

  • Timeless and distinctive exterior design
  • First-class road handling (sharp chassis, precise steering)
  • Overall reliability of JTDM diesel engines (among the best on the market)
  • DNA system allowing to modify the car's character
  • Excellent passive safety (5 Euro NCAP stars)

Weaknesses 5

  • Extremely fragile rear tailgate wiring harness (wires that cut)
  • Quality of some interior plastics and assemblies (rattles)
  • C635 manual gearbox notchy when cold (1st and 2nd gear)
  • Obsolete Blue&Me multimedia system and Bluetooth module prone to failures
  • Poor rear visibility and high boot sill

Check before buying

  • Open the boot and check the operation of ALL rear equipment (wiper, reverse lights, fog lights, boot opening): a malfunction indicates a severed tailgate wiring harness (very common).
  • On diesels (JTDM): check the DPF regeneration history and the absence of an engine warning light (EGR/DPF clogging).
  • On diesels (JTDM): ensure that the timing belt AND water pump have been changed recently (frequent water pump leaks).
  • On the 1.4 MultiAir: demand service invoices proving regular oil changes with the correct oil. A metallic clicking noise at the top of the engine indicates a faulty MultiAir module.
  • Check for swelling or detachment of the dashboard trim near the passenger airbag (known aesthetic defect due to heat).
  • Test gear changes when cold (1st to 2nd) to detect premature wear of the manual gearbox synchros.

Official recalls

  • - **November 2010**: Inspection and possible replacement of front suspension arms (risk of non-compliant welds). - **December 2011**: Recall concerning the front wiper motor (risk of malfunction under heavy load, e.g., snow). - **May 2012**: Inspection of the brake pedal (risk of a retaining clip detaching from the push rod).

Verdict

The Alfa Romeo Giulietta Phase 1 is a passionate purchase that remains reasonable, especially with a diesel engine (1.6 or 2.0 JTDM). These Fiat-sourced engines are true workhorses, capable of high mileages if maintenance is followed and if the car is driven enough to avoid DPF clogging. The main drawbacks of the Giulietta are not mechanical, but peripheral: the tailgate wiring harness cutting is a classic issue, as are minor interior rattles. For petrol, favor the simple 1.4 T-Jet 120 hp for total peace of mind, or be extremely vigilant about the service history if you opt for the powerful but temperamental 1.4 MultiAir.
