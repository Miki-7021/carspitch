BMW Série 2

G42 Coupé · 2021 – 2025

BUY Reliability 89/100 — excellent

Summary

The **BMW 2 Series Coupé (G42)**, launched in late 2021, represents a welcome exception in the modern automotive landscape. Unlike the 1 Series (F40) or 2 Series Gran Coupé (F44) which switched to front-wheel drive (UKL platform), the G42 retains BMW's noble architecture: the **CLAR platform**, with a longitudinal engine and rear-wheel drive (or rear-biased xDrive all-wheel drive).

This technical choice gives it first-class dynamic behavior, although the weight is significantly higher compared to the previous generation (F22). The interior was updated in 2022/2023 with the integration of the large curved dual screen (iDrive 8). In terms of reliability, the G42 benefits from the exceptional maturity of the modular "B" series engines (B48, B58, B47), making this coupé one of the most reliable models in the current BMW range. **Overall design score: 8.5/10.**

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of B48 and B58 engines
  • Pure rear-wheel drive handling (CLAR platform)
  • Significantly improved finish and assembly quality
  • ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox still as perfect as ever
  • Infotainment system (iDrive 7 then 8) fluid and ergonomic

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high weight for a compact coupé (often > 1600 kg)
  • Poor rear passenger space and access
  • Divisive design (especially the taillights and proportions)
  • Elite pricing and always expensive options policy
  • Disappearance of the manual gearbox (except on the M2)

Check before buying

  • Coolant level (B engines tend to consume very slightly, a rapid drop indicates a leak at the oil filter housing or water pump).
  • Operation of active grille shutters: a blockage can lead to a fault code or overheating.
  • Tire wear on xDrive versions: the tolerance for wear difference between front and rear is very low (risk of damaging the transfer case).
  • Software update history (iDrive 8) to avoid intermittent black screens.

Performance

  • Dynamic Performance and Proven Engines — The BMW 2 Series Coupe offers an exhilarating driving experience with its B48 and B58 petrol engines. The M240i, with its inline 6-cylinder engine, delivers supercar-like performance and generous torque, all paired with the perfect 8-speed ZF automatic transmission.

quality

  • Premium Finish and Advanced Technology — Step into a cabin where the quality of finish and assembly is significantly enhanced. The iDrive infotainment system (version 7 or 8), with its large curved dual screen, is fluid, intuitive, and ergonomic, offering a modern and connected user experience.

braking

  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — The BMW 2 Series Coupe is equipped with high-performance braking systems, ensuring excellent deceleration capability and remarkable endurance. Sport versions benefit from reinforced brakes for short stopping distances and a firm, progressive pedal feel, even during dynamic driving.

handling

  • Agile and Precise Road Handling — Built on the CLAR platform, this coupe offers agile and balanced road handling. Its precise and responsive steering, combined with great stability, ensures exceptional driving pleasure and total confidence in corners, even at the limits.

safety

  • Advanced Structural Safety — Benefiting from the CLAR platform, shared with BMW models that achieved 5 stars in Euro NCAP tests, the 2 Series Coupe incorporates a robust structural design for optimal occupant protection.

Alternatives

Verdict

The BMW 2 Series Coupé (G42) is an **excellent buy**. In an era where the automotive industry is turning towards front-wheel drive, small hybrid engines, and all-electric, the G42 stands out as a modern dinosaur, in a good way. The reliability of the petrol engines (B48 and B58) is now well-proven and ranks among the best in the premium market. The **220i or 230i** version will be more than sufficient for 90% of drivers thanks to a robust 2.0L block. For purists, the **M240i** with its B58 6-cylinder is a future collector's item, offering supercar performance with the reliability of a family sedan. Avoid the diesel (220d) unless you exclusively drive on highways.
