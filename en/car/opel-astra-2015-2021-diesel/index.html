Opel Astra

K · 2015 – 2021

NEGOTIATE Reliability 58/100 — moyen

All Opel models

Summary

The Opel Astra K, voted Car of the Year 2016, marks a major break from the heavy J generation. Lighter by 120 to 200 kg depending on the version, it is based on General Motors' D2XX platform. It stands out for its transformed road handling, a technological endowment rare for its time in this segment (IntelliLux LED matrix headlights, AGR ergonomic seats, advanced connectivity), and modernized engines.

Important historical note: Although Opel was acquired by PSA (Stellantis) in 2017, the Astra K is a pure General Motors product until the end of its life. Even during the 2019 facelift, the new 3-cylinder engines (petrol and diesel) are new GM blocks and not PSA engines (PureTech/BlueHDi), as the platform did not allow for cost-effective integration of French powertrains.

Diesel Focus: The core diesel range is provided by the 1.6 CDTI (4-cylinder), nicknamed « Whisper Diesel » at its launch for its quiet operation. It was replaced in 2019 by a new 1.5 Diesel (3-cylinder).

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic road handling and lightweight chassis
  • IntelliLux LED matrix lighting (exceptional for the category and its time)
  • AGR-certified ergonomic front seats (excellent support)
  • Very low real-world fuel consumption on 1.6 CDTI engines
  • Very favorable price/equipment ratio on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous timing chain reliability on the 1.6 CDTI (prohibitive repair cost)
  • Engine failure (pistons) on the 1.4 Turbo 125/150 hp (LSPI phenomenon)
  • Recurring bugs in the Intellilink multimedia system
  • Average sound insulation for road noise
  • Cost of replacing an IntelliLux headlight unit in case of failure or impact (> €1200 per unit)

Check before buying

  • Metallic noise (clanking/rattling) on cold start on 1.6 CDTI (Tensioner/Chain).
  • Oil history on the 1.4 Turbo petrol (Dexos 1 Gen 2 mandatory).
  • Clutch pedal behavior (must return firmly, without hard spots or looseness).
  • Functionality of all IntelliLux headlight LEDs (night test or via OBD diagnostic).
  • Absence of juddering or jerking during acceleration (Dual-mass flywheel on diesel, LSPI on petrol).

Official recalls

  • December 2018 (Braking - 1.6 CDTI): Major recall. Premature wear of the vacuum pump on 1.6 diesel engines can lead to a loss of braking assistance. Reprogramming or replacement of the pump.
  • November 2019 (Clutch): Possible hydraulic fluid leak at the clutch slave cylinder, which can lead to a loss of pressure and a risk of fire if the fluid drips onto a heat source.
  • April 2017 (Airbags): Inadvertent deployment or non-deployment of side/curtain airbags due to a wiring defect.
  • June 2016 (Steering): Risk of a steering column component detaching on a small series of early production models.

Alternatives

  • Asia/Oceania: Mazda 3 (exemplary reliability, superior finish), Toyota Corolla, Hyundai i30.

Verdict

The Opel Astra K is a car full of intrinsic qualities: lightweight, dynamic, economical, and over-equipped. However, the reliability record of its flagship engines is very bleak.

If you are looking for a Diesel, the 1.6 CDTI is a lottery: its timing chain problem on the gearbox side is a sword of Damocles, with repairs often exceeding €1500 to €2000. Avoid it, unless the chain and tensioner have just been replaced with supporting invoices. The post-2019 1.5 Diesel is a much safer choice.

For petrol, steer clear of the 1.4 Turbo 125/150 hp without a meticulous service history (risk of piston failure). The best choices remain the small 1.0 Turbo 105 hp (very reliable) or the post-2019 facelifted versions (1.2 Turbo).
