Tesla Model Y

I · 2020 – 2024

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The Tesla Model Y (Generation I) is a 100% electric compact SUV based on the Model 3 platform. Launched in 2020 in North America and 2021 in Europe, it became the best-selling car in the world in 2023, across all powertrains. Unlike traditional manufacturers, Tesla does not proceed with marked "restylings" (facelifts), but with continuous hardware and software improvements (OTA). The manufacturing location (Fremont, Shanghai, Berlin, Texas) has a major impact on assembly quality and the type of battery installed. Overall, the Model Y offers unparalleled energy efficiency, an exceptional native charging network, and cutting-edge technology, although its suspension comfort (on early models) and the absence of parking sensors (since late 2022) are subjects of debate.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent energy efficiency and real-world range
  • Integrated, reliable, and plug & charge Supercharger network
  • Gigantic interior space (rear trunk + front frunk)
  • Software updates (OTA) that genuinely improve the car
  • Ultra-smooth infotainment system (especially with AMD Ryzen)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very firm suspension comfort on 2020-2022 models
  • Absence of ultrasonic parking sensors since late 2022
  • Automatic wipers and automatic high beams sometimes erratic (camera-managed)
  • Occasional phantom braking under adaptive cruise control (Autopilot)
  • Fragile paint quality and uneven body panel gaps (especially Fremont models)

Check before buying

  • Front upper suspension wishbones: creaking or squeaking (known issue, covered under warranty if < 80,000 km).
  • Heat pump: test heating at full power, especially on 2020-2021 models (risk of compressor failure).
  • Water ingress: check for moisture in the rear sub-trunk and condensation in the taillights.
  • Tire wear: pronounced wear on the inner edge of the rear tires (geometry often poorly adjusted from the factory).
  • Wheels: check the condition of the wheels (especially the 21" Überturbine), very prominent and vulnerable.

Verdict

The Tesla Model Y is an essential choice in the used electric vehicle market. Its powertrain (motors and batteries) is extremely reliable, and its efficiency remains the segment's benchmark. The main concerns relate to the running gear (suspension wishbones) and divisive ergonomic choices (Tesla Vision without sensors). **Buying advice** : Prioritize a model produced from 2022 (AMD Ryzen processor) or ideally 2023 (Comfort suspension). Models manufactured in Shanghai (VIN starting with LRW) or Berlin (VIN starting with XP7) exhibit significantly superior assembly quality compared to early American models.
