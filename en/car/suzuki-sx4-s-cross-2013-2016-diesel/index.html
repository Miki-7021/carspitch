Suzuki SX4 S-Cross

I (JY) · 2013 – 2016

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation Suzuki SX4 S-Cross (phase 1, code JY) marks a break from the old SX4, designed with Fiat. Larger, it is positioned on the border between urban SUVs (B-SUV) and compact SUVs (C-SUV). Known for its pragmatism, it offers excellent roominess, a generous trunk (430 L) and a particularly effective 'AllGrip' AWD system. Although its exterior design was considered a bit bland before the late 2016 facelift, and its interior plastics are hard, it is an extremely rational and reliable vehicle. The 1.6 DDiS diesel engine, of Fiat origin (1.6 Multijet), spearheaded the launch in Europe, offering generous torque ideal for towing or for the AWD system.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent overall reliability, particularly in petrol (1.6 VVT)
  • Very capable AllGrip (4x4) system with 4 driving modes
  • Generous roominess and very decent trunk volume (430 L)
  • Torquey and very fuel-efficient 1.6 DDiS engine on road/highway
  • Very competitive price/equipment ratio on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Hard interior plastics, sensitive to scratches and prone to rattles
  • Soundproofing could be improved, especially with the 1.6 DDiS engine (cold clattering)
  • Slow and sometimes temperamental Bosch infotainment system (screen bugs)
  • FAP and EGR valve very sensitive to clogging on the diesel in urban use
  • TCSS automatic gearbox (on diesel) sometimes jerky at low speeds

Check before buying

  • FAP regeneration history and EGR valve condition (1.6 DDiS): avoid models that have been driven exclusively in the city.
  • Clattering noises at idle or vibrations at the biting point on the 1.6 DDiS (sign of a tired dual-mass flywheel).
  • Service book for AllGrip versions: verify that the axle oil changes have been carried out.
  • Operation of the touch screen and Bluetooth system (software updates sometimes necessary).
  • Tire wear on AllGrip versions (should ideally be replaced in sets of 4).

Verdict

The first-generation Suzuki SX4 S-Cross is an extremely rational used choice. If you are targeting the **1.6 DDiS**, you will benefit from a very robust Fiat-sourced engine (D16AA block), offering excellent torque for towing or exploiting the AllGrip system. However, like any modern diesel, it is imperative to buy it only if you make regular long trips; in urban use, the FAP and the EGR valve will cost you dearly. If your use is mixed or urban, turn without hesitation to the **1.6 VVT petrol**, whose reliability is legendary, even if it means sacrificing a little torque and acceleration.
