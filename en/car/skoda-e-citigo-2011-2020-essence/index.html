Skoda e-Citigo

I · 2011 – 2020 (Citigo-e iV : 2019 – 2020)

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The Skoda Citigo is part of the Volkswagen Group's 'New Small Family' (NSF) trio, alongside the VW Up! and the SEAT Mii. Designed as a pure city car (A-segment), it shines with its astonishing roominess given its size (3.56 m) and its very sound road handling. **Expert note regarding your request:** Although you are targeting the years 2019-2020 with a petrol engine, it is important to specify that Skoda stopped production of the petrol versions (1.0 MPI) in mid-2019. From late 2019 to 2020, the model was exclusively marketed as a 100% electric version under the name **Skoda Citigo-e iV**. This sheet exhaustively covers the excellent petrol engines (2011-2019) as well as the final electric version (2019-2020).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability (especially with the manual gearbox)
  • Front seat roominess and trunk volume (251 L) record for the category
  • Derisory fuel consumption of the 1.0 MPI (often under 5 L/100 km in real-world conditions)
  • Road handling worthy of a higher segment (very stable)
  • Highly relevant range of the electric version (Citigo-e iV)

Weaknesses 5

  • Light soundproofing on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • ASG automated gearbox (optional on petrol) is slow, jerky, and less reliable
  • Very bare-bones standard equipment on early trim levels (Active)
  • Reverse gear sometimes difficult to engage (manual gearbox)
  • Slightly firm suspension at low speeds

Check before buying

  • **Clutch:** Test the biting point. City cars often suffer from premature wear due to city driving.
  • **Gearbox (Manual):** Check that reverse and first gear engage without crunching (linkage or synchro problem).
  • **Gearbox (ASG):** To be avoided if possible. If present, check for the absence of shuddering when pulling away.
  • **Water pump (1.0 MPI):** Inspect for pink traces (coolant) on the left side of the engine.
  • **Air conditioning:** The condenser is very exposed to gravel at the front. Check that the A/C blows cold air.

Safety

  • Maximum Safety for All Occupants — With an impressive 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, the Citigo offers remarkable protection, ensuring 89% safety for adults and 80% for children, a major asset for a city car.

Practicality

  • Record Cabin Space and Generous Boot Volume — Despite its compact size, the Citigo surprises with its front cabin space and a boot volume of 251 liters, a record in its category, facilitating all your daily commutes and loads.

Economy

  • Ultra-Economical Running Costs — Enjoy incredibly low fuel consumption with the 1.0 MPI petrol engine, often under 5 L/100 km in real-world driving, or the highly relevant range of the electric Citigo-e iV version, significantly reducing your fuel budget.

Handling

  • Agile and Stable Road Handling — The Citigo offers road handling worthy of a higher segment, with exceptional agility and maneuverability in the city. The electric version benefits from a low center of gravity for increased stability and predictable driving.

Braking

  • Effective and Predictable Braking — Designed for urban environments, the Skoda Citigo ensures adequate and safe braking performance for its class. It provides reliable and predictable stopping power, guaranteeing serene daily driving.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Silent Urban Performance — The Citigo-e iV version, with its 83 hp, offers a lively and silent driving experience, ideal for urban and suburban journeys. Its responsiveness and driving pleasure make it a very enjoyable city car.

Verdict

The Skoda Citigo (and its electric version e-Citigo) is one of the most reliable and rational city cars on the used market. The 1.0 MPI petrol engine is a model of robustness, devoid of the complex problems associated with turbos or direct injections of the larger VW Group engines. It is a highly recommended purchase for urban use or young drivers. **Buying advice:** Favor a 60 or 75 hp 1.0 MPI version with a manual gearbox (absolutely avoid the ASG automated gearbox). If your budget and usage allow it, the electric Citigo-e iV version (2019-2020) is a little-known gem offering excellent urban range.
