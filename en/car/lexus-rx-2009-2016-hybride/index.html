Summary

The Lexus RX type AL10 (generally recognized as the 3rd global generation, marketed between 2009 and 2015/2016) is the undisputed pioneer of premium hybrid SUVs. Designed to offer royal comfort and exceptional quietness of operation, it has established itself as the most reliable alternative to German SUVs (X5, ML, Q7). In Europe, the RX 450h hybrid version accounted for the overwhelming majority of sales. This model shines with its hybrid powertrain (HSD) of almost unmatched robustness, coupled with an indestructible e-CVT transmission (epicyclic gear train). Its 2012 facelift introduced the famous trapezoidal hourglass grille ('Spindle Grille'). Overall reliability rating: Exceptional.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional mechanical and electronic reliability (often #1 in reliability surveys)
  • Indestructible and very smooth HSD hybrid system in use
  • Very reasonable fuel consumption for a 2-ton petrol V6 (around 8 to 9 L/100 km real-world for the 450h)
  • Very high level of suspension comfort and soundproofing
  • Quality of materials and assembly (excellent aging)
  • Lower maintenance cost than its German competitors (no timing belt, very slow brake wear thanks to regenerative braking)

Weaknesses 5

  • Dated, complex, and unergonomic infotainment system (Remote Touch)
  • Sluggish road handling, no sporty vocation (body roll)
  • Disappointing trunk volume (496 liters) for the size (due to the batteries)
  • Fragile 12V (accessory) battery if the vehicle is driven infrequently
  • Very expensive inverter replacement in case of failure out of warranty (although rare)

Check before buying

  • Demand a recent 'Hybrid Health Check' performed at Lexus (extends the battery warranty up to the vehicle's 10th year).
  • Check the cleaning history of the high-voltage battery ventilation filter.
  • Inspect the water pump (traces of pink/red coolant around the pulley).
  • Check the rear shock absorbers (frequent oil seepage on high-mileage models).
  • Test all electrical functions (heated/ventilated seats, electric tailgate, HUD) which are expensive to repair.

Official recalls

*(Expert note: The recall data below, from the recent official database, includes model codes of later generations (Gen 4/Gen 5) shared under the RX name. For the RX AL10 (2009-2016), major historical recalls concerned the hybrid inverter and floor mats).*

  • 07/11/2025: Software issue with the parking assist control unit (PVM) that can freeze the camera image. Risk of injury. (Concerns multiple models including RX).
  • 10/03/2025: High-pressure fuel pump. Insufficient pressure of the parts can cause cylinder movement, leading to a fire risk. (Concerns RX300 / NX200).
  • 22/08/2024: Camera housings. The housings can fall apart under stress due to incorrect welding, allowing water entry. Risk of injury.
  • 26/06/2024: Immobilizer. Insufficiently robust design allowing the vehicle to unexpectedly enter 'ready on' mode. (Concerns recent generations: RX350h, RX450h+, RX500h).

Verdict

The Lexus RX (AL10) is undoubtedly one of the most reliable used SUVs in the world. If you are looking for a premium family vehicle that is extremely comfortable and free of mechanical worries, it is the number one choice. The RX 450h version is absolutely to be preferred for its driving pleasure and controlled fuel consumption. Purchase is highly recommended, on the sole condition of verifying that maintenance has been followed (especially the hybrid health checks at Lexus) and that the water pump is not leaking. Avoid examples that have towed heavy loads without a clear history of transmission oil changes.
