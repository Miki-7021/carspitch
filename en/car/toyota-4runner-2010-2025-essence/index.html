Toyota 4Runner

V (N280) · 2010 – 2025

BUY Reliability 95/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The fifth-generation Toyota 4Runner (N280) is a true dinosaur of the automotive industry, and that is exactly what makes it so successful. Launched in 2009 for the 2010 model year and facelifted in 2014, it sits on an authentic ladder frame (body-on-frame). Designed for off-roading and extreme longevity, it deliberately ignores modern trends (downsizing, 8- or 10-speed gearboxes, complex hybridization) in favor of proven mechanics. According to J.D. Power and Consumer Reports, it consistently dominates its segment in terms of reliability. It is an off-road icon, particularly in North America, which retains exceptional resale value (65 to 70% of its value after 5 years, compared to 45% for the segment average). Overall reliability rating: Exceptional.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability (indestructible 1GR-FE engine and A750F gearbox)
  • Very high-level off-road capabilities (especially TRD Off-Road and TRD Pro versions)
  • Stratospheric resale value (almost non-existent depreciation in some markets)
  • Vast cargo space and roll-down rear tailgate window (model signature)
  • Breakdown rate below 10% over 3 years (compared to 20% for the sector average)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high fuel consumption (average of 13 to 15 L/100 km)
  • Dated road handling (nose dive under braking, body roll in corners)
  • Slow and technologically outdated 5-speed automatic gearbox
  • Rustic interior and aging onboard technologies
  • Anemic 2.7L engine (2010) to be avoided

Check before buying

  • Chassis corrosion: Carefully inspect the frame rails and crossmembers, a historical weak point of Toyota 4x4s.
  • KDSS (Kinetic Dynamic Suspension System): On equipped models, check for leaks on the hydraulic cylinders and accumulator (very expensive repair).
  • Front differential needle bearing: Can cause cyclical vibrations at the front, particularly if the vehicle has been lifted (lift kit).
  • Transfer case actuators: Ensure that shifting into 4H and 4L occurs without binding (the electric motors can seize if not used regularly).

Official recalls

NHTSA Recalls (United States - Main Market)

  • Takata Airbags (2016-2019): Multiple recalls (e.g., 19V741000, 19V005000, 18V024000) regarding the front passenger airbag inflator which may explode. Affects 2010-2016 models.
  • Fuel Pump (2020): Recalls 20V682000 and 20V012000. Failure of the low-pressure fuel pump (Denso) which can lead to engine stall while driving (2018-2019 models).
  • Distributor Accessories (SET / GST): Numerous minor recalls related to accessories installed by American regional distributors (Southeast Toyota / Gulf States Toyota): incorrect load labels (24V548000, 22V480000), exhaust tips (16V577000), roof rack fasteners (17V291000), poorly glued hood scoops (17V425000), heated seats (16V396000).

What sets it apart 5

reliability

  • Legendary Mechanical Reliability — The 4.0L V6 (1GR-FE) engine and A750F automatic transmission are benchmarks for robustness, designed for exceptional longevity and lasting performance.
  • Proven Durability and Low Breakdown Rate — With a breakdown rate of less than 10% over 3 years, this vehicle is a guarantee of peace of mind, designed for unwavering reliability and extreme longevity.

Performance

  • Unrivaled Off-Road Capabilities — Built on an authentic body-on-frame chassis, the 4Runner excels off-road, offering very high-level conquering capabilities, especially in its TRD versions.

economy

  • Exceptional Resale Value — Benefit from minimal depreciation and remarkable value retention, with the 4Runner keeping 65 to 70% of its value after 5 years, well above the market average.

Alternatives

North America & Imports

  • Jeep Grand Cherokee: More comfortable, more technological, but significantly less reliable in the long term.
  • Jeep Wrangler (Unlimited): Better pure off-roader, removable roof, but inferior on-road comfort and hit-or-miss reliability.
  • Ford Bronco: Recent direct competitor, very capable off-road, modern turbo engines, but hindsight on reliability is still limited.
  • Lexus GX 460: The luxury cousin (based on the Land Cruiser Prado). Offers a 4.6L V8, more comfort, the same legendary reliability, but even higher fuel consumption.

Verdict

The Toyota 4Runner (N280) is an exceptional purchase for anyone who prioritizes absolute reliability, durability, and off-road capabilities over modernity and fuel economy. Its powertrain (4.0L V6 + 5-speed automatic gearbox) is a technological relic, but that is precisely what makes it indestructible. It is imperative to check the chassis for corrosion and the proper functioning of the KDSS system (if equipped) before purchase. Avoid the very rare 2010 2.7L 4-cylinder version. If you accept its gargantuan fuel consumption and clumsy road handling, it is a vehicle you can pass down to your children.
