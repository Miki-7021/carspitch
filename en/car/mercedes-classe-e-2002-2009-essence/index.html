Summary

The Mercedes-Benz E-Class (W211 for the sedan, S211 for the estate) is an iconic executive car that marked a major technological and stylistic leap compared to the W210. However, its history is split into two distinct eras. Phase 1 (2002-2006) was marred by major electronic reliability issues, notably the infamous *SBC (Sensotronic Brake Control)* braking system. Phase 2 (Facelift, mid-2006-2009) corrected almost all of these flaws (abandonment of SBC for conventional hydraulic braking, improved manufacturing quality), transforming the W211 into one of the most reliable executive cars of its time. In terms of petrol engines, the transition between the robust but older M112/M113 blocks and the new M272/M273 also brought its share of challenges (balance shaft sprockets).

Strengths 5

  • Imperial ride comfort, especially with Airmatic
  • Build quality and sound insulation (especially Phase 2)
  • Exceptional reliability of M112/M113 (petrol) and OM646/OM648 (diesel) engines
  • Timeless design that ages very well
  • Spacious interior and gigantic boot volume on the estate (S211)

Weaknesses 5

  • SBC braking system on Phase 1 (financial time bomb)
  • Balance shaft sprocket issue on early new-generation V6/V8s (M272/M273)
  • Maintenance cost of Airmatic air suspension
  • Capricious electronics on 2002-2004 models (battery drain, sensors)
  • 7G-Tronic gearbox (early generations) sometimes harsh and requiring strict fluid changes

Check before buying

  • SBC system history (if Phase 1): has it been replaced recently?
  • Engine serial number on E280/E350/E500 (2005-2008) to rule out balance shaft defect.
  • Airmatic suspension operation (raise/lower the car, listen to the compressor).
  • Traces of oil in the coolant or vice versa (Valeo radiator on 2002-2003).
  • Timing chain noise when cold on 4-cylinder petrol engines (M271).
  • Automatic gearbox (5G-Tronic or 7G-Tronic) gear changes: should be smooth, without jerks.

Official recalls

  • May 2004 / March 2005 (SBC): Massive recall for reprogramming or replacement of the SBC hydraulic unit following loss of braking assistance.
  • 2020 / 2023 (Sunroof): Recall on many older models (including W211) because the sunroof glass panel can detach due to adhesive degradation.
  • 2008 (Crankshaft sensor): Replacement on certain V6/V8 engines.
  • 2003 (Seat belts): Check of front seat belt buckles.

Verdict

The Mercedes E-Class W211 is a car with two faces. Phase 1 models (2002-2006) should be approached with extreme caution due to the SBC system and early electronic issues. In contrast, a Phase 2 (2006-2009) is one of the best modern Mercedes ever built. For a petrol purchase, the safest and most economical choice is an E 240 or E 320 (Phase 1, if the SBC has been changed and radiator checked) or, ideally, an E 350 Phase 2 from late 2008 or 2009 (with the M272 engine made reliable). V8 enthusiasts will turn to the indestructible E 500 (M113 engine, 2002-2006) or the legendary E 55 AMG, keeping a substantial budget for peripheral maintenance (Airmatic).
