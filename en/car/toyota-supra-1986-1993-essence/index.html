Toyota Supra

Summary

The **Toyota Supra A70 (Mk3)** marks a historical turning point: it is the first generation to completely detach from the Celica to become a standalone model. Positioned as a true Grand Tourer (GT) rather than a pure lightweight sports car, it stands out for its comfort, its onboard technologies (TEMS electronically controlled suspension, ABS), and its significant weight (often over 1,500 kg). It underwent a major facelift (Phase 2) in late 1988 / early 1989, modifying the front fascia, taillights, and steering wheel. Although famous for its timeless pop-up headlight design, its mechanical reputation is mixed: while the JZ blocks introduced late in its career are legendary, the 7M engines suffer from a factory design flaw (head gasket) that requires imperative reliability upgrades. **Overall generation rating: 7.5/10 (post-reliability upgrade).**

Strengths 5

  • Iconic 80s/90s design (pop-up headlights, sleek lines)
  • Exceptional ride comfort for long journeys (true GT)
  • 1JZ-GTE engine (on JDM versions) of exceptional robustness
  • Very rich standard equipment for its time (auto AC, cruise control, electric seats)
  • Very high mechanical tuning potential (once reliability issues are addressed)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high weight (1,500 - 1,600 kg) penalizing agility
  • Major head gasket design flaw on 7M engines
  • Availability of body and interior trim parts has become critical
  • High fuel consumption (especially 7M-GTE)
  • Tendency to corrosion (a common issue for Japanese cars of that era)

Check before buying

  • **Head Gasket History (7M):** Demand invoices proving the installation of a metal head gasket (MHG) and reinforced studs (ARP).
  • **Corrosion:** Inspect the rear wheel arches, rocker panels, windshield cowl, and trunk floor (under the spare wheel).
  • **Targa Roof (Sport Roof):** Check the condition of the seals. Water leaks are very common and lead to rust in the cabin.
  • **TEMS Suspension:** Verify that the electronically controlled shock absorbers are functioning (original replacement parts are unobtainable, often replaced by coilovers).
  • **Electronics:** Functionality of the digital dashboard (if equipped), air conditioning, and check for absence of engine misfires (sign of a faulty ECU).

Verdict

The Toyota Supra A70 is a formidable time machine, offering undeniable '80s charm. However, **it is not a purchase for novices**. If you are targeting a European or American version (7M engines), the purchase is conditional on absolute proof that the head gasket issue has been resolved properly (MHG + ARP). Without this, plan for an immediate repair budget. Japanese imported versions equipped with the 1JZ-GTE are mechanically superior but involve right-hand drive (RHD). In all cases, hunt for rust with the utmost severity.
