Mercedes EQA

H243 · 2021 – 2025

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The Mercedes-Benz EQA (H243 generation) is the German manufacturer's entry-level 100% electric compact SUV. Derived directly from the combustion-engine GLA (adapted MFA2 platform, not a dedicated electric platform), it retains the overall silhouette but stands out with specific front and rear fascias (light bars, closed grille). Launched in early 2021, it received a facelift in late 2023 (new star-pattern grille, capacitive steering wheel, aerodynamic optimization for range). While it shines with its ride comfort, premium soundproofing, and cutting-edge MBUX infotainment system, it suffers from the compromises of its shared platform: a very high weight (over 2 tons) and a reduced trunk volume (340 liters compared to 435 liters for a GLA). Overall reliability is good, with the main annoyances focusing on early software bugs and premature wear of the running gear due to the excess weight.

Strengths 5

  • Suspension comfort (especially with adaptive damping)
  • Very meticulous soundproofing (wind and road noise well filtered)
  • Responsive, comprehensive MBUX multimedia system and efficient electric route planner
  • Rewarding interior finish (materials, ambient lighting)
  • Very decent range on the 250+ version

Weaknesses 5

  • Excessive weight (over 2040 kg) penalizing agility and consumption
  • Very disappointing trunk volume for a 4.46 m SUV (340 liters)
  • Average rear roominess (raised floor due to batteries, high knees)
  • DC fast charging speed limited to 100 kW peak (a bit low compared to the competition)
  • Spongy brake pedal feel that is difficult to modulate (regeneration/friction transition)

Check before buying

  • Tire wear: the weight and electric torque prematurely wear out the rubber, especially at the front on FWD versions (250/250+).
  • Running gear: clunking noises over speed bumps (silent blocks, stabilizer bar links fatigued by the weight).
  • Charging port door: socket locking mechanism sometimes temperamental (occasionally requires emergency manual unlocking).
  • MBUX system: check for the absence of random reboots or black screens (requires a dealership update if OTA fails).
  • Charging cables: verify the presence and good condition of the original supplied Type 2 (T2) cables and home charger.

Verdict

The Mercedes EQA is a premium electric SUV that will appeal to fans of comfort, silence, and onboard technology. Its plush interior and MBUX system are its best assets. However, its combustion-engine origins (shared platform with the GLA) impose heavy compromises: a cramped trunk, average rear roominess, and a pachydermic weight that hampers dynamism and wears out the running gear. In terms of reliability, the electric foundation is sound, with problems limited to software bugs and minor recalls.

**Buying advice:** Without hesitation, favor the **EQA 250+** version. Its more efficient synchronous motor and slightly larger battery (70.5 kWh) offer the best range in the lineup, partially erasing the flaw of highway consumption. Avoid 20-inch wheels, which degrade comfort and range.
