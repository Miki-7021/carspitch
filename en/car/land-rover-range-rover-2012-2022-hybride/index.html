Land Rover Range Rover

IV (L405) · 2012 – 2022

NEGOTIATE Reliability 52/100 — faible

Summary

The fourth-generation Range Rover (code name L405) marked a major technological breakthrough by adopting an all-aluminum monocoque structure, allowing it to shed up to 420 kg compared to its predecessor. Positioned at the pinnacle of automotive luxury, it combines exceptional off-road capabilities with comfort worthy of the best limousines. The 2018 facelift introduced the Touch Pro Duo infotainment system and, most importantly, the P400e plug-in hybrid powertrain, designed to meet emissions standards and offer tax advantages. Although majestic, this model suffers from a highly mixed reliability reputation, particularly due to its complex electronic architecture and certain problematic engines.

Strengths 5

  • Imperial ride comfort and exceptional soundproofing
  • Outstanding off-road capabilities (Terrain Response 2)
  • Material quality and cabin finish
  • Tax advantages of the P400e version (depending on the country)
  • Interior space, particularly in the LWB (long wheelbase) version

Weaknesses 5

  • Highly temperamental overall electronic reliability
  • Complexity and fragility of the P400e hybrid system
  • Frequent engine failure on Diesel V6s (TDV6/SDV6)
  • Astronomical maintenance, insurance, and repair costs
  • High weight (even in aluminum) causing rapid wear on brakes and tires

Check before buying

  • Crystal-clear maintenance history exclusively from the network or a Land Rover specialist
  • Air suspension operation (smooth raising/lowering, no sagging after parking)
  • Infotainment system (sluggishness, black screens, touch bugs)
  • Signs of coolant leaks (petrol V6/V8) in the center of the V
  • On the P400e: test full charge, real-world EV range, and the combustion/electric transition

What sets it apart 10

security

  • Unrivalled Safety and Absolute Control — The Range Rover L405 achieved 5 stars in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 91% for adult protection and 84% for child protection. Its braking system is rated as excellent and reassuring, offering competent stopping distances. Handling is characterized by exceptional stability and sovereign road manners, both on-road and off-road, ensuring a secure drive in all conditions.

economy

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Porsche Cayenne (very reliable in PHEV), BMW X7, Mercedes-Benz GLS, Audi Q8. - **North America**: Cadillac Escalade, Lincoln Navigator, Lexus LX (absolute benchmark for reliability). - **Asia / Middle East**: Lexus LX, Toyota Land Cruiser, Mercedes-Benz G-Class.

Verdict

The Range Rover L405 is a paradoxical vehicle: it offers an unparalleled driving experience and luxury, but demands a colossal maintenance budget and a high tolerance for breakdowns. The **P400e** hybrid version is attractive on paper (taxation, silence), but it superimposes the complexity of a high-voltage electrical system onto an already temperamental basic electronic architecture. If you opt for a P400e, demand a model under manufacturer warranty or with a comprehensive extended warranty covering the battery and electronics. **Absolutely avoid the Diesel V6s (TDV6/SDV6)** due to the risk of crankshaft failure. The 'safest' choices (all things considered) remain the Supercharged V8s (with preventive maintenance of the cooling system) or the recent Ingenium inline-6 engines.
