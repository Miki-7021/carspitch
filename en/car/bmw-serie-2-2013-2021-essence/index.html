BMW Série 2

F22/F23 Coupé/Cabrio · 2013 – 2021

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The BMW 2 Series (F22 for the Coupe, F23 for the Convertible) succeeds the 1 Series Coupe (E82). Based on a rear-wheel-drive architecture (or xDrive all-wheel drive), it embodies the very essence of BMW's compact sports coupe. Praised for its dynamic balance, ideal weight distribution (50/50), and the excellence of its 6-cylinder engines, it underwent a facelift (LCI) in 2017, bringing new LED headlights, a modernized interior, and a technological update. The transition from the old-generation engines (N20/N55) to the new modular architecture (B48/B58) in 2016 marked a major turning point in terms of reliability.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road handling (RWD, 50/50 weight distribution)
  • Responsive and reliable ZF 8-speed automatic transmission (BVA8)
  • Remarkable reliability of 'B' generation engines (B48, B58)
  • Improved finish and assembly quality (especially post-2017 LCI)
  • Very intuitive iDrive infotainment system

Weaknesses 5

  • Very limited rear passenger space
  • Reliability of N20 (timing chain) and N47 (timing chain) engines before 2015
  • High maintenance cost at official dealerships
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (especially on F23 Convertible)
  • Sometimes stingy standard equipment on lower trims

Check before buying

  • Clear service history (essential for 6-cylinder and M models)
  • Condition of the oil filter housing gasket (OFHG) on N55 engines
  • Smooth operation of the soft top on F23 and absence of moisture in the trunk (clogged drains)
  • Presence of condensation or water in the taillights (known sealing issue)
  • Clunking noises in the steering (often requires a steering rack repair kit - *Thrust piece*)
  • Proof of ZF8 automatic transmission fluid change if mileage exceeds 80,000 km

Official recalls

  • **EGR Cooler (2018-2022)**: Massive recall on diesel engines (B47) due to risk of glycol leakage potentially leading to fire.
  • **Takata Airbags (2020)**: Replacement of the driver's airbag gas generator (risk of metallic fragment projection).
  • **Steering Rack (2015)**: On some models, the steering gear housing bolts could loosen.
  • **Fuel Pump (2014-2015)**: Replacement of the low-pressure fuel pump on some petrol models (risk of stalling).

Reliability

  • Reliability of Modern Engines — The 'B' generation engines (B48, B58) are renowned for their remarkable reliability, offering robust and durable performance, notably the B58 in the M240i, a masterpiece of engineering.

Safety

  • High-Level Active and Passive Safety — The BMW 2 Series achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult and child protection.
  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — Benefit from excellent braking performance, with short stopping distances and very good fade resistance, even under intensive use.

Comfort

  • Premium Interior Quality and Intuitive Technology — The cabin stands out with improved finish and assembly quality, especially after the 2017 facelift, complemented by the highly intuitive iDrive infotainment system.

Economy

  • A Future Rear-Wheel Drive Classic — The 2 Series is considered a future classic, representing one of the last compact rear-wheel drive BMWs, a key asset for its value and unique character.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Audi TT (FWD/Quattro, superior interior finish but less playful), Porsche Cayman (more expensive, mid-engine), Mercedes C-Class Coupe (more bourgeois). - **North America**: Ford Mustang EcoBoost/V8, Chevrolet Camaro, Nissan 370Z. - **Japan / Asia**: Toyota GR86 / Subaru BRZ (similar RWD philosophy, but less refined naturally aspirated engines), Lexus RC.

Verdict

The BMW 2 Series (F22/F23) is a future classic, representing one of the last true compact rear-wheel-drive BMWs. **Engine choice is crucial.** Models equipped with 'B' series engines (230i with the B48, M240i with the B58) produced after 2016 are remarkably reliable and represent the best buys. Earlier versions (228i, M235i) remain desirable but require a more substantial preventive maintenance budget (timing chain on N20, peripherals on N55). To avoid: unreliabilized N47 diesels and N20 petrol models without a clear service history.
