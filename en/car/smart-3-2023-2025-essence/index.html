Summary

**EXPERT WARNING:** Contrary to the query targeting a "petrol" engine, the **Smart #3 (HX12 generation) is an EXCLUSIVELY ELECTRIC model (BEV)**. There is no petrol, diesel, or hybrid version.

The Smart #3 is a 100% electric compact coupe SUV born from the joint venture between Mercedes-Benz (in charge of design) and the Chinese group Geely (in charge of engineering and the SEA platform). Longer, lower, and more aerodynamic than the Smart #1, it is positioned as a more dynamic alternative. Being a very recent model, hindsight on long-term mechanical reliability is limited, but Geely's SEA platform (shared with the Volvo EX30 and Zeekr X) shows solid hardware foundations. The main challenges for this model lie in software stability.

**Overall design rating: 8/10**

Strengths 5

  • Distinctive design and careful aerodynamics (0.27 Cd)
  • Top-flight performance, even on base versions (272 hp)
  • Generous interior space thanks to the long wheelbase (SEA platform)
  • Interior finish quality (Mercedes-Benz supervision)
  • More dynamic and rigorous road handling than the Smart #1

Weaknesses 5

  • Complex all-touch ergonomics and sometimes temperamental multimedia system
  • Driving aids (ADAS) deemed too intrusive and noisy by users
  • Very poor rear visibility (sloping rear window)
  • Trunk volume (370 L) disappointing for the size (4.40 m)
  • Firm suspension, particularly on the Brabus version (20-inch wheels)

Check before buying

  • Onboard software version (SmartOS): demand the latest update before purchase.
  • Smooth operation of the central screen and the companion mobile app.
  • Condition of the tires (the immediate torque of 343 to 543 Nm wears out the rubber quickly).
  • Body panel alignment (some disparities on the very first 2023 units).
  • Locking/unlocking mechanism of the charging flap.

Verdict

The Smart #3 is a highly accomplished electric coupe SUV on the hardware front thanks to Geely's excellent SEA platform. It offers first-rate performance and a presentation worthy of the premium segment. The absence of an internal combustion engine (petrol/diesel) is offset by efficient electric powertrains. The main point of vigilance does not concern the mechanics, but the **software** (interface bugs, annoying driving aids). It is a recommendable purchase, provided you ensure that the OTA updates have been carried out. The **Premium (NMC 66 kWh)** version represents the best range/equipment compromise.
