Summary

The seventh-generation Nissan Sentra (B17), also known as the Nissan Sylphy in certain Asian markets, is a compact sedan. Not officially sold in Western Europe, it has enjoyed great success in North America, Asia, and Latin America. Designed to offer maximum interior space and comfort at an affordable price, it falls short, however, with subpar driving dynamics and modest performance. A major facelift occurred in 2016, bringing better soundproofing, revised suspension, and a modernized design. While the gasoline engines are generally very robust, the reliability of this generation is heavily marred by frequent failures of its continuously variable automatic transmission (Jatco CVT), particularly on the 2013 to 2015 models.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional roominess for the category (rear legroom)
  • Very generous trunk volume (428 liters)
  • Suspension comfort (especially after the 2016 facelift)
  • Inherently very reliable naturally aspirated engines (1.8L and 1.6L)
  • Controlled fuel consumption in smooth driving
  • Attractive price on the used market

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of the Jatco CVT gearbox on 2013-2015 model years
  • Anemic performance of the naturally aspirated 1.8L
  • Clumsy road handling and uncommunicative steering
  • Dated and slow infotainment system
  • Soundproofing could be improved on pre-2016 models
  • Numerous recalls regarding the airbag system and seat sensors

Check before buying

  • CVT gearbox behavior: Test drive the car cold and hot. Walk away if there is jerking, excessive slipping, whining, or hesitation upon starting.
  • CVT maintenance history: Demand invoices proving the gearbox oil was changed every 50,000 km maximum.
  • Airbag warning light: Verify that it turns on with the ignition and then turns off. OCS (Occupant Classification System) sensor problems are endemic.
  • Brake lights: Verify their proper operation (recall on the brake pedal switch which can get dirty).

What sets it apart 11

Comfort

  • Generous rear legroom for passengers — The cabin offers exceptional legroom in the back, so adult passengers can sit comfortably on longer trips without feeling cramped.
  • Comfortable suspension after 2016 update — The revised suspension introduced with the 2016 restyling absorbs road imperfections better, making everyday commuting noticeably more comfortable.
  • Quiet cabin from 2016 onwards — Improved sound insulation after the restyling reduces road and engine noise, making long journeys calmer for driver and passengers.

Practicality

  • Large trunk for daily practicality — With a 428-litre boot, the Sentra swallows weekly shopping, luggage or strollers easily, reducing the need for a larger vehicle.

Reliability

  • Proven naturally aspirated engine reliability — The 1.8L and 1.6L atmospheric engines are intrinsically very robust, so buyers avoid costly engine repairs when maintenance is kept up.
  • Manual gearbox versions highly reliable — Rare manual-transmission examples are described as extremely reliable, offering a safer long-term choice than the troubled early CVT units.

Economy

  • Controlled fuel use when driving smoothly — Fuel consumption stays reasonable in relaxed driving, which keeps running costs low for commuters covering regular daily distances.
  • Attractive used-market pricing — The model is affordable on the second-hand market, letting budget-conscious buyers get a spacious compact sedan for a modest outlay.
  • Chain-driven engine reduces upkeep — The 1.8L engine uses a timing chain, so owners avoid scheduled timing belt replacements and the associated workshop costs.
  • Economical 1.6L atmospheric maintenance — The HR16DE engine is proven and cheap to service, keeping routine maintenance costs low for urban and suburban drivers.

Design

  • Modernised design after restyling — The 2016 update brought a refreshed exterior look, giving buyers a more contemporary appearance without paying for a newer generation.

Verdict

The Nissan Sentra B17 is a rational, spacious, and comfortable compact sedan, ideal for small budgets looking for an everyday vehicle. However, the catastrophic reliability of the CVT gearbox on the 2013 to 2015 models requires the utmost caution. If you must buy this model, absolutely favor the facelifted versions (2016-2019) where the CVT was improved, or look for the rare manual gearbox versions which are extremely reliable. A crystal-clear maintenance history, including automatic gearbox oil changes, is non-negotiable.
