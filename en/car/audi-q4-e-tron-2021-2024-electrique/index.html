I (F4Z) · 2021 – 2024

All Audi models

Summary

The Audi Q4 e-tron (and its Sportback coupe variant) represents the 100% electric entry-level model of the brand with the four rings. Based on the Volkswagen Group's MEB platform (shared with the VW ID.4 and Skoda Enyaq), it stands out with a more premium finish, meticulous soundproofing, and comfort-oriented damping. While it shines with its interior space and driving pleasure, it suffered in its early days from the same ailments as its technical cousins: an unstable and improvable software interface (infotainment), progressively corrected via updates (OTA and at the dealership). At the end of 2023, the model received a major technical update (new APP550 motors on the 45 and 55 versions, optimized battery, increased charging power).

Strengths 5

  • Excellent interior space (rear legroom worthy of the segment above)
  • Very meticulous soundproofing, premium ride comfort
  • Exceptional turning radius on rear-wheel drive versions (35, 40, 45)
  • Superior assembly quality compared to its cousins (VW ID.4, Skoda Enyaq)
  • Efficiency significantly improved on 45 and 55 versions (APP550 motor)

Weaknesses 5

  • Improvable ergonomics (annoying haptic steering wheel buttons)
  • Frequent software bugs on 2021-2022 model years (black screens, disconnections)
  • Very high weight (over 2.1 tons) impacting driving dynamics
  • Average DC fast charging power on early versions (125-135 kW)
  • Hard plastics in the lower part of the cabin, unworthy of the price

Check before buying

  • Software update history (demand proof of upgrade to at least version 3.2 for 2021/2022 models)
  • Tire wear condition (instant torque and weight prematurely wear out the tires, especially at the rear on rear-wheel drive models)
  • Operation of the central screen and rearview camera (prone to display bugs)
  • Condition of the charging flap and cable locking mechanism (sometimes temperamental)
  • Actual replacement of the 12V battery as part of the manufacturer campaigns

What sets it apart 7

Safety

  • Top-tier Safety — The Audi Q4 e-tron achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 93% for adult occupant protection and 89% for child occupant protection, ensuring optimal peace of mind.

Braking

  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Enjoy strong and competitive braking performance, with a stopping distance measured at just 52.7 meters from 113 km/h, ensuring perfect control in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Stable and Predictable Road Handling — The Q4 e-tron offers secure dynamic behavior, a composed and comfortable ride, with well-controlled body roll and precise steering, for an overall sense of safety on the road.

Comfort

  • Premium Comfort and Soundproofing — Benefit from exceptional ride comfort and very refined soundproofing, transforming every journey into a serene and pleasant experience, worthy of a higher segment vehicle.

Practicality

  • Generous Interior Space — Rear legroom is comparable to a vehicle from a higher segment, offering excellent spaciousness for all passengers and making the Q4 e-tron an ideal family SUV.

Reliability

  • Superior Build Quality — The Audi Q4 e-tron stands out with its premium assembly quality and finish, surpassing its direct competitors and guaranteeing exemplary robustness and durability.

Performance

  • Optimized Efficiency and Enhanced Performance — Recent versions (from late 2023) benefit from the new APP550 motor, offering improved thermal efficiency, increased power, and optimized range for a dynamic and economical driving experience.

North America * Tesla Model Y: Undisputed market leader, best price/range ratio. * Ford Mustang Mach-E: Sportier, responsive software interface. * Hyundai Ioniq 5 / Kia EV6: 800V architecture (much faster charging), distinctive design.

Verdict

The Audi Q4 e-tron is a highly accomplished family electric SUV in terms of comfort, interior space, and manufacturing quality. Mechanically, the MEB platform is robust and the electric motors are reliable. However, the 2021 and 2022 model years were marred by numerous software bugs (infotainment, charging management, rearview camera) that imperatively require dealership updates.

Buying advice: Favor the 40 e-tron version for its excellent range-to-price ratio. If your budget allows, aim for a model produced from late 2023 (45 or 55 versions): they benefit from the new motor (more powerful and more efficient), improved charging power, and finally mature software. Avoid 2021 models that do not have a crystal-clear maintenance history regarding software updates and the 12V battery replacement.
