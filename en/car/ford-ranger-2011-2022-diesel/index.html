Summary

The **Ford Ranger T6** (PX1, PX2, PX3 generations) is a mid-size pickup developed by Ford Australia for the global market. Launched in 2011 (PX1), it received a major facelift in 2015 (PX2 - interior and driving aids improvement) and a technical update in 2018 (PX3 - introduction of the 2.0L EcoBlue engines and the 10-speed automatic gearbox, as well as the Raptor version).

A true best-seller in Europe and Oceania, it stands out for its excellent towing capacities (up to 3.5 tonnes), its SUV-like comfort, and its overall robustness. Reliability data (ADAC, TÜV) place it at the top of its category, particularly with the 2.2L and 3.2L diesel engines paired with the 6-speed Aisin automatic gearbox.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent overall reliability of the Puma diesel engines (2.2L and 3.2L) confirmed by ADAC (2.8 breakdowns/1000 veh/year) and TÜV (10.5% defect rate at 3 years).
  • Extremely robust 6-speed Aisin automatic gearbox (on 2.2 and 3.2).
  • Towing capacity of 3,500 kg (among the best of its generation).
  • Road handling and suspension comfort above the pickup average (Hilux, Navara).
  • Very reassuring What Car? reliability score (4/5, only 15% minor breakdowns reported over 12 months).

Weaknesses 5

  • Dual-mass flywheel (manual gearbox) showing signs of fatigue after 150,000 km (juddering).
  • Chassis rust sensitivity on early models (PX1 2011-2014).
  • 2.0L Bi-Turbo engine (PX3/Raptor) more complex and prone to oil leaks and transmission issues (10-speed gearbox).
  • Imposing size making urban parking difficult.
  • Rear differential can fail under very heavy loads (official 2022 recall).

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history of the 3.2L: ensure oil changes were done by professionals familiar with the oil pump peculiarity (loss of prime).
  • Condition of the chassis and underbody (surface rust, off-road impacts).
  • Operation of the transfer case (shifting into 4H and 4L) and the rear differential lock.
  • On 2.0L EcoBlue versions: check for oil leaks around the timing cover and smooth shifting of the 10 gears (no jerking).
  • Check if the recall concerning the driveshaft and rear differential has been performed.

Verdict

The **Ford Ranger T6** is an excellent choice on the used market, justifying its status as a sales leader. ADAC and TÜV statistics confirm that it is a **very reliable** vehicle, provided you choose the right engine.

**We highly recommend the 2.2L and 3.2L engines (PX1 and PX2 generations)**. The 5-cylinder 3.2L is a towing monster, while the 2.2L is perfect for daily use. The 6-speed automatic gearbox is a model of robustness.

**Be more cautious with recent models (PX3) equipped with the 2.0L Bi-Turbo and the 10-speed gearbox**. Although more efficient and economical, they have suffered from teething problems (leaks, transmission pump) requiring strict monitoring of manufacturer recalls. Systematically check the condition of the chassis (rust) on pre-2015 models.
