Summary

The sixth-generation Hyundai Elantra (code AD) marks an evolution towards maturity for the Korean compact sedan. Abandoning the exuberant lines of the previous generation (MD) for a more sober and statutory European-inspired design, it benefits from a chassis made of 53% ultra-high-strength steel, significantly improving torsional rigidity and soundproofing.

Globally recognized for its excellent price-to-equipment ratio and generous manufacturer warranty, the Elantra AD displays a reliability record ranging from "Average to Good" according to ADAC and J.D. Power. Important point: Unlike other models of the brand (Sonata, Santa Fe), the Elantra AD is not affected by the infamous recall of the Theta II engines. It remains a rational and economical alternative to the Japanese benchmarks, although one must be vigilant regarding certain series of the 2.0L engine (piston rings) and the behavior of the automatic gearboxes (DCT/IVT).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional manufacturer warranty (often 5 years unlimited mileage in Europe, 10 years/100,000 miles on the powertrain in the USA)
  • Rigid chassis and secure road handling (especially the Sport version with independent rear suspension)
  • Intuitive interior ergonomics and responsive infotainment system
  • Absence of the Theta II engine (known to be problematic at Hyundai/Kia)
  • Soundproofing significantly improved compared to the previous generation

Weaknesses 5

  • Critical piston ring defect on the 2.0L MPI (2019-2020 years)
  • Dual-clutch gearbox (DCT7) sometimes hesitant and generating jerks at low speeds
  • Quality of some interior plastics (lower parts) sensitive to scratches
  • Electric power steering (MDPS) lacking feel and prone to recalls (connectors)
  • Base versions (1.6 MPI) lacking dynamism

Check before buying

  • History of the 21V301000 recall on the 2.0L engine (2019-2020): demand proof of inspection or engine replacement.
  • Behavior of the automatic transmission (DCT or IVT): absence of jerking when pulling away, smooth gear shifts.
  • Noises in the steering column during rapid steering maneuvers at a standstill (wear of the MDPS steering flexible coupling).
  • Operation of the brake assist (brake booster recall on 2017 models).
  • Condition of the trunk latch (recall on early 2016 models).

Official recalls

  • [2024-11-04] Transmission (France): The transmission software does not activate the fail-safe mode during a fault, which can lead to sudden deceleration.
  • [2024-02-23] Trunk latch (NHTSA 24V146000): On 2015-2016 models, the trunk latch can become damaged, preventing opening from the inside.
  • [2021-04-28] 2.0L Nu MPI engine (NHTSA 21V301000): Affects 2019-2020 models. Improper heat treatment of the piston oil scraper rings, which can cause engine damage and stalling while driving.
  • [2019-10-09] Running gear (NHTSA 19V720000 / 19V721000): On certain 2020 models, right rear wheel nuts or lower suspension arm ball joint fasteners insufficiently tightened.
  • [2017-03-28] Power steering (NHTSA 17V213000): On 2017 models, damaged electric power steering (MDPS) connector leading to a loss of assist.
  • [2017-01-26] Braking (NHTSA 17V063000): On 2017 models, possible failure of the brake booster leading to a loss of braking assist.
  • [2016-04-21 / 2016-12-30 / 2026-05-15] Airbags (NHTSA 16V232000 / 16V956000 / 26V307000): Multiple recalls regarding the frontal airbag (driver) inflator modules risking rupture upon deployment (2016-2017 models).

What sets it apart 14

Safety

  • Advanced Safety and Recognized Distinctions — The Hyundai Elantra VI achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult (91%) and child (84%) protection. It has also been repeatedly awarded "Top Safety Pick+" and "Top Safety Pick" by the IIHS, demonstrating its commitment to occupant safety.
  • Effective and Reassuring Braking — Equipped with a high-performance braking system, the Elantra VI ensures short stopping distances and great responsiveness. It stops in approximately 36.6 meters from 96 km/h, offering essential peace of mind in all driving situations.
  • Safe and Comfortable Road Handling — Its rigid chassis, composed of 53% ultra-high strength steel, gives the Elantra VI stable and predictable road holding. Precise steering and comfort-oriented behavior ensure a smooth and serene daily drive.

Reliability

  • Recognized Reliability and Extended Warranty — The Elantra VI is praised for its "Medium to Good" reliability by ADAC and J.D. Power. It benefits from an exceptional manufacturer's warranty (often 5 years unlimited mileage in Europe, 10 years/100,000 miles in the USA for the powertrain), offering unparalleled peace of mind. Furthermore, it is not affected by the Theta II engine issues.

Comfort

  • Acoustic Comfort and Refined Ergonomics — Enjoy a quiet cabin thanks to significantly improved sound insulation compared to the previous generation. Intuitive interior ergonomics and a responsive infotainment system contribute to a pleasant and stress-free driving experience.

Economy

  • Excellent Value for Money and Efficient Engines — The Elantra VI stands out for its excellent price/equipment ratio, offering numerous features for a controlled budget. Its gasoline engines, such as the 2.0 Nu MPI with Atkinson cycle, are designed for optimized fuel consumption, reducing your running costs.

Performance

  • Driving Dynamics and Pleasure — For those seeking more dynamism, the 1.4 T-GDI and 1.6 T-GDI (Sport) engines offer excellent low-end torque and very responsive performance. The Sport version, with its 204 hp engine and multi-link rear suspension, transforms the driving experience into a sporty pleasure.

Alternatives

  • North America: Honda Civic (dynamic benchmark), Toyota Corolla (reliability benchmark), Mazda 3 (premium finish).
  • Europe: Volkswagen Golf, Peugeot 308, Ford Focus (the Elantra is rare there, often replaced by the i30 in compact hatchback format).
  • Asia / Emerging markets: Kia Cerato/Forte (technical cousin), Nissan Sentra/Sylphy, Skoda Octavia.

Verdict

The Hyundai Elantra VI (AD) is a rational, well-built, and generally reliable compact sedan that has managed to correct the eccentricities of the previous generation. Its main asset lies in the absence of the Theta II engine, which spares it the brand's worst problems. However, vigilance is required regarding the 2.0L engine from the 2019-2020 years, hit by a major recall concerning the piston rings. If you are targeting a model from these years, the service history is non-negotiable. The 1.6 MPI and 1.6 T-GDI (Sport) versions are the safest choices mechanically, provided you accept the sometimes rough character of the dual-clutch gearbox in the city.
