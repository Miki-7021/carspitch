Summary

The second-generation Mercedes-Benz GLA (codename H247) marks a clear break from its predecessor. Gone is the raised A-Class look; the H247 adopts the proportions of a true compact SUV (+10 cm in height). Based on the MFA2 platform, it offers significantly increased interior space, a sliding rear bench, and the MBUX technological interface. Facelifted in mid-2023 (minor aesthetic tweaks, 48V mild-hybridization on petrol engines, MBUX update), it positions itself as a versatile premium offering. **Diesel Focus:** Unlike the A-Class, which used the Renault-sourced 1.5L engine, the GLA H247 exclusively relies on the in-house 2.0L unit (OM654q) for its diesel versions, ensuring superior reliability and driving pleasure.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent diesel engines (OM654q): fuel-efficient, torquey, and very reliable
  • Significantly improved interior space and modularity compared to the previous generation
  • Spectacular and responsive MBUX interface
  • Suspension comfort (especially with adaptive damping)
  • Refined sound insulation on long journeys

Weaknesses 5

  • DCT gearbox jerks at very low speeds (traffic jams, maneuvers)
  • Quality of some plastics in the lower part of the cabin unworthy of the badge
  • Frequent rattling noises (squeaks) around the dashboard and door panels
  • Reduced boot volume on the plug-in hybrid version (250 e)
  • Very high purchase prices and network maintenance costs

Check before buying

  • Clear service history (especially for DCT gearboxes and AMG versions)
  • Smooth operation of the MBUX screen (test navigation, cameras, and Bluetooth)
  • Absence of engine light related to the anti-pollution system (AdBlue/NOx) on diesels
  • Condition of the rims (very exposed to curbs) and wear of the front tires (front axle sometimes overwhelmed by torque)
  • Interior trim noises on degraded roads during the test drive

Official recalls

  • - **eCall System (2021)**: Risk of transmitting incorrect GPS coordinates in case of an accident. Software update (often OTA).
  • - **Steering Column (2021)**: Electrical harness potentially rubbing against the steering shaft, risk of short circuit. Harness fastening modified.
  • - **Panoramic Sunroof (2022)**: Bonding defect of the polycarbonate panel between the windshield and the sunroof, risk of detachment while driving.
  • - **High-Voltage Battery - PHEV (2023)**: Risk of sealing defect in the battery housing on some 250 e models, potentially leading to a short circuit or fire risk.
  • - **Fuel Hose - Diesel (2022)**: Risk of fuel leak on some models equipped with the OM654 engine due to hose rubbing.

Verdict

The Mercedes GLA H247 is a highly accomplished compact SUV that corrects the main flaws of its predecessor (interior space, comfort). **For high-mileage drivers, the diesel versions (200 d and 220 d) equipped with the OM654q engine are an excellent choice:** they offer remarkable reliability, incredibly low fuel consumption on the highway, and superior driving pleasure compared to the smaller petrol engines. The petrol versions (1.33L) do the job for daily use but lack refinement, while the plug-in hybrid is aimed at a very specific audience who can recharge at home. Purchasing a diesel is highly recommended, provided that the vehicle has not been used exclusively in the city (risk of DPF/EGR clogging) and that DCT gearbox maintenance has been respected.
