Summary

The Smart #3 (HX12 generation) is a 100% electric compact SUV coupe, the result of a joint venture between Mercedes-Benz (in charge of design) and the Chinese group Geely (in charge of engineering and production). It is based on Geely's modular SEA (Sustainable Experience Architecture) platform, shared with the Smart #1, the Volvo EX30, and the Zeekr X.

EXPERT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL: You requested to target the diesel engine. As an automotive expert, I must correct this expectation: the Smart #3 has never been produced with a diesel engine, nor any other internal combustion engine (petrol or hybrid). The Smart brand became 100% electric well before the launch of this model. Consequently, this fact sheet will focus on the actual electric powertrains that equip this vehicle, while highlighting the total absence of an internal combustion alternative for traditional high-mileage drivers.

Verdict

The Smart #3 is a highly convincing electric SUV coupe that benefits from Geely's excellent engineering and Mercedes-Benz's design flair. If you were looking for a diesel vehicle, you must absolutely turn to another manufacturer (such as the BMW X2 or the Audi Q3 Sportback), as Smart has definitively abandoned internal combustion engines. As an electric vehicle, the #3 is high-performing, well-finished, and rests on a solid hardware foundation. Its main flaws are software-related (annoying driver assistance systems), a point that is gradually improving via remote updates. It is a recommendable purchase for anyone looking for a stylish and dynamic EV.
