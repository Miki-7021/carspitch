Summary

The fifth-generation Subaru Forester (chassis code SK), launched in 2018, marks a significant evolution for the brand's iconic SUV. Based on the new Subaru Global Platform (SGP), it offers increased rigidity, improved comfort, and enhanced passive safety. The Forester SK retains Subaru's DNA with its standard Symmetrical AWD all-wheel drive and Boxer engine, but it also introduces e-Boxer hybrid technology in many markets, particularly in Europe.

This model positions itself as a versatile family SUV, renowned for its off-road capabilities, exemplary safety (thanks notably to the EyeSight driver-assistance system standard on most trims), and generous cabin space. A minor facelift occurred in 2021/2022, bringing aesthetic updates (grille, headlights) and improvements to the EyeSight system. Overall, the Forester SK is a robust and reliable vehicle, true to Subaru's reputation.

Strengths 6

  • General mechanical reliability (naturally aspirated and hybrid engines)
  • High-level active and passive safety (EyeSight system, SGP platform)
  • Highly effective and capable Symmetrical AWD all-wheel drive
  • Generous cabin space and trunk volume
  • Suspension comfort and off-road capabilities
  • Strong resale value

Weaknesses 5

  • High fuel consumption for non-hybrid versions (2.5L NA) and on the highway for the e-Boxer
  • Modest performance for the e-Boxer on the highway (acceleration/reprises)
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds or during strong accelerations (CVT noise)
  • Outdated multimedia system on early versions (pre-facelift)
  • Absence of diesel or turbo petrol engine in Europe

Check before buying

  • Complete service history, especially Lineartronic CVT fluid changes
  • Engine oil level (monitor for potential consumption, though less frequent in this generation)
  • EyeSight system operation (clean cameras, no error lights)
  • Tire condition (even wear thanks to AWD, but check alignment)
  • 12V battery condition, especially on e-Boxer models
  • Check for unperformed recalls

What sets it apart 6

Safety

  • Benchmark Safety with EyeSight and 5-Star Euro NCAP — The Subaru Forester V stands out for its advanced active and passive safety, evidenced by an exceptional 5-star Euro NCAP rating (97% adult protection, 91% child protection) and the IIHS Top Safety Pick+ award. Its standard EyeSight system offers unparalleled peace of mind on the road.
  • Stable Road Handling and Confident Braking — The Forester V offers stable and predictable road handling, ensuring a safe and comfortable driving experience. Its consistent and effective braking system provides reliable and controlled stopping power in all circumstances.

Performance

  • Total Control with Symmetrical AWD — Enjoy exceptional grip and stability in all conditions thanks to the standard Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive. This Subaru technology ensures predictable and high-performance handling, whether on wet, snowy roads or unpaved paths.

Reliability

  • Proven Mechanical Reliability for Lasting Serenity — The Subaru Forester V is renowned for its robustness and general mechanical reliability, a legacy of the brand. Its atmospheric and hybrid powertrains are designed for durability, offering peace of mind and years of reliable service.

Comfort

  • Generous Comfort and Space for the Whole Family — With its generous interior space and spacious boot volume, the Forester V offers optimal comfort for all passengers and their luggage. Its comfortable suspension ensures pleasant journeys, even over long distances or varied terrain.

Economy

  • A Smart Investment with Strong Resale Value — The Subaru Forester V maintains excellent value in the used car market, testifying to its quality and desirability. It is a wise investment that guarantees good capital recovery in the long term.

Verdict

The fifth-generation Subaru Forester (SK) is an excellent choice for those seeking a reliable, safe, and capable family SUV in all conditions. Its SGP platform and EyeSight system make it a very secure vehicle. The Symmetrical AWD all-wheel drive is a major asset for road holding and off-pavement excursions.

For Europe, the choice is limited to the e-Boxer, which offers reasonable fuel consumption in the city and a smooth drive, but its performance can be just adequate on the highway. It is a BUY if you prioritize reliability, safety, and versatility, and if you are not a fan of strong accelerations.

For North America and Australia, the 2.5L naturally aspirated engine is a very robust and capable choice, ideal for long journeys and light towing. It is also an unequivocal BUY for its proven reliability.

Regardless of the market, the key is rigorous maintenance, especially for the Lineartronic CVT gearbox. The absence of a diesel engine in this generation eliminates problems specific to this type of engine, reinforcing the model's overall reliability. The Forester SK is a safe investment for many years.
