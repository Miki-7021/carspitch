Summary

The BMW X6 G06 represents the third generation of the Bavarian brand's Sports Activity Coupé (SAC), launched in 2019. Based on the CLAR platform, it shares many elements with the X5 G05 but stands out with its sloping roofline and sportier character. Positioned as a high-performance luxury vehicle, it combines the aggressiveness of a coupé with the (relative) versatility of an SUV.

A facelift (LCI) was introduced in early 2023, bringing aesthetic (headlights, grille, bumpers) and technological (new iDrive 8, curved digital instrumentation) evolutions. The powertrains were also updated, notably integrating 48V mild-hybrid technology on most gasoline and diesel engines, and the introduction of the new S68 V8.

Overall, the X6 G06 benefits from solid reliability, particularly with the inline six-cylinder engines. The V8s, although improved compared to previous generations, remain complex mechanical systems requiring particular attention. The very rich onboard electronics can sometimes be a source of minor inconveniences, but without recurring major failures.

Strengths 7

  • Assertive and distinctive exterior design
  • High-quality interior finish and premium materials
  • Exceptional engine performance (especially V8 and 6-cylinder)
  • Dynamic and precise road handling for an SUV
  • Advanced onboard technology (iDrive, driving aids)
  • Long-distance comfort (depending on suspension)
  • Reliability of 6-cylinder engines (B58 gasoline, B57 diesel)

Weaknesses 7

  • High purchase price and substantial maintenance cost
  • Significant fuel consumption on V8 versions
  • Limited rear visibility due to the coupé roofline
  • Rear passenger space and trunk volume slightly inferior to the X5
  • Firm suspension comfort on M Sport or M Competition versions
  • Complexity of onboard electronics (potential software bugs)
  • Reliability of V8s (N63/S63), though improved, remains more demanding

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for V8s)
  • Condition of tires and brakes (rapid wear on powerful versions)
  • Functioning of all electronic equipment (screens, driving aids, air conditioning)
  • Absence of oil or coolant leaks (particularly on V8s)
  • Abnormal engine or transmission noises
  • Condition of suspensions (adaptive dampers, air suspension if present)
  • Functioning of the AdBlue and DPF system on diesels (warning lights illuminated)
  • Verification of manufacturer recalls performed

Verdict

The BMW X6 G06 is a vehicle with character, offering a unique blend of luxury, sportiness, and technology. Its reliability is generally good, but it heavily depends on the chosen engine and maintenance history.

* **For daily use and maximum reliability**, the **xDrive40i (B58)** is the most sensible choice. It offers ample performance, reasonable fuel consumption for its category, and recognized mechanical robustness. Diesel versions (B57) are also excellent choices for high-mileage drivers, with exemplary reliability. * **For extreme performance enthusiasts**, the **M50i/M60i and X6 M Competition** versions are racing beasts. However, they demand a substantial maintenance budget and increased vigilance regarding the mechanics (very frequent oil changes, monitoring of temperatures and leaks). They are reserved for informed enthusiasts, ready to bear the costs and complexity.

Regardless of the choice, a clear maintenance history and a thorough inspection before purchase are imperative, especially for the most powerful models. The X6 G06 is an excellent vehicle, provided you choose the right engine and do not neglect maintenance.
