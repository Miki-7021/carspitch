Summary

The second-generation Chrysler 300 (LX2) is a large American executive sedan, a technical cousin of the Dodge Charger. Launched in 2011 and facelifted in 2015, it is based on an evolution of the LX platform (whose origins date back to the DaimlerChrysler era). In continental Europe, it was briefly marketed under the name Lancia Thema (2011-2014), while it retained the Chrysler badge in the UK and Oceania. A true dinosaur of the modern automobile, it survived until 2023 by relying on its mafia/executive design, its ride comfort, and its large naturally aspirated engines. According to data from J.D. Power and the TÜV, its overall mechanical reliability is in the upper average of its segment, although it suffers from some finish shortcomings and electronic bugs.

Strengths 6

  • Remarkable reliability of the 3.6L Pentastar V6 and the 8-speed ZF gearbox
  • General robustness of the 5.7L HEMI V8 block
  • High-level ride comfort and soundproofing
  • Intuitive and responsive Uconnect infotainment system
  • Generous interior space
  • Efficient and trouble-free all-wheel drive (AWD)

Weaknesses 6

  • Quality of some interior plastics (hard and scratch-prone)
  • Minor electronic bugs (Uconnect screen freezing)
  • High fuel consumption of the V8 engines
  • Imposing size poorly suited to European roads
  • Hit-or-miss reliability of the 3.0 CRD diesel engine (Lancia Thema)
  • Almost non-existent maintenance network in Europe (requires a US specialist)

Check before buying

  • History of the very numerous manufacturer recalls (Takata airbags, alternator, transmission).
  • Ticking noise on the top end of the engine (V6: rocker arms; V8: MDS lifters).
  • Smooth operation of the Uconnect touchscreen and air conditioning.
  • Condition of the air suspension compressor (if the vehicle is equipped with it).
  • On AWD versions: absence of vibrations coming from the front driveshaft.

Official recalls

The Chrysler 300 has been the subject of numerous recalls (NHTSA / Rappel Conso): * Airbags (Takata & Curtain) (2013, 2016, 2018, 2019, 2024): Risk of inflator rupture projecting metal fragments, or uncontrolled inflation. Affects very many model years. * Parking brake (Feb. 2024): Improper adjustment that could lead to unexpected vehicle movement (2023 models). * Windshield (July 2021): Poor factory bonding that could lead to its detachment (2020-2021 models). * Instrument cluster (March 2019): Failure of driver alerts illumination (2019 models). * Wiring harness / Regulator (May 2018): Risk of short circuit. * Alternator (Oct. 2014, July 2017): Alternator failure on models with electro-hydraulic steering, which can cause engine stalling (2011-2014 models). * AWD driveshaft (Feb. 2017): Loosening of the front driveshaft bolts that could lead to a disconnection (2014-2017 models). * Gear selector / Control module (April 2016): Risk of vehicle rollaway if the driver exits without the gearbox (monostable selector) actually being in 'Park' (2012-2014 models). * Uconnect Cybersecurity (July 2015): Vulnerability allowing remote hacking of vehicle systems (2015 models). * AWD gearbox (Dec. 2013): Defect on the output shaft of the ZF 8HP45 gearbox (2013 models). * ABS/ESC module (May 2012): Loss of function due to overheating of the electrical distribution center (2011-2012 models). * Airbag logo (Aug. 2022 - France): The logo on the steering wheel can detach during airbag deployment.

What sets it apart 10

Reliability

  • Reliable Pentastar V6 and ZF gearbox — The 3.6L V6 and its 8-speed ZF gearbox are described as very reliable, giving buyers a dependable daily driver with a robust timing chain.
  • Robust HEMI V8 engine block — The 5.7L HEMI V8 is noted for the robustness of its block, offering buyers a solid foundation for long trips and driving pleasure.
  • Solid overall mechanical reliability — J.D. Power and TÜV data place its overall mechanical reliability in the upper average of its segment, reassuring buyers about the core mechanics.

Comfort

  • High-level ride comfort and insulation — The car delivers a high level of ride comfort and sound insulation, making long journeys calmer and less tiring for driver and passengers.
  • Intuitive and responsive Uconnect system — The Uconnect infotainment system is described as intuitive and responsive, so buyers get an easy-to-use interface rather than a fiddly one.

Practicality

  • Generous interior space on board — The Chrysler 300 offers generous space inside, giving occupants room to sit comfortably on everyday trips and longer journeys alike.

Handling

  • Effective and trouble-free AWD — The all-wheel-drive system is described as effective and rarely problematic, giving buyers extra traction without a known reliability penalty.

Economy

  • Strong value on the used market — The verdict calls its price-to-performance-to-looks ratio unbeatable on the used market, so buyers get a lot of car for the money.
  • Reasonable consumption from the V6 — The 3.6L Pentastar V6 is noted for reasonable fuel consumption, making it the more economical choice than the thirsty V8 versions.

Design

  • Distinctive stately design — Its mafia-style, stately design is highlighted as a key selling point, giving buyers a car with real presence and character.

Alternatives

  • North America: Ford Taurus (discontinued), Chevrolet Impala (discontinued), Dodge Charger (technical cousin), Toyota Avalon.
  • Europe: Audi A6, BMW 5 Series, Mercedes-Benz E-Class, Volvo S90.
  • Asia / Middle East: Genesis G80, Lexus ES.

Verdict

The Chrysler 300 (Gen II) is an endearing sedan that offers an unbeatable price/performance/looks ratio on the used market. Mechanically, the gasoline versions are very solid, especially the Pentastar V6 coupled with the 8-speed ZF gearbox, which is the most rational and reliable choice. The HEMI V8 is robust but requires rigorous oil maintenance to preserve its lifters. The main black mark of the model lies in the astronomical amount of recalls (electronics, airbags, alternator): it is imperative to verify via the VIN that all fixes have been applied. Avoid the diesel engine (Lancia Thema) due to hit-or-miss reliability and prohibitive repair costs.
