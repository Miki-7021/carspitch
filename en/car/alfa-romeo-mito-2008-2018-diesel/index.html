Summary

The Alfa Romeo MiTo (for Milano-Torino) is the brand's first premium city car, designed to compete with the Mini and Audi A1. Based on the SCCS platform (shared with the Fiat Grande Punto and Opel Corsa D), it stands out with its design inspired by the 8C Competizione supercar, its frameless doors, and its standard 'DNA' driving selector.

Produced for a decade with two minor facelifts (2013 for the grille/infotainment, 2016 to adopt the new Giulia-type visual identity), the MiTo offered a very wide range of engines. Although the demand here focuses on **diesel (JTDm)** engines, known for their fuel efficiency and torque, the petrol range (notably the T-Jet blocks and the innovative but temperamental MultiAir) accounted for the bulk of sales. Overall, the MiTo is an endearing car but suffers from some early issues (steering column, M32 gearbox) that absolutely must be checked before purchase.

Strengths 5

  • Timeless design and undeniable charm
  • Standard DNA system genuinely alters driving behavior
  • Very powerful and robust 1.6 JTDm engine for high-mileage drivers
  • Very reliable T-Jet petrol engines
  • Dynamic road handling and sharp front end

Weaknesses 6

  • M32 manual gearbox (6-speed) very fragile (bearings) before 2012
  • Fragility of the steering column (angle/torque sensor)
  • Very firm comfort, especially with 17 or 18-inch wheels
  • Very temperamental DPF on the 1.3 JTDm in urban use
  • Rear tailgate wiring harness that breaks over time
  • Quality of some interior plastics below premium positioning

Check before buying

  • **Gearbox (M32):** On 1.6 JTDm, 1.4 T-Jet 155 and 1.4 MA 135/170. Listen for a whistling sound in 5th and 6th gear, or observe if the lever moves during acceleration in 1st/5th.
  • **Power steering:** Turn from lock to lock when stationary. No hard points, groaning, or red warning light should appear.
  • **Service history (MultiAir):** Check that the correct oil is used and that oil changes have been done on time.
  • **DPF and Chain (1.3 JTDm):** Check the oil level (if it's above max, there's diesel in the oil). Listen to the chain on cold start.
  • **Tailgate wiring harness:** Check the operation of the rear wiper, defrost, and trunk opening.

Official recalls

  • - **November 2008:** Risk of rear seat belts not locking (models produced in 2008).
  • - **December 2009:** Reprogramming of the ABS/VDC control unit due to a risk of braking assistance malfunction.
  • - **February 2010:** Inspection and possible replacement of front brake hoses (risk of rubbing).
  • - **May 2012:** Software update for the TCT dual-clutch gearbox to prevent erratic gear changes.
  • - **October 2016:** Recall on certain models for a non-conformity defect of the front seat belt pretensioners.

Verdict

The Alfa Romeo MiTo is a city car with character, aimed at design and dynamic driving enthusiasts. **If you're looking for a diesel**, the **1.6 JTDm 120 hp** is the best choice: it is extremely robust, torquey, and transforms the MiTo into an excellent road car, provided the condition of the manual gearbox (M32) is checked. The **1.3 JTDm** should be avoided if you drive in the city, as it risks ruining the DPF and timing chain. For petrol, prefer a 1.4 T-Jet or a 1.4 MultiAir from after 2012. Buying a MiTo requires a rigorous inspection (steering, gearbox, service history), hence a verdict leaning towards negotiation.
