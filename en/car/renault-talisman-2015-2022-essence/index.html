All Renault models

Summary

The Renault Talisman (KFE generation) succeeds the Laguna III with the ambition to reclaim the D-segment (family sedans and wagons) thanks to a stately design and advanced technologies like the 4-wheel steering chassis (4Control). Based on the modular CMF-C/D platform, it had a difficult start due to finish issues and electronic bugs (R-Link 2 system). The 2020 facelift (Phase 2) corrected most of these flaws, introducing the EasyLink system, Matrix LED headlights, and a significantly improved interior finish. Although demand in this report focuses on gasoline engines (TCe), the range was long dominated by diesel in Europe. It was also sold in South Korea under the name Renault Samsung SM6.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional 4Control chassis (city agility, highway stability)
  • Suspension comfort (especially with adaptive damping)
  • Generous boot volume (608 L in sedan, 572 L in Estate wagon)
  • Reliability and driving pleasure of 1.3 TCe and 1.8 TCe gasoline engines
  • Stately and elegant exterior design
  • Very attractive price/equipment ratio on the used market

Weaknesses 6

  • Disappointing interior finish on models before 2018 (squeaks, adjustments)
  • Recurring bugs in the R-Link 2 multimedia system (black screens, slowness)
  • EDC7 gearbox sometimes harsh at low speeds (especially with the 1.6 TCe)
  • Asymmetrical rear tire wear on models equipped with 4Control
  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.6 dCi 160 Twin Turbo diesel engine
  • Slightly small fuel tank (51 liters on 4Control versions)

Check before buying

  • EDC gearbox behavior: no jerks or excessive slipping should be felt when starting or shifting gears.
  • Tire wear: check the inner tread of the rear tires (4Control geometry often needs adjustment).
  • Onboard electronics: test all functions of the central screen, GPS, Bluetooth, and rearview camera.
  • Bodywork adjustments: the hood and tailgate were often misaligned from the factory on 2015-2017 models.
  • Maintenance history: essential, especially for EDC gearbox oil changes and software updates.

Official recalls

- January 2016: Inspection and tightening of front axle mounting screws (risk of loss of control).,- May 2016: Replacement of front brake hoses (risk of premature wear due to friction).,- October 2018: Reprogramming of the injection ECU on diesel engines (NOx sensor issue).,- July 2019: Verification of rear door child safety lock (risk of unexpected deactivation).,- February 2020: Inspection of battery harness (risk of short circuit due to friction).

What sets it apart 7

Safety

  • Top-tier Safety — The Renault Talisman achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, ensuring optimal protection for all occupants.
  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Benefit from a solid and stable braking system, with very competitive stopping distances, ensuring perfect control in all circumstances.

Performance

  • Exceptional Road Handling with 4Control — The advanced chassis, especially with the 4Control four-wheel steering system, offers remarkable agility in the city and imperial stability on the highway, for a serene and dynamic drive.

Comfort

  • High-end Suspension Comfort — The Talisman envelops you in a cocoon of well-being thanks to its remarkable suspension comfort, particularly with controlled damping, transforming every journey into a moment of relaxation.

Practicality

  • Generous Boot Volume — With a boot volume of 608 liters in the sedan and 572 liters in the Estate version, the Talisman offers impressive loading space, ideal for family trips or professional needs.

Reliability

  • Reliable and Enjoyable Petrol Engines — The 1.3 TCe and 1.8 TCe petrol engines are renowned for their reliability, driving pleasure, and performance, offering a dynamic and serene driving experience.

Economy

  • Unbeatable Price/Equipment Ratio on the Used Market — Enjoy a stately and fully-equipped sedan at a very attractive price on the used car market, representing an exceptional opportunity for a vehicle of this caliber.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 508 (more dynamic but less spacious), Volkswagen Passat (benchmark for finish and spaciousness), Skoda Superb (record space), Mazda 6 (excellent gasoline reliability).
  • South Korea (against the SM6): Hyundai Sonata, Kia K5, Chevrolet Malibu.
  • Emerging markets: Toyota Camry (hybrid, untouchable reliability).

Verdict

The Renault Talisman is a sedan full of road qualities, often wrongly shunned due to the teething problems of its early model years (2015-2017). If you are looking for a gasoline engine, AVOID the 1.6 TCe whose EDC gearbox management is erratic. You should definitely opt for models produced from late 2018 equipped with the 1.3 TCe 160 (the most balanced and reliable choice) or the 1.8 TCe 225 (for performance). The absolute ideal is to aim for a Phase 2 (from 2020): the finish is worthy of the segment, the electronics are finally stable (EasyLink), and its depreciation makes it a golden deal on the used market.
