All Renault models

Summary

The Renault Zoe Phase 2 (often referred to as ZE 50) represents a major evolution of the original model launched in 2012. Although it retains the same platform, it benefits from a complete redesign of its interior (foamed materials, digital screens, Easy Link system), a new 52 kWh battery offering up to 395 km of WLTP range, and the introduction (optional) of 50 kW DC fast charging via a Combo CCS port. The 22 kW AC charger (Caméléon), a real asset for public charging stations, is retained. In terms of reliability, this Phase 2 has corrected the early flaws of the first electric motors but remains sensitive to onboard electronics and thermal management. **Overall note: A mature model, very relevant as a used car for urban and peri-urban use.**

Strengths 5

  • Unbeatable 22 kW AC charger (Caméléon) for public charging
  • Comfortable real-world range (approx. 300-330 real km for the ZE 50)
  • Very low battery degradation (SOH) over time
  • Interior finish and soundproofing significantly improved compared to Phase 1
  • Extremely robust electric motors (R110/R135)

Weaknesses 5

  • DC fast charging limited to 50 kW... and often optional!
  • Driving position too high (seats not height-adjustable)
  • Euro NCAP score degraded in 2021 (0 stars) following protocol tightening and removal of the head side airbag
  • Extreme sensitivity to 12V battery voltage (phantom error messages)
  • Fragility of the air conditioning compressor

Check before buying

  • Traction battery health (SOH): verifiable via a certificate (e.g., Moba) or the CanZE application.
  • Air conditioning operation (cooling and heating): crucial for battery cooling.
  • 12V battery replacement date (must be less than 3 years old).
  • Clunking noises from the front axle (stabilizer bar links, shock absorber mounts).
  • Charging socket locking/unlocking (the locking pin can seize).

Official recalls

  • - **March 2021 (HV Battery):** Risk of internal short circuit in the high-voltage battery on a very limited series manufactured between 2021-01-13 and 2021-02-22. Replacement of affected modules.
  • - **May 2021 (Steering):** Risk of steering loss due to a manufacturing defect in the steering column (models produced late 2020).
  • - **2023 (Software):** Technical campaign (non-safety related) for reprogramming the battery management system (BMS) computer to prevent unexpected charging interruptions.

Verdict

The Renault Zoe Phase 2 is an **excellent used car purchase** for anyone looking for a reliable, efficient daily electric vehicle with reassuring range. The R110 motor is amply sufficient, but the R135 provides undeniable comfort on expressways. **Essential purchase condition:** prioritize a model equipped with the 50 kW DC fast charging option (Combo CCS) for resale, demand a battery health certificate (SOH > 90%), and ensure the air conditioning works perfectly. Avoid models whose 12V battery has not been maintained, as this often masks complex electronic diagnostics.
