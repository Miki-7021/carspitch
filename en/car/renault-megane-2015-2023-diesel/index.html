Renault Megane

Summary

The Renault Mégane IV marks a stylistic and technological break from its predecessor. Based on the modular CMF-C/D platform, it offers first-class road handling, refined comfort, and ambitious technological features (vertical R-Link 2 screen, 4Control system on GT/RS).

Phase 1 (2015-2020): Launch with a wide range of engines. The early days were marred by electronic bugs (R-Link 2) and the presence of the notoriously problematic 1.2 TCe. In diesel, the 1.5 dCi confirmed its status as a benchmark. Phase 2 (2020-2023): Mid-life facelift. Introduction of the Easy-Link multimedia system (much more reliable), 100% digital gauges, gradual abandonment of diesel in favor of the plug-in hybrid (E-Tech), and widespread adoption of the excellent 1.3 TCe petrol engine.

Expert's overall rating: An excellent compact car if you choose the right engine. Diesel versions (especially the 1.5 dCi) are among the most reliable on the market for high-mileage drivers, provided that the emission control systems (AdBlue) are monitored on recent versions.

Strengths 6

  • Exemplary reliability of the 1.5 dCi (K9K) engine, a road marathoner
  • Excellent chassis, high-level comfort/road holding compromise
  • 4Control system (on GT and RS) offering stunning agility
  • Very successful 1.3 TCe engine (powerful and economical)
  • Significantly improved finish quality compared to the Mégane III
  • Easy-Link system (Phase 2) responsive and ergonomic

Weaknesses 6

  • 1.2 TCe engine to be absolutely avoided (risk of failure)
  • R-Link 2 multimedia system (Phase 1) slow, buggy, and prone to black screens
  • Capricious AdBlue systems on recent diesels (Blue dCi)
  • Rapid wear of front tires, particularly on versions equipped with 4Control
  • Rear passenger space and boot volume in the lower average of the category
  • Dry-clutch EDC gearbox (on 1.5 dCi) sometimes causes jerks at low speeds

Check before buying

  • Clear service history, particularly for EDC gearbox oil changes (wet versions).
  • Functionality of the central R-Link 2 screen (test Bluetooth, GPS, responsiveness).
  • On diesels: absence of 'Check Anti-pollution System' warning light (sign of a clogged DPF or an AdBlue fault).
  • Body panel alignment (rear tailgate often poorly adjusted at the factory on early model years).
  • Condition of stabilizer bar links (clunking noises on degraded roads at the front).
  • Yellowing of LED daytime running lights ('C' light signature) on Phase 1.

Official recalls

  • November 2016: Child safety. Risk of unexpected deactivation of child safety lock on rear doors (models produced until June 2016).
  • May 2018: Airbags. Possible incorrect deployment of curtain airbags (late 2016 production).
  • October 2019: Cooling. Risk of short-circuit on the engine cooling fan motor (2018-2019 production).
  • February 2020: Emission control (Diesel). Reprogramming of the injection ECU to correct NOx emissions on 1.5 dCi engines.
  • March 2023: E-Tech Hybrid. Risk of power loss or fire related to the traction battery (BMS reprogramming).

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Unrivalled Diesel Reliability — Diesel engines, especially the legendary 1.5 dCi (K9K), are renowned for their robustness and exceptional longevity, making the Mégane IV an ideal choice for high-mileage drivers.

Economy

  • Controlled Fuel Consumption — Enjoy remarkable fuel efficiency with diesel engines, particularly the 1.5 dCi, which offers some of the lowest fuel consumption in its category, thereby reducing your running costs.

Safety

  • High-Level Passive Safety — The Renault Mégane IV ensures optimal protection for all its occupants, as evidenced by its excellent crash test ratings.

Braking

  • Efficient and Reassuring Braking — Benefit from competitive and stable braking performance, ensuring short and safe stops in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Precise Road Handling — The Mégane IV's chassis offers a perfect balance between comfort and dynamism, with stable, predictable road holding and precise steering for a serene drive.

Comfort

  • Comfort and Quality Finish — Appreciate refined comfort and a significantly improved finish quality, creating a pleasant and well-insulated cabin for your daily commutes and long journeys.

Performance

  • Dynamic Diesel Engines — The 1.6 dCi and 1.7 Blue dCi engines offer generous torque and excellent responsiveness, providing superior driving pleasure for highway journeys and overtakes.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 308 II and III (beware of the 1.2 PureTech, prefer the 1.5 BlueHDi or 2.0 BlueHDi), Volkswagen Golf VII and VIII (very reliable 2.0 TDI), Ford Focus IV (1.5 EcoBlue), Toyota Corolla (Hybrid, unbeatable in the city).
  • Asia / Oceania: Mazda 3, Hyundai i30, Honda Civic.
  • Emerging Markets (Sedan): Skoda Octavia, Fiat Tipo, Toyota Corolla Sedan.

Verdict

The Renault Mégane IV is a car with two faces. If you opt for a petrol model from before 2018 equipped with the 1.2 TCe, it's a financial black hole in terms of power. However, in diesel, it's a master choice. The 1.5 dCi is one of the most reliable and economical engines of its generation, ideal for high-mileage drivers. The 1.6 dCi offers an excellent compromise for those seeking more punch. For a worry-free purchase, prioritize a Phase 2 model (after 2020): you'll benefit from the excellent Easy-Link system, refined finish, and engines (1.5 Blue dCi or 1.3 TCe) that have reached full maturity. Firmly negotiate Phase 1 models due to the obsolescence of the R-Link 2.
