Summary

The 5th generation Renault Scenic (E-Tech Electric) marks a complete break with its monospace past. Based on the CMF-EV platform (now called AmpR Medium), it transforms into a 100% electric family SUV. Voted 'Car of the Year 2024', it stands out for its generous interior space, its OpenR Link infotainment system (based on Google Automotive, one of the best on the market), and the extensive use of recycled materials. In terms of reliability, it benefits from the teething problems ironed out by the Megane E-Tech released two years earlier, which gives it an excellent level of maturity from its launch.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent real-world range on the 87 kWh version (often over 450 km on highways)
  • Ultra-smooth OpenR Link multimedia system (integrated Google) and very precise trip planner
  • Generous rear spaciousness (flat floor) and large boot (545 liters)
  • Electric motor without rare earths (wound rotor synchronous)
  • Dynamic road handling and precise steering
  • Very practical 22 kW AC charger option for public charging stations

Weaknesses 5

  • Absence of a front trunk (Frunk) for storing cables
  • Slightly firm comfort at low speeds with 20-inch wheels
  • Poor rear visibility (compensated by camera rearview mirror on high trims)
  • Haptic buttons on the steering wheel sensitive to unintentional presses
  • No all-wheel drive (front-wheel drive only)

Check before buying

  • Multimedia system: Ensure that the latest OTA updates have been installed (system stability).
  • 12V battery: Check the voltage; vehicles stored for a long time before delivery may have a weakened accessory battery.
  • Charging flap: Test the locking/unlocking mechanism of the T2/Combo CCS socket.
  • Interior adjustments: On the very first models from 2023/early 2024, some interior noises (squeaks) around the dashboard have been reported.
  • Tires: Check front axle wear, as the instantaneous torque of electric vehicles heavily stresses the tires.

Verdict

The Renault Scenic E-Tech Electric is one of the most accomplished offerings in its category. By abandoning the monospace silhouette for that of an aerodynamic SUV, it gains versatility without sacrificing interior space. In terms of reliability, it starts its career under the best auspices thanks to the AmpR Medium platform, already proven reliable by the Megane E-Tech. **The EV 220 (87 kWh) version is the expert's choice**: it transforms the Scenic into a true long-distance cruiser capable of crossing the country without anxiety, greatly aided by a remarkably efficient Google planner. A highly recommended purchase for families transitioning to electric.
