Summary

The sixth-generation Subaru Outback, known by the chassis code BT, was launched in 2020. True to Subaru's philosophy, it positions itself as a robust raised wagon, combining the capabilities of an SUV with the comfort and handling of a sedan. It is built on the Subaru Global Platform (SGP), which improves structural rigidity, safety, and driving comfort.

The Outback BT stands out with its standard Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive, flat Boxer engines, and the latest generation EyeSight driver assistance system. It offers a spacious cabin, a large trunk, and generous ground clearance, making it versatile for families and adventurers. The exterior design is more assertive, and the interior has been modernized with a large vertical touchscreen for infotainment.

Overall, the Subaru Outback VI is a reliable, safe, and very capable vehicle, particularly appreciated in regions with harsh weather conditions or for those seeking off-the-beaten-path versatility without sacrificing on-road comfort. Its reliability is generally very good, inheriting the brand's solid reputation, although the CVT transmission may not appeal to everyone.

Strengths 6

  • General mechanical reliability (Boxer engines)
  • High-level active and passive safety (EyeSight as standard)
  • Exceptional light off-road capabilities (ground clearance, Symmetrical AWD, X-Mode)
  • Improved suspension comfort and sound insulation
  • Generous cabin space and trunk volume
  • Robust and durable build quality

Weaknesses 5

  • Fuel consumption of naturally aspirated engines (FB25D) can be high
  • Feel of the Lineartronic CVT transmission (slipping, lack of responsiveness for some)
  • Infotainment system sometimes slow or complex on early versions
  • Functional interior design but less 'premium' than some European competitors
  • Price of spare parts and dealership maintenance sometimes high

Check before buying

  • **Lineartronic CVT Transmission:** Check fluid change history. Test the smoothness of shifts, absence of excessive slipping or abnormal noises. A poorly maintained CVT is a financial black hole.
  • **EyeSight System:** Ensure all driving aids are functioning correctly. Check for cracks or impacts on the windshield (affects camera calibration).
  • **Engine (all versions):** Check for oil leaks, oil level. For turbos (FA24F, CB18), listen for any abnormal turbo whistling.
  • **Suspension and Running Gear:** Check the condition of shock absorbers, bushings, especially if the vehicle has been off-road. Clunking or grinding noises may indicate wear.
  • **Electronics and Infotainment:** Test all touchscreen functions, connectivity (Apple CarPlay/Android Auto), USB ports. Check for software bugs.
  • **Bodywork and Underbody:** Inspect for rust, especially in areas with road salt in winter, and any impacts under the chassis.

Verdict

The Subaru Outback VI (BT) is an excellent choice for those looking for a versatile, safe, and capable family vehicle with a real aptitude for off-road adventures. Its reliability is a major asset, especially for the naturally aspirated Boxer engines which are very robust.

**Buying advice:**

* **For maximum reliability and a controlled budget:** Opt for the 2.5L FB25D naturally aspirated engine. It is the most proven and simplest to maintain. Its fuel consumption can be a bit high, but its robustness compensates. * **For performance and towing:** The 2.4L FA24F turbo is an excellent choice, offering superior driving pleasure. Ensure an impeccable maintenance history, especially for the turbo and CVT. * **For city driving and optimized fuel consumption:** The 2.0L e-Boxer is relevant, but its higher purchase cost and the added complexity of the hybrid system must be weighed against actual fuel savings.

Regardless of the engine, Lineartronic (CVT) transmission maintenance is the most crucial point. A complete history of CVT fluid changes is non-negotiable. The EyeSight system is an undeniable plus for safety. Negotiate the price if the CVT maintenance is not perfectly documented or if the infotainment system is slow. Overall, it's a vehicle to **BUY** if maintenance has been rigorously followed.
