Summary

The Ford Maverick (Generation I) marks Ford's triumphant return to the compact pickup segment. Based on the C2 unibody platform (shared with the Ford Escape/Kuga and Bronco Sport), it stands out with its SUV-like road handling, ultra-practical bed (Flexbed), and above all, its hybrid powertrain offered as standard at launch. Designed primarily for North and South America, it offers an economical and rational alternative to traditional pickups (mid-size and full-size). Its price/performance/reliability ratio makes it one of the most sought-after vehicles in its class, generating significant delivery delays for new orders.

Strengths 5

  • Above-average overall reliability (Consumer Reports, J.D. Power IQS 2023)
  • Highly refined and extremely fuel-efficient 2.5L hybrid powertrain
  • Steel bodywork (unlike the aluminum F-150): less expensive body repairs
  • Excellent initial quality and unbeatable reliability-to-price ratio
  • 'Flexbed' very well designed for DIY and daily use

Weaknesses 5

  • Very long delivery times due to extremely high demand
  • Hard interior plastics prone to scratching
  • Numerous manufacturer recalls (mainly electronic and software)
  • Some jerks reported on the 2.0L EcoBoost transmission
  • Frequent failures of the original 12V battery

Check before buying

  • Recall history: The vehicle has been subject to numerous recalls (software, airbags, lighting, brakes). It is imperative to verify that all have been performed at a dealership.
  • 12V battery: Test the voltage and health of the 12V battery, which is prone to premature degradation that can immobilize the vehicle.
  • Transmission (2.0L): Test drive the vehicle at low speeds to detect any jerks or hesitations from the gearbox.
  • On-board electronics: Check the proper operation of the dashboard, turn signals, and taillights (prone to BCM module bugs).

Official recalls

The Ford Maverick has been subject to numerous recalls in the United States (NHTSA), often related to early software or assembly issues:

  • Engine / Fire risk (May 2025 - 25V345000): On 2.5L HEV, engine failure that could lead to a fire risk (following an incorrect repair of a previous recall).
  • 12V Battery (Jan 2025 - 25V019000): Degradation and sudden failure of the 12V battery.
  • Electronics / Software (Feb/Mar 2025): Multiple recalls for improperly programmed control modules (TCM/PCM/BCM) affecting battery detection (25V127000), the dashboard (25V123000), ABS (25V124000), and the hybrid module (25V133000).
  • Lighting (Feb 2025): Unintentional deactivation of the rear turn signals (25V094000) or taillights (25V095000) by the BCM module.
  • Suspension (May 2026 - 26V340000): Improperly installed front lower control arm ball joints.
  • Engine heater (Apr 2026 / Oct 2025): Possible overheating of the engine block heater on 1.5L/2.0L engines.
  • Airbags / Safety (Mar 2026 / Dec 2025): Failure of the occupant classification sensor (OCS), detachment of the dashboard cover during airbag deployment, and improperly secured airbag warning light.
  • Sunroof (Mar 2026 - 26V157000): Poor bonding of the sunroof glass that could lead to its detachment while driving.
  • Braking / Transmission (2025-2026): Overheating of the brake booster circuit board (25V787000), failure of the park module (25V863000), and trailer brake control issues (26V104000).
  • Engine Mechanics (Dec 2025 / Mar 2026): Cylinder head manufacturing defect (25V828000) and EGR valve failure (26V122000).
  • Child seats (Oct 2024 - 24V778000): Improperly threaded second-row tether anchors.

What sets it apart 11

Handling

  • Comportement routier proche d'un SUV — Reposant sur la plateforme monocoque C2 partagée avec le Ford Escape et le Bronco Sport, il offre une conduite plus stable et confortable qu'un pick-up traditionnel à châssis échelle.

Economy

  • Motorisation hybride sobre de série — Le système hybride 2.5L Atkinson associé à une eCVT affiche des consommations dignes d'une berline compacte, réduisant fortement le coût d'usage au quotidien.
  • Carrosserie en acier moins coûteuse — Contrairement à l'aluminium du F-150, la carrosserie en acier du Maverick permet des réparations de carrosserie moins coûteuses en cas de choc ou de dommage.
  • Rapport fiabilité prix imbattable — Le Maverick combine une excellente qualité initiale et un prix contenu, ce qui en fait l'un des véhicules les plus demandés de sa catégorie selon le texte.

Reliability

  • Fiabilité mécanique du bloc hybride — Dérivé du Ford Escape Hybrid, ce système hybride éprouvé obtient un score de fiabilité de 9/10, ce qui limite les risques de pannes coûteuses pour l'acheteur.
  • Qualité initiale reconnue par les organismes — Le véhicule est classé parmi les meilleurs par J.D. Power IQS 2023 et Consumer Reports, ce qui rassure l'acheteur sur la qualité de fabrication à la livraison.
  • Bloc EcoBoost robuste et fiable — Le moteur 2.0L EcoBoost est décrit comme un bloc robuste avec un score de fiabilité de 8/10, ce qui en fait un choix rassurant pour les acheteurs recherchant de la durabilité.

Practicality

  • Benne Flexbed bien pensée — La benne Flexbed est conçue pour le bricolage et le quotidien, avec des aménagements pratiques qui facilitent le transport d'objets variés sans adaptation supplémentaire.
  • Alternative rationnelle aux gros pick-up — Le Maverick se positionne comme une alternative économique et rationnelle aux pick-up mid-size et full-size, avec un format compact plus adapté à un usage quotidien.

Performance

  • Version EcoBoost avec transmission intégrale — Le bloc 2.0L EcoBoost offre une puissance confortable et la disponibilité de la transmission intégrale AWD, utile pour les climats difficiles et les besoins de polyvalence.
  • Capacité de remorquage supérieure — Avec le 2.0L EcoBoost et le pack 4K Tow Package, le Maverick propose une capacité de remorquage supérieure à celle de la version hybride, adaptée aux usages plus lourds.

Alternatives

  • North America: Hyundai Santa Cruz (more SUV/comfort-oriented, less utilitarian), Honda Ridgeline (higher segment, more expensive), Toyota Tacoma (ladder frame, more rustic and off-road capable).
  • South America: Fiat Toro, Chevrolet Montana, Ram Rampage.
  • Europe: No direct competition in compact unibody pickups (the Ford Ranger or Toyota Hilux are much larger and sit on ladder frames).

Verdict

The Ford Maverick is a true success that redefines the compact pickup segment. Its 2.5L Hybrid powertrain is the best buy: it offers top-tier mechanical reliability (inherited from the Escape) and fuel consumption worthy of a compact sedan. The 2.0L EcoBoost block remains an excellent choice for those who need all-wheel drive or greater towing capacity. Although the core mechanics are very solid (ranked among the best by J.D. Power and Consumer Reports), the Maverick suffered from numerous electronic and software bugs during its early production years, resulting in a long list of recalls. Before any used purchase, it is imperative to verify that all software updates and safety recalls have been performed at a dealership. Once these fixes are applied, it is a highly recommendable vehicle.
