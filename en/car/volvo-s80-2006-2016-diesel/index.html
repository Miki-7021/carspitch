Summary

The second-generation Volvo S80 (AS), based on the EUCD platform (shared with Ford and Land Rover), served as the Swedish brand's flagship for a decade. Designed to cover long distances in supreme comfort, it stands out with its exceptional orthopedic seats, advanced sound insulation, and avant-garde safety arsenal (City Safety, BLIS, adaptive cruise control). It underwent several evolutions: a slight redesign in 2009, a technological and interior update in 2011 (Sensus), and a major facelift in 2013 (new bumpers, digital gauges). Although it doesn't offer the dynamism of a BMW 5 Series, it shines with its overall reliability, particularly with the legendary in-house 5-cylinder engines. The end of its production run (2014-2016) saw the introduction of the more efficient 4-cylinder VEA (Drive-E) engines, which were, however, marked by early issues.

Weaknesses 6

  • Clumsy road behavior, high weight (often > 1700 kg)
  • Turning radius worthy of a truck (especially with 18/19-inch wheels)
  • Aisin automatic gearbox (Geartronic 6) slow and comfort-oriented
  • Powershift dual-clutch gearbox (on 4-cylinders) unreliable
  • Premature wear of front suspension bushings (due to weight)
  • Pre-2011 multimedia system (retractable GPS) completely obsolete today

Check before buying

  • Accessory belt and its tensioner on ALL 5-cylinder diesel engines (D3, D4, D5). Preventive replacement every 60,000 km recommended.
  • Automatic gearbox oil change history (Geartronic or Powershift). Hot jerks on the Geartronic 6 (especially 2006-2008) indicate a worn valve body.
  • Play in the front axle: suspension wishbones, ball joints, and shock absorbers suffer from the engine's weight (especially D5, T6, and V8).
  • Air conditioning compressor: if the AC cuts out after 15-20 minutes of driving, the compressor's magnetic clutch has too much play (adjustable with shims without replacing the compressor).
  • Steering rack: check for power steering fluid leaks at the bellows.
  • Water ingress: check the front carpet (clogged windshield cowl or sunroof drains, which can flood the CEM electronic module).

What sets it apart 7

Safety

  • Benchmark Safety — The Volvo S80 II is a benchmark in safety, having achieved 5 stars in Euro NCAP tests and the 'Top Safety Pick' distinction from IIHS. Its rigid chassis and pioneering driver assistance systems ensure optimal protection for all occupants.
  • Powerful and Safe Braking — The Volvo S80 II ensures very good braking performance, with short stopping distances and excellent stability. Its powerful and durable system, complemented by ABS, EBD, and EBA, guarantees optimal control in all circumstances.
  • Stable and Serene Road Handling — Its dynamic behavior is characterized by great stability and driving comfort. Road holding is safe and predictable, with precise steering and suspension that effectively filters out irregularities, offering a relaxing driving experience over long distances.

Comfort

  • Exceptional Travel Comfort — Enjoy imperial ride comfort and top-notch sound insulation. Volvo seats, recognized as among the best in the world, offer perfect support for long journeys, transforming every trip into a serene experience.
  • Immersive Audio Experience — Immerse yourself in an exceptional sound experience thanks to the available High Performance and Premium Sound Dynaudio/Harman Kardon audio systems, transforming the cabin into a true concert hall.

Reliability

  • Legendary Reliability of 5-Cylinder Diesel Engines — Volvo's 5-cylinder Diesel engines (D3, D4, D5) are renowned for their robustness and exceptional longevity, capable of exceeding 400,000 km with regular maintenance. An ideal choice for high-mileage drivers.
  • Build Quality and Durability — The Volvo S80 II stands out with superior paint quality and absolute resistance to corrosion, demonstrating the robustness and durability of its design, for preserved value over time.

Alternatives

  • Europe: BMW 5 Series (E60 / F10) (more dynamic), Mercedes E-Class (W211 / W212) (superior interior space), Audi A6 (C6 / C7) (interior finish), Peugeot 607 / Citroën C6 (for comfort).
  • North America: Lexus GS (equivalent reliability, RWD), Acura RL, Lincoln MKS.
  • Asia: Audi A6L, BMW 5 Series Li (the Chinese market favors long-wheelbase versions, hence the local S80L).

Verdict

The Volvo S80 II is a connoisseur's choice in the used car market. It is one of the most comfortable and robust sedans of its generation, provided you choose the right engine. The best buy is a model equipped with the 5-cylinder diesel (D3, D4, or D5) produced between 2010 and 2014, paired with the Geartronic 6 gearbox (with documented oil changes). These engines can easily exceed 400,000 km if the weak point of the accessory belt is closely monitored.

Absolutely to avoid: Versions equipped with the Powershift gearbox (often coupled with the 2.0D 136hp or 2.0T) without a pristine maintenance history, as well as the first 4-cylinder VEA diesels (2014-2016) prone to high oil consumption. Petrol enthusiasts should opt for the excellent 2.5T (or FT), a monument of reliability.
