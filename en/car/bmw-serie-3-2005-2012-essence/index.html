E90 · 2005 – 2012

All BMW models

Summary

The fifth-generation BMW 3 Series (E90 sedan, E91 Touring wagon, E92 coupe, E93 convertible) marks a major evolution for the Bavarian brand. Replacing the legendary E46, it introduces a design by Chris Bangle, the iDrive interface, and, most importantly, a complex technological transition under the hood.

Positioning: Dynamic premium sedan, an absolute benchmark in its category in terms of driving pleasure and road feel.

Evolution (LCI): The facelift (Life Cycle Impulse) in late 2008 (sedan/wagon) and 2010 (coupe/convertible) corrected many early flaws (faster iDrive CIC, wider tracks, improved finish) but coincided with the massive introduction of direct injection gasoline and the N47 diesel engine, drastically altering the model's reliability profile.

Expert's overall rating: A two-faced car. Exceptional with indirect injection 6-cylinders (N52) or older diesels (M57), it can turn into a financial black hole with direct injection 4-cylinder gasoline engines (N43), the notorious twin-turbo (N54), or the sadly famous N47 diesel.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road handling (rear-wheel drive, 50/50 weight distribution)
  • Enjoyment and sound of the inline 6-cylinder engines (especially N52 and N54/N55)
  • Significantly improved finish and assembly quality on facelifted versions (LCI)
  • Driving position ergonomics and iDrive CIC system (from late 2008)
  • Variety of body styles (sedan, very practical wagon, elegant coupe, hardtop convertible)

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of direct injection gasoline engines (N43, N53) in Europe
  • Endemic timing chain issue on the N47 diesel
  • Very high maintenance cost (specific parts, BMW labor rates)
  • Average rear passenger space and trunk volume for the category
  • Absence of water temperature gauge on most models (hidden menu required)
  • Premature wear of RunFlat tires (and degraded comfort on bumpy roads)

Check before buying

  • Clear oil change history (avoid LongLife intervals of 30,000 km, prefer 15,000 km)
  • On N43/N53/N54: invoices for injector replacement (index 11 or 12 required), HP pump, and NOx sensor
  • On N52/N54/N55: presence of oil leaks (oil filter housing gasket - OFHG, valve cover, oil pan)
  • On N47: timing chain noise when cold and hot (windshield side)
  • Functionality of all electrical equipment (windows, lighting) to rule out FRM module failure
  • Condition of the front axle (thrust arm bushings) and irregular tire wear

Official recalls

  • Takata Airbags (Multiple years): Replacement of driver and passenger airbag gas generators (risk of metallic fragment projection).,- B+ Battery Cable (2013): Poor contact at the fuse box (Junction Box) potentially leading to a complete loss of electrical power while driving.,- Blower Motor / Heater (2017/2020): Risk of overheating and fire in the wiring of the climate control blower motor regulator.,- VANOS Bolts (2014): On N51, N52, and N55 engines (especially North American market), possible breakage of VANOS housing bolts leading to power loss.,- PCV Valve Heater (2017/2022): Risk of short circuit and fire in the crankcase ventilation system (PCV valve) on certain gasoline models.

What sets it apart 6

handling

  • Exceptional Driving Dynamics — The BMW 3 Series E90 is an absolute benchmark for driving pleasure, offering a dynamic experience thanks to its rear-wheel drive and ideal 50/50 weight distribution.

Performance

  • Legendary Inline Six-Cylinder Engines — The petrol inline 6-cylinder engines, especially the N52 (325i, 330i), are renowned for their melodious sound, smoothness, and reliability, delivering exhilarating performance.
  • Refined Performance with the N55 Engine — The 335i engine equipped with the N55 (from 2010 onwards) offers an excellent balance between power and driving pleasure, with advanced turbo technology for a sporty and smooth ride.

comfort

  • Premium Finish Quality and Ergonomics — The E90 3 Series interior stands out with its meticulous finish and assembly quality, particularly improved in the LCI (facelifted) versions, featuring intuitive cockpit ergonomics and the iDrive CIC system.

practicality

  • A Range of Body Styles for Every Need — Available as an elegant sedan, a highly practical Touring wagon, a sporty coupé, or a hardtop convertible, the 3 Series E90 offers rare versatility to adapt to all lifestyles.

reliability

  • Proven Reliability of N52 Engines — Models equipped with the N52 6-cylinder petrol engine (325i, 330i before 2007 in Europe, 328i in the USA) are particularly valued for their robustness and longevity, ensuring peace of mind.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Audi A4 B7/B8 (beware of TFSI oil consumption and Multitronic gearboxes), Mercedes C-Class W204 (very reliable from the 2011 facelift), Alfa Romeo 159 (heavy but timeless design).
  • North America: Lexus IS 250/350 (XE20) (exceptional V6 reliability, an absolute benchmark against the E90's problems), Infiniti G37 (very robust, sporty VQ37 V6).
  • Japan / Asia: Toyota Mark X, Lexus IS.

Verdict

The BMW 3 Series E90 is an engineer's car, brilliant on the road but demanding in the workshop. For a gasoline purchase, engine choice is vital. In Europe, absolutely prioritize a 325i or 330i from before 2007 equipped with the N52 engine (indirect injection), or import a North American model (328i N52). Avoid the 4-cylinder (N43) and 6-cylinder (N53) direct injection engines, whose injector and HP pump failures will ruin you. The 335i (N54/N55) is reserved for enthusiasts with a solid preventive maintenance budget. For diesel, the old M57 (330d before 2008) remains a rock, while the N47 (320d after 2007) is a lottery to avoid without proof of recent timing chain replacement.
