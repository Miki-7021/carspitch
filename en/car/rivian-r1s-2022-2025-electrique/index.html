Summary

The first-generation Rivian R1S is a large luxury electric SUV (up to 7 seats) based on the same "skateboard" platform as the R1T pickup. Designed to offer exceptional off-road capabilities while maintaining high-level on-road behavior, it is positioned as a direct rival to the Tesla Model X, with a much more "adventure" oriented approach and superior interior space. Although the first units (2022-2023) suffered from teething problems typical of a new manufacturer (body panel alignments, software bugs), reliability data shows a clear improvement in build quality on the 2024-2025 models. The electric motors prove to be very robust and the vehicle benefits from continuous improvements via over-the-air (OTA) software updates. Note: the native adoption of the NACS charging port (Tesla standard) from 2025, facilitating access to Superchargers.

Strengths 6

  • Gigantic interior space (7 real seats) and very large front trunk (frunk)
  • Exceptional off-road capabilities (adjustable air suspension)
  • Extremely robust and high-performance electric motors
  • Frequent, useful software updates (OTA) that genuinely improve the vehicle
  • Reassuring battery warranty: 8 years / 175,000 km
  • Compatibility with the NACS (Tesla) network integrated from 2025

Weaknesses 5

  • Uneven build quality on 2022-2023 models (rattles, body panel alignments)
  • High power consumption due to aerodynamics and massive weight
  • Physical service center network still in development
  • Extremely high body repair costs in the event of a collision
  • Overall reliability below average according to J.D. Power (IQS 2023) and Consumer Reports, mainly due to teething problems

Check before buying

  • Complete recall history (very numerous on 2022-2023 model years)
  • Asymmetrical tire wear (particularly on the inside of the front axle)
  • Body panel alignment and smooth operation of the flush door handles
  • Absence of metallic noises (clunking) during acceleration/braking transitions (driveshafts)
  • Proper operation of the integrated air compressor and air suspension at all ride heights

Official recalls

The Rivian R1S has been the subject of numerous teething recalls (NHTSA). Here are the main ones:

  • 2026-01-15 (26V009000): Incorrect tightening of the 2nd-row seat belt retractor bolts (2022-2026 models).
  • 2026-01-05 (26V003000): Possible improper reassembly of the suspension toe link during previous maintenance.
  • 2025-09-08 (25V585000): Software bug (ADAS) preventing the correct identification of a preceding vehicle (fixed via OTA).
  • 2025-08-21 (25V537000): Poor grounding in the high-voltage distribution box (HVDB) that could lead to a loss of power.
  • 2025-06-11 (25V387000): Possible failure of the front turn signals.
  • 2025-06-03 (25V370000): Incorrect installation of the front seat belt anchor (D-ring) bolts.
  • 2025-02-13 (25V085000): Low beam headlights may not illuminate in cold weather.
  • 2024-11-01 (24V827000): Software bug related to the "Car Costume" feature affecting exterior lighting.
  • 2024-09-13 (24V686000): Missing markings for the cruise control on the steering column module.
  • 2024-06-20 (24V458000): Incorrect maximum load capacity label.
  • 2024-06-05 (24V408000): B/C-pillar trim retaining clips potentially damaged during maintenance.
  • 2024-05-23 (24V367000): Incorrect headlight aim adjustment.
  • 2024-05-09 (24V319000): Missing airbag warning label on the dashboard.
  • 2023-12-21 (23V883000): Accelerator pedal may not detect driver release (failure of auto-hold activation).
  • 2023-11-21 (23V783000): Software update mistakenly disabling the defrost/defog controls.
  • 2023-03-31 (23V233000): Insufficient visibility of the reverse light beam from certain angles.
  • 2023-03-09 (23V159000): Improperly secured side curtain airbag fasteners.
  • 2023-02-22 (23V109000): Defective passenger seat belt retractor (ALR) sensor.
  • 2022-10-06 (22V744000): Incorrect tightening of the fastener connecting the front upper control arm to the steering knuckle.
  • 2022-08-25 (22V641000): Front seat belt anchors improperly secured to the B-pillar.

What sets it apart 10

Practicality

  • Seven Genuine Seats Plus Large Frunk — The cabin accommodates up to seven passengers while a very spacious front trunk adds extra storage, making it practical for large families and long trips with plenty of luggage.
  • Native NACS Charging From 2025 — From 2025 the vehicle adopts the NACS standard natively, making it easier to access Tesla Superchargers and simplifying charging on long journeys.

Handling

  • Adjustable Air Suspension For Off-Road — The air suspension can be adjusted to tackle exceptional off-road terrain, letting the buyer confidently drive on demanding surfaces while keeping high-level road behavior.

Reliability

  • Robust And Powerful Electric Motors — The electric motors are described as extremely robust and high-performing, giving the buyer dependable propulsion and strong capability whether on the road or off it.
  • Frequent Over-The-Air Software Updates — Frequent and genuinely useful OTA updates improve the vehicle over time, so the buyer benefits from continuous enhancements without needing to visit a service center.
  • Reassuring Eight-Year Battery Warranty — The battery is covered for 8 years or 175,000 km, giving the buyer long-term peace of mind about one of the most expensive components of an electric vehicle.
  • Improved Build Quality On 2024-2025 Models — Reliability data shows a clear progression in build quality on 2024-2025 models, so buyers choosing these years avoid the early assembly issues of the first units.

Economy

  • Efficient Dual-Motor Enduro Powertrain — The in-house Enduro dual-motor setup is more efficient and offers excellent range with the Max Pack, suiting daily versatility and long family journeys.

Performance

  • Independent Torque Vectoring Per Wheel — The Quad-Motor delivers independent torque vectoring at each wheel for extreme off-road capability, giving the buyer precise control on very demanding terrain.

Comfort

  • Spacious Interior Rivaling A Minivan — The interior offers the space of a large minivan combined with real 4x4 abilities, giving families generous room without sacrificing off-road capability.

Alternatives

  • North America: Tesla Model X (superior charging network, less spacious), Kia EV9 (cheaper, excellent warranty), Mercedes-Benz EQS SUV (more luxurious, less off-road oriented), BMW iX.
  • Europe / Asia: Kia EV9, Volvo EX90, Tesla Model X (the R1S being available only through very expensive parallel import).

Verdict

The Rivian R1S is a family electric SUV with extraordinary capabilities, combining the space of a large minivan with the aptitudes of a true 4x4. The electric motors and battery (8-year / 175,000 km warranty) are reliable. However, as the brand is young, the 2022 and 2023 models suffered teething problems with numerous recalls (assembly, fasteners, software bugs). The 2024 and 2025 models show significantly higher quality, and the arrival of the NACS port in 2025 is a major asset. Buying a used 2022-2023 model requires a scrupulous check of the recall history and tire wear.
