Rivian R1S

I · 2022 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 75/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation Rivian R1S is a large luxury electric SUV (up to 7 seats) based on the same "skateboard" platform as the R1T pickup. Designed to offer exceptional off-road capabilities while maintaining high-level on-road behavior, it is positioned as a direct rival to the Tesla Model X, with a much more "adventure" oriented approach and superior interior space. Although the first units (2022-2023) suffered from teething problems typical of a new manufacturer (body panel alignments, software bugs), reliability data shows a clear improvement in build quality on the 2024-2025 models. The electric motors prove to be very robust and the vehicle benefits from continuous improvements via over-the-air (OTA) software updates. Note: the native adoption of the NACS charging port (Tesla standard) from 2025, facilitating access to Superchargers.

Strengths 6

  • Gigantic interior space (7 real seats) and very large front trunk (frunk)
  • Exceptional off-road capabilities (adjustable air suspension)
  • Extremely robust and high-performance electric motors
  • Frequent, useful software updates (OTA) that genuinely improve the vehicle
  • Reassuring battery warranty: 8 years / 175,000 km
  • Compatibility with the NACS (Tesla) network integrated from 2025

Weaknesses 5

  • Uneven build quality on 2022-2023 models (rattles, body panel alignments)
  • High power consumption due to aerodynamics and massive weight
  • Physical service center network still in development
  • Extremely high body repair costs in the event of a collision
  • Overall reliability below average according to J.D. Power (IQS 2023) and Consumer Reports, mainly due to teething problems

Check before buying

  • Complete recall history (very numerous on 2022-2023 model years)
  • Asymmetrical tire wear (particularly on the inside of the front axle)
  • Body panel alignment and smooth operation of the flush door handles
  • Absence of metallic noises (clunking) during acceleration/braking transitions (driveshafts)
  • Proper operation of the integrated air compressor and air suspension at all ride heights

Alternatives

- **North America**: Tesla Model X (superior charging network, less spacious), Kia EV9 (cheaper, excellent warranty), Mercedes-Benz EQS SUV (more luxurious, less off-road oriented), BMW iX. - **Europe / Asia**: Kia EV9, Volvo EX90, Tesla Model X (the R1S being available only through very expensive parallel import).

Verdict

The Rivian R1S is a family electric SUV with extraordinary capabilities, combining the space of a large minivan with the aptitudes of a true 4x4. The electric motors and battery (8-year / 175,000 km warranty) are reliable. However, as the brand is young, the 2022 and 2023 models suffered teething problems with numerous recalls (assembly, fasteners, software bugs). The 2024 and 2025 models show significantly higher quality, and the arrival of the NACS port in 2025 is a major asset. Buying a used 2022-2023 model requires a scrupulous check of the recall history and tire wear.
