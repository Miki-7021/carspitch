Suzuki Swift

IV (AZ) · 2017 – 2023

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

All Suzuki models

Summary

The fourth-generation Suzuki Swift (Type AZ), launched in 2017 and facelifted in 2020, is based on the lightweight *Heartect* platform. This architectural change allowed it to drop below the one-ton mark (often around 850-900 kg), thus offering agile, dynamic road handling and among the lowest fuel consumptions on the market. Positioned as a versatile B-segment city car, it stands out for its attractive price/equipment ratio and the widespread integration of mild-hybrid technology (SHVS) in Europe. The 2020 facelift primarily updated the powertrains to meet Euro 6d-Full standards (replacing K12C/K14C with K12D/K14D) and slightly revised the grille. It is a model renowned for its exceptional mechanical reliability, although the perceived quality of interior materials remains basic.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall mechanical reliability (K-Series petrol engines).
  • Extremely low fuel consumption (often < 5L/100km in real-world driving for the 1.2).
  • Agile and fun road handling thanks to its featherlight weight (Heartect platform).
  • Very competitive price/equipment ratio (adaptive cruise control, LED lights, heated seats often standard).
  • Availability of all-wheel drive (AllGrip), rare in this segment.

Weaknesses 5

  • Extremely fragile paint (prone to micro-scratches and stone chips).
  • Quality of interior plastics (hard, scratch easily) and light sound insulation on the highway.
  • Dated infotainment system (Bosch), sometimes slow or prone to bugs (Apple CarPlay/Android Auto disconnecting).
  • Boot volume (265 liters) below the category average (Clio, 208).
  • Tiny fuel tank (37 liters), limiting range on the highway.

Check before buying

  • Condition of the bodywork and paint (impacts on the bonnet and front bumper).
  • Smooth operation of the multimedia system (test USB ports and smartphone connection).
  • Absence of leaks on the rear shock absorbers (some cases of premature weeping before 60,000 km).
  • Maintenance history: oil changes must be annual, especially on Boosterjet engines (turbo).
  • On SHVS versions: check that the Start&Stop system activates correctly (indicator of a healthy auxiliary battery).

Official recalls

  • May 2019 (Airbags): Software update for the airbag control module. Too violent closing of the rear doors could trigger the curtain airbags and seatbelt pre-tensioners (models produced between 2017 and 2019).
  • February 2022 (Brake assist): On some Boosterjet engines (1.0 and 1.4), the vacuum pump valve can swell due to alcohol in the fuel, leading to a loss of braking assistance (hard pedal). Replacement of the vacuum pump.
  • 2017-2018 (Belt): On early SHVS versions, recall to check or replace the starter-generator (ISG) belt which could fray or break.

What sets it apart 7

reliability

  • Exceptional Mechanical Reliability — The Suzuki Swift IV is renowned for its mechanical robustness, particularly its K-Series petrol engines, offering remarkable peace of mind and enduring durability.

economy

  • Ultra-Low Fuel Consumption — Thanks to its lightweight design and efficient petrol engines, the Swift IV boasts some of the lowest fuel consumption figures in its class, often below 5L/100km in real-world use for the 1.2 engine.

safety

  • Enhanced Safety — The Suzuki Swift IV achieved excellent results in Euro NCAP tests, ensuring optimal protection for its occupants.

handling

  • Agile Handling and Driving Pleasure — Its lightweight "Heartect" platform gives the Swift IV agile and dynamic road behavior, making every journey fun and engaging, with excellent body control and responsive steering.

braking

  • Effective and Reassuring Braking — Enjoy solid braking performance, with a firm pedal feel and respectable stopping distances, ensuring increased safety in all situations.

practicality

  • Comprehensive and Competitive Equipment — The Swift IV stands out for its excellent price-to-equipment ratio, often offering modern features as standard, such as adaptive cruise control, LED lighting, and heated seats.
  • Unique Versatility with All-Wheel Drive — Rare in this segment, the availability of AllGrip all-wheel drive offers increased versatility and grip, ideal for challenging conditions or varied terrains.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Toyota Yaris IV (for its mature hybridization), Mazda 2 (similar reliability, superior finish), Renault Clio V (more versatile and better finished), Peugeot 208 II.
  • Japan / Asia: Honda Fit/Jazz, Nissan Note, Toyota Vitz/Yaris.
  • Emerging markets (India, Latin America): Hyundai Grand i10 Nios, Tata Tiago, Ford Figo.

Verdict

The Suzuki Swift IV (AZ) is one of the most reliable and economical city cars on the used market. Devoid of the chronic problems affecting some of its European competitors (such as wet belts), it offers almost total peace of mind mechanically. The 1.2 Dualjet engine (with or without SHVS) is the quintessential sensible choice for daily use, while the Swift Sport (1.4 Boosterjet) offers an unbeatable price/pleasure/reliability ratio. However, one will have to accept compromises on interior finish quality, sound insulation, and paint fragility. A highly recommended purchase.
