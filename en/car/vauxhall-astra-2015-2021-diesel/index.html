Vauxhall Astra

K (B16) · 2015 – 2021

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The Vauxhall/Opel Astra K (B16 generation) marks a major turning point for the manufacturer. Voted 2016 European Car of the Year, it corrects the main flaw of its predecessor (the Astra J) by shedding up to 200 kg thanks to General Motors' new D2XX platform. More compact on the outside but more spacious on the inside, it introduced unprecedented technologies for the segment at the time, such as IntelliLux LED matrix headlights and AGR ergonomic seats. The 2019 facelift brought a whole new range of 3-cylinder engines (petrol and diesel) from GM development (and not PSA, despite the takeover of Opel/Vauxhall by PSA in 2017). It is a well-balanced, dynamic, and frugal compact car, but its mechanical reliability, particularly on certain pre-facelift diesels and petrols, requires increased vigilance when buying.

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic road handling and lightweight chassis
  • IntelliLux LED matrix headlights (rare in this segment at the time)
  • AGR-certified ergonomic seats (excellent for the back)
  • Very low real-world fuel consumption on 1.6 CDTi engines
  • Very competitive price/equipment ratio on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Hit-and-miss reliability of the 1.6 CDTi (timing chain on the gearbox side, clutch)
  • Possible engine failure on the 1.4 Turbo (LSPI) if poorly maintained
  • IntelliLink infotainment system prone to bugs (black screens, disconnections)
  • Quality of some lower interior plastics
  • Exorbitant replacement cost of IntelliLux headlights in case of failure

Check before buying

  • Clutch pedal: on 1.6 CDTi diesels, if the pedal is soft, does not return completely, or stays on the floor, the hydraulic release bearing is dead.
  • Cold noise (1.6 CDTi): a metallic clatter lasting more than 3 seconds on startup indicates a tired chain tensioner.
  • Oil history (1.4 Turbo): demand invoices proving the exclusive use of Dexos 1 Gen 2 oil.
  • IntelliLux headlights: test the automatic high beams at night to ensure no LED segment is burnt out.
  • On-board electronics: test the GPS, Bluetooth, radio, and reversing camera to detect IntelliLink bugs.

Verdict

The Vauxhall/Opel Astra K is a car full of intrinsic qualities: it is lightweight, pleasant to drive, very well equipped, and frugal. However, the reliability record is tarnished by costly design flaws. If you are looking for a **diesel**, the 1.6 CDTi is a risky bet due to its poorly placed timing chain and fragile clutch release bearing; favor a model whose chain has already been replaced with an invoice, or opt for the post-2019 1.5 Diesel. In petrol, avoid the 1.4 Turbos that have not been scrupulously maintained with Dexos 1 Gen 2 oil. The 1.0 Turbo (pre-2019) or 1.2 Turbo (post-2019) versions are the safest choices for peace of mind.
