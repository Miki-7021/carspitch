Summary

The second-generation Mercedes-Benz GLA (codename H247) marks a break from its predecessor (X156). Gone is the 'raised A-Class' look; the H247 fully embraces its compact SUV status with an increased height of 10 cm, offering better interior space and a more generous trunk. Based on the MFA2 platform, it integrates the spectacular MBUX dual-screen infotainment system. In 2023, the model received a facelift, introducing mild-hybridization (MHEV 48V) on gasoline engines, a revised light signature, and the removal of the central touchpad. While technologically very appealing, this premium model requires a significant maintenance budget and exhibits some electronic quirks typical of complex modern architectures.

Strengths 5

  • Spectacular interior presentation (MBUX system, ambient lighting)
  • Significantly increased interior space compared to the previous generation
  • Suspension comfort (especially with adaptive damping)
  • 1.3L gasoline engines (M282) generally very reliable and economical
  • Careful sound insulation on high-end versions

Weaknesses 5

  • Prohibitive purchase price and option costs
  • Quality of some lower-part plastics unworthy of the badge
  • Dual-clutch gearbox (7G-DCT) sometimes hesitant and causing jerks in urban driving
  • Frequent electronic bugs on the MBUX system (black screens, restarts)
  • Poor rear visibility (reversing camera essential)

Check before buying

  • Onboard electronics: Test all MBUX functions, navigation, Bluetooth, and ambient lighting.
  • Gearbox: Ensure there are no pronounced jerks during low-speed maneuvers or downshifts (sign of a need for reprogramming or fluid change).
  • Running gear: Premature wear of front tires on front-wheel-drive versions (especially PHEV due to weight and torque).
  • Panoramic sunroof: Check for absence of wind noise, creaks, and proper functioning of the sunshade.
  • Service history: The Mercedes network is strict; a missed DCT gearbox oil change voids any warranty coverage in case of failure.

Official recalls

  • November 2023 (Software): Update of the communication module (eCall) which could transmit an erroneous position to emergency services.
  • July 2022 (Steering): Risk of detachment of the universal joint of the steering shaft (loss of control). Concerns a small series produced in 2021.
  • March 2022 (Bodywork): Poor bonding of the polycarbonate panel between the windshield and the panoramic sunroof, risk of detachment while driving.
  • October 2021 (PHEV): Risk of short circuit or fire due to a sealing defect in the high-voltage battery housing on 250 e models.
  • February 2021 (Braking): Possible brake fluid leak at the front calipers on some AMG models.

  • Confident and Serene Road Handling — Enjoy stable and predictable road handling, inspiring confidence in all conditions. Its braking system offers excellent performance with short stopping distances and a firm pedal for optimal control.

Comfort

Alternatives

  • Europe: BMW X1 (U11) (better finished, more dynamic), Audi Q3 (F3) (more classic, very well-rounded), Volvo XC40 (excellent comfort, successful PHEV).
  • Japan / Asia: Lexus UX (ultra-reliable non-rechargeable hybrid, but tiny trunk).
  • North America: BMW X1, Audi Q3, Acura ADX, Lexus NX (slightly larger but price competitor).
  • Emerging Markets: Often competed against by local high-end SUVs or the BMW X1 sDrive18i.

Verdict

The Mercedes GLA H247 is a premium compact SUV that relies heavily on its design and technological content. If you're targeting a gasoline engine, the 1.3L unit (GLA 200) is the most rational choice: it's sufficiently powerful, economical, and boasts an excellent reliability record thanks to its shared design with the Renault-Nissan Alliance. The 2.0L (GLA 250) offers more refinement but had chaotic beginnings (cylinder heads). Purchasing a recent used model is recommended, provided you demand a 100% clear service history, especially for the dual-clutch gearbox and software updates. Avoid models used exclusively in the city, especially diesels or if the DCT gearbox is jerky.
