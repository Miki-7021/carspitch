Summary

The Vauxhall Grandland (sold under the Opel brand in continental Europe) benefited from a major facelift in 2021, losing the 'X' suffix from its name. Based on the Stellantis EMP2 platform (shared with the Peugeot 3008 II), this phase 2 stands out with the adoption of the 'Vizor' grille characteristic of the brand's new identity and a modernized interior with the 'Pure Panel' (dual digital screen). Although comfortable and well-equipped, the Grandland inherits powertrains from the Stellantis group, which requires extreme vigilance regarding reliability, particularly concerning the 1.5 Turbo D diesel engine and the 1.2 Turbo petrol block, both subject to documented major design flaws.

Strengths 5

  • Modernized and assertive exterior design (Vizor grille)
  • Good level of suspension comfort and soundproofing
  • Ergonomics of the physical controls retained for the climate control
  • Generous trunk volume (on combustion versions)
  • Performance of the 300 hp PHEV version (Hybrid4)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the entry-level combustion engines (1.2 petrol and 1.5 diesel)
  • Aging and sometimes slow infotainment system
  • Average rear seat space for the category
  • Reduced trunk volume on plug-in hybrid versions (PHEV)
  • Significant depreciation due to the reputation of Stellantis engines

Check before buying

  • On the 1.5 Diesel: Demand proof of the camshaft chain replacement (upgrade from 7 mm to 8 mm) and check the operation of the AdBlue system (dashboard warning lights).
  • On the 1.2 Petrol: Check the condition of the timing belt through the oil filler cap and demand a 100% up-to-date service book.
  • On the PHEV versions: Request a high-voltage battery health certificate (SOH - State of Health) and check the smoothness of gear shifts (e-EAT8).
  • Running gear: Check for the absence of suspicious noises at the front (frequent recalls on suspension arms).

Official recalls

  • [2026-06-15] Power steering: Certain vehicles equipped with dual-pinion electric power steering may lose steering assistance due to a failure. Risk of injury.
  • [2026-02-23] Front suspension: The fixing screws on the left and right front support of the ball joint on the front lower suspension arm may break. Risk of injury.
  • [2026-01-28] Fuel system: Possible leak in the high-pressure pipe, between the pump and the rail. Risk of fire.
  • [2025-09-19] High-Voltage Battery (PHEV): Defective high-voltage battery that can cause overheating and lead to a vehicle fire.
  • [2025-07-25] 1.5 Diesel Engine (Camshaft chain): Due to oil carbonization and high contact pressure, the strength of the 7 mm camshaft chain may be reduced, leading to its breakage. Risk of engine failure and injury.
  • [2025-06-19] Fuel system: The low-pressure fuel line may touch the ignition coil screw, damaging it and causing a leak. Risk of fire.
  • [2025-03-07] Wiring harness: Incorrect routing of the front seat wiring harness which may lead to damage. Risk of injury or fire.
  • [2025-01-31] High-pressure fuel pump: Weakness in the crimping interface of the quick coupling connector, possible leak. Risk of fire.
  • [2025-01-10] Front suspension: Defective fixing screws on the left or right front ball joint support. Risk of injury.
  • [2023-11-27] Emission control system (Diesel): Software error preventing the malfunction indicator light (MIL) from illuminating if the urea (AdBlue) injector stops working. Environmental risk.

Verdict

The facelifted Vauxhall/Opel Grandland is a compact SUV full of objective qualities (comfort, design, equipment), but it is weighed down by the catastrophic reliability of its main combustion engines. If you are looking for a Diesel (1.5 Turbo D), caution is advised: *only* buy if the vehicle has already undergone the camshaft chain replacement (upgrade to 8 mm) justified by an invoice, otherwise you risk total engine failure. The classic 1.2 petrol is also to be avoided due to its wet belt. The only recommendable versions are the recent 1.2 MHEV (with a chain) or the PHEV versions, provided you carefully check the battery condition and the completed recalls.
