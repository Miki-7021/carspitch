Nissan Patrol

Y61 (GU) · 1997 – 2013

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Nissan models

Summary

The Nissan Patrol Y61 (often called the GU series in certain markets like Australia) is a true off-road legend. Designed around a robust ladder chassis and two rigid axles, it is renowned for its exceptional off-road capabilities and durability in extreme conditions. Although European markets primarily saw diesel engines (often problematic in their early days), the Middle East, Australian, and Asian markets benefited from inline-6 gasoline engines (TB45E and TB48DE) with almost mythical reliability. The 4.8L engine (TB48DE) in particular is famous for its ability to exceed 500,000 km without issue and for its immense tuning potential. In summary, the gasoline Patrol Y61 is an indestructible dinosaur, whose only real flaw is gargantuan fuel consumption.

*Note: Although official production for most markets stopped around 2013 (replaced by the Y62), the Y61 continued to be produced under the name 'Safari' or 'Super Safari' for the Middle East until 2022/2023.*

Strengths 5

  • Legendary reliability of the gasoline engines (TB45E, TB48DE) and the TD42 diesel.
  • Exceptional off-road capabilities (rigid axles, rear differential lock).
  • Robustness of the transmission (manual and automatic gearboxes, transfer case).
  • Extremely solid ladder chassis, ideal for expeditions and heavy towing.
  • Generous interior space and excellent visibility.

Weaknesses 5

  • Astronomical fuel consumption on gasoline versions (often > 20L/100km).
  • Disastrous reliability of the early 3.0L ZD30 diesel engines (2000-2006).
  • Clumsy road handling, vague steering, and barely sufficient braking given the weight (2.5 tonnes).
  • Susceptibility to corrosion on models used in cold or coastal regions.
  • Aging interior and soundproofing that could be improved at high speeds.

Check before buying

  • Corrosion: Carefully inspect the chassis, wheel arches, rocker panels, and trunk floor.
  • Overheating history: On all models, check the condition of the radiator, viscous coupling, and the absence of 'mayonnaise' in the oil.
  • Off-road wear: Check the underbody, radius arms, leaks at the front axle swivel hubs (oil seals), and play in the steering.
  • ZD30 engine (if buying diesel): Demand a clear maintenance history and check if reliability modifications (NADS) have been made.
  • Airbag recalls: Ensure the Takata recall has been performed (fatal risk).

Verdict

The Nissan Patrol Y61 is a true tank, designed to survive the most hostile environments on the planet. If you opt for the gasoline engines (TB45E or the incredible TB48DE), you are buying one of the most reliable vehicles ever built, capable of handling astronomical mileages with minimal maintenance. The tradeoff is a colossal fuel budget. The diesel versions require more discernment: the legendary TD42 is a safe investment, while the 3.0L ZD30 from the 2000-2006 years should be absolutely avoided unless it has been heavily modified for reliability. For daily road use, its rustic behavior makes it obsolete, but for raids, off-roading, or long-distance travel, it is a masterful choice.
