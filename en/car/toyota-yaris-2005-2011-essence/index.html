Toyota Yaris

II (XP90) · 2005 – 2011

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The second-generation Toyota Yaris (code name XP90) marked a major evolution compared to its predecessor. Designed at Toyota's European design center (ED2), it grew significantly to offer record spaciousness in the city car category, notably thanks to its sliding rear bench seat. Renowned for its legendary reliability, it achieved 5 stars in the Euro NCAP crash test. A facelift occurred in early 2009, bringing slight aesthetic touches (bumpers, lights) and introducing the new 1.33 Dual VVT-i gasoline engine coupled with a 6-speed manual gearbox and a Stop & Start system. It's a true urban 'workhorse', particularly sought after on the used market for its robustness, although the perceived quality of its interior plastics is a step down.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability of gasoline engines (especially 1.3 and 1.5).
  • Remarkable spaciousness for its size (3.75 m long).
  • Sliding rear bench seat (2/3 - 1/3) and flat floor (Easy Flat system).
  • Very low maintenance and insurance costs.
  • Chain timing on all engines (gasoline and diesel).
  • Passive safety (5 Euro NCAP stars, up to 9 airbags).

Weaknesses 6

  • MMT (Multi-Mode Transmission) robotic gearbox: slow, jerky, and unreliable.
  • Interior plastic quality (hard, scratches very easily).
  • Light sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise).
  • Limited boot volume when the bench seat is moved back to the maximum (272 liters).
  • Driving position could be improved for taller drivers.
  • Central digital display that doesn't appeal to everyone.

Check before buying

  • Traces of pink coolant on the accessory belt side (sign of a leaking water pump, very common on 1.0 and 1.3).
  • Engine oil level, particularly on the 1.0 VVT-i and 1.33 VVT-i before 2011.
  • Gearbox behavior: if it's an MMT, flee in case of jerks on startup or slipping.
  • Clunking in the steering column during low-speed turns (worn universal joint or steering shaft).
  • Condition of the paint clear coat (tends to peel on red and blue colors after 10 years).
  • On the D-4D diesel: absence of black smoke on acceleration and stable idle (injectors).

Official recalls

  • Accelerator Pedal (January 2010): The famous 'PedalGate' recall. Risk of the accelerator pedal getting stuck in the depressed position. Installation of a reinforcement shim.
  • Takata Airbags (2013 and subsequent years): Replacement of the passenger and/or driver airbag gas generator (risk of metallic fragment projection). Concerns almost the entire production.
  • Steering Column (January 2009): Risk of crack on the steering column support. Inspection and replacement if necessary.
  • Window Regulators (October 2012): Risk of short circuit and overheating of the driver's side window control panel (lack of thermal grease).
  • Front Seats (August 2014): Spring of the front seat slide locking mechanism may break, making the seat movable.

safety

  • High-Level Passive Safety — Travel with complete confidence thanks to optimal protection for all occupants, attested by rigorous tests. The Yaris II incorporates up to 9 airbags for maximum safety.

practicality

  • Ingenious Habitability and Modularity — Despite its compact size, the Yaris II surprises with its remarkable interior space and its sliding rear bench (2/3 - 1/3) with the Easy Flat system, offering a flat floor and exceptional adaptability for your passengers and luggage.

economy

  • Controlled Running and Maintenance Costs — Benefit from a particularly economical vehicle to use. Its petrol engines are fuel-efficient, and its maintenance and insurance costs are among the lowest in its category, making the Yaris II a smart choice for your budget.

handling

  • Stable and Reassuring Road Handling — The Yaris II offers predictable and reassuring road handling, ideal for both city and longer journeys. Its light steering and well-controlled body roll contribute to a sense of safety and driving comfort.

braking

  • Efficient and Safe Braking — Equipped with ABS with EBD (Electronic Brakeforce Distribution) and Brake Assist (BA), the Yaris II ensures competent and safe braking performance, guaranteeing adequate deceleration in all circumstances.

Performance

  • Lively and Efficient Petrol Engines — The petrol engines, notably the 1.3 VVT-i (87 hp) and the newer 1.33 Dual VVT-i (100 hp) with its 6-speed gearbox, offer sufficient dynamism for all situations and optimized fuel consumption, even outside the city.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Renault Clio III (more comfortable, better finished), Peugeot 207 (better road handling), Honda Jazz II (superior spaciousness, equivalent reliability), VW Polo V (superior finish, more fragile TSI engines). - **North America**: Honda Fit (Jazz), Nissan Versa, Ford Fiesta. - **Asia / Emerging Markets**: Suzuki Swift, Honda City/Jazz, Hyundai i20.

Verdict

The Toyota Yaris II is one of the safest used car purchases on the market, provided you choose the right version. **The verdict is a big YES for gasoline engines with a manual gearbox.** The 1.3 VVT-i (87 hp) engine represents the perfect compromise: indestructible, sufficient for leaving the city, and very economical. The 1.0 is a good purely urban choice but requires monitoring its water pump. The D-4D diesel is only for those who drive a lot, otherwise, costly clogging problems may arise. **Golden rule: absolutely avoid models equipped with the MMT robotic gearbox**, as failures are frequent and repairs are prohibitively expensive compared to the vehicle's value.
