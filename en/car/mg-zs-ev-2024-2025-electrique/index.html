MG ZS EV

II · 2024 – 2025

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation MG ZS (or Phase 2 to Gen II transition depending on the market) marks a stage of maturity for the Sino-British manufacturer SAIC Motor. Over the 2024-2025 period, the catalog is undergoing a major transition: the continuation of the very popular restyled ZS EV (often called Phase 2), and the introduction of the true Generation II (inaugurated in late 2024 by the Hybrid+ powertrain before the arrival of the new EV in 2025). The ZS EV has established itself as one of the electric SUVs with the best price/range ratio on the market. It is available with two battery capacities (51 kWh LFP and 72.6 kWh NMC). Although the electric powertrain is the spearhead in Europe, the ZS remains a global model offered with internal combustion engines (naturally aspirated and turbocharged) and now hybrid (HEV) powertrains to meet different markets. Overall mechanical reliability is reassuring, covered by a 7-year / 150,000 km warranty, although the user experience is sometimes marred by software shortcomings.

Strengths 6

  • Unbeatable price/range ratio in the electric market
  • Reassuring manufacturer warranty (7 years or 150,000 km)
  • Generous interior space and boot volume (448 liters)
  • Extremely durable LFP chemistry (51 kWh) and tolerant of 100% charges
  • Presence of the very practical V2L (Vehicle-to-Load) function
  • Extensive standard equipment (Luxury trim)

Weaknesses 6

  • iSmart infotainment system slow and sometimes buggy (CarPlay/Android Auto disconnections)
  • Limited DC fast charging power (92 kW max), average charging curve
  • Air conditioning ergonomics (mandatory use of the touchscreen)
  • Intrusive and sometimes abrupt driving aids (ADAS) (lane keeping assist)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind noise from 110 km/h)
  • Dealership network still developing in some regions

Check before buying

  • History of software updates (BMS, iSmart, ADAS) performed at the dealership
  • Condition of the charging socket (pin wear, locking latch operation)
  • Front suspension noises (clunking over speed bumps reported by some owners)
  • Operation of air conditioning and heating (heat pump absent on some trims/markets)
  • Body panel alignment (variable quality control ex-factory)

Verdict

The MG ZS EV (2024-2025) is a pragmatic and highly recommendable choice for anyone looking for a family electric SUV without breaking the bank. The electric powertrain (SAIC/CATL motor and batteries) is fundamentally reliable, and the 7-year warranty covers major risks. The 51 kWh (LFP) version is the most robust in the long term, while the 72.6 kWh offers true road versatility. However, some compromises must be accepted: a capricious multimedia interface, imperfect driving aids, and a fast-charging speed that lags behind Korean benchmarks. The arrival of the Hybrid+ (Gen II) powertrain offers an excellent transitional alternative for those reluctant to plug in.
