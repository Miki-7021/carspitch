Summary

Opel Insignia B (Grand Sport / Sports Tourer)

The second-generation Opel Insignia (Insignia B) was launched in 2017. Although Opel was acquired by the PSA Group (now Stellantis) the same year, the Insignia B is entirely based on a General Motors architecture (E2XX platform). It was marketed under various identities worldwide (Vauxhall in the UK, Buick Regal in North America and China, Holden Commodore in Oceania).

Lighter by nearly 200 kg compared to the heavy A-generation, it offers transformed road handling, generous interior space, and an excellent price/performance ratio. The facelift occurred in 2020, introducing new engines (including new GM 3 and 4-cylinder units) and improving the IntelliLux matrix lighting system. Production ceased in early 2023 without a direct replacement.

Overall design note: It is one of the best road cars in its category, particularly suited for high-mileage drivers with a diesel engine, although it suffers from some peripheral weaknesses (AdBlue, electronics).

Strengths 5

  • Superb road handling and suspension comfort (especially with FlexRide adaptive damping).
  • Rear passenger space and boot volume (Sports Tourer).
  • Highly effective IntelliLux LED matrix headlights (up to 84 LEDs per headlight on the facelift).
  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used market.
  • Excellent sound insulation on high-end versions.

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous timing chain reliability on the 1.6 CDTi.
  • Capricious AdBlue system (pump, injector, NOx sensor) on all diesels.
  • Imposing size (nearly 5 meters for the estate) and poor rear visibility.
  • Infotainment system (IntelliLink) prone to bugs, slowness, and intermittent restarts.
  • Quality of some plastics in the lower part of the cabin.

Check before buying

  • Cold engine noise (1.6 CDTi): Any metallic rattling on the gearbox side indicates a stretched chain (invoice > €2000).
  • AdBlue system: Check for absence of engine light or 'Starting impossible in XXX km' message.
  • IntelliLux Headlights: Check that all LEDs illuminate. A defective headlight costs over €1200 to replace.
  • Automatic gearbox: Aisin (8-speed) and GM (9-speed) gearboxes should shift smoothly. Demand a gearbox oil change around 80,000 km.
  • Onboard electronics: Test the GPS, Bluetooth, rearview camera, and heated/ventilated seats.

Official recalls

Official Manufacturer Recalls

  • November 2020 (Code 20-C-172): Risk of fuel leak. A fuel line can rub against a support and puncture. Concerns 2018-2020 models.
  • August 2019 (Code 19-C-074): Rear suspension links. A welding defect can lead to the link breaking, affecting road holding. Models manufactured between 2017 and 2019.
  • May 2018: Seat belts. The right rear seat belt retractor may not lock correctly in the event of an impact.
  • February 2021: Braking software. Update to the Electronic Brake Control Module (EBCM) to prevent loss of braking assistance (primarily concerns North American Buick Regal models, but some Insignias affected).

What sets it apart 6

safety

  • Advanced Safety and Intelligent Lighting — The Opel Insignia B achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult occupant protection and 82% for child occupant protection. Its highly effective IntelliLux LED matrix headlights provide exceptional visibility, enhancing active safety.

comfort

  • Exceptional Travel Comfort — Enjoy imperial road handling and remarkable suspension comfort, especially with the FlexRide system. The excellent soundproofing in high-end versions transforms every journey into a serene and relaxing experience, ideal for long distances.

practicality

  • Generous Space and Boot Volume — The Insignia B stands out with its generous interior space, offering optimal comfort for rear passengers. The Sports Tourer version provides an impressive boot volume, perfect for families and significant loading needs.

Performance

  • Robust and Efficient Diesel Engines — Designed for high-mileage drivers, the Insignia B offers 2.0 CDTi diesel engines (170 hp and 174 hp post-2020) renowned for their robustness, generous torque, and excellent efficiency on highways, ensuring reliable and enjoyable performance.

economy

  • Unbeatable Price/Equipment Ratio — Benefit from a road car offering premium segment features at a generalist price. Its excellent price/equipment ratio makes it a unique opportunity in the used car market, combining luxury and accessibility.

handling

  • Stable Handling and Effective Braking — Experience stable and secure handling, focused on comfort, with excellent body control and reassuring predictability. The braking system offers good and progressive performance, with effective stopping distances, competitive for its segment.

  • North America (vs Buick Regal): Honda Accord (more reliable), Toyota Camry (reliability benchmark), Mazda 6 (sportier).

  • China: Volkswagen Magotan/Passat, Honda Accord, Nissan Altima.

Verdict

Final Verdict

The Opel Insignia B is an underrated road car that offers performance worthy of the premium segment for the price of a mainstream model. For a diesel purchase, engine choice is crucial.

It is absolutely essential to avoid the 1.6 CDTi (110/136 hp) unless the timing chain has been recently replaced with supporting invoices. The best choice for high-mileage drivers is the 2.0 CDTi 170 hp (or its 174 hp evolution post-2020), which proves robust, torquey, and suited for highway driving. However, be aware of the anti-pollution peripherals (AdBlue, NOx sensors) which require regular road use and the use of preventive additives. For petrol, the 1.5 Turbo is an excellent compromise provided it has been scrupulously maintained with the specific Dexos 1 Gen 2 oil.
