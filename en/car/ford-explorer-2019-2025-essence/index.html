Ford Explorer

VI (U625) · 2019 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 64/100 — moyen

Summary

The sixth-generation Ford Explorer (U625) marks a major technical turning point by abandoning the FWD architecture of its predecessor to return to a rear-wheel-drive platform (CD6), shared with the Lincoln Aviator. This change gives it more dynamic road handling, better proportions, and increased towing capacity.

In North America, it stands out as a pillar of the family SUV segment (Midsize SUV), while in Europe, it is positioned as a large premium SUV, imported exclusively as a plug-in hybrid (PHEV) until the arrival of the 100% electric model (which is a completely different vehicle based on the Volkswagen MEB platform). A mid-cycle facelift was introduced for the 2025 model year (from 2023 in China), modernizing the interior and the infotainment system.

**Overall reliability rating:** Initially ranked below average by ADAC, TÜV Report, and J.D. Power, the launch of this generation was marred by serious quality control issues at the Chicago plant. The 2019-2021 model years suffered an avalanche of recalls (transmission, rear axle, electronics). Reliability gradually improved from 2022 onwards, although the model remains subject to regular recall campaigns.
