Summary

The third generation of the Chevrolet Colorado (code name GMT595) marks a major technological and mechanical breakthrough for the midsize pickup segment. Launched for the 2023 model year, it abandons the engine diversity of the previous generation (which offered a naturally aspirated 4-cylinder, a V6, and a diesel) to focus exclusively on a turbocharged 2.7L 4-cylinder block in North America.

Note on the targeted Diesel engine: The famous 2.8L Duramax (LWN) diesel engine was removed from the North American catalog with the arrival of this 3rd generation (GMT595). However, it remains an essential benchmark on the used market (Gen 2: 2016-2022) and continues to be offered on international markets (notably in South America under the name Chevrolet S10). This sheet therefore details the 2.8L Duramax to meet demand, while analyzing the new 2.7L Turbo that replaced it on the GMT595. The Gen 3 Colorado stands out with a stiffened chassis, a modernized interior with standard screens, and increased off-road capabilities (Trail Boss and ZR2 versions).

Strengths 5

  • Proven reliability and masterful torque of the 2.8L Duramax (on models/markets where available)
  • Impressive performance of the new 2.7L Turbo L3B (torque equivalent to a V8 or a diesel)
  • Excellent towing capacities for the category (up to 3,492 kg / 7,700 lbs)
  • Muscular exterior design and very modern interior (11.3-inch screens as standard)
  • Extremely competent factory off-road versions (Trail Boss, ZR2)

Weaknesses 4

  • Regrettable disappearance of the 2.8L Duramax diesel engine on the North American market for the 3rd generation
  • Teething problems on 2023 models (software, phantom emergency braking, assembly recalls)
  • Fuel consumption of the 2.7L Turbo under load or in the ZR2 version is quite high
  • Omnipresent hard plastics in entry-level trims (WT, LT)

Check before buying

  • Check if safety recalls (notably the hub bolts and front camera) have been performed on 2023-2024 models.
  • On the 2.8L Duramax (used/import): check the history of the DEF (AdBlue) system and the condition of the DPF.
  • On the 2.7L Turbo: ensure oil changes were done on time, listen for any abnormal turbo noise when cold.
  • Inspect the underbody on Z71, Trail Boss, and ZR2 versions to detect off-road related damage.

Official recalls

The 3rd generation (2023+) has undergone several teething recalls (NHTSA):

  • 26/02/2026 *(Anticipated/administrative NHTSA date)*: Incomplete owner's manual or containing errors (affects several GM models).
  • 19/09/2024 (NHTSA 24V703000): Front seat belt buckle attachment bolt (left or right) potentially improperly tightened at the factory (2023-2024 Colorado).
  • 28/06/2024 (NHTSA 24V491000): Fuel pump locking ring improperly secured to the tank, risk of fuel leak (2024 Colorado).
  • 23/05/2024 (NHTSA 24V366000): Installation of incorrect tires not matching the label, leading to erroneous TPMS system data (2024 Colorado).
  • 28/03/2024 (NHTSA 24V237000): Front wheel hub bolts overtightened and potentially damaged during assembly (2023 Colorado).
  • 22/02/2024 (NHTSA 24V133000): Defective front camera module (FCM) that can detect false obstacles and trigger unexpected automatic emergency braking (AEB) (2023 Colorado).

What sets it apart 6

Performance

  • Unrivaled Diesel Engine Torque — The 2.8L Duramax engine delivers exceptional low-end torque, ideal for heavy towing and robust performance, with proven reliability.
  • Exceptional Off-Road Performance — Specialized versions like the Trail Boss and ZR2 offer unparalleled off-road capabilities, ready to tackle the most demanding terrains straight from the factory. The 2.7L Turbo L3B delivers massive torque for impressive agility.

Practicality

  • Class-Leading Towing Capabilities — The Chevrolet Colorado excels with impressive towing capacities, positioning it among the leaders in its category for hauling your heaviest loads.

Comfort

  • Modern Design and Connected Interior — Enjoy a muscular exterior design and a very modern interior, equipped as standard with 11.3-inch screens for an intuitive and comfortable driving experience.

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability and Robust Technology — Whether it's the robust 2.8L Duramax or the new 2.7L Turbo derived from the Silverado, the Colorado benefits from reliable and durable engineering.

Economy

  • Optimized Fuel Efficiency — The 2.8L Duramax engine is renowned for its excellent fuel economy, offering superior range for your long journeys and professional use.

Alternatives

  • North America: Toyota Tacoma (absolute benchmark in reliability, transition to 4-cyl turbo hybrid in 2024), Ford Ranger (new 2024 model with V6 available), Nissan Frontier (very reliable naturally aspirated V6).
  • Global Markets (Asia/South Am./Europe): Toyota Hilux (indestructible 2.4/2.8 D-4D), Ford Ranger (2.0 Bi-Turbo or 3.0 V6 Diesel), Isuzu D-Max (very robust 1.9/3.0 diesel).

Verdict

The 3rd generation Chevrolet Colorado (GMT595) is an extremely accomplished midsize pickup dynamically and technologically. If you are specifically looking for the 2.8L Duramax diesel engine, you will have to turn to the previous generation (2016-2022) or export markets (S10), where this engine shines with its reliability, torque, and longevity (despite some quirks of the emissions control system).

For the new 2023+ model, the 2.7L Turbo 4-cylinder brilliantly replaces the diesel in terms of pure performance, although it consumes more under load. Initial reliability data (J.D. Power, ADAC) places the Colorado in the upper average of its category. However, the numerous assembly and software recalls on the 2023 model year urge caution: favor a 2024 model or ensure that all recall campaigns (AEB camera, wheel hubs, fuel pump) have been rigorously applied by the dealer.
