Toyota Hilux

VIII (AN120/AN130) · 2015 – 2020

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The eighth generation of the Toyota Hilux (AN120/AN130 series) was launched in 2015, marking a major evolution for the world's best-selling pickup. While retaining its legendary ladder frame chassis and bulletproof robustness, this iteration focused on ride comfort, soundproofing, and perceived cabin quality to compete with increasingly 'SUV-like' rivals (such as the Ford Ranger). It inaugurates the new 'Global Diesel' (GD) family of diesel engines, designed to offer more low-end torque and better efficiency. Although the model underwent a major facelift in late 2020 (with a power increase), the 2015-2020 period represents the first phase of this generation. Overall, the Hilux VIII remains the absolute benchmark for commercial reliability, although it experienced some teething problems with its emission control systems (FAP).

Strengths 5

  • Unrivaled overall reliability and chassis robustness
  • Off-road capabilities (4x4) and payload (1 ton)
  • Exceptional resale value on the used market
  • Worldwide availability of spare parts
  • Notable improvement in comfort and soundproofing compared to generation VII

Weaknesses 5

  • Recurring FAP (Particulate Filter) issues on GD diesels from 2015 to 2018
  • Very firm suspension comfort when unloaded (typical of leaf-sprung pickups)
  • Dated, slow infotainment system without Apple CarPlay/Android Auto before 2020
  • Lack of active safety equipment on entry-level trims before 2019
  • 2.4 D-4D engine a bit sluggish in acceleration when overtaking under load

Check before buying

  • FAP regeneration history and presence of the manual regeneration button (crucial on 2.4 and 2.8 D-4D).
  • Underbody condition: signs of off-road impacts, superficial rust on the ladder frame.
  • Airbox sealing (check for the absence of dust in the intake duct).
  • Squeaking from the rear leaf springs (often requires cleaning and greasing of the pads).
  • Drivetrain maintenance: fluid changes for the axles and transfer case, greasing of the shafts.

Official recalls

Data from official registers (Rappel Conso France):

  • [2023-07-03] Auxiliary battery terminal (Affects Hilux, Aygo, Yaris, Land Cruiser, etc.): The auxiliary battery terminal may be defective. This can disable the power steering assist. Additionally, the battery terminal can overheat. Risk: fire.
  • [2024-08-22] Camera housings (Affects various Lexus/Toyota models): The camera housings can separate under stress due to improper welding, allowing water ingress. Risk: injuries. *(Expert note: Mainly affects recent TNGA platforms and Land Cruiser 300, marginal application on Hilux VIII).*
  • [2024-06-26] Immobilizer device (Affects C-HR, RAV4, Lexus NX/RX): Design of the accessory immobilizer device is not robust enough, allowing the vehicle to be in "ready on" mode. *(Expert note: Does not directly affect the Hilux).*
  • [2024-06-10] Front transmission oil leak (Affects Proace City, Berlingo, Partner, Combo): A defective seal can cause an oil leak at the front transmission. *(Expert note: Affects Toyota-rebadged Stellantis commercial vehicles, not the Hilux).*

*Notable international recalls (excluding Rappel Conso):* - FAP / DPF (Australia/Europe): Massive service campaigns to replace or reprogram the particulate filter systems on 1GD/2GD engines (2015-2018) following premature clogging and white smoke emissions.

Verdict

The eighth-generation Toyota Hilux (2015-2020) remains the undisputed king of the commercial pickup category. If you are looking for a vehicle to work hard, tow, or do intensive off-roading, there is no better choice on the used market. However, its transition to modern emission standards has blemished its perfect reliability record: the 2.4 and 2.8 diesel engines (GD family) from 2015 to 2018 suffer from chronic FAP issues if they are not used under load or on long trips. Buying advice: Favor a model from late 2018 onwards (equipped with the manual FAP regeneration button) or ensure that the Toyota software updates have been performed. Avoid examples that have been driven exclusively in the city.
