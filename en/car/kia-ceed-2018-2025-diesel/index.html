Kia Ceed

III (CD) · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

All Kia models

Summary

The third-generation Kia Ceed (code CD) marks the maturity of the Korean compact car designed for Europe. Gone is the apostrophe in its name (formerly cee'd), it adopts a more assertive design, a significantly improved interior finish, and embedded technologies on par with European benchmarks. Based on the K2 platform, it offers an excellent compromise between comfort and dynamism. Restyled in late 2021 (new logo, revised bumpers, technological update), it shines with its 7-year / 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty, which provides reassurance on the used car market. Although demand is shifting towards gasoline and hybrid, its 1.6 CRDi (Smartstream) diesel engine remains a formidable option for high-mileage drivers, combining exemplary fuel efficiency and proven reliability.

Strengths 5

  • 7-year / 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty (transferable)
  • Excellent overall reliability, particularly the 1.6 CRDi engine
  • Interior ergonomics with physical controls retained
  • Sound road handling and precise front axle
  • Very competitive price/equipment ratio on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • DCT7 automatic gearbox sometimes rough at low speeds (dry clutches)
  • Imperfect sound insulation (road noise on highways)
  • Firm suspension, especially on GT-Line and GT trims
  • Infotainment system prone to bugs (black screens) before update
  • Average rear three-quarter visibility

Check before buying

  • Service book: essential to maintain the 7-year warranty (very low tolerance for delays at Kia).
  • DCT7 gearbox: check for absence of juddering or excessive slipping during hill starts.
  • 1.6 CRDi Diesel: check for absence of engine light related to the anti-pollution system (AdBlue/DPF).
  • Front lights: flickering or failure of LED daytime running lights (DRL) on 2018-2020 models (headlight unit replacement often covered by warranty).
  • Electronics: test proper functioning of the touchscreen, rearview camera, and driving aids (LKA).

Official recalls

  • December 2023 (PHEV) : Risk of loss of motive power due to a software defect in the high-voltage battery management system (BMS).
  • May 2021 : Update to the eCall system which might not transmit correct GPS coordinates in case of an accident.
  • October 2020 : Recall on certain models equipped with the 1.6 CRDi engine for a check of the brake assist vacuum pump (risk of pedal hardening).
  • 2019 : Check of rear seat belt pretensioners on a small production series.

Verdict

The Kia Ceed III is one of the most rational and reliable compact cars on the market. The choice of the 1.6 CRDi diesel engine is particularly relevant for high-mileage drivers. This Smartstream (U3) family engine is a success: it is quiet at steady speeds, extremely frugal, and free from major mechanical flaws (reliable chain timing). The only potential pitfalls concern the anti-pollution peripherals (AdBlue, DPF), a common issue with all modern diesels, which require regular road trips to prevent clogging. Coupled with the 7-year warranty, a 1.6 CRDi Ceed with a clear service history is a highly recommendable purchase, offering rare peace of mind in its category.
