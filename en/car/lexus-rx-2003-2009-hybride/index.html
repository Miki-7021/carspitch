Lexus RX

III (XU30) · 2003 – 2009

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The **Lexus RX (XU30)**, corresponding to the second generation of the model (although sometimes mistakenly referred to as the 3rd depending on the market or the inclusion of the Toyota Harrier), is an absolute pioneer in the premium SUV segment. It made automotive history by introducing the **RX 400h** in 2005, the world's very first luxury hybrid SUV. Based on the Toyota K platform, it prioritizes cushioned comfort, silent operation, and exemplary finish over sportiness. The RX 400h combines a 3.3L gasoline V6 with two electric motors (E-Four system) to offer V8 performance with 4-cylinder fuel consumption. A slight facelift took place in 2007, notably introducing the RX 350 to replace the RX 300 in Europe. **Overall rating: A monument of reliability, particularly in the hybrid version, which ages exceptionally well if maintenance is kept up.**

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of the hybrid system (HSD) and V6 engines
  • Masterful ride comfort and very high-level soundproofing
  • Assembly quality and aging of materials (excluding dashboard)
  • Excellent quality Mark Levinson audio system
  • Reasonable maintenance cost for a luxury SUV (especially the hybrid with no accessory belts, conventional alternator, or starter)

Weaknesses 5

  • Chronic cracking of the dashboard covering (XU30 disease)
  • Air suspension (if equipped) very expensive to replace in case of airbag leaks
  • Infotainment system and GPS totally obsolete today
  • Clumsy road handling, significant body roll (no sporty vocation)
  • High fuel consumption of the 100% combustion versions (RX 300/330/350)

Check before buying

  • **Hybrid Battery (RX 400h):** Demand a battery health test. An original battery over 15 years old may require reconditioning or replacement (€2000 to €3000).
  • **Inverter (RX 400h):** Check that the inverter coolant is circulating properly (functional electric water pump).
  • **Air suspension:** Check that the vehicle raises and lowers correctly and does not sag after being parked overnight.
  • **Dashboard:** Carefully inspect for cracks above the glove box and instrument cluster.
  • **Steering rack:** Inspect the boots to detect any power steering fluid leaks.
  • **Timing history:** On RX 300, RX 330, and RX 400h, verify that the belt has been done (every 150,000 km or 10 years).

Verdict

The **second-generation Lexus RX (XU30)** is one of the most reliable used SUVs in the world, particularly in its **RX 400h** version. Despite its age, it offers a level of comfort, silence, and refinement that still surpasses many modern vehicles. Purchase is **highly recommended**, provided you favor an example with a clear maintenance history. For the RX 400h, plan a precautionary budget for the high-voltage battery if it is original. Avoid models equipped with air suspension if possible to limit maintenance costs, and inspect the condition of the dashboard before purchase.
