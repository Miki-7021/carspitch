Honda Jazz

I (GD) · 2001 – 2008

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

All Honda models

Summary

The first-generation Honda Jazz (factory code GD), marketed as the Honda Fit in Japan, China, and the Americas, revolutionized the versatile city car segment. Thanks to the central placement of its fuel tank, it offers record-breaking interior space and exceptional modularity with its famous “Magic Seats” (foldable rear seat bases). Launched in Japan in 2001 and in Europe in 2002, it received a facelift in late 2004 (revised bumpers, turn signals in mirrors on some trims, improved sound insulation). It is a model renowned for its mechanical robustness, although vigilance is required regarding CVT gearbox maintenance and certain sealing defects. Overall design score: 8.5/10.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional interior space and trunk volume (380 liters) for its size (3.83 m)
  • Unbeatable modularity (Magic Seats allowing tall objects to be loaded)
  • Overall reliability of engine blocks (chain timing)
  • Very controlled fuel consumption (i-DSI engines)
  • Ergonomics of controls and peripheral visibility

Weaknesses 5

  • Water ingress in the trunk (very common issue for GD models)
  • Capricious CVT gearbox if maintenance has not been meticulous
  • Very firm suspension comfort (especially at the rear)
  • Light sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Engine jerks related to clogged EGR valve on i-DSI models

Check before buying

  • Lift the trunk carpet and check for water or rust in the spare wheel well.
  • Test the CVT gearbox cold and hot: no judder or trembling should be felt on takeoff.
  • Check maintenance invoices: have the 8 spark plugs been changed on 1.2 and 1.4 i-DSI engines?
  • Listen to the rear wheel bearings during a test drive (dull hum from 50 km/h).
  • Check that Takata airbag recalls have been performed at the dealership.

Official recalls

  • Takata Airbags (Multiple dates, 2010-2019): Replacement of front airbag gas generators (driver and passenger) at risk of exploding and projecting metal fragments.
  • Headlight Switch (March 2011): Risk of overheating and melting of low beam headlight wiring, leading to a loss of illumination.
  • Power Window Switch Panel (September 2011): Possible water ingress into the driver's side master control, potentially causing a short circuit, or even a fire.
  • Handbrake (2002-2003): Adjustment of the parking brake pawl which could release unexpectedly.

What sets it apart 6

Practicality

  • Unrivaled "Magic Seats" Modularity — Thanks to its innovative "Magic Seats" rear seats, the Honda Jazz offers exceptional modularity, allowing you to transport tall or long items with surprising ease.
  • Record Interior Space and Trunk Volume — Despite its compact size, the Jazz impresses with its generous interior space and a trunk volume of 380 liters, among the best in its class.

Reliability

  • Legendary Engine Reliability — The Honda Jazz's i-DSI petrol engines are renowned for their robustness and unwavering reliability, notably thanks to a maintenance-free timing chain.

Economy

  • Controlled Fuel Consumption — The Jazz's i-DSI engines are designed to offer excellent fuel efficiency, ensuring very low fuel consumption for your daily commutes.

Comfort

  • Intuitive Ergonomics and Optimal Visibility — The Jazz's cabin is designed with the driver in mind, featuring ergonomic controls and exceptional peripheral visibility, making driving pleasant and safe.

Performance

  • Urban and Road Versatility — With its compact size and flexible engines, the Honda Jazz excels in urban environments while offering sufficient driving pleasure for road trips.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Toyota Yaris II (excellent reliability), Ford Fiesta Mk6 (better road handling), Skoda Fabia I/II, Nissan Micra K12.
  • Japan / Asia: Toyota Vitz/Yaris, Nissan March, Mazda Demio (Mazda 2).
  • North America: Toyota Yaris, Nissan Versa, Chevrolet Aveo.

Verdict

The Honda Jazz I (GD) is a masterpiece of automotive packaging and remains, even years later, one of the most practical city cars ever designed. In terms of reliability, the engines are indestructible if basic maintenance is respected. Purchase is highly recommended with a manual gearbox. For CVT gearbox versions, purchase is conditional on a clear maintenance history (regular gearbox fluid changes with Honda oil). Budget for replacing the rear bearings and do not hesitate to inspect the trunk to track down water ingress. An excellent first car or a formidable second utility vehicle.
