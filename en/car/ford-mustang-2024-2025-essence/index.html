Summary

The 7th generation Ford Mustang (codename S650) succeeds the highly popular S550. Although it is based on an evolution of the previous platform, it marks a technological breakthrough with a completely redesigned interior centered around a digital panel (Sync 4). Mechanically, it remains one of the last representatives of traditional 'Pony Cars', retaining 100% internal combustion engines. The Coyote V8 moves to its 4th generation (dual intake), while the 4-cylinder EcoBoost is heavily revised. The range is topped by the new 'Dark Horse' version, oriented for the track. While the mechanical fundamentals are proven, the early production years of this S650 are marked by numerous recalls related to electronics and assembly.

Strengths 4

  • Intrinsic reliability of the Coyote V8 block (very positive S550 heritage according to J.D. Power and ADAC)
  • Tremec TR-3160 manual gearbox on the Dark Horse version (robust and precise)
  • Maintenance of a naturally aspirated V8 offering in a shrinking market
  • Improvement in perceived interior quality compared to the S550

Weaknesses 4

  • Abnormal amount of early recalls (electronics, assembly, safety) on the 2024 model year
  • Temperamental infotainment system and comfort electronics (black screens, BCM)
  • MT-82 manual gearbox (on GT) still lagging behind the Tremec
  • 10-speed automatic gearbox sometimes hesitant when cold and at low speeds

Check before buying

  • **Recall history:** This is THE crucial point. Verify via the VIN that all recalls (clutch, BCM, suspension, screens) have been performed.
  • **Screen operation:** Ensure that the instrument cluster (IPC) turns on instantly upon startup (subject to recall).
  • **Clutch pedal (Manual Gearbox):** Check for the absence of abnormal play (recall on the nut and hydraulic line).
  • **Exterior lighting:** Test all functions, as water leaks into the BCM can cause lighting failures.

What sets it apart 14

Performance

  • The Coyote V8 Heart, a Proven Legend — Enjoy the incomparable power and sound of the 4th generation Coyote V8 engine, renowned for its robustness and exceptional reliability, inheriting a lineage awarded by J.D. Power.
  • The Authenticity of a Mythical Pony Car — The Mustang S650 perpetuates the tradition of Pony Cars with 100% thermal powertrains, offering a pure driving experience and iconic status in a changing market.
  • Absolute Control with the Tremec Manual Gearbox — For purists, the Dark Horse version is equipped with the Tremec TR-3160 manual gearbox, praised for its robustness, precision, and direct engagement, guaranteeing unparalleled driving sensations.
  • Responsive 10-Speed Automatic Transmission — The 10-speed automatic transmission (10R80) ensures fast and smooth shifts in sporty driving, while contributing to optimized fuel consumption on the highway for increased versatility.

Verdict

The Ford Mustang S650 blows hot and cold. Mechanically, it relies on the excellent Coyote V8, whose reliability has been widely demonstrated since the previous generation. However, its launch was marred by an **astronomical amount of recalls** affecting electronics, assembly, and safety (fire risks, loss of control, black screens).

**Buying advice:** If you are targeting a used 2024 model, demand a clear history proving that **all** manufacturer recalls have been applied. It is highly advisable to favor models produced in late 2024 or 2025, once the 'teething issues' have been corrected at the factory. The Dark Horse version with its Tremec gearbox represents the best choice for manual transmission reliability.
