Renault Clio

II · 1998 – 2012

NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

Summary

The Renault Clio II is an iconic versatile city car that marked a turning point for Renault in terms of comfort and road handling. Marketed in several phases (Phase 1: 1998-2001, Phase 2: 2001-2003, Phase 3: 2004-2005) and extended under the "Campus" designation until 2012 in Europe (and beyond in South America), it democratized direct diesel injection with the famous 1.5 dCi. While its dynamic qualities and comfort are undeniable, its reputation has been heavily tarnished by major reliability issues on the first generations of dCi engines (Delphi pump) and electronic quirks (UCH).

Strengths 5

  • Extremely low fuel consumption on the 1.5 dCi (often under 4.5 L/100 km)
  • Suspension comfort and very sound road handling
  • Very low cost of spare parts and maintenance (excluding dCi injection system)
  • Excellent reliability of petrol engines (especially the 1.2 D7F/D4F) and the 1.9D
  • Massive availability of parts in scrapyards and on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic reliability of Delphi injection on 1.5 dCi from 2001 to 2005 (metal filings)
  • Recurring electronic problems: UCH (Central Cabin Unit) module managing wipers, turn signals, etc.
  • Fickle immobilizer (key recognition or ignition barrel ring problem)
  • Airbag warning light permanently on (wiring under the front seats)
  • Aging interior plastics (peeling steering wheel, sticky plastics) and opacification of the front headlights

Check before buying

  • On 1.5 dCi: Demand maintenance invoices. Open the fuel filter to check for metallic flakes (filings).
  • On 1.5 dCi: Listen for turbo whistling and check for the absence of black smoke (EGR) or blue smoke (Turbo/Oil) during acceleration.
  • Electronics: Absolutely test all electrical controls (windows, central locking, wipers) to rule out an UCH fault.
  • Starting: Check that the red immobilizer light turns off when the ignition is switched on with all provided keys.
  • Running gear: Listen for rear wheel bearings (very prone to wear) during a road test.

Official recalls

  • - **February 2007** : Risk of bonnet opening while driving. Replacement of the bonnet latch on models produced between 1998 and 2005.
  • - **December 2002** : Accelerator cable problem that could get stuck (2001-2002 models).
  • - **May 2002** : Risk of short circuit on the power steering pressure sensor harness.
  • - **October 1999** : Possible fragility of the front lower suspension arms (1998-1999 models).

Alternatives

- **Europe** : Peugeot 206 (the 1.4 HDi is generally more reliable than the first 1.5 dCi engines, the 1.9D is equivalent), Volkswagen Polo IV (1.4 TDI noisy but robust, 1.9 SDI indestructible), Ford Fiesta, Fiat Punto II (1.3 JTD very reliable). - **South America** : VW Gol, Fiat Palio, Chevrolet Corsa. - **Asia** : Honda Jazz/Fit, Toyota Yaris (absolute benchmarks in petrol reliability).

Verdict

The Renault Clio II is a car with two faces. If you are looking for a diesel, **avoid at all costs the 1.5 dCi produced between 2001 and 2005** unless the complete injection system (pump, injectors, tank) has been recently replaced with an invoice. Prioritize 'Campus' versions post-2006 where the dCi has been made more reliable, or opt for the rustic but indestructible 1.9D if performance is not a priority. Paradoxically, the petrol versions (1.2 and 1.4) are excellent choices, very reliable and economical, often preferable to diesel for this generation.
