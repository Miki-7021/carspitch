Ford Fiesta

VI (Mk6) · 2002 – 2008

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The sixth-generation Ford Fiesta (often called Mk6 in continental Europe and Mk5 in the UK) marked a complete break from its predecessor thanks to an all-new platform offering significantly increased interior space and first-class road handling. Launched in 2002, it benefited from a major facelift in late 2005 (Phase 2) which corrected its main flaw: a low-end plastic interior finish, replaced by soft-touch materials and a modernized design. Mechanically, this generation sealed the partnership with PSA (Peugeot-Citroën) for the diesel engines (TDCi), which offer excellent fuel efficiency but require increased vigilance regarding maintenance over the years.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent road handling (precise and dynamic chassis)
  • Very low fuel consumption for TDCi engines
  • Generous interior space and boot volume (284 liters) for its time
  • Overall reliability of petrol engines
  • Dramatic improvement in finish on Phase 2 models (after late 2005)
  • Affordable spare parts and insurance costs

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.6 TDCi if maintenance has not been ultra-rigorous
  • Recurring injector seal issue on the 1.4 TDCi
  • Poor sound insulation (wind and road noise on the highway)
  • Very low-end interior plastics prone to scratches on Phase 1 (2002-2005)
  • Slow Durashift EST automated manual gearbox, prone to electronic and mechanical failures (to be avoided)
  • Frequent breakage of front shock absorber springs

Check before buying

  • **Diesel smell / 'Hissing' sound (TDCi):** Open the hood with the engine running. An exhaust smell or an air sound indicates leaking injector seals (mechanical emergency).
  • **Turbo whistling (1.6 TDCi):** A loud whistle during acceleration indicates imminent turbocharger failure.
  • **Water ingress:** Check for dampness under the front floor mats (seal defect in the pollen filter housing or door seals).
  • **Front suspension springs:** Inspect the condition of the coils, very prone to corrosion and sudden breakage on this generation.
  • **Power steering:** Check for power steering fluid leaks (pump and rack) and the absence of stiff spots in the steering.
  • **'EAC Fail' message (Petrol):** Indicates an electronic accelerator management problem (throttle body, pedal, or wiring harness).

Official recalls

  • - **November 2004:** Risk of fuel leak on the injector return pipe (concerns 1.4 TDCi models produced between July 2001 and March 2004).
  • - **February 2005:** Welding defect on the steering column that could lead to loss of control (models produced in September 2004).
  • - **May 2007:** Risk of short circuit and fire in the cooling fan control module (models produced between July 2004 and November 2006).
  • - **October 2008:** Reprogramming of the engine management module on certain diesels to prevent engine runaway related to oil in the intake.

Verdict

The Ford Fiesta Mk6 is an excellent used city car, provided you choose the right engine. **If you are looking for a diesel**, the 1.4 TDCi is the most rational choice: it is very economical and its main ailment (injector seals) is inexpensive to repair if caught in time. However, **the 1.6 TDCi should be avoided** unless you have an impeccable service history (annual oil changes, turbo sieve removed), otherwise repair bills may exceed the car's value. In any case, opt for a facelifted version (Phase 2, after late 2005) to benefit from a much more appealing interior and more reliable electronics. Absolutely avoid the Durashift automated manual gearbox.
