Volvo S40

II (MS) · 2004 – 2012

BUY Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The **second-generation Volvo S40 (MS)** marked a turning point for the Swedish manufacturer. Based on Ford's excellent C1 platform (shared with the Ford Focus II and Mazda 3 BK), it shed the sluggish handling of its predecessor to offer first-class dynamism while retaining Volvo's DNA: exceptional passive safety, orthopedic seats of unparalleled comfort, and a clean Scandinavian design (with the famous floating center console).

Restyled in 2007 (revised bumpers, LED lights, interior improvements), it was available with a multitude of engines. While diesels (from PSA/Ford or Volvo) dominated the European market, **petrol engines are now the most relevant and reliable choice on the used market**, especially the melodious and robust in-house 5-cylinders.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional reliability of 5-cylinder petrol engines (2.4, 2.4i, T5)
  • Seat comfort (among the best in automotive production)
  • Passive and active safety (SIPS, WHIPS, rigid chassis)
  • Dynamic road handling (Ford Focus II chassis)
  • Paint quality and excellent corrosion resistance
  • Exceptional high-end audio system (Dynaudio)

Weaknesses 6

  • Very average rear passenger space (limited legroom)
  • Trunk volume (404 liters) and narrow trunk opening
  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.6D diesel engine (109 hp) before 2010
  • Large turning radius (especially with 17/18 inch wheels)
  • Cupping wear on rear tires (sensitive geometry adjustment)
  • High cost of genuine Volvo spare parts

Check before buying

  • **CEM Module (Central Electronic Module):** Check for absence of moisture under the front passenger mats. Water can infiltrate (windshield cowl or clogged sunroof) and burn out this costly module.
  • **Air conditioning condenser:** Very exposed to gravel behind the grille. Check that the AC blows cold.
  • **Ignition switch (SCU):** If the key refuses to turn or displays "Key error", the steering column module is faulty.
  • **Wheel bearings:** Tendency to wear prematurely (humming noise from 80 km/h).
  • **Engine whistling (5-cyl Petrol):** Indicates a torn PCV breather membrane. Replace quickly to avoid damaging engine oil seals.

Official recalls

  • - **2006:** Risk of power steering return hose leak (2004-2005 models).
  • - **2008:** Cooling fan control module potentially overheating (fire risk, 2008 models).
  • - **2009:** Defective brake assist vacuum pump on 2.0D engines.
  • - **2010:** Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF) software requiring an update to prevent oil dilution by diesel fuel (4-cyl diesel engines).
  • - **2011:** Defective clutch slave cylinder on 6-speed manual gearboxes.

What sets it apart 14

Safety

  • Benchmark Safety — The Volvo S40 II is a safety benchmark, designed to protect its occupants with advanced passive and active systems (SIPS, WHIPS, rigid chassis).
  • Powerful and Safe Braking — The S40 II ensures solid and reliable braking performance, with short stopping distances and optimal control thanks to well-calibrated ABS and EBD systems.

Reliability

  • Exemplary Petrol Engine Reliability — Petrol engines, especially Volvo's 5-cylinders (2.4, 2.4i, T5), are renowned for their robustness and longevity, offering remarkable peace of mind. The Ford 4-cylinders (1.8, 2.0) are also very solid.
  • Superior Build Quality and Durability — Benefit from excellent paint quality and superior corrosion resistance, ensuring preserved aesthetics and lasting value for your vehicle.

Comfort

  • Unrivaled Interior Comfort — Enjoy exceptional travel comfort thanks to Volvo's ergonomic seats, considered among the best in the automotive industry, and a well-insulated cabin.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Safe Road Handling — Thanks to its platform shared with the Ford Focus II, the S40 II offers balanced, dynamic, and predictable road handling, with precise steering that inspires confidence.
  • Driving Pleasure and 5-Cylinder Sound — Volvo's 5-cylinder petrol engines offer unique smoothness and sound, providing a distinctive and refined driving pleasure.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Audi A4 (B7/B8), BMW 3 Series (E90), Mercedes C-Class (W203/W204), Saab 9-3 (excellent Swedish alternative), Alfa Romeo 159. - **North America:** Acura TSX, Lexus IS, Subaru Legacy. - **Asia / Emerging Markets:** Mazda 3 (sedan version), Honda Civic Sedan.

Verdict

The Volvo S40 II is an excellent compact sedan, often undervalued on the used car market. **If you opt for a petrol engine (especially the 5-cylinder 2.4i or T5), it is a highly recommended purchase.** These engines offer rare driving pleasure and bulletproof reliability, at the cost of slightly higher fuel consumption. The Ford-sourced 4-cylinder petrol engines (1.8 and 2.0) are pragmatic and very solid choices. However, **avoid the 109 hp 1.6D diesel**, whose costly failures (turbo, DPF, injectors) will ruin the experience. Prioritize a post-2007 (facelifted) model for better finish and more reliable electronics.
