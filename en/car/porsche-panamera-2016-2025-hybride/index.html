Summary

The second-generation Porsche Panamera (Type 971) marks a spectacular break from its predecessor (Type 970). Based on the Volkswagen Group's MSB platform, it corrects the aesthetic flaws of the first version while making a giant leap forward in terms of reliability and technology. Available as a sedan and a shooting brake (Sport Turismo), it has established itself as a benchmark for large sporty grand tourers. The core of its range is the plug-in hybrid powertrain (E-Hybrid), which accounted for the majority of sales in Europe thanks to favorable taxation. In terms of reliability, the data is clear: the 971 is significantly superior to the 970. ADAC notes a breakdown rate of only 2.8/1000 for this generation (compared to >4.2 for the old one), and the TÜV places it in the upper average of the premium segment (17% defect rate at 3 years). The new 8-speed PDK gearbox erases the serious issues of the previous PDK7. A facelift (Phase 2) took place at the end of 2020, optimizing the electric range of the hybrid versions.

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability significantly improved compared to generation 1 (ADAC rate divided by 1.5)
  • Extremely robust and responsive 8-speed PDK gearbox
  • Very well-engineered and durable 2.9 Biturbo V6 engine (combustion and hybrid)
  • Integration of the hybrid system (smoothness of electric/combustion transition)
  • Exceptional interior finish quality, aging of materials

Weaknesses 5

  • Excessive weight of the E-Hybrid versions (>2.2 tons) leading to rapid wear of the running gear and brakes
  • Complex electronics (PCM bugs, assist sensors)
  • Prohibitive maintenance cost in the official Porsche network
  • Frequent recalls in the early years of production (2017-2019)
  • Auxiliary air conditioning pump on hybrids (risk of corrosion/fire)

Check before buying

  • On E-Hybrids: demand a high-voltage battery capacity test (SOH) certified by Porsche.
  • Verify that the recall concerning the auxiliary air conditioning pump (hybrids) has been carried out.
  • Condition of the brake discs (especially if PCCB carbon-ceramic brakes: a replacement costs more than 15,000 €).
  • Operation of the air suspension (sagging after a night of parking = leak).
  • Clear history of the 8-speed PDK gearbox oil changes.

Official recalls

The Panamera 971 has been the subject of several recalls. It is crucial to verify their execution via the service book or a Porsche Center:

  • [2025-02] Wheel fastening (Center lock): Defect in the central locking bolt that could lead to the loss of the wheel (concerns models with the center lock option).
  • [2025-02] Rearview camera (USA): The image may not display (2024-2025 models).
  • [2025-02] High-pressure fuel pump (USA): Improperly tightened fasteners that could lead to a leak (2024-2026 models).
  • [2024-03] Front wheel carriers: Manufacturing defect that could cause the right front wheel carrier to break.
  • [2023-10] Auxiliary air conditioning pump (Hybrids): MAJOR RECALL. Moisture infiltration leading to corrosion of the control unit, with a risk of short circuit and fire.
  • [2023-10] Rear seatbelts: End brackets improperly fastened.
  • [2022-01] Suspension: Possible breakage of the lower trailing arm.
  • [2021-05 / 2021-07] Running gear: Improperly tightened drive shaft nuts or front suspension defect.
  • [2018-2019] Electronics & Steering: Multiple recalls (USA/Europe) for bugs in the instrument cluster, electric power steering, and airbag module.

What sets it apart 6

Reliability

  • Advanced Mechanical Reliability — The second-generation Panamera boasts significantly improved mechanical reliability, notably thanks to its exceptionally robust 8-speed PDK gearbox and its 2.9 V6 Biturbo engine, renowned for its solid design and durability.

Performance

  • Dynamic Hybrid Performance — The E-Hybrid powertrains offer a perfect combination of sporty power and efficiency, with seamless integration between electric and thermal modes, ensuring high-flying performance.

Comfort

  • Exceptional Interior Comfort and Quality — The Panamera 971's interior stands out with exceptional finish quality and premium materials, ensuring imperial comfort and excellent long-term durability.

Safety

  • Precision Braking and Enhanced Safety — Equipped with a remarkably efficient braking system, the Panamera ensures very powerful stopping performance and optimal endurance, even under demanding conditions, enhancing active safety.

tenue de route

  • Sporty Handling and Surprising Agility — Despite its stature, the Panamera offers exceptional road holding and dynamic behavior. Its precise steering and controlled body roll provide surprising agility and reassuring stability at high speeds.

Economy

  • Daily Efficiency and Tax Advantages — The plug-in hybrid (E-Hybrid) versions stand out for their controlled daily consumption and versatility, benefiting from advantageous taxation that optimizes running costs.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Mercedes-AMG GT 4-Door (more brutal), Audi RS7 Sportback (shares many mechanical components), BMW 8 Series Gran Coupe, Porsche Taycan (100% electric internal alternative).
  • North America: Tesla Model S (for the green alternative), BMW Alpina B8 Gran Coupe.
  • Asia: Lexus LS 500h (more focused on comfort and absolute reliability, less sporty).

Verdict

The second-generation Porsche Panamera (971) is a technical success that erases the reliability wanderings of the first generation. The 4 E-Hybrid version is the most rational and well-rounded choice, offering sports car performance, controlled daily fuel consumption, and now-proven mechanical reliability (2.9 V6 + 8-speed PDK). However, the technological complexity of this car requires rigorous monitoring. Major mechanical breakdowns are rare, but electronic issues and recalls (notably on the auxiliary pump of the hybrids) are a reminder that it is a highly complex piece of machinery. The maintenance budget must match the badge, particularly for consumables (tires, brakes) which wear out quickly under the weight of the hybrid versions. Favor a post-2019 model (or 2020 Phase 2) with a 100% Porsche history (Porsche Approved highly recommended).
