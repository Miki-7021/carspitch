Toyota Tacoma

II (N200) · 2005 – 2015

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

Summary

The **second-generation Toyota Tacoma (N200)** is a true legend in the midsize pickup segment (Midsize Trucks), primarily designed for the North American market. Renowned for its nearly indestructible robustness, it dominated its segment for a decade. Offered in three cab configurations (Regular, Access, Double Cab) and with highly sought-after off-road-oriented trims (TRD Off-Road, TRD Sport, Baja), it received minor facelifts in 2009 and 2012. According to J.D. Power and ADAC, it is one of the most reliable vehicles on the market, easily capable of exceeding 300,000 km with basic maintenance. Its only real historical enemy is chassis corrosion, a problem addressed by Toyota through massive replacement campaigns.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability (1GR-FE and 2TR-FE engines are nearly indestructible)
  • Extremely robust Aisin A750 5-speed automatic gearbox (ADAC)
  • Stratospheric resale value on the used market
  • Off-road capabilities (especially TRD versions with rear differential lock)
  • Midsize Pickup segment leader according to J.D. Power (1st or 2nd every year for 10 years)
  • Extremely low breakdown rate (4.5/5 according to What Car?, 10% over 12 months)

Weaknesses 5

  • Perforating chassis corrosion (major problem on models exposed to salt, which led to a chassis replacement program by Toyota)
  • Very high fuel consumption (especially the 4.0L V6)
  • Rear drum brakes (lack of endurance under heavy load)
  • Peculiar driving position (high floor, low seating, legs stretched out)
  • Soundproofing could be improved at high speeds

Check before buying

  • CHASSIS CONDITION (Frame Rust): This is the absolute critical point. Check if the chassis has been treated, inspected, or replaced by Toyota under warranty.
  • Rear leaf springs: Prone to sagging or breaking (official recall in 2014).
  • Drivetrain vibrations: Often caused by wear of the driveshaft Center Support Bearing or ungreased U-joints.
  • Airbag warning light on: Generally indicates a failure of the Clockspring in the steering wheel (official recall).
  • V6 maintenance history: Check that the spark plugs have indeed been changed every 50,000 km.

Verdict

The Toyota Tacoma N200 is a monument of reliability. Data from ADAC, TÜV, and J.D. Power are unanimous: it is one of the most durable vehicles ever built, capable of reaching astronomical mileages with minimal hassle. The 4.0L V6 engine (1GR-FE) coupled with the Aisin gearbox is a solid gold combination. However, this reputation comes at a price: its used market value is extremely high. **The only absolute black mark is chassis corrosion.** If you find an example with a sound chassis (or one that has been replaced by Toyota) and whose basic maintenance has been followed, it is a blind buy, despite its gargantuan fuel consumption.
