Summary

The second-generation Jaguar XF (X260) received a major mid-cycle facelift at the end of 2020. While the exterior evolves subtly (new LED headlights, redesigned bumpers), the interior undergoes a true revolution with much higher quality materials and the integration of the excellent Pivi Pro infotainment system on an 11.4-inch curved screen. Under the hood, Jaguar has drastically simplified the offering: farewell to the noble V6s, the range focuses exclusively on the 2.0-liter 4-cylinder Ingenium engines (gasoline and mild-hybrid diesel). This rational choice aims to reduce emissions, but deprives the XF of prestigious powertrains compared to its German competitors. However, the XF remains one of the most dynamic and elegant sedans in its segment.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional chassis: top-flight comfort/dynamism compromise
  • Timeless and elegant exterior design
  • Transfigured interior (premium materials, significantly improved finish)
  • Pivi Pro multimedia system finally responsive and modern
  • Very meticulous soundproofing

Weaknesses 5

  • Total disappearance of 6-cylinder engines (V6)
  • Average rear seat space and trunk volume for the category
  • Sparse dealer network and high maintenance costs
  • Very high depreciation on the used market
  • Reliability of Ingenium engines still uneven depending on maintenance

Check before buying

  • Clear maintenance history: avoid vehicles that followed the manufacturer's 30,000 km interval (demand 15,000 km).
  • Engine compartment inspection: look for oil traces around the turbocharger (feed pipe).
  • Cold start listening: no metallic noise (rattling) should come from the timing chain.
  • Electronics: test all functions of the central screen, backup cameras, and driving aids.
  • D200 Diesel: check the DPF regeneration history via the diagnostic tool if possible.

Official recalls

Major safety recalls (Rappel Conso & NHTSA)

  • [2024-09-06] Turbo Oil Leak (2021-2025 Models): An oil leak from the turbocharger oil feed pipe can lead to oil spills on the road or a risk of fire/injury.
  • [2022-09-02 / NHTSA 22V524000] Seatbelt Pretensioners (2022 Models): The pretensioner tubes may have been damaged during assembly, preventing the seatbelts from properly restraining occupants in the event of a crash.
  • [2022-07-01] Fuel Return Hose: Manufacturing defect in the support wire mesh of the rubber return hose, which could lead to a leak.
  • [NHTSA 21V667000 - 2021-08-26] Seatbelt Retractors (2021 Models): The automatic locking retractors may deactivate prematurely, preventing the proper securing of a child seat.
  • [2025/2026] Takata Airbags: The propellant in the driver/passenger airbag may degrade over time, projecting metal fragments upon deployment.
  • *Note: The 2022-10-07 recall concerns the previous generation (X250).* The I-Pace and F-Pace PHEV recalls do not apply to the XF.

Verdict

The facelifted Jaguar XF (2020-2024) is a sedan with character aimed at those who want to drive something different. The facelift corrected its biggest flaw: a dated interior. Now luxurious and technologically up to date with the Pivi Pro system, it shines with its road handling. However, the restriction to only 4-cylinder Ingenium engines limits its mechanical appeal. Favor the gasoline versions (P250/P300), which are more reliable in the long term than the diesel if maintenance is rigorous (annual oil changes are imperative). The staggering depreciation makes it an excellent deal on the used market, provided it is purchased with a solid warranty and a clear history.
