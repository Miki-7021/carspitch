Summary

The third-generation BMW X5 (code name F15) replaces the E70, retaining its wheelbase but thoroughly modernizing the interior, onboard technologies, and aerodynamics. Although diesel engines (30d, 40d) dominated the European market, gasoline versions (35i, 50i) and the plug-in hybrid (40e) achieved strong success in North America, Asia, and certain European markets penalizing diesel. The F15 stands out for its supreme comfort, its ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (a benchmark), and its highly developed iDrive infotainment system. In terms of reliability, while the 6-cylinder gasoline engines (N55) are robust, the V8s (N63TU) demand extreme vigilance and a substantial maintenance budget.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional driving pleasure from the inline 6-cylinder gasoline engine (35i)
  • ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (smooth, fast, reliable)
  • Significantly improved finish quality and sound insulation compared to the E70
  • Intuitive and responsive iDrive multimedia system
  • Dynamic road handling for an SUV of this size

Weaknesses 5

  • Erratic reliability and prohibitive maintenance costs for the V8 (50i)
  • Fragile rear air suspension (frequent leaks)
  • Very high weight (especially on the 40e hybrid version, > 2.3 tons)
  • Rapid tire and brake wear
  • Possible water ingress in the trunk (damaging electronic modules)

Check before buying

  • Oil leaks on the N55 (oil pan gasket, oil filter housing gasket, valve cover).
  • Sagging of the vehicle's rear after prolonged parking (sign of air spring leaks).
  • Clear service history for oil changes (especially on 50i V8s, which tolerate no delays).
  • Condition of the EGR valve cooler (on diesels, check if the recall has been performed).
  • iDrive screen operation (cases of delamination or intermittent restarts).
  • Presence of moisture under the trunk floor (expensive electronic modules located there).

Official recalls

  • Takata Airbags (2016-2020): Replacement of driver and passenger airbag gas generators (risk of metal fragment projection).
  • EGR Cooler (2018-2022): Concerns diesel engines (4 and 6 cylinders). Coolant leak in the EGR module potentially leading to a fire risk.
  • Front Driveshaft (2017): On certain xDrive versions, the front driveshaft universal joint may break.
  • ISOFIX Anchors (2016): Rear ISOFIX mounts may break under stress (defective weld).
  • EPS Power Steering (2015): Possible loss of power steering assistance due to a control unit defect.

What sets it apart 6

safety

  • Advanced Safety — The BMW X5 F15 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, ensuring optimal protection for adult and child occupants.

braking

  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — Benefit from exceptional braking performance, with remarkable stopping distances of approximately 35-37 meters from 100 km/h, ensuring perfect control in all circumstances.

handling

  • Dynamic Road Behavior — Despite its size, the X5 F15 offers impressive agility and driving precision, with well-controlled body roll and responsive steering, positioning it among the most dynamic SUVs in its category.

Performance

  • Exceptional 6-Cylinder Petrol Engine — The 3.0L inline 6-cylinder engine (xDrive35i) delivers outstanding driving pleasure, combining dynamic performance, a melodious sound, and excellent responsiveness for an unparalleled driving experience.

comfort

  • Sovereign Comfort and Premium Finish — Enjoy superior ride comfort and a significantly improved interior finish quality, complemented by meticulous sound insulation and an intuitive, responsive iDrive multimedia system.

reliability

  • Reference ZF 8-Speed Automatic Transmission — The ZF 8-speed automatic transmission is a benchmark for smoothness, speed, and reliability, ensuring fluid gear changes and optimal efficiency.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Porsche Cayenne II (958) V6 or S E-Hybrid (sportier, superior finish), Mercedes-Benz GLE (W166) (more comfort-oriented), Audi Q7 (4M) (more technological and spacious).
  • North America: Lexus RX 350 / 450h (unbeatable reliability but less dynamic), Acura MDX (excellent value for money).
  • Japan / Asia: Volvo XC90 II (T6 or T8 PHEV, sleek design, 7 true seats).

Verdict

The BMW X5 F15 is a sophisticated luxury SUV that has corrected many of its predecessor's finish defects. For gasoline engines, the xDrive35i (N55) is the choice of both reason and passion: it offers an excellent compromise between performance, sound, and reliability, provided preventive maintenance (water pump, seals) is respected. The 50i's V8 is to be avoided unless you have an unlimited maintenance budget and a perfectly transparent service history. The 40e hybrid version is fiscally interesting, but its electric range has aged poorly, and the reliability of the N20 engine (before 2015) calls for caution. When purchasing, it is imperative to check the rear air suspension and the absence of water ingress in the trunk.
