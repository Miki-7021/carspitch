Summary

Renault Austral I (JRA)

Replacing the Kadjar, the Renault Austral marks a complete break for the diamond-brand manufacturer in the highly competitive compact SUV (C-SUV) segment. Based on the CMF-CD3 platform (shared with the Nissan Qashqai III), it stands out with a spectacular leap in perceived quality, the integration of the OpenR Link multimedia system (based on Google Automotive, a market benchmark), and the introduction of the *4Control Advanced* four-wheel steering system.

Mechanically, the Austral completely foregoes diesel and plug-in hybrid (PHEV), relying exclusively on electrified gasoline (12V/48V mild hybrid and *Full Hybrid* E-Tech). While the 1.3 TCe (4-cylinder) engine is now a proven model of reliability, the new 3-cylinder 1.2 TCe (HR12) and its complex dog-clutch transmission (E-Tech) still require a bit more time for assessment, although initial results are reassuring compared to older generations of Renault engines.

**Overall presentation score: 8/10** - A highly accomplished, technological, and dynamic SUV that restores Renault's image in this segment.

Strengths 5

  • Ultra-smooth and ergonomic OpenR Link infotainment system (Google)
  • Exceptional agility with the 4Control Advanced option (4-wheel steering)
  • Remarkable reliability of the 4-cylinder 1.3 TCe engine (Mild Hybrid 140/160)
  • Significantly improved interior finish and material quality compared to the Kadjar
  • Energy efficiency of the E-Tech 200 system in urban environments

Weaknesses 5

  • Ride comfort a bit firm, especially with 20-inch wheels
  • E-Tech system's dog-clutch gearbox sometimes slow or causing jerks
  • Absence of diesel engine for very high-mileage motorway drivers
  • Early electronic bugs on models produced in 2022
  • Reduced boot volume on E-Tech versions (battery)

Check before buying

  • Software update history (crucial for the E-Tech system and OpenR screen).
  • Gearbox behavior on the E-Tech during thermal/electric transitions (absence of violent jerks).
  • Rear tire wear on models equipped with 4Control (specific geometry).
  • Alignment of body panels and interior trim (some squeaks on early series).
  • Condition of the 12V battery on Mild Hybrid versions (intermittent error messages if voltage is low).

Verdict

Expert's Verdict

The Renault Austral is a successful conquest vehicle that makes you forget the shortcomings of the Kadjar. In terms of reliability, Renault has learned from past lessons (notably the disastrous 1.2 TCe H5F).

**If you are looking for absolute reliability:** Go for the **1.3 TCe (Mild Hybrid 140 or 160)** with your eyes closed. This 4-cylinder engine is now one of the most reliable on the market, performing well and quietly.

**If you are looking for fuel economy:** The **1.2 E-Tech 200** is very appealing, but its technical complexity (3 cylinders + 2 electric motors + clutchless dog-clutch gearbox) requires prioritizing a recent model (post-mid-2023) or one benefiting from an extended warranty, to avoid early software management bugs.
