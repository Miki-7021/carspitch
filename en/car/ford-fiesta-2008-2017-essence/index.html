Ford Fiesta

· 2008 – 2017 (Mk7/7.5)

NEGOTIATE Reliability 70/100 — bon

Summary

The Ford Fiesta Mk7 (and its facelifted Mk7.5 version from 2013) is an absolute benchmark in the B-segment for dynamism and driving pleasure. Featuring a particularly well-tuned chassis, it has appealed to a very wide global audience. While its design has aged very well, its overall reliability depends heavily on the chosen engine and transmission. The introduction of the 1.0 EcoBoost engine and the PowerShift dual-clutch gearbox brought its share of costly problems, contrasting with the robustness of older naturally aspirated engines.

Strengths 6

  • Benchmark road handling and dynamism
  • Precise steering and excellent feel
  • Exterior design that has aged very well
  • Reliability of naturally aspirated petrol engines (1.25 Duratec)
  • Fantastic and very high-performance ST version
  • Affordable spare parts cost

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic PowerShift automatic gearbox (DPS6 dry clutch) (juddering, failure)
  • Disastrous reliability of the wet belt on the 1.0 EcoBoost
  • Average interior finish (hard plastics, fit and finish)
  • Rear passenger space and boot volume lagging behind competitors
  • Central console ergonomics (pre-facelift, too many buttons)

Check before buying

  • **Maintenance history (1.0 EcoBoost)** : Verify that the oil used strictly complies with the Ford standard (5W20) and that the belt has been changed (recommended every 10 years/240,000 km, but ideally done at 6 years/100,000 km).
  • **PowerShift gearbox** : Avoid any model showing jerks, slipping, or metallic noises when changing gears.
  • **Injector seals (TDCi)** : Remove the engine cover and check for the absence of black paste (carbon build-up) around the injectors and exhaust smell.
  • **Water ingress** : Lift the boot mat to check for water in the spare wheel well (bumper vent sealing issue).
  • **Steering column** : Listen for any clunking noises when steering at low speeds (worn steering universal joint).

Alternatives

Verdict

The Ford Fiesta Mk7 is a car with two faces. If you opt for a manual gearbox version equipped with the modest but indestructible 1.25 Duratec, or for the fiery ST version, you will make an excellent purchase. However, versions equipped with the 1.0 EcoBoost require extreme vigilance regarding their maintenance (wet belt), and models with the PowerShift automatic gearbox are to be absolutely avoided due to major design flaws. Diesels are economical but require usual checks (injectors, DPF).
