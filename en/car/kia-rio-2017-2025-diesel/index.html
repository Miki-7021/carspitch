Kia Rio

IV (YB) · 2017 – 2025

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The fourth-generation Kia Rio (code name YB) is a versatile B-segment city car that has distinguished itself with its maturity, cabin space, and 7-year / 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty. Launched in 2017, it received a facelift in summer 2020, introducing mild-hybrid (MHEV) powertrains and new driving aids.

Although demand for diesel has drastically fallen in this category, Kia offered the **1.4 CRDi (U2)** engine at launch, an engine known for its robustness, intended for high-mileage drivers. This engine was removed from the European catalog in late 2019 / early 2020 due to Euro 6d anti-pollution standards. The Rio YB is a rational car, very reliable overall, which compensates for a certain lack of flair with remarkable peace of mind.

Strengths 5

  • 7-year / 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty (often still active on recent used models)
  • Excellent overall reliability, very few immobilizing breakdowns
  • 1.4 CRDi engine very durable and economical on long journeys
  • Cabin space and trunk volume (325 liters) among the best in its category
  • Logical interior ergonomics and responsive infotainment system

Weaknesses 5

  • Firm suspension, degraded comfort on bumpy roads (especially with 17-inch wheels)
  • Average sound insulation, particularly with the 1.4 CRDi diesel engine (cold knocking)
  • Hard interior plastics and prone to scratches
  • Rapid disappearance of diesel (end of 2019), limiting choice in recent used models
  • DCT7 gearbox sometimes hesitant at low speeds

Check before buying

  • On the 1.4 CRDi: Journey history. Avoid models used exclusively in the city (risk of clogged DPF/EGR).
  • On the 1.4 CRDi: Check for any oil seepage around the timing cover.
  • On 1.0 T-GDI models: Listen to the engine when cold (abnormal metallic noises from the chain or high-pressure pump).
  • On MHEV (iMT) versions: Test the electronic clutch's biting point, which should be smooth and without judder.
  • Electronics: Check the proper functioning of the touchscreen and rearview camera (some display bugs reported).

Verdict

The Kia Rio IV (YB) is an extremely rational and reassuring used car choice. If you specifically target the **1.4 CRDi diesel**, you are choosing a very robust engine, equipped with a reliable timing chain, ideal for covering miles at a lower cost. However, it is imperative to buy it for the right use: road and highway journeys. In urban use, the DPF and EGR valve will inevitably clog up. For mixed or urban use, gasoline engines (especially the 1.2 MPI for the city or the 1.0 T-GDI for versatility) are to be preferred. The 7-year warranty, often still current on models from after 2018, is a major asset.
