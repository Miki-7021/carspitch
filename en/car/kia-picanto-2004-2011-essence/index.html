Kia Picanto

Summary

The **first-generation Kia Picanto (SA code)**, also known as Kia Morning in South Korea or Naza Suria in Malaysia, marked Kia's serious entry into the mini-city car segment (Segment A) in Europe. Launched in 2004, it shares its platform with the Hyundai Atos Prime and later the Hyundai i10. Designed to be economical, maneuverable, and practical (available only as a 5-door), it achieved great success thanks to its unbeatable price/equipment ratio at the time.

It benefited from **two facelifts**: - **Phase 2 (2007)**: New rounded headlights, redesigned bumpers, generalized electric power steering (EPS), and improved interior quality. - **Phase 3 (2010)**: Adoption of the 'Tiger Nose' grille, dear to designer Peter Schreyer, slight aesthetic retouches before its replacement in 2011.

Although generally robust and very inexpensive to maintain, the first models (2004-2006) were marred by a major design flaw in the 1.1 petrol engine (crankshaft), requiring great vigilance when buying used.

Strengths 5

  • Extremely low purchase and insurance costs
  • Decent space for 4 adults despite its 3.50 meters
  • Maneuverability in the city (excellent turning radius)
  • Standard 5-door configuration (rare in this segment at the time)
  • Excellent overall reliability on post-2007 models (Phase 2 and 3)

Weaknesses 5

  • Fatal crankshaft defect on 1.1 petrol models from 2004 to 2006
  • Tiny boot volume (157 liters)
  • Almost non-existent sound insulation on the highway
  • Interior plastic quality (scratch-prone and basic)
  • Susceptibility to corrosion (underbody, wheel arches, fuel filler neck)

Check before buying

  • **Crankshaft pulley (1.1 petrol 2004-2006)**: With the engine running, visually check for wobble or play in the damper pulley. If in doubt, walk away.
  • **Clutch cable**: A stiff or squeaky clutch pedal indicates a cable about to fail (a very common issue).
  • **Corrosion**: Inspect the front subframe, rear wheel arches, and fuel filler neck.
  • **Rear brakes**: Rear brake calipers (if equipped with discs) tend to seize, causing asymmetrical pad wear.
  • **ABS rings**: The ABS rings on the front drive shafts can crack with rust, triggering the ABS warning light on the dashboard.

Verdict

The Kia Picanto I is an excellent small used city car, provided you **absolutely target a facelifted model (Phase 2 or 3, from 2007 to 2011)**. Models from 2004 to 2006 equipped with the 1.1L petrol engine should be avoided or inspected with the help of a professional due to the risk of engine failure related to the crankshaft. The 1.0L engine is the safest choice for the city, while the 1.1 CRDi diesel, although powerful, makes little economic sense today given the risks of clogging. Prioritize a clear service history, especially regarding timing belt replacement.
