Summary

The Mercedes-Benz W166 was launched in 2011 as the M-Class (ML), before undergoing a major facelift in 2015 where it was renamed GLE to align with the E-Class nomenclature. This premium SUV emphasizes supreme comfort, safety, and on-road capabilities (and off-road with the On&Offroad package). Although European demand heavily focused on diesels (OM642 and OM651), the gasoline engines (which are the subject of this report) offer superior driving pleasure and, for some, significantly more reassuring reliability in the face of complex anti-pollution standards. Build quality is excellent, but the vehicle's high weight (often over 2.1 tons) puts a strain on the running gear and brakes.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with Airmatic)
  • Sound insulation worthy of an S-Class
  • Remarkable reliability of gasoline V6 engines (M276)
  • Build quality and aging of interior materials
  • Towing capacity (up to 3.5 tons)

Weaknesses 5

  • Excessive weight (rapid brake and tire wear)
  • Disastrous reliability of the M278 V8 (cylinder scoring)
  • Very high maintenance and spare parts costs
  • Aging Comand multimedia system (especially before 2015)
  • Very temperamental AdBlue anti-pollution system on diesels

Check before buying

  • Airmatic Suspension: Raise and lower the vehicle, listen to the compressor, check for air leaks on the air springs.
  • Water Ingress (Blind Spot Sensors): Water often infiltrates the rear bumper, burning out the blind spot radars and causing cascading error messages (inoperative Pre-Safe).
  • Transfer Case (4MATIC): On high-torque models (V8, Diesels), the transfer case chain can stretch, causing jerking during tight turns at low speeds.
  • Automatic Transmission Service History: An unserviced 7G-Tronic gearbox is a source of costly failures (electro-hydraulic control unit).
  • V8 Engines (M278/M157): Demand an oil analysis or borescope inspection to rule out the risk of cylinder scoring.

Official recalls

  • Takata Airbags (2016-2019): Replacement of front airbag gas generators (risk of abnormal explosion).
  • Power Steering (2017): Sealing defect in the electric steering gear housing potentially leading to loss of assistance (contact corrosion).
  • Engine Software / AdBlue (2018-2020): Forced update on diesels following the emissions scandal (Dieselgate).
  • ISOFIX Mounts (2016): Inspection and reinforcement of child seat anchors on the rear bench.
  • Chain Tensioner O-ring (2015): Possible oil leak on some 4-cylinder and V6 gasoline engines.

What sets it apart 6

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability of V6 Petrol Engines — The V6 petrol engines (M276), whether naturally aspirated or biturbo, are praised for their robustness and longevity, offering lasting peace of mind on the road.

Comfort

  • Exceptional Ride Comfort — Enjoy a commanding driving experience thanks to unparalleled ride comfort, enhanced by the Airmatic air suspension, transforming every journey into a moment of pure relaxation.
  • S-Class Level Sound Insulation — The GLE's cabin benefits from remarkable sound insulation, perfectly isolating you from external noise for a calm and refined atmosphere, even at high speeds.

qualite

  • Premium Finish Quality and Interior Durability — The GLE's interior stands out with its superior material quality and rigorous assembly, ensuring exemplary aging and a lasting sense of luxury.

Practicality

  • Impressive Towing Capabilities — With a towing capacity of up to 3.5 tonnes, the GLE is the ideal partner for your caravans, trailers, or equipment, combining power and versatility.

Performance

  • Dynamic Performance of V6 BiTurbo Engines — The V6 BiTurbo engines (M276) deliver generous torque and dynamic performance, ensuring strong acceleration and responsive driving in all circumstances.

Alternatives

  • Europe: BMW X5 (F15) (more dynamic, excellent inline 6-cylinders), Audi Q7 (4M) (more modern and technological), Porsche Cayenne (958) (superior finish, sporty handling).
  • North America: Lexus RX 350 (unbeatable reliability, indestructible naturally aspirated V6), Acura MDX.
  • Asia / Emerging Markets: Toyota Land Cruiser Prado (more rustic but indestructible), Volvo XC90 (T6/T8).

Verdict

The Mercedes GLE (W166) is an excellent highway cruiser that ages very well aesthetically and internally. For gasoline engines, the choice is binary. The V6s (M276), whether naturally aspirated (ML 350) or biturbo (GLE 400 / 43 AMG), are excellent, reliable, and powerful engines that fully justify the purchase. In contrast, the V8s (ML 500 / GLE 500) equipped with the M278 engine are financial ticking time bombs due to the fragility of their cylinders; they should be avoided unless the engine has already been replaced or you have a repair budget of €15,000. Avoid models equipped with Airmatic if you are looking to minimize long-term maintenance costs.
