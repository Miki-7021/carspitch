Subaru Crosstrek

III (GU) · 2024 – 2025

BUY Reliability 87/100 — excellent

Summary

The third-generation Subaru Crosstrek (code name GU) marks the global unification of the nameplate, permanently replacing the "XV" name in European and international markets. Based on a stiffened evolution (+10%) of the Subaru Global Platform (SGP), this compact SUV retains what made it successful: standard Symmetrical AWD, a generous ground clearance of 22 cm (8.7 inches), and unparalleled versatility. While the design evolves gently, the interior is modernized with the integration of a large 11.6-inch vertical screen and the latest generation of the EyeSight preventive safety system. Although very recent, this generation inherits proven Boxer engines, which suggests excellent overall reliability, confirmed by the first North American satisfaction surveys.

Strengths 6

  • Standard Symmetrical AWD, formidably effective
  • Exceptional ground clearance (22 cm) allowing real off-road capabilities
  • Proven mechanical reliability (mature FB engines)
  • Suspension comfort and filtration of irregularities
  • Highly effective standard EyeSight safety system
  • Excellent resale value (particularly in North America)

Weaknesses 5

  • 2.0L engine (ICE or e-Boxer) lacking punch during overtaking
  • CVT gearbox that tends to make the engine howl under hard acceleration
  • Fuel consumption of the e-Boxer version disappointing compared to competing hybrids (e.g., Toyota C-HR)
  • Soundproofing at high speeds could be improved
  • Disappearance of the manual gearbox on this generation

Check before buying

  • Check if early recalls (driveshafts, wiring harness) have been carried out at the dealership.
  • Inspect the underbody: the ground clearance invites off-roading, check for the absence of impacts on the oil pan or exhaust.
  • Ensure the smoothness of the CVT gearbox when cold (no jerking should be felt).
  • Check the operation of the 11.6-inch central screen (some software bugs reported on early models, fixed by OTA update).

Alternatives

Europe * **Toyota C-HR:** Much more efficient hybridization, bold design, but less roomy and zero off-road capabilities. * **Dacia Duster 4x4:** Budget alternative with real off-road capabilities, but finish and technologies lag behind. * **Jeep Renegade / Compass 4xe:** Plug-in hybrids with good 4x4 capabilities, but more temperamental electronic reliability.

North America * **Honda HR-V:** More spacious in the rear, neat finish, but standard ground clearance and less capable AWD in deep snow. * **Mazda CX-30:** Premium finish, dynamic driving, turbo engine available, but cramped rear space and reduced visibility. * **Toyota Corolla Cross:** Very reliable, hybrid version available, but placid road behavior and limited off-road capabilities.

Verdict

The third-generation Subaru Crosstrek (GU) is an extremely rational choice for drivers living in mountainous, snowy, or rural regions. By reusing the proven technical base of the previous generation while improving rigidity and onboard technology, Subaru considerably limits the risks of early teething problems. The FB series Boxer engines are now very reliable, with oil consumption issues a thing of the past. If you are in North America, favor the 2.5L engine, which is much better suited to the vehicle's weight without penalizing fuel consumption. In Europe, the 2.0L e-Boxer is mandatory: it is reliable and smooth, but do not expect miracles at the pump. It is a vehicle to **BUY** with your eyes closed for its robustness and safety, provided you accept the placid character of its CVT gearbox.
