Nissan Frontier

III (D41) · 2022 – 2025

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

All Nissan models

Summary

The third-generation Nissan Frontier (D41) marks a major breakthrough for the North American market after 17 years of loyal service from the previous generation (D40). Designed specifically for North America, it rests on a heavily revised ladder frame chassis and adopts a muscular design inspired by the legendary Nissan Hardbody. Contrary to the current trend of *downsizing* and forced induction, the Frontier D41 bets on old-school reliability by offering a single engine option: a large naturally aspirated 3.8-liter V6 paired with a 9-speed automatic gearbox. Although it is slightly less radical in pure off-roading than a Toyota Tacoma TRD Pro, it stands out as one of the most reliable and comfortable mid-size pickups in its class, backed by excellent ratings from J.D. Power and Consumer Reports.

Strengths 5

  • Above-average mechanical reliability (proven naturally aspirated V6)
  • Smooth and responsive 9-speed automatic gearbox
  • Excellent ride comfort and soundproofing for a pickup
  • Simple and ergonomic infotainment system
  • Excellent reliability ratings (J.D. Power IQS, Consumer Reports)

Weaknesses 5

  • High fuel consumption (no hybridization or effective Stop&Start before 2025)
  • Heavy hydraulic steering at low speeds
  • Limited rear legroom (Crew Cab)
  • Off-road capabilities (suspension travel) slightly behind the Tacoma
  • Numerous minor software and assembly recalls in the early years

Check before buying

  • Effective engagement of the 'P' (Park) position on the gearbox (risk of rollaway).
  • Operation of the rearview camera (frequent black screen bugs).
  • Condition of the front lower suspension ball joints (recall on 2024 models).
  • Presence and integrity of the towing hitch (recall on 2022 Long Bed models).
  • Alignment and welds of the door strikers (2025-2026 models).

Official recalls

NHTSA Recalls (United States / North America)

  • [2026-01-21] Doors (26V023000): On 2025-2026 models, the door strikers may be improperly welded and break, allowing the door to open while driving.
  • [2025-08-07] Lighting (25V508000): On 2025 models, a software error reduces the intensity of the daytime running lights (DRL) during the Start-Stop cycle.
  • [2025-06-04] Rearview camera (25V324000): On 2025 models, the center screen may remain black when shifting into reverse (software update).
  • [2024-08-06] Headlights (24V582000): On 2024 models, headlight aiming markings are missing or illegible.
  • [2024-06-26] Suspension (24V479000): On 2024 models, an oversized right front lower ball joint could lead to corrosion and separation.
  • [2024-02-27] Airbags (24V154000): On 2024 models, manufacturing defect in the driver's airbag inflator module.
  • [2023-04-17] Tires (23V273000): On 2023 models, possible tearing of the inner tire bead leading to rapid pressure loss.
  • [2022-10-13 & 2022-07-21] Infotainment (22V772000 / 22V527000): Continuous system reboot or loss of the rearview camera linked to a Sirius XM bug.
  • [2022-09-07 & 2022-06-24] Gearbox (22V671000 / 22V457000): On 2020-2022 models, the parking pawl may not engage, resulting in a risk of vehicle rollaway.
  • [2021-12-09] Seatbelts (21V958000): On 2022 models, left rear seatbelt bracket bolts improperly tightened.
  • [2021-11-22] Towing hitch (21V917000): On 2022 Crew Cab Long Bed models, towing hitch not installed at the factory, compromising fuel tank integrity in the event of a rear impact.

*Note: French recalls (Rappel Conso) exist under codes PN0C0, PN9B9, PN9B3, but they concern the NV250/NV300 commercial vehicles sharing Nissan Europe databases, and not the Frontier D41 pickup.*

What sets it apart 10

Reliability

  • Proven Naturally Aspirated V6 Engine — The 3.8L V6 avoids turbocharging, reducing wear-prone parts and offering linear power delivery, which supports long-term mechanical robustness for buyers prioritizing durability.
  • Strong Reliability Ratings From Experts — Excellent scores from J.D. Power IQS and Consumer Reports back the Frontier's reputation, giving buyers independent confirmation of its dependable mechanical base.

Comfort

  • Smooth Nine-Speed Automatic Gearbox — The nine-speed automatic transmission shifts smoothly and responsively, making daily driving and towing more comfortable without the complexity of a dual-clutch setup.
  • Quiet And Comfortable Ride — Excellent ride comfort and sound insulation for a pickup mean occupants experience less fatigue and noise on long trips or commutes.

Practicality

  • Simple, Ergonomic Infotainment System — The infotainment setup is straightforward and ergonomic, so drivers can operate key functions quickly without a steep learning curve or distracting menus.
  • Versatile For Work And Daily Use — The V6 and automatic pairing suits towing, utility tasks and everyday driving, giving buyers one truck that covers professional and personal needs.

Performance

  • Linear Power Without Turbo Lag — The naturally aspirated V6 delivers power progressively, so acceleration feels predictable when merging, hauling or driving with a loaded bed.

Handling

  • Ladder Frame Revised For North America — A heavily revised ladder frame built specifically for North America provides a sturdy foundation for the truck's everyday and utility duties.

Design

  • Muscular Hardbody-Inspired Design — The styling draws on the classic Nissan Hardbody, giving the Frontier a bold, muscular look that stands apart from smoother competitors.

Economy

  • Avoids Complex Turbo Downsizing — By keeping a large atmospheric V6 instead of small turbo engines, the Frontier offers simpler mechanics that appeal to buyers wary of costly turbo repairs.

Alternatives

  • North America: Toyota Tacoma (the benchmark, but transition to a more complex 4-cylinder turbo hybrid on the new generation), Chevrolet Colorado / GMC Canyon (more modern, excellent off-road, but temperamental electronic reliability), Ford Ranger, Honda Ridgeline (unibody chassis, superior comfort but less utilitarian).
  • Europe / Rest of the world: The Frontier D41 is not sold. Alternatives are the Ford Ranger, Toyota Hilux or Nissan Navara (D23).

Verdict

The Nissan Frontier D41 is a true 'modern dinosaur', and that is exactly what makes its strength. By keeping a large naturally aspirated V6 (VQ38DD) where the competition is moving to complex 4-cylinder turbos, Nissan offers a pickup with formidable mechanical reliability, praised by J.D. Power and Consumer Reports. While the early production years (2022) suffered several recalls (notably on the gearbox and software bugs), the mechanical foundation is indestructible. It is a highly recommended purchase for those who prioritize longevity, mechanical simplicity, and ride comfort, at the expense of modern fuel consumption.
