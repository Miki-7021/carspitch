BMW Série 3

F30 · 2012 – 2019

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The sixth-generation BMW 3 Series (F30 for the sedan, F31 for the Touring wagon, F34 for the Gran Turismo, F35 for the long-wheelbase version in China) marks a major turning point for the Bavarian brand with the generalization of turbocharging across the entire range and the introduction of the ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox.

**Positioning:** Premium family sedan focused on driving dynamics.

**Major Evolution (LCI - Life Cycle Impulse):** The mid-cycle facelift, which occurred in **July 2015**, is crucial. It only marginally alters the aesthetics but revolutionizes reliability with the abandonment of 'N' generation engines (N47 diesel, N20 petrol) in favor of the new modular 'B' architecture (B47 diesel, B48 petrol, B58 petrol).

**Overall generation rating:** Very heterogeneous. Pre-2015 models require extreme vigilance (timing chains), while post-2015 (LCI) models are among the most reliable vehicles in their segment.

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic road handling and balanced rear-wheel drive (50/50 distribution).
  • Exceptional ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (Steptronic) for its smoothness and responsiveness.
  • Ergonomics of the iDrive infotainment system (especially NBT and NBT Evo versions).
  • Remarkable reliability of 'B' generation engines (post-2015).
  • Extremely well-controlled fuel consumption on diesels (often < 5.5 L/100km in real-world driving for a 320d).

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous timing chain reliability on pre-2015 4-cylinder engines (N47 diesel and N20 petrol).
  • Interior plastic quality unworthy of a premium car (interior door handles that melt and become sticky).
  • Aerodynamic sound insulation and road noise are imperfect at high speeds.
  • Electric power steering (EPS) lacking feel compared to the previous E90 generation.
  • Massive and recurrent recalls on the diesel EGR system.

Check before buying

  • **Service history:** Avoid vehicles that followed the *LongLife* interval of 30,000 km. Prefer oil changes every 15,000 km.
  • **Chain noise (N47/N20):** Start the engine cold, with the hood open. A metallic scraping or "cricket" noise at idle or around 1500 rpm indicates a stretched chain.
  • **Steering rack:** Turn the wheel from left to right while stationary. A sharp clunk indicates play in the steering rack (BMW repair kit available, or costly complete replacement).
  • **EGR cooler (Diesels):** Check on the BMW portal with the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) if the vehicle is affected by the recall and if it has been carried out.
  • **Interior door handles:** Check if the rubbery coating melts or sticks to fingers (very common defect, cheap aftermarket replacement parts available).
  • **Rear lights:** Check the operation of turn signals and position lights. The ground connector tends to melt.

Official recalls

  • - **EGR Cooler (2018, 2019, 2022):** Massive global recall on diesel engines (4 and 6 cylinders). Coolant leak into the EGR module which can mix with soot and cause an intake manifold fire. - **Blower motor wiring (2017/2019):** Risk of overheating and melting of the heater fan wiring (fire risk). Concerns models produced between 2012 and 2014. - **Takata Airbags (Multiple):** Replacement of the driver's airbag gas generator (risk of metallic fragments being projected upon deployment). - **Steering gear housing screws (2015):** The fastening screws for the power steering motor can break, leading to a loss of assistance. 2012-2013 models.

What sets it apart 14

Safety

  • Benchmark Passive Safety — The BMW 3 Series F30 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult (95%) and child (84%) protection, ensuring optimal peace of mind.
  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — Benefit from first-class active safety thanks to a high-performance braking system, offering very short stopping distances, typically between 35 and 37 meters from 100 to 0 km/h, and excellent endurance.

Performance

  • Unrivaled Driving Dynamics — The 3 Series F30 is a benchmark for its dynamic road behavior, balanced chassis with 50/50 weight distribution, and precise steering, guaranteeing exceptional driving pleasure.

Reliability

  • Exemplary Reliability of B47/B57 Diesel Engines (post-2015) — Models equipped with the new generation B47 and B57 diesel engines (from July 2015 onwards) are renowned for their robustness and remarkable reliability, offering lasting peace of mind.

Economy

  • Controlled Consumption and Optimal Performance — The diesel engines of the 3 Series F30 combine extremely low fuel consumption (often below 5.5 L/100km for a 320d) with dynamic performance and generous torque.

Comfort

  • ZF 8-speed Automatic Transmission: Smoothness and Responsiveness — The ZF 8-speed Steptronic automatic transmission is universally praised for its smooth operation, responsiveness, and ability to optimize driving comfort and performance.

Practicality

  • Intuitive Ergonomics with the iDrive System — The iDrive infotainment system, particularly in its NBT and NBT Evo versions, offers cutting-edge ergonomics and an intuitive interface, making every journey more enjoyable and connected.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Audi A4 (B8/B9) (superior finish, FWD/Quattro), Mercedes C-Class (W204/W205) (more comfort-oriented), Alfa Romeo Giulia (even sharper chassis, but infotainment lagging). - **Japan/Asia:** Lexus IS (300h) (unbeatable hybrid reliability, but less dynamic CVT gearbox). - **North Amer.:** Cadillac ATS (excellent chassis, very limited rear passenger space), Acura TLX.

Verdict

The BMW 3 Series F30 is a car with two faces. **Phase 1 models (2012-early 2015)**, equipped with N47 (diesel) and N20 (petrol) engines, are plagued by serious and costly timing chain problems. Unless you have an invoice proving a recent timing chain replacement, these models are to be avoided.

In contrast, **LCI models (post-July 2015)** equipped with modular B47, B48, and B58 engines are remarkably reliable and make excellent used car purchases. For high-mileage drivers, a **320d LCI (B47)** paired with the ZF8 automatic gearbox is one of the best compromises of performance/fuel consumption/reliability on the market, provided that the EGR valve recall has been properly carried out.
