Toyota Prius

V (XW60) · 2022 – 2025

ACHETER Reliability 92/100 — excellent

Summary

The fifth-generation Toyota Prius (XW60) marks a radical departure from its predecessors. Unveiled in late 2022 and launched for the 2023 model year, it abandons the traditional wedge shape for a sleek, aerodynamic, and highly attractive fastback design. Built on the second-generation TNGA-C platform, it boasts a lower center of gravity, increased rigidity, and a significantly more engaging driving experience. The biggest news is the massive bump in power: the new 2.0L powertrains offer up to 223 hp in the PHEV version, transforming the Prius from a sluggish eco-warrior into a genuinely quick commuter. While it sacrifices some rear headroom and cargo space for style, its 5th-generation hybrid system remains the gold standard for efficiency and reliability. **Overall Rating: Excellent.**

Strengths 6

  • Stunning, head-turning exterior design
  • Massive performance increase (up to 223 hp)
  • Exceptional fuel economy (50+ MPG easily achievable)
  • Proven, bulletproof Dynamic Force and ZR engine architectures
  • Improved handling and driving dynamics (TNGA-C platform)
  • Standard wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto

Weaknesses 5

  • Rear headroom is compromised by the sloping roofline
  • Rearward visibility is poor (thick C-pillars, small rear window)
  • Cargo capacity is smaller than the previous generation
  • Gauge cluster placement can be blocked by the steering wheel for some drivers
  • Cabin noise at highway speeds is still noticeable

Check before buying

  • Electronic rear door latches (ensure the recall has been performed)
  • 12V battery health (common failure point on low-mileage/stored hybrids)
  • Infotainment system stability (check for the latest OTA software updates)
  • Front bumper and undercarriage scrapes (the car sits lower than previous generations)

Performance

  • Dynamic and Responsive Performance — Thanks to its 5th generation hybrid system, especially the 223 hp PHEV version, the Prius offers impressive acceleration, reaching 100 km/h in just 6.8 seconds, for an exhilarating driving experience.

economy

  • Remarkable Energy Efficiency — Enjoy a generous electric range, often exceeding 70 km in real-world conditions for the PHEV version, and consistently frugal fuel consumption, around 4.5 L/100 km, even with an empty battery.

reliability

handling

  • Unmatched Driving Pleasure — Built on the second-generation TNGA-C platform, the Prius offers a transformed, much more dynamic road behavior and a rigorous chassis, guaranteeing precise and enjoyable handling.

technology

  • Cutting-Edge Hybrid Technology — Equipped with Toyota's 5th generation Hybrid System Drive (HSD), featuring a more compact and silent Power Control Unit and a lighter transaxle, the Prius embodies innovation for efficiency and refinement.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Honda Civic e:HEV, Toyota Corolla Hybrid, Kia Niro HEV/PHEV, Peugeot 308 PHEV. - **North America:** Honda Accord Hybrid, Hyundai Elantra Hybrid, Kia Niro, Tesla Model 3 (as a similarly priced EV alternative). - **Japan / Asia:** Honda Civic e:HEV, Nissan Note Aura (e-Power), Toyota Corolla Sport Hybrid.

Verdict

The Generation 5 Toyota Prius is a triumph. It fixes the biggest historical complaint about the Prius—its styling and sluggish performance—while retaining the legendary fuel efficiency and reliability the nameplate is known for. Mechanically, the M20A and 2ZR engines paired with Toyota's 5th-gen hybrid synergy drive are virtually indestructible if basic maintenance is followed. The only real compromises are practical ones: less rear headroom and a smaller trunk. If the styling and space work for you, this is one of the most reliable and efficient vehicles on the road today. **Highly recommended.**
