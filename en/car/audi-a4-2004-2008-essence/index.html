Summary

The Audi A4 B7 (internal code name Typ 8E for the sedan/Avant wagon, and 8H for the convertible) is not an entirely new generation, but a significant facelift of the B6 generation (PL46 platform). It marks the introduction of the *Singleframe* grille, revised suspension geometry, recalibrated steering, and, most importantly, the widespread adoption of direct petrol injection (FSI and TFSI). Although its interior finish quality remains an absolute benchmark of its era, the B7 is a transitional car that has experienced mixed fortunes in terms of reliability, particularly due to the introduction of new engine technologies (EA113) and the notoriously problematic 2.0 TDI Pumpe Düse engines. In the used car market, petrol versions (especially the 1.8T and well-maintained 2.0 TFSI) are now highly sought after compared to aging diesels.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional interior finish quality (materials, assembly), ages very well.
  • Secure road handling, especially with Quattro all-wheel drive (Torsen).
  • Very robust 1.8T 20V engine and very powerful 2.0 TFSI.
  • Corrosion resistance of the bodywork (galvanized steel).
  • Exterior design (Singleframe) that has aged well compared to the B6.

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the first 2.0 TDI engines (oil pump, cylinder head).
  • Very fragile Multitronic continuously variable automatic gearbox (on FWD models).
  • Maintenance cost of V6 and V8 engines (timing chain at the rear of the engine).
  • Multi-link front suspension prone to premature wear (bushings, ball joints).
  • Limited rear passenger space and trunk volume for the category.

Check before buying

  • Service history of the automatic gearbox (Multitronic or Tiptronic: oil change every 60,000 km).
  • On 2.0 TFSI: ask if the high-pressure pump tappet (cam follower) has been changed recently.
  • On V6/V8 petrol: listen to the engine when cold (hood open) to detect chain noise (rattle).
  • Check the operation of all electrical equipment (power windows, air conditioning, convertible top mechanism).
  • Inspect the windshield cowl area (under the battery): water drain holes can clog, flooding the cabin and the ECU.
  • Play in the front suspension (clunking over speed bumps indicating worn suspension arms).

Official recalls

⚠️ Official Manufacturer Recalls

  • Takata Airbags (2020-2021): Massive recall for the replacement of the driver's airbag gas generator, risk of metal fragment projection.
  • Ignition Coils (2010): Free replacement of ignition coils on many petrol engines (1.8T, 2.0 TFSI, V6, V8) due to misfires.
  • Brake Assist (2006): On 2.0 TDI engines, risk of leakage on the brake booster vacuum hose, hardening the brake pedal.
  • Water Ingress (Various Years): Cleaning of windshield cowl drains during services to prevent flooding of the brake booster and cabin.

What sets it apart 5

Comfort

  • Premium Interior Quality and Exceptional Durability — The Audi A4 B7 stands out with a benchmark interior finish quality, featuring materials and assemblies that have aged remarkably well, ensuring a luxurious and time-resistant cabin.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Precise Road Handling — Enjoy excellent road holding and dynamic behavior, characterized by stable driving, precise steering, and great predictability in corners. The suspension offers a perfect balance between comfort and sportiness, enhanced by the efficiency of the Quattro all-wheel drive.

Safety

  • Enhanced Safety and Powerful Braking — The Audi A4 B7 offers optimal protection for its occupants, as evidenced by its excellent crash test results. Its powerful and consistent braking system, assisted by ABS and EBD, ensures total vehicle control in all circumstances.

Reliability

  • Recognized Reliability of Petrol Engines — The petrol engines, notably the very robust 1.8T 20V and the high-performance 2.0 TFSI, are particularly sought after in the used car market for their proven reliability and driving pleasure.

Practicality

  • Timeless Design and Anti-Corrosion Bodywork — With its elegant exterior design and Singleframe grille, the Audi A4 B7 maintains a modern and appealing look. Its galvanized steel bodywork ensures excellent corrosion resistance, guaranteeing aesthetic and structural longevity.

Verdict

⚖️ Expert's Verdict

The Audi A4 B7 is an excellent engineer's car whose reputation has been tarnished by the design flaws of the 2.0 TDI and the Multitronic gearbox. If you are looking for a petrol version, it is an excellent opportunity in the used car market. The 1.8T 20V is the sensible choice: reliable, easy to maintain, and sufficiently powerful. The 2.0 TFSI is the enthusiast's choice: it offers modern performance but requires a meticulous owner (monitoring of the *cam follower* and oil). Avoid the 3.2 V6 and 4.2 V8 (S4) unless you have an unlimited repair budget for the timing chains. Always prefer a manual gearbox or a Quattro version (which uses the robust Tiptronic gearbox instead of the fragile Multitronic).
