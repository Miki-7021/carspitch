Summary

The first-generation Citroën C4 Picasso (internal code UD) is a compact MPV launched in 2006, replacing the Xsara Picasso. It was offered in a 5-seat version (C4 Picasso, 2006) and a 7-seat version (Grand C4 Picasso, 2006). A notable facelift took place in 2010 (grille, lights, interior, engines). It is based on the PSA group's PF2 platform, shared with the C4 I, the Peugeot 307/308 and the Citroën Berlingo. Positioned as a comfortable, well-equipped family MPV, it enjoyed strong commercial success in Europe. Build quality is decent, but ageing (electronics, running gear) and the mechanical reliability of the EP6/THP petrol engines make it a purchase to watch closely. Overall reliability rating: average, with significant variation depending on the engine.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent interior space and modularity (5 or 7 seats, individual seats, generous trunk)
  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation of a good standard for the era
  • Panoramic visibility and huge windscreen (depending on trim)
  • 7-seat version (Grand C4 Picasso) rare and practical in the segment
  • Simple, low-maintenance naturally aspirated 1.6/1.8/2.0 engines
  • Good value for money on the used market

Weaknesses 7

  • EP6 VTi/THP petrol engines (PSA/BMW Prince) with documented concerning reliability: timing chain, VVT solenoid, head gasket, oil consumption
  • TÜV Report 2018: quality judged below average from 5 years old — tie rod ends, springs/shock absorbers, lighting failing more often than average
  • Fragile driveshafts on almost all model years (TÜV)
  • On-board electronics (control units, sensors, displays) a source of intermittent faults
  • Oil consumption on some petrol engines
  • Ageing of interior plastics and controls
  • No recent ADAC Pannenstatistik data (model too old)

Check before buying

  • Full maintenance history, particularly on EP6 VTi/THP engines (chain, tensioner, VVT solenoid, oil changes)
  • Timing chain noise on cold start (EP6)
  • Engine oil level and colour, reported oil consumption
  • Condition of driveshafts and tie rod ends
  • Condition of springs and shock absorbers (leaks, noises)
  • Operation of the lighting (dipped beam, rear lights)
  • Operation of the electronics: central screen, parking sensors, air conditioning
  • History of recalls carried out (timing, engine software, airbags)
  • Condition of the automatic gearbox (4-speed/6-speed automatic) if equipped
  • Corrosion and condition of the underbody

What sets it apart 10

Practicality

  • Generous Space and Flexible Layout — The C4 Picasso offers excellent habitability with five or seven seats and independent seats, giving families room to configure the cabin for passengers or cargo as needed.
  • Large Boot for Family Loads — A generous boot makes this compact MPV practical for everyday shopping, pushchairs and holiday luggage without forcing you to fold seats constantly.
  • Rare and Practical Seven Seats — The Grand C4 Picasso seven-seat version is rare on the segment and adds two usable seats, useful for occasional extra passengers or children.

Comfort

  • Comfortable Suspension and Insulation — Suspension comfort and sound insulation are of a good level for the period, reducing fatigue on longer journeys and making daily family trips more relaxed.

Safety

  • Panoramic Visibility and Huge Windscreen — Depending on trim, the panoramic visibility and giant windscreen improve forward and side views, helping you place the car in traffic and park with confidence.

Economy

  • Simple Atmospheric Petrol Engines — The 1.6, 1.8 and 2.0 atmospheric petrol engines are simple and inexpensive to maintain, which suits buyers who want low running costs and straightforward servicing.
  • Strong Value in Used Market — The model offers a good price-to-benefit ratio on the used market, giving access to a spacious family MPV for a relatively modest outlay.

Performance

  • Proven 2.0 Petrol for Loaded Use — The 2.0 16V EW10A provides correct torque from a proven, turbo-free mechanical design, making it suitable for a loaded Grand C4 Picasso or motorway driving.

Reliability

  • Balanced 1.8 Petrol for Families — The 1.8 16V EW7A atmospheric engine delivers acceptable drivability with controlled maintenance costs, fitting polyvalent family use on roads and longer routes.
  • Robust 1.6 Petrol for Small Budgets — The 1.6 16V TU5JP4 is a simple, robust mechanical unit with low maintenance costs and no turbo, well suited to urban and peri-urban use on a small budget.

Verdict

The Citroën C4 Picasso I is a very spacious and comfortable family MPV, with attractive value for money on the used market. However, its reliability is uneven: the naturally aspirated petrol engines (1.6 TU5JP4, 1.8 EW7A, 2.0 EW10A) remain the safest choices, with simple and low-cost maintenance. Conversely, the EP6 VTi 120 and especially THP petrol engines (PSA/BMW Prince engine) have documented defects (timing chain, VVT solenoid, head gasket, oil consumption) that require an impeccable maintenance history and increased vigilance. The TÜV Report 2018 rates the model below average from 5 years old (tie rod ends, shock absorbers, lighting, driveshafts). Recommendation: favour a naturally aspirated 1.6/1.8/2.0 with a full history; negotiate hard or avoid a THP without proof of maintenance. Imperatively check that the recalls have been carried out (timing 2021, airbags 2018, engine software 2018).
