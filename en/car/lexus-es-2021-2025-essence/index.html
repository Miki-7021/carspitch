Lexus ES

VII facelift (XZ10) · 2021 – 2025

BUY Reliability 94/100 — excellent

All Lexus models

Summary

The seventh-generation Lexus ES (XZ10) received a facelift in mid-2021 for the 2022 model year. Based on the excellent TNGA-K platform (shared with the Toyota Camry), this large executive sedan embodies rational luxury. The facelift corrected the main flaw of phase 1 by replacing the unergonomic trackpad with a touchscreen moved closer to the driver, while also stiffening the rear axle and optimizing braking. Although Europe only gets the hybrid version (ES 300h), the rest of the world (North America, Asia, Middle East) benefits from 100% internal combustion (gasoline) engines of legendary reliability, ranging from the placid 4-cylinder to the noble naturally aspirated V6. It is one of the most reliable cars in the world in its class.

Overall design rating: 9/10

Strengths 6

  • Very high level of mechanical and electronic reliability (among the best on the market)
  • Imperial ride comfort and exceptional soundproofing
  • Ergonomics greatly improved by the arrival of the touchscreen (facelift)
  • Build and assembly quality (durable interior materials)
  • Mark Levinson audio system (optional) of exceptional quality
  • Smooth and powerful 3.5L V6 (ES 350) (in markets where available)

Weaknesses 5

  • Non-folding rear bench seat (presence of a V-shaped structural reinforcement behind the seats)
  • Purely cosmetic F-Sport trim (handling remains placid, no sporty vocation)
  • Multimedia interface still visually somewhat dated despite the touchscreen
  • Imposing size (nearly 5 meters) poorly suited to European city centers
  • Absence of traditional gasoline engines (ES 250/350) on the European market

Check before buying

  • Recall history (especially regarding PVM cameras and airbag sensors)
  • Condition of the 12V battery (hybrid or highly equipped models driven infrequently tend to discharge it)
  • Front tire wear (the torque of the V6 or hybrid on the front axle can wear out the rubber quickly)
  • Smooth operation of the 8-speed automatic gearbox on gasoline versions (ES 250/350) during cold shifts

Official recalls

Rappel Conso (France / Europe)

  • [2025-11-07] PVM Camera (Software): A software issue in the parking assist control unit (Panoramic View Monitor - PVM) may prevent the image from displaying. Risk of injury (lack of visibility). Affects many Toyota/Lexus models including the ES.
  • [2025-03-10] High-pressure fuel pump: Insufficient pressure of parts in the pump can cause cylinder movement, leading to a fire risk. (Global Toyota/Lexus recall, ES applicability to be verified by VIN).
  • [2024-08-22] Camera housings: Camera housings can separate under stress (incorrect welding), allowing water ingress and system failure.
  • [2024-07-19] Advanced recognition camera: Software programming can cause a memory failure when the ignition is turned off, deactivating driving aids upon restart.
  • [2024-06-26] Immobilizer: Insufficiently robust design allowing the vehicle to be in "Ready ON" mode without the key nearby.

NHTSA (United States / North America)

  • [2025-10-30] 25V744000 - PVM Camera: Software recall regarding the Panoramic View Monitor (PVM) system which may fail to display the rearview camera image.
  • [2024-02-21] 24V124000 - PVM Camera: Affects ES250, ES300h, ES350 (2023-2024). Rearview camera display issue related to the PVM system.
  • [2023-12-20] 23V865000 - Airbag sensor (OCS): Affects notably the ES350 (2020-2021). The front passenger seat Occupant Classification System (OCS) sensor may short circuit, preventing the airbag from deploying in the event of a crash.

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Legendary Reliability and Exceptional Durability — The Lexus ES is globally renowned for its very high level of mechanical and electronic reliability, ranked as the #1 luxury brand by J.D. Power. Its petrol engines are designed to exceed 300,000 km with routine maintenance, offering absolute peace of mind.

Comfort

  • Imperial Comfort and Exceptional Soundproofing — Enjoy an unparalleled driving experience thanks to supreme ride comfort and cabin soundproofing that transforms every journey into a serene bubble. The Lexus ES transports you in cathedral-like silence.

Safety

  • Maximum Safety with Prestigious Distinctions — The Lexus ES offers optimal protection for all its occupants, as evidenced by its 5-star Euro NCAP rating, with impressive scores of 91% for adult protection and 87% for child protection. It has also been designated an IIHS Top Safety Pick+ for several consecutive years.

Braking

  • Excellent and Controlled Braking — Benefit from exceptional, progressive, and reassuring braking performance. The Lexus ES boasts short stopping distances for its category, at approximately 52 meters from 113 km/h, ensuring increased safety in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Serene and Stable Road Holding — The Lexus ES offers stable and serene road holding, even at high speeds, with precise yet light steering. Its handling is very safe and predictable, comfort-oriented, guaranteeing reassuring driving in all conditions.

qualite_interieure

  • Superior Build Quality and Optimized Ergonomics — The Lexus ES interior stands out with impeccable build quality and assembly, using durable materials. The facelift significantly improved ergonomics with the integration of an advanced touchscreen angled towards the driver, making the interface more intuitive.

Performance

  • Refined and Powerful Petrol Engines (market dependent) — For markets where they are available, the petrol engines, notably the 3.5L V6 (ES 350) with 302 hp, offer exceptional smoothness, a noble sound, and generous power. These engines are renowned for their refinement and legendary reliability.

Alternatives

Europe

  • BMW 5 Series (G30/G60): More dynamic, large choice of engines, but more hit-and-miss internal combustion reliability.
  • Mercedes-Benz E-Class (W213/W214): Equivalent comfort, cutting-edge technologies, but higher maintenance costs.
  • Volvo S90: Sleek design, excellent safety, high-performance PHEV engines.

North America & Asia

  • Acura TLX: Sportier (especially in Type S), excellent SH-AWD system, but cramped rear seats.
  • Genesis G80: Luxurious finish, status-enhancing design, powerful engines, but higher fuel consumption and long-term reliability still lagging behind Lexus.
  • Toyota Crown / Avalon: Technical cousins of the ES, cheaper but less well soundproofed and finished.

Verdict

The Lexus ES (XZ10 facelift) is the archetype of the worry-free luxury sedan. If you live in a region where the gasoline versions (ES 250 and especially the ES 350 with its fabulous V6) are available, they represent an exceptionally safe purchase, capable of exceeding 300,000 km with simple routine maintenance. The 2021 facelift corrected the car's only real flaw by introducing a touchscreen. Although it offers no sporty pretensions (even in F-Sport trim), it excels in its field: transporting its occupants in cathedral-like silence, with regal comfort and absolute peace of mind. The few electronic recalls (cameras, sensors) are handled by the brand's highly reputable network.
