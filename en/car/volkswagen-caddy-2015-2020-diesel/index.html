Volkswagen Caddy

IV (SA) (2015–2020) · 2015–2020

BUY Reliability 77/100 — bon

Summary

The fourth-generation Volkswagen Caddy (type SA), launched in 2015, is a major evolution of its predecessor, although it is based on a similar technical platform (modified PQ35/PQ46). It positions itself as a versatile leisure activity vehicle and light commercial van, appreciated for its robustness, generous interior space, and modularity. Available in short and long (Maxi) versions, as well as commercial (Panel Van) and passenger (Combi, Trendline, Comfortline, Highline, Alltrack) variants, the Caddy IV benefited from a significant facelift compared to the Caddy III, with a modernized front end, a revised interior, and the integration of new driver assistance technologies. Its reliability reputation is generally good, particularly for the diesel engines which are the core of the range, but certain points of attention deserve to be highlighted.

**Overall reliability rating:** 7.5/10

Strengths 5

  • Modularity and generous interior space (Maxi versions)
  • Overall robustness and Volkswagen build quality
  • Efficient and economical 2.0 TDI diesel engines
  • Safe and comfortable road handling for its category
  • Wide choice of versions (commercial van, leisure activity vehicle, 4Motion)

Weaknesses 5

  • High price new and used
  • Firm suspension comfort when unladen (commercial van versions)
  • Perceptible diesel engine noise when cold and accelerating
  • Potential DPF and EGR valve issues on diesels in urban use
  • DSG gearbox maintenance cost if recommendations are not followed

Check before buying

  • Complete service history, especially for diesels and DSG gearboxes
  • Condition of the DPF and EGR valve (warning lights on, loss of power)
  • Absence of water pump leaks (all engines)
  • DSG gearbox operation (gear changes, jerks, noises)
  • Wear of the flywheel and clutch (manual versions, especially diesel)
  • Condition of the running gear (bushings, shock absorbers) and tires, especially on vehicles that have carried heavy loads
  • Operation of electrical and electronic equipment (air conditioning, multimedia system)

Verdict

The Volkswagen Caddy IV is a versatile and reliable vehicle, particularly appreciated for its robustness and space. The 2.0 TDI diesel engines are the core of the range and offer an excellent compromise between performance, consumption, and durability, provided that rigorous maintenance is observed and exclusively urban journeys, which can clog the DPF and EGR valve, are avoided. The TSI petrol versions are a good option for more urban use or moderate annual mileages.

**Verdict: BUY** if the service history is clear, especially for diesel versions and DSG gearboxes. Prioritize models that have regularly undertaken road journeys. **NEGOTIATE** if signs of fouling (DPF/EGR) are present or if DSG maintenance is not documented. The Caddy IV remains an excellent choice for professionals and families looking for a practical and durable vehicle.
