Volvo S60

III (Z252) · 2018 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The third-generation Volvo S60 (code name Z252) marks a historic turning point for the Swedish brand: it is the first modern-era Volvo model to completely forgo diesel engines. Based on the excellent modular SPA (Scalable Product Architecture) platform, it is also the first Volvo produced in the United States (Ridgeville plant, South Carolina).

Positioned as a dynamic premium sedan against the German trio (3 Series, C-Class, A4), it stands out with its minimalist Scandinavian design, high-level comfort, and unparalleled safety arsenal. The engine range is exclusively built around the 2.0L VEA (Volvo Engine Architecture) 4-cylinder block, available in 100% internal combustion (at launch), 48V mild-hybrid (B-Series), and high-performance plug-in hybrid (Recharge / Twin Engine) versions.

A discreet facelift took place in 2022, bringing minor aesthetic touches, but above all a major overhaul of the infotainment system (transition from Sensus to Android Automotive) and a crucial technical update for the PHEV versions (18.8 kWh battery and removal of the mechanical supercharger in favor of a more powerful electric motor).

Strengths 6

  • Timeless, elegant, and distinctive design
  • Exceptional seat comfort (among the best on the market)
  • Sound, safe, and dynamic road handling
  • Post-2022 PHEV versions (18.8 kWh) offering class-leading electric range
  • Interior finish quality and masterful Bowers & Wilkins audio system (optional)
  • Complete absence of diesel engines (an asset for ULEZ/LEZ)

Weaknesses 5

  • Android Automotive infotainment system (post-2022) very buggy at launch (black screens, GPS loss)
  • Limited boot volume (390 liters), further reduced in PHEV versions
  • Mechanical complexity and inconsistent reliability of pre-2022 PHEV versions (ERAD, supercharger)
  • Very high weight of Recharge versions (nearly 2 tons), quickly wearing out brakes and tires
  • Aisin 8-speed automatic gearbox sometimes slow in sporty driving

Check before buying

  • On PHEV: Heating function in 100% electric mode (HVCH failure very common).
  • On pre-2022 PHEV: Abnormal noises (whistling/clunking) from the rear during acceleration/deceleration in electric mode (ERAD failure).
  • On all models: Wear of front brake discs (tendency to warp under vehicle weight during heavy braking).
  • On 2022+ models: Stability of the Android Automotive system (verify that all OTA updates have been performed).
  • Service history: Demand meticulous follow-up within the Volvo network or by a qualified independent specialist, as VEA mechanics do not tolerate approximations well.

Verdict

The Volvo S60 III is an extremely appealing premium sedan, excelling in comfort, safety, and design. As an expert, **the choice of year and engine is crucial**.

If you are targeting a plug-in hybrid (Recharge) version, **avoid 2019-2021 models (11.6 kWh battery) if possible**: their mechanical complexity (turbo + supercharger) and recurring weaknesses of the rear electric motor (ERAD) pose a significant financial risk outside of warranty.

In contrast, **post-2022 PHEV versions (18.8 kWh battery, 350 or 455 hp) are true masterpieces**: the mechanics are simplified (no more supercharger), reliability is significantly improved, and the electric range becomes truly usable daily. For more restricted budgets, a T4/T5 internal combustion or B4 mild-hybrid version makes for a very solid and recommendable used purchase.
