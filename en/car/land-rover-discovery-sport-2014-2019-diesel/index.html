Land Rover Discovery Sport

I (L550) · 2014 – 2019

AVOID Reliability 42/100 — faible

Summary

The first-generation Land Rover Discovery Sport (L550) succeeded the Freelander 2 in 2014. Positioned as a premium compact family SUV, it stands out with its 5+2 seating configuration (rare in the category) and its genuine off-road capabilities thanks to the Terrain Response system. While its design and roominess have been appealing, its career has been heavily marred by major reliability problems, particularly on the Ingenium diesel engines introduced in late 2015 and on the early 9-speed ZF automatic gearboxes. Independent statistics (TÜV, ADAC, What Car?) consistently rank it at the bottom of the table for reliability during this period (before the 2019 facelift). Overall reliability rating: Poor.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional roominess for its size (very practical 5+2 seating option)
  • Genuine off-road capabilities (highly capable Terrain Response system)
  • Suspension comfort and soundproofing (especially on Ingenium versions)
  • Elegant and status-enhancing design that ages very well
  • Towing capacity (up to 2500 kg depending on version)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the Ingenium diesel engines (2015-2019)
  • Very high TÜV defect rate at 3 years (20.5%, well below average)
  • Temperamental and fragile ZF9 automatic gearbox on 2014-2016 model years
  • Very high weight (nearly 2 tons) penalizing fuel consumption and driving dynamics
  • Original infotainment system is slow and prone to bugs (pre-facelift)

Check before buying

  • Clear maintenance history: avoid any Ingenium diesel that has followed the manufacturer's interval of 34,000 km / 2 years (imminent risk of engine failure).
  • Behavior of the ZF9 automatic gearbox: no jerking or hesitation should be tolerated during gear shifts.
  • Oil level on Ingenium diesels: a level above the maximum indicates dilution by diesel (danger).
  • Condition of the underbody and suspension: weak point reported by TÜV, check for impact marks (off-road use) and corrosion.
  • Operation of the onboard electronics and absence of warning lights (fragile ABS sensors according to ADAC).

Performance

  • Off-Road Mastery with Terrain Response System — Inheriting Land Rover's DNA, this SUV offers genuine off-road capabilities. Its Terrain Response™ system adapts the vehicle to all surfaces, ensuring optimal grip and progress, even in the most demanding conditions.
  • Impressive Towing Capacity — Ideal for activities requiring towing, the Discovery Sport offers a towing capacity of up to 2500 kg depending on the version. This versatility meets the most demanding needs, whether for a caravan, boat, or trailer.
  • Robust and Torquey 2.2L Diesel Engine — Versions equipped with the 2.2L diesel engine (available on earlier model years) benefit from a proven powertrain, renowned for its robustness and generous torque. Ideal for long journeys and towing, it offers a pleasant and reliable drive.

Comfort

  • Refined Suspension Comfort and Sound Insulation — Enjoy a serene driving experience thanks to high-level suspension comfort and meticulous cabin sound insulation. The Discovery Sport absorbs road imperfections with ease, offering relaxing journeys for all passengers.

Safety

  • Maximum Protection with 5-Star Euro NCAP Rating — Designed for occupant safety, the Discovery Sport achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 82% for child protection. Its powerful and progressive brakes ensure quick and controlled stops, complemented by stable and predictable road holding.

Verdict

The first-generation Land Rover Discovery Sport (2014-2019) is an extremely attractive vehicle on paper, but its real-world reliability is alarming. Data from TÜV (20.5% defect rate at 3 years), ADAC, and What Car? (2.5/5) confirm a problematic design. The 2.0 Ingenium diesel engine (the most common) suffers from a major design flaw related to DPF regeneration, leading to oil dilution and timing chain failure. If you absolutely must buy this model, favor the very first models (2014-2015) equipped with the older 2.2 diesel engine (PSA/Ford origin), which is much more robust, or turn to the petrol engines, provided you accept high fuel consumption. In all cases, a solid mechanical breakdown warranty is essential.
