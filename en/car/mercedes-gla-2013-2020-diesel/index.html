Mercedes GLA

X156 · 2013 – 2020

NEGOTIATE Reliability 80/100 — bon

Summary

The **Mercedes-Benz GLA (X156 Generation)** marks the entry of the star brand into the highly competitive premium compact SUV segment. Based on the MFA platform (shared with the A-Class W176 and CLA), it resembles a raised sedan more than a true off-roader.

Launched in late 2013, it received a **facelift in 2017** (LED headlights replacing bi-xenons, redesigned bumpers, modernized multimedia screen, and Apple CarPlay/Android Auto integration).

In terms of reliability, the GLA X156 is generally a good student, but it requires rigorous monitoring, particularly concerning its 7G-DCT dual-clutch gearbox and the emission control peripherals on diesel engines (which represent the majority of sales in Europe).

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic design and low roofline (shooting brake look)
  • Paint quality and corrosion resistance
  • Remarkable fuel efficiency of diesel engines (OM607 and OM651)
  • Safe road handling, similar to a sedan
  • Effective 4MATIC system in snow and rain

Weaknesses 5

  • Rear passenger space and boot volume (421 L) limited for a family
  • Uneven interior finish (hard plastics in lower sections, frequent rattling noises)
  • 7G-DCT gearbox sometimes jerky at low speeds and during maneuvers
  • Imperfect sound insulation on diesel engines (especially the 2.1L OM651, very rough)
  • Poor rear visibility (reversing camera essential)

Check before buying

  • Service history of the 7G-DCT automatic gearbox (oil change invoice mandatory around 100,000 km).
  • Metallic noise on cold start on the 2.1 Diesel (OM651) indicating timing chain wear.
  • Operation of the panoramic sunroof (creaks, slowness, or water ingress due to clogged drains).
  • Condition of parking sensors and reversing camera (sensitive to humidity).
  • Asymmetrical tire wear (sensitive front axle geometry).

Alternatives

Verdict

The Mercedes GLA X156 is a relevant choice for those looking for the Mercedes badge and design in a compact format, without needing a large loading volume.

**For buyers targeting Diesel:** The **GLA 200 d (OM651)** is the best power/reliability compromise, provided that the absence of cold chain noise and the 7G-DCT gearbox oil change history are scrupulously checked. The **GLA 180 d** is economical but its Renault-sourced engine requires timing belt replacement.

Avoid diesel models if you only drive in the city (guaranteed clogging). In this case, opt for a **GLA 200 petrol**, which is more suitable and very reliable despite some thermostat weaknesses.
