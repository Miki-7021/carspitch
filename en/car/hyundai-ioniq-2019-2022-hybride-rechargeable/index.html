Hyundai Ioniq

I facelift (AE) · 2019 – 2022

BUY Reliability 90/100 — excellent

Summary

The **first-generation Hyundai Ioniq (AE)**, in its facelifted version introduced in 2019, is a pioneering compact sedan: it is the first model in the world to have been designed from the outset to offer three electrified powertrains (hybrid, plug-in hybrid, and 100% electric) on a single platform. The 2019 facelift brings a modernized grille, a new 10.25-inch touchscreen, enhanced driving aids, and, for the electric version, a larger capacity battery (38.3 kWh). The **plug-in hybrid (PHEV)** version, the focus of this report, represents an excellent compromise: it combines a 1.6 GDI petrol engine with a more powerful electric motor than the standard hybrid version, powered by an 8.9 kWh battery offering around fifty kilometers of real-world all-electric range. Overall, the Ioniq AE shines with its aerodynamic efficiency (drag coefficient of 0.24) and its remarkable reliability, confirmed by European inspection agencies.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability (Very low ADAC breakdown rate: 1.8 breakdowns/1000 veh/year)
  • Very low technical inspection (TÜV) defect rate at 3 years (8.5%)
  • Extremely low fuel consumption (HEV and PHEV)
  • Minimal brake wear thanks to efficient regenerative braking (highlighted by TÜV)
  • Very robust McPherson strut front suspension
  • Manufacturer warranty of 5 years (unlimited mileage) and 8 years/160,000 km for the battery

Weaknesses 4

  • Dual-clutch gearbox (DCT6) sometimes hesitant or generating slight jerks at very low speeds (HEV/PHEV)
  • Major recalls concerning the high-voltage battery on 100% electric versions (BEV)
  • Rear headroom limited by the sloping roofline
  • Trunk volume reduced on the PHEV version compared to the HEV version (due to the larger battery)

Check before buying

  • On the PHEV version: check the condition of the charging cable (Type 2) and the proper functioning of the flap.
  • On HEV/PHEV versions: ensure that the DCT6 gearbox oil changes have been done according to recommendations.
  • On the BEV (Electric) version: demand the certificate of completion for recalls concerning the high-voltage battery (BMS and/or module replacement).
  • Check front tire wear (the instant torque of the electric motor can accelerate wear).

Economy

  • Drastic Fuel Savings and Reduced Maintenance — Benefit from extremely low fuel consumption thanks to its advanced plug-in hybrid powertrain, offering significant electric range for your daily commutes. The regenerative braking system minimizes brake wear, thereby reducing maintenance costs and extending the lifespan of consumables.

Safety

  • Maximum Safety for All Occupants — Travel with complete confidence thanks to state-of-the-art passive and active safety. The Hyundai Ioniq achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with high scores for adult and child protection, and was recognized as a "Top Safety Pick" by the IIHS.
  • Efficient and Controlled Braking — The Ioniq's braking system delivers solid and consistent performance, with short stopping distances for its category. The intelligent integration of regenerative braking ensures controlled deceleration and contributes to the vehicle's overall efficiency.

Comfort

  • Stable Road Holding and Driving Comfort — Enjoy a serene and predictable driving experience. The Ioniq offers stable road holding and remarkable ride comfort, with light and precise steering, ideal for both city and long-distance journeys, ensuring a reassuring and competent drive.

Practicality

  • Versatility and Technological Innovation — The Ioniq is a pioneer, designed from the outset for electrification. Its plug-in hybrid version offers exceptional versatility, combining the power of a robust gasoline engine with the efficiency of an electric motor, all with optimized aerodynamics (Cd of 0.24) for maximum energy performance.

Alternatives

* **Europe / North America:** Toyota Prius (the historical rival, excellent reliability but polarizing design), Kia Niro PHEV (technical cousin of the Ioniq, compact SUV format), Volkswagen Golf GTE (sportier but less efficient hybrid system and DSG gearbox reliability to watch). * **Asia:** Honda Insight, Toyota Corolla Hybrid.

Verdict

The facelifted Hyundai Ioniq (AE) is one of the most reliable and efficient electrified vehicles on the used market. The **plug-in hybrid (PHEV)** version is particularly recommendable: it combines the robustness of the 1.6 GDI engine, battery thermal management praised by ADAC, and a real-world electric range that is highly relevant for daily use. Statistics from TÜV (8.5% defect rate at 3 years) and What Car? (4.2/5) confirm its long-term durability, with very reduced wear on consumables (brakes). If you opt for the 100% electric version, it is imperative to verify that the critical battery recalls have been performed. For the HEV and PHEV versions, purchase is highly recommended, subject to regular maintenance of the dual-clutch gearbox.
