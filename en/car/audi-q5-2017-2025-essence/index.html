Audi Q5

II (FY) · 2017 – 2025

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The second-generation Audi Q5 (Type FY) is based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MLB Evo platform. Launched in 2017 and facelifted in late 2020 (new OLED light signature, abandonment of the MMI rotary controller for a fully tactile interface), it stands out as one of the most well-rounded premium SUVs on the market. Although Europe long favored diesel (TDI), petrol (TFSI) and plug-in hybrid (TFSI e) powertrains have taken over, driven by significantly improved reliability compared to the previous generation. The interior is near perfection in terms of assembly, and acoustic comfort is top-notch. It is a mature vehicle, whose EA888 (4-cylinder) and EA839 (V6) petrol engines offer an excellent compromise between driving pleasure and durability, provided rigorous maintenance is followed.

Strengths 5

  • Proven reliability of 2.0 TFSI petrol engines (EA888 Gen3/4)
  • Benchmark finish and assembly quality in the category
  • Excellent sound insulation, especially with optional acoustic glazing
  • Ergonomics of the MMI system (especially the pre-2020 version with rotary controller, very intuitive)
  • Efficiency of the quattro all-wheel drive (ultra on 4-cylinders, Torsen on V6)

Weaknesses 5

  • High maintenance cost within the Audi network
  • S tronic gearbox sometimes hesitant at low speeds (jerks on startup)
  • Reliability of the starter-generator on mild-hybrid (MHEV) versions
  • Significant weight penalizing agility and urban fuel consumption
  • Reduced boot volume on plug-in hybrid (TFSI e) versions

Check before buying

  • S tronic gearbox service history (strict oil change every 60,000 km)
  • Coolant level (frequent water housing leaks on 2.0 TFSI)
  • Metallic clicking noise when cold on the 3.0 V6 TFSI (2017-2018 models)
  • Functionality of the SOS emergency call module (roof antenna often faulty)
  • Absence of moisture under the rear seat (risk of burning out the Gateway module)

Comfort

  • Benchmark Finish Quality and Acoustic Comfort — The Audi Q5 II's interior is near perfection in terms of assembly and materials. Sound insulation is top-notch, especially with optional acoustic glazing, ensuring a serene and premium travel experience for all occupants.

Safety

  • Exemplary Passive Safety — The Audi Q5 II achieved the maximum rating in Euro NCAP safety tests, effectively protecting adults and children. Its recognition as an "IIHS Top Safety Pick+" further confirms its commitment to passenger protection.

Braking

  • Powerful and Stable Braking Performance — This premium SUV stands out with its excellent braking performance. It offers short stopping distances and great stability, ensuring perfect vehicle control even in emergency situations.

Handling

  • Precise Handling and Reassuring Dynamic Behavior — The Audi Q5 II's road behavior is characterized by its high stability and predictability. Precise steering and well-controlled body roll offer an excellent balance between comfort and agility, inspiring driver confidence.

Performance

  • Optimal Traction with quattro All-Wheel Drive — Audi's quattro technology ensures exceptional grip and flawless traction, regardless of road conditions. It guarantees increased safety and enhanced driving dynamics, even on slippery surfaces.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: BMW X3 (G01) (excellent dynamism and very reliable B48/B58 petrol engines), Mercedes-Benz GLC (X253/X254), Volvo XC60 II (very comfort-oriented and PHEV). - **North America**: Lexus RX / NX (absolute benchmark in reliability), Acura RDX, Porsche Macan (shares the MLB platform but sport-oriented). - **Asia / China**: BMW X3 xDrive, Mercedes GLC L (long-wheelbase version), NIO ES6 (local electric alternative).

Verdict

The Audi Q5 II (FY) is an extremely solid choice on the used car market, particularly with petrol (TFSI) engines. Audi has corrected past errors: the 2.0 TFSI has become one of the most reliable engines in its category, no longer suffering from excessive oil consumption. The SQ5's 3.0 V6 TFSI is a delight, but requires avoiding 2017-2018 model years or ensuring that the rocker arms have been inspected. Plug-in hybrid versions are fiscally relevant but add a layer of electronic complexity. For a worry-free purchase, opt for a post-2019 45 TFSI with a clear service history, especially regarding the S tronic gearbox.
