Hyundai Elantra

VII (CN7) · 2021 – 2025

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

Summary

The seventh-generation Hyundai Elantra (code name CN7) marks a major stylistic departure with its bold design dubbed "Parametric Dynamics", which earned it the title of *World Car Design of the Year* in 2021. Positioned in the highly competitive compact sedan segment, it moves upmarket by offering a rich technological equipment list and, for the first time, a true, highly efficient hybrid powertrain (HEV) to compete with the Toyota Corolla. The lineup is rounded out by classic internal combustion versions (MPI), a dynamic N Line version (1.6 T-GDI), and a formidable Elantra N sports version (2.0 T-GDI). Overall, the reliability of this generation is rated "Good" by the ADAC and sits in the solid average of the segment according to J.D. Power, backed by Hyundai's excellent manufacturer warranty.

Strengths 5

  • Bold and award-winning exterior design (World Car Design of the Year 2021)
  • Remarkable efficiency of the hybrid powertrain (1.6 GDI HEV)
  • Manufacturer warranty among the best on the market (up to 10 years/100,000 miles on the powertrain in the USA)
  • Generous standard technological equipment and driving aids (ADAS)
  • Sound road handling and highly accomplished N/N-Line versions

Weaknesses 4

  • Quality of some interior plastics in the lower part of the cabin
  • Rear headroom limited by the sloping roofline (fastback profile)
  • Soundproofing could be improved on the highway (road noise)
  • DCT-7 gearbox (on 1.6 T-GDI) sometimes hesitant in traffic jams

Check before buying

  • Recall history: it is imperative to check if the seat belt pretensioners have been replaced (multiple recalls in 2021-2022).
  • On the Hybrid (HEV) version: ensure that the recall concerning the EGR valve (potential short circuit) has been carried out.
  • On versions equipped with the IVT gearbox (2.0 MPI): check for the absence of whining or jerking during hard acceleration.
  • On 2021 models: check the sealing and mounting of the windshield (December 2021 recall).

Alternatives

* **North America / Asia:** Toyota Corolla (absolute benchmark in hybrid), Honda Civic (excellent dynamics, e:HEV version available), Mazda 3 (premium finish), Nissan Sentra. * **Europe:** The Elantra is not officially distributed in the majority of Western European countries (Hyundai favors the i30 there), but it is present in certain Eastern European markets competing against the Skoda Octavia or the Toyota Corolla Sedan.

Verdict

The Hyundai Elantra VII (CN7) is a highly recommendable compact sedan, particularly in its **1.6 GDI Hybrid (HEV)** engine. It offers a very serious alternative to the Toyota Corolla thanks to a more assertive design, a dual-clutch gearbox offering better drivability than competing e-CVTs, and equally frugal fuel consumption. Overall mechanical reliability is very good (rated "Good" by the ADAC and in the upper average by J.D. Power). However, the significant number of recalls (notably on seat belt pretensioners and electronics) requires scrupulously checking the vehicle's history before purchase. If the service book is up to date and the recalls have been carried out, it is a smart and long-lasting purchase.
