Hyundai i30

II (GD) (2012–2017) · 2012 – 2017

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

Summary

The **second-generation Hyundai i30 (Type GD)** marks a major turning point for the Korean manufacturer in Europe. Designed at the R&D center in Rüsselsheim, Germany, it was specifically conceived to compete with European benchmarks (VW Golf, Peugeot 308). It stands out with an impressive qualitative leap in interior finishes, improved soundproofing, and a very successful « Fluidic Sculpture » design.

A **facelift occurred in early 2015**, bringing a new hexagonal grille, equipment improvements, the introduction of the DCT-7 dual-clutch gearbox (replacing the old 6-speed torque converter automatic) and a dynamic i30 Turbo version.

**Diesel Focus :** The CRDi engines (U2 family) are the core of the range for this generation in Europe. They are renowned for their great robustness (timing chain) and frugality, although they are associated with a manual gearbox whose clutch can sometimes show signs of premature fatigue in intensive urban use.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent overall reliability, particularly for CRDi diesel engines (timing chain).
  • Significantly improved finish and assembly quality (ages very well).
  • Generous interior space and boot volume (378 liters in sedan, 528 liters in SW estate).
  • Very rich standard equipment (especially on Creative or Pack Premium trims).
  • Careful soundproofing on diesel versions.

Weaknesses 4

  • Electric power steering (Flex Steer) with a very artificial feel and vague around the center point.
  • High vehicle weight, penalizing dynamism and fuel consumption of small petrol engines.
  • DCT-7 dual-clutch gearbox (after 2015) sometimes hesitant and prone to wear in urban driving.
  • Aging and sometimes slow multimedia/GPS system (before the arrival of Apple CarPlay/Android Auto late in its career).

Check before buying

  • **Steering coupler (Rubber star) :** A knocking sound in the steering column when turning at a standstill or on degraded roads indicates wear of this small part (negligible part cost, but labor required).
  • **Dual-mass flywheel (1.6 CRDi manual) :** Test the clutch engagement point. Significant vibrations in the clutch pedal or at idle indicate a costly replacement.
  • **Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF) :** On diesels, check usage history. An engine light or jerks can indicate a DPF clogged by excessive urban use.
  • **LED daytime running lights :** The daytime LEDs tend to flicker then burn out. Replacement of the complete light unit is often necessary and expensive.
  • **Leather steering wheel :** The leather coating tends to peel prematurely on models before 2015.

Official recalls

  • - **November 2015 (DCT-7 Gearbox) :** Reprogramming of the dual-clutch gearbox control unit to correct traction losses or hesitations.
  • - **June 2016 (Bonnet Safety) :** Replacement or adjustment of the front bonnet latch, which could engage incorrectly and risk opening while driving.
  • - **2017 (Brake switch) :** Replacement of the rubber stopper on the brake pedal. Its deterioration could leave the brake lights permanently on and drain the battery.

Alternatives

- **Europe :** Volkswagen Golf VII (segment benchmark, superior finish but TDI diesels sometimes more temperamental), Peugeot 308 II (better chassis, but HDi diesels prone to AdBlue issues), Kia Cee'd II (its technical cousin, identical performance), Ford Focus III. - **Asia / Japan :** Honda Civic (exceptional mechanical reliability), Mazda 3 (very reliable naturally aspirated engines). - **North America :** Honda Civic, Toyota Corolla, Mazda 3 (the Elantra GT is positioned there as a European « hatchback » alternative).

Verdict

The second-generation Hyundai i30 is an **extremely rational and safe used car choice**, particularly with the 1.6 CRDi diesel engine. Unlike some European competitors of the same era, it suffers from very few chronic flaws. The U2 (CRDi) engines are true workhorses capable of handling high mileages if basic maintenance is respected. Prefer a manual gearbox version for total peace of mind, the DCT-7 gearbox requiring more vigilance. It's a trouble-free compact, ideal for high-mileage drivers looking for an unbeatable quality/price/reliability ratio.
