Summary

The **second-generation Nissan X-Trail (T31)**, launched in 2007, retains the cubic and pragmatic silhouette of its predecessor (T30) while being based on the Renault-Nissan Alliance C platform (shared with the Qashqai). It positions itself as a robust family SUV, offering an exceptional boot volume (up to 1,773 liters) with a washable floor much appreciated by outdoor enthusiasts. Unlike the trend of urban SUVs, the T31 retains real off-road capabilities thanks to its intelligent **All-Mode 4x4-i** all-wheel-drive system. A restyling (facelift) occurred in late 2010, modernizing the grille, lights (LED technology at the rear), and improving the perceived quality of the interior. Although European sales were dominated by the excellent 2.0 dCi (M9R) diesel, the petrol engines (MR20DE and QR25DE) shine with their great mechanical reliability, although they are often associated with a CVT gearbox (Jatco) which requires rigorous maintenance.

**Overall generation rating: 7.5/10**

Strengths 5

  • Remarkable reliability of petrol engine blocks (MR20DE and QR25DE) with timing chain
  • Giant boot volume and practical aspects (washable floor, underfloor drawers)
  • True off-road capabilities thanks to the All-Mode 4x4-i system (50/50 lock possible)
  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation significantly improved compared to the T30
  • Excellent peripheral visibility thanks to the cubic shape and large windows

Weaknesses 5

  • Chronic fragility of the continuously variable automatic transmission (Jatco JF011E CVT) if maintenance has been neglected
  • High fuel consumption on petrol versions (especially the 2.5L)
  • Hard interior plastics and very prone to scratches
  • Susceptibility to corrosion on the rear subframe and wheel arches (especially in mountainous areas)
  • Pronounced body roll in corners due to soft suspension

Check before buying

  • **CVT Gearbox History (Petrol):** Demand invoices for gearbox oil changes (every 60,000 km). Test the vehicle cold and hot: no high-pitched whistling, no jerks on startup should be tolerated.
  • **Cylinder Head (2.0 MR20DE Engine):** Check for any coolant or oil leaks around the spark plug wells (sign of a cracked cylinder head due to improper tightening).
  • **Corrosion (Underbody):** Inspect the rear subframe, suspension arms, and exhaust, particularly on vehicles that have driven on salted roads.
  • **Running Gear:** Listen for wheel bearings (dull noise from 70 km/h) and check front wishbone silentblocks (clunking over speed bumps).
  • **DPF (Diesel):** Check that the DPF warning light does not illuminate and monitor the oil level (a level that is too high indicates dilution by diesel due to failed regenerations).

Verdict

The Nissan X-Trail T31 with a petrol engine is an extremely relevant choice for buyers looking for a robust, off-road capable family SUV, and avoiding problems related to modern diesels (DPF, EGR). The 2.0 (MR20DE) and 2.5 (QR25DE) engines are models of mechanical reliability. **However, the purchase verdict depends entirely on the transmission.** If you find a manual gearbox version, it is a highly recommended purchase (BUY). If the vehicle is equipped with the automatic CVT gearbox, caution is advised (NEGOTIATE/AVOID): only buy if the owner can prove meticulous maintenance of the transmission (regular oil changes). Without this history, the risk of CVT failure (prohibitive replacement cost) negates the benefits of the petrol engine's reliability.
