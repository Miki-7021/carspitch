Summary

The first-generation Alfa Romeo Stelvio (Type 949) received a second facelift in late 2022 (often called Phase 2 or MY23) to boost its career. This update is aesthetically distinguished by the adoption of adaptive Full LED Matrix headlights with three modules (inspired by the Tonale and SZ) and smoked taillights. Inside, the major evolution is the arrival of a 100% digital 12.3-inch instrument cluster replacing the analog gauges, as well as software updates for the infotainment system and the addition of NFTs for maintenance tracking. Still based on the excellent Giorgio platform, the Stelvio remains the dynamic benchmark in its segment. In this Phase 2, reliability is at its peak: the early defects of the 2017-2019 models (fickle electronics, multimedia bugs, steering rack issues) have been eradicated. However, the model suffers from a complete lack of electrification (neither MHEV nor PHEV), which penalizes it against ecological penalties in some European countries, although its internal combustion engines, particularly the 2.2 Diesel, are now very mature.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional road handling (ultra-direct steering, incisive chassis)
  • Excellent overall reliability for this Phase 2 (model maturity)
  • Responsive and perfectly geared ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox
  • Timeless design enhanced by the new Matrix LED headlights
  • Retained physical ergonomics (real climate control buttons)
  • Very reasonable fuel consumption of the 2.2 Diesel on the highway

Weaknesses 5

  • Complete absence of mild-hybrid or plug-in hybrid technology
  • Very penalizing ecological penalty in France (especially for petrol engines)
  • Infotainment system still slightly behind German standards (screen size, rearview camera resolution)
  • Firm comfort at low speeds (especially with 20 or 21-inch wheels)
  • Inherent fragility of AdBlue systems on modern diesels

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: Alfa engines (especially the MultiAir petrol and Diesel with DPF) do not tolerate overdue oil changes.
  • Oil level on the 2.2 Diesel: a level above maximum indicates dilution by diesel fuel (aborted DPF regenerations).
  • AdBlue system operation: absence of engine light, history of urea refills.
  • Tire condition and geometry: the very incisive front axle can lead to asymmetrical tire wear if the alignment is out of adjustment.
  • Condensation in the new Matrix LED headlight units.

Verdict

The Alfa Romeo Stelvio Phase 2 (2022+) is a highly recommendable purchase for driving enthusiasts. Having reached full maturity, it has shed the electronic quirks of its early days. The **2.2 Diesel** engine is an excellent choice for high-mileage drivers: it is torquey, economical, and mechanically very reliable, provided that oil changes are scrupulously respected and exclusively urban use, which clogs the DPF and damages the AdBlue system, is avoided. If you drive less than 20,000 km per year or do a lot of city driving, you should definitely opt for the **2.0 Turbo petrol**, which is remarkably reliable, although you will have to accept higher fuel consumption and a potential ecological penalty. In summary: a characterful SUV, now as reliable as its German competitors, or even more so in certain mechanical aspects.
