Ford EcoSport

II (B515) · 2017 – 2022

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The "second generation" Ford EcoSport (often referred to as Phase 2 or Gen 2 in Europe) corresponds to the major facelift of the B515 model initially launched in 2012. Produced in Craiova (Romania) for the European market from 2017, it corrects the main flaws of the first version: the interior finish takes a leap forward (dashboard borrowed from the Fiesta Mk8), soundproofing is improved, and the backpack-style spare wheel becomes optional. Mechanically, the abandonment of the disastrous PowerShift dual-clutch gearbox in favor of a torque converter automatic gearbox (6F15) is excellent news. The introduction of the 1.5 EcoBlue diesel engine also makes it possible to offer an all-wheel drive system (Intelligent AWD), which is rare in this segment. Although it remains narrow and its side-hinged trunk door is impractical in the city, it becomes a robust and accomplished small urban SUV in this production phase.

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability of diesel engines (TDCi and EcoBlue) if used properly
  • Availability of a real all-wheel drive (AWD) system with the 1.5 EcoBlue
  • Abandonment of the PowerShift gearbox in favor of a real torque converter automatic gearbox
  • Very responsive and ergonomic SYNC 3 multimedia system (Apple CarPlay / Android Auto)
  • High driving position highly appreciated in the city

Weaknesses 5

  • Side-hinged trunk door (hinged on the left): a nightmare in urban parking
  • Disastrous reliability of the wet belt on the 1.0 EcoBoost petrol engine
  • Modest trunk volume for the category (356 liters)
  • Clumsy road handling compared to a Ford Puma or a Peugeot 2008
  • Fuel consumption of petrol versions (1.0 EcoBoost) a bit high

Check before buying

  • On diesels (TDCi/EcoBlue): check the trip history. Avoid models that have only been driven in the city (DPF/FAP/EGR risk).
  • On the 1.5 EcoBlue: check the oil level. A level above the maximum indicates dilution by diesel (failed DPF/FAP regenerations).
  • On the 1.0 EcoBoost: demand maintenance invoices proving the use of the correct oil. Open the oil filler cap to visually inspect the condition of the belt.
  • Trunk door hinges: check that it does not sag under its own weight (especially if it carries the spare wheel).
  • Running gear: premature wear of the front anti-roll bar silent blocks (clunking noises over bumps).

Verdict

The facelifted Ford EcoSport (2017-2022) is a paradoxical vehicle. If you are looking for a small **diesel** SUV, it is a very relevant choice: the 1.5 TDCi and 1.5 EcoBlue engines are robust, torquey, and the AWD version is a rarity in this segment. On the other hand, if you are aiming for a petrol version, caution is advised: the 1.0 EcoBoost suffers from the same issue as PSA's 1.2 PureTech (fragile wet belt). The abandonment of the PowerShift gearbox on this generation is a huge relief for reliability. Buy in diesel for high mileage drivers or in the mountains (AWD), avoid in 1.0 EcoBoost petrol without an impeccable maintenance history.
