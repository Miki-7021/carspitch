Summary

The third-generation Mercedes-Benz CLS, internal code C257, was launched in 2018, continuing the tradition of the "four-door coupé" initiated by its predecessors. Based on the E-Class (W213) platform, it stands out with a more fluid and refined design, incorporating the brand's modern stylistic codes. Positioned as a more stylish and exclusive alternative to the E-Class, it offers a blend of luxury, technology, and dynamism. The 2021 facelift brought minor aesthetic touches, new customization options, and technological updates. The CLS C257 is recognized for its ride comfort, material quality, and the excellence of its powertrains, particularly the inline six-cylinders, whether gasoline or diesel. Overall, it is a reliable vehicle for its category, but its technological complexity and high-end positioning imply potentially high maintenance and repair costs.

Strengths 6

  • Elegant and distinctive 4-door coupé design
  • Exceptional ride comfort, especially with air suspension
  • Quality of finish and interior materials
  • Very refined, powerful, and reliable 6-cylinder engines (OM656 and M256)
  • Advanced onboard technology (MBUX, driving aids)
  • Cabin sound insulation

Weaknesses 5

  • Limited rear visibility
  • Less generous rear passenger space than the E-Class, especially for headroom
  • High acquisition and maintenance costs
  • Complexity of electronic and 48V systems can lead to costly repairs
  • Decent trunk volume but limited access due to opening

Check before buying

  • 48V system (EQ Boost) operation: no warning lights, smooth start
  • AdBlue system status on diesels: no error messages, sufficient level
  • Air suspension operation (if equipped): no sagging, no noises
  • Onboard electronics: MBUX, driving aids, electric seats, ambient lighting
  • Complete and up-to-date service history at Mercedes or a specialist
  • Tire and brake wear, especially on AMG or 4MATIC versions

Verdict

The Mercedes CLS C257 is a highly accomplished luxury vehicle, offering a distinctive design, first-class comfort, and powerful, refined engines. Its reliability is generally good for a vehicle of this complexity, particularly with the excellent inline six-cylinder engines (OM656 diesel and M256 gasoline).

**For diesel engines**, the **CLS 400 d (OM656)** is the preferred choice for its power, smoothness, and proven reliability, ideal for long journeys. The **CLS 300 d (OM654)** is an excellent compromise for more mixed use, but be vigilant about the AdBlue system if you make many short trips.

**For gasoline engines**, the **CLS 450 (M256)** is a wise choice for its refinement and performance, with reliability comparable to the 6-cylinder diesels. The AMG CLS 53 offers sporty sensations without sacrificing the reliability of the M256.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize a model with a complete and transparent service history, ideally from Mercedes. Check the proper functioning of all electronic systems and the 48V system. Maintenance and repair costs can be high, so it is advisable to budget accordingly. If these conditions are met, the CLS C257 is an excellent purchase for anyone seeking a luxurious and high-performance coupé sedan.
