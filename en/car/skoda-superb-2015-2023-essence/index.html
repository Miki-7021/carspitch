Skoda Superb

III (3V) · 2015 – 2023

Summary

The **third-generation Skoda Superb (Type 3V)**, launched in 2015, is based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MQB platform. As the Czech brand's flagship, it has established itself as the absolute benchmark in its segment for spaciousness (especially in the rear seats) and boot volume.

A **facelift occurred in mid-2019**, bringing aesthetic updates, the introduction of Matrix LED headlights, an infotainment system update (MIB3), and, most notably, the arrival of the **iV** plug-in hybrid version.

Although the European market has historically favored diesel in this segment, the Superb III benefited from an excellent range of petrol (TSI) engines from the EA211 and EA888 Gen 3 families, which proved to be particularly reliable and powerful, erasing the severe oil consumption issues of the previous generation. It is a stately, rational, and extremely accomplished road car.

Strengths 6

  • Rear legroom worthy of a limousine
  • Gigantic boot volume (625L in sedan, 660L in Combi)
  • Overall reliability of petrol engines (TSI EA211 and EA888 Gen 3)
  • Finish quality and material aging
  • Unbeatable price/performance/spaciousness ratio
  • Royal comfort, especially with DCC adaptive suspension

Weaknesses 5

  • Fragile dry-clutch DSG7 (DQ200) gearbox on smaller engines (1.4, 1.5, 1.8 TSI, 1.6 TDI)
  • Frequent electronic bugs on the MIB3 multimedia system (post-2019)
  • DCC adaptive dampers expensive to replace and prone to leaks around 120,000 km
  • Capricious AdBlue system on diesel engines
  • Road noise insulation could be improved at high speeds

Check before buying

  • DSG gearbox service history: avoid any wet-clutch model (2.0 TSI/TDI) without proof of oil change every 60,000 km.
  • Coolant level: a drop often indicates a leak from the water pump or thermostat housing (a common issue with TSI and TDI engines).
  • DQ200 gearbox behavior (1.4, 1.5, 1.8 TSI): no juddering, vibration, or metallic noise should be felt when shifting odd gears (1-3).
  • DCC suspension: visually inspect the dampers for oil leaks.
  • Panoramic sunroof: check for absence of creaking and proper drainage of water channels (risk of cabin infiltration).

Braking

  • Ultra-Performing Braking — Equipped with an excellent braking system, the Superb III ensures remarkably short stopping distances, with measured performance of 100 to 0 km/h in just 35.1 to 35.5 meters, for total control in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Serene and Stable Road Handling — Its balanced chassis offers very safe and predictable road handling, providing great confidence behind the wheel, especially appreciated on long motorway journeys thanks to impeccable stability.

Practicality

  • Unrivaled Cabin Space and Boot Volume — Enjoy limousine-like rear legroom and a gigantic boot volume (up to 625L in sedan and 660L in Combi), making the Superb III the absolute benchmark for comfort and luggage transport.

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability of Petrol Engines — The TSI petrol engines (EA211 and EA888 Gen 3) of the Superb III are renowned for their robustness and longevity, offering reliable performance and long-term peace of mind.

Comfort

  • Exceptional Travel Comfort — The Superb III offers royal comfort to its occupants, enhanced by meticulous finish quality and, for equipped versions, the DCC adaptive suspension that transforms every journey into a most pleasant experience.

Economy

  • Unbeatable Value for Money — The Skoda Superb III stands out with an exceptional price/performance/spaciousness ratio, offering superior category features at a very competitive acquisition and running cost, especially with the 1.4 and 1.5 TSI engines.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Volkswagen Passat B8 (its technical cousin, a bit more premium but less spacious), Peugeot 508 II (more dynamic but cramped), Mazda 6 (excellent reliability, naturally aspirated engines), Toyota Camry (ultra-reliable hybrid). - **North America:** The Superb is not sold there. Alternatives include the Honda Accord, Toyota Camry, or VW Arteon. - **China / Asia:** Volkswagen Magotan (local version of the Passat), Honda Inspire, Toyota Avalon.

Verdict

The Skoda Superb III is one of the smartest road cars on the market. In **petrol**, it particularly shines. The **1.4 TSI 150** or **1.5 TSI 150** are more than sufficient for daily use and offer surprising fuel efficiency. However, the prize for driving pleasure and reliability goes to the **2.0 TSI (190 or 280 hp)**, paired with the robust wet-clutch DSG gearboxes.

**To avoid:** The 1.8 TSI, not for the engine itself, but because it is detuned and associated with the fragile dry-clutch DSG7 (DQ200) gearbox, which poorly handles the car's weight in urban use. Diesel versions remain relevant for very high mileage drivers, provided potential AdBlue issues are budgeted for. For a used car, prioritize a post-2019 (facelift) 2.0 TSI or 1.5 TSI model with a clear service history.
