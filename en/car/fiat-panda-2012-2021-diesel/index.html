Fiat Panda

III (319) · 2012 – 2021

BUY Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The third-generation Fiat Panda (Type 319) is an iconic city car that focuses on practicality, spaciousness, and a rounded design ('Squircle'). Launched in 2012, it has retained the spirit of its predecessors while improving comfort and safety. Although its primary purpose is urban, it has been offered in popular adventurous versions (Trekking, 4x4, Cross) that make it a true Swiss Army knife. **Expert's Note:** Although the request here concerns the diesel (1.3 MultiJet), it is crucial to remember that a diesel engine in a micro-city car is nonsensical for strictly urban use, leading to costly breakdowns. The Panda shines above all with its simple gasoline engines (1.2 Fire).

Strengths 5

  • 4x4 and Cross versions with surprising off-road capabilities
  • Exceptional reliability and negligible maintenance cost of the 1.2 Fire engine
  • Excellent interior space and modularity for its size (3.65 m)
  • Raised driving position and good visibility
  • Very durable 1.3 MultiJet engine if used outside urban areas

Weaknesses 5

  • Diesel engine (1.3 MultiJet) unsuitable for city driving (DPF, EGR, chain)
  • Light sound insulation on the highway (especially with TwinAir and Diesel)
  • Euro NCAP rating degraded over the years (0 stars in 2018 with the new rating system, due to lack of active aids)
  • Disappointing fuel consumption of the 0.9 TwinAir in real-world conditions
  • Basic interior finish and hard plastics prone to scratches

Check before buying

  • **On the 1.3 MultiJet:** Oil change history (crucial for the timing chain) and DPF status (engine light, sputtering).
  • **On the 1.3 MultiJet:** Presence of 'mayonnaise' or excessively high oil level (sign of dilution by diesel fuel).
  • **On 4x4 versions:** Condition of the longitudinal driveshaft and rear differential (bearing noises, vibrations).
  • **On the 0.9 TwinAir:** Smooth operation without misfires (otherwise, UniAir module is faulty, repair > €1000).
  • **General:** Premature wear of front shock absorbers and suspension wishbone bushings.

Official recalls

  • - **2014 (TwinAir):** Risk of short circuit on the ignition harness requiring the installation of a protective sleeve.
  • - **2016 (General):** Recall for a locking defect of the rear seat belt buckles.
  • - **2018 (General):** Inspection and potential replacement of the steering column due to a risk of loss of control.
  • - **2019 (1.3 MultiJet):** Engine mapping update to correct non-compliant NOx emissions under certain conditions.

Verdict

The Fiat Panda III is an excellent small car, full of common sense. **Regarding the 1.3 MultiJet diesel:** it's a double-edged sword. If you live in the mountains, the countryside, or drive more than 20,000 km/year on the road, it's an indestructible and torquey engine, perfect for the 4x4 version. However, if you buy a 1.3 MultiJet for city driving, run away: DPF, EGR, and timing chain failures will ruin you. For 80% of buyers, the modest but indestructible **1.2 Fire 69 hp** remains the absolute best buy for its flawless reliability and low running costs.
