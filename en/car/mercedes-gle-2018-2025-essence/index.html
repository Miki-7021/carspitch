Mercedes GLE

V167 (2018–2025) · 2018–2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The Mercedes GLE (V167) represents the fourth generation of the luxury SUV from the star brand, launched in 2018 to succeed the W166. It positions itself as a direct competitor to the BMW X5, Audi Q7, and Porsche Cayenne. The V167 stands out with a more modern and aerodynamic design, a significantly improved interior with the integration of the MBUX dual-screen infotainment system, and a range of electrified powertrains (48V mild-hybrid and plug-in hybrids). It offers exceptional ride comfort, especially with Airmatic air suspension or E-Active Body Control, and state-of-the-art onboard technology. A facelift was introduced in 2023, bringing aesthetic updates, technological improvements, and slightly revised engines, all now electrified (MHEV or PHEV). Overall, the GLE V167 is a very accomplished vehicle, but its technological complexity can sometimes lead to costly breakdowns.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with Airmatic/E-Active Body Control)
  • High-end finish quality and interior materials
  • Advanced onboard technology (MBUX, driving aids)
  • Range of powerful and efficient engines (6-cylinder diesel)
  • Generous interior space and 7-seat option
  • High towing capacity

Weaknesses 6

  • Complexity and reliability of the 48V Mild-Hybrid system (starter-alternator, battery)
  • High maintenance and repair costs, especially for AMG and PHEV versions
  • High vehicle weight, impacting fuel consumption and consumable wear
  • Some MBUX software bugs on early model years
  • Potential long-term reliability of Airmatic air suspension
  • High purchase price new and used

Check before buying

  • **Complete and up-to-date service history from Mercedes or a specialist.**
  • **48V EQ Boost system operation:** Smooth start-up, absence of error messages related to the battery or starter-alternator. An electronic diagnostic is highly recommended.
  • **Airmatic air suspension:** Check for air leaks, maintenance of vehicle ride height when stationary, and proper functioning of height adjustments.
  • **MBUX system:** Test all functions (navigation, radio, connectivity, voice commands) to detect slowness or freezes.
  • **AdBlue system (for diesels):** Check for absence of error messages related to the SCR system.
  • **Brake and tire condition:** Rapid wear due to weight and power, especially on AMG versions.
  • **Plug-in hybrid system (PHEV) operation:** Check actual electric range, proper charging function, and absence of error codes related to the high-voltage battery.
  • **Presence of unperformed manufacturer recalls.**

Verdict

The Mercedes GLE V167 is a highly desirable luxury SUV, offering exceptional comfort, cutting-edge technology, and powerful engines. However, its technological complexity, particularly the 48V Mild-Hybrid system and plug-in hybrid versions, can be a source of costly breakdowns.

**For a confident purchase:**

* **Prioritize the 6-cylinder diesel engines (OM656)**, which have proven to be the most robust and efficient for high-mileage drivers. The GLE 400 d or 450 d is an excellent choice. * **If gasoline is imperative, the GLE 450 (M256)** offers excellent driving pleasure, but be extremely vigilant about the history of the 48V system. A thorough diagnostic is essential. * **PHEV versions** are interesting for tax benefits and urban use, but their long-term reliability is still uncertain, and battery replacement costs are prohibitive. Only consider with an extended warranty. * **AMG versions** are performance beasts, but their maintenance costs and fuel consumption are very high. Reserved for informed enthusiasts.

**Advice:** Demand a complete and transparent service history, ideally from Mercedes. A full electronic diagnostic before purchase is non-negotiable. Verify that all manufacturer recalls have been performed. An extended warranty is strongly recommended, especially for models equipped with the 48V system or PHEVs. Negotiate the price based on the condition of the 48V system and the service history.
