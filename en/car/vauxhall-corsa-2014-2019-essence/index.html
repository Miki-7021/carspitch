Summary

The Vauxhall/Opel Corsa E represents the fifth generation of the popular city car. Although technically a deep evolution (major facelift) of the Corsa D's SCCS platform, it brings significant improvements: a redesigned suspension, recalibrated steering, a modernized interior with the IntelliLink infotainment system, and the introduction of the all-new 3-cylinder 1.0 Turbo direct injection engine. Positioned as a rational and economical alternative to the Ford Fiesta or VW Polo, it stands out for its price/equipment ratio. Overall reliability is good, particularly for the naturally aspirated petrol engines, which benefit from blocks proven over many years.

Strengths 5

  • Very competitive price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Excellent reliability of naturally aspirated petrol engines (1.2 and 1.4)
  • Highly responsive IntelliLink R4.0 multimedia system (Apple CarPlay / Android Auto)
  • Significantly improved suspension comfort compared to the Corsa D
  • Reduced maintenance and insurance costs

Weaknesses 5

  • Aging platform (perceived heaviness in driving)
  • Rear passenger space and boot volume (285L) inferior to competitors
  • Notchy manual gearbox on some versions
  • Naturally aspirated engines (1.2/1.4) lacking dynamism on the motorway
  • Reliability of the M32 gearbox on powerful turbocharged versions

Check before buying

  • Gearbox (M32) : On 1.4T and 1.6T models, drive in 5th and 6th gear and listen for any whistling or movement of the gear lever during acceleration.
  • Timing chain : On 1.2, 1.4, and 1.3 CDTi models, listen to the engine when cold. A metallic rattling sound lasting more than 3 seconds indicates a worn tensioner.
  • PCV Valve (1.4T) : Remove the oil dipstick with the engine running. If a strong suction is felt with a whistling sound, the rocker cover needs to be replaced.
  • Electronics : Check that the IntelliLink touchscreen does not freeze and that Bluetooth connects correctly.
  • Steering : Turn the steering wheel from lock to lock while stationary to detect any cracking noises (steering column or strut top mounts).

Official recalls

  • Steering (2014-2015) : Risk of a steering rack component breaking. Replacement of the affected part.
  • Handbrake (2015) : The parking brake cable may detach, leading to a risk of the parked vehicle moving.
  • Bonnet (2018) : The secondary bonnet latch may not engage correctly, risking opening while driving.
  • Airbags (2019) : Curtain airbags may not deploy correctly in the event of a side impact (gas generator issue).
  • Emissions (1.3 CDTi) : Software recall to correct NOx emissions (compliance campaign).

What sets it apart 7

security

  • Top-tier Safety — The Vauxhall Corsa E achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult occupant protection and 86% for child occupant protection, ensuring peace of mind for all passengers.

road_handling

  • Safe and Predictable Road Handling — Enjoy a serene drive thanks to stable road handling and predictable on-road behavior. The Corsa E offers a feeling of safety and ease of control, ideal for city driving and daily commutes.

reliability

  • Proven Petrol Engine Reliability — The naturally aspirated petrol engines (1.2i and 1.4i) of the Corsa E are renowned for their exceptional robustness and proven design, offering lasting peace of mind and reduced maintenance costs.

comfort

  • Enhanced Suspension Comfort — The Corsa E stands out with significantly improved suspension comfort compared to the previous generation, effectively absorbing road imperfections for smoother and more pleasant journeys.

economy

  • Excellent Value and Controlled Costs — Benefit from a highly competitive price-to-equipment ratio in the used car market, combined with reduced maintenance and insurance costs, making the Corsa E an economical and rational choice.

  • Modern Connectivity with IntelliLink — Stay connected with ease thanks to the IntelliLink R4.0 multimedia system, compatible with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. Its responsive and intuitive interface transforms every journey into a connected and entertaining experience.

Performance

  • 1.0T Engine: Dynamism and Efficiency — The 1.0 Turbo engine offers excellent driving pleasure with its generous torque and remarkable fuel efficiency. It represents the ideal compromise for optimal versatility between city and highway driving.

Verdict

The Vauxhall/Opel Corsa E is an extremely rational used car choice. While it doesn't offer the driving thrill of a Ford Fiesta or the refinement of a VW Polo, it compensates with attractive prices and generous equipment. For maximum reliability, opt for the 1.4i petrol engine (90 hp): although a bit placid, it is almost indestructible if regularly serviced. The 1.0T is the best compromise for versatility but requires more rigorous monitoring. Avoid the 1.3 CDTi diesel unless you cover more than 20,000 km per year on motorways.
