Summary

The third-generation GMC Canyon (Gen III) marks a major break from its predecessor. Positioned as a premium midsize pickup, it shares its platform and mechanicals with the Chevrolet Colorado, but stands out with a more statutory design, luxurious trims (Denali), and extreme off-road capabilities (AT4X). Gone are the V6 and diesel: GMC has streamlined the lineup around a single, powerful 2.7L turbocharged 4-cylinder engine (L3B) in the "High Output" version. Although the vehicle is recent, its powertrain is already proven on its big brother, the Silverado. Early reliability feedback is generally positive regarding the mechanicals, although the model has faced several recalls related to assembly errors and early software bugs.

Strengths 5

  • Extremely torquey and high-performing 2.7L Turbo engine
  • Very robust L3B engine block (already proven reliable on the Silverado since 2019)
  • Significantly improved interior finish quality (especially Denali and AT4X)
  • Towing capacities among the best in the category
  • High-level road handling and soundproofing

Weaknesses 5

  • Disappearance of V6 and Diesel options (which were very reliable on Gen II)
  • Frequent electronic and software bugs on 2023 models
  • Fuel consumption remains high despite the switch to a 4-cylinder
  • Factory assembly errors leading to several recalls (bolts, fasteners)
  • High purchase price compared to the competition

Check before buying

  • Software update history (multimedia system and safety modules)
  • Verification that all safety recalls have been performed (especially hub and seatbelt bolts)
  • Operation of the front camera module (FCM) and automatic emergency braking (AEB)
  • Absence of LED headlight flickering when stationary (known issue on 2024 models)

Official recalls

The model has been subject to several early recalls, mainly related to assembly and electronics:

  • February 2026 *(Ref: 26V114000)*: Issue related to the owner's/service manual (affects several GM models).
  • December 2025 *(Ref: 25V856000)*: Airbags - On AT4X AEV versions (2024-2026), defective sensing diagnostic module (SDM) software may cause incorrect deployment of the passenger airbag.
  • September 2024 *(Ref: 24V703000)*: Seatbelts - On 2023 models, the front seatbelt buckle attachment bolt (left or right) may not have been tightened correctly.
  • September 2024 *(Ref: 24V673000)*: Lighting - On 2024 models, the headlights may flicker while driving or when stationary (federal non-compliance).
  • June 2024 *(Ref: 24V491000)*: Fuel system - The lock ring securing the fuel pump to the tank may not have been fully locked during assembly.
  • March 2024 *(Ref: 24V237000)*: Running gear - On 2023 models, the front wheel hub bolts may have been over-tightened and damaged during factory installation.
  • February 2024 *(Ref: 24V133000)*: Electronics/ADAS - The front camera module (FCM) may detect a false obstacle, inadvertently triggering the automatic emergency braking (AEB).

What sets it apart 10

Performance

  • Torque comparable to a V8 — The 2.7L turbo four-cylinder delivers torque worthy of a V8, giving you strong pulling power for towing and confident acceleration when loaded or climbing.

Reliability

  • Proven L3B engine reliability — The L3B block is overbuilt with a diesel-inspired design and has already been proven in the Silverado since 2019, so you benefit from a mechanically solid powertrain.
  • Late-2024 fixes address early flaws — Models from late 2024 and 2025 have corrected the factory assembly and software issues of early production, giving you a more dependable vehicle.

Practicality

  • Strong towing capacity — This Canyon ranks among the best in its class for towing, letting you haul heavy trailers and equipment with a midsize pickup.

Comfort

  • Refined interior finish — Interior quality is significantly improved, especially in Denali and AT4X trims, giving you a more premium cabin feel than the previous generation.
  • High-level road insulation — Road behavior and sound insulation are top-level, so daily driving and long highway trips stay quiet and composed for you and your passengers.
  • Smooth eight-speed automatic — The excellent eight-speed automatic transmission pairs with the turbo engine to deliver smooth, well-managed gear changes during normal driving and towing.

Handling

  • Extreme off-road AT4X capability — The AT4X trim offers extreme off-road capabilities, giving you the hardware and setup to tackle demanding terrain beyond standard midsize pickups.

Design

  • Premium positioning and design — The Canyon is positioned as a premium midsize pickup with a more status-oriented design than its Chevrolet Colorado platform twin, distinguishing it visually.

Safety

  • Recall verification protects buyers — For a 2023 model, requiring proof that all NHTSA recalls were performed at a dealership helps you avoid inherited safety and assembly defects.

Alternatives

  • Toyota Tacoma (Gen 4): The historic rival. Less powerful in its base versions, but legendary reputation for reliability (although the new hybrid generation has yet to prove itself).
  • Chevrolet Colorado (Gen 3): The technical twin of the Canyon. Cheaper, with more utilitarian trims and detuned engine versions available.
  • Ford Ranger (Gen 6): Excellent alternative with V6 engine options (Ranger Raptor) and highly advanced onboard technology.
  • Nissan Frontier: The "old school" alternative with a classic naturally aspirated V6, very reliable but technologically lagging behind.

Verdict

The third-generation GMC Canyon is an extremely competent midsize pickup. GM's choice to abandon the V6 and Diesel in favor of the 2.7L Turbo 4-cylinder (L3B) pays off in terms of performance: this engine, designed with the robustness of a diesel block, is very solid and has already proven itself on the Silverado. However, first-year production models (2023) and early 2024 models suffered from assembly issues (poorly tightened bolts, fasteners) and software bugs (cameras, phantom braking) that generated multiple recalls.

Buying advice: Favor a late 2024 or 2025 model where the early factory defects have been corrected. If you buy a 2023 model, demand proof that all NHTSA recalls (especially for the wheel hubs and seatbelts) have been performed at the dealership.
