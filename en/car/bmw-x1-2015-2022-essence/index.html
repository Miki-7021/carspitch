BMW X1

F48 · 2015 – 2022

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The **BMW X1 (F48)** marks a historic break for the Bavarian brand: it abandons rear-wheel drive in favor of the UKL2 front-wheel drive platform (shared with MINI and the 2 Series Active Tourer). This change in architecture allowed for maximizing interior space and trunk volume, transforming the X1 into a very pragmatic family SUV. Mechanically, it introduced the 'B' family of modular engines (B38, B48 for gasoline; B37, B47 for diesel), which proved to be significantly more reliable than the previous generation (N20/N47). A facelift (LCI) occurred in mid-2019, bringing aesthetic touches, an infotainment update, and the introduction of the DKG7 dual-clutch gearbox on some gasoline versions. Overall, the F48 is a mature and very reliable vehicle, especially with gasoline engines.

Strengths 5

  • Remarkable reliability of modular gasoline engines (B38 and B48)
  • Excellent interior space and trunk volume for the category (thanks to the transverse engine)
  • Significantly improved finish and assembly quality compared to the E84 generation
  • Ergonomics of the iDrive infotainment system (intuitive and responsive)
  • Dynamic road handling despite the switch to front-wheel drive

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect road noise insulation on the highway (especially with 19-inch wheels)
  • Firm damping, typical of BMW, accentuated by Runflat tires
  • Basic seats lacking support (prefer 'Advanced' / Sport seats)
  • High maintenance cost at official dealerships
  • Reliability marred by the EGR cooler scandal on diesel versions

Check before buying

  • Service history: prefer shorter oil change intervals (15,000 km / 1 year) rather than the LongLife program (30,000 km / 2 years).
  • Automatic gearbox: demand proof of oil change if the vehicle is approaching or exceeding 100,000 km.
  • Front axle: listen for dull noises over speed bumps (sign of worn wishbone bushings or engine mounts, especially on the 18i 3-cylinder).
  • Cooling (Gasoline): inspect the perimeter of the coolant reservoir for white traces (leak).
  • Recalls (Diesel): check via VIN if the vehicle is affected by the EGR cooler recall and if it has been addressed.

Verdict

The BMW X1 F48 is one of the most recommendable premium compact SUVs on the used car market, provided you choose the right engine. By abandoning the old N20/N47 engines for the modular 'B' generation, BMW drastically improved the reliability of its entry-level models. **Gasoline versions (18i and especially 20i/25i/28i) are exceptionally robust** and represent the best choice. The 2.0L 4-cylinder (B48) offers the perfect compromise between driving pleasure, performance, and reliability. Diesel versions should be avoided for low-mileage drivers due to chronic EGR cooler issues. A well-maintained gasoline model (with Aisin gearbox oil change performed) will take you very far without major problems.
