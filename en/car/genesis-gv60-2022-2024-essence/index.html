Genesis GV60

I (JW) · 2022 – 2024

NEGOTIATE Reliability 76/100 — bon

Summary

The Genesis GV60 (JW generation) is the first 100% electric compact SUV from the Hyundai group's premium division. Based on the excellent E-GMP modular platform (shared with the Hyundai Ioniq 5 and Kia EV6), it stands out with its bold design, luxurious interior integrating innovative technologies (Face Connect facial recognition, Crystal Sphere gear selector), and its 800V architecture allowing ultra-fast charging.

**Important clarification regarding the engine:** Although the request targets a "petrol" engine, **the Genesis GV60 is an exclusively electric vehicle (BEV)**. It has never been produced with a combustion engine (petrol or diesel) or as a hybrid version (the "GV60 hybrid" nomenclature anomaly in the NHTSA databases is a recognized administrative error). This sheet therefore focuses exhaustively on the electric powertrains equipped on this model.

Strengths 5

  • 800V architecture allowing a 10 to 80% charge in 18 minutes
  • Exceptional interior finish quality and top-tier soundproofing
  • Leading performances (especially in the Performance AWD version)
  • Innovative onboard technologies (Crystal Sphere, biometrics)
  • Generous manufacturer warranty (depending on markets, often 5 years vehicle / 8 years battery)

Weaknesses 5

  • Endemic ICCU problem (common to the E-GMP platform) that can cause a total breakdown
  • Trunk volume (432 liters) disappointing for its size
  • Very limited rear visibility (highly sloped rear window and spoiler bar)
  • High weight (over 2 tons) leading to rapid tire wear
  • Infotainment system ergonomics sometimes complex

Check before buying

  • Recall history: Demand proof of the ICCU (Integrated Charging Control Unit) update/replacement.
  • Driveshafts: On 2023 AWD models, verify that the recall for risk of breakage has been carried out.
  • 12V battery condition: Often damaged by discharge cycles linked to ICCU bugs.
  • Tire wear: Instant torque and weight prematurely wear out the tires, especially on the Performance version.
  • Dashboard screen operation: Check for the absence of cutouts or black screens (known software bug).

Verdict

The Genesis GV60 is an extremely refined premium electric SUV in terms of comfort, performance, and charging speed. However, its initial reliability was marred by the endemic ICCU problem (common to the Hyundai/Kia group) and serious early production defects (driveshafts). Buying used is recommended **only if the recall history is crystal clear**. If the ICCU and transmission fixes have been applied by the official network, the GV60 becomes an excellent, luxurious, and quirky choice.
