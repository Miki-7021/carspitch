Peugeot 504

· 1969 – 1983 (Introduction du V6 en 1974)

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The **Peugeot 504** is a monument of French automotive history. Elected Car of the Year in 1969, it built its legend on extraordinary mechanical robustness, earning it the nickname "Queen of Africa". Replacing the 404, it introduced independent rear wheels (on sedans and coupes/convertibles) offering an exceptional comfort/handling compromise for the time. The sublime Coupe and Convertible versions, designed and assembled by Pininfarina, welcomed the famous PRV V6 starting in 1974. Today, the 504 is a highly sought-after collector's vehicle (especially in Coupe/Convertible and Injection), whose main enemy is not the mechanics, but corrosion.

**Overall collector rating: 8.5/10**

Strengths 5

  • Legendary mechanical robustness (carbureted petrol and diesel engines)
  • Exceptional suspension comfort (independent rear wheels)
  • Timeless lines of the Coupe and Convertible versions (Pininfarina)
  • Availability of common wear parts (L'Aventure Peugeot and remanufactured parts)
  • Roominess and trunk volume (Sedan and Estate)

Weaknesses 5

  • Endemic and destructive corrosion (disease of the era)
  • Maintenance complexity of the Kugelfischer injection (XN2)
  • Very high fuel consumption of the PRV V6
  • Anemic performances and deafening noise of the Diesel versions
  • Prices of specific body parts (Coupe/Convertible) have become exorbitant

Check before buying

  • Corrosion: floors, rocker panels, wheel arches, shock absorber towers, windshield cowl, trunk floor
  • Torque tube (drivetrain): check for play and abnormal noises while driving
  • Kugelfischer injection: check hot/cold starting and absence of jerking
  • PRV V6: listen to the top end (ticking = camshaft wear on pre-1977 models)
  • Cooling system: scaled radiator, porous hoses (head gasket risk)
  • Interior: condition of upholstery and dashboard (plastics baked by the sun, impossible to find new)

Comfort

  • Exceptional Suspension Comfort — Thanks to its independent four-wheel suspension, particularly its independent rear wheels, the 504 offers remarkable ride comfort, absorbing road irregularities with unparalleled smoothness for its era.

tenue de route

  • Remarkable Road Holding and Active Safety — Renowned for its excellent road holding, even on difficult terrain, the 504 benefits from an independent four-wheel suspension (MacPherson at the front, trailing arms at the rear) which gives it stable and predictable dynamic behavior, contributing to high active safety for its time.

Safety

  • Advanced Braking for Optimal Safety — The 504's braking system was considered advanced for its time, incorporating front disc brakes (and sometimes rear) and a load-sensitive brake compensator, providing adequate braking power and good stability.

Practicality

  • Generous Interior Space and Practical Boot Volume — The Peugeot 504, particularly in its Sedan and Estate versions, offers generous interior space for its occupants and a substantial boot volume, making it ideal for family trips or luggage transport.

Performance

  • Timeless Design and Recognized Prestige — Voted Car of the Year in 1969, the 504 captivates with its elegant and timeless design, especially the sublime Coupé and Cabriolet versions designed by Pininfarina, which embody French prestige and refinement.

Economy

  • Easy Maintenance and Parts Availability — The availability of common wear parts, thanks to L'Aventure Peugeot and remanufacturing, ensures serene and economical maintenance of your 504, guaranteeing its longevity.

Verdict

The Peugeot 504 is a masterpiece of French engineering from the great era. As a collector's vehicle today, the choice of engine depends on your budget and skills. The carbureted versions (1.8 and 2.0) are the most recommendable for serene use thanks to their biblical simplicity. The injection (XN2) offers superior driving pleasure but requires a specialist in case of breakdown. The PRV V6, although noble and inseparable from the sublime Coupes/Convertibles, demands increased vigilance regarding its maintenance. **The purchase must be dictated 90% by the condition of the bodywork**: a 504's mechanics can be rebuilt easily and at a lower cost, a shell eaten away by rust is a financial bottomless pit.
