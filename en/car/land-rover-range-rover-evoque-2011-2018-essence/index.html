Land Rover Range Rover Evoque

I (L538) · 2011 – 2018

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The first-generation Land Rover Range Rover Evoque (codename L538) marked a historic turning point for the British brand. Derived from the LRX concept, it introduced a spectacular design that redefined the premium compact SUV segment. Available as a 5-door, Coupé (3-door), and even a Convertible (from 2016), it is based on a heavily modified Freelander 2 platform (Ford's EUCD).

Mechanically, its career is divided into two main eras: the era of Ford/PSA-sourced engines (until 2015) and the era of Jaguar Land Rover's 'in-house' Ingenium engines (from the 2015 facelift). While the design and road handling are praised, the Evoque's overall reliability is marred by recurring electronic problems, gearbox issues (the 9-speed ZF introduced in 2014), and major defects in the Ingenium diesel engines. Petrol versions (Si4), although fuel-thirsty, paradoxically prove to be the safest long-term choices.

Strengths 5

  • Timeless and spectacular design, still relevant
  • Interior presentation quality and premium materials
  • Genuine off-road capabilities (Terrain Response) for a compact SUV
  • Driving pleasure and sound insulation on Si4 petrol versions
  • Excellent resale value on the used car market

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of Ingenium diesel engines (post-2015)
  • Capricious onboard electronics (black screens, system bugs, door locks)
  • ZF 9-speed automatic gearbox (2014+) often harsh and hesitant without updates
  • Very limited rear passenger space and boot volume
  • Excessive fuel consumption of petrol engines (Si4 Ford)

Check before buying

  • Clear service history: avoid vehicles that followed the 'Long Life' interval of 34,000 km (especially for diesels).
  • Automatic gearbox behavior: no jerks should be felt during 1-2 and 2-3 shifts.
  • AWD system operation: check for absence of 'Traction Fault' error message (Haldex pump failure).
  • Door locks: test locking/unlocking of each door (lock motors frequently fail).
  • Oil level on Ingenium diesels: a level above maximum indicates dilution by diesel fuel.

Alternatives

In **Europe**, the Evoque competes with the Audi Q3 (more rigorous but more austere), the BMW X1 (more dynamic and spacious), and the Volvo XC40 (more modern and comfortable). In **North America**, it competes with the Lexus NX (unbeatable reliability) and the Porsche Macan (sportier and more expensive). In **Asian markets**, the Mercedes-Benz GLA is its main urban rival.

Verdict

The Range Rover Evoque I is an 'impulse buy' that requires great caution. If you are attracted by its design, **absolutely prioritize petrol engines (2.0 Si4 of Ford origin, pre-2017)**. Although they are fuel-thirsty, they will save you from astronomical bills related to Ingenium diesel engine failures. If you must opt for a diesel, look for a model before 2015 equipped with the older 2.2L block (PSA/Ford origin), which is much more robust. Avoid 2.0L Ingenium diesels (2015-2018) unless the engine has already been replaced and maintenance is performed every 10,000 km. In any case, plan an 'unexpected' budget for electronics and door locks.
