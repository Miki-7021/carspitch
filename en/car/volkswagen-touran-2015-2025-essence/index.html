Volkswagen Touran

III (5T) (2015–2025) · 2015–2025

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The third-generation Volkswagen Touran (Type 5T), launched in 2015, represents a significant evolution compared to its predecessors. Based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MQB platform, it shares many elements with the Golf VII and Passat B8, giving it an excellent technical foundation. Longer, wider, and lower than the previous generation, it offers increased interior space, especially for rear passengers and in 7-seat configuration. Its design, though classic and understated, is timeless and functional. The Touran 5T has benefited from regular updates over the years (Model Year changes) but has not undergone a major facelift, retaining its general lines. It positions itself as a family compact MPV, prioritizing practicality, modularity, and a perceived build quality superior to the average in its category. Overall, it is a reliable and well-designed vehicle with proven powertrains.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional modularity and spaciousness (up to 7 real seats)
  • Good build quality and interior finish
  • Safe, comfortable, and balanced road handling (MQB platform)
  • Wide choice of reliable and efficient engines (especially TSI)
  • Good resale value on the used car market
  • Modern technological equipment and driving aids (depending on trim and year)

Weaknesses 5

  • Understated design, lacks flair for some
  • High purchase price new and used
  • Maintenance cost for diesel versions (AdBlue, FAP, EGR) and the DSG gearbox
  • The 1.0 TSI might be underpowered for a loaded family use or on the highway
  • Some isolated electronic issues (multimedia system, sensors)

Check before buying

  • Complete and transparent service history (logbook, invoices)
  • DSG gearbox operation (smooth gear changes, no jerks, oil changes performed if applicable)
  • Condition of the FAP (recent diesel and petrol) and AdBlue system (diesel: level, sensors, pump)
  • Abnormal engine noises (timing chain, injectors, turbo)
  • Functioning of on-board electronics (air conditioning, multimedia, driving aids, power windows)
  • Tire and brake wear, especially on heavy or heavily loaded models

Verdict

The Volkswagen Touran III (5T) is an excellent choice for families looking for a reliable, spacious, and well-finished compact MPV. Its MQB platform gives it undeniable road qualities and a good basis for reliability. The TSI petrol engines, particularly the 1.4 TSI and 1.5 TSI, offer an excellent compromise between performance and fuel consumption, and are generally very reliable. The 2.0 TDI diesel versions are also robust but require more rigorous maintenance and are better suited for high-mileage drivers. Buying a Touran III is recommended, provided the service history is checked, especially for models equipped with the DSG gearbox (oil changes are imperative for wet-clutch versions). Opt for well-equipped versions to fully enjoy its advantages. It is a safe investment that will retain good value on the used car market.
