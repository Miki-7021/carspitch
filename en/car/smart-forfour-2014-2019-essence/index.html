Smart Forfour

W453 · 2014 – 2019

BUY Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The **second-generation Smart Forfour (W453)** marks a major turning point for the brand. Developed in close partnership with Renault (it is the technical twin of the Twingo III), it abandons the architecture of the first Forfour (based on the Mitsubishi Colt) to adopt a **rear-engine, rear-wheel-drive** configuration. This technical choice gives it an exceptional turning radius, ideal for urban use. Available exclusively as a 5-door, it offers decent roominess for its ultra-compact size (3.49 m). In 2019, Smart facelifted the model and abandoned combustion engines to go 100% electric (EQ models), marking the end of production for the gasoline versions covered here. Overall, it is an endearing, highly maneuverable city car, but its firm comfort and small trunk limit its versatility.

Strengths 5

  • Unbeatable turning radius (8.65 m curb-to-curb) thanks to the rear engine
  • Twinamic automatic gearbox (dual-clutch) infinitely superior to the old automated manual gearbox (Softouch)
  • Original, customizable design and cheerful interior presentation
  • Front seat roominess and feeling of space
  • Overall reliability of the Renault-sourced engine blocks (no major problem engines like the old PureTech or TSI)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very limited trunk volume (185 liters) and high loading sill (engine underneath)
  • Heat generated by the engine in the trunk (watch out for frozen groceries)
  • Very firm suspension comfort, especially with 16 or 17-inch wheels
  • Sensitivity to crosswinds on the highway (despite the standard assistant)
  • Fuel consumption of the 0.9 Turbo in the city sometimes disappointing (often > 7.5 L/100 km)

Check before buying

  • **Twinamic gearbox:** Test drive the car at very low speeds and in reverse. Violent jerking or excessive slipping indicates a need for reprogramming or clutch wear.
  • **Front axle:** Listen for noises (clunks) over speed bumps. The stabilizer bar links and wishbone bushings wear out prematurely in intensive urban use.
  • **Multimedia system:** The Smart Media system (derived from Renault's R-Link) can suffer from bugs, black screens, or untimely reboots. Check that the touchscreen responds well.
  • **Air conditioning:** The air conditioning condenser, highly exposed at the front, is vulnerable to stone chips. Check that the AC produces cold air quickly.
  • **Cooling (0.9 Turbo):** Inspect the expansion tank at the front (under the plastic hood) to detect any drop in level (sign of a water pump leak).

Verdict

The Smart Forfour (W453) is a purebred city car that excels in its natural environment: the city. Its rear-engine architecture gives it unparalleled maneuverability. In terms of reliability, the assessment is very positive. The alliance with Renault has allowed the integration of proven engines (SCe and TCe) that do not suffer from major flaws, unlike some competitors (no problematic wet belt here, the engines are chain-driven).

**Buying advice:** Favor the **1.0 71 hp** engine if you never leave the city; it is the most reliable and the smoothest. If you regularly use expressways, the **0.9 Turbo 90 hp** is essential, but check the maintenance history (water pump). In all cases, the **Twinamic** automatic gearbox is highly recommended for urban driving pleasure, provided it has been well maintained and updated.
