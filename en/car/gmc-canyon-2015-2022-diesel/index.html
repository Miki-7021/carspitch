GMC Canyon

II (AT2) · 2015 – 2022

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The second-generation GMC Canyon (code name AT2) marks General Motors' return to the midsize pickup segment. A technical twin to the Chevrolet Colorado, the Canyon distinguishes itself with a more premium positioning, a design inspired by its big brother the Sierra, and superior trims (notably the luxurious Denali version). Its major asset in this North American segment is the introduction of a 4-cylinder diesel engine (2.8L Duramax), offering exceptional towing capacities and rare fuel efficiency for the category. According to data from J.D. Power and Consumer Reports, it ranks among the best in its class in terms of long-term reliability.

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability above the category average (J.D. Power / Consumer Reports)
  • Exceptionally robust and durable 2.8L Duramax diesel engine
  • Impressive towing capacities for a midsize pickup (up to 3,490 kg with the diesel)
  • Very robust Hydra-Matic 8-speed automatic gearbox (on V6 LGX)
  • Interior finish and soundproofing superior to its twin, the Chevrolet Colorado

Weaknesses 3

  • Numerous early recalls on the 2015 model year (steering, airbags, brakes)
  • 2.5L petrol engine a bit weak under load
  • High used purchase price, particularly for the diesel and Denali versions

Check before buying

  • On the 2.8L Diesel: condition of the accessory belt and absence of seepage around the injectors
  • On 2015 models: check if the electric power steering recall has been performed (possible loss of assistance)
  • On 2015 models: inspect the front brake calipers (risk of brake fluid leak)
  • Oil change history on the V6 petrol engines (crucial for the timing chain)

Alternatives

- **Chevrolet Colorado (Gen II)**: Its technical twin, offering exactly the same engines (including the 2.8L Duramax) but with a slightly more utilitarian finish and a lower price. - **Toyota Tacoma (Gen III)**: The market leader. According to TÜV/J.D. Power data, it is paradoxically considered *less reliable but more powerful* than the Canyon on this specific generation, although it retains an unbeatable resale value. - **Ford Ranger (T6)**: Returning to North America in 2019, it offers an excellent 2.3L EcoBoost engine but does not offer a diesel in this market. - **Nissan Frontier (D40 / D41)**: A more rustic and aging alternative (until 2021), but very affordable.

Verdict

The second-generation GMC Canyon is an excellent choice in the midsize pickup market. Reliability data (ADAC, TÜV, J.D. Power) places it above its category average. The **2.8L Duramax diesel** engine is the true star of the lineup: it offers exceptional longevity, controlled fuel consumption, and top-tier towing capacities, provided the accessory belt and injector seals are monitored. The 3.6L V6 (especially the post-2017 LGX) is also an extremely solid choice. However, it is strongly advised to **avoid the 2015 model year**, which suffered early production teething problems with numerous recalls (steering, brakes, airbags). From 2016 onwards, and particularly after 2017, it is a highly recommended purchase.
