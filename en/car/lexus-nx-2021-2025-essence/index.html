BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

All Lexus models

Summary

The second-generation Lexus NX (code name AZ20), launched in 2021, marks a major evolution for the Japanese brand's premium compact SUV. Based on the TNGA-K modular platform (shared with the Toyota RAV4), it gains in rigidity, driving dynamics, and interior space. Above all, this generation marks the abandonment of the much-criticized touchpad in favor of the new "Lexus Interface" multimedia system with a large touchscreen (up to 14 inches). Although the European market is heavily dominated by hybrid (350h) and plug-in hybrid (450h+) versions, the NX offers highly refined 100% internal combustion (gasoline) engines in other markets (North America, Asia, Eastern Europe), ranging from a naturally aspirated 4-cylinder to the all-new 2.4L Turbo. True to Lexus's reputation, overall reliability is exceptional, with problems mainly limited to early software bugs on the new infotainment system.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall mechanical reliability (both ICE and hybrid)
  • Quality of finish and assembly (interior materials, soundproofing)
  • New infotainment system that is finally modern and touch-sensitive
  • Indestructible naturally aspirated gasoline engines (200/250) thanks to D-4S injection
  • Very high resale value on the used market

Weaknesses 4

  • Naturally aspirated gasoline engines (200/250) lack character and are noisy under hard acceleration
  • Early electronic and software bugs (unusual for Lexus, but fixed via OTA)
  • Run-Flat tires (depending on trim) slightly degrade ride comfort
  • Zero availability of 100% gasoline versions in Western Europe

Check before buying

  • Software update history: Crucial for the stability of the center screen and driving aids.
  • 12V battery: Tends to discharge quickly if the vehicle remains parked for several weeks (a common problem with highly connected vehicles).
  • Surround cameras: Check for the absence of condensation in the lenses and proper display on the screen (subject to recalls).
  • Front brake caliper bolts: Verify that the tightening recall has been performed (2024-2025 models).

What sets it apart 14

Reliability

  • Legendary Mechanical Reliability — The Lexus NX II, especially with its petrol engines, embodies the brand's legendary reliability. Its atmospheric engines (NX 200/250) are renowned for their exceptional robustness and longevity, thanks to technologies like D-4S injection that prevents carbon buildup. The turbo engine (NX 350) also offers remarkable solidity, ensuring long-term peace of mind.

Comfort

  • Premium Comfort and Finish — Immerse yourself in a cabin where the quality of finish and assembly is impeccable. The Lexus NX II offers refined interior materials and meticulous soundproofing, creating an atmosphere of serenity and well-being on board, even on long journeys.

Safety

  • Advanced Active and Passive Safety — The Lexus NX II is a benchmark for safety, as evidenced by its excellent crash test results. It achieved the maximum 5-star rating from Euro NCAP, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 87% for child protection, and was designated an IIHS Top Safety Pick+.
  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Enjoy absolute confidence thanks to a remarkably efficient braking system. The Lexus NX II offers excellent, consistent, and easy-to-modulate braking performance, ensuring powerful and safe stops in all circumstances.
  • Stable and Serene Road Handling — The Lexus NX II's road behavior is designed for serenity. It offers stable and predictable handling, excellent absorption of road irregularities, and precise steering, guaranteeing a safe and comfortable drive.

Practicality

  • Modern and Intuitive Infotainment — Benefit from a first-class connected experience thanks to the new "Lexus Interface" infotainment system. Featuring a large touchscreen, it is finally modern, intuitive, and seamlessly integrates your favorite applications for simplified daily use.

Economy

  • Exceptional Resale Value — Investing in a Lexus NX II is a wise choice. Thanks to its reputation for reliability and perceived quality, this vehicle maintains a very high resale value on the used car market, thus protecting your investment.

Alternatives

  • Europe: BMW X3 (more dynamic), Audi Q5 (clinical finish), Mercedes GLC (comfort and technology), Volvo XC60 (design and high-performance PHEV).
  • North America: Acura RDX (excellent price/dynamics ratio), Genesis GV70 (luxury and bold design), Lincoln Corsair.
  • Asia / Emerging markets: Toyota Harrier / Venza (cheaper internal alternative), Infiniti QX50.

Verdict

The second-generation Lexus NX is a highly recommended purchase, regardless of the chosen engine. While the hybrid versions (350h/450h+) are the undisputed stars for their efficiency, the 100% gasoline versions (NX 200, 250, 350) offered outside Western Europe shine with their foolproof mechanical reliability. The naturally aspirated engines (2.0L and 2.5L) are true "tanks" thanks to their D-4S injection, while the 2.4L Turbo offers the driving pleasure that was missing from the previous generation. The only real issues with this model are concentrated on the onboard electronics (cameras, infotainment), which are largely addressed by official recalls and OTA updates. An absolute rational choice in the premium SUV category.
