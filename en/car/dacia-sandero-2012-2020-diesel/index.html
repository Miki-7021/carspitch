Summary

The Dacia Sandero II (sold under the Renault brand in some markets) is the archetype of the rational and economical versatile city car. Based on the M0 platform (derived from the Clio II/III), it offers an unbeatable price/space ratio. Restyled in late 2016 (new grille, LED daytime running lights, four-spoke steering wheel), it achieved phenomenal success, particularly in its adventurous Stepway version.

Diesel Focus (1.5 dCi - K9K): Although the market has gradually shifted from diesel to petrol in this segment, the 1.5 dCi in the Sandero II is a fully mature engine. Free from the serious injection issues (Delphi pumps) of the 2000s, it proves to be a real workhorse, extremely reliable if used according to its purpose (extra-urban driving).

Strengths 6

  • Excellent overall reliability, particularly for the mature 1.5 dCi
  • Among the lowest maintenance and insurance costs on the market
  • Extremely low 1.5 dCi fuel consumption (often below 4.5 L/100 km in real-world driving)
  • Cabin space and boot volume (320 liters) worthy of the segment above
  • Raised ground clearance (especially in Stepway), ideal for poor roads
  • Very low depreciation on the used car market

Weaknesses 5

  • Poor sound insulation (wind and road noise on the highway)
  • Interior plastic quality (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Dated ergonomics (MediaNav screen placed too low, poorly positioned window controls before restyling)
  • Safe but sluggish road handling, heavy hydraulic steering during maneuvers
  • Seats lacking lateral support and seat cushion thickness on long journeys

Check before buying

  • Left stalk (indicators/headlights/horn): wires break inside, leading to loss of horn or lights (very common problem).
  • MediaNav system: bugs, intermittent restarts, or frozen screen (often requires an update or replacement).
  • On 1.5 dCi: check trip history. A diesel used only in the city will have a clogged DPF and EGR valve (check engine light on).
  • On 0.9 TCe: pink/white traces around the water housing (coolant leak).
  • Running gear: premature wear of steering and suspension ball joints (often reported during technical inspection around 80,000 km).
  • Air conditioning: compressor sometimes noisy or refrigerant gas leak.

Official recalls

  • November 2014: Risk of axial steering rod breakage (models produced between September and December 2013).
  • May 2015: Incorrect assembly of front brake calipers potentially leading to brake fluid leak.
  • April 2018: Risk of short-circuit on the starter wiring harness rubbing against a hose (models produced until 2016).
  • July 2019: Replacement of the driver's airbag (deployment issue) on a series manufactured in 2018.
  • March 2021: On 1.5 Blue dCi versions, reprogramming required for an oxygen sensor (NOx) fault.

Economy

  • Record Fuel Economy — Benefit from extremely low real-world fuel consumption, often below 4.5 L/100 km, leading to substantial daily savings.
  • Unbeatable Maintenance and Insurance Costs — The Dacia Sandero II stands out with some of the lowest maintenance and insurance costs on the market, optimizing your budget.

Practicality

  • Generous Cabin and Boot Space — Enjoy a spacious interior and a 320-liter boot volume, worthy of a higher segment, for all your needs.

Safety

  • Proven Euro NCAP Safety — The Sandero II achieved 4 stars in Euro NCAP, with excellent protection for adults (80%) and children (79%).

Braking

  • Effective and Reassuring Braking — Its braking performance is adequate and safe for its category, with average stopping distances of 40-42 meters from 100 km/h.

Handling

  • Safe and Comfortable Road Handling — Experience stable, predictable, and comfort-oriented road handling, ensuring serene daily driving.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Renault Clio IV (shares the same engines but offers better finish and soundproofing), Peugeot 208 I (1.4/1.6 HDi also very fuel-efficient), Skoda Fabia III (1.4 TDI, superior finish), Ford Fiesta.
  • South America: Chevrolet Onix, Volkswagen Gol, Fiat Argo.
  • Russia/Eurasia: Lada Granta, Lada Vesta, Hyundai Solaris.

Verdict

The Dacia Sandero II is an absolutely rational purchase. If you are looking for a reliable, spacious, and inexpensive daily driver, it is unrivaled. The 1.5 dCi is particularly relevant for high-mileage drivers: this engine is indestructible if well-maintained and allows for massive fuel savings. However, avoid diesel if you only drive in the city (risk of costly clogging) and opt for the 0.9 TCe or 1.2 16v. The Stepway version offers a real aesthetic advantage and better resale value, although the tires are slightly more expensive.
