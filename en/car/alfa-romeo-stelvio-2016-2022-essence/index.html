Alfa Romeo Stelvio

I (949) · 2016 – 2022

NEGOTIATE Reliability 80/100 — bon

Summary

The Stelvio (Type 949) is the first SUV in Alfa Romeo's history. Based on the excellent Giorgio platform shared with the Giulia sedan, it immediately established itself as the dynamic benchmark in its segment, offering road handling worthy of a sports sedan, ultra-direct steering, and ideal weight distribution (50/50). While Phase 1 models (2016-2019) suffered from shortcomings in interior finish and an outdated infotainment system, the 2020 model year update corrected these issues with more upscale materials, a redesigned touchscreen, and the integration of Level 2 Advanced Driver-Assistance Systems (ADAS). In terms of reliability, the petrol engines (GME) are generally robust, although the onboard electronics experienced some early issues. Overall note: An enthusiast's SUV, demanding in terms of maintenance but rewarding to drive.

Weaknesses 6

  • Interior finish and materials are subpar on Phase 1 (2016-2019)
  • Slow and outdated infotainment system before 2020
  • Frequent electronic bugs (sensors, false dashboard alerts)
  • Large turning radius (tedious city maneuvers)
  • Sparse dealer network and sometimes inconsistent after-sales service
  • Faster depreciation than its German competitors

Check before buying

  • Condition and age of the battery (source of 80% of electronic bugs if weak)
  • Clear service history (especially for MultiAir module oil on petrol models)
  • Traces of coolant around the radiator and expansion tank (2.0 Turbo)
  • Asymmetrical tire wear (sensitive geometry, normal scrubbing at low speeds due to Ackermann steering geometry)
  • Smooth operation of the central screen and rotary knob
  • On the V6 Quadrifoglio: condition of the brakes (carbon-ceramic are prohibitively expensive to replace) and recall campaign history.

What sets it apart 12

Performance

  • Exhilarating Petrol Engine Performance — The 2.0 Turbo petrol engine delivers generous power and an captivating sound, ensuring sharp accelerations and unparalleled driving pleasure. The perfectly calibrated 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox provides smooth and responsive gear changes.

Handling

  • Agility and Sports Sedan-like Road Handling — Built on the excellent Giorgio platform, the Stelvio stands out with exceptional road handling, the most dynamic in its category. Its ultra-direct and precise steering, combined with an ideal weight distribution, offers agility and driving pleasure worthy of a sports sedan.

Braking

  • Powerful and Responsive Braking — Equipped with a remarkably efficient braking system, notably thanks to the Integrated Brake System (IBS), the Stelvio ensures a rapid response and short stopping distances, guaranteeing optimal safety in all circumstances.

Safety

  • Maximum Safety Certified by Euro NCAP — The Alfa Romeo Stelvio achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult (97%) and child (84%) protection, attesting to its first-class passive and active safety level.

Design

  • Timeless and Distinctive Italian Design — The Stelvio boasts an elegant and athletic exterior design, recognizable among all. Its fluid lines and sleek appearance give it a timeless character that is sure to turn heads.

Reliability

  • Proven Mechanical Reliability — The 2.0 Turbo petrol engine is renowned for its robustness and mechanical reliability. Designed to last, it offers daily peace of mind and consistent performance.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Porsche Macan (dynamic competitor benchmark), BMW X3 (more balanced, better finished), Audi Q5 (more comfortable, less sporty), Mercedes GLC, Jaguar F-Pace. - **North America**: BMW X3, Porsche Macan, Acura RDX, Genesis GV70. - **Asia / Japan**: Lexus NX (more reliable, hybrid, but less sporty), BMW X3.

Verdict

The Alfa Romeo Stelvio is a choice of passion that proves surprisingly rational with the petrol engine (2.0 Turbo 200 or 280 hp). Contrary to brand clichés, the mechanics are robust if maintenance is scrupulously respected. The main inconveniences come from the onboard electronics and a light finish on the first model years. For a worry-free purchase, favor a petrol model from the 2020 model year onwards (revised interior, touchscreen infotainment, better sound insulation) with a complete service history. Avoid examples with neglected oil changes or displaying "Christmas trees" on the dashboard (often linked to a weak battery, but sometimes to corroded BCM modules).
