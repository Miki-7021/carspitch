Kia Ceed

II (JD) · 2012 – 2018

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation Kia cee'd (code name JD) marks a major turning point for the Korean manufacturer in Europe. Designed in Frankfurt and assembled in Slovakia, it sheds the 'low-cost' image of its predecessor to directly compete with the Volkswagen Golf VII and Peugeot 308 II. It stands out with a dynamic design (by Peter Schreyer), significantly improved interior finish, and its famous 7-year warranty (now expired for this generation). The late 2015 facelift (MY2016) brought Euro 6 engines, the new 3-cylinder 1.0 T-GDI, a DCT7 dual-clutch gearbox for the diesel, and improved soundproofing. In the used car market, it is a rational and extremely reliable choice, particularly with the diesel engine (U2 engine family), which proves to be one of the most robust in its category.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of CRDi diesel engines (robust timing chain, durable injectors)
  • Significantly improved interior finish quality compared to the previous generation
  • Generous interior space and boot volume (380L in hatchback, 528L in SW estate)
  • Sound and safe road handling (multi-link rear suspension as standard)
  • Overall controlled maintenance cost (outside manufacturer network)

Weaknesses 5

  • DCT7 dual-clutch gearbox (on 1.6 CRDi 136) prone to juddering and premature wear
  • LED daytime running lights (DRL) that flicker or burn out (printed circuit board design flaw, headlight replacement often necessary)
  • Clunking in the steering (wear of a small star-shaped elastic coupling in the column, a €5 part but significant labor cost)
  • Imperfect soundproofing on models before the 2015 facelift (road noise)
  • Aging and sometimes slow multimedia/GPS system

Check before buying

  • Operation of front LED daytime running lights (flickering = headlight needs changing).
  • Noise in the steering during small steering wheel movements when stationary (elastic coupling in the column).
  • On manual diesel models: absence of excessive vibrations at idle or clunking when switching off the engine (Dual-Mass Flywheel).
  • On DCT7 diesel models: smooth hill starts and absence of juddering when hot.
  • Condition of the steering wheel leather and gear knob (tendency to peel prematurely on 2012-2014 models).
  • Operation of the reversing camera (frequent water ingress into the lens).

Alternatives

- **Europe** : Peugeot 308 II (better chassis, but HDi diesels temperamental with AdBlue), Volkswagen Golf VII (segment benchmark, reliable but more expensive TDIs), Hyundai i30 II (technical cousin, identical reliability), Ford Focus III (excellent handling, robust TDCi diesels). - **Asia / Japan** : Toyota Auris II (unbeatable hybrid in the city, but BMW-sourced D-4D diesels less reliable), Mazda 3 (high-performance Skyactiv-D but camshaft problems on early models). - **Emerging markets** : Skoda Octavia (more spacious), Chevrolet Cruze.

Verdict

The Kia cee'd II (JD) is an excellent used car choice, particularly with the diesel engine (1.4 and 1.6 CRDi). These U2 family engines are true 'workhorses' capable of high mileages without complaint, provided basic maintenance is respected. The safest choice is the 1.6 CRDi 110 or 128 hp with a manual gearbox. However, extreme caution is advised with versions equipped with the DCT7 dual-clutch gearbox (introduced in late 2015 on the 136 hp), as the reliability of its dry clutches is inconsistent. Minor peripheral defects (LEDs, steering coupling) are known and documented, which avoids unpleasant surprises. It is a formidable and often cheaper alternative to a Golf VII or a 308 II.
