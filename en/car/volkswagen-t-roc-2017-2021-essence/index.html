Volkswagen T-Roc

I (A1) · 2017 – 2021

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

All Volkswagen models

Summary

The first-generation Volkswagen T-Roc (Phase 1) is a compact SUV based on the famous modular MQB A1 platform (shared with the Golf VII and Audi Q2). Positioned between the urban T-Cross and the family Tiguan, it achieved immense commercial success thanks to its dynamic design, excellent road qualities, and generous interior space for its size (4.23 m).

However, this Phase 1 (before the late 2021 facelift) was sharply criticized for the quality of its interior finish, deemed unworthy of Volkswagen's usual standards, with an omnipresence of hard plastics prone to scratches. Mechanically, it offers a wide range of engines, with a predominance of highly efficient petrol (TSI) units, although some versions experienced early defects (notably the 1.5 TSI and the DSG7 DQ200 gearbox).

Weaknesses 5

  • Quality of interior plastics (hard, hollow-sounding, easily scratched)
  • DSG7 (DQ200) gearbox sometimes generating jerks at low speeds
  • Reduced boot volume on 4Motion versions (392 liters)
  • Very high used purchase price
  • Suspension a bit firm with 18 or 19-inch wheels

Check before buying

  • DSG gearbox history: Test the vehicle cold, in the city. Any stuttering, excessive slipping, or metallic noise when shifting gears (especially 1-2-3) indicates wear of the dual clutch or mechatronics.
  • 1.5 TSI cold behavior: Start cold and move forward in first gear. If the car stutters significantly ("kangaroo effect"), the software update has not been done.
  • Interior trim noises: The hard plastics of this Phase 1 tend to creak over time and with temperature variations.
  • Water pump / Coolant leak: Inspect the coolant level in the expansion tank (a known issue on EA888 and EA288 engines).
  • Haldex maintenance (4Motion versions): Demand invoices proving the 4x4 system oil change.

Verdict

The Volkswagen T-Roc Phase 1 is an excellent choice on the used car market if you prioritize driving pleasure, interior space, and style, at the expense of the perceived quality of the interior plastics.

For petrol engines, the 1.0 TSI 115 hp is the sensible choice: extremely reliable, sufficient for daily use, and economical. The 1.5 TSI 150 hp is the most versatile, but it is essential to ensure it is a post-2019 model or that the anti-stuttering update has been performed.

Be careful with the DSG7 (DQ200) gearbox on 1.0 and 1.5 engines: it is pleasant but its long-term reliability in intensive urban use remains uncertain. Opt for a manual gearbox if you do a lot of city driving, or budget for clutch replacement around 100,000 km.
