Suzuki Ignis

II (MF) · 2016 – 2025

BUY Reliability 90/100 — excellent

All Suzuki models

Summary

The second-generation Suzuki Ignis (internal code MF) is an ultra-compact A-segment crossover. Launched in late 2016 in Europe (and early 2016 in Japan), it stands out with its atypical design, featherlight weight (often under 900 kg), and availability of all-wheel drive (AllGrip), a rarity in this category. The model received a facelift in 2020, introducing an engine evolution (transition from K12C to K12D to meet Euro 6d standards) and an improved mild-hybrid system (SHVS) with a larger capacity battery. It is a pragmatic, extremely reliable vehicle thanks to proven naturally aspirated engines, but it makes concessions on sound insulation and suspension comfort.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability (K12 engines with timing chain)
  • Extremely low fuel consumption (often under 5L/100km in real-world driving)
  • Availability of AllGrip all-wheel drive (rare in this segment)
  • Excellent footprint/habitability ratio (sliding rear bench on high trims)
  • Featherlight weight promoting agility and reducing wear on consumables (brakes, tires)

Weaknesses 6

  • Very firm suspension comfort, especially at the rear
  • Light sound insulation (wind and road noise on the highway)
  • Slow AGS automated manual gearbox that generates jerks
  • Quality of interior plastics (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Thin and fragile body paint (prone to stone chips)
  • Reduced boot volume on AllGrip (4x4) versions

Check before buying

  • AGS gearbox operation: test in the city to detect excessive juddering on startup (sign of a worn clutch).
  • Underbody on AllGrip versions: check for absence of impacts if the vehicle has been off-road.
  • Rear tire wear: sometimes uneven on AllGrip versions due to the rigid axle.
  • Condition of the bodywork and bumpers: paint chips easily on impact.
  • Operation of the Bosch/Pioneer multimedia system: some touchscreen or Bluetooth connection bugs reported (often resolved by update).

Official recalls

  • November 2017: Recall concerning a risk of power steering loss due to a software defect in the EPS module (vehicles produced in 2016 and 2017).
  • May 2021: Recall for the replacement of the fuel tank vent valve, risk of leakage in case of overfilling (vehicles produced between 2016 and 2020).
  • December 2022: (Indian market/Maruti) Recall to check and replace the 12V battery ground cable which could loosen.

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Unrivaled Mechanical Reliability — Enjoy absolute peace of mind thanks to petrol engines (K12C and K12D) renowned for their exceptional robustness and chain distribution, ensuring remarkable durability.

Economy

  • Exemplary Fuel Consumption — Reduce your expenses at the pump with extremely low fuel consumption, making the Ignis an economical choice for daily use.

Safety

  • Certified Optimal Safety — Travel with confidence. The Suzuki Ignis achieved the maximum rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult and child protection.

Braking

  • Efficient and Stable Braking — Benefit from a powerful and stable braking system, ensuring short stopping distances and perfect vehicle control, even in emergency situations.

Handling

  • Remarkable Agility and Maneuverability — Enjoy dynamic driving and exceptional agility, especially in the city. Its light and precise steering makes every maneuver easy and pleasant.

Practicality

  • Ingenious Habitability and 4x4 Versatility — Despite its compact size, the Ignis offers an excellent space-to-size ratio, with a sliding rear bench seat on high trims and the unique availability of AllGrip all-wheel drive for all adventures.

Performance

  • Everyday Lightness and Dynamism — Its featherlight weight gives the Ignis surprising agility and responsiveness that makes driving enjoyable, while reducing wear on consumables.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Fiat Panda (and Panda Cross for the 4x4 alternative), Hyundai i10, Kia Picanto, Toyota Aygo X.
  • Japan / Asia: Daihatsu Taft, Honda N-WGN, Toyota Passo.
  • India / Emerging Markets: Tata Punch, Renault Kwid, Hyundai Grand i10 Nios.

Verdict

The Suzuki Ignis II is a highly recommended purchase for those seeking a pragmatic, economical, and indestructible city car. Its naturally aspirated gasoline engines (K12C and K12D) are models of reliability, free from the complex issues associated with turbos or high-pressure direct injection found in competitors. The ideal choice is a manual gearbox (5-speed manual) version, with 2-wheel drive for the city, or AllGrip for mountainous areas. The AGS automated manual gearbox should be avoided if you are sensitive to driving comfort. Although rustic in terms of sound insulation and damping, it compensates with unbeatable running costs.
