Audi A6

C8 (4K) · 2018 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The eighth-generation Audi A6 (internal code C8 / 4K) marks a major technological turning point for Ingolstadt's executive sedan. Launched in 2018 and facelifted in 2023, it stands out with its ultra-modern cabin featuring a dual-touchscreen (MMI Touch Response) and the widespread integration of mild-hybrid (MHEV) technology across almost all its powertrains. Based on the MLB Evo platform, it offers imperial comfort, top-notch sound insulation, and impeccable finish quality. While gasoline engines (TFSI) have gained reliability compared to previous generations (notably the mature 2.0 EA888 unit), this C8 generation is marred by a recurring and costly problem related to its 48V mild-hybrid system (starter-generator) on V6 engines. It is an excellent road car, but its electronic complexity requires rigorous monitoring.

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 48V starter-generator (BSG) on V6 engines
  • Questionable ergonomics of the all-touch interface while driving (climate control)
  • S tronic gearbox sometimes hesitant on takeoff or at low speeds
  • Very high maintenance and spare parts costs
  • Significant weight, especially on PHEV (TFSI e) versions

Check before buying

  • Replacement history of the starter-generator (BSG) on 55 TFSI, 45 TDI, 50 TDI, and S6 versions.
  • Smooth and bug-free operation of both central MMI screens and the Virtual Cockpit.
  • Proof of S tronic gearbox oil change at a maximum of 60,000 km.
  • Absence of coolant leaks (water housing) on 2.0 TFSI.
  • Condition of tires and brakes (rapid wear due to vehicle weight, > 1.9 tonne).

Official recalls

  • - **March 2020 (Starter-generator)**: Risk of moisture infiltration into the starter-generator (BSG) potentially causing a short circuit and fire. Concerns V6 models produced between 2018 and 2020.
  • - **May 2021 (Rear axle)**: Potentially defective trailing arm nuts on the rear axle, which could affect geometry and road holding. 2019-2021 models.
  • - **March 2022 (Fuel gauge)**: The fuel level sensor may get stuck against a ventilation hose in the tank, displaying an incorrect level. 2019-2021 models.
  • - **December 2022 (Reversing camera)**: Software bug preventing the display of the reversing camera (MMI update required).
  • - **August 2023 (Gateway Module)**: Risk of water infiltration under the rear seat potentially damaging the Gateway control module, leading to engine power loss. 2019-2022 models.

Verdict

The Audi A6 C8 is an impressive technological showcase and an exceptional road car. If you are looking for a gasoline engine, the **2.0 TFSI (45 TFSI)** is the most rational and reliable choice: the EA888 unit has now shed its old demons and offers an excellent compromise. However, purchasing a V6 version (55 TFSI or TDI) requires great caution: the 48V starter-generator (BSG) problem is a real financial sword of Damocles if the vehicle is no longer under warranty or if the part has not been replaced by the revised version. Prioritize a model with a clear service history within the Audi network, and do not hesitate to subscribe to an extended warranty for V6 or PHEV models.
