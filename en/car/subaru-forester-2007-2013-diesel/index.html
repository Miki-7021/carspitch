Subaru Forester

III (SH) (2007–2013) · 2007–2013

NEGOTIATE Reliability 60/100 — moyen

Summary

The third-generation Subaru Forester, known by the chassis code SH, marks a significant evolution from its predecessors. Launched in 2007, it abandoned the raised wagon look to adopt a more assertive compact SUV style, meeting market expectations. It nevertheless retained the brand's fundamentals: permanent symmetrical all-wheel drive and Boxer (flat) engines, ensuring a low center of gravity and excellent traction.

A minor facelift occurred in 2011, bringing minor aesthetic changes (headlights, grille) and some interior improvements. Renowned for its robustness and off-road capabilities, the Forester SH offers generous interior space and appreciable comfort. However, the introduction of the Boxer Diesel engine unfortunately tarnished its reputation for reliability, while the gasoline engines, although generally reliable, were not without known defects. It is a versatile vehicle, but the choice of engine is absolutely crucial.

Weaknesses 6

  • Catastrophic reliability of the 2.0 Boxer Diesel engine (EE20Z)
  • Recurring head gasket problems on the 2.5 gasoline engine (EJ253/EJ255)
  • High fuel consumption for gasoline engines
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speed
  • Dated interior finish and sometimes basic materials
  • Cost of parts and maintenance at Subaru

Check before buying

  • **For the Diesel (EE20Z)**: Complete maintenance history, engine repair invoices, abnormal noises (knocking, clattering), exhaust smoke, DPF operation. **To be avoided unless irrefutable proof of a recent engine replacement or a major crankshaft repair.**
  • **For the 2.5 gasoline (EJ253/EJ255)**: Presence of oil or coolant leaks at the head gaskets, excessive oil consumption, head gasket replacement history.
  • **For all engines**: Engine oil level and quality, absence of abnormal noises (ticking, knocking), condition of the cooling system.
  • All-wheel drive operation (no noises or vibrations in tight turns).
  • Condition of suspensions and bearings (noises, play).
  • Operation of the clutch and gearbox (manual or automatic).
  • Check for completed manufacturer recalls.

Verdict

The Subaru Forester III (SH) is a multifaceted vehicle. Its symmetrical all-wheel drive is a major asset for safety and traction, and its interior space is appreciable. However, the choice of engine is the determining factor for its reliability and your satisfaction.

* **If you are looking for a gasoline model (2.0i EJ204)**: This is the safest choice. Check oil consumption and maintenance history. It is a robust and pleasant vehicle. * **If you are considering a 2.5 gasoline (EJ253/EJ255)**: Be extremely vigilant about the head gasket history. If the replacement was done with quality parts and the follow-up is impeccable, this can be an option, but the risk remains. The XT version is powerful but requires meticulous maintenance. * **If you are tempted by the 2.0 Boxer Diesel (EE20Z)**: **Absolutely avoid it, unless you have formal and documented proof of a recent engine replacement or a major crankshaft repair by a competent professional, with warranty.** The risk of engine failure is too high and repair costs are prohibitive, turning a good deal into a financial black hole.

In summary, the Forester SH is a good companion for 4x4 and versatility enthusiasts, provided you choose the right engine and check its history. Prioritize naturally aspirated gasoline versions for peace of mind.
