Toyota Highlander

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The second-generation Toyota Highlander (code name XU40) is a midsize family SUV offering up to 3 rows of seats. Designed primarily for the North American market (and sold under the name Kluger in Oceania), it is based on the Toyota K platform (shared with the Camry and the Lexus RX). It stands out for its ride comfort, interior space, and, above all, its hybrid powertrain (Hybrid Synergy Drive), which makes it an absolute benchmark in terms of total cost of ownership (TCO) and reliability in its segment. A facelift occurred in late 2010 (2011 model year), bringing cosmetic tweaks and replacing the 3.3L hybrid V6 with a more efficient 3.5L V6. According to Consumer Reports and J.D. Power, it consistently ranks above average in its class.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of the hybrid system (very durable NiMH battery)
  • Unbeatable total cost of ownership (TCO) in the 3-row SUV segment
  • High-level suspension comfort and soundproofing
  • Generous interior space for the first two rows
  • Extremely high resale value

Weaknesses 5

  • Sluggish road handling (focused on comfort, no dynamism)
  • Cramped third row of seats (reserved for children)
  • Quality of some interior plastics (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Dated infotainment system
  • Electric power steering lacking feel

Check before buying

  • Hybrid battery health check (Toyota Hybrid Health Check) to ensure remaining capacity.
  • Signs of oil leaks on the passenger side of the engine (timing cover on the 3.5L V6s).
  • Condition of the water pump (bearing noises, traces of dried pink coolant).
  • Operation of the main power window switch (subject to recall).
  • Seizing of the rear brake calipers (common on hybrids because the mechanical brakes are used less due to regenerative braking).

Official recalls

NHTSA (United States) - Main recalls for the XU40 generation:

  • [2010-01-21 / 2010-01-27] Accelerator pedal (10V017000 / 10V023000): Risk of the accelerator pedal sticking in the depressed position (unsuitable floor mat or internal friction of the mechanism). Affects 2008-2010 models.
  • [2012-10-10 / 2015-10-22] Power windows (12V491000 / 15V689000): The main power window switch may melt or catch fire due to uneven lubrication during manufacturing. Affects 2008-2011 models.
  • [2014-04-09] Airbags (14V168000): The steering wheel spiral cable (clockspring) can rub and break, deactivating the driver's airbag. Affects 2008-2010 models.
  • [2011-04-21] Curtain airbags (11V245000): Defective curtain airbag deployment sensors. Affects 2008 models.
  • [2014-03-27] Seats (14V148000): The left seat of the second row may not lock properly. Affects certain 2013 models.
  • [2008-04-18] Seat belts (08V181000): Non-compliance of the third-row seat belts on 2008 models.
  • [2009-04-27 / 2009-05-14] Accessories (09E022000 / 09E028000): Stainless steel exhaust tips (optional accessory) that can detach while driving.

What sets it apart 7

reliability

  • Legendary Hybrid System Reliability — The Toyota Highlander Hybrid is renowned for the exceptional durability of its Hybrid Synergy Drive system, with NiMH batteries designed to exceed 250,000 km without major failure. It's a safe and lasting investment.

economy

  • Unbeatable Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) — Thanks to its proven reliability, optimized fuel consumption, and extremely high resale value, the Highlander Hybrid offers one of the lowest total costs of ownership in its category, ensuring excellent long-term profitability.

safety

  • Award-Winning Safety and Effective Braking — Recognized as an "IIHS Top Safety Pick" for its robustness, the Highlander ensures optimal protection. Its braking system is efficient, allowing a stop from 96 km/h (60 mph) in just 38.7 meters, a respectable performance for a family SUV.

comfort

  • Premium Driving Comfort and Sound Insulation — Enjoy a serene driving experience thanks to high-level suspension comfort and meticulous sound insulation, transforming every journey into a relaxing moment for all occupants.

practicality

  • Generous and Versatile Interior Space — The Highlander offers spacious and flexible interior space, particularly for the first two rows, ideal for families and long trips, ensuring everyone's comfort.

Performance

  • Powerful and Efficient Hybrid Powertrain — The V6 Hybrid engine (especially the 3.5L with 280 combined hp) delivers smooth and responsive power, combined with remarkable efficiency, for dynamic performance and controlled fuel consumption.

handling

  • Stable and Predictable Road Handling — The Highlander ensures predictable and safe road handling, prioritizing stability and comfort. Its composed driving inspires confidence, even on long journeys.

Verdict

The second-generation Toyota Highlander (2008-2013) is a highly recommended purchase, especially in its Hybrid version. It represents the archetype of the 'buy it and keep it' vehicle. The reliability of its Hybrid Synergy Drive system is proven, with NiMH batteries easily exceeding 250,000 km without major failure. The optimal choice is a facelifted model (2011-2013) equipped with the 3.5L Hybrid V6 (2GR-FXE), which eliminates the timing belt constraint of the previous 3.3L while offering more power and better efficiency. Ensure that the recalls (particularly concerning the accelerator pedal and the power window switch) have been performed.
