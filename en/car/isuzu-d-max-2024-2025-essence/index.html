Summary

The third-generation Isuzu D-Max (codenamed TFR for 4x2 versions and TFS for 4x4s) received a major facelift in late 2023 for the 2024 model year. Known globally as one of the most robust pickup trucks on the market thanks to its heavy-duty commercial vehicle DNA, this facelift brings a modernized front fascia, optimized aerodynamics, an improved 'Rough Terrain' mode, and a more technological cabin. Although the D-Max is legendary for its diesel engines (1.9L and 3.0L), a 2.0L Turbo petrol engine is offered in certain specific markets (notably in China via the Jiangxi Isuzu joint venture and in the Middle East) to meet local emissions standards and the demand for driving refinement. In 2025, the lineup will also expand with the announced arrival of a 100% electric version (BEV) and a mild hybrid (MHEV) on the diesel.

Strengths 6

  • Legendary robustness of the ladder chassis and running gear
  • Towing capacity of 3.5t and payload of one ton
  • Refinement and quietness of the petrol version (in markets where available)
  • Significant improvement in interior finish quality on the 2024 facelift
  • Recalibrated and less intrusive advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS)
  • Excellent off-road capabilities (rear differential lock standard on high trims)

Weaknesses 5

  • Almost zero availability of the petrol engine in Europe and Oceania
  • High fuel consumption on the 2.0L Turbo Petrol version
  • Rear suspension comfort (leaf springs) very firm when unladen
  • Soundproofing could be improved on diesel engines when cold
  • Isuzu dealership network less dense than mainstream brands in Europe

Check before buying

  • Condition of the rear leaf springs (subject to a manufacturer recall, see recalls section)
  • Tightness of the steering column (lower connection bolt)
  • Underbody and oil pan: check for impact damage from intensive off-road use
  • Maintenance history of the automatic gearbox (oil change recommended every 80,000 km under severe use)
  • On petrol versions: check for the absence of blue exhaust smoke (sign of turbo wear)

Official recalls

  • 14/04/2023 (Rappel Conso - France): D-Max van. Reason: A defective tightening torque on the lower steering column connection bolt can lead to failure and loss of steering. Risk: Injuries.
  • 27/05/2021 (Rappel Conso - France, Code Isuzu W9080): D-Max van. Reason: A production defect can cause the rear axle leaf spring to break. Consequently, there is a risk of loss of control. Risk: Injuries. *(Note: Although predating the facelift, this recall concerns the current Gen III platform and must be checked on the used market).*

What sets it apart 6

Performance

  • 2.0L Turbo Gasoline Engine: Power and Smoothness — Enjoy a refined and dynamic driving experience thanks to the 2.0L Turbo gasoline engine. Its generous power and quiet operation, combined with the optimized 8-speed ZF automatic transmission, offer exceptional on-road comfort.

reliability

  • Unrivaled Robustness — Inheriting Isuzu's heavy-duty vehicle manufacturing DNA, the D-Max is built on a ladder frame chassis and running gear of legendary robustness, ensuring extreme durability and reliability, even in the most demanding conditions.

practicality

  • Exceptional Work Capabilities — The D-Max is a true professional partner, capable of towing up to 3.5 tonnes and carrying a payload of one tonne. These performances make it the ideal tool for all your missions, from the lightest to the heaviest.

comfort

  • Modernized and Connected Interior — The 2024 facelift brings a significant improvement in interior finish quality. Enjoy a more technological cabin, optimized aerodynamics, and enhanced comfort for both long journeys and daily commutes.

safety

  • Intelligent Driver Assistance Systems — The D-Max integrates recalibrated Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) for optimal safety. These advanced technologies discreetly assist you, making every journey safer and more serene.

handling

  • Absolute Off-Road Mastery — With its excellent off-road capabilities, its new and improved 'Rough Terrain' mode, and standard rear differential lock on high-end trims, the D-Max takes you anywhere with remarkable confidence and agility.

Alternatives

  • Europe / Global: Ford Ranger (segment benchmark, offers a 2.3 EcoBoost petrol in some markets or the 3.0L V6), Toyota Hilux (the historical rival in terms of reliability), Volkswagen Amarok (technical cousin of the Ranger).
  • Asia / Middle East: Mitsubishi Triton / L200 (new generation), Nissan Navara, GWM Poer (highly competitive on petrol engines in China).

Verdict

The facelifted third-generation Isuzu D-Max (2024+) remains an exceptional workhorse that has become just refined enough to appeal to private buyers. If your search is specifically for the petrol engine, be aware that it offers superior refinement and appreciable quietness, but at the cost of high fuel consumption and limited availability to imports or specific markets (Asia/Middle East). For 95% of buyers, the diesel engines (1.9L and especially the indestructible 3.0L) remain the sensible choice for ownership and resale. It is a highly recommended purchase for its durability, provided you verify that the early recalls (steering, suspension leaf springs) have been carried out.
