Summary

The first-generation Audi Q8 (code 4M8) is the flagship coupe SUV of the brand with the four rings. Based on the MLB Evo platform (shared with the Porsche Cayenne, VW Touareg, and Lamborghini Urus), it stands out with a statutory design, preserved roominess despite its sloping roofline, and an exceptional level of interior finish. A true technological showcase, it features mild hybridization (MHEV 48V) on the majority of its V6 and V8 engines. While it excels in ride comfort (especially with the air suspension) and soundproofing, its colossal weight (often exceeding 2.2 tons) penalizes agility and accelerates the wear of consumables (brakes, tires). Overall reliability is good on a purely mechanical level, but marred by electronic failures and a major, recurring problem with the 48V system's alternator-starter. Overall rating: 8/10.

Strengths 5

  • Benchmark finish and assembly quality in the category
  • Imperial comfort with the adaptive air suspension
  • Soundproofing worthy of a limousine (double glazing)
  • Noble and high-performance V6 and V8 engines (both gasoline and diesel)
  • Generous rear roominess despite the coupe profile

Weaknesses 5

  • Excessive weight penalizing dynamism and fuel consumption
  • Endemic problem with the 48V alternator-starter (BSG) on MHEV engines
  • All-touch ergonomics (dual screen) distracting while driving
  • Astronomical maintenance, insurance, and repair costs
  • Premature tire wear (often on 21, 22, or 23 inches)

Check before buying

  • Replacement history of the 48V alternator-starter (BSG): absolute critical point.
  • Smooth operation of the MMI dual touch screen and reversing camera (frequent bugs).
  • Condition of tires and brakes (steel discs heavily stressed, carbon-ceramic discs exorbitantly priced on RSQ8).
  • Air suspension: check that it raises and lowers without suspicious noise from the compressor.
  • Rear axle alignment (asymmetrical wear of rear tires reported by recall).

  • [2021-11-15] Crash sensors: Defective sensors in the front doors for airbag deployment.

Electronics & Software

  • [2026-03-04] Lane departure warning: Software error deactivating the system (NHTSA 26V125).
  • [2025-12-19 / 2022-10-05 / 2021-10-20] Reversing camera: Software error preventing the image from displaying (NHTSA 25V900 / 22V742 / 21V825).
  • [2025-03-31 / 2022-10-26] Screens / MMI: Software error affecting the display of the dashboard or infotainment screen (NHTSA 25V201 / 22V806).

Chassis, Steering & Braking

  • [2024-07-12] Brake hoses (e-tron/PHEV): Excessive tightening that can break the screws and cause a brake fluid leak.
  • [2022-09-12] Rear axle geometry: Alteration of camber/toe leading to rapid and uneven wear of the rear tires.
  • [2021-11-15 / 2021-04-26] Rear axle: Nuts of the eccentric bolt of the control arm that can break due to corrosion (Rappel Conso / NHTSA 21V295).
  • [2020-04-10 / 2020-03-16] Steering: Screw connection between the shaft and the steering box insufficiently tightened, risk of loss of control (Rappel Conso / NHTSA 20V149).
  • [2019-02-20] Front shock absorbers: Possible cracks on the front suspension forks (NHTSA 19V114).

Mechanical & Electrical

  • [2025-11-07] High Voltage Battery (PHEV/e-tron): Overheating of cellular modules during charging, risk of fire.
  • [2022-07-20] Fuel pump: Rupture of an internal component leading to engine failure (NHTSA 22V516).
  • [2020-07-10 / 2020-05-13] Gearbox: Welding defect on an oil line of the automatic gearbox causing an ATF leak (Rappel Conso / NHTSA 20V272).

Verdict

The Audi Q8 is a formidable highway cruiser, offering a rare compromise between the dynamism of a coupe and the comfort of a limousine. The gasoline engines (55 TFSI and V8) are smooth and highly performant. However, buying a used Q8 requires extreme financial caution. The endemic problem of the 48V alternator-starter (BSG) on MHEV engines is a sword of Damocles that costs several thousand euros to repair. Furthermore, its gargantuan weight devours brakes and tires at a crazy speed. To be bought imperatively with a crystal-clear history, proof of the BSG replacement, and ideally an extended manufacturer's warranty.
