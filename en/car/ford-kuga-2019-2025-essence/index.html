Summary

The third-generation Ford Kuga (codename C519), sold as the Ford Escape in North America, marks a major turning point for the blue oval brand's compact SUV. Based on the C2 platform (shared with the Focus IV), it abandons the sharp lines of its predecessors for a more fluid and aerodynamic, crossover-like design. This generation stands out with a massive electrification offering, with the Kuga PHEV becoming the best-selling plug-in hybrid vehicle in Europe for several consecutive years. A mid-cycle facelift was carried out (late 2022 in the USA, early 2024 in Europe), bringing a modernized front end, the removal of many physical controls in favor of the SYNC 4 infotainment system with a 13.2-inch screen. Although demand targets gasoline engines (notably the 1.5 EcoBoost 3-cylinder), the range covers a very wide spectrum from diesel (EcoBlue) to hybrids (FHEV/PHEV).

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic road handling, true to Ford's DNA (excellent C2 chassis)
  • Reliability of the 1.5 EcoBoost ('Dragon' engine with chain, well-designed)
  • Generous interior space and very practical sliding rear bench seat
  • Remarkable efficiency of hybrid versions (FHEV and PHEV)
  • SYNC 4 multimedia system (on facelifted versions) very responsive and intuitive

Weaknesses 5

  • Uneven interior finish (hard plastics in the lower part, sometimes imperfect assembly)
  • Heavy early recalls on PHEV versions (battery and engine block)
  • Imperfect sound insulation during acceleration phases (eCVT on hybrids, 3-cylinder on 1.5 EB)
  • Recurring 12V auxiliary battery discharge problems
  • Reduced boot volume on the PHEV version (battery under the floor)

Check before buying

  • Recall history: CRUCIAL for PHEV/FHEV versions (check if the HV battery has been changed and if the engine recall has been performed).
  • 12V battery: Test its charging capacity, source of 90% of the vehicle's electronic bugs.
  • Manual gearbox (1.5 EcoBoost): Check the fluidity of gear changes (some cases of sticky synchros).
  • Panoramic roof: Check for air noises or creaks (bonding defect on some series).
  • Onboard electronics: Test the touchscreen, eCall system, and rearview camera (frequent bugs on SYNC 3, resolved by updates).

Official recalls

  • - **August 2020 (PHEV)**: Risk of high-voltage battery fire during charging due to cell contamination. Complete replacement of the battery pack on models produced before June 2020.
  • - **July 2022 (HEV/PHEV)**: Risk of fire in the engine compartment. A crankshaft machining defect can lead to engine failure and oil/fuel leakage onto hot surfaces. Modification of the lower fairing and grille shutters (2019-2022 models).
  • - **December 2022 (1.5 EcoBoost)**: Risk of oil leak at the oil separator potentially causing a fire. Inspection and replacement if necessary.
  • - **February 2021 (All)**: eCall emergency call system malfunction (software update).
  • - **2023 (Escape NA)**: Recall concerning a welding defect on the brake pedal that could lead to its breakage.

Verdict

The Ford Kuga III (Escape) is a very well-rounded compact SUV, offering one of the best comfort/dynamism compromises in its category. If you are targeting a pure gasoline engine, the **1.5 EcoBoost is an excellent choice**: this 'Dragon' 3-cylinder with a timing chain proves to be robust, willing, and free from the flaws of the old 1.0 EcoBoost. Hybrid versions (FHEV/PHEV) are remarkably efficient but require absolute vigilance regarding maintenance history due to major recalls early in their career. A 1.5 EcoBoost model or a post-2022 PHEV (or with certified recalls) constitutes a highly recommendable purchase. Diesels are best reserved for pure highway drivers, otherwise, costly depollution expenses may arise.
