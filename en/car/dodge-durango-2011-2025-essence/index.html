Dodge Durango

III (WD) · 2011 – 2025

BUY Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The third-generation Dodge Durango (codename WD) is a large 3-row family SUV that stands out with its "Muscle SUV" positioning. Based on the same platform as the Jeep Grand Cherokee (WK2) and the former Mercedes-Benz ML (W166), it benefits from a rear-wheel drive (RWD) or all-wheel drive (AWD) architecture. Over its long 15-year career, it received two major updates: in 2014 (introduction of the excellent 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox and "Racetrack" LED taillights) and in 2021 (new dashboard, Uconnect 5 system). While it shines with the robustness of its engines and its exceptional towing capacity, it shows its age in terms of fuel efficiency. In Europe, it is mainly distributed via specialized importer networks (such as AEC Europe).

Strengths 5

  • Extremely reliable 3.6L V6 and 5.7L V8 engines over the long term
  • Very robust and responsive 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox (since 2014)
  • Royal roominess with 3 real rows of seats (up to 7 seats)
  • Towing capacity superior to the competition (up to 3945 kg on SRT)
  • Intuitive Uconnect infotainment system (especially version 5 since 2021)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high fuel consumption, particularly on the V8s
  • Capricious electronics (Uconnect screen bugs, reversing cameras)
  • Imposing size and weight, poorly suited to European city centers
  • Interior finish and plastics of pre-2021 models below European standards
  • Significant number of manufacturer recalls (although often minor)

Check before buying

  • Recall history: it is imperative to check if the fuel pump relay and ABS module have been fixed.
  • Cooling system: hunt for coolant leaks (gaskets, radiator, water pump on V8).
  • On-board electronics: test all functions of the Uconnect touch screen, the reversing camera, and the tri-zone climate control.
  • AWD transmission: check for the absence of jerks and ensure the transfer case oil has been replaced.
  • Engine noises (V8): listen carefully when cold to detect a ticking sound (sign of a broken exhaust manifold stud).

What sets it apart 14

Reliability

  • Proven Mechanical Reliability — The gasoline engines, particularly the Pentastar V6 and HEMI V8, are praised for their endurance and robust design, ensuring thousands of miles of serene driving.
  • Robust and Proven Platform — Based on a solid architecture shared with the Jeep Grand Cherokee, the Durango benefits from a robust and reliable design, ensuring durability and excellent road holding.

Performance

  • Smooth and Robust Transmission — Enjoy a refined and dynamic driving experience thanks to the 8-speed ZF automatic transmission (available since 2014), renowned for its robustness and responsiveness.
  • Breathtaking V8 Performance — For thrill-seekers, the HEMI V8 engines offer impressive power and torque, transforming every journey into an exhilarating driving experience.

Practicality

  • Exceptional Towing Capacity — Ideal for caravans, trailers, or boats, the Dodge Durango offers an impressive towing capacity, allowing you to transport everything you need.

Comfort

  • Royal Seating for 7 Passengers — With its three true rows of seats, the Durango offers generous space and optimal comfort to accommodate up to 7 adults, perfect for large families and trips.
  • Intuitive Infotainment System — The Uconnect system, especially version 5 (since 2021), offers a modern, intuitive, and connected interface for easy entertainment and navigation on board.

Alternatives

* **Americas:** Ford Explorer, Chevrolet Traverse, Jeep Grand Cherokee L, Toyota Highlander, Honda Pilot. * **Europe:** Volkswagen Touareg, Audi Q7, BMW X5, Volvo XC90 (although the Durango is more rustic and less premium). * **Japan/Asia:** Toyota Land Cruiser Prado, Nissan Patrol (in some markets).

Verdict

The third-generation Dodge Durango is a survivor of the traditional large SUV era. Its exceptional longevity on the market is explained by a sound platform (shared with the Jeep Grand Cherokee) and engines (Pentastar V6 and HEMI V8) of remarkable mechanical reliability. The 8-speed ZF gearbox (post-2014) is a major asset. However, this mechanical robustness is tarnished by sometimes capricious electronics and a long list of manufacturer recalls concerning accessories, braking modules (ABS), or wiring harnesses.

**Buying advice:** Favor a facelifted model from 2014 onwards (for the ZF 8 gearbox) or ideally post-2021 (for the modernized interior). The 3.6L V6 engine is the most rational and reliable choice for daily use. The 5.7L V8 is fantastic for towing, but requires a substantial fuel budget. Make absolutely sure that all electronic and safety recalls have been performed before buying.
