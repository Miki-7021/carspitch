Honda Accord

IX (CR) · 2012 – 2017

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The **9th generation Honda Accord (CR chassis for the sedan, CT for the coupe)** marks a strategic turning point for the manufacturer. Faced with declining sales of family sedans (D-segment) in Western Europe, Honda made the radical decision not to market this generation there, reserving it primarily for North America, Asia, Russia, and Oceania.

However, this model remains highly relevant for the Francophone market (particularly in Canada/Quebec, and via imports in Africa and French overseas territories). This generation introduces **Earth Dreams** technology (direct injection, optimized CVT gearboxes) and an innovative hybrid system (i-MMD). More compact externally than the 8th North American generation, it nevertheless offers better interior space. A major facelift occurred in 2016, bringing increased rigidity, revised suspension, the integration of Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, and the Honda Sensing safety package.

**Expert's overall rating:** The Accord IX is an absolute benchmark for reliability in its category, often surpassing its German rivals, although it requires rigorous maintenance of its CVT transmission and has some known peripheral weaknesses (starter, VTC actuator).

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall mechanical reliability (engines and transmissions if well-maintained)
  • Very generous interior space (rear seats and trunk)
  • Controlled fuel consumption (especially with the 2.4L CVT and Hybrid)
  • Ergonomics of controls and seat comfort
  • Sound and dynamic road handling (especially after the 2016 facelift)
  • Excellent peripheral visibility (thin pillars, LaneWatch blind spot camera)

Weaknesses 5

  • Lack of official marketing in Western Europe (body parts sometimes long to obtain)
  • Dual-screen infotainment system (2013-2015) slow, unintuitive, and prone to bugs
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (road noise), although improved in 2016
  • Frequent failure of LED daytime running lights (DRL) requiring complete headlight assembly replacement
  • Paint quality (thin clear coat, susceptible to stone chips and micro-scratches)

Check before buying

  • Listen carefully to the engine during a cold start (a 2-second rattling noise indicates a worn VTC actuator on the 2.4L).
  • Test hot start: a laborious start or a 'click' without rotation indicates an end-of-life starter (very common on the 2.4L).
  • Check the CVT gearbox fluid change history (should be done every 40,000 to 50,000 km). Test the CVT's smoothness at low speeds.
  • On V6 models, check the oil level and ask if the timing belt has been replaced.
  • Check the operation of the LED daytime running lights (light strip at the bottom of the headlights): they tend to burn out partially.
  • Ensure the lower touchscreen does not exhibit 'ghost touch' (phantom presses) or extreme slowness.

Verdict

The 9th generation Honda Accord is a **highly recommended purchase**. It is one of the most reliable mid-size sedans of its decade. The 2.4L engine coupled with the CVT is the most rational choice, offering an excellent balance between performance and economy, provided you are prepared for the probable starter replacement and scrupulously drain the gearbox. The Hybrid version is an engineering marvel, perfect for high-mileage urban drivers. The V6, while enjoyable, requires a higher maintenance budget (belt, VCM). Prioritize facelifted models (2016-2017) which correct early infotainment flaws and improve sound insulation.
