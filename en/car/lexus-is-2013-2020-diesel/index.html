Lexus IS

III (XE30) · 2013 – 2020

BUY Reliability 93/100 — excellent

Summary

The third-generation Lexus IS (code name XE30) marks a radical turning point for the Japanese premium family sedan. **Caution: contrary to the initial request, the Lexus IS III was NEVER offered with a diesel engine.** Burned by the reliability issues of the 2.2 D-4D block (IS 220d/200d) on the previous generation (XE20), Lexus made the strategic decision to completely abandon diesel in favor of its hybrid technology (IS 300h). This generation stands out with a bold design (trapezoidal grille, L-shaped daytime running lights), exceptional finish quality, and a highly dynamic chassis. It establishes itself as one of the most reliable sedans in its segment, offering a prime alternative to the German trio (3 Series, C-Class, A4).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability (ADAC breakdown rate: 1.2 breakdowns/1000 veh/year, well above average)
  • Indestructible 300h hybrid system and very economical in urban/suburban areas
  • Cabin manufacturing and assembly quality (excellent aging)
  • Extremely robust Aisin automatic gearboxes on all versions
  • Very low TÜV defect rate at 3 years (9.5%), among the best in the premium segment

Weaknesses 4

  • Total absence of a diesel engine (may deter exclusive heavy highway drivers)
  • Dated, slow infotainment system, and unergonomic joystick/mouse interface
  • Reduced trunk volume on the 300h version (hybrid battery) and cramped rear bench seat
  • Premature wear of the inner tread on the front tires (known alignment/bushing defect)

Check before buying

  • Hybrid health check history (essential for the battery warranty on the 300h)
  • Condition of the inner sidewalls of the front tires (wear often invisible from the outside)
  • Operation of the central screen (a few cases of anti-reflective coating delamination)
  • Cleanliness of the hybrid battery ventilation filter (if clogged, the battery overheats and degrades)

  • High-Level Active and Passive Safety — Designed to protect its occupants, the Lexus IS III has achieved top safety distinctions. It offers exemplary protection for adults and children, confirming its status as a safe family vehicle.

Verdict

The Lexus IS III (XE30) is a masterful choice on the used market. **If you were looking for a diesel, you need a paradigm shift: Lexus had the intelligence to abandon this engine (too fragile on the previous generation) in favor of the 300h hybrid.** This 300h version offers a reliability that competing German diesels (N47, OM651, TDI) simply cannot match in the long term, while offering similar fuel consumption in mixed use. With a What Car? reliability score of 4.5/5 and glowing ADAC/TÜV statistics, it is an absolute 'peace of mind' purchase. Only its dated multimedia system and reduced rear roominess prevent it from reaching perfection.
