Summary

The Mercedes-Benz B-Class (W246) marks a major break from the previous generation (W245). Gone is the 'sandwich' floor: the W246 adopts the MFA (Modular Front Architecture) platform, shared with the A-Class (W176). This change lowers the center of gravity, drastically improves road handling, and modernizes the interior. Positioned as a premium compact MPV, it targets families looking for the three-pointed star badge with generous interior space. A facelift occurred in late 2014, bringing aesthetic touches (LED headlights), a modernized infotainment screen, and an update to the designations ('CDI' became 'd'). Overall, the W246 is a very accomplished vehicle and significantly more reliable than its predecessor.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent interior space and trunk volume (488 to 1547 liters) for its size
  • Significantly improved finish quality and interior presentation compared to the W245
  • Very satisfactory overall reliability, particularly for gasoline engines and the small OM607 diesel
  • Safe and dynamic road handling
  • Standard active safety equipment (Collision Prevention Assist)

Weaknesses 5

  • Suspension comfort a bit firm, especially with the AMG package or 18-inch wheels
  • 7G-DCT dual-clutch gearbox sometimes hesitant or causing jerks at low speeds
  • Sound insulation of OM651 (2.1L) diesel engines could be improved under acceleration
  • High maintenance cost within the Mercedes-Benz network for a family vehicle
  • Average rear visibility (reversing camera highly recommended)

Check before buying

  • Service history of the 7G-DCT automatic gearbox (mandatory oil change every 5 years / 100,000 km).
  • Metallic noise when cold on OM651 diesel and M270 gasoline engines (sign of a stretched timing chain).
  • Operation of the panoramic sunroof (drain cleaning necessary to prevent water infiltration).
  • Condition of the DPF and EGR valve on diesels (especially if the vehicle has been driven a lot in the city).
  • Wear of front tires (the front axle can be stressed by the torque of the 200d/220d versions).

Verdict

The Mercedes B-Class W246 is an excellent choice for families looking for a compact, spacious, and well-finished vehicle. Contrary to the sometimes capricious reputation of MPVs, the W246 boasts a very solid reliability record.

**For Diesel:** The most rational choice is the **B 180 d (OM607)**. Although it originates from Renault, it has been perfectly made reliable, and its maintenance cost (outside the MB network) is reasonable. High-mileage drivers will prefer the **B 200 d or B 220 d (OM651)** for their highway cruising ability, but will need to be vigilant about timing chain and automatic gearbox maintenance.

**For Gasoline:** The B 180 and B 200 versions are excellent compromises, very reliable and adapted to current anti-pollution standards (LEZ). Buy with confidence with an up-to-date service history.
