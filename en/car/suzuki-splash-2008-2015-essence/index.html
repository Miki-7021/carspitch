Summary

The Suzuki Splash (Generation I - Type EX) is a high-roof mini-city car (microspace) developed in partnership with General Motors. It is the technical twin of the Opel Agila B. Mainly produced at the Magyar Suzuki plant in Hungary for the European market, it was also marketed in India and Southeast Asia as the Maruti Suzuki Ritz. Designed to maximize interior space on a small footprint (3.71 m), it benefited from a slight facelift in 2012 (new bumpers, enlarged grille). Overall, it is an extremely rational, economical vehicle renowned for the great robustness of its Japanese-origin gasoline engines.

Strengths 6

  • Exemplary reliability of gasoline engines (K10B and K12B blocks)
  • Generous front headroom and legroom
  • Slightly elevated driving position
  • Maneuverability in the city (short turning radius)
  • Very low maintenance and insurance costs
  • Timing chain on all engines

Weaknesses 5

  • Ridiculous boot volume (178 liters, expandable to 1050 liters with seats folded)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Hard interior plastics prone to scratches
  • Quite firm rear suspension comfort
  • Diesel engine (DDiS) unsuitable for the vehicle's urban purpose

Check before buying

  • Manual gearbox: Listen for any whistling or grinding on gears (especially 1st and 2nd), a sign of primary shaft bearing wear.
  • Clutch: Test the biting point. On gasoline models driven exclusively in the city, the clutch can wear out as early as 80,000 km.
  • Running gear: Check the condition of the anti-roll bar bushes and linkages (clunking on degraded roads).
  • Diesel history: If purchasing the 1.3 DDiS, demand a service book with regular oil changes and check for no DPF/Engine warning light illuminated.

Official recalls

  • November 2015 (Fuel leak): Recall of models produced between 2010 and 2014. Possible friction between a fuel hose and the engine cover could lead to a leak.
  • May 2014 (Water pump): On some gasoline models, risk of water pump bearing failure leading to overheating or accessory belt breakage.
  • 2008 (Airbag): Potentially incorrect deployment of the passenger airbag on the very first production series.

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Legendary Petrol Engine Reliability — The Suzuki Splash's petrol engines (1.0L and 1.2L) are renowned for their exceptional robustness and longevity, further enhanced by a maintenance-free timing chain. A choice synonymous with peace of mind.

Practicality

  • Optimized Space and Driving Comfort — Despite its compact size, the Splash offers generous front cabin space and excellent headroom, complemented by a slightly elevated driving position for enhanced comfort in both city and highway driving.
  • Unrivaled Urban Agility — With its short turning radius and compact dimensions, the Suzuki Splash maneuvers and parks with surprising ease, making it the ideal city car for urban environments.

Economy

  • Controlled Running Costs — The Suzuki Splash stands out for its particularly low maintenance and insurance costs, making it an economical vehicle to own and operate, perfect for managing your automotive budget.

Safety

  • Reassuring Passive Safety — The Suzuki Splash achieved 4 stars in Euro NCAP tests, with solid scores for adult (69%) and child (79%) protection, ensuring reliable safety for all occupants.

Handling

  • Safe and Agile Road Handling — Its chassis offers predictable and safe road behavior, with light and precise steering that facilitates maneuvers and ensures excellent agility in the city.

Braking

  • Efficient and Suitable Braking — Equipped with ABS and EBD, the Suzuki Splash benefits from an adequate and effective braking system for its category, offering acceptable stopping distances for serene urban driving.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Opel Agila B (strict twin), Renault Twingo II, Hyundai i10 (Generation 1 & 2), Kia Picanto, Fiat Panda III.
  • Japan / Asia: Honda Fit/Jazz, Toyota Passo, Daihatsu Boon.
  • Emerging markets (India): Hyundai Grand i10, Maruti Suzuki Swift, Ford Figo.

Verdict

The Suzuki Splash is an excellent used car choice for anyone looking for a reliable, economical, and spacious city car in the front. The gasoline engines (1.0 and 1.2) are absolutely to be preferred: they are almost indestructible if basic maintenance is respected. The 1.2 engine (86 or 94 hp) offers the best compromise for leaving cities without straining. However, the 1.3 DDiS diesel engine is to be avoided unless you drive exclusively on the open road, as it suffers from the classic ailments of modern diesels used in urban environments (DPF, EGR, fragile chain).
