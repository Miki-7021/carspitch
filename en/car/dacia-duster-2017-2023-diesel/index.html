Dacia Duster

II · 2017 – 2023

ACHETER Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The Dacia Duster II (also marketed as the Renault Duster in Latin America, Russia, and the Middle East) is a pragmatic, budget-friendly compact SUV that builds upon the massive success of its predecessor. Introduced in late 2017, it retained the rugged B0 platform but featured completely redesigned exterior body panels, vastly improved interior ergonomics, better soundproofing, and upgraded technology (including blind-spot monitoring and a multi-view camera). A mid-cycle facelift in 2021 brought Y-shaped LED signatures, an updated 8-inch infotainment system, and the return of the EDC dual-clutch automatic transmission. While it remains a low-cost vehicle with hard interior plastics and a modest 3-star Euro NCAP safety rating, its unbeatable value-for-money, proven Renault-sourced powertrains, and genuine off-road capabilities (in 4x4 guise) make it a highly sought-after model on the used market. The diesel variants, powered by the legendary 1.5 dCi (K9K) engine, are particularly prized by high-mileage drivers, rural users, and off-road enthusiasts for their low fuel consumption and strong low-end torque.

Strengths 7

  • Unbeatable value for money on the used market
  • Excellent off-road capability in 4x4 versions (Nissan-sourced system)
  • 1.5 dCi engine offers superb fuel economy and low-end grunt
  • 1.3 TCe engine provides genuine dynamic performance
  • Factory LPG (ECO-G) option drastically reduces running costs
  • Improved soundproofing and interior comfort compared to Generation I
  • Robust suspension designed for poor road conditions

Weaknesses 7

  • AdBlue system fragility on the 1.5 Blue dCi (expensive repairs)
  • Catastrophic reliability record of the early 1.2 TCe petrol engine
  • Basic safety features (3 stars Euro NCAP)
  • Interior plastics are hard and scratch easily
  • Wind noise is still noticeable at highway speeds (above 110 km/h)
  • Seats lack lateral support and long-distance comfort
  • MediaNav infotainment system is basic and can be laggy

Check before buying

  • **AdBlue System (Blue dCi):** Check for any warning lights on the dashboard ('Check Anti-Pollution'). Ask if the AdBlue tank has already been replaced.
  • **DPF & EGR (Diesel):** Ensure the car hasn't been used exclusively for short city trips. Look for excessive black smoke under hard acceleration.
  • **4x4 Transmission:** Check the operation of the 2WD/AUTO/LOCK dial. Ensure the rear differential fluid has been changed if the car was used off-road.
  • **Oil Level (1.2 TCe & 1.6 SCe):** Check the dipstick. A low oil level on the 1.2 TCe is a massive red flag.
  • **Air Conditioning:** The AC compressor can be noisy or fail prematurely on 2018-2019 models.
  • **Steering Rack:** Listen for clunking noises when turning the wheel at low speeds, a known issue on early Gen II models.
  • **Bodywork & Undercarriage:** Inspect for rust or impact damage underneath, especially on 4x4 models used for off-roading.

Verdict

The Dacia Duster II is a brilliant utilitarian SUV that democratized access to rugged, practical motoring. If you are specifically looking for a **diesel**, the 1.5 dCi is a fantastic workhorse, offering great torque and incredible fuel efficiency. However, you must be extremely cautious with the **Blue dCi** versions (post-2018) due to the widespread and costly AdBlue tank failures. If you buy a Blue dCi, try to find one that has already had its AdBlue tank replaced under warranty, and ensure your driving profile includes regular highway runs to keep the DPF clean. If you don't strictly need a diesel, the **1.3 TCe** is the best engine in the lineup for performance, while the **1.0 TCe ECO-G (LPG)** is the ultimate choice for low running costs. **Absolutely avoid the 1.2 TCe.**
