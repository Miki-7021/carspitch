Summary

The Dacia Logan II, launched in 2012, represents the second generation of the economical compact sedan from the Romanian manufacturer, a subsidiary of Renault. Based on the B0 platform (shared with the Sandero II and Duster), it retained its predecessor's philosophy: offering maximum space and practicality for an unbeatable price.

Aesthetically, it marked a clear improvement over the first generation, with more modern lines and a more refined interior, although the materials remained simple. A **facelift** took place in **2016**, bringing aesthetic touches (new grille, LED daytime running lights) and interior improvements (new steering wheel, finishes).

The Logan II is appreciated for its robustness, generous interior space, large trunk, and reduced maintenance costs. It positioned itself as a pragmatic alternative to more expensive sedans, targeting families and professionals mindful of their budget. Its general reliability is one of its major assets, particularly with proven powertrains.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent price/performance ratio
  • Generous interior space and large trunk (510 liters)

Weaknesses 5

  • Basic interior material quality and rudimentary finish
  • Imperfect sound insulation, especially on the highway
  • Standard equipment sometimes sparse on entry-level versions
  • Limited performance with small atmospheric petrol engines
  • Exterior and interior design considered austere by some

Check before buying

  • **Maintenance history:** Essential, especially for the 1.5 dCi (regular oil changes with good oil) and the 0.9 TCe (timing chain monitoring).
  • **Bodywork and paint condition:** The paint can be thin, watch out for impacts and scratches.
  • **Air conditioning operation:** Check its effectiveness.
  • **Suspension noises:** On degraded roads, listen for abnormal noises (bushings, shock absorbers).
  • **EGR valve and DPF (1.5 dCi):** If the vehicle has been driven a lot in the city, clogging is possible. Check for the absence of engine warning lights.
  • **Engine noises (0.9 TCe):** Listen for any clicking or whistling at cold start (timing chain).
  • **Electronics:** Test electric windows, central locking, Media Nav system (if present).

Alternatives

These models are often developed specifically for these markets and offer similar performance to the Logan II.

Verdict

The Dacia Logan II is a wise choice for anyone looking for a reliable, spacious, and economical vehicle to buy and to use. It's a no-frills car, but it perfectly fulfills its role as a family or professional transporter.

**For predominantly road or highway use, and for high-mileage drivers, the 1.5 dCi is the most relevant choice.** Its fuel efficiency and torque make it an excellent road companion, provided it has an impeccable maintenance history and you prioritize post-2012 versions.

For urban or suburban use, the 1.0 SCe or 1.2 16V petrol engines are amply sufficient and extremely reliable. The 0.9 TCe offers a good compromise for mixed use, but requires increased vigilance regarding timing chain maintenance.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize models after the 2016 facelift for a slightly improved interior and optimized engines (1.0 SCe, 1.5 dCi 95 hp). Always check the maintenance history and general condition, as the Logan is often purchased for intensive use. It's a car that ages well if properly maintained.
