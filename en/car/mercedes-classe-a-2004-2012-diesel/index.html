Mercedes Classe A

W169 · 2004 – 2012

NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

All Mercedes models

Summary

The second-generation Mercedes-Benz A-Class (W169) retains the atypical "sandwich" architecture of its predecessor (W168), offering an exceptional spaciousness/footprint ratio (3.83 m long) and high-level passive safety. Available in 3-door (C169) and 5-door (W169) versions, it positions itself as a premium monobox city car. The mid-life facelift (Phase 2) introduced in spring 2008 is crucial: it corrects many early defects, notably severe corrosion problems on the openings, and improves perceived quality. While the car is practical, its architecture makes any mechanical intervention complex and expensive (the engine is tilted under the floor).

Strengths 5

  • Unbeatable footprint/spaciousness ratio (flat floor, large 435L trunk).
  • Raised driving position (highly appreciated in the city).
  • Significantly improved finish quality and sound insulation on Phase 2 (post-2008).
  • Excellent passive safety (sandwich architecture that slides the engine under the floor in case of a frontal impact).
  • Very reliable gasoline engines (M266).

What sets it apart 6

Practicality

  • Exceptional Roominess and Ingenious Modularity — Despite its compact size, the A-Class W169 offers remarkable interior space and boot volume thanks to its unique flat-floor architecture. Its elevated driving position is a major asset in urban environments.

Safety

  • Optimal Occupant Protection — Designed with an innovative 'sandwich' architecture, the A-Class W169 ensures exemplary passive safety, allowing the engine to slide under the floor in a frontal collision. It achieved excellent results in crash tests.

Braking

  • Braking Control and Safety — The A-Class W169 is equipped with advanced braking systems such as ABS, EBD, and Brake Assist (BAS) as standard, ensuring short stopping distances and high efficiency for increased safety.

Handling

  • Safe and Agile Road Behavior — Thanks to its revised suspension and standard ESP, the A-Class W169 offers safe and predictable road handling. It proves agile in urban areas and stable on long motorway journeys.

Comfort

  • Refined Finish Quality and Soundproofing — The A-Class W169, particularly in its restyled version (Phase 2), benefits from significantly improved finish quality and careful soundproofing, offering a pleasant and quiet driving environment.

Economy

  • Efficient and Durable Diesel Engine — The OM640 diesel engines of the A-Class W169 are renowned for their fuel efficiency, generous torque, and the inherent robustness of their chain-driven engine block, ideal for long journeys.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Audi A2 (older but similar aluminum concept), BMW 1 Series E87 (rear-wheel drive, less spacious but more dynamic), Volkswagen Golf Plus (bulkier but very spacious), Honda Jazz (excellent reliability, magical modularity).
  • Japan / Asia: Honda Fit/Jazz, Toyota Ractis, Nissan Note.
  • North America: Not marketed (internal alternative: Mercedes B-Class W245 in Canada).
