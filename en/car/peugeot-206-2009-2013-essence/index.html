Peugeot 206+

I (2A/C) · 2009 – 2013

BUY Reliability 80/100 — bon

Summary

The **Peugeot 206+** is a clever evolution of the legendary 206, designed to extend its lifespan at a lower cost while offering an affordable alternative to the heavier and more expensive 207. Aesthetically, it adopts a front end inspired by the 207 and a modernized dashboard, but retains the platform and underpinnings of the original 206 (generation 2A/C).

Positioned as an entry-level vehicle (often referred to as an internal "low-cost" model at Peugeot), it stands out for its mechanical simplicity and still-relevant road handling. While its older design is noticeable in terms of sound insulation, driving position, and passive safety (older generation crash tests), it remains an extremely rational used car choice, particularly with a gasoline engine, thanks to its extremely low maintenance costs and time-proven overall reliability.

Strengths 5

  • Extremely low maintenance and insurance costs
  • Infinite availability of spare parts (new and used)
  • Sound and agile road handling
  • Indestructible gasoline (TU) engines if oil and water levels are monitored
  • Modernized look that ages quite well

Weaknesses 5

  • Dated driving position (steering wheel not adjustable for reach)
  • Very light sound insulation on the highway
  • Quality of interior plastics (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Outdated passive safety (1998 design)
  • Chronic rear axle issue (needle bearings)

Check before buying

  • **Rear Axle:** This is THE weak point of the 206 family. Check if the rear wheels have excessive negative camber (/-/ effect) or if grinding noises are heard over bumps. A faulty rear axle costs between €400 and €600 to replace.
  • **Cylinder Head Gasket (Gasoline):** Inspect the front right corner of the engine (passenger side, above the alternator). A greasy oil seep is very common. If there's no "mayonnaise" in the expansion tank, it's often benign but should be monitored.
  • **Engine Idle (Gasoline):** An oscillating or stalling idle often indicates a clogged throttle body or a faulty stepper motor.
  • **Injector Seals (Diesel):** Remove the engine cover and check for black carbon buildup (tar) around the injectors, accompanied by an exhaust smell in the cabin.
  • **Clutch Cable:** The automatic adjustment system tends to seize, making the pedal stiff or causing creaking noises.

Verdict

The Peugeot 206+ is an excellent choice for new drivers, those on a tight budget, or as a second car. By targeting a **gasoline engine (ideally the 1.4i 75 hp)**, you ensure mechanical peace of mind, provided you absolutely check the condition of the rear axle and the absence of any major cylinder head gasket leaks before purchase. Avoid diesel models if you only drive in the city, as the 1.4 HDi will inevitably get clogged. It's a rational, economical purchase with no unpleasant surprises if the maintenance history is clear.
