Summary

The Renault Safrane Phase 2 (B54) marks the maturity of the diamond brand's flagship. Launched in July 1996, it corrected the early flaws (especially electrical and finish-related) of Phase 1. Its design was softened (bonnet, grille, rear lights) and, above all, it revolutionized its petrol engine offering by abandoning the aging Douvrin blocks in favor of excellent Volvo-sourced engines (the 2.0 16v 4-cylinder and the legendary 2.5 20v 5-cylinder). Comfortable, spacious, and boasting imperial road holding, it remains a highly sought-after 'youngtimer' today, particularly in its high trim levels (Initiale Paris, Baccara). The overall reliability of this Phase 2 is excellent for petrol engines, provided you avoid unmaintained automatic gearboxes.

Strengths 5

  • Imperial ride comfort (ergonomic seats, soundproofing)
  • Exceptional reliability of Volvo-sourced petrol engines (2.0 and 2.5)
  • Giant interior space and modularity (very practical tailgate)
  • Extensive equipment on high trim levels (Initiale: leather, GPS, electric memory seats, xenons)
  • Excellent corrosion resistance (galvanized body)

Weaknesses 5

  • Automatic gearboxes (AD8, LM0) very fragile if not serviced (frequent failure around 150,000 km)
  • High fuel consumption on all petrol engines
  • Availability of spare parts has become critical (many parts classified as NFP - Not Supplied Anymore by Renault)
  • Heater matrix replacement very costly in labor
  • Wear of running gear (wishbones, silent blocks, shock absorber mounts) due to vehicle weight

Check before buying

  • Automatic gearbox oil change history (must have been done every 60,000 km). Test gear changes cold and hot (no jerks tolerated).
  • Presence of coolant smell in the cabin or damp carpet on the passenger side (sign of a punctured heater matrix).
  • Functioning of all electrical equipment (automatic air conditioning, heated/inflatable seats, voice synthesis, cruise control).
  • Condition of Xenon headlights (if equipped): ballasts and bulbs are very expensive and hard to find.
  • Timing belt invoices, particularly on the V6 24v (L7X) where the operation often exceeds €1000.

Official recalls

  • 1998: Recall concerning a risk of inadvertent deployment of seatbelt pretensioners (modification of the wiring harness under the front seats).
  • 1999: Inspection and possible replacement of the airbag control unit following cases of warning lights illuminating without reason.
  • *Note: Phase 2 underwent very few official recalls, as the design had been largely made more reliable compared to Phase 1.*

What sets it apart 7

comfort

  • Imperial Travel Comfort — Enjoy exceptional ride comfort, worthy of the finest grand tourers, thanks to ergonomic seats and meticulous soundproofing that transform every journey into a serene experience.

reliability

  • Legendary Essence Engine Reliability — The Volvo-derived petrol engines (2.0 16v and 2.5 20v) are renowned for their robustness and longevity, offering remarkable peace of mind on the road. The 5-cylinder 2.5 20v is particularly appreciated for its character and mechanical reliability.

practicality

  • Generous Space and Smart Modularity — The Safrane Phase 2 offers giant habitability for all passengers and a very practical tailgate, making it easy to load and unload your luggage or purchases.

equipment

  • Extensive and Refined Equipment — Discover an exceptional level of equipment, especially on high-end trims like Initiale, including leather, GPS, electric memory seats, and xenon headlights, for a luxurious driving experience.

handling

  • Imperial Road Holding — Benefit from exemplary road holding, offering great stability and a sense of security, even on long journeys, for a serene and controlled drive.

durability

  • Galvanized Bodywork, Corrosion Resistance — The fully galvanized bodywork of the Safrane Phase 2 ensures excellent resistance to corrosion, guaranteeing the preservation of its aesthetics and structure over the years.

Performance

  • Smoothness and Pleasure of the V6 24v — For enthusiasts of power and refinement, the 3.0 V6 24v (L7X) engine offers smoothness, silent operation, and power that transform every acceleration into pure pleasure.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 605 / 607 (more dynamic handling), Citroën XM (superior hydraulic comfort but erratic electrical reliability), BMW 5 Series E39 (segment benchmark, RWD), Mercedes E-Class W210, Audi A6 C5.
  • Asia / Others: Toyota Camry, Nissan Maxima (in the rare markets where the Safrane was exported).

Verdict

The Renault Safrane Phase 2 is an excellent choice for enthusiasts of comfortable and affordable large sedans, provided you choose the right version. The absolute best buy is the 2.5 20v (Volvo engine) with a manual gearbox: it offers a high level of driving pleasure, a magnificent sound, and bulletproof reliability. Automatic gearbox versions should be approached with extreme caution and require an impeccable service history. The V6 24v is appealing but its maintenance cost (timing belt) reserves it for informed enthusiasts. The main challenge today is no longer the car's intrinsic reliability, but the shortage of specific spare parts (NFP).
