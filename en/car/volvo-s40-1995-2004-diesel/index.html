Volvo S40

I (VS) · 1995 – 2004

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The **first-generation Volvo S40 (type VS)** marked a historic turning point for the Swedish manufacturer. Gone were the sharp, angular lines, replaced by curves. Developed within the NedCar joint venture in the Netherlands, it shares its platform with the Mitsubishi Carisma.

The model went through two distinct phases: **Phase 1 (1995-2000)**, which was imperfect in terms of finish and road handling, and **Phase 2 (2000-2004)**, which benefited from a major redesign (wider tracks, revised suspension, increased sound insulation, improved safety).

Although the demand here is for **diesel** engines, it is crucial to note that Volvo did not have its own 4-cylinder diesel engine at the time. The brand therefore turned to **Renault** (F8Q and F9Q blocks). While Volvo's petrol engines (modular) are renowned for being indestructible, the French-sourced diesels require extreme vigilance on the used market due to well-documented chronic weaknesses.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent passive safety for its time (SIPS, WHIPS, curtain airbags on Phase 2)
  • Seat comfort (typical Volvo), ideal for long journeys
  • Exceptional reliability of Volvo 4-cylinder petrol engines
  • Build quality and sound insulation significantly improved on Phase 2 (2000-2004)
  • Very attractive price/performance ratio on the used market today

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of diesel engines (Renault-sourced) and the 1.8i GDI (Mitsubishi-sourced)
  • Clumsy road handling and harsh damping on Phase 1 (1995-2000)
  • Very large turning radius (tedious city maneuvers)
  • Limited rear passenger space for a sedan of this size
  • Premature wear of suspension bushings and anti-roll bar links

Check before buying

  • **On Diesels (102/115 hp):** Excessive turbo whistling, blue (oil) or black (EGR/injection) smoke, oil change history (crucial).
  • **On Diesels (90/95 hp):** Traces of oil leaks around the cylinder head gasket, condition and age of the accessory belt.
  • **On Petrols:** Clattering noise at idle on the timing side (faulty VVT pulley on Phase 2).
  • **Chassis:** Dull thuds over speed bumps (shock absorber mounts, front wishbone bushings or rear trailing arm bushings).
  • **Electrics:** Functioning of central locking, immobilizer, and automatic air conditioning.

Official recalls

  • - **November 2004:** Risk of fuel leak on petrol models (model years 2000-2004) due to hose rubbing.
  • - **February 2004:** Replacement of the brake booster vacuum pump on certain models.
  • - **June 2001:** Inspection and possible replacement of front seat belt anchorages.
  • - **1998:** Power steering rack sealing issue (Phase 1).

Verdict

Buying a first-generation Volvo S40 requires being very selective. **If you are looking for a diesel, caution is advised.** The Renault-sourced engines (1.9 TD, D, and dCi) have tarnished this model's reliability reputation. The 102 and 115 hp versions offer good driving pleasure but require a clear maintenance history (frequent oil changes, monitored turbo and EGR) to avoid financial ruin.

**The true expert choice for the S40 I is the petrol engines (excluding 1.8i GDI)**, ideally a 2.0 or a 2.0T in **Phase 2 (after mid-2000)**. You will then get an extremely robust, comfortable, safe, and very affordable sedan.
