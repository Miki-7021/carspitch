Peugeot 208

II · 2019 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 58/100 — moyen

Summary

The **second-generation Peugeot 208 (P21)**, launched in 2019, is based on the Stellantis group's modular CMP platform. A true commercial success (best-selling car in Europe in 2022), it stands out with its bold design, "fang" daytime running lights, and 3D i-Cockpit interior. Voted Car of the Year 2020, it received a **facelift in late 2023** (new three-claw light signature, new logo, updated multimedia system). While its dynamic qualities and style are undeniable, its career has been heavily marred by the **chronic reliability issues of its internal combustion engines**, particularly the 1.2 PureTech (wet belt) and 1.5 BlueHDi (AdBlue and camshaft chain). The arrival of micro-hybrid (MHEV) versions with a timing chain in 2024 marks a saving turning point.

  • Very average rear spaciousness and narrow access
  • Boot volume in the lower average (especially on e-208)
  • Slow and unintuitive multimedia system (before 2023 facelift)
  • Reliability of the AdBlue system on the diesel

Check before buying

  • **On 1.2 PureTech**: Clear service history is MANDATORY. Check if the belt has been changed, visually inspect its condition, look for oil pressure warning lights.
  • **On 1.5 BlueHDi**: Presence of 'Antipollution fault' or 'Engine fault' warning lights (AdBlue). Metallic rattling noise at the top of the engine (camshaft chain).
  • **On e-208**: Air conditioning operation (the compressor also cools the battery). AC charging test to validate the onboard charger.
  • **Electronics**: Touchscreen bugs, intermittent restarts, operation of driving aids (reversing camera often blurry or faulty).

Verdict

The Peugeot 208 II is a 'love at first sight' car, but buying a used internal combustion version (before 2024) is a lottery. **We strongly advise against buying a 1.2 PureTech from before 2023** without absolute proof of recent timing belt replacement AND strict annual maintenance. The 1.5 BlueHDi diesel is also risky (AdBlue, chain).

**How to buy a 208 with peace of mind?** 1. Opt for the **new 1.2 Hybrid 48V versions (100 or 136 hp)** introduced in 2024, equipped with a timing chain. 2. Turn to the **electric e-208**, generally satisfactory despite some peripheral weaknesses (charger). 3. If you are outside Europe (Mercosur), the 1.6 VTi or 1.0 Firefly versions are excellent, very robust choices.
