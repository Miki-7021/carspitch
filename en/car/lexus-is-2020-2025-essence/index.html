Lexus IS

III facelift (XE30) · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 94/100 — excellent

Summary

The third-generation Lexus IS (XE30) received a major facelift in late 2020 (often referred to as 'generation 3.5' or the 2021 model). Although the platform remains identical, the bodywork was completely redesigned, the tracks widened, the chassis stiffened, and the infotainment updated with a touchscreen brought closer to the driver. Withdrawn from most Western European markets in favor of the brand's SUVs, this version focused on North America, Japan, and the Middle East. It stands out with a rich offering of traditional gasoline engines (4-cylinder turbo, naturally aspirated V6, and the spectacular return of the V8 with the IS 500). Overall reliability is exceptional, true to Lexus's reputation, positioning it as one of the most durable premium sedans on the market.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability (ADAC breakdown rate of 1.2/1000, among the best on the market)
  • Flawless assembly and interior finish quality (TÜV Report 3-year defect rate of only 9.5%)
  • Extremely robust Aisin automatic gearboxes (8-speed on RWD, 6-speed on AWD)
  • Rare availability of a naturally aspirated V8 (IS 500) and a naturally aspirated V6 (IS 350) in this segment
  • Greatly improved road handling since the 2020 facelift (stiffened chassis, new suspensions)

Weaknesses 5

  • High fuel consumption on V6 and V8 engines compared to German 4-cylinder turbos
  • Rear seat space and trunk volume below the category average
  • Infotainment system still somewhat dated in its menus, despite the welcome addition of the touchscreen
  • 6-speed automatic gearbox on AWD versions (V6) a bit slow compared to current standards
  • Model almost impossible to find in Western Europe (importation required for gasoline versions)

Check before buying

  • Low-pressure fuel pump: check if the vehicle is affected by the NHTSA recall (2022 models).
  • Front tire wear: F Sport trims have a suspension geometry (camber/caster) that tends to prematurely wear the inside of the front tires.
  • Condition of the rims: alloy rims (especially the 19-inch BBS or Enkei) are highly exposed to curb scratches.
  • Maintenance history: although very reliable, the V6 and V8 engines require regular and documented maintenance to maintain their longevity.

Alternatives

- **Europe / Global:** BMW 3 Series (G20) (more technological, efficient turbo engines), Mercedes-Benz C-Class (W206), Audi A4 (B9). - **North America / Asia:** Acura TLX (excellent Japanese alternative with the V6 Turbo Type S), Genesis G70 (very good quality/price/performance ratio), Infiniti Q50 (aging but equipped with a twin-turbo V6).

Verdict

Buying a '3.5' generation Lexus IS (2020-2025) is one of the safest and most rational choices in the premium automotive market. If you are looking for the latest cutting-edge technologies or the most spacious interior, the German competitors will do better. However, if your absolute priority is **long-term reliability**, build quality, and 'old school' mechanical driving pleasure (especially with the fantastic naturally aspirated V6 and V8 engines), the IS is untouchable. Data from ADAC, TÜV Report, and What Car? (4.5/5) confirm that this sedan is built to last for decades with minimal issues. Just make sure the fuel pump recall has been performed on 2022 models.
