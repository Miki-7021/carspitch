Ram 1500

4th gen (DS) · 2009 – 2018

All Ram models

Summary

The 4th generation of the Ram 1500 (code name DS) marks a major turning point in the history of American pickup trucks. Initially launched under the Dodge brand in 2009, it simply became "Ram 1500" in 2010 following the split of the Chrysler group brands. It radically distinguishes itself from its competitors (F-150, Silverado) by abandoning rear leaf springs in favor of coil springs (and an optional air suspension), offering unmatched ride comfort in the category.

In 2013, a facelift brought the highly renowned 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox (TorqueFlite 8) and the Pentastar V6. In 2014, Ram struck a major blow by introducing the 3.0L V6 EcoDiesel (supplied by the Italian VM Motori), becoming the first modern light-duty pickup to offer a diesel engine in the United States. While this engine appealed with its torque and record fuel consumption, it unfortunately proved to be a nightmare in terms of reliability, accumulating major recalls (EGR, injection pump, crankshaft sensor). The model continued to be produced beyond 2018 under the name "Ram 1500 Classic".

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (rear coil springs)
  • Responsive and reliable 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox (from 2013)
  • Very intuitive Uconnect infotainment system (8.4 inches)
  • Timeless "mini-truck" look (Big Rig styling)
  • Spacious and well-finished cabin on high-end trims (Laramie, Longhorn)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 3.0L EcoDiesel engine (exorbitant repair costs)
  • Very temperamental air suspension (Active-Level) in cold weather (leaks, dead compressor)
  • Lower payload than the competition (trade-off for the coil springs)
  • Very high fuel consumption of the older engines (3.7 V6, 4.7 V8) and pre-2013 4/5/6-speed gearboxes
  • Susceptibility to corrosion on the rear wheel arches and rocker panels

Check before buying

  • Complete recall history (CRUCIAL on the EcoDiesel, see NHTSA list)
  • Operation of the air suspension: raise and lower the vehicle in all positions without error messages
  • Ticking noise on cold start on the 5.7 Hemi (broken exhaust studs or worn lifters)
  • Smell of coolant or drop in level on the EcoDiesel (EGR leak)
  • Condition of the tailgate (frequent recalls for unexpected opening)

Official recalls

The Ram 1500 (2009-2018) has been the subject of numerous recalls by the NHTSA. Here are the most critical ones, with particular attention to the EcoDiesel engine:

3.0L ECODIESEL ENGINE (Critical):

  • [2023-06-08] & [2020-08-13] (Crankshaft sensor): Delamination of the crankshaft position sensor reluctor wheel, causing a loss of synchronization and sudden engine stalling while driving.
  • [2022-06-09] (HPFP fuel pump): Internal failure of the high-pressure pump (Bosch CP4) introducing metallic debris (shavings) throughout the fuel system, requiring the complete replacement of the injection system.
  • [2019-10-24] (EGR cooler): Cracking of the EGR valve cooler allowing coolant to enter the intake. Risk of coolant combustion (fire) or hydrostatic engine lock.

SAFETY & AIRBAGS:

  • [2025-09-11], [2025-01-16] & [2021-08-12]: Possible rupture of the side curtain airbag inflators (manufacturing defect), projecting metallic fragments.
  • [2017-05-09]: Inadvertent deployment of the airbags and pretensioners during off-road driving or in the event of an underbody impact.

TRANSMISSION & STEERING:

  • [2019-04-25] (Driveshaft): Incomplete weld on the rear driveshaft that could lead to its rupture.
  • [2018-08-23] (Differential): Insufficient filling of the rear differential at the factory, leading to its destruction.
  • [2018-05-01], [2018-02-08] & [2017-12-20] (Automatic gearbox): Problems related to the Park position interlock (BTSI) or the linkage, allowing the vehicle to roll even without the key or without pressing the brake.
  • [2017-03-23]: Differential axis retaining screw that can loosen and lock the axle.
  • [2016-05-16]: Manufacturing defect of the front half-shafts (risk of rupture).
  • [2016-03-23] (Steering): Contamination of the electric power steering (EPS) control board leading to a loss of assist.

BODYWORK & EQUIPMENT:

  • [2019-05-09] & [2018-07-27] (Tailgate): Breakage of a limiter tab on the power tailgate actuator, causing the tailgate to open unexpectedly while driving and the loss of cargo.
  • [2018-06-14]: Loss of the rearview camera image.
  • [2018-05-17]: Wiring defect preventing the deactivation of the cruise control.
  • [2018-03-08] & [2017-07-07] (Fuel): Problems with fuel tank straps (corrosion) or the tank control valve (leak in the event of a rollover).

What sets it apart 6

economy

  • Optimized Fuel Consumption — The EcoDiesel engine offers remarkable efficiency, allowing you to cover long distances with reduced fuel consumption, a major asset for professionals.

Performance

  • Impressive Engine Torque — Enjoy generous torque from low RPMs, ideal for towing and transporting heavy loads, ensuring exceptional power and responsiveness.

comfort

  • Unrivaled Ride Comfort — Thanks to its innovative coil-spring rear suspension, the Ram 1500 offers a smooth and refined driving experience, even on challenging terrain.

technology

  • ZF 8-Speed Automatic Transmission — The TorqueFlite 8 automatic transmission, renowned for its smoothness and responsiveness, optimizes engine power and contributes to a pleasant and efficient drive.

Design

  • Iconic "Big Rig" Styling — Its imposing stance and heavy-duty truck-inspired design give the Ram 1500 an incomparable road presence and a timeless character.

interior

  • Spacious Cabin and Intuitive Technology — The interior offers generous space and refined finishes, complemented by the Uconnect infotainment system for easy connectivity and control.

Alternatives

  • Ford F-150 (2009-2014 / 2015-2020): The leader. Offers better payload capacities, an aluminum body (from 2015) impervious to rust, and highly performant EcoBoost V6 engines (but requiring rigorous maintenance of the timing chains).
  • Chevrolet Silverado 1500 / GMC Sierra 1500: Very reliable V8 engines (5.3L and 6.2L) (despite some issues with AFM cylinder deactivation). The 3.0L Duramax diesel (which appeared later) is infinitely more reliable than Ram's EcoDiesel.
  • Toyota Tundra (2007-2021): The absolute benchmark for reliability (indestructible 5.7L V8). In return: dated technology, gargantuan fuel consumption, and lower comfort than the Ram.

Verdict

The 4th generation Ram 1500 is an extremely appealing pickup, offering the best comfort in its category and a killer look. However, the 3.0L EcoDiesel engine is to be avoided at all costs, unless the vehicle has already undergone the replacement of the HPFP pump, the EGR cooler, and the reluctor wheel, and you have a substantial repair budget. Breakdowns of this VM Motori engine are catastrophic and frequent.

The best choice: Opt without hesitation for the 5.7L V8 Hemi coupled with the 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox (post-2013). Although the exhaust manifold studs must be monitored (the famous "Hemi tick"), it is a robust, high-performance package with excellent resale value. Also avoid the air suspension if you live in a cold region.
