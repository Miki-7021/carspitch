Summary

The Renault Scénic I (initially called Mégane Scénic until the 1999 facelift) is the pioneer of compact MPVs in Europe. Based on the Mégane I platform, it revolutionized the family market thanks to its spaciousness, modularity (3 independent rear seats), and numerous storage compartments.

Major evolutions:

  • Phase 1 (1996-1999): Launch with 8-valve petrol engines (evolved Energy and Cléon-Fonte) and indirect injection diesels.
  • Phase 2 (1999-2003): Deep facelift (front end, lights, improved interior). Introduction of 16-valve petrol engines (more powerful but with early issues) and direct injection diesels (dTi then dCi).
  • Scénic RX4 (2000-2003): All-wheel drive (4x4) adventurer version developed with Steyr-Daimler-Puch.

Today, it is a very low-priced used vehicle, sought after for its utility aspect. The mechanical reliability of the petrol engines is generally good if maintenance has been followed, but the model suffers from numerous electrical ailments and aging peripherals.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional spaciousness and modularity (3 real rear seats)
  • Suspension comfort and smooth driving
  • Excellent peripheral visibility (high driving position)
  • Negligible maintenance cost and parts availability (junkyards, oscaro)
  • Mechanically very durable 8v (F3R) and 16v (K4M, F4R) petrol engines

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous electrical and electronic reliability (UCH, power windows, immobilizer)
  • Ignition coils on 16v petrol engines (Phase 2)
  • Interior plastic quality (peeling, squeaks)
  • Light soundproofing on the highway (especially Phase 1)
  • Scénic RX4 transmission (very fragile and expensive driveshaft)

Check before buying

  • Immobilizer: Major problem. The key (infrared on Ph1, radio on Ph2) desynchronizes. Check that both keys start the vehicle.
  • Cabin electrics: Absolutely test all power windows, central locking, ventilation, and the dashboard.
  • Rear axle: A common issue with Renaults of this era. Check if the rear wheels have excessive negative camber (Gordini style) or if grinding noises are heard (worn suspension arm bearings).
  • Water ingress: Check for dampness under the front carpets (clogged windshield scuttle drains) and the operation of sunroofs (if equipped).
  • 16v Petrol Engines: Ask for invoices for the timing belt (must include the water pump and ideally the dephaser pulley) and check the brand of the coils.

Official recalls

  • 1999: Recall on dTi models for a risk of brake assistance loss (vacuum pump).,- 2000: Replacement of the airbag ECU due to inadvertent deployments.,- 2001: Inspection and possible replacement of front seat belts.,- 2002: Risk of front lower suspension arm breakage (models manufactured until 1999).,- 2003: Alternator wiring harness potentially rubbing and causing a short circuit.

What sets it apart 6

practicality

  • Generous interior space and modularity — Enjoy exceptional spaciousness and three independent rear seats, offering unparalleled flexibility for all your family adventures. Numerous clever storage compartments complete this optimized space.

comfort

  • Superior driving comfort — Appreciate a soft suspension and pleasant driving experience, complemented by a high driving position that ensures excellent visibility and a sense of control on the road.

reliability

  • Robust petrol engines — The petrol engines, particularly the 8v (F3R) and 16v (K4M, F4R) units, are renowned for their mechanical endurance and reliability, guaranteeing many serene kilometers.

economy

  • Controlled maintenance and accessible parts — Benefit from advantageous maintenance costs and wide availability of parts, making this vehicle economical for daily use.

Performance

  • Dynamic petrol powertrains — Choose from a range of powerful petrol engines, offering an excellent balance between power and flexibility, ideal for both city and long-distance journeys. The 1.6 16v is particularly valued for its versatility.

safety

  • Optimal peripheral visibility — The design of the Scénic I, with its elevated driving position, offers exceptional panoramic visibility, actively contributing to your safety and that of your passengers.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Citroën Xsara Picasso (its main rival, very reliable in petrol), Opel Zafira A (7 seats), Fiat Multipla (divisive design but 6 seats and very wide), Volkswagen Touran (appeared in 2003, more expensive).
  • Asia / Japan: Honda Stream, Toyota Corolla Verso.
  • Americas: Chevrolet Zafira (Opel rebadge).

Verdict

The Renault Scénic I is today a utility 'runabout' par excellence. While the diesels (dTi and dCi) of this era are to be avoided due to catastrophic failures, the petrol versions are much more recommendable. The 1.6 16v (107 hp) from Phase 2 is the best choice, provided that the ignition coils have been replaced with reliable models and that the timing belt is up to date. The main enemy of this vehicle is not its petrol engine, but its onboard electronics (immobilizer, UCH) and the aging of its running gear. To be bought at a very low price with a clear maintenance history.
