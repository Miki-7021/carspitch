Summary

Presentation of the Tesla Model S (Generation II / 'Palladium' Refresh)

Launched in 2021, this major update (often called 'Phase 2' or Project Palladium) of the Model S redefines the standards of the luxury electric sedan. While the exterior design evolves subtly (wider bumpers, chrome delete), the interior and technical underpinnings are completely redesigned.

It stands out with the introduction of the controversial 'Yoke' steering wheel (the round steering wheel would become available later), the complete removal of stalks (turn signals and wipers on the steering wheel, gear selection on the touchscreen), and a tiltable horizontal central screen. Technically, it introduces a new thermal architecture (ultra-efficient heat pump), new battery modules, and especially the three-motor Plaid version, featuring carbon-sleeved rotors, offering over 1000 hp.

Expert's overall rating: A technological showcase with ballistic performance and unparalleled efficiency, but whose divisive ergonomics and certain finish details require compromises.

Strengths 5

  • Astonishing performance (especially in Plaid version)
  • Excellent energy efficiency and real-world range
  • Integrated and infallible Tesla Supercharger network
  • Ultra-smooth infotainment system (AMD Ryzen processor)
  • Interior space and cargo capacity (rear trunk + Frunk)

Weaknesses 5

  • Ergonomics without stalks (turn signals on the steering wheel) dangerous in roundabouts
  • Rapid degradation of the Yoke steering wheel coating (on 2021-2023 models)
  • Fragility of front driveshafts on the Plaid version (vibrations)
  • Original braking undersized for the weight and power of the Plaid (requires the Track Package for track use)
  • Vertiginous depreciation on the used car market

Check before buying

  • Vibrations in the steering wheel or floor during strong accelerations (sign of worn driveshafts, very common on Plaid).
  • Condition of the steering wheel coating (Yoke or round) at 10 and 2 o'clock.
  • Asymmetrical tire wear (indicates a common geometry issue from the factory).
  • Functionality of the rear screen and the tilt of the front screen.
  • Panel gaps (doors, trunk) and paint quality (micro-scratches).

Official recalls

Official Manufacturer Recalls

*Note: The majority of Tesla recalls are resolved remotely via software updates (OTA - Over The Air).*

  • December 2023 (OTA): Autopilot. Improved driver monitoring alerts following an NHTSA investigation.
  • July 2023 (Hardware): Seat belts. Possible improper connection of front seat belt pretensioners (Service Center intervention).
  • February 2023 (OTA): FSD Beta. Autonomous driving software update to correct potentially unsafe behaviors at intersections.
  • September 2022 (OTA): Power windows. The automatic reversal system (anti-pinch) might not react quickly enough.
  • December 2021 (Hardware): Front trunk latch (Frunk). Risk of opening while driving due to misalignment of the secondary latch.

Verdict

Expert's Verdict

The Tesla Model S 'Palladium' is a technological tour de force. The Dual Motor version is the sensible choice: it offers gargantuan range, performance already worthy of a supercar, and solid mechanical reliability. The Plaid version is a road-legal dragster, but its power takes a toll on its own running gear (driveshafts, tires, brakes).

Buying a recent used model is particularly attractive due to the significant depreciation these models experience. However, the ergonomics (absence of stalks) require an extended test drive before purchase. Prioritize a 2023+ model (Hardware 4) with a round steering wheel if the Yoke puts you off, and thoroughly inspect the running gear on Plaid versions.
