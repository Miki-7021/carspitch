Summary

Seat Arona I Facelift (KJ7)

Initially launched in 2017, the Seat Arona received a mid-life facelift in April 2021. This refresh brings a modernized front end (round fog lights repositioned on the Xperience trim), 100% LED headlights as standard, and most notably, a thoroughly redesigned interior with soft-touch materials and a new floating infotainment screen (MIB3 system).

**⚠️ EXPERT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:** Although your request targets the diesel engine, it is imperative to specify as an expert that **the facelifted Seat Arona (2021-2025) has NEVER been marketed with a diesel engine**. The Volkswagen group removed the 1.6 TDI engine (95 and 115 hp) from the Arona and Ibiza catalog at the end of 2020, just before the facelift, due to Euro 6d anti-pollution standards and the collapse of demand for diesel in the urban B-SUV segment. This generation of the Arona is exclusively offered with petrol (TSI) and natural gas (TGI) engines. The data concerning the 1.6 TDI below is provided for context for pre-facelift models (2017-2020).

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic and precise road handling (excellent MQB-A0 chassis)
  • Generous rear passenger space for its size (4.15 m)
  • Very decent boot volume (400 liters, except TGI at 282 liters)
  • Perceived quality significantly improved since the facelift (foamed dashboard)
  • Ergonomics retaining physical climate controls

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of hybrid (even mild MHEV) or diesel engine
  • Recurring bugs in the MIB3 multimedia system (especially on 2021-2022 model years)
  • Firm suspension, particularly on FR trim with 18-inch wheels
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Reliability of the DSG7 gearbox (DQ200) still questionable in 100% urban use

Check before buying

  • Infotainment system stability: test navigation, Bluetooth, and screen responsiveness during the test drive.
  • DSG7 gearbox behavior: no jerking, shuddering, or slipping should be felt when shifting from 1st to 2nd gear.
  • Presence of the engine cover on the 1.5 TSI (often removed following an official recall and sometimes not replaced).
  • Front tire wear: the incisive front axle can lead to asymmetrical wear if the alignment is out of adjustment.

Verdict

The facelifted Seat Arona (2021-2025) is an excellent urban SUV, having reached full maturity. The facelift corrected its main flaw: an interior that was once too austere and low-end. Mechanically, the absence of diesel (removed in 2020) is not a handicap in this segment, as the 1.0 TSI engines prove to be economical and very reliable.

**Buying advice:** Avoid looking for a diesel in this generation, it does not exist. The most rational and reliable choice is the **1.0 TSI 110/115 hp with a manual gearbox**. If you opt for the DSG7 automatic gearbox, prioritize a model that has been driven on the open road rather than exclusively in the city, to preserve the dry clutches. Negotiate if the multimedia system shows sluggishness (a dealership update will be necessary).
