Suzuki Jimny

III (JB64) · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The Suzuki Jimny produced since 2018 (JB64 codes for the Japanese market and JB74 for the global market, often called Jimny Sierra) represents the 4th generation of the famous small off-roader, although sometimes classified as the 3rd generation in some catalogs depending on the inclusion of the very first LJ models.

A true automotive phenomenon, it generated such enthusiasm that delivery times sometimes exceeded two years. Built on a true ladder frame with rigid axles and switchable 4x4 transmission with a transfer case (ALLGRIP PRO), it's a purebred off-roader. In Europe, due to CO2 emission standards, it was withdrawn from the passenger vehicle market to return exclusively as a 2-seater commercial vehicle.

Its rustic and proven design makes it a vehicle of formidable mechanical reliability, although one must accept its compromises: dated road handling, light soundproofing, and high fuel consumption given its size. **Overall design rating: 8.5/10 (as an off-roader).**

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional off-road capabilities (ladder frame, approach/departure angles, low-range gearbox)
  • Overall mechanical reliability (indestructible K15B engine, robust gearboxes)
  • Timeless and very successful neo-retro design
  • Almost non-existent depreciation (highly sought-after used vehicle)
  • Ideal compactness for narrow trails

Weaknesses 5

  • Imprecise road handling and sensitive to crosswinds
  • High fuel consumption (aerodynamics of a Norman wardrobe)
  • Non-existent trunk in 4-seater configuration (85 liters)
  • Very light soundproofing on the highway
  • Fragile paint (sensitive to branch scratches)

Check before buying

  • Underbody and chassis: look for impacts, deep scratches, or deformations related to intensive off-road use.
  • Corrosion: although better protected than its predecessors, a preventive anti-rust treatment of the chassis is strongly advised in cold/salty regions.
  • Steering vibrations: test the vehicle between 70 and 90 km/h to detect the famous "Death Wobble".
  • Windshield: very vertical, it is extremely exposed to gravel impacts and cracks easily.
  • Transmission fluid change history: axles and transfer case must be drained regularly.

Verdict

The Suzuki Jimny (2018-2025) is an impulse buy combined with an extremely rational choice in terms of reliability. Its 1.5L naturally aspirated engine is a marvel of simplicity that will protect you from the costly breakdowns of complex modern powertrains. However, you must buy it with full awareness: it is not a comfortable urban SUV, but a true off-road tool. If you accept its rustic road handling and fuel consumption, it is an endearing, indestructible vehicle with exceptional resale value.
