Mercedes EQB

X243 · 2021 – 2025

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

Summary

The Mercedes-Benz EQB (X243 generation) is the 100% electric counterpart to the GLB family SUV. Based on the MFA2 platform (modified to integrate the batteries), it stands out in the electric vehicle market with its ability to offer up to 7 seats in a relatively compact format (4.68 m). Although it does not benefit from a dedicated electric-only platform (unlike the EQE/EQS range), it offers an excellent level of comfort, appreciable modularity, and the MBUX technological interface. A facelift was carried out at the end of 2023, bringing a starry 'Black Panel' grille, a new capacitive steering wheel, and an update to the infotainment system. Overall rating: A highly accomplished electric family SUV in terms of interior space, but penalized by average efficiency and fast charging power (100 kW) that lags behind the competition.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional interior space for its size (7-seat option very rare in electric vehicles)
  • Top-tier suspension comfort and soundproofing
  • Fluid, intuitive MBUX infotainment system and efficient route planner
  • Interior presentation quality (screens, ambient lighting)
  • Overall reliability of the electric powertrain

Weaknesses 5

  • DC fast charging speed limited to 100 kW (longer stop times on the highway)
  • Slightly high highway consumption (boxy SUV aerodynamics)
  • Very heavy weight (over 2.1 tons unladen) penalizing agility
  • 'Piano Black' plastics on the center console very sensitive to scratches and dust
  • Reduced trunk volume when the 7 seats are deployed

Check before buying

  • Tire wear: The high weight and instant torque wear out the rubber quickly, especially at the front on the 250/250+ versions.
  • Brake discs: Check for rust or deep scratches. Regenerative braking limits the use of mechanical brakes, which can seize or oxidize.
  • MBUX system: Ensure the screen does not show sluggishness or unexpected restarts (often requires a simple software update).
  • Parasitic noises: Some interior trim creaks may appear on rough roads (typical of the MFA2 platform).
  • Charging cables: Check the condition of the supplied cables (Type 2 and domestic plug) and the absence of overheating at the connectors.

Verdict

The Mercedes EQB is an extremely relevant niche choice for large families looking for a compact premium 7-seat electric SUV. Its mechanical and electronic reliability has been excellent so far, with Mercedes electric motors proving to be robust. Its main flaw lies in its non-dedicated electric architecture, which limits its charging speed (100 kW) and highway efficiency. For a purchase, favor the **EQB 250+** version, which offers the best range thanks to its slightly larger battery and more efficient motor. Negotiate if the tires are worn or if the vehicle has not received its latest software updates.
