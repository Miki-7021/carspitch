KGM Korando

Summary

The 4th generation KGM Korando (code name C300), initially launched under the SsangYong brand before the takeover and name change to KG Mobility (KGM) in 2023, marks a spectacular leap forward for the Korean manufacturer. Positioned in the highly competitive compact SUV segment (C-SUV), it abandons the divisive styling of its predecessors for a more consensual and European design. It stands out with generous interior space, abundant equipment right from the mid-range trims, and top-flight safety (5 stars Euro NCAP). While its badge lacks prestige compared to the market leaders, it compensates with a very aggressive price-to-features ratio. The 1.5 GDI-Turbo petrol engine, developed in-house, offers good drivability but suffers from higher-than-average fuel consumption for the category. **Overall generation rating: 7.5/10**.

Strengths 6

  • Unbeatable price-to-equipment ratio on the used market
  • Very generous rear roominess and accommodating trunk (551 liters)
  • Smooth and very reliable 6-speed Aisin automatic gearbox
  • Passive and active safety (5 stars Euro NCAP, numerous standard driving aids)
  • Towing capacity (up to 1.5t in petrol, 2t in diesel)
  • Long-term manufacturer warranty (often 5 to 7 years depending on markets)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very disappointing fuel consumption of the 1.5 GDI-Turbo engine (easily 9L/100km in the city or on the highway)
  • Steep depreciation on the used market (little-known brand, name change SsangYong -> KGM)
  • Slightly firm damping at low speeds (especially with 19-inch wheels)
  • Sparse network of dealerships and authorized repairers
  • Multimedia system sometimes slow and prone to display bugs

Check before buying

  • Complete maintenance history (crucial to maintain the 5/7 year manufacturer warranty)
  • Smooth operation of the automatic gearbox (no jerks when shifting from D to R)
  • Front tire wear (the front axle is heavy and can wear out tires prematurely)
  • Absence of warning lights related to driving aids (sensors sensitive to dirt or requiring recalibration)
  • On the diesel: check for the absence of black smoke upon acceleration (sign of clogged EGR/DPF)

Verdict

The KGM (SsangYong) Korando C300 is the pragmatic choice par excellence. If you target the 1.5 GDI-Turbo petrol engine, you will benefit from a flexible, quiet, and overall very reliable engine, coupled with an excellent Aisin automatic gearbox. However, you must absolutely accept its main flaw: high fuel consumption, which makes it costly for high-mileage drivers. Buying it used is an excellent financial deal thanks to massive depreciation, but it should be considered a vehicle to keep until the end, as its subsequent resale may prove difficult. Favor a model with a clear history to take advantage of the remainder of the long manufacturer warranty.
