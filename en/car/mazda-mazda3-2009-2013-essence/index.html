Mazda Mazda3

II (BL) · 2009 – 2013

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

All Mazda models

Summary

The second-generation Mazda3 (Type BL), launched in 2009 and facelifted in late 2011, builds on the excellent chassis of the first generation (BK) while correcting its main flaw: susceptibility to corrosion. Positioned as a dynamic compact, it offers a driving experience superior to the segment average thanks to precise steering and sharp running gear. While the gasoline engines (MZR and the first Skyactiv-G at the end of their career in some markets) shine with their almost irreproachable reliability, the diesel versions (shared with PSA or developed in-house) tarnish the record with costly failures. It is a highly recommendable used model, provided you target the gasoline versions.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of naturally aspirated gasoline engines (MZR and Skyactiv-G)
  • Dynamic road handling and precise steering
  • Cockpit ergonomics and driving position
  • Corrosion resistance significantly improved compared to the previous generation (BK)
  • Manual gearboxes with excellent feel

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 2.2 MZR-CD diesel engine
  • Imperfect sound insulation (road and wind noise on the highway)
  • Dashboard that can melt or deform under the effect of the sun (especially in hot regions)
  • Suspension comfort a bit firm at low speeds
  • Fragile paint (sensitive to stone chips and micro-scratches)

Check before buying

  • Condition of the dashboard (peeling above the gauges or sticky appearance).
  • On diesels: oil level (dilution) and DPF history.
  • On the 1.6 diesel: cleanliness around the injectors (absence of black tar).
  • On the MPS: exhaust smoke at idle (turbo seals) and chain noise when cold.
  • Running gear: wear of rear trailing arm bushings and anti-roll bar links (clunking on degraded roads).

Official recalls

  • September 2016 (Tailgate Struts): Risk of corrosion and breakage of the rear tailgate struts. Free replacement at the dealership.
  • February 2017 (Driver's Seat): Risk of cracking and breakage of the driver's seat height adjustment links, which could lead to unexpected tilting of the seat.
  • June 2011 (Wipers): Possible failure of the front wiper motor relay, leading to their sudden stop.
  • Technical Note (Non-safety recall): Warranty extension offered in some countries (notably North America and Australia, rarer in Europe) for the replacement of dashboards melted by the sun.

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Unrivaled Petrol Engine Reliability — Enjoy absolute peace of mind thanks to the MZR and Skyactiv-G petrol engines, renowned for their exceptional robustness, maintenance-free timing chain, and ability to cover very high mileages without fault.
  • Enhanced Structural Durability — The Mazda3 II stands out with significantly improved corrosion resistance compared to the previous generation, ensuring increased longevity and preservation of its value over the years.

Safety

  • Optimal Safety — Travel with complete confidence. The Mazda3 II achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult (89%) and child (82%) protection, ensuring a high level of safety for all occupants.
  • Effective and Reassuring Braking — Benefit from a powerful and responsive braking system, ensuring short stopping distances and a reassuring pedal feel. A major asset for your active safety every day.

Performance

  • Dynamic Driving Pleasure — Discover a superior driving experience. Its sharp chassis, precise and responsive steering, and controlled body roll offer sporty and engaging road holding, transforming every journey into true pleasure.
  • Precise Manual Gearbox — Enjoy a fluid and engaging driving experience thanks to manual gearboxes renowned for their excellent handling and precision, adding to the pleasure of every gear change.

Comfort

  • Ergonomics and Driving Comfort — Appreciate a driver-focused cockpit, with intuitive ergonomics and an excellent driving position, making long journeys as enjoyable as urban commutes.

Alternatives

  • Europe : Volkswagen Golf VI (higher trim but TSI/TDI engines from that era are temperamental), Honda Civic VIII/IX (excellent gasoline reliability), Ford Focus III (similar chassis, reliable gasoline engines), Toyota Auris (hybrid available).
  • North America : Honda Civic, Toyota Corolla, Hyundai Elantra, Subaru Impreza.
  • Asia / Emerging Markets : Nissan Tiida/Pulsar, Mitsubishi Lancer, Toyota Corolla Altis.

Verdict

The Mazda3 (BL) is an excellent used compact, on the strict condition of opting for a gasoline engine. The 1.6 and 2.0 MZR blocks are models of robustness that handle high mileages without flinching, with minimalist maintenance (chain timing). The chassis is a delight for driving enthusiasts. However, the diesel versions (especially the 2.2 MZR-CD) are to be avoided due to costly design flaws (chain, DPF, oil dilution). The sporty MPS version is a collector's item in the making, but requires a clear maintenance history and a substantial budget.
