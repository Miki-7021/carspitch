Summary

The second-generation Ford C-Max (code name C344) is a compact MPV based on Ford's global C platform (shared with the Focus Mk3). It is available in two body styles: the standard C-Max (5 seats) and the Grand C-Max (extended wheelbase, 7 seats, and sliding rear doors). Renowned for its excellent driving dynamics, which is rare in this segment, it received a major facelift in 2015 (new trapezoidal grille, refined interior, SYNC 2 then SYNC 3 multimedia system, and transition to Euro 6 engines). As requested, this sheet focuses on the **diesel (TDCi)** engines, which make up the core of European sales for this type of family vehicle, while comprehensively covering the petrol and hybrid offerings (particularly North American).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road handling for an MPV (Focus chassis)
  • Reliability and fuel efficiency of the 1.5 TDCi and 2.0 TDCi diesel engines
  • Very practical sliding rear doors on the Grand C-Max
  • Soundproofing and ride comfort (especially after the 2015 facelift)
  • SYNC 3 multimedia system (from late 2016) responsive and compatible with Apple CarPlay/Android Auto

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of Powershift automatic gearboxes if poorly maintained
  • 1.0 and 1.6 EcoBoost petrol engines from the early years are very problematic
  • Trunk volume of the 5-seat C-Max is a bit tight compared to the competition
  • Dashboard ergonomics on phase 1 (too many buttons, tiny screen)
  • Rear seats of the Grand C-Max are narrow (center seat) and heavy to handle

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history of the Powershift automatic gearbox (oil change invoices every 60,000 km are mandatory)
  • On diesels: absence of jerking during acceleration (EGR valve) and FAP warning light off
  • On the 1.0 EcoBoost: condition of the timing belt (visible through the oil filler cap) and coolant level
  • Operation of the door locks (subject to recall, risk of opening while driving)
  • Front tire wear (the sharp front axle tends to consume rubber, especially with the heavy 2.0 TDCi)

Verdict

The Ford C-Max II is an excellent choice for families who refuse to sacrifice driving pleasure. **With a diesel engine, it particularly shines.** The 2.0 TDCi is a real workhorse, reliable and high-performing, ideal for the Grand C-Max. The 1.5 TDCi is the sensible choice for daily use.

**Purchasing conditions:** 1. Favor manual gearboxes. If you opt for the Powershift automatic gearbox, demand invoices proving an oil change every 60,000 km. 2. Avoid the 1.0 EcoBoost and 1.6 EcoBoost petrol engines from the early years, whose design caused numerous engine failures. 3. Target a facelifted model (post-2015) to benefit from better soundproofing, Euro 6 engines (1.5 TDCi), and the SYNC 3 multimedia system (far superior to SYNC 2).
