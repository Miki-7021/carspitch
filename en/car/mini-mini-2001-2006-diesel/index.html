MINI Mini

I (R50/R53) · 2001 – 2006

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation MINI under BMW's era (codename R50 for naturally aspirated/diesel versions and R53 for the Cooper S) marked the masterful rebirth of the British icon. Launched in 2001, it stood out with its timeless neo-retro design and exceptional road handling, often compared to that of a go-kart.

**Diesel Focus (MINI One D):** Introduced in 2003, the One D is the first MINI in history to run on diesel. BMW made a pragmatic and wise choice by borrowing the 1.4 D-4D block from Toyota (code 1ND-TV, from the Yaris). This aluminum common rail direct injection engine proved to be extremely robust. Initially offered with 75 hp, it increased to 88 hp in 2005 during the facelift (Phase 2). Unlike gasoline versions before 2004, which were plagued by the fragile 5-speed Midland manual gearbox, the One D benefited from an excellent 6-speed Getrag manual gearbox from its launch, making it paradoxically one of the most reliable versions of this generation in terms of transmission.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of the original Toyota diesel engine block (1ND-TV)
  • Presence of a very robust 6-speed manual gearbox (Getrag) on the diesel, avoiding the flaws of the gasoline versions
  • Ultra-dynamic road handling (go-kart feel)
  • Timeless exterior and interior design that ages very well
  • Very moderate fuel consumption on the One D (around 5L/100 km)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very fragile electro-hydraulic power steering pump (common issue for all R50/R53s)
  • Very firm, even harsh, suspension comfort on degraded roads
  • Ridiculous rear passenger space and trunk volume (150 liters)
  • Uneven interior finish (hard plastics that creak with age)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)

Check before buying

  • Power steering pump: listen for excessive whistling or if the steering becomes stiff (failure of the pump's cooling fan leading to overheating).
  • Gearbox (Gasoline pre-2004): avoid bearing noises or gears that snag (Midland gearbox).
  • Front suspension wishbone bushings: wear out very quickly, causing vagueness in steering and clunking noises.
  • Electric windows: motors tire and cables break.
  • Corrosion: check under the rear lights, at the bottom of the doors, and under the trunk seal.

Verdict

The MINI R50/R53 is a beloved car whose overall reliability has been marred by fragile peripherals (power steering) and disastrous gearboxes on early gasoline versions. **However, the diesel version (One D) is the exception that proves the rule.** Thanks to its indestructible Toyota engine and its 6-speed Getrag gearbox fitted as standard from 2003, it avoids the worst pitfalls of this generation. If you are looking for a first-generation MINI for economical daily use, the One D (ideally the 88 hp post-2005 version for a bit more versatility) is an excellent choice. Still, budget for the running gear (bushings) and make sure the power steering pump has already been replaced.
