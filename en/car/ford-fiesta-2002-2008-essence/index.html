Ford Fiesta

VI (Mk6) · 2002 – 2008

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The 2002-2008 generation Ford Fiesta (often called Mk6 in continental Europe and Mk5 in the UK) marked a major turning point for the model. Gone was the bio-design of the 90s, replaced by sharper lines (New Edge) and, above all, a chassis offering first-class driving pleasure, a benchmark in its category at the time. The late 2005 facelift corrected the main flaw of phase 1: the perceived quality of the interior plastics, while modernizing the headlights and bumpers. This report focuses particularly on gasoline engines, which today represent the most relevant and reliable used car choice for this model, although diesels (from the PSA/Ford partnership) are also detailed for an exhaustive overview.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent road handling (precise and dynamic chassis)
  • Overall reliability of gasoline engines (1.25, 1.4, 1.6)
  • Reduced maintenance cost and affordable spare parts
  • Decent interior space for its size (3.92 m)
  • Clear improvement in finish after the 2005 facelift

Weaknesses 5

  • Low-grade interior plastics prone to scratches (especially phase 1 before 2005)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Driving position a bit high for some body types
  • Variable reliability of diesel engines (especially 1.6 TDCi)
  • Manual gearbox (IB5) sometimes notchy (reverse gear difficult to engage)

Check before buying

  • Front shock absorber springs: known tendency to break (a common issue for Fiesta/Fusion of this era).
  • Water ingress: check for dampness under the passenger mats (defective cabin filter seal) and in the trunk.
  • 'EAC Fail' message on the dashboard: indicates a communication problem between the accelerator pedal and the throttle body (often a simple cleaning is sufficient).
  • Power steering: check for leaks at the pump and rack, and absence of abnormal noises at full lock.
  • Injector seals (Diesels): listen to the engine at idle with the hood open. A 'pschitt pschitt' locomotive-like sound and an exhaust smell indicate a leak.

Official recalls

  • - **2002 (Airbags)**: Risk of non-deployment of side and curtain airbags due to a wiring defect. - **2004 (Braking)**: Incorrect routing of a rear brake hose potentially leading to wear by friction and fluid leakage. - **2005 (Steering)**: Replacement of the intermediate shaft of the steering column (risk of loss of control on a small series). - **2007 (Cooling - Diesels)**: The cooling fan motor may overheat and melt due to water ingress (mainly concerns 1.4 TDCi).

Verdict

The Ford Fiesta Mk6 (2002-2008) is an excellent choice for a low-budget used car, **provided you prioritize gasoline engines**. The 1.25 and 1.4 Duratec (16v) engines are particularly recommendable: they combine remarkable reliability, negligible maintenance costs, and driving pleasure enhanced by a very successful chassis. The late 2005 facelift is preferable for a more upscale interior. Diesel versions (TDCi), although economical, should be avoided today due to potential repair costs (turbo, injectors) that often exceed the vehicle's market value.
