Summary

The Abarth 500 (rebranded primarily as the 595 and 695 from 2012 onwards) is a high-performance derivative of the Fiat 500. Powered almost exclusively by the proven 1.4-liter turbocharged FIRE engine (T-Jet in Europe, MultiAir in North America), it offers a raw, old-school hot hatch experience characterized by a loud exhaust note and aggressive styling. Despite its aging platform and ergonomic quirks, it remains a highly sought-after enthusiast car. Recently, the lineup expanded to include the fully electric Abarth 500e.

Strengths 5

  • Incredible exhaust note (especially with Record Monza exhaust)
  • Bulletproof 1.4 T-Jet engine block (European models)
  • Strong residual values and enthusiast demand
  • Massive aftermarket support for tuning and parts
  • Compact size makes it an excellent city hot hatch

Weaknesses 5

  • Awkward, 'bus-like' driving position (steering wheel lacks reach adjustment)
  • Harsh and bouncy ride quality, especially on Competizione models
  • Outdated interior plastics and ergonomics
  • Small boot and cramped rear seats
  • Surprisingly poor turning circle for a small car

Check before buying

  • Record Monza exhaust valve: Prone to seizing open or closed due to rust. Needs regular lubrication.
  • Rear hatch wiring loom: Wires can fray and break where they bend at the hinge, causing lighting/wiper failures.
  • Front suspension top mounts and wheel bearings: Wear out quickly due to the stiff suspension.
  • Timing belt and water pump: Must be replaced every 5 years or 60,000 miles (100,000 km) without fail.
  • Oil level and quality: Crucial for all models, but absolutely vital for US MultiAir engines to prevent actuator failure.

Official recalls

  • 2016 (Seatbelts): Potential issue with the seatbelt buckle latching mechanism not securing properly.
  • 2019 (Wiring): Steering column wiring harness may chafe, leading to loss of indicators or airbag warning lights.
  • 2014 (US Market - Clutch): Premature wear and failure of the clutch release bearing on manual transmission models.
  • 2016 (US Market - Half-shafts): Right-hand half-shaft fracture risk due to improper heat treatment.

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Exceptional Mechanical Reliability — The heart of the Abarth 500, its 1.4 T-Jet engine, is an extremely robust and reliable cast-iron block, ensuring lasting performance and peace of mind on the road.

Performance

  • Thrilling Driving Experience — The Abarth 500 offers raw driving pleasure and unparalleled go-kart sensations, with dynamic and agile handling that makes every turn exciting.

Safety

  • Enhanced Safety — Designed for protection, the Abarth 500 achieved excellent results in crash tests, offering peace of mind thanks to its robust structure and safety features.

Braking

  • High-Performance Braking — The Abarth 500 is equipped with a powerful braking system, capable of very short stopping distances and excellent endurance, even in sporty driving, for total control.

tenue de route

  • Dynamic and Agile Road Holding — Its sporty chassis and short wheelbase provide exceptional responsiveness and very engaging road holding, for perfect control in all situations.

Economy

  • Lasting Value and Appeal — Beyond its performance, the Abarth 500 is a vehicle that maintains excellent value on the used car market, thanks to its popularity and iconic status.

Comfort

  • Modern and Connected Features — Newer versions of the Abarth 500 integrate modern technologies such as the TFT digital dashboard and the Uconnect multimedia system with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, for optimal connectivity.

Alternatives

  • Ford Fiesta ST: Superior chassis dynamics, better driving position, and more practical, but less 'exotic' character.
  • MINI Cooper S (F56): Higher build quality, better ergonomics, and stronger tech, but more expensive to maintain.
  • Suzuki Swift Sport: Lighter, more reliable, and cheaper to run, though less powerful and lacks the Abarth's exhaust note.
  • Volkswagen Up! GTI: Similar retro-charm and city-car footprint, but significantly less power.

Verdict

The Abarth 500/595/695 is a triumph of character over perfection. Objectively, it suffers from a bouncy ride, an awkward driving position, and outdated interior ergonomics. However, subjectively, it is one of the most charismatic hot hatches of the 21st century. The European 1.4 T-Jet engine (especially with the Garrett turbo) is remarkably reliable and can handle high mileage if the timing belt and oil changes are strictly adhered to. North American buyers must be more cautious with the MultiAir engine. Buy on condition, favor unmodified examples with full service history, and ensure the Record Monza exhaust valve isn't seized.
