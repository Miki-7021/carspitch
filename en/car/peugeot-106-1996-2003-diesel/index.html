Summary

The Peugeot 106 Phase 2 (type 1A/1C), launched in April 1996, marked a major evolution for the small lion-badged city car. Now sharing its platform and many components with the Citroën Saxo, it adopted softer, more rounded lines. Designed as an economical, lightweight (often under a ton), and agile car, it established itself as a benchmark choice for new drivers and those on a tight budget.

The 1.5 Diesel (TUD5) engine, which replaced the old 1.4D from Phase 1, is a true legend of French automotive history. This naturally aspirated engine is renowned for its exceptional frugality (less than 5L/100 km) and its ability to cover astronomical mileages with basic maintenance. Although its performance is modest and its sound insulation dated, it embodies the very essence of an indestructible utility vehicle.

Strengths 5

  • Legendary reliability of the 1.5 Diesel engine (TUD5)
  • Extremely low fuel consumption (Diesel and small TU engines)
  • Negligible maintenance and insurance costs
  • Agile and fun road handling (featherlight)
  • Availability and price of spare parts (new and scrapyard)

Weaknesses 5

  • Fragile rear axle (a common PSA issue of the era: wheels forming a V-shape /\)
  • Obsolete passive and active safety (crash tests from another era, ABS rare)
  • Almost non-existent sound insulation (especially on the 1.5D at 130 km/h)
  • Imperfect driving position (non-adjustable steering wheel on many trims)
  • Paint clear coat peeling over time (red, blue, light clear coats)

Check before buying

  • Rear Axle: Look at the car from behind. If the rear wheels lean inwards (excessive negative camber) or if grinding noises are heard over bumps, the rear axle needs to be rebuilt (cost: 300 to 500€ for a reconditioned part).
  • Injection Pump (1.5D): With the engine running, check for any diesel drips under the pump (especially if it's a Lucas/Delphi brand).
  • Cylinder Head Gasket (TU Petrol): Run a finger or a flashlight over the front right of the engine, just above the alternator. A greasy oil leak is common.
  • Corrosion: Although better protected than Phase 1, check the rear wheel arches, boot floor, and front chassis rails.
  • Electrics: Check the operation of the central locking, electric windows, and immobilizer keypad (if equipped, wires can break in the door hinges).

What sets it apart 6

Reliability

  • Legendary Diesel Engine — Benefit from the proven reliability of the 1.5 Diesel (TUD5) engine, renowned for its robustness and ability to cover impressive distances.

Economy

  • Ultra-Low Fuel Consumption — Achieve significant savings thanks to exceptionally low fuel consumption, often less than 5L/100 km.
  • Extremely Low Running Costs — Control your budget with some of the lowest insurance and maintenance costs on the market, making the 106 an economical choice in the long run.

Practicality

  • Simplified and Affordable Maintenance — Enjoy an easy-to-maintain vehicle, with simple mechanics and excellent availability of spare parts at very competitive prices.
  • Ideal for All Budgets — The Peugeot 106 1.5 Diesel is the perfect choice for young drivers and high-mileage users looking for a reliable and economical daily vehicle.

tenue de route

  • Agility and Driving Pleasure — Appreciate agile and dynamic road handling, offering a fun and responsive driving experience, ideal for both city and open roads.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Citroën Saxo (its technical twin, same engines), Renault Clio II (more comfortable and safer), Volkswagen Polo III (better finished but less agile), Ford Fiesta Mk4.
  • Japan / Asia: Nissan Micra K11 (very reliable, timing chain).
  • Emerging Markets: Fiat Uno / Mille, Chevrolet Corsa.

Verdict

The Peugeot 106 Phase 2 is the archetype of the economical 'youngtimer' city car. If you're looking for an indestructible daily driver and modern comfort or passive safety aren't your priorities, the 1.5 Diesel version is a masterful choice. It's a true workhorse capable of enduring the worst treatment if its fluids are up to date. However, beware of the rear axle, a real sword of Damocles for this model. The petrol versions (1.1i and 1.4i) are excellent alternatives for short trips, avoiding the Crit'Air restrictions that affect the valiant 1.5D in large urban areas.
