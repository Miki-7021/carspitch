Summary

The Ineos Grenadier (Generation I) is a pure, hard-core off-roader, born from Sir Jim Ratcliffe's desire to create a spiritual successor to the old Land Rover Defender. Built in the former Smart factory in Hambach (France), it rests on a robust ladder frame chassis, Carraro rigid axles, and uses powertrains supplied by BMW coupled with an 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox. Designed for durability, towing, and off-roading, it positions itself against the grain of modern SUVs. Although mechanically very solid thanks to proven components, the early models suffered some teething problems related to early assembly and software bugs.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional off-road capabilities (ladder frame chassis, rigid axles, differential locks)
  • Top-tier mechanical reliability (BMW B57/B58 engines, ZF8 gearbox, Carraro axles)
  • Utilitarian and washable design (floor drain plugs, aviation-style switches)
  • Towing capacity (3.5 tonnes) and payload
  • Excellent all-round visibility

Weaknesses 5

  • Very vague recirculating ball steering on the road (requires constant corrections)
  • Colossal weight (around 2.7 tonnes unladen) penalizing driving dynamics and braking
  • Very high fuel consumption (especially petrol)
  • Significant wind noise on the highway (wardrobe-like aerodynamics)
  • Early electronic bugs on the first model years (2022-2023)

Check before buying

  • Recall history (crucial for the steering rod and wiring harness)
  • Underbody and skid plates (check for impact damage from extreme off-road use)
  • Operation of the central touchscreen and absence of phantom warning lights
  • Correct locking of all doors (grease issue on the handles)
  • Condition of the DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter) on Diesel versions if the vehicle has been driven a lot in the city

What sets it apart 6

Performance

  • Unrivalled Off-Road Capabilities — Engineered for adventure, the Grenadier excels in all-terrain conditions thanks to its robust ladder frame chassis, rigid axles, and differential locks, taking you wherever the journey calls.
  • Exceptional Diesel Engine Torque — The 3.0L Diesel engine delivers a massive 550 Nm of torque, perfect for off-roading and heavy towing, while offering more controlled fuel consumption for a vehicle in its class.

reliability

  • Exemplary Diesel Mechanical Reliability — Enjoy absolute peace of mind with the 3.0L BMW B57 Diesel engine, renowned for its robustness and durability, paired with the ZF 8-speed automatic transmission, a proven combination for reliable performance.

practicality

  • Impressive Towing and Payload Capacity — With a towing capacity of 3.5 tonnes and a generous payload, the Grenadier is your ideal partner for transporting heavy equipment and gear, whether for work or leisure.
  • Robust and Functional Interior Design — The Grenadier's interior is built for action, featuring durable materials and practical functionalities like floor drain plugs, allowing for easy cleaning after every adventure.

safety

  • Optimal All-Round Visibility — Benefit from excellent visibility around the entire vehicle, a major asset for control in off-road conditions and for everyday driving safety.

Verdict

The Ineos Grenadier is a formidable tool for work and adventure. By choosing to partner with BMW for the engines (B57 Diesel and B58 Petrol) and with ZF for the transmission, Ineos avoided the trap of unreliable powertrains. The 3.0L Diesel (B57) block is the most rational choice for this 2.7-tonne vehicle: its massive torque is perfect for towing and off-roading, while limiting the damage at the pump.

However, you must buy the Grenadier with full knowledge of the facts: its vague steering and weight make it a poor choice for urban or exclusively highway trips. Used buyers must absolutely verify that the early recalls (especially on the steering) have been carried out. If you are looking for a true old-school 4x4 with modern and reliable mechanics, it is a resounding YES.
