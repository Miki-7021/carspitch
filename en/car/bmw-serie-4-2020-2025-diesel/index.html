Summary

The BMW 4 Series G22 (Coupé) and G23 (Cabriolet), launched in 2020, represents the second generation of this sporty model derived from the 3 Series (G20). It positions itself as a more dynamic and stylish alternative to the sedan, offering a driving experience focused on pleasure. Its design has generated much discussion, particularly due to its prominent vertical grille, but its intrinsic qualities (chassis, engines, finish) are unanimously praised. A facelift (LCI) is expected for 2024, bringing aesthetic and technological updates. Overall, the 4 Series G22/G23 is a well-built premium car, offering an excellent compromise between sportiness and comfort, with solid general reliability for a car in this category.

Strengths 5

  • Premium build quality and interior finish
  • Driving dynamics and excellent chassis
  • Wide choice of powerful and efficient engines (especially MHEV diesels)
  • Comfort on long journeys (depending on configuration)
  • Modern and intuitive onboard technology (iDrive 7/8)

Weaknesses 5

  • Controversial front grille design (subjective)
  • High purchase price and expensive options
  • Limited rear visibility on Coupé and Cabriolet
  • Significant weight, especially for the Cabriolet
  • High maintenance and spare parts cost

Check before buying

  • Complete and up-to-date service history (BMW digital service book)
  • Tire and brake wear (particularly on powerful versions)
  • Operation of the 48V MHEV system (if present): absence of warning lights, proper functioning of Start&Stop
  • Condition of the DPF and EGR valve on diesel engines (absence of suspicious smoke, regular regenerations)
  • Onboard electronics: check the proper functioning of all systems (iDrive, driving aids)
  • Condition of the soft top and its mechanism on Cabriolet versions (water tightness, noises, fluidity)

Official recalls

Official recalls for the BMW 4 Series G22/G23 are as follows (non-exhaustive list and subject to change):

  • 2020-2021: Teleservices communication module (eCall)
  • Issue: A software defect may prevent the communication module from correctly transmitting the vehicle's position in the event of an automatic emergency call (eCall).
  • Affected population: Numerous BMW models produced between 2017 and 2021, including the 4 Series G22/G23.
  • 2021: Front seat belt tensioner
  • Issue: The front seat belt tensioner might not function correctly in the event of a collision, increasing the risk of injury.
  • Affected population: Certain G22/G23 vehicles produced during a specific period in 2021.
  • 2022: Rearview camera software
  • Issue: A software defect may result in an incorrect display or absence of the rearview camera image.
  • Affected population: Certain G22/G23 vehicles produced during a specific period in 2022.
  • 2023: Fuel pump control unit
  • Issue: A defect in the fuel pump control unit may lead to an unexpected engine shutdown.
  • Affected population: Certain G22/G23 vehicles produced during a specific period in 2023.

It is imperative to check with a BMW dealership whether the desired vehicle has benefited from all recall campaigns.

Verdict

The BMW 4 Series G22/G23 is an excellent choice for anyone looking for a premium, sporty, and well-finished coupé or cabriolet. Its general reliability is very good, in line with the brand's standards, provided rigorous maintenance. The diesel engines, particularly the 430d and M440d, are wise choices for high-mileage drivers: they combine impressive performance, generous torque, and remarkable fuel efficiency for their power level, all with proven reliability of the B47 and B57 blocks. The 48V MHEV system, although efficient, is the main new element to monitor in the long term. For gasoline enthusiasts, the B48 and B58 are also safe bets. Before purchasing, prioritize a vehicle with a clear service history and check for the application of manufacturer recalls. Negotiate the price based on options and general condition, as the used car market is well-supplied. It's a car that provides a lot of driving pleasure and ages well if properly maintained.
