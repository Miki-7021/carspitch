IV (K13) (2010–2016) · 2010–2016

Summary

The fourth-generation Nissan Micra, known by its internal code K13, was launched in 2010 and produced until 2016. Designed as a "global car" (V-Platform), it aimed to be economical to produce and sell in many markets, especially emerging ones. This resulted in a simplification compared to the previous K12, with more basic interior materials and less soundproofing.

Positioned in the versatile city car segment, the K13 was marketed as the Micra in Europe, India, and parts of Asia, and as the March in Japan, Thailand, Latin America, and South Africa. In Canada, it was also sold as the Micra.

A facelift occurred in 2013, bringing aesthetic changes (bumpers, grille, headlights, taillights) and interior improvements (new center console, upholstery).

Overall, the Micra K13 is a simple and functional car. Its reliability is decent, benefiting from proven gasoline engines, but it can suffer from perceived quality and some weaknesses related to its economical design, particularly the CVT transmission.

Strengths 5

  • Naturally aspirated gasoline engines (HR12DE, HR15DE, HR16DE) generally reliable and simple.
  • Generally low maintenance and insurance costs.
  • Excellent maneuverability and turning radius for city driving.
  • Reasonable fuel consumption for gasoline versions.
  • Decent interior space for the segment.

Weaknesses 5

  • Interior material quality and soundproofing are inferior compared to European competitors.
  • Less dynamic road handling than some rivals.
  • Reliability of the CVT transmission (noises, jerks, lifespan) if maintenance is not rigorous.
  • Safety equipment sometimes limited on entry-level versions.
  • 1.2 HR12DE engine a bit underpowered on roads and highways.

Check before buying

  • **Service history:** Essential, especially for the CVT (fluid changes).
  • **CVT Transmission:** Test the car hot and cold. Listen for abnormal noises, check for jerks, slipping. A CVT fluid change is costly.
  • **Engine:** Abnormal noises (knocking, whistling), exhaust smoke. Check oil level.
  • **Air conditioning:** Operation and efficiency.
  • **Suspension:** Noises on degraded roads (shock absorbers, bushings).
  • **Electronics:** Functioning of all equipment (windows, radio, lights).

Verdict

The Nissan Micra K13 is an honest and economical city car, particularly suited for urban and suburban use. Its mechanical simplicity, especially with the naturally aspirated gasoline engines (HR12DE, HR15DE, HR16DE), is a guarantee of reliability.

**Buying Tips:** * **For a tight budget and predominantly urban use:** The 1.2 HR12DE with a manual gearbox is an excellent choice. It is simple, economical, and reliable. * **For more versatility or in markets where they are available:** The 1.5 HR15DE and 1.6 HR16DE are safe bets, offering more comfort on the road. * **To consider with caution:** Versions equipped with the CVT transmission. They can be comfortable in the city, but their reliability is lower than that of manual gearboxes, and they require rigorous maintenance (specific fluid changes) to avoid costly problems. If you opt for a CVT, ensure the service history is impeccable. * **The 1.2 DIG-S (HR12DDR):** Offers a good performance/consumption compromise, but its complexity (supercharger) can lead to slightly higher maintenance costs.

Avoid examples without a clear service history, especially for the CVT. The Micra K13 is a no-frills car, but it fulfills its role as a reliable city car well if the right engine/transmission is chosen and maintenance is diligently performed.
