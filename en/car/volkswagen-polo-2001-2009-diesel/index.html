Volkswagen Polo

IV (9N) · 2001 – 2009

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The Volkswagen Polo IV was marketed in two phases: the 9N (2001-2005) with its double round headlights in the Lupo style, and the 9N3 (2005-2009) which adopted the V-shaped grille and unified headlights. Positioned as a 'premium' city car at the time, it stood out with above-average interior finish quality and excellent corrosion resistance. However, its mechanical reliability is extremely heterogeneous. While the 4-cylinder diesel engines (1.9 TDI) are renowned for their legendary robustness (despite some fragile peripherals), the entry-level gasoline engines (especially the 1.2 MPI 3-cylinder) suffered from serious design flaws. The road handling is safe but sluggish, prioritizing comfort over dynamism.

Strengths 5

  • Interior finish quality and aging of plastics (foamed on high trims)
  • Excellent bodywork corrosion protection
  • Exceptional performance/consumption ratio of the 1.9 TDI
  • Availability and price of spare parts (VAG parts bin)
  • Ergonomics of controls and driving position

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.2 MPI gasoline engine (timing, valves)
  • Poor sound insulation on diesels (especially the 1.4 TDI 3-cylinder)
  • Premature wear of front suspension bushings (PQ24 chassis common issue)
  • Recurring electrical problems (power windows, door lock micro-switches)
  • Automatic climate control (Climatronic) mixing flap motors that click and fail

Check before buying

  • **Front wishbone bushings:** Check for play, knocking over speed bumps, or asymmetrical tire wear.
  • **Dual-mass flywheel (1.9 TDI):** Vibrations in the clutch pedal at idle and metallic knocking when the engine is switched off.
  • **Turbo (TDI):** Accelerate in 3rd and 4th gear on a highway to check for any power cut-offs (stuck variable geometry).
  • **Onboard electronics:** Imperatively test all power windows, central locking for each door, and climate control temperature variation.
  • **Gearbox (SDI / 1.4 MPI):** Listen for any whistling or groaning while driving (gearbox bearings or differential rivets).

Official recalls

  • - **December 2004 (TDI):** Recall for inspection and replacement of tandem pump cover screws (possible diesel leak). - **March 2005:** Risk of short circuit on the cooling fan motor. - **February 2008 (TDI):** On some 2007 models, fire risk due to a defect in the coolant heater (PTC). - **2001-2002:** Recall concerning rear seat belt buckles that could open in the event of a collision.

What sets it apart 14

Reliability

  • Legendary Diesel Engine Robustness — The 1.9 TDI and 1.9 SDI diesel engines are renowned for their exceptional longevity and ability to cover vast distances, ensuring unwavering reliability.
  • Exemplary Anti-Corrosion Protection — The Polo IV's bodywork benefits from exemplary anti-corrosion protection, guaranteeing excellent durability and preserving the vehicle's aesthetics in the long run.

Economy

  • Optimized Fuel Consumption — Benefit from remarkably low fuel consumption, especially with the 1.9 TDI offering an excellent performance-to-consumption ratio, and the 1.4 TDI/1.9 SDI for maximum daily efficiency.

Comfort

  • Superior Interior Finish Quality — Enjoy an interior finish quality superior to the average for its category, featuring durable materials and excellent aging of plastics, ensuring a pleasant and time-resistant cabin.

Performance

  • Strong Road Performance of the 1.9 TDI — The 1.9 TDI engine transforms this city car into an excellent road vehicle, offering dynamic performance and generous torque for comfortable and efficient highway journeys.

Practicality

  • Easy Parts Availability & Affordable Cost — Easy access and affordable cost of spare parts, thanks to the vast VAG group parts bank, simplify maintenance and help control long-term costs.

Safety

  • Safe and Comfortable Road Handling — Benefit from safe and predictable road handling, prioritizing driving comfort for a serene experience on all types of routes, both in the city and on the open road.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Peugeot 206 / 207 (better chassis, smoother HDi diesels), Renault Clio II / III (excellent comfort, dCi to watch before 2006), Ford Fiesta Mk6 (very good handling), Toyota Yaris II (superior gasoline reliability). - **Japan / Asia:** Honda Jazz / Fit (record spaciousness, impeccable mechanical reliability). - **Americas / Emerging Markets:** Chevrolet Corsa, Fiat Palio, Ford Fiesta RoCam.

Verdict

The Polo IV is a car with two faces. If you're looking for a diesel, the **1.9 TDI 100 hp** is the best buy: it transforms this city car into an excellent road car, capable of covering many kilometers with remarkable engine reliability, provided you accept its rough side and monitor the dual-mass flywheel. The **1.4 TDI** is economical but too noisy and vibrating to be pleasant for daily use. As for gasoline, absolutely avoid the 1.2 MPI and 1.4 FSI. A 1.4 MPI 80 hp model (after 2006) is the most rational choice for mixed use. In any case, negotiate the price if the front suspension bushings or timing belt haven't been replaced recently.
