Summary

The Renault Clio IV marked a major stylistic break for the brand under the leadership of Laurens van den Acker. A true European best-seller, it dominated sales thanks to its dynamic design and first-class road handling.

Major evolutions:

  • Phase 1 (2012-2016): Successful launch but heavily criticized for the perceived quality of its interior (hard plastics, imperfect assembly) and its sound insulation.
  • Phase 2 (Facelift 2016-2019): Correction of main defects with foamed materials, new door panels, an update of the R-Link multimedia system, and the arrival of the C-Shape light signature.

Although this sheet focuses on gasoline engines, the overall range is covered. The reliability of the Clio IV is generally satisfactory, with the notable and critical exception of the 1.2 TCe (H5Ft) engine, infamously known for the 'Motorgate' scandal.

Strengths 5

  • Exterior design still current and appealing
  • Excellent road handling (precise chassis, good comfort/road holding compromise)
  • Overall reliability of 0.9 TCe, 1.2 16v, and 1.5 dCi engines
  • Reasonable maintenance cost (excluding 1.2 TCe and EDC gearbox)
  • Spectacular improvement in perceived quality on Phase 2 (from 2016)

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic 1.2 TCe 120hp engine (risk of engine failure)
  • Quality of plastics and assembly on Phase 1 (2012-2016)
  • Light sound insulation on the highway (wind noise around the mirrors)
  • EDC automatic gearbox sometimes temperamental (juddering, clutch wear)
  • Poor rear visibility (reversing camera or parking sensors highly recommended)

Check before buying

  • Engine code: Avoid the H5Ft (1.2 TCe). Prefer the H4Bt (0.9 TCe) or D4F (1.2 16v) for gasoline.
  • Coolant level on the 0.9 TCe (pink trace around the water housing).
  • EDC gearbox behavior during a test drive: no juddering on startup in 1st gear or reverse should be tolerated.
  • Touchscreen operation (MediaNav or R-Link): test GPS, Bluetooth, and radio.
  • Wear of running gear: suspension ball joints and anti-roll bar links (dull noises over speed bumps).

Official recalls

  • April 2014: Risk of wear of front brake hoses due to rubbing against wheel arch liners (production until March 2013).
  • August 2015: Dashboard reprogramming following an erratic fuel gauge.
  • May 2016: Risk of rear spoiler detachment (poor factory bonding).
  • September 2019: Incorrect adjustment of LED headlights (glare), requiring recalibration.

What sets it apart 7

Practicality

  • Timeless and Seductive Design — The Renault Clio IV stands out with its dynamic and modern style, which retains all its appeal and elegance on the road, turning heads wherever it goes.

Safety

  • Exemplary and Award-Winning Safety — With its robust structure and advanced safety features, the Clio IV achieved the maximum Euro NCAP rating, offering optimal protection for all passengers and distinguishing itself as 'Best in Class Supermini'.
  • Efficient and Reassuring Braking — Enjoy a powerful and progressive braking system, guaranteeing short stopping distances and perfect vehicle control in all circumstances.

Performance

  • Agile and Comfortable Road Handling — Its precise chassis offers an excellent balance between dynamism and suspension comfort, ensuring a pleasant and stable drive, both in the city and on the highway.

Reliability

  • Proven and Reliable Petrol Engines — The 0.9 TCe and 1.2 16v engines are renowned for their robustness, mechanical simplicity, and low maintenance requirements, ensuring peace of mind for daily use.

Economy

  • Controlled Maintenance Costs — Thanks to reliable engines and intelligent design, the Clio IV offers reasonable maintenance costs, contributing to a balanced car budget.

Comfort

  • Refined Interior and Improved Soundproofing (Phase 2) — Models from 2016 onwards benefit from a cabin with higher quality materials and enhanced soundproofing, for increased comfort and a more serene driving experience.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 208 I (beware of the 1.2 PureTech, prefer the 1.6 VTi or HDI), Volkswagen Polo V/VI (very well finished, reliable TSIs after 2014), Ford Fiesta Mk7/Mk8 (excellent chassis, robust 1.0 EcoBoost if belt is maintained), Toyota Yaris III (unbeatable in hybrid for the city).
  • Americas / Emerging Markets: Chevrolet Onix, Volkswagen Gol, Fiat Argo, Hyundai HB20.

Verdict

The Renault Clio IV is an excellent versatile city car, provided you choose the right engine and year. Definitely prioritize a Phase 2 (after summer 2016) to benefit from a decent interior and better sound insulation. For gasoline, the 0.9 TCe 90 is the most rational and reliable choice. The old 1.2 16v 75 is perfect for small urban budgets. However, flee the 1.2 TCe 120 like the plague, whose design flaws (Motorgate) can lead to engine destruction. If you drive a lot, the 1.5 dCi remains an absolute benchmark.
