Summary

Genesis GV60 I Facelift (JW)

The Genesis GV60 is the first 100% electric vehicle from the premium Korean brand, based on the excellent E-GMP platform (shared with the Hyundai Ioniq 5 and Kia EV6). The facelift introduced for the 2024/2025 model year brings subtle aesthetic tweaks, a new infotainment system (ccNC) and, in certain markets, a larger capacity battery (increasing from 77.4 kWh to 84 kWh) to improve range.

⚠️ **CRUCIAL EXPERT NOTE REGARDING DIESEL:** The request targets a *diesel* engine. It is imperative to clarify that **the Genesis GV60 does not exist and has never existed with a diesel engine, nor even with a combustion engine**. It is an exclusively electric vehicle (BEV). For the sake of rigor and professional accuracy, this sheet will exhaustively cover the actual electric powertrains that equip this model, while highlighting the total absence of a combustion engine.

The GV60 is positioned as a premium compact crossover, banking on a bold design, luxurious finish, and an 800V architecture allowing for ultra-fast charging (10 to 80% in 18 minutes).
