Renault Twingo

II (CN0) · 2007 – 2014

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Renault models

Summary

The Renault Twingo II (codename CN0) marks a radical break from the first generation. Gone is the monobox profile and frog-like face; it adopts the silhouette of a classic 3-door city car. Based on the proven Clio II platform, it gains significantly in road handling, safety, and sound insulation.

Phase 1 (2007-2011): Design often considered too conservative, but excellent modularity thanks to independent and sliding rear seats (depending on trim level).

Phase 2 (2011-2014): Deep restyling of the front end (return of round headlights) to give it back some personality.

In terms of reliability, it is one of the most robust Renaults of its era. The gasoline engines (D and K family) are older, largely proven blocks. It is a highly recommendable used car purchase, provided you avoid the disastrous Quickshift automated manual gearbox and carefully consider its use for diesel versions.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent overall reliability of gasoline engines (proven blocks).
  • Safe road handling (Clio II chassis).
  • Exceptional modularity with independent and sliding rear seats.
  • Very low maintenance and insurance costs.
  • RS version (133 hp) very fun and a future collector's item.

Weaknesses 4

  • Basic interior finish (hard plastics prone to scratches).
  • Light sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise).
  • Slow Quickshift automated manual gearbox, causing jerks and unreliable.
  • Slightly high driving position, steering wheel not adjustable for reach.

Check before buying

  • Running gear: Premature wear of steering ball joints and shock absorber mounts (clunking over bumps).
  • Electronics: Airbag warning light on (often due to wiring under the front seats).
  • Ignition (1.2 16v): Unstable idle or sputtering indicating a worn ignition coil or wiring harness.
  • Air conditioning: Condenser exposed to gravel, check for cold air production.
  • Gearbox: Oil leaks at the selector on manual gearboxes.

Official recalls

  • Spring 2009: Inspection and eventual replacement of front seat runners (poor locking).
  • Autumn 2010: Reprogramming of the injection computer on 1.5 dCi versions.
  • 2011: Risk of valve collet breakage on the 1.2 16v engine (late 2010 production). Cylinder head replacement if necessary.
  • 2014: Inspection of the ABS/ESP unit on a small series (risk of malfunction).

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability of Petrol Engines — Enjoy daily peace of mind thanks to petrol engines (1.2 16v 75 hp, 1.2 TCe 100 hp, 1.6 16v 133 hp) renowned for their robustness and longevity, ensuring serene journeys.

Practicality

  • Smart Modularity for All Your Needs — The Twingo II stands out with its unique modularity, notably thanks to its independent and sliding rear seats, offering remarkable adaptability for transporting passengers or luggage.

Safety

  • Optimal Safety for a City Car — Built on a robust platform, the Twingo II ensures effective protection for its occupants, as evidenced by its excellent safety test results.

Handling

  • Urban Agility and Secure Road Holding — Its chassis, inherited from the Clio II, gives it remarkable agility in the city and safe, predictable road handling on all roads, guaranteeing a serene and pleasant drive.

Braking

  • Efficient and Controlled Braking — The Twingo II offers adequate braking performance for its category, ensuring stable and controlled deceleration thanks to its ABS system, for increased safety at all times.

Economy

  • Very Advantageous Running Costs — Benefit from a vehicle with particularly attractive maintenance and insurance costs, making the Twingo II an economical and wise choice for everyday use.

Performance

  • RS Version: Sporty Driving Pleasure — For thrill-seekers, the 133 hp RS version offers a sporty character and exceptional driving enjoyment, promising moments of pure pleasure on the road.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 107 / Citroën C1 / Toyota Aygo (very reliable but less versatile), Fiat Panda II (practical but less stable handling), Volkswagen Fox or Up! (better finished but more expensive), Kia Picanto.
  • North America / Asia: Model not sold in these markets.

Verdict

The Renault Twingo II is an extremely rational and reassuring used car choice. By abandoning the originality of the first generation, it gained in maturity and reliability. The gasoline engines, especially the 1.2 16v 75 hp, are models of robustness and are very inexpensive to maintain. It's the ideal city car for a new driver or as a second vehicle. Absolutely avoid the Quickshift automated manual gearbox and steer clear of diesels if you only drive in the city.
