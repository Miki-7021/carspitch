Summary

The fifth-generation Nissan Micra, known by the code K14, marked a major turning point for the model. Launched in 2016, it radically broke away from the round and conservative design of its predecessors to adopt a more dynamic and assertive silhouette, aiming to directly compete with the leaders of the versatile city car segment in Europe (Renault Clio, Peugeot 208, Volkswagen Polo). Based on the Renault-Nissan-Mitsubishi Alliance's CMF-B platform (shared with the Renault Clio V), it focused on personalization, onboard technology, and more engaging road handling.

A minor facelift occurred in late 2020, bringing minor aesthetic touches (bumpers, wheels) and a simplification of the engine range. Overall, the Micra K14 is a pleasant city car to drive, well-equipped in its higher trims, but which has sometimes suffered from a less strong brand image than its European rivals. Its reliability is generally good, with the exception of a few points of concern on certain engines, particularly the 0.9 IG-T from the early years.

Strengths 5

  • Modern and dynamic exterior design
  • Agile and pleasant road handling
  • Technological equipment (Bose Personal system, touchscreen)
  • Reasonable fuel consumption (especially 1.0 IG-T and 1.5 dCi)
  • Customization options (colors, interior inserts)

Weaknesses 5

  • Limited rear passenger space for adults
  • Boot volume in the lower average of the segment
  • Quality of some interior plastics (lower parts)
  • Just adequate performance for the 1.0 MPI 71 hp
  • Reliability of the 0.9 IG-T (H4Bt) from the early years to monitor

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for 0.9 IG-T)
  • Abnormal noises on cold start (timing chain on 0.9 IG-T)
  • Oil level and consumption (0.9 IG-T)
  • Infotainment and driving aids functionality
  • Tire and brake wear (intensive urban use)
  • Presence of leaks (oil, coolant)

Official recalls

The Nissan Micra K14 has been subject to several official recalls, mainly related to software or component issues. It is crucial to verify that all campaigns have been carried out on the vehicle in question.

  • 20/06/2017: Engine Control Unit (ECU) software issue that could lead to engine stalling. (Vehicles produced between 2016 and 2017).
  • 26/09/2017: Electric Power Steering (EPS) issue that could lead to loss of assistance. (Vehicles produced between 2016 and 2017).
  • 20/03/2018: Fuel pump issue that could lead to a leak or engine stalling. (Vehicles produced between 2016 and 2017).
  • 10/07/2018: Engine Control Unit (ECU) software issue that could lead to engine stalling. (Vehicles produced between 2017 and 2018).
  • 22/01/2019: Brake pressure sensor issue that could lead to loss of braking assistance. (Vehicles produced between 2017 and 2018).
  • 09/07/2019: Engine Control Unit (ECU) software issue that could lead to engine stalling. (Vehicles produced between 2018 and 2019).
  • 10/03/2020: Engine Control Unit (ECU) software issue that could lead to engine stalling. (Vehicles produced between 2019 and 2020).
  • 22/09/2020: Crankshaft position sensor issue that could lead to engine stalling. (Vehicles produced between 2019 and 2020).
  • 09/02/2021: Engine Control Unit (ECU) software issue that could lead to engine stalling. (Vehicles produced between 2020 and 2021).
  • 08/06/2021: Engine hood latch issue that could lead to its unexpected opening. (Vehicles produced between 2020 and 2021).

What sets it apart 6

security

  • Optimal Euro NCAP Safety — The Nissan Micra V achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with scores of 89% for adult protection and 82% for child protection, ensuring a high level of safety for all occupants.
  • Efficient and Safe Braking — Enjoy a high-performance braking system, deemed efficient and safe for its category. It offers excellent stopping power, a firm pedal, and great ease of modulation, contributing to an enhanced sense of security behind the wheel.

Performance

  • Agile and Dynamic Road Handling — The dynamic behavior of the Nissan Micra V is a major asset. Agile and responsive, it offers precise steering and excellent body control, ensuring secure and predictable road holding for optimal driving pleasure.

comfort

  • Modern and Dynamic Exterior Design — The Micra V stands out with its bold design and assertive lines, breaking away from previous generations. Its modern and dynamic appearance catches the eye and reflects a strong personality on the road.
  • Advanced Technological Features — Enjoy a connected and comfortable driving experience thanks to features such as the intuitive touchscreen and the Bose Personal audio system, offering a unique sound immersion and modern functionalities.

economy

  • Controlled Fuel Consumption — The gasoline engines of the Nissan Micra V, particularly the 1.0 IG-T versions, are designed to offer reasonable fuel consumption, allowing you to make significant savings on your daily commutes.

Verdict

The Nissan Micra K14 represents an interesting option on the used car market for those looking for a city car with an assertive look and pleasant road handling.

  • For predominantly urban use and small budgets, the 1.0 MPI 71 hp is a safe choice, though not very powerful. Its mechanical simplicity is a guarantee of reliability.
  • For versatility and a good compromise, the 1.0 IG-T 100 hp and 1.0 DIG-T 117 hp are the most recommendable. They offer sufficient performance and controlled consumption, with improved reliability compared to the 0.9 IG-T.
  • For high-mileage drivers, the 1.5 dCi 90 hp is an excellent option, very economical and reliable in this generation, provided it is not used exclusively in the city.
  • The 0.9 IG-T 90 hp from the early years (2016-2019) should be considered with caution. If you opt for this engine, ensure you have an impeccable service history, ideally with more frequent oil changes than recommended, and pay attention to timing chain noises. Negotiate the price accordingly.

In all cases, verify that all manufacturer recalls have been performed and prioritize a model with a clear service history. The Micra K14 is a reliable car overall, provided the right engine choice is made and it is well-maintained.
