Mazda CX-5

I (KE) (2012–2017) · 2012 – 2017

NEGOTIATE Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation Mazda CX-5 (chassis code KE) marked a major turning point for the Japanese brand. Launched in 2012, it was the first model to fully embody the 'Kodo – Soul of Motion' design philosophy and integrate all 'Skyactiv' technologies (engines, transmissions, chassis, body). Positioned in the highly competitive compact SUV segment, the CX-5 quickly distinguished itself with its driving pleasure, fuel efficiency (especially for gasoline engines), and dynamic design.

A minor facelift occurred in early 2015, bringing aesthetic improvements (headlights, grille, taillights), improved sound insulation, a more modern MZD Connect infotainment system, and updates to safety equipment.

Overall, the CX-5 KE is a well-designed and pleasant vehicle to live with. Its reliability is very good for gasoline engines but requires increased vigilance for diesels from the first years of production.

Weaknesses 5

  • Reliability of Skyactiv-D 2.2 diesel engines on models produced before mid-2014
  • Imperfect sound insulation on pre-facelift models (improved in 2015)
  • Dated infotainment system on pre-facelift models (replaced by MZD Connect in 2015)
  • Firm suspension comfort on some versions (large wheels)
  • Some hard plastics in the cabin

Check before buying

  • **For diesels (SH-VPTS):** Complete service history, proof of injector seal and oil strainer replacements. Check for absence of abnormal turbo noises.
  • Engine oil level and quality (especially diesel, beware of dilution by diesel fuel).
  • Condition of brake discs and pads (rapid wear in some uses).
  • Air conditioning operation (compressor, leaks).
  • Tire wear (can be rapid with dynamic driving).
  • Presence of rust under the chassis or on suspension components (depending on region and salt exposure).
  • Operation of onboard electronics (MZD Connect system on facelifted models).

Verdict

The first-generation Mazda CX-5 is an excellent choice on the used car market, provided you choose the right engine. The **Skyactiv-G gasoline versions (2.0L and 2.5L) are safe bets**, offering exemplary reliability, reasonable fuel consumption, and undeniable driving pleasure. They are to be preferred for peace of mind and suit most uses.

For the **Skyactiv-D 2.2L diesel versions**, caution is advised. If you are considering a diesel, **it is imperative to target a model produced from late 2014 or, ideally, a facelifted model (2015-2017)**. For these, ensure that the service history is impeccable and that the fixes related to the injector seals and oil strainer have been applied. Predominantly road/highway use is essential for the longevity of the DPF and the diesel engine. A diesel from the early years (2012-2014) without a clear repair history should be viewed with great suspicion, or even avoided.

Overall, the CX-5 KE remains a highly recommendable SUV for its dynamism and quality, especially in gasoline.
