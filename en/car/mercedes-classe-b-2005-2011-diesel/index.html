Mercedes Classe B

W245 · 2005 – 2011

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The Mercedes-Benz B-Class (W245) is a premium compact MPV that adopts the innovative "sandwich" architecture of the A-Class (W169). This double floor allows the engine to be housed in a very inclined position, offering exceptional passive safety (the engine slides under the passenger compartment in the event of a frontal impact) and remarkable interior space for a vehicle measuring 4.27 m. The restyling (facelift) occurred in spring 2008, bringing slight aesthetic touches, new equipment (NTG 2.5 multimedia systems), and, most importantly, better protection against corrosion, a weak point of the early model years. Although very practical and comfortable, its architecture makes mechanical interventions complex and costly. The overall rating reflects an aging vehicle whose maintenance profitability heavily depends on the chosen engine and gearbox.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional interior space relative to exterior footprint
  • Highly appreciated elevated driving position
  • High level of passive safety (sandwich floor)
  • Quality of finish and materials (especially post-2008 facelift)
  • OM640 diesel engines very durable if driven well on the road

Weaknesses 5

  • Exorbitant labor cost (sandwich architecture requiring engine removal to change a starter, alternator, or accessory belt)
  • Disastrous reliability of the continuously variable automatic gearbox (CVT Autotronic 722.8)
  • Frequent corrosion on lower door sills and tailgate on models before 2008
  • Very fragile slatted sunroof (panoramic) (plastic mechanism that breaks)
  • Firm damping, especially with 17 or 18-inch wheels

Check before buying

  • Clear history of CVT gearbox oil changes (every 60,000 km maximum). If no invoices: flee.
  • Cold start on diesels: a laborious start can indicate faulty glow plugs or a tired starter motor (repair > €1000).
  • Presence of rust under lower door seals, on wheel arches, and the bottom of the tailgate (especially 2005-2007 models).
  • Smooth and crack-free operation of the slatted sunroof (if equipped).
  • Exhaust gas smell in the cabin (sign of a leak at the injector seals on the OM640 diesel).

Official recalls

  • **2005**: Seat belts (risk of rear seat belts not locking).
  • **2008**: Wipers (wiper motor defect potentially leading to sudden stop).
  • **2010**: Brake assist (risk of leak in the brake booster vacuum line).
  • **2019 (Late)**: Takata Airbags (massive global recall concerning the driver airbag gas generator).

Verdict

The Mercedes B-Class W245 is a vehicle full of family qualities, but it hides a formidable financial trap: its sandwich architecture. While the OM640 diesel engine is intrinsically very robust and capable of exceeding 300,000 km, the slightest peripheral intervention (starter, alternator, AC compressor) requires hours of labor.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize a facelifted model (post-2008) to avoid rust, **exclusively with a manual gearbox**. Avoid the Autotronic CVT automatic gearbox unless the control unit has already been replaced and oil changes have been scrupulously performed. For urban use, prefer petrol versions (B180); the diesel (B180/B200 CDI) must absolutely be driven on the open road to avoid clogging of the EGR and DPF.
