Kia Forte

II (YD) · 2014 – 2018

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation Kia Forte (codename YD), also marketed under the names **Cerato** or **K3** depending on the market, is a compact car offered in 4-door (sedan), 5-door (Forte5/hatchback), and 2-door (Koup) body styles. Designed by Peter Schreyer, it marks a significant leap forward in terms of design, equipment, and perceived quality compared to the previous generation. Although it offers an excellent price-to-equipment ratio and a generous manufacturer warranty, its mechanical reliability is mixed. While the base engines (MPI) are generally robust, several major recall campaigns (fire risks, oil pump) tarnish the record of this generation, placing it in the average of its category, below the Japanese benchmarks (Civic, Corolla).

Strengths 5

  • Unbeatable price-to-equipment ratio on the used market
  • Exterior and interior design aging very well (especially after the 2017 facelift)
  • Intuitive and responsive UVO infotainment system
  • MPI engines (1.6 and 1.8) generally very durable if well maintained
  • Long initial manufacturer warranty (often 7 years/150,000 km or 10 years/100,000 miles depending on the market)

Weaknesses 5

  • History of major recalls (fire risks, oil pump on the 2.0 MPI)
  • 1.8 MPI engine considered underpowered and noisy under load
  • Soundproofing could be improved (noticeable road noise on the highway)
  • Quality of some interior plastics (scratch easily)
  • Sluggish road handling compared to a Mazda 3 or a Honda Civic

Check before buying

  • Execution of safety recalls: This is the crucial point (oil pump, airbag module, fire risk).
  • Engine oil level and color: Nu engines (especially GDI) can consume oil as they age.
  • Brake light operation: The brake pedal stopper pad deteriorates, leaving the brake lights on permanently (NHTSA Recall).
  • Trunk release from the inside: The latch can be damaged (NHTSA Recall).
  • Cooling fan: Check that it triggers correctly (risk of resistor overheating on 2014 models).

Verdict

The second-generation Kia Forte / Cerato (2014-2018) is an attractive compact car on paper thanks to its abundant equipment and successful design. However, its reliability record is heavily marred by a series of critical safety recalls (fire risks, oil pump failure on the 2.0 MPI, airbags).

**Buying advice:** If you are targeting this model, favor versions equipped with the **1.6 MPI or 1.8 MPI** engines, which are mechanically simpler and less prone to catastrophic failures than the 2.0L versions. It is **absolutely imperative** to check the vehicle's history via the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) to ensure that all manufacturer recalls (especially those related to fire risks and the oil pump) have been performed at a dealership. Without this clear history, walk away.
