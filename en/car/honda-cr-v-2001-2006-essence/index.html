Honda CR-V

II (RD4-RD9) · 2001 – 2006

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The second-generation Honda CR-V (chassis RD4 to RD9) marks a major evolution compared to its predecessor. More spacious, safer, and better finished, it retains the practical spirit that made it successful (flat floor, picnic table integrated into the trunk, side-opening tailgate with spare wheel).

Mechanically, it introduced the legendary "K-Series" petrol engine family (K20 and K24) featuring chain timing and the i-VTEC system, offering exceptional reliability. A facelift occurred in late 2004 (model year 2005), bringing aesthetic touches, an improved all-wheel-drive system (more responsive Real Time 4WD), a 5-speed automatic gearbox (instead of 4), and, for Europe, the introduction of Honda's very first in-house diesel engine, the 2.2 i-CTDi.

It is a family SUV par excellence, whose petrol versions are now absolute benchmarks for longevity in the used car market.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional reliability of petrol engines (K20 and K24)
  • Giant cabin space and completely flat rear floor
  • Practical aspects (picnic table, sliding and reclining rear bench)
  • Very sound road handling for an SUV of its era
  • Excellent peripheral visibility
  • Very good aging of interior materials

Weaknesses 6

  • Chronic fragility of the air conditioning compressor
  • Imperfect sound insulation (road and aerodynamic noise on the highway)
  • Non-permanent 4WD system, unsuitable for true off-roading
  • Side-opening tailgate (to the right), impractical for parallel parking
  • High cost of original Honda spare parts
  • Diesel engine (2.2 i-CTDi) prone to costly breakdowns with age

Check before buying

  • **Air Conditioning:** Ensure it blows cold. A breakdown often costs more than 1000 € (compressor + condenser + circuit cleaning).
  • **Rear Differential (AWD):** Make tight circles at low speed in a parking lot. Any groaning or rubbing indicates worn differential fluid.
  • **Airbag Recalls:** Verify that the global Takata recall has been performed.
  • **Corrosion:** Inspect the rear wheel arches, sills, and engine subframe, especially on models driven in mountainous or cold regions.
  • **Oil Level (Petrol):** VTEC engines can consume a little oil at high RPMs. Prolonged low oil levels can stretch the timing chain.
  • **Clutch (Diesel):** Test for slippage in 3rd/4th gear and listen for knocking at idle (dual-mass flywheel).

Verdict

The second-generation Honda CR-V is a true "tank" if chosen with a petrol engine (2.0 or 2.4 i-VTEC). These blocks are among the best 4-cylinder engines ever produced. It is a highly recommended purchase for families looking for a utilitarian, spacious, and indestructible vehicle on a budget.

However, it is imperative to check the condition of the air conditioning compressor and ensure that the rear differential has been regularly serviced.

**To avoid:** High-mileage diesel versions (2.2 i-CTDi) without a clear service history, as the cost of replacing a flywheel, turbo, or exhaust manifold will often exceed the vehicle's residual value.
