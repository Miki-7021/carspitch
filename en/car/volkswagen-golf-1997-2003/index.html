Volkswagen Golf

IV · 1997 – 2003 (jusqu'en 2006 pour le break Variant, et jusqu'en 2010/2014 pour certains marchés émergents comme le Brésil ou le Canada sous le nom City Golf)

NEGOTIATE Reliability 75/100 — bon

Summary

The **Volkswagen Golf IV (Mk4)** marked a major turning point in the history of the compact car category. Launched in 1997, it set new standards in terms of perceived quality, interior finish, and sound insulation, bordering on the premium segment (Audi A3 8L with which it shares its PQ34 platform). While its road handling was often deemed sluggish and too comfort-oriented compared to a Peugeot 306 or a Ford Focus, it made up for it with a plethora of engine options, notably introducing the formidable pump-injector TDI engines and the legendary VR6 of the R32. Now entering the *youngtimer* era, its reliability depends heavily on its service history, with some engines being indestructible and others veritable problem magnets.

**Expert's overall rating: 7.5/10** (Smoothed average, wide gap between a temperamental 1.4 16v and an indestructible 1.9 TDI 90).

Strengths 6

  • Interior finish and material quality (for its time)
  • Corrosion resistance (fully galvanized body)
  • Huge engine range (from the placid SDI to the furious R32)
  • Performance/consumption ratio of the 1.9 TDI engines
  • Availability and price of spare parts (used market and aftermarket)
  • Ergonomics of controls and driving position

Weaknesses 6

  • 'Soft-touch' interior plastics that peel and become sticky over time
  • Very fragile window regulator mechanisms (plastic clips that break)
  • Failing door lock micro-switches (car no longer detects door opening)
  • Sluggish road handling, original suspension too soft (pumping)
  • Rapid wear of anti-roll bar bushes and front wishbone bushes
  • Headliner that detaches and sags over the years

Check before buying

  • Operation of all electric windows (listen for cracking noises).
  • Central locking and interior light when doors open (test micro-switches).
  • Condition of the headliner and center console plastics.
  • History of timing belt and water pump (metal or plastic impeller?).
  • On TDIs: absence of tremors at idle or during clutch engagement (sign of a failing dual-mass flywheel).
  • On TDIs: power cut under full load (limp mode due to a seized variable geometry turbo).
  • On 1.4 and 1.6: oil traces around the gearbox (risk of rivet breakage).

Verdict

The Volkswagen Golf IV is an icon of the 2000s. Today, buying one is either an economic choice (for a new driver or a budget-conscious commuter) or a passion purchase (GTI, V6, R32). **The verdict is to NEGOTIATE firmly**, as most models on the market have high mileage and have often suffered from neglected maintenance or dubious modifications.

**Expert's choice:** Look for a **1.9 TDI 90 or 110 hp** (rotary pump) for bulletproof reliability, or a **1.8T 20v** for petrol enjoyment. Avoid the 1.4 16v and the early TDI 115 (AJM). Budget for chronic minor flaws (window regulators, door locks, headliner) that will inevitably affect all examples.
