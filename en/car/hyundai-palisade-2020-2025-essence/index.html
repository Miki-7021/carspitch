Hyundai Palisade

I (LX2) · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The first-generation Hyundai Palisade (code name LX2) is the Korean brand's flagship and status-enhancing family SUV. Designed primarily for the North American market, it shares its platform with the Kia Telluride. Offering up to 8 seats, it stands out with a high-end interior finish quality that rivals premium models, exceptional ride comfort, and a very rich technological equipment list. It received a facelift in 2022 for the 2023 model year, modernizing its front fascia and equipment. Overall, the Palisade boasts an excellent reliability record, supported by proven V6 engines and strong customer satisfaction.

Strengths 5

  • Remarkable reliability of the 3.8L V6 engine and HTRAC transmission
  • Interior finish quality and soundproofing worthy of the premium segment
  • Generous onboard space (up to 8 real seats) and ergonomics
  • Unbeatable price-to-equipment ratio compared to the competition
  • Strong owner satisfaction (Consumer Reports)

Weaknesses 4

  • High fuel consumption on V6 engines
  • Imposing size making urban parking difficult
  • Several recalls concerning accessories (wipers, tow hitch) and electronics
  • Absence of a hybrid powertrain (HEV/PHEV) on this generation

Check before buying

  • Recall history (very important: tow hitch harness, oil pump, 2024 valve springs)
  • Operation of the front wiper motor (prone to failure in snowy/icy weather)
  • Transfer case maintenance on models equipped with HTRAC AWD
  • Condition of the driving assistance (ADAS) sensors on the front bumper

Alternatives

* **North America / Asia:** Kia Telluride (technical cousin, same reliability), Toyota Highlander (benchmark for reliability and hybridization), Honda Pilot (very spacious), Ford Explorer. * **Europe:** The Palisade is not officially imported into Western Europe. Similar-sized alternatives are the Hyundai Santa Fe (slightly smaller), the Kia Sorento, or the Toyota Highlander.

Verdict

The Hyundai Palisade is an excellent choice in the large 3-row SUV category. It stands out as one of the best alternatives to the Toyota Highlander thanks to its luxurious cabin, generous space, and the robustness of its 3.8L V6 engine. Although the list of recalls may seem long, the majority concern accessories or peripheral electronic components covered by the manufacturer, and not major powertrain design flaws (with the exception of a specific batch of valve springs in 2024). It is a highly recommended vehicle, provided you verify that all recalls (especially the tow hitch harness) have been performed.
