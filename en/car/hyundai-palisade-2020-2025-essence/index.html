Summary

The first-generation Hyundai Palisade (code name LX2) is the Korean brand's flagship and status-enhancing family SUV. Designed primarily for the North American market, it shares its platform with the Kia Telluride. Offering up to 8 seats, it stands out with a high-end interior finish quality that rivals premium models, exceptional ride comfort, and a very rich technological equipment list. It received a facelift in 2022 for the 2023 model year, modernizing its front fascia and equipment. Overall, the Palisade boasts an excellent reliability record, supported by proven V6 engines and strong customer satisfaction.

Strengths 5

  • Remarkable reliability of the 3.8L V6 engine and HTRAC transmission
  • Interior finish quality and soundproofing worthy of the premium segment
  • Generous onboard space (up to 8 real seats) and ergonomics
  • Unbeatable price-to-equipment ratio compared to the competition
  • Strong owner satisfaction (Consumer Reports)

Weaknesses 4

  • High fuel consumption on V6 engines
  • Imposing size making urban parking difficult
  • Several recalls concerning accessories (wipers, tow hitch) and electronics
  • Absence of a hybrid powertrain (HEV/PHEV) on this generation

Check before buying

  • Recall history (very important: tow hitch harness, oil pump, 2024 valve springs)
  • Operation of the front wiper motor (prone to failure in snowy/icy weather)
  • Transfer case maintenance on models equipped with HTRAC AWD
  • Condition of the driving assistance (ADAS) sensors on the front bumper

Official recalls

The Palisade has been the subject of several recalls, primarily in North America (NHTSA). The most notable include:

  • [2026-01-23] Curtain airbags (26V034000): Potentially incorrect deployment of the curtain airbags for 3rd-row passengers (2020-2025 models).
  • [2025-09-12] Seat belts (25V607000): Seat belt buckles (driver, passenger, 2nd row) may fail to latch (2020-2025 models).
  • [2025-05-06] Electric oil pump (25V291000): Poor sealing of the electric oil pump controller allowing moisture accumulation on the printed circuit board (2025 models).
  • [2024-02-13] Valve springs (24V106000): Risk of valve springs breaking while driving, which can lead to a loss of power or puncture the engine block (2024 models).
  • [2023-08-25] Passenger presence sensor (23V599000): The ODS system may not detect an occupant on dark premium suede seats, deactivating the airbag (2024 models).
  • [2023-07-27] Idle Stop & Go oil pump (23V526000): Damaged electronic oil pump controller (2023-2024 models).
  • [2023-06-08] Brake assist (23V415000): Misalignment of the brake booster diaphragm causing a vacuum leak and loss of assist (2023 models).
  • [2023-03-17 & 2022-08-15] Windshield wipers (23V180000 / 22V627000): Wiper motor failure, particularly in the event of snow/ice accumulation (2020-2023 models).
  • [2022-08-18] Tow hitch harness (22V633000): Fire risk due to debris and moisture accumulation in the tow hitch harness module (2020-2022 models).
  • [2022-05-18] Instrument cluster (22V353000): Excess adhesive that can deactivate the dashboard screen (2022 models).
  • [2021-10-27] Brake fluid (21V840000): Possible brake fluid contamination causing the master cylinder seals to swell (2021 models).
  • [2019-11-07] Curtain airbags (19V802000): Mounting bolts that could damage the airbag during deployment (2020 models).

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Exceptional V6 Gasoline Engine Reliability — Enjoy absolute peace of mind thanks to the legendary robustness of its 3.8L V6 gasoline engine, renowned for its durability and smooth operation, ensuring serene journeys in the long term.

Comfort

  • A Luxurious and Quiet Cabin — Immerse yourself in a premium-segment interior, with impeccable finish quality, refined materials, and remarkable soundproofing, transforming every journey into an experience of pure comfort.

Practicality

  • Generous Space for 8 Passengers — The Palisade offers exceptional interior space, comfortably accommodating up to 8 adults across three rows, with family-oriented ergonomics and smart modularity for all your needs.

Safety

  • Advanced Active and Passive Safety — Travel with confidence thanks to an exemplary level of safety, attested by 5 Euro NCAP stars and the IIHS Top Safety Pick+ distinction, effectively protecting adults and children with its advanced systems.

Braking

  • Confident and Efficient Braking — The Palisade's braking system ensures powerful and secure deceleration, offering a competitive stopping distance for a large SUV, guaranteeing perfect control in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Serene Driving and Impeccable Stability — Enjoy handling focused on comfort and stability, with a suspension that absorbs road irregularities and well-controlled body roll, for a smooth and soothing driving experience, ideal for long journeys.

Economy

  • Rich Equipment at a Competitive Price — The Hyundai Palisade stands out with an unbeatable price/equipment ratio, offering a very rich technological endowment and high-end features, without compromising on quality, compared to its competitors.

Alternatives

  • North America / Asia: Kia Telluride (technical cousin, same reliability), Toyota Highlander (benchmark for reliability and hybridization), Honda Pilot (very spacious), Ford Explorer.
  • Europe: The Palisade is not officially imported into Western Europe. Similar-sized alternatives are the Hyundai Santa Fe (slightly smaller), the Kia Sorento, or the Toyota Highlander.

Verdict

The Hyundai Palisade is an excellent choice in the large 3-row SUV category. It stands out as one of the best alternatives to the Toyota Highlander thanks to its luxurious cabin, generous space, and the robustness of its 3.8L V6 engine. Although the list of recalls may seem long, the majority concern accessories or peripheral electronic components covered by the manufacturer, and not major powertrain design flaws (with the exception of a specific batch of valve springs in 2024). It is a highly recommended vehicle, provided you verify that all recalls (especially the tow hitch harness) have been performed.
