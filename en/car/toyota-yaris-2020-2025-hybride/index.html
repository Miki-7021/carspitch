Toyota Yaris

IV (XP210) · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 94/100 — excellent

Summary

The fourth-generation Toyota Yaris (XP210) marks a complete break from its predecessors. Based on the new TNGA-B modular platform, it gains in dynamism, rigidity, and driving pleasure, while maintaining a compact size (under 4 meters). Its design is much more assertive and muscular. The core of its sales in Europe is the 1.5 hybrid engine (Dynamic Force), which offers formidable efficiency in urban and suburban environments. A slight facelift and technological updates (new multimedia system, digital gauges) occurred in late 2023/early 2024, accompanied by the introduction of a more powerful 130 hp hybrid version. Overall, it is one of the most reliable and accomplished city cars on the market.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional fuel consumption (hybrid version)
  • Overall reliability and longevity of the Toyota hybrid system
  • Dynamic road handling and rigid chassis (TNGA-B)
  • Very comprehensive standard safety equipment (Toyota Safety Sense)
  • Manufacturer's warranty extendable up to 10 years / 160,000 km (Toyota Relax)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very average rear passenger space (legroom and access)
  • Limited boot volume (286 liters) compared to competitors
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Quality of some interior plastics (hard and scratch-prone)
  • Pre-2024 multimedia system a bit dated and slow

Check before buying

  • Charge status and age of the 12V battery (recurrent discharge problem on hybrids driven infrequently).
  • History of hybrid health checks (necessary for extended warranty).
  • Operation of driving aids (clean sensors, no error messages on the dashboard).
  • Wear of front tires (instant electric torque can accelerate wear in the city).
  • Body panel alignment and bumper condition (vehicle often used in the city).

Economy

  • Record Fuel Economy — Enjoy exceptional energy efficiency thanks to its advanced hybrid powertrain, with fuel consumption often below 4.5L/100km, significantly reducing your running costs.

Safety

  • Maximum 5-Star Safety — Benefit from optimal protection for all occupants, confirmed by its 5-star Euro NCAP rating and comprehensive standard Toyota Safety Sense equipment.

Handling

  • Dynamic and Agile Driving Behavior — Its TNGA-B chassis offers remarkable agility and driving precision, making every urban and suburban journey particularly enjoyable and confident.

Braking

  • Ultra-Efficient and Stable Braking — Enjoy enhanced safety with short braking distances and exemplary stability, even in emergency situations, supported by high-performance assistance systems.

Practicality

  • Urban Comfort & Smoothness — Appreciate exceptional driving smoothness and maneuverability in urban environments, making the Yaris Hybrid the ideal city car for your daily life.

Alternatives

- **Europe** : Renault Clio V E-Tech (excellent hybrid alternative, more spacious, but dog-clutch gearbox sometimes temperamental), Honda Jazz e:HEV (unbeatable for interior space and modularity), Peugeot 208 (attractive design, but PureTech engine problematic for reliability). - **Japan / Asia** : Honda Fit (Jazz), Nissan Note e-Power (very pleasant series hybrid in the city), Mazda 2. - **Americas** : The subcompact segment is almost abandoned, the Yaris XP210 is not sold there (only the GR Yaris is available in Mexico).

Verdict

The Toyota Yaris IV is a highly recommended purchase, especially in its hybrid versions (116 or 130 hp). It dominates its segment in terms of reliability and running costs. While it may not be the most spacious or the quietest on the highway, it compensates with significantly improved driving pleasure and unparalleled peace of mind, reinforced by the Toyota Relax warranty program (up to 10 years). It is the ideal city car for daily use.
