Honda HR-V

I (RU) · 2015 – 2021

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The Honda HR-V of the RU generation (often considered the 2nd global generation, but marking the return of the nameplate after the 1999 GH model) is an urban/compact SUV that stands out for its exceptional interior space. Thanks to the central placement of its fuel tank, it benefits from the famous "Magic Seats" (fold-up rear seat bases). Positioned between the B-SUV and C-SUV segments, it received a facelift in late 2018, improving sound insulation and introducing the Sport version (1.5 Turbo). The 1.6 i-DTEC diesel engine, although discontinued in some markets after the facelift due to anti-pollution standards, remains one of the most frugal and reliable options in its category.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability, particularly the 1.6 i-DTEC engine
  • Record-breaking interior space for its size (4.29 m) and unmatched modularity (Magic Seats)
  • Extremely low real-world fuel consumption for the diesel engine
  • Dynamic road handling and excellent manual gearbox control
  • Generous boot volume (470 liters)

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation (road and aerodynamic noise), especially before the 2018 facelift
  • Infotainment system (Honda Connect) slow, dated, and prone to bugs
  • Suspension comfort a bit firm at low speeds
  • Thin body paint, susceptible to chips and micro-scratches
  • Fragile start/stop button on early model years

Check before buying

  • DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter) maintenance history on the 1.6 i-DTEC: check for absence of engine light or limp mode.
  • CVT gearbox operation (if equipped): no jerking or excessive slipping at startup is tolerable.
  • Push-button start: ensure it starts the engine on the first try without hesitation.
  • Rear tire wear: a rear axle geometry defect (torsion beam axle) can cause feathered wear.
  • Electric parking brake (EPB) operation and absence of abnormal noises during activation.

Verdict

The Honda HR-V (RU) is an extremely rational and pragmatic choice. If you target the **1.6 i-DTEC** engine, you are opting for one of the most reliable and economical diesels of its generation. It is ideal for high-mileage drivers looking for a vehicle that is compact on the outside but immense on the inside. The purchase is highly recommended, provided you avoid examples that have been used exclusively in the city (risk of DPF/EGR clogging). Prioritize a post-2018 (facelifted) model for better sound insulation, although the diesel version is rarer in these.
