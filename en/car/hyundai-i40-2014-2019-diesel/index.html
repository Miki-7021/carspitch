Summary

The Hyundai i40 (VF generation), in its facelifted version introduced in late 2014 (2015 model year), is a D-segment family car designed specifically for the European market. Available as a sedan and especially as an estate (CrossWagon / CW) which accounts for the majority of sales, it stands out for its generous interior space, rich equipment, and its 5-year unlimited mileage manufacturer warranty (which covered most models until recently). The facelift brought a modernized front end (hexagonal grille), new lights, reworked suspension for better handling, and above all the introduction of the DCT-7 dual-clutch gearbox coupled with the 1.7 CRDi diesel engine boosted to 141 hp. At the end of its career (2018), the 1.7 CRDi was replaced by the new 1.6 CRDi (Smartstream) to meet the Euro 6d-Temp standard. It is a pragmatic vehicle, shunned in favor of SUVs, which makes it an excellent bargain on the used market.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent interior space, particularly in the rear seats and the trunk (CW version)
  • Overall reliability of the 1.7 CRDi engine block (robust timing chain)
  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Improved soundproofing and ride comfort on this phase 2
  • Very intuitive ergonomics of physical controls (climate control, radio)

Weaknesses 5

  • DCT-7 gearbox sometimes hesitant at low speeds and prone to dry clutch wear
  • Aging infotainment system (although CarPlay/Android Auto appeared late in its career)
  • Electric power steering lacking feel and center point
  • Imposing size in the city (4.77 m for the estate)
  • GDI petrol engines lacking punch compared to rival TSIs

Check before buying

  • DCT-7 Gearbox: Test the vehicle at very low speeds (ramping) and on an incline. Any juddering or shaking indicates worn clutches.
  • Steering star (Flector): Clunking or play in the steering column during slight steering wheel movements at a standstill (a €5 rubber part, but labor-intensive).
  • LED daytime running lights (DRL): Flickering or failure of the front light signature (internal electronic board issue often requiring replacement of the headlight unit).
  • DPF / EGR (Diesel): Check the usage history. A diesel used only in the city will have a clogged DPF (check engine light, power loss).
  • Reversing camera: Lens prone to water ingress, making the image blurry or blue.

Official recalls

What sets it apart 7

Safety

  • High-level Safety — The Hyundai i40 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 92% for adult protection and 86% for child protection, ensuring peace of mind for the entire family.

Braking

  • Efficient and Reassuring Braking — The i40's braking system is recognized for its competence and reliability. The brakes are effective and progressive, offering an excellent sense of security in all driving situations.

Handling

  • Stable and Comfortable Road Holding — The i40 offers safe and predictable road holding, prioritizing comfort and stability, especially on long motorway journeys. Its dynamic behavior is balanced and reassuring.

Reliability

  • Proven Mechanical Reliability of the Diesel Engine — The 1.7 CRDi engine block is renowned for its robustness and reliability, notably thanks to its chain timing, guaranteeing longevity and controlled maintenance costs.

Practicality

  • Generous Cabin Space and Boot Volume — The i40 stands out for its exceptional cabin space, particularly in the rear seats, and a very generous boot volume (especially in the CW wagon version), ideal for families and long trips.

Comfort

  • Optimized Ride Comfort and Sound Insulation — This restyled version benefits from improved ride comfort and refined sound insulation, offering a serene and pleasant driving experience, even over long distances.

Economy

  • Excellent Value for Money on the Used Market — The Hyundai i40 offers an unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used car market, providing a rich level of equipment for a very competitive price, making it a pragmatic and advantageous choice.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Volkswagen Passat (segment benchmark), Skoda Superb (superior interior space), Peugeot 508, Ford Mondeo, Renault Talisman, Mazda 6.
  • Asia / Oceania: Toyota Camry, Kia Optima (technical cousin), Honda Accord.

Verdict

The facelifted Hyundai i40 is an extremely rational used car choice, particularly in the estate (CW) body style with the 1.7 CRDi engine. While the diesel block itself is very robust (reliable timing chain, no major flaws), it is imperative to favor examples that have been driven on the highway to avoid DPF/EGR issues. The manual gearbox is the choice for absolute peace of mind. The DCT-7 dual-clutch gearbox brings real driving comfort but requires a rigorous test drive before purchase to ensure the clutches do not judder. Faced with sometimes temperamental European competitors, the i40 offers appreciable peace of mind at a very competitive price.
