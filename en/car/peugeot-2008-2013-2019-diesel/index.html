Peugeot 2008

I · 2013 – 2019

NEGOTIATE Reliability 55/100 — moyen

Summary

The **first-generation Peugeot 2008 (A94)** marked the successful entry of the lion brand into the highly competitive urban SUV (B-SUV) segment, effectively replacing the 207 SW. Based on the 208's PF1 platform, it stands out for its dynamic road handling, excellent comfort/roadholding compromise, and its famous *i-Cockpit* (small steering wheel, raised instrument cluster).

A **2016** facelift gave it a more adventurous look (vertical grille, fender flares) and updated the engines to meet Euro 6 standards. While the vehicle is intrinsically very well designed, its overall reliability score is heavily impacted by major design flaws in its flagship engines (wet belt on PureTech petrols, AdBlue system and camshaft chain on BlueHDi diesels).

Strengths 5

  • First-class road handling (agility, precise front axle)
  • Extremely low fuel consumption for diesel engines (often under 5L/100km)
  • Refined interior finish for the category (especially in Allure/Crossway/GT Line trims)
  • Very decent boot volume (350 to 1194 liters) with a low loading sill
  • Reliability of Euro 5 diesel engines (e-HDi)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the AdBlue system on 1.6 BlueHDi (repair cost > €1000)
  • Risk of engine failure on the 1.5 BlueHDi (7 mm chain) and 1.2 PureTech (belt)
  • i-Cockpit driving position that obscures the instrument cluster for some body types
  • Multimedia system (SMEG/SMEG+) slow and prone to bugs on models before 2017
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)

Check before buying

  • **UREA light / Engine fault (BlueHDi)**: Almost certain sign of an AdBlue tank or pump failure. A deal-breaker if not repaired.
  • **Tapping noise from upper engine (1.5 BlueHDi)**: Indicates a slack camshaft chain. Flee immediately.
  • **Belt condition (PureTech)**: Open the oil cap and check if the belt is cracked or frayed.
  • **Maintenance history**: Crucial. Oil changes must have been done on time with the exact oil recommended by PSA.
  • **Front axle**: Listen for suspension noises (shock absorber mounts, wishbone bushings) on degraded roads.
  • **Touchscreen**: Check responsiveness, Bluetooth and GPS functionality (frequent spontaneous reboots).

Official recalls

  • - **November 2020 / December 2022 (PureTech)**: Massive recall for inspection and replacement of the timing belt, vacuum pump, and oil strainer. - **February 2020 (BlueHDi)**: Engine management software update to optimize NOx emissions and AdBlue management. - **May 2016 (Handbrake)**: Risk of unexpected loosening of the parking brake. Mechanism replacement. - **October 2019 (Relay)**: Replacement of a starter control relay that could cause an untimely start or a short circuit.

Verdict

The Peugeot 2008 I is an excellent urban SUV dynamically, but a **minefield on the used market** due to its engines.

**If you're looking for a diesel:** Absolutely prioritize **1.6 e-HDi (92 or 115 hp)** versions from before 2015. They are robust, economical, and free from the capricious AdBlue system. If you opt for a **1.6 BlueHDi**, demand an invoice proving that the AdBlue tank has already been replaced with the new reference. Avoid the **1.5 BlueHDi** unless you have formal proof (Peugeot invoice) that the camshaft chain modification (change to 8 mm) has been carried out.

For petrol engines, PureTechs require extreme vigilance regarding their maintenance history.
