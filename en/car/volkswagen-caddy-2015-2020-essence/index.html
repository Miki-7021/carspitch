Summary

The fourth-generation Volkswagen Caddy (Type SA), launched in 2015, represents a significant evolution of the brand's leisure activity vehicle and light commercial vehicle. Based on a platform combining elements of the MQB architecture (for the front) and PQ35 (for the rear), it offers a compromise between modernity and robustness. Aesthetically, it benefited from a notable facelift in 2015, giving it a more contemporary look in line with other VW models of the era. It positions itself as a versatile vehicle, appreciated by professionals for its loading capacities and by families for its space and modularity. The build quality is typically Volkswagen, solid and functional, although the interior may appear austere on entry-level versions. Overall, the Caddy IV is a reliable and durable vehicle, particularly with diesel engines, but gasoline versions also deserve special attention.

Strengths 5

  • Exemplary spaciousness and modularity
  • Volkswagen build quality, general robustness
  • Very reliable and fuel-efficient TDI engines
  • Safe and comfortable road handling for the category
  • Wide choice of versions (commercial, leisure activity vehicle, 4Motion)

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (especially commercial versions)
  • Functional but austere interior on basic trims
  • Limited performance of small gasoline engines (1.0/1.2 TSI) with load
  • Higher fuel consumption for gasoline engines under load or on the highway
  • Cost of parts and maintenance at VW

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for diesels and CNG)
  • Condition of the DPF and EGR valve on 2.0 TDI (check regenerations)
  • Timing belt on gasoline engines (date and mileage)
  • Functioning of the AdBlue system on recent TDIs
  • Wear of suspensions and brakes (vehicle often subjected to heavy loads)
  • Condition of CNG tanks and the specific circuit on 1.4 TGI
  • Presence of rust on commercial versions that have suffered impacts or been poorly maintained

Verdict

The Volkswagen Caddy IV is an excellent choice for anyone looking for a versatile, spacious, and robust vehicle. Its general reliability is very good, especially with the 2.0 TDI (EA288) diesel engines, which are safe bets for high-mileage drivers and professionals. The gasoline versions (1.0, 1.2, 1.4 TSI) are also reliable, but their driving pleasure and fuel consumption can be limited with heavy loads or on long highway journeys. They are better suited for urban or suburban use, or for families not excessively loading the vehicle. The 1.4 TSI offers the best compromise in gasoline. The 1.4 TGI is interesting for its running costs but requires specific maintenance and access to CNG stations.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize a model with a clear service history, especially for diesels (DPF, AdBlue) and CNG (tanks). For intensive family or professional use, the 2.0 TDI 102 or 122 hp is the most relevant. For lighter, lower-mileage use, the 1.4 TSI is a highly recommendable gasoline option. Avoid the most basic versions if comfort is a priority. The Caddy IV is a safe investment that will retain good resale value.
