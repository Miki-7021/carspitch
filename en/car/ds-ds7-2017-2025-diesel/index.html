Summary

The DS7, initially launched as the DS7 Cross in 2017, marked DS Automobiles' entry into the highly competitive premium SUV segment. Positioned as the brand's flagship, it stands out with its bold design, luxurious interior, and ride comfort. Based on the Stellantis (formerly PSA) group's EMP2 platform, it shares its technical underpinnings with models like the Peugeot 3008/5008 but distinguishes itself with a more refined and technological approach.

A major facelift occurred in 2022, dropping the 'Cross' designation to become simply 'DS7'. This update brought aesthetic changes (front fascia, light signature), technological improvements (infotainment, driving aids), and the introduction of the E-Tense 360 plug-in hybrid powertrain.

Overall, the DS7 offers a driving experience focused on comfort and refinement, with reliability that, while generally good, presents a few points of concern, particularly regarding diesel emission control systems and the management of plug-in hybrid powertrains.
