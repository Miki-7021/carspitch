Summary

The MG Marvel R (Generation I) is a D-segment family SUV, positioned as the technological flagship of the MG brand (SAIC Motor group) in Europe at its launch. Based on the Chinese Roewe Marvel R, it stands out with a meticulous interior finish, a plethora of standard equipment, and a statutory design. **ATTENTION - IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION:** Although the request targets a diesel engine, **the MG Marvel R is an exclusively 100% electric vehicle (BEV)**. It has never been offered with an internal combustion engine (neither diesel, nor petrol, nor hybrid) in any global market. The analysis will therefore focus on its electric powertrains, which constitute the only available offering. The model suffered from a 400V electrical architecture limiting its fast charging speed against fierce competition, precipitating its announced end of career for 2025 in favor of models based on the new MSP (Modular Scalable Platform).

Strengths 5

  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Very well-crafted interior finish (leather, soft-touch plastics, assembly)
  • Very meticulous soundproofing (acoustic glazing)
  • Performance of the AWD version (3 motors)
  • Safe and comfortable road handling

Weaknesses 5

  • No diesel or petrol engine available (contrary to the request)
  • Very disappointing DC fast charging speed (92 kW max), lengthening highway travel times
  • Infotainment system (19.4-inch screen) slow, complex, and prone to bugs
  • Ridiculous rear trunk volume for the size (357 liters)
  • High electrical consumption on the highway (often > 22 kWh/100 km)

Check before buying

  • Condition of the 12V battery (source of 90% of immobilization breakdowns on this model)
  • Smooth operation of the central touchscreen (demand the latest software update)
  • Tire wear, particularly on the rear axle and on the AWD version
  • Operation of the heating and air conditioning (possible failures of the heat pump sensors)
  • Locking/unlocking of the Type 2 cable on the vehicle's charging socket

Verdict

The MG Marvel R is a paradox: it is an extremely well-finished, comfortable, and high-performing electric SUV, but it suffers from charging technology (92 kW) and a multimedia system that are already obsolete compared to current competition. **Let us recall that there is no diesel version.** On the used market, its steep depreciation makes it an excellent deal for a buyer looking for a premium family vehicle for daily or regional trips, charging primarily at home. For heavy highway drivers, the slow fast charging and high consumption make it less relevant than a Tesla Model Y or a Hyundai Ioniq 5.
