Skoda Kodiaq

II (2024–2025) · 2024 – 2025

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The **second-generation Skoda Kodiaq (2024+)** is based on the Volkswagen Group's MQB Evo platform. It builds on the strengths of its predecessor (giant spaciousness, 'Simply Clever' features, immense trunk) while massively modernizing its technological content and powertrains. The interior takes a leap forward with the integration of 'Smart Dials' (physical rotary controllers with digital displays) which correct the criticized all-touch ergonomics of other group models. Although electrification is at the heart of this new iteration (with an impressive PHEV offering ~100 km of range), **the 2.0 TDI diesel engine remains a central and strategic offering**, particularly optimized (EA288 evo with double AdBlue injection) for high-mileage drivers, large families, and trailer towing.

*Expert's note: As the Kodiaq II is very recent, long-term reliability feedback is still in the consolidation phase. However, the mechanics are largely proven (VW parts bin).*

**Overall design score: 8.5/10**

Strengths 6

  • 2.0 TDI engine still in the catalog, extremely refined and economical
  • Exceptional spaciousness and trunk volume (up to 910 L in 5-seater configuration)
  • Physical ergonomics preserved thanks to 'Smart Dials' (intelligent rotary controllers)
  • Real-world electric range of the PHEV version (often > 80 km in real conditions)
  • Masterful suspension comfort with the DCC Pro option
  • Towing capacity maintained on 4x4 Diesel versions

Weaknesses 5

  • Imposing size (4.76 m) making urban parking tricky
  • PHEV version not available in 7-seater configuration (due to the battery under the floor)
  • Persistent software bugs on the MIB4 infotainment system at launch
  • Significant price increase compared to the first generation
  • Significantly increased weight, particularly on the plug-in hybrid version

Check before buying

  • Software update history (multimedia system and engine/hybrid management)
  • On the 2.0 TDI: check for absence of AdBlue/Engine warning light (SCR system sensitivity to short trips)
  • On 4x4 versions (TDI 193 and TSI 204): maintenance of the Haldex/BorgWarner coupling (oil change every 3 years or 30,000 km)
  • Body panel alignment and interior trim noises on the very first 2024 models
  • Smooth operation of 'Smart Dials' (the screens integrated into the rotary controllers can sometimes 'freeze')

Verdict

The Skoda Kodiaq II is undoubtedly one of the most pragmatic and accomplished family SUVs on the current market. **For the Diesel engine (2.0 TDI 150 or 193 hp), the verdict is a big YES.** It's the sensible choice for those who drive a lot, tow, or travel with heavy loads. The EA288 evo engine has corrected the early flaws of previous TDI generations and offers an unbeatable performance/consumption ratio on the highway. If your usage is primarily urban, **avoid Diesel** (risk of DPF clogging) and opt for the 1.5 TSI mHEV or the excellent (but heavy) 1.5 TSI iV PHEV. Just make sure the vehicle has received all the latest software updates (MIB4) before purchase.
