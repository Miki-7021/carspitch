Toyota RAV4

V (XA50) · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The fifth-generation Toyota RAV4 (code name XA50) marks a radical departure from its predecessors. Based on the excellent TNGA-K modular platform, it abandons rounded shapes for an angular and robust design, inspired by North American off-road vehicles. This model has consolidated Toyota's dominance in the hybrid SUV market thanks to formidable efficiency and generous interior space. A slight refresh (facelift) occurred in late 2021/early 2022, bringing new optics, finish improvements, and, most importantly, a modernized infotainment system. Although the hybrid (HEV) powertrain is the undisputed star in Europe, the RAV4 offers a full range of internal combustion and plug-in hybrid (PHEV) powertrains globally. Overall, it is one of the most reliable vehicles in its category, although a few specific early issues need to be monitored.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability of the hybrid powertrain (4th generation HSD)
  • Impressive fuel consumption for an SUV of this size (often under 6L/100km in hybrid mode)
  • Generous interior space and trunk volume (580 liters)
  • Extremely high resale value on the used car market
  • Very comprehensive standard safety features (Toyota Safety Sense)

Weaknesses 5

  • Engine revving (whining) effect during strong accelerations due to the e-CVT transmission
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Dated and slow infotainment system on pre-facelift models (2018-2021)
  • Quality of some lower interior plastics disappointing for the price
  • Costly high-voltage cable corrosion issue on early AWD-i versions (Cablegate)

Check before buying

  • **High-Voltage Cable (AWD-i only):** Check the corrosion status of the orange connector at the rear electric motor. An out-of-warranty repair costs over 3000 €.
  • **12V Battery:** Test the voltage. Many owners report rapid discharge if the vehicle remains stationary for more than a week.
  • **Water Ingress:** On 2019-2020 models equipped with roof rails, check for the absence of stains on the headliner (defective mounting seals).
  • **Fuel Tank (Hybrid):** Ask if the vehicle has undergone the fix if the tank fills up to 40 liters instead of the advertised 55 liters.
  • **Hybrid Health Check History:** Essential to benefit from the traction battery warranty extension (up to 10 years with Toyota).

Official recalls

  • **Suspension Arms (May 2020):** Risk of cracking of the front lower suspension arms on certain models produced in 2019 and 2020. Replacement by Toyota.
  • **eCall System (2021):** Possible malfunction of the emergency communication module in case of an accident. Software update.
  • **DC-DC Converter (July 2023):** On PHEV models, risk of short-circuit in the DC-DC converter potentially leading to power loss or fire risk.
  • **Fuel Pump (2020):** Massive Denso recall affecting certain internal combustion RAV4s (especially 2.5L NA) for a risk of engine stalling.
  • **Brake Assist (2019):** Replacement of the brake assist pump on a small series of early production models.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Honda CR-V e:HEV (excellent hybrid system, very spacious), Hyundai Tucson / Kia Sportage Hybrid (bold design, 7-year Kia warranty), Volkswagen Tiguan (superior finish, but less efficient). - **North America:** Honda CR-V Hybrid (the direct competitor), Subaru Forester (better mechanical AWD system), Mazda CX-50 (more dynamic and premium). - **Asia / Japan:** Nissan X-Trail e-Power (electric drive with thermal generator), Subaru Forester e-Boxer. - **Emerging Markets:** Haval H6 HEV, Chery Tiggo 7 Pro (cheaper Chinese alternatives).

Verdict

The 5th generation Toyota RAV4 is a highly recommendable purchase, establishing itself as the pragmatic benchmark for family SUVs. Its hybrid system is a model of efficiency and durability. However, if you opt for an all-wheel-drive (AWD-i) version from before 2022, a rigorous inspection of the rear high-voltage cable is **mandatory** to avoid a hefty bill. The two-wheel-drive (Traction) versions are exempt from this problem and represent the safest and most economical choice. Prioritize a post-2021 model to benefit from an finally up-to-par multimedia system.
