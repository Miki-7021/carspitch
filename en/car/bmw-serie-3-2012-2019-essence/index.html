BMW Série 3

F30 · 2012 – 2019

NEGOTIATE Reliability 76/100 — bon

Summary

The sixth-generation BMW 3 Series (F30 for the sedan, F31 for the Touring wagon, F34 for the Gran Turismo, F35 for the long-wheelbase version in China, and F80 for the M3) marks a major turning point for the Bavarian brand with the shift to 'all-turbo' for petrol engines. Launched in 2012, it benefited from a mid-cycle facelift (LCI - Life Cycle Impulse) in 2015. This facelift is crucial: it marks the abandonment of 'N' generation engines (N13, N20, N55) in favor of the new modular 'B' architecture (B38, B48, B58), thus correcting most of the early issues. Although the F30 is praised for its dynamism, its excellent 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox, and its iDrive multimedia system, pre-LCI versions (2012-2015) require great vigilance due to timing chain problems, particularly on the N20 4-cylinder petrol and N47 diesel engines.

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic road handling and balanced rear-wheel drive (50/50 weight distribution)
  • Exceptional 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox (Steptronic) for smoothness and responsiveness
  • Extremely reliable and powerful 'B' generation petrol engines (B48, B58)
  • Intuitive and responsive iDrive infotainment system (especially from NBT Evo onwards)
  • Perfect driving position and driver-oriented ergonomics

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous timing chain reliability on the N20 petrol engine (pre-2015)
  • Disappointing quality of some interior plastics (door grab handles melt and become sticky with heat)
  • Average aerodynamic sound insulation and road noise for the premium category
  • Electric Power Steering (EPS) lacking feel compared to the older E90 generation
  • High maintenance cost at official BMW dealerships

Check before buying

  • Service history: 'LongLife' intervals of 30,000 km are too long. Prefer a model with oil changes every 15,000 km.
  • Timing chain noise (whistling or rattling) on N20 and N47 engines.
  • Clunking in the steering during slight movements of the steering wheel when stationary (known rack problem, a BMW repair kit exists).
  • Condition of interior door handles (melted/sticky rubber).
  • Oil leaks around the oil filter and valve cover (especially on N55 6-cylinders).
  • iDrive screen operation (cases of intermittent restarts or screen delamination).

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Audi A4 (B8/B9) for interior finish and Quattro; Mercedes C-Class (W204/W205) for comfort; Alfa Romeo Giulia for an even sharper chassis. - **Japan / Asia:** Lexus IS (XE30) for unbeatable hybrid reliability and superior finish, although the infotainment is dated. - **North America:** Cadillac ATS (excellent chassis but reduced interior space), Acura TLX, Infiniti Q50 (very powerful 3.0t VR30 V6 engine but divisive *steer-by-wire* steering).

Verdict

The BMW 3 Series F30 is a car with two faces. Phase 1 models (pre-LCI, 2012-2015) equipped with N20 petrol or N47 diesel engines should be approached with extreme caution due to costly timing chain problems. In contrast, facelifted models (LCI, from mid-2015 onwards) equipped with 'B' series engines (B48 in 320i/330i, B58 in 340i) transform the F30 into one of the most reliable and desirable premium sedans on the market. **Our advice:** Avoid 320i/328i models from before 2015 unless the timing chain has been replaced with an invoice. Prioritize a 320i/330i LCI (B48) for daily use, or a 340i (B58) for enthusiasts. Don't forget to budget for a ZF automatic gearbox oil change around 90,000 km.
