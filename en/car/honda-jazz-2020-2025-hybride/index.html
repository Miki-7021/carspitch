Honda Jazz

IV (GR) · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 94/100 — excellent

All Honda models

Summary

The fourth-generation Honda Jazz (code name GR/GS, sold as Fit in some markets) marks a major turning point for the model in Europe, where it is only offered as an e:HEV hybrid version. True to its DNA, it retains record-breaking interior space for its size (3.99 m) thanks to its central fuel tank and its famous "Magic Seats". The i-MMD hybrid system (renamed e:HEV) is an engineering marvel: it primarily functions as a series hybrid (the internal combustion engine acts as a generator) and only connects directly to the wheels at cruising speed via a lock-up clutch, thus doing without a traditional gearbox. An adventurous "Crosstar" version completes the range. In 2023, a facelift brought a power increase (from 109 to 122 hp) and an Advance Sport trim with specific suspension settings. It is one of the most reliable and accomplished city cars on the market.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability (mechanical and electronic)
  • Extremely low real-world fuel consumption in city and on road
  • Giant interior space for a car under 4 meters
  • Unbeatable modularity (Magic Seats rear bench)
  • Excellent peripheral visibility (slimmer A-pillars)
  • Smooth driving in urban environments

Weaknesses 5

  • Internal combustion engine 'revving' effect during strong accelerations (hills, overtakes)
  • Very high purchase price new and used
  • Infotainment system a bit dated compared to European competitors
  • Highway sound insulation could be improved
  • Slightly reduced boot volume compared to the previous generation (due to the hybrid battery)

Check before buying

  • Condition of the 12V battery (frequent discharge issue on models driven infrequently or stored for long periods).
  • Service history within the Honda network (essential for the hybrid system warranty, often extended to 5 or 10 years depending on the market).
  • Operation of driving aids (Honda Sensing): check for absence of error messages on the dashboard.
  • Wear of front tires (the immediate torque of the electric motor can accelerate wear if driven aggressively).

Verdict

The Honda Jazz IV (GR) is undoubtedly the most rational and reliable city car of its generation. Its e:HEV hybrid system is a model of efficiency in urban and suburban environments, offering astonishing real-world fuel consumption. If one accepts its minivan-like design and the noise of its engine during strong accelerations, it's a 'zero hassle' purchase. On the used car market, its value is strong, but this initial investment is largely offset by negligible running and maintenance costs, as well as an almost total absence of major breakdowns.
