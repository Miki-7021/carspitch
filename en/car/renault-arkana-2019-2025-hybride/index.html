Summary

The first-generation Renault Arkana is an SUV coupe with a major industrial peculiarity: it exists on two distinct platforms depending on the market. First launched in Russia in 2019 on the rustic B0+ platform (that of the Dacia Duster), it arrived in Europe and South Korea (under the name Samsung XM3) in 2021 on the modern CMF-B platform (Clio V, Captur II).

In the European market, the Arkana skipped diesel to focus on hybridization, spearheaded by the 1.6 E-Tech Full Hybrid (145 hp) engine. This innovative technology (naturally aspirated engine + two electric motors + clutchless dog-clutch gearbox) offers excellent efficiency in urban and peri-urban environments. However, its technical complexity has led to early issues (particularly with gearbox management) that require special vigilance when buying used. A light facelift was carried out in mid-2023, correcting most of the electronic bugs.

Strengths 6

  • Distinctive and appealing SUV-Coupe design
  • Rear spaciousness preserved despite the roofline
  • Generous boot volume (except E-Tech which loses some volume)
  • Very low consumption for the E-Tech version in urban environments (4.5 to 5 L/100km)
  • Excellent mechanical reliability of the 1.3 TCe engine
  • Safe and secure road handling (CMF-B platform)

Weaknesses 6

  • Variable reliability of the dog-clutch gearbox on E-Tech models before 2023
  • E-Tech engine's driving pleasure on the highway (combustion engine revs high during battery recharging)
  • Very poor rear visibility
  • Quality of some lower interior plastics
  • Suspension a bit firm at low speeds (especially with 18-inch wheels)
  • No 4-wheel drive version in Europe (reserved for the Russian market on B0+ platform)

Check before buying

  • On E-Tech: Complete absence of violent jerks or gear noise during electric/thermal transitions.
  • On E-Tech: Verify that all recall campaigns (OTS) concerning the reprogramming of the hybrid control unit have been carried out.
  • On 1.3 TCe: Smoothness of the EDC7 gearbox at startup and during low-speed maneuvers.
  • Easy Link multimedia system: test responsiveness, GPS, and Bluetooth/CarPlay connection.
  • Front tire wear (the electric torque on the E-Tech can prematurely wear out the tires).

Official recalls

  • November 2023 (Fire risk): Recall for models manufactured between early 2022 and mid-2023. A potential short circuit in the 12V battery or electrical harness can cause a fire.
  • March 2022 (Power steering): Recall for a risk of loss of power steering assistance due to a control unit calibration defect (models produced late 2021).
  • OTS (Special Technical Operation) E-Tech: Silent campaigns during revisions to reprogram the hybrid gearbox control unit to prevent dog-clutch failure.

Verdict

The Renault Arkana is an attractive choice for its design and spaciousness. If you are considering the 1.6 E-Tech Hybrid engine, caution is advised: prioritize a model from late 2022 or newer, or demand a clear service history proving that the gearbox software updates were performed on time. In case of doubt or for predominantly highway use, the 1.3 TCe (140 or 160 hp) engine is a much safer choice, offering remarkable reliability and better overall driving pleasure.
