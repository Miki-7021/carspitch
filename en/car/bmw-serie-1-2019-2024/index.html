Summary

The BMW 1 Series F40 marks a historic and controversial turning point for the Bavarian brand: the abandonment of the traditional rear-wheel-drive (RWD) layout and longitudinal engines in favor of the FAAR front-wheel-drive (FWD) architecture with transverse engines. While purists mourned the loss of the inline-6 engine and unique driving dynamics, the F40 gained significantly in everyday practicality. It offers better rear legroom, a larger trunk, and a massive leap in interior material quality and onboard technology (featuring the excellent iDrive 7.0 system). From a reliability standpoint, this generation benefits from the mature 'B-Series' modular engines (B38, B48, B37, B47), which have proven to be vastly superior to the older N-Series engines. Overall, it is a highly accomplished, premium compact hatchback that trades ultimate driving purity for everyday usability and robust reliability.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of the B-Series modular engines (B38/B48/B37/B47)
  • Massive improvement in interior material quality and assembly over the F20
  • iDrive 7.0 infotainment system is intuitive, fast, and class-leading
  • Increased rear passenger legroom and trunk space due to FWD packaging
  • Excellent Aisin 8-speed automatic transmission (on 4-cylinder models)

Weaknesses 5

  • Loss of the traditional BMW RWD driving dynamics and inline-6 engines
  • Firm ride quality, especially on M Sport models with 18/19-inch wheels
  • Front-wheel drive architecture leads to faster front tire wear (torque steer on 128ti)
  • Polarizing front-end design (oversized kidney grilles)
  • Intrusive lane-keeping assist system (needs to be turned off every trip on early software)

Check before buying

  • EGR Cooler status on Diesel models (B37/B47) - check if it has been replaced under recall
  • Front tire tread wear (FWD models tend to eat front tires if driven aggressively)
  • Coolant levels on B48 engines (check for micro-leaks around the water pump and plastic housing)
  • iDrive software version (ensure it has been updated over-the-air to fix early Bluetooth/CarPlay glitches)
  • Service history: ensure oil changes were done on time, preferably shorter than the 30k km interval

Official recalls

  • December 2020 (Steering): Potential issue with the steering gear tie rod which may not have been manufactured to specifications, leading to a loss of steering control (Affects early 2019-2020 builds).
  • August 2021 (Seatbelts): The seatbelt retractor sensor may be faulty, potentially affecting the restraint system in a crash.
  • Ongoing (EGR Cooler - Diesels): While the F40 uses the updated B47 engine, some early diesel models were still inspected for the widespread BMW EGR cooler leak issue, which could cause a fire risk. Always verify the VIN with a dealer.

Verdict

The BMW 1 Series F40 is a highly recommended purchase on the used market. While enthusiasts may reject it for abandoning rear-wheel drive, the objective truth is that the F40 is a vastly superior daily driver compared to its predecessor. The interior is premium, the tech is flawless, and most importantly, the B-Series engines are proving to be incredibly robust and reliable.

Buying Advice: - For city and mixed use: The 118i is the sweet spot. - For performance: The 128ti offers the best FWD hot-hatch fun, while the M135i is a brutally fast all-weather cruiser. - For high mileage: The 118d remains an excellent, frugal choice, provided you check the EGR cooler history. Avoid diesels if you only drive in the city to prevent DPF issues.
