KGM Korando

IV (C300) · 2019 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

Launched in 2019 under the SsangYong brand, the fourth generation of the Korando (code name C300) accompanied the manufacturer's transition to its new KGM (KG Mobility) identity in 2023/2024. This compact SUV is positioned as a pragmatic and economical alternative to the segment leaders (Peugeot 3008, Hyundai Tucson, Nissan Qashqai). It stands out with generous interior space, abundant equipment from the mid-range trims upwards, and excellent towing capacity, particularly in its 1.6 e-XDi diesel version. Although its design is more consensual than previous generations, it suffers from a brand image that is still relatively unknown in Europe, which leads to heavy depreciation. It is a rational vehicle, with highly satisfactory overall mechanical reliability, backed by a long-term manufacturer's warranty (often 5 to 7 years depending on the market).

Strengths 6

  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Very generous rear passenger space and large trunk (551 liters)
  • Excellent overall mechanical reliability, especially the 1.6 e-XDi diesel block
  • Remarkable towing capacity for the category (2,000 kg in diesel automatic)
  • Smooth and highly proven 6-speed Aisin automatic gearbox
  • Long manufacturer's warranty (5 to 7 years depending on years and markets)

Weaknesses 6

  • Higher than average fuel consumption for the category (both petrol and diesel)
  • Sparse KGM / SsangYong dealership network in Europe
  • Heavy depreciation on the used market (an advantage when buying, a drawback when reselling)
  • Soundproofing could be improved at high speeds (wind and road noise)
  • Slightly dated and sometimes slow infotainment system
  • Firm suspension damping at low speeds, especially with 19-inch wheels

Check before buying

  • Service book: verify that oil changes have been done on time (especially for the warranty).
  • Automatic gearbox: demand proof of an oil change if the vehicle is approaching or exceeding 60,000 km.
  • Diesel (DPF/EGR): check for the absence of an engine warning light and ensure the vehicle hasn't only been driven in the city (risk of clogging).
  • Electronics: test all driving aids (ADAS), the touchscreen, and the reversing camera, which can sometimes freeze.
  • Underbody: check for impact damage if the vehicle has been used for towing or on dirt roads.

Verdict

The KGM (SsangYong) Korando IV is the quintessential rational choice. If you are looking for a compact SUV to show off, look elsewhere. On the other hand, if you are looking for a robust, spacious vehicle capable of heavy towing and equipped with comprehensive features for the price of a used city car, it is an excellent deal. **The 1.6 e-XDi diesel engine is the best choice in the lineup**: it is reliable, torquey, and perfectly suited to the vehicle's size, provided you drive enough to avoid DPF clogging. The 1.5 petrol should be reserved for low mileage drivers due to its thirst for fuel. Buy it used to let the first owner absorb the heavy initial depreciation.
