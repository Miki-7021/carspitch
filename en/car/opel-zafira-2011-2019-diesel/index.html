Opel Zafira

C Tourer · 2011 – 2019

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The Opel Zafira C, initially named **Zafira Tourer** to distinguish it from its predecessor (the Zafira B, which remained in the catalog until 2014), marks a clear move upmarket. Gone is the purely utilitarian minivan; instead, it features a dynamic design (inspired by the Ampera with its 'boomerang' headlights before the facelift) and a 'lounge'-oriented interior. It retains the famous **Flex7** retractable seating system, greatly improved to offer individual comfort in the second row.

In 2016, a major facelift abandoned the boomerang headlights for a more classic front end (like the Astra K), modernized the infotainment (IntelliLink, removal of the button-heavy center console), and dropped the 'Tourer' designation.

**Overall note:** It's an excellent cruiser, very comfortable and spacious, but penalized by its significant weight (often over 1.7 tons unladen), which impacts fuel consumption, prematurely wears out running gear, and strains certain engines and gearboxes.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional spaciousness and modularity (Flex7 system, independent and sliding second-row seats)
  • Ride comfort and sound insulation (especially on facelifted versions and with FlexRide chassis)
  • Safe and secure road handling despite its size
  • Significantly improved finish quality compared to the Zafira B
  • Excellent towing capacity with 2.0 CDTI engines

What sets it apart 12

Safety

  • Exemplary Safety — The Opel Zafira C Tourer achieved a 5-star Euro NCAP rating, with outstanding protection for adults (94%) and children (83%), ensuring maximum peace of mind for the entire family.
  • Powerful Braking — Benefit from short and reassuring stopping distances, typically between 38 and 40 meters from 100 km/h, guaranteeing top-tier active safety in all situations.
  • Stable and Comfortable Road Handling — Its healthy, predictable, and secure road behavior, with well-controlled body roll and precise steering, is ideal for long family journeys, offering a serene driving experience.

Practicality

  • Exceptional Modularity and Space — Enjoy the improved Flex7 system, offering individual and sliding seats in the second row, for unparalleled adaptability and interior space, perfect for all family configurations.

Comfort

  • Superior Travel Comfort — Experience remarkable ride comfort and refined sound insulation, transforming every journey into a serene and pleasant experience for all passengers, even on long distances.

Performance

  • Efficient and Robust Diesel Engines — Diesel engines, especially the 2.0 CDTI, are renowned for their generous torque, excellent towing capacity, and perfect adaptation to the vehicle's weight, ensuring power and reliability on the road.

Alternatives

Europe - **Renault Grand Scénic III / IV:** The direct competitor. The Scénic III (1.6 dCi) is very reliable. The Scénic IV focuses more on design but loses modularity. - **Citroën Grand C4 Picasso / SpaceTourer:** Royal comfort, very bright interior, but BlueHDi diesel engines affected by the same AdBlue issues, and PureTech petrol to be absolutely avoided. - **Volkswagen Touran (5T):** Superior finish, excellent resale value, generally reliable TDI engines (after 2015), but much higher used car price. - **Ford S-Max / Galaxy:** More dynamic road handling, gigantic interior space, robust TDCi engines (PSA/Ford) if well maintained.

Asia / Oceania - **Kia Rondo / Carens:** More economical alternative, long-term warranty, reliable CRDi engines. - **Honda Odyssey:** Larger, very reliable, but higher price positioning.
