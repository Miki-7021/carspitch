Mazda MX-5

II (NB) · 1998 – 2005

NEGOTIATE Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation Mazda MX-5, codenamed **NB** (1998-2000) then **NBFL** after its facelift (2001-2005), succeeds the legendary NA. Constrained by pedestrian safety standards, it abandons pop-up headlights in favor of fixed optics, while retaining the *Jinba Ittai* philosophy (the oneness of horse and rider). More rigid, more aerodynamic, and equipped with a real glass rear window (standard on most trims), it gains in comfort without sacrificing its agility. Mechanically, it is a model of exceptional reliability. However, its reputation is heavily tarnished by a major and recurring problem: **structural corrosion**. This is an enthusiast's car, and its purchase must be conditional on a rigorous inspection of the underbody.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability (engines and gearboxes almost indestructible)
  • Exemplary road handling (rear-wheel drive, 50/50 weight distribution)
  • Very low maintenance and insurance costs
  • Availability and price of spare parts (huge aftermarket)
  • Manual gearbox among the best in automotive history
  • Very active enthusiast community (tutorials, forums, clubs)

Weaknesses 5

  • Devastating structural corrosion (the worst enemy of this model)
  • Almost non-existent soundproofing on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Limited interior space for people over 1.85m
  • Restricted trunk volume (144 liters)
  • Original paint (especially the clear-coated red) prone to fading and flaking

Check before buying

  • **Front Chassis Rails:** MAJOR FLAW OF THE NB. The double-sheet metal design traps moisture. They rot from the inside out. Must be inspected from underneath or via the wheel arch.
  • **Sills and rear wheel arches:** The soft top drains get clogged, water stagnates and causes the sills to rust from the inside out.
  • **Soft top drains:** Check that they are clear (behind the seatbelts).
  • **Soft top condition:** Original vinyl soft tops tend to split at the folds with age. Prefer an alpaca (cloth) soft top.
  • **Radiator color:** A radiator with a brown/khaki plastic top is about to explode. It should be black.
  • **Clutch judder:** On 1.8 models from 2001-2002, test starting in first gear when cold to detect abnormal jerking.

handling

  • Exceptional Road Handling — Globally celebrated for its agility, the MX-5 NB offers first-class road holding thanks to its lightness, near-perfect 50/50 weight distribution, and precise steering. It is extremely responsive and predictable, guaranteeing unparalleled driving pleasure and great ease in avoiding obstacles.

braking

  • Effective and Predictable Braking — Equipped with disc brakes on all four wheels, often complemented by ABS, the MX-5 NB offers an adequate and predictable braking system for a lightweight vehicle of its era. The pedal feel is good, ensuring sufficient effectiveness for its category.

economy

  • Controlled Running Costs — Benefit from very low maintenance and insurance costs, complemented by excellent availability and affordable prices for spare parts, thanks to a huge aftermarket and an active enthusiast community.

Performance

  • Pure Driving Pleasure and Legendary Gearbox — Embodying the "Jinba Ittai" philosophy (the oneness of horse and rider), the MX-5 NB offers an immersive driving experience. Its manual gearbox is recognized as one of the best in automotive history, providing fast and precise gear changes.

comfort

Verdict

The Mazda MX-5 NB is a fantastic sports car, offering an unbeatable price/pleasure/mechanical reliability ratio. The 1.6 and 1.8 engines are models of robustness that can easily exceed 300,000 km with basic maintenance. **However, buying an NB comes down to one word: RUST.** The mechanics should only be a secondary criterion during the visit. If the front chassis rails or sills are attacked, the bodywork costs will often exceed the value of the car. Prioritize a healthy example, even if it means paying more, or a model imported from a dry region. If the chassis is sound, it's a no-brainer purchase.
