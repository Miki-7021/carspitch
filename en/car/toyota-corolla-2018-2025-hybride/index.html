Toyota Corolla

XII (E210) · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 93/100 — excellent

Summary

The 12th generation Toyota Corolla (E210) marks a major turning point for the Japanese manufacturer. In Europe, it drops the Auris name to revert to its historical designation. Based on the excellent TNGA-C platform, it offers a transformed driving experience, high-level comfort, and a much more assertive design. Available as a 5-door hatchback, wagon (Touring Sports), and 4-door sedan (depending on the market), it has established itself as the absolute benchmark for hybrid powertrains (HEV). The 2022/2023 facelift introduced the 5th generation of Toyota's hybrid system, bringing more power, a lighter lithium-ion battery, and an infotainment system finally on par with the competition. It is a rational, extremely reliable vehicle, with one of the lowest total cost of ownership (TCO) on the market.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability, particularly for hybrid powertrains
  • Extremely low fuel consumption (often below 4.5 L/100 km with the 1.8 HSD)
  • TNGA chassis offering an excellent comfort/dynamism compromise
  • Reduced maintenance cost (no conventional clutch, no timing belt, minimal brake wear thanks to regenerative braking)
  • Very high resale value on the used car market
  • Toyota Relax warranty (up to 10 years or 160,000 km in Europe if serviced within the network)

Weaknesses 5

  • Dated and slow infotainment system on pre-facelift models (before 2023)
  • Very average rear passenger space and trunk volume on the 5-door version (Hatchback)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (road and wind noise)
  • Engine revving effect (rubber band effect) during strong accelerations (especially on the 1.8 HSD)
  • Limited rear headroom

Check before buying

  • 12V Battery: Very strong tendency to discharge if the vehicle remains stationary for more than 2 weeks (recurrent problem with Toyota hybrids).
  • HV Battery Cooling Filter: Check that it is not obstructed by dust or pet hair (risk of battery overheating).
  • EGR Valve and Cooler: On high-mileage models (150k+ km), cold stumbling may indicate clogging.
  • Tires: On the 2.0 HSD version (often with 18-inch wheels), front tire wear can be rapid due to the immediate torque of the electric motor.
  • Maintenance history: Essential to benefit from the extended warranty on the high-voltage battery (annual hybrid health check).

reliability

  • Unrivaled Hybrid Reliability — The Toyota Corolla Hybrid is synonymous with peace of mind. Its multi-award-winning hybrid powertrain is globally recognized for its exceptional robustness and longevity, ensuring thousands of worry-free kilometers.

economy

  • Extremely Low Total Cost of Ownership — Enjoy exceptionally low fuel consumption, often below 4.5 L/100 km, combined with reduced maintenance costs thanks to the absence of a clutch and timing belt, and minimal brake wear. An economical choice in the long run.

safety

  • Advanced Active and Passive Safety — With 5 Euro NCAP stars, an impressive 95% score for adult protection and 84% for children, and excellent braking distances (38-40 meters from 100 to 0 km/h), the Corolla offers maximum protection for all occupants. It is also an IIHS Top Safety Pick.

comfort

  • Optimized Driving Pleasure and Comfort — Thanks to its TNGA platform, the Corolla offers a perfect balance between suspension comfort and driving dynamics. Its road holding is safe, predictable, and agile, ensuring a pleasant driving experience in all circumstances.

Performance

  • Latest Generation Hybrid Technology — Benefit from Toyota's 5th generation hybrid system, offering more power (up to 196 hp), a lighter and more efficient lithium-ion battery, and increased responsiveness for a smooth and efficient driving experience.

value

  • Durable Investment and High Resale Value — Toyota's reputation for reliability and the popularity of its hybrid powertrains guarantee exceptional resale value. Furthermore, the Toyota Relax warranty extends your peace of mind up to 10 years or 160,000 km.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Volkswagen Golf (historical benchmark, declining ergonomics), Honda Civic (excellent e:HEV hybridization, more spacious), Peugeot 308 (attractive design, problematic PureTech reliability). - **North America:** Honda Civic (very popular, hybrid version returning), Mazda 3 (premium finish, no full hybrid), Hyundai Elantra (very efficient hybrid version). - **Asia / Emerging Markets:** Nissan Sentra/Sylphy (very comfortable), Honda Civic, Hyundai Avante.

Verdict

The 12th generation Toyota Corolla is undoubtedly the most rational and reassuring purchase in the compact car category. If you are looking for a vehicle to get from point A to point B with negligible running costs and absolute peace of mind, this is the number one choice. The 1.8 Hybrid engine is perfect for city driving and daily commutes, while the 2.0 Hybrid brings the dynamism that was historically lacking in Toyota hybrids, making it very versatile. Simply avoid the 5-door version if you need family space (prefer the Touring Sports wagon). The 2023 facelift corrects its main flaw: the multimedia interface.
