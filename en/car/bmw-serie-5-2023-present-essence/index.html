BMW Série 5

G60 · 2023 – Présent

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The **BMW 5 Series (G60)**, launched in late 2023, represents the 8th generation of the famous Bavarian executive sedan (also available as the G61 Touring estate). Based on an evolution of the modular CLAR platform, it has the particularity of being multi-energy: it accommodates internal combustion engines (petrol and diesel with 48V mild hybridisation), plug-in hybrids (PHEV), and 100% electric powertrains (i5).

Although the model is very recent, which limits long-term reliability feedback on the onboard electronics (iDrive 8.5, driving aids), **the petrol engines (B48 and B58) are technical evolutions (TU2) of existing, extremely reliable blocks**. The 5 Series G60 becomes more luxurious, grows in size (over 5 meters), and gains weight, but retains benchmark dynamism. Manufacturing quality is at the top of its class.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of B48 and B58 petrol engines (among the best on the market).
  • Impeccable finish and assembly quality.
  • Very high level of sound insulation.
  • Superb road handling despite increased weight.
  • Real-world electric range of PHEV versions (often > 80 km).

Weaknesses 4

  • Imposing size (5.06 m) and high weight (often > 2 tonnes), accelerated tire wear.
  • Complex ergonomics of the iDrive 8.5 system (too many touch functions).
  • Frequent software bugs on early 2023/early 2024 models.
  • Disappearance of 6-cylinder petrol engines (540i) from the European market (reserved for North America and Asia).

Check before buying

  • **Integrated Braking System (IBS):** Check if the vehicle has been subject to the massive Continental recall (loss of braking assistance).
  • **Infotainment system:** Ensure that the latest OTA (Over-The-Air) update has been installed to correct black screen bugs or intermittent restarts.
  • **Driving aids (ADAS):** Check the proper calibration of cameras and radars; some owners report phantom braking.
  • **Body panel adjustments:** On the very first units (late 2023), a few rare cases of misaligned panels (doors/hood).

Comfort

  • Benchmark Finish and Assembly — Every detail of the BMW 5 Series G60 demonstrates impeccable finish quality and assembly, placing the vehicle at the top of its category for a premium experience.
  • Superior Soundproofing — Travel in a cocoon of silence thanks to advanced soundproofing, ensuring exceptional serenity and acoustic comfort on all your journeys.

Safety

Braking

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Mercedes-Benz E-Class (W214) (more comfort-oriented, slightly less refined combustion engines), Audi A6 (C8) (end of life, very reliable but aging technology). - **North America:** Genesis G80 (excellent value for money, V6 available), Lexus ES (absolute reliability, but front-wheel drive and less dynamic). - **Asia / China:** Mercedes-Benz E-Class L (long wheelbase), Audi A6L, Nio ET7 (very strong local electric alternative).

Verdict

The BMW 5 Series G60 is a technological tour de force. If you are targeting a petrol engine, you are making the best possible mechanical choice: the B48 (520i/530i) and B58 (540i) engines have reached a remarkable level of reliability, with their early issues corrected (TU2). The main point of vigilance does not concern the mechanics, but the onboard electronics and the braking system (IBS recall to be checked imperatively). This is a highly recommended purchase, provided that software updates have been carried out.
