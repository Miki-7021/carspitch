Summary

The Volkswagen ID.5 (E39A) is the SUV-Coupe derivative of the ID.4. While the model was initially launched in 2022, the major technical update at the end of 2023 (2024 model year) is akin to a true 'facelift' that is invisible on the outside but crucial on the inside. This generation corrects the main teething problems of the ID range: it introduces the new, much more powerful and efficient 'APP550' rear motor, as well as version 4.0 of the infotainment software with a more responsive 12.9-inch screen and finally backlit temperature sliders. The ID.5 positions itself as a more dynamic and aerodynamic alternative to the ID.4, targeting families looking for a distinctive design without sacrificing roominess. Overall update rating: Excellent.

Strengths 5

  • New APP550 motor (286 hp) transforming driving pleasure and efficiency
  • Multimedia system 4.0 finally fluid, intuitive, and equipped with an efficient route planner
  • Battery preconditioning to optimize fast charging in winter
  • Generous rear roominess and preserved trunk volume (549 liters) despite the coupe profile
  • Excellent soundproofing and suspension comfort (especially with DCC adaptive damping)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high weight (over 2.1 tons) penalizing agility
  • Touch controls on the steering wheel still present (and not very ergonomic)
  • Mediocre rear visibility due to the sloping roofline and spoiler
  • High prices, particularly on the GTX version
  • Absence of a rear wiper

Check before buying

  • Tire wear: The immediate torque of 545 Nm (Pro) quickly wears out the rear tires.
  • Software version: Check in the settings that the vehicle is indeed running on software 4.0 or higher.
  • Body panel alignment and interior fitment (some assembly disparities reported from the factory).
  • Operation of the flush door handles (sensitive to freezing in winter).

Verdict

The 2024 update of the ID.5 is a game-changer. By correcting the two biggest flaws of the early ID generations (a slow/buggy multimedia system and a rear motor lacking punch), Volkswagen finally offers a mature electric SUV-Coupe. The new 286 hp APP550 motor provides superior driving pleasure while improving real-world range. The hardware reliability of the MEB platform is generally very good, and the exasperating software issues are now a thing of the past thanks to version 4.0. It is a highly recommendable purchase for families looking for a comfortable long-distance cruiser, provided they monitor tire wear.
