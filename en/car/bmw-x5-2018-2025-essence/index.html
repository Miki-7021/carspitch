BMW X5

G05 · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The fourth-generation BMW X5 (code name G05) was launched in late 2018. Based on the modular CLAR platform, it marks a significant leap forward in terms of finish, onboard technology, and ride comfort, notably thanks to the adoption (optional or standard depending on the version) of air suspension on both axles. The G05 benefited from a mid-cycle refresh (LCI - Life Cycle Impulse) in spring 2023, introducing a redesigned front fascia, the integration of the iDrive 8 interface with a dual curved display, and generalized mild hybridization (48V MHEV) on thermal powertrains. In gasoline, the X5 particularly shines thanks to the legendary B58 inline 6-cylinder engine, now considered one of the most reliable engines on the global market. V8 versions offer supercar performance but require a corresponding maintenance budget. Overall, the G05 is an extremely accomplished premium SUV, whose mechanical reliability (especially with the 6-cylinder gasoline engine) largely surpasses that of its predecessors (E70 and F15).

Strengths 5

  • Masterful reliability of the inline 6-cylinder gasoline engine (B58)
  • Very high level of interior finish quality and sound insulation
  • Smooth and intuitive iDrive infotainment system (7 and 8)
  • Real-world electric range of plug-in hybrid versions (45e and 50e)
  • Dynamic road behavior for an SUV of this size (especially with rear-wheel steering)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high weight (up to 2.5 tons for PHEVs), rapidly wearing out brakes and tires
  • Early electronic bugs on LCI versions (iDrive 8, 50e hybrid management)
  • Prohibitive maintenance and insurance costs for V8 versions (M50i, M60i, X5 M)
  • Extreme sensitivity of the transfer case (xDrive) to tire wear differences
  • Complex and expensive air suspension to replace out of warranty (air springs, compressor)

Check before buying

  • Clear service history: oil changes spaced 30,000 km apart are too long, prioritize models with oil changes every 15,000 km.
  • Transfer case: turn the steering wheel fully at low speed and listen/feel for any jerks or dull noises (sign of VTG wear).
  • Air suspension: check that the vehicle raises and lowers without abnormal compressor noise and that it does not sag after a night of parking.
  • Electronics: test all cameras (360°), the electric tailgate (actuator noise), and check for the absence of any engine warning light (especially on 50e).
  • Tires: check for the star marking (BMW homologation) on the sidewalls and even wear between front and rear.

What sets it apart 7

reliability

  • Exceptional reliability of the B58 inline 6-cylinder petrol engine — The B58 petrol engine is globally renowned for its robustness and longevity, ensuring unparalleled peace of mind. It is highly praised by independent organizations for its excellent results.

safety

  • Advanced passive and active safety — Designed to maximize occupant protection, the X5 G05 integrates advanced safety systems and has achieved top distinctions in crash tests.

braking

  • Powerful and reassuring braking performance — Despite its size, the X5 G05 offers remarkable braking performance, ensuring total vehicle control in all circumstances and great confidence behind the wheel.

handling

  • Dynamic and agile road handling — The X5 G05 surprises with its agility and exemplary road holding for an SUV, offering a sporty and secure driving experience with perfectly controlled body roll.

comfort

  • Luxurious interior and perfect sound insulation — Enjoy a sumptuous cabin with very high-quality materials, impeccable finish, and first-class sound insulation, transforming every journey into a serene and refined experience.

Performance

  • Exhilarating performance from petrol powertrains — Whether it's the inline 6-cylinder or the powerful V8s, the X5 G05's petrol engines offer generous power, remarkable smoothness, and impressive acceleration.

economy

  • Remarkable electric range for plug-in hybrid versions — Plug-in hybrid (PHEV) models offer substantial electric range, allowing most daily commutes to be made with zero CO2 emissions and significantly reducing fuel consumption.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Mercedes-Benz GLE (W167 - superior comfort, diesel PHEV available, but capricious electronics), Porsche Cayenne (E3 - sportier, impeccable finish, more expensive), Audi Q7 (4M - more spacious, aging).
  • North America: Lexus RX (absolute reliability, but less dynamic), Acura MDX, Genesis GV80 (excellent value for money, distinctive design).
  • Asia: Mercedes-Benz GLE, Audi Q7, Volvo XC90 (very popular as PHEV, minimalist design).

Verdict

The BMW X5 G05 is undoubtedly the best premium SUV of its generation. Unlike its predecessors, which suffered from costly mechanical flaws, the G05 shines with the exceptional reliability of its 6-cylinder engines, particularly the B58 gasoline unit (xDrive40i) and its hybrid derivative (xDrive45e). It is a heavy and complex vehicle: maintenance tolerates no negligence (star-marked tires are mandatory, frequent oil changes). V8 versions are reserved for enthusiasts willing to bear a very high running budget. For a used purchase, a 2021-2022 xDrive40i or xDrive45e (before the transition to iDrive 8, with corrected early bugs) represents the safest and most balanced choice on the market.
