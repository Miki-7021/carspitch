Honda Jazz

I (GD) · 2001 – 2008

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The first-generation Honda Jazz (code GD, sold as Honda Fit in some markets) is a versatile city car that revolutionized its segment thanks to its unique architecture. With its fuel tank placed under the front seats, it offers record interior space and introduced the famous “Magic Seats” (foldable rear seats).

**⚠️ EXPERT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:** You have targeted the diesel engine. As an expert, I must correct a common misconception: **the Honda Jazz I (GD) was NEVER marketed with a diesel engine**, in any global market. Honda has always favored its excellent gasoline engines (and later hybrids) for this model. The analysis will therefore focus on the comprehensive range of gasoline engines actually available, which also offer fuel consumption close to a diesel of the era without its drawbacks (DPF, dual-mass flywheel, etc.).

Overall, the Jazz GD is a monument of reliability, often ranked at the top of satisfaction surveys (J.D. Power, TÜV).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of gasoline engines (L-Series)
  • Huge interior space for its size (3.83 m)
  • Unbeatable modularity (Magic Seats)
  • Very low real-world fuel consumption (close to a diesel without the drawbacks)
  • Reduced maintenance cost (timing chain)

Weaknesses 5

  • Absence of a diesel engine (for high-mileage drivers of the era)
  • Frequent water ingress in the trunk (roof seals, tailgate sealant)
  • Capricious CVT gearbox if poorly maintained (hesitation)
  • Light sound insulation on the highway
  • Fairly firm suspension comfort

Check before buying

  • Lift the trunk mat and check for water or rust in the spare wheel well.
  • For CVT gearbox: perform a test drive with frequent stops and starts. No shaking or hesitation should be felt on takeoff.
  • For manual gearbox: listen if a whistling sound disappears when disengaging the clutch at idle (worn primary shaft bearing).
  • Check invoices to ensure that all 8 spark plugs (on 1.2 and 1.4 i-DSI) have been changed.
  • Verify that the Takata airbag recall has been performed (crucial for safety).

Official recalls

Practicality

  • Unrivaled Cabin Space and Modularity — Despite its compact size, the Jazz offers giant cabin space and unbeatable modularity thanks to its famous "Magic Seats," allowing for unique loading configurations.

Economy

  • Remarkable Fuel Economy — Its i-DSI petrol engines achieve very low real-world fuel consumption, rivalling that of diesels from its era, while benefiting from a timing chain for simplified maintenance.

Safety

  • Proven Safety — The Honda Jazz I achieved a 4-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with 73% adult protection and 65% child protection, ensuring solid safety for its occupants.
  • Effective and Predictable Braking — Equipped with ABS and EBD as standard, the Jazz offers good and predictable braking, with competitive stopping distances for its category, generally around 40-42 meters from 100 km/h.

tenue de route

  • Agile and Safe Road Handling — Praised for its maneuverability and ease of driving, the Jazz offers predictable and safe road handling, with good agility, especially in urban environments.

Alternatives

Verdict

The Honda Jazz I (GD) is one of the most reliable and intelligent used city cars on the market. **Regarding your diesel search: forget it, it doesn't exist.** However, the i-DSI gasoline engines are so economical and reliable that they make the presence of a diesel completely unnecessary for this size. It is a highly recommended purchase, provided you check for the absence of water ingress in the trunk and prioritize a manual gearbox, or a CVT with a clear service history (exclusive Honda fluid). Beware of examples where maintenance has been neglected (rear spark plugs not changed).
