Opel Mokka

B · 2020 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The second-generation Opel Mokka (Mokka B) marks a complete break from its predecessor (Mokka X). Gone is the General Motors era; this model is based on the Stellantis group's modular CMP platform (shared with the Peugeot 2008 II and DS 3 Crossback). It inaugurates the brand's new stylistic language, with the famous "Opel Vizor" grille and the "Pure Panel" digital dashboard. Shorter by 12.5 cm but with a similar wheelbase, it loses rear passenger space what it gains in dynamism and lightness (up to 120 kg less). Although primarily targeting the European market, it offers a complete multi-energy range: petrol, diesel, mild hybrid (MHEV), and 100% electric. **Overall design score: 7/10** (Very successful design, but reduced passenger space and combustion engines marred by the flaws of the Stellantis parts bin).

Strengths 5

  • Bold and distinctive exterior design (Opel Vizor)
  • Dynamic road handling and rigorous chassis
  • Interior ergonomics (retention of physical controls for air conditioning)
  • Contained weight compared to the previous generation
  • Multi-energy offering (Combustion, MHEV, Electric)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very cramped rear passenger space (legroom and access)
  • Average boot volume (350 L for combustion, 310 L for electric)
  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.2 PureTech (Turbo) with wet belt until 2022/2023
  • Interior plastics (gloss black) very prone to scratches
  • Slow and sometimes buggy infotainment system

Check before buying

  • Service book (1.2 Turbo): Oil changes must have been performed on time, without any delays.
  • Timing belt condition (1.2 Turbo): To be visually checked via the oil filler cap.
  • Engine light / UREA (1.5 Diesel): Sign of an imminent AdBlue system failure.
  • Onboard charger (Electric): Test charging at a public AC 11 kW charging station.
  • Body panel alignment: Some assembly defects on 2020-2021 models.

Official recalls

  • - **December 2021 (Wipers):** Risk of wiper motor malfunction in cold weather (water infiltration). Concerns models produced in 2020 and 2021.
  • - **October 2021 (Steering):** A steering column tie rod may not have been tightened to the correct torque. Models produced between late 2020 and mid-2021.
  • - **2022 (Software / Emissions):** Engine control unit update on 1.5 Diesel versions to optimize NOx emissions.
  • - **2023 (Traction Battery - EV):** BMS (Battery Management System) reprogramming to prevent an unexpected loss of traction on the Mokka-e.

Verdict

The Opel Mokka B is a beloved urban SUV thanks to its assertive design and driving pleasure. However, caution is advised on the used car market. The 1.2 Turbo petrol versions (100 and 130 hp) from before 2024 are equipped with the notoriously problematic PureTech engine with a wet timing belt: avoid if the service history is not 100% perfect. If you are looking for a petrol Mokka, absolutely prioritize the new 1.2 Turbo Hybrid 136 hp version (with chain) that appeared in 2024. The Mokka Electric is an excellent alternative for urban/peri-urban use, provided you check the proper functioning of its onboard charger.
