Honda Accord

X (CV) · 2017 – 2022

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The 10th generation Honda Accord (CV series) marks a major break in the model's history. Adopting a 'fastback' sloping roofline, it definitively abandons V6 engines in favor of turbocharged 4-cylinder engines and a highly developed hybrid powertrain. It is based on a lightened and stiffened platform, partly shared with the Civic and CR-V.

**⚠️ EXPERT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:** Although your search targets a diesel engine, it is imperative to clarify as an expert that **the 10th generation Honda Accord was NEVER offered in a diesel version**, in any global market. Following the withdrawal of the Accord from the European market after the 8th generation and facing the global decline in demand for diesel (Dieselgate scandal, emission standards), Honda simply removed the i-DTEC blocks from the Accord range. This sheet therefore exhaustively covers the gasoline and hybrid powertrains that constitute 100% of the offering for this generation.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional chassis: one of the best comfort/dynamism compromises in its class
  • Gigantic interior space (especially in the rear seats) and large trunk
  • Very powerful 2.0T engine and responsive 10-speed automatic gearbox
  • Extremely efficient and reliable i-MMD hybrid system
  • Significantly improved interior ergonomics (return of physical buttons for volume and climate control)
  • Very comprehensive standard Honda Sensing safety suite

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of diesel engine (a disadvantage for some import markets)
  • Oil dilution and head gasket problems on the 1.5T engine
  • Imperfect sound insulation (road noise on highways)
  • Front seat base a bit low and firm for some body types
  • Infotainment system prone to bugs on 2018-2019 models

Check before buying

  • Engine oil level and smell on the 1.5T (a strong gasoline smell indicates a dilution problem).
  • Air conditioning operation (does it blow truly cold? The condenser is a known weak point).
  • Steering feel at highway speeds (look for a stiff spot or 'stickiness' requiring constant micro-corrections).
  • Recall history, especially the replacement of the low-pressure fuel pump (Denso).
  • Infotainment system stability (unintended restarts, Bluetooth/CarPlay connection).

Official recalls

  • - **Fuel pump (2020-2021):** Massive recall (NHTSA 21V215000 / 20V314000) concerning the Denso low-pressure fuel pump whose impeller can deform and block the pump, leading to engine stalling. - **Body Control Module / BCM (2020):** Software update (NHTSA 20V770000) because a bug could disrupt the operation of the wipers, defroster, rearview camera, and exterior lighting. - **Rearview camera (2018):** The rearview camera display may not function correctly due to a software bug in the central screen (NHTSA 18V601000). - **Seat belts (2018-2019):** Replacement of front seat belt buckles that might not latch correctly.

Alternatives

- **North America & Asia:** Toyota Camry (absolute benchmark in reliability and hybrid), Mazda 6 (premium finish, dynamic handling), Hyundai Sonata / Kia K5 (onboard technology, warranty), Nissan Altima (seat comfort, available all-wheel drive). - **Europe (as import):** Volkswagen Passat, Skoda Superb, Peugeot 508 (these models offer diesel engines, unlike the Accord 10G).

Verdict

The 10th generation Honda Accord is a brilliant family sedan, offering a driving experience superior to the average for its segment. **However, engine choice is crucial.** The entry-level 1.5T engine should be approached with caution due to its documented oil dilution problems and head gasket fragility at high mileage. In contrast, the **2.0T and Hybrid versions are true benchmarks in terms of reliability and driving pleasure**. If you are looking for an alternative to European sedans (and the absence of diesel is not a deal-breaker), an imported 2.0T or Hybrid Accord is a highly recommended purchase.
