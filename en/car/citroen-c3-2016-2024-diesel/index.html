Summary

The third-generation Citroën C3 (codename SX) marked a major stylistic break from its predecessor, adopting a crossover-inspired look with its famous side 'Airbumps' and two-tier headlights. Based on the PF1 platform (and not the CMP of the Peugeot 208 II), it overtly prioritizes ride comfort and spaciousness over pure dynamism. A restyling (facelift) occurred in early 2020, bringing a redesigned front end with standard LED headlights and the integration of 'Advanced Comfort' seats. Although this report focuses on the diesel (BlueHDi) engines, which are very popular with high-mileage drivers and company fleets, the C3 was predominantly sold with petrol (PureTech) engines. The overall reliability of the model is heavily marred by major design flaws in both its engine families (AdBlue tank on diesel, wet belt on petrol).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional suspension comfort for the category
  • 'Advanced Comfort' seats (after facelift) very plush
  • Extremely low fuel consumption for BlueHDi engines (often < 4.5 L/100 km real-world)
  • Original and customizable design (two-tone roof, Airbumps)
  • Decent boot volume (300 liters)

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of pollution control systems (AdBlue) on diesels
  • Risk of engine failure on the 1.5 BlueHDi (camshaft chain)
  • Catastrophic reliability of the wet belt on PureTech petrol engines
  • Slow and buggy infotainment system
  • Clumsy road handling, significant body roll
  • Average sound insulation on the highway

Check before buying

  • AdBlue tank replacement history (invoice essential for a BlueHDi purchase).
  • Metallic rattling noise at idle on the 1.5 BlueHDi (sign of camshaft chain wear).
  • Condition of the timing belt on petrol versions (cracks visible through the oil filler opening).
  • Touchscreen operation (air conditioning, radio, GPS).
  • Suspension noises over speed bumps (premature wear of front shock absorbers or strut mounts).

Official recalls

  • November 2020 (PureTech): Massive recall for timing belt inspection and vacuum pump/oil strainer cleaning (models produced from 2013 to 2017).
  • October 2019 (BlueHDi): Non-compliance with NOx emissions, engine management software update.
  • September 2021: Risk of short circuit related to the battery cable harness rubbing against a metal part.
  • May 2023: Engine Control Unit (ECU) software issue potentially leading to unexpected engine shutdown.
  • February 2024: Recall concerning the particulate filter (FAP) on certain diesel models (risk of cracking in the surrounding plastic).

What sets it apart 7

comfort

  • Unrivalled Driving Comfort — The Citroën C3 III stands out with its exceptional suspension comfort, absorbing road imperfections for a smooth and pleasant driving experience, even on long journeys. The 'Advanced Comfort' seats (available after restyling) add an incomparable touch of softness.

economy

  • Ultra-Low Fuel Consumption — Equipped with BlueHDi diesel engines, the C3 III offers extremely low fuel consumption, often less than 4.5 L/100 km in real-world conditions, making it an economical choice for high-mileage drivers.

safety

  • Proven Euro NCAP Safety — With a 4-star Euro NCAP rating, the Citroën C3 III ensures solid protection for its occupants, with high scores for adult and child protection.

braking

  • Reliable and Safe Braking — The C3 III's braking system is perfectly suited to its category, offering average braking distances and overall safe performance for daily use in the city and on the road.

road_holding

  • Safe and Predictable Road Handling — Comfort-oriented, the C3 III offers safe and predictable road handling. Its soft suspension ensures a smooth ride, while its light steering facilitates city maneuvers.

Design

  • Unique and Customizable Style — The C3 III stands out with its original and bold, crossover-inspired design, featuring its famous 'Airbumps' and customization options like the bi-tone roof, allowing you to express your style.

practicality

  • Generous Boot Volume — Despite its compact size, the Citroën C3 III offers a decent boot volume of 300 litres, ideal for shopping or weekend luggage, providing good everyday versatility.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 208 (same engines, more dynamic handling), Renault Clio V (very reliable 1.5 Blue dCi, excellent finish), Volkswagen Polo (robust 1.6 TDI), Toyota Yaris (Hybrid, unbeatable reliability), Dacia Sandero.
  • Asia / Oceania: Suzuki Swift, Mazda 2, Toyota Yaris.

Verdict

The Citroën C3 III is an extremely endearing city car due to its comfort and looks. However, as an expert, buying a diesel (BlueHDi) version is akin to a financial lottery. The 1.6 BlueHDi is a good engine marred by a faulty AdBlue system (expect 1000 to 1500€ for tank replacement). The 1.5 BlueHDi adds the risk of camshaft chain breakage. If you absolutely must buy a diesel for very high mileage, demand a model whose AdBlue tank has already been replaced recently (with invoice) and, for the 1.5 BlueHDi, prioritize a late 2023 model or plan for the preventive replacement of the chain kit (switching to 8 mm). The petrol (PureTech) versions are no better off due to the wet belt. A purchase that requires extreme vigilance regarding the maintenance history.
