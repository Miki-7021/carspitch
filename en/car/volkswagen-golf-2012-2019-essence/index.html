Volkswagen Golf

VII · 2012 – 2019

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

All Volkswagen models

Summary

The Volkswagen Golf VII (Type 5G) marks a major turning point for the brand with the inauguration of the modular MQB platform. Unlike the Golf VI which was a deep evolution of the V, the VII is 100% new. In terms of reliability, it corrects the catastrophic errors of the previous generation (notably the fragile timing chains of the EA111 TSI engines, replaced here by belts on the EA211 family). The 2017 facelift (often called Golf 7.5) brings new driving aids, digital gauges (Active Info Display) and the new 1.5 TSI Evo engine. It is one of the most homogeneous and reliable compact cars of its decade, provided you choose your transmission carefully (beware of the dry-clutch DSG7 DQ200 gearbox).

Strengths 5

  • Reliability of EA211 gasoline engines (1.0, 1.2, 1.4 TSI) greatly improved compared to the Golf 6
  • Exceptional interior finish quality and material aging
  • Ergonomics of controls and infotainment system (especially post-2015)
  • Superb road handling and high-level sound insulation
  • Parts availability and ease of resale

Weaknesses 5

  • DSG7 automatic gearbox (dry-clutch DQ200) paired with engines < 250 Nm (1.0, 1.2, 1.4, 1.5 TSI): premature clutch wear and mechatronics failures
  • Chronic coolant leaks on 2.0 TSI (GTI/R)
  • Electronic bugs on early Discover Pro multimedia systems (2012-2014)
  • Possible water ingress in the trunk or rear doors (defective speaker seals)
  • Rear shock absorbers sometimes noisy (clunking) on early model years

Check before buying

  • DSG gearbox history: if DQ200 (engines < 150hp), check for absence of juddering when shifting 1st/2nd.
  • Coolant level: an unexplained drop often indicates a leaky water pump or thermostat housing (especially on 2.0 TSI and TDI).
  • Smooth cold-start operation of the 1.5 TSI engine (absence of jerks).
  • Traces of moisture under the rear floor mats and in the spare wheel well.
  • Air conditioning operation (compressors sometimes fragile on 2013-2015 models).

Official recalls

  • Airbags (2020-2021): Recall related to defective Takata gas generators (mainly concerns hot/humid markets, but some European models affected).
  • Wheel bearings (2019): Replacement of rear bearings on a series of models manufactured in 2018 (risk of failure).
  • Child safety (2016): Manual locking of rear doors could deactivate (2015-2016 models).
  • ABS/ESP (2017): Software update for the ABS control unit following a risk of failure during emergency braking (2016-2017 models).
  • Panoramic sunroof (2014): Risk of cracking of the sunroof frame, requiring the addition of reinforcement plates.

  • Premium Interior and Intuitive Ergonomics — Enjoy exceptional interior finish quality, durable materials, and command ergonomics designed for your comfort, even after years of use.

safety

  • Maximum Safety with 5 Euro NCAP Stars — The Golf VII achieved 5 stars in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores (94% for adult protection, 89% for children), and received the Euro NCAP Advanced Award for its multi-collision braking system.
  • Exemplary Road Handling and Ultra-Efficient Braking — Benefit from imperial, safe, and balanced road handling, complemented by very short braking distances (often 36-38 meters from 100 to 0 km/h) and remarkable stability.

practicality

  • A Safe Investment with Easy Resale — The Golf VII is renowned for its versatility, spare parts availability, and strong demand in the used car market, ensuring excellent resale value.

performance|economy

  • The 1.4 TSI: The Perfect Balance of Performance and Economy — The 1.4 TSI engine (125 or 150 hp) is the ideal choice, offering an outstanding performance-to-consumption ratio for dynamic and economical driving.

Alternatives

  • Europe : Peugeot 308 II (excellent chassis but very problematic 1.2 PureTech engine), Ford Focus III/IV (good compromise), Seat Leon III (technically identical to the Golf but cheaper).
  • Japan / Asia : Honda Civic (superior mechanical reliability, polarizing design), Mazda 3 (very reliable naturally aspirated engines, premium finish), Toyota Corolla (unbeatable hybrid in the city).
  • North America : Honda Civic, Toyota Corolla, Mazda 3 (the Golf is often perceived as more premium but more expensive to maintain there).
  • Emerging markets : Chevrolet Cruze, Hyundai Elantra.

Verdict

The Volkswagen Golf VII is an excellent used car choice, particularly with gasoline engines. It has successfully erased the flaws of the Golf VI. The top choice is the 1.4 TSI (125 or 150 hp) with a manual gearbox: it offers an outstanding performance/consumption/reliability ratio. The 1.5 TSI is also recommendable if the software update has been performed. GTI and R versions (2.0 TSI) are robust but require a significant maintenance budget (water pump, DSG/Haldex oil changes). The only real caveat concerns the DSG7 (DQ200) automatic gearbox on smaller engines: opt for a manual gearbox if you are buying a high-mileage model without a history of clutch or mechatronics replacement.
