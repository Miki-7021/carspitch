Summary

The 4th generation Lexus RX (code AL20 — often confused with the 5th generation AL30 released at the end of 2022) marked a major stylistic departure for the brand with its angular design, gaping trapezoidal grille, and floating roof. Positioned in the premium SUV segment (E-SUV), it competes with the BMW X5 and Mercedes GLE by focusing on supreme comfort, top-flight soundproofing, and legendary reliability. Although the hybrid powertrain (RX 450h) is dominant in Europe, the 100% petrol versions (RX 200t/300 and RX 350) have enjoyed great success in North America, Russia, and Asian markets. The 2019 facelift (2020 model year) corrected its biggest flaw by replacing the touchpad multimedia interface with a touchscreen compatible with Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, while also stiffening the chassis.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability (often ranked 1st in its class)
  • Imperial ride comfort and limousine-like soundproofing
  • Flawless finish and assembly quality (materials that age very well)
  • Highly efficient hybrid system (450h) for a vehicle weighing over 2 tons
  • Very high resale value on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Pre-2020 infotainment system (Remote Touch) is archaic, slow, and distracting
  • Clumsy road handling, focused on comfort to the detriment of dynamism
  • Disappointing trunk volume for its size (especially on the 450h due to the batteries)
  • "Rubber-band" effect (CVT) on the 450h during hard acceleration
  • Imposing size (4.89 m) making urban parking tricky

Check before buying

  • Verify that the recalls concerning the fuel pumps (low and high pressure) have been carried out.
  • On hybrid versions, demand the annual "Hybrid Health Check" certificates for the battery warranty.
  • Test the multimedia screen and touchpad (frequent bugs on 2016-2018 models).
  • Inspect tire and brake wear (the vehicle's high weight accelerates their wear).
  • Check for water leaks around the panoramic sunroof (seals can sometimes be temperamental).

Official recalls

The following data is sourced from official registries (Rappel Conso FR and NHTSA US):

  • [2025-11-07] (Rappel Conso - France): Software issue with the parking assist control unit (PVM) that can lead to an incorrect display. Risk of injury (pedestrian impact). Affects multiple models including the RX.
  • [2025-03-10] (Rappel Conso - France): High-pressure fuel pump (RX 300 / NX 200). Insufficient pressure of the parts can cause cylinder movement in the pump, leading to a risk of fuel leak and fire.
  • [2025-01-22] (NHTSA 25V028000 - USA): Failure of the low-pressure fuel pump located in the fuel tank (specifically affects 2019 RX350L). Can cause the engine to stall while driving.
  • [2024-08-22] (Rappel Conso - France): Welding defect on the camera housings (panoramic/rear view) allowing water infiltration, which can disintegrate the housing and cut the image.
  • [2024-06-26] (Rappel Conso - France): Immobilizer design is insufficiently robust. The vehicle may unexpectedly remain in "Ready On" mode.
  • [2023-12-20] (NHTSA 23V865000 - USA): Defective Occupant Classification System (OCS) sensor on the front passenger seat (2020-2021 RX350, RX450h). Risk of airbag non-deployment in the event of a crash.

Alternatives

  • North America: Acura MDX (very reliable V6, sportier handling, more spacious 3rd row), Genesis GV80 (modern luxury, extended warranty), Lincoln Nautilus.
  • Japan / Asia: Toyota Harrier (more compact but similar philosophy), Infiniti QX60.

Verdict

The Lexus RX (AL20) is one of the most reliable luxury SUVs ever built. If you are looking for a vehicle to swallow up the miles in absolute comfort without ever worrying about costly breakdowns, it is the ideal choice. The petrol engines (RX 300 and RX 350) are models of robustness, although the 3.5L V6 is penalized by its fuel consumption. In Europe, the RX 450h remains the best buy thanks to its efficiency. It is imperative to favor a facelifted model (from late 2019 / 2020 model year) to benefit from the touchscreen and an improved chassis. Just make sure that the fuel pump-related recalls have been carried out.
