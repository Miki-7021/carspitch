Summary

The **Alfa Romeo Stelvio I Phase 2** (often called MY23, introduced late 2022) represents the maturity of the Italian premium SUV based on the excellent Giorgio platform. This aesthetic and technological update finally brings the "3+3" Matrix LED headlights inspired by the Tonale and a 12.3-inch full digital instrument cluster, closing its technological gap with German competitors. While its road handling remains the absolute benchmark in the category (ultra-direct steering, 50/50 weight distribution, carbon fiber driveshaft), this Phase 2 particularly shines with **significantly improved electronic and mechanical reliability** compared to 2017-2019 models. The complete absence of electrification (neither MHEV nor PHEV) heavily penalizes it in markets with high ecological taxation, but makes it a purist's choice, especially with the 2.0 Turbo petrol engine.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional chassis: agility, front axle precision, and dynamism unmatched in its category
  • Perfectly calibrated ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (smoothness and speed)
  • Mechanical reliability of 2.0 GME petrol engines now excellent
  • Successful technological update (highly effective Matrix LED headlights, legible digital gauges)
  • Timeless design that ages remarkably well
  • Retained physical ergonomics (real climate control buttons)

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of hybridization (prohibitive ecological penalty in France for petrol versions)
  • Quality of some plastics in the lower part of the cabin below German standards
  • Less dense Alfa Romeo dealer network and variable after-sales service quality
  • Faster depreciation than its direct competitors (Audi Q5, Porsche Macan)
  • Large turning radius

Check before buying

  • **12V battery condition**: Number 1 cause of electronic bugs (engine warning lights, Start&Stop deactivation, IBS alerts).
  • **Maintenance history (Petrol)**: Verify that the exact oil standard has been respected to preserve the MultiAir electro-hydraulic system.
  • **Tire wear**: The front axle naturally scuffs during maneuvering (scrubbing), but pronounced wear on the inside of the tires indicates a need for alignment.
  • **Infotainment system operation**: Ensure that the latest OTA or dealership updates have been applied to avoid unexpected reboots.

Verdict

The **Alfa Romeo Stelvio Phase 2** is a vehicle that has reached full maturity. The exasperating early-life flaws have been eradicated, making this SUV one of the most mechanically reliable in its category, particularly with the 2.0 Turbo petrol engine. If you are looking for a premium SUV for pure driving pleasure, with surgical steering and a communicative chassis, it has no equivalent (apart from the Porsche Macan, which is much more expensive). The ideal choice is the **2.0 Turbo 280 hp**, robust and powerful. Buying a recent used model is highly recommended, where depreciation works in the buyer's favor, provided you accept the absence of hybridization and entrust maintenance to a meticulous specialist.
