Citroën C5 Aircross

II (2024–2025) · 2024 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 74/100 — bon

Summary

The Citroën C5 Aircross for the 2024-2025 period marks a major technical transition. Although it still relies on the EMP2 platform (Generation I restyled in 2022, while awaiting the true Generation II based on STLA Medium), this period introduces a complete overhaul of its mechanical offerings. The model is gradually abandoning the criticized 1.2 PureTech with a wet belt in favor of the brand-new 1.2 Hybrid 136 hp (48V MHEV) equipped with a timing chain and an e-DCS6 dual-clutch gearbox. Positioned as the family SUV focused on absolute comfort (progressive hydraulic cushion suspensions, Advanced Comfort seats, 3 independent rear seats), it finally corrects its main engine reliability flaws, although the onboard electronics and diesel emission control systems still need monitoring.

**Overall editorial rating: 7.5/10** (significantly increasing thanks to the new MHEV powertrains).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (suspensions with hydraulic progressive cushions)
  • Unique rear modularity in its class (3 independent, sliding, and retractable seats)
  • Generous boot volume (up to 720 L in ICE versions)
  • New 1.2 Hybrid 136 hp engine finally solves the timing belt issue
  • Careful soundproofing on PHEV versions

Weaknesses 5

  • Aging and sometimes slow infotainment system
  • All-touch ergonomics (climate control integrated into the screen)
  • Disastrous reliability of older 1.2 PureTech 130 and 1.5 BlueHDi (before corrective actions)
  • No all-wheel drive (4x4) available
  • Clumsy road behavior, focused solely on comfort

Check before buying

  • Service history: crucial for PureTech and BlueHDi engines (adherence to schedules and oil standards).
  • Timing belt condition (PureTech 130): check for cracks or swelling via the oil filler cap.
  • AdBlue system (BlueHDi): check for absence of engine light or 'Antipollution fault' message.
  • Electronics and Updates: ensure all recalls (BMS, inverter, Stop&Start) have been performed at the dealership.
  • Charging cable (PHEV): check the condition of the charging pin (identified overheating risk).

Verdict

The Citroën C5 Aircross (2024-2025) is an excellent family SUV for those who prioritize comfort and modularity. **However, the choice of engine is absolutely crucial.**

You must **AVOID** at all costs the unserviced 1.2 PureTech 130 hp (EB2 Gen 2) versions, whose reliability is compromised by the wet belt. Similarly, the 1.5 BlueHDi requires great vigilance (camshaft chain, AdBlue).

On the other hand, we strongly advise you to **BUY** the new **1.2 Hybrid 136 e-DCS6** version. This new chain-driven engine corrects past flaws while offering excellent urban driving pleasure. PHEV versions are relevant but require verification that all recalls (numerous regarding electrical management and fire risks) have been scrupulously applied.
