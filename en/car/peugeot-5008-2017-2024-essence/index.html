Peugeot 5008

II (P87) · 2017 – 2024

NEGOTIATE Reliability 58/100 — moyen

Summary

The **second-generation Peugeot 5008 (P87)** marks a complete break with its predecessor: it abandons the MPV silhouette to adopt that of a 7-seater family SUV. Based on the extended EMP2 platform of the 3008, it adopts its front end, dashboard (the famous i-Cockpit), and powertrains. A true commercial success in Europe, it established itself thanks to its dynamic road handling (the famous Peugeot 'road feel'), its spaciousness (3 independent seats in row 2), and its assertive design. It benefited from a facelift in late 2020 (new frameless grille, 'fang' daytime running lights).

*Expert note: Unlike the 3008, the 5008 was never offered in a plug-in hybrid (PHEV) version, as the battery's bulk was incompatible with the presence of the two retractable seats in row 3. It was not until 2023 that a mild hybrid (MHEV) version arrived.*

However, its career is heavily marred by the **serious reliability problems of its flagship engines** (1.2 PureTech and 1.5 BlueHDi), requiring extreme vigilance when buying a used one.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road handling for a 7-seater SUV (dynamism, front axle precision)
  • Spaciousness in row 2 (3 real independent, sliding, and reclining seats)
  • Gigantic boot volume (up to 780 liters in 5-seater configuration)
  • Spectacular interior design (i-Cockpit) and improved finish quality
  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation (especially on high trim levels)

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic reliability of the 1.2 PureTech 130 (wet belt)
  • Reliability of the 1.5 BlueHDi (camshaft chain and AdBlue)
  • Slow multimedia system (NAC) and aging interface compared to competitors
  • Row 3 seats reserved for children or occasional use (thin seating, reduced legroom)
  • No all-wheel drive (4x4) available, nor a plug-in hybrid (PHEV) version

Check before buying

  • On 1.2 PureTech: Visual condition of the timing belt via the oil filler opening (cracks, fraying).
  • On 1.2 PureTech: Meticulous service history (zero tolerance for delayed oil changes) and oil consumption.
  • On all Diesels: History of AdBlue tank replacements and absence of dashboard warning lights.
  • On 1.5 BlueHDi: Metallic clicking noise at the top of the engine (sign of camshaft chain wear).
  • EAT8 automatic gearbox: Smoothness of gear changes (jerks may require reprogramming or an oil change, although 'lubricated for life' according to Peugeot).

Verdict

The Peugeot 5008 II is an automotive paradox. In terms of family and dynamic performance, it is one of the best 7-seater SUVs on the market. However, **buying a used 1.2 PureTech 130 petrol version is an extremely risky gamble** that we advise against without an impeccable service history and a recently replaced belt. If you are looking for a petrol engine, prioritize the rare 1.6 PureTech 180, or opt for 2023/2024 models equipped with the new 1.2 Hybrid 136 with a chain. For high-mileage drivers, the 2.0 BlueHDi 150/180 is an excellent workhorse, provided you budget for AdBlue quirks.
