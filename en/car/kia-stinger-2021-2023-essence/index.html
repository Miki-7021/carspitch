Summary

The first-generation Kia Stinger (code CK) received a facelift in late 2020, marketed primarily between 2021 and 2023 before the model was permanently discontinued. This 5-door coupe-style Grand Tourer (GT) marked a turning point for Kia's image, proving its ability to compete with German premium brands in terms of driving dynamics and design. The facelift brings subtle aesthetic tweaks (new LED light bar rear signature, checkered turn signals, enlarged exhaust tips), a 10.25-inch infotainment screen, and improved driving aids. Mechanically, the major novelty of this facelift is the rationalization of the lineup: in Europe, only the fiery 3.3 V6 T-GDI was retained, while other markets (North America, Korea) saw the introduction of the new 2.5 T-GDI 4-cylinder replacing the old 2.0. It is an overall highly reliable vehicle, penalized only by high running costs (fuel consumption, ecological penalty) and significant weight.

Strengths 5

  • Spectacular and timeless design, sloping roofline (Fastback)
  • Dynamic road handling (chassis developed by Albert Biermann, ex-BMW M)
  • Excellent overall reliability, particularly on the V6
  • Plethoric standard equipment (ventilated seats, Harman Kardon, HUD...)
  • 7-year manufacturer warranty (reassuring on the used market)

Weaknesses 5

  • Gargantuan fuel consumption on the V6 (11 to 14 L/100 km in real-world driving)
  • Very high weight (nearly 1,900 kg) penalizing agility and wear of consumables
  • Limited rear headroom for taller passengers
  • Prohibitive ecological penalty in France (making the model rare)
  • Quality of some plastics in the lower part of the cabin

Check before buying

  • Braking system: steering wheel vibrations under braking indicating warped front discs (very common problem, often resolved by higher quality aftermarket pads/discs).
  • Turbocharger oil lines (V6): check for seepage or leaks (see official recall).
  • Tire wear: camber and high torque quickly wear out the rear tires (often under 20,000 km).
  • Maintenance history: the V6 requires very high-quality oil and frequent oil changes. The spark plugs must imperatively have been changed at 60,000 km.
  • Parasitic noises (rattles): frequent around the panoramic sunroof and rear tailgate on rough roads.

Documented safety recalls (Sources: Rappel Conso / Manufacturer)

The oil supply pipe and hose assembly on the left side of the turbocharger can degrade due to heat from the exhaust manifold. What sets it apart 6

Reliability

  • Exceptional Reliability and Extended Warranty — The restyled Kia Stinger stands out with excellent overall mechanical reliability, especially for its V6 engine, and offers unparalleled peace of mind thanks to its 7-year manufacturer's warranty.

Performance

  • Exhilarating Grand Touring Performance — Enjoy a dynamic and sporty driving experience, powered by a robust 3.3 T-GDI V6 engine delivering high-flying performance, an captivating sound, and remarkable flexibility.

Design

  • Spectacular and Timeless Design — With its flowing Fastback roofline and subtle aesthetic updates from the facelift (LED light signature, checkered turn signals), the Stinger boasts a bold and distinctive style that leaves no one indifferent.

Comfort

  • Extensive Equipment and Premium Comfort — The Stinger offers an exceptional level of standard equipment, including ventilated seats, a Harman Kardon sound system, and a Head-Up Display (HUD), ensuring premium comfort and experience on every journey.

Handling

  • Dynamic and Precise Road Handling — Developed by experts, the Stinger's chassis ensures dynamic road behavior and precise handling, offering surprising agility for a vehicle in its segment.

Safety

  • Advanced Technologies for Enhanced Safety — Benefit from the latest innovations in enhanced safety and driver-assistance systems (ADAS), as well as an updated multimedia system with a 10.25-inch screen, for a serene and connected drive.

Alternatives

Direct competitors by market

  • Europe: Audi A5 Sportback (more prestigious, more efficient engines but more expensive), BMW 4 Series Gran Coupe (dynamic benchmark, wide choice of engines), Volkswagen Arteon R (more spacious, 4-cylinder only), Peugeot 508 PSE (plug-in hybrid, less mechanically noble).
  • North America: Genesis G70 (technical cousin, shared platform but classic sedan format), Acura TLX Type S, Lexus IS 350 / IS 500.
  • Asia / Korea: Genesis G70, Hyundai Grandeur (more comfort-oriented).

Verdict

The facelifted Kia Stinger is a future collector's item. By abandoning smaller engines in Europe to focus on the 3.3 V6 T-GDI, Kia has embraced the pure GT status of its model. It is an extremely endearing, high-performance car with remarkable mechanical reliability, far superior to some European competitors. The Lambda II engine is a rock, provided that maintenance (oil changes, spark plugs) is scrupulously respected and that the recall on the turbo oil lines has been carried out. Purchase is highly recommended for enthusiasts ready to take on a substantial fuel and consumables (brakes, tires) budget. It is an exotic and reliable alternative to the German premium brands.
