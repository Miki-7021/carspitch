Renault Koleos

I (HY0) · 2008 – 2016

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The first-generation Renault Koleos (internal code HY0) is the result of the Renault-Nissan Alliance. Manufactured in South Korea by Renault Samsung Motors (where it is sold as the Samsung QM5), it is based on Nissan's C platform, shared with the Nissan X-Trail T31 and the Qashqai.

Positioned as a compact/family SUV, it suffered in Europe from a polarizing design at its launch, but distinguished itself with exceptional ride comfort, refined sound insulation, and a very efficient all-wheel drive system (Nissan's *All-Mode 4x4-i* system).

It underwent two facelifts (Phase 2 in 2011, Phase 3 in 2013) which softened its front end and modernized its equipment (integration of the R-Link system). Although the European market favored diesel (2.0 dCi), the petrol engines (of Nissan origin) shine with their all-proof robustness, making the petrol Koleos an excellent used car deal for those who drive little or seek maximum reliability.

Strengths 6

  • Remarkable reliability of petrol engines (Nissan origin)
  • Very high level of suspension comfort
  • Very refined sound insulation (especially with petrol engines)
  • Efficiency of the 4x4 system (Nissan All-Mode)
  • Rich equipment (especially in Privilège/Initiale trim)
  • Unbeatable value for money on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high consumption for the 2.5 petrol (often > 10L/100km)
  • Polarizing exterior design (especially Phase 1)
  • Disappointing boot volume for its size (450 liters)
  • Peculiar CVT gearbox feel ("moped" effect under strong acceleration)
  • Pronounced body roll in corners (comfort-oriented)

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history of the automatic/CVT gearbox (crucial for petrol versions)
  • Wear of front axle silent blocks and suspension wishbones (heavy vehicle)
  • Operation of the double-opening tailgate (lower section struts)
  • Condition of the DPF on diesel versions (journey history)
  • Operation of onboard electronics (Carminat or R-Link GPS, hands-free card)

Official recalls

  • - **Steering (November 2009):** Risk of loss of steering control due to a loosened rack nut. Dashboard reprogramming.

Verdict

The Renault Koleos I is the best-kept secret of the used car market. SNUBBED for its style, it hides an extremely proven Nissan technical base under its bodywork. **With the petrol engine (2.5 16v), it is a highly recommended purchase** for its almost infallible reliability, provided you accept significant fuel consumption. It is the ideal vehicle for low-mileage drivers looking for a comfortable, robust, and inexpensive 4x4 SUV. However, be sure to prioritize a model whose CVT gearbox has been drained, or plan for this operation immediately after purchase.
