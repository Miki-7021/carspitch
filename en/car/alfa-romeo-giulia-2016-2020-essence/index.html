Alfa Romeo Giulia

Summary

The **Alfa Romeo Giulia (Type 952)** marks the great return of the Milanese manufacturer to rear-wheel drive (classic architecture) thanks to the excellent modular *Giorgio* platform. Designed to directly compete with the German trio (3 Series, C-Class, A4), it stands out with an exceptional chassis, ultra-direct steering, and perfect weight distribution (50/50). While its dynamic qualities are universally praised, this first phase (2016-2020) suffered from early issues, mainly electronic and software-related, as well as an infotainment system that lagged behind the competition. A significant update (MY2020) corrected the perceived quality of the interior and the electronics before the aesthetic facelift in 2023. The ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox is standard on almost all versions and proves to be masterful.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional chassis (Giorgio platform) and ultra-precise steering
  • Superior driving pleasure compared to German rivals
  • Perfectly calibrated ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox
  • Very powerful 2.0 Turbo petrol engine (even at 200 hp)
  • Timeless and distinctive design
  • Ideal weight distribution (50/50)

Weaknesses 6

  • Frequent electronic bugs on 2016-2018 models (phantom warning lights)
  • Interior finish and plastic quality lagging before MY2020
  • Dated infotainment system and screen prone to delamination
  • Dealership network sometimes inconsistent in terms of after-sales service
  • Average ingress/egress and rear passenger space
  • No station wagon (Sportwagon) version available

Check before buying

  • **Battery condition:** A weak battery causes a cascade of error messages (Start&Stop unavailable, DNA mode blocked).
  • **Central screen:** Look for traces of bubbles, internal cracks, or delamination (very common defect, expensive to replace out of warranty).
  • **Cooling system (2.0T):** Inspect the radiator and hoses for traces of pink/red fluid leaks.
  • **Starter relay:** Located behind the front right wheel arch, it gets wet and oxidation prevents starting (easy and inexpensive to replace).
  • **Tire wear:** The aggressive camber of the front axle (for road holding) causes rapid asymmetrical wear of the front tires (normal low-speed scrubbing, called Ackermann effect).

What sets it apart 12

Performance

  • Unrivaled Driving Pleasure — Experience an exhilarating drive thanks to an exceptional chassis (Giorgio platform), ultra-precise steering, and an ideal 50/50 weight distribution. The 2.0 Turbo petrol engine and 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox complete this dynamic picture.
  • Excellent Braking Performance — Equipped with high-performance brakes, often Brembo, the Giulia offers very short stopping distances and remarkable fade resistance, ensuring perfect control in all circumstances.
  • Sporty and Precise Handling — Its balanced chassis, communicative steering, and well-tuned suspension give the Giulia exceptional agility and stability, allowing it to effectively avoid obstacles and corner with confidence.

Verdict

The **Alfa Romeo Giulia (952)** is undoubtedly the most rewarding sedan to drive in its category. If you're looking for a petrol engine, the **2.0 Turbo (200 or 280 hp)** is a brilliant choice, offering very reassuring mechanical reliability, far from the brand's clichés. However, 2016-2018 models require patience with electronic quirks often linked to a weak battery or poorly insulated relays.

**Buying advice:** If possible, avoid 2016-2017 models. Target a **2018 or newer model**, and ideally a **Model Year 2020 (MY2020)** which corrects interior finish defects and modernizes the infotainment. Maintenance must be rigorous (annual oil changes) to ensure the longevity of the MultiAir system.
