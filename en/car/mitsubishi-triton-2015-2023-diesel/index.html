Mitsubishi Triton

V (MQ/MR) · 2015 – 2023

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The 5th generation Mitsubishi Triton (known as the **L200** in Europe and other markets) was launched in 2015 (MQ series) before receiving a major facelift in late 2018/early 2019 (MR series). This facelift introduced the aggressive "Dynamic Shield" front fascia, improved suspension, larger brakes, and a new 6-speed automatic gearbox. Positioned as a more affordable alternative to the segment leaders (Ford Ranger, Toyota HiLux), it stands out with its excellent **Super Select II** AWD system (allowing 4x4 driving on dry roads) and its very tight turning radius. It is a pickup renowned for its robustness, particularly popular in Australia, Southeast Asia, and Europe for its unbeatable price-to-performance ratio.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent overall reliability (rated Good to Very Good by ADAC and RACQ)
  • Very robust 2.4L 4N15 engine with timing chain
  • Super Select II 4x4 system (allows permanent 4x4 on tarmac)
  • 6-speed automatic gearbox (MR series) smooth and reliable
  • Maneuverability (5.9 m turning radius, excellent for a pickup)
  • Quality/price/equipment ratio compared to the Toyota HiLux

Weaknesses 5

  • Firm suspension comfort when unladen (typical of pickups, but more pronounced here)
  • Towing capacity (3100 kg) slightly lower than segment benchmarks (3500 kg)
  • 5-speed automatic gearbox on the MQ series (2015-2018) a bit dated
  • Thin paint sensitive to scratches
  • DPF/FAP sensitive to repeated short urban trips

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history of the FAP (DPF) and EGR valve on diesel engines
  • Condition of the chassis underneath (surface rust, off-road impacts)
  • Play in the driveshaft and condition of the universal joints
  • Premature wear of the rear leaf springs if the vehicle has been constantly overloaded
  • Timing belt replacement (only if equipped with the old 2.5L 4D56 engine)

What sets it apart 12

Reliability

  • Unrivaled Mechanical Reliability — The 2.4L DI-D (4N15) engine with its timing chain is renowned for its robustness and durability, rated "Good to Very Good" by ADAC and RACQ. The 6-speed automatic transmission (MR series) further enhances this reliability.

Practicality

  • 4x4 Versatility and Agility — The Super Select II 4x4 system allows permanent 4x4 driving on dry roads, offering unique versatility. Its turning radius of 5.9 m ensures exceptional maneuverability in both urban and off-road environments.

Safety

  • Certified Optimal Safety — The Mitsubishi Triton/L200 achieved excellent safety test results, with 4 Euro NCAP stars (81% adult, 84% child protection) and 5 ANCAP stars, guaranteeing maximum protection.

Economy

  • Excellent Value for Money and Equipment — Offering unbeatable value for money and equipment in its segment, the Triton is a rational alternative to overpriced pickups, without compromising on robustness and performance.

Handling

  • Pleasant and Stable Driving — Despite its pickup nature, the Triton offers agile road handling and good stability, often compared to that of a car. Its road holding is predictable and reassuring, even on highways.

Braking

  • Efficient and Confident Braking — The Triton's braking system ensures adequate performance for a vehicle in its category, with good pedal feel and reliable braking distances, contributing to confident driving.

Alternatives

- **Europe / Oceania**: Ford Ranger (more comfortable, more technological but more expensive), Toyota HiLux (absolute benchmark in reliability but overpriced), Isuzu D-Max (very robust, 3.0L engine in Oceania, 1.9L in Europe), Nissan Navara (comfortable with its coil springs). - **Americas**: The Triton is not sold in North America. In Brazil/Mexico, it competes with the Chevrolet S10 and the Toyota Tacoma/HiLux. - **Asia**: Toyota HiLux (Vigo/Revo), Isuzu D-Max, Ford Ranger.

Verdict

The 5th generation Mitsubishi Triton / L200 (MQ/MR) is an extremely wise choice for anyone looking for a reliable, maneuverable, and affordable pickup. Data from ADAC and Australian organizations (RACQ/ACCC) confirm its great robustness, ranking it from 'Good to Very Good'. The 2.4L 4N15 engine offers an excellent balance between power and durability. We highly recommend targeting a facelifted version (MR series, post-2019) equipped with the 6-speed automatic gearbox and the Super Select II system. It is one of the most rational purchases in the segment, avoiding the overpricing of a Toyota HiLux while offering comparable reliability.
