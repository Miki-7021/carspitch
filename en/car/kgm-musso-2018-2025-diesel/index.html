Summary

The KGM Musso (known as the SsangYong Musso until 2023, and Rexton Sports in South Korea), Q200 generation, is a pick-up resting on a robust ladder chassis shared with the Rexton SUV. It stands out for its excellent price-to-performance ratio, its spacious cabin (one of the best in the category for rear seats), and its legal towing capacity of 3.5 tonnes. It is available in two lengths: standard (coil spring rear suspension for more comfort) and Musso Grand / Rhino (extended wheelbase, longer bed, with an optional leaf spring suspension for an increased payload exceeding one tonne). Facelifted in 2021 (more aggressive front end) and then technologically updated in 2023/2024 during the transition to the KGM brand, it establishes itself as a reliable workhorse and an economical alternative to the market leaders.

Strengths 5

  • 3.5-tonne towing capacity (with automatic gearbox)
  • Exceptional rear roominess for a double cab pick-up
  • Overall mechanical reliability (2.2 diesel engine and Aisin automatic gearbox)
  • Unbeatable quality/price/equipment ratio
  • Generous manufacturer warranty (often 5 to 7 years depending on markets)

Weaknesses 5

  • Bouncy suspension comfort when unladen (especially on coil spring versions)
  • Fuel consumption slightly above the category average
  • Sparse KGM/SsangYong dealer network depending on regions
  • Lower resale value than Japanese benchmarks (Hilux, D-Max)
  • Slightly vague and low-geared steering

Check before buying

  • Underbody and chassis: Check for rust (factory anti-corrosion protection is average, preventive treatment is advised) and off-road related impacts.
  • 4x4 system: Ensure that shifting into 4H and 4L occurs without metallic noises or jerks (classic part-time system).
  • Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF): On the 2.2 diesel, check the regeneration history if the vehicle has done a lot of city driving.
  • Rear suspension: Check for sagging springs or leaf springs if the vehicle has been used to carry heavy loads constantly.
  • On-board electronics: Test the central touchscreen which can suffer from sluggishness or Bluetooth/CarPlay connection bugs on 2018-2020 models.

Official recalls

  • 2019 (Steering): Risk of steering column detachment due to an improperly tightened bolt at the factory (models produced in 2018).
  • 2020 (Fuel): Possible rubbing of a fuel hose against the wiring harness, which could lead to a leak (2018-2019 models).
  • 2022 (Seatbelts): Potential defect of the rear seatbelt pretensioner (2021 models).

Alternatives

  • Europe / Asia: Toyota Hilux (absolute benchmark for reliability), Ford Ranger (more modern and technological), Isuzu D-Max (very utilitarian and robust), Nissan Navara (comfortable but discontinued in Europe).

Verdict

The KGM (SsangYong) Musso Q200 is an extremely pragmatic choice. Less refined on the road than a Ford Ranger and less valued than a Toyota Hilux, it compensates with a very high level of mechanical reliability, notably thanks to its proven 2.2 diesel engine / Aisin gearbox duo. It is a true workhorse, ideal for professionals, farmers, or individuals needing to tow heavy loads without breaking the bank. Buying used is highly recommended, provided you check the condition of the underbody (rust) and ensure that maintenance has been rigorously followed.
