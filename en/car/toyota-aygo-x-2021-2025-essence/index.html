Toyota Aygo X

I (AB70) · 2021 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The Toyota Aygo X (pronounced « Aygo Cross ») first generation (code AB70) marks a break from its predecessors. The partnership with PSA (Peugeot 108 / Citroën C1) is over; Toyota is going it alone. Based on the modular TNGA-B platform (shortened compared to the Yaris), the Aygo X positions itself as a micro-city car with a crossover look (raised ground clearance, large 17 or 18-inch wheels). To keep weight and selling price down, Toyota made a radical choice: a single, unique 100% thermal engine, without any hybridization. It's a vehicle with a polarizing design, equipped with an excellent safety arsenal (Toyota Safety Sense as standard), but which sacrifices rear passenger space on the altar of style.

Weaknesses 5

  • Poor rear passenger space (limited legroom and headroom)
  • Difficult rear seat access (tiny doors, pop-out windows)
  • Light sound insulation (noisy engine at high RPMs and wind noise on the highway)
  • Modest performance outside urban areas (laborious overtakes)
  • Hard interior plastics prone to scratches

Check before buying

  • Multimedia system (Toyota Smart Connect): some connection bugs with wireless Apple CarPlay / Android Auto (requires OTA updates).
  • Clutch (on 5-speed manual): test the biting point; city cars often suffer from premature wear due to intensive urban use.
  • Wheels: the large 17 or 18-inch wheels are very exposed to curbs. Check the condition of the rims.
  • Body panel adjustments: a few isolated cases of parasitic noises around the glass tailgate.

Official recalls

  • November 2022 (Brake Pedal) : On certain models manufactured between September 1st and October 20th, 2022, a defective weld on the brake pedal rod may cause it to detach. Pedal replacement.
  • 2023 (eCall Software) : Update of the emergency communication module (eCall) which might not correctly transmit location in case of a crash (update often performed at the dealership during maintenance).

What sets it apart 6

reliability

  • Unrivaled Mechanical Reliability — The 1.0 VVT-i (1KR-FE) engine is a monument to robustness, renowned for its exceptional reliability and chain timing, ensuring lasting peace of mind.

economy

  • Ultra-Controlled Running Costs — Benefit from remarkably low fuel consumption, often under 5L/100 km, and some of the lowest maintenance and insurance costs in its category.

safety

  • Comprehensive and Advanced Safety — With a 4-star Euro NCAP rating and standard Toyota Safety Sense (including adaptive cruise control and lane keeping assist), the Aygo X ensures optimal protection for all occupants.

practicality

  • Urban Agility and Easy Maneuverability — The Aygo X excels in the city thanks to its agility and ease of maneuver, offering predictable and safe road behavior, ideal for navigating and parking effortlessly.

comfort

  • Everyday Driving Pleasure — The Aygo X offers a pleasant and predictable drive, with good brake pedal feel and reassuring stability for its class, making every urban and suburban journey serene.

Performance

  • Optimized and Efficient Engine — The 1.0 VVT-i engine, in its latest Euro 6d-ISC-FCM iteration, benefits from optimized injection and thermal management, ensuring remarkable energy efficiency and performance suited for urban use.

Alternatives

  • Europe : Suzuki Ignis (the only true micro-SUV competitor, available with AllGrip 4x4), Hyundai i10 (more spacious, 4-cylinder available), Kia Picanto, Fiat Panda (aging but cheaper), Dacia Spring (100% electric alternative at a similar price).
  • Asia / North Amer. / Emerging Markets : Model not sold in these markets.

Verdict

The Toyota Aygo X is a pragmatic choice disguised as an impulse buy. If you're looking for a city car with an assertive look, extremely reliable, and inexpensive to run, it's an excellent choice. The 1.0 VVT-i engine is a monument to robustness that will protect you from costly breakdowns. However, if you regularly carry rear passengers or do a lot of highway driving, look elsewhere: its dynamic performance and interior space are strictly limited to urban and peri-urban use. Buy it with your eyes closed for reliability, but with full awareness of its practical aspects.
