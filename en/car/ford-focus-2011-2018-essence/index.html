Ford Focus

III (DYB) · 2011 – 2018

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The third-generation Ford Focus (codename DYB) marks the era of the “One Ford” program, aiming to design a global model for all markets. Renowned for its excellent comfort/handling compromise, it introduced a plethora of driving assistance technologies for its segment. The late 2014 facelift (often called Mk3.5) brought the famous “Aston Martin” style grille, a welcome redesign of the center console (fewer buttons), and the introduction of the SYNC 2 multimedia system (then SYNC 3 in 2016). While its dynamic qualities are undeniable, its mechanical reliability is very heterogeneous, particularly for EcoBoost petrol engines and PowerShift automatic gearboxes.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional chassis: precise steering, sharp front end, excellent comfort/handling compromise
  • Significantly improved sound insulation compared to the previous generation
  • Rich technological equipment for its time (automatic braking, lane keeping assist, automatic parking)
  • Very reliable 2.0 TDCi and 1.6 Ti-VCT engines
  • SYNC 3 multimedia system (from 2016) responsive and compatible with Apple CarPlay / Android Auto

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the PowerShift dry-clutch automatic gearbox (DPS6)
  • 1.0 EcoBoost engine: risky and costly-to-maintain wet belt design
  • Rear passenger space and boot volume (316 liters) below the category average (Golf, 308)
  • Dashboard ergonomics before the facelift (too many buttons, small screen)
  • Quality of some interior plastics and uneven assembly

Check before buying

  • 1.0 EcoBoost service history: the oil used must imperatively comply with the Ford standard, otherwise the belt disintegrates.
  • Coolant level on 1.0, 1.5 and 1.6 EcoBoost (risk of leak or engine infiltration).
  • Automatic gearbox behavior: any jerk, shudder, or hesitation on a PowerShift petrol should lead to canceling the purchase.
  • Moisture in the boot: the vents located behind the rear bumper lose their seal, causing water ingress.
  • Operation of the electric power steering (EPAS): no hard spots or abnormal noises should be felt.

Alternatives

- **Europe** : Volkswagen Golf VII (segment reference, reliable TSIs after 2014), Peugeot 308 II (excellent chassis, but same wet belt issues on the 1.2 PureTech), Honda Civic 9/10 (superior mechanical reliability). - **North America** : Honda Civic, Toyota Corolla, Mazda 3 (excellent alternative with very reliable torque converter automatic gearbox). - **Asia / Emerging** : Toyota Auris/Corolla, Hyundai Elantra.

Verdict

The Ford Focus III is a brilliant car on the road, but requires extreme caution when buying used. If you're looking for a petrol engine, the sensible choice is the modest 1.6 Ti-VCT with a manual gearbox, which is indestructible. The very common 1.0 EcoBoost offers excellent driving pleasure but its wet belt system is a sword of Damocles: only buy with a 100% clear Ford service history and budget for the belt replacement. Absolutely avoid PowerShift automatic gearboxes on petrol engines (DPS6). High-mileage drivers will find their happiness with the excellent 2.0 TDCi diesel engines.
