Skoda Kodiaq

I (NS7) (2016–2021) · 2016 – 2021

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The **first-generation Skoda Kodiaq (NS7)**, launched in 2016, marked the Czech manufacturer's notable entry into the large family SUV segment (D-segment). Based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MQB A2 platform (shared with the VW Tiguan Allspace and SEAT Tarraco), it stands out for its exceptional interior space, the ability to accommodate up to 7 passengers, and its practical « Simply Clever » features. Positioned as a rational and spacious alternative to premium SUVs, it achieved immense commercial success. A facelift occurred in mid-2021, bringing aesthetic tweaks and technological updates. Although generally well-built, its reliability heavily depends on the chosen engine and production year, with early petrol models requiring particular vigilance.

Strengths 5

  • Giant interior space and boot volume (up to 720 liters in 5-seater configuration)
  • Overall robustness of 2.0 TDI engines (confirmed by ADAC)
  • Retained physical ergonomics (real climate control knobs on this generation)
  • Unbeatable price/performance/space ratio on the used market
  • Suspension comfort, especially with DCC adaptive damping

Weaknesses 5

  • Variable reliability of early 1.4 TSI (oil overconsumption, turbo, timing chain/belt according to ADAC)
  • TÜV defect rate at 3 years of 16.8% (average, but improvable)
  • Premature wear of suspension and steering components (reported by TÜV)
  • Average reliability according to What Car? (3/5, 26% of breakdowns reported over 12 months)
  • Hard plastics in the lower part of the cabin prone to scratches

Check before buying

  • **Suspension and steering:** Clunking noises over speed bumps (wishbone bushings, tie rods), frequent defects reported by TÜV.
  • **Rear axle and brakes:** Pronounced wear of rear discs and pads, axle inspection recommended by TÜV.
  • **DSG service history:** Demand invoices for gearbox oil changes every 60,000 km (and Haldex coupling every 3 years for 4x4 versions).
  • **1.4 TSI Engine:** Oil traces around the turbo, abnormal noises on cold start.
  • **Electronics:** Infotainment system bugs (black screens, intermittent restarts), check the functioning of all driving assistance sensors.

Verdict

The Skoda Kodiaq I is an excellent choice for families looking for space and practicality without the premium badge surcharge. However, reliability data (ADAC, TÜV, What Car?) advises caution when buying used. **Early models equipped with the 1.4 TSI should absolutely be avoided** if the service history is not clear, due to reported weaknesses (turbo, timing, oil). The most rational and robust choice remains the **2.0 TDI**, particularly praised for its reliability, or the **1.5 TSI** for low-mileage drivers (after verifying software updates). A rigorous inspection of the running gear (suspension, steering, rear brakes) is essential before purchase, as these components suffer from the vehicle's weight.
