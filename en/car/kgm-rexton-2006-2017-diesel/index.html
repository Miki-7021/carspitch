KGM Rexton

II (Y220) · 2006 – 2017

NEGOTIATE Reliability 74/100 — bon

Summary

The **KGM Rexton II and Rexton W** (sold under the **SsangYong** brand at the time) is a large traditional SUV based on a ladder frame (body-on-frame). Designed for heavy duty work, off-roading, and towing (up to 3.2 tonnes), it relies heavily on technologies licensed from Mercedes-Benz (engines and gearboxes). The 2006-2017 period covers two major evolutions: the Rexton II (2006-2012) with its massive styling, and the Rexton W (2012-2017) which modernizes the front end and introduces new diesel engines (2.0 then 2.2) to meet Euro 5 and Euro 6 standards. It is a rustic, heavy vehicle, but extremely robust if well maintained. It represents a very economical alternative to the market leaders (Land Cruiser, Pajero) for utility or family use in the countryside.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional towing capacity (up to 3200 kg depending on version)
  • Robustness of the ladder frame in off-road use
  • Generous onboard space (available with 7 seats)
  • Mercedes-Benz origin engines and automatic gearboxes (proven)
  • Unbeatable price/performance ratio on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Clumsy road handling and significant body roll (ladder frame)
  • High fuel consumption, even in diesel
  • Quality of interior plastics and aging of the cabin
  • Sparse dealer network and availability of certain parts
  • Sensitivity to chassis corrosion if untreated

Check before buying

  • **Chassis corrosion:** Carefully inspect the frame rails, especially on vehicles driven in the mountains or near the sea.
  • **Injection (2.7 XDi):** Presence of black tar around the injectors (seal leak). Difficult cold start or black smoke upon acceleration.
  • **4x4 Transmission:** Test shifting into 4H and 4L. If the indicator light flashes or the front axle does not engage, suspect a vacuum leak or a seized transfer case motor.
  • **Automatic gearbox:** Gear shifts must be smooth. Jerks indicate a lack of maintenance (fluid change not done).
  • **Suspension ball joints:** Given the weight of the vehicle (over 2 tonnes), the bushings and ball joints of the front axle wear out prematurely.

Verdict

The KGM (SsangYong) Rexton II / W is a true 'tractor' disguised as a family SUV. It should not be bought for its driving dynamics, which are from another era, but for its utility capabilities. If you need to tow a horse trailer, a large boat, or drive on rutted roads on a tight budget, it is an excellent choice. **Favor the 2.2 e-XDi (post-2015 Rexton W)** for its drivability and modern 7-speed gearbox. If your budget points you towards the **2.7 XDi**, demand a clear history regarding the injection system (injector seals replaced, healthy HP pump) and check for the absence of perforating rust on the chassis.
