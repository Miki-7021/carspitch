Mazda Mazda2

III (DJ) · 2015 – 2024

NEGOTIATE Reliability 85/100 — excellent

All Mazda models

Summary

The third-generation Mazda2 (code name DJ) is a versatile city car that stands out for its 'Kodo' design, driving dynamics, and refined interior finish, often superior to the segment average. Contrary to the trend of 'downsizing' and 3-cylinder turbo engines, Mazda opted for the 'Rightsizing' philosophy with naturally aspirated 4-cylinder blocks (Skyactiv-G) and a 4-cylinder turbodiesel with a very low compression ratio (Skyactiv-D). Restyled in 2019 and again in 2023, it was also sold under the Scion iA and Toyota Yaris badges in North America. Overall Note: An excellent performer in gasoline, the diesel version (which is given particular attention here) however requires very specific use, otherwise it risks serious financial setbacks.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of gasoline engines (Skyactiv-G)
  • Driving pleasure (dynamic chassis, excellent manual gearbox control)
  • Interior finish quality and ergonomics (premium-style multimedia dial)
  • Very low real-world fuel consumption, even with naturally aspirated gasoline
  • Rich standard equipment (especially on high-end Sélection/Exclusive trims)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the diesel engine (1.5 Skyactiv-D) in urban use
  • Rear spaciousness and boot volume (280L) below the category average
  • Imperfect sound insulation on models before the 2019 facelift
  • Suspension a bit firm at low speeds
  • Aging infotainment system (MZD Connect) and sometimes prone to 'Ghost Touch'

Check before buying

  • On the Diesel (Skyactiv-D): Clear oil change history, current oil level (presence of diesel in the oil?), DPF condition.
  • Touchscreen: Check for absence of 'Ghost Touch' (screen clicks by itself) or panel delamination (known issue covered by warranty at the time).
  • Electrically folding mirrors: Internal motors tend to seize with humidity (fragile plastic gears).
  • Air conditioning: Check proper functioning of the compressor (some refrigerant gas leaks reported on early model years).

Official recalls

- November 2021 (Fuel Pump): Massive recall (2017-2019 models) for the replacement of the low-pressure fuel pump (Denso supplier) whose impeller can deform and cause engine failure.,- October 2019 (PCM Software - Diesel): Engine control unit update on 1.5 Skyactiv-D models to correct a soot management defect that could lead to engine stalling.,- February 2017 (Fuel Return Hose - Diesel): Replacement of the diesel fuel return hose on 1.5 Skyactiv-D models (risk of leakage due to pressure pulsations).,- September 2016 (Shock Absorbers): Replacement of front shock absorber mounting nuts (risk of loosening).

What sets it apart 6

economy

  • Optimized Fuel Consumption — Benefit from remarkable fuel efficiency, especially on long journeys, thanks to its Skyactiv-D diesel engine designed for very low consumption.

Performance

  • Generous Torque and Dynamic Acceleration — The 1.5 Skyactiv-D diesel engine delivers 250 Nm of torque, ensuring strong acceleration and efficient responsiveness for an enjoyable drive.

comfort

  • Refined and Ergonomic Interior — The cabin stands out with superior finish quality, pleasant-to-touch materials, and intuitive ergonomics, featuring a premium multimedia control knob.

Design

  • Elegant and Distinctive Kodo Design — The Mazda2 showcases the 'Kodo - Soul of Motion' design language, giving it a dynamic, fluid silhouette and an elegant presence on the road.

equipment

  • Comprehensive Standard Equipment — Enjoy a generous level of standard equipment from mid-range trims, including comfort and assistance technologies for an enhanced driving experience.

handling

  • Dynamic and Agile Chassis — Its Skyactiv chassis offers excellent road holding and remarkable agility, providing definite driving pleasure and great maneuverability in all conditions.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 208 (prefer the 1.5 BlueHDi for diesel, although subject to AdBlue issues), Renault Clio V (very reliable 1.5 Blue dCi), Toyota Yaris (Hybrid, unbeatable in the city).
  • Japan / Asia: Honda Fit/Jazz (excellent spaciousness), Nissan Note, Toyota Aqua.
  • North America: Honda Fit, Hyundai Accent, Kia Rio (the Mazda2 was sold there as the Toyota Yaris Sedan/Hatchback).

Verdict

The Mazda2 (DJ) is a brilliant city car, but its verdict depends entirely on the chosen engine. If you are considering the diesel (1.5 Skyactiv-D): extreme caution is advised. This engine is an engineering marvel on paper, but it absolutely does not tolerate short urban trips. Clogging and oil dilution can destroy the engine. Only buy it with full knowledge of the facts, with an impeccable service history, and only if you drive more than 20,000 km/year on rural roads/highways. However, if you opt for a gasoline version (Skyactiv-G), it is one of the most reliable city cars on the global market.
