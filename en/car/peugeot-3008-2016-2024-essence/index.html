Peugeot 3008

II (MC) · 2016 – 2024

NEGOTIATE Reliability 58/100 — moyen

Summary

The second-generation Peugeot 3008 (internal code P84) marked a radical turning point for the lion brand. Abandoning the MPV look of the first iteration to adopt the codes of the compact SUV, it met with dazzling commercial success, crowned by the title of 'Car of the Year 2017'. Based on the excellent EMP2 platform, it stands out for its dynamic road handling, bold design, and futuristic cabin (i-Cockpit). It benefited from a mid-life restyling (facelift) at the end of 2020, modernizing its front end (borderless grille, LED fang lights) and updating its infotainment system. While its dynamic qualities are undeniable, its reliability record is heavily marred by the chronic weaknesses of its most widespread engines (1.2 PureTech and 1.5 BlueHDi).

Strengths 6

  • Benchmark road handling (comfort/dynamism compromise)
  • Exterior design that ages very well
  • Spectacular interior presentation (i-Cockpit)
  • Generous boot volume (520L on combustion models)
  • Careful soundproofing
  • Excellent EAT8 automatic gearbox (Aisin)

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.2 PureTech (belt) and 1.5 BlueHDi (camshaft chain)
  • Infotainment system (NAC) slow and sometimes buggy (black screens)
  • Ergonomics of the all-touch controls (air conditioning)
  • Reduced boot volume on PHEV versions (395L)
  • Chronic AdBlue system problems on diesels
  • Reversing camera quality unworthy of the category (before facelift)

Check before buying

  • Service book: absolute requirement for the 1.2 PureTech (strict adherence to deadlines and oil standard).
  • Condition of the timing belt (1.2 PureTech): cracked or swollen appearance visible through the oil filler opening.
  • Cold engine noise (1.5 BlueHDi): a metallic rattling sound indicates wear of the camshaft chain.
  • UREA light / Anti-pollution fault (Diesels): often indicates an AdBlue tank replacement (€1000 to €1500).
  • Operation of the central screen and piano keys (frequent bugs).
  • Body panel alignment and front tire wear (incisive front axle but tire-hungry).

Official recalls

  • - **November 2020 (Code JZR)**: Massive recall of 1.2 PureTech engines produced between 2013 and 2017. Timing belt inspection and ECU update to improve vacuum pump diagnosis. - **December 2022 (Code JZR follow-up)**: Recall extension for 1.2 PureTech engines produced until 2019. - **May 2021**: DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter) on 2.0 BlueHDi. Risk of DPF cracking leading to non-compliant emissions. - **October 2023**: Traction battery management software on PHEV versions (risk of overheating or power loss). - **February 2020**: Trailer hitch attachment (risk of detachment).

What sets it apart 14

Safety

  • Benchmark Safety — The Peugeot 3008 II achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with high scores for adult (85%) and child (85%) protection. It was even named "Best in Class" in 2016 in its category, ensuring optimal protection for all occupants.

tenue de route

  • Dynamic Road Handling — Built on the excellent EMP2 platform, the 3008 offers benchmark road handling. Agile and comfortable, its road holding is precise, with excellent control of body movements and contained roll, providing both safe and dynamic driving.

Braking

  • Efficient and Enduring Braking — The 3008 II's braking system stands out for its very good performance. It ensures short and stable stopping distances, even in emergency situations, and demonstrates remarkable endurance for increased safety on all roads.

Design

  • Bold Design and Immersive i-Cockpit — With its exterior design that ages very well and its spectacular interior presentation thanks to the i-Cockpit, the 3008 offers a unique visual and ergonomic experience. Its futuristic cabin and bold lines make it a vehicle that doesn't go unnoticed.

Comfort

  • Exceptional Acoustic and Ride Comfort — The 3008 II offers an ideal compromise between comfort and dynamism. Its careful soundproofing ensures a serene atmosphere on board, while its chassis provides high-level filtering of road irregularities, even on long journeys.

Reliability

  • Innovative and Robust Petrol Engine — Opt for peace of mind with the latest petrol engines. The 1.2 PureTech 136 MHEV, featuring a timing chain, or the powerful 1.6 PureTech 180, offer excellent reliability and remarkable driving pleasure, with dynamic performance and controlled consumption.

Performance

  • Smooth and Responsive EAT8 Automatic Gearbox — The excellent EAT8 (Aisin) automatic gearbox equips the 3008 II, offering smooth, fast, and precise gear changes. It greatly contributes to driving pleasure, fluidity, and transmission efficiency, thereby optimizing performance and consumption.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Volkswagen Tiguan (more austere but reliable TSI engines), Renault Kadjar / Austral (Austral highly recommended as a hybrid), Hyundai Tucson / Kia Sportage (5 to 7-year warranty, very reliable), Nissan Qashqai. - **Asia / China**: Honda CR-V, Toyota RAV4, Haval H6. - **Latin America**: Jeep Compass, Volkswagen Taos, Chevrolet Equinox.

Verdict

The Peugeot 3008 II is a brilliant car on the road, but a minefield on the used market. If you're looking for a petrol engine, **avoid the 1.2 PureTech 130 at all costs** from before 2023, unless the engine has been recently replaced or the belt has been changed to the latest specification (and maintained every 10,000 km). Prioritize the **1.6 PureTech 180**, which is much more robust, or opt for 2023/2024 models equipped with the new **1.2 MHEV 136 with a chain**. For diesel, the **2.0 BlueHDi** is a rock, while the 1.5 BlueHDi requires extreme vigilance regarding its timing chain. Buying a used 3008 demands clear maintenance traceability and, ideally, a solid mechanical warranty.
