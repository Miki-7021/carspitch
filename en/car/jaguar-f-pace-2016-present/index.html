Jaguar F-PACE

· 2016 – Présent (Restylage majeur fin 2020)

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The Jaguar F-PACE (X761) marks the historic entry of the feline brand into the SUV era. Based on the iQ-Al aluminum platform (shared with the XE/XF sedans and the Range Rover Velar), it stands out for its highly dynamic road behavior, akin to a sports sedan, and critically acclaimed design.

However, its career is divided into two distinct chapters. **Phase 1 (2016-2020)** suffered from interior plastics unworthy of its status, a slow and buggy infotainment system (InControl), and above all, serious reliability issues with its 4-cylinder diesel engines. **Phase 2 (late 2020)** masterfully corrects the situation: the cabin is entirely redesigned with luxurious materials, the onboard electronics upgrade to the Pivi Pro system (excellent), and the powertrains adopt mild-hybrid (MHEV) and plug-in hybrid (PHEV) technologies, with significantly improved overall reliability.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road behavior (aluminum chassis, precise steering)
  • Timeless and statutory exterior design
  • Generous trunk volume (650L on pure combustion versions)
  • Sumptuous cabin and highly responsive Pivi Pro system (Phase 2 post-2020)
  • SVR V8 engine with a volcanic character and captivating sound

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the early 2.0 Ingenium diesels (2016-2019)
  • InControl multimedia system (Phase 1) slow, dated, and prone to bugs (black screens)
  • Disappointing interior finish on Phase 1 (hard plastics, average assembly)
  • Sparse dealer network and very high maintenance/repair costs
  • Reduced trunk volume on the P400e plug-in hybrid version

Check before buying

  • Metallic rattling noise when cold or at idle (symptom of a stretched timing chain on 2.0d Ingenium).
  • Software update history and operation of the central screen (Phase 1).
  • Oil level on diesels: a level that is too high indicates dilution by diesel (failed DPF regenerations).
  • Asymmetrical tire wear (the chassis is sensitive to alignment issues).
  • Creaking or clunking in the front axle when going over speed bumps (silent blocks, wishbones).

Performance

  • Sports Sedan Dynamics — Thanks to its lightweight aluminum chassis and precise steering, the F-PACE offers remarkably agile and precise road handling, providing a unique driving pleasure for an SUV.
  • Powerful and Reliable Petrol Engines — Petrol engines (P250/P300, V6, V8 SVR) are renowned for their robustness, driving pleasure, and exhilarating performance, offering a dynamic and serene driving experience.

Practicality

Verdict

The Jaguar F-PACE is an SUV full of dynamic qualities, but which paid dearly for its early mistakes. **Phase 1 (2016-2020) should be approached with extreme caution**, particularly with the 2.0 Ingenium diesel engine where the risk of engine failure (timing chain) is real and documented. On the other hand, **Phase 2 (late 2020+) transforms the attempt**: the interior becomes worthy of the badge, the electronics are finally made reliable (Pivi Pro), and the engines are updated. For a peaceful purchase, avoid the early 4-cylinder diesels. Favor a petrol model (P250/P300), a V6/V8 for pleasure, or a post-2020 model with a clear maintenance history and shortened oil change intervals.
