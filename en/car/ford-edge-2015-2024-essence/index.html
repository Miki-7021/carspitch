Ford Edge

II · 2015 – 2024

NEGOTIATE Reliability 74/100 — bon

All Ford models

Summary

The second-generation Ford Edge (CD4) is a midsize SUV designed to offer sedan-like comfort with generous interior space. Launched in 2015, it received a major facelift in 2019, introducing a new 8-speed automatic gearbox, cosmetic tweaks, and the replacement of the *Sport* trim with the *ST* version (developed by Ford Performance). Positioned above the Kuga/Escape and below the Explorer, it stood out for its excellent soundproofing and ride comfort. According to J.D. Power and the ADAC, its reliability is average for the category. The model was discontinued in North America at the end of 2024 to make way for the brand's electric transition, although a new generation (Edge L) is marketed in China.

Overall reliability rating: Average to Good, conditional on rigorous maintenance of the AWD system.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort and excellent soundproofing
  • Very generous onboard space (cabin and trunk)
  • 2.7L V6 EcoBoost engine (ST/Sport) very high-performing and reliable
  • SYNC 3 infotainment system (and SYNC 4 on the latest models) responsive and intuitive
  • Automatic gearboxes (6- and 8-speed) generally robust

Weaknesses 5

  • High fuel consumption on petrol engines (especially V6)
  • Power transfer unit (PTU) on AWD versions very fragile if not maintained
  • Imposing size and high weight, hindering agility (especially in Europe)
  • Quality of some interior plastics falling short of premium competitors
  • Significant number of electronic and software recalls (camera, sensors)

Check before buying

  • AWD maintenance history: demand proof of the PTU oil change (every 60,000 km).
  • Coolant level on the 2.0L EcoBoost (risk of internal leak).
  • Presence of oil leaks under the engine (oil pan on the 2.7L EcoBoost).
  • Operation of the reversing camera (subject to multiple recalls for black screen/distortion).
  • Automatic gearbox shifting (absence of jerking, particularly on the 8-speed gearbox from 2019-2020).

Official recalls

The Ford Edge II has been the subject of numerous recalls, mainly in North America (NHTSA) and Europe (Rappel Conso):

  • Reversing camera / APIM (2021-2026): Multiple recalls (e.g., 26V124000, 25V442000, 22V151000) concerning a software bug or overheating of the APIM module causing a black or distorted screen when reversing.
  • 2.7L EcoBoost engine (August 2024 - 24V635000): The intake valves can break on 2021-2022 models, leading to catastrophic engine failure.
  • Brake hoses (2020 & 2025 - 25V544000, 20V469000): Possible premature rupture of the front and rear brake hoses on 2015-2018 models, leading to fluid leak and loss of braking.
  • Gearbox / Cable (June 2022 - 22V413000): The bushing attaching the shifter cable to the transmission can degrade (2015-2018 models), preventing shifting into the 'Park' position.
  • Takata Airbags (2017 & 2021): Global recall concerning the ammonium nitrate propellant which can degrade and project metal fragments upon deployment (2016-2017 models).
  • Transmission / Torque converter (2017-2018 - 18V390000): On 2.0L models with a 6-speed gearbox, the torque converter weld studs may be inadequate, leading to a loss of power.
  • EPAS Power steering (August 2019 - 19V632000): Possible loss of steering assist due to corrosion of the steering motor bolts (2015-2016 models in regions using road salt).
  • Rear axle lubricant (January 2021 - 21V011000): Insufficient amount of lubricant in the rear drive unit (2020-2021 models).

What sets it apart 12

Comfort

  • Confort de roulement de type routière — Le Ford Edge offre un confort de roulement exceptionnel qui rend les longs trajets autoroutiers reposants pour le conducteur comme pour les passagers.
  • Excellente insonorisation de l'habitacle — L'insonorisation très poussée réduit les bruits extérieurs et mécaniques, créant un habitacle calme qui améliore le confort sur route.
  • SYNC 3 réactif et intuitif — Le système d'infodivertissement SYNC 3, et SYNC 4 sur les derniers modèles, reste réactif et intuitif, facilitant l'usage quotidien.
  • Essence 2.0L souple et silencieux — Le 2.0L EcoBoost est décrit comme souple et silencieux, avec un bon compromis entre puissance et consommation pour un usage polyvalent.
  • V6 atmosphérique d'une grande douceur — Le 3.5L V6 Ti-VCT, moteur atmosphérique éprouvé, procure une grande douceur de fonctionnement appréciable sur autoroute et en tractage.

Practicality

  • Habitacle et coffre très généreux — L'espace intérieur et le volume de coffre importants permettent d'accueillir confortablement une famille et ses bagages au quotidien.

Performance

  • Moteur 2.7L V6 très performant — Le V6 EcoBoost des versions ST et Sport délivre des performances élevées et un couple abondant, avec une sonorité agréable à l'usage.

Reliability

  • V6 2.7L réputé très robuste — Ce moteur essence est décrit comme très robuste, ce qui rassure l'acheteur d'occasion sur la durabilité de la mécanique dans le temps.
  • Boîtes automatiques globalement robustes — Les boîtes automatiques à 6 et 8 rapports sont jugées globalement robustes, limitant les risques de réparations coûteuses après l'achat.
  • Fiabilité moyenne à bonne en catégorie — Selon J.D. Power et l'ADAC, sa fiabilité se situe dans la moyenne de la catégorie, un repère utile pour évaluer le risque à l'achat.

Economy

  • Diesel au couple important et sobre — Le 2.0L TDCi ou EcoBlue offre un couple important et une consommation maîtrisée pour le gabarit, avantageux sur les longs parcours.

Handling

  • Bon cruiser pour les longs trajets — Le Ford Edge est présenté comme un excellent cruiser autoroutier, associant espace et confort pour les déplacements prolongés.

Verdict

The second-generation Ford Edge is an excellent highway cruiser, offering first-rate space and comfort. The EcoBoost petrol engines (2.0L and 2.7L) are generally solid and high-performing, making this SUV a relevant choice on the used market.

However, the purchase must be conditional on a crystal-clear maintenance history. The major weak point of this model (and of transverse-engine AWD Fords of this era) is the power transfer unit (PTU): if its oil has not been changed every 60,000 km, walk away, as its replacement is expensive. Furthermore, ensure that the numerous electronic and safety recalls (camera, brake hoses, valves on the 2.7L) have been carried out at a dealership.
