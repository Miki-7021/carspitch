Volkswagen Polo

VI (AW) · 2017 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The sixth-generation Volkswagen Polo (AW) marks a turning point by adopting the VW group's modular MQB A0 platform. Often described as a "mini-Golf", it offers record-breaking passenger space for its category, a serious finish, and superior segment technology. Restyled in 2021, its engine range has evolved. **Diesel Focus:** Although demand for diesel has sharply declined in the B-segment, VW offered the 4-cylinder 1.6 TDI (EA288) at its launch in 2017, available in 80 and 95 hp. This engine was withdrawn from the European catalog in mid-2020 due to anti-pollution standards (Euro 6d) and the disfavor of this fuel in urban environments. It is a relevant engine only for high-mileage drivers but requires particular attention on the used car market.

Strengths 6

  • Passenger space and boot volume (351 liters) worthy of the segment above
  • Very sound and safe road handling
  • Successful sound insulation, including on the 1.6 TDI at steady speed
  • Assembly quality and material aging
  • Frugal consumption of the 1.6 TDI on long journeys (often under 4.5 L/100 km)
  • Onboard technology (Digital Cockpit, driving aids)

Weaknesses 5

  • The 1.6 TDI is unsuitable for city driving (high risk of DPF/EGR/AdBlue failures)
  • Hard plastics omnipresent on the dashboard (before the 2021 facelift)
  • DSG7 (DQ200) gearbox sometimes causes jerks at low speeds
  • Frequent infotainment system bugs (black screens, reboots)
  • High used car price compared to mainstream competitors

Check before buying

  • On the 1.6 TDI: AdBlue history (pump/tank replacement already done?), illuminated engine warning lights, turbo whistling.
  • On the 1.6 TDI: Traces of coolant leak (water pump).
  • On DSG7 gearboxes: Absence of metallic noises when shifting gears (1st to 2nd) and absence of juddering on startup.
  • Touchscreen functionality (test GPS, Bluetooth, and CarPlay/Android Auto).
  • Check if the recall regarding the rear seatbelt has been performed (2018 models).

Alternatives

Europe - **Peugeot 208 II (1.5 BlueHDi 100):** Bolder design, excellent chassis, but less rear passenger space and boot capacity. The 1.5 BlueHDi suffers from camshaft chain and AdBlue issues. - **Renault Clio V (1.5 Blue dCi 85/100/115):** The fiercest competitor. Significantly improved finish, very good comfort. The 1.5 dCi engine is generally more reliable than VW's 1.6 TDI, especially regarding the emissions control system. - **Ford Fiesta Mk8 (1.5 TDCi 85/120):** More dynamic and fun handling, but more restricted interior space and less premium interior presentation.

South America / Emerging Markets - **Chevrolet Onix:** Brazilian market leader, very good price/equipment ratio, efficient turbo engines. - **Hyundai HB20:** Very popular, long-term warranty, robust engines.

Verdict

The Volkswagen Polo VI is an excellent, mature, and spacious versatile city car. **Regarding the 1.6 TDI engine specifically:** this is a purchase that requires careful consideration. If you drive less than 20,000 km per year or primarily in the city, **avoid diesel**. Fuel savings will be negated by costly failures of the emissions control system (DPF, EGR, AdBlue). However, for a high-mileage driver making long daily journeys on roads or highways, the 1.6 TDI proves to be an indefatigable workhorse. In all cases, demand a clear service history and prioritize 1.0 TSI (petrol) models for 90% of uses, as they are much better suited to the car's purpose and generally more reliable.
