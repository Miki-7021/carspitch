Summary

The second-generation Opel Mokka (Mokka B) marks a complete break from its predecessor. Gone is the General Motors era; this model is based on the Stellantis group's CMP/e-CMP platform (shared with the Peugeot 2008 and DS 3 Crossback). It inaugurates the brand's new stylistic language with the 'Opel Vizor' grille and the 'Pure Panel' digital dashboard.

**Focus on Electric (Mokka-e / Mokka Electric):** This is the model's flagship powertrain. Initially launched with a 136 hp (100 kW) unit and a 50 kWh battery, it was updated in 2023 with a new 156 hp (115 kW) 'e-Motors' engine and a 54 kWh battery offering better efficiency. While rear spaciousness and boot capacity are behind the competition, the Mokka B shines with its dynamic handling, assertive design, and generally reassuring electric reliability, strongly contrasting with the setbacks of the petrol internal combustion versions.

Strengths 5

  • Very successful exterior design (Opel Vizor) and strong personality
  • Dynamic road handling and well-tuned chassis
  • Overall very satisfactory reliability of the electric version
  • Interior ergonomics retaining physical controls for air conditioning
  • Efficient consumption on the new 156 hp electric version

Weaknesses 5

  • Very cramped rear spaciousness (legroom and headroom)
  • Limited boot volume (310 liters for the electric version versus 350 liters for ICE)
  • Disastrous reliability of 1.2 Turbo petrol engines (PureTech) before 2024
  • Infotainment system a bit slow and MyOpel mobile app temperamental
  • Highway range of the 136 hp electric version (around 200 km real-world)

Check before buying

  • For Electric: Air conditioning and heat pump operation (abnormal noise or lack of cold air = compressor faulty).
  • For Electric: It is imperative to test charging on three-phase alternating current (AC) to ensure the onboard charger (OBC) is not burnt out.
  • For Electric: Request a battery State of Health (SOH) certificate.
  • For 1.2 Turbo Petrol: Condition of the timing belt (cracks, swelling) and maintenance history (mandatory annual invoices).
  • For 1.5 Diesel: Traces of AdBlue crystallization under the vehicle or illuminated engine warning light.

Verdict

The Opel Mokka B is a 'love at first sight' urban SUV that puts all its chips on style. **In its electric version (Mokka-e / Mokka Electric), it's an excellent used car choice.** The electric powertrain avoids the chronic flaws of Stellantis group's internal combustion engines. If possible, opt for the 156 hp (54 kWh) version for its better range, although the 136 hp is amply sufficient for peri-urban use.

**Warning:** Avoid the 1.2 Turbo petrol versions (100 and 130 hp) from before 2024 equipped with the wet belt (PureTech), unless the vehicle is heavily discounted and the timing belt has just been replaced. The new 1.2 Hybrid 136 hp (chain-driven) version is the only recommendable internal combustion alternative to date.
