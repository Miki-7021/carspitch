Volvo S40

I (VS) · 1995 – 2004

Summary

The first-generation Volvo S40 (VS) marks a historic turning point for the Swedish manufacturer. Developed in partnership with Mitsubishi (sharing its platform with the Carisma) and assembled at the NedCar factory in the Netherlands, it abandons Volvo's traditional cubic lines for a more fluid design.

**Beware, there are two very different cars under this same designation:** - **Phase 1 (1995-1999):** Imperfect finish, sluggish road handling, average sound insulation, and early issues. - **Phase 2 (2000-2004):** Volvo revised over 1,500 parts. The running gear was modified (wider tracks, new suspension), build quality took a leap forward, sound insulation was improved, and passive safety was reinforced (WHIPS, curtain airbags).

It is a premium compact sedan which, in its gasoline versions (Volvo modular engines), offers excellent longevity, provided the engine and production year are carefully chosen.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional reliability of Volvo modular gasoline engines (excluding 1.8i GDI)
  • Seat comfort (among the best of its generation)
  • High level of passive safety (SIPS, WHIPS, airbags)
  • Build quality and sound insulation on Phase 2 models (2000-2004)
  • Driving pleasure of low-pressure turbo versions (2.0T)
  • Corrosion resistance (well-treated bodywork)

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.8i GDI engine (Mitsubishi)
  • Disappointing road handling and finish on Phase 1 models (1995-1999)
  • Very large turning radius (difficult city maneuvers)
  • Reliability of Renault-sourced diesel engines (especially before 2002)
  • Limited rear passenger space for a sedan of this size
  • High cost of original Volvo spare parts

Check before buying

  • **PCV System (Gasoline):** Remove the oil dipstick with the engine running. If there is smoke or positive pressure, the crankcase breather is clogged (risk to oil seals).
  • **VVT Pulley (Gasoline Phase 2):** Listen to the engine at hot idle. A knocking sound (diesel-like) indicates a faulty VVT pulley.
  • **Running Gear:** Wear of front wishbone bushings and anti-roll bar (dull thuds over bumps), particularly on T4 models.
  • **Electrics (Phase 1):** Check the operation of all accessories (windows, air conditioning, central locking), often temperamental before 2000.
  • **Manual Gearbox (M56):** Very robust, but the plastic reverse gear ring on the lever tends to break with age.

Verdict

The first-generation Volvo S40 is a car with two faces. Phase 1 models (before 2000) are to be avoided due to a light finish and imperfect road handling. In contrast, a **Phase 2 (2000-2004) equipped with a 100% Volvo gasoline engine (1.8, 2.0, or 2.0T)** makes an excellent used purchase, capable of easily exceeding 300,000 km with regular maintenance. Absolutely avoid the 1.8i GDI (Mitsubishi) engine, whose carbon buildup problems are insoluble, as well as the early Renault-sourced diesels.
