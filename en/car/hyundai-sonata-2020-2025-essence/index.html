Summary

The eighth-generation Hyundai Sonata (codename DN8) marks a radical break from its predecessors. Based on the new N3 platform, it adopts a bold "four-door coupe" design (Sensuous Sportiness) and introduces the new Smartstream engine family. This generation is crucial as it definitively abandons the Theta II engine family (2.0T and 2.4 GDI), infamous for their excessive oil consumption problems and fire risks (which earned Hyundai multiple recalls and massive warranty extensions). The DN8 stands out with significantly improved reliability, cutting-edge technological equipment, and a particularly efficient hybrid version. A major facelift was carried out for the 2024 model year, introducing the "Seamless Horizon" lighting signature.

Strengths 5

  • Lifesaving abandonment of the Theta II engines (end of major risks of failure/fire)
  • Overall reliability sharply increasing (rated 'Good' by ADAC and in the upper average of J.D. Power)
  • Generous manufacturer warranty (5 years/unlimited mileage in Europe, 10 years/100,000 miles in the USA)
  • Remarkable efficiency of the Hybrid (HEV) version
  • Very comprehensive onboard technology and driving aids (ADAS)

Weaknesses 4

  • Dual-clutch gearbox (DCT-8) on the N Line version sometimes prone to software bugs
  • Numerous safety recalls in the early production years (2020-2022)
  • Soundproofing could be improved at high speeds compared to segment benchmarks
  • Rear visibility limited by the sloping roofline design

Check before buying

  • Imperatively check the recall history (NHTSA/manufacturer), especially for the fuel tank and the ISG oil pump.
  • On the N Line version (2.5 T-GDI): ensure the DCT-8 gearbox update has been performed and check for the absence of fuel leaks.
  • Test the operation of the multimedia system and cameras (some screen bugs reported).
  • Check the operation of the trunk lock (recall on 2020 models).

Official recalls

Based on NHTSA (United States) data:

  • [18/11/2025] Fuel system (2020-2023): A damaged check valve can allow air to enter the tank, causing it to expand, contact the hot exhaust, and melt the tank.
  • [07/08/2024] Brake lights (2024): Software bug that can cause the brake lights to flash or prevent them from illuminating correctly.
  • [27/07/2023] ISG oil pump (2023): The electronic controller for the Stop & Go system's oil pump may contain damaged components (risk of overheating/fire).
  • [06/10/2022] Automatic transmission (2021-2022): The "fail-safe" emergency driving mode may be impaired, resulting in a loss of power (affects DCT-8 gearboxes).
  • [28/03/2022] Trunk lock (2020): The trunk latch can become damaged, preventing opening from the inside.
  • [28/12/2021] Windshield (2021): Factory bonding defect that could cause the windshield to detach in the event of a crash.
  • [09/07/2021] Fuel leak (2021-2022 - 2.5T): Possible leak at the connection between the high-pressure pump and the fuel rail.
  • [09/04/2020] RSPA software (2020): The Remote Smart Parking Assist system may fail to prevent vehicle movement in the event of a malfunction.
  • [28/02/2020] Labels (2020): Incorrect tire pressure label.

Verdict

The 8th generation Hyundai Sonata (DN8) is an excellent choice on the used market, marking a true renaissance in terms of reliability for the model. The introduction of the Smartstream engines makes it possible to forget the serious problems (failures, fires) of the old Theta II blocks that equipped the YF and LF generations. The naturally aspirated 2.5L and 2.0L Hybrid versions are particularly recommended for their robustness and peace of mind. The sporty N Line version (2.5 T-GDI) is appealing but requires strict monitoring of recalls (fuel leak, DCT gearbox reprogramming). Before purchase, checking the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) is essential to ensure that the various early recalls (tank, ISG pump) have been properly corrected at the dealership.
