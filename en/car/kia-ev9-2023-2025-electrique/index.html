Kia EV9

NEGOTIATE Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The **Kia EV9 (Generation I - Code MV)** is the large flagship electric SUV from the South Korean manufacturer. Based on the E-GMP modular platform (shared with the Kia EV6 and Hyundai Ioniq 5/6/7), it stands out with its 800V architecture allowing ultra-fast charging, its bold cubic design, and its capacity to carry up to 7 passengers. Positioned in the premium segment, it aims to compete with models like the Volvo EX90 or the Tesla Model X, while offering an aggressive price-to-performance ratio. Being a very recent model, long-term reliability data is limited, but early feedback highlights teething problems (mainly software and assembly) typical of highly technological vehicle launches.

Strengths 5

  • 800V architecture allowing a 10 to 80% charge in 24 minutes
  • Exceptional interior space (true 6 or 7-seater depending on configuration)
  • Highly practical V2L (Vehicle-to-Load) technology
  • Top-level soundproofing and ride comfort
  • Efficient OTA (Over-The-Air) updates

Weaknesses 5

  • Imposing size (5.01 m long, nearly 2 m wide) unsuited for European city centers
  • Very high weight (up to 2.6 tons) impacting tire and brake wear
  • Numerous teething recalls (software and mechanical assemblies)
  • Ergonomics of certain touch controls could be improved
  • High-speed highway consumption (aerodynamics of a brick)

Check before buying

  • Complete recall history (very numerous on 2023-2024 model years)
  • Condition of the 12V battery (tendency to discharge if the vehicle remains inactive or if the ICCU malfunctions)
  • Tire wear (especially on AWD GT-Line versions)
  • Operation of the center screen and dashboard (known display bugs)
  • Presence and correct tightening of the 2nd and 3rd-row seat bolts (official recall)

Alternatives

* **Europe:** Volvo EX90 (more expensive, premium), Peugeot E-5008 (smaller, cheaper, 7-seater), Mercedes EQB (more compact). * **North America:** Rivian R1S (more rugged), Tesla Model X (more expensive, native Supercharger network), Chevrolet Traverse EV. * **Asia:** Li Auto L8/L9 (PHEV/EREV), Nio ES8.

Verdict

The Kia EV9 is a technological and family-oriented feat that democratizes the 800V architecture in the large 7-seater SUV segment. However, its launch was marred by numerous teething problems (software, assemblies, welds) that led to a wave of recalls in 2024 and 2025.

**Buying advice:** If you are buying a recent used model, demand proof that **all manufacturer recalls (especially the ICCU, IEB braking, and differential/seat bolts)** have been performed. Favor the RWD Long Range (99.8 kWh) version for the best range-to-price compromise, as the AWD version is very hard on tires. It is an excellent vehicle, but one that requires rigorous dealership follow-up during its early years.
