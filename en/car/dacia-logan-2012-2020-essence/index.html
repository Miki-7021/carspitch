Summary

The Dacia Logan II, launched in 2012, represents the second generation of the economical compact sedan from the Romanian manufacturer, a subsidiary of Renault. Based on the B0 platform (shared with the Sandero II and Duster), it continued to capitalize on the strengths of its predecessor: an unbeatable price, generous interior space, and proven robustness.

Aesthetically modernized compared to the first generation, it gained in driving pleasure and equipment (air conditioning, cruise control, Media Nav multimedia system). A significant facelift occurred in late 2016 (phase 2), bringing aesthetic changes (new LED light signature, grille, bumpers) and the introduction of new gasoline (1.0 SCe) and diesel (more modern 1.5 dCi) engines.

The Logan II positioned itself as a pragmatic alternative to versatile city cars and more expensive compact sedans, targeting budget-conscious families and professionals. Its general reliability is good, especially with naturally aspirated gasoline engines, but some points of attention exist for more modern or diesel units.

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation, especially on the highway
  • Basic interior materials and simple finish
  • Limited performance with small naturally aspirated engines
  • High fuel consumption of the 1.2 16V and 1.6 MPI/K7M for the power output
  • Reliability of the 0.9 TCe (timing chain) on early model years

Check before buying

  • **0.9 TCe (H4B)**: Timing chain noises on startup (rattling, especially when cold), check maintenance history and the quality of oil used.
  • **1.2 16V (D4F)**: Excessive oil consumption, minor oil leaks, condition of ignition coils and TDC sensor.
  • **1.5 dCi (K9K)**: Abnormal turbo noises, exhaust smoke, engine jerking (injectors), operation of the EGR valve and DPF (if present).
  • General condition of the bodywork and underbody (basic anti-corrosion protection).
  • Operation of electrical equipment (power windows, air conditioning, Media Nav).
  • Wear of tires and brakes (often stressed by weight and driving).
  • Play in the steering or running gear (shock absorbers, ball joints).

Verdict

The Dacia Logan II is a wise choice for anyone looking for a reliable, spacious, and economical vehicle to buy and to run. It excels in its role as a pragmatic, no-frills car.

**For predominantly urban and suburban use**, the **1.0 SCe** (post-2016) and **1.2 16V** (pre-2016) engines are preferable for their simplicity and low maintenance costs, despite modest performance. The 1.0 SCe is the most recommendable for its modernity and proven reliability.

**For more versatile use, including road trips**, the **0.9 TCe** offers better driving pleasure thanks to its turbo, but it will be necessary to be vigilant about the maintenance history and favor post-2015 models to minimize risks related to the timing chain. The **1.6 SCe (H4M)**, available in some markets, is an excellent alternative for its robustness and performance.

The **1.5 dCi** remains an excellent choice for high-mileage drivers, provided that maintenance is scrupulously respected and Euro 6 versions (post-2016), which have benefited from improvements, are favored.

**In summary:** The Logan II is a safe bet if simplicity and reliability are prioritized. Avoid the first model years of the 0.9 TCe if the history is not clear. For the rest, it's an honest car that will provide proud service without emptying your wallet.
