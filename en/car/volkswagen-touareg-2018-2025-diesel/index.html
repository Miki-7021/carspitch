Volkswagen Touareg

III (CR7) (2018–2025) · 2018 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The third-generation Volkswagen Touareg, known by the internal code CR7, was launched in 2018. Based on the Volkswagen Group's MLB Evo platform (shared with the Audi Q7, Porsche Cayenne, Bentley Bentayga, and Lamborghini Urus), it positions itself as a high-end SUV, focused on comfort, technology, and versatility. Less off-road oriented than its predecessors, it excels on the road and offers a spacious and luxurious cabin.

It received a significant facelift in 2023 (for the 2024 model year), bringing aesthetic changes (front fascia, rear lights), technological improvements (infotainment system, IQ.Light HD Matrix headlights), and engine adjustments.

Overall, the Touareg III is a well-built vehicle, generally reliable, but its technological complexity and powerful engines can lead to high maintenance costs. The reliability of the diesel engines, although generally good, requires monitoring on certain specific points.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with air suspension)
  • High-end finish quality and interior materials
  • Advanced onboard technology (Innovision Cockpit)
  • Powerful and torquey V6/V8 engines
  • High towing capacity
  • Safe and dynamic road handling

Weaknesses 6

  • High purchase cost and significant depreciation in the first years
  • Potentially very high maintenance and repair costs (parts, labor)
  • High fuel consumption for gasoline and V8 TDI versions
  • Complexity of electronic systems (infotainment, driver assistance) which can lead to bugs
  • Reliability of the AdBlue system on diesels (error messages, failures)
  • High weight impacting agility and wear of consumables

Check before buying

  • Complete and up-to-date service history (logbook, invoices)
  • AdBlue system operation (no warning light, correct level)
  • Condition of the DPF and EGR valve (especially on diesels with urban use)
  • Air suspension operation (if equipped): no noises, no sagging, correct raising/lowering
  • Condition of tires and brakes (rapid wear due to weight)
  • Operation of all electronic equipment (Innovision Cockpit, driver assistance systems, air conditioning)
  • Absence of engine or gearbox leaks
  • Verification of manufacturer recalls performed

Verdict

The Volkswagen Touareg III is an excellent SUV, offering a level of comfort and technology worthy of premium brands. Its general reliability is good, but it is not without points of vigilance, particularly concerning diesel engines and electronic complexity.

**For diesel engines (3.0 V6 TDI)**: This is a relevant choice for high-mileage drivers and those who tow. The engine's reliability itself is solid, but the AdBlue system and EGR are known weak points that can incur costs. An impeccable service history and appropriate use (long journeys) are crucial. Avoid models that have been driven extensively in the city.

**For gasoline engines (3.0 V6 TSI)**: This is the most reassuring choice in terms of long-term reliability, with fewer constraints related to anti-pollution systems. Ideal for versatile use, but fuel consumption will be higher.

**For plug-in hybrids (PHEV)**: Interesting for tax benefits and urban consumption, but the complexity of the hybrid system and battery aging are long-term unknowns. To be preferred if you can charge regularly.

**Buying advice**: Prioritize a model with a complete and transparent service history, ideally at a Volkswagen dealership. Verify that all recalls have been performed. An extended warranty can be a wise investment. Negotiate the price, as depreciation is significant and maintenance costs can be high.
