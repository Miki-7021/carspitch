Toyota Camry

VIII (XV70) · 2017 – 2024

BUY Reliability 94/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The eighth-generation Toyota Camry (codename XV70) marks a major turning point for the model. Based on the excellent modular TNGA-K platform, it sheds its image as a placid sedan to offer dynamic road behavior, a lowered center of gravity, and increased structural rigidity. In Western Europe, it marks the model's grand return after a 14-year absence, indirectly replacing the Avensis with an exclusively hybrid offering (2.5 HSD). Globally (North America, Asia, Middle East), it remains an absolute best-seller, offering traditional internal combustion engines ranging from the economical 4-cylinder to the powerful V6. Restyled in late 2020 (2021 model), it received technological updates (floating touchscreen) and improved driving aids (Toyota Safety Sense 2.5+). It is one of the most reliable sedans in the world, across all categories.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability, particularly for the hybrid version
  • Impressive real-world fuel consumption for its size (2.5 Hybrid)
  • TNGA chassis offering an excellent comfort/dynamics compromise
  • Rear passenger space worthy of a limousine
  • Very high resale value
  • Controlled maintenance costs

Weaknesses 5

  • Dated infotainment system, slow and with obsolete graphics (pre-facelift)
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds (road and wind noise)
  • Intrusive trunk hinges that crush luggage
  • No station wagon version (unlike the former Avensis or Passat)
  • Low front ground clearance (the bumper easily scrapes on speed bumps)

Check before buying

  • Cleaning history of the hybrid battery ventilation filter (crucial for battery longevity).
  • Condition of brake discs and pads: on the hybrid, they wear very little, but caliper pins can seize due to lack of use.
  • Check if the fuel pump recall (Denso) has been performed on 2018-2019 models.
  • Condition of the lower front bumper (often scratched or cracked due to curbs).
  • Smooth operation of the multimedia system (intermittent reboots sometimes require a firmware update).

Official recalls

  • January 2020 (Fuel Pump): Massive global recall (2018-2019 models). The low-pressure fuel pump (supplied by Denso) may stop functioning, causing the engine to stall. Free pump replacement.
  • April 2018 (Brake Assist): On some 2018 models, premature wear of a component in the brake assist vacuum pump, extending braking distance.
  • February 2018 (Fuel Leak): Improper connection of fuel lines in the engine compartment on some V6 models (North America).
  • March 2018 (Pistons / Engine Block): Very limited recall (approximately 1700 vehicles in North America) for oversized pistons relative to cylinders on the 2.5L ICE engine, potentially causing power loss or smoke.

What sets it apart 6

reliability

  • Exceptional Hybrid Reliability — The Toyota Camry Hybrid is globally recognized for its exceptional reliability, scoring 95/100 for its 2.5 Hybrid powertrain. Its proven mechanics and indestructible e-CVT transmission guarantee absolute peace of mind in the long term, making it a rational and worry-free choice.

economy

  • Record Fuel Consumption and Controlled Costs — Enjoy truly impressive fuel consumption for a sedan of its size, averaging 5 to 6 liters per 100 km for the 2.5 Hybrid version. Combined with controlled maintenance costs, the Camry Hybrid represents an economical and sustainable solution for all your journeys.

safety

  • Optimal Protection for All — The Camry VIII achieved 5 stars in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 86% for child protection. It is also an IIHS 'Top Safety Pick', ensuring optimal protection for all occupants.

Performance

  • Control and Agility on the Road — Thanks to its TNGA platform, the Camry offers dynamic road behavior, exemplary cornering stability, and precise steering. Its reliable braking system ensures competitive stopping distances, around 37-39 meters from 100 km/h, for increased safety and confidence.

comfort

  • Travel First Class — The rear passenger space in the Camry is worthy of a limousine, offering exceptional comfort for all occupants. Its chassis ensures an excellent balance between comfort and dynamism, transforming every journey into a pleasant and relaxing experience.

practicality

  • A Smart and Sustainable Investment — The Toyota Camry is renowned for its very high resale value, making it a particularly wise investment. Its legendary reliability and popularity guarantee excellent value retention over time.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Skoda Superb (benchmark for space and trunk), Volkswagen Passat, Peugeot 508, Lexus ES (premium cousin of the Camry).
  • North America: Honda Accord (the historical rival, excellent dynamics), Nissan Altima, Hyundai Sonata, Kia K5.
  • Asia / Oceania: Mazda 6, Honda Accord.
  • Emerging Markets: Hyundai Grandeur/Azera, Kia Cadenza.

Verdict

The Toyota Camry VIII (XV70) is the epitome of a rational, worry-free purchase. If you are looking for a stately, spacious sedan capable of covering many kilometers without ever visiting the garage outside of regular servicing, this is the ultimate choice. The 2.5 Hybrid engine is the jewel of the range: it offers the refinement of a large displacement engine with the fuel consumption of a city car. Although its multimedia system lags behind European competitors and it lacks a station wagon variant, its intrinsic qualities of reliability and comfort make it a highly recommendable vehicle, whether for family use, professional use (ride-sharing/taxi), or grand touring.
