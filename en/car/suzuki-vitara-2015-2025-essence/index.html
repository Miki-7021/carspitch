Suzuki Vitara

IV (LY) · 2015 – 2025

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The **fourth-generation Suzuki Vitara (LY)** marks a radical turning point in the model's history. Abandoning the ladder frame chassis and reduction gears of its predecessors for a lightweight monocoque platform (shared with the S-Cross), it positions itself as a versatile urban/compact SUV (B-SUV).

Launched in 2015, it benefited from a first facelift in 2018 (abandonment of the 1.6 atmospheric engine in favor of Boosterjet turbo engines, interior material improvements) and a major technical update in 2020 with the introduction of 48V mild-hybridization, followed by a 'Strong Hybrid' version in 2022. A second aesthetic and technological facelift occurred in 2024.

Renowned for its price/performance ratio, its contained weight (often under 1.2 tons), and the efficiency of its **AllGrip** all-wheel drive (rare in the segment), the Vitara LY excels in overall excellent mechanical reliability, particularly with its gasoline engines. Its main drawbacks lie in imperfect sound insulation, rigid interior plastics, and fragile paintwork.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall mechanical reliability (especially M16A, K14C, and K14D gasoline engines).
  • Very efficient and affordable AllGrip all-wheel drive system.
  • Very low weight (often < 1200 kg) promoting agility and low fuel consumption.
  • Reduced maintenance cost and timing chain on all gasoline engines.
  • Simple ergonomics and retained physical controls (air conditioning).

Weaknesses 5

  • Quality of interior plastics (hard, scratch-prone) and sometimes flimsy assemblies (squeaks and rattles).
  • Imperfect sound insulation (wind and road noise on the highway).
  • Very thin and fragile paintwork (prone to stone chips and micro-scratches).
  • Dated, slow infotainment system (Bosch) and screen prone to touch bugs on phase 1 models.
  • Very disappointing driving experience with the AGS automated manual gearbox on the 1.5 Strong Hybrid version.

Check before buying

  • **Rear axle (AllGrip):** Asymmetrical rear tire wear on 2015-2017 models (factory alignment defect, sometimes corrected under warranty).
  • **Manual gearbox:** Shifting from 1st to 2nd can sometimes be notchy when cold. A gearbox oil change with quality oil (e.g., 75W-80 GL-4) often improves things.
  • **Cabin electronics:** Check proper functioning of the touchscreen (dead zones) and automatic air conditioning (temperature sensor sometimes faulty).
  • **Bodywork:** Inspect the hood and front bumper for paint chips, and the wheel arches for any signs of superficial corrosion (mountain vehicles).
  • **Diesel (if applicable):** DPF regeneration history and absence of metallic noises at idle (dual-mass flywheel).

What sets it apart 10

safety

  • Enhanced Safety and Optimal Control — The Suzuki Vitara achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, ensuring exemplary protection for occupants. Its high-performance braking system and precise handling guarantee total vehicle control in all circumstances.

reliability

  • Unrivaled Mechanical Reliability — Recognized by ADAC and TÜV for its excellent results, the petrol Vitara stands out for its exceptional overall mechanical reliability. Its petrol engines, particularly the M16A, K14C, and K14D units, are renowned for their robustness and longevity, often featuring a timing chain for added peace of mind.

economy

  • Controlled Running Costs and Remarkable Efficiency — Thanks to its very low weight, the petrol Vitara offers optimized fuel consumption. The presence of a timing chain on all petrol engines contributes to reduced long-term maintenance costs, making this vehicle an economical and smart choice.

Performance

  • Agility and Dynamic Performance — Lightweight and responsive, the Vitara provides an agile and dynamic driving experience. Its petrol engines, especially the 1.4 Boosterjet, offer an excellent performance-to-consumption ratio, while the AllGrip all-wheel-drive system ensures exceptional traction and versatility on all terrains.

practicality

  • Versatility and Intuitive Ergonomics — The AllGrip all-wheel-drive system, rare in this segment, gives the Vitara remarkable versatility to adapt to all conditions. Its interior ergonomics are designed for simplicity, with physical controls retained for intuitive, distraction-free use.

Alternatives

Segment alternatives: Chevrolet Trax, Honda HR-V, Subaru Crosstrek (for 4x4 capabilities). - **Emerging Markets:** Hyundai Creta, Kia Seltos, Nissan Kicks.

Verdict

The Suzuki Vitara IV is a pragmatic and extremely rational choice. If you are looking for a compact, lightweight SUV, capable of venturing off the beaten path (in AllGrip version) and boasting above-average mechanical reliability, it is an excellent candidate.

**The expert's choice:** Definitely favor gasoline engines. The **1.4 Boosterjet (140 hp or 129 hp MHEV)** is the best compromise, offering superior driving pleasure and flawless reliability. The 1.6 VVT atmospheric engine from the early years is a rock for tighter budgets.

**To avoid:** The 1.5 Dualjet Hybrid (Strong Hybrid) version, whose AGS gearbox spoils driving pleasure, as well as the 1.6 DDiS if you don't exclusively drive on highways.
