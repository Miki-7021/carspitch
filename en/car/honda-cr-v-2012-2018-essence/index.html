Honda CR-V

IV (RM) · 2012 – 2018

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The 4th generation Honda CR-V (codename RM) is a compact family SUV that has established itself as one of the best-selling vehicles worldwide. Positioned as a pragmatic, spacious, and ultra-reliable vehicle, it abandons the off-road pretensions of its predecessors to focus on road comfort and spaciousness (giant 589-liter trunk). A facelift occurred in 2015, bringing aesthetic touches, a new infotainment system (Honda Connect), and replacing the aging 2.2 diesel with a 1.6 bi-turbo paired with a ZF 9-speed automatic gearbox. In terms of gasoline engines, the CR-V shines with exceptional reliability, worthy of the Japanese manufacturer's reputation.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of gasoline engines (2.0 and 2.4 i-VTEC)
  • Record spaciousness and gigantic trunk volume (589 to 1669 liters)
  • Ingenious modularity (one-hand seat folding system)
  • Suspension comfort and smooth driving
  • General ergonomics and numerous storage compartments

Weaknesses 5

  • Infotainment system (Honda Connect) slow, dated, and prone to bugs
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (road noise)
  • Quality of certain interior plastics (prone to scratches)
  • Naturally aspirated gasoline engines lacking low-end torque
  • 5-speed automatic gearbox (pre-2015) from another era

Check before buying

  • Dull noise or groaning from the rear axle during tight turns at low speed (sign that the AWD differential fluid needs to be replaced).
  • Rattling/metallic grinding noise lasting one second at cold start on 2.4L engines (VTC Actuator).
  • Valve clearance adjustment service history on gasoline engines (often neglected by non-Honda garages).
  • Smooth operation of the touchscreen and GPS (frequent crashes sometimes requiring a reboot or update).
  • On diesels: check for absence of DPF warning light and smoothness of gear changes on the 9-speed automatic gearbox.

Practicality

  • Record-Breaking Space and Ingenious Modularity — Enjoy generous interior space for all passengers and a gigantic boot volume, complemented by an ultra-practical one-hand seat folding system.
  • Intuitive Ergonomics and Abundant Storage — The CR-V's interior is designed for ease of use, featuring well-thought-out ergonomics and numerous clever storage compartments to organize your daily life.

Comfort

  • Suspension Comfort and Smooth Driving — The CR-V IV offers a soothing driving experience thanks to its suspension comfort and overall smoothness, ideal for long journeys and daily use alike.

Safety

  • Certified Optimal Safety — With 5 Euro NCAP stars, including an impressive 93% score for adult protection, the CR-V IV ensures a high level of safety for all its occupants.
  • Safe and Stable Braking — The CR-V IV's braking system is rated as good and stable, offering a reassuring pedal feel and adequate braking distances for enhanced safety.
  • Secure and Predictable Road Holding — Its dynamic behavior is focused on stability and predictability, with well-controlled body roll, ensuring a serene and reassuring drive in all circumstances.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Toyota RAV4 (for hybrid and reliability), Volkswagen Tiguan (for finish and technology), Mazda CX-5 (for driving pleasure and gasoline reliability). - **North America:** Toyota RAV4, Subaru Forester (for its superior AWD system), Nissan Rogue. - **Asia / Emerging Markets:** Nissan X-Trail, Hyundai Tucson, Kia Sportage.

Verdict

The 4th generation Honda CR-V is a masterful choice for rational buyers looking for a trouble-free family SUV. If you opt for a gasoline engine (the 2.0 i-VTEC in Europe or the 2.4 in North America), you are buying one of the most reliable vehicles of its decade, capable of exceeding 300,000 km with simple routine maintenance. Diesel versions are also well-made but require highway use to avoid classic clogging. Prioritize a post-2015 (facelifted) gasoline model to benefit from modernized equipment, while keeping in mind that onboard technology is not its strong suit. It is the ultimate 'peace of mind' purchase.
