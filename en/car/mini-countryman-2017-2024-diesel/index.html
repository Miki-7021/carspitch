MINI Countryman

II (F60) · 2017 – 2024

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

All MINI models

Summary

The **second-generation MINI Countryman (F60)** marks the age of maturity for the British SUV. Based on BMW Group's modular UKL2 platform (shared with the BMW X1 F48), it grows considerably (+20 cm) to become a true family vehicle. Gone are the creaky plastics of the R60 generation: the finish makes a spectacular leap forward, worthy of the premium segment.

Although demand for diesel has fallen in favor of plug-in hybrid (Cooper SE), the diesel engines (from BMW's modular B37/B47 family) are of **remarkable reliability** and represent the most pragmatic choice for high-mileage drivers, given the vehicle's high weight (often over 1.5 tons). The model benefited from a facelift (LCI) in mid-2020, bringing 'Union Jack' lights, a digital instrument cluster, and infotainment updates.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of B47 diesel engines (Cooper D and SD)
  • Significantly improved finish and assembly quality (BMW standard)
  • Spaciousness and trunk volume (true family vehicle)
  • Dynamic road handling despite weight and size
  • Smooth and very reliable Aisin automatic gearboxes (8-speed on 2.0L)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high weight (penalizes typical MINI agility and small engines like the One D)
  • Firmness of suspensions (especially with 18/19 inch wheels and Runflat tires)
  • Used purchase price and maintenance cost (BMW prices)
  • Disappointing real electric range on early PHEVs (7.6 kWh)
  • Average peripheral visibility (thick pillars)

Check before buying

  • Recall history: imperatively check if the EGR cooler has been replaced on diesels.
  • Automatic gearbox: demand or plan for an oil change if the vehicle approaches or exceeds 80,000 - 100,000 km.
  • Running gear: wear of front wishbone bushings and anti-roll bar links (dull noises over bumps).
  • Engine mounts: particularly on 3-cylinder engines (B37/B38), check for absence of excessive vibrations at idle.
  • Panoramic sunroof: check for absence of wind noise, mechanism blockage, and water ingress.

Official recalls

  • **EGR Cooler (2018-2023)**: Major recall (codes 0011970500, 0011360600, etc.) for risk of coolant leak into the intake, potentially leading to fire. Concerns almost all B37/B47 diesels.
  • **High-Voltage Battery (2020)**: Risk of short circuit and fire on PHEV models (Cooper SE) produced between March and August 2020 due to impurities in battery cells.
  • **Collision Sensor (2017)**: Reprogramming or replacement of a defective airbag sensor on models produced in 2017.
  • **Fuel Pump (2018)**: Risk of leak or failure of the high-pressure fuel pump on certain gasoline models.

Comfort

  • Premium Finish and Refined Comfort — Discover an interior worthy of the premium segment, with significantly improved finish and assembly quality. The 8-speed Aisin automatic transmissions offer remarkable smoothness for a most pleasant driving experience.

Practicality

  • A Truly Spacious Family Vehicle — With a significant growth of 20 cm compared to the previous generation, the Countryman F60 offers generous interior space and a substantial boot volume, transforming it into a versatile and comfortable family SUV for all your journeys.

Safety

  • High-Level Overall Safety — The MINI Countryman II achieved the maximum rating in Euro NCAP, ensuring optimal protection for adults and children. Its robust design and advanced safety systems provide peace of mind on the road.
  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Benefit from an excellent braking system, powerful, progressive, and easy to modulate. It ensures very short stopping distances, actively contributing to your safety and that of your loved ones.

Performance

  • Driving Pleasure and MINI Agility — Despite its increased size, the Countryman F60 maintains dynamic road behavior and surprising agility. Its precise steering and good body roll control offer an engaging driving experience, true to the MINI spirit.

Economy

  • Controlled Consumption for Long Journeys — The diesel engines, especially the 2.0L, are renowned for their excellent efficiency. They offer generous torque to move the vehicle while maintaining remarkably low fuel consumption, ideal for high-mileage drivers.

Alternatives

- **Europe** : BMW X1 (F48) (its technical twin, more austere but more spacious), Audi Q2 / Q3, Volvo XC40, Mercedes GLA. - **North America** : Volvo XC40, Lexus UX, Audi Q3 (diesel not being an option, competition is in gasoline and hybrid). - **Asia / Japan** : Lexus UX, Mazda CX-30, BMW X1.

Verdict

The MINI Countryman F60 is an excellent premium compact SUV. By specifically targeting **diesel** engines, it is an extremely relevant choice. The 2.0L engine (Cooper D 150 hp and Cooper SD 190 hp) is one of the most reliable diesel engines of its generation, offering perfect torque to move the vehicle's significant mass while consuming little.

**Buying Tip:** Opt for a **Cooper D or SD** from 2018 onwards, ensuring that the EGR cooler recall campaign has been carried out by the BMW/MINI network. Avoid diesels that have only been driven in the city (risk of DPF clogging). If you find a model with clear maintenance history and a drained automatic gearbox, you can go for it with your eyes closed to pass the 250,000 km mark without major issues.
