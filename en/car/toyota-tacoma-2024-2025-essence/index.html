Summary

The **fourth-generation Toyota Tacoma (N400)**, launched for the 2024 model year, marks a major technological breakthrough for the famous North American mid-size pickup. Now based on the global **TNGA-F** platform (shared with the Tundra and Land Cruiser), it abandons its historic naturally aspirated engines (the 2.7L 4-cylinder and the legendary 3.5L V6) in favor of an exclusive turbocharged 2.4L 4-cylinder engine, available in a classic combustion version (i-FORCE) and a hybrid version (i-FORCE MAX). It benefits from a coil-spring rear suspension on higher trims, greatly improving comfort. Although the N400 model is too recent to display 10-year reliability statistics, the Tacoma remains the undisputed leader of its segment in North America, renowned for its exceptional resale value and robustness.

Strengths 6

  • Ultra-rigid TNGA-F platform (shared with the Land Cruiser)
  • Impressive torque from the new 2.4L Turbo engines (especially in i-FORCE MAX)
  • Maintenance of a manual gearbox offering (rare in the segment)
  • Multi-link rear suspension with coil springs (on high trims) transforming comfort
  • Exceptional off-road capabilities (TRD Pro and Trailhunter versions)
  • Brand's reliability history and unbeatable resale value

Weaknesses 5

  • Disappearance of the legendary and indestructible naturally aspirated 3.5L V6
  • Lack of hindsight (long-term) on the reliability of the new 2.4L Turbo block in intensive use
  • Rear seat space (Double Cab) still a bit tight compared to the competition
  • Prices sharply up compared to the previous generation (N300)
  • Technical complexity of the i-FORCE MAX (hybrid) version

Check before buying

  • Front constant velocity (CV) joints: risk of deformation/breakage (NHTSA Recall).
  • Rear brake hoses: risk of premature wear due to mud/dirt accumulation in 4x4 use (NHTSA Recall).
  • Instrument cluster: possible electronic bugs requiring an update (NHTSA Recall).
  • Recall history: verify that the first-year production software and mechanical fixes have been applied.

Performance

  • Impressive Power and Torque — The new 2.4L Turbo powertrains (i-FORCE and i-FORCE MAX) deliver remarkable low-end torque, reaching up to 630 Nm for the hybrid version, ensuring dynamic performance and superior towing capability.

Comfort

  • Redefined Driving Comfort — Thanks to its multi-link coil-spring rear suspension (on higher trims), the Tacoma N400 offers SUV-like comfort and handling, transforming every journey into a pleasant experience.

Practicality

  • Unrivaled Off-Road Capabilities — Designed for adventure, the Tacoma N400, especially in its TRD Pro and Trailhunter versions, is equipped to tackle the most demanding terrains, allowing you to explore without limits.
  • Engaging Driving Experience — The availability of a 6-speed manual transmission option on certain versions is a rare and appreciated feature, offering a more direct connection and enhanced driving pleasure for enthusiasts.

Reliability

  • Legendary Reliability and Resale Value — Inheriting Toyota's reputation for excellence, the Tacoma is renowned for its exceptional reliability (4.5/5 in What Car? survey, J.D. Power leader), ensuring peace of mind and unbeatable resale value.

Alternatives

North America * **Chevrolet Colorado / GMC Canyon**: Recently renewed, excellent 2.7L Turbo engines, very good capabilities, but historical reliability lower than Toyota. * **Ford Ranger (Gen 4)**: New model, offers a powerful EcoBoost V6 (Raptor) and a proven 2.3L, very good road handling. * **Nissan Frontier**: One of the last to offer a classic naturally aspirated V6 (3.8L), very reliable but more rustic design. * **Honda Ridgeline**: Unibody chassis, SUV comfort, very reliable V6, but limited towing and off-road capabilities.

Rest of the World (Europe, Asia, Africa) * **Toyota Hilux**: The international cousin of the Tacoma. More utilitarian, diesel engines (2.4 and 2.8 D-4D), absolute benchmark in global reliability. * **Ford Ranger / VW Amarok**: European market leaders, excellent diesel engines (3.0 TDI/EcoBlue V6).

Verdict

The **Toyota Tacoma N400 (2024+)** represents a spectacular evolution for the best-selling pickup in its category. The switch to the TNGA-F platform and the adoption of coil springs transform its road handling. However, abandoning the naturally aspirated V6 in favor of the 2.4L Turbo 4-cylinder (i-FORCE) and its hybrid variant (i-FORCE MAX) requires a paradigm shift for purists. Although Toyota enjoys an exceptional reputation for reliability (4.5/5 at What Car?, J.D. Power leader), this fourth generation is experiencing teething problems with several notable mechanical recalls (front CV joints, brake hoses).

**Our advice**: It is an excellent vehicle, but as with any all-new generation model with new engines, slight caution is required. The 2024 models should be inspected to ensure that early recalls have been performed. If you are looking for absolute and proven reliability, an N300 Tacoma (2015-2023) with the 3.5L V6 remains the conservative choice. If you prioritize torque, technology, and comfort, the N400 is superior in every way.
