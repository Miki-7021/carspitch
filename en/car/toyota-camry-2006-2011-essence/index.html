Summary

The sixth-generation Toyota Camry (codename XV40) is a D/E segment sedan, designed as a global vehicle but primarily tailored for North America, Asia, Australia, and Russia (it was not officially imported into Western Europe, where Toyota favored the Avensis). Renowned for its plush comfort, generous interior space, and quiet operation, it embodies the quintessential family sedan. A facelift occurred in late 2009 (2010 model year), bringing minor aesthetic changes but, more importantly, a major mechanical evolution: the replacement of the problematic 2.4L engine with an all-new, much more reliable 2.5L. Although its overall reliability reputation is excellent, this generation is marked by some notable flaws in the early production years (oil consumption, publicized recalls).

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional ride comfort and sound insulation worthy of the upper segment
  • Very generous interior space (rear legroom)
  • Outstanding reliability of 2.5L (post-2010) and V6 versions
  • Smoothness and fuel efficiency of the Hybrid version in urban environments
  • Paint quality and corrosion resistance
  • High resale value in markets where it is popular

Weaknesses 5

  • 2.4L engine (2006-2009) unworthy of Toyota's reputation (oil consumption)
  • Clumsy road handling, vague steering (100% comfort-oriented)
  • Uneven interior materials: dashboard plastics that melt or become sticky in the sun
  • Cost of replacing the ABS actuator on the hybrid version (very expensive)
  • Parasitic noises (squeaks and rattles) in the cabin over time

Check before buying

  • Engine oil level (imperative on 2.4L models before 2010)
  • Dashboard condition (sticky, shiny, or cracked appearance due to UV exposure)
  • Clunking noise in the steering column (intermediate steering shaft to be greased or replaced)
  • Water pump replacement history (common around 100,000 - 150,000 km on V6 and 2.5L)
  • On the hybrid: high-voltage battery health (hybrid test at Toyota) and brake operation (actuator)

Verdict

The Toyota Camry XV40 is a car with two faces. Models from 2006 to 2009 equipped with the 2.4L (2AZ-FE) engine are to be avoided due to serious oil consumption and cylinder head problems, unless the engine has been completely rebuilt. In contrast, the facelifted versions (2010-2011) with the 2.5L (2AR-FE) engine or the V6 versions (with the corrected VVT-i hose) are monuments of reliability capable of exceeding 400,000 km without issue. It is a highly recommended purchase for anyone looking for an ultra-comfortable, spacious, and trouble-free daily driver, provided they target the right years and engine options.
