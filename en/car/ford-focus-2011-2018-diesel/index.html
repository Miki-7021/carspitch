Summary

The third-generation Ford Focus (codename DYB) marked a turning point with the 'One Ford' strategy, becoming a truly global model. Recognized for its excellent comfort/handling compromise and dynamic chassis, it benefited from a major facelift in late 2014 (Phase 2) introducing the 'Aston Martin-style' grille, redesigned interior ergonomics (abandoning the button-heavy center console in favor of SYNC 2 then SYNC 3 systems), and new Euro 6 engines. While the diesel engines (TDCi) developed with PSA proved particularly robust and economical, the model's overall reputation was tarnished by the troubles of the dry-clutch PowerShift automatic gearbox and the fragilities of certain EcoBoost petrol engines (wet belt on the 1.0, overheating on the 1.6).

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional chassis: comfort/handling compromise among the best in its class
  • Remarkable reliability of diesel engines (1.5 and 2.0 TDCi) and the 1.6 naturally aspirated petrol
  • Precise steering and sharp front axle
  • Significantly improved sound insulation on Phase 2 (after 2014)
  • Highly effective ergonomics and SYNC 3 multimedia system (from late 2016)
  • Very competitive price/equipment ratio on the used market

Weaknesses 6

  • Average rear passenger space and disappointing trunk volume (316 liters with spare wheel)
  • Dashboard ergonomics on Phase 1 (2011-2014): too many buttons, small screen
  • Disastrous reliability of the dry-clutch PowerShift automatic gearbox (DPS6/DCT250)
  • Chronic fragility of the submerged timing belt on the 1.0 EcoBoost
  • Quality of some interior plastics and uneven assembly
  • Large turning radius

Check before buying

  • For diesels (TDCi): check usage history (avoid vehicles used 100% in the city to preserve the DPF and EGR).
  • For automatic gearboxes: identify the gearbox type. Avoid the dry-clutch PowerShift (on 1.6 atmo, 1.0 EB). Demand oil change invoices (60k km) for the wet-clutch PowerShift (on 2.0 TDCi).
  • For the 1.0 EcoBoost: open the oil filler cap and visually inspect the belt's condition (cracks, fraying). Demand annual maintenance invoices with the correct oil specification.
  • For the 1.6 EcoBoost: check the coolant level and ensure the recall for the level sensor installation has been performed.
  • For the Focus RS: verify that the cylinder head gasket recall (FSA 17B32) has been performed by Ford (pressure test or cylinder head/gasket replacement).
  • Electronics: test all electric windows, the SYNC system's functionality (Bluetooth, GPS), and the air conditioning.

Verdict

The Ford Focus III is an excellent compact car, particularly brilliant in terms of dynamics. **If you are looking for a diesel, it is a top-tier choice**: the 1.5 TDCi and 2.0 TDCi blocks (with manual gearbox) are among the most reliable and economical engines of their generation. Phase 2 (post-2014) is highly recommended for its modernized interior (SYNC 3) and improved sound insulation. However, caution is advised for petrol engines: the 1.0 EcoBoost requires meticulous maintenance (wet belt) and dry-clutch PowerShift automatic gearboxes are to be absolutely avoided. Buy a diesel with your eyes closed (with a clear history), but negotiate or avoid poorly maintained small petrol engines.
