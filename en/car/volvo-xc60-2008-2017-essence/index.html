Volvo XC60

Summary

The first-generation Volvo XC60 (Type DZ) was a true global best-seller for the Swedish brand and redefined safety standards in its category by introducing the *City Safety* autonomous braking system as standard. Based on the EUCD platform (shared with Ford and Land Rover), it offers an excellent compromise between comfort, spaciousness, and safety.

Its career is divided into two phases: Phase 1 (2008-2013) with a rounder design, and Phase 2 (2013-2017) which benefits from a modernized front fascia, an updated interior (digital gauges, improved Sensus system), and a major mechanical transition. Under the hood, the XC60 experienced three eras: engines from the Ford/Yamaha group (4 and 6 cylinders), the legendary in-house 5-cylinders, and finally the introduction in 2014 of the modular 4-cylinder Volvo *VEA (Drive-E)* engine family. Although the European market favored diesel, the petrol engines (particularly popular in North America and Asia) offer superior driving pleasure and excellent overall reliability, provided the correct version and transmission are targeted.

Strengths 6

  • Very high level of passive and active safety (City Safety).
  • Exceptional seat comfort (among the best on the market).
  • Remarkable reliability of 6-cylinder petrol engines (3.2 and T6) and 5-cylinder petrol engines.
  • Build quality and aging of interior materials.
  • Timeless design that ages very well, especially in Phase 2.
  • Excellent sound insulation.

Weaknesses 6

  • High weight (often > 1.8 tons) leading to rapid tire and brake wear.
  • Unreliable Powershift dual-clutch gearbox (on early 4-cylinders).
  • High fuel consumption on 6-cylinder petrol engines.
  • Dated and unergonomic Phase 1 infotainment system (Sensus).
  • Large turning radius, impractical in urban environments.
  • Fatal design flaw of the accessory belt on 5-cylinder diesel engines.

Check before buying

  • Service history of the automatic gearbox (Aisin or Powershift) and the Haldex (AWD) system.
  • On 6-cylinder petrol engines (3.2 / T6): listen carefully to the passenger side of the engine at idle. A whistling sound indicates wear of the READ (Rear Engine Accessory Drive) unit bearings (very costly repair).
  • On 5-cylinder diesel engines: check the date of the last accessory belt and tensioner replacement.
  • On VEA engines (2014+): look for blue exhaust smoke or oil top-up invoices (segmentation issue).
  • Operation of the electric tailgate (struts wear out and the tailgate drops).
  • BLIS (blind spot) system cameras under the mirrors on Phase 1 (take on water and malfunction).

Official recalls

Seatbelt cable (2020): Major global recall. The steel cable of the front seatbelts can wear out and break. Replacement with a reinforced part.,Accessory belt tensioner (2010-2011): On 5-cylinder diesel engines, replacement of the tensioner to prevent engine failure.,Electric tailgate (2019): Risk of weak struts in cold weather, software update or replacement.,Power steering (2009-2010): Risk of a leak in a power steering hose, leading to loss of assistance.,EGR cooler (2019): On 4-cylinder VEA diesel engines, risk of melting of the intake manifold (or even fire) due to a defective EGR cooler.

What sets it apart 6

safety

  • Benchmark Safety and Pioneering Innovation — The Volvo XC60 is a safety pioneer, having achieved a maximum 5-star Euro NCAP rating and featuring the revolutionary City Safety system as standard, ensuring optimal protection for all occupants.

comfort

  • Exceptional Comfort and Refined Ambiance — Enjoy unparalleled travel comfort thanks to seats among the best on the market and excellent sound insulation, transforming every journey into a serene and relaxing experience.

reliability

  • Remarkably Reliable Petrol Engines — The XC60's 6-cylinder (3.2 and T6) and 5-cylinder petrol engines are renowned for their exceptional robustness and longevity, offering long-term peace of mind.

braking

  • Absolute Control with Powerful Braking — The XC60 ensures optimal active safety thanks to its excellent braking performance, characterized by short stopping distances and great endurance, inspiring confidence in all circumstances.

handling

  • Safe and Reassuring Road Handling — Benefit from stable and predictable road handling, focused on comfort and safety, with excellent absorption of irregularities for a serene and confident drive on all roads.

quality

  • Premium Finish Quality and Timeless Design — The XC60's interior stands out for the quality of its materials and excellent finish, while its timeless exterior design ages remarkably well, retaining its elegance over the years.

Alternatives

  • Europe : Audi Q5 (equivalent trim, powerful TDI/TFSI engines but issues with S-Tronic gearbox and timing chain), BMW X3 F25 (more dynamic, beware of chains on N47/N20 engines), Mercedes GLK/GLC.
  • North America : Acura RDX (very reliable, naturally aspirated V6), Lexus NX (unbeatable reliability, hybrid available), BMW X3.
  • Japan / Asia : Lexus RX (larger but similar target), Subaru Outback (more wagon-like, very reliable in petrol).

Verdict

The first-generation Volvo XC60 is an excellent family SUV, particularly recommendable as a used car if the right engine is chosen. In petrol, versions equipped with the 6-cylinder (3.2 or T6 3.0) are true tanks of formidable reliability, provided you accept their fuel appetite and check the READ unit. The post-2014 4-cylinder petrol versions (VEA) offer a better consumption/performance compromise, but require vigilance regarding oil consumption in early model years. Absolutely to avoid: the 4-cylinder petrol versions (2.0T / T5) from the 2010-2013 period equipped with the Powershift automatic gearbox, whose reliability is disastrous without meticulous maintenance. For diesel enthusiasts, the 5-cylinder remains a monument of driving pleasure, but requires strict preventive replacement of its accessory belt.
