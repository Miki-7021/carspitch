Summary

The BMW i3 (chassis code I01), produced from 2013 to 2022, marked a bold turning point for the Bavarian manufacturer. Designed from the outset as an electric vehicle, it stands out with its innovative "LifeDrive" structure, combining a carbon fiber reinforced plastic (CFRP) passenger cell and an aluminum chassis. This technological choice, rare for a mass-produced vehicle, aimed to compensate for the weight of the batteries and offer exemplary structural rigidity. Positioned as a premium electric city car, the i3 was praised for its futuristic design, urban agility, and eco-friendly interior.

A minor facelift occurred in 2017, bringing minor aesthetic tweaks, new options, and the introduction of the sportier i3s version, as well as a larger capacity battery (94 Ah). The battery capacity was increased again in 2018 (120 Ah).

Overall, the BMW i3 is a reliable vehicle, particularly in its 100% electric versions, offering a unique driving experience and reduced maintenance costs. It has maintained a high level of affection thanks to its distinctive character and relevance in urban environments.

Check before buying

  • **High-voltage battery state of health (SOH)**: Request a BMW diagnostic report or use specific OBD tools.
  • **REx operation**: If present, ensure it starts and runs correctly, without abnormal noises or excessive smoke. Check usage history.
  • **12V Battery**: Check its replacement date and condition. A weak battery can cause electronic malfunctions.
  • **Charging system**: Test AC and DC charging (if available) to ensure proper operation.
  • **Tire wear**: Check condition and brand (specific BMW i tires, often Bridgestone Ecopia EP500).
  • **Air conditioning/Heating**: Ensure proper operation, especially the heat pump which is crucial for energy efficiency.
  • **Suspension and running gear**: Check for absence of noises or play, particularly in the shock absorbers and ball joints.
  • **Service history**: Ensure all maintenance has been performed, including software updates and manufacturer recalls.

What sets it apart 12

Safety

  • Exceptional Safety and Agility — The BMW i3 offers reassuring safety, attested by its 4-star Euro NCAP rating (81% adult protection, 77% child protection). Its braking is highly effective, stopping from 60 to 0 mph (96 to 0 km/h) in just 36.6 meters, complemented by efficient regenerative braking. Its handling is excellent, characterized by remarkable agility and responsiveness thanks to its low center of gravity and lightweight CFRP structure, providing a stable and engaging driving experience.

Reliability

  • Exemplary Electric Reliability — The 100% electric versions of the BMW i3 stand out for their excellent overall reliability, particularly models equipped with 94 Ah and 120 Ah batteries. This robustness ensures peace of mind and remarkable durability for daily use.

Economy

Performance

  • Lively Agility and Acceleration — With its instant torque and lightweight design, the BMW i3 offers lively acceleration and agile, dynamic driving, particularly well-suited to urban environments. Every journey becomes a pleasure, with impressive responsiveness.

Verdict

The BMW i3 is a pioneering electric vehicle and remains an excellent option on the used car market, especially in its 100% electric (BEV) versions.

Its innovative design, driving pleasure, and low running costs make it a relevant choice for urban and peri-urban use.

**For BEV versions (60, 94, 120 Ah)**: This is a **RECOMMENDED BUY**. Prioritize 94 Ah or 120 Ah versions for more comfortable range and better versatility. Reliability is generally very good, and maintenance costs are minimal.

**For REx versions**: The verdict is rather **NEGOTIATE**. While offering appreciable flexibility, the additional complexity of the thermal engine and potential problems related to its underuse can lead to higher maintenance costs. Ensure that the REx has been regularly used and maintained, and that all related recalls have been performed.

In all cases, a complete service history and a high-voltage battery state-of-health (SOH) diagnostic are essential before purchase. The BMW i3 remains a vehicle with a strong personality and definite relevance for the future.
