Ford Kuga

I (CBS) · 2008 – 2012

Summary

The first-generation Ford Kuga (code name CBS) marked Ford Europe's successful entry into the highly competitive compact SUV segment. Based on the excellent C1 platform (shared with the Focus Mk2, C-Max, and several Volvo/Mazda models), it stands out for its particularly dynamic, incisive, and sedan-like road handling. Its 'Kinetic' design has aged very well. However, it sacrifices some practicality with a modest trunk volume (360 liters) for its size. The engine range is limited but targeted, with an overwhelming dominance of diesel (2.0 TDCi blocks from the PSA/Ford partnership) and an atypical and noble gasoline offering (the Volvo-sourced 2.5 Turbo 5-cylinder). A slight technical overhaul took place in 2010 for the transition to the Euro 5 standard, introducing new diesel power outputs and the Powershift dual-clutch gearbox.

Strengths 5

  • First-class road handling (very dynamic C1 chassis)
  • Exterior design that has aged very well
  • Intrinsic reliability of engine blocks (PSA-sourced TDCi and Volvo 2.5T)
  • Sound insulation and ride comfort on the road
  • Paint quality and corrosion resistance

Weaknesses 5

  • Ridiculous trunk volume for the category (360 liters)
  • Average rear passenger space
  • Quality of some interior plastics (center console) and parasitic noises
  • Powershift automatic gearbox very risky on the used market if history is unclear
  • 2.5T gasoline engine consumption prohibitive for many

Check before buying

  • Clear history of Powershift gearbox oil changes (every 60,000 km). Flee if absent.
  • AWD transmission operation: turn the steering wheel fully at low speed, listen for groaning (sign of an unserviced Haldex).
  • Dashboard warning lights related to the DPF or EGR valve on diesels (especially the 136 hp).
  • Alternator whistling on the 2.0 TDCi 136 hp (sign of imminent fatigue).
  • Asymmetrical rear tire wear (multi-link rear axle geometry needs adjustment).

Official recalls

  • Heated windshield washer jets (2012): Risk of overheating and fire at the jets. Recall for replacement or deactivation.
  • Brake pedal (2009): On some models, the brake pedal pivot could dislodge, leading to a loss of braking. Pivot replacement.
  • Panoramic roof (2008): Bonding defect of the panoramic roof glass that could lead to its detachment while driving. Inspection and re-bonding.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Volkswagen Tiguan I (more spacious, superior finish, but TDI temperamental in its early days), Toyota RAV4 III (reliability benchmark, less dynamic), Nissan Qashqai I (the pioneer, cheaper), Peugeot 3008 I (excellent chassis, larger trunk).

Verdict

The Ford Kuga Mk1 is an excellent choice for those looking for a compact SUV with sedan-like handling, provided they don't need a large trunk. On the used car market, the diesel engines (which represent 95% of the offering) are generally robust as they come from the PSA parts bin (DW10). However, vigilance is required regarding the transmission. Opt for a 2.0 TDCi 140 or 163 hp version with a manual gearbox. If you choose the Powershift automatic gearbox, the purchase is conditional on the presence of a service book proving gearbox oil changes every 60,000 km. The 2.5T gasoline is a fantastic collector's item but very fuel-thirsty.
