Honda Odyssey

V (RL6) · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The **fifth-generation Honda Odyssey (factory code RL6)** is a minivan designed specifically for the North American and Middle Eastern markets. *Expert note: It should not be confused with the Odyssey sold in Japan and Asia (RC generation), which is a smaller vehicle.*

Launched for the 2018 model year, this RL6 generation stands out by abandoning alternative powertrains in favor of a single, highly robust naturally aspirated V6. Faced with an electrifying competition (Toyota Sienna hybrid, Chrysler Pacifica PHEV), the Odyssey plays the card of proven mechanical reliability, ease of maintenance, and driving pleasure.

According to data from *Consumer Reports* and *J.D. Power*, it regularly establishes itself as the most reliable minivan in its segment over the long term. A slight facelift was carried out in 2021, bringing cosmetic tweaks, an update to the driving aids (Honda Sensing as standard) and, above all, the standardization of the excellent 10-speed automatic gearbox across all trim levels.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of the J35Y6 V6 (proven over decades)
  • Gigantic interior space and modularity ('Magic Slide' seats)
  • Excellent 10-speed automatic gearbox (from 2020)
  • Dynamic road handling for a minivan
  • Ease of maintenance compared to hybrid competitors (PHEV/HEV)

Weaknesses 4

  • 9-speed automatic gearbox (ZF) on lower trims 2018-2019 (jerks)
  • Higher fuel consumption than hybrid competitors (e.g., Toyota Sienna)
  • Frequent bugs in the infotainment system (MOST network) on early model years
  • Second-row seats are heavy and difficult to remove completely

Check before buying

  • Software update history for the gearbox (2018-2019 models with 9-speed automatic).
  • Smooth operation of the center screen and rearview camera (known MOST coaxial cable issue).
  • Opening and closing of the power sliding doors (check for abnormal noises or jamming).
  • Timing belt replacement if the vehicle is approaching 160,000 km (100,000 miles).
  • Verification of recalls regarding the fuel pump and connecting rod bearings (specific model years).

Verdict

The Honda Odyssey (RL6) is undoubtedly one of the most reliable family vehicles on the North American market. Its J35Y6 V6 engine is a true institution of reliability, offering peace of mind that the complex hybrid powertrains of the competition sometimes struggle to match over the very long term (beyond 10 years).

**Buying advice:** Strongly favor models from **2020** onwards. They benefit as standard from the excellent 10-speed automatic gearbox (which erases the inconveniences of the 9-speed gearbox of the 2018-2019 models) and have corrected the early defects related to the sliding doors and the infotainment system. Ensure that the recalls concerning the MOST network (screen) and the fuel pump have been performed. If maintenance is followed (especially the timing belt at 160,000 km), it is a vehicle capable of easily exceeding 300,000 km without a major breakdown.
