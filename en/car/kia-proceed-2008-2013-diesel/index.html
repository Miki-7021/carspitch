Kia ProCeed

I (ED) · 2008 – 2013

BUY Reliability 83/100 — bon

Summary

The **first-generation Kia pro_cee'd (ED code)** is the 3-door variant of the cee'd compact hatchback. Designed in Germany and assembled in Slovakia, it was specifically conceived for the European market. It stands out with a lowered roofline, an inclined tailgate, and a decidedly more dynamic profile than the 5-door version, while retaining the same wheelbase. This model marked a turning point for Kia thanks to its famous 7-year warranty, a testament to confidence. The late 2010 facelift (MY2011) introduced the 'Tiger Nose' grille, higher quality interior materials, and mechanical optimizations (notably the transition to a 6-speed manual gearbox on more versions). It is a rational compact coupé, offering an excellent price/performance/reliability ratio.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent overall reliability, particularly the 1.6 CRDi engine with chain drive
  • Successful exterior design that ages well (especially in Sport trim)
  • Sound road handling and dynamic chassis (multi-link rear suspension)
  • Surprising rear passenger space for a 3-door coupé
  • Reasonable maintenance cost and accessible spare parts

Weaknesses 5

  • Very firm suspension, degraded comfort on bumpy roads (especially with 17-inch wheels)
  • Electric power steering (EPS) lacking feel and with a vague on-center feel
  • Poor rear visibility (very wide C-pillars)
  • Quality of some interior plastics (easy to scratch) and dashboard detachment at the windshield
  • Imperfect sound insulation (road and aerodynamic noise on the highway)

Check before buying

  • Clunking in the steering: wear of the rubber star (flex disc) in the electric steering column (very common problem, €5 part but labor required).
  • Clutch and flywheel (Diesel): test the biting point, listen for metallic noises at idle or when switching off the engine.
  • Multimedia system/Radio: the original car radio tends to freeze, lose display, or drain the battery in standby.
  • Dashboard: check if the trim is not lifting at the junction with the windshield (glue defect with heat).
  • Running gear: premature wear of stabilizer bar links (clunking noises over bumps).

Design

  • Sporty and Timeless Design — With its dynamic profile, lowered roofline, and sloped tailgate, the pro_cee'd boasts a successful design that ages very well. Restyled models (post-2010) feature the "Tiger Nose" grille for an added touch of modernity.

economy

  • Controlled Running Costs — This compact coupé offers excellent value for money on the used car market. The 1.6 CRDi engine ensures controlled fuel consumption and reasonable maintenance costs, making the pro_cee'd an economical choice for daily use.

Performance

  • Dynamic Diesel Engines — Enjoy the liveliness of the diesel engines, particularly the 128 hp 1.6 CRDi, which, combined with the 6-speed manual gearbox (on restyled versions), offers optimized driving pleasure and efficiency for all journeys.

practicality

  • Surprising Interior Space — Despite its 3-door coupé appearance, the Kia pro_cee'd surprises with its generous rear passenger space, offering unexpected comfort for occupants and appreciable versatility for daily use.

handling

  • Healthy Road Behavior — Designed for the European market, the pro_cee'd benefits from a dynamic chassis and multi-link rear suspension, ensuring healthy and engaging road behavior for an enjoyable driving experience.

confidence

  • European Mark of Confidence — Conceived and designed in Germany, then assembled in Slovakia, this model was a pioneer for Kia, symbolizing the brand's confidence with its famous initial 7-year warranty, a major asset for its reputation.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Renault Mégane 3 Coupé (excellent alternative with 1.5 dCi), Volkswagen Scirocco (more premium, TDI engines), Opel Astra GTC, Citroën C4 Coupé, Hyundai i30 (technical cousin, but the 3-door version arrived later in the next generation). - **Asia / Other:** Honda Civic 3-door (Type S), Toyota Auris (though less coupé-like).

Verdict

The Kia pro_cee'd (ED) is an extremely wise choice on the used car market for anyone looking for a sporty-looking vehicle without the associated maintenance costs. When targeting diesel engines, the **1.6 CRDi (115 or 128 hp)** is the best buy: its chain drive and proven robustness make it a strong ally for accumulating kilometers. You simply need to ensure that the vehicle has not been used exclusively in the city (risk for DPF/EGR) and check the clutch condition. Prioritize a facelifted model (post-2010) to benefit from the 6-speed gearbox, improved interior finish, and a modernized look.
