Summary

The third-generation BMW X6, known by the internal code G06, was launched in 2019, succeeding the F16. It follows in the lineage of the "Sports Activity Coupé" (SAC) initiated by BMW, combining the robustness of an SUV with the sloping roofline of a coupé. Based on the CLAR platform, shared with the X5 G05, it benefits from the brand's latest technological advancements in chassis, powertrains, and infotainment (iDrive 7, then iDrive 8 after the 2023 facelift).

The G06 is distinguished by a more assertive design, increased dimensions, and an imposing road presence. The interior moves upmarket, offering high-quality materials and refined ergonomics. The engine range is wide, from inline six-cylinder petrol and diesel engines to powerful V8s, including mild hybrid (MHEV) and plug-in hybrid (PHEV) versions. The 2023 facelift (LCI) brought aesthetic evolutions (headlights, bumpers), technological updates (iDrive 8, curved screens), and engine optimizations, notably the widespread integration of 48V mild-hybridization and the introduction of the new S68 V8.

Overall, the X6 G06 is a technologically advanced and high-performing vehicle, but its complexity and premium positioning imply potentially high maintenance and repair costs. Reliability is good for the six-cylinder engines, while the V8s and hybrid systems require particular attention.

Strengths 6

  • Assertive and distinctive exterior design
  • High-level finish quality and interior materials
  • Advanced onboard technologies (iDrive 7/8, driving aids)
  • Dynamic and precise road handling for an SUV
  • Suspension comfort (especially with air suspension)
  • Wide choice of powerful and efficient engines (especially the 6-cylinders)

Weaknesses 6

  • Limited rear visibility due to the coupé roofline
  • High purchase price and expensive options
  • Potentially very high maintenance and repair costs, especially for V8s and M versions
  • Significant fuel consumption for V8s
  • Complexity of electronic and hybrid systems
  • Degraded comfort on large wheels without adaptive suspension

Check before buying

  • Complete and up-to-date service history, ideally at BMW
  • Functioning of the AdBlue and DPF system on diesels (warning lights, error messages)
  • Condition of the 48V system on MHEV versions (voltage, fault codes)
  • Oil consumption on V8s (N63/S68/S63)
  • Condition of the high-voltage battery and charging system on PHEVs
  • Absence of oil or coolant leaks
  • Functioning of all electronic equipment (screens, cameras, driving aids)
  • Wear of tires and brakes (particularly on powerful versions)
  • Absence of abnormal noises from the running gear or suspension (air suspension if equipped)

Official recalls

Official recalls for the BMW X6 G06 are often shared with the X5 G05 due to the common platform. Here is a non-exhaustive list of known recalls:

  • 2020-01-20 (NHTSA 20V034000) : Software issue with the rearview camera system that may not display an image. Corrected by software update.
  • 2020-03-09 (NHTSA 20V141000) : The forward collision warning system may not function correctly. Corrected by software update.
  • 2021-02-08 (NHTSA 21V062000) : Software issue with the integrated braking system (IBS) that may result in a loss of braking assistance. Corrected by software update.
  • 2021-03-22 (NHTSA 21V190000) : The steering knuckle may not have been properly tightened, risking loosening. Inspection and replacement if necessary.
  • 2022-01-24 (NHTSA 22V037000) : Certain high-voltage battery modules on PHEV models (xDrive45e) may have a manufacturing defect, increasing the risk of fire. Replacement of defective modules.
  • 2023-03-20 (NHTSA 23V180000) : The fuel pump control unit may be defective, leading to engine stalling. Replacement of the unit.
  • 2023-05-01 (NHTSA 23V295000) : The driver's seat belt position sensor may be defective, preventing airbag deployment in a collision. Inspection and replacement if necessary.
  • 2024-01-22 (NHTSA 24V030000) : Software issue with the integrated braking system (IBS) that may result in a loss of braking assistance. Corrected by software update (similar recall to 2021, but for a different population or new detection).

It is imperative to check if all recalls concerning a specific vehicle have been carried out via the VIN number with a BMW dealership.

Verdict

The BMW X6 G06 is a vehicle that leaves no one indifferent, combining luxury, performance, and a bold design. Its reliability is generally good, especially with the inline six-cylinder engines (B57 diesel and B58 petrol) which are benchmarks in this regard. These versions offer an excellent compromise between performance, driving pleasure, and relative running costs.

For a worry-free purchase, I strongly recommend the xDrive30d and xDrive40d diesel versions, as well as the xDrive40i petrol. These engines are robust, proven, and offer a very satisfying driving experience. However, vigilance will be required regarding the maintenance of the AdBlue and DPF systems for diesels, especially if your usage involves many short trips.

The V8 versions (M50i, M60i, X6 M) are performance beasts, but their mechanical complexity and associated maintenance costs must be considered. They are aimed at informed buyers, ready to invest in rigorous and potentially costly maintenance. Oil consumption is a point to monitor on these blocks.

The plug-in hybrid versions (xDrive45e, xDrive50e) are interesting for their versatility and tax advantages, provided they can be recharged regularly. The reliability of the hybrid system is still young, but the B58 internal combustion engine is a guarantee of peace of mind.

Whatever the choice, a clear service history and a thorough pre-purchase inspection are paramount. The X6 G06 is a complex and high-end vehicle; repairs can be expensive. Prioritize well-equipped models, with options like air suspension for optimal comfort. It's an excellent choice for anyone looking for a sporty and luxurious SUV, provided they choose the right engine and do not neglect maintenance.
