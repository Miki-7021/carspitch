Summary

The Opel Grandland X (which became simply 'Grandland' after its facelift in late 2021) marks Opel's renewal under the PSA era (now Stellantis). Based on the excellent EMP2 platform, it is the direct technical cousin of the Peugeot 3008 II and Citroën C5 Aircross. It stands out with a more conservative design, Germanic ergonomics (retaining physical controls for climate control), and very comfortable AGR-certified seats. While its road handling is imperial, its overall reliability is heavily conditioned by the PSA-sourced engines it uses. Engine choice is absolutely crucial for this model.

Strengths 6

  • Suspension comfort (PSA-tuned but well-calibrated)
  • AGR ergonomic seats (excellent for the back)
  • Generous boot volume (514 liters for ICE versions)
  • Classic and intuitive ergonomics (physical buttons for climate control)
  • Very sound and safe road handling
  • Excellent EAT8 automatic gearbox (Aisin)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of high-volume engines (1.2 Turbo and 1.5 Diesel)
  • Slow and dated multimedia system (IntelliLink)
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds (wind noise)
  • Quality of some lower plastics
  • Reduced boot volume on plug-in hybrid versions (390 liters)

Check before buying

  • Clear service history, 100% within the network (essential for any warranty claim)
  • On the 1.5 Diesel: chain noise on cold start
  • On all Diesels: absence of UREA or engine light (very costly AdBlue problem)
  • On the 1.2 Turbo: condition of the timing belt via the oil filler cap
  • On PHEVs: state of health (SOH) of the traction battery and proper functioning of electric/thermal transition

Official recalls

  • November 2020 (1.2 Turbo): Massive recall for timing belt and vacuum pump inspection (risk of loss of braking assistance).
  • October 2019 (Diesels): Risk of particulate filter (FAP) cracking leading to non-compliant emissions.
  • February 2021: Incorrect tightening of an earth cable potentially leading to a fire risk.
  • May 2021: Problem with the fastening of the factory-fitted trailer hitch.
  • 2022 (PHEV): Risk of short circuit on the traction battery requiring a software update or cell replacement.

Verdict

The Opel Grandland X is a family SUV packed with objective qualities: it is comfortable, spacious, and very pleasant to drive. Unfortunately, its record is tarnished by the design flaws of this generation's PSA engines. If you are looking for a Diesel, avoid the 1.5 (130 hp) unless the chain modification (8.4 mm) has been done and invoiced. The 1.6 Diesel (120 hp) is a reasonable choice, but the 2.0 Diesel (177 hp) is by far the best engine in the range: robust, torquey, and reliable. For petrol, the 1.2 Turbo is to be avoided. Buy this vehicle only with full awareness and an impeccable service history.
