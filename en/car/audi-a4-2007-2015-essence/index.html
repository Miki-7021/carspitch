Audi A4

Summary

The Audi A4 B8 (code name 8K) marks a major turning point for the family car with the rings. Based on the new modular MLB platform, it moves its engine further back for better weight distribution, reducing the model's historical understeer. Launched in late 2007 and facelifted in late 2011 (Model Year 2012), it offers an interior finish quality that remains an absolute benchmark. However, this generation is notoriously known for serious reliability issues with its early-production TFSI petrol engines (EA888 Gen 2), tarnishing an otherwise excellent record. The 2012 facelift corrected the vast majority of these early flaws.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional interior finish and assembly quality (ages very well)
  • Safe and precise road handling (especially with Quattro)
  • High-level sound insulation
  • Timeless design, particularly in Avant (estate) and S-line versions
  • Post-2012 TFSI engines (Gen 3) are very powerful and efficient

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of 1.8 and 2.0 TFSI engines before 2012 (oil consumption, chain)
  • Fragile and unpleasant Multitronic automatic gearbox (front-wheel drive)
  • Very high maintenance and spare parts costs
  • Average rear passenger space and boot volume for its size (4.70m)
  • Meager standard equipment on entry-level trims (Ambiente)

Check before buying

  • On pre-2012 TFSI: Demand invoices proving the replacement of piston rings/pistons and the chain tensioner.
  • History of automatic gearbox oil changes (Multitronic and S tronic) every 60,000 km maximum.
  • Metallic rattling noise on cold start (sign of a loose timing chain, valid for TFSI and V6 TDI).
  • Door lock operation (actuators frequently fail).
  • Condition of front suspension arm bushings (rapid wear leading to steering vibrations).

Official recalls

  • Airbags (2014-2019) : Massive recall related to defective Takata gas generators (risk of metallic fragment projection).
  • Coolant pump (2017) : On 2.0 TFSI engines (2013-2015), risk of overheating or short-circuit of the auxiliary water pump potentially leading to a fire.
  • Auxiliary heater (2017) : Risk of overheating of the electrical connections of the auxiliary heater (PTC) potentially causing a fire in the passenger compartment.
  • Brake booster (2014) : On 3.0 TDI engines, risk of brake booster diaphragm rupture leading to loss of braking assistance.

What sets it apart 7

comfort

  • Benchmark Interior Finish Quality — The Audi A4 B8 stands out with exceptional interior finish and assembly quality, which ages remarkably well and remains an absolute benchmark in its category. Every detail is designed for the well-being of its occupants.
  • High-Level Soundproofing — The Audi A4 B8's cabin benefits from high-level soundproofing, ensuring exceptional silence and serenity on board, even at high speeds. A true cocoon for your journeys.

safety

  • Exemplary Passive Safety — With a robust design and advanced safety features, the Audi A4 B8 achieved excellent results in crash tests, ensuring optimal protection for all passengers.

handling

  • Precise and Stable Road Handling — Enjoy balanced and precise road handling, offering great stability and very secure roadholding. The steering is responsive and the car remains predictable, even in demanding conditions, ensuring a serene drive.

braking

  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — The Audi A4 B8 is equipped with a braking system offering excellent performance, with short and consistent stopping distances for its category. The system is durable and provides good pedal feel, for increased safety.

Performance

  • Modern and Efficient TFSI Engines (post-2012) — The 3rd generation TFSI petrol versions (from 2012/2013) offer remarkable performance, generous torque, and controlled fuel consumption. These reliable engines are an excellent choice for optimal driving pleasure.

Design

  • Timeless and Elegant Design — Its design, particularly in Avant or S-line versions, is renowned for its timeless elegance. The Audi A4 B8 maintains a modern and stately appearance that gracefully withstands the test of time.

Alternatives

  • Europe : BMW 3 Series (E90 then F30 - more dynamic but interior finishes are less refined), Mercedes C-Class (W204 - very reliable at the end of its production run), Volvo S60/V60.
  • Japan / Asia : Lexus IS (XE20 - unbeatable mechanical reliability, very robust but thirsty naturally aspirated V6 petrol).
  • North America : Acura TSX / TL, Infiniti G37 (very reliable V6), Cadillac ATS.

Verdict

The Audi A4 B8 is a car with two faces. If you are looking for a petrol model from before 2012 (1.8 or 2.0 TFSI), run away unless the engine has been completely rebuilt with supporting invoices (segmentation issue). In contrast, facelifted models (from late 2012 / 2013) equipped with 3rd generation TFSI engines are excellent family cars, reliable, powerful, and whose finish withstands the test of time with rare grace. Prioritize a Quattro version with a manual or S tronic gearbox (well-maintained), and avoid the Multitronic gearbox at all costs.
