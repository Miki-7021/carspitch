Summary

The first-generation Mazda3 (code BK), launched in 2003 to replace the aging 323, marked a major turning point for the brand. Based on Ford's excellent C1 platform (shared with the Ford Focus Mk2 and Volvo S40/V50), it immediately distinguished itself with its driving dynamics, precise steering, and sporty design. A facelift occurred in mid-2006, bringing slight aesthetic touches, improved sound insulation, the introduction of a 6-speed manual gearbox on the 2.0 diesel, and a very slight improvement in anti-corrosion protection (which remains the model's Achilles' heel). Although the gasoline engines are exemplarily reliable, the diesel versions (MZ-CD), very popular in Europe at the time, require extreme vigilance due to major design flaws, particularly on the 1.6 of PSA/Ford origin.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional chassis (C1 platform), dynamic and precise road handling
  • Remarkable reliability of gasoline engines (MZR)
  • Ergonomics of controls and driving position
  • Design that has aged well
  • Manual gearbox with excellent handling (typical Mazda)

Weaknesses 5

  • CORROSION: The biggest flaw. Rear wheel arches, sills, and underbody rust very quickly
  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.6 MZ-CD (PSA) if poorly maintained
  • Poor sound insulation (road and wind noise), especially before the 2006 facelift
  • Firm suspension, stiff comfort on degraded roads
  • Quality of some interior plastics (easy to scratch)

Check before buying

  • Thorough rust inspection: rear wheel arches (also under the trunk carpet), sills, front/rear subframes.
  • On 1.6 MZ-CD: Lift the engine cover and look for traces of black tar around the injectors. Listen for loud turbo whistling.
  • On 2.0 MZR-CD: Pull the oil dipstick. If the level is above 'Max' (towards 'X'), the engine is in danger (dilution).
  • On all versions: Clunking noises from the rear (worn trailing arm bushings, a common issue on the C1 platform).
  • Air conditioning operation (fragile compressor on early model years).

Official recalls

  • August 2010 (Power Steering): Risk of loss of power steering assistance due to the presence of particles in the hydraulic circuit (electro-hydraulic pump). Concerns 1.6 MZ-CD and 2.0 gasoline models produced between 2007 and 2008.
  • June 2006 (Wipers): The front wiper motor may stop working due to water infiltration. 2003-2005 models.
  • November 2004 (Airbags): Risk of incorrect deployment due to a defective collision sensor (early 2003-2004 models).

Verdict

The Mazda3 BK is a car with two faces. As a gasoline model, it's a fantastic, reliable, fun-to-drive, and affordable compact. As a diesel, it's a minefield. The 1.6 MZ-CD (PSA-sourced) suffers from turbo lubrication defects related to injector seals, while the 2.0 MZR-CD (Mazda) is plagued by its DPF system which dilutes engine oil. If you're looking for a Mazda3 of this generation, avoid the diesels unless you have a service history as thick as a phone book proving that chronic defects have been addressed. Absolutely prioritize a gasoline model (1.6 or 2.0 MZR). In any case, the number one enemy remains corrosion: an inspection on a lift is mandatory before purchase.
