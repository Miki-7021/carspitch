Summary

The seventh-generation Hyundai Sonata (codename LF) was introduced to succeed the very bold YF generation. It adopts a more conservative and mature design (Fluidic Sculpture 2.0), while significantly improving chassis rigidity, soundproofing, and interior finish quality. A facelift occurred in 2017 (2018 model year), bringing a more aggressive front fascia and new driver assistance technologies.

Although highly appreciated for its roominess, comfort, and price-to-equipment ratio, the reputation of this generation is heavily tarnished by the catastrophic failures of the Theta II gasoline engines (2.4 GDI and 2.0 T-GDI), which led to massive recalls, fire risks, and warranty extensions (especially in North America). The hybrid versions and the small 1.6 T-GDI engine fare much better.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent roominess (rear legroom)
  • Ergonomics of controls and intuitive infotainment system
  • Soundproofing significantly improved compared to the previous generation
  • Highly refined and reliable Hybrid and Plug-in Hybrid versions
  • Long-term manufacturer warranty (5 years/60,000 mi or 10 years/100,000 mi depending on markets), extended to 10 years/120,000 mi on problematic engines in the USA

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic reliability of the 2.4 GDI and 2.0 T-GDI (Theta II) engines
  • DCT7 gearbox (on 1.6T and 1.7 CRDi) sometimes hesitant at low speeds
  • Quality of certain interior plastics on entry-level trims
  • Safe road handling but lacking dynamism compared to a Mazda 6
  • Numerous early-life recalls (2015-2016) regarding electronics and assembly

Check before buying

  • Maintenance and recall history: has the KSDS (knock sensor) recall been performed on the 2.4 and 2.0T engines?
  • Engine oil level and color (Theta II engines consume a huge amount of oil before failing)
  • Automatic gearbox behavior (jerking on the DCT7, smoothness on the 6-speed automatic)
  • Operation of the panoramic sunroof (rattling noises, jamming)
  • Dashboard warning lights (ABS, parking brake - subject to recalls)

Verdict

The Hyundai Sonata (LF) is a family sedan full of qualities: spacious, comfortable, and well-equipped. However, purchasing a version equipped with the 2.4 GDI or 2.0 T-GDI (Theta II) engine is strongly discouraged due to proven risks of engine failure and fire, unless the engine has been recently replaced by Hyundai and covered by a lifetime warranty.

On the other hand, the 1.6 T-GDI (Eco) versions and especially the Hybrid variants (HEV/PHEV) are excellent choices, offering significantly higher reliability and reduced running costs. Favor the facelifted models (2018-2019) which corrected most of the early-life defects and electronic recalls of the 2015-2016 years.
