Opel Astra

K · 2015 – 2021

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The Opel Astra K marks a major break with the previous generation (Astra J). Voted **Car of the Year 2016**, it is based on the GM D2XX platform, allowing it a drastic weight reduction (up to 200 kg less). This weight gain transforms its driving behavior, making it more dynamic and efficient. It also distinguished itself by introducing higher-segment technologies, such as IntelliLux LED matrix headlights and excellent AGR-certified ergonomic seats.

**Important note on the facelift (2019)**: Although Opel was acquired by the PSA group (now Stellantis) in 2017, the Astra K retained its General Motors underpinnings until the end of its career. The 3-cylinder engines introduced during the 2019 facelift (1.2 Turbo and 1.4 Turbo) are of **GM design (with timing chain)** and have absolutely nothing to do with PSA's 1.2 PureTech (with wet belt), contrary to popular belief.

Strengths 5

  • Driving behavior transformed compared to the Astra J (agile and light)
  • AGR ergonomic seats (among the best in the segment)
  • Highly effective IntelliLux LED matrix headlights for their time
  • Responsive multimedia system with Apple CarPlay / Android Auto standard very early on
  • Reliable and chain-driven 3-cylinder gasoline engines post-2019 (GM)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.4 Turbo 4-cylinder (LSPI, pistons)
  • Timing chain issue on the 1.6 CDTI (enormous repair cost)
  • Quality of plastics in the lower part of the cabin
  • Recurring clutch master/slave cylinder problems (manual gearboxes)
  • Average sound insulation on 3-cylinder versions

Check before buying

  • **Oil history (1.4T 4 cyl.)**: Demand invoices proving the use of Dexos 1 Gen 2 oil.
  • **Cold noise (1.6 CDTI)**: Start the vehicle cold, with the hood open, to listen for any metallic chain noise.
  • **Clutch pedal**: Test the pedal's return. If it's soft or remains stuck, the master cylinder is faulty.
  • **IntelliLux Headlights**: Check that no error message appears on the dashboard concerning the adaptive lighting.
  • **Touchscreen**: Ensure it doesn't flicker and that the touch response works across the entire surface (cases of black screens).

Official recalls

  • - **Braking (November 2018)**: Recall of 2016-2018 models (1.4T and 1.6T engines) for loss of braking assistance due to vacuum pump failure.
  • - **Clutch / Brakes (2016)**: Possible hydraulic fluid leak affecting clutch operation and potentially braking.
  • - **Airbags (2017)**: Risk of non-deployment of curtain and side airbags in case of impact (connector issue).
  • - **Steering (2017)**: Sudden loss of electric power steering assistance requiring reprogramming or replacement of the steering rack.

Verdict

The Opel Astra K is an excellent compact on paper: light, dynamic, well-equipped, and affordable as a used car. However, **the choice of engine entirely dictates the ownership experience**.

If you're looking for a gasoline engine, **avoid the 1.4 Turbo (125/150 hp) from before 2019 at all costs** unless you have a pristine service history proving the exclusive use of Dexos 1 Gen 2 oil. Prefer the small 1.0 Turbo for city driving, the rare 1.6 Turbo 200 hp for the open road, or opt for facelifted models (post-2019) equipped with the excellent 1.2 Turbo (which, let's remember, are very reliable chain-driven GM engines, not PSA PureTech). For diesel, the 1.6 CDTI should be purchased with full awareness regarding the timing chain.
