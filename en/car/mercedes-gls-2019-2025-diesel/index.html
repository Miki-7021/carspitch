Summary

Mercedes-Benz GLS (X167 Generation)

Often called the « S-Class of SUVs », the Mercedes-Benz GLS (X167) is the flagship of the star-badged manufacturer's SUV range. Launched in 2019 to counter the BMW X7, it offers superlative luxury, generous space with 7 true seats, and an impressive technological arsenal (MBUX system, optional E-Active Body Control suspension). A restyling (facelift) occurred in 2023, bringing minor aesthetic touches, an MBUX system update, and generalized mild hybridization (48V) to all powertrains, including diesels.

Although majestic and extremely comfortable, this behemoth weighing over 2.5 tons incorporates complex electronics. The 48V onboard network, essential for the operation of micro-hybrid powertrains and active suspensions, is the Achilles' heel of this generation. The diesel engine (OM656 inline 6-cylinder), which particularly interests us here, is paradoxically the most rational and reliable choice to move this mass, especially in Europe.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional OM656 diesel engine: torquey, economical, and very mechanically reliable
  • Imperial ride comfort (especially with wheels < 22 inches)
  • Gigantic interior space and 7 true seats usable by adults
  • Soundproofing worthy of an S-Class
  • Towing capacity (up to 3.5 tons)

Comfort

  • Imperial Ride Comfort — Enjoy imperial ride comfort and sound insulation worthy of an S-Class, transforming every journey into a luxurious and serene experience.

Practicality

  • Gigantic Interior Space and 7 True Seats — With its gigantic interior space, the GLS offers 7 true seats comfortably usable by adults, ideal for large families and long trips.
  • Exceptional Towing Capacity — The GLS stands out with its impressive towing capacity, able to tow up to 3.5 tonnes, offering unparalleled versatility for all your needs.

Performance

  • Remarkable Power and Efficiency — The OM656 diesel engine delivers truck-like torque, combining impressive power with surprisingly controlled fuel consumption for its category.

Safety

  • Powerful and Safe Braking — Despite its size, the GLS offers excellent braking performance, with powerful systems ensuring short stopping distances and optimal safety.
  • Stable and Predictable Road Holding — Benefit from very stable road holding and predictable, safe handling, with surprisingly good body control for a large SUV, even at high speeds.
