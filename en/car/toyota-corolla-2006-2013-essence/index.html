Toyota Corolla

XI (E140) · 2006 – 2013

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The Toyota Corolla type E140 (narrow version for Japan) and E150 (wide international version), produced between 2006 and 2013, corresponds to the 10th global generation (though sometimes designated differently depending on the markets). In the European market, the hatchback version was renamed Auris, while the Corolla name was retained for the three-box sedan. A true institution of reliability, this generation shines with its proven design, prioritizing robustness and comfort over sportiness. Gasoline engines, in particular, are renowned for their exceptional longevity, easily exceeding 300,000 km with basic maintenance. A facelift occurred in 2010, modernizing the headlights, bumpers, and introducing new, more efficient engines (ZR and NR families).

Strengths 5

  • Legendary reliability of gasoline engines (ZZ and ZR families)
  • Assembly quality and aging of interior materials
  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation (for its time)
  • Negligible maintenance cost and wide availability of parts
  • Indestructible torque converter automatic transmissions (U340E/U341E)

Weaknesses 5

  • Placid road behavior, vague electric power steering (EPS)
  • Problematic 2.0 D-4D diesel engines (head gasket)
  • Oil consumption on the 2.4L engine (North America)
  • Very conservative design, lack of passion
  • Paint sensitive to micro-scratches and chips

Check before buying

  • Recall history (Takata airbags and accelerator pedal must have been performed)
  • Coolant level (pink traces around the water pump on ZR engines)
  • Engine oil level (especially on 1.8L 1ZZ and 2.4L 2AZ)
  • Clunking noise in the steering column (intermediate shaft to be greased or replaced)
  • Operation of the valve lift control (Valvematic) on FAE engines (unstable idle, fault code P1047)

Official recalls

  • Takata Airbags (Massive, 2013-2019) : Replacement of front airbag inflators (driver and passenger) at risk of exploding and projecting metal fragments.
  • Accelerator pedal (2009-2010) : Risk of accelerator pedal sticking in the depressed position (internal friction or unsuitable floor mat). Installation of a reinforcement shim.
  • Power window switch (2012) : Risk of short circuit and fire in the driver's side window control panel. Lubrication or replacement.
  • Steering column (2012) : Replacement of a component of the intermediate steering shaft at risk of developing play.

What sets it apart 7

reliability

  • Unrivaled Petrol Engine Reliability — The Corolla's petrol engines (ZZ and ZR families) are renowned for their legendary robustness and exceptional longevity, ensuring hundreds of thousands of kilometers of trouble-free driving.

safety

  • Proven Safety — Achieving a 5-star Euro NCAP rating, the Corolla offers remarkable protection for its occupants, ensuring peace of mind on the road.
  • Competent and Stable Braking — The Corolla's braking system is known for its competence and predictability, offering excellent stability during emergency stops for enhanced safety.
  • Safe and Predictable Road Handling — The Corolla prioritizes stability and safety, with predictable road behavior that inspires confidence, even on long journeys.

comfort

  • Comfort and Smooth Driving — Enjoy remarkable suspension comfort and refined sound insulation, transforming every journey into a pleasant and relaxing experience.

practicality

  • Durable and Well-Finished Interior — The excellent build quality and durability of the interior materials ensure a cabin that ages remarkably well, retaining its new appearance over the years.

economy

  • Economical and Easy Maintenance — Benefit from negligible maintenance costs and wide availability of parts, making the Corolla a smart choice for your budget.

Alternatives

  • Europe : Honda Civic (8th gen, excellent gasoline reliability), Volkswagen Jetta / Golf (superior finish but capricious TSI/TDI), Mazda 3 (dynamic but sensitive to rust).
  • North America : Honda Civic, Mazda 3, Hyundai Elantra (good value for money but less durable engines).
  • Asia / Emerging Markets : Nissan Sylphy/Sentra, Honda City/Civic.

Verdict

The Toyota Corolla E140/E150 is the archetype of a sensible purchase. If you're looking for a reliable, economical, and trouble-free daily driver, it's one of the best choices on the used car market. The 1.6L and 1.8L gasoline engines (ZR family) are absolutely to be preferred for their almost unrivaled robustness, coupled with a manual or classic automatic transmission. However, you should avoid the 2.4L gasoline engine (North America) and the 2.0L D-4D diesel engine (Europe), which tarnish the reliability record. Ensure that major recalls (Takata, pedal) have been performed.
