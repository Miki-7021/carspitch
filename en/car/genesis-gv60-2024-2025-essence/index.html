Genesis GV60

I facelift (JW) · 2024 – 2025

BUY Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The Genesis GV60 (JW generation, facelift) is a premium compact SUV based on the Hyundai-Kia group's E-GMP platform (shared with the Ioniq 5 and EV6). **ATTENTION: Contrary to the request targeting a gasoline engine, the Genesis GV60 is an EXCLUSIVELY 100% electric model (BEV).** It has never been produced with a combustion engine (neither gasoline nor diesel). The facelift introduced in 2024/2025 brings subtle aesthetic tweaks, a new infotainment system (ccNC) and, depending on the market, a larger capacity battery (increasing from 77.4 kWh to 84 kWh). It is a luxurious vehicle, equipped with an 800V architecture allowing ultra-fast charging, but whose initial reliability was marred by recurring problems related to the ICCU (Integrated Charging Control Unit). Overall rating: An excellent premium EV, provided that the critical updates have been performed.

Strengths 5

  • 800V architecture allowing ultra-fast charging (10-80% in 18 min)
  • Exceptional interior finish quality and soundproofing
  • Top-flight performances (especially in Performance AWD version)
  • Very practical V2L (Vehicle-to-Load) functionality
  • Suspension comfort (with camera-based adaptive suspension)

Weaknesses 5

  • Recurring and critical ICCU problems (loss of power while driving)
  • Disappointing trunk volume for the size (432 liters)
  • Very high weight (over 2.1 tons) impacting tire wear
  • Highway power consumption could be improved compared to Tesla
  • Genesis distribution and maintenance network still limited in Europe

Check before buying

  • Status of ICCU (Integrated Charging Control Unit) recalls: the most critical point
  • State of Health (SOH) of the 12V battery, often damaged by ICCU failures
  • Tire wear (instant torque and weight wear out the rubber quickly)
  • Attachment of exterior moldings (especially windshield, subject to recall)
  • Proper functioning of screens and cameras (known software bugs)

Verdict

The Genesis GV60 is an extremely accomplished electric vehicle in terms of comfort, charging technology (800V), and performance. However, its reliability is heavily marred by the endemic ICCU problem affecting the E-GMP platform. Purchasing a 2024-2025 model is recommended **only** if the dealer can prove that the software and hardware recalls regarding the ICCU (24V868000 / 24V204000) have been rigorously applied. Once this defect is corrected, it is a formidable premium alternative to German electric SUVs and Tesla.
