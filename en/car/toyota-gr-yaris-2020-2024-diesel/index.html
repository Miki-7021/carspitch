Toyota GR Yaris

I · 2020 – 2024

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The **Toyota GR Yaris (Generation I)** is a true anomaly in the modern automotive landscape: a "homologation special" designed specifically to serve as the basis for Toyota's WRC rally car. Developed in collaboration with Tommi Mäkinen Racing, it sits on a hybrid platform (Yaris GA-B front, Corolla/C-HR GA-C rear) to integrate the GR-Four AWD system. Featuring a specific 3-door body, a carbon roof, and aluminum panels, it is a marvel of engineering.

**⚠️ EXPERT NOTE REGARDING DIESEL:** The query targets a diesel engine. As an expert, I must point out that **there has never been a diesel Toyota GR Yaris**, nor even a standard diesel Yaris of this generation (XP210). The GR Yaris is exclusively powered by petrol engines (1.6 Turbo globally, and 1.5 naturally aspirated in Japan). The analysis will therefore focus on the actual engines to ensure technical accuracy.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional chassis and formidable GR-Four AWD system
  • Characterful and very robust 3-cylinder turbo engine (G16E-GTS)
  • Almost non-existent depreciation (collector's vehicle from release)
  • Assembly quality from the Motomachi plant (Japan)
  • Powerful and enduring factory braking

Weaknesses 5

  • Driving position considered too high by many drivers
  • Forward visibility hindered by the central rearview mirror/camera block
  • Tiny trunk (174 liters) and symbolic rear seats
  • Non-existent soundproofing on the highway
  • Total absence of a diesel engine (for high-mileage drivers)

Check before buying

  • Strict maintenance history: transmission fluids (axles, transfer case) must have been replaced according to recommendations.
  • Signs of intensive track use: wear on two-piece brake rotors, tire condition, stone chips on the front end.
  • ECU report: check the number of recorded over-revs (downshifting errors from 5th to 2nd are common and fatal for the valves).
  • Underbody condition: check for the absence of impacts related to off-track excursions or overly aggressive driving over curbs.

What sets it apart 14

Performance

  • G16E-GTS Turbo Engine: A Rally Heart — Enjoy a 1.6L 3-cylinder turbo engine, an engineering masterpiece delivering impressive power and generous torque. Designed for performance, it offers exhilarating driving sensations worthy of a rally car.
  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — Benefit from impressive deceleration capability thanks to large ventilated discs and 4-piston calipers. This system guarantees remarkably short stopping distances and exceptional fade resistance.

Reliability

  • Exceptional Mechanical Reliability — Benefit from surprising mechanical robustness for a vehicle in this category. The G16E-GTS engine is renowned for its solidity, and the assembly quality from the Motomachi factory in Japan ensures remarkable durability.

Safety

  • High-Level Active and Passive Safety — Designed for performance and safety, the GR Yaris achieved excellent results in crash tests. Its powerful and enduring braking system ensures short, controlled stops, even under intensive use.

Handling

  • Formidable Handling and Unrivaled Agility — Experience exceptional dynamic behavior thanks to its ultra-rigid chassis, sport suspension, and sophisticated GR-FOUR all-wheel drive. It offers remarkable agility and phenomenal grip for perfect control.

Economy

  • A Sound Investment with Lasting Value — The Toyota GR Yaris is a future classic and a collector's vehicle from its release. Its depreciation is almost non-existent, making it an excellent investment with very stable and high resale value.

Practicality

  • Engineered for Sporting Excellence — A true WRC "homologation special," the GR Yaris is optimized for sporty driving and track days. Every detail is designed to offer a pure, uncompromising driving experience.

Verdict

The Toyota GR Yaris is a future classic, a road-legal rally car that offers surprising mechanical reliability for its performance level. The G16E-GTS engine is a masterpiece of robustness, provided that warm-up times and transmission fluid change intervals are strictly respected. **Regarding the search for a diesel model: this idea must be abandoned**, as the car only exists in petrol. If you are looking for a utility or economical vehicle to do 30,000 km a year on the highway, steer clear. If you are looking for one of the best sports cars of the decade for winding roads and trackdays, it is a must-buy.
