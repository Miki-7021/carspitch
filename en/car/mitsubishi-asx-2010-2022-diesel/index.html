Summary

The Mitsubishi ASX (Active Sports Crossover), known as Outlander Sport in North America and RVR in Canada and Japan, is a compact SUV that was introduced in 2010. It shares its platform with the Outlander and Lancer, offering a more compact and urban alternative. Throughout its long career, the ASX has undergone several restyling phases (2012, 2016, 2019) to maintain its appeal, notably with the adoption of the brand's 'Dynamic Shield'. Positioned as a versatile vehicle, it stands out for its mechanical simplicity (especially in gasoline versions) and general robustness, although some diesel engines have faced reliability challenges. It's an interesting choice for those looking for a no-frills compact SUV with good interior space and decent road holding.

Strengths 6

  • General reliability of MIVEC gasoline engines
  • Decent interior space and trunk volume for the category
  • Mechanical simplicity (especially in gasoline versions), easy to maintain
  • Good road holding and suspension comfort
  • Good price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Effective all-wheel drive (4WD) on some versions

Weaknesses 6

  • Perceived interior quality (hard plastics) on early model years
  • Imperfect sound insulation, especially on highways
  • Potential reliability issues on diesel engines (DPF, EGR, turbo)
  • Noisy and demanding CVT gearbox in terms of maintenance
  • Naturally aspirated gasoline engines a bit underpowered for acceleration in heavy or loaded versions
  • Successive facelifts have not always masked the age of the design

Check before buying

  • Complete service history, especially for diesels and CVT versions
  • Operation of DPF and EGR valve on diesels (warning lights, smoke, regenerations)
  • Engine oil level and condition on diesels (fuel dilution)
  • Abnormal CVT noises, jerks, slipping (sign of wear or poor maintenance)
  • Condition of suspension (bushings, shock absorbers) and brakes
  • Presence of rust on underbody, exhaust, and wheel arches
  • Operation of air conditioning and onboard electronics

Verdict

The Mitsubishi ASX is a vehicle that has stood the test of time thanks to a solid foundation, particularly its MIVEC gasoline engines which are exemplarily reliable. If you are looking for a simple, robust, and economical compact SUV to use (in gasoline versions), the ASX is an excellent choice on the used market.

**For gasoline engines (1.6, 1.8, 2.0, 2.4 MIVEC):** This is a **RECOMMENDED BUY**. Prioritize manual gearbox versions for maximum peace of mind. If you opt for a CVT, ensure that all oil changes have been scrupulously performed every 60,000 km. These engines are safe bets.

**For diesel engines (1.8 DI-D, 2.2 DI-D, 1.6 DI-D PSA):** The verdict is more nuanced, leaning towards **NEGOTIATE**. These engines are more complex and prone to DPF, EGR, and turbo problems, especially if they have been used predominantly in the city or if maintenance has been neglected. An impeccable service history is imperative. They are better suited for high-mileage drivers who make regular long journeys. A thorough inspection by a professional is strongly advised before purchase.

Overall, the ASX offers good value for money on the used market, but caution is advised with diesels. Restyled versions (from 2016 onwards) offer a better presentation and more modern equipment.
