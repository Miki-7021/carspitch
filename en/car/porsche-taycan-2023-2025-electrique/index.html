Summary

The Porsche Taycan (J1 generation) benefited from a major facelift (often referred to as Gen 1.5) unveiled in early 2024 for the 2025 model year, although the 2023-2025 period also covers the end of the first phase. This exclusively electric model (BEV) redefines the standards of driving dynamics in its class. The facelift brings higher capacity batteries (up to 105 kWh gross), an optimized charging architecture (up to 320 kW peak), and lighter, more powerful rear motors. While the build quality and performance are exceptional, the reliability record is heavily marred by a series of critical recalls concerning the high-voltage battery cells (fire risk) and weaknesses in the brake hoses. Rigorous maintenance within the official network is imperative.

Strengths 5

  • Unrivaled road handling and driving dynamics for an EV of this weight
  • 800V architecture allowing extremely fast charging (up to 320 kW on facelift)
  • Top-tier interior finish quality and soundproofing
  • Maintains performance even at low charge levels or after multiple accelerations
  • Practical body variants (Cross Turismo and Sport Turismo)

Weaknesses 6

  • Very heavy recall history (batteries, brakes, suspension)
  • Documented fire risk linked to manufacturing defects in the battery cells
  • Frequent failures of the heating module (PTC heater) in winter
  • Recurring software bugs (black screens, cameras, false alerts)
  • Rear seat space and trunk volume limited compared to its size
  • Astronomical cost of options and out-of-warranty repairs

Check before buying

  • Status of high-voltage battery recalls (demand the Porsche certificate of module compliance)
  • Condition of the front brake hoses (look for cracking or fluid seepage)
  • Operation of the cabin heating (test thoroughly, very common failure of the high-voltage heater)
  • Suspension noises (clunking over speed bumps, check the rear suspension arms and air shock absorbers)
  • Condition of the domestic charging cable (check for signs of overheating on the 220V/240V plug)

Official recalls

The Taycan (2023-2025) is affected by multiple critical safety recalls (Sources: Rappel Conso FR, NHTSA US):

  • High-Voltage Battery / Fire Risk (2023, 2024, 2025): Numerous recalls (e.g., NHTSA 23V615000, 23V840000, 24V215000, 24V217000, 24V731000, 24V732000; Rappel Conso 2024-06-06, 2024-07-09, 2024-08-22, 2025-05-12) concern production defects in the cells (torn anode tab, folded/detached cathode) leading to internal short circuits and a fire risk. Replacement of defective modules is required.
  • Brake Hoses (June 2024): NHTSA recall 24V455000. The front brake hoses can crack and leak, resulting in a loss of braking power.
  • Suspension and Steering (2021, 2022, 2025): Recalls for risk of detachment of the rear axle track arms (Feb 2022), breakage of the lower axle tie rod (Jan 2022), and loosening of the MacPherson strut retaining ring (NHTSA 25V415000, July 2025).
  • Charging Cable (December 2023): NHTSA recall 23V841000. Possible overheating when using the 220V/240V industrial plug at 100% charge.
  • Software / Electronics (2023, 2024, 2025): Recalls for rearview camera not displaying (NHTSA 24V730000, 25V896000), headlight control module error (NHTSA 24V654000), and erroneous deactivation of the passenger airbag (NHTSA 25V221000).

What sets it apart 7

Performance

  • Ultra-Fast 800V Charging — Thanks to its advanced 800V electrical architecture, the Taycan facelift enables extremely fast charging, reaching up to 320 kW. This minimizes downtime and maximizes your time on the road, offering unparalleled flexibility for long journeys.
  • Unrivaled Driving Dynamics — The Taycan redefines dynamic standards for an electric vehicle. It offers exceptional road handling and sustained performance, even after repeated accelerations or at low charge levels, guaranteeing a consistent and sporty driving experience.

Comfort

  • Premium Finish and Comfort — The Taycan's interior is a luxurious sanctuary, characterized by high-quality interior finishes and remarkable sound insulation. Every detail is designed to offer a serene and refined travel experience, even at high speeds.

Safety

  • Exemplary Passive Safety — The Porsche Taycan achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 85% for adult protection and 83% for child protection, confirming its commitment to the safety of all occupants.

Braking

  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — True to Porsche's DNA, the Taycan offers exceptional braking performance. Its stopping distances are short, and its power is impressive and consistent, even under intensive use, ensuring perfect control in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Sporty and Precise Road Handling — Despite its weight, the Taycan surprises with its agility and precise road holding. Its remarkably balanced chassis, communicative steering, and minimal body roll provide a sporty and engaging driving experience, typically Porsche.

Practicality

  • Versatile Body Styles — The Taycan offers increased versatility thanks to its practical body variants, including the Cross Turismo and Sport Turismo versions. They combine the elegance of a sporty sedan with increased cargo space and functionality, ideal for all adventures.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Audi e-tron GT (technical cousin, shares the same platform qualities and flaws), Mercedes-Benz EQE AMG / EQS (more focused on comfort and technology, less sporty).
  • North America: Tesla Model S Plaid / Long Range (native Supercharger network, superior range, but lower finish quality), Lucid Air (record range, superior interior space, less sporty handling).
  • Asia: Lotus Emeya, Zeekr 001 FR (emerging high-performance alternatives).

Verdict

The Porsche Taycan (2023-2025) is a marvel of dynamic engineering that proves electric vehicles can offer genuine sporty driving sensations. The 2024 facelift corrects the main shortcomings in range and power. However, its reliability record is alarming due to major manufacturing defects in the battery cells (supplied by LG) that can lead to fires, as well as brake hose issues. Purchasing a used model from this period should only be considered on the strict and non-negotiable condition that the recall history is crystal clear, certified by a Porsche Center, and that the vehicle benefits from an extended manufacturer warranty (Porsche Approved).
