Skoda Octavia

IV · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The fourth-generation Skoda Octavia (code name NX) is based on the Volkswagen Group's MQB Evo platform, shared with the Golf VIII and Seat Leon IV. A true pillar of the Czech brand, it moves upmarket with a very appealing interior presentation, class-leading spaciousness (especially in the Combi estate), and extensive digitalization. While this generation marked a technological leap, its early days (2020-2021) were marred by numerous software bugs (MIB3 system). The 2024 facelift corrected these issues with an improved interface, the return of some physical controls, and a rationalization of the engine range. In the used car market, diesel versions (2.0 TDI Evo) remain absolute benchmarks for high-mileage drivers thanks to their exceptional fuel efficiency and high mechanical reliability, contrasting with the electronic quirks of the cabin.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional fuel efficiency of 2.0 TDI Evo engines (range > 1,000 km)
  • Unbeatable boot volume (600L in sedan, 640L in Combi)
  • Suspension comfort (especially with DCC adaptive damping)
  • Significantly improved finish quality compared to generation III
  • Highly refined aerodynamics benefiting highway sound insulation

Weaknesses 4

  • Annoying all-touch ergonomics (climate/volume adjustment via unlit sliders before 2024)
  • Frequent electronic bugs on 2020-2022 model years (infotainment, driving aids)
  • DSG7 gearbox (DQ200) on small petrol engines still prone to clutch wear in urban use
  • Significant weight of plug-in hybrid (iV) versions penalizing dynamism

Check before buying

  • History of software updates (multimedia system and ECUs)
  • Absence of error messages related to 'Travel Assist' or 'Front Assist' on the dashboard
  • On TDIs: DPF status (check exhaust tips, they should be clean) and AdBlue refill history
  • On 1.0/1.5 TSI with DSG: absence of juddering on startup in 1st and 2nd gear (sign of dry dual-clutch wear)
  • On iV (PHEV) versions: battery state of health (SOH) and proper functioning of the charging flap

Official recalls

  • - **March 2022 (Engine cover)** : Risk of fire due to the decorative engine cover detaching and touching hot parts. Concerns 2.0 TSI (RS) engines. The cover was removed.
  • - **November 2021 (eCall)** : Malfunction of the emergency communication module. Software update required.
  • - **February 2022 (Seatbelts)** : Possible defect of the front seatbelt pretensioners.
  • - **2023 (PHEV)** : Risk of short circuit related to a defective insulating mat near the high-voltage battery on iV models.

Verdict

The Skoda Octavia IV is a formidable road car, particularly in its diesel variants (2.0 TDI 115 or 150 hp). The EA288 Evo engine is a mechanical success, offering high reliability and exceptionally low fuel consumption, ideal for high-mileage drivers. However, the user experience was marred at launch by an electronic architecture (MQB Evo) released too early, riddled with bugs.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize a 2.0 TDI 150 hp model from late 2022 onwards (or a 2020-2021 model with a history proving that all software and hardware fixes, such as the steering wheel, have been applied). Avoid PHEV (iV) versions if you don't charge daily, as their technical complexity and weight negatively impact overall reliability and driving pleasure.
