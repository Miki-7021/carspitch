Summary

The third-generation Volkswagen Touareg (internal code CR7) was launched in 2018, marking a significant evolution from its predecessors. Based on the Volkswagen Group's MLB Evo platform (shared with the Audi Q7, Porsche Cayenne, Bentley Bentayga, and Lamborghini Urus), it positions itself as a premium SUV, offering a blend of luxury, advanced technology, and on-road and off-road capabilities.

This generation emphasizes digitalization with the Innovision Cockpit, a spacious interior, and powerful engines, including plug-in hybrid options. A minor facelift was introduced in 2023, bringing aesthetic (front fascia, taillights) and technological (chassis, IQ.Light HD Matrix headlights) updates.

Overall, the Touareg III is a well-built and comfortable vehicle, but its technological complexity, especially on hybrid versions, requires particular attention regarding reliability and maintenance. Its general reliability is good, but certain points need monitoring, particularly on the most sophisticated systems.

Check before buying

  • Innovision Cockpit operation (absence of bugs, fluidity)
  • Condition and operation of the air suspension (absence of leaks, compressor)
  • Complete service history and invoices (engine oil changes, gearbox, AdBlue, etc.)
  • For eHybrid: actual electric range, battery status (diagnosis), charging history
  • Absence of engine, AdBlue, DPF or hybrid warning lights
  • Tire and brake wear (heavy and powerful vehicle)
  • Operation of all driver assistance systems and sensors

Official recalls

Official recalls for the Volkswagen Touareg III (CR7) include (non-exhaustive list, to be checked with the manufacturer using the VIN):

  • 2018-2019: Rear spoiler attachment potentially non-compliant, risk of detachment. (Affected population: certain vehicles produced between 2018 and 2019).
  • 2019: Software update for the engine control unit for various engines, to optimize emissions or operation. (Affected population: large, depending on engine and production date).
  • 2020: Brake pedal pivot pin potentially defective, risk of detachment. (Affected population: certain vehicles produced in 2019-2020).
  • 2021: Front passenger airbag module potentially non-compliant, risk of incorrect deployment. (Affected population: certain vehicles produced in 2020-2021).
  • 2022: Rear axle bolts potentially not tightened correctly, risk of loss of control. (Affected population: certain vehicles produced in 2021-2022).
  • 2023: Battery Management System (BMS) on eHybrid versions, risk of short circuit or fire in extreme cases. (Affected population: certain Touareg eHybrid produced between 2020 and 2023).
  • 2023: Panoramic sunroof frame potentially non-compliant, risk of detachment. (Affected population: certain vehicles produced in 2022-2023).

Verdict

The Volkswagen Touareg III is a highly accomplished premium SUV, offering excellent comfort, refined finish, and cutting-edge technologies. It's a relevant choice for those seeking a versatile vehicle, capable of long journeys as well as light adventure.

For diesel engines (V6 TDI): This is the most rational choice for high-mileage drivers and towing. Reliability is generally good, provided that the AdBlue system and DPF are properly maintained.

For petrol engines (V6 TSI): They offer superior driving pleasure in terms of quietness and performance, but fuel consumption is higher. Intake valve maintenance should be anticipated.

For the hybrid engine (eHybrid): This is an interesting option if you have the ability to recharge regularly and your daily commutes are primarily electric. In this case, fuel consumption can be very low. However, the complexity of the hybrid system and the potential cost of replacing the battery or high-voltage components make it a choice to consider with caution in the used car market. An impeccable service history and a battery diagnostic are imperative.

Buying advice: Prioritize models with a complete and transparent service history, ideally from Volkswagen. Check the proper functioning of the Innovision Cockpit and the air suspension. For eHybrid models, ensure the battery holds its charge well and that no alerts are present on the hybrid system. The Touareg III is an excellent vehicle, but its sophistication requires rigorous monitoring to avoid unpleasant surprises.
