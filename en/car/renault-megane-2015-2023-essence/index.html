Renault Megane

IV · 2015 – 2023

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The **Renault Mégane IV** marks a major stylistic and technological break from its predecessor. Based on the Alliance's modular CMF-C/D platform, it introduces equipment from the segment above (vertical R-Link 2 screen, head-up display, 4Control rear-wheel steering on GT/RS versions).

The facelift (Phase 2) in mid-2020 corrected many early flaws: replacement of the R-Link 2 system with Easy-Link (much smoother), minor aesthetic touches, and the introduction of the E-Tech plug-in hybrid powertrain.

**Positioning:** A mainstream compact hatchback (C-segment) focusing on comfort, design, and dynamism (especially in R.S. Line/GT trims).

*Expert's note:* The reliability of the Mégane IV is extremely dependent on the year and engine chosen. The used car market is plagued by the disastrous 1.2 TCe, but also abounds with excellent deals featuring the very successful 1.3 TCe.

Strengths 6

  • Successful exterior design that ages very well
  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation (especially Phase 2)
  • Enjoyment and reliability of the 1.3 TCe engine (all power outputs)
  • Superb road handling, devilish agility with 4Control (GT/RS)
  • Easy-Link multimedia system (Phase 2) finally responsive and modern
  • Very low fuel consumption of 1.5 dCi versions

Weaknesses 6

  • Catastrophic reliability of the 1.2 TCe engine (2015-2018)
  • R-Link 2 system (Phase 1) slow, buggy, and questionable ergonomics
  • EDC7 automatic gearbox (dry clutches) sometimes harsh and fragile in urban driving
  • Rear passenger space and boot volume in the lower average of the segment
  • Body panel alignment and parasitic noises (interior trim) on early model years
  • Reliability of the AdBlue system on recent diesels (Blue dCi)

Check before buying

  • Service history: avoid any 1.2 TCe without clear service records, or prioritize the 1.3 TCe.
  • EDC gearbox: test the vehicle when cold, in town, in traffic. No juddering or metallic jerks should be felt on startup.
  • Electronics (Phase 1): test all R-Link 2 functions (Bluetooth, GPS, reversing camera, air conditioning). Black screens are common.
  • Running gear: asymmetrical wear of front tires (especially on GT versions equipped with 4Control) and knocking from anti-roll bar drop links.
  • AdBlue system (Diesels post-2018): check for the absence of an 'Antipollution system to be checked' warning light on the dashboard.

What sets it apart 12

Safety

  • Certified Maximum Safety — The Renault Megane IV achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with scores of 88% for adult protection and 87% for child protection, ensuring optimal safety for all occupants.
  • Efficient and Durable Braking — The Megane IV ensures good braking performance, effective and reassuring in all circumstances, with excellent overall endurance contributing to a high level of active safety.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Precise Road Handling — Enjoy imperial, stable, and predictable road handling, offering an excellent balance between comfort and dynamism. The efficient and secure chassis ensures exemplary road holding in all conditions, with enhanced agility thanks to 4Control on certain versions.

Reliability

  • 1.3 TCe Petrol Engine: Reliability and Driving Pleasure — The 1.3 TCe engine (115, 140, 160 hp), co-developed with Mercedes, is widely praised for its excellent reliability, driving pleasure, generous torque, and fuel efficiency. It's the ideal choice for a powerful and durable petrol engine.

Comfort

  • Premium Suspension Comfort and Soundproofing — The Megane IV stands out with its remarkable suspension comfort and refined soundproofing, particularly improved on Phase 2 models, transforming every journey into a serene and pleasant experience.

Practicality

  • Intuitive Easy-Link Multimedia Technology (Phase 2) — On restyled models (Phase 2), the Easy-Link multimedia system offers a modern, responsive, and intuitive interface, facilitating access to navigation, entertainment, and connected features for a fluid user experience.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Peugeot 308 II and III (beware of the 1.2 PureTech), Volkswagen Golf VII and VIII (homogeneous benchmark), Toyota Corolla (for hybrid reliability), Seat Leon, Ford Focus. - **Asia / Japan:** Mazda 3 (superior finish, reliable naturally aspirated engines), Honda Civic. - **Emerging Markets (Sedan):** Skoda Octavia, Fiat Tipo, Toyota Corolla Sedan.

Verdict

The Renault Mégane IV is a car with two faces. In **Phase 1 (2015-2020)** with the 1.2 TCe petrol engine, it is a high-risk purchase that we formally advise against due to the 'Motorgate'. However, if you opt for a model equipped with the **1.3 TCe (introduced in 2018)**, the Mégane becomes one of the most recommendable compact cars on the market: powerful, economical, comfortable, and very reliable. **Phase 2 (post-2020)** is the mature choice, correcting electronic flaws (R-Link replaced by Easy-Link) and offering a more refined finish. In diesel, the 1.5 dCi remains a marathon runner, provided you monitor the AdBlue on recent versions.
