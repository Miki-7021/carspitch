Skoda e-Citigo

I · 2019 – 2020

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The Skoda Citigo-e iV (often called e-Citigo) is the Czech manufacturer's first mass-produced 100% electric car. Based on the Volkswagen Group's NSF (New Small Family) platform, it is the technical twin of the VW e-Up! and the SEAT Mii Electric. It replaces the internal combustion versions of the Citigo at the end of their career. **EXPERT CLARIFICATION:** The query targets a **diesel** engine, but the Skoda Citigo has **NEVER** been offered in diesel (TDI), neither in its internal combustion version (only 1.0 MPI petrol and CNG), nor obviously in this 'e-Citigo' version which is exclusively electric (BEV). This sheet will therefore focus on the model's actual electric powertrain, while listing the petrol internal combustion blocks for historical context. It is an excellent city car, reliable, with a real-world range of about 200-250 km in urban use, making it a benchmark choice on the used market.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent real-world range for the battery size (often >220 km in the city)
  • Overall reliability of the electric powertrain (proven on the VW e-Up!)
  • Surprising roominess for a 3.60 m vehicle
  • Extremely low running and maintenance costs
  • Sound road handling and comfortable damping

Weaknesses 5

  • DC fast charging limited to 40 kW (CCS standard optional on some base trims)
  • Total absence of a diesel engine (for high-mileage drivers, although not relevant in this segment)
  • Basic infotainment system (requires a smartphone via the Move&Fun app)
  • Soundproofing on fast roads
  • Production stopped very quickly (2020), making the model rare on the used market

Check before buying

  • State of health of the high-voltage battery (SOH) via an OBD diagnostic (e.g., Aviloo type tool or dealership)
  • Operation of the Type 2 charging cable and the domestic charger (CRO)
  • Noises from the front suspension silent blocks (premature wear due to the weight of the batteries)
  • Condition of the 12V battery (number 1 cause of immobilizing breakdowns on this EV)
  • Presence of the CCS (Combo) fast charging option if you plan trips outside built-up areas

Alternatives

(Gen 2):** The exact twin, often better equipped but slightly more expensive on the used market. - **SEAT Mii Electric:** The other VW Group clone, strictly identical technically. - **Renault Twingo Z.E.:** Less range (22 kWh battery), but 22 kW AC charging very practical in the city. - **Dacia Spring:** Cheaper, but road performance, comfort, and safety are far inferior to the Skoda. - **Smart ForFour EQ:** Very low range (often <100 km real-world), to be avoided compared to the e-Citigo.

Verdict

The Skoda Citigo-e iV is one of the best used electric city cars on the market. Its size/roominess/range ratio is exceptional thanks to its 36.8 kWh battery. **Regarding the search for a diesel model: this idea must be abandoned entirely, as this vehicle has never existed in diesel.** If you are looking for an economical city car for urban and peri-urban use, the e-Citigo is a highly recommended purchase. Just make sure the desired model has the CCS fast charging port if you plan to leave the city, and check the condition of the 12V battery.
