Kia ProCeed

III (CD) · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

Summary

The **third-generation Kia ProCeed (CD)** marks a radical turning point in the model's history. Gone is the 3-door coupé of previous generations; the ProCeed becomes an elegant **'Shooting Brake'** (5-door estate). Designed, developed, and manufactured in Europe (Slovakia) specifically for the European market, it positions itself as a more dynamic and stylish alternative to the classic Ceed SW, while retaining a generous boot volume (594 litres). It is only offered in high trims (GT-Line and GT). A mid-life facelift occurred at the end of 2021, bringing aesthetic changes (new logo, revised bumpers), a technological update, and the generalization of mild-hybridization (MHEV 48V). Overall, it is a very reliable vehicle, supported by the famous 7-year / 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty.

Strengths 6

  • Spectacular and unique design in the mainstream compact car category
  • Excellent boot volume (594 L) despite the sloping roofline
  • Very reassuring 7-year / 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty
  • Dynamic road handling, especially in GT version
  • Very rich standard technological equipment (GT-Line/GT trims)
  • Overall reliability of T-GDI petrol engines is very satisfactory

Weaknesses 6

  • Almost zero rear visibility (reversing camera essential)
  • Limited rear headroom for tall occupants
  • DCT7 dual-clutch gearbox sometimes hesitant at low speeds
  • Firm suspension comfort (especially with 18-inch wheels on the GT)
  • Road noise insulation could be improved on the highway
  • Fuel consumption of the 1.6 T-GDI 204 hp is quite high

Check before buying

  • **DCT7 Gearbox:** Test the vehicle when cold and in traffic jams. Any excessive juddering or slipping on startup indicates wear of the dry clutch discs.
  • **MHEV (48V) System:** Check that no hybrid warning light is on and that 'Sailing' mode activates correctly when lifting off the accelerator.
  • **Onboard Electronics:** Test the touchscreen (slowness or unexpected reboots reported on 2018-2020 models) and driving aids (Lane Assist sometimes too intrusive).
  • **Service History:** The 7-year warranty is conditional on strict adherence to the service book (very low tolerance for exceeding service intervals at Kia).
  • **Tire Wear:** The front axle can wear quickly on the GT version (204 hp) due to the significant torque.

Official recalls

  • - **FCA (Front Collision Assist) (2019):** Software update for the front radar which could unexpectedly brake the vehicle without an obstacle. - **e-Call System (2021):** Replacement or reprogramming of the emergency call module whose internal battery could drain prematurely. - **MHEV Belt Tensioner (2022):** On some 1.5 T-GDI and 1.6 CRDi 48V models, risk of starter-alternator belt tensioner pulley rupture. Replacement covered. - **Wiring Harness (2020):** Risk of a wiring harness rubbing in the engine compartment, potentially causing a short circuit (concerns a small production series from 2018-2019).

Verdict

The Kia ProCeed (CD) is a 'love at first sight' choice that doesn't sacrifice practicality. It offers a design worthy of the premium segment while maintaining mainstream manufacturer maintenance costs and excellent overall reliability. **Petrol engines are to be preferred**, especially the **1.4 T-GDI 140 hp** (used) or the **1.5 T-GDI 160 hp** (newer), which offer the best performance/consumption compromise. The GT version (204 hp) is very appealing but more expensive to run. Diesel should be avoided except for very high mileage drivers, due to the constraints related to modern emission control systems. When purchasing, prioritize a model with an impeccable service history to fully benefit from the remaining 7-year warranty.
