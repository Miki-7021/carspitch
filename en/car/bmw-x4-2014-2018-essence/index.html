Summary

The BMW X4 F26, launched in 2014, marked the arrival of the first generation of the Bavarian brand's "Sports Activity Coupé" (SAC) in the premium compact SUV segment. Based on the X3 F25 platform, it distinguishes itself with its sloping coupé-like roofline, offering a more dynamic and sporty aesthetic, at the expense of slightly reduced rear passenger space and trunk volume. Positioned as a more emotional alternative to the X3, it targets customers seeking a blend of SUV versatility and coupé dynamism. Overall, the X4 F26 benefits from the proven technical foundations of the X3, offering satisfactory reliability, although certain engines and the first years of production require particular attention.

Strengths 5

  • Distinctive and sporty design (SAC).
  • Dynamic and agile road handling for an SUV.
  • Premium interior finish quality and materials.
  • Wide choice of powerful and enjoyable engines.
  • Efficient and safe xDrive all-wheel drive.

Weaknesses 5

  • Reduced rear passenger space and trunk volume compared to the X3.
  • Firm suspension on some versions (M Sport, large wheels).
  • High maintenance and parts cost, typical of premium vehicles.
  • Limited rear visibility due to the sloping roofline.
  • Potential issues with the N20 engine from the first years of production.

Check before buying

  • Complete and clear service history: Essential for all BMW models, attesting to revisions and interventions.
  • N20 engine (4-cylinder petrol): Check if the timing chain has been replaced or if the vehicle is post-mid-2015. Listen for any suspicious noises (clicking, rubbing) on cold start.
  • Oil leaks: Examine the engine and underbody for traces of oil (valve cover gasket, oil filter housing, oil pan).
  • Cooling system: Check the condition of the electric water pump and thermostat, known weak points on some BMW engines.
  • EGR/DPF system (diesel): Ensure that official recalls have been carried out. Check usage history to avoid a clogged DPF (prefer vehicles that have made long journeys).
  • ZF 8HP automatic gearbox: Test the fluidity of gear changes, absence of jerks. Preventive oil change is recommended every 100,000-120,000 km.
  • Electronics: Test all functions (iDrive, driving aids, power windows, air conditioning, etc.).
  • Tire wear: Abnormal wear can indicate a geometry or suspension problem.

Official recalls

Official Recalls for the BMW X4 F26

  • 2017-2018 (Rappel Conso, NHTSA): Potential issue with the exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) cooler module on certain diesel models (B47, N47, N57 engines). Risk of overheating and fire. It is imperative to check if the recall has been performed.
  • 2016-2017 (NHTSA): Recall for the replacement of the front passenger airbag (Takata supplier) on certain vehicles. Risk of incorrect deployment.
  • 2015 (NHTSA): Recall for a potential issue with the fuel pump module, which could lead to engine failure or starting difficulties.
  • 2014 (NHTSA): Recall for an issue with the rear driveshaft mounting bolts, which could lead to power loss or abnormal noise.

*It is crucial to check with a BMW dealership whether all recalls concerning the targeted vehicle have been carried out.*

Verdict

The BMW X4 F26 is an attractive choice for those seeking a compact SUV with a strong personality, assertive design, and dynamic road handling. Its reliability is generally good, especially with the 6-cylinder engines (N55 petrol and N57 diesel), which are safe bets, known for their robustness and driving pleasure. The 4-cylinder diesel versions (B47) are also reliable, provided that the EGR recalls have been carried out and the usage is appropriate (avoid frequent short urban trips).

Caution is advised with the first versions of the N20 4-cylinder petrol engine (before mid-2015) due to the risk of timing chain issues. If you opt for an N20 from this period, ensure that the chain has been replaced or negotiate the price accordingly to cover any potential intervention.

A complete service history and a thorough pre-purchase inspection by a professional are absolutely crucial to minimize risks. If these conditions are met, the X4 F26 can be an excellent road companion, offering undeniable driving pleasure and appreciable comfort on long journeys. It is a vehicle that requires rigorous maintenance but repays it in return.
