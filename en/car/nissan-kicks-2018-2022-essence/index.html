Nissan Kicks

I (P15) · 2018 – 2022

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The **first-generation Nissan Kicks (P15)** is an urban SUV (B-segment) initially designed for emerging markets and the Americas, indirectly replacing the Juke in certain markets like North America. Based on the Nissan V platform (shared with the Versa/Micra), it stands out for its aggressive pricing, generous interior space for its size, and great mechanical simplicity. It is exclusively available in front-wheel drive (FWD). A facelift occurred in 2021, bringing a widened grille (Double V-Motion), slimmer LED headlights, and technological updates (standard Apple CarPlay/Android Auto).

*Note: The Kicks P15 was not marketed in Western Europe. A specific version (D15) based on the Dacia B0 platform was sold in India.*

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability well above average (J.D. Power IQS, Consumer Reports)
  • Maintenance and insurance costs among the lowest in the segment
  • Extremely robust 1.6L HR16DE engine (absence of turbo and complex direct injection)
  • Generous interior space and trunk volume for a vehicle of this size
  • Comprehensive standard safety equipment (Nissan Safety Shield 360) from 2020

Weaknesses 5

  • Modest performance and labored acceleration (1.6L NA)
  • Room for improvement in soundproofing: noisy "droning" effect from the CVT gearbox during hard acceleration
  • Total absence of all-wheel drive (AWD), even as an option
  • Basic interior plastic quality (hard and prone to scratching)
  • Slightly harsh suspension comfort on degraded roads

Check before buying

  • CVT gearbox maintenance history: avoid models with over 100,000 km that have never had a gearbox oil change.
  • Electric power steering (EPS) torque sensor: check if the recall has been performed (possible loss of assistance).
  • Premature wear of front suspension components (stabilizer bar links) on heavily degraded roads.
  • Rearview camera operation (black or frozen screen, subject to a recall).

Alternatives

* **North America:** Hyundai Venue (equivalent reliability, similar size), Kia Soul, Chevrolet Trax, Honda HR-V (more expensive but more spacious). * **South America / Emerging markets:** Volkswagen T-Cross, Hyundai Creta, Chevrolet Tracker. * **Asia:** Toyota Yaris Cross, Honda WR-V.

Verdict

The first-generation Nissan Kicks (P15) is a **highly recommended** choice for buyers looking for an economical, practical, and hassle-free small SUV. While it does not shine in terms of performance or soundproofing, it more than compensates with exemplary mechanical reliability. The naturally aspirated 1.6L engine, coupled with a CVT gearbox that is lightly stressed by the low torque, forms a durable duo provided transmission oil changes are respected. It regularly ranks at the top of reliability surveys (J.D. Power, Consumer Reports) against its direct competitors like the Hyundai Venue or the Kia Soul.
