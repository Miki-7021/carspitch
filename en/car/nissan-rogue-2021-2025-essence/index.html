Summary

The third-generation Nissan Rogue (T33), marketed as the X-Trail in Europe and Asia, marks a significant move upmarket compared to the previous generation (T32). It stands out with a more spacious cabin, a marked improvement in finish, and the successful integration of the ProPilot Assist semi-autonomous driving system. While the CVT transmission has been revised to correct the severe overheating problems of the past, this generation undergoes a major mechanical transition: the abandonment of the robust but aging naturally aspirated 2.5L 4-cylinder (only offered in 2021 in North America) in favor of an all-new 1.5L VC-Turbo (variable compression) 3-cylinder from 2022 onwards. Although praised by J.D. Power for its initial quality, this new engine is accumulating heavy recalls (NHTSA) concerning its internal parts and oil degradation, prompting the utmost caution.

Strengths 5

  • Generous and very well-thought-out interior space (rear doors opening to nearly 90 degrees)
  • Highly effective and well-calibrated ProPilot Assist semi-autonomous driving system
  • Finish quality and soundproofing significantly improved compared to the T32 generation
  • Very reliable and proven 2.5L engine (2021)
  • Driving experience of the e-POWER version (in markets where available)

Weaknesses 4

  • Very concerning reliability of the new 1.5L VC-Turbo engine (recalls for internal breakage and oil degradation)
  • Infotainment system prone to bugs and continuous reboots
  • Exceptionally high number of safety recalls (brakes, seat belts, seats, fuel) on the first model years
  • CVT gearbox which, although improved, smooths out driving sensations and requires rigorous maintenance

Check before buying

  • Complete history of NHTSA recalls (crucial on this model given their number and severity)
  • Engine oil condition and level on the 1.5L VC-Turbo (risk of rapid thermal degradation)
  • Absence of brake fluid leaks at the rear calipers (potentially missing internal bushing, 2021/2023 recall)
  • Smooth operation of the central screen and rearview camera when shifting into reverse
  • Correct mounting of the power seats and operation of the rear seat belts

Official recalls

The Rogue T33 has been the subject of numerous recalls in North America (NHTSA). *Note: French recalls concerning the NV250/NV300 commercial vehicles do not apply to this model.*

  • 1.5L VC-Turbo Engine (Internal Parts / Oil): Major recalls indicating that high temperatures can degrade engine oil, and failures of internal mechanical parts can occur (Affects 2021-2025 models equipped with the 1.5L and 2.0L VC-Turbo).
  • 1.5L VC-Turbo Engine (Throttle Body): The gears of the electronic throttle body can break (2024-2025 models).
  • Braking (NHTSA 23V791000 / 21V286000): Potential absence of an internal bushing on the rear brake calipers, allowing the O-ring to move and brake fluid to leak (2021 models).
  • Electronics / Camera (NHTSA 24V748000 / 22V772000 / 22V527000): Rearview camera screen going black or infotainment system continuously rebooting, notably linked to the SiriusXM subscription.
  • Fuel (NHTSA 21V957000 / 22V660000 / 22V259000): Abnormal wear of the fuel pump that can lead to overheating; fuel tank walls too thin or punctured during modifications.
  • Passive Safety (Seats and Seat Belts): Multiple recalls concerning defective seat belt pretensioners (2023), improperly tightened anchors (2023), faulty driver seat frame welds (2023), and rear seat belts failing to retract (2021-2022).

Verdict

The Nissan Rogue (T33) blows hot and cold. On paper and in use, it is an excellent family SUV: spacious, comfortable, technologically up-to-date, and equipped with an excellent ProPilot system. J.D. Power even ranks it above average for its initial quality. However, long-term reliability is heavily compromised by the new 1.5L VC-Turbo engine (2022+). The complexity of variable compression causes serious mechanical problems (oil degradation, internal breakage) confirmed by major NHTSA recalls.

Buying advice: Prioritize looking for a 2021 model equipped with the naturally aspirated 2.5L engine, which offers proven mechanical reliability. If you opt for a 2022 or newer model (1.5L VC-Turbo), an extended manufacturer warranty is absolutely essential, as is strict verification that all safety recalls (brakes, seats, engine) have been performed.
