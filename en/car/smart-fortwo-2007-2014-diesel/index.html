Summary

The second-generation Smart Fortwo (code name W451) succeeds the W450, growing by 19 cm to improve safety (crash tests) and comfort. It abandons Mercedes-Benz petrol engines for 1.0L 3-cylinder blocks of Mitsubishi origin (3B2 family), but retains a Mercedes-Benz diesel engine (OM660), which is the focus of this report. This 0.8 cdi block holds the title of the smallest mass-produced diesel engine in the world. While the Tridion safety cell remains a benchmark, the overall reliability of the 451 is marred by poor statistics: ADAC notes a breakdown rate of 4.1/1000 vehicles/year (below average), the TÜV Report points to a 3-year defect rate of 21.5% (among the worst in the segment), and *What Car?* gives it a reliability rating of 2/5 (35% breakdown rate over 12 months). The automated gearbox (Softouch/Softip) and electronics are the main culprits.

Practicality

  • Unrivaled Urban Agility — With its record-breaking compactness of just 2.69 meters and an exceptional turning radius, the Smart Fortwo II diesel is the undisputed queen of the city, simplifying parking and the most complex maneuvers.

Economy

  • Record Fuel Economy and Low Running Costs — The 0.8 cdi engine offers incredibly low fuel consumption, achieving just 3.3L/100km, combined with generally low insurance costs, ensuring significant daily savings.

Safety

  • Enhanced Passive Safety — Equipped with the Tridion safety cell, a benchmark for its size, the Smart Fortwo II achieved 4 stars in Euro NCAP tests, offering remarkable protection for its occupants.

Braking

  • Effective and Stable Braking — The Smart Fortwo II's braking system is renowned for its effectiveness and stability, especially in urban environments, with ABS and ESP as standard for optimal control in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Agile and Secure Handling — Its short wheelbase gives it exceptional agility in the city. The standard ESP (Electronic Stability Program) ensures crucial stability and precise control, even in the most dynamic situations.

Reliability

  • Robust Diesel Engine Ideal for Peri-urban Use — The 0.8 cdi engine is a robust and ultra-frugal unit, perfectly suited for daily peri-urban commutes, offering appreciable mechanical longevity for regular use on ring roads and expressways.

Comfort

  • Surprising Interior Space for Two — Despite its compact exterior dimensions, the Smart Fortwo II surprises with the generous space it offers inside for two adults, ensuring unexpected comfort for daily journeys.
