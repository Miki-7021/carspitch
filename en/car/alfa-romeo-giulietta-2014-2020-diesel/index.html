Alfa Romeo Giulietta

I phase 2 (940) · 2014 – 2020

NEGOTIATE Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The Alfa Romeo Giulietta (Type 940) received its first major facelift in late 2013 (marketed in 2014), marking the transition to Phase 2. This facelift brought notable improvements: a new grille, redesigned seats offering better support, revised soundproofing, and the replacement of the aging Blue&Me system with the Uconnect touchscreen interface. A second minor facelift occurred in 2016 (new logo, 'Veloce' designation replacing 'Quadrifoglio Verde').

In terms of reliability, Phase 2 corrects most of the early defects of Phase 1 (interior noises, running gear). The diesel engines (JTDM), which are the focus of this analysis, are among the most robust units on the European market, provided that maintenance is scrupulously respected and short urban trips, which clog the emission control systems, are avoided.

Strengths 5

  • Remarkable reliability of JTDM diesel engines (among the best on the market)
  • Sharp road handling and precise steering
  • Timeless design that ages very well
  • Clear improvement in finish and soundproofing on this Phase 2
  • Uconnect multimedia system much more intuitive than the old Blue&Me

Weaknesses 5

  • Rear passenger space and boot volume lagging behind competitors (Golf, 308)
  • Fragile rear tailgate wiring harness (chronic issue)
  • TCT dual-clutch gearbox sometimes rough at low speeds
  • Large turning radius, penalizing in urban areas
  • Ergonomics of some controls and limited interior storage

Check before buying

  • **Tailgate wiring harness:** Check the operation of the rear wiper, defrost, and reverse lights. Wires break in the rubber sheath from repeatedly opening/closing the boot.
  • **TCT gearbox:** Test the vehicle when cold and hot. Gear changes should be smooth. Jerks may indicate a worn actuator or a need for recalibration.
  • **Service history (Diesel):** Check that the vehicle has not only been driven in the city. A clogged DPF or a fouled EGR valve leads to power loss and engine warning lights.
  • **Water pump (1.6 JTDM):** Inspect for traces of coolant on the timing belt side.
  • **MultiAir Module (Petrol):** Demand invoices proving the use of the correct engine oil specification.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Peugeot 308 II (better chassis, but BlueHDi diesels affected by AdBlue issues), Volkswagen Golf VII (segment benchmark, reliable but more expensive TDIs), Audi A3 8V (superior finish), BMW 1 Series F20 (rear-wheel drive, sporty handling), Seat Leon III. - **Asia/Oceania:** Mazda 3 (excellent reliability, but rare/problematic diesels), Honda Civic.

Verdict

The Alfa Romeo Giulietta Phase 2 is a highly recommendable choice with a diesel (JTDM) engine. Contrary to persistent clichés about the brand, the 1.6 and 2.0 JTDM engines are benchmarks for longevity and reliability, often surpassing their French or German equivalents of the same era. Phase 2 corrected most of the finish defects of the first version. For a worry-free purchase, prioritize a model with a clear service history, imperatively check the rear tailgate wiring harness, and ensure the vehicle has been driven enough to preserve its DPF. The manual gearbox is the choice for absolute peace of mind, although the TCT is pleasant if it has been well maintained.
