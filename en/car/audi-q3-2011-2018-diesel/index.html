Audi Q3

8U (2011–2018) · 2011 – 2018

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation Audi Q3 (Type 8U) marked the entry of the four-ring brand into the highly sought-after premium compact SUV segment. Based on the proven PQ35 platform (shared with the Volkswagen Tiguan I), it stands out for its excellent interior finish quality, safe road handling, and a wide range of engines. A facelift occurred in late 2014 (model year 2015), bringing aesthetic touches (Singleframe grille connected to the headlights), new standard xenon/LED headlights, and above all, a major transition under the hood: the switch to Euro 6 standards with the abandonment of EA189 diesel engines in favor of EA288, and the correction of early issues with TFSI petrol engines. Although its rear passenger space and boot volume are average for the category, it remains a safe bet on the used market, provided the engine is carefully chosen and the service history is checked (especially for S tronic gearboxes and the Quattro system).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional interior finish quality and material aging
  • Very refined sound insulation, worthy of the segment above
  • Reliability of EA288 diesel engines (post-2015) and 1.4 TFSI petrol engines
  • Efficiency of Quattro transmission and S tronic gearboxes (if well-maintained)
  • Ideal compactness for urban environments while maintaining a high driving position

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of early 2.0 TFSI (oil consumption, timing chain)
  • Consequences of the Dieselgate update on 2.0 TDI EA189 (EGR, injectors)
  • Limited rear passenger space and boot volume (460L) for a family
  • High maintenance cost in the Audi network (especially S tronic/Haldex services)
  • Infotainment system (MMI) that has aged poorly technologically

Check before buying

  • Clear history of S tronic gearbox oil changes (every 60,000 km) and Haldex differential.
  • On 2.0 TDI EA189: ask if the emissions update has been performed and check for the absence of an engine warning light (EGR/DPF).
  • On 2.0 TFSI models before 2014: demand proof of oil consumption repair or chain tensioner replacement.
  • Operation of the retractable MMI screen (the gear mechanism can jam or make noise).
  • Condition of the air conditioning compressor (seizure cases reported around 100,000 km).

Verdict

The Audi Q3 (8U) is a premium compact SUV that ages remarkably well in terms of aesthetics and quality. However, its reliability record is split into two. First-phase models (2011-2014) are plagued by chain and oil consumption issues on the 2.0 TFSI, and by the aftermath of Dieselgate on the 2.0 TDI EA189. **It is imperative to prioritize facelifted versions (from late 2014 / model year 2015 onwards)**. The 2.0 TDI EA288 (150 hp) is an excellent choice for high-mileage drivers, while the 1.4 TFSI 150 hp will delight urban dwellers. The purchase of a used Q3 must be conditional on the presentation of a rigorous service history, particularly for automatic gearboxes and all-wheel drive.
