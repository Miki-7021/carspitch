Summary

The second-generation Hyundai i10, known by the internal code IA, was produced from 2013 to 2019. It marked a significant evolution compared to its predecessor, establishing itself as a mature and well-equipped city car. Primarily designed for the European market and manufactured in Turkey, it stood out with a more modern design, a spacious interior, and a noticeable increase in perceived quality. A facelift in late 2016 brought minor aesthetic changes, infotainment improvements, and new safety options. Overall, the i10 IA is recognized for its excellent value for money, surprising spaciousness for the segment, and very solid mechanical reliability, especially for its gasoline engines.

Strengths 6

  • General reliability of gasoline engines
  • Surprising interior space and trunk volume for the category
  • Appreciable suspension comfort for a city car
  • Generous equipment from mid-range trims
  • Excellent maneuverability in the city
  • Reasonable maintenance and insurance costs

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway, especially before the facelift
  • 4-speed automatic gearbox a bit slow and less efficient
  • Limited performance with the 1.0 MPI on rural roads or highways
  • Some hard interior plastics
  • Sensitivity to crosswinds at high speed

Check before buying

  • Service history: Essential, especially for diesel versions.
  • Engine noises: Listen carefully when cold and hot (ticking, chain noises, knocking).
  • Clutch and gearbox operation: Check for smooth gear changes and absence of slipping.
  • Onboard electronics: Test the multimedia screen, power windows, air conditioning, steering wheel controls.
  • Suspension: Test the vehicle on degraded roads to detect abnormal noises or worn damping.
  • Braking: Check disc and pad wear, as well as braking effectiveness.
  • Bodywork: Inspect for absence of corrosion (rare) or poorly executed body repairs.

Official recalls

  • 2014 (Europe): Potential issue with the brake vacuum hose that could lead to a loss of braking assistance. Concerns vehicles produced between April 1st and October 1st, 2014.
  • 2014 (Europe): Risk of loosening of the steering column fixing nut, which could affect steering precision. Concerns vehicles produced between April 1st and October 1st, 2014.
  • 2016 (Europe): Software update for the engine control unit (ECU) to optimize operation and reduce emissions. Concerns a wide range of vehicles, including the i10.

Verdict

The second-generation Hyundai i10 (IA) is an excellent choice in the city car segment. It offers a balanced package of spaciousness, equipment, and driving pleasure. The gasoline engines, particularly the 1.2 MPI, are models of reliability and fuel efficiency, ideal for daily use, whether urban or suburban. The 1.0 MPI is perfect for purely city driving and tight budgets. The 1.1 CRDi diesel, although very economical, should be reserved for those who primarily drive on highways to avoid issues related to clogging and the DPF. With a clear service history and a check of minor weak points, an i10 IA represents a wise and largely trouble-free purchase for many years.
