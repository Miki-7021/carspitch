Subaru WRX

V (VB) · 2022 – 2025

BUY Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The fifth-generation Subaru WRX (code name VB) marks a major turning point for the model. Now resting on the Subaru Global Platform (SGP), it gains considerably in torsional rigidity and refinement. Under the hood, it abandons the 2.0L (FA20) of the previous generation in favor of the 2.4L turbo block (FA24F), offering a much fuller torque curve at low revs. Notable fact: Subaru officially canceled the development of an STI version for this generation, making this WRX the sole sporty representative of the lineup. It is offered with an excellent 6-speed manual gearbox or a reworked CVT automatic transmission, dubbed 'Subaru Performance Transmission' (SPT). Although its design with its raw plastic wheel arches has been divisive, it remains an undisputed benchmark for its all-wheel drive traction (Symmetrical AWD) in all weather conditions. Overall rating: A highly capable niche vehicle, requiring rigorous maintenance.

Strengths 5

  • Imperial traction thanks to the Symmetrical AWD system
  • Extremely rigid SGP chassis and secure road handling
  • FA24F engine more flexible and torquey at low revs than the old FA20
  • Precise and very reliable 6-speed manual gearbox
  • Excellent peripheral visibility (typical Subaru)

Weaknesses 5

  • CVT transmission (SPT) prone to jerks and less suited to very sporty use
  • High fuel consumption (average of 10-11 L/100 km)
  • Absence of an STI version for this generation
  • Infotainment system (large vertical screen) sometimes slow to start
  • Divisive aesthetics (prominent black plastic cladding)

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: Oil changes must be done every 8,000 km or 6 months, especially in sporty use.
  • CVT gearbox behavior (if equipped): Test the vehicle cold and hot to detect abnormal jolts during simulated gear changes.
  • Front driveshaft: Check if the November 2023 recall has been performed (risk of cracking).
  • Underbody: Check for the absence of impacts or premature corrosion if the vehicle has been used on snow/salt or dirt roads.

Alternatives

- **North America / Asia:** Honda Civic Si (FWD, less powerful but exceptional manual gearbox), Hyundai Elantra N (very high-performance FWD, sharp chassis), Toyota GR Corolla (AWD, more expensive, very pronounced rally spirit), Volkswagen Golf GTI / R. - **Europe:** Since the WRX is not imported, direct alternatives are the Toyota GR Yaris, the Volkswagen Golf R, or the Audi S3 (all with all-wheel drive).

Verdict

The VB-generation Subaru WRX is a mature and technically accomplished evolution of the famous rally sedan. The transition to the 2.4L engine (FA24F) and the SGP platform has corrected many flaws of the previous generation (low-end lag, improvable rigidity). In terms of reliability, the engine block proves to be very robust if, and only if, maintenance is scrupulous (frequent oil changes, respecting warm-up times).

**Buying advice:** Absolutely favor the **manual gearbox (6-speed manual)** versions. They are not only more reliable in the long term, but they perfectly match the car's DNA. The CVT automatic transmission (SPT), although improved, suffers from reports of jerks and restricts the vehicle's sporty potential. It is a highly recommended passion purchase for all-weather driving enthusiasts, provided you accept a substantial fuel budget.
