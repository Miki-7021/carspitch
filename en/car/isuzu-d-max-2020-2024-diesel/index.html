Isuzu D-Max

III (TFR) · 2020 – 2024

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The third-generation Isuzu D-Max (codename TFR for 4x2 versions and TFS for 4x4s) marks a major turning point for the Japanese manufacturer. Launched in late 2019 and arriving in most markets in 2020, it abandons its purely rustic pickup image to align with modern standards of comfort, technology, and safety (scoring 5 stars in the Euro NCAP crash test). It also shares its platform and engines with the third-generation Mazda BT-50. Although it has become more refined inside with the integration of driving aids (ADAS) and touchscreens, it retains its pure, hard-working utility DNA: a reinforced ladder chassis, a towing capacity of 3.5 tonnes, and a payload exceeding one tonne. It is a vehicle designed by a truck manufacturer, which is evident in its overall robustness.

Strengths 6

  • Structural robustness (chassis, suspension, transmission)
  • Reliability of the engine blocks (design derived from heavy goods vehicles)
  • Off-road capabilities (rear differential lock as standard on many trims)
  • Active and passive safety (5 Euro NCAP stars, rare for a utility pickup)
  • Towing capacity of 3500 kg
  • Reasonable maintenance cost compared to the competition

Weaknesses 5

  • 1.9 RZ4E-TC engine a bit noisy and rough under load
  • Rear suspension (leaf springs) very firm when unladen
  • Driving aids (ADAS) sometimes too intrusive (abrupt lane keeping)
  • Infotainment system slow and prone to some bugs (Apple CarPlay/Android Auto disconnecting)
  • Absence of the 3.0L engine on the European market

Check before buying

  • **Emissions control system (1.9L):** Check the DPF regeneration history and the absence of an engine light related to the EGR valve.
  • **Underbody:** Inspect the chassis, sumps, and axles to detect impacts related to intensive off-road use.
  • **Rear leaf springs:** Check for cracks or breakage (see official recalls).
  • **Steering column:** Ensure that the recall concerning the connection bolt has been carried out.
  • **On-board electronics:** Test all driving aids (stereo cameras) and the central screen.

Practicality

  • First-Class Towing and Payload Capacities — With an impressive towing capacity of 3500 kg and a payload exceeding one tonne, the D-Max is the ideal tool for all your professional and intensive leisure needs.

Safety

  • Maximum Safety with 5-Star Euro NCAP Rating — The D-Max offers state-of-the-art protection for its occupants, having achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP crash tests, a rare feat for a utility pickup.
  • Powerful and Controlled Braking System — Benefit from a braking system deemed good and progressive, offering adequate and improved power, ensuring enhanced safety in all conditions.

Comfort

  • Significantly Improved Road Holding and Driving Comfort — The D-Max's road holding has been optimized, offering precise steering, good body control, and remarkable on-road stability, providing appreciable comfort for its category.

Economy

  • Controlled Maintenance Costs for Peace of Mind — The D-Max stands out with reasonable maintenance costs compared to its competitors, contributing to economical vehicle management in the long term.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Toyota Hilux (the absolute benchmark in reliability), Ford Ranger (more comfortable and technological), Volkswagen Amarok (twin of the Ranger). - **Asia / Oceania:** Mazda BT-50 (technical clone of the D-Max, same factory, same engines), Mitsubishi Triton / L200, Nissan Navara. - **Emerging markets:** Toyota Hilux, Mitsubishi L200.

Verdict

The third-generation Isuzu D-Max is a pragmatic and extremely rational choice. Designed by an industrial vehicle specialist, it shines with its mechanical reliability and foolproof robustness. While the European market has to settle for the 1.9L (very valiant but a bit lacking compared to competing V6s), markets benefiting from the 3.0L enjoy one of the best pickups in the world. It is a vehicle to buy with your eyes closed for professional, agricultural, or intensive leisure use (raids, towing). For purely urban or peri-urban use when unladen, its firm comfort and the sensitivity of its DPF make it less relevant than a traditional SUV.
