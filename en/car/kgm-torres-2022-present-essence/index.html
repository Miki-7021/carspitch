Summary

The KGM Torres (Generation I, codename J100) is a family SUV that marks a historic turning point: it is the first model launched under the new KGM (KG Mobility) identity, succeeding the historic SsangYong brand. Positioned between the Korando and the Rexton, it stands out with a very assertive, cubic, and adventurous design, inspired by the Torres del Paine National Park in Chile. Beneath this robust bodywork lies a modernized platform, offering giant interior space and exceptional trunk volume (over 700 liters). While it skips diesel, it offers a proven gasoline engine and a 100% electric variant (Torres EVX) equipped with LFP battery technology supplied by BYD. It is a pragmatic choice for families looking for space and a distinctive look, although the rebuilding distribution network and steep depreciation must be taken into account.

Overall design rating: 7.5/10

Strengths 6

  • Distinctive, robust, and original exterior design
  • Gigantic trunk volume (703 liters in 5-seat configuration)
  • Excellent rear roominess (legroom and headroom)
  • Very reliable and smooth 6-speed Aisin automatic gearbox
  • Reassuring manufacturer warranty (often 5 to 7 years depending on markets, 10 years on the EVX battery)
  • LFP battery technology (BYD) on the electric version, a guarantee of longevity

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high fuel consumption of the gasoline version (unfavorable aerodynamics and weight)
  • All-touch ergonomics (climate control via a lower screen) not very intuitive while driving
  • Intrusive and sometimes abrupt driving aids (ADAS) (lane keeping assist)
  • KGM dealer network still sparse in some European countries
  • Steep depreciation to be expected on the used market

Check before buying

  • Software update history (multimedia system and driving aids)
  • Condition of interior plastics (prone to scratches in the trunk and lower door panels)
  • Smooth operation of the lower touch screen (climate control management)
  • On the AWD version: condition of the underbody if the vehicle has been off-road
  • Front tire wear, particularly on the EVX electric version

Official recalls

  • 2023 (South Korea / Europe) - ADAS Software: Dealership update to recalibrate the autonomous emergency braking (AEB) system and lane keeping assist, deemed too sensitive.
  • 2024 (Europe) - Infotainment: Software update campaign to resolve black screen issues at startup and rearview camera crashes.

*Note: As the vehicle is very recent, no major mechanical recall (engine/gearbox) has been recorded to date by Rapex or Rappel Conso.*

What sets it apart 6

Design

  • Robust and Distinctive Design — The KGM Torres stands out with its bold and original exterior style, inspired by vast landscapes, offering a unique and assertive presence on the road.

Practicality

  • Giant Space and Boot Volume — With a boot volume of 703 liters in 5-seater configuration and excellent rear passenger space, the Torres offers unparalleled room for passengers and their luggage.

Reliability

  • Proven Mechanical Reliability and Aisin Gearbox — Benefit from the robustness of the 1.5 T-GDi gasoline engine and the smoothness of the 6-speed Aisin automatic gearbox, a proven combination for peace of mind.
  • Reassuring Manufacturer Warranty — Enjoy enhanced peace of mind thanks to the extended manufacturer warranty (often 5 to 7 years depending on markets), a testament to KGM's confidence in its vehicle's durability.

Safety

  • 5-Star Euro NCAP Safety — The KGM Torres achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 88% for adult protection and 85% for child protection, ensuring optimal safety.

Comfort

  • Driving Comfort and Safe Handling — The Torres offers safe and predictable road behavior, prioritizing comfort thanks to a suspension that effectively absorbs road irregularities, for serene journeys.

Verdict

The KGM Torres is an excellent surprise for those looking for a family SUV with the look of a real 4x4 without sacrificing interior space. Mechanically, the 1.5 gasoline engine coupled with the Aisin gearbox is a robust and trouble-free combination, inherited from SsangYong's years of experience. However, its brick-like aerodynamics and weight are paid for dearly at the pump: fuel consumption is its biggest flaw. If you drive a lot, the 100% electric version (EVX) with its BYD battery is paradoxically the most rational and technologically advanced choice. As a recent used car, it is a vehicle to negotiate firmly due to the brand image (transition from SsangYong to KGM) which still frightens some buyers, leading to steep depreciation.
