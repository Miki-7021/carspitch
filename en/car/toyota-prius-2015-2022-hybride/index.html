Toyota Prius

IV (XW50) · 2015 – 2022

BUY Reliability 95/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The fourth-generation Toyota Prius (XW50) marks a major turning point for the model. Inaugurating the modular TNGA (Toyota New Global Architecture) platform, it offers transformed road handling, a lowered center of gravity, and increased rigidity compared to the XW30. Its divisive design at launch was softened during the 2019 facelift. Under the hood, the 4th generation HSD hybrid system optimizes the thermal efficiency of the 1.8L engine (up to 40%), offering impressive real-world fuel consumption (often below 4.5 L/100 km). It is an absolute benchmark in automotive reliability, highly praised by taxi drivers and high-mileage users.

Expert's overall rating: A driving machine, economical and indestructible if basic maintenance is respected.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical and electronic reliability
  • Extremely low fuel consumption (often < 4.5L/100km)
  • Dynamic road handling and significantly improved comfort (TNGA chassis)
  • Very low maintenance cost (no clutch, no timing belt, minimal brake wear)
  • Hybrid system warranty extendable up to 10 years (via annual hybrid health checks)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very divisive exterior design (especially before the 2019 facelift)
  • Very low ground clearance (watch out for speed bumps and curbs)
  • Dated, slow infotainment system with an unintuitive interface
  • Highway sound insulation (road and wind noise)
  • Reduced trunk volume on the plug-in hybrid version (PHEV)

Check before buying

  • Toyota service history: essential to benefit from the extended hybrid battery warranty (Hybrid Health Check).
  • Condition of the underbody and bumpers: as the car is very low, scrapes are frequent.
  • 12V (auxiliary) battery: it discharges quickly if the vehicle remains stationary for several weeks. A replacement costs approximately €150-200.
  • High-voltage battery cooling filter: check that it is not obstructed by dust or pet hair.
  • Road noise: check for feathered wear on the rear tires, a minor but common geometry defect.

Official recalls

  • September 2018 (Electrical harness): Risk of short circuit and fire due to friction of the hybrid engine wiring harness against a cover. Concerns models produced between June 2015 and May 2018. Installation of protective tape or replacement of the harness.
  • June 2020 (Seat belt): The seat belt reminder chime may not activate correctly. Software update.
  • October 2021 (Hybrid system): A software bug may prevent the hybrid system from entering fail-safe mode in the event of an anomaly, leading to a sudden loss of power. ECU reprogramming.

What sets it apart 7

reliability

  • Legendary Reliability — The Toyota Prius IV is globally recognized as one of the most reliable vehicles ever produced, praised by professionals for its exceptional mechanical and electronic robustness.

economy

  • Exceptional Savings — Enjoy extremely low fuel consumption, often below 4.5 L/100 km, and reduced maintenance costs thanks to the absence of a clutch and timing belt. The hybrid system warranty is extendable up to 10 years.

comfort

  • Dynamic and Comfortable Driving — Thanks to its innovative TNGA platform, the Prius IV offers a transformed road behavior, increased agility, reduced body roll, and precise steering, ensuring a dynamic yet comfortable driving experience.

safety

  • Certified Maximum Safety — The Toyota Prius IV achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 92% for adult protection and 82% for child protection, and was distinguished by the IIHS as a Top Safety Pick+.
  • Efficient and Controlled Braking — The Prius IV's braking system offers effective performance with competitive stopping distances for its category, ensuring optimal safety in all driving situations.
  • Precise and Stable Road Holding — The TNGA platform gives the Prius IV significantly improved road holding, offering exemplary stability, increased agility, and precise steering for predictable and safe driving.

Performance

  • Advanced Hybrid Technology — Benefit from the 4th generation HSD hybrid technology, which optimizes the thermal efficiency of the 1.8L engine up to 40%, providing cutting-edge energy efficiency and a smooth drive.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Hyundai Ioniq Hybrid (excellent alternative, more conventional dual-clutch gearbox), Toyota Corolla Hybrid (same mechanics, more consensual design), Kia Niro HEV.
  • North America: Honda Insight, Hyundai Elantra Hybrid, Toyota Corolla Hybrid.
  • Japan / Asia: Nissan Note e-Power, Honda Fit e:HEV.

Verdict

The Toyota Prius IV (XW50) is one of the most reliable vehicles ever produced. If you can overlook its atypical design and aging multimedia system, it is an absolutely rational purchase. Breakdowns are extremely rare, fuel consumption is negligible, and depreciation is very low. Prioritize a model with a 100% Toyota service history to ensure the high-voltage battery is guaranteed for up to 10 years. The 2019 facelift is the optimal choice for a more subdued look and the integration of Apple CarPlay / Android Auto (depending on trim levels).
