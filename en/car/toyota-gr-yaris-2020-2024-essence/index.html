Toyota GR Yaris

I · 2020 – 2024

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The Toyota GR Yaris (Generation I) is a true anomaly in the modern automotive market: a 'homologation special' designed by Gazoo Racing to serve as the basis for Toyota's WRC rally car. Unlike the standard Yaris, it sits on a hybrid platform (GA-B front, GA-C rear), features a specific 3-door body with a forged carbon roof and aluminum doors and lids. Equipped with the world's most powerful 3-cylinder engine at its release and a sophisticated AWD system (GR-Four), it offers supercar performance on winding roads. Its overall reliability is exceptional for a sports car of this caliber, although it requires rigorous maintenance, particularly when used on the track.

Strengths 5

  • Oversized and ultra-robust mechanical design
  • Dual injection (D-4S direct and indirect) preventing valve carbon buildup
  • Exceptional dynamic behavior and adjustable GR-Four transmission
  • Almost non-existent depreciation (collector's vehicle)
  • Build quality from the Motomachi plant (Japan)

Weaknesses 5

  • High maintenance cost (frequent transmission fluid changes)
  • Overheating of the AWD system (ITCC) during intense track sessions
  • Driving position considered too high by some body types
  • Forward visibility hindered by the rearview mirror/camera block
  • Cabin noise (minimal soundproofing to save weight)

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: axle/transfer case oil changes must be scrupulously respected.
  • Brake condition (two-piece rotors with aluminum bells on Circuit Pack): very expensive to replace.
  • Underbody: check for impact damage (track excursions, amateur rally).
  • Gearbox: test shifting into 2nd and 3rd gear at high RPM (synchros).
  • Alignment: very sensitive to poor settings, watch for asymmetrical tire wear.

Safety

  • Advanced Passive and Active Safety — The GR Yaris achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 86% for adult protection and 81% for child protection, ensuring optimal safety for all occupants.

Braking

  • Supercar-level Braking — Equipped with oversized ventilated discs (356 mm front, 297 mm rear) and multi-piston calipers, the GR Yaris offers exceptionally short stopping distances, often around 32-34 meters from 100 km/h, with remarkable endurance and pedal feel.

tenue de route

  • Exceptional Dynamic Handling — Its balanced chassis, precise steering, and configurable GR-Four all-wheel drive give the GR Yaris unparalleled agility and grip, offering absolute confidence and unmatched driving pleasure on all roads.

Performance

  • World's Most Powerful and Ultra-Reliable 3-Cylinder Engine — The 1.6 Turbo G16E-GTS engine, designed with oversized robustness and dual injection, delivers explosive performance while ensuring exceptional reliability, even under high stress.
  • Uncompromising WRC-Inspired Design — Featuring a unique hybrid platform, a specific 3-door body with a forged carbon roof and aluminum openings, the GR Yaris is an engineering marvel directly stemming from Toyota Gazoo Racing's rally expertise.

Economy

  • A Future Classic with Virtually No Depreciation — A true "homologation special" assembled with impeccable quality at the Motomachi plant, the GR Yaris is a collector's vehicle whose value holds remarkably well, making it an excellent passion investment.

Alternatives

MarketCompeting alternatives
**Europe**Hyundai i20 N (FWD, less powerful but very fun), Honda Civic Type R (higher segment, FWD), Ford Fiesta ST (FWD), Volkswagen Golf R (AWD, heavier and more bourgeois).
**Japan / Asia**Subaru WRX STI (used market), Honda Civic Type R, Nissan Z.
**North America**The GR Yaris is not sold there. Direct alternative: Toyota GR Corolla (which uses the same G16E-GTS engine pushed to 300 hp).

Verdict

The Toyota GR Yaris is a future classic and one of the most reliable sports cars of its generation. Designed without compromise, its G16E-GTS engine withstands enormous stress without flinching, provided that maintenance is followed to the letter. If you are buying a used model, favor an example with the 'Circuit Pack' (Torsen differentials) and demand a crystal-clear service book, proving that the transmission fluids have been replaced regularly. It is a highly recommended passion purchase, coupled with a safe financial investment.
