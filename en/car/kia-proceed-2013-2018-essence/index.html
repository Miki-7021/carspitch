Kia ProCeed

II (JD) · 2013 – 2018

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The **second-generation Kia pro_cee'd (code JD)** is the 3-door coupé variant of the Cee'd compact car. Designed in Germany and assembled in Slovakia, it targets almost exclusively the European market. It stands out with a lowered roofline, a dynamic profile, and a specific rear end, while retaining the wheelbase of the 5-door hatchback. This generation marks a major qualitative turning point for Kia, with significantly improved interior materials and the introduction of the sporty **GT (204 hp)** version, the brand's first true 'GTI'. A facelift occurred in late 2015, bringing slight aesthetic changes, the introduction of the 1.0 T-GDI 3-cylinder engine, and the replacement of the torque converter automatic gearbox with a DCT7 dual-clutch transmission on certain engines. Overall, it is a very reliable model, whose 7-year manufacturer's warranty mandated rigorous servicing within the official network for the majority of units in circulation.

Strengths 6

  • Very successful exterior design, true coupé profile
  • Excellent overall reliability, especially for naturally aspirated petrol engines
  • 7-year manufacturer's warranty (which mandated good network follow-up)
  • GT version (204 hp) very well-rounded, communicative chassis and excellent Recaro seats
  • Improved interior ergonomics and finish quality compared to the previous generation
  • Decent rear passenger space for a 3-door coupé

Weaknesses 6

  • Catastrophic rear and rear three-quarter visibility (reversing camera essential)
  • DCT7 dual-clutch gearbox (post-2015) sometimes harsh at low speeds and prone to dry clutch wear
  • 1.6 GDI 135 hp engine lacking character and linear
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (road noise)
  • Firm suspension, particularly on the GT version (18-inch wheels)
  • High boot loading sill

Check before buying

  • LED daytime running lights (DRL): Very frequent flickering or complete extinction on this generation (optic replacement often necessary).
  • Steering column: Clunking or play when turning the steering wheel at a standstill (wear of the elastic coupling / star-shaped flex disc, inexpensive part but labor-intensive).
  • DCT7 gearbox: Check for absence of juddering during hill starts or at very low speeds (sign of worn clutches).
  • Touchscreen / GPS: Slowness, touch bugs, or screen delamination on pre-2015 models.
  • Service history: Crucial. The 7-year warranty was only valid if annual services were scrupulously adhered to.

Verdict

The Kia pro_cee'd (JD) is an excellent choice on the used car market for anyone looking for a compact coupé with a strong design without sacrificing reliability. The petrol engines are generally trouble-free. The **1.4 MPI** is indestructible but anemic, best reserved for city driving. The **1.0 T-GDI** is the perfect rational choice post-2015. For enthusiasts, the **pro_cee'd GT (1.6 T-GDI 204 hp)** is an unknown gem: it offers GTI performance with superior mechanical reliability compared to its European competitors of the same era, provided maintenance has been rigorous. If possible, avoid models equipped with the DCT7 gearbox if you do a lot of city driving, as the manual gearbox is much more durable.
