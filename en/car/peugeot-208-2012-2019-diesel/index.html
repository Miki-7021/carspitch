Peugeot 208

I · 2012 – 2019

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

All Peugeot models

Summary

The first-generation Peugeot 208 (code name A9) marked a major break with the 207. More compact, lighter (up to 110 kg less) and more aerodynamic, it introduced the famous “i-Cockpit” (small steering wheel, raised gauges, central touchscreen). Restyled in 2015 (LED optics, wider grille, Euro 6 compliance), it established itself as one of the best-selling city cars in Europe. While its road handling is an absolute benchmark, its overall reliability is highly contrasted depending on the engines. The diesel blocks (HDi / e-HDi) from the first years are excellent workhorses, but the transition to BlueHDi introduced costly weaknesses related to the emissions control system.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road handling (comfort/road holding compromise)
  • Remarkable fuel efficiency of diesel engines (HDi and BlueHDi)
  • Ultra-fast and vibration-free Stop&Start system (e-HDi)
  • Modern interior presentation and significantly improved finish compared to the 207
  • Contained weight promoting agility and braking

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the AdBlue system on BlueHDi engines
  • Major timing belt problems on PureTech petrol engines
  • Divisive i-Cockpit ergonomics (the steering wheel can obscure the gauges depending on driver height)
  • Slow multimedia system (SMEG), prone to bugs and technologically outdated
  • Rear passenger space and boot volume in the lower average of the category

Check before buying

  • On BlueHDi: Presence of 'Engine fault' or 'Antipollution fault' warning lights (sign of a faulty AdBlue tank, cost > 1000€).
  • On HDi/e-HDi: Exhaust gas smell in the cabin (leaking injector seals, a known issue).
  • On PureTech: Visual condition of the timing belt via the oil cap (cracks, fraying).
  • FAP history: Check if the diesel vehicle has mainly been driven in the city (risk of clogging).
  • Electronics: Proper functioning of the central touchscreen and air conditioning.

Official recalls

  • November 2020 (PureTech): Massive recall for timing belt and vacuum pump inspection (risk of loss of braking assistance).
  • October 2019 (BlueHDi): Engine control unit calibration software update to optimize NOx emissions (FAP/AdBlue).
  • May 2016: Risk of short-circuit on the starter motor wiring harness (rubbing against the EGR valve on some diesels).
  • February 2014: Replacement of front suspension wishbone fixing bolts (risk of loss of control).

What sets it apart 6

Handling

  • Exceptional Road Handling — Enjoy benchmark road handling, combining agility, precision, and an excellent balance between comfort and dynamism. The responsive steering ensures outstanding maneuverability in all conditions.

Economy

  • Remarkable Diesel Engine Fuel Economy — The HDi and e-HDi diesel engines of the 208 I are renowned for their exemplary fuel efficiency, allowing you to achieve significant savings on every journey.

Safety

  • Optimal Safety — Travel with complete peace of mind thanks to a high level of passive safety, attested by a 5-star Euro NCAP rating, with remarkable protection for both adults and children.

Braking

  • Powerful and Stable Braking — Benefit from a highly effective braking system, offering short stopping distances and impeccable stability, even during emergency braking, for total vehicle control.

Comfort

  • Modern and Comfortable Interior — Discover a cabin with a modern presentation and refined finish, offering appreciable comfort and a pleasant atmosphere for all passengers.

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability of HDi/e-HDi Engines — Opt for peace of mind with the 1.4 HDi and 1.6 e-HDi diesel engines, known for their robustness and proven reliability, making them particularly wise choices for used vehicles.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Renault Clio IV (the 1.5 dCi is generally more reliable than the BlueHDi), Volkswagen Polo V (1.4 TDI / 1.6 TDI), Ford Fiesta (1.5 TDCi), Toyota Yaris III (Hybrid, unbeatable for urban reliability).
  • South America: Chevrolet Onix, Hyundai HB20, Fiat Argo.

Verdict

The Peugeot 208 I is a city car full of dynamic qualities, but its reliability record is heavily marred by PSA's technical choices. If you are looking for a diesel: absolutely prioritize the 1.4 HDi or 1.6 e-HDi versions (before 2015). They are robust, economical, and free from the capricious AdBlue system. The 1.6 BlueHDi versions should be approached with extreme caution: only buy if the AdBlue tank has already been replaced with the latest manufacturer reference, with invoices to prove it. On the petrol side, avoid the 1.2 PureTech unless it has meticulous maintenance and a new belt. In summary: an excellent car if you choose the right engine (e-HDi) and avoid the trouble spots (uncorrected BlueHDi, PureTech).
