Summary

The second-generation Volvo V90 (based on the modular SPA platform) marks a stylistic and technological break from its predecessor, the V70. Gone is the cubic design; the V90 adopts a sleek 'shooting brake' line, at the slight expense of raw boot volume. Positioned against the German trio (A6, 5 Series, E-Class), it relies on supreme comfort, advanced active safety, and a refined Scandinavian-designed interior. Under the hood, Volvo made the radical choice to offer only 2.0-liter 4-cylinder engines (VEA architecture), available in petrol, diesel, mild-hybrid, and plug-in hybrid versions. A discreet facelift occurred in 2020, introducing the mild-hybrid (B) powertrains and, later, the Google Android Automotive-based infotainment system.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with rear air suspension)
  • Quality of finish and very soothing Scandinavian interior design
  • Seats among the best in automotive production (ergonomics, support)
  • Active and passive safety systems (very effective Pilot Assist)
  • Bowers & Wilkins audio system (option) of exceptional quality
  • Very refined sound insulation

Weaknesses 6

  • Boot volume (560L) disappointing for a large Volvo wagon (less than a Passat or E-Class)
  • 4-cylinder engines lacking acoustic refinement compared to German 6-cylinders
  • Diesel (EGR) reliability capricious on 2016-2019 model years
  • Sensus multimedia system sometimes slow to start
  • Large turning circle
  • High weight penalizing agility and city fuel consumption

Check before buying

  • Recall history: imperatively check that the EGR recall (code R29939) has been performed on diesels.
  • PowerPulse hose (D5): open the hood and check if the large black hose at the front of the engine is securely attached and not cracked.
  • Air suspension (if equipped): check for any sagging of the rear axle after prolonged parking (air spring leaks or tired compressor).
  • AWD transmission: check for any jerking and have the Haldex coupler drained (not recommended by Volvo, but advised every 60,000 km).
  • Panoramic sunroof: check the proper functioning of the sunshade and the absence of water infiltration (clogged drains).
  • PHEV: listen carefully to the rear axle in 100% electric mode (a high-pitched whistle indicates ERAD wear).

Official recalls

  • July 2019 (Intake Manifold / EGR): Risk of melting plastic intake manifold potentially leading to fire. Concerns 2.0L diesel engines (2014-2019). Cooling circuit modification and software update.
  • July 2020 (Seatbelt): The steel cable securing the front seatbelt may wear out and break. Concerns 2016-2020 models. Replaced with a fixed attachment.
  • March 2020 (AEB Emergency Braking): The automatic emergency braking system may not detect certain obstacles due to a software issue. Update required.
  • November 2020 (Wipers): The fixing nuts for the wiper arms may loosen. Tightening at the dealership.
  • October 2023 (Turn Signals): On some recent models, a software bug may prevent turn signals from functioning. OTA or dealership update.

What sets it apart 6

Safety

  • Benchmark Active and Passive Safety — The Volvo V90 sets high standards in safety, evidenced by its 5-star Euro NCAP rating and advanced driver-assistance systems like Pilot Assist, offering maximum protection for all occupants.
  • Powerful and Precise Braking — Benefit from excellent braking performance, with strong and consistent stopping power. The V90 boasts very competitive braking distances, ensuring perfect control in any situation.
  • Stable and Reassuring Road Holding — The V90's road holding is stable, secure, and predictable, prioritizing comfort and long-distance capability. Body roll is well-controlled, and steering is precise for increased confidence.

Comfort

  • Unrivaled Travel Comfort — Enjoy exceptional ride comfort, meticulous sound insulation, and benchmark ergonomic seats, making every journey an absolute relaxation experience, even over the longest distances.
  • Scandinavian Interior Design and Premium Finish — Be captivated by a cabin with an uncluttered Scandinavian design, impeccable finish quality, and noble materials, creating a soothing and luxurious atmosphere.

economie|performance

  • Modern and Efficient Diesel Engines — The V90's diesel engines, especially the optimized post-2019 and mild-hybrid versions, offer a perfect balance of controlled consumption, generous torque, and dynamic performance for serene and economical driving.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Audi A6 Avant (benchmark in finish and V6 TDI), BMW 5 Series Touring (better dynamism), Mercedes E-Class Estate (unbeatable boot volume), Jaguar XF Sportbrake (distinctive alternative).
  • North America: Audi A6 Allroad, Mercedes E-Class All-Terrain (the North American market favors raised versions like the V90 Cross Country).
  • Asia / China: Wagons are very unpopular, customers opt for the S90 sedan (often in the long-wheelbase S90L version) or SUVs (XC90).

Verdict

The Volvo V90 is a majestic wagon, built to cover miles in royal comfort. If you're targeting a diesel, caution is advised for 2016-2018 models: ensure that EGR recalls have been performed and that maintenance records are clear. The D4 engine is the ideal compromise, while the D5 offers a real plus in terms of driving pleasure but suffers from the fragility of its PowerPulse hose. From 2019 onwards, and especially with the arrival of mild-hybrids (B4/B5), diesel reliability has significantly improved. For a worry-free purchase today, prioritize a post-2019 model or a petrol/PHEV version if your usage includes city driving. It's an excellent alternative to German rivals, provided you accept a boot that's less voluminous than the category average.
