Summary

The **second-generation Citroën C3 (codename SC)** marked a stylistic break from its predecessor while retaining its characteristic roundness. Positioned as a versatile city car focused on comfort, it stood out with its panoramic "Zenith" windshield offering exceptional brightness. Facelifted in 2013 (new grille, LED daytime running lights, integration of PureTech engines), it achieved immense commercial success. In terms of reliability, the record is very mixed: while older engines (TU petrol, early HDi) and mid-career diesels (e-HDi) are generally robust, the end of its career was marred by chronic problems with PureTech petrol engines (timing belt) and BlueHDi diesels (AdBlue tank). **Overall reliability rating: Average to Good (highly dependent on the chosen engine).**

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional suspension comfort for the category
  • Zenith windshield offering unique visibility and brightness
  • e-HDi diesel engines (92hp) very economical and generally reliable
  • Interior finish significantly improved compared to the C3 I
  • Generous boot volume (300 liters)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of PureTech petrol engines and BlueHDi diesels (end of career)
  • Clumsy road handling, significant body roll
  • Average sound insulation on diesel versions (especially 1.4 HDi)
  • Quality of some interior plastics (easily scratched)
  • SMEG multimedia system (after facelift) slow and prone to bugs

Check before buying

  • **Takata Airbag Recall Campaign**: ABSOLUTELY CRITICAL VERIFICATION. Request proof of replacement.
  • **Belt history (PureTech)**: If petrol, demand invoices proving the timing belt replacement.
  • **UREA warning light (BlueHDi)**: On diesels after 2015, ensure no engine or AdBlue warning light is illuminated.
  • **Injector seals (HDi)**: Lift the engine cover and look for traces of black carbon (tar) around the injectors.
  • **Front shock absorbers**: Tendency for premature leaks and wear of the top mounts (clunking over bumps).

Official recalls

  • - **Takata Airbags (2020-2024)**: Massive recall (codes JZR, JYY, etc.) to replace driver and passenger airbags whose propellant gas deteriorates, risking projection of deadly metal fragments.
  • - **Brake assist / PureTech (2020)**: Recall to check the vacuum pump, potentially clogged by timing belt residues (1.2 engines).
  • - **Battery harness (2016)**: Risk of friction of the ground cable potentially leading to a short circuit.
  • - **AdBlue tank lid (2018)**: Replacement of the cap on BlueHDi models to prevent the tank from depressurizing.
  • - **Bonnet catch fastening (2012)**: Risk of the bonnet opening while driving on models produced until 2012.

Verdict

The Citroën C3 II is an excellent city car for those who prioritize comfort over dynamism. **For diesel, the choice is clear: favor the 1.6 HDi 90 or e-HDi 92 versions (2010-2014)**. They offer the best performance/reliability/consumption ratio, provided you don't drive exclusively in the city to preserve the DPF. The 1.4 HDi 70 is a good economical choice but lacks power outside urban areas. **Absolutely avoid the 1.6 BlueHDi versions (after 2015)** due to the ruinous AdBlue tank issues, unless the system has already been replaced with the latest manufacturer reference. For petrol, steer clear of the VTi and PureTech; opt for the old but valiant 1.4i 73hp if you drive little. **Absolute attention to the Takata airbag recall before any purchase.**
