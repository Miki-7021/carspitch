Land Rover Range Rover Evoque

I (L538) · 2011 – 2018

NEGOTIATE Reliability 58/100 — moyen

Summary

The **first-generation Range Rover Evoque (L538)** marked a historic turning point for Land Rover. With its spectacular design faithful to the LRX concept, it transformed the brand's image and attracted a new urban clientele. Available in 5-door, Coupé (3-door), and even Convertible (from 2016) versions, it prioritizes style over interior space.

Under the hood, the Evoque experienced two distinct eras, particularly for diesel engines: Phase 1 (2011-2015) used the robust 2.2-liter engine from the PSA/Ford partnership (DW12), while the 2015 facelift introduced the entirely in-house **Ingenium (2.0-liter)** engine family. Paradoxically, this transition to more modern engines led to a significant drop in reliability, with Ingenium diesel engines suffering from serious design flaws (timing chain, oil dilution).

Strengths 5

  • Timeless and spectacular design, still relevant
  • Interior finish quality and choice of materials (leathers, soft-touch plastics)
  • True off-road capabilities (very effective Terrain Response system)
  • Dynamic road handling for an SUV of this weight
  • Reliability of the 2.2 diesel engine (phase 1) compared to the competition of its time

Weaknesses 6

  • Catastrophic reliability of Ingenium diesel engines (2.0L from 2015 onwards)
  • Mediocre rear passenger space and limited boot volume
  • Very poor peripheral visibility (reversing camera essential)
  • ZF9 automatic gearbox (from 2014 onwards) often poorly calibrated (jerks, slowness)
  • Maintenance and spare parts costs worthy of the luxury segment
  • Infotainment system (InControl) slow and dated on early model years

Check before buying

  • **Metallic noise on startup (Ingenium Diesel):** Sign of a stretched timing chain. Very costly repair.
  • **Oil level (Ingenium Diesel):** If the level is above maximum, there is diesel dilution. Risk of engine runaway or failure.
  • **4x4 system operation:** Turn the steering wheel fully at low speed. If the vehicle shudders or a 'Terrain Response Fault' message appears, the Haldex pump is faulty.
  • **Door locks:** Central locking actuators frequently fail (the door no longer closes or opens).
  • **Tailgate wiring harness:** Wires can break in the hinge, blocking the electric boot opening.

Verdict

The Range Rover Evoque I is an 'impulse buy' that requires great caution. **The paradox of this model is that the older versions are the most reliable.** If you're looking for a diesel, absolutely prioritize a Phase 1 model (2011-2014) equipped with the 2.2 engine (TD4 or SD4) of PSA/Ford origin, which is significantly more robust. Avoid the 2.0 Ingenium diesels (from 2015 onwards) unless the seller can prove oil changes every 10,000 km and a recent timing chain replacement. For petrol, the 2.0 Si4 is thirsty but generally safer than the recent diesels. In any case, plan for a substantial maintenance budget.
