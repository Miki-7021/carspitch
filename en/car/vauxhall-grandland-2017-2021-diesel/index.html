Vauxhall Grandland

I (P1UP) · 2017 – 2021

AVOID Reliability 55/100 — moyen

Summary

The Vauxhall Grandland X (sold under the Opel brand in continental Europe and Vauxhall in the UK) is a compact SUV based on the EMP2 platform of the PSA group (now Stellantis). A technical cousin of the Peugeot 3008 II and the Citroën C5 Aircross, it stands out with a more conservative design and more traditional interior ergonomics. Launched in 2017 under the name 'Grandland X', it lost its 'X' during its facelift at the end of 2021. While it offers an excellent compromise between comfort, roominess, and road handling, its reliability record is heavily marred by the chronic weaknesses of the engines inherited from PSA, notably the 1.2 PureTech (petrol) and the 1.5 BlueHDi/Turbo D (diesel), as well as by recurrent failures of the AdBlue emission control system across the entire diesel range.

Strengths 5

  • Safe and secure road handling
  • Generous trunk volume (514 liters in ICE versions)
  • Ergonomics of physical controls (climate control separate from the screen)
  • Careful soundproofing on long journeys
  • Very controlled fuel consumption on diesel engines

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.2 PureTech (belt) and the 1.5 BlueHDi (7mm chain)
  • Extremely temperamental and expensive to repair AdBlue system on all diesels
  • Slow and dated infotainment system compared to the competition
  • Quality of some lower interior plastics
  • Reduced trunk volume on plug-in hybrid versions (390 liters)

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: essential invoices proving strict compliance with manufacturer recommendations.
  • 1.5 Diesel: Listen carefully to the top of the engine when cold (metallic clicking = loose camshaft chain).
  • Diesel (All): Check for the absence of an engine warning light or 'Anti-pollution fault / Starting impossible in XXX km' message (AdBlue).
  • 1.2 Petrol: Inspect the condition of the timing belt via the oil filler hole (cracks, fraying).
  • Electronics: Test all functions of the touch screen, the reversing camera, and the driving aids.

Official recalls

  • - **[2026-06-15] Power steering**: Possible loss of steering assistance due to a failure on dual-pinion models. Risk of injury.
  • - **[2026-02-23] Suspension (Grandland V2)**: Possible breakage of the fixing screws on the ball joint articulation support of the front lower suspension arm.
  • - **[2026-01-28] Fuel system**: Possible leak in the high-pressure pipe between the pump and the rail. Risk of fire.
  • - **[2025-09-19] High Voltage Battery (PHEV)**: Defect in the HV battery that can cause overheating and a fire.
  • - **[2025-07-25] 1.5 Diesel Engine (Camshaft chain)**: Reduction in the strength of the 7 mm chain due to oil carbonization and pressure. Risk of engine failure and loss of braking assistance.
  • - **[2025-06-19] Low-pressure fuel system**: Possible rubbing of the line against the ignition coil screw, leading to a leak and a risk of fire.
  • - **[2025-03-07] Wiring harness**: Incorrect routing of the harness under the front seats which can cause a short circuit. Risk of fire.
  • - **[2025-01-31] High-pressure fuel pump**: Weakness in the crimping of the quick connector leading to a fuel leak.
  • - **[2025-01-10] Front suspension**: Defective fixing screws on the ball joint support of the lower suspension arm.
  • - **[2023-11-27] Anti-pollution software (AdBlue)**: Software error preventing the engine warning light (MIL) from illuminating if the urea injector stops working. Environmental risk.

Verdict

The Vauxhall/Opel Grandland X is an SUV full of family qualities, but its reliability record is weighed down by the PSA group engines. If you are looking for a diesel, the **177 hp 2.0 Turbo D** is the only one to recommend for its mechanical robustness, although you must budget for potential AdBlue failures. The **1.6 Turbo D** is an acceptable intermediate choice. On the other hand, the **130 hp 1.5 Turbo D is to be avoided** unless the camshaft chain (upgraded to 8 mm) and the AdBlue system have been recently replaced with supporting invoices. On the petrol side, the 1.2 PureTech is also to be avoided due to its failing wet belt. A purchase that requires extreme caution and a solid mechanical warranty.
