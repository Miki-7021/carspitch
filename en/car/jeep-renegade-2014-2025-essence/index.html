Summary

The Jeep Renegade (internal code BU) is a compact SUV (B-segment) launched in 2014, marking Jeep's entry into the urban small SUV market. Developed on the "Small Wide 4x4" platform shared with the Fiat 500X, it stands out with its cubic and robust design, true to Jeep's heritage, while offering urban versatility and surprising off-road capabilities for its category, especially in its Trailhawk versions.

A **major facelift** occurred in **2018**, bringing aesthetic modifications (headlights, grille, bumpers), an infotainment update (Uconnect), and most importantly, the introduction of new petrol engines (FireFly 1.0 T3 and 1.3 T4) compliant with Euro 6d-Temp standards, gradually replacing the older 1.4 MultiAir and 1.6 E.torQ. In 2020, the 4xe plug-in hybrid version was launched, strengthening its position in the European market.

Overall, the Renegade offers a good compromise between style, practicality, and capabilities, but its reliability can be inconsistent depending on the powertrains and markets, with recurring weaknesses in electronics and certain automatic transmissions.

Weaknesses 7

  • High fuel consumption on certain powertrains (1.6 E.torQ, 2.4L Tigershark)
  • Inconsistent reliability of certain powertrains (1.4 MultiAir, 2.4L Tigershark)
  • ZF 9HP automatic transmission sometimes slow and hesitant, reliability issues
  • Electronic bugs and Uconnect system issues (especially before 2018)
  • Improvable quality of certain interior plastics
  • Average sound insulation at high speeds
  • Trunk volume just adequate for the category

Check before buying

  • **Complete service history and invoices**: crucial for MultiAir engines and diesels.
  • **Engine oil level and quality**: particularly on 1.4 MultiAir, 2.4L Tigershark, and FireFly engines (oil consumption).
  • **MultiAir unit operation** (on 1.4 MultiAir): cold knocking noises, unstable idle, engine light.
  • **ZF 9HP automatic transmission**: smooth gear changes, no jerks, no abnormal noises. Check if the fluid change has been done.
  • **Uconnect system**: test all functions, GPS, Bluetooth, rearview camera.
  • **General electronics**: all warning lights, sensors, power windows, air conditioning.
  • **DPF/FAP and EGR valve** (on diesels and FireFly): check for absence of engine light, excessive smoke, and usage history (avoid vehicles used extensively in the city).
  • **Suspension and running gear**: noises, play in ball joints, tire wear.
  • **Sealing**: check for absence of moisture in the cabin, especially around the sunroof (if equipped).
  • **Manufacturer recalls**: verify that all recalls have been performed.

Verdict

The Jeep Renegade is a vehicle with a strong character, offering a unique style and real off-road capabilities for its category. However, its purchase should be carefully considered, as reliability can vary significantly depending on the powertrain and production year.

**BUY** if you opt for the **1.0 T3, 1.3 T4 FireFly (post-2018)** powertrains or the **MultiJet diesels** (with sufficient road/highway use for the DPF), and you are prepared for rigorous maintenance. Manual transmission versions are generally more reliable. The 4xe versions are interesting for their versatility but require a larger budget and particular attention to the hybrid system's maintenance.

**NEGOTIATE** strongly if you are targeting a model equipped with the **1.4 MultiAir** (before 2018) or the **2.0L Tigershark (China)**. Ensure you have an impeccable service history and budget for potential MultiAir unit replacements. The 9HP automatic transmission requires a thorough check of its operation and service history.

**AVOID** models equipped with the **2.4L Tigershark (North America)**, unless an engine replacement has been performed and documented, or if the price is negligible and you are willing to take significant risks. Excessive oil consumption is a major and costly problem.

In all cases, a pre-purchase inspection by a professional is highly recommended, as well as a check of manufacturer recalls. The Renegade is a choice from the heart, but you need to choose the right version to avoid disappointments.
