Dacia Sandero

III · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 80/100 — bon

Summary

The **third-generation Dacia Sandero (Sandero III)** marks a major turning point for the Romanian manufacturer. Based on the modern CMF-B LS (Low Spec) platform from the Renault-Nissan Alliance, shared with the Renault Clio V, it abandons its status as a basic 'low-cost' car to become a highly accomplished 'essential' model. It offers transformed road performance, significantly increased passive and active safety, and an appealing design, particularly in its adventurous **Stepway** version, which accounts for the majority of sales. A key feature of this generation is the complete abandonment of diesel in favor of 3-cylinder gasoline engines and a very relevant gasoline/LPG bi-fuel offer (ECO-G). A slight facelift (new visual identity, Dacia Link logo) was carried out at the end of 2022. Overall, it is one of the most rational purchases on the European market.

Strengths 6

  • Unbeatable price/performance/spaciousness ratio on the market
  • CMF-B platform offering healthy and modern road handling
  • ECO-G 100 (LPG) engine extremely economical to use
  • Appealing design, especially in Stepway trim
  • Essential technological equipment present (wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto)
  • Very contained maintenance and insurance costs

Weaknesses 6

  • Light soundproofing (wind and road noise on the highway)
  • Hard interior plastics, prone to scratches
  • Manual gearbox sometimes notchy
  • Average CVT gearbox experience (slipping effect during strong accelerations)
  • No hybrid or diesel option for very high mileage drivers
  • Fragile paint (sensitive to stone chips)

Check before buying

  • Multimedia system operation (slowness, unexpected reboots, black screen)
  • On ECO-G versions: smooth transition from LPG to gasoline and gauge accuracy
  • Metallic noise from the turbo during deceleration (wastegate rod on TCe 90/100)
  • Body panel alignment and paint condition on the front fascia
  • Front tire wear (the front axle can easily get out of alignment on potholes)

Verdict

The Dacia Sandero III is a **master choice** for anyone looking for a new or recent used vehicle with a controlled budget, without sacrificing safety or modern comfort. Its overall reliability is very good, supported by proven Renault mechanics. The **1.0 ECO-G 100** engine is the smartest choice in the range thanks to the ridiculously low cost of LPG, despite some electronic gauge quirks. The **1.0 TCe 90** is an excellent alternative for those who do not want LPG. The SCe 65 should be avoided, as it is too sluggish outside of cities, and vigilance should be maintained regarding the maintenance history of the TCe 110 (direct injection).
