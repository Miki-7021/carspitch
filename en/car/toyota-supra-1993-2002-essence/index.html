Toyota Supra

A80 · 1993 – 2002

BUY Reliability 89/100 — excellent

Summary

The **fourth-generation Toyota Supra (A80)** is a true icon of 90s sports cars. Made world-famous by popular culture and cinema, it has forged a reputation for exceptional mechanical reliability and robustness, primarily thanks to its legendary inline 6-cylinder engine block, the **2JZ**. Designed to compete with European GTs and high-end Japanese sports cars, it stands out for its exceptional build quality. Today, the main challenge is no longer to evaluate the model's intrinsic reliability (which is excellent), but to contend with the age of the components (over 25 years old) and, above all, with the often heavily modified history of examples on the used car market. Finding a strictly original Supra A80 (dubbed a "unicorn") is a feat.

Strengths 5

  • 2JZ engine block (GE and GTE) of legendary robustness (cast iron block, forged crankshaft on GTE).
  • Getrag V160/V161 6-speed manual gearbox, virtually indestructible.
  • Timeless design that ages remarkably well.
  • Sound road handling and powerful braking for its era (especially with 'Euro/US spec' brakes).
  • Constantly increasing resale value (a true investment).

Weaknesses 5

  • Used market value has become astronomical.
  • Availability of original (OEM) body and interior parts is becoming increasingly critical.
  • Interior plastics (dashboard) tend to scratch easily or deform in the sun.
  • Significant weight (around 1,550 kg), it's not a lightweight.
  • Extreme difficulty in finding an unmodified or accident-free model.

Check before buying

  • **Modification history:** Tampered electrical harness, dubious mappings, excessive turbo pressure increase without adapted management.
  • **Exhaust smoke:** Blue at startup/idle = valve stem seals. Blue under load = worn piston rings or turbo bearings.
  • **Damper pulley condition:** Visually check if the central rubber is cracked or if the pulley is warped with the engine running.
  • **Corrosion:** Although well treated from the factory, check the rear wheel arches, sills, and trunk floor (under the spare wheel) for water ingress (tailgate seal).
  • **Sequential system operation (GTE):** The first turbo should spool around 1800 rpm, followed by a slight dip, then the second turbo engages violently around 4000 rpm. Linear or sluggish behavior indicates a vacuum leak.

Design

  • A Design That Transcends Time — With its fluid and muscular lines, the Supra A80 boasts a timeless design that continues to captivate and age remarkably well, asserting its status as an automotive icon.

economy

  • A Guaranteed Investment — Beyond being an exceptional sports car, the Toyota Supra A80 represents a true investment, with its value on the collector car market constantly increasing.

safety

  • Remarkable Braking for its Era — Equipped with large ventilated disc brakes (often 4-piston front, 2-piston rear), the Supra A80 offered exceptional braking power and endurance, with very competitive stopping distances for its category.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Precise Road Handling — Thanks to its double wishbone suspension, near-ideal weight distribution, and a rigid chassis, the Supra A80 is renowned for its high-speed stability, steering precision, and agility, offering excellent control.

quality

  • Exceptional Build Quality — The Supra A80 stands out for its exceptional build quality and structural robustness, including a virtually indestructible Getrag V160/V161 manual gearbox, a testament to its durability.

Verdict

The Toyota Supra A80 is a masterpiece of 90s engineering. Its 2JZ engine is a monument to reliability, capable of handling indecent mileage (and power) if basic maintenance is respected. The buying verdict is a big YES, but today it is aimed at collectors or informed enthusiasts due to the stratospheric prices. **Expert advice:** Avoid heavily modified models without invoices from recognized professionals. Prioritize a naturally aspirated model (2JZ-GE) with a manual gearbox (W58) if your budget is limited, or pay top dollar for an original 2JZ-GTE. Plan an immediate budget to refresh all rubber components (bushings, hoses, damper pulley) that have inevitably suffered the ravages of time.
