Honda Accord

VII (CL/CM) · 2002 – 2008

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Honda models

Summary

The 7th generation Honda Accord (CL/CM chassis for Europe and Japan, sold as Acura TSX in North America) is often considered one of the most reliable sedans of its era, particularly in its gasoline powertrains. Positioned as a dynamic family car (D-segment) leaning towards premium, it stands out with a sharp chassis (double wishbone front suspension) and naturally aspirated VTEC engines (K series) with exceptional efficiency.

*Note: There is a major distinction between the European/Japanese Accord (CL/CM) and the North American Accord of the same era, which is based on a larger platform and offers V6 engines. This report exhaustively covers both versions for a global overview, with a strong focus on gasoline powertrains.*

The facelift, which occurred in late 2005, brought minor aesthetic touches, Bluetooth integration, an improved onboard computer, and the correction of rare early issues (particularly on the diesel).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of gasoline engines (K series)
  • Dynamic road handling (double wishbone front suspension)
  • Build quality and aging of interior materials
  • Ergonomics of controls and driving position
  • Gigantic trunk volume on the wagon version (Tourer)

Weaknesses 5

  • Fragile paint (prone to micro-scratches and stone chips)
  • Imperfect sound insulation (road noise on the highway)
  • Reliability of the automatic transmission on V6 models (North American market)
  • Costly problems on the diesel (manifold, flywheel)
  • Large turning radius

Check before buying

  • Condensation in the headlights (very common sealing issue)
  • Seizing of rear brake calipers (cleaning and greasing of slides necessary)
  • Operation of central door locking actuators (electric motors that wear out)
  • Groaning noise from the power steering pump (often resolved by a simple €2 O-ring)
  • Air conditioning compressor clutch (check that the AC blows cold continuously)

Official recalls

  • - **Takata Airbags (Massive, 2010-2019)**: Replacement of front airbag inflators (driver and passenger) at risk of exploding and projecting metal fragments. CRITICAL RECALL.
  • - **Power Steering (2012)**: On V6 models (US), risk of power steering hose leakage potentially leading to fire.
  • - **Automatic Transmission (2004)**: On V6 models (US), installation of a cooling kit for the 2nd gear pinion to prevent transmission lock-up.
  • - **Electrical Harness (2007)**: Risk of water infiltration into the fuel pump relay (European models).

Verdict

The 7th generation Honda Accord is a true 'dinosaur' of automotive reliability, in the best sense of the term. If you opt for a gasoline powertrain (2.0i or 2.4i), you are buying one of the most robust vehicles ever built, capable of exceeding 300,000 km with simple routine maintenance. The chassis is excellent and the driving experience remains modern.

**Buying advice**: Avoid the diesel (2.2 i-CTDi) which accounts for 90% of the model's costly breakdowns (flywheel, manifold, chain). Look for a 2.0 or 2.4 gasoline model, preferably post-2005 (facelift), with a clear service history (regular oil changes). Check if the Takata airbag recall has been performed.
