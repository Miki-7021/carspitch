Summary

The Nissan X-Trail IV (T33), launched in 2022, represents the fourth generation of the brand's family SUV. Known as the Rogue in North America, it shares its CMF-CD platform with the Qashqai III. This generation stands out with a more modern and assertive design, a completely redesigned interior, and a significant upgrade in terms of technology and materials. Its main innovation lies in the introduction of the e-POWER hybrid powertrain, which is at the heart of its offering in Europe and Japan. The X-Trail T33 is available in 5 or 7-seat configurations, reinforcing its positioning as a versatile and capable family vehicle. A slight facelift is expected around 2025, primarily focused on minor aesthetic and technological updates. Overall, the X-Trail T33 is a comfortable, spacious, and technologically advanced vehicle, but its long-term reliability is yet to be confirmed due to the youth of its main powertrains.

Strengths 5

  • Innovative and pleasant e-POWER hybrid technology (smoothness, responsiveness).
  • Suspension comfort and cabin soundproofing (especially on e-POWER).
  • Generous interior space and modularity, with 7-seat option.
  • High-performance and secure e-4ORCE all-wheel drive system.
  • Perceived quality improved compared to the previous generation, modern interior.

Weaknesses 5

  • Lack of long-term reliability data for the e-POWER system and VC-Turbo engine.
  • Potentially high e-POWER fuel consumption on highways at steady speeds.
  • Limited engine offering in Europe (no diesel, no PHEV).
  • High purchase price for e-POWER versions, especially in high trim levels.
  • CVT gearbox (on some non-e-POWER versions) may be disliked for its 'rubber band effect'.

Check before buying

  • Complete and up-to-date service history, especially for e-POWER and VC-Turbo versions.
  • Check for any unperformed manufacturer recalls (software, safety).
  • Functionality of all electronic, infotainment, and driver-assistance systems.
  • Condition of tires and brakes, particularly on e-4ORCE versions (heavier and more powerful).
  • For e-POWER, listen carefully to the behavior of the internal combustion engine under load (noise, abnormal vibrations).

Verdict

The Nissan X-Trail T33 is a modern and well-equipped family SUV, whose main innovation lies in its e-POWER powertrain. This technology offers a unique, smooth, and responsive driving experience, particularly suited for urban and suburban journeys. However, its recent introduction means there is a lack of long-term reliability data for the complex hybrid system and the VC-Turbo engine. Initial feedback is generally positive, but caution is advised for a vehicle incorporating so many new technologies.

For a confident purchase, prioritize models with a clear service history and ensure all manufacturer recalls have been performed. e-POWER versions are an excellent choice for those who want an alternative to conventional hybrids or EVs, without the charging constraints. Naturally aspirated versions (PR25DD, MR20DD) available in other markets remain safe bets for their mechanical simplicity and proven reliability.

**Advice**: Negotiate the price, especially for early model years, due to the novelty of the technology and the lack of very long-term reliable data. A manufacturer's extended warranty is highly recommended for e-POWER and VC-Turbo versions.
