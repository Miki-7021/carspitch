All Lexus models

Summary

The XU30 type Lexus RX corresponds to the 2nd generation of the brand's luxury SUV (although sometimes confused with the 3rd generation depending on the market, the true 3rd being the 2008 AL10). Launched in 2003, it redefined the premium SUV segment by prioritizing comfort, ride silence, and unparalleled reliability compared to its German competitors. It is especially famous for introducing the RX 400h in 2005, the world's very first premium hybrid SUV. Facelifted in 2006 (introduction of the 3.5L engine on the RX 350), this model ages exceptionally well. Its build quality (notably the factory assemblies in Kyushu, Japan, and Cambridge, Canada) makes it one of the most robust used vehicles of its decade, despite a few peripheral weaknesses related to age.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall mechanical reliability (bulletproof V6 engines)
  • Very high level of suspension comfort and soundproofing
  • Quality of the Mark Levinson audio system (optional)
  • RX 400h hybrid system far ahead of its time and durable
  • Durability of the leather upholstery and interior assemblies

Weaknesses 5

  • Dashboard that cracks or becomes sticky (known issue, especially in the south)
  • Air suspension (if equipped) expensive to repair in case of airbag leaks
  • High fuel consumption on 100% ICE versions (RX 300/330/350)
  • Infotainment system and GPS completely obsolete today
  • Large turning radius

Check before buying

  • Timing belt replacement history (RX 300, 330, 400h).
  • Condition of the VVT-i oil line on the RX 350 (must be metal).
  • Air suspension operation (raise/lower the vehicle, listen if the compressor runs constantly).
  • Condition of the hybrid battery on the RX 400h (a hybrid health check at Toyota/Lexus is recommended).
  • Leaks at the steering rack and cooling radiator.

Official recalls

*(Expert note: The recent Rappel Conso data provided concerns later generations of the Lexus RX (AL20, AL30) sharing the same commercial name. The 2010 NHTSA recall directly concerns the XU30 generation covered in this sheet).*

  • [2010-10-21] NHTSA 10V499000 (XU30 Generation): Recall of 2004-2006 RXs. The use of brake fluid containing polymers can lead to a leak at the brake master cylinder, reducing braking effectiveness.
  • [2025-11-07] Rappel Conso (Recent generations): Software issue with the parking assist control unit (PVM). Risk of injury.
  • [2025-03-10] Rappel Conso (Recent generations): Insufficient pressure in the high-pressure fuel pump that can cause a leak and a fire risk (concerns notably recent RX 300 turbos).
  • [2024-08-22] Rappel Conso (Recent generations): Camera housings that can disintegrate under stress (incorrect welding), allowing water entry.
  • [2024-06-26] Rappel Conso (Recent generations): Immobilizer device allowing the vehicle to unexpectedly be in "ready on" mode.

Verdict

The second-generation Lexus RX (XU30) is a true monument of reliability. If you are looking for a used premium SUV on a budget, it is one of the safest choices on the market, far ahead of its European counterparts from the same era. The RX 300 is a sensible choice, indestructible if maintenance is kept up. The RX 350 offers superior driving pleasure but requires strict verification of the VVT-i line. Finally, the RX 400h remains an excellent choice to reduce fuel consumption, provided you budget for a potential reconditioning of the high-voltage battery given its age. Avoid models equipped with air suspension if possible to limit costs.
