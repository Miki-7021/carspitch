Jaguar XE

I (X760) · 2015 – 2019

NEGOTIATE Reliability 58/100 — moyen

Summary

The Jaguar XE (X760) marks the return of the Coventry firm to the highly contested premium family sedan segment (D-segment), dominated by the Germans (3 Series, C-Class, A4). Based on the new iQ[Al] modular architecture with a high aluminum content (75%), it stands out for its exceptional road handling, precise steering, and elegant design by Ian Callum. However, this first phase (2015-2019) suffered from teething problems: a capricious infotainment system (InControl), interior finishing below Germanic standards, and above all, serious reliability issues on its flagship engine in Europe: the all-new 4-cylinder "Ingenium" diesel block (AJ200D).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional chassis: comfort/dynamism compromise among the best in the category
  • Precise steering and incisive front axle
  • Timeless and statutory exterior design
  • Aluminum structure promoting rigidity
  • Pleasantness of the petrol engines (V6 in particular)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the early 2.0 Ingenium diesels (chain, DPF, turbo)
  • Slow and buggy InControl Touch multimedia system (before the arrival of Touch Pro)
  • Quality of interior plastics and assemblies disappointing for the segment
  • Limited rear roominess and trunk volume
  • Very high repair costs (Jaguar hourly rate and complexity of access)

Check before buying

  • Timing chain noise on cold start (clunking) or whining (balance shafts) on the 2.0d.
  • Oil level on the diesel: a level above the maximum indicates dilution by diesel (danger of engine failure).
  • Maintenance history: avoid any diesel model that followed the 34,000 km oil change interval.
  • Operation of the central screen (touch, GPS, Bluetooth) and absence of black screens.
  • Signs of coolant leaks (sweet smell, whitish traces) on petrol engines (2.0t and V6).

Alternatives

* **Europe:** BMW 3 Series (F30/G20 - dynamic benchmark), Mercedes C-Class (W205 - comfort/finish), Audi A4 (B9 - technology/finish), Alfa Romeo Giulia (equivalent dynamism, more robust diesel engines). * **Japan/Asia:** Lexus IS (very reliable hybrid, superior finish), Infiniti Q50. * **North Amer.:** Cadillac ATS / CT4, Genesis G70, Acura TLX.

Verdict

The first-generation Jaguar XE is a sedan with fantastic road handling, but it requires a rigorous selection of its engine. **If you are targeting the diesel (2.0d Ingenium), the utmost caution is required.** This engine suffers from major design flaws (fragile rear timing chain, oil dilution) that can lead to engine failure. Only buy a diesel if the chain has already been replaced with an invoice, if the vehicle has been driven exclusively on the highway, and if oil changes have been done every 10,000 km. On the other hand, the petrol versions (2.0t and especially the melodious 3.0 V6) are much more recommendable and transform the XE into an excellent alternative to German sedans.
