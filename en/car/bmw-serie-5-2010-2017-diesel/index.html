BMW Série 5

F10/F11 · 2010 – 2017

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

All BMW models

Summary

The sixth-generation BMW 5 Series (F10 sedan, F11 Touring wagon, and F18 long-wheelbase version for China) marks a return to classicism after the controversial Chris Bangle era (E60). Positioned as a benchmark premium executive car, it shares its architecture with the 7 Series (F01), which gives it imperial comfort, high-level sound insulation, and impeccable finish quality. On the other hand, it carries a significant weight. The mid-2013 facelift (LCI) is crucial: it corrects many early flaws, modernizes the infotainment (transition to the iDrive NBT system), and gradually introduces new, much more reliable engines (notably the transition from the criticized N47 to the robust B47 diesel).

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional ride comfort and sound insulation worthy of the 7 Series
  • Masterful ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (ZF8HP) (responsiveness and smoothness)
  • Enjoyment and fuel efficiency of 6-cylinder diesel engines (530d)
  • Interior finish quality and material aging
  • iDrive infotainment system (especially the NBT version from 2013 onwards)
  • Imperial road handling on the highway

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous timing chain reliability on the 2.0d N47 (2010-2014)
  • Catastrophic reliability of the 550i's V8 (N63)
  • Very high weight (often > 1800 kg) leading to rapid wear of brakes and tires
  • Rear air suspension on the Touring wagon (F11) prone to leaks
  • Very high maintenance and spare parts cost at official dealerships
  • Imposing size making urban use tedious

Check before buying

  • Cicada noise/metallic rubbing at the rear of the engine at idle (symptom of stretched chain on N47 and N20)
  • BMW recall history: the EGR cooler must have been checked or replaced (proven fire risk)
  • ZF8 automatic gearbox oil change invoice (to be done around 100,000 km)
  • Rear sagging on F11 Touring models after prolonged parking (porous air springs or faulty compressor)
  • Traces of oil leak around the oil filter housing gasket (OFHG) on 6-cylinder gasoline engines (N55)
  • Operation of onboard electronics (iDrive screen, 360 cameras, blind spot sensors)

Official recalls

  • EGR Cooler (2018-2023): Massive global recall concerning almost all 4 and 6-cylinder diesel engines (N47, B47, N57). Coolant leakage into the intake, potentially creating a flammable mixture with soot. Risk of fire.
  • ISOFIX Mounts (2016): Weakness of rear ISOFIX anchors that could fail in the event of a crash (models produced between 2010 and 2016).
  • VANOS Timing Bolts (2014): On some 6-cylinder gasoline engines, the bolts of the variable valve timing system can loosen or break.
  • Takata Airbags: Replacement of defective driver/passenger airbag inflators (concerns many production years).

What sets it apart 6

comfort

  • Premium Comfort and Refinement — Exceptional ride comfort and sound insulation worthy of the 7 Series, offering a serene and luxurious travel experience. The interior finish quality and material aging are impeccable.

Performance

  • Modern and Powerful Diesel Engines — Enjoy the pleasure and efficiency of the diesel engines, including the robust 6-cylinder (530d) or the highly efficient 4-cylinder B47 (post-2014), offering a perfect balance of power and controlled consumption.
  • Masterful ZF8 Automatic Transmission — The 8-speed ZF automatic transmission is universally praised for its responsiveness, smoothness, and ability to optimize fuel consumption, ensuring a fluid and dynamic driving experience.

safety

  • Advanced Active and Passive Safety — The BMW 5 Series F10/F11 achieved a 5-star Euro NCAP rating, with high scores for adult (89%) and child (83%) protection, ensuring maximum peace of mind.
  • Imperial Braking and Road Holding — Benefit from excellent braking performance, with short stopping distances (approximately 35-37 meters from 100 km/h), and balanced, precise road holding, offering safety and dynamism on all roads.

praticity

  • Advanced Infotainment Technology — The iDrive infotainment system, especially the NBT version available from 2013, offers an intuitive interface and numerous features for optimal connectivity and entertainment.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Mercedes-Benz E-Class (W212 - excellent reliability in 220 CDI), Audi A6 (C7 - very good finish, robust V6 TDI), Jaguar XF (X250/X260).
  • North America: Lexus GS (unbeatable gasoline reliability), Cadillac CTS, Audi A6.
  • Asia: Lexus GS, Audi A6L (long-wheelbase version very popular in China), Mercedes E-Class LWB.

Verdict

The BMW 5 Series F10/F11 is a fantastic executive car, but its reliability record is split in two. Early production models (2010-2013) are plagued by costly problems (N47 diesel timing chain, N63 V8 gasoline). In contrast, facelifted versions (LCI from mid-2013, and especially 2014 for the arrival of the B47 engine) are excellent choices. The best diesel buy is a 520d (B47 engine with 190 hp, post-2014) for practicality, or a 530d (N57 with 258 hp) for passion. Avoid 550i models and 520d models from before 2014 without a clear service history. Demand a vehicle with the EGR recall performed and an automatic gearbox that has been serviced.
