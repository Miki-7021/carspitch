Summary

The fifth-generation Subaru Forester (chassis code SK) was launched in 2018, building on the new Subaru Global Platform (SGP) which significantly improves structural rigidity, safety, and driving comfort. Positioned as a compact family SUV, it retains the brand's DNA with its standard Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive (AWD) and Boxer engine.

This generation emphasizes safety with the EyeSight driver-assistance system standard in most markets, and introduces more efficient powertrains, notably the e-Boxer mild hybrid in Europe and Japan. The Forester SK is recognized for its robustness, off-road capabilities, and generous interior space. A minor facelift occurred in 2021, bringing minor aesthetic tweaks and improvements to the EyeSight system.

Strengths 6

  • General mechanical reliability (Boxer engines and Lineartronic CVT)
  • Exceptional off-road capabilities thanks to Symmetrical AWD and X-Mode
  • High level of passive and active safety (EyeSight standard on most trims)
  • Generous interior space and practical boot volume
  • Comfortable suspension and reassuring road holding
  • Excellent peripheral visibility

Weaknesses 6

  • High fuel consumption for naturally aspirated engines (FB20D, FB25D), especially in the city
  • Modest performance of naturally aspirated engines, particularly the 2.0L
  • Lineartronic CVT noise under strong acceleration
  • Infotainment system sometimes dated or less intuitive than competitors
  • Exterior design considered conservative by some
  • Dealer network less dense than mainstream brands

Check before buying

  • Complete and regular maintenance history (with invoices)
  • Engine oil level and color (no abnormal oil consumption)
  • CVT fluid condition (color, odor, no abnormal noises during shifts)
  • EyeSight system operation (dashboard warning lights)
  • Tire wear (indicator of misalignment or uneven wear due to AWD)
  • Brake disc and pad condition (especially rear, sometimes more stressed by AWD)
  • Absence of leaks under the vehicle (engine, transmission)
  • Operation of all electrical equipment (air conditioning, windows, multimedia system)

Official recalls

Known Official Recalls for the Subaru Forester V (SK)

  • 2019-2020 (depending on market): Fuel pump
  • Issue: Risk of fuel pump failure, which may lead to engine stalling or inability to start.
  • Affected Population: Certain vehicles produced between 2018 and 2020, equipped with a defective fuel pump supplied by a component manufacturer.
  • Action: Replacement of the fuel pump.
  • 2020 (depending on market): Crankshaft/camshaft position sensor
  • Issue: The sensor may crack and lead to engine stalling or inability to start.
  • Affected Population: Certain vehicles equipped with FB25D engines produced during a specific period.
  • Action: Replacement of the affected sensor.
  • 2021 (depending on market): Engine Control Unit (ECU) software
  • Issue: A software problem may lead to improper engine management, affecting emissions or vehicle behavior.
  • Affected Population: Certain vehicles produced during a given period.
  • Action: ECU reprogramming.
  • 2022 (depending on market): Rear suspension arm mounting bolts
  • Issue: Possibility that the rear suspension arm mounting bolts may not be properly tightened, potentially leading to loss of vehicle control.
  • Affected Population: A limited number of vehicles produced over a short period.
  • Action: Inspection and tightening or replacement of the bolts.

*It is imperative to check with a Subaru dealership, providing the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), to confirm that the desired vehicle has benefited from all recall campaigns.*

What sets it apart 6

safety

  • Award-Winning Passive and Active Safety — The Forester V stands out with its exceptional safety level, confirmed by top-tier crash test results and prestigious awards. Its standard EyeSight system offers unparalleled proactive protection.
  • Absolute Braking Control — Enjoy peace of mind thanks to a highly efficient braking system, ensuring short stopping distances and remarkable stability in all conditions, enhanced by the intelligent integration of the EyeSight system and all-wheel drive.

Performance

  • Impeccable Road Holding — The standard Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive (AWD) ensures safe, predictable road behavior and excellent road holding, offering surprising agility for an SUV and appreciable comfort on long journeys.
  • Unrivaled Off-Road Capabilities — With its standard Symmetrical AWD system and X-Mode, the Forester V offers exceptional off-road capabilities, allowing you to explore challenging terrains with confidence and safety, far beyond the expectations of a classic SUV.

reliability

  • Legendary Mechanical Reliability — The Forester V continues Subaru's reputation for reliability, notably thanks to its Boxer gasoline engines and Lineartronic (CVT) transmission, renowned for their robustness and longevity, ensuring thousands of serene kilometers.

practicality

  • Generous Space and Comfort — Enjoy a spacious interior with generous room for all passengers, a practical boot volume, and excellent peripheral visibility, all complemented by suspension comfort that makes every journey pleasant.

Verdict

The fifth-generation Subaru Forester (SK) is a wise choice for those seeking a reliable, safe, and particularly capable SUV off the beaten path. Its SGP platform provides excellent rigidity and appreciable comfort.

For predominantly urban and suburban use, the 2.0L e-Boxer is an interesting option for its slightly optimized fuel consumption and pleasant city driving, although its performance remains modest on the highway.

For long journeys, light towing, or hilly regions, the 2.5L naturally aspirated (FB25D), available in many markets, is the most relevant choice thanks to its superior torque and legendary robustness, despite higher fuel consumption.

The 1.8L Turbo (CB18), if you are lucky enough to find it (mainly in Japan), offers the best performance/consumption compromise.

Regardless of the engine, the Lineartronic (CVT) gearbox is reliable but requires rigorous maintenance. The EyeSight system is a major asset for safety.

Verdict: BUY if you prioritize reliability, safety, off-road capabilities, and interior space, and are willing to accept sometimes high fuel consumption for naturally aspirated engines and unexciting performance. Don't forget to check the maintenance history and recalls.
