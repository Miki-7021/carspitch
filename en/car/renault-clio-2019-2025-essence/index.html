Summary

The Renault Clio V (codename BJA) marks a gentle stylistic evolution compared to the Clio IV, but a true technical and qualitative revolution. Based on the new CMF-B modular platform, it corrects its predecessor's main flaw: the interior finish, which becomes one of the category's benchmarks (foamed materials, rigorous assembly). It also introduces the EasyLink multimedia system and, for the first time, a full hybrid powertrain (E-Tech). The mid-life restyling (phase 2) in 2023 brings a more aggressive front end (new light signature) and updates technological equipment. Overall, the Clio V is a mature, versatile, and very sound car in terms of reliability, especially with gasoline engines.

Strengths 5

  • Interior finish significantly improved (materials, assembly)
  • Excellent road handling (comfort/dynamism compromise)
  • Remarkable reliability of gasoline engines (1.0 TCe and 1.3 TCe)
  • Generous boot volume for the category (391 liters in thermal versions)
  • Physical ergonomics preserved (real climate control buttons)

Weaknesses 5

  • Recurring bugs on the EasyLink multimedia system (black screens, reboots)
  • Reliability of the dog-clutch gearbox on early E-Tech models (2020-2021)
  • Average rear passenger space (limited legroom)
  • Poor 3/4 rear visibility
  • Capricious AdBlue system on diesel versions

Check before buying

  • History of software updates (EasyLink and engine/gearbox management).
  • On E-Tech versions: absence of jerks or excessive slipping during thermal/electric transitions.
  • On GPL versions: smooth gasoline/gas switchover without stalling or warning light.
  • Air conditioning operation (some cases of noisy compressors or gas leaks).
  • Body panel alignment (tailgate sometimes poorly adjusted at the factory).

Official recalls

  • Power steering (2020) : Risk of short-circuit on the power steering motor (models produced between June and November 2019).
  • Brake hoses (2020) : Incorrect positioning potentially leading to wear by friction (late 2019 production).
  • GPL tank (2022) : Risk of leak at the safety valve on a small series of Clio TCe 100 GPL.
  • E-Tech gearbox (2023) : Reprogramming of the hybrid gearbox control unit to prevent a risk of dog-clutch breakage (2020-2021 models).

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Exceptional Petrol Engine Reliability — The 1.0 TCe and 1.3 TCe petrol engines of the Clio V are renowned for their robustness and remarkable reliability, notably thanks to a durable timing chain.

Safety

  • High-Level Safety — The Renault Clio V achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 96% for adult protection and 89% for child protection, ensuring optimal peace of mind.

Handling

  • Balanced Road Handling — Its agile and balanced chassis offers an excellent compromise between comfort and dynamism, ensuring precise road holding and inspiring confidence on all types of journeys.

Braking

  • Powerful and Safe Braking — The Clio V stands out for its very good, stable, and efficient braking performance, with short distances, often measured around 36-38 meters from 100 to 0 km/h.

Comfort

  • Refined and High-Quality Interior — The Clio V's cabin benefits from significantly improved finishes, with soft-touch materials and rigorous assembly, positioning it as a benchmark in its category for perceived quality.

Practicality

  • Generous Boot Volume — With a boot volume of 391 liters in the thermal version, the Clio V offers generous loading capacity for its category, ideal for family needs and leisure activities.
  • Driver-Focused Ergonomics — The Clio V retains physical controls for essential functions like air conditioning, offering intuitive ergonomics and appreciated ease of use in daily life.

Alternatives

  • Europe : Peugeot 208 II (strong design but very problematic 1.2 PureTech reliability), Volkswagen Polo VI (more austere but spacious), Toyota Yaris IV (hybrid benchmark), Dacia Sandero III (same technical basis, cheaper).
  • Asia / Japan : Honda Fit/Jazz (unbeatable in interior space), Nissan Note.
  • Americas / Emerging Markets : Chevrolet Onix, Hyundai HB20, VW Polo Track.

Verdict

The Renault Clio V is an excellent choice on the used and new car market, particularly with gasoline engines. Unlike its main rival (the Peugeot 208 and its fragile 1.2 PureTech), the Clio benefits from extremely reliable gasoline engines (1.0 TCe and 1.3 TCe), equipped with a robust timing chain. The most rational choice is the 1.0 TCe 90/100 (or its very economical GPL variant). The 1.3 TCe 140 will delight those looking for more dynamism. E-Tech versions before 2022 should be approached with caution (demand a clear history and software updates), while diesel is best reserved for pure highway drivers due to AdBlue system weaknesses.
