Summary

The BMW X3 (F25 Generation) marks a major qualitative leap compared to its predecessor (E83). More spacious, better finished, and with imperial road handling, it established itself as a benchmark among premium SUVs. The facelift (LCI) introduced in spring 2014 is crucial: it corrects many early defects, improves the finish, and introduces the new generation of diesel engines (B47), replacing the problematic N47. The ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (optional or standard depending on the version) is a model of driving pleasure. However, its overall reliability is strongly conditioned by the production year and the chosen engine.

Strengths 5

  • Driving pleasure and dynamic handling (typical BMW)
  • Excellent ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox
  • Fantastic 6-cylinder engines (diesel and petrol)
  • Significantly improved finish quality (especially after LCI facelift)
  • Very intuitive iDrive infotainment system

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the N47 diesel engine (2010-2014)
  • Extreme sensitivity of the xDrive transfer case to tire wear differences
  • Steering rack prone to knocking (noise on degraded roads)
  • High maintenance cost in the official network
  • Recurring recalls for the EGR system (diesel)

Check before buying

  • Timing chain history: Mandatory for 18d/20d models before 2014 (N47). Listen for any cricket/rubbing noise when cold around 1500 rpm.
  • Transfer case (xDrive): Check for absence of jerks during acceleration when turning the wheels at low speed. Check the brand and wear of all 4 tires.
  • EGR cooler (Diesel): Check if the vehicle has undergone the manufacturer recall (risk of coolant leakage into the intake).
  • Steering rack: With the engine running at a standstill, make small, rapid left/right movements with the steering wheel to detect a metallic knocking sound.
  • Oil leaks (6-cyl Petrol): Inspect the oil filter housing and valve cover gasket on 35i versions.

Verdict

The BMW X3 F25 is a two-faced vehicle. Phase 1 models (2010-2014) with 4-cylinder diesel (N47) engines are to be avoided unless the timing chain has been replaced with supporting invoices. In contrast, facelifted versions (LCI, from mid-2014) equipped with the B47 engine or 6-cylinder versions (30d/35d) are excellent choices. Driving pleasure is at the top of the category, but maintenance tolerates no approximations: automatic gearbox fluid change, transfer case fluid change, and strict adherence to tire specifications are imperative to guarantee the vehicle's longevity.
