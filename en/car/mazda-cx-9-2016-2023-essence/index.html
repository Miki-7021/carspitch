All Mazda models

Summary

The second-generation Mazda CX-9 (codename TC) marks the Japanese manufacturer's move upmarket in the large 3-row family SUV segment. Abandoning the Ford-sourced V6 of the previous generation, this model introduces the 2.5-liter 4-cylinder Turbo SKYACTIV-G engine, designed to offer the torque of a V6 with better efficiency. Primarily intended for the North American, Australian, and Middle Eastern markets (very rare in Western Europe), the CX-9 stands out with its elegant Kodo design, an interior finish quality that flirts with the premium segment, and driving dynamics that are above average for its class. It achieves excellent overall reliability ratings.

Strengths 6

  • Manufacturing and finish quality above the Japanese average (close to premium)
  • Excellent overall reliability (recommended by ADAC and J.D. Power)
  • Robust 2.5T SKYACTIV-G engine with a perfectly integrated turbo
  • Smooth and extremely reliable 6-speed automatic gearbox (SKYACTIV-Drive)
  • High-performing and trouble-free all-wheel drive (i-ACTIV AWD)
  • Generous onboard space with 3 real rows of seats

Weaknesses 4

  • Slight oil consumption inherent to the 2.5T design (to be monitored)
  • Lack of mechanical alternatives (no hybrid, no diesel)
  • Aging infotainment system (Mazda Connect) on early model years (2016-2019)
  • Trunk volume slightly lower than some direct competitors (e.g., Chevrolet Traverse or Honda Pilot)

Check before buying

  • Engine oil level (verify that the previous owner topped it up if necessary)
  • Recall history: ensure the low-pressure fuel pump has been replaced (2018-2019 models)
  • Mounting and rail of the left second-row seat (recall on 2020-2021 models)
  • Operation of onboard electronics and turn signals on 2018 models (wiring harness recall)

Official recalls

  • [2022-05-18 / 2021-11-12] Fuel pump (Rappel Conso / NHTSA 21V875000): On 2018-2019 models, the low-pressure fuel pump impeller may crack and deform, leading to a pumping failure and a risk of the engine stalling while driving.
  • [2022-06-02] Front shock absorber (Rappel Conso): The lower nut and bolt securing the shock absorber to the steering knuckle could loosen, potentially leading to detachment.
  • [2021-09-27] 2nd row seats (NHTSA 21V750000): On 2020-2021 models equipped with the 60/40 bench seat, the left seat may slide forward unexpectedly during a crash.
  • [2019-10-28] Tires (NHTSA 19V770000): On certain 2019 models equipped with Sumitomo Falken ZIEX CT50 A/S tires, an incorrect rubber compound may have been used during manufacturing.
  • [2019-05-30] Wiring harness (NHTSA 19V403000): On 2018 models, the wiring harness may disrupt electrical communications, risking the deactivation of the passenger airbag, turn signals, or engine starting system.

What sets it apart 10

Comfort

  • Premium interior finish quality — Materials and assembly exceed the average Japanese standard and approach premium segment levels, giving buyers a cabin that feels more expensive than the price suggests.

Reliability

  • Excellent overall reliability record — The CX-9 earns strong reliability scores and is recommended by ADAC and J.D. Power, reducing the risk of costly unexpected repairs for families.
  • Robust 2.5T SKYACTIV-G engine — The turbocharged four-cylinder is described as very reliable with a well-integrated turbo, offering generous low-end torque and great flexibility for daily family use.
  • Smooth and proven six-speed automatic — The SKYACTIV-Drive automatic gearbox is smooth and extremely reliable, with robustness now widely proven according to ADAC, TÜV and What Car? assessments.
  • Peace of mind versus complex rivals — Unlike many competitors that suffered transmission or complex V6 problems, the CX-9 relies on a proven four-cylinder turbo and six-speed automatic for greater peace of mind.

Handling

  • Capable i-ACTIV AWD system — The all-wheel-drive transmission is described as performant with no major issues, giving buyers extra traction and confidence in varied road and weather conditions.

Practicality

  • Three genuine rows of seats — The cabin offers generous space with three real seating rows, making it a practical choice for larger families who need usable space for all passengers.

Performance

  • V6-like torque with better efficiency — The 2.5-litre turbo was designed to deliver the torque of a V6 while improving efficiency, giving buyers strong pulling power without the complexity of a V6.
  • Driving pleasure above segment average — The CX-9 offers an enjoyment behind the wheel superior to the average of its category, so family transport does not mean sacrificing driving satisfaction.

Design

  • Elegant Kodo exterior design — The Kodo design language gives this large family SUV a refined, elegant appearance, letting buyers stand out from boxier competitors in the segment.

Verdict

The second-generation Mazda CX-9 (TC) is an excellent choice for families looking for a large 3-row SUV combining reliability, driving dynamics, and a premium finish. Unlike many of its competitors that have suffered from transmission issues or complex V6 engines, the CX-9 relies on a 2.5 Turbo 4-cylinder and a 6-speed automatic gearbox whose robustness is now widely proven (highly rated by ADAC, TÜV Report, and What Car?). The only regular mechanical point of attention concerns slight oil consumption, normal for this block, which requires a simple periodic check. If the official recalls (notably the fuel pump on 2018-2019 models) have been performed, it is a vehicle that offers peace of mind well above the average for its segment.
