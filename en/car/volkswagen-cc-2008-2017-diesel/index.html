Volkswagen CC

· 2008 – 2017

NEGOTIATE Reliability 60/100 — moyen

Summary

Launched in 2008 as the **Volkswagen Passat CC** (for *Comfort Coupé*), it was simply renamed **Volkswagen CC** during its facelift in late 2011/early 2012. Based on the PQ46 platform of the Passat B6/B7, it stands out with its flowing four-door coupé roofline, frameless windows, and a more upscale positioning. While it shares most of its underpinnings and engines with the classic Passat, it offers slightly more dynamic road handling, often aided by the DCC adaptive suspension. In the used car market, its reliability is very heterogeneous: diesel engines (especially post-2013) are robust high-mileage performers, while the early TSI petrol engines (EA111 and EA888 Gen 1/2) suffered from serious design flaws (timing chain, piston rings).

Strengths 5

  • Timeless and elegant design (roofline, frameless doors)
  • Interior finish quality and soundproofing (especially post-2012)
  • Superb road handling and comfort (with DCC adaptive suspension)
  • Generous trunk volume (532 liters)
  • Smooth and powerful 3.6 V6 engine (paired with 4Motion)

Weaknesses 5

  • Limited rear headroom for passengers over 1.80m
  • Poor rear visibility
  • Disastrous reliability of early TSI engines (chain, oil) and the DSG7 DQ200 gearbox
  • Strictly 4 seats on early models (3-seat bench optional later)
  • Frequent faceted wear of rear tires (sensitive geometry)

EngineEngine CodeKnown Problems and Reliability
**1.4 TSI (160 hp)**EA111 (Twincharger)**AVOID (before 2013).** Recurring problems with timing chain stretch, piston ring/piston failure, and magnetic clutch compressor failures.
**1.8 TSI & 2.0 TSI**EA888 (Gen 1 & 2)**RISKY (2008-2012).** Major oil consumption due to defective piston rings. Frequent timing chain tensioner failure potentially leading to engine failure. Water pump leaks. Made reliable from 2013.
**3.6 V6 FSI**EA390**ROBUST.** Very solid engine. Some cases of valve carbon buildup (direct injection) and defective ignition coils. Expensive maintenance (Haldex, brakes).
**2.0 TDI (140/170/177)**EA189 (Common Rail)**ACCEPTABLE.** Beware of the "Dieselgate" software update which tends to prematurely clog the EGR valve and DPF, and weaken the injectors. Fragile dual-mass flywheel on manual gearbox.
**2.0 TDI (150/184)**EA288**VERY RELIABLE.** New generation engine. EGR problems are resolved. Only weak point: the water pump which can leak or seize prematurely (to be monitored during belt replacement).
**DSG7 Gearbox (DQ200)**Dry clutch (1.4 / 1.8 TSI)**PROBLEMATIC.** Premature wear of the dry dual clutch, juddering on startup, mechatronic failure.
**DSG6 Gearbox (DQ250)**Wet clutch (2.0 TSI, V6, TDI)**RELIABLE.** On the strict condition that the oil change and filter replacement have been performed every 60,000 km.

Check before buying

  • **Oil consumption history**: Crucial for 1.8 TSI and 2.0 TSI engines before 2013. Ask if the piston rings have been replaced.
  • **Cold start noise (TSI)**: A metallic clatter indicates a worn timing chain tensioner (risk of engine failure).
  • **DSG gearbox maintenance**: Demand oil change invoices every 60,000 km for the DSG6 (DQ250). Test the DSG7 (DQ200) for any juddering or jerking.
  • **Frameless windows**: Check that the windows drop a few millimeters when opening the doors. Window regulator mechanisms and seals are fragile (and freeze in winter).
  • **DCC suspension**: Check for leaks on the adaptive dampers; their replacement is extremely expensive.
  • **Dieselgate update (TDI EA189)**: If the update has been performed, check the proper functioning of the EGR valve and the absence of a DPF warning light.

Alternatives

Verdict

The Volkswagen CC is a superb alternative to the traditional Passat, offering a design that has aged remarkably well and high-flying road performance. However, it is a vehicle to buy with **extreme caution**. Early TSI petrol engines (pre-2013) and the DSG7 (DQ200) gearbox are true problem magnets that can ruin a budget. For a worry-free purchase, opt for a facelifted model (post-2012), ideally equipped with the 2.0 TDI (especially the EA288 engine introduced in 2015) or the rare and melodious 3.6 V6. A clear service history, particularly for DSG gearboxes, is absolutely non-negotiable.
