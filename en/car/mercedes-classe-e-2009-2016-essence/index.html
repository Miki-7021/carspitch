Summary

The Mercedes-Benz E-Class generation W212 (saloon), S212 (estate), C207 (coupe) and A207 (cabriolet) marks a major turning point for the star brand. After a W211 generation marred by electronic issues in its early days (notably the SBC braking system), the W212 was designed with a drastic specification focused on reliability and robustness.

Aesthetically, it abandons curves for sharp lines and square dual headlights. In 2013, it benefited from one of the most expensive facelifts in automotive history (over a billion euros in investment), unifying the front headlights and profoundly modernizing the interior and powertrains.

Although this sheet focuses on petrol engines (which underwent a major transition from indirect injection to CGI direct injection, then the arrival of the turbo), the W212 remains an essential reference in the used car market, offering imperial comfort and manufacturing quality worthy of Mercedes' historical reputation.

Strengths 7

  • Manufacturing and finish quality significantly improved compared to the W211
  • Exceptional ride comfort, especially with Airmatic suspension
  • Very high level of cabin sound insulation
  • Overall very satisfactory reliability of the onboard electronics
  • Excellent corrosion resistance
  • Particularly successful and reliable V6 petrol engines (M276)
  • Gigantic boot volume on the estate version (S212)

Weaknesses 6

  • Fragile M271 4-cylinder petrol engine (2009-2013) (timing chain)
  • High maintenance and spare parts cost in the official network
  • Airmatic air suspension (optional or standard on estate rears) prone to leaks (air springs, compressor) after 100,000 km
  • 7G-Tronic automatic gearbox sometimes slow and prone to jerks if not serviced
  • Blind spot sensors in the rear bumper that take on water and fail
  • Decorative wood trim (burr walnut) that dulls and whitens with UV exposure (varnish defect)

Check before buying

  • Clear service history of the automatic gearbox oil changes (7G-Tronic or 9G-Tronic on later models)
  • Absence of metallic rattling on cold start (sign of timing chain wear on M271, M276, M278, OM651)
  • Airmatic suspension operation: the vehicle should not sag after a night of parking
  • Condition of interior wood trim (frequent discoloration, partially covered by Mercedes at the time)
  • Functionality of all driving assistance sensors (parking radar, blind spot, Distronic)
  • On V6 Diesel (OM642): inspect the bottom of the engine V with a flashlight to detect oil traces (oil cooler)

safety

  • Advanced Safety and Innovations — The E-Class W212 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, thanks to a robust structure and the integration of pioneering safety systems for its time, such as Attention Assist, Adaptive Highbeam Assist, and PRE-SAFE Brake.

braking

  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — Equipped with a high-performance and durable braking system, the E-Class W212 ensures very short stopping distances, typically between 36 and 38 meters from 100 km/h, providing complete driver confidence thanks to a firm pedal and aids like ABS and ESP.

road_handling

  • Imperial and Stable Road Handling — Designed for comfort and safety, the E-Class W212 offers exemplary stable road handling. Its precise and well-assisted steering ensures serene and predictable driving, even at high speeds or on long journeys, with well-controlled body roll.

reliability

  • Superior Build Quality and Overall Reliability — The W212 represents a significant leap in terms of build quality and finish compared to the previous generation. It boasts excellent corrosion resistance and highly satisfactory reliability of its onboard electronics, ensuring long-term durability.

comfort

  • Exceptional Ride Comfort and Soundproofing — Enjoy a very high level of ride comfort, especially with the optional Airmatic suspension, and remarkable cabin soundproofing, transforming every journey into a most pleasant and relaxing experience.

Performance

  • Modern V6 and 4-Cylinder Petrol Engines — The V6 petrol engines (M276) are particularly successful, offering power, driving pleasure, and high reliability. The 2.0 Turbo 4-cylinder engines (M274) in the facelifted versions are also an excellent choice, combining fuel efficiency with daily driving pleasure.

Alternatives

  • Europe: BMW 5 Series (F10) (more dynamic but fragile N20 4-cylinder petrol engines), Audi A6 (C7) (excellent finish, powerful V6 TFSI), Jaguar XF (X250/X260).
  • Japan / Asia: Lexus GS (L10) (the absolute benchmark for reliability, especially in hybrid), Infiniti M/Q70.
  • North America: Lexus GS, Cadillac CTS, Genesis G80 (Hyundai Genesis).
  • Emerging Markets: Toyota Camry (less premium but indestructible), Skoda Superb (for interior space).

Verdict

The Mercedes E-Class W212 is an excellent vintage that has restored the brand's reputation for reliability. If you are looking for a petrol engine, the top choice is the 3.5 V6 (M276) from the post-2011 E 300 and E 350, which offers exceptional driving pleasure for high-level reliability.

For 4-cylinders, avoid 2009-2013 models equipped with the M271 engine (E 200 CGI, E 250 CGI) unless the timing chain has been completely redone. Prefer facelifted versions (from 2013) equipped with the much more robust M274 engine.

V8 versions are exhilarating but require a significant maintenance budget, with particular vigilance on the V8 BiTurbo (M278). Overall, a well-maintained W212, ideally in its facelifted (Phase 2) version, is a highly recommendable purchase that ages remarkably well.
