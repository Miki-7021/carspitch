Summary

The Mercedes-Benz S-Class (W222) represents the pinnacle of luxury and technology for the brand with the three-pointed star at the time of its release. Replacing the W221, it introduced major innovations such as the predictive *Magic Body Control* suspension, 100% LED lighting (without a single incandescent bulb), and a dual 12.3-inch screen interface. The facelift (Phase 2) introduced in mid-2017 brought a complete overhaul of the engine range, marking the return of inline 6-cylinder engines (OM656 in diesel, M256 in petrol) to replace the V6s, offering a leap forward in drivability and reliability. The W222 is widely considered one of the most accomplished and reliable S-Classes of the modern era, although its technological content requires rigorous and costly maintenance.

Strengths 5

  • Unrivaled ride comfort (especially with Magic Body Control)
  • Exceptional cabin soundproofing
  • Remarkable reliability of the inline 6-cylinder engines (OM656 and M256) post-2017
  • Quality of finish and interior presentation that ages very well
  • Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) technologies far ahead of their time

Weaknesses 5

  • Prohibitive cost of maintenance and spare parts (air suspension, brakes, electronics)
  • OM651 4-cylinder diesel engine (S 300 h) unworthy of the status and prone to problems (chain, injectors)
  • Chronic oil cooler leaks on the OM642 V6 diesel (S 350 d pre-2017)
  • Risk of cylinder scoring on the M278 V8 (S 500 pre-2017)
  • Bulkiness (difficult to park in the city, especially in the Limousine version)

Check before buying

  • Airmatic suspension: check for sagging after prolonged parking and the operation of the compressor.
  • Oil cooler (OM642): inspect the bottom of the undertray beneath the engine for traces of oil.
  • On-board electronics: absolutely test all functions (massaging seats, screens, 360° cameras, electric blinds).
  • Gearbox (7G-Tronic or 9G-Tronic): check the smoothness of gear shifts when cold and the oil change history.
  • Tire wear: the high weight (often > 2 tons) leads to rapid wear of the running gear and tires.

The exceptional cabin soundproofing creates a cocoon of serenity, transforming every journey into a first-class experience.

Safety

  • Pioneering Safety and Benchmark Braking — Benefit from maximum protection thanks to Mercedes-Benz Intelligent Drive advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS), far ahead of their time (PRE-SAFE® Impulse Side, DISTRONIC PLUS). The braking system is remarkably excellent, offering powerful and enduring performance, with short stopping distances and impressive stability, even in the most demanding conditions. The pedal feel is consistently reassuring.

Performance

  • Impeccable Road Holding and Controlled Dynamics — Enjoy exemplary road behaviour, stability, and safety, typical of a large luxury sedan. Thanks to its sophisticated suspensions (AIRMATIC or MAGIC BODY CONTROL) and precise steering, the S-Class W222 offers predictable and reassuring road holding, even at high speeds, inspiring total confidence and a perfect balance between supreme comfort and dynamic safety.

Reliability

  • Exceptional Reliability of 6-Cylinder Diesel Engines (Post-2017) — Opt for peace of mind with the in-line 6-cylinder diesel engines (OM656) introduced from 2017 (S 350 d and S 400 d). These units are renowned for their high-level reliability, exceptional driving pleasure, quiet operation, and controlled fuel consumption, making them the optimal choice for demanding high-mileage drivers.

Practicality

  • Timeless Luxury and Cutting-Edge Technologies — The S-Class W222's interior is a masterpiece of design and materials, with a quality of finish and presentation that ages remarkably well. It integrates major innovations such as 100% LED lighting and a dual 12.3-inch screen interface, offering a modern and intuitive user experience that enhances daily life.

Economy

  • Optimized Efficiency for a Luxury Sedan — Despite its status as a luxury sedan, the S-Class W222, particularly with the post-2017 in-line 6-cylinder diesel engines (OM656), offers remarkably controlled fuel consumption for its category. This efficiency, combined with the recognized reliability of these engines, allows you to reconcile the pleasure of driving an exceptional car with optimized fuel budget management.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** BMW 7 Series (G11/G12) for more dynamic handling, Audi A8 (D4/D5) for the Quattro transmission and clinical finish, Porsche Panamera for sportiness. - **Japan / Asia:** Lexus LS (XF40/XF50) for absolute reliability and a highly accomplished hybrid powertrain. - **North America:** Lexus LS, Genesis G90 (excellent value/price/reliability ratio).

Verdict

The Mercedes S-Class W222 is an engineering masterpiece that redefined the standards of its category. If you are looking for a diesel engine, **the optimal choice is undoubtedly a facelifted version (post-2017) equipped with the OM656 inline 6-cylinder (S 350 d or S 400 d)**. This engine combines top-flight reliability with exceptional drivability. The pre-2017 V6 diesel versions (OM642) remain good cruisers but require a contingency budget for the notorious oil cooler. Avoid the S 300 h (OM651), whose mechanical reliability and lack of refinement clash with the badge. In petrol, also favor post-2017 models (M256 or M176) to avoid the cylinder issues of the M278 V8. The purchase must imperatively be accompanied by a crystal-clear maintenance history within the Mercedes-Benz network.
