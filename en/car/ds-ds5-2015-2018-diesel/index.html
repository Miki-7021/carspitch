Summary

The DS 5 (phase 2, type M32) marks the emancipation of the DS Automobiles brand, permanently abandoning the Citroën chevrons on its grille in favor of the DS logo ('DS Wings'). This 2015 facelift brings a technological update (touchscreen, Apple CarPlay/Android Auto) and, above all, new shock absorbers (PLV technology) intended to correct the major flaw of phase 1: an excessively stiff suspension. A true automotive UFO halfway between a shooting brake and a compact sedan, the DS 5 shines with its airplane 'cockpit' style interior and premium finish (watchstrap leather). Mechanically, it generalizes the latest generation BlueHDi and THP blocks. Overall score: 6.8/10.

Strengths 5

  • Spectacular and timeless exterior design
  • Unique interior atmosphere (cockpit roof with 3 skylights)
  • Quality of materials (semi-aniline watchstrap style leather)
  • Excellent mechanical reliability of the 2.0 BlueHDi block (excluding emissions control)
  • Smooth and reliable EAT6 (Aisin) automatic gearbox

Weaknesses 5

  • Suspension comfort still too firm despite the 2015 improvements
  • Highly problematic AdBlue emissions control system (BlueHDi)
  • Disappointing rear roominess and trunk volume for the size
  • Dated ergonomics (many buttons) and poor rear visibility
  • ETG6 gearbox on the Hybrid4 completely outdated in terms of drivability

Check before buying

  • AdBlue system history: has the tank been replaced recently? (Crucial on BlueHDi models)
  • Verification of the Takata airbags recall (see recalls section)
  • Absence of engine light or 'Anti-pollution fault' message on the dashboard
  • Fluidity of gear shifts on the EAT6 gearbox (an oil change is recommended around 80,000 - 100,000 km)
  • Condition of the alloy wheels (very exposed to curbs) and even tire wear

Official recalls

According to official data from Rappel Conso France, the DS 5 is affected by the following critical recall:

[2024-02-19] Takata Airbags (C3, C4, DS3, DS4, DS5): The inflator structure in the driver and front passenger airbags can deteriorate over time. In the event of deployment, metal fragments could be projected, causing serious injuries. Correction: Mandatory replacement of the affected airbags.

What sets it apart 7

Design

  • Avant-Garde Design and Premium Interior — The DS 5 stands out with its bold and timeless look, complemented by a unique aircraft cockpit-inspired cabin, featuring high-quality materials such as semi-aniline leather.

Reliability

  • Robust and Powerful Diesel Engine — Benefit from the excellent mechanical reliability of the 2.0 BlueHDi engines, renowned for their robustness, generous torque, and driving pleasure, ideal for long journeys.

Comfort

  • Optimized Driving Comfort — The EAT6 automatic gearbox offers smooth and reliable gear changes, while PLV shock absorbers significantly improve suspension comfort, making journeys more enjoyable.

Safety

  • Certified Maximum Safety — The DS 5 ensures optimal protection for all its occupants, as evidenced by its excellent Euro NCAP test score.
  • Efficient and Reassuring Braking — Benefit from a powerful and responsive braking system, ensuring short stopping distances and an increased sense of security in all circumstances.
  • Stable and Secure Road Handling — The DS 5 offers exemplary road handling, stable and secure, particularly appreciated on highways for serene travels.

technologie

  • Modern Technologies and Connectivity — Enjoy an intuitive touchscreen and Apple CarPlay/Android Auto compatibility, offering a connected and entertaining experience on board.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 508 I (shares many components), Audi A4 B9 (more rigorous but more classic), BMW 3 Series F30, Volkswagen CC / Arteon.
  • Asia: Buick Regal, Volkswagen Magotan, Honda Accord.

Verdict

The facelifted DS 5 (2015-2018) is a passion purchase for lovers of design and atypical interiors. If you are targeting a diesel, the 2.0 BlueHDi 180 EAT6 is by far the best choice: it offers the necessary torque to move this heavy sedan while enjoying an excellent automatic gearbox. However, buying a BlueHDi model (1.6 or 2.0) must be done with extreme vigilance regarding the AdBlue system: favor an example whose tank has already been replaced with an invoice. Avoid the Hybrid4 version, whose automated gearbox completely ruins the premium driving experience. Finally, make sure the critical Takata airbags recall has been carried out.
