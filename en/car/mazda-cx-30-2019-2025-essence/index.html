Summary

The Mazda CX-30 (Generation I, code DM) is a compact SUV positioned between the CX-3 (now withdrawn from many markets) and the CX-5. Based on the Skyactiv-Vehicle Architecture platform shared with the Mazda 3 (BP), it stands out for its near-premium positioning, a very high-quality interior finish, and driving pleasure superior to the segment average. It benefited from minor technological updates (infotainment, driving aids) in 2022 and 2024. In terms of reliability, Mazda remains true to its 'rightsizing' philosophy with large, very robust naturally aspirated engines, while innovating with the complex but fascinating compression ignition engine (Skyactiv-X). It is one of the most reliable SUVs in its category.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of naturally aspirated gasoline engines (Skyactiv-G)
  • Interior finish worthy of the premium segment (materials, assembly)
  • Very refined sound insulation
  • Driving pleasure (precise steering, excellent manual gearbox, smooth automatic gearbox)
  • Very appealing exterior design (Kodo design)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very fragile paint (frequent stone chips, especially on Soul Red Crystal)
  • Cramped rear legroom and average boot volume (430L)
  • Peripheral visibility (very thick C-pillars)
  • Infotainment system not touch-sensitive while driving (Mazda's ergonomic choice, but divisive)
  • 2.0 122 hp engine a bit underpowered for highway acceleration

Check before buying

  • Condition of the bodywork and front bumper (stone chip impacts due to thin paint).
  • Operation of the electric tailgate (cases of battery discharge or defective struts on early models).
  • History of software updates (Skyactiv-X engine management and SBS driving aids).
  • Oil level on 2.5 Turbo versions (North American market).
  • Parasitic noises (squeaks/rattles) around the B-pillar or instrument cluster cowl in cold weather.

Official recalls

  • December 2019 (SBS Emergency Braking): The Smart Brake Support system may activate unexpectedly by detecting a non-existent obstacle. Software reprogramming.
  • June 2020 (Electric Tailgate): Risk of tailgate falling if struts lose pressure, or battery discharge when stationary. Control module update.
  • November 2021 (Fuel Pump): Massive recall related to Denso supplier. The low-pressure pump impeller can deform and block the pump, leading to engine stalling. Pump replacement.
  • October 2021 (Tires): Defective tire valve that can lead to sudden pressure loss (concerns a small batch).

What sets it apart 6

Reliability

  • Exceptional Mechanical Reliability — The Mazda CX-30's Skyactiv-G and Skyactiv-X petrol engines are renowned for their robustness and longevity, offering superior peace of mind compared to the market average.

Comfort

  • Premium Interior Ambiance and Refined Soundproofing — Enjoy a cabin worthy of a higher segment, with high-quality materials, precise assembly, and exemplary soundproofing that transforms every journey into a moment of serenity.

Performance

  • Dynamic Driving Pleasure — The Mazda CX-30 offers an engaging driving experience thanks to precise steering, an excellent gearbox, and a balanced chassis, ensuring agility and control.
  • Smooth and Powerful Petrol Engines — Whether you choose the Skyactiv-G or Skyactiv-X, the CX-30 offers petrol engines providing remarkable smoothness and performance suitable for all types of journeys.

Design

  • Elegant and Valued Kodo Design — Its Kodo design, with its fluid lines and athletic silhouette, gives the CX-30 a distinctive and refined look that elegantly stands out on the road.

Economy

  • Innovative Skyactiv-X Engine Technology — Discover the Skyactiv-X engine, an engineering marvel that combines the benefits of a petrol engine with the efficiency of a diesel, for optimized fuel consumption without compromising driving pleasure.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Toyota C-HR (very reliable hybrid but less dynamic), Peugeot 2008 (more spacious but disastrous 1.2 PureTech reliability), Renault Captur, Volkswagen T-Roc.
  • North America: Subaru Crosstrek (excellent all-wheel drive), Honda HR-V (very spacious), Toyota Corolla Cross.
  • Asia: Honda Vezel/HR-V, Toyota Yaris Cross.

Verdict

The Mazda CX-30 is a highly recommended purchase. Going against the trend of 'downsizing' and fragile small 3-cylinder turbocharged engines, Mazda offers large displacement 4-cylinders whose reliability is significantly above the market average (especially compared to PureTech and TCE). If rear space is not your absolute priority, the CX-30 offers a near-premium experience without the associated maintenance costs. Opt for the 2.0 Skyactiv-G 150 hp or the new 2.5 e-Skyactiv G 140 hp versions for the best compromise. The Skyactiv-X is a fine engineering feat, reliable, but its higher purchase cost is not always offset by fuel savings.
