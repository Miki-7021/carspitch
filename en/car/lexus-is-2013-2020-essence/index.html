Lexus IS

III (XE30) · 2013 – 2020

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

Summary

The **third-generation Lexus IS (XE30)** marks a stylistic and technical turning point for the Japanese premium compact sedan. Introduced in 2013 and facelifted in 2016 (then heavily revised in 2020 in certain markets), it definitively abandons diesel engines (which caused problems on the previous XE20 generation) to focus on gasoline and hybrid powertrains. In Europe, the **IS 300h** hybrid version accounted for the overwhelming majority of sales. In North American and Asian markets, naturally aspirated V6s (IS 250, IS 350) and the 4-cylinder turbo (IS 200t / IS 300) dominated. The XE30 is renowned for its exceptional build quality, dynamic chassis, and overall reliability that vastly outperforms its German competitors (ADAC breakdown rate of only 1.2/1000 vehicles/year).

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability (ADAC breakdown rate: 1.2/1000, What Car?: 4.5/5)
  • Finish and build quality (interior materials aging very well)
  • Extremely refined hybrid system (IS 300h), economical and without heavy maintenance
  • Very robust automatic gearboxes (6- or 8-speed Aisin) and e-CVT
  • Dynamic road handling and balanced chassis (especially in F Sport trim)
  • Absence of diesel-related problems (DPF/FAP, EGR, injectors) from the previous generation

Weaknesses 5

  • Obsolete, slow infotainment system and highly criticized ergonomics of the joystick/touchpad (Remote Touch)
  • Limited rear seat space and headroom for taller passengers
  • Reduced trunk volume on the hybrid version (batteries) and non-flat folding rear bench
  • Premature wear of the front tires on the inner edge (design flaw of the suspension bushings)
  • High fuel consumption on V6 versions (IS 250 and IS 350)

Check before buying

  • Front tire wear: Turn the wheels fully to inspect the inner tread (often smooth while the outside looks new).
  • Multimedia screen: Check for scratches or delamination of the anti-reflective coating (common cosmetic issue).
  • Air conditioning servomotors: Listen for a clicking noise (tick-tick-tick) behind the dashboard when changing the temperature (worn actuators).
  • Water pump (V6): Inspect for pink traces (Toyota coolant) around the water pump and pulleys.
  • Hybrid health check: On IS 300h, demand a high-voltage battery test (can be done at a Lexus dealership) to extend the hybrid system warranty up to 10 years.

Verdict

The third-generation Lexus IS (XE30) is one of the most reliable premium sedans on the used market. By turning its back on diesel, Lexus eliminated the main sources of breakdowns from the previous generation. **The IS 300h is the most rational and safest choice in Europe**, offering unmatched hybrid reliability and negligible maintenance costs. For fans of traditional combustion engines, **the IS 350 (3.5L V6)** is a monument of reliability and driving pleasure, although rare in Europe. The IS 200t offers a good modern compromise. The only real flaws of this car are its dated infotainment system, its limited rear space, and the asymmetrical wear of the front tires (easily correctable with reinforced bushings). A highly recommended purchase for anyone seeking peace of mind.
