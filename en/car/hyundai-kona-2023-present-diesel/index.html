Hyundai Kona

II (SX2) · 2023 – Présent

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The second-generation Hyundai Kona (code SX2) marks a major break from its predecessor. Designed with an "EV-first" approach (developed first as an electric vehicle before being adapted for internal combustion engines), it has grown considerably (+15 cm in length) to position itself at the upper edge of the urban SUV (B-SUV) segment, finally offering rear passenger space and trunk volume worthy of family use.

**⚠️ EXPERT'S CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:** Although your search targets a diesel powertrain, it is imperative to specify that **Hyundai has definitively abandoned diesel (1.6 CRDi) for this second generation (SX2)**, across all global markets. This decision is part of the brand's electrification strategy and anticipation of Euro 7 standards. For high-mileage drivers seeking the equivalent in terms of running costs, Hyundai positions the **1.6 GDI Hybrid (HEV)** version as the direct and natural alternative to diesel. If you absolutely insist on a Kona Diesel, you will have to look to the used car market for the first generation (Kona OS, produced until 2023).

Strengths 5

  • Significantly increased cabin space (rear legroom and 466 L trunk).
  • Advanced onboard technology (panoramic dual screen, OTA updates).
  • Highly refined and fuel-efficient hybrid (HEV) powertrain, advantageously replacing diesel.
  • 5-year unlimited mileage manufacturer's warranty (Europe) or 10 years/100k miles (USA).
  • Significantly improved sound insulation compared to the first generation.

Weaknesses 4

  • Complete disappearance of the Diesel offering (detrimental for some very high-mileage highway drivers).
  • Intrusive ergonomics of driving aids (ADAS), particularly the overspeed alert which is difficult to deactivate.
  • Significantly higher prices compared to the previous generation.
  • Hard plastics still very present in the lower part of the cabin.

Check before buying

  • On HEV versions: smoothness of 6DCT gearbox shifts at low speed.
  • On EV versions: verify that recall campaigns concerning the ICCU have been carried out.
  • Infotainment system: ensure the latest software version is installed (black screen bugs on early 2023 models).
  • Body panel alignment (some early production defects reported from the factory in 2023).

Economy

  • Ultra-Efficient Hybrid (HEV) Powertrain — The 1.6 GDI Hybrid (HEV) engine is a powerful and very fuel-efficient alternative, especially in urban and road driving, offering optimized consumption and a smooth ride, free from the constraints of older diesels.

Reliability

Comfort

Safety

Handling

  • Safe and Balanced Road Handling — Experience predictable and stable road handling, with well-controlled body roll and precise steering, offering an excellent balance between comfort and dynamism for daily driving.

Performance

Verdict

The Hyundai Kona II (SX2) is a mature vehicle. Although the absence of a diesel powertrain may frustrate purists of this fuel, the **1.6 GDI Hybrid (HEV)** version stands out as a formidable alternative: it is extremely reliable, very fuel-efficient in urban and road driving, and free from the DPF or EGR valve issues inherent in modern diesels. The pure internal combustion versions (1.0 T-GDI and 2.0 MPI) are robust and benefit from Hyundai's expertise. It is a highly recommended purchase, provided one accepts the transition to hybrid or electric for high mileage.
