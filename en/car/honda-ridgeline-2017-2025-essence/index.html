Honda Ridgeline

II · 2017 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Honda models

Summary

The second-generation Honda Ridgeline is an atypical midsize pick-up on the North American market. Unlike its competitors that use a ladder frame (Body-on-Frame), the Ridgeline is based on a unibody platform shared with the Honda Pilot and Passport. This architecture gives it ride comfort, handling, and soundproofing worthy of a premium SUV, while eliminating the perforating rust issues of separate chassis. It stands out with practical innovations like the trunk integrated under the bed floor (*In-Bed Trunk*) and a dual-action tailgate. Facelifted in 2021 to adopt a more rugged look, it is acclaimed by *Consumer Reports* and *J.D. Power* as the most reliable pick-up in its class for daily use, although its towing and pure off-road capabilities are lower than those of traditional pick-ups.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability (Leader J.D. Power VDS 2023 and Consumer Reports)
  • Unibody chassis offering SUV-like ride comfort and handling
  • Absence of a ladder frame: no risk of major structural rust
  • Waterproof trunk integrated under the bed (In-Bed Trunk) unique on the market
  • Highly effective i-VTM4 AWD system for winter conditions
  • Spacious and very well-finished cabin for the category

Weaknesses 5

  • Limited towing capacity (2,270 kg / 5,000 lbs) compared to competitors (often > 3,000 kg)
  • Limited off-road capabilities (ground clearance, suspension travel)
  • Aging, slow infotainment system with a less intuitive interface
  • Decent fuel consumption but no hybrid option available
  • Not officially available on the European market

Check before buying

  • Tailgate wiring harness: premature wear causing the loss of the backup camera (subject to recall).
  • Maintenance history: it is imperative to check the rear differential fluid changes (AWD).
  • Timing belt: to be replaced around 160,000 km (interference engine, destructive failure if it breaks).
  • Verify that the recalls concerning the fuel pump have been performed.

Official recalls

Although the Ridgeline is ranked among the most reliable vehicles, several recalls (often shared with the Honda Pilot, Passport, and Acura MDX) have been issued by the NHTSA.

NHTSA (United States) - Main recalls:

  • Tailgate wiring harness / Backup camera (Nov 2022, May 2024): Opening/closing the tailgate can damage the wiring harness, leading to a failure of the backup camera.
  • Fuel pump (Jan 2019, Mar 2021, Dec 2023): Risk of cracking (exposure to car wash acids) or internal failure of the low-pressure pump, which can cause engine stalling.
  • Connecting rod bearings (Nov 2023): Manufacturing error on certain 2017-2019 models that can lead to premature wear and engine failure.
  • Passenger classification sensor / Airbag (Feb 2024, May 2026 [sic]): Possible failure of the passenger seat weight sensor, affecting airbag deployment.
  • Timing belt (Apr 2019): On certain 2019 models, the belt teeth can separate, risking engine damage.
  • Structure / Hood (Nov 2021): The hood striker can become damaged and open while driving.
  • Steering (Mar 2024): Inner tie rod of the steering rack that can loosen (2023 models).

What sets it apart 12

Comfort

  • Unibody Chassis Delivers SUV Comfort — The unibody platform shared with the Pilot and Passport gives the Ridgeline a smooth ride, secure handling and quiet cabin that feel closer to a premium SUV than a traditional truck.
  • Spacious, Well-Finished Cabin — The interior is roomy and very well finished for the category, giving occupants a comfortable environment suited to daily family use.
  • Quiet Engine Operation — The 3.5L V6 is noted for silent operation, contributing to a calmer cabin experience during routine driving and longer trips.

Reliability

  • No Frame Rust Structural Risk — Because it uses a unibody structure instead of a ladder frame, there is no separate chassis to suffer major structural perforation rust, simplifying long-term ownership.
  • Proven 3.5L V6 Longevity — The naturally aspirated J35Y6 engine is described as very robust with linear power and excellent longevity, giving buyers confidence in long-term mechanical durability.
  • Top Reliability Ratings From Experts — Consumer Reports and J.D. Power recognize the Ridgeline as the most reliable pickup in its category for daily use, supporting lower ownership worry.

Practicality

  • Unique Watertight In-Bed Trunk — A sealed storage compartment integrated under the bed floor keeps tools, gear or groceries dry and out of sight, a practical feature not offered by rival midsize pickups.
  • Dual-Action Tailgate Adds Versatility — The tailgate opens either downward or to the side, making it easier to load and reach items in the bed from different angles during everyday use.

Safety

  • i-VTM4 AWD For Winter Conditions — The i-VTM4 all-wheel-drive system is described as very effective in winter conditions, helping the truck maintain traction on slippery roads.

Handling

  • Maneuverable Like A SUV — The monocoque architecture provides SUV-like maneuverability, making the truck easier to handle and place in everyday driving than traditional body-on-frame pickups.

Economy

  • Rational Choice For Most Buyers — For buyers who do not need to tow more than 2.2 tonnes or tackle extreme off-roading, the Ridgeline is presented as the most rational and intelligent pickup choice.

Performance

  • Moderate Towing Matches Light Use — With a 2,270 kg towing capacity, it suits moderate towing and light utility work, covering the needs of many daily and family users without excess.

Alternatives

North America:

  • Toyota Tacoma: The king of the category, ladder frame, indestructible off-road, but spartan comfort and room for improvement in the driving position.
  • Chevrolet Colorado / GMC Canyon: Excellent towing capabilities, good engines, but lower overall reliability.
  • Ford Ranger: Good power/towing compromise, but lower comfort than the Ridgeline.
  • Nissan Frontier: Robust V6, classic design, good value for money.

Europe:

  • *The Ridgeline has no direct equivalent in Europe.* The alternatives (Ford Ranger, Toyota Hilux, VW Amarok) are all ladder-frame pick-ups with diesel engines.

Verdict

The Honda Ridgeline (Gen II) is the most rational and intelligent choice for 90% of pick-up buyers. If you do not need to tow more than 2.2 tons or do extreme off-roading, it outclasses all its competitors in terms of comfort, road handling, and practical aspects (In-Bed Trunk). Powered by Honda's legendary J35 V6, its mechanical reliability is excellent, confirmed by *Consumer Reports* and *J.D. Power*. You simply need to ensure that the specific maintenance (timing belt, AWD system fluid changes) has been scrupulously followed and that the recalls (notably the fuel pump and tailgate wiring harness) have been addressed. It is a highly recommended purchase for daily and family use.
