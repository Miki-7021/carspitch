Summary

The third-generation Subaru Crosstrek (GU), launched for the 2024 model year, globally replaces the 'XV' nameplate (previously used in Europe and Asia). Based on a stiffened version (+10%) of the SGP (Subaru Global Platform), it retains what makes the brand successful: standard permanent *Symmetrical AWD* and an excellent ground clearance of 22 cm (8.7 inches). In Europe, it focuses exclusively on the 2.0 e-Boxer mild hybrid engine, reworked to meet emission standards (power reduced to 136 hp). Although very recent, it inherits the excellent reliability track record of the previous generation, with a particularly high resale value and above-average off-road capabilities for the compact SUV segment.

Strengths 6

  • Standard Symmetrical AWD, formidably effective
  • Exceptional ground clearance for the category (22 cm)
  • Overall mechanical reliability inherited from previous generations (highly rated by J.D. Power and Consumer Reports)
  • Suspension comfort and filtering of irregularities
  • Cutting-edge active safety (highly capable standard EyeSight)
  • Excellent resale value

Weaknesses 5

  • e-Boxer engine (136 hp) sorely lacking in dynamism (0 to 100 km/h in 10.8 s)
  • Lineartronic CVT gearbox that tends to make the engine 'rev out' during hard acceleration
  • Disappointing fuel consumption of the e-Boxer for a hybrid (often > 7.5 L/100 km in real-world conditions)
  • Average trunk volume (315 liters on the e-Boxer due to the battery)
  • Large central touchscreen prone to glare and fingerprints

Check before buying

  • Check if early recalls (driveshaft, wiring harness) have been performed at the dealership.
  • Condition of the underbody: the ground clearance invites off-roading, check for the absence of impacts under the vehicle.
  • Behavior of the CVT gearbox: it must be smooth, without jerks at low speeds.
  • Tire wear: on a permanent AWD system, it is imperative to change all 4 tires simultaneously to preserve the differential.

United States (NHTSA) - 2024 Models * [2023-11-09] Driveshaft (NHTSA 23V754000): On certain 2024 Crosstreks, the outer race of the front driveshaft assemblies may crack and break, resulting in a loss of motive power. What sets it apart 7

Safety

  • Advanced and Recognized Safety — The Crosstrek offers maximum protection, attested by its 5 Euro NCAP stars (89% adult, 87% child) and its IIHS Top Safety Pick distinction. Its latest-generation EyeSight system ensures proactive and high-performance driving assistance.

Performance

  • Control and Serenity on All Roads — Thanks to its rigidified SGP chassis and Symmetrical AWD, the Crosstrek offers balanced and predictable road handling, ensuring excellent stability and appreciable driving comfort, even in varied conditions.
  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — The Crosstrek's braking system stands out for its solid and predictable performance, offering excellent pedal feel and competitive stopping distances for its category, for increased safety in all circumstances.

Reliability

  • Legendary and Proven Reliability — The Crosstrek perpetuates Subaru's reputation for mechanical reliability excellence. Its e-Boxer hybrid system has been proven since 2019 and is highly rated, ensuring lasting peace of mind.

Practicality

  • Unmatched Versatility and Off-Road Capabilities — With its standard, highly efficient Symmetrical AWD and an exceptional ground clearance of 22 cm, the Crosstrek is a true Swiss Army knife, offering off-road capabilities superior to the average in its category.

Comfort

  • Optimized Driving Comfort — The Crosstrek benefits from remarkable suspension comfort, effectively filtering out road irregularities. Its improved sound insulation contributes to a serene and pleasant driving experience on all journeys.

Economy

  • A Lasting and Wise Investment — Thanks to its robustness, recognized reliability, and strong market demand, the Subaru Crosstrek maintains excellent resale value, making its acquisition a wise and lasting investment.

Alternatives

Europe * Toyota C-HR (Hybrid): Significantly lower fuel consumption, bold design, but less roomy and without such a capable AWD system. * Honda HR-V (e:HEV): Very spacious (magic seats), efficient hybrid system, but no AWD. * Nissan Qashqai (e-Power): Electric driving experience, more spacious, but slightly more family-oriented positioning.

North America * Mazda CX-30: Premium finish, dynamic driving, but less rear space and inferior off-road capabilities. * Toyota Corolla Cross: Hybrid option available, very reliable, but ground clearance and AWD system less capable in extreme conditions.

Verdict

The third-generation Subaru Crosstrek (GU) is a sensible choice for drivers operating in mountainous, snowy, or rural regions. Its AWD and ground clearance make it a true Swiss Army knife, much more capable off-road than 95% of its competitors.

Regarding the e-Boxer hybrid engine (Europe): It shines with its smoothness and reliability (the system is proven), but disappoints with its modest performance and fuel consumption that remains that of a conventional combustion engine. It is a vehicle to be driven smoothly. If you reside in North America, the naturally aspirated 2.5L engine is by far the best choice in the lineup. Despite a few minor early production recalls (typical for a new generation), overall reliability promises to be excellent, right in line with previous generations.
