Honda Jazz

III (GK) · 2015 – 2020

BUY Reliability 91/100 — excellent

All Honda models

Summary

The third-generation Honda Jazz (code name GK, or GP for the hybrid) is a versatile city car (B-segment) renowned for its exceptional spaciousness thanks to its central fuel tank and famous "Magic Seats". While the European market only received petrol engines (1.3 and 1.5 i-VTEC), this report places particular emphasis on the 1.5 i-DTEC diesel engine, developed under the *Earth Dreams Technology* label and primarily sold in the Indian market and certain emerging markets. This all-aluminum diesel block stands out for its record-breaking frugality, although it suffers from imperfect sound insulation. The Jazz GK received a facelift in late 2017, bringing aesthetic touches, improved sound insulation, and the introduction of the 1.5 i-VTEC engine in Europe. Overall, it is one of the most reliable city cars of its generation.

Strengths 5

  • Unrivaled spaciousness and modularity (Magic Seats, trunk volume from 354L to 1314L)
  • Exceptional overall reliability, typical of Honda
  • Extremely low fuel consumption of the 1.5 i-DTEC engine (often below 4.5 L/100 km in real-world driving)
  • Sound and safe road handling
  • Rear legroom worthy of a larger sedan

Weaknesses 5

  • Poor sound insulation, particularly noticeable with the 1.5 i-DTEC diesel engine (very prominent knocking in the cabin)
  • Infotainment system (Honda Connect) slow, dated, and prone to bugs
  • Fairly firm suspension comfort, especially over road imperfections at low speeds
  • Quality of some interior plastics (hard and scratch-prone)
  • Diesel engine unavailable in the European market (reserved for India and other specific markets)

Check before buying

  • On the 1.5 i-DTEC (Diesel): Trip history. Avoid models that have only been driven in the city (risk of clogged DPF). Check for absence of black smoke during acceleration.
  • On the 1.5 i-VTEC: Listen to the engine during a cold start (after a night). A brief rattling noise indicates a worn VTC actuator.
  • Fuel pump: Check if the vehicle is affected by the global Denso recall (defective impeller).
  • Water ingress: Lift the trunk mat to check for moisture (tailgate or taillight seals sometimes faulty, though rarer than on the previous generation).
  • Start/Stop button: On higher trims, the push button may show signs of weakness (intermittent contacts).

What sets it apart 7

Practicality

  • Unrivaled Practicality and Modularity — The Honda Jazz III stands out with its exceptional interior space and unparalleled modularity, notably thanks to the famous 'Magic Seats'. It offers remarkable versatility to adapt to all your transport needs, from passengers to bulky items.

Reliability

  • Legendary Honda Reliability — Renowned for its robustness and durability, the Honda Jazz III, and its 1.5 i-DTEC engine, embodies the typical reliability of the brand. A serene choice for thousands of kilometers.

Economy

  • Record Fuel Economy of the 1.5 i-DTEC Diesel Engine — The 1.5 i-DTEC Earth Dreams Technology engine is a model of frugality. It offers exceptionally low fuel consumption, guaranteeing extremely low running costs and a long range.

Safety

  • Optimal Euro NCAP Certified Safety — The Honda Jazz III ensures maximum protection for all its occupants, as evidenced by its excellent results in the most rigorous safety tests.
  • Efficient and Controlled Braking — Enjoy increased confidence thanks to reliable and predictable braking performance. The Jazz III guarantees safe and controlled stops in all conditions.
  • Agile and Safe Road Handling — The Honda Jazz III offers healthy and secure road handling. Its agility in the city and stability on the road provide a pleasant and reassuring driving experience.

Comfort

  • Generous Rear Space — Rear passengers benefit from exceptional legroom, worthy of a higher-category sedan, ensuring optimal comfort even on long journeys.

Verdict

The Honda Jazz III (GK) is an exceptionally rational purchase choice. It is one of the most reliable and practical cars in its category. Regarding the 1.5 i-DTEC (diesel) engine, although it is unavailable in Europe, it represents a formidable tool for high-mileage drivers in markets where it is sold (such as India). Its aluminum block is indestructible if well-maintained, but one must accept a rustic noise level and ensure it is not clogged by short urban trips. For Europe and North America, the 1.3 and 1.5 i-VTEC petrol engines offer almost absolute peace of mind. A highly recommended purchase, provided that the fuel pump recall has been carried out on recent models.
