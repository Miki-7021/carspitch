Peugeot 2008

I · 2013 – 2019

NEGOTIATE Reliability 48/100 — faible

Summary

The **first-generation Peugeot 2008 (A94)** marked the lion brand's successful entry into the highly competitive urban SUV (B-SUV) segment. Based on the 208 I's PF1 platform, it indirectly replaced the 207 SW estate. It stands out for its dynamic road handling, its famous *i-Cockpit* (small steering wheel, raised instrument cluster), and a refined finish for the category.

A significant facelift took place in **2016**, giving it a more adventurous look (vertical grille, wheel arch extensions) and introducing the excellent EAT6 automatic gearbox, replacing the disastrous ETG automated manual gearbox.

However, this model's career is heavily marred by the **systemic problems of its flagship engines**: the 1.2 PureTech (petrol) and the 1.6/1.5 BlueHDi (diesel). Buying a used 2008 I today requires absolute vigilance regarding its maintenance history.

Strengths 5

  • First-class road handling (Peugeot comfort/dynamism compromise).
  • Original and upscale interior presentation (i-Cockpit).
  • Excellent EAT6 automatic gearbox (Aisin) available since the facelift (2016).
  • Compact size (4.16 m) ideal for the city while offering a decent boot (350 L).
  • Contained weight promoting agility and fuel consumption.

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic reliability of 1.2 PureTech petrol engines (wet belt).
  • Reliability of the AdBlue emission control system on BlueHDi diesels.
  • ETG automated manual gearbox (before 2016) slow, jerky, and unreliable.
  • Multimedia system (SMEG/SMEG+) slow, unintuitive, and prone to bugs.
  • i-Cockpit driving position that does not suit all body types (steering wheel obscuring the instrument cluster).

Check before buying

  • **Maintenance history (PureTech):** Demand invoices. Has the belt been changed? Has the oil strainer been cleaned? Have oil changes been done on time?
  • **Belt condition (PureTech):** Open the oil filler cap and visually inspect the belt (cracks, fraying).
  • **AdBlue system (BlueHDi):** Check for the absence of a 'Engine Fault' or 'UREA' warning light on the dashboard. Ask if the tank has already been replaced.
  • **Touchscreen:** Test screen responsiveness, Bluetooth, and GPS. Frequent spontaneous reboots are common.
  • **Front axle:** Listen for suspension noises on degraded roads (fragile shock absorber mounts or anti-roll bar bushes).

Official recalls

  • - **November 2020 (Code JZR/JRP):** Massive recall of 1.2 PureTech (produced from March 2013 to April 2017). Belt width check, oil strainer cleaning, and vacuum pump replacement if necessary (risk of braking loss).
  • - **December 2022:** Extension of the PureTech recall for models produced until August 2018 with ECU update to alert earlier of an oil pressure drop.
  • - **February 2016:** Recall concerning the handbrake mechanism which can loosen (models produced until November 2014).
  • - **October 2019:** Risk of fuel leak on 1.2 PureTech engines due to hose friction (2018 models).
  • - **Silent campaigns (BlueHDi):** Partial coverage (depending on age/mileage) for the replacement of the defective AdBlue tank.

Verdict

The Peugeot 2008 I is an excellent urban SUV on paper: beautiful, pleasant to drive, and practical. Unfortunately, **the choice of engine is a minefield**.

If you are looking for a **petrol** version, the 1.6 VTi 120 is the least risky choice, although it consumes a bit of oil. **Avoid the 1.2 PureTech** unless the seller provides an impeccable maintenance record, a recent invoice for timing belt replacement AND oil strainer cleaning.

For **diesel**, prefer the older 1.6 e-HDi (92 or 115 hp), which are robust and lack the capricious AdBlue system that ruins the reliability of more recent BlueHDi versions. Finally, avoid the ETG automated manual gearbox at all costs; prefer the manual gearbox or the excellent EAT6 automatic.
