Audi Q7

4M (2015–2025) · 2015 – 2025

Summary

The second-generation Audi Q7 (Type 4M) is based on the Volkswagen Group's MLB Evo platform (shared with the Porsche Cayenne, VW Touareg, and Bentley Bentayga). Launched in 2015, it marked a break from its predecessor with a drastic weight reduction (up to -325 kg), significantly improving its dynamism and efficiency. This large luxury SUV (available in 5 or 7 seats) has undergone two major facelifts: a first in 2019 (new dashboard with dual touchscreens, 48V mild hybridization) and a second in 2024 (aesthetic tweaks, HD Matrix LED headlights). Although the European market was initially dominated by diesel (TDI), gasoline (TFSI) and plug-in hybrid (TFSI e) powertrains have taken over, driven by anti-pollution standards and global demand (particularly in North America and Asia).

Strengths 6

  • Imperial ride comfort (especially with adaptive air suspension)
  • Very high level of sound insulation (double glazing)
  • Interior finish and assembly quality (segment benchmark)
  • Gigantic interior space and modularity (usable 7 seats)
  • Impressive road handling for its size (optional rear-wheel steering)
  • Intrinsic mechanical reliability of gasoline engines (excluding 48V peripherals)

Weaknesses 5

  • Bulky size in the city (over 5 meters long, 1.97m wide)
  • Disastrous reliability of the 48V starter-generator on post-2019 models
  • Cost of maintenance, insurance, and wear parts (20 to 22-inch tires, brakes)
  • MMI infotainment system bugs (black screens, sluggishness on 2015-2017 models)
  • Very high weight (often > 2.2 tons), penalizing agility and city fuel consumption

Check before buying

  • Air suspension: check that it raises and lowers without suspicious compressor noise, and that it doesn't sag after a night of parking (air spring leak).
  • 48V system (2019+ models): demand the history to know if the starter-generator has been replaced. An 'Electrical system fault' message on the dashboard is a deal-breaker.
  • Running gear: asymmetrical tire wear (complex geometry to adjust), knocking from front wishbone bushings (rapid wear due to weight).
  • Onboard electronics: test all cameras (360°), the Virtual Cockpit, the electric deployment of the 3rd row of seats.
  • V6 engines (TFSI and TDI): inspect the engine's 'V' for coolant leaks (cracked water housing/thermostat).

Braking

  • Absolute Control: Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Equipped with an excellent braking system, the Q7 offers remarkable stopping power, capable of going from 70 mph to a complete stop in just 168 feet (51.2 meters), ensuring optimal safety in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Surprising Dynamism for a Large SUV — Despite its imposing size, the Audi Q7 offers remarkably composed road behavior, with precise steering, excellent body control, and high levels of grip, ensuring a serene and agile drive.

Comfort

  • First-Class Travel: Exceptional Comfort and Sound Insulation — Enjoy imperial ride comfort and very high-level sound insulation, transforming every journey into a luxury experience. The interior finish quality is a segment benchmark.

Practicality

  • Space and Modularity: The Ultimate Family SUV — With its gigantic interior space and exemplary modularity, including 7 usable seats, the Audi Q7 is the ideal companion for large families and trips requiring significant cargo volume.

Performance

  • Refined and Powerful Gasoline Engines — The TFSI gasoline engines of the Audi Q7 offer a perfect combination of power, flexibility, and refinement, guaranteeing strong acceleration and a first-class driving experience.

Reliability

  • Mechanical Robustness of Gasoline Engines — Audi Q7 gasoline engines, particularly the 3.0 TFSI supercharged V6, are renowned for their great mechanical robustness and intrinsic reliability, ensuring long-term peace of mind.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: BMW X5 (G05 - benchmark for dynamism and PHEV), Mercedes-Benz GLE (V167 - comfort and space), Volvo XC90 (T8 hybrid, 7 seats), Porsche Cayenne (sportier, same platform). - **North America**: Acura MDX, Lexus RX/TX, Genesis GV80, Lincoln Aviator. - **Asia / Emerging Markets**: Volkswagen Touareg (same platform, cheaper), Lexus RX, Toyota Land Cruiser (more rugged but indestructible).

Verdict

The Audi Q7 4M is a formidable flagship, capable of transporting 7 people in absolute comfort. If you are targeting a gasoline engine, the **3.0 TFSI supercharged (2015-2019)** is the most sensible choice in terms of pure reliability, although it is rare in Europe (frequent imports). The **55 TFSI (2019+)** versions offer superior driving pleasure and cutting-edge technology, but the Damocles' sword of the 48V hybrid system dictates buying a model under warranty or one that has already had the starter-generator replaced. Avoid models with a vague service history: the technical complexity of this SUV does not forgive any negligence.
