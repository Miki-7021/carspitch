Summary

Expert's Presentation and Clarification

**ATTENTION - Important Clarification:** As an independent expert, I must correct two common confusions regarding your request. 1. **The Generation:** The Fiat Panda sold between 2021 and 2025 is not the IV generation (project 320 does not officially exist in this form; the true 4th generation is the *Grande Panda* launched in late 2024 on the Stellantis platform). The 2021-2025 models are actually the **continuation of the Panda III (project 319)**, updated with mild hybridization (MHEV) and recently renamed *Pandina*. 2. **The Diesel:** **The diesel engine (1.3 MultiJet) was definitively removed from the Panda catalog in 2018** due to anti-pollution standards (Euro 6d-Temp). **Therefore, there is no Fiat Panda diesel produced between 2021 and 2025.**

This report therefore covers the market reality for the Panda during the 2021-2025 period (dominated by the 1.0 FireFly MHEV), while also including a historical analysis of the 1.3 MultiJet for buyers who might opt for older used cars out of necessity.

  • Maintenance and insurance costs among the lowest on the market

Weaknesses 5

  • Basic onboard technology despite the 7-inch touchscreen

Check before buying

  • On the 1.0 MHEV: Condition of the starter-alternator belt (cracks) and Stop&Start operation.
  • On the 0.9 TwinAir: Cold knocking noises (sign of a tired MultiAir module) and clear oil change history.
  • On older 1.3 Diesels: DPF regeneration history (via OBD scanner) and timing chain noise on startup.
  • Underbody on 4x4 and Cross versions (traces of off-road impacts).
  • Premature wear of front suspension bushings (noises over speed bumps).

Verdict

Expert's Verdict

If you are absolutely looking for a **2021-2025 Fiat Panda Diesel, the verdict is simple: it's impossible**, as this model has not been available new since 2018. If a seller offers you such a vehicle, it is a model year error or fraud.

However, if you are evaluating the **2021-2025 Panda with its 1.0 FireFly MHEV engine**, it is a **RECOMMENDED BUY** for urban use. It is a rustic, ultra-proven car, whose early flaws have been ironed out over 10 years. It is economical to buy and to run. Avoid the 0.9 TwinAir engine on the used market unless you have an absolute need for 4x4 transmission and an impeccable service history.
