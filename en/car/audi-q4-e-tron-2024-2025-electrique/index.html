Audi Q4 e-tron

I facelift (F4Z) · 2024 – 2025

BUY Reliability 83/100 — bon

Summary

The Audi Q4 e-tron (F4Z) benefited from a major technical update for the 2024 model year (often referred to as a 'technical facelift' although the aesthetics remain unchanged). Based on the Volkswagen Group's MEB platform, this evolution corrects the main flaws of the first iteration (2021-2023). The introduction of the new rear motor (code name APP550) transforms the vehicle: power jumps (from 204 to 286 hp for the base model with the large battery), efficiency is greatly improved, and DC fast charging power climbs up to 175 kW on quattro versions. The infotainment system also receives a welcome software update, finally integrating battery pre-conditioning. It is a refined premium compact SUV, although its weight remains substantial.

Strengths 5

  • Efficiency and performance of the new APP550 motor (45 e-tron)
  • Improved fast charging speed (up to 175 kW on quattro)
  • Excellent roominess thanks to the long wheelbase (MEB platform)
  • Very meticulous soundproofing, worthy of the premium segment
  • Battery pre-conditioning finally available as standard

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high weight (over 2.1 tons) affecting agility
  • Some interior plastics (lower doors) unworthy of the Audi badge
  • Questionable ergonomics of the touch controls on the steering wheel
  • High prices and still expensive options policy
  • Native Audi charging network less integrated than the Tesla ecosystem

Check before buying

  • Condition of the 12V battery (unexplained deep discharges have been reported, linked to the OCDC onboard charger)
  • Tire wear (instant torque and weight prematurely wear out the tires, especially at the rear on RWD versions)
  • Stability of the infotainment system (verify that software version 4.0 or higher is installed)
  • Operation of the charging port locking mechanism (cable sometimes stuck)

Verdict

The 2024 update of the Audi Q4 e-tron (F4Z) transforms the experience. By replacing the sluggish 40 e-tron with the new 45 e-tron equipped with the APP550 motor, Audi corrects the main flaw of its SUV: the lack of dynamism compared to its weight. Efficiency also improves, making this model highly relevant for high-mileage drivers. The reliability of the MEB platform has generally stabilized, although peripheral issues (12V battery, onboard charger) are the subject of recent recalls. **Verdict:** The Q4 45 e-tron is the version to favor. It is a recommendable purchase, provided you ensure that all software updates and recalls (especially on the OCDC charger) have been performed.
