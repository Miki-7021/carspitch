Summary

The BMW 2 Series Coupé (G42) represents a return to form for BMW purists, retaining the classic front-engine, rear-wheel-drive layout (CLAR platform) in a compact segment where most competitors have shifted to front-wheel drive. Introduced in late 2021, it boasts exceptional chassis rigidity, near 50:50 weight distribution, and high build quality. In 2023, the interior received a significant update with the introduction of the curved dual-screen display (iDrive 8).

While this guide covers all powertrains, a special focus is placed on the 220d (Diesel). Equipped with the B47 2.0-liter turbodiesel engine and a 48V Mild-Hybrid (MHEV) system, the 220d is an outstanding long-distance cruiser, offering a rare blend of high torque, exceptional fuel economy, and robust reliability. It is widely considered one of the best diesel coupes remaining on the European market.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of the B-series modular engines (B47, B48, B58)
  • The 220d offers an unbeatable mix of torque (400 Nm) and fuel efficiency
  • Superb ZF 8-speed automatic transmission (standard across the range)
  • Excellent chassis dynamics and RWD architecture (CLAR platform)
  • High-quality interior materials and class-leading infotainment (iDrive 7/8)

Weaknesses 5

  • Heavy curb weight for a compact coupe (often exceeding 1,600 kg)
  • Cramped rear seats and difficult ingress/egress for rear passengers
  • No manual transmission option available (reserved only for the G87 M2)
  • Diesel 220d is not available in North American markets
  • Controversial exterior styling (subjective, but widely debated)

Check before buying

  • DPF soot accumulation on the 220d if the car lacks highway mileage history
  • Coolant levels on B48 and B58 engines (check for white residue around the expansion tank cap)
  • Uneven tire wear, particularly on the inner edges of the front tires (common with M Sport suspension setups)
  • Functionality of the iDrive 8 curved display (ensure the latest OTA software updates are installed to prevent blank screens)
  • Condition of the 48V MHEV battery on the 220d (diagnostics check recommended before purchase)

Official recalls

  • August 2024 (Recall 0034670200 / 0034680200): Integrated Braking System (Continental module) may fail, leading to a loss of power braking assist and ABS/DSC functions. Affects many 2023-2024 BMW models, including the G42.
  • February 2023: Software glitch causing the digital instrument cluster to go blank. Fixed via Over-The-Air (OTA) update.
  • December 2021: Potential issue with the steering gear tie rod not being tightened to the correct specification during assembly. Affects early production models.

What sets it apart 7

braking

  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — The robust braking system of the BMW 2 Series Coupe ensures short, consistent, and fade-resistant stops, providing total control in all circumstances.

Performance

  • Exceptional Dynamic Behavior — Enjoy a pure driving experience thanks to the CLAR rear-wheel-drive platform, an ideal 50:50 weight distribution, and remarkable road holding, guaranteeing agility and pleasure.

safety

  • Enhanced Active Safety — With its balanced chassis, precise steering, and high grip levels, the 2 Series Coupe offers confident control and exceptional agility, actively contributing to your safety on all roads.

reliability

  • Proven Mechanical Reliability — The 220d (B47) diesel engine is renowned for its robustness and maturity, making this coupe an ultra-reliable and durable choice for long journeys.

economy

  • Remarkable Fuel Efficiency — The 220d powertrain, combined with 48V mild-hybrid technology, offers astonishing fuel consumption, often below 5L/100km on the highway, for extended range.

comfort

  • Perfect ZF 8-speed Automatic Transmission — The ZF 8-speed automatic transmission is a benchmark for smoothness, responsiveness, and reliability, ensuring imperceptible gear changes and superior driving pleasure.
  • Premium Finish Quality and Ergonomics — The 2 Series Coupe's interior benefits from significantly improved assembly quality and intuitive ergonomics, notably with the iDrive system and its physical rotary controller, for optimal daily comfort.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Audi TT (discontinued but strong used market rival), Mercedes-Benz CLE (larger, more GT-focused), Porsche 718 Cayman (more expensive, mid-engined).
  • North America: Toyota GR Supra (shares the exact same platform, B48/B58 engines, and interior tech), Ford Mustang, Nissan Z.
  • Asia / Emerging Markets: Toyota GR86 / Subaru BRZ (cheaper, lighter, but less premium and less powerful).

Verdict

The BMW 2 Series G42 is a triumph of modern automotive engineering, successfully blending old-school RWD dynamics with cutting-edge technology. The 220d is a standout choice for European buyers: it is arguably the ultimate daily driver for those covering high mileages, offering bulletproof reliability from the B47 engine, punchy performance, and incredibly low running costs. For enthusiasts or North American buyers, the B48 (230i) and B58 (M240i) petrol engines are equally robust. Regardless of the engine chosen, the G42 is a highly recommended purchase, provided the 220d is not subjected exclusively to short city trips.
