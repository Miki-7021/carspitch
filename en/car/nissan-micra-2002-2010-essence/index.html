Nissan Micra

III (K12) (2002–2010) · 2002 – 2010

ACHETER Reliability 75/100 — bon

Summary

The third-generation Nissan Micra, known internally as the K12, marked a significant departure from its predecessors with its distinctive 'bubble' styling and a focus on urban practicality and ease of use. Launched in Europe in late 2002 and in Japan as the March, it quickly gained popularity for its compact dimensions, surprisingly spacious interior, and agile handling. The K12 was built on Nissan's B platform, shared with the Renault Clio III, reflecting the Nissan-Renault Alliance.

Two main facelifts occurred: a minor update in 2005 (Phase 2) with revised bumpers, lights, and interior trim, and a more significant one in 2007 (Phase 3) which introduced new engine options (like the 1.6 HR16DE) and further styling tweaks. Overall, the K12 is generally considered a reliable supermini, particularly its petrol variants, offering good value on the used market. Its quirky design might not appeal to everyone, but its practicality and low running costs are undeniable.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent maneuverability and compact size for city driving.
  • Surprisingly spacious and practical interior for its class.
  • Generally reliable petrol engines (CR and HR series) with good fuel economy.
  • Comfortable ride quality.
  • Affordable to buy and run (petrol models).
  • Good visibility.

Weaknesses 6

  • Diesel 1.5 dCi engine (K9K) has significant reliability issues, especially pre-2006.
  • Interior plastics can feel cheap and scratch easily.
  • Road noise can be noticeable at higher speeds.
  • Electric power steering pump can fail.
  • Timing chain wear on CR-series petrol engines can be an expensive repair.
  • Quirky styling is not to everyone's taste.

Check before buying

  • **Engine Noise (Petrol CR series):** Listen for a rattling or grinding noise from the engine, especially on cold start or at idle. This indicates timing chain wear, a potentially costly repair.
  • **Engine Noise (Diesel K9K):** Listen for any knocking or unusual metallic sounds, which could indicate big end bearing failure. Check for excessive smoke from the exhaust.
  • **Electrical System:** Test all electric windows, central locking, radio, and dashboard lights. Check for warning lights (engine, airbag, ABS).
  • **Power Steering:** Ensure the electric power steering operates smoothly and quietly throughout the steering range. Listen for groaning or whining noises.
  • **Suspension:** Check for worn suspension bushes (especially rear axle) and shock absorber leaks. Listen for clonks or squeaks over bumps.
  • **Brakes:** Check for even brake wear and proper function of ABS. Rear drums can seize if not regularly serviced.
  • **Service History:** Crucial, especially for diesel models. Look for evidence of regular, on-time oil changes with the correct oil specification.
  • **Rust:** Inspect exhaust system, subframe, and wheel arches for excessive rust.

Verdict

The Nissan Micra K12 is a charming and practical supermini, best enjoyed in its petrol guises. The 1.2L and 1.4L CR-series engines offer a good balance of performance and economy, making them ideal for city and suburban driving. The 1.6L HR16DE is a strong, reliable choice for those seeking more power or a convertible C+C model.

**Verdict for Petrol Models: BUY.** These are generally robust and offer excellent value. Prioritize models with a full service history and listen carefully for timing chain noise. A well-maintained petrol K12 can be a faithful and inexpensive companion.

**Verdict for Diesel Models: NEGOTIATE / AVOID.** While economical, the 1.5 dCi engine's well-documented reliability issues, particularly with injectors and big end bearings, make it a risky proposition, especially for pre-2006 models. If considering a diesel, it must have an impeccable, documented service history with frequent oil changes using the correct specification oil. Be prepared for potentially expensive repairs. For most buyers, the petrol engines are the safer and more sensible choice.
