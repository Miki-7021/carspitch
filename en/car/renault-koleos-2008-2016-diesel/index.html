Summary

The first-generation Renault Koleos (code HY0) is a product of the Renault-Nissan Alliance. Manufactured in South Korea by Renault Samsung Motors (where it is sold as the Samsung QM5), it is based on the Alliance's C platform, shared with the Nissan X-Trail (T31). While it suffered from a polarizing design at its launch (Phase 1), it benefited from two facelifts (Phase 2 in 2011, Phase 3 in 2013) that softened its lines. Positioned as a compact family SUV, it stands out for its ride comfort, sound insulation, and, above all, mechanical reliability far superior to the brand's average at that time, thanks to the excellent 2.0 dCi (M9R) engine and Nissan transmission components. Today, it is a pragmatic and very affordable choice on the used market.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent overall reliability (M9R chain-driven engine and Nissan platform)
  • High level of suspension comfort and sound insulation
  • Very efficient 4x4 system (Nissan-derived All-Mode 4x4-i)
  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used car market
  • Towing capacity (up to 2000 kg depending on version)
  • Dual-opening tailgate (practical for heavy loads or as a bench)

Weaknesses 5

  • Polarizing exterior design (especially on Phase 1)
  • Disappointing boot volume for its size (450 liters)
  • High fuel consumption for 4x4 automatic versions (often > 8.5L/100km for diesel)
  • Pronounced body roll in corners (comfort-oriented)
  • Interior plastics prone to scratches and average aging of some leathers

Check before buying

  • Clogging of the DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter) and EGR valve on dCi engines (check dashboard warning lights)
  • Premature wear of front tires and front axle bushings (significant weight on the front axle)
  • Air conditioning operation (fragile compressor on 2008-2010 models)
  • Service history of gearbox oil changes (6-speed torque converter automatic and CVT) and rear differential on 4x4 versions
  • Onboard electronics: operation of the hands-free card, power windows, and R-Link system bugs on Phase 3 models

Official recalls

  • November 2008: Risk of steering column failure (loss of control). Concerns models produced until September 2008.
  • December 2011: Risk of rear spoiler detachment at high speed due to poor fastening.
  • 2012: Welding issue on front suspension arms that could lead to loss of control.
  • 2015: Risk of short circuit on the fuel pressure sensor wiring harness (on dCi engines).

Verdict

The Renault Koleos I is often considered the 'ugly duckling' of the Renault range in the 2010s due to its styling. Paradoxically, this is what makes it an excellent used car bargain. Beneath its controversial exterior lie the excellent underpinnings of the Nissan X-Trail and the very robust 2.0 dCi (M9R) engine with a timing chain, which has largely erased the woes of the older 1.9 dCi. If you are looking for a comfortable, reliable family SUV, capable off-road (in 4x4 version) and inexpensive, this is an extremely pragmatic choice. Prioritize Phase 2 or 3 versions (after 2011) for modernized styling and improved finish.
