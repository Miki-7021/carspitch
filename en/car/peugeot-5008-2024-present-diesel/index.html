Summary

The third-generation Peugeot 5008 (codename P54) marks a radical turning point for the Lion brand's large family SUV. Based on Stellantis' new multi-energy STLA Medium platform, it retains its troop-carrier vocation (up to 7 seats) but revolutionizes its mechanical offering. **Crucial point regarding your query: Peugeot has definitively abandoned diesel engines (BlueHDi) on this generation**, both in Europe and on announced international markets. The manufacturer has opted for a total transition to mild hybridization (MHEV), plug-in hybrid (PHEV), and 100% electric (E-5008). The model stands out with its 'Panoramic i-Cockpit' of 21 inches and optimized third-row spaciousness.

Strengths 5

  • Abandonment of the 1.2 PureTech with wet belt in favor of a more reliable chain-driven block
  • Significantly improved third-row spaciousness compared to the previous generation
  • Spectacular interior presentation (Panoramic i-Cockpit)
  • Advertised range of the Long Range electric version (98 kWh)
  • Still dynamic road handling despite the increase in weight

Weaknesses 4

  • Total disappearance of diesel, penalizing for very high-mileage drivers and those who tow
  • Very high weight (especially for electric and PHEV), impacting highway consumption
  • Ergonomics of the all-touch interface (i-Toggles) requires an adaptation period
  • Significantly increased entry price

* **Europe:** * **Skoda Kodiaq II:** The perfect alternative for those looking for a **Diesel engine** (the 2.0 TDI 150/193 hp is still in the catalog), giant interior space. * **Renault Espace VI:** Only available as a hybrid (E-Tech 200), lighter, very spacious, but no electric or diesel. * **Volkswagen Tayron:** Replacement for the Tiguan Allspace, still offers diesel and PHEV. * **Asia / International:** Nissan X-Trail (e-Power), Hyundai Santa Fe, Kia Sorento (still offering diesel in some markets).

Verdict

The Peugeot 5008 III (P54) is a technologically very accomplished family SUV, but it imposes a paradigm shift on its buyers. **If you were absolutely looking for a diesel to tow heavy loads or travel across Europe daily, you will have to turn to the competition (Skoda Kodiaq, VW Tayron).** However, Peugeot has corrected its biggest historical flaw: the entry-level gasoline engine (Hybrid 136) is now equipped with a timing chain, which suggests much higher reliability. The purchase is recommendable, but as with any vehicle inaugurating a new platform (STLA Medium) and new electronic architectures, it is prudent to favor a model under warranty to mitigate the inevitable early bugs.
