Summary

The Audi Q2 (Type GA) is the premium urban SUV from the four rings brand, based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MQB A1 platform (shared with the VW T-Roc and Seat Arona). Launched in 2016 and facelifted in late 2020, it stands out with a more angular design than the rest of the Audi range and a very high-quality interior finish, although some lower plastics are disappointing for the price.

**Diesel Focus (TDI):** Although the market has moved away from diesel, the Q2 offered excellent TDI engines (EA288 family), particularly suited for high-mileage drivers. The 1.6 TDI offers exemplary frugality, while the 2.0 TDI (available in front-wheel drive or Quattro) provides superior driving pleasure. The mechanical reliability of the TDI blocks is solid, but they are penalized by emission control peripherals (AdBlue, DPF, EGR) if confined to urban use.

Strengths 5

  • Presentation and assembly quality (upper dashboard)
  • Retained physical ergonomics (real climate controls, very practical MMI knob)
  • Exceptional fuel efficiency of TDI engines on the road
  • Dynamic and precise road handling (especially with sport chassis or Quattro)
  • Overall reliability of engine blocks (excluding emission control peripherals)

Weaknesses 5

  • Capricious and expensive-to-repair AdBlue system on the 2.0 TDI
  • Fragile S tronic 7 gearbox (DQ200 dry clutch) on 1.6 TDI and small petrol engines
  • Average rear passenger space and boot volume for the category
  • Hard plastics on door panels unworthy of the Audi badge
  • Very high purchase price and official network maintenance costs

Check before buying

  • Service history: check for DSG (DQ381) gearbox and Haldex coupler (Quattro) oil changes every 60,000 km.
  • On TDIs: look for traces of pink coolant around the timing belt (water pump leak).
  • On TDIs: check for absence of engine warning light or 'AdBlue fault - Engine start impossible in 1000 km' message.
  • On S tronic 1.6 TDI / 1.0 / 1.4 / 1.5 TFSI versions: test the gearbox at low speed. No juddering or hesitation should be felt when shifting from 1st to 2nd.
  • Electronics: test the MMI infotainment system (sluggishness or frequent unexpected reboots before update).

Official recalls

  • March 2022 (1.5 TFSI Engines): Recall to remove the decorative engine cover. Risk of detachment and fire upon contact with hot exhaust parts.
  • November 2019 (Quattro): Welding defect on the rear wheel bearing housing (axle stub) potentially leading to loss of control. Part replacement.
  • August 2019: Passenger airbag deployment issue in case of collision. Reprogramming or module replacement.
  • August 2018: The electric parking brake (EPB) may unexpectedly disengage due to a software error. Control unit update.
  • February 2018: Risk of wheel hub detachment due to incorrect factory tightening.

Verdict

The Audi Q2 is a very accomplished urban SUV, offering driving pleasure worthy of the segment above. For **diesel**, the choice must be dictated by your usage. The **2.0 TDI** is an excellent engine, designed for highway driving, but you need to budget for a potential AdBlue issue (often partially covered by Audi if maintenance is exclusively within the network). The **1.6 TDI** should be avoided if you drive in the city, as it risks clogging the DPF/EGR and stressing the S tronic DQ200 gearbox. If you drive less than 15,000 km/year, opt for a 1.4 or 1.5 TFSI with a manual gearbox for maximum peace of mind.
