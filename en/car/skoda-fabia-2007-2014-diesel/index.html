Skoda Fabia

II (5J) · 2007 – 2014

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The Skoda Fabia II (internal code 5J) is based on an evolution of the Volkswagen Group's PQ24 platform. Replacing a very successful first generation, it stands out with record interior space for its category (especially headroom) and a generous boot volume, particularly in its estate (Combi) version.

**Range Evolution:** - **Phase 1 (2007-2010):** Use of noisy but generally robust pump-injector diesel engines (TDI IP), and proven naturally aspirated petrol engines. - **Phase 2 (Facelift 2010-2014):** Mechanical revolution with the introduction of common rail diesels (TDI CR - EA189 family), which are quieter but prone to emissions system problems, and turbocharged petrol engines (TSI - EA111 family) affected by timing chain issues.

Although the focus here is on **diesel** engines, it is crucial to note that buying a Fabia II diesel today requires great vigilance regarding its maintenance history (EGR, DPF) and the application of the "Dieselgate" software update, which has often weakened these components.
