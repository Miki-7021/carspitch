Audi A1

GB · 2018 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation Audi A1 (Type GB) marks a clear break from its predecessor (8X). Based on the Volkswagen Group's MQB A0 platform (shared with the VW Polo VI and Seat Ibiza V), it definitively abandons the 3-door body style to be offered exclusively as a 'Sportback' (5-door). Notably, Audi completely banned diesel (TDI) and hybridization from this generation, offering a 100% gasoline range. While its exterior design is praised for its aggressiveness (inspired by the 1984 Audi Sport quattro) and its road handling is significantly improved, this generation has been sharply criticized for the regression in the quality of its interior plastics, unworthy of the premium badge, especially given the prices charged. A 'Citycarver' version (renamed 'Allstreet' in 2022) with an adventurous look completed the range in 2019.

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic road handling and rigorous chassis (MQB A0)
  • Very refined sound insulation for the category
  • Onboard technology (Virtual Cockpit as standard, responsive MMI)
  • Efficient and powerful gasoline engines (especially the 35 TFSI)
  • Assertive and customizable exterior design

Weaknesses 5

  • Significantly reduced interior plastic quality (many hard plastics)
  • Prohibitive purchase price and option costs
  • Reliability of the S tronic 7 gearbox (DQ200) still imperfect
  • Total absence of hybrid or electric powertrains
  • Average rear passenger space and access to rear seats

Check before buying

  • Behavior of the S tronic 7 gearbox (DQ200): no juddering, slipping, or metallic noise should occur during 1st/2nd gear changes.
  • Infotainment system (MMI): check for the absence of unexpected reboots or black screens (often requires a software update).
  • Coolant level: a drop may indicate a leak at the water pump/thermostat housing (a common issue with EA211/EA888 engines).
  • Software update history (especially on the 1.5 TFSI for cold jerks).
  • Condition of interior plastics (door panels, lower console) very susceptible to scratches.

Braking

  • Powerful and Ultra-Stable Braking — Benefit from first-class braking performance, with short stopping distances and remarkable stability, even in emergency situations. The system is powerful and durable, true to Audi's quality standards.

Handling

  • Dynamic and Agile Road Handling — Its rigorous chassis and precise steering offer exceptional road holding. The A1 GB proves agile and predictable, providing great confidence and genuine driving pleasure in all conditions.

Reliability

  • Robust and Efficient TFSI Petrol Engines — TFSI petrol engines, particularly the 30 TFSI and 35 TFSI, are renowned for their maturity, robustness, and excellent balance between dynamic performance and controlled fuel consumption, ensuring a reliable and enjoyable driving experience.

Comfort

  • Acoustic Comfort and Premium Onboard Technology — Enjoy very refined soundproofing for its category, guaranteeing a serene journey. The integration of the standard Virtual Cockpit and the responsive MMI system offers an intuitive and high-end technological experience.

Design

  • Assertive and Customizable Exterior Design — The Audi A1 Sportback stands out with its aggressive and modern exterior design, inspired by the Audi Sport quattro. Its assertive silhouette is complemented by numerous customization options, allowing you to create a unique vehicle in your image.

Verdict

The Audi A1 GB is a city car with a striking design and first-class road performance. However, it commands a very high price for its badge, especially since its interior finish has regressed compared to the previous generation. In terms of reliability, the gasoline (TFSI) engines are generally mature and robust. The real downside remains the S tronic 7 automatic gearbox (DQ200) on smaller engines, which requires absolute vigilance when buying used.

**Buying advice:** Opt for a **30 TFSI (110/116 hp)** version with a manual gearbox for complete peace of mind, or a **35 TFSI** if you do a lot of road driving. If you insist on an automatic gearbox, demand a clear service history and a warranty.
