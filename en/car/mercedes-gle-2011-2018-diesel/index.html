Summary

The Mercedes GLE W166 is actually the second phase of the third generation of Mercedes-Benz's luxury SUV. Initially launched in 2011 under the ML (W166) designation, it underwent a significant facelift in 2015, adopting the new GLE nomenclature. This model is positioned in the large premium SUV segment, offering a blend of luxury, comfort, on-road capabilities, and, for some versions, off-road performance. It is renowned for its spacious interior, quality materials, and high level of equipment. General reliability is good for a vehicle in this category, but like any complex premium SUV, it presents specific points of attention, particularly concerning electronic systems and sophisticated diesel engines.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with Airmatic)
  • Quality of finish and interior materials
  • Generous interior space and large trunk
  • Powerful and pleasant V6 diesel engines (OM642)
  • On-road and off-road capabilities (with Offroad package)
  • Brand image and decent residual value

Weaknesses 6

  • High maintenance cost, especially for V8 and AMG versions
  • Imperfect reliability of certain electronic systems (AdBlue, COMAND)
  • Air suspension (Airmatic) prone to wear with age
  • High fuel consumption for V8 gasoline engines
  • Significant weight impacting agility and consumption
  • Technical complexity of modern diesel engines (AdBlue, DPF)

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (automatic gearbox oil changes, AdBlue, etc.)
  • Functioning of the air suspension (raising/lowering, absence of leaks)
  • Absence of engine or AdBlue warning lights on the dashboard
  • Condition of tires and brakes (expensive to replace)
  • Functioning of all electronic equipment (COMAND, driving aids)
  • Presence of oil leaks under the engine (OM642, M276/M278)
  • Abnormal noises on startup or acceleration (timing chain, turbo)

Verdict

The Mercedes ML/GLE W166 is an excellent premium SUV, offering undeniable comfort and luxury. Its reliability is generally good, but it is a complex vehicle whose maintenance must not be neglected. The diesel engines, particularly the V6 OM642 (GLE 350 d), are the most recommendable for their driving pleasure, performance, and relatively controlled consumption. However, be vigilant about the maintenance history of the AdBlue system and the oil cooler. The V6 gasoline versions are also good choices for those who drive less or prefer the smoothness of gasoline. The V8 and AMG versions are performance beasts, but their maintenance and consumption costs are very high. The air suspension (Airmatic) is a plus for comfort but a point to watch with age. A clear maintenance history and a thorough inspection before purchase are imperative. If these conditions are met, the GLE W166 remains a relevant choice on the used car market.
