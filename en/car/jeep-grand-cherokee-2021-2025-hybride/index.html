Summary

The fifth generation of the Jeep Grand Cherokee (code name WL) marks a major break from its predecessor (WK2). Based on the Giorgio platform (shared with Alfa Romeo), it moves significantly upmarket with a luxurious interior, cutting-edge technologies (Uconnect 5), and the introduction of a long-wheelbase version (3-row Grand Cherokee L). The main innovation under the hood is the arrival of the 4xe plug-in hybrid powertrain, which has become the sole offering in many European markets. However, while its on-road and off-road qualities are undeniable, the launch of this generation was marred by very serious quality control problems, resulting in an avalanche of safety recalls (NHTSA) affecting the suspension, steering, and onboard electronics. Organizations such as ADAC and TÜV Report rank its overall reliability below the category average.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional off-road capabilities (Quadra-Trac II, Quadra-Drive II, Quadra-Lift air suspension)
  • Luxurious interior finish and high-quality materials (Summit/Overland trims)
  • Significantly improved roominess, especially on the L version (3 rows)
  • Driving pleasure of the 4xe version when it works correctly
  • Mechanical robustness of the Pentastar V6 and Hemi V8 blocks

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous electronic reliability in early production (black screens, inoperative cameras)
  • Record number of critical recalls (NHTSA) affecting vital safety components (steering, suspension detaching)
  • Documented fire risks on the high-voltage battery of 4xe versions
  • Very high weight penalizing electric range (4xe) and fuel consumption (V6/V8)
  • Prices have become elitist, significant depreciation

Check before buying

  • Recall history: This is the most critical point. Demand the manufacturer report proving that the suspension, steering, and battery recalls have been done.
  • Uconnect 5 system: Test all functions of the central screen, the rearview camera (prone to numerous failures), and the instrument cluster.
  • Air suspension (Quadra-Lift): Check raising/lowering without abnormal noises or sagging after parking.
  • 4xe version: Check for the absence of error messages related to the hybrid system, test charging at a station, and the operation of heating/defrosting in 100% electric mode.

Official recalls

The Grand Cherokee WL has accumulated an exceptional number of recalls. Here are the most critical ones (Sources: NHTSA / Rappel Conso):

  • High-Voltage / Hybrid Battery (4xe):
  • *2025-10-30 (NHTSA 25V741000) & 2026-03-13 (Rappel Conso):* Internal failure of the HV battery (damaged separator) that can lead to a vehicle fire, even when parked.
  • *2025-09-04 (NHTSA 25V576000):* Software error in the hybrid control processor (HCP) causing a loss of drive power.
  • *2024-02-15 (NHTSA 24V111000):* Software bug preventing the defrost/defog system from working.
  • Running Gear & Steering (All versions):
  • *2026-01-29 (26V051000) & 2023-06-08 (23V413000):* The rear coil springs can detach from the vehicle while driving due to improper factory installation.
  • *2024-02-22 (24V132000):* Possible separation of the upper control arm ball joint.
  • *2024-02-22 (24V131000):* Improperly machined steering knuckle that can separate and cause the loss of a wheel.
  • *2023-05-18 (23V352000):* Improperly assembled intermediate steering shaft that can disconnect.
  • Electronics & Safety:
  • *2024-12-19, 2024-06-13, 2024-04-25 (Multiple NHTSA):* Recurrent failures of the rearview camera display (coaxial cables, modules, software).
  • *2026-05-21 (26V328000):* Delayed airbag deployment due to a software bug.
  • *2024-05-16 (24V346000):* Missing energy-absorbing foam in the front bumper, altering airbag deployment.

What sets it apart 6

Safety

  • Certified Advanced Safety — The Grand Cherokee 4xe offers maximum protection for all occupants, as evidenced by its excellent crash-test results.
  • Road Control and Stability — Its braking performance is adequate for its category, and its road holding is safe and predictable, with well-managed body roll, ensuring serene driving on long journeys.

Performance

  • Legendary Off-Road Capabilities — Equipped with Quadra-Trac II and Quadra-Drive II systems, along with Quadra-Lift air suspension, it excels on all terrains, offering unparalleled freedom of exploration.
  • Dynamic and Efficient Driving Pleasure — The 4xe plug-in hybrid powertrain delivers impressive combined power, offering a fluid and responsive driving experience, while benefiting from advantageous taxation.

Comfort

  • Luxurious and Refined Interior — Enjoy a sumptuous interior with high-quality materials and advanced technologies (Uconnect 5), ensuring a most pleasant travel experience. The silence in EV mode adds to the serenity.

Practicality

  • Optimized Space and Versatility — Cabin space is significantly improved, especially with the 3-row L version, offering exceptional comfort for the whole family and great modularity for your adventures.

Verdict

The 5th generation Jeep Grand Cherokee (WL) is a paradoxical vehicle. On paper and behind the wheel, it is an extremely accomplished premium SUV, luxurious and formidable off-road. However, its reliability record is catastrophic for the early production years (2021-2023). The problems are not limited to annoying infotainment bugs, but affect vital safety components (suspensions detaching, loss of steering, fire risks on the 4xe).

For the 4xe (hybrid) version: Buying used is a risky bet. Only consider it if the vehicle has an extended manufacturer warranty and the recall history is crystal clear and complete. For the V6/V8 versions (Import or North America): The mechanics are solid, but you will not escape the electronic and running gear issues. Favor models from late 2023 / 2024 onwards, when factory quality control began to improve.
