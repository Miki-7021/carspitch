Subaru Crosstrek

I (GP/GJ) · 2012 – 2017

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation Subaru Crosstrek (known as the **Subaru XV** in Europe and other markets) is a compact SUV based on the Impreza platform (GP/GJ generation). It stands out with its standard Symmetrical AWD and generous ground clearance of 22 cm (8.7 inches), giving it real off-road capabilities.

**Focus on the Hybrid version:** Introduced in 2014 (mainly in North America and Japan), this version pairs the 2.0L Boxer engine with a small 13.4 hp electric motor integrated into the CVT gearbox, powered by a 0.6 kWh NiMH battery. It is a mild-hybrid system aimed at slightly reducing urban fuel consumption. Although technically interesting, this version offers only marginal fuel savings compared to the standard combustion model, while adding weight and technical complexity that now weigh on its used-market record.

Strengths 5

  • Formidably efficient Symmetrical AWD (robust viscous differential)
  • Exceptional ground clearance for the category (22 cm)
  • Excellent resale value (particularly in North America and mountainous areas)
  • Passive and active safety (introduction of the EyeSight system at the end of its career)
  • Excellent peripheral visibility

Weaknesses 5

  • Hybrid version not very relevant on the used market (minimal gains, financial risk linked to the battery)
  • Chronic excessive oil consumption on FB20 petrol engines before 2016
  • Lineartronic CVT gearbox sometimes noisy ("rubber band" effect) and fragile valve body
  • Soundproofing could be improved on the highway
  • Dated and slow infotainment system

Check before buying

  • **Engine oil level:** Crucial on the 2.0i and Hybrids from 2012 to 2015 (risk of oil starvation if neglected).
  • **Hybrid battery:** On the hybrid version, an electronic diagnostic is mandatory to check the remaining capacity of the NiMH battery.
  • **CVT behavior:** Jerks at low speed or excessive slipping indicate a tired valve body.
  • **Brake light switch:** Check if the recall has been performed (can prevent starting or the lights from turning on).
  • **FAP (on EE20 Diesel):** Check the regeneration history and the absence of a check engine light.

Verdict

The first-generation Subaru Crosstrek (XV) is an excellent choice for those who truly need permanent AWD and high ground clearance.

**Regarding the Hybrid version:** It is to be **AVOIDED** or heavily negotiated. The extra cost at purchase and the risk of having to replace the aging NiMH battery completely cancel out the very meager fuel savings achieved.

**The best choice:** Opt for a standard 2.0L petrol (FB20) model, preferably **from the 2016 model year onwards** to escape the piston ring problems (excessive oil consumption). If you buy a 2013-2015 model, demand proof that the oil problem was handled under warranty or check the level obsessively.
