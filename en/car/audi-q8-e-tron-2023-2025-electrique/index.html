Summary

The Audi Q8 e-tron (GE generation) is not an entirely new model, but the deep facelift and renaming of the original Audi e-tron (launched in 2018/2019). Introduced in 2023 to clarify Audi's electric lineup, it is positioned as the brand's flagship electric SUV. This facelift brought crucial improvements: optimized aerodynamics, a new battery chemistry offering higher energy density (up to 114 kWh gross), and revised thermal management. Available in classic and Sportback body styles, it is a heavy vehicle (over 2.5 tonnes) that prioritizes supreme comfort, top-flight soundproofing, and highway stability over pure agility. *Note: The Q8 e-tron is exclusively electric (BEV). The combustion and plug-in hybrid versions retain the classic 'Audi Q8' name.*

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (standard air suspension)
  • Soundproofing worthy of a luxury limousine
  • Very stable fast charging curve (maintains its max power for a very long time)
  • Flawless finish and assembly quality
  • Generous interior space and trunk volume

Weaknesses 5

  • Excessive weight (often over 2,600 kg), penalizing agility
  • High power consumption compared to the competition (BMW iX, Mercedes EQE)
  • Ergonomics of the dual touchscreen that requires taking eyes off the road
  • Premature tire wear (linked to weight and torque)
  • Imposing size making urban maneuvers tricky

Check before buying

  • Brake line connections (subject to a major official recall in 2024)
  • Tire condition (asymmetrical or premature wear on the edges)
  • Smooth operation of the motorized charging flaps (on both sides)
  • Absence of air leaks or abnormally loud compressor noises (air suspension)
  • Software update history (BMS and MMI)

Official recalls

Major safety recalls

  • [2024-07-12] (Europe - Rappel Conso) / [2024-06-10] (USA - NHTSA 24V428000): Concerns the Q8 e-tron and Q8 Sportback e-tron (2024 models). Reason: Excessive tightening of the screw connections on the brake hoses (lines) during assembly can lead to screw breakage and a brake fluid leak. Risk: Loss of braking efficiency increasing the risk of an accident. Correction: Inspection and replacement of the affected brake lines at the workshop.

*(Note: Recalls dating from 2020 concerning the Q7, combustion Q8, A8, and Q5 models do not apply to the electric Q8 e-tron launched in 2023).*

Alternatives

Direct competitors by market

  • Europe: BMW iX (more efficient, polarizing design), Mercedes-Benz EQE SUV (more technological, similar range), Polestar 3 (more dynamic).
  • North America: Tesla Model X (native Supercharger network, more space, lower finish quality), Rivian R1S (more adventurous, 7 seats), Cadillac Lyriq.
  • Asia / China: Nio EL7, Xpeng G9 (800V ultra-fast charging, more aggressive pricing).

Verdict

The Audi Q8 e-tron is a mature and highly accomplished evolution of Audi's first electric attempt. By correcting its predecessor's main flaw (range) thanks to larger capacity batteries and better motor efficiency, it becomes an excellent highway cruiser. Its mechanical and electrical reliability is very good, with the asynchronous motors having proven themselves since 2019. The main annoyances stem from its pachydermic weight (which devours tires) and a few persistent software bugs. The 55 e-tron version is the most rational choice to fully enjoy its travel capabilities. Make absolutely sure that the recall on the brake lines has been performed if you are targeting a 2024 model.
