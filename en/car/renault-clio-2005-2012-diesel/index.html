Summary

The Renault Clio III marks a major turning point for the diamond-badged city car. Larger, heavier, and significantly better finished than its predecessor, it positions itself almost as a compact car. The first in its category to achieve 5 stars in the Euro NCAP crash test, it offers benchmark suspension comfort and sound insulation.

Diesel Focus (1.5 dCi - K9K): This is the dominant engine in Europe for this generation. However, its history is split into two. Phase 1 (2005-2009) suffered from serious injection failures (Delphi pumps generating metal swarf), ruining the reputation of the 68 and 85 hp versions. Phase 2 (2009-2012) corrected the issue with new injection suppliers and the introduction of the DPF, making the 1.5 dCi extremely reliable and recommendable.

*Overall generation note: Excellent car, but reliability drastically depends on the year and the injection system for diesels.*

Strengths 6

  • Suspension comfort worthy of a higher category
  • Very refined sound insulation (especially on Phase 2)
  • Extremely low fuel consumption of 1.5 dCi engines (often under 5L/100km)
  • Passive safety (5 Euro NCAP stars, very sound road handling)
  • Generous interior space and boot volume for its time
  • Reliability of petrol engines (especially the 1.2 16v)

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic reliability of early 1.5 dCi (Delphi pump, metal swarf)
  • High weight that stifles small engines (1.2 16v, dCi 68)
  • Electric power steering (EPS) lacking feel and sometimes sticky
  • Recurring electrical problems on Phase 1 (stalks, power windows, sensors)
  • Premature wear of the leather steering wheel and interior plastics (peeling)

Check before buying

  • On diesels before 2009: Demand service history. If in doubt, check for metal swarf in the fuel filter.
  • Windshield scuttle: Lift the hood and check that the water drainage holes under the wipers are not blocked by leaves (risk of burning out the wiper motor or flooding the cabin).
  • Electric windows: Test all windows. The cables and motors are very fragile.
  • Stalks (Phase 1): Check the proper functioning of turn signals, headlights, and wipers (frequent loose connections).
  • Gearbox (RS): Try shifting gears at high revs to detect crunching synchros.

Official recalls

  • 2006 (Braking): Recall to check and replace the brake light switch (risk of brake lights not illuminating or lever locking on automatic gearbox).
  • 2007 (Quickshift Gearbox): Reprogramming of the automated gearbox ECU due to erratic gear changes.
  • 2011 (1.2 16v Engine): Risk of engine failure due to a machining defect of the valve collets (concerns a small series produced in late 2010).
  • 2015 (Seatbelts): Inspection of the front seatbelt attachment on certain models produced in 2012.

What sets it apart 6

Safety

  • Exemplary Passive Safety and Road Handling — The Clio III was the first in its category to achieve the maximum rating in crash tests, offering optimal protection and very safe road handling for complete peace of mind.

Comfort

  • Superior Suspension Comfort and Refined Soundproofing — Enjoy exceptional ride comfort and very careful soundproofing, transforming every journey into a pleasant experience, even over long distances.

Economy

  • Remarkably Low Fuel Consumption — The latest generation 1.5 dCi diesel engines are renowned for their exceptional fuel efficiency, allowing you to significantly reduce your fuel budget.

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability of 1.5 dCi Diesel Engines — 1.5 dCi versions produced from 2009 benefit from modernized and robust injection systems, ensuring great reliability and long-term peace of mind.

Practicality

  • Generous Cabin Space and Boot Volume — The Clio III surprises with its welcoming interior space and generous boot volume, making it perfectly suited for daily needs and getaways.

Performance

  • Enjoyable on Motorways with 6-Speed Gearbox — The most powerful 1.5 dCi versions, equipped with a 6-speed gearbox, offer optimal driving pleasure on motorways, combining dynamism and economy.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 207 (its main rival, more dynamic but with temperamental 1.4/1.6 HDi engines), Ford Fiesta Mk7 (excellent chassis, TDCi engines shared with PSA), Volkswagen Polo V (superior finish, reliable but noisy 1.6 TDI).
  • Japan / Asia: Toyota Yaris II (rock-solid reliability, robust D-4D), Honda Jazz (record-breaking interior space, petrol only).
  • Mercosur: Volkswagen Gol, Fiat Palio, Chevrolet Corsa.

Verdict

The Renault Clio III is an excellent used car, provided you choose the right engine. If you are looking for a diesel, the golden rule is to AVOID 2005-2008 (Phase 1) models with 68 and 85 hp, whose Delphi injection pump is a real financial time bomb.

On the other hand, the dCi 75 and 90 hp versions of Phase 2 (2009-2012) are highly recommendable: they are reliable, extremely fuel-efficient, and benefit from a car whose early flaws (electrical, finish) have been ironed out. For low-mileage drivers, the petrol engines (1.2 16v and 1.2 TCe) are safe bets, inexpensive to maintain.
