Summary

The fourth-generation Subaru WRX (chassis code VA) marks a historic turning point: it is the first time the model officially detaches from the "Impreza" name to become a standalone lineup. Launched in 2014 for the 2015 model year, it offers two distinct philosophies. The standard WRX adopts the new FA20F engine (2.0L direct-injection turbo) coupled with a 6-speed manual gearbox or a CVT transmission (Lineartronic). The WRX STI, the radical version, retains the iconic but aging EJ257 block (2.5L indirect-injection turbo) associated with a reinforced manual gearbox and a driver-controlled center differential (DCCD). Although the interior and soundproofing lag behind European standards, the WRX (VA) shines with its ultra-rigid chassis and Symmetrical AWD system offering imperial traction, superior to its direct competitors.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional traction thanks to the Symmetrical AWD (especially with the STI's DCCD)
  • Very rigid chassis and secure road handling
  • Very robust 6-speed manual gearbox (particularly the STI's)
  • Versatility of a 4-door sedan with a decent trunk
  • Enthusiast community and huge aftermarket parts market

Weaknesses 5

  • High fuel consumption (especially on the 2.5L STI)
  • Dated interior plastic quality and infotainment system
  • Mediocre soundproofing (road and wind noise on the highway)
  • EJ257 engine (STI) fragile if poorly maintained or poorly tuned
  • Often expensive insurance

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: avoid any vehicle without a service book or complete invoices (very frequent oil changes mandatory).
  • Modifications (Tuning): favor a strictly stock model. Poorly done tuning destroys these engines.
  • Cold engine noises: listen carefully for the absence of knocking (sign of connecting rod bearing wear or piston slap).
  • Exhaust smoke: blue or white smoke upon acceleration indicates turbo or piston ring wear.
  • Clutch condition: on manual versions, a stiff or slipping clutch indicates an expensive replacement.

What sets it apart 6

Performance

  • Legendary Traction and Driving Precision — Experience exceptional grip and stability thanks to Subaru's Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive system, ensuring perfect control in all conditions. Its ultra-rigid chassis offers remarkably precise road handling.
  • An Inimitable Engine Character and Pure Sensations — Whether it's the responsive torque of the FA20F or the explosive character of the legendary EJ257, each engine offers a thrilling driving experience, with a typical sound and rare sensations.

Reliability

  • Unrivaled Robustness of the 6-Speed Manual Gearbox — The 6-speed manual transmission is renowned for its strength and durability, providing an engaging and reliable driving experience, even in the most demanding conditions.

Practicality

  • Sportiness for Everyday Versatility — With its 4-door sedan body and a generous trunk, the WRX perfectly combines its sporty performance with everyday practicality and comfort, ideal for all your adventures.

Comfort

Verdict

Buying a used Subaru WRX or WRX STI (VA) is an exercise that requires patience and rigor. These are cars with a unique character, offering traction and sensations rare in this price range. However, mechanical reliability depends almost exclusively on the previous owner's behavior. The WRX's FA20F engine is generally more modern and reliable for daily use, while the STI's venerable EJ257 requires obsessive maintenance (oil changes every 8,000 km, strict adherence to warm-up times). Avoid heavily modified models or those without a clear history. If you find a stock example, well-maintained and driven with respect, it is a highly recommendable passion purchase.
