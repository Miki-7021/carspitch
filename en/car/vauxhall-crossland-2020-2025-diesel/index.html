Summary

The Vauxhall/Opel Crossland (facelifted 1st generation, codename P17) loses its "X" suffix with its late 2020 facelift. It adopts the brand's new visual identity, the famous *Vizor*, which considerably modernizes its front end. Based on PSA's older PF1 platform (shared with the first Peugeot 2008 and the Citroën C3 Aircross), it is positioned as an urban B-SUV with a family focus, prioritizing interior space and modularity (sliding rear bench) over driving dynamics. While its practical qualities are undeniable, its career is heavily marred by the disastrous reliability of the engines from the Stellantis (formerly PSA) parts bin, whether it be the 1.2 petrol units (wet belt) or the 1.5 diesel (7 mm camshaft chain and emission control system).

Strengths 5

  • Remarkable roominess for its size (4.21 m)
  • Sliding rear bench (depending on trim) offering a trunk of 410 to 520 liters
  • Successful and modern front-end design (Vizor)
  • Very frugal fuel consumption of the 1.5 diesel engine on roads/highways
  • Front seat comfort (AGR certification on some versions)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the engines (both petrol and diesel)
  • Aging PF1 platform (clumsy road handling, firm comfort at low speeds)
  • Dated and slow infotainment system
  • Soundproofing could be improved on the highway
  • Significant depreciation due to the reputation of Stellantis engines

Check before buying

  • On the 1.5 Diesel: Metallic rattling noise at the top of the engine (sign of wear on the 7 mm chain).
  • On the 1.5 Diesel: History of anti-pollution warning lights (AdBlue, NOx sensor) and condition of the high-pressure pump (traces of fuel leaks).
  • On the 1.2 Petrol: Condition of the timing belt via the oil cap (cracks, fraying).
  • On the 1.2 Petrol: Oil consumption history and possible replacements of the vacuum pump or VVT solenoid valve.
  • Operation of the touch screen and reversing camera (frequent bugs).

Official recalls

  • [2025-07-25] Timing system (1.5 Diesel): Due to oil carbonization and high contact pressure, the strength of the 7 mm camshaft chain can be reduced, leading to a risk of breakage and loss of power (risk of accident/injuries). Affects models equipped with the 1.5 diesel (Crossland, Corsa, Mokka, etc.).
  • [2025-02-07] ECU Software: Defective software in the engine electronic control unit (ECU) may provide incorrect values regarding emissions data. Environmental risk.
  • [2024-12-16] NOx Emissions (1.5 Diesel): On certain vehicles equipped with the DV5R E6.3 engine, nitrogen oxide (NOx) emissions are above regulatory limits. Update required.
  • [2021-10-29] High-pressure pump: The high-pressure fuel pump was not tightened to the engine with the correct torque. Risk of fuel leak on the road and fire/injuries.

What sets it apart 7

Practicality

  • Exceptional Roominess and Modularity — Enjoy remarkable interior space for its category and a sliding rear bench seat (depending on trim) offering a generous boot volume, ideal for all your family adventures.

Design

  • Modern and Distinctive Design — With its redesigned front fascia incorporating the 'Vizor', the Crossland boasts a modern and assertive look that is sure to turn heads.

Economy

  • Highly Fuel-Efficient — The 1.5 Turbo D diesel engine stands out for its exceptionally low fuel consumption on roads and motorways, allowing you to cover long distances with a controlled budget.

Comfort

  • Optimized Driving Comfort — Appreciate the superior comfort of the front seats, AGR-certified on some versions, and a supple suspension that effectively absorbs road irregularities, for serene journeys.

Safety

  • Maximum Safety Rated by Euro NCAP — Travel with complete peace of mind thanks to a high level of passive safety, attested by the 5 stars obtained in Euro NCAP tests, with scores of 85% for adult protection and 84% for child protection.

Braking

  • Effective and Predictable Braking — The Crossland's braking system is deemed adequate and predictable for its category, ensuring respectable stopping distances for optimal control in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Stable and Comfortable Road Handling — Benefit from road behaviour focused on comfort and stability. Its light steering facilitates city manoeuvres, while its supple suspension guarantees a pleasant drive on all types of roads.

Alternatives

  • Europe / UK: Renault Captur II (very reliable 1.5 Blue dCi, excellent finish), Ford Puma (1.5 EcoBlue, dynamic chassis), Skoda Kamiq (1.6 TDI or 1.0 TSI, very roomy), Peugeot 2008 II (same problematic engines, but more modern CMP platform).
  • Asia / Other markets: The Crossland is primarily a European product. Alternatives like the Honda HR-V or Toyota Yaris Cross (very reliable hybrids) are vastly superior.

Verdict

The facelifted Vauxhall/Opel Crossland is a vehicle full of paradoxes. On paper, it is an excellent small family SUV: roomy, practical, and featuring a refreshed design. Unfortunately, it is weighed down by the congenital defects of the Stellantis engines of this generation. The 1.5 Turbo D engine, although very frugal, is a real sword of Damocles because of its 7 mm camshaft chain and its temperamental AdBlue system. Buying a diesel version can only be considered if the vehicle has benefited from the recall/replacement for the 8 mm chain (supported by an invoice) and has an extended warranty. The petrol versions (1.2 PureTech) are just as risky. As it stands, it is a model to be approached with extreme caution.
