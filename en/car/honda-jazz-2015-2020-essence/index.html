Honda Jazz

III (GK) · 2015 – 2020

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

All Honda models

Summary

The 3rd generation Honda Jazz (code GK), also marketed as the Honda Fit in North America and Asia, is a versatile city car (B-segment) renowned for its record-breaking interior space. Thanks to the central placement of its fuel tank, it retains the famous "Magic Seats" (fold-up rear seat bases). Launched in Europe in 2015 (although it appeared in Japan as early as 2013), it benefited from a late 2017 facelift (marketed in 2018) which introduced the 1.5 i-VTEC 130 hp engine to the European market (Dynamic trim). It is an extremely rational model, prioritizing space, modularity, and very high-level mechanical reliability, at the expense of soundproofing and suspension comfort. Overall design score: 8.5/10.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of gasoline engines (1.3 and 1.5 i-VTEC)
  • Giant interior space for a 4-meter car (legroom worthy of a sedan)
  • Unbeatable modularity thanks to Magic Seats (fold-up seat bases, flat floor)
  • Very low real-world fuel consumption, even without hybridization (often under 5.5 L/100 km with 1.3L)
  • Excellent peripheral visibility (large glass surfaces)
  • Perfectly handling 6-speed manual gearbox (precise and short)

Weaknesses 6

  • Poor soundproofing (road and aerodynamic noise on the highway)
  • Very firm suspension comfort, jolts at low speed
  • Infotainment system (Honda Connect) slow, dated, and unintuitive
  • CVT gearbox that makes the engine "rev high" during strong accelerations
  • Fragile water-based paint (sensitive to micro-scratches and gravel chips)
  • Small fuel tank (40 liters), limiting range on the highway

Check before buying

  • CVT gearbox service history: the fluid change must be done every 40,000 km (HCF-2 fluid).
  • Rattling/growling noise on cold start on the 1.5 i-VTEC (sign of a worn VTC actuator).
  • Condition of the bodywork and bumpers (thin paint and exposed in the city).
  • Smooth operation of the infotainment system (black screen bugs are frequent, sometimes requiring a reboot or update).
  • Check if the Denso fuel pump recall has been performed (2018-2020 models).

Official recalls

  • Fuel pump (2020-2021): Massive global recall (including 2018-2020 Jazz/Fit). The impeller of the low-pressure fuel pump (supplied by Denso) can swell and block the pump, causing the engine to stall while driving.
  • Takata Airbags (Various years): Although primarily affecting previous generations, some GK models produced in specific factories were recalled for airbag inflator replacement.
  • i-DCD gearbox software (2013-2015, Japan/Asia): Multiple recalls to reprogram the dual-clutch gearbox control module which caused acceleration delays or inability to engage a gear.
  • Start/Stop button (2015-2017, North Amer./Asia): Replacement of the ignition switch (push button) which could get dirty and prevent the vehicle from starting.

What sets it apart 7

reliability

  • Legendary Petrol Engine Reliability — The Honda Jazz III is widely recognized for the robustness and durability of its i-VTEC petrol engines. It stands out with very favorable breakdown statistics and consistently ranks among the most reliable vehicles in its category.

practicality

  • Giant Interior Space and Revolutionary Modularity — Despite its compact size, the Jazz offers record-breaking interior space, with rear legroom comparable to a sedan. Its famous "Magic Seats" provide unique modularity, transforming the interior to transport bulky items with surprising ease.

economy

  • Outstanding Everyday Fuel Economy — Enjoy very low fuel consumption, even without hybridization. The Jazz's i-VTEC petrol engines are renowned for their efficiency, allowing you to make significant savings on every journey.

safety

  • Optimal Protection with 5 Euro NCAP Stars — The Honda Jazz III ensures maximum safety for all its occupants. It achieved the maximum rating of 5 stars in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult and child protection.

road_holding

  • Urban Maneuverability and Reassuring Stability — Enjoy a pleasant and safe driving experience. The Jazz offers stable and predictable road holding, with light and responsive steering that makes it extremely maneuverable in the city and reassuring on the open road.

braking

  • Powerful and Confident Braking — The Honda Jazz III is equipped with a powerful and predictable braking system, offering adequate stopping distances and a well-balanced pedal feel. This ensures complete confidence in all driving conditions.

comfort

  • Exceptional All-Round Visibility — Thanks to its large glass surfaces, the Honda Jazz offers unparalleled panoramic visibility, facilitating parking maneuvers and increasing safety in both urban and road driving.

Alternatives

  • Japan / Asia: Nissan Note e-Power, Toyota Aqua / Yaris, Mazda 2.
  • North America: Toyota Yaris Hatchback, Chevrolet Sonic, Kia Rio, Hyundai Accent.
  • Emerging Markets: Hyundai i20, Maruti Suzuki Baleno, Tata Altroz.

Verdict

The Honda Jazz III (GK) is one of the most rational and safest used car purchases on the market. If you are looking for a city car capable of transporting bulky items, comfortably accommodating 4 adults, and never breaking down, this is the number one choice. The 1.3 i-VTEC engine is the ideal compromise in Europe for its legendary reliability and fuel efficiency. The 1.5 i-VTEC offers a real plus on the road but is rarer. However, you must accept firm comfort and light soundproofing on the highway. Highly recommended purchase, provided you check the service history if you opt for the CVT automatic gearbox.
