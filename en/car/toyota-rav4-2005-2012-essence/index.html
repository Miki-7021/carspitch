Toyota RAV4

III (XA30) · 2005 – 2012

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The third-generation Toyota RAV4 (code name XA30) marks a major turning point in the model's history. It definitively abandons the 3-door body and permanent all-wheel drive in favor of an on-demand AWD system (or front-wheel drive on some versions). More family-oriented, heavier, and more comfortable, it even comes in a long-wheelbase (LWB) 7-seater version for the North American and Asian markets (sold as the Toyota Vanguard in Japan).

Although this report focuses on gasoline engines (which stand out for their overall high reliability, with the exception of the 2.4L), it is crucial to note that this generation's European reputation was tarnished by the troubles of the diesel engines (2AD series). The model underwent two facelifts: a minor one in 2008/2009 and a more significant one in 2010 (redesigned front end).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of 2.0L (1AZ and 3ZR) and 2.5L/V6 gasoline engines
  • Spaciousness and modularity (sliding and flat-folding 'Easy Flat' rear bench seat)
  • Safe road handling and suspension comfort
  • Interior build quality (ages very well despite hard plastics)
  • Practical side-opening tailgate (depending on the situation) with a low loading sill

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of diesel engines (2AD series) before 2010
  • Catastrophic oil consumption on the 2.4L gasoline (non-European markets)
  • Side-opening tailgate (hinges on the right) impractical for European urban parking
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Hard interior plastics prone to scratches

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: prioritize gasoline models with regular servicing.
  • On 4x4 versions: check the operation of the central differential lock and the absence of seepage on the rear axle.
  • Steering rack: possible clunking during low-speed turns (steering column universal joint or steering rack).
  • Corrosion: inspect the rear suspension arms (subject to a recall) and the underbody, especially on models from mountainous regions.
  • Automatic gearbox (if equipped): verify that the fluid change was done around 100,000 km, even though Toyota states it is 'lubricated for life'.

Official recalls

  • Accelerator Pedal (2010): The famous 'PedalGate'. Risk of the accelerator pedal sticking in the depressed position. Installation of a reinforcement shim.
  • Rear Suspension Arms (2012 & 2016): Risk of corrosion and breakage of the rear suspension arms if wheel alignment was not performed correctly (loose nuts). Replacement or epoxy resin sealing.
  • Power Window Switch (2012 & 2015): Risk of overheating and fire in the driver's side control module due to lack of lubricant during manufacturing.
  • Takata Airbags (2015+): Replacement of the passenger and/or driver airbag inflator (risk of metallic fragment projection).
  • Rear Seatbelts (2016): Risk of webbing severance by the seat's metal frame in the event of a severe frontal impact. Addition of resin protection.

What sets it apart 6

reliability

  • Legendary Petrol Engine Reliability — The RAV4 III's petrol engines are renowned for their exceptional robustness and durability, ensuring years of trouble-free ownership and driving pleasure.

practicality

  • Ingenious Space and Modularity — Enjoy a spacious interior and remarkable modularity thanks to the sliding and flat-folding rear bench seat ('Easy Flat'), ideal for all your adventures and loading needs.
  • Practical and Easy-Access Boot — The side-opening tailgate and its low loading sill greatly facilitate the loading and unloading of your belongings, even the bulkiest ones.

comfort

  • Suspension Comfort and Interior Quality — The RAV4 III offers appreciable suspension comfort and interior build quality that ages very well, ensuring serene and pleasant journeys for all occupants.

safety

  • Confident and Safe Road Handling — Its balanced and safe road handling ensures perfect control of the vehicle in all circumstances, for optimal peace of mind on the road.

Performance

  • Smooth and Capable Petrol Engines — The petrol engines offer a smooth and responsive drive, perfectly suited for both city and long-distance travel, with ample power for all situations.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Honda CR-V III (excellent 2.0 i-VTEC engine), Nissan X-Trail II, Volkswagen Tiguan I (beware of TSI engines from this era), Subaru Forester.
  • North America: Honda CR-V, Ford Escape, Chevrolet Equinox, Subaru Outback.
  • Japan / Asia: Nissan X-Trail, Honda CR-V, Mitsubishi Outlander.

Verdict

The Toyota RAV4 III (XA30) is a vehicle with two faces. If you opt for a gasoline engine (2.0L in Europe, 2.5L or V6 in North America), it is a highly recommended purchase: you will benefit from an extremely robust, practical, and durable family SUV. However, the diesel versions (D-4D / D-CAT) that flood the European used car market are to be absolutely avoided due to major design flaws in the engine block, unless you have absolute proof of an engine replacement by Toyota. Similarly, the 2.4L gasoline (import/North America) is to be avoided due to its oil consumption. Prioritize a post-2009 (facelifted) gasoline model for the best compromise.
