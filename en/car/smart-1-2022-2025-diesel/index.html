Summary

**WARNING - IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION:** Although the request targets a "diesel" engine, the **Smart #1 (HX11 generation) is an exclusively 100% electric vehicle (BEV)**. It has never been offered with a diesel, petrol, or hybrid engine.

The result of a joint venture between Mercedes-Benz (in charge of design) and Geely (in charge of engineering), the Smart #1 marks the revival of the brand. It abandons the micro-city car format to become a premium urban SUV (B-SUV segment). Based on Geely's excellent SEA modular platform (shared with the Volvo EX30 and Zeekr X), it stands out with a very spacious interior, meticulous finish, and top-flight performances. Mechanical and electrical reliability is very good, although early models suffered from software bugs (infotainment and driving aids) that have been gradually corrected by over-the-air (OTA) updates.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional roominess for its size (2.75 m wheelbase)
  • Very high-level performances (272 hp as standard, 428 hp in Brabus)
  • Interior finish quality and original design (by Mercedes)
  • Very well-born and robust Geely SEA platform
  • Efficient fast charging (up to 150 kW in DC, 22 kW in AC on Premium/Brabus)

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of combustion/diesel engines (for high-mileage drivers resistant to electric)
  • Limited trunk volume (323 liters) for family use
  • Complex all-touch ergonomics and overly intrusive driving aids (ADAS)
  • Frequent software bugs on early production models (2022-2023)
  • High weight (over 1,800 kg) impacting agility and highway consumption

Check before buying

  • Software update (OTA) history: crucial for system stability
  • Tire wear, particularly on the Brabus version (destructive torque for the rubber)
  • Operation of the charging flap and recognition of fast chargers
  • Absence of rattling noises in the interior trim on rough roads
  • Body panel alignment (some assembly disparities in early production)

Verdict

The Smart #1 is an excellent surprise that completely redefines the brand. Relying on Geely's electric expertise, it offers very solid mechanical features and impressive performances. **It does not exist in diesel**, which excludes traditional high-mileage highway driver profiles. However, for urban, suburban, and regular road use, it is a very relevant choice. Hardware reliability is excellent; the only real black mark concerns software stability (infotainment and driving aids), which fortunately improves with over-the-air updates. Opt for a Premium version (66 kWh NMC battery and 22 kW AC charger) for the best compromise.
