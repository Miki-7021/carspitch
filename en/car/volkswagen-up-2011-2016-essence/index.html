Volkswagen Up

I · 2011 – 2016

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The first-generation Volkswagen Up! (phase 1, pre-facelift) marked Volkswagen's successful return to the mini-city car segment (segment A) after the lukewarm reception of the Fox. Based on the NSF (New Small Family) platform shared with the Seat Mii and Skoda Citigo, it stands out for its rational design, surprising interior space for its size (3.54 m), and road handling worthy of a higher segment. Under the bonnet, it introduced the EA211 family of 3-cylinder petrol engines (1.0 MPI), renowned for their fuel efficiency and overall reliability, despite some early issues with the transmission. It's an excellent used car choice, especially with a manual gearbox.

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability of the naturally aspirated 3-cylinder engines (EA211)
  • Very sound and safe road handling for the category
  • Front passenger space and boot volume (251 liters)
  • Very low maintenance and insurance costs
  • Assembly quality superior to the segment A average

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic ASG automated manual gearbox (slow, uncomfortable, fragile)
  • Reverse gear difficult to engage (manual gearbox without synchro on reverse)
  • Normal but unpleasant mechanical noises (clutch release bearing, injection clicking)
  • Hard interior plastics sensitive to scratches
  • Light sound insulation on the highway

Check before buying

  • Clutch test: listen in neutral (release bearing noise that disappears when disengaging the clutch) and check the fluidity of reverse gear engagement.
  • Water pump: check for absence of pink traces (coolant) on the left side of the engine.
  • Air conditioning condenser: very exposed to gravel behind the lower grille, check that the AC produces cold air.
  • Water ingress: check for moisture under the floor mats and in the spare wheel well (door seals or clogged drains).
  • Front braking: sometimes premature wear of brake discs (warping).

Official recalls

  • - **Airbags (2013)**: Reprogramming or replacement of the airbag control unit which could deploy inadvertently. - **Child safety (2015)**: Child lock malfunction on rear doors (2015 models). - **Sunroof (2017)**: Risk of detachment of the panoramic sunroof frame (models produced between 2011 and 2016). - **Towing eye (2017)**: Defective welding of the towing eye (2011-2016 models).

Safety

  • High-Level Passive Safety — The Volkswagen Up! achieved the maximum Euro NCAP rating, offering remarkable protection for adult and child occupants.

Braking

  • Efficient and Reassuring Braking — Its braking performance is excellent for a city car, ensuring short stopping distances and great stability.

Handling

  • Agile and Safe Road Handling — Enjoy dynamic driving in the city and surprising stability on the road, thanks to precise and responsive steering.

Economy

  • Controlled Running Costs — With very low fuel consumption, reduced maintenance and insurance costs, the Up! is a particularly economical city car.

Practicality

  • Surprising Cabin Space and Boot Volume — Despite its compact size, the Up! offers generous front cabin space and a 251-liter boot volume, ideal for city use.

Comfort

  • Superior Build Quality — The Up! stands out with build quality and interior finish above the average for its segment, providing a feeling of robustness.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Renault Twingo III, Peugeot 108 / Citroën C1 / Toyota Aygo, Hyundai i10, Kia Picanto, Fiat 500 / Panda. - **South America**: Fiat Mobi, Renault Kwid, Chevrolet Spark / Beat. - **Japan/Asia**: Not widely marketed (competition from local Kei cars).

Verdict

The first-generation Volkswagen Up! is one of the most recommendable city cars on the used market. Its 1.0 MPI engine is a model of robustness, spared from the timing chain issues or excessive oil consumption seen on older VW blocks. The verdict is a **CLEAR BUY**, with one strict condition: **avoid the ASG automated manual gearbox** which spoils the driving experience and risks expensive repairs. Opt for a 75 hp version with a manual gearbox for more versatility, and don't hesitate to look at its siblings (Skoda Citigo, Seat Mii) which are often cheaper for equivalent equipment.
