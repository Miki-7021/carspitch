Summary

The second-generation Kia Carens (internal code UN), marketed from 2006 to 2012, represents an important step for the Korean manufacturer in the compact MPV segment. Larger, more spacious, and better equipped than its predecessor, it aimed to compete with established benchmarks such as the Renault Scénic, Citroën C4 Picasso, or Volkswagen Touran. Available in 5 or 7-seater configurations, it emphasized practicality and an attractive price/equipment ratio.

Its design, though understated, was modern for its time. A minor facelift occurred in 2009, bringing minor aesthetic touches (bumpers, grille, lights) and some interior improvements. In the North American market, it was known as the Kia Rondo.

Overall, the Carens II is an honest vehicle, offering good interior volume and appreciable suspension comfort. Its reliability is average, with generally robust petrol engines and diesels that require more maintenance, as is often the case during this period. It's a relevant choice for budget-conscious families looking for a versatile vehicle.

Strengths 6

  • Spaciousness and modularity (5 or 7-seater versions)
  • Suspension comfort
  • Attractive price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Robust naturally aspirated petrol engines (G4KA, G4KC, G6EA)
  • Good peripheral visibility
  • Light and pleasant steering in the city

Weaknesses 6

  • Reliability of 2.0 CRDi (D4EA) diesel engines: injectors, DPF, flywheel
  • Fuel consumption of petrol engines (especially the V6 and 2.0 CVVT in the city)
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds
  • Quality of some interior plastics
  • Modest performance of petrol engines under load
  • Rapid wear of tires and brakes on some models

Check before buying

  • **Diesel Engine (2.0 CRDi)**: Abnormal noises (knocking, whistling), exhaust smoke, service history (injector, DPF invoices).
  • **Clutch and Flywheel**: Noises on start-up/shutdown, slipping, vibrations (especially on diesel).
  • **Gearbox**: Smooth gear changes (manual), absence of jerks (automatic). Automatic gearbox fluid change if not done.
  • **Suspension**: Clunking noises on degraded roads (bushings, shock absorbers).
  • **Air Conditioning**: Correct operation (cold, hot), absence of abnormal compressor noises.
  • **Electronics**: Functioning of all equipment (power windows, cruise control, radio, etc.).
  • **Bodywork**: General condition, signs of impact, potential corrosion (exhaust, underbody).

Verdict

The Kia Carens II is a compact MPV that offers an interesting proposition on the used car market, especially for families looking for space and modularity at an affordable price.

**For predominantly urban or suburban use**, the 2.0 CVVT (G4KA) petrol versions are to be preferred. Their mechanical simplicity and robustness make them reliable companions, despite fuel consumption that can be high in the city. Maintenance is simpler and less expensive.

**For frequent road or highway use**, the 2.0 CRDi (D4EA) can be considered for its fuel efficiency and torque. However, it is imperative to choose a model with a clear service history, proving the replacement of injectors, flywheel, or DPF if necessary. A thorough inspection by a professional is crucial for this engine. Repair costs can be high if typical problems are not already addressed.

**In North America**, the Rondo versions with the 2.4L I4 and 2.7L V6 engines are solid choices, offering good general reliability and appreciable driving comfort, at the expense of fuel consumption for the V6.

In summary, the Carens II is a good buy if you prioritize petrol engines or if you are willing to invest in a diesel with an impeccable history. Negotiate the price if signs of wear or known diesel problems are present.
