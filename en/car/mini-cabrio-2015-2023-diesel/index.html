MINI Cabrio

III (F57) · 2015 – 2023

NEGOTIATE Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The third-generation MINI Convertible (code name F57) marks a major technological break with its predecessor (R57). Based on BMW's modular UKL platform, it abandons the capricious 'Prince' engines (PSA/BMW) in favor of the modular B-family blocks (B37/B47 for diesel, B38/B48 for gasoline). Although demand for diesel convertibles has become anecdotal (leading to their withdrawal from the catalog around 2019), the Cooper D and Cooper SD versions appealed to high-mileage drivers thanks to frugal consumption and generous torque. The model benefited from two facelifts (LCI in 2018 with 'Union Jack' lights, and LCI 2 in 2021). Overall, the F57 is a very accomplished premium vehicle, whose reliability has made a giant leap compared to previous generations, despite some peripheral weaknesses (engine mounts, EGR cooler on diesels).

Strengths 5

  • Mechanical reliability greatly improved (BMW B-Series engines) compared to the R57 generation
  • Interior finish quality worthy of the premium segment
  • 'Go-kart' handling preserved despite the weight
  • Ridiculously low fuel consumption of diesel versions (Cooper D / SD)
  • Electric soft top with very practical 'sunroof' function

Weaknesses 5

  • Diesel sound and odors not very compatible with the convertible spirit
  • Recurring EGR cooler problems on diesels (B37/B47)
  • Premature wear of engine mounts (a common issue on UKL platforms)
  • Almost zero rear visibility when the soft top is folded
  • Symbolic trunk volume (160 to 215 liters) and difficult access

Check before buying

  • Recall history: the EGR cooler MUST have been checked/replaced on diesels.
  • Coolant level (Diesels): a drop indicates an internal EGR leak.
  • Soft top condition: look for signs of rubbing or premature wear at folding points (possible misalignment defect).
  • Vibrations at idle or acceleration: sign of a faulty upper right engine mount (very common).
  • Moisture in the trunk or under the carpets: check the seals of the soft top and the water drainage channels.

Verdict

The MINI Convertible F57 is undoubtedly the most reliable generation ever produced. If you specifically target a **diesel (Cooper D or SD)**, you will benefit from exceptional range and very pleasant torque. However, buying a used diesel requires absolute vigilance regarding the history of the EGR cooler (BMW recall) and necessitates regular road use to avoid DPF clogging. For purely 'pleasure' or urban use, the gasoline engines (Cooper or Cooper S) are infinitely more relevant, more reliable for short trips, and offer a sound much more in line with open-top driving.
