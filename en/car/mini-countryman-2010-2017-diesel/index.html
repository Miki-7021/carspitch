MINI Countryman

I (R60) · 2010 – 2017

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The first-generation MINI Countryman (codename **R60**) marks a historic turning point for the brand: it is the first MINI model to exceed 4 meters, to offer 5 doors, and to provide all-wheel drive (ALL4). Positioned as a premium urban SUV, it retains MINI's stylistic DNA and dynamic "go-kart" handling, while offering unprecedented versatility.

A facelift (LCI) occurred in **July 2014**, bringing improved sound insulation, better quality interior plastics, LED daytime running lights, and minor engine updates to meet the Euro 6 standard. Although very appealing, the R60 inherited engines shared with BMW and PSA (Prince engines for petrol, N47 engines for diesel) which require rigorous monitoring due to well-documented chronic weaknesses.

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic and precise road handling (rare for an SUV)
  • Surprising interior space for 4 adults
  • Efficiency of the ALL4 system in snow and rain
  • Iconic design and strong residual value
  • Very controlled fuel consumption on diesel versions (Cooper D)

Weaknesses 5

  • Variable engine reliability (N47 diesel and Prince petrol)
  • Very firm suspension, degraded comfort with 18/19-inch wheels and Runflat tires
  • Imperfect sound insulation on pre-2014 models
  • Quality of some interior plastics unworthy of the premium positioning (pre-LCI)
  • Boot volume (350L) a bit tight for a family

Check before buying

  • Timing chain noise when cold (clattering/scraping) on ALL engines, especially N47 diesels.
  • Service history: avoid vehicles that followed the "Long Life" interval of 30,000 km. Prefer annual/15,000 km oil changes.
  • Electronics operation: FRM (Footwell Module) controlling lighting and windows, very sensitive to battery voltage drops.
  • Traces of oil leaks (valve cover gasket, oil filter housing) and coolant leaks (water housing).
  • Clutch and dual-mass flywheel on manual diesel models (juddering on startup).

Official recalls

  • - **Auxiliary water pump (2012)**: Risk of overheating of the turbo water pump's circuit board (Cooper S/JCW), potentially leading to fire.
  • - **Temperature sensor (2013)**: Replacement of the coolant temperature sensor.
  • - **Passenger airbag (2015-2017)**: Passenger seat occupancy sensor issue (primarily North American market).
  • - **FRM Module**: Not a strict safety recall, but warranty extension granted in some countries (e.g., USA) following frequent failures of the lighting module.

Performance

  • Unrivaled "Karting" Agility — Enjoy dynamic road behavior and agile handling, true to MINI's DNA, with precise steering and excellent body roll control.
  • Generous Torque and Dynamic Acceleration — The diesel engines deliver generous torque, offering lively acceleration and an enjoyable drive, even with the ALL4 system.

Economy

  • Remarkable Diesel Efficiency — The Countryman R60's diesel engines offer very controlled fuel consumption, ideal for long journeys and an optimized budget.
  • Iconic Style and Lasting Value — Its distinctive and timeless design ensures a strong residual value, making the Countryman a smart and elegant investment.

Practicality

  • Surprising Space and Versatility — Despite its compact size, the Countryman R60 offers generous interior space for four adults and increased versatility thanks to its five doors.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Nissan Juke (less premium but more reliable in petrol), Audi Q3 (more comfortable, superior finish), BMW X1 E84 (shares the same N47 engines), Fiat 500X. - **North America**: Buick Encore, Fiat 500X, Mazda CX-3. - **Asia / Emerging Markets**: Honda Vezel/HR-V, Toyota C-HR.

Verdict

The MINI Countryman R60 is a beloved vehicle that offers a unique driving experience in its category. However, as an expert, I urge extreme caution when purchasing, particularly for **Diesel (N47)** versions. The risk associated with the timing chain is real, and the repair bill is exorbitant. If you drive a lot, it is imperative to look for a **post-2014 (LCI)** model with a clear service history and frequent oil changes. For mixed or urban use, prefer a more robust naturally aspirated petrol version (Cooper 122 hp), or accept the maintenance budget of a Cooper S by purchasing it with full awareness.
