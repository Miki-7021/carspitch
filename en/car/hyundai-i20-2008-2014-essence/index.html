Hyundai i20

I (PB) · 2008 – 2014

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The **first-generation Hyundai i20 (codename PB)** was launched in late 2008 to replace the aging Getz. Designed at Hyundai's European R&D center in Rüsselsheim (Germany), it was specifically conceived to meet Old Continent standards in terms of road handling, interior space, and safety (5 Euro NCAP stars).

It stands out for its excellent price/equipment ratio and a 5-year manufacturer's warranty, which greatly contributed to its success. In **2012**, it received a **facelift (Phase 2)** adopting the brand's *Fluidic Sculpture* design language, improving the perceived quality of interior plastics and introducing new engines (including the 1.1 CRDi and an evolution of the 1.2 gasoline engine with 85 hp).

It is a rational versatile city car, particularly renowned for the robustness of its gasoline engines, which form the core of this report.

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability of gasoline engines (chain timing)
  • Generous interior space for the category (295-liter trunk)
  • Rich standard equipment from mid-range trims
  • Reduced maintenance cost and affordable parts
  • Sound and safe road handling

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation (road and wind noise on the highway)
  • Interior plastic quality (hard and scratch-prone, especially before 2012)
  • Firm damping, bumpy comfort at low speeds
  • Electric power steering (EPS) lacking feel and a center point
  • Fragile paint and clear coat (sensitive to gravel and bird droppings)

Check before buying

  • **Steering column:** Knocking or play when turning the steering wheel (wear of the EPS electric power steering motor's flex disc/rubber star - a very common problem with Hyundai/Kia, inexpensive repair if only the star-shaped part is replaced).
  • **Clutch:** Juddering or trembling when starting in first gear (common on the 1.2 gasoline engine).
  • **Onboard electronics:** Malfunction of the car radio/CD player and central locking.
  • **Running gear:** Wear of stabilizer bar links (noises on degraded roads).
  • **DPF/EGR (Diesels):** Engine light on or loss of power if the vehicle has only been driven in the city.

Safety

  • Optimal Safety with 5-Star Euro NCAP Rating — Designed to protect its occupants, the Hyundai i20 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with high scores for adult and child protection.

Practicality

  • Generous Interior Space and Practical Boot — Despite its compact size, the i20 offers surprising spaciousness for its category, ensuring appreciable comfort for all passengers and a practical boot volume for daily use.

Performance

  • Efficient and Controlled Braking — The Hyundai i20 benefits from an efficient and progressive braking system, offering competitive stopping distances and excellent modulation for increased safety in all circumstances.

Comfort

  • Safe and Stable Road Handling — Its well-balanced chassis gives the i20 predictable road holding and good grip, ensuring a serene and stable drive, even on longer journeys.
  • Comprehensive and Modern Standard Equipment — From intermediate trims upwards, the Hyundai i20 stands out with rich standard equipment, offering features that enhance daily comfort and driving pleasure.

Economy

  • Controlled Running and Maintenance Costs — Thanks to the robustness of its petrol engines and the availability of competitively priced parts, the Hyundai i20 represents an economical choice to run, reducing the maintenance budget.

Verdict

The Hyundai i20 (PB) is an **excellent used car choice if you opt for a gasoline engine**, especially the 1.2 (78 or 85 hp). It's a trouble-free city car, economical to use, and spared from major mechanical issues thanks to its chain timing. Diesel versions (CRDi) should be avoided unless you are certain to make regular long journeys, as they suffer from classic urban fouling problems. When purchasing, it is imperative to check for any knocking in the steering (EPS flex disc) and proper clutch operation.
