Summary

The Opel Crossland, initially launched as the Crossland X in 2017, is an urban SUV (B-segment) that succeeded the Meriva minivan. Developed on the PSA Group's PF1 platform (shared with the first-generation Peugeot 2008 and Citroën C3 Aircross), it marked Opel's integration into the PSA orbit before the Stellantis merger. Its positioning is that of a family-friendly and practical vehicle, prioritizing spaciousness and comfort over sportiness.

A significant facelift in late 2020 saw the "X" suffix disappear, with the model becoming simply "Crossland". This facelift brought a new front end (the Opel "Vizor"), minor interior improvements, and a slight revision of the suspension.

Overall, the Crossland is a relevant choice for urban and suburban families looking for a versatile, spacious, and economical vehicle to use, provided they choose the right engine and monitor certain known reliability issues.

Strengths 6

  • Interior spaciousness and modularity (sliding rear bench on some versions)
  • Suspension comfort, ideal for long journeys and poor roads
  • Raised driving position and good visibility
  • Reasonable fuel consumption, especially with diesel engines
  • Good value for money on the used market
  • Easy to handle and drive in the city

Weaknesses 6

  • Less dynamic road handling than some competitors (prioritizes comfort)
  • Perceived quality of interior materials could be improved in the early years
  • Reliability of the timing belt on 1.2 PureTech engines
  • Reliability of the AdBlue system on diesel engines (1.6 and 1.5 BlueHDi)
  • Noise insulation could be improved at high speeds
  • Exterior design is not universally appealing (before the facelift)

Check before buying

  • **Complete service history** : Invoices, recalls performed, proof of timing belt replacement (PureTech) or AdBlue repairs (BlueHDi).
  • **1.2 PureTech engines** : Check the condition of the timing belt (by a professional), listen for abnormal noises on startup or acceleration, check the cleanliness of the engine oil.
  • **BlueHDi engines** : Ensure no error messages related to the AdBlue system are displayed. Check the history of AdBlue tank, injector, or pump replacements.
  • **Electronics** : Test the proper functioning of the touchscreen, multimedia system, driving aids (if present).
  • **Running gear** : Listen for suspension noises, check tire wear (indicator of poor alignment or suspension problems).
  • **Bodywork and interior** : Check the general condition, panel alignments, wear of seats and plastics.

Verdict

The Opel Crossland is an urban SUV that fulfills its role as a practical and comfortable family vehicle well. Its main assets are its spaciousness and modularity, as well as its ride comfort.

**For a worry-free purchase, vigilance is required regarding the engines:**

* **If you opt for a 1.2 PureTech petrol engine** : It is imperative to check the service history and ensure that the timing belt has been replaced according to recommendations (or even earlier) and that the specific engine oil has always been used. Rigorous monitoring is essential to avoid costly breakdowns. These engines are suitable for urban and mixed use.

* **If you opt for a BlueHDi diesel engine** : The **1.5 BlueHDi is clearly the most sensible choice** compared to the 1.6 BlueHDi, as it is generally more reliable, although the AdBlue system remains a major point of vigilance on both. These engines are to be preferred for high-mileage drivers and road/motorway journeys, where their fuel efficiency is an asset. Ensure that the AdBlue system operates without error messages and that any necessary repairs have been carried out.

In summary, the Crossland is a good used vehicle, but it requires a thorough inspection and a transparent service history, especially concerning the known weaknesses of the engines. Do not hesitate to negotiate the price if the history is not perfect or if repairs are anticipated.
