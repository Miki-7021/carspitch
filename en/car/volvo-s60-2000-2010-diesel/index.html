Summary

The first-generation Volvo S60 (based on the famous P2 platform shared with the V70, S80, and XC90) marked a turning point for the Swedish brand with its 'broad shoulders' design by Peter Horbury. Positioned as a premium family sedan, it stands out for its exceptional ride comfort, seats among the best in the automotive industry, and very high levels of passive safety. Under the hood, it prominently features inline 5-cylinder engines, in both petrol and diesel versions. The restyling (facelift) carried out in late 2004 (model year 2005) brought painted bumpers, clear-lens headlights, a modernized center console, and significant electronic and mechanical updates (transition to Euro 4 standards for diesels). Although this report focuses on the excellent D5 diesels, the S60 offered a full range of engines to meet all markets.

Strengths 6

  • Unrivaled seat comfort in its category
  • Charismatic and very durable 5-cylinder engines (petrol and diesel)
  • Quality of the original audio system (especially Premium Sound HU-803/850 with Dolby Pro Logic)
  • Passive and active safety (SIPS, WHIPS) very advanced for its time
  • Excellent corrosion resistance
  • Very well-controlled fuel consumption on D5 diesels (Euro 3)

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic turning radius (like a truck)
  • Limited rear legroom for a sedan of this size
  • Fragile front axle (wishbone bushings, linkages, shock absorber mounts wear out quickly)
  • Reliability of the AW55-50SN automatic gearbox (5-speed) if not flushed
  • Odometer (DIM) prone to display failures (dry solder joints, repairable)

Check before buying

  • Automatic gearbox fluid change history (if automatic). Avoid if gear changes are jerky when hot.
  • On Euro 4 diesels (126/163/185hp): condition of the accessory belt and presence of the swirl rod.
  • On Euro 3 diesels (130/163hp): absence of 'hissing' or black tar around the injectors.
  • On petrol engines: glove test to check the PCV system (breather).
  • Full dashboard (DIM) functionality: check that the clock isn't running backward and that messages are displayed.
  • Clunking noises from the front axle when going over speed bumps (mounts or wishbones).

Official recalls

  • Driver airbag (Takata) (2020/2021) : Risk of metal fragments being projected during deployment. Airbag replacement on 2001-2009 models.
  • Cooling fan (2004) : The fan control module can overheat and cause a fire. Concerns 2000-2001 models.
  • Front suspension ball joints (2004) : Risk of ball joint separation from the suspension arm on certain 2001-2003 models.
  • Fuel pump relay (2006) : Risk of engine stalling while driving on 2001-2005 models.

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Legendary Reliability of the D5 Euro 3 Engine — The D5 Euro 3 engine (130/163 hp) is a monument to reliability, renowned for its exceptional longevity and its ability to exceed 500,000 km with regular maintenance.

Comfort

  • Unrivaled Travel Comfort — Enjoy unparalleled seat comfort in its category and exceptional ride comfort, transforming every journey into a most pleasant experience.

Economy

  • Controlled Fuel Consumption — The D5 diesel engines offer very controlled fuel consumption, making this S60 an economical and efficient sedan for long journeys.

Safety

  • Advanced Passive and Active Safety — Benefit from passive and active safety well ahead of its time, with systems like SIPS and WHIPS, and a 4-star Euro NCAP rating for adult protection.

Braking

  • Powerful and Stable Braking — The Volvo S60 I stands out with very good braking performance, offering short distances and excellent stability thanks to standard ABS and EBD, ensuring optimal efficiency in emergency situations.

tenue de route

  • Safe and Reassuring Road Holding — Its road behavior is safe, predictable, and focused on stability, offering very reassuring road holding, ideal for long journeys and varied conditions, enhanced by the DSTC system.

Practicality

  • Premium Audio Quality and Corrosion Resistance — Immerse yourself in an exceptional sound experience thanks to the quality of the original audio system (Premium Sound with Dolby Pro Logic) and benefit from excellent corrosion resistance.

Verdict

The Volvo S60 I is a fantastic 'mileage eater'. If you're looking for a diesel, the D5 Euro 3 (163 hp, 2001-2005) is a monument to reliability, capable of exceeding 500,000 km with basic maintenance, while consuming very little. The Euro 4 versions (185 hp, 2005-2009) are more powerful and modern, but require absolute vigilance regarding the accessory belt and the DPF. Prioritize a manual gearbox, or an automatic gearbox with a clear fluid change (flush) history. For petrol, the naturally aspirated versions or the 'Low Pressure Turbo' (2.4T / 2.5T) offer royal driving pleasure for a reasonable maintenance cost.
