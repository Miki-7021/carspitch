Summary

The Mercedes-Benz E-Class (W213 generation for the sedan, S213 for the wagon, C238/A238 for coupe/cabriolet) represents a major technological leap for the star brand. Launched in 2016, it introduced the famous 'Widescreen Cockpit' (dual screen) and advanced semi-autonomous driving aids. The 2020 facelift brought the MBUX multimedia system, new capacitive steering wheels, and almost generalized mild hybridization (48V) on gasoline engines. Although this sheet focuses on gasoline engines (very popular in recent used cars and international markets), the W213 has also been a benchmark diesel long-distance cruiser. Overall, it is an extremely comfortable and well-finished car, but its electronic complexity (especially the 48V EQ Boost systems) requires rigorous monitoring.

Strengths 5

  • Imperial ride comfort, especially with Airmatic air suspension
  • Very high-quality interior finish and sound insulation
  • Onboard technology (Widescreen, MBUX post-2020) still relevant
  • Exceptional 6-cylinder engines (V6 M276 and L6 M256) for driving pleasure
  • Controlled fuel consumption on 4-cylinder gasoline and diesel versions

Weaknesses 5

  • Erratic reliability of the 48V mild hybrid system (EQ Boost) on 2019-2021 models
  • Replacement cost of air suspension dampers (Airmatic)
  • Creaking of center console plastics (gloss black) on degraded roads
  • Reduced trunk volume on plug-in hybrid (PHEV) versions due to the battery
  • Price of spare parts and hourly rate in the Mercedes-Benz network

Check before buying

  • 48V network: Ensure that no yellow or red error message appears on the dashboard concerning the battery.
  • Airmatic suspension: Check that the car does not sag on one side after prolonged parking (air spring leak or tired compressor).
  • Cabin electronics: Test ambient lighting (some LEDs burn out), the operation of both screens and the touchpad.
  • 9G-Tronic gearbox service history: Mandatory oil change every 125,000 km or 5 years.
  • Panoramic sunroof: Check for absence of wind noise and clean water drainage channels to prevent cabin flooding.

Official recalls

  • Steering (2017): Risk of short circuit in the power steering control unit potentially leading to loss of assistance.
  • Airbags (2018): Possible inadvertent deployment of the driver's airbag due to a rupture of the contact spiral in the steering column.
  • Alternator / Fire (2020): On gasoline engines with EQ Boost, risk of overheating of an electrical connection of the starter-alternator.
  • eCall (2021): Software bug sending erroneous GPS coordinates to emergency services in case of an accident (OTA update).
  • Coolant pump (2022): On some diesels, possible leak leading to pump deactivation and fire risk (massive recall).

Verdict

The Mercedes E-Class W213 is an exceptional long-distance cruiser that has aged brilliantly in terms of aesthetics and technology. For gasoline engines, the choice must be strategic. The V6 versions (E400) are the most reliable in the long term. The recent 4-cylinders (M264/M254) and the L6 (M256) offer fantastic driving pleasure thanks to 48V hybridization, but this technology has experienced costly failures: an extended warranty is highly recommended for these models. Avoid poorly maintained models or those without a clear service history, as the slightest electronic or pneumatic (Airmatic) breakdown can cost thousands of euros.
