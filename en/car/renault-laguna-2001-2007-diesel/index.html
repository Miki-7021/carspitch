Renault Laguna

Summary

The Renault Laguna II (BG0 generation for the sedan, KG0 for the Estate wagon) is a notoriously famous model in the history of French automobiles. Launched in 2001, it was a pioneer in terms of safety (the first car in the world to obtain 5 stars in the Euro NCAP crash test) and technology (hands-free card, advanced multiplexing, tire pressure sensors).

However, its rushed launch led to an **industrial catastrophe in terms of reliability** for Phase 1 (2001-2005), particularly concerning diesel engines (1.9 dCi 120 and 2.2 dCi 150) and onboard electronics. The March 2005 facelift (Phase 2) corrected the vast majority of defects and introduced the excellent chain-driven 2.0 dCi engine, making the 2005-2007 models very reliable. Unfortunately, the damage was done, and the model's popularity (as well as its resale value) plummeted. Today, it's a car with two faces: a financial nightmare in Phase 1 diesel, and an excellent deal in Phase 2 (2.0 dCi or petrol).

Strengths 5

  • High-level suspension comfort and soundproofing
  • Exceptional passive safety (5 Euro NCAP stars)
  • Very rich equipment for its time (Initiale Paris)
  • Remarkable reliability of Phase 2 versions (2005-2007) with the 2.0 dCi
  • Ridiculously low used purchase price (victim of its reputation)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of Phase 1 diesels (1.9 dCi 120 and 2.2 dCi 150)
  • Capricious electronics on 2001-2004 models (cards, sensors, warning lights)
  • Fragile PK6 manual gearbox (gearbox bearings)
  • Interior plastics that age poorly (peeling soft-touch coating)
  • Ruined brand image, difficult resale

Check before buying

  • Clear history and maintenance invoices (especially on diesels)
  • Turbo whistling on acceleration (sign of imminent wear on 1.9 dCi)
  • Functionality of both start cards (central locking and keyless start if hands-free option)
  • Functionality of all electric windows
  • Absence of dashboard warning lights (especially injection, airbag, ESP)
  • Gear shifting (PK6 gearbox): no crunching or bearing whistling

What sets it apart 14

Safety

  • Pioneer in Road Safety — The Renault Laguna II made history by being the first car in the world to achieve the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with an impressive 89% score for adult occupant protection. It integrates advanced safety features for optimal peace of mind.

Comfort

  • Serene and Refined Journeys — Enjoy top-tier suspension comfort and remarkable sound insulation, transforming every journey into a pleasant and relaxing experience, even over long distances.

Reliability

  • Exemplary Mechanical Reliability (Phase 2 2.0 dCi) — Phase 2 versions (2005-2007) equipped with the 2.0 dCi engine (150 or 175 hp) offer exceptional mechanical robustness thanks to its chain timing and a refined design, ensuring remarkable longevity.

Economy

  • A Smart Investment — Benefit from an exceptional value for money on the used car market. The Laguna II Phase 2 diesel represents a unique opportunity to acquire a comfortable and reliable grand tourer for a very accessible budget.

Handling

  • Safe and Predictable Road Handling — Designed for serenity, the Laguna II offers stable and predictable road handling, ideal for long journeys. Its balanced behavior is enhanced by available systems like ESP, ensuring confident driving.

Braking

  • Control and Braking Efficiency — The Laguna II ensures effective braking performance and excellent stability, even under heavy solicitation. Equipped with ABS, electronic brake force distribution (EBD), and emergency brake assist (AFU), it guarantees optimal active safety.

Practicality

  • Rich and Innovative Equipment — Enjoy a very comprehensive level of equipment for its era, including innovations like the hands-free card and advanced multiplexing, offering a modern and practical driving experience.

Alternatives

- **Europe** : Peugeot 406 / 407 (the 2.0 HDi is much more reliable than the 1.9 dCi), Citroën C5 I, Volkswagen Passat B5.5 / B6 (robust but noisy 1.9 TDI), Ford Mondeo II, Toyota Avensis II (reliability benchmark). - **Asia / Other** : Honda Accord, Mazda 6.

Verdict

The Renault Laguna II is the perfect example of a car to avoid or cherish depending on its production year. **If you are looking for a diesel from before 2005 (1.9 dCi 120 or 2.2 dCi), walk away without the slightest hesitation**, the risk of engine failure or financial ruin is critical. On the other hand, **a Laguna II Phase 2 (2005-2007) equipped with the 2.0 dCi (150 or 175 hp) is an excellent deal**: it offers the comfort and safety of a grand tourer for the price of a used city car, with mechanical reliability that has become irreproachable. Petrol versions (excluding 2.0 IDE) are also very good economic choices.
