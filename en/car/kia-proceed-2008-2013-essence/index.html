Summary

The **Kia pro_cee'd (Generation I, internal code ED)** is the 3-door sporty hatchback variant of the highly successful Kia Cee'd. Designed in Frankfurt and manufactured in Žilina, Slovakia, it was created exclusively for the European market to compete in the compact coupe/hatchback segment. It features a more aggressive roofline, unique rear styling, and a slightly stiffer suspension setup compared to the 5-door model. The pro_cee'd was a game-changer for Kia, largely due to its unprecedented 7-year/150,000 km warranty, which forced owners to maintain the cars meticulously at official dealerships. A mid-cycle facelift was introduced in late 2010, bringing the 'Tiger Nose' grille, interior upgrades, and Euro 5 compliant engines. Overall, it remains a highly rational, reliable, and stylish choice on the used market, particularly in its petrol variants.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of the 1.4 and 1.6 naturally aspirated petrol engines
  • Sporty and attractive exterior design that has aged well
  • Most used models have full service histories (due to the original 7-year warranty requirements)
  • Spacious interior and decent boot space (340 liters) for a 3-door coupe/hatch
  • Multi-link rear suspension provides good handling and stability

Weaknesses 6

  • Electric Power Steering (EPS) 'clunk' noise due to a failing €2 rubber star coupling in the steering column
  • Interior build quality issues: dashboard material peeling/lifting near the windshield
  • Stiff ride quality, especially on 17-inch alloy wheels
  • High road and wind noise at highway speeds
  • Original factory paint is relatively thin and prone to stone chips and minor scratches
  • Factory radio/CD units are prone to freezing or failing completely

Check before buying

  • **Steering Column:** Turn the wheel left and right while stationary. A clicking or clunking sound indicates a worn EPS flexible coupling.
  • **Dashboard:** Inspect the top of the dashboard near the air vents and windshield for bubbling or peeling.
  • **Suspension:** Listen for knocking noises over bumps (anti-roll bar drop links wear out frequently but are cheap to fix).
  • **Bodywork:** Check for rust around the third brake light on the tailgate and at the bottom seams of the doors (mostly pre-2010 models).
  • **Service History:** Ensure oil changes were done annually. For 2.0L engines, verify timing belt replacement.

Verdict

The first-generation Kia pro_cee'd is an excellent, budget-friendly choice for buyers seeking a reliable and stylish 3-door hatchback. **The petrol engines (1.4 and 1.6 CVVT) are the absolute stars here**, offering bulletproof reliability thanks to their simple naturally aspirated design and timing chains. While the interior plastics and steering column coupling may show their age, these are minor, easily fixable flaws compared to the catastrophic engine failures seen in some European rivals of the same era (e.g., VW's early 1.4 TSI). **Buy a 1.6 CVVT petrol** for the best daily driving experience, preferably a post-2010 facelift model for the updated interior and Euro 5 compliance.
