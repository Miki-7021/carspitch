Hyundai i30

I (FD) (2007–2012) · 2007 – 2012

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

All Hyundai models

Summary

The first-generation Hyundai i30 (internal code FD) marks a historic turning point for the Korean manufacturer. Designed at the R&D center in Rüsselsheim, Germany, it was specifically conceived to meet the demands of the European market. A technical cousin of the Kia Cee'd (ED), it shares its platform and powertrains. Positioned in the ultra-competitive C-segment (compacts), it stood out for its excellent price/equipment ratio, generous interior space, and the adoption of a multi-link rear suspension (rare at this price level). A facelift occurred in early 2010, bringing minor aesthetic touches (grille, bumpers) and suspension adjustments. Although demand here focuses on diesel (very popular at the time), the i30 offered a full range of reliable engines, making it an excellent choice on the budget used car market today.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent overall reliability, particularly the 1.6 CRDi engine with timing chain
  • Sound and safe road handling (multi-link rear suspension)
  • Generous rear passenger space and decent boot volume (340L / 415L in CW wagon)
  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Simple and intuitive interior ergonomics

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation (road and aerodynamic noise on the highway)
  • Quality of some interior plastics (prone to scratches)
  • Electric power steering (EPS) with an artificial and sticky feel around the center point
  • Somewhat firm damping at low speeds
  • Fragile paint clear coat (especially on Red and White)

Check before buying

  • Clunking in the steering column (wear of the electric power steering's star-shaped flex disc, a very common issue).
  • Stabilizer bar links (clunking noises on degraded roads, rapid wear).
  • Operation of the car radio/CD player (the screen can become unreadable with heat).
  • Timing belt replacement history (ONLY on 2.0 CRDi and 2.0 MPI).
  • Clutch biting point on diesels (cold judder indicating wear of the flywheel or release bearing).
  • Brake light operation (pedal switch often faulty).

Official recalls

  • Brake light switch (April 2013) : Massive recall (2007-2011 models) to replace the brake pedal switch which could prevent the brake lights from illuminating or deactivate the ESP.
  • Door handle cable (2012) : Risk of inadvertent door opening in case of impact (models manufactured in the Czech Republic between 2007 and 2012).
  • ESP module (2011) : Reprogramming or replacement of the ESP module due to a risk of brake pressure loss on some 2007-2008 models.

Verdict

The Hyundai i30 I (FD) is an extremely rational and recommendable used car choice. If you are looking for an economical and indestructible diesel, the 1.6 CRDi (ideally 115 hp) is one of the most reliable engines of its generation, thanks to its timing chain and robust architecture (U block). Avoid the 2.0 CRDi, which is more expensive to maintain (belt) and less relevant today. Although the sound insulation and plastic quality betray its age, its mechanical reliability largely compensates for these cosmetic flaws. Be sure to check for any clunking in the steering (flex disc) before purchase.
