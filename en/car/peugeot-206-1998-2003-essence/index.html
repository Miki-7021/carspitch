Peugeot 206

I (2A/C) · 1998 – 2003

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The Peugeot 206 (Generation I, phase 1 and early phase 2) had the challenging task of succeeding the legendary 205. A true global best-seller, it stood out for its timeless design, dynamic road handling (typical of Peugeot), and wide range of engines. The 1998-2003 period covers its launch up to the introduction of the facelifted versions. A major turning point occurred in late 2001 with the introduction of multiplexing (VAN/CAN network), which brought new features but also numerous electronic bugs (the famous COM2000 unit). Although demand targeted petrol engines (TU and EW families), the 206 also popularized common rail diesel (HDi). Today, it is an excellent youngtimer or student car, but its advanced age requires rigorous inspection, especially of the rear axle.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road handling (sharp front axle)
  • Design that has aged very well
  • Negligible maintenance cost and parts availability (new and scrapyard)
  • Very durable 1.6 16v petrol and 2.0 HDi diesel engines
  • Ideal size for the city with good interior space

Weaknesses 5

  • Trailing arm rear axle: needle bearings wear out, wheels develop negative camber (/ \) and rub against the wheel arches (high repair cost).
  • Capricious electronics from late 2001 (multiplexing): faulty COM2000 unit (stalks), airbag warning lights.
  • Light interior finish, hard plastics that age poorly, and omnipresent rattles.
  • Imperfect driving position (steering wheel not adjustable for reach).
  • Very fragile cylinder head gasket on 8-valve petrol engines (TU).

Check before buying

  • Rear axle: stand behind the car and check that the rear wheels are perfectly straight. Listen for squeaks over speed bumps.
  • TU petrol engines: check for oil leaks on the front right of the engine block (sign of a tired cylinder head gasket).
  • Electronics (late 2001+ models): test all stalk functions (self-cancelling indicators, horn, wipers).
  • Airbag warning light: often illuminated due to connectors under the front seats (to be soldered directly).
  • Exhaust system: very susceptible to corrosion, particularly the rear silencer.

Official recalls

  • - **2000**: Recall for a risk of inadvertent deployment of side airbags (wiring harness issue).
  • - **2001**: Inspection and possible replacement of front suspension wishbones (fragile ball joints).
  • - **2002**: Recall on the intelligent service unit (BSI) causing various electrical malfunctions (headlights, wipers).
  • - **2003**: Handbrake. Risk of unintended release, modification of the locking pawl.
  • - **2004**: (Concerns 2003 models) Recall on the motorized throttle body of 1.6 16v versions.

Verdict

The first-generation Peugeot 206 is an affordable icon, but it is not without flaws related to its age. For a confident petrol purchase, prioritize the **1.4i 75hp** (accepting the risk of a cylinder head gasket issue, an inexpensive repair if anticipated) or the **1.6 16v 110hp** (the most balanced and reliable). Avoid models with a sagging rear axle ('Gordini' wheels) or whose electronics (post-2001) light up like a Christmas tree. Non-multiplexed versions (1998-mid 2001) are more basic but spare you electrical meltdowns. It's an excellent first car if it has been rigorously maintained.
