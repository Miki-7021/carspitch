BMW Série 1

F40 · 2019 – 2024

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The **BMW 1 Series (F40 Generation)** marks a historic break for the Bavarian manufacturer: the abandonment of rear-wheel drive and longitudinal engines in favor of a front-wheel drive architecture (UKL2 platform) with transverse engines. While purists grumbled, this choice significantly improved rear passenger space and trunk volume, thus correcting the main flaws of the previous generation (F20).

In terms of reliability, the F40 benefits from the maturity of the modular B-series engines (B38, B48 for gasoline; B37, B47 for diesel), which have been largely made reliable since their introduction in 2014. The interior finish quality takes a leap forward, reaching or even surpassing that of its direct rivals (Audi A3, Mercedes A-Class). The iDrive 7.0 infotainment system is one of the most stable on the market. **Overall design rating: Excellent.**

Strengths 5

  • Excellent overall reliability, particularly for gasoline engines (B38/B48).
  • Very high level of interior finish quality and assembly.
  • Intuitive, fluid iDrive 7.0 multimedia system, free of major bugs.
  • Rear passenger space and trunk volume (380L) significantly improved compared to the previous generation.
  • Sharp and safe road handling, despite the switch to front-wheel drive.

Weaknesses 5

  • Loss of the "rear-wheel drive" character that made the 1 Series uniquely charming.
  • Very firm damping, particularly with M Sport suspension and 18/19-inch wheels.
  • DKG7 gearbox sometimes hesitant at very low speeds or during parking maneuvers.
  • Rapid wear of front tires on torquey gasoline versions (118i, 128ti).
  • Poor rear visibility (thick C-pillars).

Check before buying

  • Service history: check that oil changes have been done on time (ideally more frequently).
  • Automatic gearbox behavior: test low-speed maneuvers to detect any jerks (DKG7).
  • Tire wear: check the front axle, prone to asymmetrical wear if alignment is off.
  • Electronics: test all functions of the central screen and verify that OTA (Over-The-Air) updates have been performed.
  • On diesels: check if the vehicle is affected by the EGR cooler recall (via the BMW portal with the VIN).

Official recalls

  • - **November 2023 (Diesels)**: Massive recall (code 0011400700) concerning the EGR valve cooler. Risk of coolant leak which could lead to a fire hazard.
  • - **February 2021**: Recall concerning the front seat belt retractors which might not lock correctly in the event of a collision.
  • - **August 2019**: On the very first models, a manufacturing defect on the steering tie rods could lead to a loss of control (very few vehicles affected, corrected before delivery for most).

Verdict

The BMW 1 Series F40 is an **excellent used car purchase choice**, particularly in its gasoline versions. While it has lost its atypical rear-wheel drive architecture, it has gained in maturity, space, and especially reliability. The B38 (118i) and B48 (120i, 128ti) engines are among the most robust blocks in their current category. For daily use, the **118i** represents the most rational and balanced choice. Dynamic driving enthusiasts will turn to the **128ti**, an excellent front-wheel drive GTI. Simply avoid diesel versions if you primarily drive in the city to prevent classic clogging issues.
