Summary

The Mercedes-Benz GLA (X156) is the first compact SUV from the star brand, based on the MFA (Modular Front Architecture) platform shared with the A-Class (W176). Closer to a raised sedan than a true off-roader, it stands out for its dynamic design, agile road handling, and premium positioning.

Evolution: A facelift occurred in early 2017, bringing aesthetic touches (bumpers, LED optics), a slightly raised ground clearance as standard, an improvement in perceived interior quality, and the integration of Apple CarPlay / Android Auto.

Overall Note: It is an overall reliable model, particularly with gasoline engines (M270 blocks), which avoids the pitfalls of modern diesels in urban use. Its main weaknesses lie in the rear spaciousness and suspension comfort, often deemed too firm.

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability of gasoline engines (M270) very reassuring
  • Dynamic road handling, close to a sedan
  • Exterior design that ages very well
  • Improved finish quality after the 2017 facelift
  • Effective 4MATIC system on wet/snowy roads

Weaknesses 5

  • 7G-DCT gearbox sometimes hesitant and generating jerks at low speeds
  • Firm suspension comfort (especially with 19-inch wheels and sport chassis)
  • Limited rear spaciousness and trunk volume (421 L) for the category
  • Imperfect sound insulation (wind noise on highway, noisy diesel engines)
  • Frequent parasitic noises (squeaks) in the cabin on pre-2017 models (screen, dashboard)

Check before buying

  • Service history of the 7G-DCT automatic gearbox (oil change invoice mandatory if > 100,000 km)
  • Operation of the panoramic sunroof (creaks, slowness, seals)
  • Condition of the thermostat on gasoline engines (check that the water temperature rises normally and stabilizes at 90°C)
  • Wear of front tires (the front axle is heavy and tends to wear out tires quickly)
  • Operation of LED daytime running lights (modules can burn out and often require complete replacement of the optic)

Official recalls

  • Airbags (2019-2021): Massive recall related to the Takata scandal. Replacement of the driver and/or passenger airbag gas generator.
  • Steering column (2017): Grounding fault in the steering column that could lead to inadvertent deployment of the driver's airbag in case of contact spiral rupture.
  • Starter current limiter (2017): Risk of fire. If the engine is blocked (e.g., hydro-lock) and the driver repeatedly attempts to start it, the limiter can overheat. Addition of an extra fuse.
  • Brake booster (2022): On some models, possible corrosion at the connection of the brake booster vacuum line, reducing braking assistance.

Verdict

The Mercedes GLA X156 is an excellent used choice if you are looking for a compact crossover with assertive styling rather than a spacious family SUV. Gasoline engines (GLA 180, 200, 250) are absolutely to be preferred: the M270 block is robust, quiet, and spared from the heavy pollution control issues of diesels. The ideal choice is a facelifted model (post-2017) in a gasoline version, which corrects early flaws (finish, gearbox jerks) and offers up-to-date technological equipment. The purchase is highly recommended, on the sole condition that the maintenance of the 7G-DCT automatic gearbox has been scrupulously respected.
