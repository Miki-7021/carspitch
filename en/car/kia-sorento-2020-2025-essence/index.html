Summary

The fourth-generation Kia Sorento (code MQ4) marks a spectacular move upmarket for the Korean manufacturer. Based on the new N3 platform (shared with the Hyundai Santa Fe), it offers an assertive design, a very spacious interior (up to 7 seats) with refined finishes, and top-tier technological equipment. This generation stands out for the massive introduction of electrified powertrains (HEV and PHEV) based on the 1.6 T-GDi petrol engine, while retaining traditional internal combustion engines (petrol and diesel) depending on the market. A mid-cycle refresh (facelift) was carried out in late 2023/early 2024, modernizing the front fascia (vertical headlights) and infotainment. Overall, it is an extremely accomplished family SUV, offering excellent value for money/performance/spaciousness.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional spaciousness (true occasional 7-seater) and boot volume
  • Significantly improved finish quality and sound insulation
  • Very rich standard technological equipment (driving aids, blind spot cameras)
  • Manufacturer's warranty of 7 years / 150,000 km (Europe) or 10 years / 100,000 miles (USA)
  • Variety of powertrains (ICE, HEV, PHEV)

Weaknesses 5

  • Recurrent oil dilution issue on 1.6 T-GDi hybrid engines
  • Initial reliability of the 8DCT gearbox (oil pump) on 2.5T models
  • Very high weight of PHEV versions impacting dynamics and wear of consumables
  • Infotainment system sometimes slow to start (before the 2024 facelift)
  • Suspension a bit firm at low speeds with 20-inch wheels

Check before buying

  • Engine oil level and smell on 1.6 T-GDi (HEV/PHEV) versions: a level too high or a strong petrol smell indicates dilution.
  • Recall history, especially for the 8DCT gearbox (electric oil pump) on 2.5 T-GDi.
  • Smooth transmission operation (no jerks when starting or shifting between forward/reverse gears).
  • Wear of tires and brake pads, particularly on the heavy PHEV version.
  • Operation of the central screen and rearview camera (prone to black screens, often fixed by update).

Official recalls

  • - **October 2022 (NHTSA Recall 22V760000 - North Amer.)**: Failure of the 8DCT transmission's electric oil pump (affects 2.5 T-GDi). Can lead to a complete loss of motive power.
  • - **September 2022 (Europe/World)**: Issue with front seat belt pretensioners that can explode during an accident, projecting metal fragments.
  • - **July 2021 (North Amer.)**: Potential fuel leak from fuel lines on the 2.5 T-GDi engine, risk of fire.
  • - **August 2023 (World)**: Software malfunction preventing rearview camera display.
  • - **2021 (Europe)**: eCall system update that could fail to transmit the vehicle's VIN to emergency services in the event of a crash.

Verdict

The Kia Sorento IV is an excellent family SUV that encroaches on premium manufacturers' territory thanks to its refined presentation and onboard technology. **Regarding petrol engines**, the choice will depend on your market. In Europe, the hybrid versions (1.6 T-GDi HEV/PHEV) are essential but require strict monitoring of the oil level (dilution phenomenon) and shorter oil change intervals if you do a lot of city driving. In North America, the 2.5 T-GDi offers superior driving pleasure but ensure the 8DCT gearbox recall has been performed. If you are a high-mileage driver, the 2.2 CRDi diesel remains paradoxically the most robust and economical choice in the long term. Opt for a post-2022 model to avoid the early production issues.
