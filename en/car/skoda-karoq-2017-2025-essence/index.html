Skoda Karoq

I (NU7) · 2017 – 2025

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

Summary

The Skoda Karoq (Type NU7) has the challenging task of succeeding the atypical Yeti. Based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MQB A1 platform (shared with the SEAT Ateca and VW T-Roc), it positions itself as a rational, spacious, and comfortable compact SUV. It stands out with its independent and removable 'VarioFlex' rear seat system (optional or depending on trim level). Facelifted in late 2021 (market launch 2022) with aesthetic touches, optimized aerodynamics, and a technological update, the Karoq is a mature model. Although it does not offer hybrid powertrains (neither MHEV nor PHEV), its internal combustion engines, particularly the TSI gasoline units (EA211 and EA888 families), offer an excellent compromise between driving pleasure and efficiency. Overall reliability is very good, although marred by some electronic bugs (infotainment) and the historical weaknesses of the dry-clutch DSG7 gearbox (DQ200).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional spaciousness and modularity (VarioFlex seats)
  • Retained physical ergonomics (real climate controls)
  • Overall reliability of gasoline engines (TSI EA211 and EA888)
  • Suspension comfort (especially with DCC adaptive damping)
  • Serious finish and material aging

Weaknesses 5

  • DSG7 gearbox (DQ200) on smaller engines still prone to premature wear in urban driving
  • Recurring electronic bugs on the MIB3 multimedia system (2020-2022)
  • Total absence of hybridization (penalizing for ecological tax in France)
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds (wind noise)
  • Capricious AdBlue system on diesel versions

Check before buying

  • DSG7 gearbox (DQ200) behavior: no jerking or excessive slipping should be felt at low speeds.
  • Smooth operation of the 1.5 TSI engine when cold (absence of 'Kangarooing').
  • Coolant level (frequent leaks at the water pump housing/thermostat on TSI and TDI).
  • Infotainment system stability during the test drive (test GPS, Bluetooth, and check for absence of SOS/eCall warning light).
  • Haldex coupler (4x4) maintenance history: mandatory oil change every 3 years.

Official recalls

  • - **November 2018 (Headrests):** Incorrect locking of front headrests on a small series. - **May 2019 (Front Seats):** Risk of crack on the driver's seat frame (2018 models). - **May 2020 (eCall):** Malfunction of the emergency call module requiring reprogramming. - **March 2022 (Engine Cover):** On 2.0 TSI engines, the decorative engine cover may detach and touch hot parts (fire risk). Removal of the cover at the dealership. - **December 2023 (Airbags):** Possible passenger airbag deployment defect on some models produced in 2023.

Verdict

The Skoda Karoq is one of the most pragmatic and reliable choices in the compact SUV category, especially with a gasoline engine. The 1.5 TSI 150 hp unit represents the perfect balance, provided that software updates have been performed on pre-2020 models. If you opt for an automatic gearbox, favor mixed driving conditions to preserve the dry-clutch DSG7 (DQ200), or turn to the 2.0 TSI 190 hp (or the 150/190 4x4 diesels) equipped with the robust wet-clutch DSG7 (DQ381). Avoid diesels if you drive mainly in the city (clogging, AdBlue). A facelifted model (post-2022) with the 1.5 TSI is a highly recommendable purchase.
