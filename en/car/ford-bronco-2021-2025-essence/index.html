Summary

The sixth-generation Ford Bronco (U725) marks the highly anticipated return of the legendary American 4x4, absent since 1996. Built on a ladder frame chassis (shared with the Ranger), it is positioned as a hardcore off-roader, a direct rival to the Jeep Wrangler. Available in 2- and 4-door versions, it stands out with its removable roof and doors (modular system). While its off-road capabilities and neo-retro design are praised, its debut was marred by numerous assembly quality and teething problems (particularly on the hardtops), penalized by below-average initial reliability scores (J.D. Power, TÜV). The situation improves significantly from the 2023 model years onwards.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional off-road capabilities (G.O.A.T modes, approach/departure angles)
  • Overall solid mechanics of the EcoBoost blocks (according to ADAC)
  • Superior reliability of the 7-speed manual gearbox compared to the automatic
  • Iconic design and modularity (removable doors and roof)
  • Excellent value retention on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Very disappointing initial quality in 2021-2022 (J.D. Power IQS below average)
  • Recurring sealing and finish issues on the hardtop (MIC)
  • Temperamental electronics (AdvanceTrac system, APIM module, cameras)
  • Interior plastics prone to scratching and sometimes approximate assembly
  • Soundproofing could be improved (significant wind noise on the highway)

Check before buying

  • Complete history of NHTSA recalls (extremely numerous on this model)
  • Condition of the modular roof panels (cracks, discoloration, water leaks)
  • Operation of the reversing camera and central screen (overheating of the APIM module)
  • Front seat and suspension arm mountings (subject to recalls for loose bolts)
  • Behavior of the 10-speed automatic gearbox (jerks, hesitations)

Official recalls

The Bronco has been subject to an exceptionally high number of recalls in the United States (NHTSA). *Note: No specific recall for the Bronco is listed in the provided Rappel Conso France database, as the model was imported there very recently and in low volumes.*

Bodywork & Structure: - Hardtop (MIC) (2021-2022): Manufacturing defect of the roof panels that could detach (26V299000). - Fender flares (2022-2026): Poor attachment that could lead to them detaching while driving (26V403000). - Windshield (2025): Poor bonding that could lead to detachment in the event of an accident (25V683000).

Electronics & Cameras: - Reversing camera / APIM (2021-2026): Multiple recalls (26V124000, 25V888000, 25V315000, 25V312000, 25V273000) for overheating of the APIM module, black screen, or software failure of the 360° camera. - Instrument cluster (2025-2026): Display failure upon startup (25V788000). - Brake assist (EBB) (2025): Malfunction of the electronic brake module (25V823000, 25V488000).

Mechanics & Engine: - Engine failure (2025-2026): Risk of engine failure leading to a loss of power (26V343000). - Fuel pump (2021-2024): Failure of the low-pressure pump (25V455000) and loose bolts on the high-pressure pump (25V597000). - EGR valve (2025): Valve failure (26V122000). - Transmission (2021-2025): Misalignment of the joint between the transmission and the transfer case (26V236000).

Chassis & Interior: - Suspension (2024-2025): Loose mountings on the lower and upper suspension arms (25V308000, 25V310000). - Seats (2021-2026): Loose or missing seat pivot or frame bolts (26V268000, 25V721000, 25V311000). - Child safety (2024-2025): Possible opening of the left rear door even with the safety lock activated (25V397000, 25V303000).

What sets it apart 10

Performance

  • Capacités de franchissement exceptionnelles — Le système G.O.A.T et les angles d'attaque permettent d'affronter des terrains difficiles avec assurance, un vrai bénéfice pour les amateurs de tout-terrain exigeants.
  • Couple généreux du 2.3L — Le moteur 2.3L EcoBoost délivre un couple généreux, utile pour le franchissement régulier et les usages polyvalents au quotidien.
  • Souplesse du V6 2.7L — Le V6 2.7L EcoBoost apporte souplesse et puissance adaptée au poids du véhicule, idéal pour les grands rouleurs et le tractage.
  • Châssis renforcé du Raptor — La version Raptor bénéficie d'un châssis renforcé et de performances exceptionnelles, destiné aux passionnés de franchissement extrême.

Reliability

  • Mécanique EcoBoost globalement solide — Les blocs EcoBoost sont reconnus solides par l'ADAC, ce qui rassure l'acheteur sur la durabilité du cœur mécanique du véhicule.
  • Boîte manuelle plus robuste que l'automatique — La boîte manuelle à 7 rapports offre une fiabilité supérieure à l'automatique, un argument pour ceux qui privilégient la longévité mécanique.
  • Fiabilité améliorée dès 2023 — Les millésimes 2023 et ultérieurs corrigent nettement les problèmes de jeunesse, offrant un véhicule plus fiable à l'usage quotidien.

Design

  • Design iconique et modularité — Le style néo-rétro et les portes et toit démontables permettent de personnaliser le véhicule et de profiter d'une expérience de conduite unique.
  • Véhicule passion au charme indéniable — Le Bronco offre une expérience de conduite et un caractère qui séduisent les acheteurs en quête d'un 4x4 authentique et charismatique.

Economy

  • Excellente tenue de la cote — Le Bronco conserve très bien sa valeur sur le marché de l'occasion, ce qui limite la dépréciation pour l'acheteur lors de la revente.

Alternatives

North America & Europe: Jeep Wrangler (the historic rival, more rustic but with equally uneven reliability), Toyota 4Runner (absolute benchmark in reliability, but older design), Land Rover Defender (more luxurious, superior road handling, but temperamental electronics).

Verdict

The 6th generation Ford Bronco is an enthusiast vehicle, with extraordinary capabilities and undeniable charm. However, its launch was rushed, resulting in poor initial assembly quality and an avalanche of recalls (over 40 recorded by the NHTSA). While the EcoBoost engines are mechanically solid (especially the 2.3L paired with the excellent manual gearbox), the peripherals (roof, electronics, cameras, mountings) require extreme vigilance. Buying advice: Avoid the 2021-2022 model years if possible. Opt for a 2023 or later model, and demand a clear history proving that all recall campaigns have been carried out.
