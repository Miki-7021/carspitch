Ford Bronco

VI · 2021 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The sixth-generation Ford Bronco (U725) marks the highly anticipated return of the legendary American 4x4, absent since 1996. Built on a ladder frame chassis (shared with the Ranger), it is positioned as a hardcore off-roader, a direct rival to the Jeep Wrangler. Available in 2- and 4-door versions, it stands out with its removable roof and doors (modular system). While its off-road capabilities and neo-retro design are praised, its debut was marred by numerous assembly quality and teething problems (particularly on the hardtops), penalized by below-average initial reliability scores (J.D. Power, TÜV). The situation improves significantly from the 2023 model years onwards.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional off-road capabilities (G.O.A.T modes, approach/departure angles)
  • Overall solid mechanics of the EcoBoost blocks (according to ADAC)
  • Superior reliability of the 7-speed manual gearbox compared to the automatic
  • Iconic design and modularity (removable doors and roof)
  • Excellent value retention on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Very disappointing initial quality in 2021-2022 (J.D. Power IQS below average)
  • Recurring sealing and finish issues on the hardtop (MIC)
  • Temperamental electronics (AdvanceTrac system, APIM module, cameras)
  • Interior plastics prone to scratching and sometimes approximate assembly
  • Soundproofing could be improved (significant wind noise on the highway)

Check before buying

  • Complete history of NHTSA recalls (extremely numerous on this model)
  • Condition of the modular roof panels (cracks, discoloration, water leaks)
  • Operation of the reversing camera and central screen (overheating of the APIM module)
  • Front seat and suspension arm mountings (subject to recalls for loose bolts)
  • Behavior of the 10-speed automatic gearbox (jerks, hesitations)

Alternatives

**North America & Europe:** Jeep Wrangler (the historic rival, more rustic but with equally uneven reliability), Toyota 4Runner (absolute benchmark in reliability, but older design), Land Rover Defender (more luxurious, superior road handling, but temperamental electronics).

Verdict

The 6th generation Ford Bronco is an enthusiast vehicle, with extraordinary capabilities and undeniable charm. However, its launch was rushed, resulting in poor initial assembly quality and an avalanche of recalls (over 40 recorded by the NHTSA). While the EcoBoost engines are mechanically solid (especially the 2.3L paired with the excellent manual gearbox), the peripherals (roof, electronics, cameras, mountings) require extreme vigilance. **Buying advice:** Avoid the 2021-2022 model years if possible. Opt for a 2023 or later model, and demand a clear history proving that all recall campaigns have been carried out.
