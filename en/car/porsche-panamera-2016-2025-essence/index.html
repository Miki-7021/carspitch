Summary

The second-generation Porsche Panamera (Type 971) marks a spectacular break from its predecessor (970). Based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MSB platform (shared with Bentley), it corrects the controversial proportions of the first generation and introduces the *Sport Turismo* shooting brake version. In terms of reliability, the 971 represents a major leap forward. The ADAC highlights a clear improvement, notably thanks to the abandonment of the capricious PDK7 gearbox in favor of an extremely robust PDK8 (supplied by ZF). The engines, now co-developed with Audi (V6 EA839 and V8 EA825), prove to be high-performing and generally reliable. Facelifted in 2020, it has become heavily electrified with its E-Hybrid versions, which are highly sought after but add a layer of electronic complexity and battery aging to monitor.

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability significantly improved compared to the 970 generation (Excellent ADAC breakdown rate at 2.8)
  • PDK8 gearbox with exemplary reliability and speed
  • Very robust and high-performing 2.9 V6 and 4.0 V8 engines (EA839/EA825)
  • Top-tier interior finish quality and assembly
  • Exceptional road handling for a vehicle weighing over 2 tons

Weaknesses 4

  • Stratospheric maintenance and spare parts costs (Porsche network)
  • Complexity of E-Hybrid versions (out-of-warranty financial risk related to the battery and power electronics)
  • Imposing size (over 5 meters long, nearly 2 meters wide) penalizing in the city
  • Sometimes capricious comfort electronics (PCM infotainment system bugs)

Check before buying

  • 100% clear maintenance history (Porsche network or recognized specialist essential for resale value)
  • State of Health (SoH) of the high-voltage battery on E-Hybrid versions (demand a Piwis test)
  • Operation of the air suspension (raise and lower the vehicle while stationary, listen to the compressor)
  • Tire wear (aggressive geometry settings wear the inside of the rear tires)
  • Presence of moisture under the carpets (clogged windshield cowl or sunroof drains)

Official recalls

The Panamera 971 has been the subject of several safety recalls (NHTSA / Rappel Conso data):

  • February 2025 (Rappel Conso): Potential defect in the central wheel locking bolt (center nut), risk of wheel detachment.
  • December 2024 (NHTSA 25V896000): Rearview camera image display issue (2024-2025 models).
  • September 2024 (NHTSA 25V634000): High-pressure fuel pump fasteners potentially improperly tightened (2024-2026 models).
  • March 2024 (Rappel Conso): Manufacturing defect on the front right wheel carriers that could lead to breakage.
  • October 2023 (Rappel Conso): Moisture infiltration into the auxiliary air conditioning pump control unit (fire risk, mainly affects PHEVs).
  • October 2023 (Rappel Conso): Improper fastening of the rear seat belt end brackets.
  • January 2022 (Rappel Conso) / May 2021 (NHTSA): Risk of breakage of the front suspension lower trailing arm.
  • July 2021 (Rappel Conso): Nuts connecting the drive shaft to the wheel hub potentially improperly tightened.
  • August 2021 (NHTSA 21V608000): Seat belt retractor issue.
  • 2018 - 2019 (Multiple NHTSA recalls): Instrument cluster software updates, airbag ECU capacitor replacement, and electric power steering assist reprogramming.

What sets it apart 6

reliability

  • Exemplary Reliability and Robust Engines — The Panamera II stands out for its significantly improved overall reliability, notably thanks to its PDK8 gearbox, which is exceptionally robust and fast, and its V6 and V8 petrol engines, renowned for their solidity and performance.

Performance

  • Breathtaking Performance and Unrivaled Dynamism — Enjoy an exhilarating driving experience thanks to powerful and responsive petrol engines, combined with the ultra-fast PDK8 gearbox, delivering blistering acceleration and first-class dynamism.

safety

  • High-Level Passive Safety — The Porsche Panamera II ensures optimal protection for all its occupants, as evidenced by its excellent results in crash tests.

braking

  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — Benefit from a remarkably efficient braking system, offering exceptional power and endurance, even under heavy demand, for perfect control in all circumstances. Ceramic-composite brake options further improve stopping distances.

handling

  • Sporty and Agile Handling — Despite its size, the Panamera II offers exceptional road behavior, with precise steering, minimal body roll, and remarkable agility thanks to its advanced chassis systems, ensuring a sporty and safe drive.

comfort

  • Luxurious Interior and Impeccable Finish — Immerse yourself in a cabin where the quality of finish and assembly are of the highest caliber, offering a luxurious and refined environment for every journey.

Alternatives

The competition is situated in the 4-door coupe and sporty limousine segment:

  • BMW Série 8 Gran Coupé (G16): Excellent alternative, very reliable inline-6 (B58) and V8 (S63) engines, flawless ZF8 gearbox.
  • Mercedes-AMG GT 4 Portes (X290): Explosive V8, very sporty handling, but firmer comfort and complex electronics.
  • Audi A7 / RS7 (C8): Shares the same engines (2.9 V6 and 4.0 V8) as the Panamera, more technological interface, slightly less dynamic.
  • Maserati Quattroporte (VI): Italian charm and captivating sound, but finish and electronic reliability significantly lagging behind Porsche.

Verdict

The Porsche Panamera II (971) is an undeniable technical success that erases the reliability wanderings of the first generation. The gasoline engines (2.9 V6 and 4.0 V8) combined with the PDK8 gearbox form a remarkably robust package for this level of performance. Purchase is highly recommended for the 100% combustion versions (led by the 4S and GTS), provided you can assume a maintenance budget worthy of the brand. The E-Hybrid versions, although fiscally attractive and very widespread on the used market, require increased vigilance: the inevitable aging of the high-voltage battery and the complexity of the hybrid system make buying a model under *Porsche Approved* warranty essential to avoid astronomical repair bills.
