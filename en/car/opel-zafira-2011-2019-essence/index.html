Opel Zafira

C Tourer · 2011 – 2019

NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

All Opel models

Summary

The Opel Zafira C, initially named Zafira Tourer (2011-2016) before losing this suffix during its facelift, marks a significant upscale compared to the B generation. Based on a hybrid platform (Insignia front axle, Astra J rear axle with Watt's linkage), it stands out for its first-class road behavior and a 'rolling lounge' type of comfort. Its *Flex7* seating system remains a benchmark in modularity. The 2016 facelift smoothed out the atypical front end (boomerang headlights) for a more conventional design and modernized the infotainment (IntelliLink with CarPlay/Android Auto). Although heavy (often over 1,600 kg unladen), which penalizes entry-level engines, it remains an excellent family choice. This report focuses on petrol engines, which are increasingly sought after due to diesel traffic restrictions.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional Flex7 modularity (independent 2nd-row seats configurable in 'Lounge' mode)
  • Ride comfort and sound insulation (especially with the panoramic windshield)
  • Secure road holding (Insignia front axle)
  • Lighting quality of AFL+ (Xenon) or LED (after 2016) adaptive headlights
  • Generous boot volume in 5-seat configuration (710 liters)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high weight (often > 1650 kg) penalizing performance and petrol consumption
  • Reliability of the M32 manual gearbox on 1.4 Turbo engines
  • Ergonomics of the center console on pre-facelift models (too many buttons)
  • Rapid wear of tires and brakes due to weight
  • Disastrous reliability of the oil pump seal on 2.0 CDTI diesels (A20 generation)

Check before buying

  • Manual gearbox (1.4T): Listen for any whistling in 5th and 6th gear, and observe if the lever moves during acceleration/deceleration.
  • 1.4T Engine: Listen for a whistling sound at idle (failed PCV valve) and check coolant level (frequent leaks).
  • 1.6 SIDI Engine: Scrupulously check invoices to ensure that Dexos 1 Gen 2 oil has been used.
  • Electronics: Test all functions of the central screen (frequent freezes on early models) and driving aids.
  • Running gear: Check front tire wear and condition of brake discs, which are heavily stressed by the vehicle's mass.

Official recalls

  • Steering (2011-2012): Risk of steering loss due to a defective steering rack (recall late 2012).
  • Airbags/Seatbelts (2014): Possible defect of the seatbelt pretensioner or curtain airbag.
  • Engine Software (2016-2018): Software updates on diesel engines (1.6 and 2.0 CDTI) to correct NOx emissions following Dieselgate.
  • Parking brake cable (2016): Risk of inadvertent loosening of the manual handbrake on some 2016 models.

What sets it apart 12

Safety

  • High-level passive safety — The Zafira Tourer achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult and child protection, ensuring peace of mind for the entire family.
  • Efficient and reassuring braking — Featuring solid braking performance, the Zafira Tourer ensures short and controlled stopping distances, actively contributing to the safety of all occupants.
  • Stable and comfortable road handling — Thanks to its Insignia-derived chassis, the Zafira Tourer offers safe and predictable road handling, with well-controlled body roll, ideal for long family journeys.

Practicality

  • Flex7 modularity and generous boot volume — The Flex7 seating system, a benchmark in its class, allows for ultra-flexible cabin configuration, complemented by an exceptional boot volume in 5-seat configuration, adapting to all your needs.

Comfort

  • Premium ride comfort and sound insulation — Enjoy ride comfort worthy of a 'rolling lounge' and refined sound insulation, particularly appreciated on long journeys, for serene and relaxing trips.
  • Advanced technologies for comfort and safety — Benefit from optimal visibility thanks to AFL+ (Xenon) or LED (after 2016) directional headlights, and a modern IntelliLink multimedia system compatible with CarPlay/Android Auto on facelifted models, for a connected and serene driving experience.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Renault Grand Scénic III and IV (more dynamic, TCe petrol to watch out for), Volkswagen Touran (superior finish, TSI made reliable after 2014), Citroën Grand C4 Picasso / SpaceTourer (royal comfort, but problematic PureTech engines), Ford S-Max (larger, excellent chassis).
  • Asia / Other markets: Toyota Verso (unbeatable petrol reliability, but aging design), Honda Jade (in some Asian markets).

Verdict

The Opel Zafira C Tourer is an excellent family minivan, unfairly shunned in favor of SUVs. In petrol, the choice is delicate but possible. The 1.4 Turbo 140 hp is the ideal compromise, provided you buy a model whose gearbox (M32) has been drained, revised, or a post-2016 model benefiting from the latest improvements. The 1.6 SIDI offers royal driving pleasure but requires an impeccable service history (specific oil) to avoid engine failure. Avoid the 1.6 CDTI (chain) and older generation 2.0 CDTI (oil pump) diesels unless preventive repairs have been documented. Prioritize a facelifted model (after 2016) with the 1.4 Turbo 140 hp for maximum peace of mind and up-to-date onboard technology.
