Summary

The BMW X1 (E84) is the first compact SUV from the Bavarian brand. Based on the proven 3 Series Touring (E91) platform, it offers dynamic road handling, closer to a raised estate car than a true off-roader. Launched in 2009, it underwent a facelift (LCI) in summer 2012, bringing an improved interior finish, slight aesthetic touches, and the excellent ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox. Although very popular in Europe thanks to its economical and powerful diesel engines, its reputation has been tarnished by the timing chain issues of the infamous N47 engine. It remains an excellent choice for driving enthusiasts, provided you are extremely vigilant about the service history.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional road handling (3 Series E90 chassis)
  • Perfect driving position, low and extended
  • Very well-controlled fuel consumption on diesel engines
  • Excellent ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (post-2012)
  • Hydraulic steering (on some versions) offering excellent feel
  • Reliability of 6-cylinder petrol engines (N52)

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous timing chain reliability on N47 diesels (especially 2009-2011)
  • Chronic fragility of the xDrive transfer case
  • Disappointing interior finish before the 2012 facelift (hard plastics)
  • Limited rear passenger space and boot volume for the category
  • Very firm comfort, especially with Runflat tires and Sport chassis
  • Maintenance and repair costs (premium)

Check before buying

  • Cold timing chain noise (metallic scraping at 1500-2000 rpm) on N47 diesels.
  • Jerks or hesitations during strong acceleration (sign of an xDrive transfer case nearing end of life).
  • Tire wear: they must be of the same brand, same model, and identical wear on all 4 wheels (xDrive versions).
  • Operation of onboard electronics (the FRM module managing lighting and windows often fails during battery voltage drops).
  • History of DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter) cleaning or replacement on diesels used extensively in urban areas.
  • Oil leaks around the valve cover and oil pan (petrol engines).

Alternatives

Verdict

The BMW X1 E84 is a two-faced vehicle. Dynamically, it is undoubtedly the most enjoyable compact SUV of its generation to drive. However, its reliability record is heavily burdened by the N47 diesel engine and the fragility of the xDrive system.

**Buying advice:** If you are looking for a diesel, absolutely prioritize a facelifted (LCI) model from after summer 2012, ideally paired with the ZF8 automatic gearbox. Demand invoices proving that the timing chain has been replaced, or negotiate the price to include this operation (approximately 1500 to 2500 €). Avoid xDrive models with mismatched tires. sDrive (rear-wheel drive) versions are less expensive to maintain and avoid transfer case issues. For maximum peace of mind, the rare 6-cylinder petrol versions (N52) are the most robust, although thirsty.
