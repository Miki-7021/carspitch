Summary

The first-generation Dacia Logan, launched in 2004, marked a turning point in the automotive industry by offering a new car at an unbeatable price. Designed under Renault's X90 program, it primarily targeted emerging markets before achieving unexpected success in Western Europe. Positioned as a simple, robust, and economical three-box sedan, the Logan I was praised for its unbeatable price/interior space/boot volume ratio.

A minor facelift occurred in 2008, bringing minor aesthetic touches (grille, bumpers, taillights) and interior improvements (dashboard, upholstery) to modernize its appearance and comfort. Its philosophy remained the same: to offer reliable and affordable mobility. Its overall reliability is good, especially for gasoline engines, but pre-2008 diesel versions require particular attention.

Strengths 5

  • Overall robustness and reliability (especially gasoline)
  • Very low purchase and maintenance cost
  • Generous interior space and large boot volume
  • Mechanical simplicity facilitating repairs
  • Proven and reliable gasoline engines

Weaknesses 6

  • Very basic interior finish and unappealing materials
  • Limited sound insulation, especially on highways
  • Often spartan standard equipment on entry-level versions
  • Passive safety just adequate (especially before the facelift)
  • Fuel consumption of older gasoline engines (K7J/K7M) a bit high for the power
  • Reliability of the 1.5 dCi before 2008 (connecting rod bearings)

Check before buying

  • Condition of ignition coils and TDC sensor (difficult starts, stalling)
  • On pre-2008 1.5 dCi: connecting rod bearing history (replacement invoices imperative)
  • Presence of abnormal noises from the front axle (silent blocks, ball joints)
  • Condition of the exhaust line and chassis (corrosion, especially in salty regions)
  • Functioning of air conditioning (if present) and heating
  • Wear of shock absorbers and brakes (often stressed by weight and use)

Official recalls

The Dacia Logan I has been subject to several recalls, often shared with the Sandero from the same platform:

  • 2007 (Europe): Braking problem. Risk of front brake hose leakage. Concerns vehicles produced during a given period in 2007.
  • 2008 (Europe): Power steering problem. Risk of power steering hose leakage. Concerns vehicles produced during a given period in 2008.
  • 2010 (Europe): Steering column attachment. Potentially non-compliant attachment. Concerns vehicles produced during a given period in 2010.
  • 2011 (Europe): Brake master cylinder. Potentially defective master cylinder. Concerns vehicles produced during a given period in 2011.
  • 2012 (Europe): Fuel tank attachment. Potentially non-compliant attachment. Concerns vehicles produced during a given period in 2012.

It is recommended to check with a Dacia/Renault dealership whether the vehicle in question has benefited from all relevant recall campaigns.

Verdict

The Dacia Logan I is a wise choice for anyone looking for a simple, reliable, and economical car, without frills. It's an honest car that fulfills its transportation mission without pretension.

For predominantly urban and suburban use, the 1.4 MPI (K7J) or 1.2 16V (D4F) gasoline engines are excellent choices, very robust and inexpensive to maintain. The 1.6 MPI (K7M) offers a bit more versatility. The 1.6 16V (K4M) is the most pleasant and reliable for more frequent road use.

For high-mileage drivers, the 1.5 dCi (K9K) is interesting for its fuel efficiency, but imperatively after 2008 to benefit from improvements to the connecting rod bearings. A complete service history is crucial for this engine.

Avoid pre-2008 diesel models without formal proof of connecting rod bearing replacement. The Logan I is a forgiving car, but minimal maintenance is the key to its longevity. Negotiate the price based on the general condition and history, but it's generally a safe purchase for a tight budget.
