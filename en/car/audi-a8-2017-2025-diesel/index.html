Summary

The fourth-generation Audi A8 (D5, type 4N) represents the technological pinnacle of the brand with the four rings. Launched at the end of 2017, it massively introduces mild hybridization (MHEV 48V) across all its engines and an ultra-sophisticated predictive active suspension. Initially designed for level 3 autonomous driving (ultimately abandoned for legal reasons), it is packed with ECUs and sensors. Facelifted at the end of 2021 (2022 model) with new OLED optics and a redesigned grille, it shines with its imperial comfort and soundproofing. Overall reliability rating: Mechanically very robust (especially the V6 TDI blocks), its rating is however burdened by extreme electronic complexity, infotainment bugs, and a recurring fragility of the 48V alternator-starter system (BSG).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with the predictive active suspension)
  • Benchmark soundproofing in the category
  • Remarkable efficiency of the 50 TDI engine on the highway (often under 6.5 L/100 km)
  • Flawless finish and assembly quality
  • Highly efficient lighting technology (Matrix LED / OLED)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 48V alternator-starter (BSG) on 2018-2022 models
  • Gearbox latency (lag) on the 50 TDI (anti-pollution mapping)
  • Staggering depreciation on the used market
  • Cost of out-of-warranty repairs (air suspension, electronics)
  • Distracting all-touch ergonomics (dual screen) while driving

Check before buying

  • Replacement history of the 48V alternator-starter (BSG): crucial point before purchase.
  • Operation of the air suspension: the vehicle must rise and lower without excessive compressor noise or sagging after parking.
  • Rear tire wear: a factory geometry defect (recalled) can cause severe asymmetrical wear.
  • Responsiveness of the MMI screens and absence of display bugs (often requires software updates).
  • On TDIs: absence of AdBlue warning light and history of DPF regenerations.

What sets it apart 7

Safety

  • Benchmark Safety and Advanced Protection — The Audi A8 D5 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with exceptional scores for adult (95%) and child (85%) protection. It was also named an IIHS "Top Safety Pick" for several consecutive years, demonstrating its commitment to occupant safety.

Braking

  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Experience exceptional braking performance, ensuring short stopping distances, typically under 37 meters from 100 km/h. The powerful and durable brakes of the Audi A8 D5 offer a firm and reassuring feel, even under intense demand, for perfect control in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Sovereign Road Holding and Surprising Agility — The Audi A8 D5 combines imperial ride comfort with exemplary road holding. Its great stability and surprising agility, enhanced by adaptive air suspension and optional all-wheel steering, provide a balanced, safe, and predictable dynamic behavior, inspiring absolute confidence in the driver.

Comfort

  • Imperial Ride Comfort and Benchmark Soundproofing — Immerse yourself in a haven of peace thanks to the Audi A8 D5's benchmark soundproofing and its exceptional ride comfort, especially with the predictive active suspension. Every journey transforms into an experience of absolute serenity, even over the longest distances.

Economy

  • Remarkable Efficiency for Long Journeys (50 TDI) — The 50 TDI engine of the Audi A8 D5 stands out for its remarkable efficiency, often achieving consumption figures below 6.5 L/100 km on the highway. Ideal for high-mileage drivers, it combines generous autonomy and optimized running costs for a luxury sedan.

Verdict

The Audi A8 D5 is an impressive technological showcase, particularly relevant in the 50 TDI engine for very high mileage drivers thanks to its range and cathedral-like silence. However, this generation is marred by a chronic fragility of its 48V network (alternator-starter), a costly and immobilizing problem. Buying a used A8 requires a crystal-clear maintenance history, strict verification of performed recalls (especially on the running gear and electronics), and ideally, an extended warranty. Avoid urban models (TDI clogging) and negotiate firmly given the steep depreciation.
