Chevrolet Malibu

VIII · 2013 – 2016

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The **8th generation Chevrolet Malibu (Gen VIII)** is a mid-size sedan based on General Motors' global Epsilon II platform. Designed to be a global car, it was marketed in nearly 100 countries. Although it offers a very quiet cabin, a smooth ride, and an intuitive myLink infotainment system, it suffered at launch from below-average rear legroom for the category, which prompted Chevrolet to do an emergency facelift as early as 2014. Quickly replaced in 2016 by the Gen IX, it represents an affordable alternative on the used market today against the untouchable Honda Accord and Toyota Camry, although its long-term reliability is rated "Average" by J.D. Power and Consumer Reports.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent cabin soundproofing (acoustic glass)
  • Very high-performance and mechanically reliable 2.0T LTG engine
  • Easy-to-use myLink infotainment system
  • Ride comfort and smoothness of the 6-speed Hydra-Matic automatic gearbox
  • Very extensive Chevrolet after-sales network (North America)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very limited rear legroom for the category (especially 2013 models)
  • Long-term reliability rated only "Average" compared to Japanese benchmarks
  • Premature wear of braking components
  • Trunk volume reduced on the eAssist version (battery)
  • Frequent electronic bugs on the myLink screen (freezes, reboots)

Check before buying

  • History of rear suspension recalls (bolts/toe links) that can loosen or break.
  • Functioning of the myLink touch screen and radio (frequent software bugs).
  • Condition of brake rotors and pads (faster than average wear).
  • On the 2014 2.5L: check if the brake vacuum pump recall has been performed.
  • Smoothness of the automatic gearbox (potential jerks if the oil has never been changed around 100,000 km).

Alternatives

* **North America**: Toyota Camry (reliability benchmark), Honda Accord (better roominess and dynamics), Ford Fusion (European design and handling), Hyundai Sonata / Kia Optima. * **Europe**: Volkswagen Passat, Ford Mondeo, Skoda Superb, Peugeot 508. * **Asia / Middle East**: Nissan Altima, Mazda 6, Hyundai Sonata.

Verdict

The 8th generation Chevrolet Malibu is a comfortable, quiet sedan equipped with excellent petrol engines (notably the very reliable 2.0T LTG). However, it is penalized by disappointing rear roominess and a particularly heavy recall history (suspension, electronics, airbags). Compared to a Toyota Camry or a Honda Accord, its long-term reliability is inferior. It is a vehicle to **NEGOTIATE**: it suffers from strong depreciation which makes it a good deal on the used market, provided you opt for a post-2014 model (facelifted), absolutely avoid the eAssist version, and scrupulously check that all safety recalls (especially the rear suspension) have been performed.
