Summary

The Renault Espace II (codename J63) marks the age of maturity for the pioneer of European minivans. Still manufactured by Matra in Romorantin, it retains the original recipe (composite bodywork on a steel structure) but corrects the major flaw of the first generation: its chassis is now fully galvanized, making it almost impervious to corrosion. Longer, rounder, and more aerodynamic, it offers exceptional modularity with its swiveling and removable seats. Now entering the *Youngtimer* era, the Espace II is sought after for its Pullman comfort, immense glass area, and mechanical robustness, although its electrical and cosmetic aging requires attention.

Strengths 6

  • Galvanized chassis (almost total absence of structural rust)
  • SMC composite bodywork (impervious to minor impacts and corrosion)
  • Avant-garde modularity (independent, swiveling seats)
  • Exceptional peripheral visibility
  • Suspension comfort and spaciousness
  • Overall mechanical reliability (Douvrin and PRV engines)

Weaknesses 6

  • Capricious electrical harness (connectors that oxidize)
  • Fragile interior plastics (dashboard cowl that cracks in the sun)
  • Headliner fabrics that detach over time
  • Nightmarish replacement of heating radiators (heater cores)
  • Very high fuel consumption for gasoline versions (especially the V6)
  • Availability of specific Matra parts (bodywork, fittings) becoming critical (NFP)

Check before buying

  • Condition of the cooling system (leaks under the front floor mats = faulty heater cores)
  • Functioning of all electrical accessories (windows, central locking, ventilation)
  • Condition of the dashboard cowl and interior trim
  • On Quadra versions: presence and condition of the carbon driveshaft
  • Timing belt replacement history (on 2.0i, 2.2i and 2.1 dT)
  • Condition of the clear coat on the composite bodywork (tendency to peel with UV)

What sets it apart 6

reliability

  • Exceptional Structural Robustness — Thanks to its fully galvanized chassis and SMC composite bodywork, the Espace II is virtually immune to rust and minor impacts, ensuring remarkable longevity.
  • Legendary Reliability of Petrol Engines — The petrol engines, especially the 2.2i, are renowned for their robustness and longevity, with many examples exceeding 300,000 km without issues.

practicality

  • Pioneering Interior Modularity — A pioneer in its class, the Espace II offers unparalleled flexibility with its independent, swiveling, and fully removable seats, allowing you to adapt the space to all your needs.

comfort

  • Superior Travel Comfort for All Passengers — Enjoy exceptional suspension comfort, generous habitability, and outstanding peripheral visibility, transforming every journey into a pleasant experience.

Performance

  • The Noble Driving Pleasure of the V6 PRV Engine — For enthusiasts, the V6 PRV engine offers an enchanting sound, remarkable smoothness, and performance worthy of a grand tourer, for effortless journeys.

Design

  • A Design Icon and Sought-After Youngtimer — The Espace II is a vehicle with timeless design, recognized as a sought-after Youngtimer, offering unique charm and a prominent place in automotive history.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Chrysler Voyager II (the big American rival, more rustic but very spacious), Peugeot 806 / Citroën Evasion / Fiat Ulysse (appeared in 1994, sliding doors), Ford Galaxy / VW Sharan (appeared in 1995).
  • North America: Not marketed. Period alternatives: Dodge Caravan, Pontiac Trans Sport.
  • Japan/Asia: Not marketed. Period alternatives: Toyota Previa, Mitsubishi Space Wagon.

Verdict

The Renault Espace II is a monument in European automotive history. As a used vehicle today, it primarily appeals to *Youngtimer* enthusiasts or families looking for an ultra-practical, low-budget secondary vehicle. The gasoline engines (2.2i in particular) are models of mechanical robustness. The PRV V6 offers royal driving pleasure but demands a significant fuel budget. The real challenge of the Espace II lies not in its mechanics or rust (thanks to the galvanized chassis), but in the aging of its electrical components, its interior plastics, and the increasing rarity of specific Matra parts. A healthy example, with a clear service history and replaced heater cores, is an excellent enthusiast purchase.
