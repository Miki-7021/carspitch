Summary

The third-generation Mercedes-Benz C-Class (W204 for the sedan, S204 for the wagon, C204 for the coupe) marks a major turning point for the star brand. After a W203 generation marred by corrosion and electronics issues, the W204 was designed with a drastic specification focused on reliability and build quality. The model underwent a significant facelift in 2011, which transformed the interior (much better quality materials, integrated GPS screen) and updated the engines and gearboxes (generalization of the 7G-Tronic Plus). Today, it is one of the most recommendable modern Mercedes on the used car market, provided you choose your engine carefully, especially in diesel where the indestructible and the capricious coexist.

Strengths 6

  • Build and finish quality (especially after the 2011 facelift)
  • Excellent corrosion resistance (unlike the W203)
  • Superb road handling and suspension comfort (Agility Control)
  • Legendary reliability of the OM646 diesel engine (2007-2009)
  • High-level sound insulation on long journeys
  • Ergonomics of controls and Comand multimedia system (on Phase 2)

Weaknesses 6

  • Very frequent failure of the ELV (electronic steering column lock): the car refuses to start (cost ~€800-1200)
  • Random reliability of the timing chain on the OM651 diesel and M271 petrol engines
  • High maintenance cost at official Mercedes-Benz dealerships
  • Interior of Phase 1 models (2007-2010) a bit austere and hard plastics
  • Average rear passenger space and boot volume for the category
  • 5-speed automatic gearbox (on smaller Phase 1 engines) slow and outdated

Check before buying

  • Steering lock (ELV): insert the key, unlocking should be clear and quick. If it struggles or makes an unusual noise, failure is imminent.
  • Automatic gearbox service history (mandatory oil change every 60,000 km).
  • Chain noise on cold start (clatter for 2 to 5 seconds) on OM651 and M271 engines.
  • Rear light cluster: check if the ground connectors are not melted (very common problem, subject of a recall).
  • Oil leaks: in the V of the engine on the V6 diesel (OM642), and around the oil filter/water pump on the 4-cylinder diesel (OM651).
  • Condition of rear tires: powerful versions (V6, AMG) tend to prematurely wear the inside of the rear tires (negative camber).

Official recalls

  • Takata Airbags (2020-2022): Replacement of front airbag gas generators (driver and passenger) susceptible to exploding.,- Rear Lights (2014): Replacement of rear light clusters and ground wiring that could melt and cause loss of illumination or fire risk.,- Power Steering (2010): Possible power steering fluid leak at a fitting, leading to loss of assistance (2009-2010 models).,- OM651 Injectors (2010-2012): Service campaign (often silent) to replace defective Delphi injectors on 220 CDI and 250 CDI.,- Diesel Heater (2014): Risk of short circuit and fire at the diesel filter on certain diesel models.

What sets it apart 7

reliability

  • Superior Build Quality and Corrosion Resistance — Designed for longevity, the C-Class W204 boasts superior assembly quality and excellent corrosion resistance, ensuring remarkable durability.
  • Legendary Reliability of the OM646 Diesel Engine — Models equipped with the OM646 engine (2007-2009) are renowned for their robustness and almost indestructible reliability, offering generous torque and simple maintenance.

comfort

  • Imperial Comfort and Premium Soundproofing — Enjoy refined road manners, exceptional suspension comfort (Agility Control), and high-level soundproofing, ideal for long journeys.

safety

  • Maximum Protection for All Occupants — The Mercedes C-Class W204 achieved the maximum rating from Euro NCAP, guaranteeing optimal safety for adults and children.
  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Benefit from exceptional braking performance, with high stopping power and short distances, ensuring first-class active safety.

handling

  • Balanced and Precise Road Handling — The W204 offers safe and predictable road handling, prioritizing stability and body control, with precise steering for a serene drive.

technology

  • Modernized Interior and 7G-Tronic Plus Gearbox — Restyled models (post-2011) feature an upgraded cabin with improved materials, an integrated Comand multimedia system, and the responsive 7G-Tronic Plus automatic gearbox for superior driving pleasure.

Alternatives

  • Europe: BMW 3 Series (E90 / F30) for more dynamism but similar chain issues (N47), Audi A4 (B8) for finish and Quattro, but beware of flywheels and oil pumps (2.0 TDI), Volvo S60 II for comfort and safety.
  • Japan / Asia: Lexus IS (XE20) for absolute reliability (especially in petrol/hybrid) but less spaciousness and dated multimedia technology.
  • Americas: Infiniti G37 (very reliable, powerful V6), Acura TL, Cadillac CTS.

Verdict

The Mercedes C-Class W204 is an excellent vintage that restored the brand's reputation for durability. The ideal choice is a facelifted model (post-2011) for its modernized interior and 7G-Tronic Plus gearbox.

For Diesel: If you're looking for absolute reliability at a low price, a 2007-2009 model equipped with the OM646 engine (C200/C220 CDI) is indestructible. For newer models (OM651), purchase is recommended *only* if the service history is perfect and the timing chain has been checked or replaced. The V6 diesel (OM642) is superb but requires a significant maintenance budget (cooler seals).

For Petrol: Avoid the M271 4-cylinders (C180/C200/C250 K and CGI) due to their fragile timing system. Prefer the V6s (M272/M276) or the recent 1.6 Turbo (M274) that appeared late in its career.
