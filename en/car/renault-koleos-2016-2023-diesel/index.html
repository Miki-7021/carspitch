Summary

The Renault Koleos II (code HZG), marketed in Europe from 2017, marks a complete break with the first generation. Based on the Renault-Nissan Alliance's modular CMF-C/D platform, it is the technical cousin of the Nissan X-Trail (T32) and is manufactured in South Korea by Renault Samsung Motors (where it is sold as the Samsung QM6). Positioned as a D-segment family SUV, it opts to offer only 5 seats to prioritize rear legroom and boot volume, unlike its direct competitors. It benefited from two minor facelifts (Phase 2 in 2019, Phase 3 in 2021). Designed to be a global vehicle, it emphasizes ride comfort and a premium presentation (Initiale Paris trim).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional rear passenger space (legroom)
  • Suspension comfort and highway sound insulation
  • Overall mechanical reliability of dCi engines (excluding depollution peripherals)
  • Highly effective All Mode 4x4-i system (Nissan origin) on snow/mud
  • Very favorable price/equipment ratio in the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Absence of a 7-seater version (reserved for the Espace or Nissan X-Trail)
  • CVT X-Tronic gearbox that smooths out sensations too much and makes the engine rev loudly under strong acceleration
  • Reliability of the AdBlue system on Blue dCi versions (post-2018)
  • Aging and slow R-Link 2 infotainment system on early model years (2017-2018)
  • Sluggish road behavior, focused on comfort at the expense of dynamism

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history of the automatic CVT gearbox (oil change performed?).
  • Absence of 'Antipollution to check' or 'AdBlue' warning light on the dashboard (Blue dCi).
  • Smooth operation of the R-Link 2 touchscreen (test GPS, Bluetooth, and radio).
  • Wear of front tires (the significant weight of the front axle on 2.0 dCi models leads to rapid wear).
  • Clunking noises from the front axle (anti-roll bar links or shock absorber mounts).

Official recalls

  • November 2019 (NOx Sensor): Risk of short-circuit on the nitrogen oxide (NOx) sensor harness on diesel engines. Repositioning or replacement of the harness.
  • July 2020 (Child Safety): On certain models manufactured in 2019, the rear door child safety lock may unexpectedly deactivate.
  • February 2018 (Passenger Seat): Poor welding on the front passenger seat frame potentially affecting its retention in the event of an impact (models produced in 2017).

Verdict

The Renault Koleos II is an excellent used car choice for families looking for a comfortable, premium, and affordable large 5-seater SUV. If you're aiming for a diesel engine, the 2.0 dCi 175 hp (before 2019) is the best buy: its engine block is virtually indestructible. More recent 'Blue dCi' versions (150 and 190 hp) offer better driving pleasure but are plagued by the chronic fragility of their AdBlue system, a costly problem out of warranty. Regardless of the chosen model, the longevity of the automatic CVT gearbox will depend on rigorous maintenance (oil change) that the Renault network often tends to neglect. Buy with full knowledge of the facts.
