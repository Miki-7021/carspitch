Summary

The third-generation Kia Forte (factory code BD), also marketed under the names Kia Cerato or Kia K3 depending on the market, is a compact sedan that has established itself as a prime alternative to the segment leaders (Civic, Corolla). Replacing the YD generation, this BD iteration marks a significant leap forward in terms of finish quality, chassis rigidity, and technological features. Facelifted in late 2021 (2022 model), it modernized its lighting signature and infotainment. Recommended by *Consumer Reports* since 2020 and highly ranked by *J.D. Power*, it shines with its excellent price/equipment/reliability ratio, although it does not offer the driving dynamics of a Mazda 3 or the hybrid efficiency of a Corolla.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent overall reliability, particularly on naturally aspirated (MPI) engines
  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio in the compact segment
  • Intuitive, responsive, and comprehensive infotainment system (Apple CarPlay / Android Auto as standard)
  • Long-term manufacturer warranty (depending on markets, e.g., 10 years/100,000 miles in the USA)
  • Very reasonable fuel consumption with the 2.0 MPI paired with the IVT gearbox

Weaknesses 5

  • Soundproofing could be improved (road and wind noise on the highway)
  • Quality of some lower interior plastics
  • DCT7 gearbox (on GT 1.6 T-GDI version) sometimes rough in urban driving
  • Safe road handling but less dynamic than a Honda Civic or Mazda 3
  • Valve carbon build-up inherent to direct injection on the 1.6 T-GDI

Check before buying

  • History of suspension and steering recalls (crucial on 2021-2023 models)
  • Behavior of the IVT gearbox during the test drive: absence of excessive whining or jerking
  • On the GT version (1.6 T-GDI): check the regularity of oil changes (ideally every 10,000 km) and the absence of DCT gearbox shuddering upon starting
  • LED headlight alignment (beam sometimes too low on 2019 models)

Official recalls

Europe (Rappel Conso - Concerns the imported Cerato/Forte variant or specific markets)

  • [2023-12-13] Engine fire risk (Cerato/Forte, Optima, Sorento, Sportage): Due to a manufacturing error, the engine may overheat following fuel or oil leaks, leading to a risk of damage and fire.

North America (NHTSA)

  • [2024-04-01 & 2023-09-22] Right lower suspension arm (2023 Models): Incorrect welding that can lead to the failure of the suspension arm and a loss of control.
  • [2022-12-08] Steering knuckle (2023 Models): Manufacturing error that can cause the front left steering knuckle to crack, resulting in a loss of steering control.
  • [2022-05-05] Steering column (2021-2022 Models): A steering column bolt may not be properly secured, risking the detachment of the column from the steering rack.
  • [2021-03-11] Airbags / Occupant sensor (2021 Models): The OCS/ODS unit may not detect the presence of a child seat on the front passenger seat.
  • [2020-08-05] Driveshaft (2019 Models): The front left driveshaft may not have undergone adequate heat treatment, risking breakage.
  • [2018-10-31] LED lighting (2019 Models): Poor factory adjustment of the LED low beams, failing to meet visibility standards.

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Exceptional Reliability and Proven Engines — The Kia Forte stands out for its excellent overall reliability, particularly with its naturally aspirated (MPI) gasoline engines, renowned for their robustness. It has been recommended by independent organizations like Consumer Reports since 2020 and highly rated by J.D. Power, attesting to its solidity and durability.

Safety

  • High-Level Safety — Designed for protection, the Kia Forte has earned major safety distinctions. It was named an IIHS Top Safety Pick+ in 2019 (for models with specific headlights) and received a 5-star ANCAP rating in 2018, confirming its commitment to occupant protection.
  • Solid and Controlled Braking — The Kia Forte offers solid braking performance, ensuring effective and safe deceleration. Independent tests measured a braking distance of 70 to 0 mph in just 52.7 meters, a reassuring performance for a compact sedan.

Comfort

  • Stable and Comfortable Ride — Enjoy a serene driving experience thanks to road holding focused on comfort and predictability. The well-balanced suspension effectively absorbs irregularities, offering a stable, secure, and easy-to-maneuver ride for daily use.

Economy

  • Exceptional Value for Money and Controlled Consumption — The Kia Forte stands out with an unbeatable price-to-equipment ratio in its segment. Combined with very reasonable fuel consumption, especially with the 2.0 MPI engine and IVT transmission, and a long manufacturer's warranty, it represents a smart and economical investment.

Practicality

  • Intuitive Infotainment Technology — Stay connected and entertained with an intuitive, responsive, and comprehensive infotainment system. With Apple CarPlay and Android Auto as standard, accessing your apps and navigation is smooth and effortless.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Enjoyable Performance — For thrill-seekers, the 1.6 T-GDI version (Forte GT) offers spirited performance with 201 horsepower and early available torque, ensuring a dynamic drive and true driving pleasure.

Alternatives

  • North America: Honda Civic (segment benchmark, more expensive), Toyota Corolla (hybrid available, legendary reliability), Hyundai Elantra (technical cousin, more polarizing design), Mazda 3 (premium finish, driving pleasure), Nissan Sentra.
  • Asia / Middle East / Oceania: Toyota Corolla, Hyundai Elantra/Avante, MG 5 (low-cost alternative), Skoda Octavia.
  • Europe: The Forte is not sold there, it is replaced by the Kia Ceed (hatchback format) which competes with the VW Golf, Peugeot 308, and Renault Mégane.

Verdict

The third-generation Kia Forte / Cerato (BD) is an extremely rational and recommendable choice. It does not claim to be the sportiest or the most luxurious in its category, but it offers top-tier mechanical reliability, particularly with the naturally aspirated engines (2.0 MPI and 1.6 MPI). Data from *J.D. Power* and *Consumer Reports* confirm its long-term durability. The GT version (1.6 T-GDI) brings a real touch of soul but will require more rigorous maintenance (frequent oil changes, DCT gearbox management). The only real point of vigilance concerns the numerous recalls related to the running gear (suspension, steering) on the 2021 to 2023 model years: it is imperative to verify that these free fixes have been applied at the dealership before purchase.
