Summary

The Renault Safrane Phase 2 (B54) marks the maturity of the diamond brand's flagship. Launched in July 1996, this stylistic and technical update corrected almost all of the early defects (especially electronic ones) of Phase 1. The road handling remained imperial, prioritizing exceptional ride comfort. Under the hood, the revolution was complete: the old petrol blocks gave way to excellent modular Volvo-sourced engines (2.0 and 2.5), while the diesel offering was rationalized around the new 2.2 dT (G8T engine with 3 valves per cylinder), replacing the aging 2.1 dT and 2.5 dT. Although the request here focuses on the diesel, it is crucial to note that the Safrane Phase 2 particularly shines with its petrol engines, the 2.2 dT now showing its age (noise, vibrations, anti-pollution standards).

Strengths 6

  • Very high level of ride comfort and sound insulation (especially with petrol engines)
  • Generous interior space and ergonomic seats (Ergomatic on high trims)
  • Overall reliability of Phase 2 significantly superior to Phase 1
  • Excellent corrosion resistance (galvanized bodywork)
  • Indestructible Volvo-sourced petrol engines (2.0 and 2.5)
  • Extensive equipment on RXT and Initiale trims

Weaknesses 6

  • 2.2 dT engine rough, noisy, and vibrating compared to current standards
  • High risk of engine failure on the 2.2 dT if timing belt maintenance is neglected
  • Significant weight penalizing dynamism and fuel consumption
  • Extremely costly heater core replacement in terms of labor (complete dashboard removal)
  • Rapid wear of front axle components (ball joints, wishbones, silentblocks) due to weight
  • Trim and interior parts becoming impossible to find new

Check before buying

  • Timing belt history (2.2 dT): Demand recent invoices. If the accessory belt squeaks or is cracked, flee or replace immediately.
  • Heater core: Check for absence of coolant smell in the cabin, greasy mist on the windshield, or damp carpets in the front.
  • Front axle: Listen for clunking noises on degraded roads (shock absorber mounts, suspension ball joints, wishbones).
  • Automatic gearbox: Check that gear changes occur smoothly. An unserviced automatic gearbox (AD8 or LM0) is a ticking time bomb.
  • Onboard electronics: Test the automatic climate control, electric seats, voice synthesis, and cruise control.
  • Cooling system (2.2 dT): Check the condition of the hoses and the absence of 'mayonnaise' (sign of a tired head gasket).

Official recalls

As the Safrane Phase 2 was a mature model, recalls were very rare: - 1998: Inspection and possible replacement of front seat belt pretensioners (risk of non-deployment). - 1999: Verification of the driver airbag harness on a limited series of vehicles.

Verdict

Buying a Renault Safrane Phase 2 today is an excellent choice for enthusiasts of comfortable youngtimers, provided you choose the right engine. If your search focuses on the diesel (2.2 dT), caution is advised: although economical at the pump at the time, this engine suffers from a very capricious timing belt system and a rustic driving experience. It is now penalized by traffic restrictions (Crit'Air). We recommend to NEGOTIATE firmly for a 2.2 dT, demanding a clear timing belt history. However, the true gem of the Safrane Phase 2 lies in its Volvo petrol engines (particularly the 2.5 20v), which transform this sedan into a flying carpet of formidable reliability.
