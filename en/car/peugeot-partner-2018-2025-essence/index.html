Summary

The Peugeot Partner III, also known by its internal code K9, is the third generation of the lion brand's leisure activity vehicle/light commercial vehicle, launched in 2018. It shares its EMP2 platform and most of its components with the Citroën Berlingo III, Opel Combo E, Toyota Proace City, and Fiat Doblò (since 2022). This generation marks a clear step up in terms of comfort, onboard technologies, and road handling compared to its predecessors. Available in utility (Partner) and family (Rifter) versions, it stands out with its i-Cockpit (small steering wheel, high-mounted instrumentation) and a more assertive design. It quickly found its place in the market thanks to its modularity, generous cargo volume, and efficient powertrains. A minor facelift is expected for 2024/2025, primarily aesthetic and technological.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional modularity and cargo volume
  • Comfortable suspension and dynamic road handling (EMP2 platform)
  • Efficient powertrains (BlueHDi and e-Partner)
  • Modern onboard technologies (depending on trim level)
  • More assertive exterior design than competitors
  • Distinct utility (Partner) and family (Rifter) versions

Weaknesses 6

  • Interior finish with many hard plastics
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds (especially utility versions)
  • Timing belt reliability on 1.2 PureTech (before fixes)
  • Recurring AdBlue system problems on 1.5 BlueHDi
  • i-Cockpit not suitable for all body types (steering wheel obscuring gauges)
  • Limited range for the e-Partner on long journeys or in cold weather

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for 1.2 PureTech and 1.5 BlueHDi)
  • Condition and replacement of the timing belt on 1.2 PureTech (check date and mileage)
  • AdBlue system operation (no warning light, level, history of failures)
  • General condition of the bodywork and interior (utility use often intensive)
  • Functioning of all electronic equipment and driving aids
  • Battery and charging system condition for the e-Partner (diagnosis if possible)
  • Check if all manufacturer recalls have been performed

Verdict

The Peugeot Partner III (K9) is a versatile and modern vehicle, offering an excellent compromise between utility and family use. Its road handling is among the best in its category.

For gasoline engines (1.2 PureTech): This is a relevant choice for predominantly urban and peri-urban use. However, the reliability of the wet timing belt is a major drawback. A purchase is NEGOTIATE if the service history is impeccable, if the belt has been recently replaced according to recommendations, and if the price reflects this potential risk. Rigorous monitoring is essential.

For diesel engines (1.5 BlueHDi): Ideal for high-mileage drivers and professionals. Fuel efficiency and torque are assets. The AdBlue system remains a point of vigilance. A purchase is BUY if the vehicle has been well maintained, the AdBlue system functions without alerts, and recalls have been performed.

For the electric version (e-Partner): This is an excellent choice for urban use and deliveries in low-emission zones. Mechanical reliability is high. A purchase is BUY if the range meets your daily needs and charging access is easy.

In summary, the Partner III is a good vehicle, but it requires particular vigilance regarding the maintenance of its internal combustion engines. Prioritize models with a clear history and do not hesitate to have the vehicle inspected by a professional.
