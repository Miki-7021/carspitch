Porsche Cayenne

II (958) · 2010 – 2018

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The **Porsche Cayenne II (Type 958)** marks a major break from the first generation (955/957). More dynamic, it benefits from a drastic weight loss (up to 250 kg less) thanks to the abandonment of the low-range transfer case in favor of a lighter active AWD. The interior takes a spectacular leap forward, inspired by the Panamera's center console. Facelifted in 2014 (Phase 2), it abandons the naturally aspirated V8 for a twin-turbo V6 on the S and GTS versions. Although overall reliability is significantly higher compared to its predecessor, it remains a complex ultra-premium SUV whose maintenance requires a substantial budget.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road handling, the absolute benchmark for SUVs of its era
  • Interior finish quality and ergonomics (airplane cockpit style)
  • Very smooth and reliable Tiptronic S automatic gearbox (8-speed Aisin) (contrary to PDK rumors)
  • Significant weight reduction compared to the 955/957 generation
  • Vast choice of engines for all uses

Weaknesses 5

  • Prohibitive cost of maintenance, insurance, and spare parts
  • Very fragile transfer case (jerking, premature wear)
  • Air suspension prone to leaks (air springs, compressor) after 80,000 km
  • Sometimes capricious electronics (PCM multimedia system bugs, various sensors)
  • Very rapid wear of brakes and tires due to weight (especially on V8 and Hybrid)

Check before buying

  • 100% clear maintenance history (Porsche network or recognized independent specialist mandatory)
  • Transfer case test: steer fully at low speed and accelerate gently. No jerking should be felt.
  • Air suspension test: raise and lower the vehicle through all its positions. The compressor should not be too noisy.
  • Traces of oil leaks under the engine (timing cover on V6 TDI, valve cover on V8)
  • Timing chain noises on cold start (metallic clattering)

What sets it apart 12

Performance

  • Benchmark Handling and Enhanced Agility — The Porsche Cayenne II sets an absolute benchmark for dynamic and sporty road handling. Thanks to significant weight reduction (up to 250 kg less), it offers remarkable agility, precise steering, and minimal body roll, providing a driving sensation akin to a sports sedan.
  • Dynamic and Refined Gasoline Engines — The range of gasoline engines offers a wide choice of powerful and refined units, from the robust 3.6L V6 with 300 hp, known for its durability, to the Bi-Turbo V8s delivering supercar performance with an captivating sound, guaranteeing an exhilarating driving experience.
  • Smooth and Responsive Tiptronic S Transmission — The 8-speed Tiptronic S automatic transmission is praised for its smoothness and responsiveness. It ensures quick and imperceptible gear changes, optimizing driving pleasure and engine power efficiency.

  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Equipped with a high-performance braking system, the Cayenne II ensures optimal safety with very short stopping distances and great endurance, even for an SUV of its size.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: BMW X5 (F15) for similar dynamics, Mercedes-Benz GLE (W166) for comfort, Range Rover Sport (L494) for luxury and off-roading. - **North America**: Acura MDX or Lexus RX (much more reliable and cheaper to maintain, but significantly less sporty), BMW X5. - **Japan / Asia**: Lexus RX, Infiniti QX70 (for the sporty side).

Verdict

The Porsche Cayenne II (958) is an exceptional vehicle that corrected the most serious teething problems of the first generation (notably the V8 coolant pipes). TÜV and ADAC statistics place it in the average for its category, which is honorable for such a complex SUV. However, make no mistake: buying a used Cayenne requires a 'supercar' level maintenance budget. The transfer case is the Achilles heel of this generation, and the air suspension will inevitably require expenses. For a petrol purchase, the 3.6 V6 (300 hp) is the choice of reason and reliability, while the V6 Bi-Turbo (Phase 2) offers the best performance/reliability compromise. Flee from examples with a blurry history or that have been modified.
