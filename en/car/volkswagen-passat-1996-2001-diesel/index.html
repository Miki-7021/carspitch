Volkswagen Passat

B5 (3B) · 1996 – 2001

Summary

The Volkswagen Passat B5 (Type 3B) marked a major turning point for the Wolfsburg manufacturer. Based on the PL45 platform shared with the Audi A4 (B5), it adopted a longitudinal engine architecture and sophisticated multi-link front suspension. This generation propelled VW into the premium segment thanks to an unprecedented interior finish quality for the era, a fully galvanized body, and extensive sound insulation. On the used car market, it is particularly sought after for its 1.9 TDI diesel engines, renowned for their fuel efficiency and exceptional longevity (for distributor pump versions), although the model suffers from chronic weaknesses in its running gear and comfort electronics.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional longevity of 1.9 TDI engines with distributor pump (90 and 110 hp)
  • Interior finish quality and materials (premium standard for the era)
  • Corrosion resistance (fully galvanized body)
  • Ride comfort and sound insulation on the highway
  • Generous interior space, especially in wagon version (Variant)

Weaknesses 5

  • Chronic fragility of the multi-link front axle (high replacement cost)
  • Frequent water infiltrations (clogged drains) destroying comfort electronics
  • Disastrous reliability of the 2.5 V6 TDI (camshafts, VP44 pump)
  • Fickle door locks (faulty micro-switches)
  • Interior 'soft-touch' plastics that peel and become sticky over time

Check before buying

  • Lift the floor mats (especially on the driver's side) to check for moisture (CCM Module).
  • Listen carefully to the front axle on degraded roads (clunking = worn suspension arms).
  • On TDIs: push gears in 3rd and 4th to check for absence of turbo cut-off (Limp Mode).
  • Check the proper functioning of central locking, electric windows, and interior lighting when opening doors.
  • On the 2.5 V6 TDI: demand invoices proving the replacement of camshafts and the injection pump.

Official recalls

  • - **1999**: Recall concerning steering ball joints (premature wear potentially leading to loss of control). - **2001**: Recall on certain models for a defective airbag control module. - **2004**: Massive recall campaign (shared with Audi) for the replacement of lower front suspension arms (wear of ball joints and bushings).

Verdict

The Volkswagen Passat B5 is an icon of the 90s/2000s, a symbol of the golden age of TDI. If you're looking for an indestructible and economical 'daily driver', a model equipped with the **1.9 TDI 90 or 110 hp** is an excellent choice, capable of exceeding 500,000 km with basic maintenance. For gasoline, the **1.8T** is the best compromise. However, the mechanical reliability of these engines is often marred by fragile peripherals: the multi-link front axle is a cash cow for mechanics, and water infiltrations can turn the electronics into a nightmare. Absolutely avoid the 2.5 V6 TDI and models with damp carpets. A purchase to consider only if the running gear history is clear.
