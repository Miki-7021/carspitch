Summary

The second-generation Mercedes-Benz A-Class (W169 for the 5-door, C169 for the 3-door) adopts and refines the atypical 'sandwich floor' architecture of its predecessor (W168). This technical choice offers an exceptional footprint/spaciousness ratio and high-level passive safety (the engine slides under the cabin in the event of a frontal impact).

However, this compactness comes at a price: mechanical accessibility is catastrophic, making even minor interventions on peripherals (starter, alternator, air conditioning compressor) extremely costly in labor, as it often requires lowering the engine cradle.

Facelifted in 2008 (Phase 2), it corrected its serious corrosion problems on opening parts and improved its interior finish. The gasoline engines (M266 family) are renowned for their great robustness, but the continuously variable automatic transmission (CVT Autotronic) is the model's true Achilles' heel.

Strengths 5

  • Extremely reliable and durable gasoline engines (M266) (chain timing)
  • Unbeatable exterior footprint (3.84m) / interior spaciousness ratio
  • Reassuring elevated driving position (sandwich floor)
  • Significantly improved finish quality and sound insulation after the 2008 facelift
  • Excellent passive safety for its time (5 Euro NCAP stars)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very fragile and prohibitively expensive to repair CVT automatic gearbox (Autotronic)
  • Disastrous mechanical accessibility: the slightest intervention (starter, accessory belt, glow plugs) costs a fortune in labor
  • Endemic corrosion on pre-2008 models (doors, tailgate)
  • Sliding-slat sunroof (optional) source of constant breakdowns and leaks
  • Firm suspension and front shock absorbers/springs prone to breakage

Check before buying

  • **Gearbox:** If CVT Autotronic, demand oil change invoices (every 60,000 km). Avoid if the gearbox jerks or if the message 'Visit workshop' appears.
  • **Corrosion:** Inspect the sheet metal fold at the bottom of all 4 doors and the trunk (especially on 2004-2007 models).
  • **Cold start:** Listen to the starter. If it shows signs of weakness, anticipate a bill of €600 to €1,000 (requires lowering the engine).
  • **Front suspension:** The front shock absorber springs tend to break cleanly (known issue). Listen for clunking over speed bumps.
  • **Sunroof:** If present, open and close it completely. Check for moisture stains on the headliner.

Official recalls

  • - **2005 (Wipers):** Risk of front wiper motor failure.

What sets it apart 14

Reliability

  • Legendary Reliability of Petrol Engines — The petrol engines (M266) are renowned for their exceptional robustness and durability, notably thanks to a reliable timing chain, ensuring remarkable longevity and peace of mind in the long term.

Practicality

  • Record-Breaking Interior Space in a Compact Format — At just 3.84 meters long, the A-Class W169 offers an unbeatable exterior footprint to interior space ratio, guaranteeing generous room for passengers and luggage, ideal for city and travel.

Safety

  • High-Level Passive and Active Safety — Benefit from exemplary passive safety thanks to its unique sandwich floor architecture and high active safety with standard ESP. It was awarded 5 Euro NCAP stars, attesting to its optimal protection.
  • Powerful and Controlled Braking — Enjoy a very powerful, stable, and efficient braking system, typical of Mercedes-Benz standards. Equipped as standard with ABS, Brake Assist (BAS), and Electronic Brakeforce Distribution (EBD), it ensures short stopping distances and excellent control.
  • Agile and Reassuring Road Holding — Its road behavior is safe and predictable, offering great agility in urban environments. The standard Electronic Stability Program (ESP) ensures excellent directional stability and high active safety, for serene driving in all circumstances.

Comfort

  • Premium Comfort and Finish — Appreciate a significantly improved finish quality and sound insulation, especially after the 2008 facelift. The elevated driving position, combined with a refined interior, provides daily premium comfort, worthy of the brand.

Performance

  • Versatile and Efficient Petrol Engines — The petrol engines (especially A 170/180) offer an excellent compromise between flexibility, torque, and controlled consumption. They are ideal for versatile use, combining urban dynamism with ease on the open road.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Audi A2 (end of production), Audi A3 (more conventional), BMW 1 Series (rear-wheel drive, less spacious), Volkswagen Golf Plus (similar concept), Honda Jazz (excellent reliability, very spacious). - **Asia / Japan:** Honda Fit/Jazz, Toyota Ractis. - **North America:** Model not sold (the B-Class W245 was sold there in Canada).

Verdict

The Mercedes A-Class W169 is a car with two faces. If you opt for a **gasoline engine (A 150/160 or A 170/180) with a manual gearbox and from 2008 onwards**, you are buying an extremely reliable, spacious, and indestructible premium city car. However, you must **absolutely avoid versions equipped with the automatic CVT gearbox (Autotronic)**, whose recurring failures often exceed the vehicle's residual value. Diesels should be avoided for urban use due to clogging and the exorbitant labor cost to replace wear parts (spark plugs, injectors).
