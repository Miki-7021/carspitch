Audi Q8

I facelift (4M8) · 2023 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 74/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation Audi Q8 (Type 4M8) received a facelift in late 2023 to boost its appeal against fierce competition. This large SUV Coupe, the flagship of Audi's combustion-engine range, is based on the MLB Evo platform (shared with the Porsche Cayenne and Lamborghini Urus). The facelift primarily brings aesthetic and technological updates: a new lighting signature with laser HD Matrix LED headlights, customizable OLED taillights, a redesigned Singleframe grille, and an update to the MIB 3 infotainment system. Mechanically, the petrol engines (which are of particular interest here) remain faithful to the noble V6 and V8, all coupled with mild hybridization (MHEV 48V) or a plug-in hybrid system (PHEV). Build quality is exceptional, but the electronic complexity and the substantial weight of the vehicle (over 2.2 tonnes) require rigorous and costly maintenance.

Strengths 5

  • Absolute benchmark interior finish quality and soundproofing
  • Suspension comfort (especially with the adaptive air suspension)
  • Noble, high-performance, and smooth V6 and V8 petrol engines
  • Cutting-edge lighting technology (HD Matrix LED and OLED)
  • Stunning road handling for a vehicle of this weight (with rear-wheel steering)

Weaknesses 5

  • Reliability of the 48V alternator-starter (BSG) on V6 engines (immobilizing breakdown)
  • Pachydermic weight (often > 2.3 tonnes) penalizing agility and wear of consumables
  • Prohibitive maintenance, insurance, and repair costs
  • Ergonomics of the dual touch screen that forces you to take your eyes off the road
  • Gargantuan fuel consumption of the V8 versions (SQ8 / RS Q8)

Check before buying

  • Recall history: it is imperative to check if the vehicle has undergone software updates and hardware corrections (seat belts, airbags).
  • 48V system (MHEV): track down any error message related to the electrical system on the dashboard (warning sign of alternator-starter failure).
  • Running gear: tire wear (especially on the inside on the rear axle) and brake condition (RS Q8 discs cost a fortune).
  • On-board electronics: test all functions of the lower screen (climate control) and driving aids (cameras, sensors).
  • Air suspension: check that the vehicle rises and lowers without suspicious noise from the compressor and that it does not sag after prolonged parking.

Alternatives

* **Europe:** BMW X6 (G06 LCI - dynamic and excellent L6 engines), Mercedes-Benz GLE Coupe (C167 - comfort and diesel PHEV), Porsche Cayenne Coupe (shares the same platform but more sport-oriented). * **North America:** Genesis GV80 Coupe (emerging premium alternative), BMW X6, Porsche Cayenne Coupe. * **Japan / Asia:** Lexus RX (more comfort-oriented and efficient hybrid, less sporty).

Verdict

The facelifted Audi Q8 (2023-2025) remains one of the most accomplished SUV Coupes on the market, offering an exceptional compromise between supreme comfort, cutting-edge technology, and performance (especially in V6 and V8 petrol). However, its reliability record is tarnished by the chronic fragility of the 48V mild hybrid system (alternator-starter) on the V6 engines, a known Volkswagen Group failure that can immobilize the vehicle. Furthermore, the recent list of recalls (airbags, seat belts, PHEV batteries) shows that the assembly complexity requires absolute vigilance. **Purchase recommended under strict manufacturer warranty or with a solid extended warranty.** Avoid used models whose maintenance history is not 100% clear and exclusive to the Audi network.
