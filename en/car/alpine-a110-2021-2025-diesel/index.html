Alpine A110

II facelift · 2021 – 2025

BUY Reliability 87/100 — excellent

Summary

The second-generation Alpine A110 (often called A110 II) benefited from a lineup overhaul at the end of 2021 (2022 model year). This facelift introduced a new nomenclature (A110, GT, S, and later the radical R) and a technological update, notably a multimedia system finally compatible with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. **EXPERT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:** It is imperative to specify that the Alpine A110 has **never** been offered with a diesel engine in any market. It is a mid-engine sports berlinetta whose philosophy (lightness, agility) is incompatible with a diesel block. The entirety of production relies on the 1.8 TCe 4-cylinder petrol block (code M5P) originating from Renault Sport. This sheet therefore focuses on the technical reality of the vehicle while clarifying the total absence of a diesel version.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional chassis and benchmark dynamic behavior
  • Unbeatable power-to-weight ratio in its category
  • Highly performing and reliable 1.8 TCe engine
  • Clear improvement of the multimedia system since the 2021 facelift
  • Very low depreciation (excellent value retention on the used market)

Weaknesses 4

  • Total absence of a manual gearbox (EDC mandatory)
  • Tiny storage spaces and trunks (front/rear)
  • Prices have become very elitist (especially on R versions)
  • Possible overheating of the EDC gearbox during long track sessions in hot weather

Check before buying

  • Windshield: Sensitive to stress cracks (known issue, check replacement history).
  • Underbody: Check for absence of impacts or scraping, as the car is very low.
  • EDC Gearbox: Ensure there are no abnormal jerks at low speeds or error messages on the dashboard.
  • Running gear: Tire wear and alignment, particularly on S and R versions often used on track.

Safety

  • Benchmark Braking and Exceptional Road Holding — Equipped with standard Brembo brakes and optimized lightness, the A110 guarantees very short braking distances and remarkable endurance. Its perfect balance and precise steering ensure exceptional road holding, offering first-class active safety.

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability of the 1.8 TCe Engine — The heart of the Alpine A110, the 1.8 TCe engine, is a robust and powerful unit, stemming from Renault Sport's expertise. Its reliability is very satisfactory, ensuring peace of mind for a sports car, even in dynamic use.

Economy

  • Excellent Value Retention — The Alpine A110 benefits from very low depreciation on the used car market, making it a wise investment. Its rarity and iconic status guarantee excellent value retention over time.

Practicality

  • Modern and Intuitive Connectivity — The 2021 facelift brought a significantly improved multimedia system, now compatible with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. Enjoy seamless and intuitive connectivity for your daily commutes or sporty getaways.

Alternatives

MarketCompeting models

Verdict

The Alpine A110 (Phase 2 / 2021+) is an automotive engineering masterpiece that proves lightness takes precedence over brute power. The facelift corrected its main flaw (an obsolete multimedia system) while sharpening its lineup. **Regarding the search for a diesel model: this idea must be abandoned, as it does not exist.** The 1.8 TCe engine (252 or 300 hp) is robust, proven, and perfectly suited. It is a highly recommended passion purchase, with very satisfactory overall reliability for a sports car, provided that warm-up times and maintenance are scrupulously respected.
