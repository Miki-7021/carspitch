Volkswagen T-Roc

I facelift (A1) · 2021 – 2025

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The first-generation Volkswagen T-Roc received a mid-life facelift (Phase 2 / A1) at the end of 2021, with deliveries starting in 2022. This *facelift* corrects the main flaw of Phase 1: perceived quality. The dashboard finally adopts foamed plastics, the infotainment system upgrades to MIB3 (floating screen), and the lighting signature integrates optional Matrix LED technology. Positioned between the T-Cross and the Tiguan, it remains one of the best-selling compact SUVs in Europe. With the diesel engine (2.0 TDI), it specifically targets high-mileage drivers, offering remarkable fuel efficiency thanks to the evolution of its EA288 block (now 'evo' with Twin Dosing technology). Overall rating for the restyled generation: Very solid, with undeniable technical maturity.

Strengths 5

  • Interior finish significantly improved compared to Phase 1 (foamed dashboard, better assembly).
  • Exceptional fuel efficiency and driving pleasure of the 2.0 TDI on long journeys.
  • Physical ergonomics retained (the T-Roc keeps real separate climate controls, unlike the Golf 8).
  • Healthy, safe road handling and well-calibrated comfort/dynamism compromise.
  • Excellent resale value on the used car market.

Weaknesses 5

  • MIB3 multimedia system sometimes temperamental (display bugs, slow startup, spontaneous reboots).
  • Firm damping at low speeds, especially in R-Line trim with 18 or 19-inch wheels.
  • High prices for both new and used, numerous and costly options.
  • Very imposing central transmission tunnel, making the central rear seat uncomfortable.
  • Diesel (TDI) is no longer relevant for low-mileage drivers (risk of rapid clogging).

Check before buying

  • Software update history (the MIB3 system must have received OTA or dealership fixes).
  • On TDI versions: check for the absence of an engine light related to the anti-pollution system (DPF/AdBlue) and ensure the vehicle has not only been driven in the city.
  • DSG gearbox oil change: demand the invoice if the vehicle is approaching or exceeding 60,000 km.
  • Front tire wear: the front axle is sometimes stressed by the significant torque of the 2.0 TDI 150 or 1.5 TSI.

Braking

  • Exceptional Braking Control — Benefit from a strong and progressive braking system, ensuring confident stopping power that is easily modulated in various conditions for enhanced road safety.

Handling

  • Dynamic and Serene Road Handling — The T-Roc offers excellent body control, precise steering, and stable road holding. It provides a composed and secure driving experience, with minimal body roll for an SUV, making it predictable and easy to handle.

Economy

  • Record Fuel Efficiency for Long Journeys — The 2.0 TDI EA288 evo engine stands out for its exceptionally low fuel consumption, often below 5.5L/100km, and unbeatable highway range, optimizing your fuel budget.

Comfort

  • Refined and Ergonomic Interior — Discover a significantly improved cabin with a soft-touch dashboard and high-quality assemblies, complemented by intuitive physical ergonomics and excellent sound insulation for optimal daily comfort.

Reliability

  • Proven TDI Engine Reliability — The 2.0 TDI EA288 evo unit boasts very good reliability, enhanced by advanced technologies like Twin Dosing, ensuring remarkable longevity and peace of mind for high-mileage drivers.

Performance

  • Driving Pleasure and Generous Torque — Enjoy the generous torque of the 2.0 TDI, ideal for overtaking and towing, combined with a responsive and well-managed DSG7 gearbox for a smooth and enjoyable driving experience on all roads.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Peugeot 2008 (more comfortable but less reliable PureTech/BlueHDi engines), Renault Captur (excellent hybrid versions), Audi Q2 (premium technical cousin, superior finish), Skoda Karoq (more spacious and family-oriented). - **Asia / China**: Honda HR-V, Toyota Corolla Cross, Nissan Qashqai. - **Americas (South)**: Jeep Compass, Chevrolet Tracker, Hyundai Creta.

Verdict

The restyled Volkswagen T-Roc (2021+) is a vehicle that has reached full maturity. By correcting the low-end plastics of the first phase, it finally justifies its pricing. The 2.0 TDI engine (115 or 150 hp) is a masterpiece of fuel efficiency for high-mileage drivers, offering unbeatable range and highway running costs. However, this diesel engine should be absolutely avoided if your use is predominantly urban, at the risk of exorbitant costs (DPF, EGR, AdBlue). For mixed use, the 1.5 TSI 150 remains the most rational choice. It is a highly recommendable purchase, provided you choose the engine carefully according to your annual mileage.
