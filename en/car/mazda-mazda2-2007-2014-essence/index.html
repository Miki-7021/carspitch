Summary

The second-generation Mazda2 (Type DE), also known as Mazda Demio in Japan, marks a major turning point for the manufacturer. Contrary to the trend of the time towards increasing weight, Mazda applied its 'gram strategy', making this generation about 100 kg lighter than its predecessor. Sharing its platform (B3) with the Ford Fiesta Mk6, it stands out for its particularly dynamic road behavior, excellent gearbox control, and remarkable reliability of its gasoline engines (designed by Mazda). Diesel engines, however, are of PSA/Ford origin and show a much more mixed record. A facelift occurred in late 2010, bringing improvements in suspension (more comfortable), sound insulation, and interior plastic quality.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of gasoline engines (MZR)
  • Very dynamic and agile chassis (weight kept under a ton)
  • Precise and pleasant manual gearbox control
  • Reduced maintenance cost for gasoline versions
  • Design that ages well

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation (wind and road noise), especially before the 2010 facelift
  • Hard interior plastics, prone to scratches
  • Modest boot volume (250 liters)
  • Variable reliability of diesel engines (PSA/Ford origin)
  • Thin paint, prone to stone chips

Check before buying

  • Steering column: Clunking or 'clong' noise when turning the steering wheel at low speeds (lubrication or replacement of the steering nut sometimes necessary).
  • Wheel bearings: Premature wear of rear bearings (dull humming noise from 70 km/h).
  • Corrosion: Inspect underbody, wheel arches, and fender edges, particularly on models imported from mountainous regions or Germany.
  • Injector seals (Diesel): Presence of black carbon ('tar') around the injectors on 1.4 and 1.6 MZ-CD.
  • Suspension: Noises over speed bumps (worn stabilizer bar links or shock absorber mounts).

Official recalls

  • September 2015 (Front Seats): Risk of weld failure in the frame of the driver's seat height adjustment mechanism. Affects models produced between 2007 and 2010.
  • December 2011 (Bearings): Replacement of rear wheel bearings on a small batch due to a sealing defect.
  • Japan/USA (Power Steering): Recall concerning the electric power steering module (loss of assistance). Less common in Europe.

Verdict

The Mazda2 (DE) is an excellent used car choice, with one strict condition: choose a gasoline engine. The 1.3 and 1.5 MZR blocks are 100% Mazda-designed, feature a timing chain, and boast bulletproof reliability. Combined with a light and playful chassis, they make the Mazda2 an extremely endearing and economical city car to use. Conversely, the diesel versions (MZ-CD), borrowed from PSA/Ford, accumulate the classic flaws of these blocks (injector seals, turbo, DPF, EGR) and should be avoided, especially since the benefit of a diesel in a city car of this weight is nil. Prioritize a post-2010 (facelifted) gasoline model to benefit from improved suspension comfort and sound insulation.
