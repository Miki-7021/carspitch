H300 (7e gen) · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The Toyota HiAce H300 (often referred to as the 6th or 7th generation depending on the market) marks a historic break for this legendary model. Abandoning the "cab-over" architecture in favor of a semi-bonnet design, it makes a giant leap in terms of safety (5-star ANCAP), acoustic comfort, and driving dynamics. Based on a stiffened platform inspired by the TNGA architecture, it is designed for global markets (Australia, Asia, Middle East, South America), with the notable exception of Europe (which receives the Stellantis-derived ProAce) and Japan (which retains the H200 for urban maneuverability reasons). It is a heavy-duty, extremely robust utility vehicle, designed to accumulate hundreds of thousands of kilometers with minimal downtime.

Strengths 5

  • Spectacular increase in passive and active safety (semi-bonnet design, Toyota Safety Sense)
  • Legendary mechanical reliability of 1GD engines and petrol units
  • Greatly improved ride comfort and sound insulation compared to the H200
  • Maximized interior space and multiple configurations (up to 17 seats in Commuter)
  • Exceptional resale value in all its markets

Weaknesses 5

  • Significantly increased size: less maneuverable in dense urban environments than the previous generation
  • Gargantuan fuel consumption of the 3.5L V6 engine
  • Basic and sometimes slow infotainment system on utility trims
  • Not available in Europe (replaced by a Stellantis clone) or Japan
  • Very firm leaf spring rear suspension when unladen

Check before buying

  • DPF regeneration history on diesel models used exclusively in the city
  • Condition of side sliding door rails and bearings (rapid wear on shuttles)
  • Underbody and rear leaf springs (check for absence of chronic overloading)
  • Premature wear of front seats and steering wheel on delivery vehicles
  • Smooth operation of the AC60 automatic gearbox (fluid change recommended every 80,000 km in severe use)

Official recalls

  • 2020 - Fuel Pump (Denso): Global recall affecting numerous Toyota models, including some petrol HiAce vehicles, for the replacement of the low-pressure fuel pump at risk of failure.
  • 2021 - Engine Management Software (Diesel): ECU update to correct a risk of premature clogging of the particulate filter (DPF) under specific low-speed driving conditions.
  • 2022 - Compliance Labels: Replacement of incorrect payload labels in certain markets (Australia in particular).

What sets it apart 6

Safety

  • Advanced Safety with 5-Star ANCAP Rating — The HiAce H300 marks a major safety leap, integrating a protective semi-bonnet design and the Toyota Safety Sense system for optimal occupant protection and confident driving.

Reliability

  • Legendary Mechanical Reliability and Exceptional Longevity — Globally recognized, the HiAce H300 features indestructible engines designed to exceed 500,000 km with regular maintenance, ensuring maximum uptime for your business.

Comfort

  • Greatly Improved Ride Comfort and Sound Insulation — Thanks to its new architecture and meticulous soundproofing, the HiAce H300 offers a more pleasant working environment and a smoother driving experience, even over long distances.

Practicality

  • Maximized Interior Space and Versatile Configurations — The HiAce H300 provides generous cargo space and flexible configurations, including passenger versions for up to 17 seats, perfectly adapting to all professional needs.

Economy

  • Exceptional Resale Value and Lasting Investment — The Toyota HiAce H300 is a smart investment, renowned for maintaining a very high resale value across all its markets, thereby optimizing your long-term operating costs.

tenue de route

  • Stable Handling and Enhanced Driving Dynamics — The shift from a cab-over to a semi-bonnet architecture gives the HiAce H300 more stable road holding and a more enjoyable driving dynamic, even when fully loaded.

Alternatives

  • Asia / Oceania: Hyundai Staria (more modern and comfortable, but less robust for pure utility use), Ford Transit Custom, LDV Deliver 9.
  • Middle East: Nissan Urvan (NV350 - cab-over architecture retained, less safe but very maneuverable).
  • Latin America: Renault Master, Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, Ford Transit.

Verdict

The Toyota HiAce H300 is undoubtedly one of the most reliable and durable utility vehicles in the world. The switch to a semi-bonnet architecture has corrected the main flaw of its predecessors: the lack of safety and comfort. Although it has become bulkier, it compensates with healthy road holding and indestructible engines. The 2.8L diesel engine (1GD-FTV) is the most rational choice for 90% of uses, provided it is not used exclusively for urban door-to-door delivery. It is a highly recommended purchase for professionals seeking a flawless work tool, capable of exceeding 500,000 km with rigorous maintenance.
