Summary

The Volkswagen ID.5 (E39A generation) is the "SUV Coupe" counterpart to the ID.4. Based on the Volkswagen Group's MEB modular electric platform, it stands out with a sloping roofline that slightly improves its aerodynamic drag coefficient (Cd of 0.26) at the expense of marginally reduced rear headroom. Unlike the ID.4, the ID.5 was launched exclusively with the large 77 kWh (net) battery. At the end of 2023, the model received a major technical update (often considered a technical facelift) introducing the new, much more powerful and efficient APP550 engine, as well as a 4th generation infotainment system (12.9-inch screen) correcting the serious ergonomic shortcomings of the early versions (sliders finally backlit, smoother interface). Overall, it is a comfortable and spacious vehicle, but its early production years were marred by recurring software bugs.

Strengths 5

  • Remarkable roominess despite the sloping roofline
  • Generous trunk volume (549 liters)
  • Exceptional turning radius on RWD versions (10.2 m)
  • Very meticulous soundproofing at high speeds
  • Highly successful technical update in late 2023 (APP550 engine and 4.0 software)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous ergonomics on 2021-2022 models (haptic buttons, unlit sliders)
  • Frequent software bugs on early versions (frozen screens, CarPlay/Android Auto disconnections)
  • Very high weight (over 2.1 tons) impacting agility and tire wear
  • Quality of some interior plastics unworthy of the price on early trims
  • No front trunk (Frunk) to store cables

Check before buying

  • Infotainment software version (aim for at least version 3.2)
  • Tire wear condition (especially the rear axle on 204 hp versions)
  • Clunking noises from the front axle when going over speed bumps (drop links/bushings)
  • Proper operation of exterior door handles (sensitive to moisture)
  • High-voltage battery State of Health (SOH) report certified by a third party or the dealership

What sets it apart 6

Safety

  • Advanced Safety for the Whole Family — The Volkswagen ID.5 achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 87% for child protection, ensuring absolute peace of mind.

Braking

  • Control and Serenity in Braking — Enjoy a powerful and progressive braking system, offering a reassuring and consistent pedal feel for effective and stable stops in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Stable and Refined Road Handling — The ID.5 offers exemplary road holding, characterized by excellent body control, precise steering, and refined ride comfort, ensuring predictable and safe driving.

Practicality

  • Everyday Space and Versatility — Despite its dynamic coupé line, the ID.5 surprises with its remarkable spaciousness and generous 549-liter boot volume, ideal for families and long journeys. Its exceptional turning radius of 10.2 meters makes city maneuvers easy.

Comfort

  • Acoustic Comfort and Technological Fluidity — Appreciate very refined soundproofing at high speeds for serene journeys. Recent versions (late 2023+) benefit from a 4th-generation infotainment system, finally fluid and intuitive, for a seamless user experience.

Performance

  • Optimized Power and Efficiency — Models produced from late 2023 integrate the new APP550 motor, offering increased power (up to 340 hp) and significantly improved energy efficiency, for dynamic performance and optimized range.

Alternatives

Alternatives by market - Europe: Skoda Enyaq Coupé iV (technical cousin, finish often considered superior), Audi Q4 Sportback e-tron (more premium), Tesla Model Y (benchmark in efficiency and software, Supercharger network), Ford Mustang Mach-E, Kia EV6, Hyundai Ioniq 5. - Asia (China): BYD Song L, Xpeng G6, Tesla Model Y, Nio EC6. - Americas: The ID.5 is not sold there. Alternatives in the SUV Coupe format include the Tesla Model Y, Ford Mustang Mach-E, and Volvo C40 Recharge.

Verdict

The Volkswagen ID.5 is a highly competent family electric SUV, penalized at its launch by rushed software development. On a strictly mechanical level (motors, battery), reliability is solid. The problems are concentrated on the onboard electronics and ergonomics.

Buying advice: - AVOID 2021/early 2022 models that have not received major software updates at the dealership. - NEGOTIATE a 2022/2023 model (Pro Performance 204 hp) provided the software is at least version 3.2 and the price reflects the steep depreciation. - BUY with your eyes closed the versions produced from late 2023 onwards (286 hp / 340 hp engine) equipped with the 4.0 software, which radically transform the user experience and the vehicle's efficiency.
