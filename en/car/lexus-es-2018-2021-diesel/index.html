All Lexus models

Summary

The seventh-generation Lexus ES (code name XZ10) marks a major turning point for the brand, particularly in Europe where it replaces the rear-wheel-drive GS sedan. Based on the TNGA-K global platform (shared with the Toyota Camry), it is a FWD (or AWD in some markets) that focuses entirely on supreme comfort, soundproofing, and the efficiency of its hybrid powertrain.

IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:

There is no diesel engine on the Lexus ES XZ10, nor on any other generation of the ES. Lexus definitively abandoned diesel long before the launch of this model, favoring its self-charging hybrid technology (HEV) which offers equivalent or even lower fuel consumption in urban/suburban cycles, without the emissions control constraints (DPF/FAP, AdBlue, clogged EGR valves) specific to modern diesels. The analysis will therefore focus on the actual powertrains (Hybrid and Petrol) that make this model famous.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability (often ranked #1 in its class)
  • Fuel consumption of the 300h version equivalent to a good diesel engine
  • Very high level of soundproofing and ride comfort
  • Build and assembly quality (Lexus standard)
  • Lexus Relax warranty (up to 10 years or 160,000 km if serviced within the network)

Weaknesses 4

  • Total absence of a diesel engine (may deter some traditional fleet managers, although the hybrid is more fiscally advantageous)
  • Multimedia system ergonomics ('Remote Touch' touchpad is unintuitive before the switch to full touchscreen)
  • Non-folding rear seat (presence of a V-brace behind the seats for chassis rigidity)
  • Placid road handling, focused on comfort rather than dynamism (unlike a BMW 5 Series)

Check before buying

  • Complete service history within the Lexus network (essential to activate/maintain the Relax extended warranty and the hybrid health check)
  • Condition of the 12V battery (hybrids put little strain on the conventional alternator, a weak 12V battery generates phantom error messages)
  • Front tire wear (the immediate torque of the electric motor on the front axle can accelerate wear)
  • Smooth operation of the touchpad and central screen (some cases of bugs requiring a reboot or update)

What sets it apart 6

reliability

  • Exceptional Reliability and Peace of Mind — The Lexus ES is globally recognized as one of the most reliable sedans in its class, offering unparalleled serenity. Furthermore, the Lexus Relax warranty, extendable up to 10 years or 160,000 km, ensures lasting peace of mind.

economy

  • Outstanding Efficiency, Ideal Diesel Alternative — The 300h self-charging hybrid powertrain offers astonishing fuel consumption, often below 6L/100km in mixed driving, rivalling the best diesels without their maintenance constraints. It's the perfect choice for maximum efficiency and optimized running costs.

comfort

  • Supreme Comfort and Absolute Silence — Enjoy top-tier sound insulation and exceptional ride comfort, transforming every journey into a relaxing experience. The quality of manufacturing and assembly, a Lexus hallmark, guarantees a refined and soothing cabin.

safety

  • Award-Winning Advanced Safety — The Lexus ES achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 91% for adult occupant protection and 87% for child occupant protection. It was also named an IIHS Top Safety Pick+ for several consecutive years.

braking

  • Powerful and Controlled Braking — Benefit from excellent braking performance, with short stopping distances and precise modulation. Independent tests measured stops from 96 km/h in just 36.6 meters, ensuring first-class active safety.

handling

  • Secure and Refined Road Handling — The Lexus ES offers very stable and predictable road behavior, focused on comfort. Its road holding is impeccable, with precise steering and well-controlled body roll, guaranteeing a smooth and refined drive, ideal for long journeys.

Alternatives

Europe

  • BMW 5 Series (G30): More dynamic, offers excellent diesel engines (520d) for high-mileage highway drivers.
  • Mercedes-Benz E-Class (W213): Comparable comfort, superior onboard technology, wide choice of diesels and plug-in hybrids.
  • Audi A6 (C8): Clinical finish, Quattro transmission, highly efficient TDI engines on the highway.
  • Volvo S90: Quirky premium alternative, highly focused on safety and comfort, available as a plug-in hybrid (T8).

North America

  • Acura TLX: Sportier, high-performance SH-AWD system.
  • Genesis G80: Excellent price/equipment ratio, rear-wheel drive or AWD, luxurious finish.

Verdict

The Lexus ES (XZ10) is one of the most reliable sedans in the world. If you were specifically looking for a diesel, you need to reconsider: Lexus made the visionary choice of going all-hybrid. The ES 300h version advantageously replaces any competing 4-cylinder diesel engine thanks to stunning urban/mixed fuel consumption (often under 6L/100km), cathedral-like silence, and an almost total absence of costly breakdowns (no turbo, no DPF/FAP, no clutch, no timing belt, no conventional alternator). It is a purchase of absolute peace of mind, provided you accept a dated multimedia system and a fixed rear seat. Favor a model with a clear history at Lexus to benefit from the extended 10-year warranty.
