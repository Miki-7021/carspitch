Volvo V40

II (525) · 2012 – 2019

NÉGOCIER Reliability 76/100 — bon

Summary

The Volvo V40 II (Type 525) is a premium compact hatchback built on the Ford Global C platform (shared with the Ford Focus Mk3). Introduced in 2012 to replace the S40/V50 and C30, it stands out for its exceptional safety features (including a world-first pedestrian airbag), Scandinavian interior design, and highly comfortable seats. A mid-cycle facelift in 2016 introduced the signature 'Thor's Hammer' LED headlights and updated trims.

Mechanically, the V40's lifespan is divided into two distinct eras: the early years (2012-2015) utilizing Ford-sourced 4-cylinder engines and Volvo's legacy 5-cylinder engines, and the later years (2015-2019) featuring Volvo's in-house 'Drive-E' (VEA) 4-cylinder engine family. While this report focuses primarily on the petrol variants, the diesel engines are also fully detailed. The V40 is generally a reliable premium hatchback, provided buyers navigate carefully around certain early engine and transmission combinations (notably the Ford-sourced 1.6 petrols and Powershift gearboxes).

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional safety record (Euro NCAP 5 stars, pedestrian airbag)
  • Extremely comfortable and supportive seats
  • High-quality, minimalist Scandinavian interior design
  • Excellent sound system options (Harman Kardon / Premium Sound)
  • Volvo VEA and 5-cylinder engines are highly reliable and characterful
  • Aisin Geartronic automatic transmissions are smooth and durable

Weaknesses 6

  • Ford-sourced 1.6 EcoBoost engines have known overheating issues
  • Ford Powershift dual-clutch transmission is jerky and prone to failure
  • Rear passenger legroom and headroom are cramped
  • Boot capacity (335 liters) is small for the class
  • Large turning circle makes tight city maneuvers difficult
  • Infotainment system (Sensus) feels dated with its numeric keypad

Check before buying

  • **Coolant Level (1.6 Petrol):** Look for any signs of leaks or low coolant. Walk away if the engine bay shows signs of overheating.
  • **Transmission History:** If automatic, identify the type. Avoid 6-speed Powershift (MPS6) without a flawless service history. Aisin Geartronic (6 or 8-speed) is highly preferred.
  • **EGR Cooler (VEA Diesels):** Check if the EGR recall has been completed. Look for soot or smell of exhaust fumes under the hood.
  • **Door Locks:** Common failure point on the V40. Check that all doors lock and unlock properly with the remote.
  • **Air Conditioning:** Condenser is exposed to stone chips. Ensure the A/C blows ice cold.
  • **Oil Consumption (Early VEA):** Check the exhaust for blue smoke on startup and ask the owner about top-ups between services.

What sets it apart 14

Safety

  • Advanced Safety and Award-Winning Innovations — The Volvo V40 II stands out for its exceptional safety, awarded 5 Euro NCAP stars, with an impressive 98% for adult protection and 88% for child protection. It was also a pioneer with the world's first pedestrian airbag, contributing to 88% pedestrian protection.
  • Ultra-Efficient and Stable Braking — Enjoy peace of mind thanks to a top-tier braking system. The V40 II boasts remarkably short stopping distances, typically around 36 to 38 meters from 100 km/h to 0, ensuring optimal safety in all circumstances.
  • Safe and Balanced Road Handling — The V40 II offers predictable and balanced road holding, ensuring a feeling of stability and comfort. Its safe road behavior provides a serene drive, capable of effectively absorbing road irregularities.

Comfort

  • Premium Interior Comfort and Refined Finish — The V40 II's cabin is an invitation to travel, offering exceptional seat comfort, typical of Volvo. The quality of materials and impeccable finish create a premium and welcoming atmosphere for all passengers.
  • Timeless and Elegant Scandinavian Design — The Volvo V40 II captivates with its distinctive Scandinavian design, both timeless and elegant. Its fluid lines and refined presence, especially in R-Design or Cross Country finishes, give it undeniable charm on the road.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Proven Petrol Engines — Choose from petrol engines offering excellent driving pleasure. Volvo's 5-cylinder blocks are renowned for their robustness and captivating sound, while the newer VEA engines (post-2017) combine performance and efficiency.

Reliability

  • Smooth and Reliable Geartronic Automatic Transmission — For the most enjoyable driving experience, the V40 II is equipped with excellent Geartronic (Aisin) 6 or 8-speed automatic transmissions. They offer fluid gear changes and contribute to the vehicle's overall reliability.

Verdict

The Volvo V40 is a stylish, incredibly safe, and comfortable premium hatchback that makes an excellent used buy—provided you choose the right powertrain.

**For Petrol Buyers:** The golden rule is to aim for a 2016 or newer model equipped with Volvo's in-house 1.5L or 2.0L VEA engines (T2, T3, T4, T5). These are robust, refined, and paired with reliable Aisin automatic gearboxes or solid manuals. If you want character, the older 2.0L or 2.5L 5-cylinder petrols are fantastic, albeit thirsty. **Avoid** the 2012-2015 1.6L petrols (Ford EcoBoost) paired with the Powershift automatic due to severe reliability risks regarding coolant leaks and transmission failures.

**For Diesel Buyers:** The later 2.0L VEA diesels are great for long distances (ensure the EGR recall is done). The older 5-cylinder diesels are legendary for longevity but require strict auxiliary belt maintenance. Avoid the 1.6 D2 if your driving is mostly urban.
