Summary

MG5 (Generation I - 2020-2022)

Expert warning: Although the request targets a diesel engine, it is imperative to clarify that a diesel MG5 has never existed, in any global market. The MG5 is a model with two faces depending on the region: in Europe, it is a 100% electric station wagon (based on the Chinese Roewe Ei5), a pioneer in its segment as it was the first affordable electric station wagon on the market. In the rest of the world (Asia, Middle East, South America), the MG5 is an internal combustion compact sedan (based on the Roewe i5) offered exclusively in gasoline.

In Europe, the MG5 has established itself thanks to its unbeatable price-to-roominess ratio and its 7-year warranty, despite a dated interior presentation (before the late 2022 facelift) and very placid driving dynamics. It is a pragmatic choice par excellence. Overall reliability rating: Very good, as the SAIC electric powertrains are proven, although the onboard electronics can sometimes be temperamental.

Strengths 5

  • Unbeatable price/volume/equipment ratio (EV station wagon version in Europe)
  • Reassuring 7-year or 150,000 km manufacturer warranty
  • Overall reliability of the electric powertrain
  • Onboard space and trunk volume (479 to 1367 liters)
  • Suspension comfort oriented towards softness

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of a diesel engine (for high-mileage internal combustion drivers)
  • Disappointing DC fast charging speed (max 87 kW, often less in reality)
  • Slow, dated, and sometimes buggy infotainment system (before facelift)
  • Very basic interior plastics quality
  • Soundproofing could be improved on the highway (wind and road noise)

Check before buying

  • 12V battery: Number 1 cause of immobilizing breakdowns on electric versions.
  • Charging port: Check that the cable locks and unlocks without forcing.
  • Underbody: On the EV version, check for the absence of impacts on the high-voltage battery casing.
  • Onboard electronics: Test the touchscreen, the reversing camera, and the Apple CarPlay/Android Auto connection.
  • DCT gearbox (on gasoline versions outside Europe): Check for the absence of juddering during hill starts.

Verdict

Expert verdict

If you are looking for a diesel MG5, move along: it does not exist.

However, if you are looking for a pragmatic family vehicle in Europe, the Electric MG5 (BEV) is an extremely rational choice. It will not spark any automotive passion, its multimedia interface will require patience, and its highway charging speed will limit very long trips. But for daily, suburban, or regional use, it is a reliable, spacious, and economical tool to use. The 7-year warranty covers major risks. Favor the Long Range (61.1 kWh) version for more versatility. In international markets, the gasoline versions do the job but suffer by comparison against Japanese and Korean benchmarks in terms of gearbox drivability.
