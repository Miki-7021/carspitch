Summary

The CUPRA Formentor is the first model developed exclusively for the CUPRA brand (independent from SEAT since 2018). Based on the Volkswagen Group's MQB Evo platform (shared with the VW Golf VIII and Audi A3 8Y), this compact coupe SUV stands out with its aggressive design, first-rate dynamic behavior, and a wide range of powertrains ranging from the sensible 150 hp 1.5 TSI to the fiery 390 hp 5-cylinder 2.5 TSI (VZ5). A facelift was presented in mid-2024, notably correcting the ergonomic shortcomings of the infotainment system (touch sliders finally backlit) and introducing new PHEV powertrains.

Overall expert rating: An excellent compromise between sportiness and daily use. Mechanical reliability is generally solid thanks to proven parts bins, but the 2020-2022 models were heavily penalized by electronic and software bugs (MIB3 system).
