Honda Civic

All Honda models

Summary

The 9th generation Honda Civic is a model with a dual identity: the FB (Sedan) and FG (Coupe) versions were mainly sold in North America, Asia, and emerging markets, while Europe received the FK (5-door Hatchback) version and the Tourer wagon.

In the United States, the 2012 model was criticized for the decline in quality of its interior plastics and soundproofing, forcing Honda to carry out an *emergency facelift* as early as 2013 to correct the issue. In Europe, the Civic FK stood out for its polarizing design, record spaciousness (Magic Seats), and excellent ergonomics.

Mechanically, this generation shines with exceptional reliability, particularly for its naturally aspirated gasoline engines (i-VTEC), which are among the most robust in global automotive production. It's a purchase that offers great peace of mind.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of gasoline engines (1.4 and 1.8 i-VTEC)
  • Unbeatable spaciousness and modularity (Magic Seats on the European FK version)
  • Driving position ergonomics (two-tier dashboard)
  • Very well-controlled real-world fuel consumption, even for gasoline models
  • Dynamic road handling and perfect manual gearbox operation

Weaknesses 5

  • Disappointing interior plastic quality on 2012 models (corrected in 2013/2014)
  • Fragile paint (prone to micro-scratches and stone chips)
  • Slow and dated infotainment system (Honda Connect)
  • Imperfect soundproofing on the highway (road noise)
  • Poor rear visibility (spoiler cutting across the rear window on the FK version)

Check before buying

  • Air conditioning operation (the condenser is highly exposed to impacts and often leaks)
  • Metallic noise on cold start on the 2.4L engine (VTC Actuator)
  • Rear tire wear (geometry sometimes tricky on European versions)
  • Oil change history (VTEC engines require clean oil for the variable valve timing system)
  • Bluetooth HFT (Hands Free Telephone) module: if defective, it can drain the battery when stationary

Official recalls

  • Takata Airbags (2015-2019): Massive global recall for the replacement of front airbag inflators (risk of metallic fragment projection).
  • CVT Transmission (2015): On certain North American models (2014-2015), software update to prevent premature wear of the CVT belt.
  • Driveshaft (2020): On certain North American models, risk of corrosion and fracture of the driveshaft (cardan) due to de-icing salt.
  • Fuel Pressure Sensor (2014): On 1.6 i-DTEC diesel engines, risk of leakage or incorrect reading leading to limp mode.

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Unrivaled Mechanical Reliability — The i-VTEC petrol engines of the Honda Civic IX are renowned for their robustness and exceptional longevity, ensuring peace of mind for hundreds of thousands of kilometers.

Practicality

  • Ingenious Practicality and Modularity — Enjoy a generous interior space and remarkable modularity, notably thanks to the famous 'Magic Seats' which offer unique and practical loading configurations.

Economy

  • Remarkably Low Fuel Consumption — Despite its performance, the petrol Honda Civic IX stands out for its very well-controlled real-world fuel consumption, allowing you to make significant savings daily.

Safety

  • Certified Maximum Safety — The Honda Civic IX achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult and child protection, ensuring optimal safety on the road.

Braking

  • Efficient and Reassuring Braking — Benefit from an efficient and consistent braking system, offering a firm and reassuring pedal feel. Stopping distances are competitive, guaranteeing your safety in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Stable and Comfortable Road Holding — The 9th generation Civic offers road behavior that prioritizes comfort and predictability. Its driving is stable and secure, ideal for daily commutes and long highway journeys.

Comfort

  • Intuitive Ergonomics and Driving Pleasure — Enjoy an ergonomic cockpit with dual-layer meters and a perfectly smooth manual gearbox, offering dynamic and intuitive driving pleasure.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Toyota Auris (for reliability and hybrid), Volkswagen Golf VII (for finish and soundproofing), Mazda 3 (for driving pleasure and gasoline reliability).
  • North America: Toyota Corolla (equivalent reliability, less dynamic), Mazda 3 (excellent dynamic alternative), Hyundai Elantra.
  • Asia / Emerging Markets: Toyota Corolla Altis, Nissan Sentra/Sylphy.

Verdict

The 9th generation Honda Civic is an ultra-rational and highly recommended choice, particularly with a gasoline engine (1.4 or 1.8 i-VTEC). While it may not shine with the modernity of its multimedia system or the quality of its plastics (especially before 2013), it compensates with mechanical reliability that borders on perfection. It's a car designed to last 300,000 km with basic maintenance. Prioritize facelifted models (2013+ in North America, 2014+ in Europe) to benefit from a better finish and increased soundproofing. Diesels are good but should be strictly reserved for high-mileage drivers to avoid DPF issues.
