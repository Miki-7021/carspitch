Opel Astra

J · 2009 – 2015

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The Opel Astra J (4th generation) marks a complete break with the H generation. Based on General Motors' Delta II platform (shared with the Chevrolet Cruze), it moves upmarket with a refined interior finish, an elegant design, and technological equipment unprecedented for its time (AFL+ headlights, AGR ergonomic seats, FlexRide adaptive suspension). Its main structural flaw is its **excessive weight** (often over 1,400 kg), which penalizes performance, fuel consumption, and prematurely wears out the running gear. Although the demand here focuses on diesel engines (very popular in this generation), the Astra J offered a plethora of engines to try and compensate for its bulk. The restyling (facelift) occurred in late 2012, bringing slight aesthetic touches and the gradual introduction of new engines (including the 1.6 CDTI 'Whisper Diesel' in 2014).

Strengths 6

  • Ride comfort and sound insulation (especially on highways)
  • Interior finish quality significantly improved compared to Astra H
  • AGR ergonomic seats (optional but exceptional for back comfort)
  • Secure road holding (HiPerStrut decoupled front suspension on GTC/OPC)
  • Intrinsic reliability of the 1.7 CDTI block and F40 gearbox (on 2.0 CDTI)
  • Exterior design that has aged very well

Weaknesses 6

  • Excessive weight (penalizes agility, fuel consumption, and brake/tire wear)
  • Catastrophic M32 manual gearbox on intermediate engines (1.4T, 1.6T, 1.7 CDTI)
  • Fatal oil strainer seal issue on the 2.0 CDTI (A20DTH)
  • Ergonomics of the center console ('one button = one function', very cluttered)
  • Poor rear visibility (very thick C-pillars)
  • Disappointing rear passenger space and boot volume given the exterior size (4.42 m)

Check before buying

  • M32 gearbox: whistling while driving, lever movement during acceleration/deceleration.
  • 2.0 CDTI: History of oil strainer seal replacement (vital).
  • 1.6 CDTI: Timing chain noise on cold start.
  • All Diesels: DPF condition (regeneration frequency) and dual-mass flywheel (clunking noise at idle, vibrations in clutch pedal).
  • Electronics: Functioning of AFL+ adaptive headlights (internal motors can seize, very expensive optics to replace).
  • Cooling: Leaks at the water housing and water pump (especially on petrol engines).

Official recalls

  • - **October 2010**: Risk of cooling fan control module malfunction (risk of overheating or short circuit). - **November 2010**: Clutch pedal. The pedal shaft may break on some models. - **May 2011**: Front seat rail attachment (bolts improperly tightened at the factory). - **August 2015**: Fire risk related to the starter motor. A short circuit can occur if the starter remains engaged (concerns some 2010-2011 models). - **2016**: Software recall on certain diesels (1.6 CDTI / 2.0 CDTI) following Dieselgate to reprogram emission management (EGR).

What sets it apart 14

Safety

  • Maximum Safety with 5 Euro NCAP Stars — The Opel Astra J achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 82% for child protection, ensuring peace of mind for all occupants.
  • Powerful and Secure Braking — Featuring top-tier braking performance for its category, the Astra J ensures effective stopping power and consistent pedal feel, actively contributing to your safety on all roads.
  • Stable and Predictable Road Handling — Enjoy a serene drive thanks to road handling characterized by its great stability and predictability, effectively absorbing irregularities for optimal comfort, even at high speeds.

Comfort

  • Touring Comfort and Refined Finish — The Astra J stands out with its exceptional ride comfort, remarkable sound insulation, and significantly improved interior finish quality, offering a premium travel experience. The optional AGR ergonomic seats are a major asset for well-being.

Reliability

  • Reliability of Key Diesel Engines — Trust in the robustness of the 1.7 CDTI (indestructible Isuzu engine) and 2.0 CDTI (with its solid F40 gearbox) diesel blocks, designed for great endurance and reliable long-term performance.

Practicality

  • Elegant and Timeless Exterior Design — Its modern and balanced exterior design has aged remarkably well, giving the Opel Astra J a consistently current and distinctive look on the road.

Performance

  • Dynamic Performance and Driving Pleasure — The 2.0 CDTI engine offers generous torque and appreciable power, ensuring brisk acceleration and superior driving pleasure, ideal for long highway journeys.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Volkswagen Golf VI / VII (lighter, better ergonomics), Peugeot 308 I / II (more dynamic handling), Ford Focus III (excellent chassis), Renault Mégane III (1.5 / 1.6 dCi very reliable at end of life). - **Japan/Asia**: Honda Civic 8/9 (2.2 i-DTEC and 1.6 i-DTEC engines ultra reliable), Toyota Auris (HSD hybrid unbeatable in city). - **Americas**: Chevrolet Cruze (technical cousin), Mazda 3.

Verdict

The Opel Astra J is an excellent road car, comfortable and well-finished, but its high weight dictates the choice of engine. **For diesel, the choice is complex but binary**: 1) If you are looking for absolute robustness without worrying about noise, the **1.7 CDTI** is the best choice, provided you scrupulously check the M32 gearbox. 2) If you want enjoyment and power, the **2.0 CDTI (160/165 hp)** is fantastic and benefits from the solid F40 gearbox, **BUT** you absolutely must have the oil strainer seal replaced upon purchase if it hasn't been done. The 1.3 CDTI is to be avoided (too weak) and the 1.6 CDTI is risky (poorly designed timing chain). For a used car, prioritize a post-2012 (facelifted) model with a clear service history.
