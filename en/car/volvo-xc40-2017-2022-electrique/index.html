Volvo XC40

I (XZ) · 2017 – 2022

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The **Volvo XC40 (Generation I - XZ)** is the first compact SUV from the Swedish brand, introducing the modular CMA (Compact Modular Architecture) platform. Voted Car of the Year in 2018, it established itself with its distinctive design, clever interior space, and first-class passive and active safety.

Although initially launched with classic internal combustion engines (petrol and diesel), the XC40 quickly made its energy transition. It first welcomed plug-in hybrid versions (PHEV - T4 and T5 Recharge) before becoming Volvo's first 100% electric model with the **Recharge Twin** (408 hp) version in 2020, followed by the **Recharge Single** (231 hp).

The electric version stands out with the native integration of the **Android Automotive** infotainment system (integrated Google), a first on the market. While manufacturing quality is excellent, the early days of the electric version were marked by initial software bugs (notably the TCAM module), which have now been largely corrected by OTA (Over-The-Air) updates.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional performance of the Twin Motor electric version (0-100 km/h in 4.9s)
  • Highly intuitive Android Automotive infotainment system (Google Maps, Assistant)
  • Benchmark passive safety and driving assistance systems (Pilot Assist)
  • Spacious interior and clever storage (31L frunk on the electric model)
  • High-quality finish and cabin sound insulation

Weaknesses 5

  • Average electric efficiency (Norman wardrobe aerodynamics, consumption > 20 kWh/100km on highway)
  • Annoying electronic bugs on early electric model years (2020-2021)
  • Very high weight of the electric version (nearly 2.2 tons) impacting agility
  • Very high fuel consumption of petrol versions (T4/T5)
  • Firm damping with 20-inch wheels

Check before buying

  • **TCAM Module (Electric):** Check that GPS, LTE/4G connection, and key recognition work perfectly.
  • **Tires (Electric Twin):** Inspect the tread, wear is often asymmetrical or very rapid at the front.
  • **Charging flap:** The locking mechanism for the T2/Combo CCS socket can sometimes get stuck (fragile actuator).
  • **12V Battery:** On PHEVs and BEVs, a weak 12V battery can generate cascading error messages.
  • **EGR Valve (Diesel):** Check for absence of sputtering at low rpm or engine warning light.

Official recalls

  • - **October 2023 (Electric):** Recall for a defect in the left turn signal that may not activate (software update). - **May 2022 (Electric):** Risk of loss of propulsion due to an inverter software bug. Resolved by OTA update. - **August 2021 (All models):** Potential defect in the front seat belt retractor. - **March 2020 (All models):** The autonomous emergency braking (AEB) system could fail to detect certain obstacles due to a software bug.

Alternatives

Verdict

The first-generation Volvo XC40 is an extremely accomplished premium SUV. **In its 100% electric version (Recharge), it represents the best choice in the range** thanks to its superior driving pleasure and integrated Google system. The *Twin Motor* version is an endearing rocket but heavy on tires; the *Single Motor* (front-wheel drive) version is a more rational and sufficient choice for daily use.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize an electric model from late 2021 or 2022, where the initial issues with the TCAM module have been resolved. If you opt for a PHEV, ensure that gearbox maintenance has been meticulously followed. Avoid diesels (D3/D4) unless you exclusively drive on highways.
