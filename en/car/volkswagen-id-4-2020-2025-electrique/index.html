Summary

The Volkswagen ID.4 (E21 generation) is the German manufacturer's first 100% electric global SUV, based on the modular MEB platform. Designed to be the 'Tiguan of the electric era,' it stands out for its generous spaciousness, ride comfort, and excellent turning radius (on RWD versions). However, the first years of production (2020-2023) were marred by numerous software bugs (slow infotainment system, black screens) and criticized ergonomics (haptic controls, unlit sliders). A major update in late 2023 (Model Year 2024) corrected most of these flaws with the introduction of the new APP550 rear motor (more powerful and efficient), a 12.9-inch screen with responsive v4.0 software, and backlit sliders. Overall rating: 7.5/10 (2020-2023 Models) / 8.5/10 (2024+ Models).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional spaciousness and trunk volume (543 liters)
  • Impressive turning radius on RWD versions (10.2 m)
  • Suspension comfort and very refined soundproofing
  • 2024 update (286 hp engine and v4.0 software) that transforms the vehicle
  • Passive and active safety (5 Euro NCAP stars)

Weaknesses 5

  • Exasperating software bugs on 2020-2022 models (slowness, reboots)
  • Ergonomics of touch controls on the steering wheel and unlit climate control sliders (corrected in 2024)
  • Quality of some interior plastics unworthy of the price on early versions
  • Very high weight (up to 2.2 tons) leading to rapid tire wear
  • Disappointing efficiency of the (optional) heat pump compared to initial promises

Check before buying

  • Software update history (demand at least version 3.2 on a used model).
  • Tire wear condition, particularly on the rear axle (RWD) and alignment.
  • Functionality of exterior door handles (sensitive to humidity).
  • Clunking noises from the front axle on degraded roads (stabilizer bar links).
  • High-voltage battery health (SOH - State of Health) via an independent OBD diagnostic (e.g., Aviloo) for high-mileage models.

What sets it apart 6

Safety

  • Advanced and Recognized Safety — The Volkswagen ID.4 achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 82% for child protection. It was also named an IIHS Top Safety Pick+ for 2021, 2022, and 2023, demonstrating its commitment to the safety of all occupants.

Comfort

  • Exceptional Comfort and Spaciousness — Enjoy a generous interior space and a boot volume of 543 litres, ideal for families and long journeys. The ID.4's meticulously designed sound insulation and suspension comfort ensure a serene and pleasant driving experience, even over long distances.

Handling

  • Stable and Agile Road Handling — Thanks to its low center of gravity and precise steering, the ID.4 offers predictable and secure road handling, with well-controlled body roll. Its impressive turning radius of just 10.2 meters on rear-wheel-drive versions ensures remarkable agility in urban environments.

Performance

  • Optimized Performance and Efficiency (2024+ Models) — 2024 and later versions of the ID.4 benefit from the new 286 hp APP550 motor, offering a significant gain in power and efficiency. This powertrain, combined with the latest 4.0 software version, transforms the driving experience, making it more dynamic and responsive.

Braking

  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — The ID.4 ensures excellent braking capability, essential for safety. Independent tests measured a braking distance from 112 to 0 km/h in just 52.7 meters, a competitive performance for an electric SUV of its size, offering enhanced peace of mind.

technologie

  • Intuitive and Advanced Onboard Technology (2024+ Models) — Models from 2024 onwards integrate an infotainment system with a 12.9-inch screen and v4.0 software, offering a responsive interface and improved ergonomics, including backlit sliders. Enjoy a fluid and modern user experience.

Verdict

The Volkswagen ID.4 is a family SUV packed with intrinsic qualities (space, comfort, handling), but its launch was marred by an unfinished software architecture. If you are looking for a 2021 or 2022 model, caution is advised: demand a clear history, verify that all recalls (12V battery, handles, 3.x updates) have been carried out, and negotiate the price firmly. If your budget allows, definitely prioritize a late 2023 or 2024 model (286 hp engine / 4.0 software): these versions correct almost all of the early flaws and make the ID.4 one of the best electric SUVs on the market.
