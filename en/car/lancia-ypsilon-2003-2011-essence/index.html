Summary

The '843' generation Lancia Ypsilon (often considered the 3rd generation if the Autobianchi/Lancia Y10 is included) is a premium 3-door city car, based on the proven platform of the Fiat Punto II (Project 188). Designed to appeal to an urban clientele looking for luxury and personalization (two-tone paintwork, Alcantara or leather interiors), it stands out for its comfort and neo-retro design. Facelifted at the end of 2006 (new bumpers, white taillights, Blue&Me connectivity), it enjoyed great success in Italy and Western Europe. While its interior finish is flattering, its Fiat technical underpinnings guarantee affordable maintenance, particularly on the petrol engines (FIRE family) which constitute the most relevant and reliable choice for this model.

Strengths 5

  • Petrol engines (FIRE) that are extremely reliable and economical to maintain
  • Timeless design and luxurious interior presentation (Alcantara, leather)
  • Suspension comfort above the category average
  • Power steering with 'City' mode very practical for maneuvering
  • Mechanical spare parts (Fiat Punto base) very affordable and available

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the D.F.N. (Dolce Far Niente) automated gearbox
  • Frequent failure of the electric power steering (steering angle sensor)
  • 1.3 Multijet diesel engines unsuited for the city (EGR, DPF/FAP, timing chain)
  • Limited rear roominess and trunk volume
  • Various electrical problems (connectors, taillight circuit boards, phantom warning lights)

Check before buying

  • Power steering: test the 'City' mode and steer from lock to lock. No hard spots, groaning, or red warning lights should appear (otherwise the steering column needs replacing).
  • Gearbox: absolutely favor the manual gearbox. If D.F.N. gearbox, check the smoothness of gear changes and the absence of hydraulic oil leaks under the robot.
  • Petrol engines: check for the absence of coolant leaks (water pump, interior heater core) and oil seepage at the rocker cover.
  • Diesel engines: listen carefully to the engine when cold. A metallic rattling indicates a slack timing chain (urgent repair).
  • Electricity: test all electric windows, the air conditioning, and check for the absence of illuminated airbag warning lights (often due to connectors under the seats).

Verdict

The Lancia Ypsilon III (843) is an excellent used city car provided you target the right version. **It is imperative to favor the petrol engines (1.2 or 1.4 FIRE) with a manual gearbox.** These versions are formidably reliable, economical to use, and benefit from cheap spare parts thanks to the Fiat parts bin. On the other hand, stay away from models equipped with the D.F.N. automated gearbox (exorbitant repair costs) and avoid the 1.3 Multijet diesels if you mainly drive in the city, otherwise you risk suffering timing chain breakages or chronic clogging. Before buying, checking the proper functioning of the electric power steering is the number one checkpoint.
