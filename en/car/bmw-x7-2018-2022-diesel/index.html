Summary

The first-generation BMW X7 (G07) (phase 1, before the late 2022 facelift) is the Bavarian brand's first true luxury 7-seater SUV. Designed to compete with the Mercedes GLS and primarily targeting the North American and Asian markets, it is based on the CLAR modular platform. As the flagship of the X range, it stands out for its exceptional ride comfort (standard air suspension), top-flight soundproofing, and flawless finish. While the six-cylinder engines (petrol and diesel) shine with their drivability and overall reliability, the model has suffered from numerous teething problems related to its electronic complexity and safety equipment, as evidenced by the long list of official recalls.

Strengths 6

  • Imperial ride comfort (standard air suspension)
  • Real roominess for 7 adults (usable 3rd row)
  • Soundproofing worthy of a 7 Series
  • Remarkable reliability of the 6-cylinder blocks (30d, 40i)
  • Cabin finish and assembly quality
  • Highly responsive iDrive 7.0 infotainment system

Weaknesses 5

  • Oversized dimensions (5.15 m) highly penalizing in European urban environments
  • Prohibitive maintenance, insurance, and tire costs
  • M50d engine (quad-turbo) frightening in case of breakdown out of warranty
  • Staggering number of manufacturer recalls (electronics, braking, safety)
  • Colossal weight (up to 2.5 tons) that quickly wears out brakes and running gear

Check before buying

  • Complete recall history (absolutely crucial given the list of teething problems)
  • Air suspension operation (raising/lowering without excessive compressor noise)
  • Tire wear (staggered 21 to 23-inch tire setups are very expensive)
  • Signs of coolant leaks (especially on N63 V8s)
  • Operation of all electrical equipment (3rd-row seats, cameras, driving aids)

Official recalls

The X7 (G07) has been the subject of numerous recalls. It is imperative to verify that these interventions have been carried out:

  • Fire risk / Starter (2024-2025): Overheating of the starter after repeated attempts or water infiltration into the starter relay. Software update or replacement (NHTSA Recalls 25V644000, 24V576000 / Rappel Conso France).
  • Braking system (2021, 2024, 2025): Potential failure of the hydraulic braking assistance (defective unit or servomotor shaft weld). Risk of increased braking distance.
  • Seats and Safety (2019-2023): Improper installation of the 3rd-row backrest locking springs (2022-2023). Durability defect of the ISOFIX anchorages (2021). Misalignment of the head airbags (2019). 'Business Lounge' seat mountings (2019).
  • Steering and Suspension (2019-2021): Risk of steering spindle detachment (2021). Potential breakage of the steering rack pinion teeth (2019). Welding defect on the front suspension arm (2020).
  • Electronics and Cameras (2019-2021): Rearview camera failure (software). Risk of short circuit on the 48V battery (2020).
  • Miscellaneous: Improperly secured high-pressure fuel pump (2019), insufficiently tightened wheel bolts (2019), headlight sealing defect (2020).

Verdict

The BMW X7 (G07) is a formidable travel machine, offering luxury and space comparable to a 7 Series, with the versatility of an SUV. On a strictly mechanical level, the six-cylinder engines (notably the 30d diesel and 40i petrol) are of exemplary reliability. However, the astronomical amount of safety-related recalls (brakes, steering, fire risk linked to the starter) dictates extreme caution when buying used. A model without a crystal-clear BMW dealership maintenance history should be avoided. If you opt for a diesel, the 30d is the most rational and reliable choice; the M50d, although fascinating, is a financial time bomb in the event of a failure of its quad-turbo system.
