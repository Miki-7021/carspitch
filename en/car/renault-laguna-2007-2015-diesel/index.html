Renault Laguna

III (BT0) · 2007 – 2015

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The Renault Laguna III (codename BT0 for the sedan, KT0 for the Estate wagon, DT0 for the Coupé) had a colossal mission: to make people forget the catastrophic reliability of the Laguna II Phase 1. To achieve this, Renault implemented drastic quality standards ('Excellence Renault' plan). The result is clear: the Laguna III is one of the most reliable family cars of its generation, all brands included. While its exterior design (especially the front end of Phase 1) divided opinion and hindered sales, its dynamic qualities are exceptional, notably thanks to the '4Control' four-wheel steering system (standard on GT and Initiale trims, or optional). It underwent two facelifts: Phase 2 in 2010 (more aggressive front end, darkened lights) and Phase 3 in 2013 (integration of LED daytime running lights and the R-Link multimedia system). Today, it is an excellent used car choice, offering an unbeatable price/performance/reliability ratio.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability (especially with 2.0 dCi and petrol engines)
  • 4Control chassis, astonishing in efficiency and safety
  • Unbeatable quality/price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Significantly improved finish quality and material aging
  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation (especially on Phase 2 and 3)

Weaknesses 5

  • Exterior design of Phase 1 often considered unappealing
  • Average rear passenger space for the category
  • Sedan boot volume a bit tight (the Estate wagon does better)
  • Fuel consumption of petrol engines (excluding E85)
  • Brand image tarnished by the previous generation (strong depreciation, although this is an advantage when buying)

Check before buying

  • Service history: prefer models with oil changes every 15,000 / 20,000 km maximum.
  • Condition of the DPF and EGR valve on diesels (check for absence of warning light and power loss).
  • Rear tire wear on models equipped with 4Control.
  • Air conditioning operation (compressor sometimes noisy).
  • Parasitic noises in the cabin (center console, dashboard) on models before 2010.
  • On automatic gearboxes (BVA6 - AJ0), check that an oil change has been done around 80,000 - 100,000 km.

Official recalls

  • - **February 2008**: Reprogramming of the injection computer on early 1.5 dCi models (risk of jerking).
  • - **December 2009**: Inspection and possible replacement of the driver's seat frame (risk of play in the runners).
  • - **August 2010**: Recall concerning front seat belt pretensioners (risk of non-deployment in case of impact).
  • - **2011**: Software update on some 1.5 dCi models to optimize DPF regeneration.

Verdict

The Renault Laguna III is the archetype of a 'good deal' on the used market. A victim of its predecessor's poor reputation, it suffered a dizzying depreciation, even though it is intrinsically one of the most reliable cars of its decade. The top choice is undoubtedly the **2.0 dCi (130 or 150 hp for economy, 175 or 180 hp for dynamism)**, preferably associated with the 4Control chassis (GT or Initiale trim) which transforms the road handling. The 2.0 16v petrol versions (especially Bioethanol) and 2.0T are also excellent choices for those who drive less. Simply avoid diesels that have been used exclusively in the city to prevent DPF issues.
