MINI Clubman

II (F54) · 2015 – 2024

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The **second-generation MINI Clubman (F54)** marks a radical turning point compared to its predecessor (R55). Gone is the asymmetrical architecture (one door on one side, two on the other): the F54 adopts a classic 4-door premium compact estate format, while retaining its emblematic split rear doors (*Split Doors*). Based on BMW's modular UKL2 platform (shared with the BMW X1 F48 and 1 Series F40), it makes a giant leap in terms of manufacturing quality, interior space, and especially **reliability**. Gone are the capricious "Prince" (THP) engines developed with PSA; the F54 exclusively features BMW's modular B-series blocks (B38, B48, B37, B47). A facelift (LCI) occurred in 2019, bringing *Union Jack* taillights, new gearboxes (7-speed DKG), and a super-powerful 306 hp JCW version. Production ceased in early 2024 without a direct replacement.

Weaknesses 5

  • Rear visibility hindered by the central pillar of the split doors
  • High weight (often over 1,400 kg), penalizing agility compared to the 5-door Hatch
  • Firm comfort, especially with 18 or 19-inch wheels and sport suspension
  • Premature wear of engine mounts (known issue on UKL platforms)
  • Used car prices and maintenance costs (BMW network)

Check before buying

  • Condition of the upper passenger-side engine mount (idle vibrations, black fluid leak if the hydraulic bush is punctured).
  • Recall history for diesel engines (EGR cooler must have been replaced).
  • Traces of coolant leak under the thermostat housing (B38/B48 petrol engines).
  • Smooth operation of the split rear doors (struts and locks sometimes temperamental).
  • Maintenance of the ALL4 all-wheel drive (transfer case oil change recommended every 80,000 km).

Verdict

The MINI Clubman F54 is an excellent used car choice. It has managed to erase the reliability flaws of the previous generation thanks to the adoption of BMW's modular engines. The petrol versions (Cooper and Cooper S) are particularly recommendable, offering an excellent compromise between driving pleasure and peace of mind. The 306 hp JCW version is a truly high-performance and reliable shooting brake. Diesels should be reserved for very high mileage drivers due to the sensitivity of the EGR system. When purchasing, it is imperative to check the condition of the engine mounts and the service history from the dealership network.
