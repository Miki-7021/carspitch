Summary

The fifth-generation Land Rover Discovery (L462) marks a radical departure from its predecessors. Gone are the ladder chassis and boxy design; it adopts an aluminum monocoque structure (shared with the Range Rover) allowing a theoretical weight saving of 480 kg, and more rounded lines. Positioned as a premium family SUV with 7 real seats, it retains exceptional off-road capabilities thanks to the Terrain Response 2 system and its air suspension. However, its reliability record is catastrophic: with a 25% defect rate at the technical inspection (TÜV) after just 3 years and a score of 2/5 from What Car?, it is among the least reliable premium SUVs on the market. Electronics, suspension, and engines are sources of recurring breakdowns.

Strengths 5

  • Unmatched off-road capabilities in its class (Terrain Response 2, approach/departure angles)
  • Regal ride comfort thanks to standard air suspension
  • Exceptional roominess with 7 real seats usable by adults
  • Impressive towing capacity (up to 3,500 kg)
  • Soundproofing and interior finish quality (premium materials)

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous overall reliability (25% failure rate at TÜV at 3 years, 38% annual breakdown rate according to What Car?)
  • Incessant electronic bugs (black screens, faulty driving aids, battery drain)
  • Fragile air suspension (compressor, sensors, air leaks)
  • Highly problematic 4-cylinder diesel engines (Ingenium) (timing chain, oil dilution)
  • Prohibitive maintenance and repair costs out of warranty
  • Very high actual weight despite aluminum, penalizing fuel consumption (especially in petrol models)

Check before buying

  • Complete recall history (over 20 official campaigns, including fire risks and loss of braking)
  • Air suspension operation (raise/lower the vehicle several times, listen to the compressor)
  • Signs of oil leaks (oil filter housing, turbo pipes) and coolant leaks (petrol V6)
  • Stability of the infotainment system and absence of warning lights on the dashboard
  • Condition of rear door locks (recall for corrosion preventing locking)
  • Presence of metallic noises on startup (sign of timing chain wear on 4-cylinder models)

Official recalls

The Discovery V has undergone an exceptionally high number of recalls. Here are the main ones:

  • Airbags (May 2025): Recall related to Takata airbags (deterioration of the propellant gas, risk of fragment projection).
  • Engine / Turbo (Oct. 2024): Oil leak from the turbocharger feed pipe that could leak onto the road (2021-2025 models).
  • Engine / Oil filter (Jul. 2024): Cracking of the plastic oil filter housing (petrol and diesel), fire risk.
  • Bodywork (Sep. 2023 & Jun. 2023): Corrosion of the rear door locking system (UniLatch) leading to involuntary unlocking while driving.
  • Engine (Jul. 2023): External oil leaks or oil transfer into the air intake, fire risk.
  • Electrical / Battery (2020 & 2021, multiple): Poor contact of the battery ground stud with the chassis, causing total electrical failures, engine shutdown, or loss of lighting.
  • 48V Hybrid (May 2021): Electrical overload of the 48V MHEV system, MOSFET failure, fire risk.
  • Safety (2021 & 2022): Defects on seatbelt pretensioners, 3rd-row anchorages, and occupant detection sensor (ODS).
  • V6/V8 Engine (Jan. 2019): Possible rupture of the crankshaft pulley bolt.
  • Electronics (2018): Unexpected deactivation of the Autonomous Emergency Braking (AEB) and fuel gauge indicating an incorrect level.

What sets it apart 6

Practicality

  • Unrivaled All-Terrain Capabilities — The Discovery V excels off the beaten path thanks to its Terrain Response 2 system and optimized approach and departure angles, offering uncompromising adventure versatility.
  • Exceptional Space and Versatility — With 7 genuine seats usable by adults and an impressive towing capacity of up to 3,500 kg, the Discovery V is the ideal partner for all your family or professional adventures.

Comfort

  • Royal Ride Comfort — Enjoy exceptional ride comfort thanks to the standard air suspension, complemented by first-class soundproofing and premium interior finish quality.

Safety

  • Advanced Safety and Controlled Road Behavior — Benefit from maximum protection with 5 Euro NCAP stars, solid and progressive braking performance, and impressive road holding with well-controlled body roll for stable and secure driving.

Performance

  • Powerful and Refined Petrol Engines — The petrol engines offer a dynamic and smooth driving experience, with units like the 3.0 Si6 V6 supercharged engine renowned for its mechanical robustness and driving pleasure.
  • Advanced Chassis Technology — Its aluminum monocoque structure, shared with the Range Rover, ensures optimal rigidity and contributes to surprising agility for an SUV of this size, enhancing the driving experience.

Verdict

The Land Rover Discovery V is a paradox: it is one of the most versatile, comfortable, and capable family SUVs in the world, but its reliability is abysmal. Statistics from ADAC, TÜV (25% defect rate at 3 years), and What Car? (38% annual breakdown rate) paint an alarming picture. Temperamental electronics and fragile air suspension are costly sources of problems.

If you absolutely must buy one, imperatively favor a petrol engine (the supercharged 3.0 Si6 V6 is the most mechanically robust, despite its gargantuan fuel consumption). Avoid the 4-cylinder diesels (Ingenium) and beware of the diesel V6s (crankshaft failures).

Strict buying condition: Only buy this vehicle if it comes with a manufacturer warranty or a comprehensive extended warranty (like Land Rover Approved) covering electronics and suspension. Without a warranty, the financial risk is far too high.
