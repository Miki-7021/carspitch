BYD Atto 3

I · 2022 – 2025

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The BYD Atto 3 (known as the Yuan Plus in China) is a 100% electric compact SUV based on the Chinese manufacturer's e-Platform 3.0. Spearheading BYD's European offensive, it stands out with its 'Blade' battery technology (LFP - Lithium Iron Phosphate) integrated into the chassis, renowned for its high safety and longevity. Featuring a highly original interior design (inspired by the worlds of music and fitness) and a plethora of standard equipment (heat pump, V2L, panoramic roof), it positions itself as a formidable alternative to European and Korean benchmarks. Although its fast charging power is modest (88 kW max), its efficiency and overall reliability make it a very rational choice. **Overall rating: 8/10**.

Strengths 5

  • Extremely safe and durable Blade (LFP) battery technology
  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio (heat pump and V2L as standard)
  • Suspension comfort and careful soundproofing
  • Surprising assembly and finish quality for the segment
  • Generous onboard space (2.72 m wheelbase)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disappointing DC fast charging power (88 kW maximum)
  • Dangerous original equipment tires on wet roads
  • Overly intrusive and sometimes erratic advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS)
  • Highly polarizing interior design (guitar strings in the doors)
  • Average trunk volume (440 liters) and absence of a 'frunk' (front trunk)

Check before buying

  • Check the tire brand (negotiate a replacement if original Chinese tires are on a used model).
  • Ensure all OTA updates have been installed (system software version).
  • Test the rotation mechanism of the central screen (12.8 or 15.6 inches depending on trim).
  • Check the condition of the 12V battery (lead-acid), a classic weak point of EVs.
  • Check the proper locking/unlocking of the charging flap.

Verdict

The BYD Atto 3 is an extremely relevant choice for buyers looking for a family-friendly, comfortable, and over-equipped electric SUV. Its mechanical and electrical reliability is excellent, driven by BYD's mastery in battery manufacturing (Blade LFP technology). The only real flaws are peripheral: original equipment tires that must be replaced for safety reasons in the rain, and driving aids that need to be deactivated or configured at each start-up. If the modest fast charging (88 kW) is not a hindrance for your occasional long trips, it is a highly recommended purchase.
