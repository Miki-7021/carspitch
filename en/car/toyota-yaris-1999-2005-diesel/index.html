Toyota Yaris

I (XP10) · 1999 – 2005

ACHETER Reliability 89/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The Toyota Yaris Generation I (XP10), also known as the Vitz in Japan and the Echo in North America and Australia, is a landmark vehicle in the supermini segment. Winning the European Car of the Year award in 2000, it revolutionized the class with its 'tall boy' design, offering exceptional interior space within a compact footprint. The XP10 is globally renowned for its bulletproof reliability and low running costs. A mid-cycle facelift was introduced in 2003, bringing minor cosmetic updates (teardrop headlights) and interior refinements. While this guide focuses on the highly regarded 1.4 D-4D diesel engine, the Yaris XP10's entire engine lineup is celebrated for its longevity, making it one of the best used-car buys even two decades after its release. Global Rating: Excellent.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability across all engines, especially the 1.4 D-4D
  • Timing chains used on all engines (no timing belts to replace)
  • The 1.4 D-4D lacks problematic modern diesel emissions equipment (No DPF, No DMF)
  • Incredible interior space utilization (sliding rear bench)
  • Very low running and maintenance costs
  • Excellent rust resistance compared to European rivals of the same era

Weaknesses 6

  • Cheap, scratch-prone interior plastics
  • Digital instrument cluster can fade or fail over time
  • Headlight lenses are highly prone to UV oxidation (yellowing)
  • Poor sound insulation at highway speeds
  • Manual gearbox input shaft bearings can wear out, causing a whining noise
  • 1.4 D-4D is restricted in many European cities due to older Euro emission standards

Check before buying

  • EGR valve condition on the 1.4 D-4D (look for black smoke under hard acceleration)
  • Cold start on the diesel: check for excessive cranking time (glow plugs or injector leak-off)
  • Listen for a whining noise from the manual gearbox when accelerating in lower gears
  • Check the digital dashboard display for missing segments or dimming
  • Inspect the water pump for pink crusty residue (coolant leak) on petrol models
  • Check the sliding mechanism of the rear seats, which can jam if filled with debris

Verdict

The Toyota Yaris I (XP10) is a masterclass in automotive longevity. If you are specifically looking at the 1.4 D-4D diesel, you are looking at one of the most robust small diesel engines ever made. Because it predates the era of Diesel Particulate Filters (DPF) and Dual-Mass Flywheels (DMF), it avoids the catastrophic repair bills associated with modern diesels. It is highly recommended for drivers with longer commutes outside of strict urban emission zones. If you drive purely in the city, opt for the 1.0L or 1.3L petrol to avoid EGR clogging. Regardless of the engine, finding a well-maintained, rust-free example is a guaranteed smart purchase.
