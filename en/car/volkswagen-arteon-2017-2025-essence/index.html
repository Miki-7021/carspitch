Volkswagen Arteon

I (3H7) (2017–2025) · 2017 – 2025

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The Volkswagen Arteon (Generation I, code 3H7) is a high-end '5-door coupé' (fastback) sedan that succeeds the Volkswagen CC. Based on the modular MQB platform, it stands out for its bold design, exceptional spaciousness, and very practical tailgate. In 2020, the model benefited from a facelift, introducing an unprecedented and very successful Shooting Brake variant, a plug-in hybrid version (eHybrid), and a sporty R variant with 320 hp. Although the pre-facelift interior was criticized for its too strong resemblance to the Passat, the 2020 update corrected this with more upscale materials, but also introduced sometimes capricious touch controls. Overall, the Arteon is a very accomplished vehicle, offering an excellent compromise between ride comfort (especially with DCC adaptive damping) and dynamism.

Strengths 5

  • Spectacular and timeless design (especially in Shooting Brake)
  • Limousine-like rear spaciousness and huge trunk (hatchback)
  • Remarkable reliability of 2.0 TSI petrol engines (EA888)
  • Suspension comfort with DCC adaptive damping
  • Careful soundproofing (acoustic glazing often present)

Weaknesses 5

  • MIB3 infotainment system (post-2020) slow and buggy
  • Ergonomics of touch controls on the steering wheel and climate control (post-2020)
  • Possible wind noise at high speed (frameless doors)
  • Pre-2020 dashboard too austere (identical to Passat)
  • Limited rear visibility (sedan)

Check before buying

  • Travel Assist operation: if a beep and an error message appear, the steering wheel must be changed (high cost out of warranty).
  • Coolant level: a drop indicates a leak from the water pump/thermostat housing (a common issue with TSI and TDI engines).
  • Seals of frameless doors: check for whistling on the highway and the condition of the rubbers (risk of sticking in winter).
  • DSG gearbox service history: oil changes must be scrupulously respected (every 60,000 km).
  • Panoramic sunroof: check for creaking on degraded roads and proper drainage of water drains.

Verdict

The Volkswagen Arteon is an excellent alternative to traditional premium sedans. In terms of reliability, it particularly shines with its petrol engines. The 2.0 TSI (EA888) block, in all its power variants, has reached full maturity and proves to be extremely robust, making the petrol Arteon a highly recommendable purchase. The main inconveniences will not come from the mechanics, but from the onboard electronics (MIB3 central screen bugs and steering wheel sensors on facelifted versions). Opt for a 2.0 TSI 190 hp model for daily use, or the 280/320 hp for performance, ensuring that the water pump has been checked and the DSG gearbox oil changed on time.
