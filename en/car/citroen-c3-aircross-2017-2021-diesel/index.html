Summary

The first-generation Citroën C3 Aircross (Phase 1) is an urban SUV that replaced the C3 Picasso minivan. Based on the PSA Group's PF1 platform (shared with the Peugeot 2008 I and Opel Crossland X), it stands out with its cheerful design, typical Citroën suspension comfort, and exemplary modularity (sliding rear bench, fold-flat passenger seat).

Although the request for this report focuses on the diesel (BlueHDi), it is crucial to note that this model was predominantly sold with gasoline (PureTech) engines. Diesel powertrains, well-suited for high-mileage drivers, underwent a major transition in 2018, moving from the 1.6 BlueHDi block to the 1.5 BlueHDi, the latter introducing new reliability challenges. The model's overall rating is heavily penalized by design flaws in the PureTech gasoline engines and the fragility of the diesel emissions control systems.

Strengths 5

  • Suspension comfort above segment average
  • Excellent modularity (sliding rear bench by 15 cm, flat floor)
  • Generous interior space for its size (4.15 m)
  • Remarkable fuel efficiency of BlueHDi engines on road/highway
  • Smoothness of the EAT6 automatic gearbox (Aisin)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of PureTech gasoline engines (belt)
  • Chronic fragility of the SCR (AdBlue) emissions control system on all diesels
  • Risk of engine failure on the 1.5 BlueHDi (camshaft chain)
  • Quality of interior plastics (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Slow infotainment system and all-touch ergonomics (air conditioning)

Check before buying

  • AdBlue History (Diesel): Ask if the tank or urea injector has already been replaced (invoice of 1000 to 1500€).
  • Top-end Engine Noise (1.5 BlueHDi): A metallic knocking sound when cold indicates wear of the camshaft chain.
  • Belt Condition (PureTech): Mandatory visual inspection through the oil filler opening (cracks, swelling).
  • Rear Axle: Premature rear tire wear or squeaking (crossmember defect on some models).
  • Electronics: Touchscreen bugs, rearview camera malfunction.

Official recalls

  • November 2020 (PureTech): Massive recall (code JZR) for inspection of the timing belt and oil pump strainer due to braking problems (clogged vacuum pump).
  • October 2019 (BlueHDi): Engine control unit reprogramming to correct NOx emissions (SCR system update).
  • September 2018: Incorrect tightening of rear axle mounting screws potentially leading to loss of control.
  • May 2018: Risk of friction of the starter motor's electrical harness, potentially causing a short circuit.
  • February 2021: Non-conformity of the particulate filter (FAP) on certain diesel models manufactured in 2018.

Verdict

The Citroën C3 Aircross I is an excellent urban SUV on paper: practical, comfortable, and modular. However, its reliability record is very grim. If you are looking for a diesel, prioritize the 1.6 BlueHDi (100 or 120 hp) from 2017-2018: its mechanics are solid, although you should budget for a potential AdBlue tank replacement. The 1.5 BlueHDi is to be avoided unless the timing chain (10mm chain) has been updated with an invoice. As for the PureTech gasoline versions, they require meticulous monitoring and a recent belt to avoid disaster. A purchase that requires extreme vigilance.
