Citroën Berlingo

III (K9) (2018–2025) · 2018 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 60/100 — moyen

Summary

The third-generation Citroën Berlingo, known by the internal code K9, was launched in 2018. It shares its EMP2 platform with its technical cousins, the Peugeot Rifter and Opel Combo Life, as well as many other models from the Stellantis group (formerly PSA). Positioned in the leisure activity vehicle segment, it stands out for its generous spaciousness, exemplary modularity, and typically Citroën suspension comfort. Available in commercial (Berlingo Van) and family (Berlingo) versions, it offers impressive cargo volume and great everyday practicality.

Aesthetically, the K9 adopts the brand's modern stylistic codes, with a distinctive front end and side Airbumps. It benefited from an update in 2022, mainly for the electric versions, and a more global facelift in 2024, notably marking the abandonment of internal combustion engines for the passenger version in some European markets, in favor of the 100% electric ë-Berlingo.

Overall, the Berlingo III is a versatile and family-friendly vehicle, but its reliability is strongly linked to the chosen powertrains, especially the PureTech gasoline engines which have experienced recurring problems.
