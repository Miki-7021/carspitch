Summary

The Skoda Superb III (Type 3V), resting on the modular MQB platform of the Volkswagen group, is the undisputed benchmark for interior space and value for money/performance in the D-segment family car category. Launched in 2015, it benefited from a mid-life facelift in 2019 (Matrix LED headlights, MIB3 infotainment system, introduction of the iV plug-in hybrid version). Designed to cover long distances, it was massively popular with company fleets, taxis, and high-mileage drivers, which explains the predominance of diesel engines (TDI) on the used car market. Although the trend reversed towards the end of its career in favor of gasoline and PHEV, the 2.0 TDI remains the historical core of this model's range. Overall, it is a very well-designed vehicle with solid reliability, provided that maintenance schedules are scrupulously adhered to, particularly for DSG gearboxes and diesel emission control systems.

Strengths 6

  • Rear legroom worthy of a limousine
  • Gigantic boot volume (625L in sedan, 660L in Combi)
  • Overall reliability of 2.0 TDI (excluding water pump) and 2.0 TSI engines
  • Ride comfort, especially with DCC adaptive damping
  • Retained physical ergonomics (real climate control knobs)
  • Unbeatable value for money/spaciousness on the used car market

Weaknesses 6

  • Chronic fragility of the water pump on 2.0 TDI (EA288) engines before 2020
  • DSG7 (DQ200) dry-clutch gearbox unreliable on 1.6 TDI and small TSI engines
  • MIB3 infotainment system (post-2019) prone to bugs (black screens, reboots)
  • Cupping wear of rear tires (road noise), especially on sport chassis or 19-inch wheels
  • Squeaky front suspension wishbone bushings in cold or dry weather
  • AdBlue system sensitive to crystallization (pump/tank to be replaced if neglected)

Check before buying

  • Coolant level (LDR) on TDIs: a drop indicates a leaking water pump (cost: 800-1200€ with timing belt).
  • Clear history of DSG gearbox oil changes (every 60,000 km for wet clutches).
  • DSG gearbox behavior during test drive: no jerking on startup, smooth gear changes, no excessive slipping.
  • Suspension noises (squeaks over speed bumps): bushings to be greased or replaced.
  • Touchscreen and GPS functionality (test responsiveness, check software updates).
  • Condition of rear tires (run hand over tread to detect 'sawtooth' wear).
  • On iV (PHEV) versions: battery state of health (SOH) and charging flap operation.

Official recalls

  • 2016 (Airbags): Possible replacement of defective airbag igniter.
  • 2018 (Starter Relay): Risk of overheating and fire of the starter relay on certain models.
  • 2019 (Sunroof): Defective bonding of the panoramic sunroof frame potentially leading to water ingress or detachment.
  • 2020 (Brake Pedal): On DSG gearbox models (2020), defective weld on the brake pedal plate which could fail during emergency braking.
  • 2022 (Hybrid iV): Risk of short circuit or fire due to defective insulation of the high-voltage battery (installation of an additional insulating mat).

Safety

  • Benchmark Passive Safety — The Skoda Superb III achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with outstanding scores for adult (86%) and child (86%) protection, confirming its status as a safe family vehicle. It was even named "Best in Class" in 2015.

braking

road_holding

reliability

  • Endurance and Robustness of the 2.0 TDI Engine — The 2.0 TDI engine is renowned for its robustness and endurance, making the Superb III an ideal choice for high-mileage drivers and long journeys, with solid reliability for thousands of kilometres.

economy

Alternatives

  • Europe : Volkswagen Passat B8 (its technical cousin, slightly superior finish), Peugeot 508 II (more dynamic but much less spacious), Ford Mondeo (comfortable but aging), Renault Talisman (decent habitability, erratic reliability at the beginning).
  • Japan / Asia : Mazda 6 (design, gasoline engine reliability, but dated infotainment), Toyota Camry (efficient hybrid, legendary reliability, but less practical trunk).
  • North America : The Superb is not sold there. Local VW group alternatives are the Volkswagen Passat (NMS) or the Arteon.

Verdict

The Skoda Superb III is a masterful rational choice. For diesel engines, the 2.0 TDI (150 or 190 hp) is the engine to absolutely prioritize for its endurance and highway fuel efficiency. However, you must buy with full knowledge of the facts: the water pump is the Achilles' heel of this engine (EA288). Plan a budget to anticipate the timing belt change if it hasn't been done. Avoid the 1.6 TDI, which is undersized and coupled with the fragile DQ200 gearbox. If you drive less than 20,000 km/year, the gasoline engines (1.4 TSI or 2.0 TSI) offer formidable reliability and are excellent alternatives. The iV (PHEV) version is relevant only if you can charge at home every day.
