Jeep Wrangler

IV (JL) · 2018 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

Summary

The fourth-generation Jeep Wrangler (code name **JL**) marks a major evolution for the off-road icon. While retaining its rustic architecture (ladder frame, rigid axles) essential for its extraordinary off-road capabilities, it takes a giant leap forward in terms of finish quality, ride comfort, and on-board technology (Uconnect system).

This generation introduces modern engines, notably a 4-cylinder turbo, a plug-in hybrid version (4xe) highly prized in Europe for tax reasons, and a monstrous V8 (392) in North America.

**Diesel Focus:** The Wrangler JL offered two distinct diesel options depending on the market: the **2.2 Multijet (CRD)** in Europe, robust but penalized by the eco-tax, and the **3.0 V6 EcoDiesel** in North America, torquey but struck by serious injection pump problems. A facelift occurred in 2024, bringing a new grille, a 12.3-inch touchscreen, and standard curtain airbags.

**Overall design rating:** A unique vehicle on the market, endearing but whose reliability is marred by temperamental peripherals (electronics, hybridization) and massive recall campaigns.
