Summary

The Citroën C5 X, launched in 2022, marks the return of the chevron brand to the executive car segment, but with a resolutely modern and transversal approach. Positioned between a sedan, a wagon, and an SUV, it embodies the 'crossover' concept with an elegant and raised silhouette. Based on Stellantis' EMP2 V3 platform, it emphasizes comfort, a Citroën signature, thanks in particular to its Advanced Comfort suspension and Advanced Comfort seats. The C5 X is offered with petrol and plug-in hybrid powertrains, reflecting current market trends. It is produced in China for the global market, including Europe. This first generation has not yet undergone a major facelift to date. Overall, the C5 X is a vehicle that prioritizes onboard well-being and stylistic originality, but its long-term reliability is still being evaluated given its recent introduction.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional suspension and seat comfort (Advanced Comfort)
  • Original and distinctive design, unique market positioning
  • Generous interior space and large boot volume
  • Improved interior finish quality compared to previous Citroën models
  • Efficient and economical plug-in hybrid powertrains (if recharged)
  • Modern technological equipment (large screen, head-up display)

Weaknesses 6

  • Limited rear visibility due to the sloped rear window
  • Multimedia interface sometimes imperfect (initial bugs, ergonomics)
  • High fuel consumption for non-PHEV petrol versions on highways
  • High purchase price, especially for plug-in hybrid versions
  • Long-term reliability of PureTech 1.2 engines (despite improvements) and PHEV systems still to be confirmed
  • Noticeable body roll in dynamic driving despite comfort

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for the 1.2 PureTech)
  • Condition of the timing belt on the 1.2 PureTech (visual inspection)
  • Absence of abnormal engine noises (knocking, whistling)
  • Functioning of all electronic equipment and the multimedia system
  • For PHEVs: battery charge status, actual electric range, functioning of the onboard charger
  • Check if all manufacturer recalls have been carried out

Verdict

The Citroën C5 X is a vehicle distinguished by its exceptional comfort and bold design. For such a recent model, long-term reliability data is still limited, but we can rely on the history of its powertrains and the EMP2 platform. The plug-in hybrid (PHEV) versions are the most relevant for mixed use, provided they can be recharged regularly to maximize fuel savings and minimize environmental impact. The PureTech 180 engine is a good compromise for those who cannot recharge, offering good performance and generally better reliability than the 1.2 PureTech. The latter, although improved, remains the most delicate point in the range due to its history. Purchasing a C5 X is recommended for those who prioritize comfort and originality, provided they thoroughly check the maintenance history and the application of manufacturer recalls. Price negotiation is always welcome, especially for non-PHEV petrol versions, which may depreciate more quickly.
