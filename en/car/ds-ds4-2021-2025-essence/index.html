Summary

The second-generation DS4, launched in 2021, marks a turning point for the French premium brand. Based on Stellantis' EMP2 V3 platform, it positions itself in the premium compact segment, with a distinctive silhouette halfway between a sedan and an SUV coupé. Its bold design, refined and technological interior, and ride comfort (especially with the DS Active Scan Suspension on some versions) make it a unique proposition against German benchmarks. It aims to attract customers looking for originality and French refinement. The DS4 II is a well-designed car, offering a pleasant driving experience and a high level of finish. Its general reliability is good for a recent model, but some points of attention related to Stellantis engines should be monitored.

Strengths 6

  • Bold and distinctive exterior design
  • Very refined interior, quality materials and premium finish
  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with DS Active Scan Suspension)
  • Advanced onboard technology (infotainment, driving aids)
  • Smooth and efficient EAT8 automatic gearbox
  • Good sound insulation

Weaknesses 5

  • Limited rear visibility due to coupé design
  • Infotainment ergonomics require an adaptation period
  • High new car purchase price
  • Rear passenger space just adequate for tall occupants
  • Average boot volume for the category

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and supporting invoices
  • Condition of the timing belt and quality of engine oil (PureTech 1.2)
  • Operation of the AdBlue system (BlueHDi): absence of warning light, level, history of failures
  • Condition and operation of the hybrid system (E-Tense): battery capacity, charging time, absence of warning light
  • Operation of all electronic equipment (screens, driving aids, air conditioning)
  • Tire and brake wear, wheel alignment

Official recalls

Official Recalls (DS4 II - 2021-2025)

  • 2022-03-01 : Subject : Risk of fuel leak. Affected Population : Vehicles produced between 26/08/2021 and 06/10/2021. Action : Inspection and potential replacement of the fuel hose.
  • 2022-06-01 : Subject : Risk of loosening of the rear suspension arm mounting bolts. Affected Population : Vehicles produced between 26/08/2021 and 21/01/2022. Action : Inspection and retightening or replacement of the bolts.
  • 2023-01-01 : Subject : Potential problem with engine management software that could lead to a loss of power. Affected Population : Vehicles produced between 01/09/2021 and 30/06/2022 (mainly PureTech and E-Tense). Action : Update of the engine management software.
  • 2023-09-01 : Subject : Risk of non-compliance of the Automatic Emergency Braking (AEB) system. Affected Population : Vehicles produced between 01/03/2022 and 31/07/2023. Action : Software update of the AEB module.

Verdict

The second-generation DS4 is a bold and attractive proposition in the premium compact market. Its comfort, design, and interior quality are real assets. For a confident purchase, favor the PureTech 180 or 225 engines, which offer an excellent compromise between performance and reliability, provided maintenance is respected. The PureTech 130 is an economical option but requires increased vigilance regarding the timing belt. The BlueHDi 130 is ideal for high-mileage drivers, but the AdBlue system remains a potential weak point. The E-Tense 225 is an excellent choice for those who can charge regularly and enjoy the benefits of the plug-in hybrid. Whatever the choice, a clear maintenance history is essential. Negotiate the price if the history is incomplete or if the vehicle shows signs of premature wear on known weak points. It's a car to buy for its character and refinement, with full awareness of its engine specificities.
