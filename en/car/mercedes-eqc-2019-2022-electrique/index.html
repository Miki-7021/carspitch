Mercedes EQC

N293 · 2019 – 2022

NEGOTIATE Reliability 76/100 — bon

Summary

The Mercedes-Benz EQC (N293 generation) marks the entry of the star brand into the 100% electric era under the 'EQ' banner. Based on a modified GLC platform (adapted MRA thermal architecture), it is positioned as a premium family SUV. While it shines with its imperial ride comfort, top-flight soundproofing, and luxurious finish, it suffers from the compromises linked to its non-dedicated platform: excessive weight (nearly 2.5 tonnes), average roominess for its size, and lower energy efficiency compared to competitors designed from the outset as electric vehicles. Its production was relatively short, with Mercedes quickly redirecting its strategy towards 100% electric platforms (EVA2). Overall rating: An excellent luxurious 'cruiser', but a first-generation electric vehicle with limited range performance.

Strengths 5

  • Remarkable suspension comfort (especially with rear pneumatic damping)
  • Cabin soundproofing worthy of an S-Class
  • Typically Mercedes finish and assembly quality
  • First-rate performance (0 to 100 km/h in 5.1 s for the 400)
  • Intuitive MBUX multimedia system with an efficient electric route planner

Weaknesses 6

  • High electrical consumption (often > 23 kWh/100 km in mixed use)
  • Disappointing real-world highway range (difficult to exceed 250-280 km)
  • Very high weight (2,495 kg) penalizing agility and consumable wear
  • DC fast charging speed limited to 110 kW (surpassed by the competition)
  • Average trunk volume (500 L) and rear roominess for a 4.76 m SUV
  • No front trunk (Frunk) due to the modified thermal architecture

Check before buying

  • Recall history: Crucial, especially for the high-voltage battery casing corrosion issue.
  • Battery State of Health (SOH): To be certified by a Mercedes diagnostic before purchase.
  • Tire and brake wear: The 2.5-tonne weight puts enormous strain on the running gear.
  • MBUX system operation: Check for the absence of unexpected restarts or sluggishness (often requires a software update).
  • Charging cables: Ensure the presence and good condition of the T2 cables and the home charger.

Practicality

  • Premium Build Quality — The EQC embodies Mercedes-Benz excellence with impeccable finish and assembly quality, offering a luxurious and durable interior that stands the test of time.
  • Intuitive MBUX Technology — The MBUX multimedia system offers an intuitive interface and an efficient electric route planner, making every journey simpler and more enjoyable with advanced connected features.

Performance

  • Dynamic Electric Performance — Experience exhilarating acceleration and abundant power, with 0 to 100 km/h achieved in just 5.1 seconds for the 400 4MATIC version, offering a responsive and thrilling drive.

Safety

  • Benchmark Passive Safety — The Mercedes EQC achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with exceptional scores of 96% for adult protection and 90% for child protection, ensuring maximum peace of mind.

Braking

  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Despite its weight, the EQC offers strong and reassuring braking performance, with excellent pedal feel and effective modulation for controlled and safe stops in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Stable and Serene Road Holding — Enjoy road behavior focused on comfort and safety, with stable and predictable road holding, good grip, and well-controlled body roll, for a serene and secure drive.

Alternatives

- **Europe / North Amer.**: Audi e-tron (now Q8 e-tron, similar comfort but more spacious), BMW iX3 (more efficient, RWD), Jaguar I-Pace (more dynamic), Tesla Model Y (significantly more efficient, Supercharger network, but less luxurious). - **Asia / China**: Nio ES6, BMW iX3, Audi e-tron.

Verdict

The Mercedes EQC is a transitional vehicle. It offers a luxurious, cushioned, and extremely quiet driving experience, true to the brand's DNA. However, its shortcomings as an electric vehicle (high consumption, slow 110 kW DC charging, limited highway range) make it less versatile than its more recent competitors. On the used market, it suffers from heavy depreciation, making it an excellent deal for a buyer looking for an ultra-comfortable premium SUV for daily or regional trips, provided they can charge at home. **Absolute caution**: verify that the recall concerning the battery casing corrosion (January 2022) has been carried out, as water infiltration into the high-voltage battery is catastrophic.
