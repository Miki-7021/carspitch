Citroën C3 Aircross

I facelift · 2021 – 2024

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The **Citroën C3 Aircross I Phase 2 (Facelifted)**, launched in 2021, modernized the front end of the urban SUV with chevrons to give it a more robust and stately appearance. Based on the PF1 platform (shared with the former Peugeot 2008 and Opel Crossland), it stands out for its exceptional ride comfort, the adoption of *Advanced Comfort* seats, and a modularity rare in its category (sliding rear bench seat). Although this report focuses on **diesel (1.5 BlueHDi)** engines, particularly suited for high-mileage drivers thanks to their remarkable fuel efficiency, the model was also widely available with petrol engines (1.2 PureTech). Overall reliability is unfortunately marred by well-known design flaws in Stellantis (formerly PSA) group engines from that era.

Strengths 5

  • Very high level of suspension comfort (typical Citroën)
  • Soft and welcoming Advanced Comfort seats
  • Excellent modularity (2-part sliding rear bench, passenger seat folds flat)
  • Remarkable fuel efficiency of the 1.5 BlueHDi engine (often under 5 L/100 km on the road)
  • Generous headroom and feeling of spaciousness inside

Weaknesses 5

  • Very erratic reliability of Stellantis engines (PureTech and BlueHDi pre-2023)
  • Slow infotainment system and aging interface
  • Clumsy road handling (pronounced body roll in corners)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind noise)
  • Notchy manual gearbox with long throws

Check before buying

  • **Service history:** Demand a 100% up-to-date service book from the manufacturer's network (essential for any out-of-warranty coverage).
  • **Engine noise (1.5 BlueHDi):** Start cold with the hood open. A metallic/rattling noise from the top of the engine indicates wear of the camshaft chain (very costly repair).
  • **Dashboard warning lights (1.5 BlueHDi):** Look for the 'UREA' or 'Engine fault' light. An AdBlue tank replacement costs approximately 1000 to 1200 €.
  • **Belt condition (1.2 PureTech):** If you opt for petrol, visually inspect the belt through the oil filler opening (cracks, fraying).
  • **Onboard electronics:** Test the touchscreen, GPS, and Bluetooth/CarPlay connectivity, which may experience bugs.

Verdict

The facelifted Citroën C3 Aircross is an urban SUV packed with family qualities: it is ultra-comfortable, modular, and very economical at the pump in its diesel version. However, purchasing a **1.5 BlueHDi** version requires great caution. If you are looking at a 2021 or 2022 model, ensure that the timing kit (including the famous 8 mm camshaft chain) has already been replaced as a precaution, or negotiate its replacement. Ideally, prioritize a model produced from spring 2023, which comes natively equipped with the reinforced chain. The use of an AdBlue additive is strongly recommended to preserve the emissions control system.
