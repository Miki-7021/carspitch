Renault Kangoo

III (JK) · 2021 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The third-generation Renault Kangoo (often internally designated KFK for the leisure activity vehicle version and XFK for the utility vehicle) marks a spectacular break from the Kangoo II (JK). Launched in 2021, it sheds its 'van' appearance to adopt the features of a true compact minivan. Based on the CMF-C platform (shared with the Nissan Townstar and Mercedes Citan/T-Class), it offers transformed road handling, careful sound insulation, and latest-generation driving aids.

The utility version (Van) stands out with the 'Open Sesame' innovation (removal of the right-side B-pillar offering a 1.45 m opening). The leisure activity vehicle version (Combospace) targets families shunning SUVs or those disappointed by the discontinuation of thermal engines by its direct competitors (Stellantis).

**Overall generation rating:** An undeniable qualitative leap forward. The Kangoo III has become the benchmark in its segment, particularly with the 1.3 TCe petrol engine, which benefits from the excellent reliability of this block co-developed with Daimler.

Strengths 6

  • Remarkable reliability of the 1.3 TCe petrol engine (H5Ht)
  • Road handling worthy of a compact sedan (CMF-C platform)
  • Giant interior space and well-thought-out modularity
  • Significantly improved sound insulation compared to the previous generation
  • Smooth and responsive EDC7 automatic gearbox (on 1.3 TCe 130 and 1.5 dCi 95/115)
  • 'Open Sesame' innovation (on utility versions)

Weaknesses 5

  • 1.3 TCe fuel consumption that quickly increases on the highway or under heavy load (due to its boxy aerodynamics)
  • Significantly higher purchase price compared to the Kangoo II
  • High weight (over 1.5 tons unladen) penalizing the 1.3 TCe 100hp
  • Easy Link multimedia system sometimes slow to start
  • Chronic fragility of the AdBlue system on Diesel versions

Check before buying

  • History of software updates (EDC gearbox and multimedia system)
  • On Diesel versions: absence of 'Check Anti-pollution System' warning light (sign of an AdBlue/DPF issue)
  • Condition of underbody and shock absorbers (vehicles that may have been heavily loaded)
  • Alignment of sliding doors and operation of latches (sometimes temperamental in cold weather)
  • Front tire wear (the front axle tends to suffer from weight and torque)

Alternatives

Direct Competitors by Market

* **Europe :** * *Volkswagen Caddy IV / Ford Tourneo Connect :* The most serious thermal competitors. Slightly superior finish, but the 1.5 TSI is comparable to the 1.3 TCe in terms of driving pleasure. * *Peugeot Rifter / Citroën Berlingo / Opel Combo :* Stellantis made the mistake of switching these models to 100% electric (before recently reversing course). Their thermal engines (1.2 PureTech and 1.5 BlueHDi) suffer from serious reliability issues (wet belt, camshaft chain), which gives the **Kangoo 1.3 TCe an overwhelming advantage**. * **Japan / Asia :** The Kangoo enjoys cult status there. Its alternatives are local minivans (Toyota Sienta, Honda Freed), which are more compact but less 'European' in style. * **Americas / Emerging Markets :** Fiat Fiorino / Ram ProMaster Rapid, Peugeot Partner (older generations). The Kangoo III is positioned as more premium there.

Verdict

The Renault Kangoo III is undoubtedly one of the best leisure activity vehicles/utility vehicles of its generation. By targeting the **1.3 TCe petrol engine (ideally the 130 hp with the EDC gearbox)**, you choose peace of mind. This engine has proven itself, is quiet, powerful, and free from the flaws of its predecessor (the 1.2 TCe).

Against competition from the Stellantis group (Berlingo/Rifter) plagued by the disastrous reliability of the 1.2 PureTech, the Kangoo 1.3 TCe stands out as the rational and safe choice par excellence. Diesel versions (Blue dCi) remain relevant for very high-mileage drivers but require increased vigilance regarding the AdBlue system. For a recent used model, prioritize one where recalls (especially the starter harness) have been carried out.
