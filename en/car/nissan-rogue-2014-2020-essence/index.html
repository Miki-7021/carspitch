Summary

The second-generation Nissan Rogue (code T32) is a compact SUV that has enjoyed immense commercial success, becoming the brand's best-seller in North America. It is the technical twin of the Nissan X-Trail sold in Europe and Asia. While it shines with its interior space, comfort, practicality (7-seat option on certain years), and safety technologies, its reliability record is heavily marred by its continuously variable transmission (CVT) supplied by Jatco. A facelift was introduced for the 2017 model year, bringing aesthetic improvements, better soundproofing, and the introduction of a hybrid version (withdrawn in 2019).

Strengths 5

  • Intrinsically very robust 2.5L (QR25DE) engine block
  • Generous interior space and trunk volume
  • Excellent suspension comfort and 'Zero Gravity' seats
  • Controlled fuel consumption for the category
  • Active safety equipment (ProPilot on high trims post-2017)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the Jatco CVT gearbox (especially 2014-2016)
  • Frequent failures of the air conditioning compressor (2014-2016)
  • Possible water leaks at the floor level (wiring harness corrosion)
  • Quality of some interior plastics and soundproofing (pre-2017)
  • Sluggish road handling, lack of dynamism

Check before buying

  • CVT gearbox maintenance history (regular oil changes or replacement performed)
  • Gearbox behavior during the test drive (no jerking, whining, or hesitation tolerated)
  • Air conditioning operation (very cold air, no metallic noise from the compressor)
  • Moisture under the floor mats (driver and passenger side)
  • Verification that all safety recalls (very numerous) have been performed

Official recalls

The T32 Rogue has been the subject of numerous recalls (NHTSA). Here are the main ones: - February 2023 (23V093000): The 'jackknife' style ignition key can fold while driving, shutting off the engine (2014-2020 models). - November 2022 & January 2022 (22V875000 / 22V024000): Water leak at the driver's floor level that can corrode the dashboard wiring harness (2014-2017 models). - October 2021 (21V839000): On Hybrid versions (2017-2019), the ECM bracket can damage the engine wiring harness and blow a fuse. - September 2019 (19V654000): Failure of the rearview camera display. - October & November 2017 (17V716000 / 17V663000): Poor welds on the front and rear seat frames (2016-2017 models). - April & December 2016 (16V911000 / 16V244000): Defect in the Occupant Classification System (OCS) sensor preventing the proper deployment of the passenger airbag. - April 2016 (16V219000): Corrosion and possible failure of the rear tailgate struts (2014-2016 models). - April 2015 & March 2016 (15V197000 / 16V149000): Poor nickel plating in the fuel pump leading to engine failure (2014 models). - November 2015 (15V775000): Problem with the gear shift knob allowing it to be shifted out of 'Park' without braking. - January 2015 (15V012000): Defect in the ABS braking system. - May 2014 (14V229000): Lug nuts potentially improperly tightened at the factory. - April 2014 (14V218000): Incorrect bolt used on the steering column.

Alternatives

  • North America: Toyota RAV4 (benchmark for reliability), Honda CR-V (very spacious, watch out for the 1.5T), Mazda CX-5 (more dynamic, very reliable conventional automatic gearbox), Subaru Forester.
  • Europe (against the X-Trail): Peugeot 5008 (watch out for the 1.2 PureTech), Skoda Kodiaq, Volkswagen Tiguan Allspace, Hyundai Santa Fe.

Verdict

The Nissan Rogue T32 is a paradoxical vehicle. Its 2.5L engine is a model of longevity, and its family qualities are undeniable. However, the Jatco CVT transmission is a real sword of Damocles that drags down its reliability record, particularly on the 2014 to 2016 years (which also suffer from air conditioning problems). If you are considering a purchase, absolutely favor a facelifted model (2017 and newer), demand a clear history of CVT gearbox oil changes, or better yet, look for an example whose gearbox has already been replaced under warranty. Without these precautions, the financial risk is too high.
