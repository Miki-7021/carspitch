Toyota Auris

II (E180) · 2012 – 2018

BUY Reliability 90/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The Toyota Auris II (E180) marks a turning point for the Japanese manufacturer in the European C-segment. Gone is the consensual design of the first generation; the Auris II adopts sharper lines and more dynamic road behavior.

The real strength of this model is the popularization of the hybrid powertrain (1.8 HSD), which accounted for over 60% of sales in Europe. This technology, inherited from the Prius 3, offers exceptional reliability thanks to the absence of traditional wear parts (clutch, alternator, starter, timing belt).

A major facelift (Phase 2) occurred in 2015, bringing a modernized dashboard, improved soundproofing, new suspension settings, and the introduction of new powertrains (1.2T gasoline and 1.6 D-4D of BMW origin). Available as a 5-door hatchback and a wagon (Touring Sports), the Auris II is today one of the most sought-after used compacts for its peace of mind, especially in its hybrid version.

Expert's overall rating: An absolute benchmark in reliability, especially in hybrid.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of the hybrid powertrain (1.8 HSD)
  • Very low maintenance cost for the hybrid (no clutch, no belt, minimal brake wear)
  • Real-world fuel consumption in urban and suburban areas (4.5L to 5L/100km in HSD)
  • Very spacious wagon version (Touring Sports) without sacrificing aesthetics
  • Significantly improved finish quality and soundproofing on Phase 2 (post-2015)

Weaknesses 5

  • Infotainment system (Touch & Go) slow, dated, and without Apple CarPlay / Android Auto
  • Engine revving (rubber band effect) during strong accelerations on the hybrid (e-CVT gearbox)
  • Average rear passenger space for the category
  • Somewhat basic interior plastic quality on Phase 1 (2012-2015)
  • Diesel powertrains' enjoyment and reliability are inferior compared to competitors

Check before buying

  • For the hybrid: Demand the certificate of the last 'Hybrid Health Check' (guarantees the HV battery).
  • For the hybrid: Check the condition of the 12V battery (often the cause of starting failures, not the HV battery).
  • For the hybrid: Ensure that the battery's ventilation grille (rear passenger side) is not obstructed by pet hair or dust.
  • For diesels: Strict maintenance history, check for absence of DPF/EGR warning light and black smoke.
  • For the 1.6 D-4D: Listen carefully on cold start to detect a metallic noise (timing chain).

Official recalls

  • October 2018 (Hybrid): Reprogramming of the hybrid system's ECU. A software anomaly could lead to a loss of power or system shutdown while driving (models produced between May 2014 and November 2015).
  • February 2018 (Airbags): Replacement of the passenger airbag inflator (Takata scandal) on models produced between 2012 and 2014.
  • July 2016 (Fuel): Replacement of the fuel vapor canister (activated carbon filter). Risk of cracking leading to a gasoline smell or a slight leak (models produced between April 2015 and May 2016).
  • November 2013 (Lighting): Replacement of halogen headlight bulbs (HIR2) that could overheat and damage the reflector.

What sets it apart 14

reliability

  • Legendary Reliability and Low Maintenance Costs — The 1.8 HSD hybrid powertrain of the Toyota Auris II is renowned for its exceptional reliability, regularly ranked in the Top 10 by the TÜV Report and praised by ADAC. Its simplified architecture (no clutch, no timing belt) ensures very low maintenance costs and unparalleled peace of mind.

economy

  • Ultra-Economical Fuel Consumption — Thanks to its advanced hybrid technology, the Auris II HSD boasts impressive real-world fuel consumption, especially in urban and suburban areas, averaging 4.5L to 5L/100km. This efficiency translates into significant daily savings.

safety

  • Maximum Safety with 5-Star Euro NCAP Rating — The Toyota Auris II achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with high scores for adult (92%) and child (84%) protection. A guarantee of serenity for all occupants.

handling

  • Safe Road Handling and Optimized Comfort — The Auris II's road handling is safe and predictable, prioritizing comfort and stability. Its excellent road holding offers a serene and pleasant driving experience, ideal for daily commutes as well as long journeys.

braking

  • Efficient and Stable Braking — The Auris II's braking system is recognized for its efficiency and stability. It offers good progression and competitive stopping distances, ensuring adequate safety in all driving situations.

comfort

  • Improved Finish Quality and Soundproofing — The restyled version (Phase 2, post-2015) of the Auris II benefits from a significant improvement in interior finish quality and enhanced soundproofing, offering a more refined cabin and superior acoustic comfort for a more pleasant driving experience.

practicality

  • Highly Practical Touring Sports Wagon Version — For those seeking more space, the Touring Sports wagon version of the Auris II offers generous loading capacity without compromising aesthetics. A major asset for families or transport needs.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Volkswagen Golf VII (segment benchmark, available as GTE plug-in hybrid), Peugeot 308 II (better dynamics, but less reliable PureTech/BlueHDi engines), Honda Civic (excellent reliability, sharp gasoline engines), Kia Cee'd / Hyundai i30.
  • North America (vs. Scion iM): Mazda 3 Hatchback (excellent dynamics and reliability), Honda Civic Hatchback, Subaru Impreza.
  • Japan / Asia: Nissan Note (e-Power), Mazda Axela (Mazda 3), Subaru Impreza.

Verdict

The Toyota Auris II is an extremely rational and reassuring purchase choice, provided you target the 1.8 Hybrid (HSD) powertrain. It is in this configuration that it reveals its full potential: smooth driving, fuel savings, and almost unrivaled reliability in the used car market. Gasoline versions (1.2T and naturally aspirated) are very good alternatives for smaller budgets. However, diesel versions (D-4D) offer little interest compared to the hybrid, especially as they suffer from the usual ailments of this type of powertrain (DPF, EGR, chain on the 1.6). Opt for a Phase 2 model (after 2015) to benefit from a more upscale interior and better soundproofing.
