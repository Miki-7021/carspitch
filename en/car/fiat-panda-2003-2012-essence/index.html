Fiat Panda

II (169) · 2003 – 2012

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The Fiat Panda II (codename 169) is an A-segment city car that brilliantly succeeded the iconic first generation. Voted Car of the Year in 2004, it stands out for its tall architecture (like a mini-MPV), offering excellent spaciousness for its reduced size (3.54 m). Designed to be economical to buy and to run, it relies on proven technical solutions, notably the indestructible FIRE family of gasoline engines. While its interior finish remains basic with hard plastics, its overall mechanical reliability (for gasoline models) is excellent. The range was crowned by formidable 4x4 versions for mountain driving and a lively 100HP sports version. A slight facelift occurred in late 2009 (new mirrors, modified grille, Euro 5 compliance for some engines).

**Overall reliability rating: Very good (for gasoline models).**

Weaknesses 5

  • Chronic fragility of the electric power steering (Dualdrive)
  • Susceptibility to corrosion (exhaust muffler, engine oil pan)
  • Very light sound insulation on the highway
  • Quality of interior plastics and rapid wear of seat fabrics
  • 1.3 MultiJet diesel engine unsuitable for short urban trips (clogging)

Check before buying

  • **Power steering:** Check for any hard spots, steering wheel trembling, or the illumination of the red warning light (steering wheel) on the dashboard. A complete column is expensive.
  • **Underbody:** Inspect the engine oil pan (very exposed to perforating rust) and the rear exhaust muffler.
  • **Suspension:** Listen for dull noises over speed bumps (wear of front wishbone bushings, shock absorber mounts, disintegrating rear bump stops).
  • **Engine (Gasoline):** Check for oil traces around the cylinder head cover (minor but common) and the timing belt history.
  • **Transmission (4x4):** Check the engagement of the rear axle (viscous coupling on early models, then electro-hydraulic) and the absence of rolling noises.

Verdict

The Fiat Panda II is a true automotive 'workhorse': pragmatic, economical, and remarkably reliable if you choose the right engine. **The best choice is the 1.2 gasoline engine (60 or 69 hp)**, which offers the best compromise for city and suburban driving, with almost infallible mechanical reliability (FIRE engine). The 1.1 is robust but a bit underpowered outside of cities, while the 1.4 100HP is a very endearing future collector's item.

**To avoid:** The 1.3 MultiJet diesel, unless you have proof of meticulous maintenance (very regular oil changes) and you don't drive in the city, otherwise you risk hefty bills (timing chain, DPF, EGR).

When buying, the number one point of attention should be the **electric power steering**, whose failure is the model's Achilles' heel. A healthy gasoline Panda with functional steering is a highly recommended purchase.
