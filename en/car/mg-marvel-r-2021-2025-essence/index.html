Summary

MG Marvel R (Generation I)

The MG Marvel R is a D-segment family SUV, positioned as the technological and premium flagship of the MG brand (SAIC Motor group) in Europe at its launch. Based on the Chinese Roewe Marvel X, it stands out with a refined design, a very well-finished cabin with a huge 19.4-inch touchscreen, and a plethora of standard equipment.

**⚠️ EXPERT CLARIFICATION:** Contrary to the request targeting a "petrol" engine, **the MG Marvel R is an exclusively 100% electric model (BEV)**. It has never been produced with a combustion engine (neither petrol, nor diesel, nor hybrid). If you are looking for an MG SUV with a petrol engine, you should look at the **MG HS / EHS** (equipped with the 1.5 T-GDI). This sheet will therefore exhaustively cover the actual electric powertrains of the Marvel R.

Despite its comfort and finish qualities, the Marvel R suffers from an older-generation electrical design: its DC fast charging power peaks at around 92-94 kW, which is low compared to the competition (Tesla Model Y, Hyundai Ioniq 5). Its infotainment system (MG iSMART) is also known for its sluggishness.

**Overall expert rating: 7/10**
