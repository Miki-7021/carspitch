Summary

The Mercedes-Benz CLA (C117 generation) is a compact '4-door coupé' based on the MFA platform of the A-Class (W176). Launched in 2013, it stands out with its spectacular design, record aerodynamics (Cd of 0.22), and frameless doors. A shooting brake variant (Shooting Brake - X117) was introduced in 2015. The model received a facelift in mid-2016, bringing aesthetic touches (LED headlights), an infotainment system update (smartphone integration), and, most importantly, an improvement in interior assembly quality, a weak point of the early model years. Although very appealing, the CLA sacrifices rear passenger space and suspension comfort for the sake of style. In terms of reliability, the gasoline engines (M270 family) are generally robust, provided they receive rigorous maintenance, particularly concerning the 7G-DCT dual-clutch gearbox.

Strengths 5

  • Spectacular and timeless exterior design
  • Exceptional aerodynamics (quiet on highways, reduced fuel consumption)
  • Very reliable and powerful M270 gasoline engines (1.6 and 2.0)
  • Secure road holding and dynamic behavior
  • Aesthetically very successful and more practical Shooting Brake version

Weaknesses 5

  • Very limited rear passenger space (critical headroom for adults)
  • Firm suspension comfort (especially with 18/19-inch wheels and sport chassis)
  • Plastic quality and parasitic noises on pre-facelift models (2013-2015)
  • Poor rear visibility (reversing camera essential)
  • 7G-DCT gearbox management sometimes hesitant at low speeds

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history of the 7G-DCT automatic gearbox (oil change invoice mandatory).
  • Absence of metallic noises on cold start (sign of timing chain wear).
  • Condition of window seals and proper functioning of automatic lowering/raising upon door opening.
  • Traces of coolant leaks around the water housing/thermostat (gasoline engines).
  • Wear of front tires and alignment (tendency to wear the inside of the tires).

Official recalls

  • Airbags (2019-2021): Massive recall related to the Takata scandal (risk of metallic fragment projection upon deployment).
  • Steering Column (2017): Risk of inadvertent driver airbag deployment due to a rupture of the contact spiral (insufficient grounding).
  • Starter (2017): Risk of fire if the engine is blocked and the driver repeatedly attempts to start it (installation of an additional fuse).
  • Brake Booster (2016): On some 2.0L models (CLA 250), the brake booster vacuum line could rupture, reducing braking assistance.
  • Camshaft (2015): On some 2014-2015 models, defective welding on the camshaft could lead to engine failure.

Performance

  • High-performance and proven robust M270 petrol engines — The 1.6L and 2.0L Turbo (M270) petrol engines offer an excellent balance of power, flexibility, and driving pleasure, ensuring dynamic performance and high reliability for serene journeys.

Safety

  • High-level passive safety — The CLA C117 achieved excellent results in Euro NCAP tests, ensuring optimal protection for occupants thanks to a robust structure and effective restraint systems.

Braking

  • Powerful, enduring, and reassuring braking — Equipped with a high-performance braking system, the CLA offers short stopping distances and excellent endurance, providing a sense of safety and control in all circumstances.

tenue de route

  • Dynamic and secure road handling — Its balanced chassis, precise steering, and great stability at high speeds guarantee effective and predictable road holding, offering driving pleasure and a sense of security.

Comfort

  • Record-breaking aerodynamics for remarkable silence and efficiency — With an exceptional drag coefficient (Cx of 0.22), the CLA minimizes wind noise on the highway and contributes to optimized fuel consumption, enhancing comfort and economy.
  • Refined and improved interior finish (post-2016 models) — Restyled models (from mid-2016 onwards) benefit from superior assembly quality, more pleasant materials, and a modernized infotainment system, elevating the onboard experience.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Audi A3 Sedan (superior trim, more spacious), BMW 2 Series Gran Coupé (appeared later, sportier handling), Volkswagen Arteon (larger but similar 4-door coupé spirit).
  • North America: Audi A3 Sedan, Acura ILX (less premium but very reliable), Cadillac ATS.
  • Asia: Lexus IS (RWD, excellent reliability), Audi A3 Sedan.

Verdict

The Mercedes CLA C117 is an 'emotional purchase'. If you prioritize style over practicality, it's an excellent choice. The gasoline engines (CLA 180, 200, 250) are to be preferred for their overall high reliability and their suitability for the vehicle's premium character. Absolutely prioritize a facelifted model (from mid-2016 onwards) to benefit from an interior finish worthy of the star and a better-managed DCT gearbox. Avoid examples with a non-existent or unclear automatic gearbox oil change history.
