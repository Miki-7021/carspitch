Mitsubishi Outlander

II (CW0) (2006–2012) · 2006 – 2012

NEGOTIATE Reliability 75/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation Mitsubishi Outlander (chassis code CW0) was launched in 2006 and marked a significant evolution from its predecessor. Positioned in the compact to mid-size SUV segment, it shared its GS platform with other group models (Lancer, ASX) as well as with its French cousins, the Peugeot 4007 and Citroën C-Crosser, of which it is the technical basis.

Recognizable by its more modern and dynamic design, it benefited from a major facelift in 2010, adopting the 'Jet Fighter' grille inspired by the Lancer Evolution X, as well as interior improvements and new engines. The Outlander II stands out for its versatility, often offering a 5+2 seat configuration, a high-performance all-wheel drive (AWC - All-Wheel Control), and a good compromise between ride comfort and light off-road capabilities. Its reliability is generally good, especially with gasoline engines, but some diesel versions have experienced notable problems.

Strengths 6

  • Reliability of gasoline engines (MIVEC)
  • Efficient and versatile AWC all-wheel drive
  • Spaciousness and modularity (7-seat option)
  • Sound and comfortable road handling
  • Good towing capacity (especially V6 and diesels)
  • Good equipment/price ratio on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Very problematic reliability of the 2.0 DI-D (VW) engine
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speed
  • Somewhat dated interior finish and hard plastics before the facelift
  • High fuel consumption of V6 gasoline versions
  • Maintenance cost of diesels (DPF, EGR)

Check before buying

  • Complete service history, especially for diesels
  • Condition of the DPF and EGR valve on diesels (warning lights on, smoke)
  • Abnormal engine noises (especially 2.0 DI-D VW for the cylinder head, or knocking)
  • Functioning of the all-wheel drive (AWC) and rear differential
  • Condition of the running gear (bushings, shock absorbers) and brakes
  • Functioning of the air conditioning and onboard electronics
  • Presence of unperformed manufacturer recalls (airbags, wipers, etc.)

Verdict

The Mitsubishi Outlander II is a versatile and generally reliable SUV, **provided you choose the right engine**. The **gasoline versions (2.0, 2.4, 3.0 V6 MIVEC) are the most recommendable** for their robustness and low preventive maintenance costs. They offer excellent durability and a certain driving pleasure, even if the V6s are thirsty.

In contrast, the **2.0 DI-D of Volkswagen origin is to be absolutely avoided** due to its serious and costly structural problems. The 2.2 DI-D diesels (PSA and Mitsubishi) are more acceptable choices, but require rigorous DPF and EGR maintenance.

For family and versatile use, the gasoline Outlander II represents an excellent used car deal. If a diesel is imperative, prioritize the 2.2 DI-D Mitsubishi (after 2010) or the 2.2 DI-D PSA with a clear maintenance history. Scrupulously check the recall history, especially for Takata airbags. A well-maintained example, especially a gasoline one, will offer many years of reliable service.
