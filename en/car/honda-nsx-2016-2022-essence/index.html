Summary

The second-generation Honda NSX (code name NC1), marketed under the Acura brand in North America and China, marks the return of the legendary Japanese supercar. Unlike its purely combustion predecessor, the NC1 is an exclusively hybrid (HEV) technological showcase. It combines a mid-mounted 3.5L twin-turbo V6 with three electric motors (*Sport Hybrid SH-AWD* system), offering a particularly sophisticated torque-vectoring all-wheel drive. Hand-assembled at the *Performance Manufacturing Center* (PMC) in Ohio (USA), it stands out for its exceptional versatility, capable of being a 'daily supercar' while offering very high-level performance. In 2019, it received chassis improvements (firmer anti-roll bars, software recalibration), before bowing out in 2022 with the exclusive Type S version (limited to 350 units worldwide).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability for a supercar of this performance level
  • Ease of daily driving (visibility, suspension comfort in Quiet/Sport mode)
  • Diabolical traction and agility thanks to the SH-AWD system's Torque Vectoring
  • Assembly and finish quality (Performance Manufacturing Center)
  • Exclusivity and rarity on European roads

Weaknesses 5

  • Dated infotainment system (derived from contemporary mass-produced Honda/Acura models)
  • V6 sound a bit muffled by the turbos compared to competing V10/V8s
  • High weight (approx. 1,760 kg) felt on technical tracks
  • Ridiculous trunk volume (110 liters) and subject to engine heat
  • Very limited service network (only a few certified NSX dealerships)

Check before buying

  • **Service history:** Must have been carried out in a certified Honda/Acura NSX center.
  • **Carbon-ceramic brakes (optional but common):** Check the condition of the discs (weighing required) as replacement costs over €15,000.
  • **Tire wear:** Factory geometry settings (especially pre-2019) tend to wear the inside of the front tires very quickly.
  • **Transmission fluids:** The 9DCT gearbox and the front electric module (TMU) require regular fluid changes according to manufacturer recommendations.
  • **Manufacturer recalls:** Ensure that recalls concerning the fuel pump and tanks have been carried out.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Audi R8 V10 (louder, less technological), Porsche 911 Turbo / Turbo S (absolute benchmark in versatility/performance), McLaren 570S (lighter, less reliable). - **Japan / Asia:** Nissan GT-R R35 (older, cheaper, purely combustion), Lexus LC 500h (more GT than supercar). - **North America:** Chevrolet Corvette C8 (Z06 or E-Ray for hybridization), Ford GT (much more expensive and radical). - **Hybrid competitors:** BMW i8 (less powerful, 3-cylinder), Ferrari SF90 Stradale (much more expensive and powerful).

Verdict

The Honda/Acura NSX (NC1) is an engineering marvel that suffered from a delayed launch and an image deficit compared to Porsche or Ferrari. However, on the used market, it represents one of the most reliable and daily-drivable supercars ever produced. Its hybrid powertrain is exemplarily robust, true to Honda's reputation. The purchase is highly recommended for technology enthusiasts seeking an exclusive supercar, provided that an authorized NSX service center is nearby, which is essential for the maintenance of the SH-AWD system and the 9DCT gearbox.
