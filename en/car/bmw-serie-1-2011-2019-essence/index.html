Summary

The second-generation BMW 1 Series (F20 5-door, F21 3-door) holds a unique place in compact car history: it's the last in its class to offer a rear-wheel drive architecture and inline 6-cylinder engines.

Range Evolution: - Phase 1 (2011-2015): Launched with petrol engines from the partnership with PSA ('Prince' N13 engines) and older generation diesels (N47). Uneven reliability. - LCI 1 (Life Cycle Impulse - 2015): Major facelift (redesigned front/rear fascias) and, crucially, the progressive introduction of BMW's new modular engine family (B series: B38, B48, B37, B47). A spectacular leap in reliability. - LCI 2 (2017): Technological update (iDrive, instrument cluster) and introduction of the B58 6-cylinder engine (M140i).

Positioning: Premium compact targeting driving pleasure. Rear passenger space and boot capacity are behind the competition, but dynamism is unmatched. Overall reliability depends drastically on the production year and engine code.

Strengths 5

  • Unique rear-wheel drive architecture in its class (driving pleasure, 50/50 weight distribution).
  • Exceptional 6-cylinder engines (M135i/M140i) with no direct competition.
  • Remarkable reliability of B-generation petrol engines (B38, B48, B58) post-2015.
  • Ergonomics of the iDrive infotainment system (especially LCI 2).
  • Excellent ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (smooth, fast, reliable).

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of N13 petrol and N47 diesel engines on Phase 1 models (2011-2015).
  • Very limited rear passenger space (imposing transmission tunnel).
  • Boot volume below the class average.
  • Cost of maintenance and spare parts (expensive BMW network).
  • Imperfect sound insulation on early models.

Check before buying

  • Service history: 'LongLife' intervals of 30,000 km are too long. Prioritize models with oil changes every 15,000 km.
  • Timing chain noises when cold (especially on N13, N20, N47).
  • Oil leaks from the valve cover and oil filter housing (N55, N20).
  • Steering rack: clunking or stiff spots when steering at low speeds (recurrent problem on F20).
  • Water ingress: check for moisture in the boot (faulty taillight seals) and under the floor mats.

Official recalls

  • EGR Cooler (4-cyl Diesels): Massive global recall (several waves since 2018). Glycol leak potentially leading to intake manifold fire risk.
  • Takata Airbags (2012-2014): Replacement of the driver's airbag gas generator.
  • EPS Steering Box (2011-2015): Risk of electric power steering assist failure due to a contact defect.
  • Fuel Pump (2014-2015): Replacement of the in-tank fuel pump (risk of stalling while driving).

What sets it apart 6

Performance

  • Unrivalled Driving Pleasure — Enjoy a pure and dynamic driving experience thanks to its unique rear-wheel-drive architecture in its class, offering perfect weight distribution and exceptional agility.
  • Agility and Precision in Road Handling — Discover a sporty and engaging dynamic behavior, with precise steering and perfect balance, offering excellent road holding and great maneuverability for enhanced driving pleasure.

Reliability

  • Reliability of BMW Petrol Engines — Benefit from the remarkable reliability of the B-generation petrol engines (B38, B48, B58) introduced from 2015, renowned for their robustness and performance.

Comfort

  • Smoothness and Responsiveness of the ZF Automatic Gearbox — Be captivated by the excellence of the 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox, ensuring smooth, fast gear changes and optimal efficiency for superior driving comfort.

Safety

  • Certified Maximum Safety — Travel with complete peace of mind thanks to first-class passive safety, attested by a 5-star Euro NCAP rating, with 91% protection for adults and 83% for children.
  • Absolute Control with Exceptional Braking — Benefit from strong braking power and reliable endurance, typical of BMW vehicles, ensuring short and consistent stopping distances for increased safety in all circumstances.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Audi A3 (8V) (FWD/Haldex, premium trim, reliable TSI engines after 2013), Mercedes A-Class (W176) (attractive design, Renault engines on entry-level diesel), Volkswagen Golf VII (versatile benchmark).
  • Japan/Asia: Lexus CT200h (hybrid, unbeatable reliability, but placid handling), Mazda 3 (dynamic, very reliable naturally aspirated engines).
  • North America: The 1 Series F20 was not sold in the USA/Canada (replaced by the 2 Series Coupe F22).

Verdict

The BMW 1 Series F20/F21 is a car with two faces. Phase 1 models (2011-2015), equipped with N13 petrol and N47 diesel engines, are veritable problem magnets (timing chain, ancillaries) and should be approached with extreme caution, or even avoided. In contrast, LCI models (post-2015) equipped with modular engines (B38, B48, B58) transform the 1 Series into one of the most reliable and desirable compact cars on the market. For a worry-free petrol purchase, absolutely aim for a 118i (B38) or 120i (B48) from after 2015. For enthusiasts, the M140i (B58) is a mechanical masterpiece that will go down in history as the last rear-wheel drive 6-cylinder compact.
