Volvo V90

II (P) · 2016 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 76/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation Volvo V90 (based on the modular SPA platform) marks the renewal of the large Swedish estate, succeeding the V70. Positioned in the E-segment (premium), it stands out with its refined Scandinavian design, luxurious interior, and advanced safety arsenal. A notable fact: Volvo made the radical choice to equip this model only with 2.0-liter 4-cylinder engines (VEA family), available in petrol, diesel, and plug-in hybrid versions. A facelift occurred in 2020, introducing 48V mild-hybrid powertrains ('B' series) and, later, the Google Android Automotive-based infotainment system. Although highly developed, the V90 experienced some early issues, particularly with onboard electronics and the complex hybrid systems of the first few years.

Strengths 6

  • Timeless design and elegance of the estate body (and Cross Country).
  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with the rear air suspension).
  • Interior finish quality and materials (open-pore wood, Nappa leather).
  • Bowers & Wilkins audio system (optional) among the best on the market.
  • Ergonomic seats offering perfect support on long journeys.
  • Class-leading active and passive safety (highly calibrated Pilot Assist).

Weaknesses 5

  • Absence of 6-cylinder engines, which detracts from its prestige compared to German rivals.
  • Sensus infotainment system (2016-2021) slow to start.
  • Standard suspension a bit firm with 20 or 21-inch wheels.
  • Boot volume (560L) lower than the old V70 and the Mercedes E-Class Estate.
  • Very high out-of-warranty repair costs (especially on T8 versions).

Check before buying

  • Service history: VEA engines are very sensitive to oil quality.
  • Rear air suspension (option): check for sagging after prolonged parking (air spring leaks or tired compressor).
  • T8/T6 Hybrids: test 'Pure' mode (100% electric) to detect abnormal noise from the rear axle (ERAD).
  • Panoramic sunroof: check for water ingress (clogged drains).
  • Door lock operation: actuators tend to weaken over time.

What sets it apart 14

Safety

  • Unrivalled Safety — The Volvo V90 is a model of excellence in safety, integrating advanced active and passive systems for maximum protection of all occupants. Its Pilot Assist offers highly calibrated driving assistance, enhancing confidence on the road.
  • Powerful and Progressive Braking — Experience exceptional braking performance with the Volvo V90. Its systems ensure short stopping distances and great confidence, even in the most demanding conditions, thanks to remarkable power and progression.
  • Outstanding Stability and Grip — The V90 offers road handling focused on safety, comfort, and exemplary stability. Its road holding is predictable and very secure, with superior grip and precise steering, ensuring a constant feeling of safety on all your journeys.

Comfort

  • Exceptional Ride Comfort — Immerse yourself in a haven of peace with the Volvo V90. Its ride comfort is exceptional, especially with the optional rear air suspension, and its ergonomic seats offer perfect support for long, serene journeys.
  • Luxurious and Refined Interior — The V90's cabin is a sanctuary of luxury and refinement. The finish quality is impeccable, with noble materials such as open-pore wood and Nappa leather. The optional Bowers & Wilkins audio system transforms every journey into an immersive sound experience.

Design

  • Timeless Scandinavian Design — Be captivated by the pure elegance and timeless design of the Volvo V90. Its majestic estate silhouette and distinctive Scandinavian lines make it a vehicle that never goes unnoticed, combining functionality and aesthetics.

Performance

  • Smooth and Powerful Petrol Engines — The V90's petrol engines, particularly the T5 and B5 versions, offer an ideal combination of smoothness, quietness, and ample performance for all situations. They are renowned for their driving pleasure and proven design.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Audi A6 Avant (equivalent trim, V6 available), BMW 5 Series Touring (superior dynamics), Mercedes-Benz E-Class Estate (unbeatable boot volume). - **North America**: Audi A6 Allroad (against the V90 Cross Country), Mercedes-Benz E-Class All-Terrain. - **Japan / Asia**: Lexus RX (although an SUV, targets the same premium clientele focused on comfort and hybrid technology).

Verdict

The Volvo V90 is a majestic premium estate that prioritizes comfort, safety, and style over pure sportiness. If you're targeting a petrol engine, the **T5** (pre-2020) or **B5** (post-2020) versions represent the best compromise: they offer ample power while avoiding the mechanical complexity of the T6 (supercharger + turbo) and the risks of costly breakdowns in the early T8 plug-in hybrids. For a worry-free purchase, opt for a facelifted model (post-2020) where most of the early issues (electronics, oil consumption, Sensus bugs) have been corrected. Cross Country versions are particularly recommended for their versatility and enhanced suspension comfort.
