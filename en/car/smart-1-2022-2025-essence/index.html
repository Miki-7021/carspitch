Smart #1

I (HX11) · 2022 – 2025

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The **Smart #1** (pronounced *Hashtag One*, internal code HX11) marks a total break in the brand's history. Gone are the micro-city cars, making way for a B-segment urban SUV (4.27 m). The result of a joint venture between Mercedes-Benz (design) and Geely (engineering), it is based on Geely's SEA modular platform (shared with the Volvo EX30 and Zeekr X).

**⚠️ EXPERT NOTE REGARDING YOUR REQUEST:** You targeted the **gasoline** engine. It is crucial to specify that **the Smart #1 does NOT exist with ANY internal combustion engine (neither gasoline nor diesel)**. It is an exclusively 100% electric vehicle (BEV). The report will therefore focus on the available electric powertrains.

Overall, the Smart #1 offers an excellent footprint-to-roominess ratio, top-flight performances, and rich technological equipment, although its all-touch ergonomics and intrusive driving aids can be divisive.

Strengths 5

  • Remarkable roominess for the size (2.75 m wheelbase)
  • Very high-level performances (even on the base 272 hp version)
  • Original design and premium interior presentation (by Mercedes)
  • Very rich standard equipment (panoramic roof, 360 cameras, driving aids)
  • Efficient DC fast charging (up to 150 kW, 10-80% in less than 30 min)

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of an internal combustion engine (for buyers not ready for electric)
  • Complex ergonomics (almost everything goes through the central touch screen)
  • Very intrusive and sometimes abrupt driving aids (ADAS)
  • Modest trunk volume for an SUV (313 liters + small 15L frunk)
  • Frequent software bugs on non-updated models

Check before buying

  • Software update history (OTA): crucial for system stability.
  • Tire wear, particularly on Brabus and Pulse versions (very high torque).
  • Operation of flush door handles (can freeze or jam in winter).
  • Charging compatibility on public stations (some 'handshake' failures reported at launch, fixed via OTA).

Performance

  • Exhilarating Electric Performance — Discover a 100% electric powertrain offering very high-level performance, with impressive power from the base version (272 hp) and lightning-fast acceleration for sportier variants.

Practicality

Verdict

The Smart #1 is an excellent electric urban SUV that benefits from the synergy between Geely's software/electric expertise and Mercedes-Benz's design. **It is imperative to forget the search for a gasoline version, as it does not exist.**

It is a recommendable purchase, provided you accept highly digitized ergonomics and ensure the vehicle has received all its software updates. The **Premium (66 kWh, 272 hp)** version represents the best compromise between performances, equipment (standard heat pump), and range. The Brabus version is fun but wears out its tires quickly and loses efficiency.
