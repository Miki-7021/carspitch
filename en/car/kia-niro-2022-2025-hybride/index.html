Kia Niro

II (SG2) · 2022 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The second-generation Kia Niro (codename SG2) marks a deliberate stylistic departure from its predecessor, adopting the 'Opposites United' design with its contrasting C-pillar (Aero C-Pillar). Based on the optimized K3 platform from the Hyundai-Kia group, it maintains its unique market philosophy: it is offered **only in electrified versions** (Hybrid HEV, Plug-in Hybrid PHEV, and 100% Electric EV). There is no 100% thermal version. The model gains in spaciousness, onboard technology, and refines the efficiency of its powertrains. The HEV version, the core of the range, benefits from a lighter and innovative dual-clutch gearbox (DCT6), devoid of a reverse gear (reverse is exclusively handled by the electric motor). It is a rational, extremely frugal crossover covered by a 7-year manufacturer's warranty, making it an excellent sensible purchase.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptionally low fuel consumption (HEV)
  • Reassuring 7-year / 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty
  • Significantly improved rear spaciousness and generous trunk
  • Well-designed physical/haptic ergonomics (dual-function strip)
  • Smooth operation in urban driving (HEV/PHEV)

Weaknesses 5

  • Significant presence of hard plastics in the lower part of the cabin
  • Noisy combustion engine during strong accelerations (HEV/PHEV)
  • EV version's fast charging (DC) power limited to 85 kW
  • Prices significantly higher compared to the first generation
  • Absence of rear wiper on some trims/markets (aerodynamics)

Check before buying

  • 12V battery status: cases of premature discharge have been reported on 2022-2023 models (often linked to the eCall system or the Kia Connect application).
  • DCT6 gearbox behavior (HEV/PHEV): check for absence of juddering during hill starts.
  • Multimedia system: ensure that the latest software update has been installed (corrects slowness and black screens).
  • Maintenance history: adherence to the annual deadline is crucial to maintain the 7-year warranty.

Verdict

The Kia Niro II (SG2) is a pragmatic and highly recommendable choice. By building on the achievements of the first generation, Kia has corrected design flaws while maintaining formidable efficiency. **The HEV (simple Hybrid) version is the master purchase**: it offers the best price/performance/consumption compromise without the constraints of recharging. Mechanical reliability is excellent, with the 1.6 GDI Smartstream engine now perfectly reliable. The only complaints concern a few early electronic bugs (easily corrected at the dealership) and the quality of some interior plastics, which are below European standards. A vehicle to buy with confidence, provided that rigorous maintenance is followed to preserve the valuable 7-year warranty.
