Peugeot 308

II (T9) · 2013 – 2021

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

All Peugeot models

Summary

The second-generation Peugeot 308 (T9) marks a major turning point for the lion brand. Based on the new modular EMP2 platform, it sheds up to 140 kg compared to its predecessor, offering benchmark road handling, combining dynamism and comfort. Voted 'Car of the Year 2014', it introduced the famous i-Cockpit (small steering wheel, raised instrument cluster). It underwent two facelifts (2017 with the integration of the capacitive NAC screen, and 2020 with the arrival of 100% digital gauges). While its dynamic qualities and finish are praised, its career has been marred by recurring reliability issues with the PureTech gasoline engines and the BlueHDi diesel emission control systems.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional road handling (comfort/road holding compromise)
  • Mechanically very durable 1.6 HDi and 2.0 BlueHDi diesel engines
  • Extremely low fuel consumption on diesel versions
  • Gigantic boot volume on the station wagon version (SW - 610 litres)
  • Significantly improved finish and material quality
  • Smooth and reliable EAT6 and EAT8 automatic gearboxes (Aisin)

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of the AdBlue emission control system (tank/pump) on BlueHDi engines
  • Camshaft chain breakage on the 1.5 BlueHDi 130
  • Submerged belt scandal on 1.2 PureTech gasoline engines
  • Very average rear passenger space on the hatchback
  • Slow and unergonomic multimedia system (SMEG) before the 2017 facelift (NAC)
  • i-Cockpit ergonomics that obscure the gauges for some driver builds

Check before buying

  • Clear service history (crucial for manufacturer warranty claims)
  • Engine light or 'Antipollution fault / UREA' message (sign of a faulty AdBlue tank, bill ~€1000)
  • Metallic knocking sound from the top end of the 1.5 BlueHDi engine (precursor sign of camshaft chain breakage)
  • Condition of the timing belt on gasoline versions (cracks visible through the oil filler cap)
  • Creaking or knocking sounds from the rear axle (defective crossmember on early model years)
  • Touchscreen functionality (integrated air conditioning, a faulty screen blocks heating)

Official recalls

  • November 2020 (Code JZR): Massive recall of 1.2 PureTech engines produced between 2013 and 2017 for inspection of the timing belt and vacuum pump.
  • October 2019: Risk of cracking on the particulate filter (FAP) on 2.0 BlueHDi diesel engines.
  • February 2017: Replacement of the starter relay which could remain engaged and cause overheating.
  • July 2016: Replacement of door locks that could open while driving.
  • May 2016: Possible fuel leak at the injection rail on 2.0 BlueHDi engines.

What sets it apart 6

performance|road handling

  • Benchmark Road Handling — Voted 'Car of the Year 2014', the Peugeot 308 II offers exceptional road handling, combining dynamism and comfort thanks to its agile and precise chassis. Its responsive steering and excellent body control guarantee a most enjoyable driving experience.

safety

  • Certified Optimal Safety — With 5 stars in the Euro NCAP crash test, including an impressive score of 92% for adult protection and 79% for children, the 308 II ensures optimal safety for all passengers.
  • Efficient and Controlled Braking — Enjoy increased peace of mind thanks to remarkable braking performance. The 308 II boasts competitive stopping distances, often around 37-38 meters from 100 km/h, ensuring perfect control in all circumstances.

reliability|economy

  • Robust and Economical Diesel Engines — The 1.6 HDi (92/115 hp) and 2.0 BlueHDi (150/180 hp) diesel engines are renowned for their exceptional mechanical endurance and exemplary fuel efficiency. The 1.6 HDi is particularly appreciated for its robustness and extremely low consumption, while the 2.0 BlueHDi offers generous torque, ideal for long journeys.

comfort

  • Refined Comfort and Interior Quality — The 308 II's interior stands out with significantly improved finish quality and materials, offering a refined and comfortable environment. The EAT6 and EAT8 (Aisin) automatic gearboxes also contribute to a smooth and pleasant drive.

practicality

  • Generous Boot Volume (SW) — For families or loading needs, the station wagon (SW) version of the 308 II stands out with a gigantic boot volume of 610 liters, among the best in its category, offering remarkable versatility.

Alternatives

Europe

  • Volkswagen Golf VII (1.6 TDI / 2.0 TDI): Segment benchmark, superior finish, better rear passenger space, but less dynamic chassis.
  • Renault Mégane IV (1.5 dCi / 1.6 dCi): Excellent diesel alternative (the 1.5 dCi is very reliable), comfortable ride, but capricious R-Link 2 multimedia system.
  • Ford Focus III & IV (1.5 TDCi / 2.0 TDCi): Road handling similar to the 308, robust diesel engines.

Asia / Japan

  • Toyota Auris / Corolla (Hybrid): The absolute alternative for overall reliability, but gasoline/hybrid oriented, no relevant diesel.
  • Honda Civic (1.6 i-DTEC): Exceptionally reliable and fuel-efficient diesel engine, giant passenger space, but polarizing design.

North America

*The Peugeot 308 was not marketed in this region.*

Verdict

The Peugeot 308 II is a brilliant car on the road, but its reliability record is heavily marred by PSA's technical choices. If you are looking for a diesel: absolutely prioritize the older 1.6 HDi (92/115 hp) from before 2015, which are AdBlue-free and indestructible. The 2.0 BlueHDi (150/180) is an excellent workhorse, but budget for the capricious AdBlue tank. Avoid the 1.5 BlueHDi 130 unless the timing chain (8mm chain conversion) has been replaced with an invoice. On the gasoline side, the 1.2 PureTech is to be avoided without meticulous and recent service history. In summary: a passionate purchase for the chassis, but one that requires surgical selection of the engine.
