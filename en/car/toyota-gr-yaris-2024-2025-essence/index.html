Toyota GR Yaris

I facelift · 2024 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The first-generation Toyota GR Yaris benefits from a major facelift (Phase 2) for 2024. Far from being a simple aesthetic update, this evolution corrects the main flaws of Phase 1 while pushing performance even further. The 1.6L 3-cylinder turbo engine (G16E-GTS) gains power (280 hp in Europe, 304 hp in Japan) and benefits from reinforced internal components (pistons, valves). The major innovation is the introduction of the 8-speed automatic gearbox (DAT - Direct Automatic Transmission) developed by Gazoo Racing, offering a formidable alternative to the iMT6 manual gearbox. The chassis gains in rigidity (+13% spot welds, +24% structural adhesive), and the interior ergonomics have been completely redesigned with a driver-oriented dashboard and a driving position lowered by 25 mm. It is a true road-legal rally car, whose mechanical reliability proves exceptional for this specific level of performance.

Strengths 6

  • Performance and efficiency of the 4-wheel drive system (GR-Four)
  • New 8-speed DAT automatic gearbox with stunning efficiency
  • Correction of the driving position (finally lowered by 25 mm)
  • New, more ergonomic and driver-oriented dashboard
  • Exceptional overall mechanical reliability for an output of 175 hp/liter
  • Body rigidity further improved

Weaknesses 5

  • Prohibitive ecological penalty in France (often higher than the price of the car)
  • Sacrificed practical aspects (tiny 174L trunk, symbolic rear seats)
  • High fuel consumption in sporty driving
  • Limited rear and rear 3/4 visibility
  • Interior plastics still basic despite the new design

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: oil changes must be scrupulously respected (engine, gearbox, axles).
  • Condition of the underbody and rims (vehicle often used very dynamically).
  • Wear of tires (Michelin Pilot Sport 4S) and brake pads/discs (expensive to replace).
  • Signs of overheating if the vehicle has done a lot of track driving without additional cooling (although the facelift largely corrects this point).

Verdict

The facelifted Toyota GR Yaris (2024+) is an engineering masterpiece that improves upon an already exceptional recipe. By listening to feedback from customers and drivers (lowered driving position, redesigned dashboard, improved cooling), Toyota has created the ultimate compact sports car. The addition of the DAT automatic gearbox broadens its audience without sacrificing track efficiency. The reliability of the G16E-GTS block is remarkable, provided meticulous maintenance is observed. If you have the budget (and can handle the punitive taxation in some countries like France), it is a must-buy, a guaranteed future classic.
