Kia Carnival

IV (KA4) · 2022 – 2025

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The fourth-generation Kia Carnival (codename KA4) marks a break from the traditional minivan image by adopting an SUV-inspired design (dubbed a "Grand Utility Vehicle" by Kia). Replacing the Sedona in certain markets (notably North American), it is based on the N3 platform shared with the Sorento. It stands out with a gigantic interior space (up to 11 seats in some Asian markets, 7 or 8 seats in the West), cutting-edge technological features, and high-level ride comfort. Praised by Consumer Reports and J.D. Power for its above-average overall reliability in the segment, it establishes itself as a formidable alternative to the Japanese leaders (Honda Odyssey, Toyota Sienna) thanks to an unbeatable price-to-equipment ratio and an excellent manufacturer warranty.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent mechanical reliability of the 3.5L V6 and the 8-speed automatic gearbox
  • Gigantic interior space and modularity (VIP Lounge seats available)
  • Exceptional price-to-equipment ratio compared to the competition
  • Long-term manufacturer warranty (5 years/100,000 km or 10 years/100,000 miles depending on markets)
  • Rewarding design, moving away from the classic minivan image

Weaknesses 4

  • High fuel consumption on the 3.5L V6 (especially in the city)
  • Imposing size making parking difficult in European/Asian urban environments
  • Lack of all-wheel drive (AWD), unlike the Toyota Sienna or Chrysler Pacifica
  • Early bugs on the power sliding doors (fixed by recalls)

Check before buying

  • NHTSA recall history (very important on 2022-2023 models: fuel injection rail, doors, tow hitch)
  • Smooth and seamless operation of the power sliding side doors
  • Absence of fuel smell in the engine bay (V6)
  • Condition of the roof rails (recall for possible detachment of moldings)

Verdict

The 4th generation Kia Carnival (KA4) is an exceptional choice for large families looking for maximum space without sacrificing style or reliability. The 3.5L V6 engine, although thirsty, is a proven and extremely robust mechanical unit, supported by an excellent automatic gearbox. The reported problems are almost exclusively concentrated on body accessories (sliding doors, roof moldings) or minor first-year assembly defects (fuel injection rail), all covered by official recalls. If you are buying a used 2022 or 2023 model, simply ensure that all NHTSA recalls have been performed. It is a highly recommended purchase.
