Summary

The eighth-generation Hyundai Sonata (DN8) marks a radical departure from its predecessors. Introduced in 2020, it adopts a bold "four-door coupe" design and sits on the new N3 platform. The most significant change in terms of reliability is the definitive abandonment of the Theta II family engines (infamous for their fire risks and premature failure) in favor of the new generation of Smartstream engines.

The Hybrid (HEV) version, particularly targeted here, stands out for its remarkable efficiency, the integration of a solar panel roof (on certain trims), and the *Active Shift Control* (ASC) technology that optimizes the automatic gearbox's shifts. In 2024, the model received a major facelift introducing the *Seamless Horizon Lamp* light signature and the addition of an all-wheel drive (AWD) option on the naturally aspirated 2.5L engine. Overall, the Sonata DN8 displays a very positive reliability record, supported by Hyundai's generous manufacturer warranty.

Strengths 6

  • Reliability of the Hybrid (HEV) engine and excellent management of the thermal/electric transition
  • Definitive abandonment of the problematic Theta II engines in favor of reliable Smartstream blocks
  • Very reassuring manufacturer warranty (5 years unlimited mileage in Europe, 10 years/100,000 miles in the USA)
  • Cutting-edge onboard technology (Remote Smart Parking, comprehensive driving aids)
  • '12V Battery Reset' button on the hybrid, avoiding accessory battery failures
  • Bold design and significantly improved finish quality compared to the LF generation

Weaknesses 5

  • DCT-8 gearbox on the N Line version (2.5 T-GDI) prone to recalls for loss of power
  • Multiple recalls concerning peripheral components (fuel tank, electric oil pump, windshield)
  • Soundproofing could be improved at high speeds (road noise)
  • Absence of all-wheel drive (AWD) on models prior to the 2024 facelift
  • Generally faster depreciation than its Japanese rivals (Camry, Accord)

Check before buying

  • Imperatively check the recall history (NHTSA/manufacturer), especially for the fuel tank and oil pump.
  • On the N Line version (2.5T): test the DCT-8 gearbox when cold and at low speeds (looking for jerks or error messages).
  • On the Hybrid version: ensure that transitions between the electric and thermal engine are smooth.
  • Check the condition of the windshield (recall for factory bonding defect on 2021 models).

Official recalls

The Sonata DN8 has been the subject of several safety recalls (NHTSA data - United States):

  • [2025-11-18] Fuel tank (2020-2023): A damaged check valve can allow air to enter the tank, causing it to expand. It can then touch hot exhaust components and melt (fire risk).
  • [2024-08-07] Brake lights (2024): A software bug can cause the brake lights to flash or prevent them from illuminating correctly.
  • [2023-07-27] Electric oil pump (2023): The electronic controller for the *Idle Stop & Go* system's oil pump may contain damaged electrical components (risk of overheating/fire).
  • [2022-10-06] DCT-8 transmission (2021-2022 N Line): The dual-clutch gearbox's 'fail-safe' driving mode can be impaired, resulting in a total loss of drive power.
  • [2022-03-28] Trunk latch (2020, incl. Hybrid): The latch can become damaged, preventing the trunk from being opened from the inside (safety non-compliance).
  • [2021-12-28] Windshield (2021): Adhesion defect during manufacturing, the windshield may detach in the event of a crash.
  • [2021-07-09] Fuel leak (2021-2022 2.5T): Possible leak at the connection between the high-pressure fuel pump and the fuel rail.
  • [2020-04-09] Smart parking (2020): The *Remote Smart Parking Assist* (RSPA) software may fail to stop the vehicle in the event of a system malfunction.
  • [2020-02-28] Pressure label (2020): Incorrect indication of tire size on the door label.

Alternatives

  • North America / Asia: Toyota Camry (absolute benchmark in hybrid), Honda Accord (excellent dynamics and hybridization), Kia K5 (technical cousin of the Sonata), Nissan Altima.
  • Europe: The Sonata has no longer been officially imported into Western Europe for several generations (replaced by the i40, then abandonment of the segment). Import alternatives or close segments are the Skoda Superb, the Peugeot 508, or the Toyota Camry.

Verdict

The eighth-generation Hyundai Sonata (DN8) is an extremely accomplished sedan that erases the heavy engine reliability problems (Theta II) of its predecessors. The Hybrid engine (2.0 HEV) is the most relevant choice: it is frugal, very reliable, and benefits from mature engineering. The naturally aspirated versions (2.5 GDI) are also excellent choices for their simplicity. The only reservation concerns the sporty N Line version (2.5 T-GDI), whose DCT-8 gearbox had a chaotic start. Although the car has undergone several peripheral recalls (fuel tank, oil pump, windshield), the main mechanical components (Smartstream engines) are robust. It is a highly recommended purchase, provided you verify that all manufacturer recalls have been performed.
