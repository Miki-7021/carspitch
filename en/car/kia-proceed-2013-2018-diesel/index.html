Kia ProCeed

II (JD) · 2013 – 2018

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The **Kia pro_cee'd II (JD generation)** is the 3-door coupe version of the Cee'd compact sedan. Designed under the direction of Peter Schreyer, it stands out with a lower roofline, a dynamic profile, and a sportier positioning than the 5-door version, while retaining the same technical basis. Manufactured in Slovakia, it was designed specifically for the European market. The model benefited from a facelift in late 2015, bringing aesthetic tweaks, improved sound insulation, the introduction of the 1.0 T-GDi 3-cylinder petrol engine, the transition to Euro 6 standards for diesels, and the arrival of a 7DCT dual-clutch gearbox replacing the old torque converter automatic gearbox on the 1.6 CRDi. Although demand for 3-door compacts significantly declined during its career, the pro_cee'd remains a very relevant choice on the used car market thanks to its excellent overall reliability, particularly with the diesel engine (U2 family), and its initial 7-year manufacturer's warranty which encouraged rigorous follow-up within the network.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of CRDi diesel engines (U2 family), among the best of their generation.
  • Very successful exterior design, true sports coupe profile.
  • Significantly improved interior finish quality compared to the previous generation.
  • 7-year manufacturer's warranty (which forced rigorous maintenance within the network for most used models).
  • Very rich standard equipment (especially on high-end Premium or GT Line trims).

Weaknesses 5

  • Very poor rear visibility (reversing camera or parking sensors essential).
  • Difficult rear seat access (inherent to the 3-door body style).
  • 7DCT dual-clutch gearbox (post-2015) sometimes hesitant at low speeds and prone to premature clutch wear in very urban use.
  • LED daytime running lights (light signature) that flicker or burn out: often requires complete replacement of the headlight unit (expensive out of warranty).
  • Imperfect sound insulation on models before the 2015 facelift (road noise).

Check before buying

  • **Front lights:** Imperatively check that the LED daytime running lights (light brows) function perfectly without flickering.
  • **Steering:** Turn from left to right while stationary. A knocking sound (cloc-cloc) indicates wear of the steering column flex disc (a very common problem with Hyundai/Kia).
  • **Clutch / Flywheel (Manual Diesels):** Test the biting point. Vibrations in the pedal or a metallic noise at idle indicate a worn dual-mass flywheel (especially on 1.6 CRDi).
  • **7DCT Gearbox:** During a test drive, check for any juddering or jerking during hill starts or in traffic.
  • **Onboard electronics:** Test the touchscreen (GPS/Radio) which can sometimes freeze, as well as the proper functioning of the reversing camera.

Performance

  • Sporty and Refined Coupé Design — With its lowered roofline and dynamic profile, the ProCeed II stands out with a true sporty coupé design, combining elegance and assertive character on the road.
  • Agile and Precise Road Handling — Enjoy an engaging driving experience thanks to balanced road holding, rigorous body control, and precise steering, offering agility and predictability on all journeys.

Safety

  • Enhanced Safety for All Occupants — Benefit from optimal protection, attested by a 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with excellent results for adult (89%) and child (83%) protection.
  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — The ProCeed II provides good and reassuring braking performance for its category, with adequate stopping power and excellent pedal feel, ensuring a high level of active safety.

Comfort

  • Generous Equipment and Optimized Comfort — Enjoy a well-finished interior and very comprehensive standard equipment, especially on high-end trims. Post-2015 models benefit from improved sound insulation for increased serenity on board.

Economy

  • Controlled Fuel Consumption — The CRDi diesel engines offer particularly controlled fuel consumption, making the ProCeed II an economical and relevant choice for high-mileage drivers and daily use.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Volkswagen Scirocco (more premium but TSI/TDI engines from that era sometimes temperamental), Renault Mégane 3 Coupé (excellent alternative, reliable dCi engines late in their career), Opel Astra GTC (very heavy, distinctive design), Hyundai i30 3-door (its technical cousin, more conservative design). - **Other markets:** Model not available outside Europe/CIS.

Verdict

The Kia pro_cee'd II (JD) is an excellent deal on the used car market, particularly for those looking for a compact coupe with a distinctive design without sacrificing reliability. **With a diesel engine, it's a top-tier choice.** The 1.4 and 1.6 CRDi engines are benchmarks for longevity, equipped with a robust timing chain. The ideal choice is a **1.6 CRDi 128 hp or 136 hp with a manual gearbox**, preferably after the late 2015 facelift to benefit from improved sound insulation. The 7DCT gearbox is pleasant but requires careful checking of its behavior during a test drive. If you drive exclusively in the city, avoid diesel (risk of DPF/EGR clogging) and opt for the very reliable 1.4 MPI or 1.6 GDi.
