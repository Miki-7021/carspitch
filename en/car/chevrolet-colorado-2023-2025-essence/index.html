Summary

The 3rd generation Chevrolet Colorado (codename GMT595) marks a major technological and mechanical departure from the previous generation. Launched for the 2023 model year, it abandons V6 and diesel engines to streamline its offering around a single 2.7L turbocharged 4-cylinder block, shared with its big brother, the Silverado. This model moves significantly upmarket with a modernized interior, generous standard technological equipment (digital screens), and a strong focus on off-road capabilities with the introduction of the Trail Boss trim and the retention of the extreme ZR2. Although the engine is already proven at GM, this new platform has experienced some teething problems (electronics and assembly) typical of early production years.

Strengths 5

  • Very torquey 2.7L Turbo (L3B) engine, rendering the V6 and diesel obsolete
  • Excellent towing capabilities for the category (up to 3,490 kg)
  • Modern interior design with good ergonomics and responsive screens
  • Extremely capable factory off-road versions (Trail Boss, ZR2)
  • Significantly improved road handling and suspension comfort

Weaknesses 5

  • Disappearance of body options (only Crew Cab / Short Bed available)
  • Interior plastics still basic on WT and LT trims
  • Frequent electronic bugs on 2023 models (unintended emergency braking)
  • Factory assembly errors leading to several recalls (bolts, seatbelts)
  • Fuel consumption that climbs quickly under load or on ZR2 versions

Check before buying

  • Recall history: crucial on 2023 and 2024 models (wheel bolts, seatbelts, fuel pump).
  • Emergency braking system (AEB): test if the vehicle brakes for no reason (requires a software update).
  • Tire alignment and wear: a recall involves incorrect tire fitments and poorly calibrated TPMS sensors.
  • Central screen operation: check for the absence of unexpected reboots or sluggishness.

  • Off-Road Mastery — For adventurers, the Trail Boss and ZR2 versions offer extreme off-road capabilities and unparalleled performance, ready to tackle the toughest terrains straight from the factory.

Practicality

  • Class-Leading Towing Capabilities — The Chevrolet Colorado excels in its category with impressive towing capabilities, allowing you to easily transport your heaviest loads.

Comfort

Verdict

The 3rd generation Chevrolet Colorado is a highly accomplished mid-size pickup dynamically and mechanically. GM's choice to bet everything on the 2.7L Turbo 4-cylinder proves successful: the engine (especially in the TurboMax version) offers superior drivability and torque compared to the old V6s, while being mechanically robust (already proven on the Silverado). However, the initial reliability of this new generation is marred by factory assembly issues (wheel bolts, seatbelts, fuel pump) and software bugs (phantom braking). If you are targeting a 2023 or 2024 model, it is imperative to verify that all recalls have been performed. The 2025 models should benefit from better production maturity.
