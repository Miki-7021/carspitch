Summary

The **fifth-generation Land Rover Range Rover (L460)**, launched in 2022, is based on the new MLA-Flex modular architecture. It represents the pinnacle of luxury and refinement at Land Rover, offering for the first time a 7-seat option and standard rear-wheel steering. While the design evolves smoothly with a very sleek style (notably the 'boat tail' rear and hidden lights), the onboard technology takes a leap forward. Historically, electronic and pneumatic reliability has always been the Achilles' heel of the Range Rover. On this L460 generation, the internal combustion engines (notably the excellent Ingenium inline-6 diesels) prove to be mechanically robust, but the vehicle remains penalized by software bugs (Pivi Pro system) and early production recalls related to assembly. It is an engineering masterpiece, but its complexity requires a solid warranty.

Strengths 5

  • Imperial ride comfort (predictive air suspension)
  • Exceptional soundproofing (active noise cancellation in the headrests)
  • Surprising maneuverability thanks to rear-wheel steering (10.95 m turning circle)
  • Diesel engines (D300/D350) perfectly suited to the vehicle's mass
  • Off-road capabilities (Terrain Response 2) intact despite the ultra-luxury positioning

Weaknesses 5

  • Elitist purchase price and dizzying depreciation
  • Electronic reliability still temperamental (screen, camera, and sensor bugs)
  • Excessive weight, particularly on PHEV versions (nearly 2.9 tonnes)
  • Prohibitive maintenance, insurance, and repair costs
  • Size (5.05 m or 5.25 m in long wheelbase) unsuited to European underground parking lots

Check before buying

  • Complete history of software updates (Pivi Pro) at the dealership
  • Smooth and noiseless operation of the air suspension (raising/lowering)
  • Absence of oil leaks under the engine (known issue on oil filter housings and coolers)
  • Alignment of body panels and interior trim (some factory assembly defects)
  • On diesels: check the DPF/FAP soot level via the diagnostic tool if the vehicle has been driven a lot in the city
  • Operation of the rearview camera (frequent water ingress)

Verdict

The Range Rover L460 is arguably the most accomplished luxury SUV in the world in terms of comfort, design, and versatility. **However, it remains true to the brand's reputation: its electronic reliability and early production assemblies leave room for improvement**, as evidenced by the long list of official recalls.

**Diesel Focus:** Against all odds in the era of electrification, the inline-6 diesel engines (D300 and D350) are the most logical choices for this behemoth. They offer truck-like torque, reasonable fuel consumption (8.5L - 10L/100km), and superior mechanical reliability compared to the complex plug-in hybrid (PHEV) versions.

**Buying advice:** Only buy this vehicle with an extended manufacturer warranty (ideally 5 years) and ensure that all recalls (especially on the oil circuit and cameras) have been performed. Avoid the very first production 2022 models.
