Fiat 500X

· 2014 – Présent (Restylages en 2018 et 2022)

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The Fiat 500X is an urban and compact crossover that shares its platform (FCA Small Wide 4x4) and assembly lines with the Jeep Renegade. Launched in 2014 to capitalize on the success of the 500 family, it stands out with its highly successful neo-retro design, an interior finish superior to Fiat's usual standards at the time, and good road handling.

The model has undergone two major evolutions: a first facelift in 2018 (introduction of the 1.0 and 1.3 FireFly petrol engines to replace the old blocks, technological update) and a second update in 2022 (introduction of the 1.5 Hybrid 48V engine and the gradual phasing out of diesels).

**Overall expert rating:** The 500X is generally a well-born vehicle, but its reliability depends heavily on the chosen engine and transmission. Manual versions with FireFly engines or well-used diesels are recommendable. On the other hand, the early 9-speed automatic transmissions (ZF) and the American 2.4 engine require great caution.

Strengths 5

  • Timeless exterior design and likability factor
  • Interior finish quality (soft-touch materials on high-end trims)
  • Dynamic and safe road handling
  • Availability of a true all-wheel drive (4x4) transmission on larger engines
  • Responsive and ergonomic Uconnect infotainment system (7-inch versions post-2018)

Weaknesses 5

  • Trunk volume (350 liters) below the category average
  • Average rear roominess (legroom and headroom)
  • Management of the 9-speed automatic transmission (ZF) often hesitant and prone to jerking
  • Firm damping at low speeds, especially with 18-inch wheels
  • Hit-or-miss reliability of onboard electronics on 2014-2017 models

Check before buying

  • Clear maintenance history: crucial for MultiAir engines (oil sensitivity).
  • Automatic transmission behavior: during the test drive, check for the absence of jerks or hesitations during downshifts (especially from 3rd to 2nd).
  • Uconnect touch screen: check that it does not freeze, restart on its own, and that the panel has no bubbles (frequent delamination on early models).
  • Front axle: listen for suspension noises (wishbone silent blocks and stabilizer bar links wear out around 80,000 km).
  • DPF (Diesels): check for the absence of an engine warning light and ensure the vehicle has not been driven exclusively in the city.

Verdict

The Fiat 500X is an attractive choice for those looking for a crossover with personality. On the used market, preference should be given to facelifted models (post-2018) equipped with FireFly petrol engines (1.0 or 1.3), which offer the best compromise between modernity, drivability, and reliability. Heavy drivers will find what they need with the solid 1.6 MultiJet, provided they avoid the city.

**Warnings:** Flee from 1.4 MultiAir models with an incomplete maintenance history, absolutely avoid the 2.4 Tigershark (imports or North American market) due to its disastrous oil consumption, and rigorously test the 9-speed automatic transmission before purchase.
