Genesis G70

I (IK) · 2018 – 2021

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation Genesis G70 (phase 1, code IK) is a premium compact sports sedan designed to compete directly with the German benchmarks (BMW 3 Series, Mercedes C-Class, Audi A4). Based on the same rear-wheel drive (or AWD) platform as the Kia Stinger, it stands out with sharp dynamic handling, excellent finish quality, and a very aggressive price-to-equipment ratio. Although its rear passenger space is somewhat lacking, it establishes itself as an exotic and highly reliable alternative, particularly in its 3.3T V6 version. Overall generation rating: 8/10.

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic road handling (RWD or AWD)
  • High-level finish and assembly quality
  • Overall reliability of the 3.3T V6 engine (Lambda II)
  • Very favorable price-to-equipment ratio
  • Generous manufacturer warranty (depending on markets)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very restricted legroom in the rear seats
  • Trunk volume below the category average
  • High fuel consumption on the 3.3T V6

Check before buying

  • Recall history: it is imperative to check if the recalls related to the fuel pump and ABS module have been carried out.
  • 3.3T V6: inspect the left turbocharger area for any signs of oil seepage (feed pipe).
  • Electrical: check the proper operation of the starter motor (risk of water infiltration according to recall).
  • Running gear: asymmetrical or premature tire wear, particularly on rear-wheel drive versions.
  • 2.0T engine: unstable idle which may indicate carbon build-up on the intake valves (typical of GDI engines).

Verdict

The Genesis G70 (2018-2021) is an excellent alternative to German premium sedans. It shines with its fundamental mechanical reliability, particularly with the robust 3.3T V6 which offers top-tier performance. Although the model has been the subject of several official recalls (notably electrical and related to fuel/oil supply), these defects are covered by the manufacturer and do not call into question the solidity of the engine blocks. It is a highly recommended used purchase, provided you verify that the recall history is up to date and accept limited rear passenger space.
