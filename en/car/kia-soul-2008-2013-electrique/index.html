Summary

The first-generation Kia Soul (code AM) was launched in 2008, marking Kia's entry into the urban crossover segment with a bold and unconventional design. Inspired by the concept car of the same name, it positioned itself as a young, trendy, and practical vehicle, offering an alternative to classic city cars and small MPVs. Its 'box on wheels' style gives it surprising interior space for its size.

This first generation underwent a slight facelift in 2011, bringing aesthetic touches (headlights, bumpers) and mechanical improvements, notably the introduction of more efficient engines. It is important to note that this generation (AM) was never offered in an electric version. The electric powertrain (Soul EV) appeared with the second generation (PS) from 2014.

Overall, the Soul I is a reliable vehicle, without major recurring problems, but with a few points of attention related to wear and specific recalls. Its overall reliability rating is good for a vehicle of this era.

Check before buying

  • Condition of suspensions and silent blocks (noises, road holding)
  • Operation of DPF and EGR valve on diesels (warning lights, regenerations)
  • Abnormal noises from the timing chain on the 1.6 G4FC (rare, but possible)
  • Brake wear (discs and pads)
  • Operation of all electrical equipment (windows, air conditioning, radio)
  • History of manufacturer recalls and their application

Verdict

The first-generation Kia Soul is an endearing and reliable vehicle, provided you choose the right engine and check its history. It is crucial to remember that this generation (AM, 2008-2013) was never offered in an electric version. If you are looking for an electric Soul, you will need to turn to the second generation (PS) from 2014.

For this first generation, the petrol engines (1.6 G4FC and 2.0 G4GC) are the most recommendable for their robustness and ease of maintenance, particularly for urban and suburban use. The 1.6 CRDi (D4FB) is a good option for high-mileage drivers, but requires particular attention to the DPF and EGR if use is predominantly urban.

Its original design, interior space, and raised driving position make it a relevant choice for those looking for a practical vehicle that stands out from the ordinary. Negotiate the price if recalls have not been carried out or if maintenance is lacking. A well-maintained example represents a good purchase.
