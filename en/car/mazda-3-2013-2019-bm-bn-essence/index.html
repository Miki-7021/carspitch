Mazda 3

· 2013 – 2019 (BM/BN)

ACHETER Reliability 80/100 — bon

Summary

The third-generation Mazda 3 (BM/BN), introduced in 2013 and facelifted in 2016 (BN), marked a significant leap forward for the brand. Featuring the full suite of Skyactiv technologies and the striking Kodo design language, it offered a premium feel, engaging driving dynamics, and exceptional fuel efficiency. It remains one of the most reliable and desirable used compact cars on the market, provided you choose the right powertrain. The naturally aspirated petrol engines are highly regarded for their longevity, while the diesel variants require much more caution.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of Skyactiv-G petrol engines
  • Engaging driving dynamics and precise manual gearbox
  • Premium interior quality and ergonomics
  • Attractive Kodo design that ages very well
  • Excellent real-world fuel economy

Weaknesses 5

  • 2.2 Skyactiv-D diesel engine is plagued with expensive issues
  • Infotainment screen 'ghost touch' issue (delamination)
  • Thin paint prone to stone chips and scratches
  • Road and wind noise at highway speeds (improved on BN facelift)
  • Cramped rear seats and average boot space compared to rivals

Check before buying

  • Infotainment screen: Check for 'ghost touches' or spider-web cracking on the display.
  • Power folding mirrors: Motors frequently fail, preventing mirrors from opening/closing.
  • Paintwork: Inspect the front bumper and hood for excessive stone chips.
  • Diesel engines: Check oil level (oil dilution raises the level) and listen for top-end ticking (camshaft wear on 2.2D).
  • Service history: Skyactiv engines require strict adherence to oil change intervals using specific low-friction oil.

Safety

  • High-Level Passive and Active Safety — The Mazda 3 achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult (93%) and child (86%) protection, and was designated an IIHS Top Safety Pick+ in 2014.
  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Benefit from excellent braking performance, with short and consistent stopping distances, and a firm, reassuring pedal feel, even in emergency situations.

tenue de route

  • Dynamic and Agile Road Handling — Enjoy superior driving pleasure thanks to a balanced chassis, precise and responsive steering, and the G-Vectoring Control system (on Phase 2), offering an engaging and secure driving experience.

Economy

  • Controlled Fuel Consumption — The Mazda 3's Skyactiv-G petrol engines are designed to offer very well-controlled real-world fuel consumption, contributing to advantageous running costs.

Comfort

  • Refined Comfort and Ergonomics — Appreciate excellent driving position ergonomics, a refined interior finish, and significantly improved sound insulation on restyled models (Phase 2), for more serene journeys.

Performance

  • Driving Pleasure with a Precise Manual Gearbox — The manual gearbox control, with its perfect feel inspired by the legendary MX-5, guarantees unparalleled driving pleasure and an authentic connection with the road.

Alternatives

- **Honda Civic (9th/10th Gen):** Similar reliability and driving dynamics, but with more interior space and a polarizing design. - **Volkswagen Golf (Mk7):** The benchmark for refinement and interior packaging, though TSI/TDI engines require more careful maintenance. - **Toyota Corolla / Auris:** Unbeatable hybrid reliability, but less engaging to drive than the Mazda. - **Ford Focus (Mk3):** Great handling, but plagued by Powershift automatic transmission issues.

Verdict

The Mazda 3 (BM/BN) is a standout choice in the compact class, provided you stick to the Skyactiv-G petrol engines. These naturally aspirated motors offer a rare blend of bulletproof reliability, good performance, and excellent fuel economy. The manual transmission is one of the best in the business, and the chassis is genuinely fun to drive. However, buyers should strictly avoid the 2.2 Skyactiv-D diesel unless it has a flawless, documented history of highway use and preventative maintenance. Look for a post-2016 facelift (BN) model for better sound insulation and refined features.
