Renault Megane

III · 2008 – 2016

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The **Renault Mégane III** marks a major turning point for the diamond brand manufacturer. After a Mégane II with disastrous electronic and mechanical reliability in its early days, Renault focused on perceived quality and robustness for this third generation. Based on an evolution of its predecessor's platform, it offers first-class road handling, refined soundproofing, and significantly improved finish.

It underwent two facelifts (Phase 2 in 2012 with the integration of LED daytime running lights, and Phase 3 in 2014 adopting the brand's new visual identity with the large diamond logo). While the historical engines (1.6 16v, 2.0 TCe, 2.0 dCi) proved extremely reliable, the introduction of downsized petrol engines (TCe series) tarnished the record at the end of its career, notably with the infamous 1.2 TCe (H5Ft).

Strengths 5

  • Excellent chassis, comfort/handling compromise (especially on RS and GT versions)
  • Overall reliability of historical engines (1.6 16v, 2.0 TCe, 2.0 dCi)
  • Significantly improved finish quality and material aging compared to the Mégane II
  • Very effective corrosion protection
  • Reasonable maintenance cost and parts availability

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.2 TCe engine (H5Ft)
  • Rear passenger space and boot volume (on the sedan) in the lower average of the category
  • Ergonomics of the R-Link 1 multimedia system (slowness, bugs) on Phase 3 models
  • Poor rear visibility (especially on the coupé)
  • EDC automatic gearbox sometimes causes jerks at low speeds

Check before buying

  • On TCe petrol engines: history of oil and coolant consumption.
  • On the 1.6 16v: listen to the engine when cold (a 2-3 second knocking sound indicates a worn camshaft dephaser pulley).
  • On diesels: condition of the DPF (warning light on, journey history) and EGR valve.
  • On RS versions: condition of the running gear (independent pivots), wear of brakes (Brembo) and clutch.
  • Operation of the air conditioning (condenser exposed to gravel) and hands-free card.

Verdict

The Renault Mégane III is an excellent used car, provided you **choose the right engine**. It has erased the electronic missteps of its predecessor and offers a very high-level chassis.

**For petrol**, the choice is binary: favor the historical blocks (1.6 16v K4M, 2.0 16v M4R, 2.0 TCe F4Rt) which are incredibly robust. On the other hand, **absolutely avoid the 1.2 TCe**, a real mechanical time bomb, and be very cautious with the 1.4 TCe (cylinder head gasket).

**For diesel**, the overall assessment is very positive, particularly with the indestructible 2.0 dCi, ideal for high-mileage drivers.
