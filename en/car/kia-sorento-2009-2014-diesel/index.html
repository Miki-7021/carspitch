Summary

The second-generation Kia Sorento (code name XM) marks a radical turning point for the model. Abandoning the ladder frame chassis of the first generation for a unibody structure (shared with the Hyundai Santa Fe), it sheds its pure off-road capabilities to become a much more road-oriented, comfortable, and dynamic family SUV (available in 5 or 7 seats).

A significant facelift (often called Phase 2 or MY2013) occurred in late 2012, bringing structural modifications to the chassis (more rigid), an improvement in perceived interior quality, new LED lights, and mechanical updates. In the European market, it was primarily sold with a diesel engine (the robust 2.2 CRDi), offering an excellent price/equipment/spaciousness ratio. Although generally reliable in diesel form, this model suffers from recurring weaknesses in its all-wheel drive system and major problems with certain gasoline engines (Theta II) sold outside Europe.

Overall generation score: 72/100

Strengths 5

  • Excellent spaciousness (true 7-seater for occasional use, huge trunk in 5-seater configuration)
  • 2.2 CRDi engine very torquey, powerful, and generally very reliable
  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used market (leather, panoramic roof, rearview camera often standard)
  • Towing capacity (up to 2500 kg depending on version)
  • Transformed road handling compared to the first generation

Weaknesses 5

  • Chronic fragility of the all-wheel drive (angle gear splines, AWD coupler pump)
  • Very firm suspension, especially at low speeds and with 18/19-inch wheels
  • Interior plastic quality (easy scratches) on pre-facelift models (2009-2012)
  • 4-cylinder gasoline engines (Theta II) with very high risk of failure (especially in North America)
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds (wind noise)

Check before buying

  • AWD operation: Test the vehicle on slippery surfaces or gravel. If the front wheels slip without the rear engaging, the angle gear or coupler is faulty (repair > €2000).
  • Clunking in the steering: A 'clunk' sound when turning the steering wheel at a standstill indicates wear of the flexible coupling (flex disc/rubber star) of the steering column. Inexpensive part but significant labor cost.
  • Automatic gearbox history: The 6-speed automatic gearbox is reliable IF it has been drained every 60,000 to 80,000 km. Abrupt gear changes indicate a worn valve body.
  • DPF and EGR (Diesels): Check for absence of engine warning light and jerks during acceleration. A history of short trips is a red flag.
  • Onboard electronics: Rearview camera often faulty (water infiltration), capricious touchscreen.

Verdict

The Kia Sorento II (XM) is an excellent used choice for large families or those who tow, provided you choose the 2.2 CRDi engine. This diesel block is a model of robustness if maintenance is followed. However, you must be extremely vigilant about the condition of the all-wheel drive (AWD) system, whose failures are frequent and costly. Opt for a 2-wheel drive (2WD) model if you don't need 4x4, you will avoid this risk. Absolutely avoid the 2.4 GDI gasoline versions (especially imports or in Canada/USA) due to their disastrous history of engine failures. A facelifted model (2013-2014) with the 2.2 CRDi 2WD is the best buy of this generation.
