Audi A3

8P · 2003 – 2012

NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

Summary

The second-generation Audi A3 (Type 8P) is based on the VAG group's PQ35 platform (shared with the VW Golf V and VI). Initially launched as a 3-door, it was joined by the Sportback (5-door) version in 2004 and a Cabriolet in 2008. It underwent two major facelifts: 2005 (adoption of the *Singleframe* grille) and 2008 (new headlights with LED daytime running lights, mirrors with integrated turn signals, and most importantly, the abandonment of pump-injector diesels for Common Rail). It is a benchmark premium compact, offering an exceptional interior finish for its time. In terms of reliability, the 8P generation is a two-faced car: extremely robust with certain engines (1.9 TDI, 1.6 MPI), it also suffered from problematic technologies (porous cylinder heads on early 2.0 TDI, excessive oil consumption on TFSI EA888, fragility of the DSG7 DQ200 gearbox).

Strengths 5

  • Very high-quality interior finish, aging extremely well
  • Sound and safe road handling (especially with Quattro)
  • Fuel efficiency and durability of 1.9 TDI and 2.0 TDI Common Rail engines
  • Ergonomics of controls and driving position
  • Wide choice of body styles (3-door, Sportback 5-door, Cabriolet)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of early 2.0 TDI 140 (BKD) and 170 (BMN)
  • Very fragile DSG7 (DQ200) gearbox (clutches and mechatronics)
  • Chronic excessive oil consumption on TFSI EA888 engines
  • Fragile air conditioning compressors (Sanden/Zexel) (central screw that loosens)
  • Firm comfort, especially in S-Line trim (sport chassis and 18" wheels)

Check before buying

  • Cylinder head history on 2.0 TDI 140 before 2007 (presence of white smoke, coolant level drop).
  • Dual-mass flywheel noise at idle (clunking, vibrations in the clutch pedal) on all TDIs.
  • Air conditioning operation (the compressor is a known issue).
  • Gear changes on DSG gearbox (no jerking should be felt, check oil change invoices every 60,000 km for DSG6).
  • Chain noise on cold start on TSI/TFSI engines and the 3.2 VR6.

Verdict

The Audi A3 8P is an excellent compact, provided you choose the right engine. **For diesel**, absolutely avoid the 2.0 TDI 140 (BKD) before 2007 and the 2.0 TDI 170 (BMN) with pump-injectors. Prioritize the rustic but indestructible 1.9 TDI 105 (avoiding the BXE code) for smaller budgets, or opt for facelifted models after late 2008 equipped with the 2.0 TDI Common Rail (140 or 170 hp), which are infinitely more reliable and pleasant. **For petrol**, the 1.6 MPI is the choice for absolute peace of mind, while TFSI engines require extreme vigilance regarding maintenance history (chain, oil). Avoid the DSG7 (DQ200) gearbox and prefer the manual gearbox or the oil-bath DSG6 (DQ250), provided it has been drained every 60,000 km.
