Summary

The BMW 5 Series E39 is often considered by purists and experts as the pinnacle of BMW's executive sedan, offering a perfect balance of comfort, build quality, and driving dynamics. Replacing the E34, it introduced aluminum suspension components to reduce unsprung weight. The facelift (LCI - Life Cycle Impulse) occurred in September 2000 (model year 2001), bringing the famous 'Angel Eyes' headlights, revised bumpers, and the new generation of M54 gasoline engines. Although the focus here is on gasoline engines (renowned for their refinement and smoothness, especially the inline-6s), the E39 also popularized high-performance diesels with the M57 block. Overall design score: 9/10 (a historical benchmark, but one that now requires collector-level maintenance).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional build and interior finish quality (ages very well)
  • Superb road handling (aluminum chassis, 50/50 weight distribution)
  • Top-notch sound insulation and ride comfort
  • Legendary inline-6 gasoline engines (M52/M54) for their driving pleasure
  • Timeless design, especially in Phase 2 (LCI) with M pack

Weaknesses 5

  • Undersized and fragile cooling system on all gasoline engines
  • Aging electronics (disappearing dashboard pixels, capricious ABS/DSC module)
  • Complex suspension components prone to wear (bushings, control arms)
  • Maintenance cost remains that of a luxury car, even 20 years later
  • Chronic oil consumption on M54 engines (520i 2.2, 525i, 530i)

Check before buying

  • Cooling System: History of radiator, expansion tank, water pump, and thermostat replacement. This is the #1 cause of death for BMW gasoline engines of this era.
  • Suspension/Chassis: Vibrations during braking or at 90 km/h (often front thrust arm bushings). Wear of rear subframe bushings.
  • Electronics: Functionality of the ABS/DSC module (warning lights on = Bosch 5.7 module often needs repair). Pixel display on the instrument cluster (MID).
  • Oil Leaks: Valve cover gasket, oil filter housing gasket, oil pan gasket (very time-consuming to change).
  • Corrosion: Jacking points under the sills, bottom of doors, tailgate on Touring (wagon) versions.
  • Ventilation: Defective blower motor resistor (nicknamed 'hedgehog') that drains the battery when parked.

Official recalls

  • Takata Airbags (Multiple years): Replacement of driver and passenger airbag inflators (risk of metallic fragment projection).
  • Auxiliary Fan (2001): Risk of short circuit and fire at the climate control fan resistor.
  • Brake Pedal (1998): On V8s, possible brake assist failure due to a defective vacuum check valve.
  • Tires (1999): On M5, recall regarding defective tire valves leading to pressure loss.

What sets it apart 6

Performance

  • Legendary Petrol Engines — Enjoy the nobility and smoothness of the inline-six petrol engines (M52/M54), renowned for their exceptional driving pleasure and refined performance.

Handling

  • Imperial Road Handling — The BMW E39 is a benchmark in dynamic behavior, offering a perfect balance between ride comfort and sporty agility thanks to its multi-link chassis and 50/50 weight distribution.

Safety

  • Solid Passive Safety — Designed with high standards for its era, the BMW E39 offers robust protection for its occupants, as evidenced by its Euro NCAP score.

Braking

  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — Benefit from an excellent braking system, recognized for its power, progressiveness, and remarkable resistance to fading, ensuring optimal safety in all circumstances.

Comfort

  • Exceptional Comfort and Build Quality — The E39 stands out with its high-level interior build quality and finish, meticulous soundproofing, and imperial ride comfort, making it a premium road sedan.

Practicality

  • Timeless Design and Durability — Its elegant and timeless design, especially in Phase 2, combined with a build quality that ages very well, ensures a distinguished presence and lasting value.

Alternatives

🇪🇺 Europe

  • Mercedes-Benz E-Class (W210): More spacious, very robust V6/V8 engines, but catastrophic corrosion problems.
  • Audi A6 (C5): High-level interior finish, Quattro all-wheel drive available, but fragile front suspension and problematic V6 TDI engines.

🇯🇵 Asia / Japan

  • Lexus GS (S160): Legendary reliability (2JZ engines), royal comfort, but less dynamic handling than the E39.

🇺🇸 North America

  • Acura RL / Lexus LS 400: Alternatives focused on absolute comfort and flawless reliability, at the expense of sportiness.

Verdict

The BMW 5 Series E39 is a masterpiece of Bavarian engineering. With its gasoline (inline-6) engines, it offers a driving experience that is still a benchmark today. However, it is no longer an ordinary used car: it is a 'youngtimer' that requires rigorous and preventive maintenance. The top choice is the 530i (M54) for its performance, or the 528i (M52) for its raw reliability. Avoid neglected or cheaply modified examples. Plan for an immediate budget of 1000 to 2000 € after purchase to ensure the reliability of the cooling system and suspension. If you accept these conditions, it is one of the best sedans ever built.
