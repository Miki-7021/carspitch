Summary

The Dacia Lodgy is a compact MPV launched in 2012, built on the M0 platform (Renault-Nissan) shared with the Dacia Dokker, Logan II and Sandero II. It was produced at the Tangier plant (Morocco) and sold mainly in Europe, the Maghreb and certain emerging markets. The Lodgy stands out for its exceptional space-to-price ratio, with a 7-seat version and a vast trunk. It received a facelift in 2017 (grille, headlights, interior). Production ended in 2022, indirectly replaced by the Jogger. Overall reliability is decent, with simple and proven engines, but finish is improvable and there are a few points of attention on the 1.5 dCi diesels (injectors, EGR, DPF) and on the onboard electronics. Overall reliability rating: 7/10.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional price/space ratio (7 seats, huge trunk)
  • Simple and proven engines (K9K, K4M) that are easy to maintain
  • Very low maintenance and spare parts costs
  • Good passive safety (4 stars Euro NCAP 2012)
  • Economical and robust 1.5 dCi diesel version if maintenance is followed

Weaknesses 6

  • Mediocre interior finish (hard plastics, rattles)
  • Soundproofing could be better, especially on diesel
  • Limited standard equipment on lower trims
  • Roadholding and suspension comfort behind competitors
  • No automatic gearbox and no hybrid/electric version
  • EGR valve and DPF sensitive on repeated urban trips (diesel)

Check before buying

  • Full maintenance history (oil changes, filters, timing belt)
  • Condition of the EGR valve and DPF on diesel (clogging, regenerations)
  • Diesel injectors: leak-off test and engine noise
  • Turbo: axial play, abnormal whistling, oil traces
  • Timing chain on 1.2 TCe (noise on cold start)
  • Condition of the running gear (ball joints, shock absorbers) and brakes
  • Operation of the electronics (instrument cluster, radio, air conditioning)
  • Presence of unperformed recalls (airbag, catalytic converter, starter)

Verdict

The Dacia Lodgy is a compact MPV with unbeatable value for money, ideal for large families on a tight budget. For diesel, favour the 1.5 dCi 90 hp (K9K) for its robustness and maintenance cost, or the 1.5 dCi 110 hp if performance is a priority. Avoid the 1.2 TCe versions (risks of excessive oil consumption and turbo issues) and diesels that have done a lot of city driving (EGR/DPF clogging). Imperatively check the maintenance history, the condition of the EGR valve, injectors and DPF, as well as whether the airbag (0C00) and catalytic converter (0D0E) recalls have been carried out. The diesel Lodgy is a rational choice for high-mileage drivers, provided maintenance is not neglected. Verdict: BUY (with the usual precautions).
