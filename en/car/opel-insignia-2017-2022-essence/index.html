Summary

The second-generation Opel Insignia (Insignia B), also known as Vauxhall Insignia (United Kingdom), Buick Regal (North America/China), and Holden Commodore ZB (Australia/New Zealand), is Opel's last large sedan designed under the General Motors era (E2XX platform).

Lightened by nearly 175 kg compared to the heavy previous generation, it offers transformed road handling, generous interior space, and a sleek design (Grand Sport for the sedan, Sports Tourer for the station wagon). The facelift carried out in 2020 modernized the front end, improved IntelliLux LED lighting, and introduced a new range of engines (new-generation GM 3 and 4-cylinder blocks) to meet Euro 6d standards.

Overall rating: It's an excellent road car, often underestimated on the used market, offering a formidable price/performance/equipment ratio, particularly with gasoline engines.

Strengths 5

  • Superb road handling and dynamic chassis (especially with FlexRide adaptive damping).
  • Gigantic interior space, especially in the rear seats and trunk (Sports Tourer).
  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used market (AGR massaging/ventilated seats, head-up display, etc.).
  • Formidable efficiency of the Twinster all-wheel drive (on 2.0T 260 hp and GSi versions).
  • Very refined sound insulation on gasoline engines.

Weaknesses 5

  • Very imposing size (nearly 4.90 m for the sedan, 4.99 m for the station wagon), difficult to park in the city.
  • Poor rear visibility (reversing camera essential).
  • Multimedia system (Navi 900 / Multimedia Navi Pro) sometimes slow and prone to black screen bugs.
  • Exorbitant replacement cost for IntelliLux LED matrix headlights in case of segment failure.
  • Rapid depreciation (advantage for the used car buyer, disadvantage for the seller).

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history of direct injection gasoline engines: strict verification of the oil standard used (Dexos 1 Gen 2/3) to avoid LSPI.
  • Functionality of all IntelliLux LED headlight segments at night (no error message on the dashboard).
  • On 4x4 (AWD) versions: absence of scrubbing noises or groaning from the rear axle during tight turns at low speed.
  • On diesels: history of replacements related to the AdBlue system (tank, injector) and absence of engine warning light.
  • Touchscreen functionality (responsiveness, absence of flickering) and Apple CarPlay / Android Auto connectivity.

Official recalls

  • November 2021 (Suspension links): Risk of corrosion and breakage of rear suspension links on models manufactured between 2017 and 2020. Free replacement at dealership.
  • October 2020 (Brake assist): A software defect can reduce brake assist at low speeds. ECU update.
  • June 2019 (Seat belts): Front seat belt pretensioners may not deploy correctly in the event of an accident.
  • April 2018 (Fuel hose): Possible rubbing of a fuel hose against an engine mount on 2.0 CDTi versions, risk of leakage.

What sets it apart 6

security

  • Advanced Safety and Powerful Braking — The Opel Insignia B ensures optimal protection for all its occupants, as evidenced by its excellent Euro NCAP test score. Its braking system is particularly efficient and durable, guaranteeing short stopping distances and great control in all situations.

comfort

  • Generous Interior Space and Exceptional Acoustic Comfort — Enjoy a spacious cabin, ideal for long journeys, with rear passenger space and boot volume (especially in Sports Tourer) among the best in its class. The meticulously designed sound insulation of the petrol engines contributes to a serene and relaxing atmosphere.

Performance

  • Imperial Road Handling and Surprising Agility — Thanks to its dynamic chassis and exemplary stability, the Insignia B offers safe and predictable road holding. The optional FlexRide adaptive damping further enhances its agility, providing a driving experience that is both comfortable and engaging, even in corners.
  • 1.6 Turbo Petrol Engine: Performance and Reliability — The 200 hp 1.6 Turbo engine is renowned for its dynamic performance, quiet operation, and exemplary reliability. It offers a smooth and responsive drive, ideal for long motorway journeys and serene daily use.

economy

  • Unbeatable Price/Equipment Ratio on the Used Market — The Opel Insignia B stands out for its particularly advantageous price/performance/equipment ratio on the used car market. It offers high-end features such as AGR massaging and ventilated seats or a head-up display, making every journey even more enjoyable.

practicality

  • Sleek Design and Versatility for All Uses — With its modern and lightweight design, the Insignia B combines elegance and functionality. Whether in Grand Sport or Sports Tourer version, it perfectly adapts to the needs of families and professionals, offering an excellent road car for all purposes.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 508 II (more dynamic but less spacious), Volkswagen Passat B8 (status symbol reference), Skoda Superb III (the only one to beat it in terms of interior space), Ford Mondeo, Mazda 6.
  • North America (vs Buick Regal): Honda Accord, Toyota Camry, Kia Stinger (for the Regal GS), Acura TLX.
  • Australia (vs Holden Commodore): Kia Stinger, Toyota Camry, Skoda Superb.

Verdict

The Opel Insignia B is a fantastic road car that suffers from an undeserved image deficit. With gasoline engines, it shines with its quietness and driving pleasure. The most rational choice is the 1.5 Turbo 165 hp (provided maintenance has been meticulous with the correct oil) or the very robust 1.6 Turbo 200 hp. The 2.0 Turbo versions (260 hp or GSi) are becoming collector's items thanks to their exceptional chassis, but require a significant fuel and maintenance budget (AWD differential oil change). Buy with confidence if the history is clear; it's one of the best deals on the used market for high-mileage drivers.
