BMW X5

G05 · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

All BMW models

Summary

The fourth-generation BMW X5 (G05), launched in late 2018, is based on the modular CLAR platform. It marks a significant leap forward in terms of interior finish, comfort (air suspension optional or standard depending on the version), and onboard technologies compared to the F15 generation.

In 2023, the model received a mid-cycle refresh (LCI - Life Cycle Impulse) introducing the *BMW Curved Display* (iDrive 8/8.5), generalized 48V mild hybridization on internal combustion engines (including diesels), and new powertrains (50e, M60i).

Although the market is massively shifting towards plug-in hybrids (45e/50e), diesel powertrains (B57 6-cylinder family) remain the preferred choice for high-mileage drivers and those who tow, offering an exceptional performance/consumption ratio and very high reliability, marred only by peripheral recall campaigns (EGR cooler).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional driving pleasure and fuel efficiency of the 6-cylinder diesel (30d/40d)
  • Very high-quality interior finish and sound insulation
  • Ultra-responsive and intuitive iDrive infotainment system (7.0 and 8.0)
  • Dynamic road handling for an SUV of this size (especially with rear-wheel steering)
  • Overall reliability of the 6-cylinder engines (B57 diesel and B58 petrol)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high weight (accelerates wear of brakes, tires, and running gear)
  • Extreme sensitivity of the xDrive transfer case to tire wear differences
  • Bulkiness in the city (over 2 meters wide with mirrors)
  • Cost of maintenance and spare parts in the official network
  • Disappearance of physical climate control buttons on LCI versions (2023+)

Check before buying

  • Recall history: imperatively check if the EGR cooler has been replaced (fire risk).
  • Transfer case (xDrive): turn the steering wheel fully at low speed; no jerking or scrubbing should be felt.
  • Tires: check for the star (*) on the tire sidewall (mandatory BMW homologation for xDrive) and symmetrical wear.
  • Air suspension: check that the vehicle raises and lowers without suspicious compressor noise and does not sag after a night of parking.
  • Active Grille Shutters: tendency to get stuck, generating an engine warning light.

Official recalls

What sets it apart 6

security

  • Unrivaled Safety — The BMW X5 G05 achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult occupant protection and 86% for child occupant protection, confirming its status as an ultra-safe family vehicle. It was also designated an IIHS Top Safety Pick.

braking

  • Powerful and Controlled Braking — Equipped with an exceptional braking system, the X5 G05 offers remarkable and consistent deceleration performance. It can stop from 70 mph (112 km/h) in just 165 feet (50.3 meters), ensuring first-class active safety.

handling

  • Dynamic Road Handling — Despite its size, the BMW X5 G05 surprises with its agility and dynamic road handling. Its precise steering, minimal body roll, and balanced chassis provide a confident and engaging driving experience on all types of roads.

reliability

  • Legendary B57 Diesel Engine Reliability — The B57 6-cylinder diesel engine (30d/40d) is globally renowned for its robustness and longevity. It is considered one of the best diesel engines, offering exceptional peace of mind for high-mileage drivers.

Performance

  • Enjoyable and Economical 6-Cylinder Diesel — Experience "truck-like" torque and linear power with the 6-cylinder diesel engine, delivering exceptional performance while maintaining surprisingly low fuel consumption for an SUV of this caliber.

comfort

  • Luxury and Onboard Technology — The X5 G05's interior stands out with its top-tier finish quality, remarkable sound insulation, and an ultra-responsive and intuitive iDrive infotainment system (7.0 or 8.0), transforming every journey into a premium experience.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Mercedes-Benz GLE (V167 - excellent comfort, robust diesels but capricious electronics), Audi Q7 (4M - very spacious, reliable V6 TDIs), Porsche Cayenne (PO536 - sportier, no recent diesel), Volvo XC90 (4-cylinder only).
  • North America: Lexus RX (unbeatable reliability, non-rechargeable hybrid), Acura MDX, Genesis GV80.
  • Asia: Mercedes-Benz GLE, Audi Q7, Lexus RX.

Verdict

The BMW X5 G05 is undoubtedly one of the best premium SUVs of its generation. While the market is pushing towards plug-in hybrids (45e/50e), the 30d and 40d diesel versions (B57 engine) remain the most pragmatic and reliable choice for high-mileage drivers. This engine combines quiet power with surprisingly low consumption for a 2.2-ton vehicle. Mechanical reliability is excellent, provided that the EGR cooler recall has been performed and the golden rules of the xDrive system (4 identical and star-marked tires) are strictly followed. Avoid the M50d if you fear astronomical maintenance bills, and opt for a well-optioned 30d.
