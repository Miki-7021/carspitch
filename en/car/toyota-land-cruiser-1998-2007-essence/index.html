Summary

The Toyota Land Cruiser 100 Series (J100) is an absolute icon in the world of off-road vehicles. Succeeding the legendary 80 Series, it marked a turning point by adopting (for most markets) an independent front suspension (IFS) to drastically improve road comfort and driving precision, while retaining a solid rear axle. A specific version, the 105 Series (80 chassis with 100 body and solid front/rear axles), was marketed for demanding markets (Australia, Africa, UN).

Positioned as a luxury 4x4 (Station Wagon), it stands out for its extraordinary reliability, exceptional build quality, and longevity. Gasoline versions, particularly the 4.7L V8 (2UZ-FE), offer royal comfort, remarkable operating silence, and almost unrivaled reliability, at the cost of gargantuan fuel consumption. It underwent two minor facelifts (2002 with the introduction of a 5-speed automatic gearbox, and 2005 with aesthetic revisions).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability (especially the 2UZ-FE V8)
  • Ride comfort and sound insulation worthy of a luxury sedan
  • Off-road capabilities preserved despite independent suspension
  • Build quality and material resistance to aging
  • Extremely high resale value

Weaknesses 5

  • Prohibitive fuel consumption on gasoline engines (15 to 20 L/100 km for the V8)
  • Exorbitant cost of certain specific parts (ABS unit/Master cylinder)
  • Capricious and expensive-to-repair AHC hydraulic suspension with age
  • Imposing size and high weight (braking sometimes just adequate)
  • Fragile front differential on pre-2000 models in intensive off-road use

Check before buying

  • AHC (Active Height Control) Suspension: Check that the vehicle raises and lowers quickly. If the system is slow, saggy, or bouncy, the spheres or pump are faulty (very costly repair, many convert to conventional springs).
  • Master Cylinder / ABS Pump Unit: Listen if the ABS pump's electric motor runs continuously or very frequently when braking. If so, the accumulator is dead. The complete unit costs several thousand euros.
  • Corrosion: Inspect the chassis (especially at the rear), wheel arches, and lower tailgate (a common issue).
  • Exhaust Manifolds (V8): Listen for a 'tic-tic-tic' sound when cold that disappears when warm, a sign of cracked manifolds or broken studs.
  • Steering Rack: Check for power steering fluid leaks at the boots.
  • Drivetrain Play: A 'clunk' when shifting from D to R or during acceleration indicates wear in the driveshaft U-joints or splines.

comfort

  • Luxury Sedan Comfort — Enjoy remarkable ride comfort and sound insulation, transforming every journey into a serene and pleasant experience, worthy of the finest high-end sedans.

  • Off-Road Master — Despite its improved road comfort, the Land Cruiser 100 retains impressive off-road capabilities, allowing you to explore the most demanding terrains with confidence and ease.

  • Robust Build Quality — Built with high-quality materials and meticulous attention to detail, this vehicle admirably withstands the test of time, ensuring exceptional durability and robustness.

economy

  • Exceptional Resale Value — The Land Cruiser 100 is a sound investment that maintains an extremely high resale value, testament to its reputation and constant demand in the used car market.

Verdict

The Toyota Land Cruiser 100 Series is a masterpiece of automotive engineering designed to last for several decades. If you're targeting a gasoline engine, the 4.7L V8 (2UZ-FE) is the king's choice: it offers monumental reliability, cathedral-like silence, and smooth torque. Its only real flaw is its gargantuan fuel consumption. For a confident purchase today, prioritize a model whose AHC suspension has been maintained (or replaced with conventional shock absorbers) and whose ABS/braking unit is sound. It's a safe investment that hardly depreciates anymore.
