Summary

The first-generation Skoda Kodiaq (NS7), in its phase 1 (before the 2021 facelift), marked the successful entry of the Czech manufacturer into the large family SUV segment. Based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MQB-A2 platform (shared with the VW Tiguan Allspace and Seat Tarraco), it stands out for its exceptional spaciousness, the ability to accommodate up to 7 passengers, and its practical 'Simply Clever' features. Positioned as a rational and voluminous alternative to premium SUVs, it achieved immense commercial success. Although this sheet covers all engine types, particular focus is given to diesel (TDI) engines, which represent the majority of sales in Europe for this type of heavy vehicle designed for long journeys.

Strengths 5

  • Giant interior space and record boot volume (up to 720L in 5-seater configuration)
  • Very satisfactory overall reliability of the 2.0 TDI (EA288) engines
  • Retained physical ergonomics (real climate control knobs)
  • Ride comfort, especially with DCC adaptive damping
  • Unbeatable price/performance ratio on the used car market

Weaknesses 5

  • Firmness of suspension on 19 and 20-inch wheels (without DCC)
  • Imperfect sound insulation of TDI engines when cold and under strong acceleration
  • DSG7 gearbox hesitation during restarts (Stop & Start) or at very low speeds
  • Hard plastics in the lower part of the cabin
  • Bulkiness in the city (4.70 m long and very wide)

Check before buying

  • Coolant level (symptom of a leaking water pump on TDI and TSI engines)
  • Clear history of DSG gearbox oil changes (every 60,000 km maximum)
  • Haldex coupler oil change history on 4x4 versions
  • Condition of front tires (the vehicle's high weight leads to rapid wear)
  • Smooth operation of the infotainment system (frequent bugs on early model years, requiring updates)

Official recalls

The following data comes from official registers (Rappel Conso France) and concerns the Kodiaq as well as other group models sharing the same components :

  • [2026-05-17] Side airbag (Kodiaq): An edge of the front seat inner frame may affect the integrity of the side airbag upon deployment, reducing its protection and increasing the risk of injury.
  • [2024-08-09] Driver airbag (Multi-models including Kodiaq): The propellant in the airbag may deteriorate over time. In the event of deployment, fragments of the inflator could be ejected.
  • [2023-08-07] Airbag (Multi-models including Kodiaq): Possible deterioration of the airbag over time. Excessive internal pressure during deployment may cause the inflator body to rupture.
  • [2022-09-12] Engine cover (Kodiaq, Octavia, Superb): The engine design covers (engine cover) could detach from their fastenings, leading to a risk of burns or fire upon contact with hot parts.
  • [2022-07-27] Airbag control unit (Superb/Kodiaq): Possible defect in the airbag control unit (ACU) which could impair the proper functioning of the airbags and seatbelt pretensioners.
  • [2021-11-22] Steering (Kodiaq, Octavia): The retaining ring for the steering system's belt pulley may not be fully engaged due to a manufacturing error.
  • [2021-09-03] Brake pedal (Karoq, Kodiaq Automatic): The brake pedal plate may not have been welded correctly, risking deformation or complete detachment.
  • [2020-03-27] Seat covers (Kamiq, Scala, Kodiaq): The seams of the seat covers may be defective, impairing the correct deployment of the side airbags.

Braking

  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Enjoy excellent and reassuring braking performance, with a strong and progressive pedal feel that ensures optimal control in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Safe and Predictable Road Handling — Despite its size, the Kodiaq offers excellent body control and precise steering, ensuring predictable and safe road handling for a serene drive.

Practicality

  • Record Space and Exceptional Modularity — With its giant interior space, the ability to accommodate up to 7 passengers, and a record boot volume of up to 720 liters in 5-seat configuration, the Kodiaq is the ultimate family SUV.

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability of Diesel Engines — The Kodiaq's 2.0 TDI (EA288) engines are renowned for their robustness and very satisfactory overall reliability, making this SUV a durable partner for long journeys.

Comfort

  • Superior Ride Comfort and Intuitive Ergonomics — The Kodiaq offers appreciable ride comfort, especially with DCC adaptive damping, complemented by preserved physical ergonomics with real dials for intuitive use.

Economy

  • Unbeatable Value for Money — In the used car market, the Skoda Kodiaq stands out for its exceptional value for money, offering a spacious, reliable, and well-equipped family vehicle at a very advantageous cost.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 5008 (more dynamic but less reliable engines), Volkswagen Tiguan Allspace (the technical cousin, more expensive), Seat Tarraco (the Spanish twin), Nissan X-Trail.
  • Asia: Toyota Highlander, Honda CR-V, Hyundai Santa Fe.
  • North America: The Kodiaq is not sold there. The direct alternative from VAG is the Volkswagen Atlas or the Tiguan LWB.

Verdict

The first-generation Skoda Kodiaq is an excellent rational choice. With the 2.0 TDI (150 or 190 hp) engine, it offers a very good level of reliability, provided that maintenance is scrupulously respected (especially DSG gearbox and 4x4 drivetrain oil changes). The EA288 engine is mature, although the water pump remains its Achilles' heel. It is an ideal vehicle for large families and high-mileage drivers. Avoid examples that have been used exclusively in the city with a diesel engine (risk of DPF/EGR clogging) and opt for a model with a 100% transparent service history.
