Summary

The first-generation Lynk & Co 01 (before its major facelift) is the first vehicle from the joint venture between Geely and Volvo. Based on the CMA modular platform (shared with the Volvo XC40), it is positioned as a premium, technological compact SUV that is disruptive in its distribution model (subscription).

**⚠️ EXPERT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:** Contrary to your request targeting a diesel engine, **the Lynk & Co 01 has NEVER been produced or marketed with a diesel engine**, in any global market. The brand's strategy has exclusively focused on gasoline and electrification (HEV and PHEV) since its launch. The detailed analysis below will therefore focus on the actually existing engines, explaining why the absence of diesel is not a handicap for this model.

Strengths 5

  • Technical base and passive safety inherited from Volvo (CMA platform)
  • Abundant standard equipment (almost no options)
  • Very high-level interior finish, rewarding materials
  • Real electric range of the PHEV model (often > 50 km)
  • Effective over-the-air (OTA) updates to correct early teething problems

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of a diesel engine for very high-mileage drivers
  • Highly buggy infotainment system on early model years (black screens, sluggishness)
  • Very high weight (nearly 1.9 tons for the PHEV) penalizing agility
  • Limited physical maintenance network (although supported by the Volvo network in Europe)
  • Fuel consumption with an empty battery on the PHEV (around 7.5 to 8.5 L/100km)

Check before buying

  • Software update history (crucial on this ultra-connected model)
  • Smooth operation of the central touchscreen and network connectivity
  • Absence of pronounced jerking from the DCT7 gearbox during hill starts
  • Condition of the 12V battery (tendency to discharge if the vehicle is driven infrequently)
  • On the PHEV: test a full charge to verify the on-board module

Reliability

  • Volvo Engineering Reliability and Robustness — The Lynk & Co 01 is built on Volvo's CMA platform, ensuring a solid and proven technical base. Petrol and hybrid powertrains benefit from this recognized reliability, complemented by remote software updates.

Practicality

  • Ultra-Comprehensive Standard Equipment — Enjoy a generously equipped vehicle right from the base version. The 01 offers a plethora of standard features, including advanced technologies and comfort functionalities, without costly options.

Comfort

  • Premium Interior and Refined Finish — Be captivated by a cabin with modern design and high-quality materials. The very good interior finish guarantees a most pleasant driving and travel experience, worthy of premium standards.

Economy

  • Plug-in Hybrid Efficiency and Tax Advantages — The Plug-in Hybrid (PHEV) version offers a real electric range often exceeding 50 km, ideal for daily commutes, and benefits from advantageous taxation for controlled consumption.

Handling

  • Safe and Stable Road Handling — Enjoy a serene drive thanks to predictable road behavior and excellent stability. Body roll is well controlled, offering appreciable daily comfort and a sense of security.

Braking

  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — The Lynk & Co 01 is equipped with a high-performance braking system, offering strong and progressive power. Good pedal feel and short braking distances ensure optimal active safety.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Volvo XC40 (its technical cousin), Peugeot 3008 Hybrid, Volkswagen Tiguan eHybrid, Ford Kuga PHEV. - **Asia / China**: BYD Song Plus, Haval H6, Changan CS75 Plus. - **North America**: The model was not marketed in this market during this period.

Verdict

The Lynk & Co 01 (2017-2021) is an excellent compact SUV that benefits from Volvo's robust engineering. **If you are absolutely looking for a diesel, you should walk away and turn to a Volvo XC40 D3/D4 or a Peugeot 3008 BlueHDi.** On the other hand, if you are ready to adopt the plug-in hybrid (PHEV), the 01 offers an unbeatable price/equipment/performance ratio on the used market. The mechanics are very reliable, but you must accept sometimes temperamental on-board electronics, although largely improved by over-the-air updates. Favor a model with a clear maintenance history (often managed via the app).
