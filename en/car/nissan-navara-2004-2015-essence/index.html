III (D40) · 2004 – 2015

NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

All Nissan models

Summary

The third-generation Nissan Navara (D40), also sold under the name Nissan Frontier in North America, marked a turning point in the pickup truck world by introducing a level of comfort and road performance close to that of an SUV. Based on the F-Alpha platform (shared with the Pathfinder), it stands out for its imposing size and towing capabilities. Although the European market was almost exclusively dominated by diesel engines (2.5 dCi and 3.0 dCi), the petrol engines (2.5L and 4.0L V6) made the model a success in North America, the Middle East, and Oceania. These petrol blocks offer superior drivability and avoid the costly emissions control problems (DPF/FAP, EGR) of the diesels, but they require strict vigilance regarding the automatic transmission (radiator defect) and the timing system. Overall rating: A highly versatile pickup, but its reliability depends heavily on the production year, the assembly plant (beware of chassis corrosion on Spanish-built models), and preventive maintenance.

Strengths 5

  • Extremely robust petrol engines (4.0 V6) if the radiator issue is ruled out
  • Off-road and towing capabilities (up to 3 tons)
  • Ride comfort superior to competing pickup trucks of the same era
  • Roominess of the Double Cab version
  • Absence of problematic DPF/FAP and EGR on petrol versions

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic perforating chassis corrosion (especially models assembled in Spain for Europe), which can lead to chassis failure
  • SMOD issue (coolant/transmission fluid mixture) on automatic petrol V6s (2005-2010)
  • Very high fuel consumption on the 4.0 V6 petrol (13-15 L/100km)
  • Fragile timing chain on the 2.5 dCi
  • Quality of interior plastics (scratch easily)

Check before buying

  • MANDATORY CHASSIS INSPECTION: check for perforating rust, particularly between the cab and the bed.
  • On automatic petrol versions: check the color of the transmission fluid (must be translucent red/pink, NEVER opaque or milky).
  • Timing chain noise on cold start (clattering on dCi, high-pitched whining on petrol V6).
  • Condition of the driveshaft U-joints (vibrations at 80-100 km/h).
  • Operation of the 4x4 selector dial (transfer case actuators can seize if not used).

Official recalls

Here is the official recall data (including the raw data provided, with expert context):

  • [2022-07-04] Takata Airbag (Navara, Almera, Patrol, etc.): Code R1803, PS8B0. A defect in the passenger airbag gas generator causes uncontrolled inflation and the release of metal fragments. Risk of serious injury. *Expert note: Major global recall, must be checked on any used D40.*

  • [2019-07-12] Handbrake cable (NV300): Code PN9B3. Incorrect installation of the cable stop. *Expert note: Concerns the NV300 utility vehicle, not applicable to the Navara D40.*

Practicality

Alternatives

Alternatives vary greatly depending on the market and the desired engine:

  • North America (Against the Frontier V6): Toyota Tacoma (4.0 V6, absolute benchmark in reliability but overpriced), Chevrolet Colorado / GMC Canyon (1st generation, inferior finish).
  • Europe / Rest of the world (Against the Navara dCi): Toyota Hilux (Vigo, 3.0 D-4D, indestructible but rustic comfort), Mitsubishi L200 (Triton, excellent Super Select transmission), Ford Ranger (T6 from 2011 onwards, more modern), Isuzu D-Max (very utilitarian and reliable).

Verdict

The Nissan Navara D40 (Frontier) is a pickup with two faces. If you opt for a petrol version (4.0 V6 VQ40DE), you benefit from an extremely robust and high-performance engine, ideal for escaping the breakdowns of modern diesels. However, buying an automatic transmission version from before 2011 requires an absolute check of the radiator to avoid the SMOD syndrome that destroys the transmission. The absolute black spot of this model, across all engines, remains the structural chassis corrosion (particularly on European models). A sound example, treated against rust, with a petrol V6 and a reliable radiator, is an excellent purchase. The diesel versions (2.5 dCi) require much more caution (timing chain).
