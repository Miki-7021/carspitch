Summary

The BMW iX3 (G08) is the first 100% electric SUV from the Bavarian brand. Based on the CLAR platform of the ICE X3 (G01), it inaugurates the 5th generation of the BMW electric powertrain (eDrive Gen5), characterized by an electrically excited synchronous motor (without rare earths). Although it is an adaptation of an ICE platform (which implies the presence of a transmission tunnel in the rear and the absence of a front trunk or 'frunk'), the iX3 shines with its remarkable energy efficiency, top-flight comfort, and flawless build quality. It received a very early facelift (LCI) at the end of 2021 to align with the aesthetic and technological evolutions of the ICE X3. It is a rear-wheel drive (RWD) model only, which incidentally motivated BMW's decision not to market it in North America.

Strengths 5

  • Impressive energy efficiency for an SUV not specifically aero-optimized
  • Finish and assembly quality (manufactured in China in Shenyang, rigorous BMW standards)
  • Suspension comfort (adaptive damping as standard) and soundproofing
  • Physical ergonomics retained (real buttons for climate control)
  • Reliability of the 5th generation eDrive powertrain

Weaknesses 5

  • No 4-wheel drive (xDrive) version available
  • Fast charging speed capped at 150 kW (in the lower average today)
  • Absence of a front trunk (Frunk) under the hood
  • Central service tunnel in the rear (legacy of the ICE platform)
  • High weight (nearly 2.2 tons) impacting agility

Check before buying

  • Tire wear, particularly on the rear axle (RWD).
  • Condition of the aerodynamic rims (very exposed to curbs).
  • Proper operation of the charging flap and the cable locking pin (sometimes capricious in cold weather).
  • Software update history (iDrive) to avoid black screen bugs.
  • Condition of the front suspension bushings and control arms (accelerated wear due to weight).

Official recalls

According to official data (Rappel Conso / BMW):

  • Airbags (Internal ref, reported for June 2026/source date error): The head airbags may not have been bolted according to specifications, risking improper deployment in the event of an impact.
  • February 2022 (Electrical system): In the event of a very severe accident damaging the high-voltage system, the 12-volt system and its components may be affected, leading to a risk of injury.
  • August 2021 (Braking): The defective hydraulic unit of the braking system can lead to limited braking assistance and an increased braking distance (recall shared with the 7 Series, 8 Series, X5, X7, etc.).

*(Note: The recalls concerning the ISOFIX mounts of the X6/X6M and the third brake light of the M6 Gran Coupé, although present in some cross-referenced databases, do not concern the G08 architecture of the iX3).*

Verdict

The BMW iX3 (G08) is an excellent choice on the used electric vehicle market. Although it betrays its ICE origins with a few architectural compromises (central tunnel, no frunk), it more than compensates with formidable efficiency, often superior to that of competitors with dedicated platforms. The reliability of the eDrive Gen5 motor has been exemplary so far, without the flaws of the brand's older ICE engines. If possible, favor a facelifted version (LCI, late 2021) to benefit from the M Sport Package as standard and a more modern infotainment system. Purchase is highly recommended, provided that the absence of all-wheel drive is not a dealbreaker for your use.
