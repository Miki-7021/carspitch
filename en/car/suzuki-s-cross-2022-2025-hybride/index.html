Summary

The second-generation Suzuki S-Cross (sometimes considered the 3rd generation if the original SX4 is included) was launched in late 2021 for the 2022 model year. It is positioned as a pragmatic, rational, and particularly reliable compact SUV (C-SUV). Unlike many competitors that focus on design or technological gadgets, the S-Cross concentrates on the essentials: decent interior space, an aggressive price-to-equipment ratio, and above all, the availability of an excellent AWD system (AllGrip). Mechanically, it opted for an all-hybrid lineup in Europe (48V mild-hybrid and 140V self-charging hybrid). It is a vehicle of choice for buyers seeking long-term peace of mind, although its interior presentation and soundproofing lag behind European standards.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall mechanical reliability (chain-driven engines, proven technologies)
  • Highly effective and affordable AllGrip AWD system
  • Very competitive price-to-equipment ratio
  • Very low real-world fuel consumption, particularly on the 1.4 MHEV
  • Physical ergonomics retained (real climate control buttons)

Weaknesses 5

  • Slow and jerky AGS automated gearbox on the 1.5 HEV
  • Interior material quality (omnipresent hard plastics)
  • Soundproofing could be improved on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Infotainment system interface is somewhat dated
  • Trunk volume reduced on the 1.5 HEV version (battery under the floor)

Check before buying

  • Brake pedal behavior (following the vacuum pump recall, check if the assistance is consistent).
  • On the 1.5 HEV: smoothness of AGS gearbox shifts. Excessive shuddering indicates a need for recalibration or clutch intervention.
  • On AllGrip versions: condition of the underbody, driveshaft, and rear differential (signs of impact or leaks if used off-road).
  • Operation of the central touchscreen (some cases of random reboots requiring a software update).

Official recalls

France / Europe - [2023-02-22] Braking (Rappel Conso): Affects Swift, Swift Sport, Baleno, Vitara, and S-Cross models. Reason: Due to the swelling of the valve in the vacuum pump, the negative pressure of the brake boosters can be temporarily blocked. Consequence: The effort required on the brake pedal can suddenly increase, lengthening the braking distance. Risk of injury/accident. Correction: Replacement of the vacuum pump valve or the complete pump depending on the diagnosis.

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Proven Mechanical Reliability — Enjoy lasting peace of mind thanks to robust mechanics, including its chain-driven engines and reliable hybrid technologies, making the S-Cross one of the most dependable SUVs in its class.

Safety

  • Certified Maximum Safety — The Suzuki S-Cross II ensures your protection with a 5-star Euro NCAP rating, guaranteeing optimal safety for adult and child occupants.

Braking

  • Effective and Progressive Braking — Benefit from an efficient and progressive braking system, offering excellent pedal feel and reassuring performance for all your daily drives.

Handling

  • Serene and Stable Road Holding — The S-Cross II offers predictable and stable road holding, prioritizing comfort and safety, particularly appreciated on long highway journeys.

Economy

  • Remarkable Fuel Economy — Enjoy very low fuel consumption, especially with the 1.4 Boosterjet MHEV engine, significantly reducing your daily running costs.
  • Excellent Value for Money — The Suzuki S-Cross II stands out with a very competitive price/equipment ratio, offering generous features for a controlled investment.

Practicality

  • AllGrip Traction for All Conditions — The highly effective and affordable AllGrip all-wheel drive system ensures optimal grip and enhanced safety on all types of terrain and in all weather conditions.

Alternatives

Europe - Dacia Duster: Direct competitor in terms of price-to-performance ratio and 4x4 capabilities, similar finishes, but the S-Cross offers better hybridization. - Toyota Yaris Cross / Corolla Cross: The Yaris Cross is smaller but offers a hybrid system (HSD) far superior in drivability to Suzuki's AGS. - Peugeot 2008: More modern, better finished, but mechanical reliability (1.2 PureTech) is vastly inferior to the Suzuki. - Hyundai Kona: Bolder design, excellent warranty, highly refined hybrid system with a dual-clutch gearbox.

Asia / Emerging markets - Hyundai Creta: Very popular, good equipment, but no AWD equivalent to AllGrip. - Honda HR-V: More expensive, excellent modularity (Magic Seats), reliable engines.

Verdict

The Suzuki S-Cross II is a highly recommendable purchase for pragmatic motorists who prioritize reliability, low running costs, and traction (AllGrip) over design or interior luxury. It is one of the most reliable SUVs in its class. Buying advice: Without hesitation, favor the 1.4 Boosterjet MHEV (129 hp) engine. It is flexible, capable, very fuel-efficient, and free of major flaws. The 1.5 Dualjet HEV (115 hp) version, although mechanically reliable, is let down by an outdated AGS automated gearbox that significantly degrades driving pleasure, except for strictly urban and very placid use.
