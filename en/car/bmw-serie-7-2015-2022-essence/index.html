G11/G12 (2015–2022) · 2015 – 2022

NEGOTIATE Reliability 72/100 — bon

Summary

The BMW 7 Series, G11 (standard wheelbase) and G12 (long wheelbase) generations, represents the technological flagship and the pinnacle of luxury for the Bavarian brand during this period. Launched in 2015, it introduced major innovations such as the Carbon Core structure (a blend of steel, aluminum, and carbon fiber), gesture control, the Display Key, and standard air suspension. Positioned against the Mercedes S-Class and Audi A8, it emphasizes a more assertive driving dynamic while offering exceptional comfort. A facelift (LCI) occurred in 2019, bringing aesthetic modifications (more imposing grille, refined lights) and technological updates, notably the introduction of more powerful plug-in hybrid powertrains and an evolution of the V8 gasoline engines. Overall, the G11/G12 is an impressive car, but its technological complexity and powerful engines imply potentially high maintenance and repair costs.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional ride comfort (standard air suspension)
  • Cutting-edge onboard technology (iDrive, gesture control, Display Key, driving aids)
  • High quality of finish and interior materials
  • Surprising driving dynamics for the category (especially with options like integral active steering)
  • Powerful and refined engines (particularly the B58 and B57 diesels)
  • Remarkable sound insulation

Weaknesses 6

  • Technological complexity can lead to high repair costs
  • Significant depreciation, especially on high-end versions
  • High maintenance costs, especially for V8 and V12 models
  • High fuel consumption for V8 and V12 models
  • Bulkiness in the city (especially the long-wheelbase G12 version)
  • Reliability of the iDrive system and electronic sensors (occasional software bugs)

Check before buying

  • Complete and detailed service history (invoices, logbook)
  • Air suspension operation (no noises, no sagging, compressor)
  • Absence of engine oil leaks (particularly on N63 and N74)
  • Condition and operation of all electronic equipment (iDrive, driving aids, massage seats, etc.)
  • Tire and brake wear (expensive to replace)
  • Condition of the 12V battery and high-voltage battery for hybrid versions
  • Check for completed manufacturer recalls
  • Thorough road test to detect any suspicious noises or abnormal gearbox behavior

Verdict

The BMW 7 Series G11/G12 is an exceptional luxury limousine, offering a unique blend of comfort, technology, and dynamism. However, its acquisition as a used car requires careful consideration. The technological complexity and powerful engines imply potentially very high maintenance and repair costs.

**For daily use and a controlled budget**, the **730i/Li (B48)** or **740i/Li (B58)** versions are the most recommendable. The B58 engine is particularly reliable and offers an excellent performance/consumption compromise. The **B57** diesels are also very good choices for high-mileage drivers, provided the EGR system is monitored.

**The 750i/Li (N63) and M760Li (N74) versions** offer exhilarating performance but are aimed at informed buyers willing to bear very significant maintenance and repair costs. The reliability of the N63 has improved, but it remains a demanding engine. The V12 is a magnificent piece of engineering but expensive to maintain.

**The plug-in hybrids (740e/745e)** are interesting for tax advantages and city fuel consumption, but require a thorough check of the hybrid system and battery.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize a model with a clear maintenance history, performed at a dealership or by a BMW specialist, and do not hesitate to have a complete pre-purchase inspection performed. Depreciation is significant, making it an excellent used car deal, but never underestimate the budget required for maintenance and potential repairs. Negotiate the price firmly, as sellers know that maintenance costs are a deterrent.
