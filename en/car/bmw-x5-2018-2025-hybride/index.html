BMW X5

G05 · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The **BMW X5 (G05)**, launched in late 2018, represents the fourth generation of the famous Bavarian SUV. Based on the modular CLAR platform, it marks a spectacular leap forward in interior finish, comfort (adaptive air suspension), and onboard technologies.

**Hybrid Focus (PHEV):** The X5 G05 has established itself as the absolute benchmark for luxury plug-in hybrid SUVs. The **xDrive45e** (2019-2023) abandoned the previous generation's 4-cylinder for the masterful B58 inline 6-cylinder, coupled with a large 24 kWh (gross) battery, offering a real-world electric range of 60 to 80 km. During the facelift (LCI) in 2023, it was replaced by the **xDrive50e**, which boosts combined power to 490 hp and integrates a 29.5 kWh battery, approaching 100 km of WLTP range.

Overall, the G05 is an extremely well-designed vehicle, supported by the remarkable reliability of the B58 petrol engine, although its enormous weight (nearly 2.5 tons in PHEV) heavily stresses the running gear.

Strengths 6

  • Perfect marriage between the B58 6-cylinder and hybridization (45e/50e)
  • Exceptional real-world electric range for a PHEV (often > 70 km)
  • Royal comfort with air suspension (standard on PHEV)
  • High-end interior finish quality and sound insulation
  • iDrive 7 infotainment system (intuitive) and iDrive 8 (spectacular)
  • Overall reliability of 6-cylinder engines (petrol and diesel)

Weaknesses 5

  • Excessive weight of PHEV versions (2,510 kg for the 45e), impacting agility and tire wear
  • Reduced boot volume on hybrid versions (battery under the floor)
  • Frequent electronic bugs on early LCI models (iDrive 8, 2023-2024)
  • Cost of options and spare parts (especially air suspension)
  • Imposing size (2 meters wide), difficult to park in European cities

Check before buying

  • Charging history (PHEV): an uncharged hybrid wears out faster and consumes more fuel.
  • Transfer case (xDrive): turn the steering wheel fully at low speed; if jerks or scrubbing are felt, the transfer case needs fluid change or replacement.
  • Air suspension: check that the vehicle raises and lowers without excessive compressor noise and does not sag after a night of parking.
  • EGR cooler (Diesel): check if the vehicle has undergone the manufacturer's recall (proven fire risk).
  • Tire wear: rear tires (often 20, 21, or 22 inches) wear out quickly under the weight and torque.

braking

  • Exceptional Braking Control — Despite its stature, the X5 G05 offers remarkable braking performance, typical of BMW. It stops powerfully and consistently, with excellent pedal feel, capable of coming to a halt from 70 mph (113 km/h) in just 165 feet (50.3 meters).

handling

  • Sport Sedan-like Dynamics — The BMW X5 G05 redefines handling for an SUV, offering precise steering, minimal body roll (especially with adaptive suspension), and surprising agility. It provides an engaging driving experience, worthy of a sport sedan.

economy

  • Record-breaking Daily Electric Range — The plug-in hybrid versions (xDrive45e and xDrive50e) stand out with exceptional real-world electric range, regularly covering over 70 km (and up to nearly 100 km for the 50e) in zero-emission mode, optimizing your daily commutes and budget.

Performance

  • Perfect Harmony of 6-Cylinder and Hybrid Power — Enjoy the refinement and power of the B58 inline 6-cylinder engine, beautifully paired with the hybrid powertrain. This combination delivers first-class smoothness and performance, with a combined output of up to 490 hp for the xDrive50e.

comfort

  • Royal Comfort and Premium Finish — The X5 G05 offers exceptional ride comfort, notably thanks to its standard air suspension on hybrid versions. The cabin boasts a very high level of interior finish and sound insulation, transforming every journey into a serene and luxurious experience.

reliability

  • Proven Reliability of the B58 6-Cylinder Engine — At the heart of the hybrid versions, the B58 inline 6-cylinder engine is renowned for its robustness and exemplary reliability. This powertrain, praised by experts, ensures lasting peace of mind and consistent performance.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Mercedes-Benz GLE 350de / 400e (excellent diesel/petrol PHEV range), Porsche Cayenne E-Hybrid (more dynamic), Audi Q8 55 TFSI e, Volvo XC90 Recharge (7-seater PHEV, unlike the X5). - **North America:** Lexus RX 450h+ / TX, Acura MDX, Genesis GV80, Lincoln Aviator Grand Touring. - **Asia:** Lexus RX, NIO ES8 (100% electric), Li Auto L9 (very popular EREV in China).

Verdict

The BMW X5 G05 is undoubtedly the most accomplished premium SUV of its generation. In its plug-in hybrid versions (**xDrive45e** and **xDrive50e**), it borders on perfection, offering the best of both worlds: the refinement and reliability of the B58 inline 6-cylinder, coupled with sufficient electric range for daily use. It is a highly recommended purchase. However, this technological behemoth requires rigorous maintenance. Avoid diesel models that have not undergone the EGR recall, steer clear of the complex M50d outside of warranty, and plan for a substantial budget for consumables (tires, brakes) which suffer under the vehicle's 2.5 tons.
