Suzuki Alto

VI · 2002 – 2008

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The Suzuki Alto marketed in Europe between 2002 and 2008 (often referred to as the 6th European generation) is actually a rebadged and adapted version of the Indian Maruti Suzuki Alto (code RF410). Designed as an ultra-economical city car (A-segment), it stands out for its mechanical simplicity, light weight (around 800 kg), and ridiculously low running costs. While it foregoes comfort, soundproofing, and modern safety equipment, it compensates with formidable mechanical reliability. In Japan, the true 6th generation (HA24) was launched in 2004 to meet *Kei car* standards with a 660 cc engine.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability (indestructible engines if maintained)
  • Maintenance and insurance costs among the lowest on the market
  • Very low fuel consumption
  • Ideal size for city parking
  • Mechanical accessibility (easy to repair yourself)

Weaknesses 5

  • Passive and active safety from another era (no ESP, poor crash test)
  • Almost non-existent soundproofing (very noisy on highways)
  • Spartan comfort (harsh suspension, basic seats)
  • Susceptibility to corrosion (chassis, wheel arches, exhaust)
  • Very low-end interior plastic quality

Check before buying

  • **Corrosion:** Thoroughly inspect the underbody, rear wheel arches, sills, and exhaust system.
  • **Clutch:** Often abused in exclusively urban use. Check the free play and absence of slippage.
  • **Wheel bearings:** Frequent premature wear, listen for humming noises while driving.
  • **Engine idle (1.1L):** An unstable or stalling idle indicates a clogged throttle body or idle actuator.
  • **Suspension:** Shock absorbers and bushings age poorly, causing clunking noises over potholes.

Economy

  • Unbeatable Running Costs — Benefit from some of the lowest maintenance and insurance costs on the market, combined with very low fuel consumption, for negligible per-mile expenses.
  • Simplified and Accessible Maintenance — Thanks to its simple and proven mechanical design, the Alto is easy to maintain, further contributing to reducing your expenses.

Practicality

  • Urban Queen — Its compact size and agility make it the ideal vehicle for navigating traffic and parking with surprising ease, even in the tightest spaces.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Proven 1.1L Engine — The 1.1L (F10D) engine offers surprising liveliness in the city and appreciable flexibility, while benefiting from a simple and reliable design.
  • Lightweight and Agile — With its featherlight weight, the Suzuki Alto offers agile and responsive driving, making every urban journey more enjoyable and contributing to its excellent fuel efficiency.

Alternatives

Verdict

The Suzuki Alto (2002-2008) is the archetype of the urban 'runabout'. If you are looking for a prestigious, comfortable, or long-distance highway vehicle, look elsewhere. However, if your goal is to find a small used car with an unbeatable per-kilometer budget, capable of squeezing anywhere, and equipped with mechanics that will never let you down, it's an excellent choice. Purchase is recommended provided you find a rust-free example with a replaced timing belt.
