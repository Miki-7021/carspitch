Summary

The first-generation Hyundai i30 (code FD) marked a historic turning point for the Korean manufacturer. Designed at the R&D center in Rüsselsheim, Germany, and a technical cousin to the Kia Cee'd (ED), it was specifically conceived to meet European standards. Initially produced in South Korea, its manufacturing was relocated to Nošovice (Czech Republic) in late 2008. It stands out for its generous spaciousness, rich standard equipment, and sound road handling, although it lagged behind European benchmarks of the time in terms of dynamism. A facelift occurred in 2010, bringing slight aesthetic touches (grille, bumpers) and finish improvements. It is a pivotal model that proved Hyundai's ability to produce reliable and durable vehicles. Overall score: 7.5/10.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent overall reliability of gasoline engines (Gamma 1.4/1.6)
  • Spaciousness and trunk volume (340L sedan, 415L CW wagon)
  • Generous standard equipment for its time (air conditioning, USB/Aux sockets)
  • Reasonable maintenance cost and accessible parts
  • Safe road handling (multi-link rear suspension)

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation (road and aerodynamic noise on the highway)
  • Quality of some interior plastics (easy scratches, peeling)
  • Electric power steering (EPS) lacking feel and prone to a bushing defect
  • Fragile paint (clear coat peeling, especially on Red and Blue)
  • Slightly high fuel consumption for gasoline engines compared to current standards

Check before buying

  • Noise in the steering column: a 'clunk' when turning the wheel at a standstill indicates wear of the rubber coupler (flector) of the EPS motor (a €5 part but 2 hours of labor).
  • Timing chain (1.4 and 1.6 gasoline): listen for any metallic rattling when cold (worn tensioner).
  • Running gear: rapid wear of stabilizer bar links (dull thuds over bumps).
  • Onboard electronics: check proper functioning of the car radio display (frequent dead pixels) and power windows.
  • Brake light switch: check that the brake lights illuminate correctly (very common defect, subject to recalls).

Official recalls

  • April 2009 / May 2013: Replacement of the brake light switch. A malfunction could prevent the brake lights from illuminating or deactivate the ESP.
  • March 2010: Reprogramming of the electric power steering (EPS) control unit following reported losses of assistance.
  • July 2012: Inspection and possible replacement of the parking brake cable on certain models produced between 2007 and 2008.

Verdict

The first-generation Hyundai i30 is an excellent choice in the budget used car market, especially with gasoline engines (1.4 or 1.6 MPI). These chain-driven engines prove to be extremely robust if basic maintenance (regular oil changes) has been respected. Although it doesn't offer the refinement of a Volkswagen Golf VI or the dynamism of a Ford Focus, it compensates with above-average reliability for its time in Europe and controlled running costs. The main issue to check before purchase remains the noise in the steering column (EPS coupler). Opt for a facelifted model (post-2010) for a better interior finish.
