Citroën C4 Picasso

I · 2006 – 2013

NEGOTIATE Reliability 55/100 — moyen

All Citroën models

Summary

The first-generation Citroën C4 Picasso (available in a short 5-seater version and a 7-seater 'Grand C4 Picasso') marked its era with its 'Visiospace' concept, offering an immense glass area, exceptional roominess, and top-flight comfort. However, its reliability record is heavily tarnished by hazardous technological choices: the slow and fragile automated manual gearbox (BMP6), the temperamental pneumatic rear suspension, and above all, widely distributed engines (1.6 HDi 110 and 1.6 THP) suffering from major design flaws. A model to buy with extreme caution, targeting exclusively the 2.0 HDi engines.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional roominess and modularity (retractable individual seats)
  • Luminosity and visibility (Visiospace panoramic windshield)
  • Very high-level ride comfort (especially with standard suspension)
  • Reliability and drivability of the 2.0 HDi engines
  • Giant trunk volume (especially on the Grand C4 Picasso)

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic reliability of the 1.6 HDi 110 (turbo) and 1.6 THP (timing)
  • Unpleasant (jolts, slowness) and unreliable BMP6 automated manual gearbox
  • Very fragile and expensive to repair pneumatic rear suspension (Exclusive trim)
  • Premature wear of interior plastics (peeling of steering wheels and handles)
  • Frequent electronic bugs (electric parking brake, phantom warning lights)

Check before buying

  • Clear oil change history on the 1.6 HDi (presence of black leak around the injectors = run away).
  • Behavior of the BMP6 gearbox during the test drive: any shuddering or violent jolt when starting indicates a clutch/flywheel at the end of its life.
  • Rear suspension (on Exclusive): the vehicle must maintain its ride height with the engine off. If it is 'sitting on the ground', air springs or compressor are dead.
  • Operation of the electric parking brake (EPB) and absence of error messages on the dashboard.
  • Condition of the front seat bases (the foams tend to sag prematurely).

Verdict

The first-generation Citroën C4 Picasso is a family vehicle full of practical qualities, but undermined by disastrous reliability on its best-selling versions. You must absolutely avoid the 1.6 HDi 110 hp, the 1.6 THP, and the BMP6 automated gearbox, under penalty of financial ruin. If you are looking for this model for its space and comfort, target only a 2.0 HDi (136, 150 or 163 hp) with a manual gearbox or a real automatic gearbox (BVA6), and favor a trim without the pneumatic rear suspension (avoid the Exclusive or verify that the air springs have been replaced by standard springs, a common modification).
