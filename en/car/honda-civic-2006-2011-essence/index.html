Honda Civic

VIII (FN/FK) · 2006 – 2011

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The 8th generation Honda Civic marked a radical stylistic break. In Europe, the 3 and 5-door versions (FN/FK chassis) earned the nickname "UFO" due to their futuristic design, two-tier instrumentation, and hidden rear door handles. Beyond its style, it shines with exceptional practicality thanks to "Magic Seats" (fold-up rear seat bases) and a record trunk volume for its category (485 liters). In other markets (North America, Asia), the Civic VIII retained more classic sedan (FD) and coupe (FG) body styles.

**Positioning:** C-segment compact, focusing on spaciousness, dynamism, and mechanical reliability. **Facelift:** Late 2008 (2009 model) with a perforated grille replacing the plexiglass panel, new taillights, higher quality interior plastics, and the introduction of the 1.4 i-VTEC 100 hp engine. **Overall reliability rating:** Excellent for gasoline engines, average for diesel due to fragile peripherals.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of gasoline engines (especially the 1.8 i-VTEC)
  • Unbeatable spaciousness and modularity (Magic Seats, 485L trunk)
  • Exterior and interior design that ages very well
  • Driver-oriented control ergonomics
  • Dynamic and precise road handling

Weaknesses 6

  • Very firm suspension (especially at the rear, uncomfortable on degraded roads)
  • Poor rear visibility (spoiler cutting the rear window in two)
  • Paint quality (very thin, susceptible to stone chips)
  • Interior plastics prone to scratches (dashboard plexiglass)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway
  • Reliability of peripherals on the 2.2 i-CTDi diesel engine

Check before buying

  • Clutch pedal: frequent squeaks or clicks (clutch master cylinder to be greased or replaced).
  • Electrically folding mirrors: the folding module (polyswitch) often burns out.
  • Central display (radio/AC): pixel loss or ghosting (dry solder joints).
  • Rear shock absorbers: rubber bump stops disintegrate over time.
  • Water ingress: check trunk floor and rear lights (porous seals).
  • 2.2 i-CTDi Diesel: exhaust gas smell in the cabin (cracked manifold) and clutch slip in 4th/5th gear.

Official recalls

  • **Takata Airbags (Massive, 2015-2019):** Replacement of front airbag inflators (driver and passenger) at risk of exploding and projecting metallic fragments.
  • **Handbrake (2007):** Risk of unintended release of the parking brake. Installation of a new locking pawl (2006-2007 models).
  • **Electrical harness (2008):** Possible rubbing of a harness near the steering column that could cause a short circuit.
  • **VSA/ABS Module (2010):** Possible air leak in the VSA modulator leading to a soft brake pedal (2006-2007 models).

Verdict

The Honda Civic VIII is an exceptional used car choice, provided you target the gasoline engines. The 1.8 i-VTEC 140 hp engine is the jewel of the range: it offers a perfect balance of performance, fuel consumption, and bulletproof reliability. The "UFO" design of the European version hides an incredibly practical everyday car. However, you will have to accept a firm suspension comfort and fragile paintwork.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize a gasoline model (1.4 or 1.8 i-VTEC) post-facelift (2009-2011) to benefit from better interior plastics and refined aesthetics. Avoid the 2.2 i-CTDi diesel unless it has a clear service history (clutch/flywheel and manifold already replaced), and steer clear of the IMA Hybrid version whose batteries age very poorly.
