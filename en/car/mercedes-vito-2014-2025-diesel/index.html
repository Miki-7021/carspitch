Summary

The Mercedes-Benz Vito W447, launched in 2014, represents the third generation of the light commercial van from the star brand. It succeeds the W639 and is positioned as a versatile vehicle, available in panel van (pure utility), Mixto (semi-glazed, 5-6 seats), and Tourer (minivan, up to 9 seats) versions. It shares its platform with the luxurious V-Class, thus offering a level of comfort and finish superior to the average for its category, particularly in the Tourer versions.

A major facelift occurred in late 2019/early 2020 (often called 'Facelift 2020'), bringing minor aesthetic changes, but above all the introduction of new, more efficient diesel engines (OM654) compliant with Euro 6d-TEMP/Euro 6d standards, as well as technological improvements (infotainment, driving aids).

The reliability of the Vito W447 is generally good, but it is highly dependent on the chosen engine and maintenance. The diesel versions, the core of the range, have undergone significant developments in terms of robustness and technical complexity. The model is also marketed as the Metris in North America, with specific petrol engines.

Strengths 7

  • Versatility of body styles (Panel Van, Mixto, Tourer)
  • Above-average driving comfort and sound insulation
  • Interior finish quality (especially Tourer/V-Class)
  • Wide choice of engines (diesel, petrol, electric)
  • High loading and towing capacities
  • Mercedes-Benz brand image
  • 4x4 versions available (OM651/OM654)

Weaknesses 7

  • Higher purchase and maintenance costs than mainstream competitors
  • Uneven reliability depending on engines (OM622, early OM651)
  • Potential problems with the AdBlue system on recent diesels
  • DPF sensitivity to short trips on all diesels
  • Some corrosion issues on chassis and wheel arches (especially on early models)
  • Standard equipment sometimes sparse on basic utility versions
  • Limited range for the eVito

Check before buying

  • Complete maintenance history and invoices (especially for oil changes and recalls)
  • Condition of the EGR valve and DPF (for diesels, check for fault codes)
  • Abnormal engine noises (timing chain on OM651/M274)
  • Oil or coolant leaks
  • AdBlue system operation (warning lights, consumption, fault history)
  • Condition of tires, brakes, and suspension (especially if intensive use)
  • Presence of corrosion under the vehicle and on opening parts
  • Operation of air conditioning and onboard electronics
  • For the eVito: battery State of Health (SoH) and charging history

Verdict

The Mercedes-Benz Vito W447 is a utility vehicle of choice for those seeking a versatile, comfortable, and well-finished vehicle with a prestigious brand image. However, its acquisition and maintenance costs are higher than average.

For a confident purchase: * Prioritize OM654 diesel versions (post-facelift 2019/2020): They offer the best combination of performance, efficiency, and reliability, despite the need for vigilance regarding the AdBlue system. * The OM651 (2.1L diesel) is a good compromise if the maintenance history is impeccable and recalls (especially injectors/chain) have been carried out. Check for any suspicious noises. * The OM622 (1.6L diesel) should be considered with caution for intensive use or long distances. They are better suited for smaller budgets and light use. * The Metris petrol (M274) in North America should be negotiated due to timing chain and consumption issues. * The eVito is a relevant option for urban fleets with dedicated charging infrastructure, but its range remains a limiting factor.

In all cases, a complete maintenance history and a thorough inspection by a professional are essential. The Vito W447 is a good investment if well-chosen and well-maintained, but it can become a financial drain if potential weaknesses are not anticipated.
