Summary

The first-generation **Land Rover Range Rover Velar (L560)** was introduced in 2017 to fill the gap between the Evoque and the Range Rover Sport. Based on the iQ-Al (D7a) aluminum platform shared with the Jaguar F-Pace, it immediately stood out with its avant-garde design, flush door handles, and uncluttered cabin inaugurating the *Touch Pro Duo* dual-screen infotainment system.

Positioned as a luxurious SUV oriented towards style and road comfort (while retaining the brand's signature off-road capabilities via *Terrain Response 2*), the Velar enjoyed strong critical acclaim. However, like many Jaguar Land Rover (JLR) productions of this era, it is marred by capricious electronic reliability and engines (notably the Ingenium diesels) requiring rigorous monitoring. The gasoline versions, although thirsty, prove to be generally more reliable in the long term if maintenance is scrupulous.

Strengths 6

  • Spectacular and timeless exterior design
  • Luxurious and minimalist interior presentation
  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with the air suspension)
  • Excellent cabin soundproofing
  • Top-tier Meridian audio system
  • Real off-road capabilities despite its boulevard SUV appearance

Weaknesses 6

  • Very capricious overall electronic reliability (screens, sensors)
  • Flush door handles prone to failure (mechanisms blocked by frost or wear)
  • Very high weight penalizing agility and fuel consumption
  • Disastrous reliability of the 4-cylinder diesel engines (Ingenium)
  • Very expensive maintenance and spare parts costs
  • Average rear seat space relative to its size

Check before buying

  • Smooth deployment and retraction of the 4 exterior door handles.
  • Lag-free and crash-free operation of the two central screens and the digital dashboard.
  • Raising and lowering of the air suspension (listen if the compressor runs too long = leak in an air spring).
  • Smell of coolant under the hood (V6 and V8 gasoline engines).
  • Clear maintenance history: avoid vehicles that followed the 30,000 km intervals (favor those with oil changes every 15,000 km).

Alternatives

Verdict

The Range Rover Velar is a purchase of passion. Its spectacular design and refined cabin make it one of the most desirable SUVs of its generation. However, reason must temper passion: electronic reliability is hit-or-miss and repair costs can be astronomical.

**Buying advice**: Steer clear of the 4-cylinder diesel engines (D180/D240) whose reliability is disastrous. Favor the gasoline versions (P250 for reason, P340/P380 for pleasure), ensure that all recalls have been performed, and meticulously check the operation of every electronic feature (screens, handles, suspension). **An extended mechanical warranty is absolutely essential** to buy this vehicle with peace of mind.
