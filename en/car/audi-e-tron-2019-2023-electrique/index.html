Audi e-tron

I (GE) · 2019 – 2023

NEGOTIATE Reliability 74/100 — bon

Summary

The Audi e-tron (GE generation) marks the historic entry of the brand with the four rings into the era of mass-produced 100% electric vehicles. Available in a classic SUV body style and then as a "Sportback" (coupe) version from 2020, it is positioned as a large, luxurious, silent, and extremely comfortable SUV. While it shines with its flawless build quality and very stable fast-charging curve, it is penalized by a colossal weight (often exceeding 2.5 tons) that hampers its energy efficiency and range compared to more recent competitors. In 2023, during its mid-cycle facelift, the model was renamed **Audi Q8 e-tron** to clarify the lineup. In terms of reliability, the mechanical base and electric motors are robust, but the early production years suffered the teething problems of the electric transition (software bugs, recalls on the braking system and the sealing of the charging port).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (standard air suspension)
  • Limousine-like soundproofing (A8)
  • Flawless finish and assembly quality
  • Very flat and efficient fast charging curve (DC)
  • Generous roominess in both the front and rear

Weaknesses 5

  • Excessive weight (over 2.5 tons unladen)
  • Very high electricity consumption (difficult to drop below 23 kWh/100 km)
  • Disappointing range compared to battery capacity
  • Slow and buggy infotainment system (MMI) on early model years
  • Significant number of manufacturer recalls (braking, battery)

Check before buying

  • Complete recall history (crucial on this model: brakes, battery, camera).
  • Tire wear (weight and torque destroy rubber quickly, check alignment).
  • Operation of the MMI central screen and Virtual Cockpit (sluggishness, black screens).
  • Condition of the charging port (presence of moisture or corrosion).
  • Air suspension noises (noisy compressor or sagging on one side).

What sets it apart 14

Safety

  • 5-Star Safety and Prestigious Awards — The Audi e-tron achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 82% for child protection. It was also named an IIHS "Top Safety Pick+" from 2019 to 2023, demonstrating its unwavering commitment to occupant safety.
  • Powerful and Controlled Braking — Experience an excellent braking system, capable of stopping the vehicle from 60 mph (96 km/h) in just 118 feet (36 meters). The braking is strong, linear, with a firm pedal feel, effectively blending regenerative and friction braking for confident and safe stops.
  • Balanced and Precise Road Behavior — The Audi e-tron offers stable and composed road holding, with excellent body control for an SUV of its size. Its low center of gravity, thanks to battery placement, contributes to confident grip and minimal body roll, ensuring a secure and predictable driving experience.

Comfort

  • Superior Ride Comfort and Sound Insulation — Immerse yourself in a haven of peace thanks to standard air suspension and sound insulation worthy of an Audi A8 limousine. Every journey becomes a luxurious, silent, and exceptionally comfortable travel experience, even over long distances.
  • Impeccable Finish and Assembly — The Audi e-tron embodies German manufacturing excellence with impeccable finish and assembly quality. Every detail is designed to offer a premium experience, reflecting the craftsmanship and luxury characteristic of the four rings brand.

Practicality

  • Fast and Stable Charging Curve — Benefit from a remarkably flat and efficient DC fast charging curve, allowing you to recover significant range in optimized time. This feature ensures great practicality for your daily recharges and long journeys.
  • Generous Interior Space for All Passengers — The Audi e-tron offers generous interior space, both front and rear, ensuring optimal comfort for all occupants. Enjoy ample legroom and headroom, making every journey pleasant for the whole family.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Mercedes-Benz EQC, BMW iX3, Jaguar I-Pace, Audi Q8 e-tron (facelifted version). - **North America:** Tesla Model X, Rivian R1S, Cadillac Lyriq. - **Asia:** Nio ES8, Hongqi E-HS9.

Verdict

The Audi e-tron (2019-2023) is a pioneer that prioritized luxury and comfort at the expense of pure efficiency. It is an exceptional vehicle for traveling in absolute silence, provided you accept frequent charging stops due to its high consumption. In terms of reliability, the electric mechanics are solid, but the model suffered from numerous teething problems (software, sealing, braking) resulting in a long list of official recalls. Buying used is entirely feasible, but it requires a **scrupulous check of the maintenance history**. Avoid models where recalls (especially on the battery and braking system) have not been performed. Favor model years from 2021 onwards, which are rid of the initial bugs.
