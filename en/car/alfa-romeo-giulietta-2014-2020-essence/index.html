Alfa Romeo Giulietta

I phase 2 (940) · 2014 – 2020

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The Alfa Romeo Giulietta (Type 940) received a first facelift (Phase 2) in late 2013/early 2014, followed by a second (Phase 3) in 2016. These updates corrected the early flaws of Phase 1 (2010-2013), notably by improving soundproofing, interior finish (new seats, steering wheel, Uconnect infotainment system), and engine reliability. In the used car market, the Giulietta Phase 2/3 is a characterful alternative to German and French compacts. Gasoline engines (T-Jet and MultiAir) are particularly sought after today given the decline of diesel. The chassis remains a benchmark in terms of dynamism, although ergonomics and interior space show their age towards the end of its career.

Strengths 5

  • Timeless design that ages remarkably well
  • Dynamic road handling and precise steering
  • Overall reliability of gasoline engines (especially the 1.4 T-Jet)
  • Clear improvement in finish and soundproofing on Phases 2 and 3
  • Uconnect multimedia system more modern than the old retractable system

Weaknesses 5

  • Very fragile rear tailgate wiring harness (chronic issue)
  • TCT gearbox sometimes rough at low speeds if not updated/maintained
  • Large turning radius (inconvenient in urban areas)
  • Average rear passenger space and trunk volume for the category
  • Some parasitic noises (interior trim, suspension bushings) over time

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: crucial for the 1.4 MultiAir (invoices proving correct oil specification).
  • Functionality of all electrical equipment on the tailgate (reverse lights, fog lights, wiper, lock).
  • TCT gearbox behavior: absence of jerks on startup and smooth gear changes.
  • Suspension noises on degraded roads (upper/lower wishbones, anti-roll bar links).
  • Condition of interior door handles (known fragility, although reinforced on Phase 2).

Alternatives

- **Europe** : Peugeot 308 II (better chassis, but problematic PureTech engines), Volkswagen Golf VII (more balanced, superior finish), Seat Leon III (technical cousin of the Golf, good value for money), Audi A3 8V (premium, more expensive). - **Japan / Asia** : Mazda 3 (excellent reliability, naturally aspirated engines), Subaru Impreza. - **North America** : The Giulietta was not sold directly there, but its platform was used for the Dodge Dart and Chrysler 200.

Verdict

The Alfa Romeo Giulietta Phase 2/3 is an excellent used car choice for those seeking a compact with assertive styling and dynamic handling. Contrary to brand clichés, mechanical reliability is very good, particularly for gasoline engines. The **1.4 T-Jet 120 hp** is the safest and most economical choice for daily use. The **1.4 MultiAir 150/170 hp** offers better driving pleasure but requires a clear maintenance history. The car's flaws are concentrated on peripherals (trunk wiring harness, road noise) rather than engine failures. Buy by prioritizing models with rigorous maintenance.
