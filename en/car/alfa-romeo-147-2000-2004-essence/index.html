Summary

The Alfa Romeo 147 (Type 937) Phase 1 made a strong impression from its launch, winning the Car of the Year 2001 title. Designed by Walter de Silva, it stands out with its timeless design, plunging cloverleaf grille, and hidden rear door handles. Positioned as a premium compact against the Audi A3 and VW Golf, it shines with its dynamism, ultra-direct steering, and characterful engines (notably the Twin Spark gasoline engines and the legendary Busso V6 of the GTA). However, this passion comes at a price: the 147 requires meticulous maintenance, much more rigorous than the average for its category, otherwise costly breakdowns may occur. Phase 1 ended in late 2004 to make way for a facelift (Phase 2).

Strengths 5

  • Timeless and very successful exterior design
  • Dynamic road handling and sharp front axle
  • Ultra-direct steering (2.2 turns lock-to-lock)
  • Sound and character of gasoline engines (Twin Spark and V6)
  • Remarkable reliability of JTD diesel engines

Weaknesses 6

  • Very fragile running gear (front suspension wishbones)
  • Demanding and costly maintenance for gasoline engines (Twin Spark)
  • Disastrous reliability of the Selespeed automated gearbox
  • Catastrophic turning radius (difficult in urban areas)
  • Interior plastics that become sticky over time
  • Capricious electronics (airbag warning lights, multiplexing)

Check before buying

  • Timing belt history: Invoice less than 3 years / 60,000 km mandatory for Twin Spark.
  • Suspension noises: Squeaks or clunks over speed bumps (upper/lower wishbones faulty).
  • Oil level: TS engines naturally consume oil; a lack of oil destroys the connecting rod bearings.
  • Idle noise (TS): A 'diesel-like' clatter on a 1.6 120 or 2.0 indicates a dead variator.
  • Airbag light: Often illuminated due to connectors under the front seats.
  • Selespeed operation: Gears must shift smoothly, the pump must prime when the door is opened.

Official recalls

  • November 2004: Risk of unexpected engine hood opening while driving (safety latch defect). Concerns models produced until 2004.
  • December 2001: Possible rubbing of a front brake hose against the rim. Replacement or repositioning.
  • 2003 (JTD): Risk of short circuit on the additional diesel fuel heater.

Verdict

The Alfa Romeo 147 Phase 1 is a 'love at first sight' car. With a gasoline engine (Twin Spark), it is aimed at informed buyers: the driving pleasure is exceptional for its time, but there is zero tolerance for neglect. A belt not replaced on time or a lack of oil will lead to engine failure. Avoid models without a clear service history and unserviced Selespeed gearboxes. The GTA version is a pure collector's item that requires a luxury sports car maintenance budget. Paradoxically, for carefree daily use, it is the diesel versions (JTD) that offer the best overall reliability.
