Opel Zafira

B · 2005 – 2014

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

All Opel models

Summary

The Opel Zafira B (second generation) consolidated the success of its predecessor by adopting the brilliant *Flex7* retractable seating system, while modernizing its chassis (based on the Astra H) and its engines. Restyled in 2008, it continued its career under the name 'Zafira Family' until 2014, even after the release of the Zafira Tourer C in 2011. While its spaciousness and road handling are excellent assets, its overall reliability has been marred by fragile peripherals in the era of strict anti-pollution standards (DPF, EGR valves) and by the notoriously problematic 6-speed manual gearbox (M32).

Strengths 5

  • Flex7 modularity still as practical (3rd-row seats fold into the floor)
  • Healthy and safe road handling (optional IDS+ chassis very effective)
  • Gigantic trunk volume in 5-seat configuration (645 liters)
  • Performance and driving pleasure of the 1.9 CDTI 150 hp engine (when it works)
  • Very affordable used car prices today

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the M32 manual gearbox (bearings)
  • Very fragile diesel engine peripherals (EGR, DPF, Alternator, Dual-mass flywheel)
  • Interior finish that ages poorly (peeling plastics, rattling noises)
  • Three-quarter front visibility hindered by thick windshield pillars
  • Suspension comfort a bit firm, especially with 17 or 18-inch wheels

Check before buying

  • Mandatory road test: shift into 5th and 6th gear, listen for any whistling and observe if the lever moves during acceleration (symptom of a failing M32 gearbox).
  • Test the horn with the headlights on and operate the steering wheel controls (CIM Module).
  • On diesels: check the replacement history of the alternator, EGR valve, and dual-mass flywheel (clunking noise at idle).
  • On the 1.9 CDTI 150: inspect the swirl flap linkage on the intake manifold (often dislodged).
  • On the 1.8 VVT: listen to the cold engine for the first 3 seconds (a 'diesel-like' knocking sound indicates failing VVT pulleys).

Official recalls

  • November 2010 (CIM Module): Installation of a clip on the steering column module to resolve contact losses (inoperative horn, faulty steering wheel controls).
  • December 2010 (Fan): Risk of short circuit and fire in the radiator fan motor (2006-2010 models).
  • May 2008 (Fuel filter): Risk of fuel leakage at the fuel filter housing on 1.9 CDTI engines.
  • April 2008 (Steering): Steering rack sealing defect potentially leading to loss of power assistance.

What sets it apart 7

Practicality

  • Ingenious Flex7 Modularity — The Flex7 seating system allows for quick configuration from 7 to 2 seats, with the third-row seats fully retracting into the floor, offering exceptional versatility for passenger or cargo transport.
  • Impressive Cargo Space — In 5-seat configuration, the Zafira B offers a gigantic boot volume, ideal for families and travel, allowing you to carry everything you need without compromise.

Safety

  • High-Level Passive Safety — Designed to protect its occupants, the Opel Zafira B achieved excellent results in collision tests, ensuring peace of mind for the whole family.

Handling

  • Safe and Stable Road Handling — Its balanced chassis offers healthy and predictable road behavior, with well-controlled body roll and precise steering, ensuring a serene and comfortable drive on all roads.

Braking

  • Competent and Controlled Braking — The Opel Zafira B's braking system is designed to offer reliable performance, with good stability and adequate stopping distances, contributing to the vehicle's active safety.

Economy

  • Excellent Value for Money on the Used Market — Today, the Opel Zafira B is available at very accessible prices on the used car market, offering a spacious and versatile family solution at a controlled cost.

Performance

  • Efficient and Economical Diesel Engines — Diesel engines, particularly the 1.7 CDTI and 1.9 CDTI 120 hp, offer an excellent compromise between pleasant performance, flexibility, and controlled fuel consumption, ideal for long journeys.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Renault Grand Scénic II / III (more comfortable, reliable dCi 130), Volkswagen Touran (superior finish, noisy but robust TDIs), Ford S-Max (more dynamic), Citroën Grand C4 Picasso (royal comfort, but BMP6 to avoid).
  • Japan / Asia: Toyota Wish, Honda Stream, Mazda Premacy (much more reliable with gasoline engines).
  • Emerging markets: Chevrolet Spin (which succeeded the Zafira A in some Latin American countries).

Verdict

The Opel Zafira B is a family minivan brilliant in its interior design, but its diesel reliability record is heavy. The 1.9 CDTI 150 hp, though very pleasant, is a nest of costly problems (swirl flaps, M32 gearbox, alternator). If you absolutely must buy a diesel, favor the 1.9 CDTI 120 hp (8 valves, without swirl flaps) or the late-production 1.7 CDTI, provided the gearbox has been rebuilt and your use is mainly on the open road. For maximum peace of mind, the 1.8 VVT 140 hp gasoline versions are by far the best used car choice.
