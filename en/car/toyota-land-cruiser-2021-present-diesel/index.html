Summary

The Toyota Land Cruiser 300 Series (J300) marks a historic turning point for the legendary off-roader. Based on the new TNGA-F architecture (lightened and stiffened ladder frame), it definitively abandons V8 engines in favor of more efficient bi-turbo V6s, while losing approximately 200 kg. Although not officially sold in Western Europe or North America (which receives its luxurious cousin, the Lexus LX 600, and the smaller Land Cruiser 250/Prado), the J300 remains the undisputed king in the Middle East, Australia, and Africa. The 3.3-liter bi-turbo diesel engine (F33A-FTV) is particularly praised for its immense torque (700 Nm) and range, advantageously replacing the old 4.5L V8 (1VD-FTV). The overall reliability score is expected to be excellent, true to the model's reputation, although the increased complexity of bi-turbo engines requires more rigorous monitoring.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional off-road capabilities (formidable E-KDSS system)
  • Torque of the 3.3L V6 Diesel (700 Nm) superior to the old V8
  • Transformed road handling thanks to the TNGA-F platform (less body roll)
  • Finish quality and soundproofing worthy of Lexus
  • Overall reliability and unbeatable resale value

Weaknesses 5

  • Astronomical delivery times (up to 3 years in Japan at launch)
  • Disappearance of the sound and simplicity of the old V8s
  • DPF (Diesel) sensitivity to short urban trips
  • Still imposing size for urban use
  • Not officially sold in Western Europe

Check before buying

  • DPF regeneration history (on the V6 Diesel) via the OBD diagnostic tool.
  • Smoothness of the 10-speed automatic gearbox when cold (jerks require an ECU update).
  • Condition of underbody and protections (vehicle often used off-road).
  • Infotainment screen operation (some lag bugs reported on 2021 models).

Performance

  • Monumental Diesel Power and Torque — Equipped with the 3.3L V6 Bi-Turbo Diesel, the Land Cruiser 300 delivers an impressive 700 Nm of torque, surpassing the previous V8. This power is ideal for heavy towing and the most demanding off-road challenges, offering superior performance in all conditions.

comfort

  • Premium Vehicle Comfort and Refinement — Thanks to its new TNGA-F platform, the J300 offers transformed road handling with minimized body roll. Sound insulation and finish quality are worthy of Lexus standards, ensuring exceptional travel comfort over all distances.

economy

  • Optimized Efficiency for Adventure — The 3.3L V6 Diesel engine combines power with controlled fuel consumption, averaging around 9-10L/100km. This efficiency, coupled with a long range, makes it the ideal partner for long journeys and expeditions, reducing fuel stops.

practicality

  • Exceptional Off-Road Capabilities — The Land Cruiser 300 is designed for the extreme, with unparalleled off-road capabilities, notably thanks to its formidable E-KDSS system. It is the undisputed king of difficult terrains, ready to face any challenge.

safety

  • Enhanced Road Holding and Reinforced Stability — The new TNGA-F architecture brings increased rigidity and an optimized center of gravity, offering remarkable road holding and stability. The Land Cruiser 300 ensures a serene and safe drive, even in the most demanding conditions.

Alternatives

- **Middle East / Australia** : Nissan Patrol Y62 (V8 gasoline, very spacious but aging), Ford Everest (smaller but very capable in diesel), Land Rover Defender 110/130 (more technological, superior comfort, but more random reliability). - **North America** : Lexus LX 600 (the luxurious twin of the J300), Toyota Sequoia, Chevrolet Tahoe. - **Europe** : Land Rover Defender, Mercedes-Benz G-Class, Ineos Grenadier (for the pure utility aspect).

Verdict

The Toyota Land Cruiser 300 successfully takes on the difficult challenge of modernizing an icon without betraying its DNA. The switch to the bi-turbo V6 diesel (F33A-FTV) is a major success: it offers superior towing and off-road performance compared to the old V8, while reducing fuel consumption. Although mechanical complexity is increasing (two turbos, DPF, 10-speed gearbox), Toyota's design rigor ensures top-tier reliability.

**Buying advice**: The 3.3L V6 Diesel is the top choice for 90% of buyers (towing, expeditions, long distances). Avoid it only if you primarily drive in the city (risk of DPF clogging). In that case, or if you're looking for pure performance, the 3.4L V6 Gasoline is a formidable alternative, though thirstier.
