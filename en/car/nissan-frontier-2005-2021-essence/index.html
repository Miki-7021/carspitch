Nissan Frontier

II (D40) · 2005 – 2021

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The **second-generation Nissan Frontier (D40)** (also known as the Navara in other markets) is a mid-size pickup with exceptional longevity. Sold for 16 years in North America with very few aesthetic changes, it has built a reputation for **legendary toughness** and bulletproof mechanical reliability, often outlasting its more modern competitors over the long term. Although its cabin, onboard technology, and fuel consumption betray its design age, it represents the archetype of the "old-school" utility vehicle: simple, robust, and proven. In 2020, Nissan introduced the next-generation powertrain (3.8L V6 and 9-speed automatic gearbox) into the old D40 body for the final two years of North American production.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of the 4.0L V6 (VQ40DE) after 2010
  • Extremely robust 5-speed automatic gearbox (RE5R05A) (if unaffected by SMOD)
  • Solid ladder frame chassis and respectable payload/towing capacities
  • Maintenance and used purchase costs significantly lower than a Toyota Tacoma
  • Excellent visibility and simple ergonomics (physical buttons)

Weaknesses 5

  • Dated design (rustic interior, hard plastics, average soundproofing)
  • Very high fuel consumption on the 4.0L V6
  • Poor turning radius, making urban maneuvers difficult
  • Catastrophic radiator defect (SMOD) on 2005 to 2010 model years
  • Sluggish and unpleasant 2.5L 4-cylinder engine (QR25DE)

Check before buying

  • **SMOD (Strawberry Milkshake of Death)**: On 2005-2010 models with an automatic gearbox, it is imperative to check the color of the transmission fluid. If it is pink/milky, the gearbox is destroyed by coolant.
  • **Timing chain noise**: On the 4.0L V6 (especially 2005-2009), listen for a whine upon acceleration, a sign of worn chain guides.
  • **Chassis corrosion**: Inspect the frame, particularly in regions exposed to road salt.
  • **IPDM module**: On 2005-2006 models, a faulty relay can cause random engine stalling (often fixed by recall).
  • **Parking pawl (9-speed gearbox)**: On 2020-2021 models, ensure that the recall regarding the risk of vehicle rollaway in the 'Park' position has been performed.

Alternatives

- **North America**: Toyota Tacoma (absolute benchmark in reliability but much more expensive), Chevrolet Colorado / GMC Canyon (more modern but less reliable engines/gearboxes), Ford Ranger (returned in 2019, good compromise), Honda Ridgeline (unibody, more comfortable but less utilitarian). - **Europe / Asia / South Am. (Against the Navara/Frontier)**: Toyota Hilux (indestructible), Ford Ranger (very popular, good road handling), Mitsubishi L200 / Triton (excellent value for money), Isuzu D-Max (very robust).

Verdict

The Nissan Frontier D40 is an **excellent used purchase** for anyone looking for a robust, no-frills pickup capable of handling high mileage. Although it is less capable in extreme off-roading than a Toyota Tacoma, it is mechanically just as reliable (if not more so regarding the automatic gearbox) and costs significantly less on the used market.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize a model equipped with the **4.0L V6 produced between 2011 and 2019**. This will help you avoid the catastrophic radiator defect (SMOD) and the timing chain issues of the early years (2005-2010). The 2020-2021 models with the new 3.8L V6 are also excellent choices, offering modern mechanics in a proven body, provided the transmission recalls are checked.
