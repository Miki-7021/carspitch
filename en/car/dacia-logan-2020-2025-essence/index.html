Summary

The third-generation Dacia Logan, launched in 2020, marks a significant evolution for the Romanian manufacturer's compact sedan. Based on the Renault group's CMF-B platform (shared with the Clio V and Captur II), it sheds its rustic image to offer a more modern design, an improved interior, and more up-to-date safety and comfort features, while retaining its positioning as a vehicle with an unbeatable price/performance ratio.

Although the Logan III is less widespread in France than its sibling the Sandero III (which is the hatchback version), it shares all of its engines and technical base. It represents an interesting option for those looking for generous boot space and a simple, reliable, and economical car to use. Overall reliability is significantly improving thanks to the adoption of the new platform and proven engines.

Strengths 6

  • Unbeatable price/performance ratio
  • Significantly improved overall reliability (CMF-B platform)
  • Low maintenance and insurance costs
  • Generous interior space and boot volume
  • Economical and proven petrol/LPG engines
  • Modernized safety and comfort features

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds
  • Simple interior materials (hard plastics)
  • Limited performance with the SCe 65 engine
  • CVT gearbox (on TCe 90) can lack responsiveness
  • Brand image still associated with 'low-cost' for some

Check before buying

  • Complete service history, especially for TCe and ECO-G
  • LPG system operation (for ECO-G): switch, indicator lights, absence of odor
  • Condition of tires and brakes (often neglected on budget vehicles)
  • Absence of abnormal noises from the front axle (suspension, ball joints)
  • Operation of onboard electronics (multimedia screen, driving aids)

Verdict

The Dacia Logan III is an excellent proposition for anyone looking for a new or very recent car that is reliable, economical, spacious, and no-frills. The adoption of the CMF-B platform and modern Renault group engines gives it a maturity and reliability far superior to previous generations.

* **For urban and suburban use**: The **1.0 SCe 65** is sufficient and incredibly robust. It's the choice for simplicity and minimal cost. * **For versatile use**: The **1.0 TCe 90** is the best compromise. It offers sufficient performance for road and occasional highway driving, with reasonable fuel consumption. * **For high-mileage drivers or a tight fuel budget**: The **1.0 TCe 100 ECO-G** is the most sensible choice. The cost per kilometer is unbeatable, provided the specific LPG system maintenance is diligently followed. * **For very high-mileage drivers on long journeys (if available)**: The **1.5 Blue dCi 95** remains a relevant option for its fuel efficiency, but it is increasingly rare and less suited for short trips.

Overall, the Logan III is a **recommended** purchase. Check the recall history and ensure that all fixes have been applied. It's an honest car that perfectly fulfills its role.
