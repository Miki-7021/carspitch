Summary

The Peugeot 407, launched in 2004, succeeded the highly acclaimed 406. It marked a stylistic turning point for the lion brand, with its pronounced front overhang and gaping grille, giving it a dynamic and distinctive look. Available as a sedan, station wagon (SW), and coupé, it was positioned in the family and executive car segment. The 407 was renowned for its suspension comfort, sound insulation, and rigorous road handling, a legacy of Peugeot tradition. A minor facelift occurred in 2008 (Phase 2), bringing minor aesthetic changes (bumpers, taillights) and engine evolutions, particularly in diesel. Overall, the 407 offers good value for money on the used market, but its reliability can vary considerably depending on the engine and maintenance history.

Strengths 6

  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation
  • Dynamic and safe road handling
  • Generous spaciousness (especially in SW)
  • Distinctive design (loved or not)
  • Comprehensive equipment for the used price
  • 2.0 HDi engine (136/140/163 hp) generally reliable and powerful

Weaknesses 6

  • Very long front overhang (watch out for curbs)
  • Uneven reliability of certain engines (1.6 HDi, 2.7 HDi V6)
  • Sometimes capricious electronics (COM2000, BSI)
  • Fuel consumption of V6 gasoline engines
  • High maintenance cost for complex engines (2.2 HDi bi-turbo, 2.7 HDi V6)
  • Large turning radius

Check before buying

  • Complete maintenance history and invoices (especially for diesels).
  • DPF and Eolys additive: Check proper DPF function and additive level (replacement/refill cost).
  • Dual-mass flywheel: Noises or vibrations on start-up/shut-down or at low rpm (especially on 1.6 HDi and 2.0 HDi).
  • Turbo: Abnormal whistling, loss of power, blue smoke (particularly on 1.6 HDi).
  • EGR valve: Idling problems, jerking, engine warning light.
  • Electronics: Functioning of all equipment (air conditioning, power windows, GPS, parking sensors, cruise control). Check error messages on the onboard computer.
  • Suspension: Clunking noises at the front (wishbone bushings, anti-roll bar links).
  • Power steering: Noises or stiff spots (pump, rack).
  • Gearbox: Gear changes (manual) or jerking (automatic).
  • Bodywork: Condition of the front bumper (often scuffed).

Official recalls

Official Manufacturer Recalls (non-exhaustive list)

  • 2004-2005 (models produced until 2005): Risk of fuel leakage at the fuel filter (concerns some 2.0 HDi). Inspection and replacement of the filter if necessary.
  • 2005-2006 (models produced until 2006): Potential problem with the electric power steering pump, which could lead to loss of assistance. Replacement of the pump or wiring harness.
  • 2006-2007 (models produced until 2007): Airbag control unit malfunction, which could prevent deployment in the event of a collision. Reprogramming or replacement of the control unit.
  • 2007-2008 (models produced until 2008): Risk of brake master cylinder failure, leading to loss of braking efficiency. Replacement of the master cylinder.
  • 2008-2009 (models produced until 2009): Engine hood fastening problem, which could lead to its unexpected opening. Inspection and reinforcement of the fastening.
  • 2009-2010 (models produced until 2010): Software update for engine management, particularly to optimize DPF operation and reduce emissions (concerns several diesel engines).

*It is imperative to check with a Peugeot dealership, by providing the VIN, whether the vehicle in question has benefited from all relevant recall campaigns.*

Verdict

The Peugeot 407 is a car with character and offers excellent comfort for its used price. However, its reliability verdict is nuanced and highly dependent on the chosen engine and maintenance history.

BUY if you opt for the 2.0 HDi (136, 140 or 163 hp), which is by far the most balanced and reliable engine in the diesel range. Ensure a complete maintenance history and rigorous monitoring of the DPF and dual-mass flywheel. The 2.0i gasoline versions are also a reasonable choice for less intensive use.

NEGOTIATE strongly and with caution if you are considering a 1.6 HDi 110 hp or a 2.2 HDi 170 hp. The 1.6 HDi can be a good deal if its maintenance history is impeccable and known weaknesses have been preventively addressed. The 2.2 HDi bi-turbo offers good performance but can lead to high repair costs in case of supercharging system failure. A thorough inspection by a professional is essential.

AVOID the 2.7 HDi V6 and the 3.0i V6 unless you have a very substantial maintenance budget and a perfect understanding of the risks. These engines, though pleasant, are complex and their repairs can be exorbitant.

In all cases, prioritize a model with an up-to-date service book and invoices, and do not hesitate to have the vehicle inspected by an independent expert before purchase.
