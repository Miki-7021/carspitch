Toyota C-HR

II (AX30) · 2023 – Présent

BUY Reliability 93/100 — excellent

Summary

The second-generation Toyota C-HR (code name AX30) was unveiled in 2023. Based on the optimized TNGA-C platform, it retains its predecessor's urban/compact coupe SUV positioning but pushes the design even further with a 'C' shaped light signature and an aggressive two-tone body. Mechanically, this generation completely foregoes 100% internal combustion engines in Europe to focus exclusively on the 5th generation Toyota hybrid system (HEV) and introduces, for the first time on this model, a plug-in hybrid (PHEV) powertrain inherited from the Prius 5. Interior finish quality takes a significant leap forward, as does the multimedia interface. **Overall design score: 8.5/10**.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability (Toyota hybrid heritage)
  • Extremely low real-world fuel consumption (often under 5L/100km in HEV)
  • Bold and distinctive design
  • Significantly improved interior material quality compared to generation 1
  • Toyota Smart Connect multimedia system finally responsive and modern
  • Toyota Relax warranty (up to 10 years or 160,000 km with network servicing)

Weaknesses 5

  • Still cramped rear passenger space (small windows, feeling of claustrophobia)
  • Average trunk volume (388L in 1.8, 364L in 2.0, and only 310L in PHEV)
  • Very poor peripheral and rear visibility (cameras essential)
  • Significantly higher purchase prices compared to the first generation
  • Highway soundproofing could be improved (road noise)

Check before buying

  • 12V battery: As with many Toyota hybrids, it can discharge quickly if the vehicle remains stationary for several weeks or only makes very short trips.
  • HV battery cooling filter: Located near the rear seats, it must never be obstructed by clothes or luggage to prevent the hybrid system from overheating.
  • Software bugs: A few isolated cases of central screen restarting or wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto disconnection (often resolved by OTA updates).
  • Driving aids (ADAS): Sensors and radars (especially Toyota Safety Sense 3.0) are very sensitive to dirt; untimely alerts may occur if the windshield or grille is dirty.

Verdict

The second-generation Toyota C-HR is a 'risk-free' purchase in terms of reliability. By capitalizing on the 5th generation of its hybrid system, Toyota refines a technology it masters perfectly. The choice of powertrain will depend on your budget and usage: the 1.8 HEV with 140 hp is the most rational and economical choice for daily use. The 2.0 HEV with 200 hp offers superior driving pleasure on the road, while the PHEV is aimed at those who can charge daily at home or work, despite a heavily compromised trunk. Its only real flaw remains its size-to-cabin space ratio, which makes it more suitable for couples or small urban families.
