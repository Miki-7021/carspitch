Tesla Model 3

Summary

The first-generation Tesla Model 3 (pre-Highland) revolutionized the automotive market by democratizing electric vehicles on a large scale. Launched in 2017 in the United States and early 2019 in Europe, this family sedan stands out for its minimalist interior, formidable energy efficiency, and advanced software integration (OTA updates).

**Major evolutions:** - **2019-2020:** Models produced in Fremont (California). Finish sometimes inconsistent (panel gaps, thin paint). - **Late 2020 / 2021 (Refresh):** Introduction of the heat pump (octovalve), front double glazing, powered trunk, chrome delete, new center console. Arrival of models produced at the Shanghai Gigafactory (significantly superior assembly quality) and LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) batteries for the entry-level. - **2022:** Replacement of the Intel Atom processor with AMD Ryzen (smoother screen), switch to a 16V Li-ion auxiliary battery (replacing the fragile 12V lead-acid), removal of ultrasonic sensors (Tesla Vision).

Despite early issues with the running gear and bodywork, the reliability of the powertrain (motors/batteries) is exceptional.

Strengths 6

  • Unmatched energy efficiency (real-world consumption often under 15 kWh/100 km)
  • Tesla Supercharger network (perfect integration with planner)
  • Regular software updates (OTA) that improve the car over time
  • LFP batteries (on post-2020 RWD) extremely durable
  • Dynamic road handling and very low center of gravity
  • Interior space and cargo volume (rear trunk + front frunk)

Weaknesses 6

  • Very fragile paint quality (susceptible to stone chips, especially on sills)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on pre-2021 models (wind and road noise)
  • Very firm suspension comfort, especially with 19 and 20-inch wheels
  • Absence of Apple CarPlay and Android Auto
  • Automatic wipers (Tesla Vision) often erratic
  • Removal of parking sensors (late 2022) making parking less precise with cameras alone

Check before buying

  • **Upper suspension wishbones:** Listen for any squeaking or clunking when steering at low speed or over speed bumps.
  • **Jack points:** Check under the car for battery damage from improper lifting (without specific pads).
  • **Sills and paint:** Inspect wheel arches and sills for paint chips (very common without mud flaps).
  • **Heating:** Test full hot air production (especially on 2021 models with heat pump).
  • **Rear lights:** Check for significant condensation or standing water inside the light units.
  • **Battery history:** Launch Service Mode to check the actual degradation of the high-voltage battery (Health Test).

What sets it apart 14

Safety

  • Advanced Safety and Prestigious Awards — The Tesla Model 3 achieved a maximum **5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests**, with exceptional scores for adult (96%) and child (86%) protection. It has also been awarded the IIHS Top Safety Pick+ and the NHTSA 5-Star Overall Safety Rating, confirming its status as one of the safest vehicles in its category.

Economy

  • Unrivaled Energy Efficiency — Benefit from remarkable electricity consumption, often **below 15 kWh/100 km** in real-world conditions, leading to very low running costs and optimized range.

Reliability

  • Exceptional Powertrain Reliability — The Tesla Model 3 is renowned for the robustness of its motors and batteries, especially the Rear-Wheel Drive (Propulsion) versions equipped with post-2020 LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) batteries, which offer remarkable durability and longevity, even with frequent 100% charging.

tenue de route

  • Sporty and Agile Road Handling — Thanks to its very low center of gravity and precise steering, the Model 3 offers dynamic and engaging road handling. It stands out for its agility and minimal body roll, guaranteeing a sporty and safe driving experience.

Braking

  • Superior and Stable Braking Performance — The Tesla Model 3 benefits from excellent braking performance, with short stopping distances and impressive stability. Continuous software improvements have further optimized this capability, making it highly competitive in its category.

Practicality

  • Integrated and Evolving Tesla Ecosystem — Enjoy the Tesla Supercharger network, perfectly integrated into the trip planner, and regular Over-The-Air (OTA) software updates that continuously improve your vehicle's features and performance over time.
  • Generous Interior Space and Cargo Volume — The Model 3 offers comfortable interior space for passengers and surprising cargo volume thanks to its spacious rear trunk and "frunk" (front trunk), making it ideal for families and long journeys.

Verdict

The first-generation Tesla Model 3 is arguably the best electric purchase on the used market, provided you target the right year and origin. **We highly recommend models produced from 2021 onwards (Shanghai factory, refreshed interior, heat pump).** The **Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD) version with LFP battery (55 or 60 kWh)** is the top choice for its bulletproof reliability and low degradation. 2019-2020 (Fremont) models are negotiable due to their inconsistent finish and lack of a heat pump, but their powertrain remains extremely robust. Be aware of running gear costs (wishbones), which are the main mechanical weakness.
