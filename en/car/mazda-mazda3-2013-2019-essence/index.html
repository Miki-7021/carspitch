Summary

The third-generation Mazda3 (BM code for 2013-2016, then BN for the 2016-2019 facelift) marks a major turning point for the Hiroshima-based manufacturer. It fully adopts the 'Kodo' stylistic language and the complete 'Skyactiv' architecture (chassis, engines, gearboxes). Positioned as a mainstream compact with premium aspirations, it stands out for its first-class driving pleasure, its naturally aspirated gasoline engines with high compression ratios (going against the downsizing trend), and its meticulous finish. The 2016 facelift (BN) corrected one of its main flaws: sound insulation, while introducing the G-Vectoring Control system to improve dynamic behavior. While the gasoline versions shine with exceptional reliability, the diesel variants have tarnished the model's overall record.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of Skyactiv-G gasoline engines
  • Driving pleasure and precision of the manual gearbox (MX-5-like)
  • Exterior design (Kodo) that ages very well
  • Cockpit ergonomics and driving position
  • Real-world fuel consumption of gasoline engines very close to advertised figures

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 2.2 Skyactiv-D engine (Diesel)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on phase 1 (BM, 2013-2016)
  • Very fragile paint (sensitive to stone chips and micro-scratches)
  • Average rear passenger space and boot volume (364 L) for the category
  • Infotainment system (MZD Connect) slow to start and fragile screen

Check before buying

  • Central screen: check for internal cracks ('spider web') and ghost touches when stationary.
  • Electric mirrors: ensure they fold and unfold without jerking or suspicious noise.
  • Paintwork: inspect the hood and front bumper to assess stone chip impact (Soul Red particularly fragile).
  • On Diesels: check the oil level when cold. If it exceeds the 'X' mark, the oil is diluted by diesel fuel (danger for the engine).
  • Service history: Skyactiv engines require specific oil (0W-20 for gasoline) to maintain their reliability.

Official recalls

  • April 2019 (Engine) : Engine control software (PCM) update to prevent stalling at low rpm (concerns certain 2018-2019 gasoline models).
  • June 2017 (Wipers) : Replacement of the front wiper control module, risk of loss of function (2015-2017 models).
  • February 2017 (Braking) : Risk of loss of braking assistance due to a defective vacuum valve on diesel engines.
  • September 2016 (Tailgate) : Replacement of tailgate struts that may corrode and fail (2013-2016 models).
  • October 2014 (Fuel) : Risk of fuel tank leakage due to a welding defect (models produced in 2013-2014).

What sets it apart 7

reliability

  • Legendary Reliability of Skyactiv-G Petrol Engines — Opt for peace of mind with the Mazda3's Skyactiv-G petrol engines. Renowned for their robustness and chain-driven design, they offer exceptional durability and long-term tranquility.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Precise Driving Pleasure — Experience an exhilarating driving experience thanks to a balanced chassis, precise steering, and a manual gearbox with MX-5-like feel. Every journey becomes a moment of pure enjoyment.
  • Agile and Secure Road Holding — Enjoy impeccable road holding. Its well-balanced chassis and precise steering guarantee remarkable agility and a sense of security, even in the tightest corners.

safety

  • Certified Maximum Safety — Travel with complete confidence. The Mazda3 III achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult and child protection, and was designated an IIHS Top Safety Pick+.
  • Ultra-Efficient Braking — Benefit from first-class active safety thanks to a high-performance braking system. The Mazda3 III stands out for its short stopping distances, offering increased control and confidence in all circumstances.

comfort

  • Timeless and Elegant Kodo Design — Be captivated by the Mazda3's Kodo design. Its fluid and dynamic lines give it a timeless elegance that continues to turn heads and age gracefully.

economy

  • Controlled and Realistic Fuel Consumption — The Mazda3's Skyactiv-G petrol engines are designed for efficiency. Enjoy real-world fuel consumption remarkably close to the advertised figures, for a controlled budget every day.

Alternatives

  • Europe : Volkswagen Golf VII (segment benchmark, powerful TSI engines), Peugeot 308 II (excellent chassis, but problematic PureTech engines), Honda Civic 9/10 (equivalent reliability, divisive design).
  • North America : Honda Civic, Toyota Corolla, Subaru Impreza (for all-wheel drive).
  • Japan / Asia : Subaru Impreza, Toyota Corolla Sport, Honda Civic.

Verdict

The third-generation Mazda3 is a highly recommendable purchase, on the strict condition of opting for a gasoline engine (Skyactiv-G). In these configurations, it stands out as one of the most reliable compact cars of its decade, offering above-average driving pleasure without sacrificing fuel consumption. Prefer a facelifted version (BN, from late 2016) to benefit from better sound insulation and a refined finish. However, diesel versions (especially the 2.2L) are to be avoided due to costly design flaws.
