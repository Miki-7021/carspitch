Honda Civic

XI (FL) · 2021 – 2025

BUY Reliability 89/100 — excellent

All Honda models

Summary

The eleventh-generation Honda Civic (FE chassis for the sedan, FL for the hatchback version) marks a return to a more mature and refined design compared to the polarizing style of the tenth generation. Positioned as a C-segment compact (although its dimensions bring it closer to the D-segment), it shines with its exceptional chassis, redesigned interior ergonomics, and technological features. In Europe, it made the radical choice to be offered only with a hybrid powertrain (e:HEV) and an ultra-sporty version (Type R). However, globally (especially in North America and Asia), 100% gasoline engines (naturally aspirated and turbocharged) remain the core of the range. It is an extremely accomplished vehicle, offering a rare balance of comfort, dynamism, and overall reliability.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional chassis: perfect balance between comfort and dynamism
  • Impeccable interior ergonomics (physical climate controls retained)
  • Formidable efficiency of the e:HEV powertrain (often under 5L/100km in real-world driving)
  • Very high overall mechanical reliability
  • Generous interior space and trunk volume for the category
  • Manual gearbox (on Type R and some 1.5T) among the best on the market

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation (road noise on the highway)
  • Infotainment system a bit graphically dated compared to competitors
  • Thin paint, sensitive to chips
  • Sticky Steering issue on 2022-2023 model years
  • Rear headroom a bit tight for tall occupants (Hatchback version)

Check before buying

  • Power steering (EPS) behavior on highways: no stiff spots should be felt at the center position.
  • CVT gearbox maintenance history (oil changes respected).
  • Engine oil level and smell on the 1.5 VTEC Turbo (strong gasoline smell = oil dilution).
  • Interior plastic fitment (some rattles possible around the B-pillar or honeycomb air vent grille).
  • Condition of the rims (very exposed to curbs, especially on high trims and Type R).

Official recalls

  • Power Steering (November 2023): Massive recall (especially in North America, global TSB) concerning a manufacturing defect of the gear in the steering rack, causing sticky steering or stiff spots. Affects 2022-2024 models.
  • Passenger Seat Weight Sensor (February 2024): A defective capacitor in the sensor's circuit board can cause inadvertent deployment of the passenger airbag. Affects certain 2022-2024 models.
  • Brake Master Cylinder (Late 2022): Risk of brake fluid leakage from the master cylinder, extending braking distance. Affects a small series of 2022 models.
  • Fuel Pump (Various Dates): Although primarily affecting the 10th generation, some very early 2021/2022 models were included in the global recall of defective Denso fuel pumps (risk of engine stalling).

Verdict

The 11th-generation Honda Civic is one of the most accomplished and reliable compact cars on the global market. If you are in Europe, the e:HEV version is a masterpiece of efficiency and driving pleasure, fully justifying its purchase. In markets offering 100% gasoline powertrains, the naturally aspirated 2.0 is the choice for absolute peace of mind, while the 1.5 VTEC Turbo offers the best compromise between dynamism and fuel consumption. The only real drawback of this generation is the sticky steering issue on 2022-2023 models: ensure that the vehicle in question has been corrected under warranty or does not exhibit the symptom during a highway test drive. Once this point is validated, it is a highly recommended purchase.
