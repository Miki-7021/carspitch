Audi A6

C7 (4G) · 2011 – 2018

NEGOTIATE Reliability 76/100 — bon

Summary

The fourth-generation Audi A6 (code name C7 or Typ 4G) is a premium executive sedan based on the MLB platform. Launched in 2011, it received a major facelift (Phase 2) in late 2014. This facelift brought Euro 6 compliant engines, an updated MMI infotainment system, optional Matrix LED headlights, and most notably, the abandonment of the fragile Multitronic continuously variable transmission in favor of the S tronic dual-clutch gearbox on front-wheel-drive versions. Renowned for its exceptional build quality, sound insulation, and the efficiency of its Quattro all-wheel drive, the A6 C7 is a formidable mile-eater. On the used market, diesel (TDI) versions largely dominate the European market, offering an ideal compromise between torque, range, and driving pleasure, although they require rigorous monitoring to avoid astronomical maintenance bills.

Strengths 5

  • Benchmark build and finish quality (ages extremely well)
  • Sound insulation and ride comfort (especially with acoustic glazing and air suspension)
  • Enjoyment and performance of V6 TDI engines (especially the BiTDI with the Tiptronic 8 gearbox)
  • Formidable efficiency of the Quattro all-wheel drive (Torsen)
  • Timeless design, particularly in estate version (Avant)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the Multitronic automatic gearbox (CVT) on front-wheel-drive models before 2014
  • Very high maintenance cost, particularly on V6s (timing chain at the rear of the engine)
  • Recurring problems related to diesel emission control systems (EGR, DPF, AdBlue)
  • Premature wear of front suspension arm bushings (heavy front axle)
  • Retractable MMI screen whose plastic gear mechanism eventually breaks

Check before buying

  • Clear history of automatic gearbox oil changes (S tronic every 60k km max)
  • Timing chain noise on cold start on V6 TDI and TFSI
  • Presence of coolant leaks in the engine's 'V' (V6 TDI)
  • Smooth operation of the MMI system's motorized screen
  • Condition of front suspension arm bushings (clunking over speed bumps)
  • Operation of the AdBlue system on Euro 6 models (no warning light illuminated)

Verdict

The Audi A6 C7 is an exceptional executive car that offers high-level performance, but it demands a maintenance budget commensurate with its badge. For a worry-free purchase, **it is imperative to prioritize a facelifted model (Phase 2, post-late 2014)**. This will help you avoid the disastrous Multitronic gearbox, benefit from more efficient Euro 6 engines, and a modernized multimedia system. The top choice remains the **3.0 BiTDI 320 hp**, whose Tiptronic 8 gearbox is much more robust than the S tronic 7, provided you check for the absence of chain noise when cold. For more rational use, the **2.0 TDI 190 hp (EA288)** is an excellent compromise, provided you monitor the AdBlue system. Avoid models with a vague history or whose gearbox oil changes have not been scrupulously respected.
