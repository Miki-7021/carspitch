Summary

The first (and only) generation Chevrolet Bolt EUV (Electric Utility Vehicle) is the big brother of the Bolt EV. Launched for the 2022 model year, it rests on the same architecture (BEV2 platform, predecessor to GM's Ultium technology) but offers a lengthened wheelbase, resulting in significantly more rear legroom and the look of a small SUV. It also stands out with the introduction of the Super Cruise semi-autonomous driving system (a first for Chevrolet). Although its production was short-lived (discontinued at the end of 2023 to make way for Ultium models), it represents one of the best value/range propositions on the used market today, provided that the famous recall of its LG Chem battery has been scrupulously carried out.

Strengths 6

  • Unbeatable price/range ratio on the used market
  • Very generous rear legroom for its size
  • Excellent overall reliability (once the battery recall is performed)
  • Availability of the Super Cruise system (semi-autonomous highway driving)
  • Almost non-existent mechanical maintenance
  • Intuitive infotainment system with wireless Apple CarPlay / Android Auto

Weaknesses 5

  • Obsolete DC fast charging speed (capped at 55 kW), making long trips tedious
  • No AWD option, FWD only
  • Disappointing trunk volume, smaller than the standard Bolt EV
  • History tainted by the massive battery recall (fire risk)
  • Basic quality of some interior plastics

Check before buying

  • High-voltage battery recall status (VIN VERIFICATION MANDATORY): ensure that the diagnostic software or the new battery has been installed.
  • Front tire wear: the 360 Nm of torque tends to cause front wheel spin and wear out the tires prematurely.
  • Super Cruise subscription: check if the vehicle is equipped with it and if the subscription (paid after the trial period) is active.
  • Operation of the charging port and condition of the dual-level charge cord provided by Chevrolet.

Official recalls

The Bolt EUV was heavily impacted by recalls related to its battery. Here are the official campaigns (NHTSA):

  • [2024-10-31] [24V812000] & [2024-06-27] [24V481000] (Traction battery / Software): Follow-up recalls concerning vehicles previously repaired under recall 21V-650. The installation of the advanced diagnostic software may have failed or been incorrectly installed, preventing the detection of battery anomalies.
  • [2021-08-20] [21V650000] (Traction battery - FIRE RISK): The major recall. The high-voltage battery (supplied by LG) could catch fire when charged to full or nearly full capacity, due to two simultaneous manufacturing defects (a torn anode and a folded separator). Solution: replacement of defective battery modules or installation of monitoring software temporarily limiting the charge.
  • [2021-07-08] [21V517000] (Frontal airbags): One of the two fasteners securing the driver's frontal airbag to the steering wheel may be missing or improperly tightened.

What sets it apart 10

Comfort

  • Generous Rear Legroom for Its Size — The extended wheelbase gives rear passengers significantly more legroom than the Bolt EV, making family trips and daily rides more comfortable for everyone on board.
  • Super Cruise Semi-Autonomous Highway Driving — Chevrolet's Super Cruise system allows semi-autonomous driving on the highway, reducing driver fatigue during longer trips and marking a first for the brand.

Economy

  • Excellent Value on Used Market — The Bolt EUV offers one of the best price-to-range ratios on the used market, letting buyers get a capable electric vehicle without paying a premium.
  • Minimal Mechanical Maintenance Required — With an electric drivetrain, mechanical maintenance is almost nonexistent, reducing ownership costs and avoiding routine engine servicing compared with combustion vehicles.

Performance

  • Strong Real-World Range and Efficiency — The 65 kWh electric motor delivers roughly 397 km of EPA range with excellent efficiency, covering most daily and peri-urban driving needs on a single charge.
  • Brisk Acceleration from Electric Motor — The 200 hp electric motor provides frank acceleration, making city driving and merging responsive while remaining smooth and quiet for occupants.

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability After Battery Recall — Once the LG Chem battery recall has been properly completed, overall reliability is excellent, and J.D. Power and Consumer Reports rate post-recall reliability as very satisfactory.

Practicality

  • Intuitive Infotainment with Wireless Connectivity — The infotainment system is intuitive and includes wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, keeping smartphone navigation and media simple and cable-free for the driver.

Design

  • Small SUV Stance with Practical Cabin — The Bolt EUV adopts a small SUV look while offering a practical cabin, giving buyers a versatile daily vehicle that is easy to place and live with.

Safety

  • Verified Recall Status Ensures Safety — Buyers who confirm the battery recalls and 2024 software updates were performed by a GM dealer gain peace of mind regarding the high-voltage battery fire risk.

Alternatives

  • North America: Hyundai Kona Electric, Kia Niro EV, Volkswagen ID.4, Nissan Leaf Plus, Tesla Model 3 (Standard Range Plus).
  • Europe: Model not officially sold (the spiritual equivalent would be the Opel Ampera-e, based on the older Bolt EV, or the Peugeot e-2008 / Hyundai Kona).

Verdict

The Chevrolet Bolt EUV is a paradoxical electric vehicle: its launch was marred by one of the largest battery recalls in automotive history, but once this issue is resolved, it proves to be an extremely reliable, practical, and economical vehicle. According to J.D. Power and Consumer Reports, post-recall reliability is very satisfactory. If you are looking for an affordable electric vehicle for daily use and highway fast charging is not a priority (55 kW limit), it is an excellent choice. Absolute prerequisite for purchase: demand formal proof that the battery recalls (and the 2024 software updates) have been performed by a GM dealer.
