Chevrolet Bolt EUV

I · 2022 – 2023

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The first (and only) generation **Chevrolet Bolt EUV** (Electric Utility Vehicle) is the big brother of the Bolt EV. Launched for the 2022 model year, it rests on the same architecture (BEV2 platform, predecessor to GM's Ultium technology) but offers a lengthened wheelbase, resulting in significantly more rear legroom and the look of a small SUV. It also stands out with the introduction of the **Super Cruise** semi-autonomous driving system (a first for Chevrolet). Although its production was short-lived (discontinued at the end of 2023 to make way for Ultium models), it represents one of the best value/range propositions on the used market today, provided that the famous recall of its LG Chem battery has been scrupulously carried out.

Strengths 6

  • Unbeatable price/range ratio on the used market
  • Very generous rear legroom for its size
  • Excellent overall reliability (once the battery recall is performed)
  • Availability of the Super Cruise system (semi-autonomous highway driving)
  • Almost non-existent mechanical maintenance
  • Intuitive infotainment system with wireless Apple CarPlay / Android Auto

Weaknesses 5

  • Obsolete DC fast charging speed (capped at 55 kW), making long trips tedious
  • No AWD option, FWD only
  • Disappointing trunk volume, smaller than the standard Bolt EV
  • History tainted by the massive battery recall (fire risk)
  • Basic quality of some interior plastics

Check before buying

  • High-voltage battery recall status (VIN VERIFICATION MANDATORY): ensure that the diagnostic software or the new battery has been installed.
  • Front tire wear: the 360 Nm of torque tends to cause front wheel spin and wear out the tires prematurely.
  • Super Cruise subscription: check if the vehicle is equipped with it and if the subscription (paid after the trial period) is active.
  • Operation of the charging port and condition of the dual-level charge cord provided by Chevrolet.

Alternatives

* **North America**: Hyundai Kona Electric, Kia Niro EV, Volkswagen ID.4, Nissan Leaf Plus, Tesla Model 3 (Standard Range Plus). * **Europe**: Model not officially sold (the spiritual equivalent would be the Opel Ampera-e, based on the older Bolt EV, or the Peugeot e-2008 / Hyundai Kona).

Verdict

The Chevrolet Bolt EUV is a paradoxical electric vehicle: its launch was marred by one of the largest battery recalls in automotive history, but once this issue is resolved, it proves to be an extremely reliable, practical, and economical vehicle. According to J.D. Power and Consumer Reports, post-recall reliability is very satisfactory. If you are looking for an affordable electric vehicle for daily use and highway fast charging is not a priority (55 kW limit), it is an excellent choice. **Absolute prerequisite for purchase: demand formal proof that the battery recalls (and the 2024 software updates) have been performed by a GM dealer.**
