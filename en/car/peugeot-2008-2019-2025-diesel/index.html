Peugeot 2008

II · 2019 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 45/100 — faible

Summary

The second-generation Peugeot 2008 (P24) is a B-segment urban SUV based on Stellantis' CMP platform. Launched in late 2019 and restyled in 2023, it has achieved immense commercial success thanks to its assertive design and i-Cockpit. However, its reliability record is heavily marred by major design flaws in its internal combustion engines (PureTech petrol and BlueHDi diesel). Independent statistics confirm this: **What Car?** gives it a mediocre rating of 2.5/5 (28% of breakdowns reported over 12 months), the **TÜV** notes a 3-year defect rate of 17.8% (in the lower average), and the **ADAC** reports a breakdown rate of 3/1000 vehicles/year (average, although an improvement compared to Generation I).

  • Modern and innovative interior with 3D i-Cockpit — The 2008 II's interior offers a resolutely modern presentation, centered around the innovative 3D i-Cockpit, which provides an immersive and intuitive driving experience, enhancing comfort and pleasure on board.

Economy

Safety

  • High level of passive safety — The Peugeot 2008 II achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, demonstrating excellent protection for adult and child occupants, ensuring peace of mind on the road.

Braking
