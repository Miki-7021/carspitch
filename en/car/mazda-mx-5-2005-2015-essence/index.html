Mazda MX-5

III (NC) · 2005 – 2015

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The third-generation Mazda MX-5 (code name NC) marks a major evolution for the famous roadster. Based on a modified Mazda RX-8 platform, it is wider, stiffer, and safer than the previous generation (NB), while retaining the *Jinba Ittai* philosophy (the oneness of horse and rider).

It went through three distinct phases: - **NC1 (2005-2008)**: Initial launch. - **NC2 (2008-2012)**: Major facelift (more aggressive front end, smiling grille), significant mechanical improvements to the 2.0L engine (forged internals, rev limiter pushed to 7500 rpm) and revised gearbox synchronizers. - **NC3 (2012-2015)**: Minor aesthetic facelift, integration of an active hood for pedestrian safety, and accelerator reprogramming.

A notable fact: it was the first MX-5 to offer, starting in 2006, an electrically retractable hardtop version (PRHT - Power Retractable Hard Top), providing increased versatility without compromising trunk volume.

*Note: This model was only sold with naturally aspirated gasoline engines. No diesel, hybrid, or electric versions were ever produced.*

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall mechanical reliability (very durable chain-driven MZR engines)
  • Exemplary road handling (50/50 weight distribution, rear-wheel drive)
  • Choice between traditional soft top and very reliable retractable hardtop (PRHT)
  • Very reasonable maintenance costs and spare parts
  • Excellent driving position and driver-focused ergonomics
  • Very relevant mechanical improvements on phase 2 (NC2)

Weaknesses 6

  • Susceptibility to corrosion (a common issue for Mazdas of this era: underbody, frame rails, wheel arches)
  • Fragile coolant expansion tank (major design flaw that can lead to engine failure)
  • Soft top water drains that clog easily, flooding the cabin
  • Highway sound insulation (especially with the soft top version)
  • Hard interior plastics that scratch easily
  • Illegible oil dipstick, sometimes leading to low oil levels due to owner oversight

Check before buying

  • **Corrosion**: Imperatively inspect the front frame rails, sills, rear wheel arches, and engine subframe.
  • **Expansion tank**: Open the hood. If the coolant reservoir is yellow/brown instead of white/translucent, the plastic is degraded and risks exploding under pressure.
  • **Water ingress**: Feel the carpets behind the seats and in the trunk. If it's damp, the soft top drains are clogged (risk of burning out the Bose amplifier or Bluetooth module located there).
  • **Oil level**: A low level during the inspection is a *red flag*. MZR engines hate oil starvation.
  • **PRHT roof**: Check the fluidity of the opening/closing mechanism (should take about 12 seconds without abnormal noises).
  • **Thermostat**: During the test drive, the temperature needle should stabilize in the middle. If it drops while driving, the thermostat is stuck open.

Performance

  • Pure and Unrivalled Driving Pleasure — The Mazda MX-5 NC embodies the Jinba Ittai philosophy with exemplary road handling, perfect 50/50 weight distribution, and agile rear-wheel drive. Its precise steering and dynamic balance offer an engaging and communicative driving experience.

safety

  • Enhanced Active and Passive Safety — Benefiting from a more rigid and safer platform, the MX-5 NC offers solid protection for its occupants, complemented by modern safety systems.
  • Efficient and Controlled Braking — The MX-5 NC's braking system ensures good performance with short stopping distances and excellent pedal feel, reinforced by ABS and Electronic Brakeforce Distribution (EBD) for optimal control.

practicality

  • Versatility with Power Retractable Hard Top (PRHT) — The PRHT (Power Retractable Hard Top) version offers the best of both worlds: the joy of a roadster and the comfort of a coupé, thanks to an electrically retractable hardtop, without compromising boot space.

economy

  • Controlled Running Costs — Enjoy a sporty roadster with very reasonable maintenance costs and spare parts, making ownership of this vehicle accessible and enjoyable for everyday use.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: BMW Z4 (E85) for 6-cylinder enthusiasts, Audi TT (8J) for premium finish and the security of front-wheel drive/Quattro, Mercedes SLK (R171) for comfort. - **Japan / Asia**: Honda S2000 (much more radical, powerful, and expensive), Toyota MR2 Roadster (W30 - mid-rear engine). - **North America**: Pontiac Solstice / Saturn Sky (spectacular design but heavier and less precise), Porsche Boxster 987 (superior prestige but incomparable maintenance budget).

Verdict

The Mazda MX-5 NC is a fantastic roadster, offering an almost unbeatable price/pleasure/reliability ratio on the used car market. The 2.0L engine, particularly from the NC2 facelift (2008+) onwards, is the preferred choice for driving enthusiasts thanks to its reinforced internals and more spirited character.

However, **two major drawbacks require absolute vigilance before purchase**: underbody corrosion (which can quickly become very expensive in bodywork) and the coolant expansion tank (to be preventively replaced for about fifty euros to avoid fatal engine failure). Prioritize a healthy example, ideally treated against rust (Dinitrol/Waxoyl), with a clear maintenance history.
