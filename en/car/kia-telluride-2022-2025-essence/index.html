Kia Telluride

I facelift (MX5) · 2022 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The first-generation Kia Telluride (code MX5) received a facelift in late 2022 for the 2023 model year. Designed specifically for the North American and Middle Eastern markets, this large 3-row SUV (up to 8 seats) is not officially sold in Europe. Sharing its platform and mechanicals with the Hyundai Palisade, it has established itself as an absolute benchmark in its class. It shines with its price-to-performance ratio, roominess, and above all, remarkable reliability, praised by J.D. Power and Consumer Reports. The facelift brings technological updates (dual curved screen) and aesthetic changes, while retaining the robust original mechanicals.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability (Top segment J.D. Power and Consumer Reports)
  • Robust and proven naturally aspirated V6 engine (Lambda II)
  • Smooth and highly reliable 8-speed automatic gearbox
  • Efficient AWD HTRAC without major issues
  • Very generous manufacturer warranty (10 years / 100,000 miles in the USA)

Weaknesses 4

  • High fuel consumption (heavy naturally aspirated V6)
  • Total absence of hybrid or PHEV alternative
  • Size unsuited to European roads (isolated import only)
  • A few safety recalls concerning accessories and cabin electronics

Check before buying

  • 2024 models: Imperatively check if the recall on the valve springs has been performed (risk of engine failure).
  • 2020-2024 models: Check the operation of the power front seats (recall for risk of overheating/fire of the adjustment motor).
  • 2020-2024 models: Check the recall on the right front driveshaft (risk of disengagement).
  • Onboard electronics: Ensure that the digital instrument cluster does not show cutouts or black screens (known and recalled issue).

Alternatives

- **North America / Middle East:** Hyundai Palisade (technical cousin, same engine, same reliability), Toyota Highlander (offers a very frugal hybrid version), Honda Pilot (highly reliable V6, excellent alternative), Ford Explorer, Chevrolet Traverse. - **Europe:** Not applicable (vehicle not marketed). Similar-sized alternatives would be the Kia Sorento (slightly smaller but available in PHEV/HEV), the Toyota Highlander, or the Ford Explorer PHEV.

Verdict

The Kia Telluride (2022-2025 facelift) is a highly recommended purchase for anyone looking for a large family SUV in the markets where it is available (North America, Middle East). Its sole 3.8L V6 engine is a model of reliability, free from the complex issues associated with turbos or hybrid systems. J.D. Power and Consumer Reports statistics consistently rank it among the most reliable vehicles in its class. The only major downside concerns a critical recall on the valve springs of 2024 models, which must imperatively be checked before purchase. If fuel consumption is not a deterrent, it is one of the best choices on the market.
