Summary

The Mazda CX-50 (Generation I) is a compact SUV with an "adventurer" positioning, designed primarily for the North American market and manufactured in the United States (Huntsville, Alabama plant, in a joint venture with Toyota). Longer, wider, and featuring optimized ground clearance compared to the CX-5, it targets customers with an active lifestyle. It stands out for its above-average build quality for its category and excellent initial reliability scores. According to J.D. Power (IQS 2023), Mazda ranked as the top brand in initial quality, with the CX-50 leading its segment, surpassing benchmarks like the Toyota RAV4 or Honda CR-V.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional initial quality (J.D. Power IQS 2023 leader ahead of Toyota and Honda)
  • Widely proven reliability of the 2.5L naturally aspirated and Turbo engines
  • Highly successful integration of the Toyota hybrid system (on Hybrid version)
  • Extremely robust and responsive 6-speed automatic gearbox (Skyactiv-Drive)
  • Build quality and interior finish superior to the category
  • High-performance and standard i-ACTIV AWD system (in North America)

Weaknesses 4

  • Model not officially available on the European market
  • Fairly high fuel consumption of the 2.5 Turbo version in dynamic driving
  • Slightly firm suspension comfort, particularly with 20-inch wheels
  • Rear headroom slightly penalized by the low roofline

Check before buying

  • NHTSA recall history (particularly regarding suspension mounts and the tow hitch)
  • Proper functioning of the infotainment system (need to have the latest OTA updates)
  • Condition of the underbody if the vehicle has been used off-road (the model's vocation)

Official recalls

As the CX-50 is not marketed in France, it does not appear in the French Rappel Conso database. Here are the official recalls issued by the NHTSA (United States) for this model:

  • [2025-10-29] Suspension (25V737000): On certain 2025 models (ICE and Hybrid), the front suspension mounting bolts may not be tightened properly, risking detachment of the wheel or components.
  • [2025-06-18] Airbags (25V413000): Improperly configured module software may result in incorrect airbag deployment.
  • [2025-03-14] Tow hitch (25V167000): On 2024-2025 models equipped with a genuine accessory tow hitch, the bolts may loosen and cause the tow hitch to separate.
  • [2024-09-03] Front camera (24V649000): A malfunction of the front sensing camera (FSC) can disable automatic emergency braking and lane keeping assist.
  • [2023-04-18] ABS brakes (23V275000): The ABS hydraulic control unit (HCU) may be damaged, reducing braking capacity.
  • [2023-03-01] Tow hitch wiring harness (23V135000): The genuine accessory tow hitch wiring harness may detach and become damaged.

What sets it apart 10

Reliability

  • Proven 2.5L Skyactiv-G Engine — The atmospheric 2.5L engine has been used for years on the CX-5 with no major defects reported, giving buyers a well-known and dependable powertrain for daily driving.
  • Robust Six-Speed Skyactiv-Drive Gearbox — The six-speed automatic transmission is described as extremely robust and responsive, offering smooth and reliable operation over time without the complexity of more elaborate gearboxes.
  • Top Initial Quality in Its Segment — J.D. Power IQS 2023 ranked Mazda the best brand for initial quality, with the CX-50 leading its segment ahead of the Toyota RAV4 and Honda CR-V.
  • No Recurring Turbo Issues Reported — The turbo engine, proven on the CX-9 and CX-5, has no recurring turbo problems reported, giving buyers confidence in the durability of the forced-induction powertrain.

Economy

  • Toyota Hybrid Technology Integration — The Hybrid version uses Toyota's proven hybrid synergy, similar to the RAV4 Hybrid, with successful software integration by Mazda, delivering excellent fuel economy for city and suburban drivers.
  • Low Maintenance Costs on Atmospheric Engine — The 2.5 Skyactiv-G atmospheric engine offers smooth operation and controlled maintenance costs, keeping long-term running expenses predictable for everyday and highway use.

Handling

  • Standard i-ACTIV All-Wheel Drive — The i-ACTIV AWD system is fitted as standard in North America, giving buyers added traction and confidence on varied road surfaces and in changing weather conditions.

Comfort

  • Above-Average Build and Interior Finish — Build quality and interior finish are superior to the category average, so buyers get a cabin that feels well assembled and durable from the first day of ownership.

Performance

  • Turbo Engine with Generous Torque — The 2.5T Skyactiv-G Turbo delivers generous torque and very good performance, making it suited to towing, mountain driving and long loaded journeys.

Practicality

  • Raised Ground Clearance for Active Use — Compared with the CX-5, the CX-50 is longer, wider and has optimized ground clearance, supporting an active lifestyle and use on less polished surfaces.

Alternatives

  • North America: Subaru Outback (direct competitor in the adventurer positioning), Toyota RAV4, Honda CR-V, Ford Bronco Sport.
  • Europe: As the model is not sold, internal alternatives are the Mazda CX-5 or the Mazda CX-60. Among competitors: Volkswagen Tiguan, Toyota RAV4.
  • China: Toyota RAV4, Honda Breeze, Nissan X-Trail.

Verdict

The Mazda CX-50 is a highly recommended choice for the markets where it is available. By relying on the Skyactiv-G internal combustion engines whose reliability is well proven, and by borrowing Toyota's hybrid technology for its electrified version, Mazda has created an extremely robust SUV. Data from J.D. Power and Consumer Reports confirm excellent initial quality and projected reliability, surpassing even the segment leaders. The only points of vigilance concern a few early recalls (suspension/tow hitch assembly) covered by the manufacturer. It is a worry-free purchase.
