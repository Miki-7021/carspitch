Rivian R1T

I · 2022 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 70/100 — bon

Summary

The Rivian R1T (Generation I) is the first mass-produced 100% electric pickup truck to see the light of day, narrowly beating its historical competitors. Positioned as an "Electric Adventure Vehicle", it stands out for its exceptional off-road capabilities, innovative design (notably the famous transverse *Gear Tunnel*), and supercar performance. As a product of a brand-new make, the R1T suffered from teething problems at launch (build quality, software bugs), earning it below-average initial reliability ratings (J.D. Power, Consumer Reports). However, the mechanical robustness of its powertrains and the brand's responsiveness via over-the-air (OTA) updates make it a constantly improving vehicle. Models produced from 2024 onwards show significantly higher build quality.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional performance and extraordinary traction (especially in Quad-Motor)
  • Real off-road capabilities (adjustable ground clearance, approach/departure angles)
  • Practical innovations (Gear Tunnel, integrated air compressor, 110V outlets)
  • Frequent software updates (OTA) adding real features
  • Luxurious and minimalist interior design

Weaknesses 5

  • Hit-or-miss build quality on early model years (2022-2023)
  • Service Center network still very limited, extending repair times
  • First-generation electric tonneau cover very unreliable
  • Extremely high bodywork repair costs (complex unibody design)
  • High power consumption (weight of over 3 tons)

Check before buying

  • NHTSA recall history (very numerous on early models, see list)
  • Smooth operation of the electric Tonneau Cover
  • Body panel alignment and wind noise at high speed
  • Tire wear (weight and torque destroy tires quickly)
  • Opening mechanisms of the front trunk (Frunk) and tailgate
  • Abnormal noises from the air suspension or compressors

Alternatives

- **North America**: Ford F-150 Lightning (more traditional, massive after-sales network), Tesla Cybertruck (polarizing design, 48V/Steer-by-wire technology), Chevrolet Silverado EV (massive range), GMC Hummer EV (heavier, less efficient). - **Europe / Asia**: Model not officially distributed. No direct electric competitor of this size, apart from a few F-150 Lightning imports.

Verdict

The Rivian R1T is a brilliantly engineered vehicle, offering unmatched versatility between on-road performance and off-road capabilities. However, as a start-up product, the 2022 and 2023 models suffered teething troubles: assembly issues, software bugs, and frequent hardware recalls. The core mechanics (motors, battery) are robust, but the peripherals require attention. If buying used, favor a late 2023 or 2024 model, or ensure the vehicle has a crystal-clear maintenance history with all recalls performed. The limited repair network is the biggest current hurdle: make sure you live near a Rivian Service Center.
