Summary

The Rivian R1T (Generation I) is the first mass-produced 100% electric pickup truck to see the light of day, narrowly beating its historical competitors. Positioned as an "Electric Adventure Vehicle", it stands out for its exceptional off-road capabilities, innovative design (notably the famous transverse *Gear Tunnel*), and supercar performance. As a product of a brand-new make, the R1T suffered from teething problems at launch (build quality, software bugs), earning it below-average initial reliability ratings (J.D. Power, Consumer Reports). However, the mechanical robustness of its powertrains and the brand's responsiveness via over-the-air (OTA) updates make it a constantly improving vehicle. Models produced from 2024 onwards show significantly higher build quality.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional performance and extraordinary traction (especially in Quad-Motor)
  • Real off-road capabilities (adjustable ground clearance, approach/departure angles)
  • Practical innovations (Gear Tunnel, integrated air compressor, 110V outlets)
  • Frequent software updates (OTA) adding real features
  • Luxurious and minimalist interior design

Weaknesses 5

  • Hit-or-miss build quality on early model years (2022-2023)
  • Service Center network still very limited, extending repair times
  • First-generation electric tonneau cover very unreliable
  • Extremely high bodywork repair costs (complex unibody design)
  • High power consumption (weight of over 3 tons)

Check before buying

  • NHTSA recall history (very numerous on early models, see list)
  • Smooth operation of the electric Tonneau Cover
  • Body panel alignment and wind noise at high speed
  • Tire wear (weight and torque destroy tires quickly)
  • Opening mechanisms of the front trunk (Frunk) and tailgate
  • Abnormal noises from the air suspension or compressors

Official recalls

The R1T has been the subject of numerous teething recalls (NHTSA). Most are minor or correctable via OTA, but some affect mechanical safety:

  • [2026-01-15] Seat belts: Incorrect tightening of the 2nd-row seat belt retractor bolts (R1T 2022-2025).
  • [2026-01-05] Suspension: Toe link potentially improperly reassembled during a previous service (R1T 2022-2025).
  • [2025-09-08] ADAS software: The Hands-Free Highway Assist system may fail to identify a preceding vehicle (fixed by OTA 2025.18.30).
  • [2025-08-21] Traction battery: Poor grounding in the high-voltage distribution box (HVDB) that can cause a loss of power (R1T 2025).
  • [2025-06-11] Lighting: Potential failure of the front turn signals (R1T 2025).
  • [2025-06-03] Seat belts: Incorrect installation of the front D-ring anchor bolts (R1T 2022-2025).
  • [2024-06-20] Labels: Incorrect maximum load capacity value on the tire label (R1T 2022-2023).
  • [2024-06-05] Airbags / Trim: B/C pillar mounting clips potentially damaged during service (R1T 2022-2024).
  • [2024-05-23] Lighting: Headlights potentially misaimed from the factory (R1T 2023-2024).
  • [2024-05-09] Labels: Missing airbag warning label on the dashboard (R1T 2024).
  • [2023-12-21] Accelerator pedal: The pedal may not detect driver release, preventing auto-hold activation (R1T 2022).
  • [2023-11-21] Climate control/Defrost: A software update mistakenly disabled the defrost controls (R1T 2022-2023, fixed by OTA).
  • [2023-02-22] Seat belts: Defective ALR sensor reporting incorrect locking of the passenger seat (R1T 2022).
  • [2022-10-06] Front suspension: Fastener between the upper control arm and the steering knuckle improperly tightened (R1T 2022).
  • [2022-08-25] Seat belts: Front anchors potentially improperly secured to the B-pillar (R1T 2022).
  • [2022-05-10] Seats / Airbag: The occupant classification system (OCS) may not deactivate the airbag in the presence of a child seat (R1T 2022).

What sets it apart 11

Performance

  • Capacités tout-terrain réelles et ajustables — La garde au sol réglable et les angles d'attaque et de fuite permettent d'affronter des terrains difficiles que la plupart des pick-up ne peuvent pas aborder.
  • Performances de supercar — La version Quad-Motor atteint environ 3 secondes de 0 à 100 km/h, une accélération rare pour un pick-up de plus de 3 tonnes.
  • Polyvalence route et hors route — Le R1T combine performances sur route et capacités hors route réelles, ce qui lui confère une polyvalence d'usage difficile à égaler.

Handling

  • Motricité vectorielle roue par roue — La version Quad-Motor répartit le couple indépendamment sur chaque roue, ce qui améliore la traction sur sol glissant et la capacité à franchir des obstacles.

Practicality

  • Gear Tunnel transversal très pratique — Ce passage traversant sous la benne permet de ranger des objets longs ou encombrants, un avantage concret pour transporter du matériel d'aventure.
  • Compresseur d'air et prises intégrés — Le compresseur d'air embarqué et les prises 110V permettent d'utiliser des outils ou de gonfler des pneus directement sur le terrain, sans équipement externe.

Reliability

  • Mises à jour logicielles à distance — Les mises à jour OTA fréquentes ajoutent de réelles fonctionnalités et corrigent des bugs, ce qui améliore le véhicule au fil du temps sans passage en atelier.
  • Mécanique des moteurs robuste — Les groupes motopropulseurs, qu'il s'agisse des moteurs Bosch du Quad-Motor ou des Enduro du Dual-Motor, sont décrits comme mécaniquement robustes.
  • Qualité améliorée dès 2024 — Les modèles produits à partir de 2024 affichent une qualité de fabrication nettement supérieure, ce qui réduit les risques liés aux premiers millésimes.

Economy

  • Dual-Motor plus efficient et autonome — Les moteurs Enduro conçus en interne offrent une efficience supérieure et l'autonomie maximale avec le Max Pack, adaptés aux longs trajets.

Comfort

  • Intérieur luxueux et minimaliste — L'habitacle au design épuré et haut de gamme offre un cadre agréable au quotidien, malgré l'orientation aventure du véhicule.

Alternatives

  • North America: Ford F-150 Lightning (more traditional, massive after-sales network), Tesla Cybertruck (polarizing design, 48V/Steer-by-wire technology), Chevrolet Silverado EV (massive range), GMC Hummer EV (heavier, less efficient).
  • Europe / Asia: Model not officially distributed. No direct electric competitor of this size, apart from a few F-150 Lightning imports.

Verdict

The Rivian R1T is a brilliantly engineered vehicle, offering unmatched versatility between on-road performance and off-road capabilities. However, as a start-up product, the 2022 and 2023 models suffered teething troubles: assembly issues, software bugs, and frequent hardware recalls. The core mechanics (motors, battery) are robust, but the peripherals require attention. If buying used, favor a late 2023 or 2024 model, or ensure the vehicle has a crystal-clear maintenance history with all recalls performed. The limited repair network is the biggest current hurdle: make sure you live near a Rivian Service Center.
