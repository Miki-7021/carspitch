Renault Kangoo

II (KW) · 2008 – 2021

NEGOTIATE Reliability 74/100 — bon

Summary

The Renault Kangoo II (codename KW) marks a major turning point compared to the first generation. Based on the Scénic II platform, it sheds its 'rustic utility vehicle' image to become a true family leisure activity vehicle, offering ride comfort, soundproofing, and road handling worthy of a compact minivan.

It had a long career divided into two main phases: Phase 1 (2008-2013) with rounded lines, and Phase 2 (2013-2021) featuring Renault's new visual identity (grille with large diamond logo). A major technical update occurred in 2012/2013 with the introduction of 'Energy' engines (optimized) and the catastrophic 1.2 TCe.

**Expert's overall note:** An excellent family and utility vehicle, extremely practical, but whose reliability drastically depends on the year and engine chosen. Diesels (1.5 dCi), which represent 90% of the market, had a difficult start before becoming excellent engines from 2013 onwards.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional usable volume and practicality (sliding doors, flat floor)
  • Suspension comfort worthy of a sedan (Scénic II platform)
  • Remarkable reliability of 1.5 dCi engines from 2012/2013 onwards
  • Very reasonable maintenance and insurance costs
  • Excellent peripheral visibility

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.2 TCe 115 petrol engine
  • Fragility of early 1.5 dCi (2008-2011): injection and connecting rod assembly
  • Utility-grade interior finish: hard plastics that scratch easily
  • Imperfect soundproofing on the highway (wind noise)
  • Rapid wear of front running gear (link rods, silentblocks) due to weight

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history for 1.5 dCi models before 2012 (invoice for connecting rod bearings or injection pump?)
  • Smooth operation of sliding side doors (rails often clogged or worn rollers)
  • Condition of the front axle: clunking over speed bumps (stabilizer bar link rods, wishbones)
  • Dashboard warning lights: DPF/EGR on urban diesels, AdBlue light on recent models
  • Wear of front seats (foam sagging at the edges)

Alternatives

Verdict

The Renault Kangoo II is an essential benchmark among leisure activity vehicles. If you are looking for a diesel model, **definitely target a model from after 2012/2013 equipped with the 1.5 dCi 'Energy' (90 or 110 hp)**. These versions are extremely reliable, economical, and capable of racking up kilometers without a hitch.

On the other hand, **absolutely avoid the 1.2 TCe 115 petrol versions**, which are true nests of financial woes. Diesels from the 2008-2011 period (70 and 85 hp) should be negotiated with extreme caution, requiring proof of preventive connecting rod bearing replacement. For low-mileage drivers, the rare 1.6 16v petrol is a rustic but indestructible choice.
