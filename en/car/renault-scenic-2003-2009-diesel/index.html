Summary

The Renault Scénic II (Type JM) was a pioneer and an absolute best-seller in the compact MPV segment in Europe. Launched in 2003, it stood out with its divisive design inspired by the Mégane II, exceptional modularity (individual seats, multiple storage compartments), and high-level comfort. It was available in a short version (5 seats) and Grand Scénic (5 or 7 seats).

However, its reputation was heavily tarnished by catastrophic reliability problems on Phase 1 (2003-2006), affecting both electronics (dashboards, hands-free cards) and mechanics (turbo and engine failures on early dCi diesels). The Phase 2 (late 2006-2009) facelift corrected the vast majority of these defects and introduced the excellent 2.0 dCi engine, making late-production models infinitely more recommendable.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional spaciousness and modularity (removable seats, flat floor)
  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation (especially on Phase 2)
  • Passive safety (5 Euro NCAP stars, very safe for its time)
  • Excellent reliability of the 2.0 dCi 150 hp engine and petrol units
  • Ubiquitous storage spaces (floor compartments, drawers)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous mechanical reliability of 1.5 dCi (82/100) and 1.9 dCi (120) diesels before 2006
  • Capricious electronics (dashboard, hands-free card, tire pressure sensors)
  • Very expensive electric parking brake to replace in case of failure
  • Premature tire wear on the front axle (high weight)
  • Peeling interior plastics ("soft touch" finish aging poorly)

Check before buying

  • Digital dashboard: Check that it doesn't flicker, turn off, or that the mileage hasn't been reset (very common failure, defective solders).
  • Windshield cowl: Water drains get clogged with leaves. Water overflows into the cabin, floods the floor compartments, and fries the UCH (central electronic unit).
  • Connecting rod bearings (1.5 dCi): Demand invoices proving rigorous maintenance. At the slightest metallic knocking sound during acceleration, flee.
  • Turbo (1.9 dCi): A loud whistle or blue/white smoke from the exhaust indicates imminent failure.
  • Automatic parking brake: Ensure it engages and disengages without abnormal noise or error message on the dashboard.
  • Window regulators: Test all 4 windows (plastic clips frequently break).

Verdict

The Renault Scénic II is a vehicle with two faces. Phase 1 (2003-2006) models equipped with 1.5 dCi (82/100) and 1.9 dCi (120) diesels are to be absolutely avoided: they accumulate the worst mechanical and electronic flaws of the decade at Renault. However, if you target a Phase 2 (2007-2009), the assessment changes completely. The 2.0 dCi 150 hp is a model of robustness, as are the petrol engines (1.6 and 2.0 16v). The purchase is therefore conditional on a drastic selection of the year, the engine, and verification of the maintenance history (especially the cleaning of the windshield cowl).
