Volkswagen ID.3

I (E11) · 2019 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 74/100 — bon

Summary

The Volkswagen ID.3 (Generation I, type E11) marks a historic turning point for the Wolfsburg manufacturer. Designed as the 'Golf of the electric era,' it inaugurates the modular MEB platform dedicated exclusively to electric vehicles (BEV). Launched in late 2019 (deliveries in 2020), it stands out with its rear-wheel drive architecture (rear motor), offering an excellent turning radius and interior space worthy of the segment above.

However, its launch was marred by major software issues (laborious development by the CARIAD subsidiary) and interior finish quality deemed unworthy of the brand. Aware of these shortcomings, Volkswagen carried out an **early facelift in 2023 (Phase 2)**, significantly improving interior materials, infotainment system stability (software v3.5+), and aerodynamics.

**Expert's overall note:** An intrinsically well-designed vehicle mechanically and electrically, but whose early model years (2020-2021) require absolute vigilance regarding the history of software and hardware updates.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional interior space for its size (2.77 m wheelbase)
  • Ultra-short turning radius (10.2 m) thanks to rear-wheel drive
  • Very refined suspension comfort and sound insulation
  • Mechanically extremely reliable electric motor (APP310)
  • Dramatic improvement in perceived quality on facelifted versions (2023+)

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic onboard software on 2020-2021 model years (slowness, bugs, connection losses)
  • Interior plastic quality unworthy of VW on Phase 1 (2019-2022)
  • Frustrating all-touch ergonomics (haptic steering wheel controls, unlit sliders)
  • Premature wear of rear tires due to weight and torque
  • Heat pump often charged as an option (and efficiency disputed by some tests)

Check before buying

  • System software version (must be ≥ 3.2 to ensure stability and OTA updates)
  • Proof of execution of campaign 97ZZ (12V battery replacement)
  • Proof of execution of campaign 93P7 (HV battery module check/replacement)
  • Wear condition of rear tires (often need changing before 30,000 km)
  • Clunking noises from the front axle (links, silentblocks)
  • Central screen operation (responsiveness, absence of unexpected restarts)

Verdict

The Volkswagen ID.3 is an excellent electric car whose reputation was tarnished by a rushed launch. From a purely mechanical standpoint (motor, chassis), it is extremely robust. Problems are almost exclusively software and electronic (12V) in nature.

**Buying advice:** - **Avoid 2020/2021 '1st Edition' models if possible** unless the seller can prove that ALL recall campaigns (12V battery, HV modules) and software updates (dealership visit for v2.4, then OTA to v3.2+) have been performed. - **Prioritize models from late 2022 onwards**, or ideally the **Phase 2 (facelifted in 2023)** which corrects finish defects and includes finally mature software. - The **Pro (58 kWh)** version represents the best compromise for 90% of uses.
