Mercedes GLC

X253 · 2015 – 2023

NEGOTIATE Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The **Mercedes-Benz GLC (X253 Generation)** succeeded the GLK in 2015, marking a complete stylistic break with softer, more aerodynamic lines. Based on the MRA platform (shared with the C-Class W205), it established itself as one of the best-selling premium SUVs in the world.

A **major facelift occurred mid-2019**, bringing the MBUX infotainment system, a revised light signature, and especially a **new generation of diesel engines (transition from the aging OM651 to the modern OM654)**. The GLC shines with its supreme comfort, sound insulation, and 9G-Tronic gearbox, but requires a premium maintenance budget, particularly on versions equipped with *Air Body Control* air suspension.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with Air Body Control)
  • Very high level of cabin sound insulation
  • Smooth and responsive 9G-Tronic automatic gearbox
  • Finish quality and interior presentation (especially post-facelift with MBUX)
  • Remarkable fuel efficiency of the OM654 diesel engine (post-2019)

Weaknesses 5

  • Reliability and replacement cost of the air suspension (Air Body Control)
  • Capricious and expensive-to-repair AdBlue/SCR emission control system
  • OM651 diesel engine (2015-2019) rough and noisy under load
  • Reduced boot volume on plug-in hybrid (PHEV) versions
  • Possible creaks from the black lacquered plastic center console

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history of the AdBlue system and any tank/pump replacements (very common).
  • Condition of air springs and compressor if equipped with Air Body Control (the car should not sag when stationary).
  • Proof of 9G-Tronic gearbox oil change (every 125,000 km or 5 years maximum).
  • Traces of coolant leakage around the water pump (OM651) or in the engine's V (OM642).
  • Verification of manufacturer recall execution (especially the EGR cooler on diesels).

Official recalls

  • - **EGR Cooler (2019-2022)**: Risk of coolant leakage into the EGR circuit potentially leading to fire (primarily concerns OM651 and OM654 diesel engines).
  • - **Steering (2017-2018)**: Electrical harness of the steering column potentially shearing, leading to inadvertent deployment of the driver's airbag.
  • - **Starter (2017)**: Risk of starter current limiter overheating if the engine is blocked, potentially causing a fire.
  • - **Takata Airbags (Various years)**: Worldwide recall for the replacement of defective airbag gas generators.
  • - **eCall System (2021)**: Software update because the emergency call system could transmit an incorrect vehicle position.

What sets it apart 14

safety

  • Exemplary Safety — The Mercedes GLC X253 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult occupant protection and 82% for child occupant protection. It was also named an IIHS Top Safety Pick+ in 2016, highlighting its commitment to occupant safety.

braking

  • Powerful and Stable Braking — Renowned for its excellent braking performance, the GLC X253 offers short stopping distances and remarkable stability, even during emergency braking. Its braking assistance systems actively contribute to optimal safety on all roads.

handling

  • Balanced Road Handling — The GLC X253 stands out with a perfect balance between comfort and agility. Its road holding is stable and predictable, with well-controlled body roll. Precise steering and refined suspension (especially the Air Body Control option) ensure a smooth and secure drive, even at high speeds.

comfort

  • Exceptional Ride Comfort — Enjoy supreme ride comfort, particularly with the Air Body Control pneumatic suspension, which absorbs road imperfections with unparalleled smoothness. Cabin sound insulation is of a very high standard, guaranteeing absolute serenity on board.

economy

  • Modern and Efficient OM654 Diesel Engine: Economy and Smoothness — For facelifted models (post-mid-2019), the OM654 diesel engine (GLC 200d, 220d, 300d) offers remarkable fuel efficiency, increased quietness, and great smoothness. It is an excellent choice for versatile and economical driving.

Performance

  • Smooth and Responsive 9G-Tronic Automatic Transmission — Mercedes-Benz's 9G-Tronic automatic transmission is renowned for its smoothness and responsiveness. It ensures imperceptible gear changes, contributing to a most enjoyable driving experience and optimized fuel consumption.

quality

  • Premium Finish Quality and Interior Presentation — The GLC X253 offers a finish quality and interior presentation worthy of the premium segment. Materials are carefully selected and assembly is rigorous, creating a luxurious and welcoming atmosphere, especially after the facelift with the MBUX system.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: BMW X3 (G01 - dynamic benchmark), Audi Q5 (FY - finish and Quattro), Volvo XC60 II (comfort and design), Porsche Macan (sportiness). - **Japan / Asia**: Lexus NX (unbeatable hybrid reliability), Genesis GV70. - **North America**: Acura RDX, Lincoln Corsair, BMW X3 (very popular in 30i/M40i). - **Emerging markets**: Often competed against by more robust SUVs like the Toyota Land Cruiser Prado, although the GLC is sold there as an urban luxury vehicle.

Verdict

The Mercedes GLC X253 is an excellent premium SUV, but it requires a wise engine choice. **If you are looking for a diesel, absolutely prioritize a facelifted model (post-mid-2019) equipped with the OM654 engine (GLC 200d, 220d, 300d)**. It is infinitely quieter, more fuel-efficient, and generally more reliable than the old OM651, despite persistent weaknesses in the AdBlue system. Avoid *Air Body Control* air suspension on high-mileage models, as its replacement will cost thousands of euros. A 2020 GLC 220d 4MATIC with conventional suspension is the ideal compromise.
