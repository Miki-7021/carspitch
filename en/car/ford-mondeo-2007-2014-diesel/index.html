Summary

The fourth-generation Ford Mondeo, known by the internal code BA7, was launched in 2007 and marked a turning point for Ford in Europe. Based on the EUCD platform (shared with the S-Max and Galaxy, as well as certain Volvos), it was quickly praised for its modern 'Kinetic Design', excellent road handling, and generous interior space. Positioned in the family car segment, it was available as a 4-door sedan, 5-door hatchback, and wagon (SW). A significant facelift occurred in late 2010 (2011 models), bringing aesthetic (front fascia, taillights) and technical evolutions (new, more powerful EcoBoost gasoline and TDCi engines, interior improvements). The Mondeo IV is renowned for its driving pleasure, comfort on long journeys, and its price/performance ratio. Its overall reliability is good, but certain engines, particularly diesel, require special attention.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent road handling and driving pleasure
  • Generous interior space and large trunk (especially in wagon)
  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation
  • Attractive price/performance ratio on the used market
  • Very reliable naturally aspirated Duratec gasoline engines
  • Timeless and modern design

Weaknesses 6

  • Quality of certain interior plastics (pre-facelift)
  • Reliability of the 1.8 TDCi (DMF, injectors)
  • Potential problems on early 2.0 EcoBoost (chain, coolant leaks)
  • DPF and EGR valve clogging on diesels with urban use
  • High fuel consumption of the most powerful gasoline engines
  • Multimedia system (pre-facelift) a bit dated

Check before buying

  • Complete service history: Invoices, up-to-date service book, especially for diesels and EcoBoost.
  • Dual-Mass Flywheel (DMF): Noises (clicking, knocking) at idle, clutch vibrations, jerks when starting/stopping the engine (especially 1.8 TDCi, but also 2.0/2.2 TDCi).
  • DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter): Warning light on, loss of power, excessive smoke (on diesels). Check for regular regenerations.
  • EGR Valve: Loss of power, jerks, black smoke (on diesels).
  • Injectors: Difficult starts, unstable idle, smoke, excessive fuel consumption (especially 1.8 TDCi).
  • Turbo: Excessive whistling, loss of power, blue smoke from the exhaust (all turbo engines).
  • Coolant leaks: Around the thermostat housing or water pump (especially 2.0 EcoBoost).
  • Timing chain noises: On cold start (especially 2.0 EcoBoost).
  • Tire and brake condition: The Mondeo is heavy, wear can be rapid.
  • Air conditioning operation: Compressor problems or leaks.

Verdict

The Ford Mondeo IV (BA7) is an excellent family car, offering driving pleasure rare in its category, high-level comfort, and generous interior space. It's a wise choice on the used market, provided you choose the right engine and check its history. The naturally aspirated Duratec gasoline engines (1.6, 2.0, 2.3) are the most reliable and recommended for versatile use without performance constraints. The 2.5 Duratec is an excellent choice for enthusiasts, but thirstier. The 2.0 EcoBoost engines are powerful but require increased vigilance on early model years.

For diesels, which are the core of this model in Europe: * The 1.8 TDCi is to AVOID unless the DMF and injectors have already been replaced and the price is very low. Repair costs can exceed the vehicle's value. * The 2.0 TDCi (115, 140, 163 hp) are the most recommended. They offer an excellent performance/consumption compromise and generally good reliability, provided rigorous maintenance and appropriate use (avoid short urban trips for the DPF). The 140 hp is the most balanced. The 163 hp is an excellent post-facelift evolution. * The 2.2 TDCi (175, 200 hp) are also good choices for those seeking more power, with the same precautions as the 2.0 TDCi.

Prioritize a post-facelift model (from late 2010) for aesthetic and interior improvements and the more modern (163/200 hp) diesel and corrected EcoBoost engines. A clear service history is imperative, especially for diesels and EcoBoost. Do not hesitate to NEGOTIATE if signs of wear on expensive parts (DMF, DPF, injectors) are present or if maintenance is lacking.
