BMW Série 5

E60/E61 · 2003 – 2010

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The BMW 5 Series E60 (sedan) and E61 (Touring/wagon) marked a major stylistic break under the Chris Bangle era. Highly controversial upon its release, its lines have ultimately aged remarkably well. It introduced cutting-edge technologies for its time: iDrive multimedia system, active steering, head-up display, and an aluminum front section for ideal weight distribution (50/50). The March 2007 facelift (LCI) brought improved interior finish, a more reliable iDrive, LED lights, and a complete overhaul of the engine range (transition to direct injection gasoline and new diesel blocks). Although its road qualities are exceptional, it is a complex vehicle whose electronic and mechanical reliability (depending on the engines) requires rigorous monitoring.

Strengths 5

  • Superb road handling, comfort/dynamism compromise (especially with Dynamic Drive)
  • 6-cylinder diesel engines (M57) combining high-level performance and fuel efficiency
  • Build quality and sound insulation (especially on post-2007 LCI versions)
  • Exterior design that has aged remarkably well
  • Interior space and trunk volume (especially in Touring E61)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very capricious onboard electronics (iDrive CCC restarting in a loop, IBS sensor)
  • Maintenance and spare parts cost worthy of the luxury segment
  • Disastrous reliability of the N47 diesel engine (timing chain)
  • Rear air suspension on E61 (fragile compressor and air springs)
  • Rapid wear of running gear (front thrust arm bushings)

Check before buying

  • Clear maintenance history (service book + invoices), avoid "DIY-repaired" vehicles
  • Smooth operation of the automatic gearbox (no jerks, fluid change done?)
  • Absence of exhaust smell in the cabin (sign of a cracked manifold on M57)
  • Full functionality of the iDrive (controller, screen, GPS, radio, Bluetooth)
  • On E61: operation of the rear air suspension (the car should not sag when stationary)
  • Condition of the trunk floor (lift the carpet to check for absence of water near the control units)

Official recalls

  • - **March 2012 (Battery Cable)**: Massive recall (over 1.3 million vehicles) for a defect in the battery cable insulation in the trunk, which could lead to overheating or even a fire.
  • - **2010 (Vacuum Pump - V8)**: Possible oil leak in the brake assist system on V8 engines (N62), reducing braking assistance.
  • - **Various Years (Takata Airbags)**: Replacement of the driver/passenger airbag gas generator (depending on regions and production years).
  • - **2011 (Fuel Filter Heater)**: Risk of short circuit on the fuel heater (diesel models), which could cause battery discharge or a fire.

Alternatives

Verdict

The BMW 5 Series E60/E61 is a formidable road car that offers a rare driving pleasure for its size. However, it is a complex car that does not tolerate approximate maintenance. **For a diesel purchase, the top choice is the M57 6-cylinder (525d or 530d)**, preferably in the facelifted (LCI) version to benefit from the more reliable iDrive and better finish, provided the swirl flaps are removed and the manifold is checked. **Absolutely avoid the 177hp 520d (N47 engine)** due to its timing chain, unless the repair has been recently done at BMW. Plan for a reserve budget for electronics and running gear.
