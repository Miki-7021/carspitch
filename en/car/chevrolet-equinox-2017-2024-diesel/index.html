Summary

The third-generation Chevrolet Equinox (based on the General Motors D2XX platform) marks a major turning point for the model: it was significantly lightened (up to 180 kg less) and shortened compared to the previous generation to better fit into the highly competitive compact SUV segment.

A rare occurrence for the North American market, this generation briefly offered a diesel engine (1.6L turbo-diesel) between 2018 and 2019, targeting high-mileage drivers and Volkswagen 'Dieselgate' orphans. Although very fuel-efficient, this engine was removed from the catalog at the end of 2019 due to low demand. The model received a facelift presented in 2020 and marketed for the 2022 model year, modernizing the front fascia and adding an RS trim. Overall, the Equinox III is praised by *Consumer Reports* and *J.D. Power* for its above-average reliability, intuitive infotainment system, and roominess, although the quality of some interior plastics lags behind Japanese or European competition.

Strengths 5

  • Above-average overall reliability (recommended by Consumer Reports since 2018)
  • Frugal fuel consumption of the 1.6L Diesel version on the highway
  • Very intuitive Chevrolet MyLink/Infotainment 3 infotainment system (Apple CarPlay/Android Auto as standard)
  • Generous rear roominess with a flat floor
  • Extensive after-sales service network (very popular in Canada and the USA)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very short career of the Diesel engine (2018-2019), which may complicate the long-term availability of certain specific parts
  • Low-end quality of some interior plastics
  • Trunk volume slightly smaller than category leaders (RAV4, CR-V)
  • 2.0L Turbo engine discontinued after 2020, limiting power options on recent models
  • Rear visibility hindered by wide C-pillars

Check before buying

  • On the Diesel (1.6 LH7): DPF/FAP regeneration history and DEF system operation (absence of emissions-related check engine light).
  • On all models: Verify that recalls regarding the rear brake calipers (incorrect lubricant) have been performed.
  • On 2018-2020 models: Test the automatic transmission (especially the 9-speed of the 2.0L) to ensure there is no jerking during downshifts.
  • Check the condition of the ISOFIX/LATCH anchors on 2020-2023 models (subject to a recall for excess powder coating).

Official recalls

The Equinox III has been the subject of several recalls by the NHTSA (United States), mostly concerning specific batches or peripheral equipment:

  • [2023-12-21] Door strikers (23V869000): Risk of door strikers breaking, which could result in the door opening unexpectedly while driving (2024 models).
  • [2023-05-11] ISOFIX anchors (23V339000): Excess powder coating on the LATCH anchorage bars preventing the proper securing of a child seat (2020-2023).
  • [2023-01-19] Fuel pump (23V013000): The pump control module may not provide enough fuel, causing an engine stall (2021-2022).
  • [2022-12-15] Airbag sensor (22V929000): The occupant classification system may not properly deactivate the passenger airbag on ventilated seats (2023).
  • [2022-09-29 & 2022-05-19] Seats (22V724000 / 22V359000): Loose bolt or incorrect weld on the driver's seat frame (2022-2023).
  • [2021-08-05] Tires (21V612000): Manufacturing defect on certain original equipment Hankook tires (2021-2022).
  • [2020-12-23] Seat belts (20V811000): Potentially non-compliant fasteners (2020).
  • [2020-10-29] Automatic transmission (20V668000): Potential transmission fluid leak due to missing bolts on the start/stop accumulator (2018-2020).
  • [2019-10-03 & 2018-08-30] Brake calipers (19V704000 / 18V576000): Use of an incorrect factory lubricant causing the rubber seals to swell and a loss of braking performance (2018-2020).
  • [2019-09-19] Fuel tank (19V667000): Improperly sealed seam on all-wheel drive (AWD) models that could cause a leak (2019-2020).
  • [2019-03-07] Rear seats (19V184000): Insufficient welds on the right rear head restraint bracket.
  • [2018-05-31] Fuel pump (18V358000): High-pressure pump defect (2018).
  • [2018-04-19] Glazing (18V252000): Front window glass potentially improperly tempered (2018).
  • [2017-08-22] Driveshaft (17V516000): Risk of the front right intermediate driveshaft fracturing (2018).

Verdict

The third-generation Chevrolet Equinox is a pragmatic, comfortable, and overall very reliable compact SUV, backed by excellent scores from J.D. Power and Consumer Reports.

Regarding the Diesel engine (1.6L LH7): It is a fascinating niche option for high-mileage drivers. If you cover long distances on the highway, its fuel economy is unbeatable in this segment. However, its purchase must be carefully considered: its short time on the market (2018-2019) and the inherent complexity of modern diesels (DPF/FAP, AdBlue) require scrupulous maintenance and prohibit strictly urban use.

For the majority of buyers, the 1.5L Turbo gasoline remains the safest, easiest to maintain, and most well-rounded choice. The 2.0L Turbo should only be favored if you need to tow or if you are looking for brisk acceleration.
