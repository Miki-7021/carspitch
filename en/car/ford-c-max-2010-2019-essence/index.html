All Ford models

Summary

The second-generation Ford C-Max (C344), launched in late 2010, is based on the excellent global C platform of the Focus Mk3. For the first time, it is available in two body styles: the classic C-Max (5 seats, standard rear doors, dynamic look) and the Grand C-Max (extended wheelbase, 7 seats, sliding rear doors). Known for its sharp handling, which is rare in the compact MPV segment, it received a facelift in 2015, adopting the 'Aston Martin' style grille and replacing the SYNC 1 multimedia system with SYNC 2 (and later SYNC 3). While its dynamic qualities are undeniable, its mechanical reliability is highly mixed, particularly on the EcoBoost petrol engines, which require rigorous maintenance.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional handling for an MPV (Focus chassis)
  • Very practical sliding rear doors on the Grand C-Max
  • Soundproofing and ride comfort (especially after the 2015 facelift)
  • Reliability of the 1.6 Ti-VCT (petrol) and 2.0 TDCi (diesel) engines
  • Rich technological equipment for the time (auto parking, power tailgate)

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.0 EcoBoost wet belt if poorly maintained
  • Critical overheating problems on the 1.6 EcoBoost
  • Dashboard ergonomics before 2015 (too many buttons, tiny screen)
  • Slow and unintuitive SYNC 1 and 2 multimedia systems (improved with SYNC 3)
  • Disappointing trunk volume on the 5-seat C-Max compared to the competition
  • Rear seats of the Grand C-Max are narrow and heavy to handle

Check before buying

  • Strict service book for the 1.0 EcoBoost: 5W20 oil meeting WSS-M2C948-B standard is mandatory.
  • Coolant level on the 1.5 and 1.6 EcoBoost (white marks around the expansion tank).
  • Operation of the PowerShift automatic gearbox: no jerking, slipping, or shuddering should be tolerated.
  • Condition of the sliding doors on the Grand C-Max (clean tracks, unworn rollers).
  • Front tire wear (the sharp front axle tends to consume rubber on heavy engine variants).

What sets it apart 7

  • Ultra-Efficient Braking — Designed for your safety, the C-Max II offers exceptional braking performance, stopping from 100 km/h to 0 km/h in just 36.5 meters, a remarkable feat for its category.

  • Dynamic and Precise Road Handling — Inheriting Ford's driving DNA, the C-Max II stands out with its agile chassis and responsive steering, offering precise road holding and unparalleled driving pleasure for an MPV.

Verdict

The Ford C-Max II is an MPV full of dynamic qualities, but buying it used requires extreme caution regarding the petrol engines. Avoid the 1.6 EcoBoost (thermal risks are too high) and be uncompromising on the service history of the 1.0 EcoBoost (wet belt). The safest petrol choice remains the modest but indestructible 1.6 Ti-VCT, or failing that, a recent 1.5 EcoBoost with a warranty. Paradoxically, for a family vehicle, the diesel versions (1.5 TDCi and 2.0 TDCi) prove to be much more robust in the long term, provided they are driven enough to prevent the DPF from clogging.
