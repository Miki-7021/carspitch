Kia Sportage

V (NQ5) · 2021 – 2025

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

Summary

The fifth-generation Kia Sportage (code NQ5) marks a major stylistic and technological break for the Korean manufacturer. Based on the N3 platform (shared with the Hyundai Tucson), it is offered for the first time with two wheelbases: short (SWB) for Europe and long (LWB) for North America and Asia. Although the brand's communication highlights hybrid (HEV) and plug-in hybrid (PHEV) powertrains, **the Sportage retains a very relevant diesel (CRDi) offering for high-mileage drivers and company fleets**. This model stands out with its spectacular interior (curved dual screen), significantly improved spaciousness, and its 7-year / 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty, which is reassuring on the used car market. Overall reliability is very good, although versions equipped with the dry dual-clutch gearbox (DCT7) require particular attention.

Strengths 5

  • 7-year / 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty (transferable)
  • Very low fuel consumption of 1.6 CRDi engines on highways (often below 5.5 L/100 km)
  • Spectacular interior presentation and improved finish quality
  • Excellent rear spaciousness and trunk volume (even in European SWB version)
  • BVA6 (on HEV/PHEV) and BVA8 (on 2.0 CRDi) gearboxes very reliable and smooth

Weaknesses 5

  • DCT7 gearbox (on 1.6 CRDi 136 and 1.6 T-GDI) generating jerks and sensitive to wear in urban driving
  • Diesel engines (CRDi) unsuitable for short urban trips (rapid clogging)
  • Ergonomics of the dual-function touch bar (climate control / multimedia) not very intuitive
  • Suspension comfort a bit firm with 19-inch wheels
  • Imperfect sound insulation during strong accelerations (especially on the 1.6 CRDi)

Check before buying

  • DCT7 gearbox behavior (1.6 CRDi 136): no excessive shuddering or slipping should be felt when starting on a hill.
  • AdBlue system maintenance history (CRDi): check for absence of engine light related to the emission control system.
  • Multimedia system: test the responsiveness of the central screen and verify that the latest software update has been performed.
  • Tire wear: particularly on the front axle of PHEV (heavy) and 4x4 (AWD) versions.
  • 12V battery (HEV/PHEV): check its health, as cases of deep discharge have been reported on 2021-2022 models.

Verdict

The Kia Sportage V (NQ5) is an extremely accomplished compact SUV that has successfully moved upmarket. **Regarding diesel powertrains (1.6 CRDi in Europe, 2.0 CRDi elsewhere), the mechanical reliability record is very positive.** The engine block itself is robust and designed to cover many kilometers. However, the choice of transmission is crucial: if you opt for the 1.6 CRDi 136 hp, keep in mind that the dry dual-clutch gearbox (DCT7) does not like daily traffic jams or heavy loads at low speeds. For predominantly highway or road use, it is an excellent, very economical choice, covered by the 7-year warranty. If your use includes a lot of city driving, avoid diesel (risk of DPF/EGR clogging and DCT7 wear) and turn to the excellent hybrid version (HEV 230) equipped with a much smoother torque converter automatic gearbox.
