Toyota Yaris

III (XP130) · 2011 – 2020

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The **Toyota Yaris III (XP130 generation)** is a versatile B-segment city car. Produced in Valenciennes for the European market (« Origine France Garantie » label), it marked a turning point by democratizing hybrid technology (HSD) in this segment. The model had an exceptionally long career thanks to **two major facelifts**: Phase 2 in 2014 (adoption of the « X » grille, improved interior plastics and soundproofing) and Phase 3 in 2017 (new front end, horizontal taillights, replacement of the 1.33 engine with a new 1.5). Renowned for its outstanding reliability, particularly in petrol and hybrid versions, it only suffers from imperfect soundproofing and an outdated infotainment system. It is one of the safest used car purchases on the market.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability (especially in petrol and hybrid)
  • Very low maintenance and running costs
  • Maneuverability and turning radius in the city
  • Generous interior space for its size (3.95 m)
  • Hybrid system warranty extendable up to 10 years (under maintenance conditions)
  • « Origine France Garantie » label (for European models)

Weaknesses 5

  • Poor soundproofing on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Quality of interior plastics (hard and prone to scratches, especially Phase 1)
  • « Touch & Go » multimedia system slow, dated, and often buggy
  • Firm suspension comfort (particularly with 16-inch wheels)
  • Fragile paintwork (prone to stone chips and micro-scratches)

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: essential for the hybrid system warranty.
  • Oil level (on the 1.33 VVT-i engine before 2014).
  • Clutch condition (on manual gearbox versions, often abused in the city).
  • Touchscreen operation (responsiveness, Bluetooth connection).
  • Check for manufacturer recalls (especially Takata airbags).

Verdict

The Toyota Yaris III is an **absolute sensible choice** in the used car market. If you are looking for a petrol city car, opt for the **1.5 VVT-iE 111 hp** (post-2017) which offers the best performance/reliability compromise, or the small **1.0 VVT-i** if you never leave the city. The 1.33 VVT-i should be avoided on 2011-2013 model years due to its oil consumption. Although this report focuses on petrol, it's impossible not to recommend the **Hybrid (HSD)** version, which remains the undisputed queen of the range for its indestructible reliability and fuel efficiency. Avoid the diesel (D-4D), unsuitable for the car's urban purpose.
