BMW Série 7

F01/F02 (2008–2015) · 2008 – 2015

NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

Summary

The BMW 7 Series F01 (short wheelbase) and F02 (long wheelbase) represents the fifth generation of the Bavarian brand's flagship. Launched in 2008, it marked a return to more classic and elegant lines after the controversial E65/E66. Positioned in the luxury sedan segment, it competes with the Mercedes S-Class and Audi A8, offering a blend of sumptuous comfort, cutting-edge technology, and BMW's characteristic driving dynamics.

This generation introduced numerous innovations, including the second-generation iDrive system, adaptive suspensions, and a range of powerful and sophisticated powertrains. A facelift (LCI - Life Cycle Impulse) occurred in 2012, bringing minor aesthetic changes (headlights, bumpers) and technical improvements, notably the introduction of new powertrains and the optimization of existing ones.

**Expert's overall note:** The F01/F02 is an impressive car for its luxury and performance. However, its technological complexity and the uneven reliability of certain powertrains, particularly the N63 V8 petrol engine, make it a purchase that requires increased vigilance and a substantial maintenance budget. Diesel versions and six-cylinder petrol engines are generally more recommendable.

Check before buying

  • **Complete and detailed service history** (invoices, up-to-date service book)
  • **Check if the BMW Customer Care Package (CCP) program has been performed on 750i/Li (N63) models** and what repairs were made.
  • **Condition of the DPF and EGR valve** on diesels (electronic diagnosis, visual inspection).
  • **Abnormal engine noises** (knocking, clattering, turbo whistling, timing chain noises).
  • **Oil consumption** on petrol engines, especially N63 V8s.
  • **Functionality of all electronic equipment** (iDrive, electric seats, air conditioning, driving aids).
  • **Condition of adaptive suspensions** (leaks, noises, operation).
  • **Condition of tires and brakes**, often expensive to replace on this type of vehicle.
  • **Fluid leaks** (oil, coolant, power steering).
  • **Condition of the automatic gearbox** (smoothness of gear changes, absence of jerks).

Verdict

The BMW 7 Series F01/F02 is a luxury sedan that offers an exceptional driving experience and level of comfort for a very attractive price on the used car market. However, it is not without risks.

**Buying advice:**

* **Prioritize N57 diesel engines (730d, 740d, 750d)**: These are the most reliable and economical to use. They offer an excellent compromise between performance and fuel consumption. The 750d is more complex but remains an excellent choice if well-maintained. * **The N55 six-cylinder petrol engines (740i post-LCI) are also good choices**: More reliable than the N54 and N63, they offer a nice sound and good performance. * **Absolutely avoid 750i/Li models equipped with the pre-LCI N63 engine (before 2012)**, unless you have irrefutable proof that the BMW Customer Care Package program has been fully carried out and all weaknesses have been corrected. Even in this case, a substantial budget for maintenance should be anticipated. * **The V12s (760i) are magnificent pieces of engineering but extremely expensive to maintain and repair.** Reserved for enthusiasts with unlimited means. * **A complete and clear service history is non-negotiable.** The slightest gap should alert you. * **Plan a substantial budget for maintenance and unforeseen repairs.** Even reliable models can have costly breakdowns due to the complexity of electronic systems and suspensions.

In summary, the F01/F02 is a fantastic car if you choose the right engine and are willing to invest in its maintenance. Diesels are the safest bets.
