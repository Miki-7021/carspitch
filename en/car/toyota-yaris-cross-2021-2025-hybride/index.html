All Toyota models

Summary

The Toyota Yaris Cross (Generation I - MXPJ) is an urban SUV (B-segment) based on the TNGA-B platform, shared with the Yaris city car. Designed primarily for Europe and Japan, it stands out for its robust design, raised ground clearance, and, above all, its ultra-efficient 4th-generation hybrid powertrain. It also offers a rare intelligent all-wheel drive (AWD-i) in this category. A true commercial success, it shines with its exemplary reliability, a direct inheritance of Toyota's hybridization expertise. A slight facelift and a technical update (introduction of the 130h version, improved sound insulation and multimedia system) occurred in early 2024.

Expert's overall rating: A pragmatic and ultra-reliable choice, the undisputed leader in energy efficiency in its category.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptionally low fuel consumption (Hybrid)
  • Overall reliability and durability of the Toyota hybrid system (HSD)
  • Availability of a true all-wheel drive (AWD-i) rare in the segment
  • Comprehensive standard safety equipment (Toyota Safety Sense)
  • Excellent resale value on the used car market
  • Toyota Relax warranty (up to 10 years or 160,000 km with network maintenance)

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation on 2021-2023 models (engine noise under load and road noise)
  • Limited rear passenger space and narrow rear door opening angle
  • Quality of some interior plastics (hard and scratch-prone)
  • Infotainment system of early versions (2021) slow and dated
  • 12V battery sensitive to long periods of inactivity

Check before buying

  • 12V battery condition: Test the voltage, especially if the vehicle has made many short trips or has been stored.
  • Hybrid battery filter: Check that it is not obstructed by dust or pet hair (risk of HV battery overheating).
  • Maintenance history: Essential to benefit from the Toyota Relax warranty extension (annual hybrid health check).
  • Tire wear (AWD-i): Check for regular wear on all 4 tires on all-wheel drive versions.
  • Multimedia system operation: Ensure that software updates have been performed to avoid screen bugs.

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Legendary Reliability and Extended Warranty — Benefit from the proven reliability of Toyota's hybrid system (HSD), recognized worldwide, and drive with complete peace of mind thanks to the Toyota Relax warranty, which can extend up to 10 years or 160,000 km with network maintenance.

Economy

  • Unrivaled Fuel Efficiency and Exceptional Resale Value — Enjoy exceptionally low fuel consumption, often below 4.5L/100km, making the Yaris Cross a leader in efficiency. Its high demand also ensures excellent resale value in the used car market.

Safety

  • Maximum Protection: 5-Star Euro NCAP Rating — The Toyota Yaris Cross guarantees optimal safety for all occupants, as evidenced by its 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with high scores for adult (86%) and child (86%) protection.
  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Experience solid and reassuring braking performance. The progressive pedal and assistance systems (ABS, EBD, BA) ensure a short stopping distance and excellent stability, even in emergency situations.
  • Safe and Stable Road Handling — The Yaris Cross offers predictable and stable dynamic behavior. Its good road holding, decent body control, and precise steering provide a feeling of safety and serenity behind the wheel.

Practicality

  • Unique Versatility with Intelligent All-Wheel Drive (AWD-i) — Stand out with the availability of a true intelligent all-wheel drive system (AWD-i), a rare and valuable feature in the urban SUV segment, offering increased grip and safety in various conditions.

Comfort

  • Enhanced Driving Experience and Modern Multimedia — Recent versions (from 2023 and 130h) benefit from improved sound insulation and the Toyota Smart Connect system, which is smoother and offers over-the-air updates, for optimized driving pleasure and connectivity.

Verdict

The Toyota Yaris Cross is a highly recommended purchase. It stands out as one of the most reliable and economical urban SUVs on the market. The hybrid powertrain (116h or 130h) is a model of robustness, free from the belt or injection problems that affect some of its European competitors. If one accepts average sound insulation during strong acceleration and modest rear passenger space, it is a worry-free vehicle. Prioritize models from 2023 onwards to benefit from the new multimedia system, or the 130h version (2024+) for improved driving pleasure. Maintenance within the network is advised to activate the extended Toyota Relax warranty.
