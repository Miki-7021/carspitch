Dacia Sandero

I · 2008 – 2012

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The **first-generation Dacia Sandero (B90)** revolutionized the European automotive market by democratizing the concept of the "low-cost" versatile city car. Based on the B0 platform (derived from the Renault Clio II), it offers interior space worthy of the segment above for the price of a small used city car. Launched in 2008, it was joined in 2009 by the **Stepway** version, with its highly popular adventurer look. While its finish is rustic and its soundproofing light, it shines with exceptional mechanical reliability, particularly for its gasoline engines, thanks to the use of widely proven Renault components. It is the archetype of the rational car, economical to buy and to maintain.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of gasoline engines (MPI in particular)
  • Record spaciousness for its size (320-liter trunk)
  • Extremely low maintenance and insurance costs
  • Raised ground clearance (especially in Stepway version), ideal for poor roads
  • Simple design allowing for DIY maintenance

Weaknesses 5

  • High fuel consumption of 1.4 and 1.6 MPI engines
  • Very improvable soundproofing (wind and road noise on the highway)
  • Basic finish and hard plastics prone to scratches
  • Outdated passive safety (only 3 Euro NCAP stars)
  • Poor equipment on entry-level trims (sometimes without power steering)

Check before buying

  • **Corrosion:** Inspect the underbody, wheel arches, and exhaust line (basic factory rust protection).
  • **Steering:** Possible leaks on the power steering pump or the steering rack.
  • **Electrics/Interior:** Fragile headlight/turn signal stalks (wires breaking), capricious central locking motors.
  • **Cooling system:** Traces of pink/yellow fluid around the water pump or water housing (thermostat) on gasoline engines.
  • **Maintenance history:** For dCi models, demand invoices proving regular oil changes and fuel filter replacement.

Official recalls

  • - **November 2008:** Recall for a risk of non-deployment of frontal airbags (ECU reprogramming).
  • - **May 2009:** Inspection and possible replacement of the steering column (risk of loss of control).
  • - **December 2011:** Recall concerning front seat belts (risk of improper pretensioner attachment).

What sets it apart 12

Practicality

  • Generous Cabin Space and Boot Volume — The Sandero offers record-breaking cabin space for its segment, ensuring optimal comfort for all passengers, complemented by a spacious boot ideal for your daily needs and travels.
  • Robust and Simple Design — Its proven design and simple mechanics guarantee great robustness and ease of maintenance, contributing to its recognized reliability and a hassle-free ownership experience.

Economy

  • Everyday Budget Control — Designed to be economical, the Dacia Sandero petrol ensures a controlled budget thanks to its optimized fuel consumption (especially the 1.2 16v) and a simple design that promotes reduced running costs.

Comfort

  • Comfort Adapted to All Roads — Enjoy a serene drive thanks to a raised ground clearance, particularly useful on less-than-perfect roads, and effective basic comfort for your daily commutes and getaways.

Performance

  • Flexible and Capable Petrol Engines — The petrol engines, notably the 1.6 16v, offer respectable performance and appreciable flexibility, making the Sandero agile in the city and pleasant on the open road.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Renault Clio II Campus (same technical base, slightly better finish), Peugeot 206+ (more dynamic but less spacious), Fiat Punto Evo (more modern), Chevrolet Aveo. - **South America:** Volkswagen Gol, Fiat Palio, Chevrolet Onix (previous generations). - **Emerging Markets:** Lada Kalina, Hyundai i10/i20 first generation.

Verdict

The Dacia Sandero I is an absolutely rational choice for anyone looking for a reliable and inexpensive daily driver. **With a gasoline engine (1.2 16v, 1.4 MPI, or 1.6 MPI), it is a highly recommended purchase.** These engines, though rustic and a bit thirsty, are true workhorses capable of handling impressive mileages with minimal maintenance. However, **dCi diesel versions from before 2011 are to be avoided** due to the high risk of injection system failure (Delphi pump). Opt for a Lauréate or Stepway version to benefit from a minimum of comfort (power steering, air conditioning, electric windows).
