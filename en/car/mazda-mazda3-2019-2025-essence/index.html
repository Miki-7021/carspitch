Mazda Mazda3

IV (BP) · 2019 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Mazda models

Summary

The fourth-generation Mazda3 (internal code BP) marks an assertive move upmarket for the Hiroshima-based manufacturer. Launched in 2019, it stands out with its very pure 'Kodo' design, an interior finish worthy of premium segments, and drastically improved sound insulation compared to the previous generation. Technically, it abandons the multi-link rear suspension for a torsion beam, a controversial but masterfully developed choice that preserves the brand's excellent dynamism. This generation also introduces the revolutionary Skyactiv-X engine (spark-controlled compression ignition) and generalizes mild hybridization (MHEV) across its gasoline engines in Europe. A slight technical revision (e-Skyactiv X update) occurred in 2021, followed by a technological update (infotainment) in 2024. Overall, it is one of the most reliable compact cars of its generation.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall reliability of gasoline engines (Skyactiv-G)
  • Interior finish and material quality worthy of the premium segment
  • Very high level of sound insulation
  • Driving pleasure and precision of the manual gearbox
  • Distinctive and elegant exterior design

Weaknesses 5

  • Very fragile paint (especially Soul Red Crystal, prone to chips)
  • Poor rear visibility (especially on the 5-door / Hatchback version)
  • Rear passenger space and trunk volume are inferior to competitors
  • Maintenance cost of the Skyactiv-X engine (spark plugs)
  • Some parasitic noises (B-pillar, dashboard) on early model years

Check before buying

  • Condition of the bodywork and paint (stone chips on the hood and front bumper).
  • History of software updates (essential for the emergency braking system and engine management).
  • Smooth operation of the infotainment system (intermittent reboots on 2019 models).
  • Up-to-date Mazda service book, especially for the Skyactiv-X which does not tolerate approximations.
  • Interior trim noises during a test drive on degraded roads.

Official recalls

  • December 2019 (Emergency Braking): Reprogramming of the Smart Brake Support (SBS) system which could activate unexpectedly (phantom braking) by detecting false obstacles. Concerns models manufactured in 2019.
  • June 2019 (Wheels): Risk of wheel nut loosening due to a manufacturing defect. Concerns a small series of 2019 models.
  • November 2021 (Fuel Pump): Replacement of the low-pressure fuel pump (Denso supplier) whose impeller can deform and cause engine failure. Concerns models produced between 2019 and 2020.
  • July 2023 (Rearview Camera): The rearview camera image may flicker or not display due to a defective wiring harness.

What sets it apart 7

Reliability

  • Legendary Reliability of Skyactiv Petrol Engines — The Mazda3 is renowned for the exceptional robustness and durability of its Skyactiv-G and e-Skyactiv X petrol engines, ensuring long-term peace of mind.

Comfort

  • Premium Interior and Remarkable Soundproofing — Enjoy a cabin worthy of higher segments, with high-quality materials and advanced soundproofing that transforms every journey into a serene and refined experience.

Safety

  • Certified Maximum Safety — The Mazda3 offers exemplary protection for all occupants, as evidenced by its impressive Euro NCAP test scores, guaranteeing absolute peace of mind on the road.
  • Total Control with Powerful Braking — Benefit from a robust and progressive braking system, offering short stopping distances and a reassuring pedal feel for increased safety in all circumstances.

Performance

  • Unrivaled Dynamics and Driving Pleasure — The Mazda3 stands out for its balanced chassis, precise steering, and excellent road holding, offering an engaging and sporty driving experience, whatever the route.
  • Exceptional Driving Enjoyment and Precise Manual Gearbox — Appreciate every journey thanks to superior driving enjoyment and a remarkably precise manual gearbox, for a perfect connection between driver and road.

Practicality

  • Sleek and Elegant Kodo Design — Be captivated by the Mazda3's Kodo design, an automotive work of art that combines elegance, fluidity, and a distinctive presence on the road.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Volkswagen Golf VIII (more technological but less reliable), Peugeot 308 III (strong design, but PureTech issues), Toyota Corolla (hybrid benchmark, less dynamic).
  • Japan / Asia: Honda Civic (excellent dynamic alternative), Subaru Impreza (standard all-wheel drive).
  • North America: Honda Civic, Toyota Corolla, Hyundai Elantra, Volkswagen Jetta.

Verdict

The fourth-generation Mazda3 is an extremely relevant used (or new) choice for anyone looking for a reliable, elegant, and rewarding compact car to drive. The classic gasoline engines (Skyactiv-G 122/150 hp or 2.5L) are models of robustness and represent the safest choice. The Skyactiv-X engine is an engineering feat offering an excellent performance/consumption compromise, but it requires meticulous follow-up within the official network. Avoid 2019 models if you fear minor early defects (electronics, parasitic noises) and prioritize a model from 2021 onwards. The diesel should be avoided unless for exclusively highway use.
