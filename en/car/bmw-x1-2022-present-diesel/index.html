BMW X1

U11 · 2022 – Présent

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

All BMW models

Summary

The third-generation BMW X1 (code name U11) marks a major technological and stylistic break compared to its predecessor (F48). Based on the FAAR (Frontantriebsarchitektur) platform optimized for electrification, it grows in all dimensions and moves upmarket with an interior dominated by the *BMW Curved Display* (OS8 then OS9).

Diesel Focus: Although the market is electrifying, BMW has wisely maintained an extremely relevant diesel offering with the 2.0L 4-cylinder B47 unit (in its TU1/TU2 technical revision). This engine, now fully mature, offers remarkable refinement and fuel efficiency. The 20d and 23d versions benefit from 48V mild hybridization (MHEV) with an electric motor integrated into the DKG7 dual-clutch gearbox, smoothing restarts and lowering fuel consumption. Today, it is one of the most accomplished premium compact SUVs on the market.

Strengths 5

  • Mature B47 diesel engine: excellent reliability and impressive fuel consumption (often under 5.5 L/100 km on the road).
  • Significantly improved interior finish quality, worthy of the segment above.
  • Generous interior space and boot volume (except for PHEV/iX1 where the floor is raised).
  • Very successful integration of 48V hybridization into the DKG7 gearbox (total transparency for the driver).
  • Dynamic road handling, typical of BMW, despite the front-wheel drive/xDrive architecture.

Weaknesses 4

  • Disappearance of the physical iDrive controller: everything goes through the touchscreen, questionable ergonomics while driving.
  • Very firm suspension comfort, especially with M Sport trim and 19/20-inch wheels.
  • Driving aids (ADAS) sometimes intrusive (lane keeping, overspeed alerts) requiring deactivation at each start-up.
  • Very expensive purchase price and options policy (subscriptions).

Check before buying

  • Recall campaign for the integrated braking system (IBS - Continental): absolutely verify if the vehicle has been corrected.
  • Infotainment system (OS8): check for fluidity, unexpected reboots, or black screens (often requires a major update at the dealership).
  • On xDrive versions: regular tire wear (front/rear wear difference should not exceed 2 mm to preserve the transfer case).
  • On diesels: trip history. Avoid a diesel that has only been driven in the city (risk of premature DPF clogging).

Official recalls

  • February 2024 (IBS Braking) : Massive recall (code 0034670200) concerning the integrated braking system supplied by Continental. An electronic defect can lead to the loss of braking assistance and ABS/ESP. Affects a large part of the 2022-2024 production.
  • May 2023 (Seat belts) : Risk of pinching or improper fastening of rear seat belt pretensioners.
  • October 2023 (Airbags) : On a small series, the driver's airbag gas generator may have a manufacturing defect.

Verdict

The BMW X1 (U11) is currently the benchmark for premium compact SUVs. While the market is pushing towards electric (iX1) or PHEV, the diesel versions (18d, 20d, 23d) represent the most rational and reliable choice for high-mileage drivers. The B47 engine has ironed out the teething problems of its predecessors (N47) and offers remarkable peace of mind, coupled with formidable efficiency. The sDrive18d is the sensible choice (simple and ultra-reliable), while the xDrive20d offers the best performance/safety compromise. Buy with confidence, provided you verify that the critical recall on the braking system (IBS) has been carried out.
