BMW X3

G01 · 2017 – 2024

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

Summary

The third-generation BMW X3 (G01) marks a major qualitative leap compared to its predecessor (F25). Based on the modular CLAR platform, it offers first-class road handling, advanced soundproofing, and an interior finish worthy of the upper segment (X5). Restyled (LCI) in late 2021, it generalized 48V mild-hybridization across its powertrains. Although demand for petrol and plug-in hybrid (PHEV) has exploded, the X3 G01 remains a formidable long-distance cruiser with its diesel engines (B47 in 4-cylinder, B57 in 6-cylinder), offering an exceptional performance/consumption ratio, coupled with the excellent ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox. Overall reliability is very good, but the diesel record is marred by the extensive global recall campaign concerning the EGR valve cooler.

Strengths 5

  • Significantly improved finish and assembly quality (X5 standard)
  • Driving pleasure and comfort/dynamism compromise
  • Excellence of the ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (smoothness and responsiveness)
  • Masterful 6-cylinder engines (B57 diesel and B58 petrol)
  • Ergonomics of the iDrive system (physical rotary controller retained)

Weaknesses 5

  • Recurrent and dangerous EGR cooler issue on diesels
  • Extreme sensitivity of the xDrive transfer case to tires
  • Firm comfort with M Sport suspension and 20/21-inch wheels
  • Significant weight, especially on the plug-in hybrid version (30e)
  • Used car prices and cost of options/spare parts

Check before buying

  • Recall history: Has the EGR cooler been checked/replaced? (Crucial on 18d, 20d, 30d, M40d)
  • Coolant level: An unexplained drop in a diesel indicates an internal EGR leak.
  • Tires: Mandatory star marking (*) and even wear to preserve the xDrive transfer case.
  • Active Grille Shutters: Tendency to get stuck, generating a check engine light.
  • Steering rack: Clunking noises on 2017-2019 models during low-speed maneuvers.

Official recalls

  • - **EGR Cooler (2018-2023)**: Massive recall (several waves) for risk of glycol leakage into the intake, potentially causing a fire. Concerns 4 and 6-cylinder diesel engines. - **ISOFIX Mounts (2021)**: Risk of rear ISOFIX anchor failure in case of impact (models produced between Sept. 2020 and March 2021). - **High-Voltage Battery (2020)**: On PHEV versions (30e), risk of short circuit due to impurities in battery cells (early 2020 production). - **Steering Rods (2018)**: Replacement of steering rods on some early production models due to a manufacturing defect.

Alternatives

Verdict

The BMW X3 G01 is undoubtedly one of the most accomplished premium SUVs of its generation. It rectifies the finish flaws of the F25 and offers imperial road handling. **For high-mileage drivers, the diesel engines (20d and especially 30d) are masterpieces of efficiency and driving pleasure.** However, buying a used diesel requires absolute vigilance regarding the EGR cooler recall. Demand an invoice or a BMW certificate proving that the part has been replaced with the corrected version. If you drive less than 20,000 km per year, opt for the excellent petrol engines (20i or M40i) or the PHEV (30e), which will spare you issues with clogging and EGR.
