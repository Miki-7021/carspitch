Summary

The 2023–2025 timeframe marks a pivotal transition for the Suzuki Swift. It encompasses the final production year of the highly successful 'AZ' generation (often referred to as the 3rd global or 5th/6th generation depending on the market) and the introduction of the all-new generation (chassis ZCEDS/ZDEDS) launched in 2024. Built on the lightweight 'Heartect' platform, the Swift remains a benchmark for agility, fuel efficiency, and urban practicality. The major shift during this period is the transition from the 4-cylinder K12D engine to the all-new, highly efficient 3-cylinder Z12E engine for the 2024+ models. Both generations heavily feature Mild Hybrid (MHEV) technology (12V for standard models, 48V for the Sport). The Swift is globally renowned for its bulletproof reliability, low running costs, and fun-to-drive dynamics, though it compromises slightly on sound insulation and interior material quality.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability across all engine options
  • Class-leading fuel economy, especially with the K12D and Z12E MHEV engines
  • Lightweight 'Heartect' platform provides excellent agility and handling
  • Low maintenance and running costs
  • Generous standard equipment (especially ADAS on 2024+ models)
  • Available with ALLGRIP Auto (AWD), a rarity in the B-segment

Weaknesses 5

  • Thin paint that is highly susceptible to stone chips and scratches
  • Interior plastics are hard and prone to scratching
  • Sound insulation is below average (noticeable wind and road noise at highway speeds)
  • Boot capacity (265 liters) is smaller than key European rivals
  • Infotainment system on 2023 models can be laggy (improved on 2024+ 9-inch units)

Check before buying

  • **Paintwork:** Inspect the front bumper and hood for excessive stone chips.
  • **ADAS Sensors:** Ensure the front radar (behind the grille) and windshield cameras are clean and undamaged; they are sensitive and can trigger dashboard warnings if obstructed.
  • **Battery Health:** For MHEV models, check the standard 12V lead-acid battery, as a weak primary battery can cause the start-stop system (ISG) to malfunction.
  • **Manual Gearbox:** Check for smooth shifting into reverse and 1st gear; some older AZ models had minor synchro stiffness, though mostly resolved by 2023.
  • **Interior Rattles:** Test drive on rough roads to check for dashboard or door panel rattles due to the hard plastics.

Verdict

The Suzuki Swift (2023-2025) is an outstanding choice for buyers prioritizing reliability, low running costs, and driving enjoyment over premium interior materials or highway refinement. Whether you opt for the final iterations of the proven 4-cylinder K12D (2023) or the brand-new, highly efficient 3-cylinder Z12E (2024+), mechanical peace of mind is virtually guaranteed. The Swift Sport (K14D) remains one of the best value-for-money warm hatches on the market. It is highly recommended as a daily commuter or city car.
