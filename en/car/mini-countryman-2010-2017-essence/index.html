MINI Countryman

I (R60) · 2010 – 2017

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The **MINI Countryman I (R60)** marks a historic turning point for the brand: it's the first 5-door MINI, the first to exceed 4 meters in length, and the first to offer all-wheel drive (ALL4). Positioned as a premium urban SUV, it retains the typical MINI dynamic behavior (the famous *Go-Kart feeling*) despite its raised center of gravity.

The model benefited from a **facelift (LCI) in July 2014**, bringing improved sound insulation, higher quality interior plastics, black-faced gauges, and minor updates to the engines to meet the Euro 6 standard.

**Expert's overall rating:** A very endearing vehicle with an iconic design, but whose mechanical reliability (Prince petrol and N47 diesel engine generations) requires rigorous monitoring and a clear service history. Post-2014 models are highly recommended.

Strengths 5

  • Dynamic road handling and very precise steering
  • Unique exterior and interior design, strong personality
  • Surprising interior space for its size (sliding rear bench seat)
  • Efficiency of the ALL4 all-wheel drive system in snow/rain
  • Smooth and reliable Aisin 6-speed automatic gearbox (if serviced)

Weaknesses 5

  • Random reliability of engines (Prince petrol and N47 diesel)
  • Very firm suspension, degraded comfort with 18/19 inch wheels and Runflat tires
  • Quality of interior plastics and assemblies on pre-2014 models (frequent squeaks)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind noise)
  • Maintenance and spare parts cost (BMW prices)

Check before buying

  • **Cold noise (Petrol and Diesel):** Any metallic clatter on startup indicates a loose timing chain. Avoid if not replaced.
  • **High-Pressure Pump (N18):** Difficult starting, unstable idle, or loss of power during acceleration = HPFP at end of life.
  • **Transfer case (ALL4):** Check for any growling or jerking during tight turns at low speed. Demand proof of ALL4 system fluid change.
  • **FRM Module (Footwell Module):** Test all power windows, interior/exterior lighting, and turn signals. This electronic unit often fails during voltage drops (low battery).
  • **Panoramic sunroof:** Check for any moisture under the floor mats (water drainage channels easily clog).

Verdict

The MINI Countryman R60 is an 'impulse buy' that requires a rational approach. If you are looking for a petrol engine, **absolutely prioritize a facelifted model (post-July 2014)** in Cooper or Cooper S version, whose early defects (HPFP, timing chain) have been largely corrected. Avoid models without a complete service book or with oil changes spaced 30,000 km apart. Plan a preventive maintenance budget (gearbox oil change, ALL4, valve cleaning) to ensure its longevity.
