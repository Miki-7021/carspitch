Alfa Romeo Tonale

I · 2022 – 2025

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The Alfa Romeo Tonale (Type 965) marks the crucial entry of the cloverleaf brand into the highly competitive premium C-SUV segment. Based on the FCA Small Wide LWB 4x4 platform (shared with the Jeep Compass), it benefited from specific chassis settings (ultra-direct steering, Koni damping) to offer the dynamism expected of an Alfa Romeo. While the launch highlighted the new mild-hybrid (MHEV) and plug-in hybrid (PHEV) powertrains, the Tonale wisely retained a **Diesel (1.6 Multijet 130 hp)** offering, particularly relevant for high-mileage drivers and company fleets. This diesel engine, proven for years within the FCA group, contrasts with the newer hybrid units and stands out as the choice for raw reliability, although it is penalized by urban traffic restrictions (Crit'Air 2 in France).

Strengths 5

  • Remarkable reliability of the 1.6 Multijet (Diesel) engine, free from the early issues of hybrid versions
  • Very sharp chassis and direct steering, worthy of Alfa Romeo's DNA
  • Very successful exterior design, faithful to the concept car
  • Modern and responsive Uconnect 5 infotainment system (after updates)
  • Very low fuel consumption on the highway with the Diesel engine
