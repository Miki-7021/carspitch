Summary

The fifth-generation Ford Mondeo (internal code CD391), also known as the Ford Fusion in North America, is a family sedan that marked a turning point for Ford by adopting a global platform. Launched in Europe in 2014 (after a slight delay compared to its American twin), it positions itself in the D-segment, offering a compromise between comfort, space, and driving dynamics, a Ford hallmark. Available as a 4-door sedan, 5-door (hatchback), and wagon (SW), it received a facelift in 2019, bringing aesthetic and technological evolutions (notably the arrival of the 8-speed automatic gearbox and new EcoBlue powertrains). Its elegant lines and spacious interior make it a serious competitor to German and French models. Overall, the Mondeo V is a reliable car, but certain engines and transmissions require particular attention.

Strengths 6

  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation
  • Dynamic and precise road handling
  • Generous interior space (especially in the wagon)
  • Attractive price/equipment ratio
  • Very fuel-efficient hybrid powertrains in the city
  • General reliability of 2.0 TDCi and hybrid engines

Weaknesses 5

  • Reliability of small EcoBoost (1.0, 1.5) and TDCi (1.5, 1.6) engines
  • Reliability of the Powershift automatic gearbox
  • Quality of certain interior plastics (pre-facelift)
  • SYNC 2 multimedia system sometimes slow and unintuitive
  • Limited rear visibility on the sedan

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for diesels and Powershift)
  • DPF and EGR valve operation (on diesels)
  • Absence of coolant leaks (EcoBoost)
  • Condition and operation of the Powershift gearbox (oil changes, jerks, slipping)
  • AdBlue system operation (on 2.0 TDCi post-2019)
  • Condition of suspensions and silent blocks (front axle)
  • Operation of the SYNC multimedia system and driving aids

Official recalls

Official Recalls (Ford Mondeo V / Fusion)

  • 2015 (NHTSA 15V-340, Ford 15S17): Battery cable corrosion issue that could lead to a risk of fire or loss of power. Affects certain 2013-2015 models (primarily Fusion).
  • 2016 (NHTSA 16V-621, Ford 16S30): Shifter cable bushing issue that could lead to non-engagement of the selected gear and a risk of vehicle rollaway. Affects certain 2013-2016 models (Fusion/Mondeo).
  • 2017 (NHTSA 17V-209, Ford 17S09): Electric power steering motor issue that could lead to loss of assistance. Affects certain 2014-2017 models (Fusion/Mondeo).
  • 2018 (NHTSA 18V-471, Ford 18S20): Front seat belt pretensioner issue that may not function correctly in the event of a collision. Affects certain 2013-2016 models (Fusion/Mondeo).
  • 2019 (NHTSA 19V-076, Ford 19S07): Fuel injector nozzle issue that could crack and cause a fuel leak, with a risk of fire (specific to 1.5 EcoBoost). Affects certain 2014-2019 models (Fusion/Mondeo).
  • 2020 (NHTSA 20V-332, Ford 20S30): Shifter cable bushing issue that could degrade and lead to a risk of vehicle rollaway. Affects certain 2013-2019 models (Fusion/Mondeo).
  • 2021 (NHTSA 21V-081, Ford 21S06): Transmission control module issue that could lead to unexpected gear shifts or loss of power. Affects certain 2017-2019 models (Fusion/Mondeo).

*It is imperative to check with a Ford dealership whether the recalls concerning the targeted vehicle have been carried out.*

Verdict

The Ford Mondeo V (CD391) is an underestimated family sedan, offering an excellent compromise between comfort, dynamism, and interior space. For a worry-free purchase, prioritize the 2.0 TDCi engines (from 150hp) or the hybrid versions (HEV/PHEV), which are the most reliable and pleasant to use. The 2.0 EcoBoost are also good choices for those who drive gasoline. Be very vigilant with the small EcoBoost (1.0 and 1.5) and TDCi (1.5 and 1.6) engines, which have experienced recurring reliability issues, and demand an impeccable service history. The Powershift automatic gearbox should be considered with the utmost caution and requires an oil change every 60,000 km. If you find a well-maintained model with a manual gearbox or the new 8-speed automatic gearbox (post-2019), it's an excellent choice. Negotiate the price if the vehicle is equipped with a risky engine or transmission, or if the history is incomplete.
