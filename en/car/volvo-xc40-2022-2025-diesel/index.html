Volvo XC40

I facelift (XZ) · 2022 – 2025

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The first-generation Volvo XC40 received a facelift in early 2022. This aesthetic update (new bumpers, refined Pixel LED headlights) is primarily accompanied by a technological overhaul with the integration of the Google Android Automotive infotainment system across the entire range.

**⚠️ IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:** As an expert, I must emphasize that **Volvo definitively removed diesel engines (D3 and D4) from the XC40 catalog in 2020**, two years before this facelift. Therefore, there are **no Phase 2 (2022-2025) XC40s with diesel engines**. If you absolutely require a diesel, you will need to look for used Phase 1 models (2018-2020). This report therefore focuses on the powertrains actually available for Phase 2 (Mild-hybrids, Plug-in Hybrids, and 100% Electric), while including a section on the diesel history to address your context.

Strengths 5

  • Very high level of suspension comfort and sound insulation
  • Interior finish quality and ergonomic seats
  • Very powerful integrated Google Maps system (when it doesn't bug)
  • Active and passive safety (excellent Pilot Assist)
  • Major technical update for electric versions in 2023 (RWD)

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of diesel powertrains for very high-mileage drivers
  • Recurring electronic bugs related to the Android Automotive system (TCAM Module)
  • Fairly high fuel consumption for gasoline (B3/B4) and PHEV (empty battery) versions
  • Significant weight (especially in PHEV and BEV) which impacts agility
  • Average rear passenger space and boot volume for the category

Check before buying

  • Connectivity functionality (GPS, 4G, Volvo Cars mobile app) to rule out a TCAM module failure.
  • History of OTA (Over-The-Air) updates: the vehicle must be on the latest software version.
  • Tire wear, particularly on Twin Motor electric versions (destructive torque for tires).
  • For PHEVs: high-voltage battery state of health (SOH) and presence of charging cables.

Verdict

The facelifted Volvo XC40 (2022-2025) is an extremely appealing, comfortable, and safe premium compact SUV. **However, if you are looking for a diesel, you are on the wrong track: Volvo eradicated this powertrain from this model in 2020.** For high-mileage drivers, you will need to turn to the excellent B3/B4 (mild-hybrid) engines, which are very reliable, albeit a bit thirsty, or to competitors (BMW X1). The real strength of this Phase 2 lies in its 100% electric versions (especially the post-2023 RWD models), despite early issues marred by annoying software bugs (TCAM module). It is a recommended purchase, provided that all electronic updates have been performed.
