Summary

The 3rd generation KGM Rexton (code name Y400, also known as G4 Rexton), initially marketed under the SsangYong brand before the takeover and name change to KGM (KG Mobility) in 2022/2023, is a large 5- or 7-seater family SUV. Unlike the majority of modern SUVs that use a monocoque structure, the Rexton remains faithful to the ladder frame chassis (Quad Frame), making it a true off-roader and an excellent tow vehicle (up to 3.5 tonnes of towing capacity). It received a major facelift in late 2020 (marketed in 2021) bringing a modernized front end, a more luxurious interior, an update to its diesel engine (going from 181 to 202 hp) and a new 8-speed automatic gearbox replacing the old 7-speed Mercedes unit. It is a niche vehicle in Europe, offering an unbeatable price/performance/space ratio, although its road handling is more rustic than that of its monocoque competitors.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional towing capacity (up to 3,500 kg in automatic version)
  • Gigantic interior space and roominess in the rear seats (including the 3rd row)
  • Unbeatable price/equipment ratio on the used market
  • Very reassuring overall mechanical reliability (robust D22DTR engine)
  • True off-road capabilities (ladder frame chassis, low range, rear differential lock on certain trims)
  • Interior finish significantly improved, particularly after the 2021 facelift

Weaknesses 6

  • Clumsy road handling and jittery suspension when unladen (typical of ladder frame chassis)
  • High fuel consumption (weight exceeding 2.1 tonnes unladen)
  • Sparse KGM/SsangYong dealer network in Europe
  • Significant depreciation and sometimes difficult resale compared to established brands
  • Slightly dated infotainment system on pre-2021 models
  • Highly geared steering lacking feedback

Check before buying

  • **Underbody and chassis:** Check for perforating rust or impact damage if the vehicle has done intensive off-roading or has been driven on heavily salted roads.
  • **4x4 system:** Test the engagement of 4H and 4L modes when stationary (shifting to neutral required). The shift must occur without crunching or error lights.
  • **Towing history:** A vehicle that has regularly towed 3.5 tonnes requires scrupulous axle and gearbox oil changes.
  • **FAP and AdBlue (Diesel):** Check for the absence of an engine warning light. A road test must confirm the absence of limp mode (often linked to a FAP clogged by urban use).
  • **On-board electronics:** Test all cameras (360° system often temperamental on early model years), the touchscreen, and the heated/ventilated seats.

Official recalls

  • - **2018 (Wiring):** Risk of a wiring harness rubbing in the engine compartment, which could lead to a short circuit. Recall for insulation and securing.
  • - **2019 (Steering column):** On a small batch, improper tightening of a steering column universal joint could cause play in the steering.
  • - **2021 (AdBlue software):** Update of the SCR control unit to correct false alerts for low AdBlue level or system fault.
  • - **2022 (Seat belts):** Inspection of the rear seat belt pretensioners on certain models produced in 2021.

Verdict

The KGM (SsangYong) Rexton Y400 is an extremely rational choice for a targeted audience: those who need to tow heavy loads (horse trailers, large boats), go off the beaten track, or transport 7 people without paying the premium price of a Toyota Land Cruiser. The 2.2 e-XDi diesel engine is a success in terms of reliability, although it is penalized by the weight of the vehicle. The facelifted models (post-2021) with the 202 hp engine and the 8-speed automatic gearbox are significantly more pleasant on a daily basis. If you are looking for a dynamic SUV for city driving or winding roads, look elsewhere. If you are looking for a robust workhorse that is luxurious inside and affordable, it is an excellent used buy.
