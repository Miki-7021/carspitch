Summary

The seventh-generation Dodge Charger (code name LD) is a full-size sedan with a unique positioning as a "four-door muscle car". Sharing its platform (LX/LD) with the Chrysler 300 and the Dodge Challenger, it had a long career marked by a major facelift in 2015 that modernized its design and interior, and standardized the excellent 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox. A true North American icon, it was available from the sensible V6 version (available in RWD or AWD) to the crazy Hellcat versions with over 800 hp. Although its basic design is old, its overall reliability is rated average for its category by J.D. Power, benefiting from mechanics proven over the years. Its production ended in late 2023 to make way for a new electrified generation.

Strengths 7

  • Inimitable look and true muscle car charisma
  • Generous interior space and large trunk (true family sedan)
  • Captivating sound of the HEMI V8 engines
  • Excellent 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox (TorqueFlite) post-2015
  • Intuitive and responsive Uconnect infotainment system
  • Availability of all-wheel drive (AWD) on the V6
  • Huge aftermarket and spare parts network

Weaknesses 6

  • Very high weight (impacts agility and fuel consumption)
  • Gargantuan fuel consumption on V8 versions
  • Disappointing interior plastic quality on pre-2015 models
  • Very rapid wear of brakes and tires on sporty versions (Scat Pack, Hellcat)
  • Extremely high theft rate in North America (requires additional OBD/immobilizer protections)
  • Imposing size difficult to park in European urban environments

Check before buying

  • Listen carefully to the engine when cold: a persistent ticking on a V8 (HEMI tick) can indicate a camshaft that needs replacing.
  • Check the condition of the Uconnect touch screen (presence of bubbles or dead zones = delamination).
  • Inspect the oil filter housing on the V6 (traces of oil in the V of the engine).
  • On AWD models, check for the absence of jerks or metallic noises coming from the transfer case.
  • Check the recall history, particularly for the Takata airbags and the alternator.
  • Inspect the wear of brake discs and pads (Brembo very expensive to replace on 392 and Hellcat).

Official recalls

The Dodge Charger has been the subject of numerous recalls (NHTSA / Rappel Conso). Here are the most critical:

  • Takata Airbags (Multiple recalls from 2015 to 2026): Risk of the gas generator rupturing, projecting metal fragments. Affects almost all production years. Replacement is imperative.
  • Alternator (Oct 2014 / Jul 2017): Alternator failure on 2011-2014 models (V6 and V8) that can lead to a loss of power or a fire.
  • Transmission / Parking Brake (Apr 2016 / Feb 2024): Risk of involuntary vehicle movement (rollaway). Affects models with the electronic gear shifter (2012-2014) and a parking brake adjustment defect on 2023 models.
  • AWD Driveshaft (Feb 2017 / May 2018): The front driveshaft bolts can loosen or the universal joint can fracture on AWD models (civilian and Police Pursuit).
  • Hellcat Oil Cooler (Feb 2015 / Aug 2017): The engine oil cooler hoses can rupture, leading to a rapid loss of oil and a fire risk on 6.2L models.
  • Windshield (Jul 2021): Poor bonding of the windshield on 2020-2021 models.
  • Electrical / Backup Camera (Aug 2020 / Mar 2019): Display defects of the backup camera or the instrument cluster.
  • Structure / Jack (Jan 2016): The vehicle can slip off the supplied jack if it is not chocked correctly (2011-2016 models).

What sets it apart 12

Practicality

  • Spacious Full-Size Family Sedan — Generous interior space and a large trunk make it a genuine family sedan, offering room for passengers and luggage on daily drives and long trips.

Design

  • Distinctive Muscle Car Charisma — Its unmistakable look and true muscle car character give the buyer a four-door that stands out visually and delivers strong emotional appeal.

Performance

  • Enthralling HEMI V8 Sound — The V8 HEMI engines produce a captivating soundtrack, rewarding the buyer with an engaging sensory experience every time the car is driven.

Comfort

  • Excellent ZF Eight-Speed Automatic — The ZF eight-speed TorqueFlite automatic, generalized after 2015, delivers smooth and responsive gear changes that improve everyday driving comfort and refinement.
  • Intuitive Uconnect Infotainment System — The Uconnect infotainment system is intuitive and responsive, making it easy for the driver to access features without distraction during daily use.
  • Improved Interior After 2015 Restyling — Models from 2015 benefit from a restyling with a much better-quality interior, giving the buyer improved materials and a more pleasant cabin environment.

Safety

  • All-Wheel Drive on V6 Models — The V6 can be ordered with all-wheel drive, giving the buyer extra traction and confidence in cold climates and on slippery road surfaces.

Reliability

  • Huge Parts and Aftermarket Network — A vast spare-parts and aftermarket network makes maintenance and repairs easier, helping the buyer source components quickly and keep the car on the road.
  • Proven Pentastar V6 Reliability — The 3.6 V6 Pentastar is considered very reliable after 2013, offering the buyer a dependable engine choice for daily driving and long journeys.
  • Robust Cast-Iron HEMI Block — The 5.7 V8 HEMI uses a robust cast-iron block, giving the buyer a durable foundation and strong torque for cruising and weekend enjoyment.

Economy

  • Strong Price-Power-Space Ratio — The Charger offers an unmatched price-to-power-to-habitability ratio, especially in North America, giving the buyer strong value for money.
  • Reasonable V6 Fuel Consumption — The 3.6 V6 Pentastar delivers reasonable fuel consumption and smooth flexibility, helping the buyer keep running costs lower than with the V8 versions.

Alternatives

  • North America: Chrysler 300 (technical twin, more luxurious), Ford Mustang / Chevrolet Camaro (although coupes, often compared for the V8 versions), Kia Stinger (AWD twin-turbo V6 alternative), Genesis G70.
  • Europe: No direct competition in mainstream V8 sedans. Premium alternatives: BMW 5 Series, Mercedes E-Class, Audi A6 (but at significantly higher prices).

Verdict

The Dodge Charger LD is an endearing car, offering an unbeatable price/power/roominess ratio, especially in North America. Overall reliability is satisfactory, particularly with the Pentastar V6 and the 8-speed ZF gearbox. The HEMI V8s are robust but require an attentive ear (the famous *HEMI tick*) and rigorous maintenance to avoid camshaft issues linked to the MDS system. Favor models from 2015 onwards to benefit from the facelift, a much higher quality interior, and the standardized 8-speed automatic gearbox. Beware of running costs (fuel, tires, brakes) on the 392 and Hellcat versions, and imperatively check that recalls (notably the Takata airbags) have been performed.
