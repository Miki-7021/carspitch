Summary

The second-generation Ford S-Max (internal code CD539) was launched in 2015, succeeding a model that had established the concept of the 'sporty' MPV. It retains this philosophy with a dynamic design, exemplary road holding for its category, and a modular interior offering 5 or 7 seats. Based on the CD4 platform, shared with the Mondeo and Galaxy, it benefits from modernized onboard technology (SYNC 2 then SYNC 3 system). A slight facelift occurred in late 2019/early 2020, bringing minor aesthetic changes, new engines (notably the introduction of EcoBlue and hybrid), and technological improvements. The S-Max II is appreciated for its compromise between driving pleasure, family space, and comfort. Its production ceased in 2024, marking the end of an era for large Ford MPVs.

Strengths 6

  • Dynamic and precise road handling for an MPV
  • Exemplary spaciousness and modularity (individual seats, flat floor)
  • Suspension comfort and sound insulation (especially on high-end versions)
  • Successful exterior design, less 'utility-like' than some competitors
  • Wide choice of engines, including a reliable hybrid version
  • Good provision of safety and comfort equipment

Weaknesses 6

  • Reliability of the Powershift automatic gearbox (DCT) if poorly maintained
  • Potential problems with the 1.5 EcoBoost (coolant intrusion)
  • Sensitivity of DPF/EGR/AdBlue systems on diesels in urban use
  • Quality of some interior plastics (easy to scratch)
  • SYNC 2/3 multimedia system sometimes slow or temperamental
  • High fuel consumption on powerful petrol versions

Check before buying

  • Complete maintenance history and invoices (especially for Powershift and AdBlue fluid changes)
  • Absence of illuminated engine or AdBlue warning lights
  • Powershift gearbox operation (smooth shifts, no jerks)
  • Coolant level and color (absence of oil sludge in the oil for the 1.5 EcoBoost)
  • DPF condition (check for regular regenerations if possible)
  • Tire and brake wear (heavy vehicle)
  • Operation of all electrical and electronic equipment (SYNC, driving aids)
  • Condition of seats and modularity (folding mechanisms)

Alternatives

Competing Alternatives

**Europe:** * **Volkswagen Sharan / Seat Alhambra:** The VAG group twins, more classic but very practical with their sliding doors. Reputable diesel reliability (TDI), but beware of DSGs. * **Renault Espace V:** A direct competitor, more crossover-like, with a bold design and good comfort. Reliability of dCi and TCe engines to be monitored on early vintages. * **Peugeot 5008 (first generation) / Citroën Grand C4 Picasso/Spacetourer:** French MPVs offering excellent modularity and good comfort. HDi engines are generally reliable. * **BMW 2 Series Gran Tourer / Mercedes-Benz B-Class:** More compact and premium alternatives, with definite driving pleasure but less space.

**Japan/Asia:** * **Toyota Verso:** Older and less dynamic, but renowned for its exemplary reliability. * **Mazda CX-8 (certain markets):** A large 7-seater SUV, offering a more SUV-like alternative with excellent reliability.

**North America:** * As the S-Max was not marketed, alternatives would be SUVs like the Ford Edge, Ford Explorer, Honda Pilot, Toyota Highlander, or minivans like the Chrysler Pacifica, Honda Odyssey.

Verdict

The Ford S-Max II is an excellent choice for families who don't want to sacrifice driving pleasure. Its road handling is a benchmark in the MPV category. However, reliability depends heavily on the engine and maintenance.

* **For urban/suburban use and maximum reliability**, the **2.5 Duratec Hybrid** version is the most sensible choice, although newer and therefore more expensive on the used market. * **For high-mileage drivers**, the **2.0 EcoBlue** engines are preferable to the older 2.0 TDCi, provided the AdBlue system is closely monitored and regular Powershift gearbox oil changes are performed if present. * The **1.5 EcoBoost** should be considered with caution, meticulously checking the history and the absence of coolant issues.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize a model with a clear maintenance history, especially for diesel versions and those equipped with the Powershift gearbox. A thorough road test is essential to ensure the gearbox functions correctly and there are no suspicious noises. The S-Max II remains a safe bet for anyone looking for a dynamic and versatile MPV, provided the engine and history are carefully chosen.
