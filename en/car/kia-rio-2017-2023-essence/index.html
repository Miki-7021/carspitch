Kia Rio

IV (YB) · 2017 – 2023 (jusqu'à 2025 sur certains marchés émergents)

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The **fourth-generation Kia Rio (YB code)** marks an evolution towards maturity for the Korean versatile city car (B-segment). Primarily designed to appeal to the European market while maintaining a global vocation, it stands out for its generous interior space, rational ergonomics, and safe road handling.

The **2020 facelift** brought a major technological update with the introduction of 'Smartstream' engines, including 48V mild-hybrid (MHEV) technology on the 1.0 T-GDI, as well as a new intelligent manual transmission (iMT) with an electronic clutch. Although less exuberant than some rivals (Peugeot 208, Renault Clio), the Rio YB shines with its excellent overall reliability, supported by the famous 7-year manufacturer's warranty. It is a pragmatic purchase par excellence.

Strengths 5

  • Overall reliability among the best in the B-segment
  • 7-year / 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty (transferable)
  • Logical interior ergonomics with physical controls
  • Generous rear passenger space and boot volume (325 liters)
  • Indestructible naturally aspirated (MPI) engines if well maintained

Weaknesses 5

  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (road noise)
  • Suspension a bit firm at low speeds (especially with 17-inch wheels)
  • 7DCT dual-clutch gearbox sometimes hesitant in urban environments
  • Hard interior plastics, well-assembled but prone to scratches
  • Naturally aspirated engines (1.2) lacking power on the open road

Check before buying

  • **Service history:** Essential to maintain the 7-year warranty (Kia has very strict tolerance for delayed revisions).
  • **7DCT gearbox behavior:** During the test drive, check for any juddering or jerking during hill starts or in first gear.
  • **Air conditioning:** Check the compressor's efficiency; some cases of refrigerant gas leaks reported on 2017-2018 models.
  • **Multimedia system:** Ensure the touchscreen does not freeze (a software update at the dealership usually resolves the issue).

Official recalls

  • - **Child safety lock (2018):** The rear door locking cable could be defective, allowing the door to be opened from the inside despite the child safety lock being activated. - **EPS power steering (2021):** Software update required on some models following a potential loss of steering assistance. - **Airbags (North America - 2022):** Recall concerning the airbag control unit (ACU) which might not deploy in specific crash scenarios. - **Fuel filter (2020):** On some 1.0 T-GDI models, risk of minor leakage requiring tightening or replacement of a high-pressure hose.

Verdict

The Kia Rio IV (YB) is an absolutely sensible choice. While it may not inspire stylistic or dynamic passion, it compensates with a rare peace of mind in its category.

**For the city:** Opt for the 1.2 MPI 84 hp. It's slow, but its reliability is bulletproof, and its maintenance is very inexpensive. **For versatility:** The 1.0 T-GDI 100 hp (with manual gearbox) is the best compromise. Avoid the 7DCT gearbox if 90% of your driving is in urban traffic jams, to spare yourself premature clutch wear.

This is a vehicle to **BUY** with your eyes closed, provided the service book is stamped on time to benefit from the remainder of the 7-year warranty.
