Summary

The Hyundai i20 Generation III (Code: BC3 / BI3 for India), launched in 2020, marks a radical departure from its conservative predecessor. Embracing Hyundai's 'Sensuous Sportiness' design language, it features sharp, angular styling, a wider stance, and a significantly upgraded, tech-heavy interior with dual 10.25-inch screens on higher trims.

Positioned in the highly competitive B-segment, it rivals the Peugeot 208, Renault Clio, and VW Polo. Hyundai dropped diesel engines entirely for the European market in this generation, focusing heavily on the 1.0 T-GDi petrol engine paired with a 48V Mild-Hybrid (MHEV) system to meet strict emissions standards. It also introduced the innovative iMT (Intelligent Manual Transmission) with an electronic clutch. A mid-cycle facelift in 2023 brought subtle exterior tweaks, standard LED lighting, and upgraded ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems). The crown jewel of the lineup is the highly acclaimed i20 N, a legitimate hot hatch powered by a 204 PS 1.6 T-GDi engine.

Strengths 6

  • Striking, modern exterior design that stands out in the B-segment
  • Class-leading interior space and rear-seat legroom
  • Excellent infotainment system (10.25-inch) with intuitive interface
  • Generous standard safety equipment (Hyundai SmartSense)
  • Outstanding 5-year/unlimited mileage warranty (Europe)
  • The i20 N is one of the best hot hatches of its generation

Weaknesses 5

  • Extensive use of hard, scratch-prone plastics in the cabin
  • Firm ride quality, especially on 17-inch wheels or N-Line trims
  • 7-speed DCT can be jerky at low speeds and during parking maneuvers
  • Road and wind noise insulation is only average at highway speeds
  • Wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto prone to random disconnections (on the 8-inch screen versions)

Check before buying

  • DCT7 Transmission: Test drive in stop-and-go traffic. Look for shuddering, hesitation, or delayed engagement which indicates worn dry clutches.
  • iMT Clutch Operation: On 48V MHEV models, ensure the electronic clutch pedal engages smoothly without delay or warning lights.
  • Infotainment Glitches: Check if the screen freezes or reboots. Ensure the latest firmware is installed.
  • Steering Column Noise: Listen for clicking or clunking sounds when turning the wheel at low speeds (indicates EPS motor or steering column coupling wear).
  • 1.0 T-GDi Cold Start: Listen for excessive rattling from the wastegate actuator or timing chain area on cold starts.

Verdict

The Hyundai i20 (BC3) is a highly rational, stylish, and dependable choice in the subcompact segment. It trades some interior material quality for class-leading space, excellent technology, and a robust warranty.

Buying Advice:

  • If you drive mostly in the city, the naturally aspirated 1.2 MPi is the safest, most reliable, and cheapest option to maintain, though it lacks passing power.
  • For mixed driving, the 1.0 T-GDi (100 PS) is the sweet spot. However, prefer the 6-speed manual over the 7DCT if you spend hours in heavy traffic to avoid premature clutch wear.
  • The 48V MHEV versions offer slight fuel savings but add long-term complexity (iMT clutch, battery).
  • The i20 N is an absolute must-buy for driving enthusiasts, offering raw, analog thrills that are becoming extinct.
