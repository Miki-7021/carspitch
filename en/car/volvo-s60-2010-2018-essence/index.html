Volvo S60

II (Y20) · 2010 – 2018

NEGOTIATE Reliability 76/100 — bon

Summary

The **second-generation Volvo S60 (Y20)** marks a stylistic and dynamic turning point for the Swedish brand. Based on the EUCD platform (shared with Ford), it abandons the cubic design for a four-door coupe line, at the expense of trunk volume.

Marketed during a property transition (from Ford to Geely), its career is marked by a major mechanical evolution: the first years used Ford (4-cylinder) and Volvo (5 and 6-cylinder) blocks, before a complete shift to the 100% Volvo **VEA (Drive-E)** modular engine family from 2014. A facelift occurred in mid-2013 (MY2014), modernizing the front end and instrumentation (digital gauges).

**Positioning:** Premium sedan (D-segment), a direct competitor to German rivals, focusing on unparalleled seat comfort, pioneering active safety (City Safety as standard), and a distinctive identity.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional seat comfort (among the best on the market)
  • Very high level of passive and active safety (City Safety)
  • Paint quality and corrosion resistance
  • Sound and driving pleasure of 5 and 6-cylinder engines (T5, T6, D4, D5)
  • Quality of audio systems (High Performance and Premium Sound Harman Kardon)

Weaknesses 5

  • Ridiculous trunk volume for the category (380 liters) and narrow access
  • Average rear passenger space (limited legroom, except for the Chinese S60L version)
  • Very large turning radius (tedious city maneuvers)
  • Unreliable Powershift automatic gearbox (on early 4-cylinders)
  • High weight penalizing agility and consumption (especially with T6 and AWD)

Check before buying

  • Automatic gearbox service history (especially if Powershift 6DCT450: oil change every 60k km imperative)
  • Oil consumption on 4-cylinder VEA (Drive-E) engines produced between 2014 and 2016
  • Coolant level on 1.6 T3/T4 engines (risk of overheating)
  • Clunking noises in the front axle (wishbone bushings, anti-roll bar links)
  • Operation of the Sensus central screen (slowness, display bugs on early models)

What sets it apart 14

Safety

  • Cutting-edge Safety — The Volvo S60 II is a benchmark in safety, having achieved 5 stars in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores for adult (89%) and child (83%) protection. It integrates the innovative City Safety system as standard, a pioneer in low-speed collision prevention, and has been recognized by the IIHS as a 'Top Safety Pick' multiple times.

Braking

  • Excellent Braking Performance — Enjoy total control thanks to a remarkably efficient braking system. The S60 II offers short stopping distances, typically measured between 36 and 38 meters from 100 km/h, ensuring first-class active safety and reassuring endurance.

tenue de route

  • Serene and Balanced Road Holding — The Volvo S60 II offers safe and predictable road behavior, striking a perfect balance between comfort and dynamism. Its road holding is stable and reassuring, ensuring a controlled and pleasant drive in all circumstances.

Comfort

  • Unrivaled Seat Comfort — One of the major strengths of the S60 II lies in the exceptional comfort of its seats, often cited among the best on the market. They offer perfect support and ergonomics designed for long journeys, transforming every trip into a relaxing experience.

Performance

  • Powerful and Enjoyable Petrol Engines — The 5 and 6-cylinder petrol engines (T5, T6) of the S60 II offer an captivating sound, generous torque, and remarkable driving pleasure. For newer models (post-2016), the VEA (Drive-E) engines paired with the 8-speed Aisin automatic gearbox provide excellent responsiveness and appreciable smoothness.

Reliability

  • Build Quality and Durability — The Volvo S60 II benefits from recognized build quality, notably through the resistance of its paintwork and its excellent protection against corrosion. This is a guarantee of durability and preservation of your vehicle's value over time.

Design

  • Distinctive and Premium Design — With its elegant four-door coupé line and unique identity, the S60 II stands out from its competitors. It embodies modern Swedish design, offering a refined and stylish premium alternative in the sedan segment.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** BMW 3 Series (F30 - more dynamic, larger trunk), Audi A4 (B8/B9 - superior finish, wide choice of engines), Mercedes C-Class (W204/W205 - comfort, rear-wheel drive). - **Japan / Asia:** Lexus IS (XE30 - unbeatable hybrid reliability, finish), Infiniti Q50 (technology, V6 engines). - **North America:** Acura TLX (reliability, SH-AWD), Cadillac ATS (very sharp chassis, rear-wheel drive).

Verdict

The Volvo S60 II is an excellent sedan for those who prioritize comfort, safety, and design over cargo volume. For **petrol** engines, the choice of powertrain is crucial. Purists will opt for the indestructible 5-cylinder (2.5 T5) or 6-cylinder (3.0 T6) engines, despite their thirst. For more economical daily use, the VEA engines (T3/T4/T5 post-2016) are excellent and well-supported by the 8-speed Aisin gearbox. **It is absolutely essential to avoid the 1.6 and 2.0 engines (T3/T4/T5 from 2010 to 2013) equipped with the Powershift gearbox**, whose reliability is too uncertain.
