Audi A1

GB · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation Audi A1 (type GB) is based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MQB-A0 platform, shared with the VW Polo and Seat Ibiza. It marks a stylistic break with a more angular and masculine design, inspired by the Audi Sport quattro. **EXPERT ATTENTION: Contrary to the request targeting diesel, it is crucial to emphasize that the Audi A1 (GB) has NEVER been offered with a diesel engine (TDI).** The Volkswagen Group made the radical decision to remove diesel from this model upon its launch in late 2018, anticipating Euro 6d standards and the decline in demand for diesel in the B-segment (city cars). The range is therefore exclusively composed of turbocharged gasoline engines (TFSI). The model positions itself as a premium city car, offering state-of-the-art technological equipment (Virtual Cockpit as standard) but has been criticized for a slight decrease in the quality of interior plastics compared to the previous generation (8X).

Weaknesses 5

  • TOTAL ABSENCE OF DIESEL ENGINE (detrimental for very high mileage drivers)
  • No hybrid offer (neither MHEV nor HEV) unlike competitors
  • Quality of certain interior plastics (door panels, lower console) unworthy of the Audi badge
  • Reliability of the S tronic 7 gearbox (DQ200) still imperfect in intensive urban use
  • Very high prices (purchase and options)

Check before buying

  • S tronic gearbox operation: no jerking, slipping, or metallic noise should occur during gear changes (especially 1st to 2nd).
  • Multimedia system (MMI): test all functions, Bluetooth, and GPS to detect slowness or reboots.
  • Coolant level (especially on 35 TFSI and 40 TFSI) to anticipate a water pump leak.
  • Mandatory cold test on the 35 TFSI with manual gearbox to check for absence of bucking (Kangarooing).

Official recalls

  • - **November 2019 (Airbag):** Possible improper deployment of the passenger airbag in extreme temperatures. Concerns models manufactured between June and August 2019.
  • - **December 2019 (Seatbelts):** Defect in the right rear seatbelt buckle (shared with Polo/Ibiza). 2018-2019 models.
  • - **May 2021 (eCall):** Malfunction of the automatic emergency call system due to a software bug. Update required.
  • - **February 2022 (Engine):** On some 1.0 TFSI, risk of engine cover loosening, potentially leading to a fire risk if it touches hot parts.

Verdict

The Audi A1 (GB) is a well-developed premium city car, but it imposes clear choices. **If you are looking for a diesel for very high mileage, look elsewhere: this model only exists as a gasoline version.** For mixed use, the 30 TFSI engine (110/116 hp) is the most rational and balanced choice. The 35 TFSI offers good driving pleasure but requires checking the software update history. Avoid models equipped with the S tronic gearbox if the vehicle has been used exclusively in the city (traffic jams), as the wear of the dry clutches remains a weak point. Opt for a model with clear service history within the Audi network to ensure coverage for electronic bugs (MMI).
