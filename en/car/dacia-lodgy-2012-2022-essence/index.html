All Dacia models

Summary

The Dacia Lodgy is a compact MPV launched in 2012, produced in Tangier (Morocco) and sold mainly in Europe. Based on the Renault (Dacia) M0 platform, it shares its technical base with the Duster I, Logan II and Sandero II. It was facelifted in 2017 (grille, headlights, interior). Positioned as the cheapest MPV on the market, it prioritizes roominess and low price at the expense of finish and perceived reliability. Overall rating: economical vehicle, but reliability below average according to TÜV (34% of defects at 3 years).

Strengths 4

  • Very competitive purchase price and low maintenance cost.
  • Generous roominess and large trunk volume for the segment.
  • Simple and robust naturally aspirated engines (1.2 16V, 1.6 MPI/SCe).
  • Cheap spare parts and dense repair network.

Weaknesses 6

  • Overall reliability below average: 34% of defects at the first TÜV inspection (3 years), more than a third of vehicles.
  • At the second TÜV inspection, nearly one in five vehicles has major defects (twice the segment average).
  • Premature suspension wear (springs/shock absorbers): at the third inspection, four times the segment average.
  • Problematic 1.2 TCe 115 (H5Ft) engine: excessive oil consumption, fragile turbo, timing chain.
  • Mediocre interior finishes and insufficient soundproofing.
  • Low Euro NCAP crash-test rating (3 stars in 2012, then 2 stars in 2019 after downgrade).

Check before buying

  • Engine oil level and oil change history (especially on 1.2 TCe).
  • Turbo condition (whistling, shaft play) on 1.2 TCe and 1.5 dCi 110.
  • Timing chain noise on cold start (1.2 TCe, 1.3 TCe).
  • Shock absorber and spring wear (visual check and road test).
  • Operation of the EGR valve and DPF on diesel (1.5 dCi).
  • Presence of rust on wheel arches and underbody (despite correct treatment).
  • Recall history: airbag, catalytic converter, starter, battery (see recalls section).

What sets it apart 10

Economy

  • Very competitive purchase price — The Lodgy is positioned as the cheapest MPV on the market, keeping the initial outlay low for buyers on a tight budget.
  • Low maintenance and running costs — Cheap spare parts and a dense repair network keep servicing affordable, which matters for buyers watching long-term ownership costs.
  • Cheap and widely available parts — Low-cost spare parts and a dense repair network mean repairs are easy to arrange and less expensive than on many rivals.
  • Rational choice for tight budgets — The Lodgy remains a rational choice for a limited budget, provided the buyer accepts higher maintenance costs than a Japanese or Korean reference.

Comfort

  • Generous cabin space for the segment — The compact MPV body offers generous habitability, giving families room for passengers without paying for a larger, pricier vehicle.

Practicality

  • Large boot volume — The boot volume is important for the segment, making it practical for luggage, shopping or family trips.

Reliability

  • Simple robust atmospheric engines — The 1.2 16V and 1.6 MPI/SCe units are simple and robust, with no turbo to maintain and inexpensive servicing.
  • 1.2 16V suited to city driving — The atmospheric 1.2 16V is robust and cheap to maintain, with no major documented problems, making it a sound choice for urban and peri-urban trips.

Performance

  • 1.3 TCe 130 with better reliability — This more modern engine, co-developed with Mercedes, offers good performance and reasonable consumption with reliability generally better than the 1.2 TCe.

Design

  • Restyled in 2017 — The 2017 restyling brought a new grille, headlights and interior, giving later examples a more updated look and cabin.

Verdict

The Dacia Lodgy is an attractively priced MPV, but its reliability is below par. The TÜV 2021 report indicates 34% of defects at 3 years, with premature suspension wear and frequent major defects at the second inspection. Absolutely avoid the 1.2 TCe 115 (H5Ft) due to documented problems with excessive oil consumption and turbo. Favor the naturally aspirated engines (1.2 16V, 1.6 SCe) or the more recent 1.3 TCe 130, and imperatively check the maintenance history and suspension condition. The Lodgy remains a rational choice for a tight budget, provided you accept higher maintenance costs than a Japanese or Korean reference. Verdict: NEGOTIATE (only with a reliable engine and full history).
