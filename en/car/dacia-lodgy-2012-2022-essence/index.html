Summary

The Dacia Lodgy is a compact MPV launched in 2012, built on the Renault-Nissan group's M0 platform (Dacia Logan II, Sandero II, Duster I). It was produced at the Tangier plant (Morocco) and sold mainly in Europe, the Maghreb and certain emerging markets. The Lodgy stands out for its exceptional space-to-price ratio, with a 7-seat version available. It received a facelift in 2017 (grille, headlights, interior). Production ended in 2022, indirectly replaced by the Jogger. Overall reliability is decent for a low-cost vehicle, with simple and proven petrol engines, but basic finishes and equipment. Overall reliability rating: 7/10.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional price/space ratio
  • 7-seat version available
  • Simple and reliable petrol engines (1.2 16v, 1.6 16v)
  • Very low maintenance and spare parts costs
  • Good level of passive safety (4 stars Euro NCAP 2012)

Weaknesses 5

  • Low-grade finishes and materials
  • Soundproofing could be better
  • 1.2 TCe engine prone to reliability issues (oil consumption, turbo)
  • Limited standard equipment on entry-level trims
  • Average road holding (especially when loaded)

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history (regular oil changes, especially for 1.2 TCe)
  • Condition of the turbo and timing chain on 1.2 TCe
  • Engine oil level (abnormal consumption)
  • Operation of the EGR valve and DPF on diesel
  • Clutch and gearbox wear
  • Underbody corrosion (depending on region)
  • Presence of outstanding recalls (airbag, catalytic converter, etc.)

Verdict

The Dacia Lodgy is a rational choice for budget-conscious families looking for a spacious and reliable MPV. Favour the naturally aspirated petrol engines 1.2 16v (75 hp) or 1.6 16v (85/100 hp), which are simpler and more reliable. Avoid the 1.2 TCe 115 hp if the maintenance history is not impeccable (risk of oil consumption and turbo issues). The 1.5 dCi is a good option for high-mileage drivers, provided the maintenance is checked (EGR, DPF, turbo). Imperatively check that the airbag and catalytic converter recalls have been carried out. The Lodgy offers excellent value for money, but do not expect a high level of finish or refinement. Verdict: BUY (with precautions regarding the engine and history).
