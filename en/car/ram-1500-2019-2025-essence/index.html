Summary

The 5th generation Ram 1500 (code name DT) redefined the standards of the *full-size* pickup segment upon its launch. It stands out with a cabin worthy of luxury vehicles, unparalleled ride comfort thanks to its rear coil spring suspension (or optional air suspension), and top-flight soundproofing. The 2025 model year marks a historic turning point with a major restyling (facelift) and the discontinuation of the legendary V8 Hemi in favor of the new « Hurricane » twin-turbo inline 6-cylinder engines. Although mechanically very robust, this model features complex electronics that have been the subject of numerous recalls.

Overall rating: 8/10 - The best pickup in its class in terms of comfort and finish, but demanding on maintenance and prone to electronic quirks.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (coil or air springs)
  • Luxurious interior finish (especially on Laramie, Limited, and Tungsten)
  • Gigantic rear seat space (Crew Cab)
  • Intuitive and responsive Uconnect infotainment system
  • Very competitive towing and payload capacities

Weaknesses 5

  • Capricious electronic reliability (numerous software recalls)
  • Recurring problems on the EcoDiesel engine (HPFP pump)
  • Fragile exhaust manifold studs on the V8 Hemi
  • Air suspension vulnerable in very cold climates
  • Size unsuited to European infrastructure (for imported models)

Check before buying

  • Listen carefully to the 5.7 Hemi engine when cold (a loud ticking indicates broken exhaust studs or a lifter problem).
  • Check the recall history (crucial for the EcoDiesel and safety systems).
  • Test all functions of the 12-inch touchscreen (look for bubbles or dead zones).
  • Inspect the headliner and rear carpet (frequent water leaks at the third brake light or rear window).
  • Raise and lower the air suspension (if equipped) to ensure the compressor is not noisy or failing.

Alternatives

Verdict

The Ram 1500 (DT) is undoubtedly the most comfortable and luxurious pickup of its generation. The choice of engine is crucial: the Pentastar V6 is the choice of reason and absolute reliability. The 5.7L Hemi V8 is fantastic but requires rigorous maintenance (frequent oil changes) to avoid lifter problems, and you will need to budget for the replacement of the exhaust studs. The 3.0L EcoDiesel must absolutely be avoided unless you have irrefutable proof that the HPFP pump and tone wheel have been replaced via official recalls. Finally, the onboard electronics are the true Achilles heel of this model: ensure that all software updates have been performed.
