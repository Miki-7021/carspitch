Mazda Mazda2

III (DJ) · 2015 – 2024

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

Summary

The **third-generation Mazda2 (Type DJ)** is a versatile B-segment city car that stands out for its counter-current approach. Launched in 2015, it adopts the "Kodo" design and "Skyactiv" technologies. Contrary to the trend of *downsizing* and forced induction, Mazda chose naturally aspirated 4-cylinder engines with high compression ratios for its gasoline versions. This strategy proved extremely successful in terms of reliability. The model underwent two major facelifts (2019 and 2023), improving sound insulation, chassis dynamics, and technological equipment.

*Note: In Europe, since 2022, Mazda also markets the "Mazda2 Hybrid" (which is a rebadged Toyota Yaris XP210). This report focuses exclusively on the true Mazda2 (DJ platform) designed and manufactured by Mazda.*

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of naturally aspirated gasoline engines (Skyactiv-G)
  • Driving pleasure (precise steering, excellent manual gearbox control)
  • Very low real-world fuel consumption, close to homologation values
  • Interior finish quality superior to the segment average
  • Ergonomics of the multimedia system with central rotary controller (premium style)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very restricted rear passenger space (legroom and headroom)
  • Modest boot volume (280 liters) with a high loading sill
  • Imperfect sound insulation on pre-2019 models
  • Fragile paint (prone to scratches and chips)
  • Problematic diesel engine (Skyactiv-D) and unsuitable for city driving

Check before buying

  • **Touchscreen (MZD Connect)**: Check for "ghost clicks" (Ghost Touch) or cracks on the edges (2015-2018 models).
  • **Electrically folding mirrors**: The internal motors tend to seize with humidity. Check their proper functioning.
  • **Maintenance history**: Skyactiv engines require specific oil (0W-20) to maintain the reliability of the timing chain and variable valve timing system.
  • **Oil level (Diesel only)**: Check that the level is not above maximum (sign of dilution by diesel fuel).
  • **Underbody and hood**: Inspect for paint chips and early signs of superficial corrosion on the suspension wishbones.

Official recalls

  • - **Fuel pump (November 2021)**: Massive (global) recall concerning the low-pressure fuel pump impeller (Denso supplier) which can deform and block the pump, leading to engine cut-out. Affects models produced between 2017 and 2019. - **DC/DC converter (September 2017)**: On models equipped with the i-ELOOP system, risk of converter overheating. Software update or replacement. - **Parking brake (June 2017)**: The handbrake control shaft on the rear calipers can corrode and seize. Affects models produced in 2015. - **Injectors / DPF (Diesel only)**: Several recall campaigns to reprogram the engine control unit to prevent soot accumulation and injector failure.

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Toyota Yaris (for the hybrid), Peugeot 208 (more comfortable but problematic PureTech engines), Renault Clio (more spacious), Honda Jazz (more habitable and reliable). - **Japan / Asia**: Honda Fit (Jazz), Toyota Vitz/Yaris, Nissan Note. - **North America**: Honda Fit, Hyundai Accent, Kia Rio (the Mazda2 was sold there under the Toyota Yaris badge).

Verdict

The Mazda2 (DJ) is an **exceptional used car choice if you opt for a gasoline engine (Skyactiv-G)**. Counter to its European rivals equipped with fragile small turbo engines (e.g., PureTech, TCE), the Mazda2 offers rare peace of mind thanks to its naturally aspirated 4-cylinder engine with a timing chain. It appeals to drivers who prioritize driving pleasure, reliability, and build quality, at the expense of rear passenger space. **Absolutely avoid the diesel engine**, which is a source of numerous costly problems.
