Summary

The **second-generation Hyundai i20 (code GB in Europe, IB in India as Elite i20)** marked a major turning point for the Korean manufacturer in the B-segment. Designed at the Rüsselsheim center in Germany, it features a European design, some of the best interior space in its class, and a significantly improved finish compared to the first generation.

The model received a **facelift in 2018**, bringing aesthetic touches (new cascading grille, license plate on the tailgate), a technological update (7-inch touchscreen with Apple CarPlay/Android Auto), and the introduction of the DCT-7 dual-clutch gearbox on the 1.0 T-GDI engine. This facelift also signaled the end of diesel engines in Europe.

Overall, the i20 II is recognized for its **very high reliability**, particularly in its naturally aspirated gasoline (MPI) versions, making it an excellent used car purchase. Its 5-year unlimited mileage manufacturer's warranty often allowed for good follow-up within the official network during the vehicles' first years of life.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent overall reliability, particularly for naturally aspirated gasoline engines (MPI)
  • Rear passenger space and trunk volume (326 liters) among the best in its class
  • Simple and intuitive interior ergonomics (physical controls retained)
  • Sound and safe road handling
  • Generous standard equipment, especially on high trims (Creative, Intuitive)
  • 5-year manufacturer's warranty which encouraged good maintenance within the network

Weaknesses 5

  • 1.2 MPI engines a bit sluggish at low revs and just adequate on the highway
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speeds (road and wind noise)
  • Suspension a bit firm at low speeds, especially with 16 or 17-inch wheels
  • 4-speed automatic gearbox (on 1.4 MPI) outdated and fuel-thirsty
  • Quality of certain paints (especially red and white) prone to clear coat peeling

Check before buying

  • **Steering column:** A clunking sound or a stiff spot when turning the steering wheel often indicates wear of the flex disc (rubber star) of the electric power steering (EPS) motor. The part is inexpensive but labor is required.
  • **Clutch (Manual gearboxes):** Tendency to judder in first gear when cold or on a hill. A known defect covered under warranty at the time, but to be checked during the test drive.
  • **DCT-7 gearbox (on 1.0 T-GDI):** Check for the absence of pronounced jerks when starting or during low-speed gear changes (sign of dry dual-clutch wear).
  • **Paint and bodywork:** Inspect the clear coat on the hood, roof, and bumpers, especially on non-metallic colors.
  • **Multimedia system:** Slowness or bugs of the touchscreen on pre-facelift models (2014-2018). Check that the GPS receives a good signal.

Official recalls

  • - **May 2018 (Airbags):** Recall concerning a risk of non-deployment or incorrect deployment of curtain airbags in the event of a collision. Affects models produced between May 2014 and March 2018. - **November 2015 (Seat belts):** Replacement of the seat belt pretensioner which might not function correctly. Affects a small series produced in 2015. - **Engine Software (Unofficial / Service Campaign):** Regular updates during revisions to correct minor engine management bugs (cold jerks on T-GDI) or optimize DPF management on CRDi.

Verdict

The second-generation Hyundai i20 (GB) is an **extremely rational and safe choice on the used market**. While it may not ignite passion with its driving or very sober interior design, it shines with mechanical reliability far superior to the European average (especially compared to PureTech engines or some TSIs of the same era).

**For urban and suburban use**, the **1.2 MPI 84 hp** is the top choice: indestructible, economical to maintain, it handles short trips without issue. **For more versatility**, the **1.0 T-GDI 100 hp** (preferably with a manual gearbox) offers an excellent compromise between dynamism and fuel consumption. Diesel versions should be avoided unless you cover more than 20,000 km per year on highways, in order to preserve the DPF and EGR valve.
