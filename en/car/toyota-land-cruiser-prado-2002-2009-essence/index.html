Toyota Land Cruiser Prado

120 (J120) · 2002 – 2009

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The **Toyota Land Cruiser Prado 120 Series (J120)** is a true legend of automotive reliability. Launched in 2002 to replace the 90 series, it retains a traditional off-roader architecture (ladder frame, rigid rear axle) while significantly improving ride comfort, soundproofing, and safety. Although the European market favored the diesel (D-4D), the petrol engines (especially the 4.0L V6) are highly sought after for their legendary reliability, quiet operation, and lack of issues related to emission control systems. It is a vehicle of choice for expeditions, heavy towing, or as an indestructible 'daily', provided you can accept substantial petrol consumption.

**Overall reliability rating: Excellent.** ADAC and TÜV statistics confirm its exceptional robustness over time.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall reliability (ADAC breakdown rate: 1.5 breakdowns/1000 veh/year - excellent)
  • Indestructible and very smooth 4.0L V6 engine (1GR-FE)
  • Outstanding off-road capabilities (ladder frame, low range gearbox, lockers)
  • Extremely robust automatic gearboxes (4-speed A340F and 5-speed A750F)
  • Exceptional resale value on the used market (confirmed by NRMA/RACQ)
  • Suspension comfort and soundproofing (especially in petrol versions)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high fuel consumption on petrol versions (13 to 16 L/100 km for the V6)
  • Sensitivity to ladder frame corrosion (especially in mountainous/salty regions)
  • Dashboard that cracks with sun and heat (very common issue on J120s)
  • Injector reliability on the D-4D diesel after 150,000 km
  • Clumsy road handling (significant body roll, average braking)

Check before buying

  • Meticulous inspection of the ladder frame to detect perforating rust (wheel arches, frame rails).
  • Condition of the dashboard (cracks above the glove box and instrument cluster).
  • On VX/VXE/TZ versions: condition of the rear air suspension airbags (frequent leaks after 10 years).
  • Sealing of the front brake calipers (pistons tend to seize).
  • On the 4.0L V6: traces of pink coolant around the water pump.
  • On the D-4D: history of injector seals replacement and inspection of the oil strainer.

Practicality

  • Undisputed Off-Road Master — Designed for adventure, the Prado J120 offers extraordinary off-road capabilities thanks to its robust ladder frame chassis, low-range gearbox, and locking differentials. It will take you anywhere, from the most demanding trails to distant expeditions, with remarkable ease.

Comfort

  • On-Road Comfort and V6 Petrol Driving Pleasure — Enjoy remarkable suspension comfort and refined sound insulation, especially with the 4.0L V6 petrol engine. Its smoothness, quiet operation, and smooth power delivery transform every journey into a pleasant experience, even over long distances.

Economy

  • A Durable Investment with Solid Value — The Toyota Land Cruiser Prado J120 benefits from exceptional resale value in the used car market, confirmed by surveys such as those from NRMA/RACQ. It is a vehicle that retains its value, guaranteeing an excellent return on investment and constant demand.

Safety

  • Optimal Safety for All Passengers — With a 4-star Euro NCAP crash test rating, the Land Cruiser Prado J120 ensures solid protection for its occupants. It integrates active safety systems such as ABS, EBD, and Brake Assist (BA) as standard for increased peace of mind on all roads.

Braking

  • Efficient and Controlled Braking — The Prado J120's braking system is designed to be adequate for its size and weight, incorporating ABS, EBD, and Brake Assist (BA) as standard. These technologies contribute to controlled and safe deceleration in various driving conditions.

Handling

  • Comfortable and Versatile Handling — The Land Cruiser Prado 120 offers a smooth and comfortable ride, ideal for long journeys and perfectly suited for challenging terrains. Its robust design prioritizes comfort and the ability to tackle all roads, from highways to the most demanding tracks.

Verdict

The **Toyota Land Cruiser Prado J120** is one of the most reliable vehicles of its generation, fully justifying its popularity and high used prices. If you are looking for absolute peace of mind, **the 4.0L V6 petrol engine (1GR-FE) is the ultimate choice**. It eliminates all the worries inherent to modern diesels (injectors, EGR, turbo) and offers superior driving pleasure, at the cost of significant fuel consumption. The D-4D diesel remains a good choice for high mileage drivers, provided it has a crystal-clear maintenance history (especially regarding the injector seals). The critical point before purchase remains the inspection of the chassis for corrosion. If it is sound, it is a vehicle you can keep for life.
