Toyota Camry

VII (XV50) · 2011 – 2017

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The seventh-generation Toyota Camry (codename XV50) is a mid-size sedan globally recognized for its exceptional reliability, comfort, and controlled running costs. Although it was not officially marketed in Western Europe (where Toyota favored the Avensis), it dominated the North American, Asian, Russian, and Middle Eastern markets.

Launched in late 2011, it received a significant facelift in 2014 (2015 model year in North America) which revised almost all body panels, improved soundproofing, stiffened the chassis, and modernized the interior. The Camry XV50 offers no diesel or 100% electric powertrains, focusing exclusively on naturally aspirated gasoline engines and a highly refined hybrid (HEV) variant. It is a rational purchase choice par excellence, often described as indestructible if basic maintenance is respected.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional overall mechanical reliability (engines capable of exceeding 400,000 km)
  • Suspension comfort and smooth ride
  • Generous interior space (very welcoming rear seats)
  • Extremely refined and economical HSD hybrid system
  • Maintenance cost and availability of spare parts
  • Notable improvement in soundproofing and finish after the 2014/2015 facelift

Weaknesses 5

  • Clumsy road handling and uncommunicative steering (except SE/XSE trim)
  • Torque converter issues on the 6-speed automatic gearbox (2.5L, 2012-2014 models)
  • Dated, slow infotainment system (Entune) without Apple CarPlay / Android Auto
  • Quality of some interior plastics and premature leather wear on early model years
  • Imperfect soundproofing on pre-facelift models (2011-2014)

Check before buying

  • Road test at 60-80 km/h (2.5L gasoline): look for any vibration or sensation of driving over rumble strips (sign of a worn torque converter).
  • Cold start (2.5L): listen for a possible metallic rattle lasting one to two seconds (VVT-i gear).
  • Hybrid: check Toyota hybrid health check history and test braking (no ABS/VSC warning lights, no continuous pump noise).
  • V6 3.5L: inspect the right side of the engine (belt side) for oil leaks at the timing cover.
  • Air conditioning: check for any musty odor (known issue with evaporator condensation drainage).

What sets it apart 7

reliability

  • Legendary Reliability and Exceptional Durability — The Toyota Camry is globally renowned for its mechanical robustness, with gasoline engines designed to exceed 400,000 km, ensuring unparalleled peace of mind in the long term.

comfort

  • Superior Ride Comfort and Generous Space — Enjoy a smooth and refined driving experience thanks to a comfort-oriented suspension and improved sound insulation, complemented by a spacious cabin offering exceptional accommodation for rear passengers.

safety

  • First-Class Overall Safety — The Camry XV50 has been praised for its safety performance, earning top distinctions from independent organizations, ensuring optimal protection for all occupants.

braking

  • Efficient and Controlled Braking — Benefit from competent and reassuring braking performance, with average stopping distances for its category, ensuring active safety in all circumstances.

handling

  • Stable and Predictable Handling — The Camry offers road behavior focused on stability and predictability, providing a sense of security and control, ideal for long journeys as well as daily driving.

Performance

  • Smooth and Capable Gasoline Engines — Whether it's the 2.5L offering an excellent balance of power/consumption or the 3.5L V6 for smooth and ample power, each gasoline engine guarantees a pleasant and reliable driving experience.

economy

  • Optimized Cost of Ownership — Thanks to its proven reliability and easy availability of parts, the Toyota Camry represents an economical choice in the long run, minimizing maintenance and repair expenses.

Alternatives

  • North America: Honda Accord (more dynamic), Nissan Altima (comfortable but fragile CVT gearbox), Ford Fusion (European design), Hyundai Sonata / Kia Optima.
  • Asia / Russia: Honda Accord, Nissan Teana, Mazda 6 (superior design and driving), Skoda Superb (unbeatable interior space).
  • Europe (Import): Volkswagen Passat, Ford Mondeo, Peugeot 508.

Verdict

The Toyota Camry XV50 is the archetype of a rational purchase. It's not the most exciting sedan to drive, but it excels in its mission: transporting its occupants in great comfort, with almost unparalleled reliability.

Buying advice: Prioritize facelifted models (from late 2014 / 2015 model year). They benefit from a stiffer chassis, better soundproofing, a more upscale interior, and, most importantly, the automatic gearbox torque converter issues (on the 2.5L) were resolved at the factory. The Hybrid version is the best choice for daily use thanks to its negligible consumption for its size, while the V6 will delight those who appreciate American-style smoothness.
