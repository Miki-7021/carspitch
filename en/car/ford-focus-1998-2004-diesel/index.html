Ford Focus

I (DAW) · 1998 – 2004

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The first-generation Ford Focus (Mk1, codes DAW/DBW/DFW/DNW) marked a radical break upon its launch in 1998. Replacing the aging Escort, it introduced the 'New Edge' design and, above all, an unprecedented multi-link rear suspension in its category ('Control Blade'), redefining the C-segment's road handling standards. Voted Car of the Year in 1999, it received a facelift in late 2001 (headlights with integrated indicators, revised bumpers, improved sound insulation and equipment). Regarding diesel engines, it transitioned between the older generation of classic direct injection (TDDi) and modern common rail (TDCi). Today, it is a very affordable vehicle, but its reliability depends drastically on the choice of engine and maintenance history, especially for diesels.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road handling (precise chassis, multi-link rear suspension)
  • Generous interior space and boot volume for its time
  • Exterior and interior design that has aged well (New Edge)
  • Remarkable reliability of Zetec-SE petrol engines (1.4 and 1.6)
  • Very affordable spare parts cost

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of Delphi injection on TDCi diesels
  • Extreme susceptibility to corrosion (a common issue for Fords of that era)
  • Interior plastic quality (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Poor sound insulation (especially on Phase 1 and TDDi models)
  • Recurring electrical problems (instrument cluster, central locking)

Check before buying

  • **Corrosion:** Inspect sills, wheel arches, bottom of doors, tailgate, and front/rear subframes.
  • **Injection (TDCi):** Difficult cold start, black smoke, glow plug light flashing while driving = faulty Delphi injection system (metal filings).
  • **Pump (TDDi):** If the engine cuts out abruptly while driving or refuses to start, the Bosch pump module is likely burnt out.
  • **Flywheel (TDCi):** Vibrations in the clutch pedal and metallic knocking at idle.
  • **Electronics:** Check that all dashboard needles are working (instrument cluster solder joint issue).
  • **Running gear:** Irregular rear tire wear (worn multi-link suspension bushes) and front shock absorber springs that can break suddenly.

What sets it apart 14

Performance

  • Benchmark Handling — The Ford Focus Mk1 redefined its category's standards with its precise chassis and innovative multi-link rear suspension, offering a dynamic and engaging driving experience.
  • Pleasant Diesel Engine (TDCi) — The 1.8 TDCi engine offers flexible driving and generous torque, combined with controlled fuel consumption and noticeable quietness for a diesel of this generation.

Safety

  • Recognized Safety — With a 4-star Euro NCAP rating, the Focus Mk1 offers solid protection for its occupants, ensuring peace of mind on the road.
  • Effective Braking — The Focus Mk1 stands out with its excellent braking performance for its class, ensuring effective stopping power and good pedal feel, especially when equipped with ABS.

Practicality

  • Spacious and Versatile — Enjoy generous interior space and a substantial boot volume, making the Focus Mk1 ideal for daily commutes as well as family getaways.

Comfort

  • Timeless and Award-Winning Design — Its bold 'New Edge' design marked its era and was celebrated with the 1999 Car of the Year title, retaining a modern and distinctive aesthetic even today.

Economy

  • Controlled Maintenance Costs and Mechanical Robustness (TDDi) — The 1.8 TDDi engine is renowned for its indestructible block and simple design (without DPF or dual-mass flywheel on most versions), ensuring affordable maintenance costs and great durability.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Volkswagen Golf IV (benchmark in finish, robust but noisy TDI engines), Peugeot 307 (excellent HDi diesels, but electronic issues), Opel Astra G, Renault Mégane I & II. - **Japan / Asia:** Honda Civic (7th generation), Toyota Corolla (E110/E120 - absolute benchmarks in reliability), Mazda 323. - **North America:** Honda Civic, Toyota Corolla, Chevrolet Cavalier, Dodge Neon. - **Emerging Markets:** Chevrolet Astra, Volkswagen Golf (Mk4/Bora).

Verdict

Buying a Ford Focus Mk1 diesel today is a risky bet. The 1.8 TDCi offers excellent driving pleasure but its Delphi injection system is a financial time bomb (metal filings), often more expensive to repair than the car's value. The 1.8 TDDi is mechanically indestructible, but its Bosch injection pump remains a known weak point and it is very noisy. **The verdict for diesels is therefore to AVOID the TDCi, and to NEGOTIATE a TDDi only if the pump has already been replaced.** Ironically, the best Focus Mk1 is a petrol version (1.6 Zetec), which proves to be extremely reliable, inexpensive to maintain, and allows full enjoyment of the car's excellent chassis. In any case, the number one enemy remains rust: a thorough inspection of the underbody is mandatory.
