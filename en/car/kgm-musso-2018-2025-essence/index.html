Summary

The second-generation KGM Musso (code name Q200), initially marketed under the SsangYong brand before the takeover by the KG Group, is a robust pickup truck based on the ladder frame chassis of the Rexton SUV. Known as the Rexton Sports in South Korea, it is available in a standard version (coil spring rear suspension) and an extended 'Grand' or 'Khan' version (available with leaf springs for increased payload). Although the European market is dominated by the excellent 2.2 e-XDi diesel engine, this sheet focuses on the 2.0 e-XGDi Turbo (G20DTR) petrol engine, which is highly popular in Asian, Middle Eastern, and South American markets. This pickup stands out for its excellent price-to-equipment ratio, its spacious cabin (inherited from the Rexton), and its 3.5-tonne towing capacity, although its unladen road handling remains rustic.

Strengths 6

  • Unbeatable price/quality/equipment ratio in the segment
  • Very spacious cabin (rear seats worthy of a large SUV)
  • 3,500 kg towing capacity (with automatic gearbox)
  • Very reliable and smooth 6-speed Aisin automatic gearbox
  • Long manufacturer warranty (often 5 to 7 years depending on the market)
  • High-performance and quiet 2.0 Turbo petrol engine (rare in this segment)

Weaknesses 5

  • Gargantuan fuel consumption of the 2.0 e-XGDi petrol engine
  • Bouncy road handling when unladen (especially with leaf springs on the Grand)
  • Sparse KGM/SsangYong dealer network in Europe
  • Rapid depreciation on the used market
  • Slightly dated infotainment system compared to current benchmarks

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history: turbo engines (both petrol and diesel) require high-quality oil and regular oil changes.
  • Underbody: check for impact damage or rust if the vehicle has been used for intensive off-roading.
  • Automatic gearbox: ensure there are no jerks when shifting from D to R when cold.
  • Petrol engine (G20DTR): listen to the cold idle (misfires can indicate worn coils or carbon-fouled valves).
  • On-board electronics: test the touchscreen, reversing camera, and driving aids (some bugs reported).

Verdict

The KGM (SsangYong) Musso Q200 is an extremely rational choice for those looking for a spacious, well-equipped double cab pickup capable of heavy towing, without paying the premium price of a Ford Ranger or a Toyota Hilux. The 2.0 e-XGDi petrol version is an interesting alternative in regions where diesel is banned or for low-mileage drivers, offering appreciable quiet operation. However, one must be prepared to accept very high fuel consumption (easily 12L/100km in mixed use). Overall mechanical reliability is very good, supported by a proven Aisin gearbox, provided that maintenance intervals are strictly respected.
