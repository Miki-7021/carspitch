Toyota GR86

All Toyota models

Summary

The Toyota GR86 (Generation II - ZN8), co-developed with Subaru (BRZ ZD8), succeeds the famous GT86. Designed by the Gazoo Racing division, it retains its predecessor's magic recipe (RWD, naturally aspirated Boxer engine, very low center of gravity, contained weight) while correcting its main flaw: the lack of mid-range torque. Thanks to the switch from a 2.0L to a 2.4L block, the famous "torque dip" (acceleration lag) has disappeared. It is one of the last pure, affordable, and analog sports cars on the market. In Europe, its career is meteoric: Toyota announced at launch that the model would only be sold for two years, as the platform is not compatible with the new European GSR2 (General Safety Regulation 2) safety standards, which become mandatory in mid-2024.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional chassis, perfect balance, and very low center of gravity
  • Precise and communicative steering
  • 2.4L engine full at all revs (end of the GT86's torque dip)
  • 6-speed manual gearbox with short and precise throws
  • Unbeatable price/pleasure ratio (excluding European ecological penalty)
  • Limited-slip differential (Torsen) as standard

Weaknesses 6

  • Factory RTV (silicone) sealant issue threatening reliability on track
  • Exorbitant ecological penalty in France (often higher than the price of a new car)
  • Very light sound insulation (significant road noise on the highway)
  • Rear seats purely symbolic (for emergencies or luggage)
  • Basic infotainment system and mediocre audio quality
  • Slow automatic gearbox (6-speed auto) and poorly suited to the car's character

Check before buying

  • Usage history: look for signs of intensive track use (tire wear on the sidewalls, rubber buildup in the wheel wells, brake condition).
  • Oil level: a low level during inspection is an absolute red flag for this Boxer engine.
  • Oil pickup: ask if the oil pan has been dropped to check/clean the oil pickup (a real plus if done by a professional with an invoice).
  • Manual gearbox synchros: 2nd gear can be notchy when cold, but should not crunch when warm.
  • Condition of the bodywork and underbody (very low car, prone to scraping).

Official recalls

  • February 2023 (North America / Japan): Recall concerning the communication module (DCM) which may not connect to emergency services (eCall) after a restart. Software update.
  • March 2022 (Certain markets): Rear turn signal issue where they may flash too quickly or stop working due to a voltage fault. Replacement of the light units or relay.
  • *Note: No global official recall has been issued by Toyota/Subaru regarding the RTV sealant issue, with brands handling engine failures on a case-by-case basis under warranty (sometimes with disputes if track use is proven).*

What sets it apart 7

Performance

  • Unrivaled Driving Pleasure — The Toyota GR86 offers a pure and authentic driving experience thanks to its exceptional chassis, perfect balance, and very low center of gravity. The 2.4L Boxer engine delivers linear power and generous torque at all revs, ensuring exhilarating acceleration and the elimination of its predecessor's "torque dip."
  • Exceptional Agility and Dynamic Precision — Universally praised for its outstanding dynamic behavior, the GR86 benefits from a light and rigid chassis, precise and communicative steering. Its predictable and engaging handling allows for precise control and an intimate connection with the road.
  • Sporty and Immersive Manual Gearbox — The 6-speed manual gearbox, with its short and precise throws, is a major asset for the driving experience. It allows for an intimate connection with the machine and optimal utilization of the 2.4L Boxer engine.

Safety

  • Efficient and Enduring Braking — The GR86's braking system is designed for both sporty and road use, offering adequate deceleration power and excellent pedal feel. It ensures optimal active safety even during dynamic driving.
  • Recognized Passive Safety — The twin model, the 2022 Subaru BRZ, was designated an "IIHS Top Safety Pick" with "Good" ratings in most crash tests and "Superior" for optional front crash prevention, attesting to a high level of safety.

Economy

  • A Future Collector and Smart Investment — The GR86 is a pure and analog sports car, whose sales in Europe are limited to just two years due to new GSR2 standards. This exclusivity makes it a future collector's item and a potentially wise investment for enthusiasts.
  • Unbeatable Price-to-Pleasure Ratio — The GR86 offers an exceptional level of driving pleasure and performance for its segment. It is one of the last pure and affordable sports cars on the market, providing fantastic value for the sensations it delivers.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Mazda MX-5 ND (2.0L 184 hp - convertible, lighter but less rigid), Alpine A110 (much more expensive, mid-engine turbo), Subaru BRZ (technical twin, very rare in Europe).
  • North America: Subaru BRZ (ZD8), Mazda MX-5 Miata, Nissan Z (RZ34 - more powerful and GT), Ford Mustang EcoBoost / GT, Honda Civic Type R (FL5 - FWD but comparable price/performance).
  • Japan / Asia: Subaru BRZ, Nissan Fairlady Z, Honda Civic Type R.

Verdict

The Toyota GR86 is a future collector's item and one of the best sports cars of its decade. It offers pure driving sensations, which have become extremely rare at this price point. However, the RTV (silicone) sealant issue in the oil pan is a real sword of Damocles for track day enthusiasts.

Buying advice: If you buy a GR86 for road use (even spirited driving), the risk of oil starvation is almost nil. If you plan to use it on the track, immediately budget for removing the oil pan to clean the oil pickup, and install an oil cooler. Absolutely prioritize the manual gearbox. In France, buying used is the only viable solution to bypass the confiscatory ecological penalty on new cars.
