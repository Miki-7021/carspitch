Summary

Alfa Romeo Giulia I Phase 2 (Type 952)

Initially launched in 2016, the Alfa Romeo Giulia received a significant update (Phase 2) in late 2019/early 2020, followed by a second aesthetic restyling in 2023. Based on the brilliant Giorgio platform, it is unanimously recognized as one of the most dynamic sedans in its segment.

Positioning and Evolutions: Phase 2 corrects the main early flaws of the first iteration. The interior takes a leap forward in terms of perceived quality (center console materials, new steering wheel, redesigned gear lever) and infotainment becomes tactile with a modernized interface. The arrival of Level 2 driving aids (ADAS) brings it up to par with German competitors. The 2023 restyling introduces three-module Matrix LED headlights (like the Tonale) and a 100% digital instrument cluster.

Overall Generation Note: Phase 2 is the version to prioritize. Electronic reliability, a weak point of early models, has been largely improved. The cruel absence of electrification (neither hybrid nor PHEV) penalizes its sales but delights purists.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional chassis (Giorgio platform) and ultra-direct steering
  • Perfectly calibrated ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox
  • Significantly improved finish and assembly quality on Phase 2
  • Very well-controlled fuel consumption on 2.2 JTDm diesel engines
  • Timeless design, enhanced by Matrix LED headlights (from 2023)

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of hybridization (heavy ecological penalty in France on petrol models)
  • Capricious AdBlue system on diesel engines
  • Average rear passenger space and ingress/egress for the category
  • No station wagon (Sportwagon) variant available
  • Sparse dealer network and sometimes inconsistent after-sales service

Check before buying

  • Condition and age of the AGM battery (number 1 cause of electronic bugs via the IBS sensor)
  • Maintenance history of the AdBlue system and absence of engine warning light (Diesel)
  • Actual replacement of the timing belt on diesels older than 5 years
  • Tire wear: aggressive front axle camber wears the inside of the tires
  • Smooth operation of the touchscreen (some cases of 'ghost touch' or screens cracked from the inside)

Official recalls

Official Manufacturer Recalls (Phase 2)

  • 2020 - Braking System (IBS): Software update for the integrated braking module (Continental IBS) following a risk of loss of braking assistance or ABS/ESP malfunction.
  • 2021 - Fuel Hose: Risk of a fuel hose rubbing against an engine component, potentially leading to a leak (concerns a small series of 2020-2021 models).
  • 2022 - Automatic Headlights: Reprogramming of the automatic high beam management module, which could dazzle other road users or be misaligned.
  • 2023 - AdBlue Level Sensor: Replacement or reprogramming of the urea level sensor on certain 2.2 JTDm diesel models displaying an erroneous range.

Verdict

Final Verdict

The Alfa Romeo Giulia Phase 2 is a characterful sedan that has successfully corrected the early flaws of its launch. It is a highly recommendable purchase for those who prioritize driving pleasure over technological gadgets.

Diesel Focus (2.2 JTDm): This engine is an excellent companion for high-mileage drivers. It is robust, torquey, and very economical. However, it requires appropriate use: avoid this engine if you primarily drive in the city, otherwise you risk clogging the DPF, EGR valve, and experiencing issues with the AdBlue system. Prioritize the 160 hp or 190 hp versions, which offer the best compromise between performance and maintenance costs. Ensure the timing belt has been replaced on time and do not hesitate to use a preventive additive for AdBlue.
