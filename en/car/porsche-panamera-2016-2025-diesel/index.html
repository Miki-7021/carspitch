Porsche Panamera

II (971) · 2016 – 2025

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The second-generation Porsche Panamera (Type 971) marks a spectacular break from its predecessor. Gone are the clumsy proportions; it adopts a sleek line inspired by the 911, and is even available in an attractive shooting brake version (Sport Turismo). Technically, it inaugurates the Volkswagen Group's MSB platform, a new, significantly more reliable 8-speed PDK gearbox (ZF), and a complete overhaul of the interior (Porsche Advanced Cockpit).

**Diesel Focus:** The 4S Diesel version, briefly marketed between 2016 and 2018, is a true engineering monster. Powered by a 4.0 biturbo V8 (EA898) developing 422 hp and a colossal torque of 850 Nm, it offered supercar performance with a range of over 1,000 km. It was removed from the catalog following Porsche's decision to abandon diesel (consequences of Dieselgate), making it a rare model today, highly sought after by high-mileage drivers.

**Overall Reliability:** The data is very reassuring. ADAC notes a major improvement compared to the 970 generation, with a breakdown rate of only 2.8 to 3.5/1000 vehicles/year (compared to >4.2 for the old one). The TÜV Report notes a 3-year defect rate of 17%, placing it in the upper average of the premium segment. What Car? awards it 3/5 with 26% of reported faults, mainly electronic.
