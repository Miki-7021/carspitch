Summary

The Hyundai IONIQ 6 (Generation I, code CE) is a 100% electric sedan based on the Hyundai-Kia group's E-GMP modular platform. Dubbed the "Electrified Streamliner" by the manufacturer, it stands out with its ultra-aerodynamic teardrop design (drag coefficient of 0.21), giving it remarkable energy efficiency and excellent highway range. It benefits from the 800-volt architecture, allowing for ultra-fast charging (10 to 80% in 18 minutes on an Ionity charger). Although praised for its comfort, soundproofing, and onboard technologies, it suffers from an impractical trunk (no tailgate) and limited rear headroom. In terms of reliability, the E-GMP platform is generally robust, but it is marred by recurring issues related to the ICCU (Integrated Charging Control Unit) and 12V battery management, which have been the subject of multiple recalls.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional energy efficiency (very low consumption on the highway)
  • 800V architecture allowing ultra-fast charging (10-80% in 18 min)

Weaknesses 5

  • Recurring ICCU and 12V battery issues (requiring updates/replacements)

  • Rear headroom penalizing for adults over 1.80m
  • Room for improvement in ergonomics (window controls in the center, complex touch menus)
  • Limited rear visibility

Check before buying

  • Recall history: Demand proof that the ICCU updates have been done.
  • 12V battery condition: Often damaged by ICCU bugs, it may require preventive replacement.
  • Charging port door: Check that it opens and closes smoothly and that the panel is securely attached.
  • Transmission (AWD): On all-wheel drive models, check for the absence of metallic noises at the rear.
  • Tire wear: Instant torque, especially on RWD and AWD versions, quickly wears out the rear tires.

Verdict

The Hyundai IONIQ 6 is a brilliant electric sedan, offering formidable efficiency and some of the best charging speeds on the market thanks to its 800V architecture. It is an excellent alternative to the Tesla Model 3 for those who prioritize comfort, stylistic originality, and legroom. However, its reliability record is tarnished by the endemic ICCU (charging and 12V battery management) issue affecting the E-GMP platform. Buying used is recommended, but on one strict condition: you must imperatively verify that the vehicle has undergone all recall campaigns (software updates and/or hardware replacement of the ICCU). Favor the 77.4 kWh RWD version for the best range/price compromise.
