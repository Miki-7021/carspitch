Genesis GV60

I (JW) · 2022 – 2024

Summary

The Genesis GV60 (JW generation) is the first 100% electric compact SUV from the Hyundai group's premium brand. Based on the excellent E-GMP modular platform (shared with the Hyundai Ioniq 5 and Kia EV6), it stands out with its 800V architecture allowing ultra-fast charging, its bold design, and its luxurious interior integrating innovative technologies (facial recognition, crystal control sphere).

**IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL ENGINES:** As an expert, it is imperative to correct a common misconception: **the Genesis GV60 has NO diesel engine, nor any internal combustion engine.** It was designed exclusively as a battery electric vehicle (BEV). This report therefore focuses exhaustively on the actual electric powertrains that equip this model, while analyzing the early teething problems of this platform, notably the critical issues related to the ICCU.

Strengths 5

  • 800V architecture allowing a 10 to 80% charge in 18 minutes (on Ionity/Kempower stations)
  • Exceptional interior finish quality and premium materials
  • Highly advanced soundproofing (acoustic glazing)
  • Performance of the AWD Performance version (impressive Boost Mode)
  • On-board technologies (Face Connect, Crystal Sphere)

Weaknesses 5

  • Endemic ICCU (Integrated Charging Control Unit) problem on the E-GMP platform
  • Disappointing trunk volume for the category (432 liters)
  • Rear visibility hindered by the tailgate design and spoiler
  • Highway power consumption could be improved compared to a Tesla Model Y
  • Genesis maintenance network still very limited in Europe

Check before buying

  • Absolute proof of completion of ICCU-related recalls (invoice or dealer certificate)
  • Condition and age of the 12V battery (often damaged by ICCU failures)
  • Verification of the rear driveshafts on AWD models (recall for risk of breakage)
  • Tire wear, particularly on the rear axle of Performance versions
  • Proper functioning of the instrument cluster screen and rearview camera (prone to software bugs)

Verdict

The Genesis GV60 is a luxurious electric SUV, ultra-fast to charge and endowed with a strong personality. However, its initial reliability is heavily marred by the endemic ICCU problem (common to the entire E-GMP platform) and driveshaft issues on AWD versions. Buying used is entirely feasible, but **only if the seller can prove that all recall campaigns (notably software and hardware for the ICCU) have been carried out**. Without this clear history, the risk of immobilization is too high.
