Summary

The first-generation GMC Terrain is a compact/mid-size SUV (according to North American standards) based on the GM Theta platform, which it shares with the Chevrolet Equinox. Launched for the 2010 model year, it stands out with a very boxy, muscular, and distinctive design, typical of the GMC DNA. Positioned as a slightly more premium alternative to the Equinox, it offers a spacious cabin, a highly practical sliding rear bench seat, and excellent soundproofing thanks to the active noise cancellation (ANC) system. A facelift was introduced for the 2016 model year, modernizing the bumpers, the LED lighting signature, and the ergonomics of the center console. According to data from J.D. Power and Consumer Reports, this generation boasts an overall reliability above the average for its category, supported by an excellent Hydra-Matic automatic gearbox and good residual value.

Strengths 6

  • Overall reliability above average (according to Consumer Reports and ADAC)
  • Excellent and very smooth 6-speed automatic Hydra-Matic gearbox (6T45/6T70)
  • Exceptional rear roominess (sliding bench seat over 20 cm)
  • Cabin soundproofing (acoustic glass and standard ANC system)
  • Very robust V6 engines (3.0L and 3.6L)
  • Good residual value on the used market

Weaknesses 5

  • Possible excessive oil consumption on early 2.4L engines (2010-2013)
  • Mediocre rear and rear 3/4 visibility (very wide C-pillars)
  • High fuel consumption on V6 engines
  • Quality of some interior plastics (hard and prone to scratching)
  • Large turning radius

Check before buying

  • Complete oil change history (crucial for the longevity of timing chains on all engines)
  • Dipstick oil level at the time of purchase (especially on the 2010-2013 2.4L Ecotec)
  • Operation of the front wiper motor (prone to corrosion/recall)
  • Condition of the transfer case on all-wheel drive (AWD) models
  • Absence of metallic noises on cold start (sign of timing chain wear)

Official recalls

  • [2022-03-17] Wipers (2014-2015): The ball joints in the wiper module can corrode, leading to the failure of one or both wipers.
  • [2022-03-08] Lighting (2010-2017): A reflection caused by the headlight housings may illuminate areas above the vehicle with excessive brightness, causing glare to other drivers.
  • [2016-08-04] Wipers (2013): Wear and corrosion of the ball joints in the wiper module, which can cause them to stop working.
  • [2016-07-05] Ignition system (2016-2017): The electronic park lock lever may allow the ignition key to be removed without the transmission being in the PARK position (risk of vehicle rollaway).
  • [2016-06-21] Labels (2016): Certification labels may contain incorrect information regarding tire/rim size and cold tire pressure.
  • [2015-10-16] Airbags (2015): Potential issue with the side airbags mounted in the front seats.
  • [2014-07-23] Power seats (2010-2012): Defect in the height adjustment system of the front power seats.
  • [2011-10-19] TPMS (2012): Non-compliance of the tire pressure monitoring system.
  • [2010-12-15] Seat belts (2011): The driver or front passenger seat belt buckle anchor may fracture near the seat attachment.
  • [2009-12-23] Air conditioning/Defrost (2010): Software non-compliance of the windshield defrosting and defogging system.

What sets it apart 12

Reliability

  • Reliability Above Category Average — Consumer Reports and ADAC data place this generation above the average for its class, reducing the risk of unexpected repairs for buyers seeking a dependable used SUV.
  • Robust V6 Engine Options — The 3.0L and 3.6L V6 engines are described as very robust, giving buyers a durable powertrain choice that suits highway driving and towing without stressing the mechanics.

Comfort

  • Smooth Hydra-Matic Automatic Gearbox — The six-speed Hydra-Matic 6T45/6T70 transmission is described as excellent and very smooth, delivering comfortable, seamless gear changes during daily driving and longer journeys.
  • Quiet Cabin With Active Noise Cancellation — Acoustic glass and the standard active noise cancellation system reduce interior noise, making long motorway drives and everyday commutes noticeably calmer for occupants.
  • Spacious Interior For Its Size — The cabin offers generous space, with rear habitability described as exceptional, so families can seat passengers comfortably despite the vehicle's compact exterior dimensions.

Practicality

  • Sliding Rear Bench Adds Flexibility — The rear bench slides over 20 cm, letting buyers prioritise legroom for passengers or expand cargo space depending on the trip, a rare feature in this segment.
  • Towing Capacity For Practical Needs — The 3.6L V6 version offers a towing capacity of 1,588 kg, allowing buyers to pull trailers or small loads without needing a larger, thirstier vehicle.

Economy

  • Strong Resale Value On Used Market — The model holds good residual value on the used market, meaning buyers face less depreciation risk and can expect a healthier return when reselling the vehicle later.
  • Refined 2.4L For Daily Driving — The 2.4L Ecotec offers reasonable fuel consumption, flexibility and quiet operation, making it well suited to urban and suburban daily trips for cost-conscious buyers.

Performance

  • Powerful 3.6L V6 Performance — The 3.6L V6 produces 301 hp and offers excellent driving pleasure, giving buyers confident acceleration and easy overtaking on motorways and long journeys.

Design

  • Distinctive Squared-Off Design — The strongly squared, muscular styling is typical of GMC's design language, giving buyers a compact SUV that stands out visually rather than blending into the crowd.
  • Improved 2016 Facelift Updates — The 2016 facelift modernised the bumpers, LED lighting signature and centre console ergonomics, giving buyers a more contemporary look and easier access to controls.

Alternatives

  • North America: Chevrolet Equinox (its technical twin), Ford Edge, Honda CR-V, Toyota RAV4, Nissan Rogue, Jeep Cherokee.
  • Middle East: Hyundai Tucson, Kia Sportage, Ford Escape.

Verdict

The first-generation GMC Terrain is an excellent choice for those looking for a compact SUV with an assertive look, offering the interior space of a higher segment. Reliability data places it above average, with a special mention for the robustness of its Hydra-Matic automatic gearbox and its V6 engines (3.0L and 3.6L). The 2.4L Ecotec 4-cylinder engine is very reliable on the 2014-2017 model years, but requires great caution on the 2010-2013 models due to known issues with excessive oil consumption. A highly recommended purchase if the maintenance history is clear, favoring the V6 versions or the post-2013 4-cylinders.
