Summary

The Renault Koleos II (internal code HZG) is a D-segment family SUV, based on the modular CMF-C/D platform of the Renault-Nissan Alliance, which it shares closely with the Nissan X-Trail (T32). Unlike its predecessor, it abandons the atypical design to adopt the brand's premium stylistic codes (Talisman-style). Manufactured in Busan (South Korea) by Renault Samsung Motors (where it is sold as the QM6) and in Wuhan (China), it stands out for its generous rear spaciousness and ride comfort, but foregoes a 7-seat version (reserved for the Espace or Nissan X-Trail).

Restylings (Facelifts):

  • Phase 2 (2019): Slight aesthetic tweaks (grille, skid plates), improved interior materials, full-screen Apple CarPlay/Android Auto integration, and a revamp of the engine range (introduction of Blue dCi).
  • Phase 3 (2021): New 100% LED light signature, late arrival of the 1.3 TCe petrol engine in Europe to compensate for the decline of diesel.

Expert's overall note: A very comfortable and premium SUV, often overlooked in Europe in favor of the Peugeot 5008, but which has enjoyed great international success. The petrol engines (especially the Nissan naturally aspirated blocks outside Europe and the 1.3 TCe) offer excellent overall reliability, far superior to complex diesels, provided that the maintenance of the CVT automatic gearbox (X-Tronic) is scrupulously monitored.

Strengths 5

  • High-level suspension comfort and sound insulation
  • Giant rear spaciousness (legroom worthy of a limousine)
  • Reliability of petrol engines (1.3 TCe and Nissan naturally aspirated blocks)
  • Premium interior presentation (especially in Initiale Paris trim)
  • Decent off-road capabilities on 4x4 versions (All Mode 4x4-i)

Weaknesses 5

  • X-Tronic (CVT) automatic gearbox that smooths out sensations too much and requires rigorous maintenance
  • R-Link 2 multimedia system slow and prone to bugs on pre-2019 models
  • No 7-seat version available (unlike the Nissan X-Trail or Peugeot 5008)
  • Reliability of anti-pollution systems (AdBlue) on recent diesels
  • Disappointing boot volume compared to exterior size (around 500 liters)

Check before buying

  • Maintenance history of the automatic gearbox (CVT or EDC): an oil change at 60,000 km is a huge plus.
  • Operation of the R-Link 2 touchscreen: test Bluetooth, GPS, and overall responsiveness.
  • On diesels: absence of "Check Anti-pollution" or "AdBlue" warning light on the dashboard.
  • Wear of front tires and suspension wishbone bushings (the vehicle is heavy).
  • Body panel alignment and wind noise around the panoramic sunroof (if equipped).

Official recalls

  • November 2020 (Child safety): Risk of accidental unlocking of rear doors from the inside. Concerns models manufactured between June and November 2020.
  • October 2019 (Oxygen sensor): On the 1.3 TCe engine, risk of non-compliance with emissions due to a lambda sensor defect. Reprogramming or replacement.
  • February 2019 (Airbags): Possible improper deployment of curtain airbags in the event of a side impact. Concerns a small series produced in 2018.
  • March 2017 (Electronics): Reprogramming of the injection computer on the first 1.6 dCi to avoid a risk of untimely stalling.

What sets it apart 7

Comfort

  • A Cocoon of Comfort and Space — Enjoy exceptional soundproofing and high-level suspension comfort, complemented by generous rear passenger space offering limousine-like legroom.
  • Statutory and Refined Interior — The Koleos II's interior stands out with its statutory presentation and quality materials, offering a refined and welcoming ambiance, especially in the high-end trims.

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability of Petrol Engines — The petrol engines, notably the modern 1.3 TCe and the robust Nissan atmospheric blocks, are renowned for their excellent reliability and durability, ensuring long-term peace of mind.

Safety

  • Maximum Safety for the Whole Family — The Renault Koleos II achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 79% for child protection, guaranteeing optimal safety.
  • Serene Road Handling and Effective Braking — Its road behavior is clearly comfort-oriented, offering safe and predictable handling with excellent stability. The braking is effective and reassuring, ideal for family use.

Performance

  • All-Terrain Capabilities for Adventure — For adventure enthusiasts, versions equipped with the All Mode 4x4-i system offer very capable off-road performance, allowing you to explore new horizons with confidence.

Economy

  • Excellent Value for Money — The Renault Koleos II represents an exceptional opportunity in the used car market, offering an unbeatable price/performance/comfort ratio compared to its competitors, making it a smart investment.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 5008 (more dynamic, 7 seats, but problematic PureTech petrol engines), Skoda Kodiaq (more spacious, very rational), Volkswagen Tiguan Allspace.
  • Japan / Asia: Nissan X-Trail T32 (its technical twin, available with 7 seats), Honda CR-V (benchmark for reliability and space), Mazda CX-5 (superior finish, reliable naturally aspirated engines).
  • Americas / Oceania: Toyota RAV4 (essential hybrid), Subaru Forester (best 4x4 system), Hyundai Tucson / Kia Sportage.

Verdict

The Renault Koleos II is an excellent used car deal if you target the right engines. Often depreciated compared to its European competitors, it offers an unbeatable price/performance/comfort ratio. For petrol, the 1.3 TCe (Europe) is a very relevant choice, combining modernity and reliability. In international markets, the 2.0 and 2.5 naturally aspirated blocks are indestructible, on the *sine qua non* condition that the CVT gearbox has been regularly drained. Diesel versions should be approached with more caution, particularly the Blue dCi (1.7 and 2.0) whose AdBlue system is a source of costly breakdowns. Opt for a facelifted model (post-2019) to benefit from a more reliable multimedia system and improved finish.
