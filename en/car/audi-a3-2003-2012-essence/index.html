Audi A3

8P · 2003 – 2012

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The second-generation Audi A3 (Type 8P) is based on the Volkswagen Group's PQ35 platform (shared with the Golf V and VI). A true pioneer of modern premium compacts, it was available as a 3-door, Sportback (5-door), and Cabriolet. Its exceptionally long career was marked by two major facelifts (2005 with the adoption of the Singleframe grille, and 2008 with the integration of LED daytime running lights and a mechanical overhaul).

Mechanically, the 8P generation represents a major technological transition period: a shift from indirect injection (MPI) to direct injection (FSI), then to turbocharging (TFSI) for gasoline engines; and a shift from unit injectors (IP) to common rail (Common Rail) for diesel engines. This complexity makes the used car market very heterogeneous: one can find indestructible engines alongside powertrains with ruinous design flaws (notably the first EA888 blocks and the porous cylinder heads of the early 2.0 TDI).

Strengths 5

  • Interior finish and material quality (ages exceptionally well)
  • Sound and safe road handling (especially with Quattro)
  • Ergonomics of controls and driving position
  • Impressive variety of engines and body styles
  • Exemplary reliability of the 1.6 MPI and Common Rail TDI (after 2008)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of 1.8 and 2.0 TFSI (EA888) gasoline engines from 2008 to 2012
  • Cylinder head problems on early 2.0 TDI 140 (2003-2006)
  • Very fragile S-Tronic 7 (DQ200) gearbox (juddering, premature wear)
  • Firm comfort, especially in S-Line trim (sport chassis + 18-inch wheels)
  • Maintenance and spare parts cost (premium pricing)

Check before buying

  • TFSI Gasoline (EA888): Demand invoices proving that the oil consumption problem has been addressed (piston/segment replacement).
  • TFSI Gasoline (EA113 - 200hp/S3): Check the invoice for the replacement of the high-pressure pump tappet and the timing belt.
  • Automatic gearbox: Demand the service history for DSG6 gearbox oil changes (every 60,000 km). Test the DSG7 at low speed (no jerks).
  • TDI IP Diesel (2003-2007): Check the coolant level (drop = porous cylinder head) and absence of idling tremors (flywheel).
  • Electronics: Functioning of air conditioning (fragile compressor), power windows, and ESP warning light (G201 sensor in ABS unit).

Official recalls

  • - **2009 - DSG (S-Tronic) Gearbox**: Mechatronic unit reprogramming due to power loss or false neutrals.
  • - **2011 - Injectors (2.0 TDI 170 BMN)**: Free replacement of Siemens piezoelectric injectors prone to short circuits.
  • - **2011 - Ignition Coils (TFSI)**: Replacement of defective coils on 1.8T and 2.0T engines.
  • - **2013 - DSG7 (DQ200) Gearbox**: Replacement of synthetic gearbox oil with mineral oil to prevent short circuits in the mechatronics.
  • - **2017 - ABS/ESP Unit**: Software update or replacement due to failure of the G201 brake pressure sensor (ESP warning light permanently on).

Verdict

The Audi A3 8P is an excellent premium compact, but buying it used is like navigating a mechanical minefield, especially for gasoline versions.

**If you're looking for a gasoline engine:** Avoid the 1.8 TFSI and 2.0 TFSI (EA888) produced between 2008 and 2012, unless the engine has been completely rebuilt with an invoice (segmentation issue). Prioritize the old but robust 1.6 MPI 102 hp for city driving, or the 2.0 TFSI 200 hp (EA113, belt-driven) from 2005-2008, provided its specific maintenance (HP tappet) is up to date. The 1.4 TFSI are good compromises if the chain has been replaced.

**If you're looking for a diesel engine:** Avoid the 2.0 TDI from 2003 to 2007 (cylinder heads, injectors). Absolutely target a model from after 2008 equipped with the 2.0 TDI Common Rail (140 or 170 hp), which offers an excellent performance/reliability/consumption ratio.
