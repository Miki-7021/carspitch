Suzuki Swift

III (ZC/ZD) · 2010 – 2017

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Suzuki models

Summary

The third-generation Suzuki Swift (ZC72S / ZD72S chassis for 4x4 versions, and ZC32S for the Sport) is a versatile city car that builds on the success of its predecessor. Launched in 2010, it retains a familiar silhouette but grows slightly (+9 cm) to improve interior space and safety (5 Euro NCAP stars). It stands out with a very dynamic chassis, precise steering, and contained weight, making it one of the most fun-to-drive city cars in its category. A slight facelift occurred in late 2013, bringing LED daytime running lights integrated into the bumper and turn signals in the mirrors on higher trims. Overall, it's a model renowned for its excellent reliability, particularly with petrol engines, although its boot (211 litres) is one of the smallest in the segment.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of petrol engines (K12B, M16A)
  • Dynamic and fun road handling (especially the Sport version)
  • Very reasonable maintenance and insurance costs
  • Chain timing on all engines
  • Availability of genuine all-wheel drive (AllGrip/4x4) rare in this segment

Weaknesses 5

  • Ridiculous boot volume for the category (211 litres)
  • Quality of interior plastics (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Limited rear passenger space for tall occupants
  • Variable reliability of the 1.3 DDiS diesel engine in urban use

Check before buying

  • Manual gearbox bearing noises (whistling at idle or in lower gears).
  • Condition of the water pump on 1.2 VVT engines (traces of pink/blue fluid around the accessory belt).
  • DPF and EGR valve maintenance history on 1.3 DDiS diesel versions.
  • Irregular front tire wear (geometry sensitive to potholes).
  • Air conditioning operation (the condenser is very exposed to gravel at the front).

Official recalls

  • February 2015: Risk of water pump pulley bolts loosening. Affects models built between September 2013 and October 2014.
  • May 2016: On Swift Sport (ZC32S) versions, possible malfunction of the rear brake calipers (fluid leak or handbrake blockage). Models produced between 2012 and 2015.
  • November 2012: Replacement of front shock absorbers on a small series due to a factory welding defect.
  • Takata Airbags: Depending on the markets (notably Asia and Oceania), recall for the replacement of the passenger airbag inflator.

Verdict

The Suzuki Swift III is a master-buy on the used car market for anyone looking for a reliable, economical, and fun-to-drive city car. It is imperative to prioritize petrol engines, with the 1.2 VVT being a model of robustness, while the 1.6 VVT of the Swift Sport offers an almost unbeatable price/pleasure/reliability ratio. The 1.3 DDiS diesel should be avoided unless you exclusively drive on the open road, as it suffers from the classic ailments of modern diesels in urban use. The only real deterrent to purchase remains its tiny boot and basic interior plastics. If space is not your absolute priority, this is a highly recommended choice.
