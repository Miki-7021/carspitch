KGM Rexton

II (Y220) · 2006 – 2017

NEGOTIATE Reliability 74/100 — bon

Summary

The **KGM / SsangYong Rexton II (Y220)**, which also encompasses the major facelift named **Rexton W (Y290)** from 2012 onwards, is a family SUV designed as a true off-roader. Built on a robust ladder frame chassis, it stands out for its use of technologies licensed from Mercedes-Benz (engines and gearboxes). Although the European market was flooded with diesel engines, the **petrol** versions (which are the focus of our review) are particularly sought after by connoisseurs for their exceptional reliability, inherited from the Mercedes blocks of the 90s. It is a heavy, rustic vehicle, an excellent tow car, but its road handling remains clumsy compared to modern unibody SUVs. Overall rating: an excellent used choice for towing or off-roading on a budget, provided you can accept the gargantuan fuel consumption of the petrol engines.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional reliability of the petrol engines (Mercedes-Benz M104 and M111 architecture)
  • Real off-road capabilities (ladder frame chassis, low-range gearbox, solid rear axle on some versions)
  • Impressive towing capacity (up to 3,200 kg)
  • Generous interior space (available in 7 seats)
  • 5-speed automatic gearbox (T-Tronic / MB 722.6) extremely robust if the fluid is changed
  • Unbeatable price/performance ratio on the used market

Weaknesses 6

  • Astronomical fuel consumption on the 3.2L petrol (difficult to drop below 13L/100km)
  • Dated road handling (body roll, vague steering, barely adequate braking)
  • Quality of interior plastics and assembly could be improved (rattles and squeaks)
  • Sensitivity to corrosion on the ladder frame chassis (mountain or seaside vehicles)
  • Sparse KGM/SsangYong dealer network for specific parts
  • Disastrous reliability of the Delphi injection system on the 2.7L diesels

Check before buying

  • Corrosion: Carefully inspect the side members of the ladder frame chassis and the wheel arches.
  • 4x4 transmission: Test the engagement of 4H and 4L modes. The transfer case electric motor tends to seize if not used regularly. Check the vacuum hubs.
  • Automatic gearbox: Demand the automatic gearbox fluid change invoices (every 60,000 km). Jerks often indicate a tired control plate (5-speed automatic).
  • 3.2 Petrol engine: Check for oil leaks at the cylinder head gasket (known issue of the M104 at the rear of the block) and the rocker cover.
  • Running gear: Wear of the front suspension ball joints and silent blocks (the vehicle is very heavy).

Performance

  • Towing Power and Off-Road Adventure — The Rexton II petrol is a true towing champion, capable of pulling up to 3,200 kg. Its robust ladder chassis and low-range gearbox also give it real off-road capabilities for all your adventures.

comfort

  • Smooth and Quiet Driving — Appreciate the smoothness and quiet operation of the petrol engines, inherited from Mercedes-Benz technology, which provide a pleasant and refined driving experience, even on long journeys.

economy

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Kia Sorento I and II (more road-oriented), Hyundai Santa Fe, Mitsubishi Pajero (more expensive but a better off-roader), Nissan Pathfinder. - **Japan / Asia**: Toyota Land Cruiser Prado (the absolute benchmark, but 3x more expensive used), Isuzu MU-X. - **North America**: The Rexton was not officially sold there. - **Emerging markets**: Ford Everest, Toyota Fortuner, Chevrolet Trailblazer.

Verdict

The SsangYong Rexton II is a paradox. If you opt for the 2.7L diesel engines (the most common), the risk of costly breakdowns (injectors) is high. On the other hand, **if you find a rare 3.2L petrol version**, you are buying a vehicle with almost infallible mechanical reliability, powered by the heart of a 90s Mercedes S-Class. It is the perfect choice for towing a horse trailer or a boat over short distances, or for a bioethanol (E85) / LPG conversion to counter its gargantuan fuel consumption. Purchase is recommended provided you rigorously inspect the chassis for rust and ensure that the 4x4 transfer case works.
