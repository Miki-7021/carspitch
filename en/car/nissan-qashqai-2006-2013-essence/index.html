Nissan Qashqai

I (J10) · 2006 – 2013

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

All Nissan models

Summary

The first-generation Nissan Qashqai (J10) is considered the pioneer of modern compact crossovers. Launched in 2006 (2007 model year), it replaced both the Almera and Primera in Europe, offering the raised driving position of an SUV with the running costs of a compact sedan. A major facelift (Phase 2) occurred in spring 2010, bringing a modernized front end, improved sound insulation, and recalibrated suspension. An extended 7-seater version (Qashqai+2, code NJ10) was also marketed. Although European sales were dominated by diesel, the petrol engines (which are the main subject of this report) are now proving to be the most sensible choices on the used market thanks to their excellent long-term reliability and timing chain.

Strengths 6

  • Remarkable reliability of petrol engines (1.6 and 2.0) with timing chain
  • Raised driving position and general ergonomics
  • Suspension comfort (especially after the 2010 facelift)
  • Reasonable maintenance cost for petrol versions with manual gearbox
  • Availability of the Qashqai+2 (7-seater) version, very practical for families
  • Safe road handling (close to a sedan)

Weaknesses 6

  • Quality of interior plastics and assembly on Phase 1 (2006-2009)
  • Imperfect sound insulation at high speed (wind and road noise before 2010)
  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.5 dCi before 2010 (connecting rod bearings)
  • Fragile Jatco CVT gearbox if maintenance has not been scrupulously followed
  • Very fragile tailgate handle (plastic breaks at the fixings)
  • Premature wear of rear shock absorbers and front axle bushings

Check before buying

  • CVT gearbox maintenance history: demand oil change invoices (every 60,000 km). Flee if the gearbox 'whistles' or jerks.
  • Dull thuds over speed bumps: indicates wear of front wishbone bushings or rear shock absorbers.
  • Air conditioning operation: the condenser is very exposed to gravel (frequent leaks) and the compressor can seize.
  • Boot handle: check that it doesn't move and that the plastic isn't cracked.
  • Oil level on the 2.0 petrol: check for absence of blue smoke from the exhaust (sign of worn piston rings on neglected models).
  • Steering wheel condition: the leather/leatherette covering tends to peel quickly.

Official recalls

  • September 2012 (Steering Wheel): Risk of steering wheel hub detachment due to casting weakness. Concerns models produced between February and May 2012.
  • April 2012 (Fuel): Possible leak at the fuel filter connector (dCi engines). Models produced from October 2011 to January 2012.
  • December 2009 (Steering): Possible loss of power steering due to a defect in the electric motor of the steering rack. Models produced between August 2006 and July 2008.
  • November 2008 (DPF): On the 2.0 dCi, risk of particulate filter overheating which could lead to a thermal insulator fire.

Verdict

The Nissan Qashqai I (J10) with a petrol engine is an excellent used car choice. Neglected at the time in favor of diesel, Nissan's atmospheric 1.6 and 2.0 engines now prove their exceptional robustness (timing chain, no turbo, no DPF). The ideal choice is a Phase 2 model (after spring 2010) with a 1.6 or 2.0 engine and manual gearbox. If you opt for an automatic gearbox (CVT) version, the transmission fluid change history is the absolute buying criterion. Diesel versions (especially the 1.5 dCi before 2010) are to be avoided unless there is a clear service history and exclusively highway use.
