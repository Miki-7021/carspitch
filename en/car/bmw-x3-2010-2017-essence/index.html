Summary

The second-generation BMW X3 (F25) marks a major break from its predecessor (E83). Larger, more comfortable, and technologically more advanced, it moved from Austrian assembly (Magna Steyr) to the Spartanburg plant in the United States. It shares many components with the 3 Series F30 and 5 Series F10.

A facelift (LCI) occurred in spring 2014, bringing aesthetic updates (headlights joining the grille), an improved interior finish, and, most importantly, the introduction of the new generation of diesel engines (B47) replacing the problematic N47.

Although this report focuses on petrol engines, which are highly sought after today due to traffic restrictions, the F25 was predominantly sold with diesel engines in Europe. The petrol units (N20, N52, N55) offer excellent driving pleasure but require increased vigilance on certain critical points (timing chain on the 4-cylinder, ancillaries on the 6-cylinder).

Strengths 6

  • Dynamic road handling, worthy of a sedan
  • Significantly improved finish quality (especially after the 2014 facelift)
  • Generous interior space and trunk volume (550 liters)
  • Very intuitive and responsive iDrive infotainment system
  • Exceptional driving pleasure from 6-cylinder engines (petrol and diesel)
  • Excellent ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (smooth and fast)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the timing chain on 4-cylinder engines (N20 petrol and N47 diesel) before 2015
  • Extreme sensitivity of the xDrive transfer case (tire wear)
  • Somewhat firm comfort, especially with large-diameter wheels and Runflat tires
  • Very high maintenance and spare parts cost
  • Sound insulation of 4-cylinder diesel engines (N47) before facelift

Check before buying

  • Transfer case (xDrive): Test drive the vehicle at low speed with full steering lock. Any jerking or scrubbing indicates a worn transfer case (often due to tires with different wear levels or non-BMW star-marked tires).
  • Timing chain (N20/N47): Cold start with the hood open. A metallic rattling noise or a high-pitched whistle (N20) should be a red flag.
  • LED taillights: Valeo circuit boards tend to burn out (flickering or complete extinction of LEDs on the tailgate).
  • Steering rack: Clunking felt in the steering wheel when driving on degraded roads or over speed bumps (thrust bushing issue).
  • Automatic gearbox maintenance: BMW states 'lubricated for life,' but manufacturer ZF recommends an oil change between 80,000 and 100,000 km.

Official recalls

- EGR cooler (2018-2023): Massive recall on diesel engines (4 and 6 cylinders). Coolant leak into the EGR which can create a flammable mixture with soot (fire risk).,- VANOS timing bolts (2014): On 6-cylinder petrol engines (N55), the bolts of the variable timing system can loosen or break, leading to power loss or engine damage.,- Brake vacuum pump (2013): On N20 engines (20i, 28i), loss of braking assistance due to a lubrication defect in the vacuum pump.,- ISOFIX anchors (2016): The rear ISOFIX anchor brackets can break due to vibrations (concerns 2010-2016 models).

Handling

  • Dynamic Driving Dynamics — Enjoy exceptional road holding for an SUV, with precise steering and perfectly controlled body roll, offering an engaging and secure driving experience.

Braking

  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — The X3 F25 benefits from excellent braking performance, powerful and enduring, with short stopping distances that inspire confidence in all circumstances.

Performance

  • Refined and Powerful Petrol Engines — The petrol engines, notably the 6-cylinders (N55, N52) for their exceptional smoothness and the 4-cylinders (N20) for their flexibility and torque, deliver high-level performance.
  • Smooth ZF 8-Speed Automatic Transmission — The excellent ZF 8-speed automatic transmission ensures smooth and rapid gear changes, contributing to superior driving pleasure and optimized efficiency.

Practicality

  • Generous Cabin Space and Large Boot — With its generous cabin space and a boot volume of 550 liters, the X3 F25 offers comfortable room for all passengers and a large loading capacity.

Comfort

  • High-Quality Finish and Intuitive Technology — The interior finish quality is significantly improved, especially after the 2014 facelift, complemented by a very intuitive and responsive iDrive infotainment system.

Verdict

The BMW X3 F25 is an excellent premium SUV that offers an ideal compromise between dynamism and practicality. However, with the 4-cylinder petrol engine (20i, 28i - N20 engine), it requires extreme caution: only buy a model from before March 2015 if the timing chain and oil pump have been replaced with an invoice. The 6-cylinder petrol engine (35i) is much more refined but requires a significant budget for ancillaries (water pump, seals). If you're looking for peace of mind with a petrol engine, aim for a 2016-2017 20i/28i model. For high-mileage drivers, a post-2014 diesel (B47 or N57 engine) remains a formidable choice, provided the EGR recall has been performed. Finally, never neglect the condition of the tires to preserve the fragile xDrive transfer case.
