BMW Série 3

G20 · 2018 – 2025

BUY Reliability 85/100 — excellent

Summary

The seventh-generation BMW 3 Series (G20 for the sedan, G21 for the Touring wagon, G28 for the long-wheelbase version in Asia) marks a return to its roots in driving dynamics compared to the previous F30. Based on the modular CLAR platform, it offers increased rigidity, top-notch sound insulation, and an interior finish that places it at the top of its class.

The mid-2022 facelift (LCI) modernized the front end and revolutionized the interior with the integration of the large curved dual screen (BMW Curved Display) running iDrive 8.

**Diesel Focus:** Although the market is shifting towards hybrids, the 3 Series G20 offers some of the best diesel engines in the world (B47 and B57 families). The introduction of 48V mild-hybrid (MHEV) technology in 2020 further optimized their refinement (imperceptible Stop & Start) and fuel efficiency, making these versions the undisputed champions for high-mileage drivers.

Strengths 5

  • Diesel engines (B47/B57) among the most accomplished, reliable, and fuel-efficient on the market
  • Exceptional road handling, perfect comfort/dynamism compromise
  • Significantly improved interior finish quality compared to the F30
  • Very refined sound insulation (acoustic glazing often present)
  • Magistral ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (BVA8) on all versions

Weaknesses 4

  • Average rear passenger space and trunk volume for its size (especially on PHEVs, which lose 105 liters)
  • Ergonomics of the all-touch interface on facelifted versions (LCI with iDrive 8)
  • Very high used car prices, numerous and expensive options
  • M Sport suspension sometimes too firm at low speeds on degraded roads

Check before buying

  • Recall history: The EGR valve cooler (Diesels) must imperatively have been checked or replaced.
  • Coolant level: A drop in diesel or gasoline engines (B48/B58) indicates a leak (EGR, water pump, or hose).
  • Running gear: Clunking noises in the steering (fragile tie rods on early 2018-2019 models).
  • Electronics: Sunroof operation (wind noise/creaks) and infotainment system stability.
  • Tire wear: xDrive versions require tires with even wear (BMW star-marked tires recommended) to avoid damaging the transfer case.

Official recalls

  • - **EGR Cooler (2019-2023):** Major recall (codes 0011970600, 0011360700, etc.) for risk of glycol leakage into the intake, potentially causing a fire. Affects almost all 4 and 6-cylinder diesels.
  • - **High-Voltage Battery (2020):** Risk of short-circuit due to welding impurities in battery cells on 330e models produced in 2020.
  • - **Tie Rods (2019):** Replacement of tie rods on models manufactured until 2019, risk of breakage under high stress.
  • - **Brake Assist (2021):** Software update or replacement of the vacuum pump following a possible loss of brake assist at startup.
  • - **Seat Belts (2021):** Defective seat belt retractor on a small series of vehicles.

Reliability

  • Unrivaled Reliability and Diesel Engine Excellence — BMW's diesel engines (B47/B57) are engineering masterpieces, renowned as some of the most refined and robust on the market. Their design ensures exceptional reliability for high-mileage drivers.

Performance

  • Unparalleled Driving Dynamics — The BMW 3 Series G20 sets the benchmark in its category for road handling. It offers exceptional dynamism, precise steering, and perfect balance, providing an incomparable driving pleasure.

Safety

  • Top-Tier Passive Safety — With exemplary occupant protection, the BMW 3 Series G20 achieved the maximum rating in safety tests, ensuring peace of mind for all passengers.
  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — The 3 Series G20's braking system is remarkably efficient, offering very short stopping distances and a progressive pedal feel, ensuring optimal safety in all conditions.

Comfort

  • Superior Interior Comfort and Refinement — The G20's cabin stands out with significantly improved finish quality, meticulous sound insulation, and the integrated BMW Curved Display. The 8-speed ZF automatic transmission ensures a supremely smooth driving experience.

Economy

  • Exceptional Efficiency and Fuel Economy — The 3 Series G20's diesel powertrains, optimized by 48V mild-hybrid technology, offer remarkably low fuel consumption and an unbeatable performance-to-consumption ratio, making it the ideal choice for long journeys.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Audi A4 (B9) (excellent finish, reliable TDI diesels but less dynamic handling), Mercedes C-Class (W206) (very technological, superior comfort, but noisier diesels), Alfa Romeo Giulia (equivalent dynamism, but infotainment and finish are lagging). - **North America:** Lexus IS (legendary reliability, naturally aspirated V6, but dated technology), Genesis G70 (excellent price/equipment ratio, long warranty), Acura TLX. - **Asia / Emerging Markets:** Mercedes C-Class L (long-wheelbase version), Audi A4L, Lexus ES.

Verdict

The BMW 3 Series G20 is undoubtedly one of the best premium family sedans of its generation. If you are looking for a vehicle to cover long distances, **the diesel versions (320d and 330d) are engineering masterpieces**, offering an unbeatable performance/consumption ratio. Basic mechanical reliability is excellent, provided that the EGR cooler recall has been carried out. The gasoline versions (320i, 330i) and the fabulous M340i are equally recommendable. The plug-in hybrid versions (320e/330e) are fiscally relevant but require more vigilance regarding their electrical history.
