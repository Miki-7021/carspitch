Toyota Prius

III (XW30) · 2009 – 2015

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The **Toyota Prius III (XW30)** is the third generation of the hybrid vehicle pioneer. Launched in 2009, it marks a major evolution compared to the previous generation by adopting a larger internal combustion engine (1.8L instead of 1.5L) to improve highway efficiency and reduce engine speed. It retains its iconic aerodynamic silhouette (Cd of 0.25) but deeply modernizes its interior and equipment. A facelift occurred in 2012, bringing increased body rigidity, recalibrated suspensions, improved sound insulation, and LED daytime running lights. This generation also introduced the first mass-produced plug-in hybrid version in 2012. A true workhorse favored by taxis, it is renowned for its exceptional longevity, although it presents some specific weaknesses to monitor after 150,000 km.

Strengths 6

  • Overall mechanical reliability and exceptional longevity (often > 300,000 km)
  • Very low fuel consumption, especially in urban environments
  • Reduced routine maintenance cost (no clutch, no timing belt, no conventional starter)
  • Extremely slow brake wear thanks to regenerative braking
  • Generous interior space and rear passenger room
  • Smooth operation of the e-CVT transmission in the city

Weaknesses 5

  • Engine revving effect (whining) during strong accelerations or in the mountains
  • Quality of interior plastics (hard and prone to scratches)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (road and wind noise), especially before the 2012 facelift
  • Very frequent theft of catalytic converters (easy to access and rich in precious metals)
  • Prohibitive cost of certain specific parts (inverter, brake accumulator, HT battery)

Check before buying

  • High-voltage battery status (perform a test with the Hybrid Assistant app or request a recent Toyota hybrid health check).
  • Brake accumulator noise (ABS pump): if it activates every 10 seconds when stopped, the system is faulty (repair > €2,000).
  • EGR system cleaning history: clogging causes jerks on cold start and can destroy the cylinder head gasket.
  • Engine oil level (especially on 2009-2011 models) to detect worn piston rings.
  • Presence of moisture traces in the trunk (near the 12V battery) due to faulty taillight seals.

Economy

  • Controlled Fuel Consumption and Maintenance Costs — Enjoy very low fuel consumption, especially in urban areas, and reduced maintenance costs thanks to the absence of a clutch, timing belt, and minimal brake wear due to the regenerative system.

Comfort

  • Smooth Driving and Generous Cabin Space — Experience a fluid and quiet drive, especially in urban environments, thanks to its e-CVT transmission. Its spacious cabin offers optimal comfort for all passengers and their luggage.

Safety

  • High-Level Passive Safety — The Toyota Prius III has been praised for its occupant protection, achieving top scores in crash tests, ensuring peace of mind for you and your family.

Braking

  • Efficient and Reliable Braking — The Prius III's braking system ensures competitive stopping distances and high reliability, enhanced by regenerative braking which also contributes to component longevity.

Handling

  • Safe and Predictable Road Handling — Designed for comfort and efficiency, the Prius III offers stable and secure road holding, ideal for daily commutes and long journeys, with light steering and a serene drive.

Practicality

  • Aerodynamic Design and Optimized Space — Its iconic silhouette is not just aesthetic; it contributes to excellent aerodynamics (Cd of 0.25) and optimizes interior space, offering generous volume for passengers and their luggage.

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Toyota Auris HSD (same mechanics, compact format), Lexus CT200h (same mechanics, premium finish), Honda Insight (mild hybrid, less efficient). - **North America:** Chevrolet Volt (PHEV with range extender), Ford C-Max Hybrid, Honda Civic Hybrid. - **Japan / Asia:** Honda Fit Hybrid, Toyota Aqua (smaller).

Verdict

The Toyota Prius III is a **top buy** on the used car market for anyone looking for an economical, spacious, and remarkably reliable vehicle. However, its status as an indestructible car should not make one forget that it requires specific maintenance. Models approaching or exceeding 200,000 km require increased vigilance regarding the high-voltage battery, the brake accumulator, and the EGR circuit. **Prioritize facelifted models (post-2012)** which correct oil consumption issues, offer better sound insulation, and benefit from a slightly superior finish. The installation of a protective plate for the catalytic converter is highly recommended.
