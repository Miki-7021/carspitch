Summary

The Hyundai i40 (VF generation) was developed at Hyundai's European R&D center in Rüsselsheim, Germany, specifically to compete with the leaders of the D-segment in Europe (VW Passat, Peugeot 508). Initially launched as a highly acclaimed station wagon (CrossWagon / CW), then as a sedan, it stands out with its "Fluidic Sculpture" design, generous interior space, and a very rich equipment level for its time. Although the European market overwhelmingly opted for diesel (1.7 CRDi), the gasoline engines (GDI and MPI) offer an excellent alternative, often more reliable in the long term, particularly for low-mileage drivers or urban trips. The 5-year unlimited mileage manufacturer warranty allowed the majority of models from this period to benefit from rigorous maintenance within the official network.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent interior space, particularly in the rear seats
  • Generous trunk volume on the station wagon (CW) version
  • Overall reliability of gasoline engines (timing chain)
  • Very rich standard equipment (heated/ventilated seats, heated steering wheel depending on trim levels)
  • Careful soundproofing on gasoline versions
  • Often clear maintenance history thanks to the initial 5-year warranty

Weaknesses 5

  • Lack of low-end torque on the 1.6 GDI (requires downshifting)
  • Electric power steering lacking feel and sometimes vague on-center
  • Slightly firm damping at low speeds, jittery ride on degraded roads
  • Poor rear visibility (rearview camera is essential)
  • Quality of some interior plastics (center console scratches easily)

Check before buying

  • Steering column: Clunking or play felt in the steering wheel when stationary or at low speeds (wear of the flexible coupling / star flector of the electric power steering, inexpensive repair if properly identified).
  • Front lighting: Flickering or failure of the LED daytime running lights (DRL). Replacing the entire headlight assembly is very expensive.
  • On-board electronics: Bugs in the GPS/multimedia touchscreen, malfunction of the rearview camera or parking sensors.
  • Manual gearbox: Catching on 1st and 2nd gears when cold (wear of synchros or linkage needs adjustment).
  • Clutch (CRDi): Vibrations at the biting point indicating fatigue of the dual-mass flywheel.

Verdict

The first-generation Hyundai i40 is an extremely rational choice on the used market. By specifically targeting the gasoline engines (1.6 GDI or 2.0 GDI), you avoid the classic pitfalls of modern diesels (DPF, EGR, flywheel) while benefiting from a spacious, over-equipped, and overall very reliable car. The 1.6 GDI is perfect for quiet daily use, although it needs to be pushed hard when loaded. The main black mark remains the fragility of the LED daytime running lights and the steering flector, known defects that are identifiable during a test drive. It is a highly recommended purchase in gasoline for families looking for an affordable and trouble-free station wagon (CW).
