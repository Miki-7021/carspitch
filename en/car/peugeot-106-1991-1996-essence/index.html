Summary

The Peugeot 106 Phase 1 (Generation I - 1A/1C) was launched in September 1991 to modernize Peugeot's entry-level range, coexisting for a time with the legendary 205. Based on the Citroën AX platform, it distinguished itself with better soundproofing, a superior finish, and typical Peugeot road handling (agile and precise). This period (1991-1996) was marked by a major technological transition: the mandatory shift from carburetors to electronic injection (single-point or multi-point) and the addition of catalytic converters in 1993 (Euro 1 standard). Although light (often less than 850 kg) and very economical, it now suffers from its age, particularly in terms of passive safety and corrosion. Today, it is an excellent choice as a *youngtimer* or as a small economical utility vehicle, provided its chronic weaknesses (rear axle, cylinder head gasket) are monitored.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent road handling (agility, precise front axle)
  • Negligible maintenance cost and great mechanical accessibility
  • Very lively and willing TU gasoline engines
  • Very low fuel consumption (especially 1.5D and 1.1i)
  • Suspension comfort (typical Peugeot of the era)
  • Collector potential (Youngtimer) for XSI and Rallye versions

Weaknesses 6

  • Chronic rear axle issue (needle bearings wear out, wheels in /-\)
  • Endemic cylinder head gasket weakness on TU engines (oil leak at the front right)
  • Passive safety from another era (poor crash test, absence of airbags/ABS on most models)
  • Interior plastic quality (squeaks, cracks, seat wear)
  • Susceptibility to corrosion (wheel arches, sills, boot floor)
  • Unreliable 1.4 Diesel (TUD3) engine (overheating)

Check before buying

  • Rear axle: Check the inclination of the rear wheels (excessive negative camber) and listen for squeaks over speed bumps.
  • Engine (TU): Inspect the front right corner of the engine block (timing/alternator side) for oil seepage (sign of a worn cylinder head gasket).
  • Corrosion: Inspect the rear wheel arches, boot floor, sills, and jack points.
  • Cooling: Check the radiator condition, the color of the coolant (often neglected, leading to rust in the system), and the activation of the cooling fan.
  • Interior: Heater operation (leaky heater matrix = wet carpet), condition of the stalks and central locking.

Official recalls

Due to the age of the vehicle (over 30 years for the first models), official recall campaigns have long been closed. Historically, recalls concerned: - 1992-1993: Replacement of front brake hoses (risk of rubbing). - 1994: Steering column inspection (risk of play). - 1995: Seat belt upgrade on certain series. *Note: Today, the condition of a 106 depends 100% on the maintenance provided by its successive owners.*

What sets it apart 6

Performance

  • Lively and Responsive Petrol Engines — The TU petrol engines of the Peugeot 106 are renowned for their liveliness and responsiveness, offering a dynamic and enjoyable driving experience, both in the city and on the open road.

Economy

  • Controlled Fuel Consumption — Thanks to its lightness and the efficiency of its petrol engines, particularly the 1.1i, the 106 Phase 1 boasts very low fuel consumption, ideal for a tight budget.

Comfort

  • Typically Peugeot Suspension Comfort — The 106 benefits from Peugeot's chassis expertise, offering remarkable suspension comfort that effectively filters out road imperfections, even on long journeys.

Reliability

  • Simple and Accessible Mechanical Design — The simple design of the TU petrol engines makes the 106 particularly easy to maintain, ensuring great mechanical accessibility and appreciable robustness for daily use.

Handling

  • Agile and Precise Road Handling — Inheriting Peugeot's agility, the 106 Phase 1 offers excellent road handling with a precise front axle and great maneuverability, providing genuine driving pleasure.

Practicality

  • A Youngtimer with Collector's Potential — Sporty versions like the XSI and Rallye, as well as more common models, are increasingly sought after by enthusiasts, making the 106 an excellent Youngtimer investment.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Renault Clio I (more comfortable, better finished), Citroën AX (technical cousin, lighter but inferior finish), Ford Fiesta Mk3, Volkswagen Polo II/III, Fiat Punto I.
  • Japan/Asia: Nissan Micra K11 (very reliable, timing chain), Suzuki Swift.
  • North America: Model not marketed.
  • Emerging markets: Chevrolet Corsa, Fiat Uno.

Verdict

The Peugeot 106 Phase 1 is an excellent entry point into the world of *youngtimers* or an ultra-economical mobility solution for small budgets. The gasoline (TU) engines are brilliant, easy to repair, and inexpensive for parts. However, it is imperative to NEGOTIATE the price if the rear axle shows signs of wear or if the cylinder head gasket is leaking, as these repairs can exceed the car's market value. Prioritize injection models (post-1993) in 1.1i or 1.4i for daily use, and avoid the 1.4 Diesel in favor of the 1.5 Diesel if you are looking for an indestructible utility vehicle.
