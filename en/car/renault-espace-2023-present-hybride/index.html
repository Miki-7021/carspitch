Summary

Renault Espace VI (SUV)

*Note: Although the request mentions generation V, the years 2023-2025 and the SUV body style correspond to the **6th generation (Espace VI)**. The Espace V (2015-2023) was a crossover/minivan.*

The Espace VI marks a definitive break with the minivan era. Based on the Alliance's CMF-CD platform, it is technically an extended Renault Austral (+21 cm), designed to offer 5 or 7 seats. This positioning as a large family SUV places it in direct competition with the Peugeot 5008. It stands out for its OpenR Link multimedia system (based on Google), its surprising agility thanks to the *4Control Advanced* four-wheel steering system (optional or standard depending on trim), and a unique, highly targeted engine strategy. Being a very recent model, long-term mechanical reliability data is limited, but initial feedback helps identify early defects, mainly software-related.

Strengths 5

  • Remarkably low fuel consumption for its size (highly efficient hybrid system in urban and road driving)
  • Ultra-smooth and ergonomic OpenR Link infotainment system (Google Automotive)
  • Exceptional agility and turning radius with the 4Control Advanced system
  • 2nd-row spaciousness (sliding bench seat over 22 cm)
  • Significantly improved interior finish compared to previous generations

Weaknesses 5

  • Lack of engine choices (no diesel for very high mileage drivers, no PHEV)
  • Very cramped 3rd-row seats (reserved for children or occasional use)
  • Suspension comfort a bit firm, especially with 20-inch wheels
  • Dog-clutch gearbox sometimes hesitant or generating jerks in dynamic driving
  • Noticeable wind noise on the highway above 110 km/h

Check before buying

  • Software update history (crucial for gearbox and hybridization management)
  • Operation of the OpenR Link central screen (check for absence of unexpected restarts or black screens)
  • Rear tire wear (the 4Control system, if poorly adjusted, can accelerate asymmetrical wear)
  • Smoothness of gear changes between electric and thermal modes during a road test

Alternatives

Alternatives by Market * **Europe:** Peugeot 5008 (its direct rival, available in hybrid, PHEV, and electric), Nissan X-Trail (e-Power, technical cousin), Skoda Kodiaq (more spacious in the 3rd row), Volkswagen Tayron. * **Asia / Japan:** Toyota Highlander (larger), Honda CR-V (highly developed hybrid). * **North America:** The Espace is not sold there. Alternatives: Kia Sorento, Hyundai Santa Fe. * **Emerging Markets:** Often replaced by specific models or more rugged SUVs (e.g., Renault Koleos at the end of its life in some countries).

Verdict

The purchase of a Renault Espace VI (2023+) is **recommended**, provided you accept its new SUV status and are vigilant about electronics. The E-Tech 200 engine offers an excellent compromise between performance and fuel economy, making the absence of diesel almost painless for most families. The new 3-cylinder unit (HR12) appears well-designed and shares no flaws with the brand's older engines. However, as this is an ultra-connected vehicle with a complex transmission, it is imperative to buy a model with a clear software update history. Prioritize models still under manufacturer warranty.
