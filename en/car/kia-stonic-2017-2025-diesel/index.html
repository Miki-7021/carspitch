Summary

The Kia Stonic (Generation I - YB) is an urban crossover (B-SUV segment) closely derived from the fourth-generation Kia Rio city car. Launched in late 2017, it stands out for its dynamic design, sedan-like road handling (low center of gravity for an SUV), and its 7-year manufacturer's warranty.

Although demand quickly shifted towards petrol, the Stonic was offered at its launch with an excellent 1.6 CRDi diesel engine, ideal for high-mileage drivers. This engine evolved in 2018 (transition to Euro 6d-Temp standard with the addition of AdBlue) before being definitively removed from the catalog in late 2020 during the restyling (facelift), which introduced mild-hybrid (MHEV) technology on petrol engines. The Stonic is an overall very reliable model, benefiting from the maturity of Hyundai-Kia group's mechanical components.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent overall reliability, very few immobilizing breakdowns
  • Very fuel-efficient and durable 1.6 CRDi engine (robust timing chain)
  • 7-year or 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty (reassuring for used cars)
  • Dynamic road handling, little body roll
  • Simple and effective interior ergonomics (physical controls retained)

Weaknesses 5

  • Diesel engine discontinued too early (2020), limiting recent used car availability
  • Very firm suspension, degraded comfort on bumpy roads (especially with 17-inch wheels)
  • DCT7 automatic gearbox sometimes causes jerks at low speeds
  • Hard interior plastics prone to scratches
  • Average sound insulation on the highway (road and wind noise)

Check before buying

  • Driving history (Diesel): avoid a 1.6 CRDi that has only been driven in the city (risk of clogged DPF/EGR).
  • AdBlue system (Diesel post-2018): check for the absence of an engine light related to the SCR system.
  • DCT7 gearbox: test the vehicle at very low speeds and on hills. Any excessive juddering indicates worn clutches.
  • iMT gearbox (MHEV): ensure that the 'coasting' mode (sail) engages smoothly.
  • Front tire wear: the incisive front axle tends to wear tires a little faster than the segment average.

Official recalls

  • November 2018 (Child Safety): On models produced between July and October 2017, the rear door child safety lock cable may be too short, risking deactivating the safety feature. Cable replacement.
  • May 2021 (48V MHEV System): On 1.0 T-GDI MHEV models, risk of internal short circuit in the 48V battery or DC/DC converter bug. Software update and battery inspection.
  • [Note]: The 1.6 CRDi engine has not been subject to any major specific recalls on the Stonic, confirming its high reliability.

Its 1.6 CRDi engine is renowned for its robustness and longevity, backed by a 7-year or 150,000 km manufacturer's warranty, offering unparalleled peace of mind.

Economy

  • Remarkably Low Fuel Consumption — Ideal for high-mileage drivers, the Stonic's 1.6 CRDi engine offers exceptional fuel efficiency, with a very low average consumption of 4.5 L/100km. Enjoy long journeys with a controlled fuel budget, without compromising on performance.

Safety

  • Maximum Safety Certified by Euro NCAP — The Kia Stonic achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 93% for adult protection and 84% for child protection. A guarantee of serenity for all occupants.
  • Efficient and Reassuring Braking — Benefit from solid and stable braking performance, ensuring competent stopping distances and an increased sense of security in all urban and road driving situations.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Safe Road Handling — Enjoy agile and predictable driving. The Stonic offers excellent road holding with responsive steering and well-managed body roll, for a balanced and pleasant driving experience.
  • Enduring Diesel Engine with Generous Torque — The 1.6 CRDi engine delivers generous torque and great endurance, making the Stonic particularly enjoyable on roads and highways. Its dynamic behavior and low body roll add to the driving pleasure.

Practicality

  • Intuitive and Functional Interior Ergonomics — The Stonic's cabin is designed for simplicity and efficiency. With well-placed physical controls, the ergonomics are intuitive, making every journey more pleasant and less distracting.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 2008 (1.5 BlueHDi - less reliable regarding AdBlue), Renault Captur (1.5 dCi - excellent diesel competitor), Seat Arona (1.6 TDI), Hyundai Kona (technical cousin, same 1.6 CRDi).
  • Asia / Japan: Mazda CX-3 (1.5 Skyactiv-D - superior finish but less spacious), Honda HR-V.
  • Americas / Emerging: Nissan Kicks, Chevrolet Tracker, Volkswagen T-Cross.

Verdict

The Kia Stonic is an extremely rational and safe choice in the used car market. If you are targeting the diesel (1.6 CRDi), you are choosing longevity and fuel economy. This engine is one of the most reliable diesels of its generation, provided its preferred use is respected: open roads and not urban traffic jams. Opt for a 110 hp version (2017-2018) if you want to avoid potential AdBlue system quirks, or a 115/136 hp version (2018-2020) for a sustainable Crit'Air sticker and optimal depollution. For city dwellers, the MPI or T-GDI petrol engines will be much more suitable.
