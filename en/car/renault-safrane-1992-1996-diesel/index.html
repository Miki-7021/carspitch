Renault Safrane

I (B54) · 1992 – 1996

NEGOTIATE Reliability 68/100 — moyen

Summary

The **first-generation Renault Safrane (Phase 1, code B54)** had the heavy task of succeeding the legendary R25 in the large sedan (E-segment) segment. Launched in 1992, it stands out for its imperial ride comfort, giant spaciousness, a very practical tailgate, and ambitious technological features for its time (controlled suspension, voice synthesis, dual-zone air conditioning). However, its high weight (often over 1.4 tons) penalizes entry-level engines. While the body benefits from excellent anti-corrosion treatment, this Phase 1 suffered from numerous electrical "gremlins" and interior finish issues in its early years. Now entering the *youngtimer* era, it is bought based on its state of preservation and maintenance history, particularly for diesel versions which often show astronomical mileages.
