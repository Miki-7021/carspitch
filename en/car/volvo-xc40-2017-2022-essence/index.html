Summary

The Volvo XC40 (Generation I, code XZ) marks the highly successful entry of the Swedish manufacturer into the ultra-competitive premium compact SUV segment. Based on the new modular CMA (Compact Modular Architecture) platform developed with Geely, it stands out with its assertive cubic design, ingenious spaciousness, and top-tier safety arsenal. Voted Car of the Year in 2018, it initiated the brand's energy transition: initially launched with 100% thermal powertrains (T gasoline and D diesel), it quickly integrated mild-hybrid (B), plug-in hybrid (Recharge T), and 100% electric (Recharge Twin/Single) versions. Overall, it is a very well-conceived vehicle, offering excellent mechanical reliability, although the first few years were marked by some electronic quirks related to the Sensus infotainment system.

  • Distinctive design and very upscale interior presentation

What sets it apart 7

Safety

  • Advanced Safety and Maximum Protection — The Volvo XC40 is a benchmark for safety, achieving 5 stars in Euro NCAP tests with exceptional scores for adult (97%) and child (87%) protection. It comes standard with the IntelliSafe system, a comprehensive suite of active and passive driving aids.

Braking

  • Superior Control and Ultra-Short Braking Distances — Equipped with a high-performance braking system, the XC40 ensures excellent stability and remarkably short stopping distances, typically between 36 and 38 meters from 100 km/h, guaranteeing optimal responsiveness in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Serene and Predictable Road Handling — The XC40 offers stable and reassuring road handling, with well-controlled body roll and precise steering. Its dynamic behavior is designed for everyday safety and comfort, providing a soothing driving experience.

Reliability

  • Robustness of Petrol Engines — The XC40's petrol engines (T3, T4, T5) are renowned for their exemplary mechanical reliability, offering remarkable durability and peace of mind for owners.

Comfort

  • Distinctive Scandinavian Design and Premium Interior — The XC40 stands out with its bold exterior design and highly appealing interior presentation. The seats offer exceptional comfort, and the overall ergonomics are designed for the well-being of occupants.

Practicality

  • Clever Practicality and Smart Storage — The XC40's cabin maximizes space with excellent rear passenger room and clever storage solutions, such as the removable waste bin and hooks, making daily life easier and more organized.

Performance

  • Prestigious Awards for Recognized Excellence — Voted Car of the Year in 2018 and honored with distinctions such as Euro NCAP Best in Class 2017 and IIHS Top Safety Pick+, the XC40 enjoys international recognition for its exceptional qualities.
