Lexus IS

III facelift (XE30) · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 92/100 — excellent

Summary

The third-generation Lexus IS (XE30) received a major facelift at the end of 2020 (often referred to as 'generation 3.5' or model year 2021). This facelift brought increased rigidity, wider tracks, redesigned suspension, and a modernized infotainment system with a touchscreen.

**IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:** As requested, we are addressing the diesel engine. However, it is crucial to specify that **Lexus has NOT offered ANY diesel engine on the Lexus IS since 2013**. The only diesel that existed on this model is the 2.2 D-4D (IS 220d / 200d) from the previous generation (XE20, 2005-2013). Faced with the reliability problems of this block (EGR, DPF/FAP, excessive oil consumption) and the global strategy of Toyota/Lexus, the brand permanently replaced diesel with hybrid (IS 300h) on the XE30 generation. The fact sheet below therefore details the history of the diesel for context, but focuses on the engines actually available between 2020 and 2025 (Hybrid, Turbo Petrol, V6, and V8). The model was also withdrawn from Western Europe at the end of 2020, continuing its career mainly in North America, Asia, and Australia.
