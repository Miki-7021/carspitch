Audi A3

8V · 2012 – 2020

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The third-generation Audi A3 (Type 8V) marked a major turning point for the Volkswagen Group by introducing the modular MQB platform. Available as a 3-door, Sportback (5-door), Sedan (Limousine), and Cabriolet, it redefined finish standards in the premium compact segment.

**Facelift:** Introduced mid-2016 (Phase 2), it brought aesthetic updates (optional Matrix LED headlights), the integration of the *Virtual Cockpit*, and an update to the engines (arrival of the 1.0 TFSI, 1.5 TFSI, and 2.0 TFSI, the 1.6 TDI increasing from 110 to 115 hp).

**Diesel Focus:** Unlike the previous generation (8P) affected by Dieselgate (EA189 engines), the A3 8V uses the **EA288** family of diesel engines. These blocks are intrinsically more reliable, quieter, and compliant with Euro 6 standards (with the addition of AdBlue on later versions). However, they require rigorous maintenance and highway driving to prevent clogging of complex emission control systems.

Strengths 5

  • Top-tier finish and assembly quality
  • Intuitive interior ergonomics and MMI multimedia system
  • Sound, safe road handling and advanced soundproofing
  • Very economical, powerful, and generally reliable 2.0 TDI (EA288) engine
  • Excellent resale value on the used car market

Weaknesses 5

  • Reliability of the S tronic 7 gearbox (DQ200) associated with the 1.6 TDI and small TFSI engines
  • Recurrent water pump and clogged heater radiator issues on TDI models
  • Firm comfort, especially with Sport or S line chassis (18/19-inch wheels)
  • Cost of maintenance and spare parts within the Audi network
  • Retractable MMI screen whose mechanism can wear out over time

Check before buying

  • Coolant (LDR) level: a drop indicates a leak from the water pump or thermostat housing.
  • Cabin heating (TDI): test the heating at full blast. If it blows cold on one side, the heater radiator is clogged by silicate.
  • S tronic gearbox history: demand oil change invoices every 60,000 km for wet-clutch gearboxes (2.0 TDI, 2.0 TFSI).
  • DQ200 gearbox behavior (1.6 TDI): look for vibrations, juddering, or hesitation during 1st -> 2nd gear changes.
  • Particulate filter (DPF): on TDI models, check the ash content with a diagnostic tool if the vehicle has been driven extensively in the city.

Verdict

The Audi A3 8V is undoubtedly one of the most accomplished premium compacts of its decade. If you're targeting a **diesel**, the **2.0 TDI (150 or 184 hp)** is the preferred choice: it erases the flaws of the old generation, offers superior driving pleasure, and proves very robust provided you monitor the cooling system (water pump, silicate reservoir).

The **1.6 TDI should be approached with more caution**, especially if it's paired with the S tronic 7 gearbox (DQ200), whose erratic reliability can lead to exorbitant costs. Regardless of the engine, avoid examples that have been used exclusively in the city or whose maintenance history (especially DSG/S tronic gearbox oil changes) is incomplete.
