Nissan Pathfinder

V (R53) · 2022 – 2025

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The **fifth-generation Nissan Pathfinder (R53)** marks a major and saving turning point for the model. Designed primarily for North America and the Middle East (it is not officially sold in Western Europe), it abandons the rounded lines of the previous generation for a more robust and boxy design. Above all, **it corrects the biggest flaw of its predecessor (the R52) by replacing the infamous continuously variable transmission (Xtronic CVT) with a true 9-speed automatic gearbox** (supplied by ZF). Paired with the robust, carried-over naturally aspirated 3.5L V6, this change propels the Pathfinder's reliability from "Below Average" to "Above Average" according to J.D. Power and Consumer Reports. It is a spacious family SUV (up to 8 seats), an excellent tow vehicle, and finally mechanically trustworthy.

Strengths 5

  • Restored mechanical reliability thanks to the abandonment of the CVT gearbox
  • Robust, proven, and high-performance 3.5L V6 engine (VQ35DD)
  • Excellent towing capacity for the category (up to 6,000 lbs / 2,720 kg)
  • Very spacious, quiet, and well-thought-out cabin for families
  • Effective Intelligent 4WD system

Weaknesses 4

  • Relatively high V6 fuel consumption in the city
  • Numerous early recalls on the 2022 model year (factory assembly defects)
  • Infotainment system somewhat dated compared to Korean competitors
  • Not available on the European market

Check before buying

  • Recall history: Crucial on 2022 models (verify that seat mountings and welds have been checked).
  • Automatic gearbox behavior: Gear shifts should be smooth. Jerks may require a software update of the transmission control module (TCM).
  • Interior fit and finish: Check for rattles or poorly fitted plastics on early production models.

Verdict

The 5th generation Nissan Pathfinder (R53) is an **excellent surprise and a strong comeback**. By listening to criticism and replacing the fragile CVT gearbox with a traditional 9-speed automatic transmission, Nissan has transformed an SUV with questionable reliability into a highly recommendable family vehicle. The 3.5L V6 engine is a monument of reliability.

**Buying advice:** Although the powertrain is solid from launch, the 2022 model year suffered from numerous factory assembly defects (welds, seat mountings, etc. - see recalls). It is therefore **strongly advised to target a model from 2023 onwards**, where these teething problems have been corrected. If you buy a 2022, demand proof that all NHTSA recalls have been performed.
