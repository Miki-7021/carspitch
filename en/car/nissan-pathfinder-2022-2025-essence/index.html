Summary

The fifth-generation Nissan Pathfinder (R53) marks a major and saving turning point for the model. Designed primarily for North America and the Middle East (it is not officially sold in Western Europe), it abandons the rounded lines of the previous generation for a more robust and boxy design. Above all, it corrects the biggest flaw of its predecessor (the R52) by replacing the infamous continuously variable transmission (Xtronic CVT) with a true 9-speed automatic gearbox (supplied by ZF). Paired with the robust, carried-over naturally aspirated 3.5L V6, this change propels the Pathfinder's reliability from "Below Average" to "Above Average" according to J.D. Power and Consumer Reports. It is a spacious family SUV (up to 8 seats), an excellent tow vehicle, and finally mechanically trustworthy.

Strengths 5

  • Restored mechanical reliability thanks to the abandonment of the CVT gearbox
  • Robust, proven, and high-performance 3.5L V6 engine (VQ35DD)
  • Excellent towing capacity for the category (up to 6,000 lbs / 2,720 kg)
  • Very spacious, quiet, and well-thought-out cabin for families
  • Effective Intelligent 4WD system

Weaknesses 4

  • Relatively high V6 fuel consumption in the city
  • Numerous early recalls on the 2022 model year (factory assembly defects)
  • Infotainment system somewhat dated compared to Korean competitors
  • Not available on the European market

Check before buying

  • Recall history: Crucial on 2022 models (verify that seat mountings and welds have been checked).
  • Automatic gearbox behavior: Gear shifts should be smooth. Jerks may require a software update of the transmission control module (TCM).
  • Interior fit and finish: Check for rattles or poorly fitted plastics on early production models.

Official recalls

*(Note: The Rappel Conso data provided for the NV250/NV300 do not concern the Pathfinder. Here are the official NHTSA recalls for the Pathfinder R53)*

  • March 2025 (NHTSA 25V173000): Risk of failure of one or both front brake calipers due to reduced strength (2025 models).
  • March 2024 (NHTSA 24V176000): Left front seat belt pretensioner potentially improperly secured (missing rivet).
  • February 2024 (NHTSA 24V154000): Manufacturing defect in the driver's airbag inflator module.
  • April 2023 (NHTSA 23V268000): Welds on the power driver's seat frame may detach.
  • July 2022 (NHTSA 22V527000): Infotainment bug causing loss of rearview camera display (linked to Sirius XM subscription).
  • February 2022 (NHTSA 22V111000): Missing mounting bolt on the second-row seat track.
  • September 2021 (NHTSA 21V742000, 21V743000, 21V706000): Series of recalls on early 2022 models for insufficient welds (3rd-row headrests, 2nd-row seat frames, and secondary hood latch bracket).

What sets it apart 10

Reliability

  • Automatic Gearbox Replaces CVT — The nine-speed automatic replaces the previous CVT, removing the main reliability concern and giving buyers a dependable, conventional transmission for long-term ownership.
  • Proven V6 Engine Reliability — The 3.5L VQ35DD V6 is a well-established Nissan engine, giving buyers a robust and proven powertrain that supports confident long-term use.
  • Improved Overall Reliability Rating — The gearbox change lifted reliability from below average to the upper average range according to J.D. Power and Consumer Reports, giving buyers greater peace of mind.
  • Later Models Avoid Early Faults — Choosing a model from 2023 onward avoids the assembly defects reported on the 2022 launch year, giving buyers a more sorted and dependable vehicle.

Practicality

  • Strong Towing Capacity — With up to 6,000 lbs (2,720 kg) of towing capacity, this SUV can pull trailers and heavy loads, making it practical for families with active outdoor needs.
  • Well-Suited to Heavy Use — The V6 and automatic gearbox combination is recommended for high-mileage drivers, large families and heavy towing, matching the vehicle to demanding daily use.

Comfort

  • Spacious Family Cabin — The interior offers up to eight seats in a roomy, quiet and well-thought-out layout, giving large families comfortable space for daily trips and long journeys.
  • Quiet and Comfortable Interior — The cabin is described as very spacious and silent, reducing noise for occupants and making long family drives more relaxing for driver and passengers.

Handling

  • Efficient Intelligent 4WD System — The Intelligent 4WD system provides effective traction across varied conditions, helping the driver maintain control on slippery or uneven surfaces.

Performance

  • Smooth and Flexible V6 Power — The naturally aspirated V6 delivers smooth, flexible performance with a pleasant sound, making the vehicle easy to drive whether loaded or unloaded.

Verdict

The 5th generation Nissan Pathfinder (R53) is an excellent surprise and a strong comeback. By listening to criticism and replacing the fragile CVT gearbox with a traditional 9-speed automatic transmission, Nissan has transformed an SUV with questionable reliability into a highly recommendable family vehicle. The 3.5L V6 engine is a monument of reliability.

Buying advice: Although the powertrain is solid from launch, the 2022 model year suffered from numerous factory assembly defects (welds, seat mountings, etc. - see recalls). It is therefore strongly advised to target a model from 2023 onwards, where these teething problems have been corrected. If you buy a 2022, demand proof that all NHTSA recalls have been performed.
