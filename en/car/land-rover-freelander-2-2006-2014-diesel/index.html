Land Rover Freelander 2

L359 · 2006 – 2014

NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

Summary

The **Land Rover Freelander 2 (L359)** marks a clear move upmarket compared to the first generation. Based on Ford's EUCD platform (shared with the first-generation Volvo XC60), it offers an excellent compromise between premium road comfort and real off-road capabilities thanks to the *Terrain Response* system. It benefited from two facelifts (2010 and 2012) that modernized its interior and headlights. Although more reliable than its predecessor, it suffers from chronic weaknesses in its all-wheel drive system (Haldex) and running gear, requiring rigorous maintenance.

**Overall reliability rating: Below average** (TÜV 3-year defect rate: 22.5%, What Car? Reliability: 2.5/5).

Strengths 5

  • Excellent off-road capabilities (highly effective Terrain Response system)
  • Suspension comfort and soundproofing worthy of the premium segment
  • Reliability of the 6-speed Aisin automatic gearbox and the manual gearbox
  • Generous interior space and commanding driving position
  • Intrinsic robustness of the 3.2 i6 petrol block

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the Haldex coupling if maintenance has not been anticipated
  • ADAC and TÜV breakdown rates below average (suspension, brakes, electronics)
  • Peripheral failures on the 2.2 Diesel (EGR, thermostat, DPF)
  • Premature wear of suspension components (silent blocks, ball joints) due to weight
  • Very high fuel consumption on petrol engines

Check before buying

  • **Haldex maintenance history:** Demand invoices for the rear axle/Haldex oil change. If absent on a model > 80,000 km, walk away or negotiate the replacement.
  • **Rear bearing noises:** A humming sound often indicates a rear differential or Haldex at the end of its life.
  • **Timing belt (Diesel):** Check the date and mileage of the last replacement (strict ADAC recommendation at 60,000 km).
  • **Underbody:** Inspect the lower protections to detect abusive off-road use.
  • **On-board electronics:** Test the operation of the Terrain Response, air conditioning, and hunt for water ingress (front carpets).

Verdict

The Land Rover Freelander 2 is a very endearing SUV, offering road and off-road performance rarely seen in this category. However, its reliability record is tarnished by chronic maintenance shortcomings, particularly on the AWD system. **The purchase of a 4WD model (TD4/SD4) should only be considered with an impeccable service book proving the regular oil change of the Haldex coupling.** The 2WD versions (eD4) are less risky on this point but lose the Land Rover DNA. Plan for a comfortable maintenance budget to compensate for the wear of the running gear and electronic quirks.
