Summary

The Peugeot 206+ (generation I, type 2A/C) is the result of Peugeot's pragmatic strategy: extending the life of the legendary 206 by grafting on a front end and dashboard inspired by the 207. Sold as the 206+ in Europe, it was also marketed as the '207 Compact' in South America and '207i' in Iran. Based on a platform proven since 1998, it benefits from remarkable reliability, as the initial youth defects of the original 206 have been eradicated. It's an early 'low-cost' city car, ideal for small budgets, offering an excellent comfort/road holding compromise, although its sound insulation and passive safety betray its design age.

Strengths 5

  • Extremely low purchase and maintenance costs
  • Proven mechanical reliability (engines at end of development)
  • Very low 1.4 HDi consumption (often under 4.5 L/100 km)
  • Healthy and dynamic road handling
  • Availability and price of spare parts (common with the 206)

Weaknesses 5

  • Dated driving position (steering wheel not depth-adjustable)
  • Poor sound insulation, especially on the 1.4 HDi
  • Very basic interior plastic quality
  • Limited rear passenger space and boot volume
  • Passive safety design dating from 1998 (crash test)

Check before buying

  • Rear axle: check if the rear wheels have excessive negative camber (/-/ effect). If the wheels rub in the wheel arches or if grinding noises are heard, the axle needs to be replaced.
  • Injector seals (1.4 HDi): lift the engine cover, look for traces of black tar around the injectors and listen for a compression noise ('pschitt pschitt') at idle.
  • Cylinder head gasket (1.1i and 1.4i): check for oil presence on the front left of the engine block (timing/alternator side).
  • EGR valve (1.4 HDi): check for absence of jerks during acceleration and black smoke, signs of clogging.
  • Exhaust line: tendency for rapid corrosion on the rear silencer.

Official recalls

  • November 2010: Recall for 1.4 HDi versions produced until 2010. Risk of fuel return harness rubbing against the air filter or engine cover, potentially leading to a diesel leak. Installation of a protective sleeve or replacement of the hose.
  • May 2011: Minor recall concerning a tightening defect of a bonnet lock striker plate screw.
  • Note: Having benefited from 10 years of corrections on the classic 206, the 206+ has undergone very few official recalls.

What sets it apart 6

economy

  • Record-low 1.4 HDi fuel consumption — Enjoy a diesel engine renowned for its exceptional fuel efficiency, allowing you to make significant savings on every journey.
  • Controlled purchase budget — The 206+ diesel is a smart choice for tight budgets, offering a very attractive acquisition cost and affordable insurance.

reliability

  • Proven mechanical reliability — Benefit from a highly reliable diesel engine, the result of many years of development on the 206 platform, ensuring daily peace of mind.

handling

  • Healthy and dynamic road behavior — Enjoy a pleasant driving experience thanks to a balanced chassis and optimized suspension, offering an excellent compromise between comfort and dynamism on all roads.

practicality

  • Simplified maintenance and available parts — The widespread distribution of the 206 platform ensures excellent availability of spare parts at very competitive prices, simplifying vehicle maintenance.

environment

  • Euro 5 diesel engine — The latest versions of the 1.4 HDi comply with the Euro 5 standard, incorporating a simplified DPF for an optimized environmental footprint.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Renault Clio II Campus (its direct rival, same life extension philosophy), Dacia Sandero I (more spacious but less dynamic), Fiat Punto Classic, Ford Fiesta V.
  • South America: Volkswagen Gol, Chevrolet Celta, Fiat Palio.
  • Iran/Asia: Saipa Tiba, IKCO Runna.

Verdict

The Peugeot 206+ is an extremely rational used car choice. By targeting the 1.4 HDi engine, you get a perfect transition vehicle or first car for a young driver: it's cheap to insure, consumes very little, and its mechanics are largely reliable. The only truly expensive point of vigilance is the rear axle, a chronic ailment of the 206 family. If you find a model with a healthy (or already replaced) rear axle and sealed injector seals, it's a highly recommended purchase for tight budgets.
