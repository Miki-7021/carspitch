Renault Espace

I (J11) · 1984 – 1991

NEGOTIATE Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The Espace I (code name J11) is a historic vehicle: it is the pioneer of the minivan (MPV) segment in Europe. Designed and assembled by Matra for Renault, it stands out with an avant-garde design: a body made of fiberglass and polyester panels bonded to a hot-dip galvanized steel chassis. This architecture makes its exterior panels impervious to rust and its structure extremely resistant.

The range is divided into two phases: - **Phase 1 (1984-1988)**: Flat grille, orange turn signals, very angular look. - **Phase 2 (1988-1991)**: Softened front end (slanted headlights), extended tailgate, modernized interior, and introduction of the "Quadra" all-wheel drive version.

Today, the Espace I is a true *youngtimer*. While its mechanics (the famous Douvrin engines) are proverbially robust, the challenge today lies in preserving the interior (fragile materials) and resolving the chronic electrical failures typical of French productions of the 80s.

Strengths 6

  • Fiberglass body impervious to corrosion
  • Hot-dip galvanized chassis extremely resistant to time
  • Revolutionary modularity (swiveling, removable seats)
  • Exceptional peripheral visibility
  • Legendarily robust Douvrin gasoline engines (J6R/J7R)
  • Suspension comfort and interior space

Weaknesses 6

  • Very fragile interior finish (brittle plastics, tearing fabrics)
  • Headliner systematically detaches over the years
  • Capricious electrical harness (oxidized connectors, melted fuse board)
  • Specific Matra trim and body parts now unobtainable
  • Fragile heater core, very complex to replace (requires dashboard removal)
  • Quadra (4x4) versions to avoid if the transmission is not perfect (parts unavailable)

Check before buying

  • Condition of the front engine cradle (only structural part susceptible to surface corrosion)
  • Functionality of ALL electrical equipment (windows, ventilation, gauges)
  • Presence of coolant leaks in the cabin (greasy carpets = faulty heater core)
  • Condition of the clear coat and paint on the fiberglass (tends to peel or craze)
  • Carburetor behavior on the 2.0 GTS (unstable idle, acceleration hesitations)
  • Timing belt history on all engines

Verdict

Buying a Renault Espace I today is a matter of passion for *youngtimers* and automotive history. It is no longer a daily family utility vehicle, but a collector's item.

**For gasoline enthusiasts:** Absolutely prioritize a **2.0i (TXE / 2000-1)** version from Phase 2. Electronic injection eliminates the whims of the Phase 1 carburetor, offering imperial mechanical reliability.

The real challenge will not be the engine, but the preservation of the interior and the hunt for electrical intermittent contacts. Avoid incomplete models, electrically tampered with, or Quadra versions with faulty transmissions, as specific Matra parts are now unobtainable.
