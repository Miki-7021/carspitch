Summary

The 5th generation Renault Scénic (Scénic E-Tech Electric) marks a complete break with its monospace past. Based on the CMF-EV platform (shared with the Mégane E-Tech and Nissan Ariya), it transforms into a 100% electric family SUV. Voted « Car of the Year 2024 », it stands out for its excellent spaciousness, its contained weight for the category (around 1,850 kg with the large battery), and its OpenR Link infotainment system based on Google Automotive, which is currently one of the best on the market. Important note: Unlike previous generations, this model is exclusively available with an electric powertrain (BEV). There are no petrol, diesel, or hybrid versions.

Strengths 6

  • Excellent real-world range on the 87 kWh version (often > 450 km on highway)
  • Ultra-fluid OpenR Link multimedia system (integrated Google) and very precise trip planner
  • Generous rear spaciousness (flat floor) and large trunk (545 liters)
  • Dynamic road behavior thanks to controlled weight and very direct steering
  • 22 kW AC charger available (optional or standard depending on trim), a major asset in the city
  • Electric motor without rare earths (reduced ecological impact)

Weaknesses 5

  • Absence of a front trunk (Frunk) for storing charging cables
  • Fast charging (DC) curve that drops quite quickly compared to 800V competitors (Hyundai/Kia)
  • Very poor rear visibility (the camera rearview mirror is essential)
  • Some hard plastics in the lower parts of the cabin
  • No all-wheel drive (4x4) available at launch

Check before buying

  • Wear condition of the front tires (the immediate torque of electric vehicles heavily stresses the front axle)
  • Proper functioning of the charging flap locking/unlocking system
  • OpenR Link software version (ensure the latest OTA updates have been installed)
  • Health of the 12V battery (modern electric vehicles are very sensitive to the discharge of the accessory battery)
  • Body panel alignment and wind noise at door seals at high speed

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot E-3008 (its direct rival, heavier but with a 73 kWh and 98 kWh version), Volkswagen ID.4 (more ponderous but very spacious), Skoda Enyaq (excellent space/price ratio), Tesla Model Y (technological benchmark, Supercharger network, but more cumbersome).
  • Asia / Emerging Markets: BYD Atto 3, BYD Seal U (very aggressive Chinese competitors on price with LFP batteries).
  • North America: Model not marketed.

Verdict

The Renault Scénic E-Tech is one of the most accomplished electric offerings on the current European market. By abandoning the monospace silhouette for that of an aerodynamic SUV, it gains sex appeal while retaining an excellent family vocation. Its great strength lies in its Google Automotive interface and its energy efficiency, particularly on the 87 kWh version which completely eliminates range anxiety. Reliability is expected to be very good, as Renault has ironed out the kinks of this platform with the Mégane E-Tech. It is a highly recommended purchase for a family looking to go 100% electric.
