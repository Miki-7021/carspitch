All Honda models

Summary

The second-generation Honda Fit (known as the Jazz in Europe and other markets, chassis code GE) is widely regarded as a benchmark in the subcompact segment. Renowned for its unparalleled interior packaging—highlighted by the ingenious 'Magic Seats'—it offers cargo space rivaling much larger vehicles. Mechanically, it is exceptionally robust, relying on Honda's proven L-series engines. While it suffers from typical economy car drawbacks like noticeable road noise and a firm ride, its long-term reliability and low running costs make it one of the smartest used car purchases available.

Strengths 5

  • Unmatched interior space and versatility (Magic Seats)
  • Bulletproof L-series engine reliability
  • Excellent fuel economy across all powertrains
  • Great outward visibility and easy maneuverability
  • Low maintenance and repair costs

Weaknesses 5

  • Massive involvement in the Takata airbag recall crisis
  • Firm ride quality, especially on pre-2012 models
  • High levels of road and wind noise at highway speeds
  • Thin paint prone to chipping and scratching
  • Uncomfortable seating position for taller drivers on long trips

Check before buying

  • Check if Takata airbag recalls have been performed (crucial safety check).
  • Listen for a ticking noise from the engine (indicates valve clearance adjustment is needed).
  • Check for water pooling in the spare tire well (caused by failing tailgate seals or roof seam cracks).
  • Test the air conditioning (AC compressor clutches and relays are known weak points).
  • On manual models, listen for whining noises indicating input shaft bearing wear.

Official recalls

NHTSA (United States) - 17 Official Recalls * Takata Airbags (Multiple Campaigns 2016-2019): The most critical issue. Recalls [19V502000, 19V500000, 19V378000, 18V661000, 18V268000, 18V042000, 17V545000, 17V030000, 16V346000, 16V061000] address defective driver and passenger frontal airbag inflators that may rupture upon deployment, causing severe injury or death. * Driveshaft Failure (2014 & 2020): Recalls [14V258000, 20V770000] for 2007-2013 manual transmission models. The passenger-side driveshaft may break due to corrosion under the dynamic damper, leading to a loss of drive or rollaway risk. * Electronic Stability Control (2013): Recall [13V157000] for 2012-2013 models. The ESC system software may allow excessive yaw rates before intervening, increasing crash risk. * 2015 Model Year (3rd Gen Bleed-over): The provided data includes early 3rd Gen (GK) recalls: Ignition wire damage [15V559000], CVT software causing pulley damage [15V574000], missing rear grab handles [15V697000], and A-pillar interior cover design [14V563000].

What sets it apart 10

Reliability

  • Proven mechanical reliability — The L-series engines are described as extremely robust, giving the buyer confidence in long-term durability and reducing the risk of major mechanical failures.
  • Strong overall reliability record — The model is presented as one of the most reliable subcompacts, supporting the case for a dependable used purchase when history is clean.
  • Recommended used purchase — The verdict recommends buying it as one of the best used cars in its category, provided safety recalls have been verified.

Practicality

  • Unmatched interior modularity — Magic Seats allow flexible configurations for passengers or cargo, making the car adaptable to varied daily needs without needing a larger vehicle.
  • Fuel tank under front seats — Placing the fuel tank beneath the front seats frees up interior space and contributes to the packaging that makes the cabin unusually roomy.

Comfort

  • Interior space of a higher segment — The cabin offers room usually found in a larger class, so passengers benefit from generous space despite the compact exterior dimensions.

Handling

  • Dynamic road behavior — The car is described as having dynamic handling, giving the driver a more engaging and responsive experience on the road.
  • Precise steering — Precise steering helps the driver place the car confidently and makes everyday maneuvering and driving more controlled.

Economy

  • Low maintenance costs — Running costs are described as low, which benefits buyers looking for an affordable used car with predictable upkeep expenses.

Safety

  • Recall verification protects safety — Checking that Takata and driveshaft recalls were performed helps the buyer avoid a vehicle with unresolved safety issues.

Alternatives

Toyota Yaris: Slightly more refined ride and equally reliable, but significantly less interior space. Nissan Versa / Note: Very spacious rear seat, but plagued by fragile Jatco CVT transmissions. Ford Fiesta: Much better driving dynamics and handling, but suffers from severe automatic transmission (PowerShift) reliability issues. Mazda 2: Fun to drive and reliable, but very cramped interior and tiny trunk compared to the Fit.

Verdict

The 2nd generation Honda Fit is a masterclass in subcompact engineering. Despite 682 NHTSA complaints—heavily skewed by the industry-wide Takata airbag crisis (205 complaints) and minor electronic/ESC glitches (142 complaints)—its core mechanicals are incredibly resilient. Engine and transmission complaints are remarkably low for a mass-market vehicle of this age. As long as the Takata airbags have been replaced and basic maintenance (like valve adjustments and fluid changes) is documented, it is an outstanding used car purchase.
