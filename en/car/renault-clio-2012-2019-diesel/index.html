Renault Clio

IV · 2012 – 2019

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The **Renault Clio IV** marks a major stylistic break for the diamond brand, under the aegis of Laurens van den Acker. Launched in 2012, it abandons the 3-door body to focus on a 5-door with coupé-like styling (hidden rear door handles) and an estate (Estate).

**Positioning & Evolution:** - **Phase 1 (2012-2016):** Seductive design but heavily criticized interior finish (hard plastics, light assembly) and imperfect sound insulation (wind noise). - **Phase 2 (Facelift 2016-2019):** Notable improvement in perceived quality (foamed materials), new Full LED headlights (C-Shape), and the arrival of the 1.5 dCi 110 hp.

**Diesel Focus (1.5 dCi - K9K):** In this generation, the renowned 1.5 dCi block reached its full maturity. The serious injection pump problems (metal filings) of the 2000s are a thing of the past. It is one of the most reliable and economical diesel engines in its category, ideal for high-mileage drivers, although vigilance is required regarding DPF clogging in urban use.

Strengths 6

  • Remarkable reliability of the 1.5 dCi engine in this generation
  • Extremely low diesel consumption (often below 4.5 L/100 km real-world)
  • Healthy, dynamic, and safe road handling
  • Exterior design that has aged very well
  • Generous boot volume for the category (300 liters)
  • Simple, intuitive, and reliable MediaNav multimedia system (unlike R-Link 1)

Weaknesses 6

  • Very disappointing plastic and assembly quality on Phase 1 (2012-2016)
  • Imperfect sound insulation (wind noise around mirrors and front windows)
  • Poor rear visibility (narrow rear window, thick C-pillars)
  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.2 TCe 120 petrol engine
  • EDC automatic gearbox sometimes prone to jerks and costly failures
  • R-Link 1 system (top-of-the-range) slow and prone to electronic bugs

Check before buying

  • On 1.5 dCi: Usage history. Avoid a diesel that has only been driven in the city (DPF/EGR risk).
  • On 1.5 dCi: Date and mileage of the last timing belt change.
  • On EDC gearbox: Test drive the car cold and hot, in the city. Look for hesitation on startup or gears jumping.
  • On Phase 1: Condition of interior plastics (scratch very easily) and steering wheel (peeling leather).
  • Running gear: Clunking noises from the front axle (stabilizer bar links, fragile wishbone bushings).

Verdict

The Renault Clio IV is an excellent used car choice if you target the right versions. **In diesel (1.5 dCi), it's a real workhorse, extremely reliable and built to cover many kilometers.** This is the engine to prioritize for high-mileage drivers. In petrol, the modest 1.2 16v and the valiant 0.9 TCe are recommendable. However, **absolutely avoid the 1.2 TCe 120**, a real ticking time bomb. Strongly favor a **Phase 2 (after September 2016)** to benefit from a decent interior and improved sound insulation. The manual gearbox is preferable to the EDC gearbox for maximum peace of mind.
