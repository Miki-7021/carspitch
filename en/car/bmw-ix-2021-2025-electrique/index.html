BMW iX

I (I20) · 2021 – 2025

NEGOTIATE Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The BMW iX (code name I20) is the large 100% electric flagship SUV of the Bavarian brand. Based on a specific architecture integrating a lightweight carbon-fiber-reinforced plastic structure (Carbon Cage), it stands out with a polarizing but extremely aerodynamic design (Cd of 0.25). Inside, it introduces a very luxurious, minimalist atmosphere, and the iDrive 8 (later 8.5) infotainment system with its large curved display. While it shines with its royal comfort, benchmark soundproofing, and the efficiency of its wound-rotor motors (without rare earths), it suffers from significant weight (over 2.5 tons) and a disappointing trunk volume for its size (500 liters).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional suspension comfort (especially with the air suspension)
  • Very high-level soundproofing
  • Remarkable energy efficiency for its size (xDrive50)
  • Quality of finish and luxurious interior materials
  • Electric motors without rare earths (wound rotor)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very polarizing exterior design
  • Excessive weight (2.4 to 2.6 tons)
  • Trunk volume (500 L) unworthy of an almost 5-meter SUV
  • All-touch ergonomics (iDrive 8) requiring an adaptation period
  • Numerous early-life recalls (software and battery)

Check before buying

  • Complete history of manufacturer recalls (essential on this highly technological model)
  • Tire wear (weight and torque degrade them quickly)
  • Operation of the Combined Charging Unit (CCU) during fast charging
  • Absence of interior trim noises (some cases of squeaks reported on early models)
  • Condition of the aerodynamic wheels (very exposed to curbs)

Alternatives

- **Europe**: Audi Q8 e-tron, Mercedes-Benz EQE SUV, Polestar 3, Volvo EX90. - **North America**: Tesla Model X, Rivian R1S, Cadillac Lyriq, Lucid Gravity. - **Asia**: Nio EL7, XPeng G9.

Verdict

The BMW iX is a technological tour de force that offers a driving experience of rare comfort and silence. The xDrive50 version is the optimal choice, offering reassuring real-world range. However, its early life was marked by an impressive number of recalls (software, battery modules, airbags). It is imperative to buy a model with a crystal-clear maintenance and recall history, preferably at a dealership with an extended warranty (BMW Premium Selection).
