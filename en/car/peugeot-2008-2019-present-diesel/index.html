Peugeot 2008

II · 2019 – Présent (Restylage en 2023)

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The second-generation Peugeot 2008 (codename P24) marks a complete break from its predecessor. Gone is the raised estate, it adopts a true muscular and aggressive urban SUV (B-SUV) silhouette, modeled on the success of the 3008. Based on the Stellantis group's modular CMP/e-CMP platform, it offers increased interior space and a spectacular interior (i-Cockpit 3D). Although the market is massively shifting towards petrol and electric, the 2008 II has retained a very relevant diesel offering (1.5 BlueHDi) for high-mileage drivers, providing remarkable fuel efficiency on motorways. The 2023 facelift modernized the front end (new three-claw light signature) and introduced new powertrains (MHEV 136 hp and electric 156 hp), while keeping diesel in the catalog for certain markets.

**Overall design score: 7.5/10** - A very successful design and excellent road handling, but mechanical reliability marred by the early issues of PSA's internal combustion engines (PureTech and BlueHDi).

Strengths 6

  • Assertive and appealing exterior design
  • Modern interior presentation (i-Cockpit 3D)
  • Dynamic road handling, excellent comfort/road holding compromise
  • Extremely low fuel consumption for the 1.5 BlueHDi on rural roads/motorways
  • Pleasant EAT8 automatic gearbox (smooth and responsive)
  • New MHEV 136 hp engine finally corrects the PureTech's flaws

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of internal combustion engines before 2023 (PureTech and BlueHDi)
  • Very temperamental AdBlue system, expensive to repair out of warranty
  • i-Cockpit ergonomics not suitable for all body types (steering wheel obscuring gauges)
  • Slow and dated infotainment system on pre-facelift versions (2019-2023)
  • Rear passenger space and boot volume in the lower average of the category
  • Very limited motorway range on the e-2008 electric version (136 hp)

Check before buying

  • On 1.5 BlueHDi: Listen carefully to the top of the engine when cold (metallic rattling noise = loose camshaft chain).
  • On 1.5 BlueHDi: Check the history of dashboard alerts (Antipollution fault / AdBlue).
  • On 1.2 PureTech: Condition of the timing belt via the oil filler cap (cracks, fraying).
  • Maintenance history: Reject any vehicle without a 100% up-to-date service book within the manufacturer's network (essential for warranty claims).
  • Electronics: Test all functions of the touchscreen, air conditioning, and driving aids (frequent bugs).

Official recalls

  • - **November 2020 (e-2008)**: Risk of short-circuit due to incorrect routing of high-voltage cables.
  • - **December 2020 (BlueHDi)**: Possible rubbing of an electrical harness against a fuel pipe, risk of leakage.
  • - **Late 2022 (PureTech)**: Massive recall (code JZR) for engine control unit update to improve diagnosis of the braking assistance system (linked to belt degradation clogging the vacuum pump).
  • - **2023 (All models)**: Inspection and retightening of rear axle or engine cradle fixings on certain 2022 series.

Verdict

The Peugeot 2008 II is an SUV full of dynamic and aesthetic qualities, but its internal combustion reliability record demands great caution. **If you are looking for a Diesel (1.5 BlueHDi)**: it is an excellent long-distance runner, but it is imperative to prioritize a model produced after spring 2023 (equipped with the 8 mm camshaft chain) or a used model whose housing/chain has already been replaced with the new reference, with supporting invoices. Avoid diesel models used exclusively in the city (DPF clogging and AdBlue crystallization guaranteed). For petrol, avoid classic PureTech engines and aim for the new 1.2 Hybrid 136 hp with a timing chain, or turn to the electric (e-2008) which proves to be the most reliable version in the range.
