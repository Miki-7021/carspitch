Peugeot 3008

I (0U) · 2009 – 2016

NEGOTIATE Reliability 62/100 — moyen

Summary

The **first-generation Peugeot 3008 (T84 / 0U)**, unveiled in late 2008 and launched in 2009, marked a major turning point for the lion brand. At the crossroads of an SUV, an MPV, and a compact sedan, it achieved immense commercial success thanks to its 'aviation-style' cabin, excellent road holding, and modularity (two-part 'hobby' tailgate). Based on the PF2 platform, it received a facelift in late 2013 (new grille, LED lights). While its road qualities are undeniable, its career was marred by major reliability issues on certain engines (notably the Prince gasoline engines and the early 1.6 HDi). On the used market, the choice of engine, particularly for diesel, is absolutely crucial.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional road handling (Dynamic Rolling Control system on 2.0 HDi and 1.6 THP)
  • Imperial reliability of the 2.0 HDi engine (150 and 163 hp)
  • Original and upscale interior presentation (for its time)
  • Trunk modularity (3-position floor, 'hobby' lower tailgate supporting 200 kg)
  • Suspension comfort and soundproofing (especially on phase 2 models)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of gasoline engines (VTi, THP, PureTech) and the 1.6 HDi 110
  • Endemic AdBlue tank issue on BlueHDi versions
  • Slow BMP6 / ETG6 automated manual gearbox, causing jerks and premature clutch wear
  • High vehicle weight (impacts fuel consumption and brake/tire wear)
  • Cosmetic wear: peeling leather steering wheel, flaking center console plastics

Check before buying

  • Service history: crucial, avoid any model without an up-to-date service book, especially for the 1.6 HDi.
  • UREA / Engine fault lights: on BlueHDi, indicates an AdBlue tank replacement (approx. €1200).
  • Injector seals (1.6 HDi): presence of black tar around injectors or a 'hissing' sound.
  • Gearbox: test the BMP6/ETG6 at low speed. Any excessive shuddering indicates a clutch/flywheel nearing end of life.
  • Electronics: operation of the electric parking brake, retractable screen (WipNav/RT6), and head-up display.

Official recalls

  • - **November 2020 (1.2 PureTech)**: Massive recall for timing belt and vacuum pump inspection (risk of loss of braking assistance). - **February 2016 (2.0 HDi)**: Risk of fuel leak at the diesel fuel heater (2012-2014 models). - **October 2015 (2.0 HDi)**: Replacement of the vacuum pump valve (risk of loss of braking assistance). - **December 2012 (All)**: Reprogramming of the intelligent service unit (BSI) due to wiper or lighting malfunctions. - **2010-2011 (1.6 HDi)**: Recalls for non-conformity of the diesel return hose harness (risk of leak).

Verdict

The Peugeot 3008 I is a vehicle with two faces. In terms of chassis and interior space, it's a major success. Mechanically, it's a minefield. **For diesel, the 2.0 HDi (150 or 163 hp) is the only choice that can be recommended without hesitation**: it is powerful, torquey, and extremely robust. The 1.6 HDi 112/115 hp is an acceptable compromise for tight budgets, provided you avoid the BMP6 automated manual gearbox. Absolutely avoid the 1.6 HDi 110 hp (turbo failures) and be extremely vigilant with the 1.6 BlueHDi 120 hp (budget to anticipate for the AdBlue tank). Gasoline engines are generally to be avoided due to major design flaws (timing chain on THP/VTi, belt on PureTech).
