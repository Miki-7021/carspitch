Introduction to the Honda Accord IX (CR Generation)

The ninth generation of the Honda Accord marks a major turning point in the model's history. Honda unified its platforms, merging the North American and Japanese/European versions into a single global model.

**⚠️ EXPERT NOTE REGARDING DIESEL :** Although your search targets a diesel engine, it is imperative to specify that **the 9th generation Honda Accord was NEVER offered with a diesel engine**. Following the discontinuation of the Accord in Western Europe (end of 2015 with the 8th generation), Honda abandoned the excellent 2.2 i-DTEC block for this sedan. The 9th generation focused exclusively on new direct-injection gasoline engines (*Earth Dreams*) and an innovative hybrid engine (i-MMD). If you absolutely need a Diesel Accord, you must look for the previous generation (VIII - CU/CW).

This CR generation stands out for its spaciousness, the introduction of advanced driver-assistance systems (Honda Sensing), and excellent overall reliability, typical of the brand.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional overall powertrain reliability
  • Very generous interior space and seat comfort
  • Very low fuel consumption on the Hybrid version (i-MMD)
  • Healthy and dynamic road handling for its size
  • Ergonomics of physical controls (climate control)

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of diesel engine (penalizing for European high-mileage drivers accustomed to the 2.2 i-DTEC)
  • Very annoying VTC actuator noise on the 2.4L
  • Dual-screen infotainment system unintuitive and slow (before the 2016 facelift)
  • Imperfect sound insulation (typical Honda road noise)
  • V6 maintenance cost (belt, specific spark plugs, engine mounts)

Check before buying

  • Listen to the 2.4L engine during a cold start (after an overnight stop) to detect the VTC rattling noise.
  • CVT gearbox fluid change history (must be done every 40-50k km).
  • On the V6: check for excessive vibrations at steady speed (sign of engine mounts fatigued by VCM).
  • Check the condition of the CV joints (boots and vibrations during acceleration).
  • Ensure that recalls (Takata Airbags, battery sensor) have been performed.

Verdict

Expert's Verdict

The 9th generation Honda Accord is a fantastic, extremely reliable, and well-built sedan. **However, if you are absolutely looking for a diesel, this model is not for you as it never existed with this type of engine.**

If you are willing to switch to gasoline or hybrid, it is a highly recommended purchase. The 2.4L engine coupled with the CVT offers an excellent performance/consumption compromise, provided you check for VTC noise when cold and gearbox maintenance. The Hybrid version is a formidable alternative to diesel for high-mileage drivers, offering urban and suburban fuel consumption comparable to the best diesel engines, without the DPF or EGR valve issues.

**Buying Tip :** Opt for a facelifted model (2016-2017) in 2.4L or Hybrid version. The multimedia system is greatly improved (Apple CarPlay/Android Auto) and reliability is at its peak.
