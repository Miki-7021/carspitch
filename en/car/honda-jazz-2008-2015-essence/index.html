Honda Jazz

II (GE) · 2008 – 2015

BUY Reliability 91/100 — excellent

Summary

The second-generation Honda Jazz (code name GE, sold as Honda Fit in North America, Japan, and China) is an absolute benchmark in the versatile city car segment. Launched in late 2007 in Japan and in 2008 in Europe, it retains the monobox silhouette of its predecessor (GD) but significantly improves its chassis rigidity, safety, and engines (transition to i-VTEC). Its major asset remains its exceptional interior space thanks to the fuel tank placed under the front seats and its retractable rear bench seat ('Magic Seats').

**Facelift (2011):** Aesthetic revisions (bumpers, lights), improved sound insulation, softened suspensions and, especially for Europe, the highly anticipated return of the continuously variable automatic transmission (CVT) which replaces the disastrous i-SHIFT automated manual gearbox of 2008-2010 models. Introduction of the IMA Hybrid version in Europe.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability (L-Series engines with timing chain)
  • Record-breaking interior space for the category (3.90 m long)
  • Unbeatable modularity thanks to 'Magic Seats' (fold-up rear seat bases)
  • Boot volume worthy of a compact car (nearly 400 liters)
  • Excellent peripheral visibility (large glass areas)
  • Reduced maintenance cost and low wear of consumables (brakes, tires)

Weaknesses 5

  • i-SHIFT automated manual gearbox (2008-2010) unpleasant and prone to breakdowns
  • Very firm suspension comfort (especially before 2011)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Thin paint, very susceptible to stone chips and micro-scratches
  • Interior plastic quality (hard and prone to scratches)

Check before buying

  • **Water leaks:** Check the boot floor (under the spare wheel). Water can infiltrate through the rear light seals or micro-cracks in the roof sealant.
  • **Air conditioning:** The condenser is highly exposed to stone chips through the grille. Check for cold air production.
  • **i-SHIFT gearbox:** If buying a 2008-2010 automatic model, test the smoothness of gear changes. Any juddering indicates a clutch at the end of its life.
  • **Rear wheel bearings:** Listen for a dull humming sound from the rear starting at 70 km/h (frequent wear around 100,000 km).
  • **IMA Battery (Hybrid):** Check that the hybrid battery charge gauge does not empty/fill erratically, a sign of a fatigued battery.

Official recalls

  • - **Takata Airbags (Massive, 2013-2019):** Replacement of front airbag inflators (driver and passenger) at risk of exploding and projecting metallic fragments.
  • - **Electric window regulator (2010 / 2013):** Risk of short circuit and fire at the driver's control panel due to water infiltration.
  • - **Valve springs (2011):** On 1.5L engines (Fit US/Japan), risk of breakage of the VTEC system's lost motion springs, leading to engine stalling.
  • - **Engine management / CVT (2014):** Software update on certain hybrid/CVT versions to prevent premature wear of the transmission pulley.

Verdict

The Honda Jazz II (GE) is one of the most reliable and rational used cars on the global market. It's a 'no-brainer' purchase with one major condition: **absolutely avoid the i-SHIFT automated manual gearbox** sold in Europe between 2008 and 2010. Opt for a manual gearbox version, or choose a facelifted model (from 2011 onwards) equipped with the excellent CVT gearbox or the Hybrid engine. Its interior space is unmatched in the category, making it a perfect small family car, despite a somewhat firm ride.
