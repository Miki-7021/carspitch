Mazda CX-3

I (DK) · 2015 – 2023

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

Summary

The **first-generation Mazda CX-3 (DK)** is an urban SUV (B-segment) based on the Mazda 2 city car platform. Launched in 2015, it stands out with its dynamic (Kodo) design, meticulous finish bordering on premium, and excellent road handling. Contrary to the *downsizing* trend, Mazda opted for *rightsizing* with large, highly reliable naturally aspirated gasoline engines (Skyactiv-G).

It received a **major facelift in 2018** (improved sound insulation, electric parking brake, new grille, replacement of the 1.5 Diesel with a 1.8 Diesel). Although it left the European and North American markets at the end of 2021 in favor of the CX-30, its production continued until 2023 for other markets (Japan, Australia). It is a benchmark choice on the used market for its gasoline reliability, although its interior space is behind the competition.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional reliability of gasoline engines (Skyactiv-G)
  • Very smooth and robust torque converter automatic gearbox (Skyactiv-Drive)
  • Dynamic and precise road handling (typical Mazda)
  • Interior finish quality and control ergonomics
  • Exterior design that ages very well
  • Availability of genuine all-wheel drive (AWD)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very restricted rear passenger space (legroom and headroom)
  • Small trunk volume for the category (350 liters, or even 287L with the Bose subwoofer)
  • Imperfect sound insulation on models before the 2018 facelift
  • Problematic reliability of the 1.5 Diesel engine
  • Somewhat firm suspension at low speeds (especially with 18-inch wheels)

Check before buying

  • **Exterior mirrors:** The electric folding mechanism is fragile (plastic gears break). Very common problem.
  • **Multimedia screen (MZD Connect):** 'Ghost Touch' phenomenon (the touchscreen activates by itself, often due to heat or screen delamination).
  • **Air conditioning:** AC condenser exposed to gravel, leading to refrigerant gas leaks (especially 2015-2017 models).
  • **Oil level (Diesel only):** Check that the level does not exceed the cross on the dipstick (sign of dilution by diesel fuel).
  • **Tire wear:** The front axle can be demanding on tires depending on driving style.

Verdict

The Mazda CX-3 is an excellent used car choice, **provided you exclusively target gasoline engines (Skyactiv-G)**. The 2.0L naturally aspirated engine is a model of reliability in an industry dominated by fragile small turbo engines. It's an ideal vehicle for a couple or a single person looking for a dynamic, well-finished small SUV with no major mechanical issues. However, you should **avoid the 1.5 Diesel** (too many DPF and oil dilution problems) and accept its practical drawbacks: a small trunk and cramped rear seats. Opt for a post-2018 (facelift) model for better acoustic comfort.
