Peugeot 508

I (8D) · 2010 – 2018

NEGOTIATE Reliability 65/100 — moyen

Summary

The first-generation Peugeot 508 (internal code name W2, 8D for the sedan, 8E for the SW estate) had the challenging task of simultaneously replacing the 407 and 607. It marked a significant move upmarket for Peugeot with a refined finish, excellent sound insulation, and imperial road handling, true to the brand's reputation (double wishbone front suspension on GT versions, pseudo-MacPherson on others). Restyled in 2014, it modernized its front end and updated its engines to meet Euro 6 standards. While its road qualities are undeniable, its overall reliability was heavily tarnished by the misfortunes of the first-generation "Prince" petrol engines (1.6 THP) and, later, by the BlueHDi diesel emission control systems.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional road handling (especially in GT version with decoupled front axle)
  • Very refined sound insulation, worthy of the upper segment
  • Finish quality and aging of interior materials
  • Generous boot volume on the SW (estate) version
  • Remarkable mechanical reliability of 2.0 HDi engines (excluding AdBlue system)
  • Driving pleasure and reliability of the 1.6 THP 165 (post-2014)

Weaknesses 6

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.6 THP 156 hp (timing, HP pump)
  • Recurring and costly problems with AdBlue tanks (BlueHDi)
  • BMP6 / ETG6 automated manual gearbox slow, jerky, and fragile
  • RT6 multimedia system (pre-2014) slow and prone to failures
  • Average rear passenger space for its size (sedan)
  • Lack of practical storage in the cabin

Check before buying

  • On 1.6 THP 156: Demand a clear service history. Check if the timing chain and high-pressure pump have been replaced.
  • On 1.6 THP (all): Start the cold engine with the bonnet open and listen for the absence of a rattling noise (passenger side).
  • On BlueHDi: Check for the absence of a 'Engine fault' or 'Urea' warning light. Ask if the AdBlue tank has already been replaced.
  • Electronics: Test the GPS/Car radio. If it reboots in a loop or freezes, the internal SD card is dead.
  • Comfort access: Check that keyless entry/exit works on all 4 handles (very fragile capacitive sensors).
  • Running gear: Listen for clunking noises on degraded roads (front wishbone bushes or anti-roll bar drop links).

Official recalls

  • - **November 2012**: Risk of starter harness chafing which could lead to a short circuit (models produced until July 2012).
  • - **October 2014**: Replacement of the diesel fuel heater cover on 2.0 HDi engines (risk of leak and fire).
  • - **November 2020**: Recall on 1.6 THP engines concerning the vacuum pump. A lubrication defect can reduce braking assistance.
  • - **2021**: Software update of the engine control unit on BlueHDi versions to optimize alerts related to the emission control system (AdBlue/DPF).

Verdict

The Peugeot 508 I is an excellent road car, but its reliability record is very mixed depending on the engine chosen. **If you are looking for a petrol engine**, absolutely avoid the 1.6 THP 156 hp and the 1.6 VTi 120 hp, whose timing chain failures and oil consumption issues can ruin your budget. You must opt for a restyled version equipped with the **1.6 THP 165 hp**, which is infinitely more reliable and very pleasant to drive. For diesel, the older 2.0 HDi (140/163) are monsters of robustness. The more recent BlueHDi versions are mechanically excellent but require budgeting for the inevitable AdBlue tank replacement. Avoid the BMP6/ETG6 automated manual gearboxes in favor of manual or EAT6 automatic gearboxes.
