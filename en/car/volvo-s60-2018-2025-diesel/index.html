Summary

The third-generation Volvo S60 (Z252) marks a historic turning point for the Swedish brand. Based on the excellent modular SPA (Scalable Product Architecture) platform, it is the first Volvo produced in the United States (Ridgeville, South Carolina) and, crucially for your request: it is the very first Volvo model to have completely banned diesel from its global catalog from its launch.

Therefore, there is NO diesel engine available for this S60 III. Volvo made the radical choice to replace the diesel offering with gasoline engines (T), quickly converted to 48V mild-hybridization (B), and highly efficient plug-in hybrids (Recharge / Twin Engine). A direct competitor to the BMW 3 Series and Audi A4, it stands out for its clean Scandinavian design, high-level comfort, and advanced safety features. In 2022, a slight facelift introduced the Android Automotive (Google) infotainment system and larger capacity batteries for the PHEV versions.

Strengths 5

  • Timeless and elegant exterior design
  • Exceptional seat comfort (among the best on the market)
  • Benchmark level of active and passive safety (IntelliSafe)
  • Stunning Bowers & Wilkins audio system (option)
  • Performance of plug-in hybrid versions (especially post-2022)

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of diesel engine (detrimental for very high-mileage highway drivers)
  • Limited trunk volume (390 liters), further reduced on PHEVs
  • Reliability of the ERAD (rear electric motor) on pre-2022 PHEVs
  • Frequent software bugs on early models equipped with Android Automotive
  • Somewhat firm damping on R-Design / Polestar trims with 19/20-inch wheels

Check before buying

  • On PHEVs (2018-2021): listen carefully to the rear axle during acceleration/deceleration (a whistling sound indicates ERAD wear).
  • History of software updates (OTA) to ensure the stability of the multimedia system and 12V battery management.
  • Tire and brake wear: the car is very heavy (over 2 tons in PHEV), which accelerates the wear of consumables.
  • Operation of the panoramic sunroof (parasitic noises or defective seals reported on some American units).

Official recalls

The S60 III has been subject to a few recalls, often shared with other SPA platform models (XC60, V60, XC90):

  • March 2020 (AEB Software): The autonomous emergency braking system (IntelliSafe) might not detect certain obstacles due to a software bug. Update required.
  • July 2020 (Seatbelts): Possible premature wear of the front seatbelt pretensioner cable. Replacement with a reinforced part.
  • July 2021 (Fuel Pump Fuse): A 15A fuse could blow, causing the engine to stop. Replacement with a 20A fuse.
  • October 2023 (Turn Signals): Software bug that could prevent turn signals from working (resolved by OTA).

Verdict

The third-generation Volvo S60 is an extremely appealing premium sedan that made the bold (and successful) gamble of doing without diesel. If you were looking for a diesel to cover long distances, the mild-hybrid B4 or B5 versions are the most relevant alternatives, offering excellent reliability and controlled consumption. If you opt for the plug-in hybrid (T6 or T8), it is imperative to choose a 2022 model or newer (18.8 kWh battery): you will avoid the costly ERAD problems of the early years, while benefiting from doubled electric range and a simplified combustion engine. It is a recommendable purchase, provided you are vigilant about the maintenance history of the electrified versions.
