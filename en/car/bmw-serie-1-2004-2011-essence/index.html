Summary

The **BMW 1 Series (E87)** marked BMW's ambitious entry into the premium compact hatchback segment. Uniquely in its class, it featured a longitudinal engine layout and rear-wheel drive (RWD), offering a near-perfect 50:50 weight distribution and unmatched driving dynamics. The E87 refers specifically to the 5-door hatchback, though the family includes the E81 (3-door), E82 (coupe), and E88 (convertible).

A major facelift (LCI - Life Cycle Impulse) was introduced in **March 2007**, bringing interior quality improvements, revised styling, and the introduction of 'EfficientDynamics' technologies. However, this LCI also introduced the N43 direct-injection petrol engines and N47 diesel engines, both of which severely tarnished the model's reliability reputation due to timing chain and injection issues. While the chassis is brilliant, the 4-cylinder petrol engines require extreme caution.

Strengths 5

  • Unmatched driving dynamics in the segment (Rear-Wheel Drive)
  • Perfect 50:50 weight distribution
  • Exceptional N52 inline-6 engine in the 130i
  • High build quality and interior finish on LCI (post-2007) models
  • Precise, heavy, and communicative hydraulic steering

Weaknesses 5

  • Catastrophic reliability of 4-cylinder petrol engines (N43, N45, N46)
  • Infamous N47 diesel timing chain issues
  • Very cramped rear seating and small boot space (330 liters)
  • Harsh ride quality, especially with factory Run-Flat Tires (RFT) and M-Sport suspension
  • High maintenance and repair costs for a compact car

Check before buying

  • Cold start noises: Listen for rattling (timing chain) on ALL 4-cylinder engines (petrol and diesel).
  • Oil leaks: Check the Oil Filter Housing Gasket (OFHG) and Valve Cover Gasket, especially on 6-cylinder models.
  • Exhaust smoke: Blue smoke on N46 indicates failing valve stem seals; black smoke on diesels indicates EGR/DPF issues.
  • Misfires/Rough Idle: On N43 engines, this almost always points to failing Piezo injectors or coil packs.
  • Water pump history: On N52/N54 engines, ensure the electric water pump has been replaced if mileage is over 80,000 miles.

Verdict

The BMW E87 1 Series is a car of extremes. As a driving machine, it is the pinnacle of the compact class thanks to its RWD chassis. However, as a used car purchase, it is a minefield.

**If you want petrol:** Avoid the 4-cylinders (116i, 118i, 120i) entirely, especially the post-2007 N43 direct-injection models, unless you have a massive repair budget. The ultimate E87 is the **130i (N52)**: it is highly reliable, sounds glorious, and is a future classic.

**If you want diesel:** Seek out a pre-2007 **M47** (118d/120d) and remove the swirl flaps. Avoid the post-2007 N47 diesels unless the seller provides a solid invoice proving the timing chain has been replaced with updated BMW parts.
