Subaru Legacy

VI (BS) · 2015 – 2019

BUY Reliability 84/100 — bon

Summary

The sixth-generation Subaru Legacy (code BS) is a mid-size sedan that stands out in its segment with its standard Symmetrical AWD. Designed to offer excellent active and passive safety (notably thanks to the EyeSight system), it abandons the eccentricities of the past for a more consensual design and an interior that is significantly better finished than the previous generation. Mechanically, it marks the maturity of the new-generation Boxer engines, definitively turning the page on the head gasket problems that tarnished the reputation of the older EJ blocks. Although its sales have declined in the face of the growing popularity of SUVs (like its sibling, the Outback), it remains a benchmark choice for harsh climates. Overall rating: Very solid, safe, and rational.

Strengths 6

  • Standard all-wheel drive (Symmetrical AWD), formidable on snow
  • Above-average overall reliability (new-generation Boxer engines)
  • Historical head gasket problem (EJ25) definitively resolved
  • Highly effective EyeSight preventive safety system
  • Excellent peripheral visibility and roominess
  • 3.6L H6 engine (EZ36) with exemplary reliability

Weaknesses 5

  • Rubber-band sensation (elastic effect) from the CVT gearbox under hard acceleration
  • High fuel consumption of the 3.6R engine
  • Slight oil consumption possible on the 2.5L (although within standards)
  • Slow infotainment system on 2015-2017 models
  • Less dense dealer network in Europe

Check before buying

  • Recall history regarding the low-pressure fuel pump (very important)
  • Cold engine oil level (to evaluate consumption on the 2.5L)
  • Behavior of the CVT gearbox (absence of jerks at low speed, whining noises)
  • Condition of the windshield (chips in front of the EyeSight cameras)
  • Operation of the touchscreen (prone to software bugs in the early years)

Verdict

The sixth-generation Subaru Legacy (2015-2019) is an excellent used choice, particularly for drivers facing difficult weather conditions. Subaru has corrected the major flaws of its previous generations (notably the head gaskets). The 2.5L engine is the most rational choice and offers solid reliability, requiring only simple monitoring of the oil level. The 3.6L is a model of robustness, ideal for those who prioritize driving pleasure over fuel economy. Make absolutely sure that the recalls (especially the fuel pump and steering column) have been performed. It is a discreet, yet formidably effective and durable sedan.
