Summary

The Lancia Ypsilon type 846 (often considered the 3rd or 4th generation depending on the nomenclature) is a versatile city car based on the Fiat Mini platform (shared with the Fiat 500 and Panda III). Unlike the previous generation, it is exclusively offered in 5 doors, with rear door handles hidden in the pillars to maintain a coupe-like appearance. Positioned as a "chic" and premium city car, it was initially sold throughout Europe (badged as the Chrysler Ypsilon in the UK and Ireland) before its marketing was restricted solely to the Italian market from 2017 onwards, where it continued to break sales records thanks to regular updates (facelifts in 2015 and 2021). Although its design is aging, it shines with its timeless style and proven engines from the Fiat parts bin, notably the indestructible 1.2 Fire. Overall rating: An endearing city car, highly reliable in the naturally aspirated 1.2 version, but whose passive safety (0 Euro NCAP stars during the 2015 re-test) and onboard technology show their age.
