Summary

The Toyota GR Supra (A90 generation, internal code J29) marks the highly anticipated return of Toyota's iconic sports coupe after more than 15 years of absence. Developed in close partnership with BMW (it shares its platform, powertrains, and interior with the BMW Z4 G29), it is assembled by Magna Steyr in Austria. Although criticized by purists at its launch for its 'Germanic DNA', the GR Supra quickly established itself as an excellent sports car. It benefits from excellent modular BMW engines (B48 4-cylinder and B58 6-cylinder), renowned for their high reliability and tuning potential. In 2022, Toyota addressed one of the main criticisms by introducing a 6-speed manual gearbox on the 3.0L version, accompanied by a revision of the suspension and steering.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional reliability of BMW B48 and B58 engines
  • Real-world performance often superior to advertised figures
  • Road handling and chassis balance (50/50 weight distribution)
  • Perfect management of the ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox
  • Highly successful introduction of the manual gearbox in 2022
  • Immense tuning potential for the B58 block

Weaknesses 5

  • Unbearable aerodynamic resonance with windows open (wind buffeting) requiring aftermarket deflectors
  • Very poor peripheral and rear visibility (significant blind spots)
  • Infotainment system based on the older BMW iDrive 6 (no update to recent versions)
  • Fake air vents on the bodywork
  • Difficult access for tall individuals (very low roof)

Check before buying

  • Service history: check if maintenance was done at Toyota (sometimes less familiar with BMW engines) or by a competent independent specialist.
  • Coolant level: B-series engines may show slight drops in level (water pump or thermostat housing).
  • Condition of the rear axle: pronounced wear of tires and bushings in case of intensive use or track days.
  • Modifications (Tuning): many 3.0L models are remapped (Stage 1/2). Prefer an original model or one modified by a recognized professional.
  • Presence of window deflectors: almost essential for driving with windows open above 70 km/h.

Official recalls

  • - **November 2021 (Braking):** Recall (shared with BMW) concerning an engine management software defect that could lead to a loss of braking assistance during startup. - **August 2021 (Safety):** Risk of loss of braking assistance function due to premature wear of a vacuum pump component. - **November 2020 (Fuel):** Potentially defective weld on the fuel tank that could lead to a leak (concerns a small series of 2020 models). - **October 2019 (Rearview Camera):** Software bug preventing the rearview camera display (software update). - **September 2019 (Seatbelts):** Defective weld on the seatbelt guide loop support (part replacement).

Alternatives

- **Europe:** Porsche 718 Cayman (dynamic benchmark, more expensive), Alpine A110 (lighter, different handling), BMW M2 (more practical, 4 seats), Audi TT RS. - **North America:** Chevrolet Corvette C8 (mid-engine, V8), Nissan Z (twin-turbo V6, standard manual gearbox), Ford Mustang Mach 1 / Dark Horse. - **Japan / Asia:** Nissan Z, Lexus RC F (naturally aspirated V8, more GT-oriented).

Verdict

The Toyota GR Supra A90 is an undeniable success dynamically and mechanically. The partnership with BMW, though controversial, allowed the Supra to be equipped with one of the best 6-cylinder engines in the world (the B58), offering remarkable reliability, far superior to older generations of European turbo engines. It is a highly recommended purchase, whether in 2.0L for dynamic daily use, or in 3.0L for purists and track enthusiasts. The manual gearbox version (post-2022) is the absolute grail for future collector value. However, pay attention to the service history, especially on heavily modified models.
