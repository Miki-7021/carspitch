Fiat Punto

Evo · 2009 – 2012

NEGOTIATE Reliability 55/100 — moyen

Summary

The **Fiat Punto Evo** is a significant facelift of the Grande Punto (generation 199). It features a redesigned front end, LED taillights, and, most notably, a significantly improved interior in terms of finish and materials. Mechanically, it introduces MultiAir petrol engines and updates its MultiJet diesels to meet the Euro 5 standard. Although appealing and economical to buy, its overall reliability is considered **below average** by control bodies (ADAC, TÜV), penalized by electronic weaknesses, fragile running gear, and capricious emission control systems on diesels in urban use.

Strengths 5

  • Exterior design still current and interior significantly improved compared to the Grande Punto.
  • 1.3 and 1.6 MultiJet diesel engines are very economical on the road.
  • FIRE petrol engines (1.2 and 1.4 8V) are extremely reliable and inexpensive to maintain.
  • Very attractive used purchase price.
  • Availability and price of spare parts (network and aftermarket).

Weaknesses 5

  • High ADAC breakdown rate (6.8 breakdowns/1000 veh/year) and poor TÜV report (23.5% defects at 3 years).
  • Random electronic reliability (power windows, central locking, various sensors).
  • Diesel emission control system (DPF, EGR) unsuitable for urban use.
  • Premature wear of suspension components (wishbones, shock absorber mounts) and brakes.
  • Notorious structural rust (sills, wheel arches) on examples over 100,000 km (reported by ADAC).

Check before buying

  • Electric power steering (City / Dualdrive): check for absence of stiff spots or red warning light on the dashboard (fragile steering column).
  • Service history on the 1.3 MultiJet: listen for any metallic noise on startup (slack timing chain).
  • Operation of all electrical equipment (air conditioning, windows, radio, Blue&Me).
  • Condition of underbody and sills (check for perforating corrosion).
  • On diesels: check for absence of jerking during acceleration (EGR valve) and oil level (if above max, DPF dilutes diesel in oil).

Verdict

The Fiat Punto Evo is a charming city car, but one that requires caution when buying. **If you are looking for a diesel (1.3 MultiJet)**, vigilance is key: avoid models that have been used exclusively in the city, meticulously check the oil change history (for the timing chain) and the condition of the DPF. The 1.6 MultiJet is much more recommendable for high-mileage drivers. However, the most rational and reliable choice for this model remains the basic petrol engines (1.2 and 1.4 FIRE), which avoid costly emission control and timing chain problems. Budget for minor electrical issues and wear of the running gear.
