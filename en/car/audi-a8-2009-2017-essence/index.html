Summary

The third-generation Audi A8 (D4, type 4H) represented the pinnacle of luxury and technological expertise from Ingolstadt upon its release. Built on the ASF (Audi Space Frame) aluminum structure, it stands out for its surprising agility for its size, its standard quattro AWD (except hybrid), and its exceptional interior finish. The late 2013 facelift (Model Year 2014) introduced the famous Matrix LED headlights, revised engines to meet the Euro 6 standard, and further improved soundproofing. Although its build quality is flawless, it is an extremely complex vehicle whose maintenance requires a substantial budget, particularly on the V8 petrol and W12 engines.

Strengths 5

  • Unrivaled interior finish and material quality (Valcona leather, wood trim, aluminum) for its time
  • Dynamic road handling for the segment thanks to the aluminum chassis (ASF) and quattro
  • Exceptional soundproofing (especially with acoustic double glazing)
  • Smooth, responsive, and highly reliable 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox
  • Stratospheric performance of the S8 and S8 Plus versions

Weaknesses 5

  • Maintenance, insurance, and repair costs worthy of a supercar
  • Critical design flaw on the 4.0 TFSI (turbo oil strainer)
  • Rapid wear of consumables (brakes, tires) due to the weight (nearly 2 tons)
  • Aging MMI infotainment system on pre-facelift models (2009-2013)
  • Fragile retractable mechanism of the central MMI screen (plastic gears that break)

Check before buying

  • Flawless maintenance history and invoices (avoid vehicles without a service book)
  • Air suspension operation (raise/lower the vehicle, listen to the compressor)
  • On the 4.0 TFSI: check if the recall or replacement of the turbo oil strainer has been performed
  • Metallic noises on cold start (timing chain tensioners on V6/V8)
  • Operation of the central MMI screen mechanism (must extend and retract smoothly without cracking noises)
  • Brake condition (optional carbon-ceramic discs cost over €10,000 to replace)

Official recalls

Safety and Mechanical Recalls (Sources: Rappel Conso FR & NHTSA US)

  • [2022-03-23] Turbochargers (NHTSA 22V178000): Affects A8 and S8 (2013-2017) equipped with the 4.0 TFSI. The turbocharger oil supply strainer can become clogged, leading to bearing wear from lack of lubrication and turbo failure.
  • [2022-06-09 & 2020-06-12] Takata Airbags (Rappel Conso): Affects the A8. The gas generator of the NADI airbags manufactured by Takata may be altered, risking the projection of metal fragments upon deployment.
  • [2021-08-20] Diesel Emissions (Rappel Conso): Affects the A8 (and other models). Presence of unauthorized engine control software (Dieselgate).
  • [2020-07-07] Fire risk (NHTSA 20V397000): Affects A8 and S8 (2013-2018). The seal around the engine compartment can detach and come into contact with hot engine components.
  • [2020-05-08] Rear suspension (Rappel Conso 40P4): The rear axle suspension forks may be defective, risking contact between the tires and the bodywork.
  • [2019-01-28 & 2018-06-25] Fuel leak (NHTSA 19V057000 / 18V430000): Affects A8 and S8 (2013-2016). The fuel supply line to the high-pressure pump can become porous and leak.
  • [2019-01-16] Fuel rails (NHTSA 19V035000): Affects A8 (2015-2018). The left and right fuel supply rails may leak.
  • [2016-08-24] Coolant leak (NHTSA 16V619000): Affects A8 (2010-2013). A coolant valve may leak, allowing fluid to migrate to the engine control module (ECU).
  • [2013-09-23] Sunroof (NHTSA 13V448000): Affects A8 and S8 (2013-2014). The sunroof glass may not be properly secured to its frame and could detach while driving.

What sets it apart 6

safety

  • Cutting-edge Safety — The Audi A8 D4 achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 89% for adult protection and 82% for child protection, ensuring absolute peace of mind.

braking

  • Exceptional Braking — Equipped with powerful brakes and advanced assistance systems (ABS, EBD, BA), the A8 D4 offers remarkably short stopping distances for its category, ensuring perfect control in all circumstances.

handling

  • Sovereign Road Handling — Thanks to its aluminum chassis (ASF), adaptive air suspension, and standard quattro all-wheel drive, the A8 D4 demonstrates exemplary road holding stability and precision, even at high speeds, combining agility with reassuring dynamism.

comfort

  • An Oasis of Luxury and Silence — The A8 D4's interior is a benchmark for finish quality and noble materials (Valcona leather, wood trim, aluminum). Its exceptional soundproofing, enhanced by acoustic double glazing, transforms every journey into an experience of absolute serenity.

Performance

  • Exceptional Gasoline Engines — Whether it's the 3.0 TFSI V6, renowned for its smoothness and responsiveness, or the 4.0 TFSI V8 and W12 offering stratospheric performance and an captivating sound, every gasoline engine provides an incomparable driving pleasure.

reliability

  • ZF 8-speed Automatic Gearbox: Smoothness and Reliability — The ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox, universally praised for its smoothness, responsiveness, and robustness, ensures fluid gear changes and optimal power transmission, contributing to the vehicle's overall driving pleasure.

Alternatives

  • Europe / Global: Mercedes-Benz S-Class (W221 / W222) - The absolute benchmark in comfort, BMW 7 Series (F01 / G11) - More focused on driving dynamics, Porsche Panamera (970) - Sportier but less spacious.
  • Japan / Asia / North America: Lexus LS (XF40) - Unbeatable reliability and smooth operation, but less modern onboard technology and sluggish handling.

Verdict

The Audi A8 D4 is a fascinating technological showcase that offers an exceptional price-to-performance ratio on the used market due to its heavy depreciation. However, this depreciation is justified by prohibitive maintenance costs. If you are looking for a petrol engine, the 3.0 TFSI is the most reasonable and reliable choice. The V8 versions (4.0 TFSI and S8) offer supercar performance but require absolute vigilance regarding the turbo recall (oil strainer), otherwise you risk bills exceeding €10,000. Buy only with a complete maintenance history from the dealer network or a recognized specialist, and keep a comfortable financial reserve for unforeseen events (air suspension, electronics).
