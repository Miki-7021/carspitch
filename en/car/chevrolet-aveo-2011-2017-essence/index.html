Summary

The second-generation Chevrolet Aveo (T300), also known as the **Chevrolet Sonic** in North America and **Holden Barina** in Australasia, marks a complete break from the previous generation. Based on General Motors' Gamma II platform (shared with the Opel Corsa E and Mokka), it offers transformed road handling that is much more rigorous and reassuring. Its bold design, characterized by unfaired round headlights and a motorcycle-inspired instrument cluster, gives it a strong personality. While roominess and trunk volume are good performers in the versatile supermini category, the quality of the interior plastics (hard and prone to scratching) betrays its budget positioning. In Europe, the Aveo's career was abruptly interrupted in late 2014 / early 2015 following General Motors' decision to withdraw the Chevrolet brand from the Old Continent, which impacts its resale value today and requires relying on the Opel network or independent garages for maintenance.

  • Sound and reassuring road handling (Gamma II platform)
  • Original exterior design and atypical instrumentation
  • Generous roominess for the category and decent trunk (290 L)

Weaknesses 5

  • Withdrawal of the Chevrolet brand from Europe (steep depreciation, reduced maintenance network)
  • Quality of interior plastics (hard, sound hollow, scratch easily)
  • Soundproofing could be improved on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Ecotec petrol engines prone to leaks (oil and coolant)
  • 5-speed manual gearbox sometimes notchy

Check before buying

  • Expansion tank: check for the absence of 'mayonnaise' (oil/water mixture), a classic symptom of a failed oil cooler on the 1.6 (F16D) or 1.4.
  • Engine idle (Petrol): an unstable idle accompanied by a whistling sound indicates a punctured PCV membrane (valve cover needs replacement).
  • 6-speed manual gearbox (M32): present on the 1.4 Turbo and 1.3 VCDi 95 hp. Listen for any whistling in 5th and 6th gear (fragile gearbox bearings).
  • Running gear: clunking over bumps indicating premature wear of the anti-roll bar drop links.
  • Recall history: imperatively verify that the Takata airbags recall has been performed.

Verdict

The Chevrolet Aveo II (T300) is a supermini that suffered from the abandonment of its brand in Europe, making it an excellent financial deal today. Its chassis is modern and its equipment is often comprehensive. However, its petrol engines (Ecotec / F16D family) require special vigilance: fluid leaks (water, oil) and PCV valve issues are commonplace. The 1.6 engine (F16D) is particularly one to watch for its head gasket issues and excessive oil consumption. Buy it with full knowledge of the facts, favor a 1.2 or 1.4 model with a clear history, and do not hesitate to negotiate the price firmly by arguing the disappearance of the Chevrolet network.
