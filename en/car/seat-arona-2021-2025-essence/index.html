Summary

Seat Arona I Facelift (KJ)

Initially launched in 2017, the Seat Arona received a mid-life facelift in 2021. Based on the Volkswagen Group's MQB-A0 platform (shared with the Ibiza, VW T-Cross, and Skoda Kamiq), this B-segment urban SUV took advantage of this update to correct its main flaw: the perceived quality of its interior. The dashboard was completely redesigned with soft-touch materials and the integration of the new MIB3 infotainment system.

Mechanically, phase 2 (2021-2025) definitively abandoned diesel (the 1.6 TDI having been discontinued in 2020) and offers **no hybridization** (neither MHEV nor PHEV), as the MQB-A0 platform has not been electrified by Seat. The offering therefore focuses exclusively on very mature and reliable petrol (TSI) engines, as well as a CNG (TGI) variant in some markets. It is a rational, dynamic, and overall very reliable model thanks to the use of widely proven components within the VAG group.

Strengths 5

  • Greatly improved interior finish (soft-touch plastics on phase 2)
  • General ergonomics and responsive MIB3 multimedia system
  • Dynamic road handling (especially in FR trim)
  • Generous boot volume for the category (400 liters, except TGI at 282 liters)
  • Excellent overall mechanical reliability (EA211 evo engines have matured)

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of mild-hybrid (MHEV) or full-hybrid technology
  • DSG7 (DQ200) gearbox still a bit jerky at low speeds and sensitive to wear in urban driving
  • Imperfect sound insulation on the highway (wind and road noise)
  • Firm suspension, especially with 18-inch wheels (FR trim)
  • Occasional electronic bugs on the central screen (wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto)

Check before buying

  • **Infotainment system:** Check that the screen does not freeze and that OTA (Over The Air) updates have been performed.
  • **DSG7 gearbox:** During the test drive, check for the absence of metallic noises when shifting gears (1st to 2nd) and the absence of juddering when starting on a hill.
  • **Water pump / Water housing:** On 1.0 TSI engines approaching 80,000 - 100,000 km, check for any traces of coolant (slow leak).
  • **Tire wear:** The front axle can be demanding on tires on 110/115 and 150 hp versions if driving is dynamic.

Verdict

The facelifted Seat Arona (2021-2025) is an **excellent purchase choice** in the urban SUV category. By correcting the hard plastics of the first phase, Seat has made its model much more welcoming. The absence of hybrid or diesel engines may deter some buyers, but in return, it guarantees a very high level of mechanical reliability, as the 1.0 TSI and 1.5 TSI engines are now free of their early defects.

**The expert's choice:** The **1.0 TSI 110 or 115 hp with manual gearbox** represents the perfect compromise. If you opt for the DSG7 automatic gearbox, favor mixed use (road/highway) rather than 100% urban to preserve the dry clutches.
