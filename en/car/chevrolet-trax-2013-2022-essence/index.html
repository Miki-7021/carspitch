Summary

The first-generation Chevrolet Trax is an urban SUV (B-segment) based on the GM Gamma II platform. It is the strict technical twin of the Opel Mokka A and the Buick Encore. Designed to be a global vehicle, it was sold in Europe until Chevrolet's withdrawal from the European market (late 2015), but continued its career in North America, Asia, and South America until 2022.

It received a major facelift in 2017, bringing a modernized front fascia, a much higher quality dashboard, and an updated infotainment system (Apple CarPlay / Android Auto integration). While it does not shine with its driving dynamics or trunk volume, it offers an appreciated high driving position, a compact size ideal for the city, and an optional all-wheel drive (AWD) transmission that is rare in this category. Its overall reliability is average, conditional on rigorous maintenance, particularly on the 1.4 Turbo engine.

Strengths 5

  • Very compact size (4.25 m) making city parking easier
  • Appreciated high driving position
  • Availability of a true all-wheel drive (AWD) transmission on a small SUV
  • Clear improvement in interior quality and infotainment after the 2017 facelift (CarPlay/Android Auto)
  • Mechanical spare parts easily found (shared with Opel Mokka and GM range)

Weaknesses 5

  • Very basic interior plastic quality on pre-2017 models
  • Limited trunk volume (356 liters) compared to the competition
  • Soundproofing could be improved at high speeds (wind and road noise)
  • Reliability of the 1.4 Turbo engine ancillaries (cooling system, PCV system)
  • 6-speed automatic gearbox sometimes slow and generating jerks if not serviced

Check before buying

  • On the 1.4 Turbo: Presence of coolant leaks (hot smell, pink/orange traces around the water pump and the water housing on the driver's side).
  • On the 1.4 Turbo: Remove the engine oil dipstick while idling. If a strong suction is felt or if the engine starts to stumble heavily, the PCV membrane of the valve cover is dead.
  • Front suspension: Listen for clunking noises on rough roads (lower suspension control arms affected by recalls for defective welds).
  • AWD transmission: Check for the absence of growling noises during tight turns at low speed (sign of a tired transfer case).
  • Electronics: Ensure that all warning chimes (seatbelt, turn signals) work through the speakers (known software issue).

Official recalls

The Chevrolet Trax has been the subject of several important safety recalls, notably concerning Takata airbags and suspensions.

  • [2024-10-18 / 2026-01-28] Takata Airbags (Rappel Conso France): The propellant in the driver's airbag inflator may degrade over time. In the event of deployment, metal fragments could be projected and seriously injure the occupants. Critical recall to be checked imperatively.
  • [2019-09-12 / 2019-04-18] Suspension control arms (NHTSA 19V652000 / 19V312000): On 2015-2019 models, a weld on one or both front lower suspension arms may be defective and break, resulting in a loss of control.
  • [2018-11-01] SDM Airbag Module (NHTSA 18V774000): On certain 2019 models, the sensing diagnostic module (SDM) may have remained in "factory mode", preventing the deployment of airbags in the event of a crash.
  • [2016-06-10 / 2016-04-28] Radio Software (NHTSA 16V422000 / 16V257000): On 2013-2016 models equipped with the "Bring Your Own Media" radio, a software bug may prevent the sounding of warning chimes (key left in ignition, seatbelt, etc.).
  • [2015-03-13] Electric power steering (NHTSA 15V146000): On certain 2015 models, possible loss of electric steering assist, increasing steering effort.
  • [2015-03-20] Tire labels (NHTSA 15V162000): On 2015 models, incorrect information on the tire pressure label.

Verdict

The first-generation Chevrolet Trax is an honest urban SUV, whose overall reliability is average for the category. Its main asset on the used market is its price, often lower than that of its direct competitors or its twin, the Opel Mokka.

However, the most common engine (the 1.4 Turbo petrol) requires particular vigilance. While the engine block itself is robust, its ancillaries (plastic cooling system, PCV valve, turbo) are prone to frequent and documented failures. A clear maintenance history and a meticulous inspection of these points are mandatory before purchase.

Strongly favor the facelifted models (from 2017 onwards) for their significantly more rewarding interior and up-to-date onboard technology. Also ensure that critical recalls (notably the Takata airbags and suspension control arms) have been properly carried out at a dealership.
