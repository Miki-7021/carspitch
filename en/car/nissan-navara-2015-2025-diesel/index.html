Summary

The fourth-generation Nissan Navara (D23 / NP300) marks a turning point in the pick-up segment by introducing (on Double Cab versions) a multi-link rear suspension with coil springs, offering ride comfort close to that of an SUV while maintaining a towing capacity of 3.5 tonnes. It served as the technical basis for the Renault Alaskan and the Mercedes-Benz Classe X. Robust and versatile, it corrected the serious chassis problems of the previous generation (D40). A major technical update in 2019 brought rear disc brakes, reinforced suspension, and engines updated to Euro 6d-Temp standards. Overall rating: An excellent comfort/utility compromise, penalized in Europe by the constraints of emission control systems (AdBlue/FAP) in urban use.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional ride comfort for a pick-up (multi-link rear suspension on Double Cab)
  • Towing capacity maintained at 3,500 kg
  • High-performing and relatively economical 2.3 dCi bi-turbo engine (190 hp)
  • Smooth and reliable 7-speed automatic gearbox (Jatco)
  • Chassis much more resistant to corrosion and stress than the D40 generation
  • Highly relevant 2019 update (rear disc brakes, increased payload)

Weaknesses 5

  • Temperamental emission control system (FAP, EGR, AdBlue) if the vehicle is driven too much in the city
  • Rear suspension on pre-2019 models sags under heavy load (Double Cab)
  • Quality of some interior plastics is very utilitarian and prone to scratching
  • Heavy steering at low speeds (hydraulic assistance)
  • Average soundproofing at high speeds

Check before buying

  • AdBlue system (Euro 6): Check for the absence of engine warning lights related to the SCR system (AdBlue pump or injector often at fault).
  • Particulate Filter (FAP): Ensure that regenerations occur correctly (driving history, avoid former urban construction vehicles).
  • Rear suspension: On pre-2019 Double Cab models, check for sagging of the coil springs if the vehicle has frequently been loaded.
  • Underbody and Chassis: Inspect for superficial rust or impacts related to intensive off-road use.
  • Transfer case and axles: Verify that the axle and transfer case oil changes have been performed every 60,000 km.

Official recalls

Data from the official Rappel Conso database (France) concerning the Navara and other Nissan commercial vehicles from the same period:

  • [2022-07-04] Takata Airbag (Codes R1803, PS8B0): Affects Navara, Almera, Patrol, X-Trail, etc. A defect in the passenger airbag gas generator can cause uncontrolled inflation and the release of metal fragments. Risk of serious injury.
  • [2021-05-31] Child safety lock (Rear doors): Affects Navara, Juke, Qashqai, etc. (right-hand drive vehicles). The child safety lock may be defective, allowing the door to be opened from the inside.
  • [2021-05-28] Brake caliper mountings (Code PN0C0): Affects the NV250 (light commercial vehicle). Rear brake caliper mountings insufficiently tightened, risk of rear wheels detaching.
  • [2019-10-04] Fuel tank (Code PN9B9): Affects the NV300. Manufacturing defect that could lead to a fuel leak on the road (fire risk).
  • [2019-07-12] Handbrake cable (Code PN9B3): Affects the NV300. The handbrake cable stop may be incorrectly installed.

What sets it apart 7

Safety

  • Advanced Safety Features — The Nissan Navara D23 achieved a maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, ensuring optimal protection for adult and child occupants.

Comfort

  • Exceptional Driving Comfort — Experience SUV-like ride comfort thanks to its innovative multi-link rear suspension (on Double Cab models), providing a smooth and pleasant driving experience.

Performance

  • Powerful and Refined Engine — Its 190 hp 2.3 dCi bi-turbo engine, paired with a smooth and reliable 7-speed automatic transmission, delivers dynamic performance and great flexibility.

Practicality

  • Robust Utility Capabilities — With an impressive towing capacity of 3,500 kg and increased payload (post-2019 models), the Navara is ready for all professional tasks.

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability and Robustness — Benefit from a much more resistant chassis and a mechanically very solid 2.3 dCi engine, ensuring long-term durability.

tenue de route

  • Dynamic Road Handling — The Navara offers above-average road handling for a pick-up, with precise steering and predictable behavior, similar to an SUV.

Economy

  • Optimized Fuel Efficiency — The 2.3 dCi bi-turbo engine combines performance with fuel economy, offering controlled fuel consumption for a vehicle in its category.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Toyota Hilux (absolute benchmark for reliability), Ford Ranger (market leader, more technological), Isuzu D-Max (very robust, pure utility oriented), Mitsubishi L200 (excellent Super Select 4x4 system).
  • Americas / Asia: Toyota Tacoma / Hilux, Chevrolet Colorado, Ford Ranger, Isuzu D-Max.
  • Technical clones: Renault Alaskan (technically identical, different design), Mercedes-Benz Classe X (identical base but widened tracks, premium soundproofing and V6 available).

Verdict

The Nissan Navara D23 is an excellent choice in the mid-size pick-up category. It has managed to erase the bad reputation of its predecessor (D40) regarding chassis fragility. Its 2.3 dCi engine (of Renault origin) is mechanically very solid, although the emission control peripherals require regular highway trips to avoid costly breakdowns (FAP/AdBlue). Strongly favor facelifted models from late 2019 onwards: they benefit from rear disc brakes, a corrected rear suspension to better support loads, and a modernized multimedia system.
