Summary

The Mercedes-Benz S-Class (W223 generation) represents the pinnacle of luxury and technology within the brand with the star. Launched at the end of 2020, it marks a major technological breakthrough with the introduction of the MBUX 2.0 system, a huge central OLED screen, flush door handles, and rear-wheel steering (up to 10 degrees). Although the industry is shifting towards electric (a role assigned to the EQS), the W223 retains a very high-level combustion engine offering. **The diesel engine (OM656 inline 6-cylinder)** holds a prominent place, particularly in Europe, offering remarkable smoothness, massive torque, and formidable efficiency for high-mileage drivers, making this limousine capable of crossing Europe on a single tank of fuel. While the mechanicals are robust, the onboard electronic complexity requires special vigilance.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional OM656 diesel engine: quiet, torquey, and incredibly frugal
  • Imperial ride comfort (AIRMATIC air suspension as standard)
  • Stunning maneuverability thanks to rear-wheel steering (essential option)
  • World-class cabin soundproofing
  • Highly efficient Digital Light lighting technology

Weaknesses 5

  • Frequent electronic bugs on the MBUX system (especially 2020-2021 models)
  • Temperamental flush door handles (freezing or sensor failure)
  • Reduced trunk volume on plug-in hybrid versions (PHEV)
  • All-touch ergonomics (steering wheel controls and climate control) requiring an adaptation period
  • Steep depreciation in the first few years

Check before buying

  • Complete history of software updates (MBUX and ECUs)
  • Smooth and noise-free operation of the retractable door handles
  • Absence of error messages related to the 48V system (on MHEV petrol engines)
  • Condition of the DPF and regeneration history on diesel models (OM656) if the mileage is low
  • Rear tire wear (rear-wheel steering can accelerate wear if the alignment is incorrect)

Verdict

The Mercedes S-Class W223 remains the undisputed queen of limousines. If you drive more than 25,000 km per year, **the diesel engine (S 350 d or S 400 d) is the absolute choice**. The OM656 engine is a masterpiece of reliability and efficiency that alone justifies the purchase of this model. However, the electronic complexity of the W223 requires caution: favor a model from 2022 onwards (the 2020-2021 models experienced the teething problems of the MBUX 2.0 system) and demand a crystal-clear maintenance history within the official network. An extended warranty is highly recommended to cover potential issues related to comfort equipment (flush handles, air suspension, screens).
