Summary

The Mercedes-Benz S-Class (W223 generation) represents the pinnacle of luxury and technology within the brand with the star. Launched at the end of 2020, it marks a major technological breakthrough with the introduction of the MBUX 2.0 system, a huge central OLED screen, flush door handles, and rear-wheel steering (up to 10 degrees). Although the industry is shifting towards electric (a role assigned to the EQS), the W223 retains a very high-level combustion engine offering. The diesel engine (OM656 inline 6-cylinder) holds a prominent place, particularly in Europe, offering remarkable smoothness, massive torque, and formidable efficiency for high-mileage drivers, making this limousine capable of crossing Europe on a single tank of fuel. While the mechanicals are robust, the onboard electronic complexity requires special vigilance.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional OM656 diesel engine: quiet, torquey, and incredibly frugal
  • Imperial ride comfort (AIRMATIC air suspension as standard)
  • Stunning maneuverability thanks to rear-wheel steering (essential option)
  • World-class cabin soundproofing
  • Highly efficient Digital Light lighting technology

Weaknesses 5

  • Frequent electronic bugs on the MBUX system (especially 2020-2021 models)
  • Temperamental flush door handles (freezing or sensor failure)
  • Reduced trunk volume on plug-in hybrid versions (PHEV)
  • All-touch ergonomics (steering wheel controls and climate control) requiring an adaptation period
  • Steep depreciation in the first few years

Check before buying

  • Complete history of software updates (MBUX and ECUs)
  • Smooth and noise-free operation of the retractable door handles
  • Absence of error messages related to the 48V system (on MHEV petrol engines)
  • Condition of the DPF and regeneration history on diesel models (OM656) if the mileage is low
  • Rear tire wear (rear-wheel steering can accelerate wear if the alignment is incorrect)

Official recalls

  • 06/10/2023 (S-Class 223): The 12-volt ground wire screw in the trunk may be defective, potentially leading to the loss of stability program (ESP) functions or a fire risk.
  • 10/05/2022 (S-Class 223 PHEV, C-Class, EQS, GLC): Incorrect material composition of the Mode 2 charging cable (domestic plug), risk of insufficient insulation resistance leading to a fire or electric shock risk.
  • 28/01/2022 (S-Class, E-Class, G-Class, GLC, etc.): The electrical connector for the side impact sensors might not be properly locked, delaying airbag deployment (mainly concerns the previous BR 222 generation, but to be checked on model year transitions).
  • 28/05/2021 (S-Class, C-Class, A-Class, GLC, etc.): The polycarbonate roof panel between the windshield and the panoramic roof may not have been properly bonded, risk of detachment while driving.

What sets it apart 6

reliability

  • Reliability and Efficiency of the OM656 Diesel Engine — The OM656 in-line 6-cylinder diesel engine is an engineering masterpiece, renowned for its exceptional robustness and impressive fuel consumption, ideal for high-mileage drivers. It offers remarkable autonomy, allowing you to cross Europe on a single tank.

comfort

  • Imperial Ride Comfort — Thanks to its standard AIRMATIC air suspension and world-class cabin soundproofing, the S-Class W223 offers an unparalleled travel experience of luxury and serenity, transforming every journey into an oasis of well-being.

safety

  • Exemplary Safety — The Mercedes S-Class W223 achieved the maximum rating in Euro NCAP tests, ensuring optimal protection for all occupants thanks to advanced passive and active safety systems.

braking

  • Benchmark Braking Performance — Despite its size, the S-Class W223 demonstrates exceptional braking performance, powerful and progressive, ensuring stable and safe deceleration in all circumstances.

handling

  • Surprising Handling and Agility — Equipped with advanced suspension systems and the option of rear-wheel steering, the S-Class W223 combines imperial high-speed stability with astonishing agility for its class, offering absolute confidence to the driver.

Performance

  • Diesel Performance and Smoothness — The OM656 diesel engine delivers immense torque and remarkable smoothness, ensuring strong acceleration and vigorous overtakes, all within an impressive operating silence, typical of a limousine of this caliber.

Alternatives

  • North America: Lexus LS 500 (superior reliability, no diesel), Genesis G90, BMW 7 Series.

Verdict

The Mercedes S-Class W223 remains the undisputed queen of limousines. If you drive more than 25,000 km per year, the diesel engine (S 350 d or S 400 d) is the absolute choice. The OM656 engine is a masterpiece of reliability and efficiency that alone justifies the purchase of this model. However, the electronic complexity of the W223 requires caution: favor a model from 2022 onwards (the 2020-2021 models experienced the teething problems of the MBUX 2.0 system) and demand a crystal-clear maintenance history within the official network. An extended warranty is highly recommended to cover potential issues related to comfort equipment (flush handles, air suspension, screens).
