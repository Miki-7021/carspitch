Summary

The Renault Captur II (based on the CMF-B platform, shared with the Clio V) marks a major qualitative leap compared to the first generation. Gone are the hard plastics; the cabin becomes one of the best-finished in the B-SUV category. It retains its practical aspects (sliding bench seat) while modernizing its technological offering (Easy Link).

Diesel Focus: Although demand today is for petrol and hybrid, the Captur II was launched with the indestructible 1.5 dCi (renamed Blue dCi with the addition of AdBlue). This engine had a very short career on this model (withdrawn late 2020 / early 2021) due to anti-pollution standards (Euro 6d-Full) and the decline in diesel sales in the urban segment. A significant facelift (new front end, Nouvel'R logo, chrome deletion) occurred in spring 2024, by which time diesel had already disappeared from the catalog for a long time.

Strengths 6

  • Significantly improved interior finish quality (soft-touch materials)
  • Excellent modularity (rear bench sliding by 16 cm)
  • Extremely low fuel consumption of the 1.5 Blue dCi on roads/motorways
  • Driving pleasure and reliability of the 1.3 TCe petrol engine
  • LPG (ECO-G) offer is very economically relevant
  • Sound and safe road handling

Weaknesses 6

  • Very short diesel career (difficult to find on the used market recently)
  • Capricious AdBlue system on the 1.5 Blue dCi (common industry issue)
  • Reliability of the dog-clutch gearbox on early E-Tech models (2020-2021)
  • Recurring bugs in the Easy Link multimedia system (black screens, reboots)
  • Slightly firm comfort with 18-inch wheels
  • Poor 3/4 rear visibility

Check before buying

  • On the 1.5 Blue dCi: Presence of 'Check Anti-pollution System' or 'AdBlue' warning lights (sign of a faulty pump or a clogged urea injector).
  • On the 1.5 Blue dCi: History of the previous owner's journeys (avoid if the vehicle has only been driven in the city).
  • On E-Tech models: Smoothness of gear changes between thermal and electric modes. No 'Electrical fault danger' message should appear.
  • On EDC gearboxes: Absence of juddering when starting in first gear or reverse.
  • Electronics: Test all functions of the Easy Link central screen and check that the GPS locates quickly.

Verdict

The Renault Captur II is an excellent urban SUV, mature and well-finished.

Regarding the 1.5 Blue dCi: It is a niche choice today. The engine block itself is one of the most reliable on the market (K9K), capable of covering hundreds of thousands of kilometers. However, Euro 6d standards have imposed an AdBlue system that tolerates short trips and low temperatures very poorly. Buy it only if you drive more than 20,000 km/year, mainly on highways. Otherwise, the risk of DPF clogging and AdBlue tank failure (costly repair > €1000) will negate all fuel savings.

For 80% of buyers, the 1.0 TCe (especially in LPG) or 1.3 TCe versions are much safer, more reliable choices, and better suited to future traffic restrictions (ZFE).
