Summary

The **first-generation Hyundai Kona (OS)** is an urban SUV (B-segment) with a bold design that marked a stylistic break for the Korean brand. Launched in late 2017, it stands out with an exceptionally wide range of powertrains, covering gasoline, diesel, hybrid (HEV), and 100% electric (EV).

A **facelift** occurred in late 2020 (MY2021), bringing a smoothed front end, the introduction of 48V mild-hybridization on some engines, a technological update (larger screens), and the arrival of the sporty N-Line trim, followed by the radical Kona N version.

Overall, the Kona OS is a reliable vehicle, supported by the 5-year unlimited mileage manufacturer's warranty, although some transmissions (DCT7) and the first generation of EV batteries experienced issues.

Strengths 6

  • Original and distinctive design
  • Ergonomics of physical controls (climate control, shortcuts)
  • Excellent reliability of the Hybrid (HEV) version and the 1.0 T-GDI
  • 5-year manufacturer's warranty (reassuring for used cars)
  • Rich technological equipment from intermediate trims
  • Remarkable efficiency of the electric version (64 kWh)

Weaknesses 5

  • Limited trunk volume for the category (361 liters)
  • Average rear passenger space (legroom)
  • Firm suspension, especially with 18-inch wheels
  • Hard interior plastics and prone to scratches (pre-facelift)
  • Driving pleasure of the DCT7 dual-clutch gearbox at low speeds

Check before buying

  • Behavior of the DCT7 gearbox (1.6 T-GDI / 1.6 CRDi): absence of juddering or slipping on startup.
  • Recall history for the Kona Electric (battery change imperative if affected).
  • Oil level on 2.0 MPI engines (imports or North American market).
  • Clutch condition on 1.0 T-GDI manual versions (high biting point).
  • Multimedia system operation (some cases of frozen screens requiring an update or reset).

Official recalls

  • - **High Voltage Battery (2020-2021)**: Risk of internal short circuit and fire on LG Chem cells of Kona EV produced between 2018 and 2020. Battery pack replacement or BMS software update. - **Piston Rings (2021 - North Amer.)**: 2.0 Nu MPI engines. Defective heat treatment of the rings leading to cylinder damage. Engine replacement if necessary. - **IEB Braking System (2020)**: Kona EV and HEV. Software defect in the integrated electronic braking system that could reduce braking assistance. Software update. - **Fuel Filter (2020)**: On some diesel models (CRDi), risk of fuel leakage at the filter.

Verdict

The first-generation Hyundai Kona is an excellent used car choice, provided you carefully select its engine. For gasoline, the **1.0 T-GDI** is the most rational and reliable choice for daily use. The **Hybrid (HEV)** version is the most recommendable of the entire range for its reliability/consumption compromise. The **1.6 T-GDI** offers good performance, but its DCT7 gearbox requires a thorough test drive before purchase. Finally, for the North American market, the 2.0 MPI should be avoided if the manufacturer's recall has not been performed.
