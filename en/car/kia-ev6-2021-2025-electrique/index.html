Summary

The Kia EV6, launched in 2021, marks a major step for the Korean manufacturer as its first all-electric vehicle based on the dedicated E-GMP (Electric Global Modular Platform). Positioned as a family crossover with a bold and futuristic design, it stands out with its dynamic lines, spacious interior, and cutting-edge technology. The EV6 is a key model in Kia's electrification strategy, offering high performance, competitive range, and ultra-fast charging capability thanks to its 800V architecture. It was quickly praised for its driving pleasure and value for money in the premium EV segment. To date (2021-2025), it has not undergone a major facelift, retaining its initial design.

Strengths 8

  • Distinctive and modern design
  • E-GMP 800V platform: ultra-fast charging (10-80% in ~18 min)
  • Competitive range for the category
  • Generous cabin and interior space
  • Ride comfort and sound insulation
  • Dynamic performance, especially in AWD and GT versions
  • Advanced onboard technology (V2L, AR head-up display)
  • Generous manufacturer's warranty (7 years/150,000 km)

Weaknesses 6

  • Limited rear visibility
  • High purchase price (like most EVs in this category)
  • Some initial software bugs (largely corrected)
  • Potentially rapid tire wear on powerful versions
  • Infotainment interface sometimes complex for the uninitiated
  • Lack of a rear wiper

Check before buying

  • Recall history (especially ICCU and parking brake) and proof of their execution
  • Battery State of Health (SoH) via diagnostics or the vehicle interface
  • Charging system operation (port, cable, V2L if applicable)
  • Condition of tires and brakes, especially on AWD/GT versions
  • Operation of all electronic equipment (infotainment, driving aids)
  • Absence of abnormal noises (motors, suspensions, HVAC)
  • Check for the original 12V battery and its condition

Official recalls

Official Kia EV6 Recalls (2021-2025)

  • February 23, 2023 (Europe, North America): Integrated Charging Control Unit (ICCU). Concerns EV6 models produced between 2021 and 2023. The ICCU may overheat and stop charging the 12V battery, leading to a loss of motive power. Remedy: Software update and inspection/replacement of the ICCU if necessary.
  • February 17, 2022 (Europe, North America): Parking brake actuator. Concerns EV6 models produced in 2022. The parking brake may not engage or disengage correctly. Remedy: Software update.

Verdict

The Kia EV6 is an excellent choice in the used electric vehicle market. Its E-GMP platform gives it major advantages such as ultra-fast charging and excellent interior space. Overall reliability is very good for such a recent and technologically advanced model. Initial issues, mainly software-related or linked to the ICCU, have been addressed by recalls and updates.

Buying advice:

  • For urban/suburban use: The Standard Range (58 kWh) RWD version is sufficient and more affordable.
  • For versatile use and long journeys: The Long Range (77.4 kWh) RWD version offers the best range/price compromise. The AWD version adds extra power and traction without significantly compromising range.
  • For performance enthusiasts: The GT version is a true powerhouse, but its price and running costs (tires, brakes) should be considered.

Ensure all official recalls have been performed and check the 12V battery condition and service history. It's a vehicle that ages well and remains very relevant in the used car market.
