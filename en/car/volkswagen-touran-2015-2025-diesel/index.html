III (5T) (2015–2025) · 2015 – 2025

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

Summary

The third-generation Volkswagen Touran (Type 5T), launched in 2015, is a compact family MPV based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MQB platform. It succeeds a model already highly regarded for its practicality and robustness. This new iteration brings a more modern and dynamic aesthetic, improved spaciousness, notably thanks to a longer wheelbase, and up-to-date technological features. Available in 5 or 7-seater configurations, the Touran III positions itself as a versatile vehicle, ideal for families seeking space, comfort, and a perceived build quality superior to the average in its category. It has benefited from continuous updates rather than a major facelift, integrating new engines (like the 1.5 TSI Evo) and improved infotainment systems over the years. Overall, it is a reliable and well-designed vehicle, but like any model, it has specific points of attention related to its engines and usage.

Strengths 6

  • Exemplary spaciousness and modularity (5 or 7 seats)
  • Good build quality and interior finish
  • Safe and comfortable road handling (MQB platform)
  • Wide choice of efficient and powerful engines
  • Good sound insulation and overall comfort
  • Solid resale value

Weaknesses 5

  • Somewhat austere exterior design for some
  • High new purchase price and expensive options
  • AdBlue system on diesels can be a source of breakdowns if poorly maintained
  • Some cases of "jerking" or "hesitation" on early versions of the 1.5 TSI Evo
  • EGR valve fouling on diesels with intensive urban use

Check before buying

  • Complete service history and invoices (especially for diesels and DSG)
  • AdBlue system operation (warning lights, consumption, breakdown history)
  • Condition of the DPF (Diesel) or GPF (1.5 TSI Gasoline) if predominantly urban use
  • Absence of leaks from the water pump (TSI and TDI)
  • Abnormal noises from the flywheel (especially on diesel manual/DSG models)
  • Operation of the air conditioning and infotainment system
  • Tire and brake wear, wheel alignment

Verdict

The Volkswagen Touran III is an excellent choice for families looking for a reliable, spacious, and well-finished compact MPV. Its MQB platform provides healthy road handling and a good level of comfort. Overall reliability is very good, as evidenced by ADAC and TÜV rankings.

**For gasoline engines (TSI)**, the 1.4 TSI and 1.5 TSI Evo are the most recommendable for their versatility and efficiency. The 1.5 TSI experienced some early issues (jerking) which were corrected by software updates. The smaller 1.0 and 1.2 TSI are sufficient for urban and suburban use.

**For diesel engines (TDI)**, the 2.0 TDI (150 or 190 hp) is the ideal choice for high-mileage drivers and families who make long journeys. It is robust and powerful. The 1.6 TDI is more economical but less comfortable when loaded on the highway. It is crucial to monitor the AdBlue system and the EGR valve on all diesels, especially if the vehicle has been used predominantly in the city. Rigorous maintenance is the key to their longevity.

**Buying advice:** Prioritize a model with a clear service history, ideally from Volkswagen. Check that manufacturer recalls have been carried out. The Touran III is a safe investment that will serve you well for many years.
