F45/F46 Active/Gran Tourer · 2014 – 2021

NEGOTIATE Reliability 78/100 — bon

All BMW models

Summary

The BMW 2 Series Active Tourer (F45 - 5-seater) and Gran Tourer (F46 - 7-seater) mark a historic turning point for the Bavarian brand: they are BMW's first MPVs and the first models based on the UKL2 front-wheel-drive platform (shared with MINI). Although shunned by purists, they have been very successful with families thanks to their spaciousness, premium finish, and surprising dynamism for the segment. The facelift (LCI) in March 2018 brought minor aesthetic changes, an infotainment update, and the introduction of dual-clutch gearboxes (DKG) on some petrol engines. The diesel engines (B37 and B47), central to this analysis, proved to be significantly more reliable than the old N47 generation, although they were affected by the infamous massive recall of EGR valve coolers.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent spaciousness and modularity (especially 7-seater Gran Tourer)
  • Very good interior finish, worthy of the BMW badge
  • B47 diesel engines (218d/220d) very powerful, economical, and with finally reliable timing chain
  • Dynamic road handling for an MPV (incisive front end)
  • Intuitive and responsive iDrive infotainment system

Weaknesses 5

  • Recurrent and dangerous EGR cooler problem on diesels
  • Broken automatic gearbox selector spring (annoying and costly error message at the dealership)
  • Rapid wear of front suspension wishbone silent blocks (significant weight on the front axle)
  • Suspension comfort a bit firm, especially with 18-inch wheels or M Sport trim
  • Very thick windshield pillars (A-pillars), obstructing 3/4 front visibility

Check before buying

  • Recall history: Absolutely check if the EGR cooler recall has been performed (diesels).
  • Coolant level: Open the hood when cold. A low level on a diesel is a major warning sign.
  • Dashboard error message: Test P-R-N-D shifting. If a "Drivetrain malfunction" or "Secure vehicle" message appears, the selector spring is broken.
  • Front axle: Listen for clunking over speed bumps (worn wishbone silent blocks or anti-roll bar links).
  • Automatic gearbox maintenance: Demand invoices for Aisin gearbox oil changes if the vehicle has more than 80,000 km.

Official recalls

  • EGR Cooler (2018, 2019, 2022, 2023): Massive global recall. Glycol leak into the intake potentially causing manifold fire. Affects almost all 4 and 3-cylinder diesels (B47/B37) produced between 2014 and 2019.
  • Crankshaft (2015): Complete engine replacement on some 218i (B38) models produced before April 2015 due to a thrust bearing defect.
  • Takata Airbags (2020): Replacement of the driver's airbag gas generator on certain models.
  • Seat belts / ISOFIX (2016): Poor welding of ISOFIX anchors on certain models produced in 2016.

What sets it apart 7

Practicality

  • Exceptional Space and Modularity — Designed for family life, the 2 Series Active/Gran Tourer offers generous interior space and smart modularity, especially with the 7-seater version, ensuring optimal room for all passengers and their luggage.

Performance

  • Dynamic and Refined Diesel Engines — Enjoy the power and flexibility of BMW diesel engines (notably the 218d and 220d). They provide excellent driving pleasure, strong acceleration, and great versatility for all your journeys.

Economy

  • Efficiency and Controlled Consumption — The diesel engines of the 2 Series Active/Gran Tourer are renowned for their fuel efficiency. They combine performance with low fuel consumption, allowing you to cover long distances with a controlled budget.

Reliability

  • Proven Reliability of B47 Diesel Engines — The B47 diesel units (218d/220d) are praised for their robustness and optimized design, offering long-term peace of mind and durability befitting BMW's expectations.

Safety

  • High-Level Passive Safety — The BMW 2 Series Active/Gran Tourer achieved the maximum rating in Euro NCAP, demonstrating exemplary protection for its occupants.

Braking

  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Benefit from a high-performance braking system, offering excellent progression and short stopping distances, ensuring optimal safety in all circumstances.

Handling

  • Dynamic and Precise Road Handling — Despite its family vocation, this BMW minivan surprises with its agility. Its precise steering and controlled road holding provide an engaging and safe driving experience.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Mercedes-Benz B-Class (W246 / W247) (the direct rival, more comfort-oriented), Volkswagen Touran (more spacious, less premium), Ford C-Max (excellent chassis, end of life in 2019), Renault Scénic IV (attractive design, average rear spaciousness).
  • Asia: Honda Jade, Toyota Prius Alpha (specific markets, very reliable hybrids).

Verdict

The BMW 2 Series Active/Gran Tourer is an excellent family MPV that has retained the brand's dynamic DNA. For diesel engines, the 2.0L unit (218d/220d) is the top choice: it eliminates the timing chain problems of the previous generation and offers a high level of driving pleasure. However, buying a used diesel model is strictly conditional on verifying the history of the EGR valve recall. If the cooler has been replaced with the corrected version and the automatic gearbox has been serviced, it is an extremely robust vehicle. Avoid diesel models used exclusively in the city, and budget for minor peripheral defects (gear lever spring, front silent blocks).
