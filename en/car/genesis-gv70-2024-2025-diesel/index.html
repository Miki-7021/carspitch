Summary

The **Genesis GV70 I Facelift (code name JK1)**, introduced for the 2024/2025 model year depending on the market, represents the mid-cycle update of the luxury compact SUV from Hyundai's premium division. This facelift focuses on a spectacular technological upgrade, highlighted by the integration of a massive 27-inch OLED screen combining instrumentation and infotainment, as well as the adoption of MLA (Micro Lens Array) matrix headlights. Mechanically, the engines are carried over, including the **2.2 Diesel (Smartstream D2.2)** block, which remains relevant in European and Asian markets for high-mileage drivers, although it has disappeared from North America. The GV70 is positioned as an exotic, ultra-equipped, and very well-finished alternative to the German trio (X3, GLC, Q5). Its overall reliability is supported by a generous manufacturer warranty (often 5 years unlimited mileage in Europe), although its technological youth requires some vigilance regarding the onboard electronics.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional interior finish quality, worthy of the segment above
  • Soundproofing and ride comfort (especially with the camera-based adaptive suspension)
  • Very rich standard technological equipment (magnificent 27-inch OLED screen)
  • Reassuring manufacturer warranty (5 years unlimited mileage in Europe)
  • Torquey and well-soundproofed 2.2 Diesel engine, ideal for long trips

Weaknesses 5

  • Very high fuel consumption of the petrol engines (2.5T and 3.5T)
  • Diesel engine unsuited for short urban trips (risk of DPF/EGR fouling)
  • Rear seat space and trunk volume in the lower average of the category
  • Genesis dealer network still sparse in Europe
  • High weight impacting agility and wear of consumables (brakes/tires)

Check before buying

  • Software update history (crucial for the OLED screen and transmission management)
  • On the 2.2 Diesel: check for the absence of an engine light related to the anti-pollution system (DPF/AdBlue) during the test drive
  • Check the tightness of the fuel lines (subject of a major recall)
  • Smooth operation of the gear selector dial (Shift-by-Wire)
  • Tire wear, particularly on the V6 and Electrified versions

Verdict

The facelifted Genesis GV70 (2024-2025) is a formidable and refreshing alternative to traditional premium SUVs. If your choice falls on the **2.2 Diesel**, it is an excellent decision for high-mileage drivers: the Smartstream block is proven, torquey, and well-insulated. However, it requires regular highway trips to avoid the woes of fouling (DPF/EGR) inherent to modern diesels. The petrol versions are high-performing but very thirsty. The overall mechanical reliability is very good, covered by an excellent warranty, but the youth of the new electronic architecture (27-inch screen) requires ensuring that all software and electrical recalls (notably the transmission harness and fuel lines) have been carried out at the dealership.
