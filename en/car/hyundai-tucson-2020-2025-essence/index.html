Hyundai Tucson

IV (NX4) · 2020 – 2025

BUY Reliability 83/100 — bon

Summary

The **fourth-generation Hyundai Tucson (NX4)** marks a radical break from its predecessors. Introduced late 2020, it features the “Sensuous Sportiness” design with its grille integrating parametric daytime running lights invisible when stationary. Based on the N3 platform, it offers a spectacular leap forward in terms of perceived quality, spaciousness, and onboard technologies.

It is available in two wheelbases: short (SWB) for Europe and the Middle East, and long (LWB) for North America, South Korea, and Australia. Mechanically, this generation emphasizes electrification with the massive introduction of the **Smartstream** engine family, integrating CVVD (Continuously Variable Valve Duration) technology on petrol engines. A facelift was presented late 2023 for the 2024/2025 model year, correcting the interior ergonomics (return of physical buttons) and optimizing hybrid software management.

Weaknesses 5

  • All-touch ergonomics on pre-facelift models (2020-2023)
  • Recurring DPF (GPF) issues on hybrid versions in urban use
  • DCT7 gearbox sometimes hesitant at very low speeds (traffic jams, maneuvers)
  • Significant weight of PHEV versions impacting dynamism and tire wear
  • Limited rear visibility due to C-pillar design

Check before buying

  • History of software updates (BMS, engine management, eCall)
  • Status of the exhaust light (DPF/GPF) when testing HEV/PHEV versions
  • Smoothness of gear changes on the DCT7 gearbox (absence of juddering on startup)
  • Wear of front tires on 230 hp and 265 hp hybrid versions
  • Operation of the multimedia system and rearview camera (some black screen bugs reported)

Official recalls

  • - **December 2023 (North Amer.)**: Recall concerning the electric oil pump of the Idle Stop & Go system on 2.5L engines (risk of overheating/fire of the circuit board).
  • - **October 2022**: Replacement of front seat belt pretensioners that could explode upon deployment (global recall).
  • - **September 2021**: Software update for the hydraulic control unit (HEV/PHEV) following a risk of loss of braking assistance.
  • - **May 2021**: Reprogramming of the eCall module which might not transmit GPS coordinates in case of an accident.
  • - **2021**: Fixing of roof moldings that could detach at high speed on early production models.

Verdict

The Hyundai Tucson IV (NX4) is an extremely accomplished compact SUV that has managed to challenge European and Japanese benchmarks. Its overall reliability is very good, supported by the maturity of the Smartstream engines. **Purchase is highly recommended**, particularly in the 1.6 T-GDI 150/180 hp or 2.5 GDI (depending on the market) versions.

However, a **strict warning** is necessary for HEV and PHEV versions: although mechanically reliable, they suffer from chronic clogging of the gasoline particulate filter (GPF) if used exclusively for short, cold journeys. If you drive less than 15 km per trip daily, opt for a standard petrol version or make sure to take a long monthly highway trip to regenerate the system.
