Summary

The Mercedes-Benz C-Class (W205 generation for the sedan, S205 for the estate, C205 for the coupé, A205 for the cabriolet) marked a major stylistic and technological break from its predecessor. Often dubbed the "mini S-Class" upon its release, it introduced an unprecedented level of interior finish for the category, although early model years suffered from some assembly defects (rattles).

The facelift, which occurred in mid-2018, corrected most of the initial flaws, introduced a 100% digital instrument cluster, made the 9G-Tronic automatic gearbox standard, and renewed almost the entire range of engines (transition from M274 petrol engines to M264 with 48V mild-hybridization, and from OM651 diesels to OM654). It is a prestigious, comfortable, and dynamic model, whose reliability largely depends on the production year and the chosen engine.

Strengths 5

  • Timeless exterior and interior design (especially in AMG Line trim)
  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with Airmatic air suspension)
  • Smooth and responsive 9G-Tronic automatic gearbox (post-2017)
  • Excellent sound insulation (with acoustic glazing option)
  • Overall reliability of post-facelift petrol engines (M264 and M276)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disappointing interior assembly quality on 2014-2017 models (creaks from the black lacquered center console)
  • Random reliability of the 2.0L M274 petrol engine (2014-2015)
  • Airmatic air suspension expensive to repair in case of failure (porous air springs, compressor)
  • Slow Audio 20 infotainment system (pre-facelift) and Garmin Map Pilot GPS prone to SD card failures
  • Average rear passenger space and boot volume for the category

Check before buying

  • Service history: essential, especially for automatic gearboxes (oil change required every 5 years or 125,000 km for the 9G-Tronic).
  • Interior rattles: press the center console and door panels during the test drive (pre-2018 models).
  • Airmatic suspension: check that the car raises and lowers without suspicious compressor noise, and that it does not sag after a night of parking.
  • Electronics: test the touchpad, which tends to crack or delaminate, and check the operation of all driving aids.
  • M274 engine (C 200, C 250, C 300 before 2016): demand an absolute cold start to track any metallic noise (piston pin or chain).

Official recalls

  • Steering (2017): Risk of unintended driver airbag deployment due to a break in the ground strap in the steering column.
  • Starter (2017): Risk of fire. If the engine is seized (e.g., hydro-lock), repeated starting attempts can melt the current limiter.
  • Alternator / 12V Battery (2021): A short circuit in the alternator can cause overheating and a risk of fire.
  • ESP / Steering Software (2019): Update required following reported power steering losses.
  • Sunroof (2020): Risk of detachment of the polycarbonate panel on some models due to a bonding defect.

Verdict

The Mercedes C-Class W205 is an excellent premium sedan, but it requires caution when purchasing. If you are looking for a petrol engine, avoid C 200 / C 250 / C 300 models from 2014 and 2015 (M274 engine) unless the engine has been replaced or made reliable with supporting invoices.

The ideal petrol choice is a facelifted model (from mid-2018) equipped with the M264 engine (C 200 or C 300), which offers an excellent compromise of performance/consumption/reliability, coupled with a much better assembled and technologically up-to-date interior. For enthusiasts, the C 43 AMG (V6 M276) is a remarkably robust choice. For diesel, prioritize the OM654 block (post-2018), which is significantly quieter and more reliable than the older OM651.
