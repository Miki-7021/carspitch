Summary

The Jaguar F-TYPE Phase 2 (facelift of the X152 generation) was introduced in late 2019 for the 2020 model year. It stands out with a deeply redesigned front end, integrating horizontally slimmed LED headlights and a redesigned hood, giving it a more aggressive and modern look. Inside, it adopts a 12.3-inch 100% digital instrument cluster and an updated infotainment system (Touch Pro). Mechanically, this facelift marks a rationalization of the range: the V6 disappears in Europe in favor of a detuned V8 (P450), while the 4-cylinder Ingenium (P300) remains the entry level. The R version (P575) benefits from revised suspension. It is Jaguar's last 100% combustion-engine sports model, giving it future collector status, particularly in its V8 versions with their captivating sound. Overall reliability is up compared to the early years (2013-2016), but the model retains some peripheral weaknesses (cooling, electronics).

Strengths 5

  • Timeless and spectacular design, enhanced by the facelift
  • Sound of the V8 (and V6) engines, among the best in automotive production
  • Responsive and highly reliable 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox
  • Improved handling on the R version (revised suspension and anti-roll bars)
  • Future collector status (last combustion V8 Jaguar)

Weaknesses 5

  • High weight (especially on V8 AWD versions), penalizing pure agility
  • Infotainment system still a bit slow compared to German standards
  • Gargantuan fuel consumption on the V8s during sporty driving
  • Engine cooling plastics (V6/V8) unworthy of this range level
  • Interior space and trunk volume (especially on the convertible) very limited

Check before buying

  • **Cooling system (V6/V8):** Inspect for traces of orange coolant under the supercharger (sign of cracked plastic pipes).
  • **Supercharger noise:** Listen to the engine at idle. A clunking or metallic rattling noise indicates wear of the supercharger isolator torsion spring (snout isolator).
  • **Maintenance history:** Verify that oil changes have been done every year or 15,000 km maximum, particularly on the 4-cylinder Ingenium which is very sensitive to oil quality.
  • **Electronics:** Test all electrical functions (flush door handles, motorized central air vents, electric soft top on the convertible).
  • **AWD:** Check for the absence of jerks during tight turns at low speed (sign of degraded transfer case oil).

Verdict

The Jaguar F-Type Phase 2 is a passion purchase par excellence. While reason would dictate a Porsche 911, the F-Type seduces with its sculptural design and the charisma of its V8 engines (P450 and P575), which represent the true appeal of this model at the end of its career. The 4-cylinder (P300) is a good daily driver but lacks the nobility expected at this price point. In terms of reliability, the V8 blocks are robust (engine block and moving parts), but require strict vigilance regarding the cooling system (plastic pipes) and peripheral maintenance. It is a guaranteed future collector: favor a V8 model with a clear maintenance history and do not hesitate to preventively replace the plastic cooling components.
