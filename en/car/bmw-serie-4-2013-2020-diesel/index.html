Summary

The BMW 4 Series F32 (Coupe), F33 (Convertible), and F36 (Gran Coupe) represents the first generation of the 4 Series, introduced in 2013 to distinguish the coupes and convertibles from the 3 Series (F30) from which it is technically derived. The Gran Coupe (F36), launched in 2014, adds a practical dimension with its four doors and tailgate, while retaining the sporty line. This generation benefited from a slight facelift (LCI) in 2017, bringing minor aesthetic touches (headlights, taillights) and technological improvements (iDrive system, suspensions).

The 4 Series is renowned for its driving dynamics, quality interior finish, and wide range of engines, from efficient gasoline and diesel units to powerful M versions. It offers an excellent compromise between sportiness and comfort, although the suspension can be firm on certain versions or with sporty options.

**Overall reliability score:** The reliability of the 4 Series F32/F36 is generally good, but it is highly dependent on the chosen engine. Early production N47 diesel engines should be monitored carefully, while newer units (B-series) show excellent robustness.

  • Firm suspension on sporty versions or with M Sport options.

Check before buying

  • Complete service history: Essential, especially for N20 and N47 engines.
  • Timing chain noise: On cold start on N20 and N47 (whistling, rubbing).
  • Oil leaks: Under the engine (oil pan, valve cover, oil filter housing).
  • EGR operation: Check engine light, loss of power (especially on diesel).
  • DPF condition: If frequent short journeys (diesel).
  • Electronics: Check proper functioning of iDrive, sensors, power windows.
  • Suspensions: Abnormal noises, shock absorber wear.
  • ZF8 automatic gearbox: Fluid change if high mileage (not recommended by BMW but advised by specialists).

Verdict

The BMW 4 Series F32/F36 is an excellent choice for anyone looking for a sporty, elegant, and well-finished coupe or Gran Coupe. However, the choice of engine is crucial for long-term reliability.

**Buying advice:** * **To prioritize:** The B48 (420i/430i) and B58 (440i) gasoline engines are the most reliable and recommended. For diesel, the B47 (418d/420d/425d) is an excellent choice for high-mileage drivers. The N57 (diesel) 6-cylinders are also robust with consistent maintenance. * **To negotiate/check:** Early production N20 (gasoline) and N47 (diesel) engines (before 2015) require an **imperative** check of the timing chain history and careful listening for engine noises. If the chain has not been replaced or if a noise is present, the risk is high. * **M4 (S55):** For informed enthusiasts, with a significant budget for maintenance and potential modifications (Crank Hub).

In all cases, a complete service history and a pre-purchase inspection by a BMW specialist are highly recommended.
