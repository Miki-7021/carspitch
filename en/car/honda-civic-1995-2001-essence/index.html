Honda Civic

VI (EJ/EK) · 1995 – 2001

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All Honda models

Summary

The 6th generation Honda Civic (EJ/EK chassis for 3-door and sedans, EM for the coupe, and MA/MB/MC for the 5-door/Aerodeck versions manufactured in the UK) is a true icon of 90s Japanese engineering. Positioned as a versatile compact, it stands out with its double wishbone suspension (front and rear), offering exceptional road handling for its category. This is also the generation that popularized high-performance VTEC engines (notably the legendary B16A2 of the VTi and the B16B of the Japanese Type R) as well as VTEC-E versions focused on fuel economy. A slight facelift occurred in late 1998, modifying the bumpers, headlights, and grille. Today, it is an extremely sought-after model (youngtimer), whose mechanical reliability is legendary, but which severely suffers from the ravages of time, particularly corrosion and hazardous modifications.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of gasoline engines (D and B Series)
  • High-level road handling thanks to double wishbone suspension
  • Enjoyable manual gearbox (short throws, precision)
  • Very low fuel consumption on VTEC-E versions (D15Z6)
  • Low driving position and sporty ergonomics
  • Massive availability of spare and optimization parts (aftermarket)

Weaknesses 6

  • Perforating corrosion (rear fenders, sills, floorboards): the model's worst enemy
  • Almost non-existent sound insulation (very present road and wind noise)
  • Interior plastic quality (scratch-prone and basic)
  • Chronic weakness of the distributor (ICM module and coil)
  • Rear trailing arm bushings that tear over time
  • Very difficult to find an original, unmodified, or mistreated example

Check before buying

  • Rust: Thoroughly inspect the rear wheel arches (under the rubber protection), sills, spare wheel well, and floorboards.
  • Maintenance history: Check the date of the last timing belt replacement and the frequency of oil changes.
  • Ignition: Unstable idle or engine cut-outs when hot indicate a distributor nearing the end of its life.
  • Suspension: Dull thuds from the rear over bumps (worn trailing arm bushings).
  • Engine (VTEC): Verify that VTEC engagement (around 5500 rpm) occurs smoothly and without excessive blue smoke from the exhaust.
  • Modifications: Avoid vehicles with cut/re-soldered electrical harnesses, low-end direct air intakes, or lowered suspensions without proper alignment.

Official recalls

  • Ignition switch (2002): Premature wear of the ignition switch electrical contacts potentially leading to the engine cutting out while driving (concerns many 1997-2000 models).
  • Lower suspension ball joints (specific markets): Risk of premature wear and ball joint separation, leading to a collapse of the front suspension.
  • Electrical harness (1999): Possible rubbing of the harness under the dashboard which could cause a short circuit (specific models).

What sets it apart 6

reliability

  • Legendary Petrol Engine Reliability — Honda's petrol engines from this generation are renowned for their exceptional robustness and ability to cover very long distances, offering unparalleled peace of mind.

road_handling

  • Agile and Dynamic Road Handling — Thanks to its double wishbone suspension front and rear, the Civic offers top-tier road handling, precise steering, and remarkable agility for an engaging driving experience.

economy

  • Remarkable Fuel Efficiency — Versions equipped with VTEC-E technology, such as the 1.5i engine, stand out for their very low fuel consumption, making this Civic particularly economical for daily use.

braking

  • Reliable Braking for its Era — The braking system, with front discs and rear drums (ABS optionally available), ensures adequate and safe stopping, significantly improving performance when equipped.

safety

  • Robust Structure for Enhanced Safety — The Honda Civic VI offers solid protection for its occupants, with a structure designed to absorb impacts, as evidenced by its Euro NCAP score for adult protection.

Performance

  • Immersive Driving Pleasure — The low driving position, sporty ergonomics, and the enjoyable short-throw manual gearbox contribute to an authentic and engaging driving pleasure.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 306 (excellent chassis), Volkswagen Golf IV (better finished but less reliable), Ford Focus Mk1 (benchmark in road handling), Nissan Almera N15.
  • Japan / Asia: Toyota Corolla E110, Mitsubishi Lancer, Nissan Sunny/Pulsar.
  • North America: Nissan Sentra, Mazda Protegé, Toyota Corolla.

Verdict

The 6th generation Honda Civic is a masterpiece of mechanical reliability. The gasoline engines, whether economical (D Series) or sporty (B Series), are capable of exceeding 300,000 km without issues if properly maintained. The real challenge today is not the mechanics, but the bodywork. Rust is the number one killer of these cars. If you find a healthy example, with intact rear fenders and a clear history, it is a highly recommended purchase, whether as an economical daily driver (1.4i or 1.5i VTEC-E) or as a sporty youngtimer (VTi). Avoid heavily modified or rust-eaten models.
