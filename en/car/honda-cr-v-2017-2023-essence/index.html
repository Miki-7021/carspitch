Summary

The 5th generation Honda CR-V (factory codes RW for internal combustion versions, RT for hybrids) marks a major turning point for the Japanese compact SUV. It abandons diesel in most Western markets (especially in Europe) in favor of a very accomplished hybrid powertrain (i-MMD / e:HEV) and a turbocharged gasoline engine (1.5 VTEC Turbo). Based on the modular platform introduced by the 10th generation Civic, it gains in spaciousness, rigidity, and for the first time offers a 7-seater option on certain gasoline versions. A slight restyling (facelift) occurred in late 2020, subtly modernizing the bumpers and updating the technological equipment. It is a family vehicle par excellence, prioritizing comfort and space over sportiness.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional spaciousness and giant trunk volume (except hybrid which loses a bit of volume)
  • Rear doors opening at 90 degrees (ideal for car seats)
  • High overall reliability, particularly on hybrid and naturally aspirated versions
  • Suspension comfort and soundproofing significantly improved compared to the 4th generation
  • Transparent, smooth, and very efficient i-MMD hybrid system in the city
  • Comprehensive standard safety equipment (Honda Sensing)

Weaknesses 6

  • Dated, slow infotainment system with unintuitive interface
  • Oil dilution issue on the 1.5 Turbo (especially before 2019 and in cold climates)
  • Engine revving (whining) effect during strong accelerations (CVT and Hybrid)
  • Quality of some interior plastics below European standards (Tiguan, 3008)
  • Undersized 12V battery causing starting failures
  • Thin body paint, sensitive to micro-scratches and stone chips

Check before buying

  • Engine oil level and smell on the 1.5 VTEC Turbo (a strong gasoline smell or a level above max indicates dilution).
  • Service history of CVT gearbox and rear differential (AWD) oil changes: lack of maintenance causes shuddering.
  • Condition of the 12V battery (often needs replacement after 3-4 years).
  • Operation of the touchscreen and GPS (test responsiveness and absence of intermittent restarts).
  • Alignment of the front radar (Honda Sensing): a dirty or misaligned radar causes phantom braking or deactivates adaptive cruise control.

Official recalls

Fuel Pump (2020/2021): Major global recall (millions of vehicles). The low-pressure fuel pump impeller (supplied by Denso) can deform, leading to engine stalling or inability to start. What sets it apart 5

praticity

  • Exceptional Space and Family Modularity — With its exceptional interior space, generous boot volume, and the 7-seater option on some petrol versions, the CR-V is the ideal companion for all families. The 90-degree rear door opening greatly facilitates access.

comfort

  • Enhanced Driving Comfort — The 5th generation CR-V offers remarkable suspension comfort and refined sound insulation, ensuring serene and pleasant journeys for all passengers.

safety

  • Advanced Safety with Honda Sensing — Equipped as standard with the Honda Sensing suite, this vehicle integrates a comprehensive set of driver-assistance technologies for optimal protection on all roads.

Performance

  • Dynamic Petrol Engine — The 1.5 VTEC Turbo engine delivers willing power (up to 193 hp) and controlled fuel consumption, ensuring smooth acceleration and enjoyable daily driving.

reliability

  • Renowned Reliability and Manufacturer Support — Benefit from Honda's reputation for reliability, enhanced by continuous software updates from the manufacturer to optimize engine performance and longevity.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Toyota RAV4 (the big hybrid rival), Volkswagen Tiguan (for finish and internal combustion engines), Peugeot 3008 (for dynamism), Mazda CX-5 (for driving pleasure and finish).
  • North America: Toyota RAV4, Subaru Forester (for symmetrical AWD), Nissan Rogue, Hyundai Tucson.
  • Asia / Oceania: Mitsubishi Outlander, Nissan X-Trail, Mazda CX-5.

Verdict

The 5th generation Honda CR-V is a pragmatic, family-oriented, and extremely rational choice. If you are aiming for the 1.5 VTEC Turbo engine, be vigilant: although powerful, this engine requires meticulous maintenance (frequent oil changes) to counteract the oil dilution phenomenon, especially if you make short trips. The true gem of this generation is the Hybrid (e:HEV) version: it offers masterful reliability, controlled fuel consumption, and incomparable driving smoothness. The naturally aspirated versions (2.0 and 2.4), available outside Europe, are monuments of robustness. A highly recommended purchase, provided you choose the powertrain suited to your driving profile.
