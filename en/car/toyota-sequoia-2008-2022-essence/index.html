Toyota Sequoia

II (XK60) · 2008 – 2022

BUY Reliability 89/100 — excellent

All Toyota models

Summary

The second-generation Toyota Sequoia (codename XK60) is a full-size SUV designed primarily for the North American and Middle Eastern markets. Based on the Tundra pickup chassis, it offers three rows of seats and impressive towing capabilities. Its exceptionally long career (14 years without a major redesign) is explained by a rustic design but a reliability that has become legendary. While it shows its age in terms of interior design, infotainment, and fuel consumption, it remains one of the most durable vehicles in its class, far surpassing its American competitors (Tahoe, Expedition) in terms of long-term peace of mind.

Strengths 5

  • Legendary reliability of the 5.7L V8 (capable of exceeding 480,000 km with regular maintenance)
  • Exceptional robustness of the 6-speed automatic gearbox
  • Gigantic interior space (true 8-seater) and trunk volume
  • Towing capabilities (up to 3,350 kg) and solid ladder chassis
  • Overall reliability far superior to the competition (GMC Yukon, Ford Expedition) according to J.D. Power and the TÜV

Weaknesses 5

  • Gargantuan fuel consumption (difficult to drop below 15 L/100 km on average)
  • Extreme sensitivity to chassis rust (particularly in cold/salty regions)
  • Totally dated interior design and infotainment system (until the 2020 facelift)
  • Size unsuited to European roads and parking lots
  • Wear of front axle components (ball joints, tie rods) due to the weight of the vehicle

Check before buying

  • MANDATORY CHASSIS INSPECTION: Check for perforating rust on the frame rails, particularly if the vehicle comes from Canada or the Northern USA.
  • Front axle: Check for play in the CV joints, suspension ball joints, and tie rod ends (common beyond 200,000 km).
  • 5.7L Engine: Inspect the sides of the cylinder heads to detect oil leaks (Cam tower leaks).
  • 5.7L Engine: Listen to the noise on cold start (a prolonged vacuum cleaner noise or a check engine light can indicate a secondary air pump problem).
  • Maintenance history: Demand proof of fluid changes (axles, transfer case, gearbox).

Official recalls

Since the Sequoia was not officially sold in Europe, Rappel Conso data does not apply to it. Here are the major recalls from the NHTSA (United States):

  • Power steering (Nov/Dec 2021): Possible oil leak on the power steering gear assembly (2008-2022 models).
  • Fuel pump (Jan/Nov 2020): Failure of the low-pressure fuel pump that can lead to engine stall while driving (2018-2019 models).
  • Electronics / Airbags / ESC (Feb/Oct 2018): Electrical interference affecting electronic stability control (ESC) or necessary reprogramming of the airbag ECU to prevent non-deployment.
  • Power windows (Oct 2012 / Oct 2015): Risk of short circuit and overheating (fire risk) of the driver's side main power window switch (2008-2011 models).
  • Accelerator pedal (Jan 2010): Massive Toyota recall regarding the risk of the accelerator pedal sticking (2008-2010 models).
  • Accessories (2014-2017): Minor recalls by regional distributors (Southeast Toyota) regarding accessory wheels, load labels, or aftermarket heated seat wiring.

What sets it apart 10

Reliability

  • Legendary V8 engine durability — The 5.7L V8 is known to exceed 480,000 km with regular maintenance, giving buyers long-term peace of mind and reducing the risk of major engine repairs.
  • Robust six-speed automatic gearbox — The AB60E/F automatic transmission is described as extremely robust, so buyers can expect dependable shifting and fewer costly transmission failures over the vehicle's life.
  • Simple engine design without turbo — The V8 has no turbo, no hybrid system and no cylinder deactivation, which means fewer complex components that could fail and simpler long-term ownership.
  • Better reliability than US rivals — J.D. Power and TÜV data show reliability superior to the GMC Yukon and Ford Expedition, giving buyers greater confidence in long-term dependability.
  • Indestructible 4.7 V8 block — The cast-iron 4.7L V8 is described as an indestructible block with smooth operation, appealing to buyers who value simple, long-lasting mechanicals.

Practicality

  • Genuine eight-seat interior space — With three rows and a huge cabin, this SUV can carry eight people plus luggage, making it a practical choice for large families or group transport.
  • Huge boot volume — The full-size body provides a large cargo area, making it easy to carry bulky items, luggage or equipment for family trips and daily practical needs.

Performance

  • Strong towing capacity up to 3,350 kg — The ladder-frame chassis and torquey V8 allow confident towing of heavy trailers or boats, useful for buyers who need a capable work and leisure vehicle.

Handling

  • Solid ladder-frame chassis — The Tundra-derived ladder chassis provides a strong foundation for heavy use, supporting durability and stability when carrying full loads or towing.

Economy

  • Proven 4.6 V8 with chain drive — The 4.6L V8 offers better efficiency than the 4.7 and uses a timing chain, reducing scheduled maintenance compared with belt-driven engines.

Alternatives

  • North America: Chevrolet Tahoe / Suburban (more modern, V8 engines with sometimes temperamental cylinder deactivation), GMC Yukon, Ford Expedition (capable EcoBoost V6 but timing/turbos to monitor), Nissan Armada (thirsty V8, decent reliability).
  • Middle East: Nissan Patrol (Y62), Toyota Land Cruiser (J200 - more luxurious and off-road oriented).
  • Europe: No official direct competition. Parallel imports of Dodge Durango or Cadillac Escalade.

Verdict

The second-generation Toyota Sequoia is an automotive dinosaur, but that is precisely its strength. Devoid of complex engine technologies (no turbo, no hybridization on this generation, no cylinder deactivation), its 5.7L V8 is a monument of reliability capable of reaching stratospheric mileages. It is a highly recommended purchase for those who need a tireless tow vehicle or an XXL troop carrier and who accept a substantial fuel budget. Absolute condition of purchase: a rigorous inspection of the chassis on a lift to hunt down corrosion, the true Achilles heel of this model.
