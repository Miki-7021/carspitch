Toyota Sequoia

II (XK60) · 2008 – 2022

BUY Reliability 89/100 — excellent

Summary

The second-generation Toyota Sequoia (codename XK60) is a full-size SUV designed primarily for the North American and Middle Eastern markets. Based on the Tundra pickup chassis, it offers three rows of seats and impressive towing capabilities. Its exceptionally long career (14 years without a major redesign) is explained by a rustic design but a reliability that has become legendary. While it shows its age in terms of interior design, infotainment, and fuel consumption, it remains one of the most durable vehicles in its class, far surpassing its American competitors (Tahoe, Expedition) in terms of long-term peace of mind.

Strengths 5

  • Legendary reliability of the 5.7L V8 (capable of exceeding 480,000 km with regular maintenance)
  • Exceptional robustness of the 6-speed automatic gearbox
  • Gigantic interior space (true 8-seater) and trunk volume
  • Towing capabilities (up to 3,350 kg) and solid ladder chassis
  • Overall reliability far superior to the competition (GMC Yukon, Ford Expedition) according to J.D. Power and the TÜV

Weaknesses 5

  • Gargantuan fuel consumption (difficult to drop below 15 L/100 km on average)
  • Extreme sensitivity to chassis rust (particularly in cold/salty regions)
  • Totally dated interior design and infotainment system (until the 2020 facelift)
  • Size unsuited to European roads and parking lots
  • Wear of front axle components (ball joints, tie rods) due to the weight of the vehicle

Check before buying

  • MANDATORY CHASSIS INSPECTION: Check for perforating rust on the frame rails, particularly if the vehicle comes from Canada or the Northern USA.
  • Front axle: Check for play in the CV joints, suspension ball joints, and tie rod ends (common beyond 200,000 km).
  • 5.7L Engine: Inspect the sides of the cylinder heads to detect oil leaks (Cam tower leaks).
  • 5.7L Engine: Listen to the noise on cold start (a prolonged vacuum cleaner noise or a check engine light can indicate a secondary air pump problem).
  • Maintenance history: Demand proof of fluid changes (axles, transfer case, gearbox).

Alternatives

* **North America:** Chevrolet Tahoe / Suburban (more modern, V8 engines with sometimes temperamental cylinder deactivation), GMC Yukon, Ford Expedition (capable EcoBoost V6 but timing/turbos to monitor), Nissan Armada (thirsty V8, decent reliability). * **Middle East:** Nissan Patrol (Y62), Toyota Land Cruiser (J200 - more luxurious and off-road oriented). * **Europe:** No official direct competition. Parallel imports of Dodge Durango or Cadillac Escalade.

Verdict

The second-generation Toyota Sequoia is an automotive dinosaur, but that is precisely its strength. Devoid of complex engine technologies (no turbo, no hybridization on this generation, no cylinder deactivation), its 5.7L V8 is a monument of reliability capable of reaching stratospheric mileages. It is a highly recommended purchase for those who need a tireless tow vehicle or an XXL troop carrier and who accept a substantial fuel budget. **Absolute condition of purchase:** a rigorous inspection of the chassis on a lift to hunt down corrosion, the true Achilles heel of this model.
