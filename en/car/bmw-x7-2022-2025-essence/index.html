Summary

The **BMW X7 (G07) LCI** (Life Cycle Impulse / Facelift), introduced in mid-2022, represents the pinnacle of luxury and space at BMW. This facelift stands out with a controversial split-headlight front end, the integration of the large curved screen *BMW Curved Display* (iDrive 8/8.5), and widespread 48V mild-hybridization across all engines. Designed primarily for the North American and Asian markets, it excels with its imperial comfort (standard air suspension), soundproofing, and the driving pleasure of its six-cylinder and V8 engines. Although imposing and heavy, it retains astonishing dynamism for its size. The absence of a plug-in hybrid (PHEV) version, however, remains a weak point compared to certain competitors on the European market.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional driving pleasure of the 6-cylinder engines (B58 petrol and B57 diesel)
  • Royal comfort thanks to the standard adaptive air suspension
  • Soundproofing worthy of a limousine (7 Series)
  • Cutting-edge onboard technology (fluid and comprehensive iDrive 8)
  • Generous onboard space, including in the 3rd row

Weaknesses 5

  • Size and weight (over 2.4 tons) unsuited to European city centers
  • Rapid wear of tires (especially in 22 or 23 inches) and brakes
  • Absence of a plug-in hybrid (PHEV) engine
  • Ergonomics of the all-touch interface (iDrive 8) requiring an adaptation period
  • Prohibitive maintenance and insurance costs

Check before buying

  • Integrated Braking System (IBS): check if the vehicle has undergone the massive 2024/2025 recall.
  • Air suspension: look out for abnormal sagging after prolonged parking (air spring leak or compressor fatigue).
  • Tire wear: frequent asymmetrical wear due to camber and weight, particularly on the rear axle.
  • Electronics: central screen bugs (iDrive 8), often requiring OTA (Over-The-Air) updates or reboots.
  • Starter / 48V System: check for recalls regarding starter overheating.

Alternatives

Europe * **Mercedes-Benz GLS (facelifted X167)**: The direct competitor, slightly more spacious, focused on absolute comfort. * **Range Rover (L460)**: More luxurious, superior off-road capabilities, offers high-performance PHEV versions. * **Audi Q7 / Q8**: Older in design, but dynamic and slightly less expensive.

North America & Middle East * **Cadillac Escalade**: More massive, gigantic interior space, traditional naturally aspirated V8. * **Lincoln Navigator**: American-style comfort, powerful twin-turbo V6. * **Lexus LX 600**: Legendary reliability, off-road capabilities, but less dynamic on the road.

Verdict

The BMW X7 LCI (2022+) is an ultra-luxury SUV that achieves the feat of combining limousine comfort with surprising dynamism for its 2.4 tons. **The xDrive40i (B58) petrol engine is the most rational and reliable choice**, offering more than sufficient performance and proven reliability. The M60i will delight V8 enthusiasts, although its running costs are astronomical. Be careful, however: the model has been affected by recent major recalls (notably the IBS braking system and the starter), which must imperatively be checked before purchase. Also, plan a substantial budget for consumables (tires, brakes) which melt away quickly under the weight of the vehicle.
