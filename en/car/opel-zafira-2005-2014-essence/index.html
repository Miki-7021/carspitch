Opel Zafira

B · 2005 – 2014

NEGOTIATE Reliability 74/100 — bon

Summary

The Opel Zafira B (based on the Astra H platform) marked a major evolution compared to the first generation, retaining the brilliant *Flex7* retractable seating system while improving finish, road handling, and safety. Restyled in early 2008 (new bumpers, modified taillights, updated engines), it established itself as one of Europe's most popular compact MPVs. Although sales were dominated by diesels (1.9 CDTI then 1.7 CDTI), petrol engines now offer a very relevant alternative on the used market, avoiding costly DPF and EGR valve problems. Overall reliability is decent, but marred by electronic weaknesses (CIM module) and the notoriously fragile 6-speed manual gearbox (M32) on certain versions.

Strengths 5

  • Still ingenious Flex7 system (7 seats retractable into the floor)
  • Healthy and dynamic road handling for an MPV
  • Overall reliability of the 1.8 VVT 140 hp engine
  • Generous interior space and boot volume (in 5-seat configuration)
  • Availability and cost of spare parts (Astra H platform)

Weaknesses 6

  • Notoriously fragile 6-speed manual gearbox (M32) (bearings)
  • Fickle electronic steering column module (CIM)
  • 2.2 Direct engine to avoid (HP pump and timing chain)
  • High weight penalizing smaller engines (1.6 105/115 hp)
  • Austere interior finish and scratch-prone plastics
  • Three-quarter front visibility (thick windscreen pillars)

Check before buying

  • Gearbox (M32): Listen for a whistling sound in 5th and 6th gear, and check if the lever moves back and forth during acceleration/deceleration (worn bearings).
  • Electronics: Turn on headlights and test the horn as well as steering wheel controls (CIM module failure).
  • VVT engines (1.6/1.8): Listen for a knocking sound (diesel-like) for 2-3 seconds on a cold start (worn VVT phasers).
  • Cooling (Petrol): Check for absence of 'mayonnaise' in the expansion tank (oil cooler gasket leak, very common on 1.6 and 1.8).
  • Air conditioning: Check compressor engagement (the electromagnetic clutch tends to burn out).

Verdict

The Opel Zafira B is a pragmatic and affordable family MPV on the used market. While diesels flooded the market, they are now aging and prone to serious breakdowns (DPF, EGR, M32 gearbox). The smartest choice is to opt for a petrol engine, especially the **1.8 VVT 140 hp**, which offers the best balance of reliability, power, and maintenance cost. Absolutely avoid the 2.2 Direct (Z22YH), whose high-pressure pump failures are ruinous. When purchasing, meticulous attention must be paid to the onboard electronics (horn/CIM) and, if the vehicle is equipped with a 6-speed gearbox, to bearing noises.
