Summary

The Citroën C5 Aircross (Generation II / Extended Phase 2) embodies the compact family SUV offering from the brand with the chevrons. For the years 2024-2025, the model ensures the transition to the future generation based on the STLA Medium platform. It still stands out for its benchmark comfort (progressive hydraulic cushion suspensions, Advanced Comfort seats) and its modularity (three independent rear seats). Mechanically, this period is marked by the introduction of the new 1.2 Hybrid 136 micro-hybrid (MHEV) engine with a timing chain, intended to correct the flaws of the 1.2 PureTech, and by the continued use of the controversial 1.5 BlueHDi diesel, targeted by multiple recall campaigns.

Overall reliability score: Burdened by the legacy of Stellantis engines (DV5RC diesel and EB2 petrol), overall reliability is average, although PHEV versions and the new MHEV raise the level.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (Advanced Comfort suspensions)
  • Rear modularity worthy of an MPV (3 independent, sliding, and retractable seats)
  • Generous boot volume (up to 580L for ICE/diesel versions)
  • New 136 hp MHEV engine that corrects the major PureTech timing defect
  • Careful soundproofing on PHEV versions

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the 1.5 BlueHDi (7mm chain, AdBlue) requiring extreme vigilance
  • Aging infotainment system compared to competitors (Peugeot 3008 III, Renault Austral)
  • Clumsy road handling, focused solely on comfort
  • Reduced boot volume on PHEV versions (460L)
  • Impressive number of safety recalls on recent model years

Check before buying

  • For the 1.5 BlueHDi Diesel: Demand service history and check if the camshaft chain recall has been performed (or if the engine has the 8mm casing).
  • For the 1.5 BlueHDi Diesel: Presence of warning lights related to the anti-pollution system (AdBlue / UREA).
  • For the MHEV / PHEV: Check that recalls concerning the inverter (e-DCT) and the high-pressure fuel pump have been applied.
  • For the PHEV: Condition of the charging cable (risk of overheating) and actual traction battery range.
  • For the 1.2 PureTech: Condition of the timing belt (swelling/cracks) and oil change history.

What sets it apart 6

comfort

  • Unrivaled Ride Comfort — Enjoy a serene and soothing driving experience thanks to Citroën's Advanced Comfort suspensions, absorbing all road imperfections for maximum well-being on board.

practicality

  • Exceptional Modularity — The C5 Aircross offers minivan-like modularity with its three independent, sliding, and retractable rear seats, adapting to all your passenger or cargo transport needs.
  • Generous Boot Volume — With a boot volume of up to 580 liters, the diesel C5 Aircross ranks among the best in its category, offering impressive loading space for all your adventures.
  • Ideal Towing Capacity — Thanks to its robust engine torque, the diesel C5 Aircross is perfectly suited for towing, whether it's a caravan, a trailer, or a bike rack, thus increasing its versatility.

economy

  • Optimized Fuel Consumption — The C5 Aircross's 1.5 BlueHDi diesel engine is renowned for its excellent efficiency, allowing you to cover long distances with controlled fuel consumption.

Performance

  • Generous Engine Torque — Benefit from generous engine torque with the 1.5 BlueHDi, ensuring dynamic acceleration and smooth driving, ideal for highway journeys and confident overtakes.

Verdict

The late-production Citroën C5 Aircross (2024-2025) remains an excellent choice for families prioritizing absolute comfort and modularity. However, the choice of engine is vital.

Regarding the 1.5 BlueHDi Diesel, it is to AVOID unless there is absolute certainty (invoices, visual inspection of the casing) that the vehicle is equipped with the 8 mm camshaft chain and that the AdBlue system has been updated or replaced. The risks of engine failure and recalls for fuel leaks are too significant.

Today's most rational choice is the new 1.2 Hybrid 136 (MHEV), which abandons the wet belt for a timing chain, although it is necessary to ensure that early recalls (inverter) have been carried out. PHEV versions are relevant for fiscal or peri-urban use, subject to rigorous monitoring of software updates.
