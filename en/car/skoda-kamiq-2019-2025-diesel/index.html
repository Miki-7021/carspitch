Summary

The first-generation Skoda Kamiq (code NW4) is the smallest SUV in the Czech manufacturer's range. Based on the Volkswagen Group's modular MQB-A0 platform (shared with the VW T-Cross and Seat Arona), it stands out for its record-breaking spaciousness for its size (4.24 m) and excellent value for money. Launched in 2019, it received a facelift in late 2023 (marketed in 2024) modernizing its front end and infotainment system.

Diesel Focus: Although the urban SUV market has largely shifted towards petrol, the Kamiq was offered at its launch with the 1.6 TDI (EA288) engine. This engine was removed from the catalog at the end of 2020 due to anti-pollution standards (Euro 6d-ISC-FCM) and low demand. It is a frugal engine designed for high-mileage drivers, but it tolerates very poorly the exclusively urban use for which this type of vehicle is often intended.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional rear spaciousness for the category (legroom)
  • Ergonomics of controls (successful blend of physical and haptic)
  • Suspension comfort (softly sprung, ideal for long journeys)
  • Very low fuel consumption of the 1.6 TDI on rural roads/motorways
  • Serious and upscale interior finish (soft-touch plastics on the upper part)

Weaknesses 5

  • 1.6 TDI unsuitable for city driving (rapid DPF/EGR clogging)
  • DSG7 (DQ200) gearbox sometimes jerky at low speeds and fragile if mistreated in the city
  • Recurring infotainment system bugs (MIB3) on 2019-2021 models
  • Imperfect sound insulation on motorways (wind noise)
  • No sliding rear bench (unlike the VW T-Cross)

Check before buying

  • Usage history for the 1.6 TDI: avoid models used exclusively in the city.
  • DSG7 gearbox operation: no juddering, slipping, or metallic noise during 1st/2nd gear changes.
  • Coolant level (TDI and TSI): a drop indicates a water pump leak.
  • Multimedia system stability during the test drive (test GPS and smartphone connection).
  • Condition of front tires (the front axle can wear quickly on the heavier 1.6 TDI).

Official recalls

  • November 2021 (Airbags): A manufacturing defect in the passenger airbag gas generator can lead to incorrect deployment. Models produced between January and August 2021.
  • May 2020 (eCall): Malfunction of the emergency call system due to a software bug. Update required. 2019-2020 models.
  • February 2020 (Seat belts): On some models, the right rear seat belt buckle may open unexpectedly if the vehicle is occupied by three rear passengers and makes a sharp turn. Buckle replacement.
  • March 2022 (Fire risk): The decorative engine cover can detach and touch hot parts (mainly concerns certain TSI engines). Cover removal.

What sets it apart 7

Safety

  • Top-tier Safety — The Skoda Kamiq achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, with impressive scores of 96% for adult occupant protection and 85% for child occupant protection, ensuring optimal peace of mind.

Comfort

  • Optimized Suspension Comfort — Its chassis is tuned to provide supple and pleasant suspension comfort, effectively absorbing road imperfections, making it an ideal vehicle for long journeys.
  • Refined Interior Finish — The Kamiq's interior benefits from a serious and upscale finish, with the use of soft-touch plastics on the upper dashboard, providing a perception of superior quality.

Practicality

  • Class-leading Rear Space — The Kamiq stands out with exceptional rear passenger space for its category, offering generous legroom that ensures comfort for all occupants, even on long trips.

Economy

  • Ultra-low Diesel Consumption — Equipped with the 1.6 TDI engine, the Kamiq boasts remarkably low fuel consumption on rural roads and highways, allowing for significant savings and extended range between refills.

Braking

  • Effective and Reassuring Braking — The Kamiq's braking system is renowned for its efficiency and responsiveness, offering excellent pedal feel and short stopping distances, for enhanced active safety.

Handling

  • Sound and Stable Road Handling — The Kamiq offers a balanced and predictable road behavior, with stable handling and well-controlled body roll, ensuring serenity and confidence behind the wheel in all circumstances.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 2008 (more dynamic but fragile 1.5 BlueHDi), Renault Captur (better finished, reliable dCi but discontinued early), Volkswagen T-Cross (technical cousin, sliding rear bench), Seat Arona (sportier, same platform).
  • Asia / China: Honda XR-V, Nissan Kicks, Hyundai ix25.
  • North America: The Kamiq is not marketed in this market (indirectly replaced by the VW Taos).
  • Emerging markets: Hyundai Creta, Kia Seltos.

Verdict

The Skoda Kamiq is one of the most rational and welcoming urban SUVs on the market.

Regarding the 1.6 TDI: It's a double-edged sword. If you drive more than 25,000 km per year, mainly on expressways or motorways, it will prove to be an extremely economical and reliable companion. However, if your use is mainly urban or peri-urban (short trips), you should avoid it.

Clogging of the DPF, EGR valve, and AdBlue issues will negate all fuel savings.

For 80% of buyers, the 1.0 TSI 110/115 hp is the most relevant choice: it is cheaper to buy, very reliable, and consumes only one liter more per 100 km compared to the diesel, without the emissions control constraints.
