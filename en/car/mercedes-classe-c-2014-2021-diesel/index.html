Summary

The Mercedes-Benz C-Class (W205 generation) marked a major stylistic and technological break from its predecessor, adopting the aesthetic codes and finish level of the S-Class (often nicknamed 'Baby S-Class'). Based on the modular MRA platform, it shed about 100 kg thanks to the extensive use of aluminum. The 2018 facelift brought 100% digital instrumentation, new driving aids, and, most importantly, a complete overhaul of the engine range, notably introducing the excellent OM654 diesel engine to replace the aging OM651. Although very luxurious, the first model years suffered from some teething problems (interior trim noises, electronic bugs).

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional ride comfort (especially with Airmatic air suspension)
  • Interior presentation quality and timeless design
  • Brilliant OM654 diesel engine (post-2018): quiet, powerful, and extremely fuel-efficient
  • Superb and safe road handling (RWD or 4MATIC)
  • Advanced onboard technology on facelifted versions (2018+)

Weaknesses 5

  • Interior trim noises and central console creaks on pre-2018 models
  • Reliability of the AdBlue system (NOx sensors, pump, tank) on diesels
  • Prohibitive replacement cost of Airmatic air suspension
  • OM651 diesel engine (pre-2018) rough and noisy on acceleration
  • Rear seat space and boot volume (especially on PHEV versions)

Check before buying

  • AdBlue system service history (frequent NOx sensor replacement).
  • Airmatic suspension operation (if equipped): the car should not sag after prolonged parking.
  • Smoothness of the automatic gearbox (7G-Tronic or 9G-Tronic): no jerks should be felt, fluid change required every 100,000 km or 5 years.
  • Creaks from the black lacquered plastic central console (press on it to test).
  • Cupping wear on tires (sensitive front axle geometry).

Official recalls

  • Steering column (2017): Grounding fault that could lead to unintended driver airbag deployment.
  • Starter (2017): Risk of overheating and fire of the starter current limiter if the engine is blocked.
  • Emissions / AdBlue (2018-2020): Massive recall (KBA) for engine management software update on diesels (OM651) due to emission standards.
  • Panoramic roof (2020): Bonding defect of the polycarbonate panel located between the windshield and the sunroof, risk of detachment.
  • Power steering (2018): Possible loss of power steering assistance due to a software defect.

Verdict

The Mercedes C-Class W205 is an excellent road car, but it requires selectivity. If you are looking for a diesel, avoid pre-2018 models if possible (noisy OM651, somewhat underpowered OM626) and absolutely prioritize a facelifted version (post-2018) equipped with the OM654 engine (C200d, C220d, C300d). This engine transforms the car, offering cathedral-like silence and negligible fuel consumption. However, be mindful of maintenance costs: steer clear of models without a clear service history, beware of Airmatic suspension out of warranty, and budget for the inevitable quirks of the AdBlue system if you drive in the city.
