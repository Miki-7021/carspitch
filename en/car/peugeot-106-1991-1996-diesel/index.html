Summary

Launched in September 1991, the Peugeot 106 Phase 1 (codename 1A for the 3-door, 1C for the 5-door which appeared in 1992) had the heavy task of succeeding the 104 while supporting the legendary 205. A light, agile city car with excellent road handling typical of Peugeot, it left its mark on the 90s.

**Diesel Focus:** The 106 had two diesel eras. The first (1992-1994) with the 1.4D (TUD3) aluminum block, known to be fragile. The second (1994-1996) with the 1.5D (TUD5) cast iron block, which became a true legend of automotive reliability, capable of exceeding 400,000 km with basic maintenance, while consuming less than 5L/100 km. Although passive safety is dated, it is an economical 'runabout' par excellence.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability and longevity of the 1.5D (TUD5) engine
  • Extremely low fuel consumption (often under 5L/100km for diesel)
  • Lively, precise, and fun road handling
  • Negligible maintenance, insurance, and spare parts costs
  • Excellent mechanical accessibility (plenty of space under the hood)

Weaknesses 5

  • Disastrous reliability of the first 1.4D (TUD3) diesel engine
  • Fragile trailing arm rear axle (a common PSA issue of the era)
  • Passive and active safety from another era (no ABS, no airbags, thin sheet metal)
  • Almost non-existent sound insulation, especially in diesel models
  • Very poor standard equipment on entry-level trims (XN, Kid)

Check before buying

  • **Rear axle:** Look at the car from behind. If the rear wheels have negative camber ('Gordini' effect /-\) or if grinding noises are heard, the arm bearings are faulty (costly repair).
  • **Cylinder head gasket (1.4D):** Presence of 'mayonnaise' in the expansion tank, overheating, hoses hard as wood.
  • **Injection pump (1.5D):** Check for absence of diesel fuel leaks at the accelerator shaft (especially on Lucas pumps).
  • **Corrosion:** Inspect the rear wheel arches, trunk floor, and jack points, although the 106 is better protected than the 205.
  • **Oil leak (TU Gasoline):** Very common seepage at the front right of the engine (timing/alternator side). Often benign but to be monitored.

Verdict

The Peugeot 106 Phase 1 is an endearing city car that embodies the transition between the 80s and 90s. **If you are looking for a diesel, the choice is binary: avoid the 1.4D (TUD3) and go for the 1.5D (TUD5).** The 1.5D is a true workhorse, capable of handling astronomical mileages for an unbeatable running cost. It's the perfect 'daily driver' for small budgets, provided you accept rustic comfort, poor sound insulation, and old-generation safety. Before purchase, inspection of the rear axle is absolutely mandatory, as its repair can cost more than the car's value.
