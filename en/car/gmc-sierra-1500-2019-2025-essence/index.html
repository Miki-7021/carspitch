Summary

The **4th generation GMC Sierra 1500 (T1XX)** is the technical and luxurious cousin of the Chevrolet Silverado 1500. Positioned in the premium *full-size* pickup segment, it stands out with its statutory design, its multifunction tailgate (MultiPro Tailgate), and its high-end trims, notably the highly sought-after **Denali** version.

Launched in 2019, it initially suffered from an interior deemed below category standards (especially compared to the RAM 1500). GMC corrected this with a **major facelift in 2022**, introducing a completely redesigned interior, giant screens, and the *Super Cruise* semi-autonomous driving system. According to J.D. Power and Consumer Reports, its overall reliability is average for the segment, with an excellent customer satisfaction rating for the Denali versions, but significant points of vigilance regarding the V8 engines.

Strengths 6

  • Statutory design and luxurious finishes (Denali, Denali Ultimate)
  • Exceptional 3.0L Duramax diesel engine (fuel consumption and drivability)
  • Very practical 6-position MultiPro Tailgate
  • Advanced towing technologies (up to 15 camera views, trailer blind spot sensor)
  • Super Cruise autonomous driving system (on recent high-end trims)
  • Gigantic rear seat space (Crew Cab)

Weaknesses 5

  • V8 engine reliability marred by lifter problems (AFM/DFM)
  • Disappointing interior quality on 2019-2021 models
  • 8-speed automatic gearbox (8L90) prone to jerking on early model years
  • Imposing size making parking difficult in urban environments
  • Very high purchase and maintenance prices (especially as a European import)

Check before buying

  • **Engine noise (V8)**: Listen carefully for a metallic ticking (tick-tick) at idle, an early warning sign of hydraulic lifter failure.
  • **Gearbox behavior**: On models equipped with the 8-speed gearbox, check for the absence of vibrations or hesitations during gear shifts under light load.
  • **Recall history**: The vehicle has been subject to numerous recalls (brakes, transmission, 6.2L engine). Verify via the VIN that everything has been performed.
  • **Oil leaks (3.0L Diesel LM2)**: Inspect the rear main seal, known to seep on some models.

Verdict

The GMC Sierra 1500 (T1XX) is an extremely competent pickup, particularly desirable in its facelifted versions (2022+) which finally offer an interior worthy of its price. **However, the choice of engine is crucial.** The 5.3L and 6.2L V8s, although very pleasant, suffer from a documented design flaw in the cylinder deactivation system (lifters), which represents a major financial risk out of warranty. The recent recall on the 6.2L further darkens the picture for the V8s.

**The best choice is undoubtedly the 3.0L Duramax diesel**, which offers remarkable reliability, perfect torque for towing, and surprisingly low fuel consumption. The 2.7L Turbo 4-cylinder is also an excellent surprise in terms of robustness for daily use. Avoid if possible the 2019-2020 models equipped with the 8-speed automatic gearbox.
