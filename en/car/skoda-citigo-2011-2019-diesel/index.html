Summary

Skoda Citigo I (2011-2019)

The Skoda Citigo is the Czech representative of the Volkswagen Group triplet (NSF project - New Small Family), sharing its platform (PQ12) and components with the Volkswagen Up! and the SEAT Mii. It is an A-segment micro-city car, designed to maximize interior space within a minimal footprint (3.56 m). It received a slight facelift in 2017 (new bumpers, LED daytime running lights, ribbed hood).

⚠️ IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION REGARDING DIESEL:

As an expert, I must correct a common misconception: the Skoda Citigo was NEVER produced with a diesel engine (TDI). The Volkswagen Group deemed a diesel engine too heavy, too expensive to make emissions-compliant, and unsuited to the purely urban purpose of this platform. The analysis will therefore focus on the actually existing powertrains (1.0 MPI petrol, G-TEC natural gas, and the e-iV electric version that appeared at the end of its career), which incidentally offer excellent overall reliability.

Strengths 5

  • Astonishing roominess for 3.56 m in length
  • Excellent overall reliability of the 1.0 MPI (EA211) engine
  • Very low maintenance and insurance costs
  • Safe road handling, worthy of a higher category
  • Very low real-world fuel consumption (often under 5L/100km)

Weaknesses 5

  • Total absence of a diesel engine (for very high mileage drivers, although rare in this segment)
  • Catastrophic ASG automatic gearbox (slow, brutal, fragile)
  • Capricious reverse gear on the manual gearbox (crunching)
  • Light soundproofing on the highway
  • Hard and basic interior plastics (exposed sheet metal on doors)

Check before buying

  • Engaging reverse gear: check if it crunches systematically (a slight stiff point is normal, a loud crunch indicates a clutch or linkage problem).
  • Noise in neutral: a slight clattering that disappears when depressing the clutch indicates a worn clutch release bearing (very common).
  • Water leaks: check for dampness under the front floor mats (windshield cowl drainage problem or door seals).
  • Air conditioning operation: the condenser is very exposed to stone chips at the front.
  • Removable screen (on phase 1): check that it turns on and connects properly to the car.

Official recalls

Official recalls (Sources: Rappel Conso / KBA)

  • [2026-05-22 / 2024-08-09 / 2023-08-07] Takata Airbags (Skoda Code 69DU/69Z9): Massive recalls concerning a deterioration of the propellant in the driver's airbag inflator over time. In the event of deployment, excessive internal pressure can cause the inflator body to rupture and project metal fragments, leading to a risk of serious injury. Affects the Citigo as well as many other models in the range (Fabia, Roomster, Rapid, Octavia, Superb, Yeti, Kodiaq).
  • [2016] Child safety lock (Code 58D1): On certain models produced in 2015 and 2016, the manual child safety lock on the rear doors can deactivate unexpectedly due to a manufacturing defect in the lock.
  • [2013] Airbag control unit: Reprogramming required on the very first models following a risk of non-deployment of the side airbags.

*(Note: The recalls concerning seat seams [2020-03-27] and the specific ACU unit [2022-07-27] mentioned in the raw data concern the Kamiq, Scala, and Superb models, and do not apply to the Citigo).*

What sets it apart 6

Safety

  • Top-tier Safety — The Skoda Citigo achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, ensuring optimal protection for its occupants.

Reliability

  • Proven Mechanical Reliability — Equipped with the robust 1.0 MPI engine, the Citigo is renowned for its excellent overall reliability and very favorable breakdown statistics, ensuring lasting peace of mind.

Economy

  • Ultra-Competitive Running Costs — Benefit from exceptionally low real-world fuel consumption, often below 5L/100km, and some of the lowest maintenance and insurance costs in its class.

Practicality

  • Surprising Interior Space — Despite its compact size of just 3.56 meters, the Citigo offers astonishing interior spaciousness, maximizing room for passengers and their luggage.

Handling

  • Agile and Stable Road Handling — Its agile road handling and reassuringly secure behavior, worthy of a higher category vehicle, make city driving enjoyable and longer journeys serene.

Braking

  • Effective and Progressive Braking — The Citigo delivers strong and progressive braking performance, ensuring predictable and reassuring stopping power in all situations.

Alternatives

  • Americas: Not marketed (the A-segment is almost non-existent there).

Verdict

Expert verdict

The Skoda Citigo is one of the most rational and reliable city cars of its generation. Forget looking for a diesel model, it does not exist. The 3-cylinder 1.0 MPI engine (60 or 75 hp) is a success: it is extremely robust, economical, and perfectly suited to the car.

Buying advice: Absolutely avoid versions equipped with the ASG automated gearbox, whose drivability is disastrous and reliability doubtful. Favor a 1.0 MPI 60 hp version with a manual gearbox (the difference with the 75 hp is imperceptible in the city) with a clear maintenance history. It is a highly recommended purchase for a new driver or as a second urban vehicle.
