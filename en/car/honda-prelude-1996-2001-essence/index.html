Honda Prelude

V (BB6/BB8) · 1996 – 2001

BUY Reliability 82/100 — bon

All Honda models

Summary

The 5th generation Honda Prelude (chassis BB6/BB8/BB9) marks a return to a more classic and angular design, breaking with the futuristic lines of the 4th generation. Designed as a front-wheel-drive Grand Tourer coupé, it shines with its technical excellence, notably thanks to its spirited VTEC engines (H22 series) and advanced chassis technologies: the 4WS (4-wheel steering) system in Europe/Japan, or the ATTS (Active Torque Transfer System) on Type SH/Type S versions, which acts as a torque-vectoring differential. Although heavy for a sports car of its era, its road handling is praised by critics. Today, it is a highly sought-after 'youngtimer' with legendary mechanical reliability, provided the disastrous SportShift automatic gearbox is avoided.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional mechanical reliability of the engines (F and H Series)
  • Impressive road handling (especially with 4WS or ATTS)
  • Low driving position and typical Honda ergonomics
  • Engine character of the 2.2 VTEC (H22)
  • Interior build quality (ages very well)

What sets it apart 6

reliability

  • Legendary Mechanical Reliability — The Honda Prelude 5G is renowned for the exceptional robustness of its gasoline engines (F and H series), guaranteeing remarkable longevity and peace of mind on the road. The 2.0i engine is even considered indestructible.

Performance

  • Pure VTEC Engine Sensations — Discover the explosive character and captivating sound of the 2.2 VTEC engines (H22 series). These blocks offer high-flying performance and pure mechanical sensations, which have become rare in today's automotive landscape.

safety

  • Stunning Road Behavior — Enjoy exceptional, precise, and engaging road holding. Thanks to its balanced chassis, responsive steering, and advanced systems like 4WS or ATTS, the Prelude V offers critically acclaimed dynamism, making it a true driver's car.
  • Confident and Effective Braking — The Prelude V is equipped with a high-performance braking system for its class and era, often featuring ABS. It ensures confident and effective stopping power, contributing to appreciable active safety.

comfort

  • Honda Ergonomics and Interior Quality — Appreciate a low driving position and typical Honda ergonomics, designed for the driver. The interior assembly quality is remarkable and ages very well, offering a pleasant and durable cabin.

economy

  • A Sought-After and Durable Youngtimer — The Prelude 5G is a highly sought-after youngtimer, whose legendary mechanical reliability and build quality make it an excellent investment. The 2.0i engine offers negligible running costs for a coupé.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Peugeot 406 Coupé (more comfortable, V6 available), Fiat Coupé (explosive Turbo engines), Opel Calibra.
  • Japan / Asia: Nissan Silvia S14/S15 (RWD), Toyota Celica T200/T230, Mitsubishi Eclipse.
  • North America: Acura Integra (lighter), Ford Probe / Mercury Cougar, Toyota Celica.
