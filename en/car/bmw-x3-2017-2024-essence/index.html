BMW X3

G01 · 2017 – 2024

BUY Reliability 88/100 — excellent

All BMW models

Summary

The third-generation BMW X3 (code name G01) marks a major turning point compared to its predecessor (F25). Based on the modular CLAR platform, it offers transformed road handling, an interior finish worthy of the upper segment (close to the X5), and remarkable sound insulation.

Launched in late 2017, it benefited from a mid-cycle refresh (LCI - Life Cycle Impulse) in summer 2021, bringing mild hybridization (48V MHEV) to almost all internal combustion engines, an updated infotainment system (iDrive 7), and aesthetic tweaks.

Gasoline Focus: The G01's gasoline engines are based on the modular 'B' engine family (B48 for 4-cylinders, B58 for 6-cylinders). These blocks have proven to be among the most reliable engines ever produced by BMW, erasing the severe timing chain and high-pressure pump issues of older generations (N20/N55). The gasoline X3 G01 is an extremely relevant choice, particularly in the M40i version, which offers an exceptional compromise between performance, driving pleasure, and reliability.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional reliability of gasoline engines (B48 and especially B58)
  • Significantly improved interior finish quality and sound insulation
  • Dynamic road handling for an SUV of this weight
  • Highly ergonomic and responsive iDrive (6 and 7) infotainment system
  • Perfect ZF 8-speed automatic gearbox (Steptronic)

Weaknesses 5

  • Fragile active grille shutters (block and illuminate the engine light)
  • Massive and anxiety-inducing recall campaigns on Diesel engines (EGR)
  • Extreme sensitivity of the xDrive transfer case to tire circumference
  • Firm damping, especially with M Sport suspension and 20/21-inch wheels
  • High maintenance cost at official BMW dealerships

Check before buying

  • Clear service history (closer oil changes preferable)
  • Operation of the grille shutters (should open/close without cracking noise)
  • Absence of jerks during tight turns at low speed (sign of a worn transfer case)
  • Presence of tires with the BMW star marking (*)
  • For diesels: proof of completion of the EGR cooler recall
  • On M40i: traces of coolant seepage around the water pump

Official recalls

- EGR Cooler (2018-2023): Major global recall on Diesel engines (4 and 6 cylinders). Coolant leak into the EGR module potentially creating a flammable mixture with soot. Risk of fire. Replacement of the cooler and sometimes the intake manifold.,- High-Voltage Battery (2020-2021): On PHEV models (xDrive30e), risk of welding slag in the HV battery potentially causing a short circuit. Replacement of affected modules.,- Brake Booster Vacuum Pump (2018): On certain gasoline models, possible loss of brake assistance.,- Steering Rods (2018): Replacement of steering rods on a small series of vehicles due to a manufacturing defect.,- ISOFIX Mounts (2019): Welding defect on rear ISOFIX anchors requiring inspection.

Safety

  • First-Class Passive and Active Safety — The BMW X3 G01 achieved top ratings in safety tests, ensuring maximum protection for all occupants and has been repeatedly awarded for its safety.

Performance

  • Dynamic Driving Behavior and Perfect Automatic Gearbox — Enjoy an engaging driving experience thanks to an agile chassis, precise steering, and the 8-speed ZF automatic gearbox, renowned for its smoothness and responsiveness.

Comfort

  • Premium Interior and Refined Soundproofing — The X3 G01's cabin offers significantly improved finish quality, worthy of a higher segment, and remarkable soundproofing for serene and comfortable journeys. The iDrive system is highly ergonomic.

Braking

  • Powerful and Enduring Braking — The X3 G01's braking system ensures excellent performance, with reassuring power and progression, guaranteeing short stopping distances for increased safety.

Practicality

  • Versatility and Everyday Enjoyment — The BMW X3 G01 combines the elegance of a premium SUV with versatile usability, ideal for both city and long-distance travel, offering a perfect balance of functionality and driving pleasure.

Alternatives

  • Europe: Mercedes-Benz GLC (more comfort-oriented, less refined gasoline engines), Audi Q5 (clinical finish, but TFSI engines a notch below the B58), Porsche Macan (sportier but more expensive and less spacious), Volvo XC60 (excellent PHEV, but sluggish chassis).
  • North America: Acura RDX (excellent value for money, but 4-cylinder only), Genesis GV70 (bold design, powerful but thirsty twin-turbo V6), Lexus RX (absolute reliability, but placid handling).
  • Asia: Lexus NX (very efficient hybrid), Audi Q5L (extended version specific to China).

Verdict

The BMW X3 G01 is undoubtedly one of the most accomplished and reliable premium SUVs of its generation, provided you choose a gasoline engine. The B48 (20i/30i) and especially the masterful 6-cylinder B58 (M40i) engines offer rare peace of mind in the modern premium segment. Diesel versions, although powerful and economical, are marred by the EGR cooler scandal, which requires strict vigilance. For a used purchase, prioritize a gasoline model (M40i if the budget allows, it's a future collector's item) with a rigorous service history and BMW-approved tires to preserve the xDrive transmission.
