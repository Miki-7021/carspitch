Summary

The DS3 Crossback, launched in 2018, marked DS Automobiles' entry into the highly competitive premium urban SUV segment. Based on the CMP (Common Modular Platform) of the PSA Group (now Stellantis), it shares its underpinnings with models like the Peugeot 2008 II and the Opel Mokka II. Its positioning is clearly high-end, relying on a bold design, a refined interior, and embedded technologies. It stands out with its flush door handles, original cabin, and generally appreciated suspension comfort. In 2022, it was restyled and simply renamed DS 3 (dropping the Crossback suffix), with aesthetic and technical evolutions, particularly for the electric E-Tense version. Overall, the DS3 Crossback offers a refined driving experience, but its reliability, especially for petrol engines, requires particular attention on the first model years.

Check before buying

  • For PureTech models: Complete service history, especially timing belt replacement and the type of oil used. Check for abnormal engine noises and the cleanliness of the oil strainer (if possible).
  • For BlueHDi models: AdBlue system operation (absence of warning light, history of failures). Check the DPF status.
  • For E-Tense models: Battery health status (SOH), charging history, operation of electrical and electronic systems.
  • Operation of the touchscreen and multimedia system (bugs, slowness).
  • Condition of tires and rims (large sizes sensitive to impacts).
  • Body panel alignment and operation of flush door handles.

Verdict

The DS3 Crossback is a vehicle that appeals with its style and refinement. However, its purchase requires some vigilance, especially for the first model years.

  • For PureTech (petrol) engines: If you are targeting a model from before 2022, it is imperative to check the complete service history, proof of timing belt replacement according to revised recommendations, and the use of specific engine oil. Prioritize more recent models (after 2022) where belt problems are less frequent or have been corrected at the factory. Urban and suburban use suits it perfectly.
  • For BlueHDi (diesel) engines: They are generally reliable for high-mileage drivers, but be aware of potential AdBlue system problems. A rigorous maintenance history is a plus.
  • For the E-Tense (electric) version: This is an excellent choice for urban and suburban use, offering silence and pleasure. Reliability is good, but range can be a limiting factor for some. Check the battery status.

In summary, the DS3 Crossback is a car with character. If you are willing to perform the necessary checks, especially on the PureTech models, and if rear spaciousness is not your priority, it can be an interesting option on the used car market. Negotiate the price based on the history and any potential work required.
