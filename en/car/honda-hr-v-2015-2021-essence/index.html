Honda HR-V

I (RU) · 2015 – 2021

BUY Reliability 86/100 — excellent

Summary

The Honda HR-V of the RU generation (often considered the 2nd global generation, but marking the model's return after the discontinuation of the first HR-V in 2006) is an urban SUV on the edge of the compact segment. Sold as **Vezel** in Japan and **XR-V** in China, it stands out with record-breaking interior space in its category, made possible by the central positioning of the fuel tank and the integration of the famous *Magic Seats* in the rear. Facelifted in late 2018 (modernized front end, improved sound insulation, arrival of the Sport version), it is renowned for its high overall reliability, particularly with naturally aspirated gasoline engines. Its main drawbacks are an outdated infotainment system and imperfect sound insulation on early model years.

Strengths 5

  • Exceptional spaciousness and unbeatable modularity (Magic Seats)
  • Very high overall mechanical reliability (especially with naturally aspirated gasoline engines)
  • Very low real-world fuel consumption for the 1.5 i-VTEC and 1.6 i-DTEC
  • Dynamic and precise road handling (excellent manual gearbox)
  • Generous trunk volume (470 liters)

What sets it apart 12

Practicality

  • Smart Modularity and Record Space — The HR-V stands out with its exceptional spaciousness and famous "Magic Seats", offering unparalleled flexibility to transport passengers and bulky items. Its generous boot volume of 470 liters is a major asset.

Reliability

  • Exemplary Mechanical Reliability — Recognized for its robustness, the Honda HR-V, particularly with its 1.5 i-VTEC atmospheric petrol engine, offers remarkable peace of mind thanks to its high-level mechanics.

Economy

  • Remarkable Fuel Efficiency — Enjoy very low real-world fuel consumption, especially with the 1.5 i-VTEC engine, making the HR-V an economical choice for daily use without compromising driving pleasure.

Safety

  • Certified Maximum Safety — The Honda HR-V achieved the maximum 5-star rating in Euro NCAP tests, ensuring optimal protection for adults and children on board.
  • Powerful and Reassuring Braking — Benefit from a strong, progressive, and particularly reassuring braking system, ensuring very good stopping distances for perfect vehicle control in all circumstances.
  • Safe and Stable Road Handling — The HR-V offers predictable road holding with excellent grip and well-controlled body roll, providing a stable and competent drive on all types of roads.
