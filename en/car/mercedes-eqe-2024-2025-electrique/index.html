Mercedes-Benz EQE (V295) - Technical update (2024-2025)

Initially launched in 2022, the Mercedes EQE (V295) represents the 100% electric equivalent of the E-Class. For the 2024-2025 model years, Mercedes has carried out a major technical update (often likened to an invisible 'facelift') to correct the teething problems of the early models.

**The major evolutions of this 2024+ generation include:** * The integration **of a heat pump as standard**, drastically improving winter efficiency. * A complete revision of the braking system to correct the spongy and artificial feel of the pedal (a major flaw of the 2022-2023 models). * The addition of a Disconnect Unit (DCU) on 4MATIC models, allowing the front motor to be decoupled at cruising speeds to increase range. * A new battery chemistry on certain versions, optimizing energy density.

Positioned as an ultra-aerodynamic grand tourer, the EQE shines with its acoustic comfort and MBUX interface, although it suffers from a very high weight (over 2.3 tons) and disappointing rear passenger space for its size.

Strengths 6

  • Exceptional aerodynamics (Cd of 0.22) favoring highway efficiency
  • Suspension comfort (especially with the optional AIRMATIC air suspension)
  • Very high-level cabin soundproofing
  • Excellent real-world range on the 350+ version (often > 500 km on the highway)
  • Lifesaving 2024 update (heat pump as standard, better brake feel)
  • Rear-wheel steering (optional) making the car very maneuverable in the city

Weaknesses 5

  • Excessive weight (over 2,350 kg unladen), impacting agility and consumable wear
  • Mediocre rear visibility (tiny rear window)
  • Impractical trunk (classic trunk lid and not a hatchback, 430L volume unworthy of its size)
  • Quality of certain interior plastics (piano black) prone to scratches and creaks
  • Rear headroom limited by the sloping roofline

Check before buying

  • Tire wear: The weight and torque destroy tires quickly (sometimes < 20,000 km).
  • Software update history: Verify that the MBUX system and BMS are up to date.
  • Interior noises: Test the car on rough roads to track down creaks from the center console.
  • Condition of aerodynamic wheels: Very exposed to curbs and expensive to replace.
  • 12V battery health: Electric vehicles place heavy demands on the accessory battery, the #1 source of immobilizing breakdowns.

Verdict

Final verdict

The 2024-2025 generation Mercedes EQE is a formidable electric grand tourer that has managed to correct the mistakes of its first launch year. The addition of the heat pump as standard and the correction of the brake pedal feel transform the driving experience. It is a silent and ultra-comfortable mile-muncher.

However, its pachydermic weight leads to rapid tire wear and its classic trunk limits its family vocation.

**The expert's choice:** The **EQE 350+** is the perfect balance point. It offers the largest battery, the best range, and more than enough power, while freeing itself from the weight and complexity of the front motor of the 4MATIC versions.
