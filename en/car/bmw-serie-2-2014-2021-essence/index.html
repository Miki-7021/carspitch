BMW Série 2

F45/F46 Active/Gran Tourer · 2014 – 2021

NEGOTIATE Reliability 78/100 — bon

Summary

The BMW 2 Series Active Tourer (F45 - 5 seats) and Gran Tourer (F46 - 7 seats) mark a historic turning point for the Bavarian brand: they are the first BMW models to adopt front-wheel drive (UKL2 platform, shared with Mini) and to enter the compact MPV segment.

Despite criticism from purists, this model was a commercial success thanks to its spaciousness, premium finish, and dynamic road handling for its category. A facelift (LCI) occurred in March 2018, bringing aesthetic revisions, an iDrive system update, and the replacement of the 6-speed Aisin automatic gearbox with a 7-speed DKG dual-clutch gearbox on 3-cylinder gasoline engines.

Overall, reliability is good, but the first vintages (2014-2015) of the 3-cylinder gasoline engines (B38) experienced a major design flaw, and diesels were affected by the widespread global recall concerning the EGR valve cooler.

Strengths 5

  • Excellent interior finish, aging very well
  • Spaciousness and modularity (sliding rear bench, flat floor)
  • Dynamic road handling for an MPV (especially with SelectDrive suspension)
  • Overall reliability of 4-cylinder gasoline engines (B48)
  • Very intuitive and responsive iDrive infotainment system

Weaknesses 5

  • Major design flaw on early 3-cylinder gasoline engines (2014-2015)
  • Suspension comfort a bit firm (especially in M Sport trim or with 18/19-inch wheels)
  • Very thick A-pillar (windshield), creating an annoying blind spot in the city
  • DKG7 gearbox (post-2018) sometimes causes jerks at very low speeds
  • Reduced boot volume on the 225xe plug-in hybrid version

Check before buying

  • Service history: check if the engine of a 2014/2015 218i has been replaced or repaired (crankshaft play).
  • EGR recalls: for diesels, demand proof of EGR cooler replacement by BMW.
  • Automatic gearbox: test gear changes when cold and hot. The absence of jerks is crucial. Ask if a gearbox oil change has been performed.
  • Running gear: listen for dull noises over speed bumps (fragile front wishbone bushings around 80,000 km).
  • Electronics: check the functioning of the central screen (some cases of touchscreen delamination) and the electric tailgate.

Official recalls

  • - **EGR Cooler (2018, 2019, 2021, 2022)**: Risk of glycol leakage into the intake, potentially causing a fire. Concerns almost all 3 and 4-cylinder diesel engines produced between 2014 and 2018.
  • - **ISOFIX Mounts (2016)**: Weakness of child seat mounting brackets. Reinforcement in workshop.
  • - **Crankshaft Sensor (2018)**: On 3-cylinder engines (B38), a defective sensor can lead to power loss or stalling.
  • - **Steering Ball Joints (2018)**: Replacement of ball joints on certain models produced in 2018 due to a manufacturing defect.
  • - **High Voltage Battery (2020)**: On 225xe PHEV models produced in 2020, risk of internal short circuit due to impurities in the battery cells.

Verdict

The BMW 2 Series Active/Gran Tourer is an excellent choice for those seeking the practicality of an MPV without sacrificing driving pleasure and premium finish.

**For gasoline models**, absolutely prioritize models produced **after summer 2015** to avoid the crankshaft issue of the 218i engine. The 4-cylinder versions (220i) are models of reliability and driving enjoyment, although rarer. **For diesel models**, caution is advised regarding the history of EGR recalls, but the B47 engines (218d/220d) are excellent for high mileage.

Purchase is recommended provided there is a clear service history and, ideally, an automatic gearbox oil change has been performed if the vehicle exceeds 80,000 km.
