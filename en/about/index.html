About

Carspitch exists to answer one simple, poorly served question: is this used car reliable?

Why this site

Reliability information exists — but it is scattered: a German report here, a British survey there, official recalls buried in a European database almost nobody checks. At buying time, the buyer is left alone with contradictory opinions and forum threads from ten years ago.

Carspitch brings those sources together, model by model, and turns them into a readable report: what holds up, what breaks, what to check before paying.

How the content is produced

Reports are compiled from documented public sources, then structured so models can be compared. Every factual claim — a recall, an engine to avoid, a model year to prefer — points to an identifiable source.

We do not claim to have tested these cars. We are neither a road-test magazine nor a garage. Our work is to compile, verify and make readable information that is otherwise scattered.

What we refuse

  • Paid rankings.
  • Verdicts influenced by an affiliate commission.
  • Content copied from one model to another to fill pages.
  • False promises: no report guarantees the condition of a specific car.
